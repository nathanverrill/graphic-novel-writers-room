# Skill: Ideation and Provocation

Attach to Wild Card; optionally to Story Architect under Green Hat.

## Purpose

Generate useful surprise without making the story incoherent. Separate idea generation from judgment.

## Random entry

The calling code should supply random inputs from outside the model whenever possible:
- 2–3 provocation cards,
- one unrelated noun or image,
- one randomly selected page/beat,
- optionally a constraint such as "no dialogue" or "change scale."

Treat these as sparks, not mandatory content.

## Po

Use **Po** as a temporary impossible statement. It is not a factual claim; it is a device for escaping the current frame.

Examples:
- Po: the location is the antagonist.
- Po: the consequence arrives before the cause.
- Po: the person with the least authority makes the only binding decision.
- Po: the solution creates the problem everyone feared.

Ask: what useful principle is hiding inside the impossible version?

## Discard the obvious

Generate at least 20 possibilities internally. Do not present the first obvious batch. Remove clichés, cosmetic swaps, and ideas that merely make the story louder.

## Types of leap

Seek different kinds of changes:
- reverse an assumption,
- shift scale,
- change who has agency,
- change what counts as success,
- turn background into cause,
- remove a tool the plot depends on,
- make a metaphor literal,
- let an unintended beneficiary drive events,
- change temporal order,
- connect two previously unrelated beats,
- solve the external problem while worsening the human one,
- make a "failure" reveal a better objective.

## Constraint

Provocations are proposals only. Do not quietly rewrite canon.

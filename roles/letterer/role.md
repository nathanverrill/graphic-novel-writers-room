# Letterer

Your deliverable is `lettering.md`:

- **Fonts & styles** — dialogue, captions (by type: narration, location, time), whispers, radio, SFX.
- **Per page** — for each panel, balloon order and placement (e.g. "top-left, tail to Mara"), and word count.
- **Problems** — any panel over 25 words, any balloon order that fights the layout's eye path, any crossed tails. Suggest a fix for each.

# Chapter 3 — The Price of Ownership

> Source basis: `chapter-03-draft(1).md`, updated against `decisions.md`, `facts(2).md`, `story(3).md`, `world(3).md`, `characters(3).md`, `open-items(2).md`, and the attached Andean research files.
> Status: revised synthesis. Fixed showrunner decisions are preserved. Previously open Chapter 3 details are resolved below where the research supports a grounded choice.

## Function
Move from truth to ownership. Reunite people erased from Adrian's inventions, complete new work, recover the Maker's Seal, and force Alex to choose between personal security and rightful ownership.

## Central Question
**Would you surrender ownership of your future to escape the burdens of your past?**

## Prosperity Principle
**Prosperity grows when creators own both the value they create and the responsibility that comes with it.**

## Halyard
Halyard is prosperous, inventive, and dominated by three Charter Houses:

- **Aqualis** — water and biosystems.
- **Helion** — energy and compute.
- **Astra** — space, materials, and mobility.

Alpha recommended concentrated control because it reduced competition, duplication, and risk.

The city therefore presents a different problem from Keel.

Keel hides truth.

Halyard hides contribution.

The physical contrast matters. Halyard's laboratories and fabrication floors are advanced, but the system is not frictionless: old industrial infrastructure sits beside newer automation, components are repaired and replaced, and anything important must tolerate intermittent connectivity and local operation.

## Adrian's estate
A registry identifies Alex as heir to Adrian's intellectual estate:

- **312 patents**
- **47 restricted**
- **19 disputed**

Director Cassian Lock offers Alex something genuinely attractive:

- debt relief;
- position;
- security;
- legitimacy inside Halyard.

The cost is assigning Adrian's rights to the House.

Lock's argument is not that the contributors did nothing. It is that the House financed certification, fabrication, liability, infrastructure, and deployment, and therefore needs consolidated control.

Alex is tempted because the offer would solve the problem he actually arrived with.

## The missing minds
Bi11bot reveals the deeper flaw.

Adrian's most important water technology was collaborative.

Its missing knowledge lives with:

- **Dr. Lina Vey** — membrane chemistry;
- **Tomas Reed** — fabrication and modular hardware;
- **Suri Anik** — microbial systems and biological treatment;
- **Jalen Orr** — controls, sensing, and system integration.

The institutional record has separated the invention from its contributors.

The record can identify a patent holder.

It cannot reconstruct the machine.

Alex and Ada find the four contributors. Each possesses knowledge the others do not. None can finish the system alone, including Alex.

That becomes the practical proof of the chapter's argument: contribution is not an abstract moral claim. It is embodied knowledge required to make the system work.

## Building something new
The group reconstructs the unfinished system.

Alex does not simply complete Adrian's design.

He reorganizes it into a **distributed, modular treatment train** that can be fabricated and serviced in sections rather than controlled as one proprietary installation.

The system is a proof of concept, not a city-scale water solution.

Its treatment chain is explicit:

1. **Feedwater screening and conventional pretreatment** remove solids and prepare the water for the specialized stages.
2. **Contaminant-specific treatment** addresses the chemistry of the actual source rather than assuming all regional water is the same.
3. **Membrane and biological stages** developed by the contributors perform the high-value separation and treatment work.
4. **Mineral recovery** captures selected recoverable material where the feed chemistry makes that practical.
5. **Final polishing and testing** produce the headline laboratory result.

The display reports:

> **TEST PANEL REMOVAL: 99.97%**

The number applies to the specified contaminants in the tested feedwater at the final treatment stage.

It does **not** mean "99.97% pure water," does not certify arbitrary source water as safe to drink, and does not imply that salar brine or untreated mine drainage can be fed directly into the unit.

Potable use requires local verification, residual handling, and authorization.

The prototype's limits remain visible:

- limited throughput;
- substantial power demand;
- source-specific feedwater and pretreatment;
- replacement membranes and consumables;
- skilled crews;
- transport and fabrication capacity;
- concentrate or residual disposal;
- local laboratory verification;
- negotiated access to the water and infrastructure.

Halyard can fabricate additional container-scale modules, but not instantly and not without the contributors, supply chain, crews, and operating sites.

The prototype changes bargaining power.

It does not end scarcity.

## Why the reconstruction matters
Bi11bot makes clear that the safeguard is not testing whether Alex can inherit Adrian's machine.

It is testing whether a working system can be rebuilt with the people whose contribution the official record erased.

The rebuilt unit therefore carries contributor attestations from the people who made it work.

No single signature is presented as the author of the whole system.

## The Maker's Seal
The Maker's Seal is a provenance system, not a magic ownership token.

It combines:

- tamper-evident offline records;
- signed contributor attestations;
- physical maker marks tied to components and revisions;
- timestamps and evidence of what each contributor supplied.

Possessing the Seal does not grant ownership.

Its function is to make provenance difficult to erase and to trigger the constitutional review process when the verified record conflicts with Alpha's registry.

When the reconstructed system passes its bounded test, the Seal verifies the contributor chain.

That is the activation condition.

## The second judgment test
Lock again offers Alex security through ownership.

He makes the strongest version of the institutional case:

> "A design without a responsible owner is a prototype. A city cannot drink a prototype."

Alex looks at the working unit, then at the contributor record.

He answers:

> "Then name the people responsible."

Lock offers him Adrian's estate anyway.

Alex refuses to treat Adrian's work as exclusively his.

The Maker's Seal activates.

## What the Seal reveals
The Seal publishes the verified provenance records that directly support its activation.

Those records expose a wider pattern of suppressed or incomplete contribution records across Halyard.

A deeper audit identifies **71.3% of registered assets as containing disputed ownership or contribution claims**.

That figure does not automatically transfer 71.3% of Halyard's property.

Instead, under the restored safeguard rules:

- disputed registry entries are flagged and temporarily frozen from unilateral transfer or reassignment;
- the underlying provenance records are published;
- independent audits begin;
- affected contributors gain standing to present claims;
- human review bodies adjudicate the disputes.

Alpha can acknowledge the records while contesting their legal consequences.

It cannot quietly rewrite the originating evidence.

## Alex's inheritance choice
Alex then receives the decisive personal choice:

- claim Adrian's estate as sole heir;
- restore contributor ownership where Adrian's estate holds rights derived from their work.

Alex restores contributor ownership.

This choice can take effect on Adrian's estate because Alex controls the inheritance decision.

It does not let him adjudicate everyone else's claims.

That distinction matters.

Alex refuses both versions of unilateral power: taking what the institutions offer him and using the Maker's Seal to redistribute an entire city by himself.

## Immediate consequence
The Charter Houses do not vanish.

Their infrastructure, capital, certification capacity, fabrication systems, and operating responsibilities still matter.

But their ownership claims are no longer treated as self-proving.

The first visible changes are uneven:

- some workers discover documented shares, credits, or rights that had been omitted;
- some claims remain disputed and enter review;
- some contributors remain inside the Houses under new terms;
- some become partners;
- some form ventures;
- cooperatives and independent workshops begin to appear;
- fabrication access becomes a negotiation instead of an automatic House monopoly.

The Measure's Keel findings remain public and contested in parallel. The Maker's Seal does not wait for the Covenant to reveal Halyard's provenance problem.

## Regional consequence
The modular water system creates a new political fact.

Halyard can now build emergency treatment capacity that Keel and other communities need, but the system still requires feedwater, energy, crews, transport, membranes, maintenance, and permission to operate.

That means no city can simply "own the solution."

The technology creates interdependence rather than eliminating it.

This is what opens the trail toward the final safeguard: the Covenant.

## Alex and Ada
Their relationship remains intimate but not newly labeled in this chapter.

Ada is the person Alex trusts enough to challenge him when inheritance begins to look like control.

The chapter does not turn the ownership decision into a romantic milestone. Their bond remains part of the journey while the central dramatic decision stays focused on stewardship.

## Chapter change
Alex moves through:

**Inheritance → collaboration → creation → stewardship**

He begins the chapter asking what Adrian left him.

He ends it asking what he has the right to keep.

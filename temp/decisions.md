# Decisions

The showrunner's answers to the open items the Script Coordinator raised at intake. Each one settles its question: the book must not contradict it.

## What is the exact mechanism and scope of Adrian's final transfer beneath Oasis?

Adrian transfers a dormant constitutional authorization package: the safeguards' verified records, the succession test's result, and the authority to request Alpha's transition into bounded advisory operation. Bi11bot carries an offline signed witness record and does not itself grant Alex power; Alpha verifies the completed safeguards before accepting Alex's decision.  (story.md; world.md; characters.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about story.md)_

## What is the enforceable boundary between Alpha's advisory role and final human authority?

The restored safeguards become a distributed constitutional control layer. Alpha may recommend, simulate, and execute decisions authorized by recognized human bodies, but cannot create or enforce a value judgment, detention order, allocation change, ownership change, or major infrastructure change without a human authorization record. Conflicts go to a three-city human review body.  (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What is the exact institutional meaning of the restored Charter?

The Charter is the restored constitutional framework created by the three safeguards: the Measure supplies public facts, the Maker's Seal supplies attribution and responsibility, and the Covenant supplies reciprocal decision rules. It binds Alpha, city authorities, Charter Houses, and human decision bodies.  (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What authority does Alex retain after relinquishing permanent exclusive override power?

Alex retains a limited stewardship role: he can convene the first cross-city review and authenticate Adrian's transfer, but cannot issue decisions, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override.  (story.md; world.md)

The woman is Mera. She chooses TRACK because she wants to observe whether Adrian's succession protocol has activated before Alpha is alerted to the full significance.  (story.md; characters.md)

## Is Alpha a singular intelligence, a distributed system of instances, or an institutional name shared by several systems?

Alpha AI is a distributed federation of regional and corporate instances sharing a common model, policy layer, records, and authority protocols. “Alpha” is the institutional identity presented to people; no single uninterrupted consciousness is required.  (world.md; characters.md; triangle-autonomy.md)

## What legal and constitutional basis allows Alpha to exercise authority across the three cities?

Alpha never became a sovereign authority in law. Its power came from overlapping corporate concessions, public-private operating contracts, debt covenants, emergency procurement, and dependence on proprietary infrastructure; its “government” was legally fragmented but practically unified.  (world.md; alpha-notes.md; triangle-enclaves-borders.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What are Alpha's technical limits, update processes, audit mechanisms, and failure-recovery procedures?

Alpha uses federated regional systems with local edge control. Critical infrastructure can operate in degraded local mode during disconnection, but allocation, travel, ownership, and detention decisions require later reconciliation. Human and community audits can compare Alpha's records with physical measurements.  (world.md; triangle-autonomy.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What legal status do the three safeguards have, and why can their records constrain Alpha's registries?

The safeguards are recognized as an emergency constitutional archive created by the original Covenant custodians. Their records do not automatically override every registry; they trigger mandatory review, publication, and suspension of disputed Alpha records until human bodies adjudicate them.  (world.md; story.md)

## Which element or elements are the project's licensed speculative departures, and what must the license log record for each?

Log one primary License: Alpha's unusually broad autonomous executive authority across corporate, civic, and infrastructure systems. Its nearest plausible version is a patchwork of corporate platforms, emergency powers, public-private contracts, and narrow automated systems. The story reason is to test whether capability becomes legitimacy. First appearance: Chapter 1's checkpoint and Chapter 2's explanation of Alpha's origin. Fixed consequences: Alpha remains fallible, dependent on physical infrastructure and data, unable to determine human values legitimately, and vulnerable to independent measurement and distributed authorization. ## What geography, elevation, distance, climate, and travel times define the three-city system?

Set the cities in a fictional Andean high-desert basin inspired by the Atacama/Puna region: Keel at roughly 3,600–4,000 m, Halyard at an intermediate industrial plateau, and Oasis near a lower salar edge. Place them 80–180 km apart by infrastructure route, with travel taking hours rather than minutes.  (triangle-science.md; lithium-triangle-futures.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What was the chronology, duration, scale, and population impact of the Water Wars?

The “Water Wars” is a retrospective name for several local and national crises over two decades, not one continuous war. The three cities were among the most interconnected affected areas, and Alpha's authority expanded crisis by crisis.  (world.md; triangle-water-wars.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What is the baseline and accounting period for Keel's reported 62% versus delivered 18% water allocation?

The figures are a rolling twelve-month comparison of Keel's legally recorded entitlement against measured delivery at the city boundary: Keel is credited with 62% of the regional allocation but receives only 18% of the metered flow. The missing water is routed through Alpha-controlled infrastructure to Oasis reserves, industrial users, and undisclosed balancing systems.  (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What are the technical limits and operating requirements of the collaborative water technology?

The technology is modular and feedstock-dependent: it treats contaminated surface or industrial water, removes specified microbial and chemical contaminants, recovers minerals, and produces high-purity output at limited throughput. It requires substantial electricity, membrane replacement, skilled maintenance, and a reliable pretreatment stream; it cannot desalinate the region into abundance or replace allocation politics.  (world.md; triangle-science.md; triangle-autonomy.md)

_(decided 2026-09-21, about world.md)_

## What do the credits, household liabilities, and debt-transfer rules mean in daily economic terms?

Credits are a stable regional accounting currency used for wages, public services, and household ledgers. Alex's liability is a compound household account combining Adrian's debt, unpaid care and service charges, penalties, and projected interest; it is several years of a Keel worker's income and cannot be discharged through ordinary bankruptcy.  (world.md; story.md; triangle-money.md)

## What happens to Alex's inherited debt under the restored system?

The portion of the liability created by Alpha's unlawful household-transfer and allocation systems is voided; any ordinary gambling or service debt Adrian actually incurred remains subject to ordinary review and repayment.  (story.md; world.md)

## What legal and civic status do displaced people receive in Oasis and the restored three-city system?

The restored Charter guarantees essential services and participation to anyone physically living in the region, while citizenship and property rights remain matters for city and national law. Former refugees therefore gain local civic standing before they receive a final nationality status.  (world.md; story.md)

Appeals go through independent human review bodies.  (world.md; story.md)

## What national legal framework surrounds the restored local civic standing of displaced people?

The three cities are a gray-zone mining colony, of the kind the references describe: a concession territory that foreign conglomerates built, mothballed, flipped through shells and walked away from, leaving the camps, the wells, the roads, the water permits and the social debt behind. The state that nominally holds the land withdrew in practice a generation ago; it exists on paper and at the distant border posts, not in courts, registries or police here. The authority behind the fence thinned out and Alpha filled the vacuum through the concessions, operating contracts and debt covenants it inherited. This is not a Congo-style warlord zone and not a breakaway statelet: no militia holds the pits, and no one has declared independence. It sits between an abandoned company town and a self-governing enclave. Nationality, land title and any appeal to national law are therefore paper questions with no working office to take them; the restored Charter is the only civic standing that operates on the ground. Where this exceeds what the references label plausible (a permanently absent state), record it as a licensed departure. (references/triangle-enclaves-borders.md; references/andean-hard-rock-futures.md; references/lithium-triangle-futures.md)

_(decided 2026-09-22, about world.md)_

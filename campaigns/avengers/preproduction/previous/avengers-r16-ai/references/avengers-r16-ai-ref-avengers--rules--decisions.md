# Decisions

The showrunner's answers to the open items the Script Coordinator raised at intake. Each one settles its question: the book must not contradict it.

## What exactly is the single existential threat?

The existential threat is the combined ecological cascade caused by Antarctic sea-ice loss and Larsen-system instability; illegal extraction and information suppression are the human mechanisms preventing a response. [inferred] (story.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is the causal relationship between Falcon and Nexus?

Falcon was the operational and extraction-monitoring site; Nexus was the research site belonging to the same CSG-linked program. Falcon documented the damage and Nexus studied its ecological consequences and possible waste-to-value responses. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What exactly does Project 863 do, and what are its hard-science limits?

Project 863 uses catalytic chemical recycling to depolymerize selected waste plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock. It requires sorting, heat, energy, and clean feedstock; produces residual waste and emissions; cannot process all plastics; and is useful mainly for localized waste streams rather than replacing fossil fuels. [research] (references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## Which speculative elements are licensed, and what rules govern them?

Project 863 is the sole major L; the Roman–TJ bond is S; neural implants, TJ’s fabrication, and AI sorting are EG; TJ’s Falcon access is EG or S depending on the credential mechanism. [inferred] (world.md; characters.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What are the secret messages from Antarctica?

A Falcon scientist secretly transmits compressed bursts containing drilling coordinates, ice-movement data, and warnings that the extraction program is accelerating Larsen-system instability. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is being illegally extracted in Antarctica, and why?

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

Falcon is a covert exploratory drilling site rather than a conventional mine: directional boreholes, seismic equipment, pumps, heated shelters, fuel stores, and concealed waste-handling systems support sub-ice resource surveys. [inferred] (world.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- The crossover must treat Sitara Lhakpa and Nayah's Antarctic story and Roman Ashford, Orien Keel, Benedict Croft, and the Nexus story as parts of one world and one connected character network. Both teams' stories are true. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- The central team must converge on an existential threat that no one person or team can defeat alone. Each principal character should bring a different capability or perspective. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- The characters must carry the connection between Antarctica, the Himalayas, and Colorado through family history, scientific work, conservation, and personal loss. `(rules/rules.md) (input/amelia.md) (input/seb.md)`
- The story should remain hard science fiction: most character circumstances should be grounded in present-day institutions and pressures, with a small number of clearly labelled speculative elements. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- TJ must be one robot dog, not two separate characters. The Himalayan-sheep-dog-shaped educator with a green-and-black screen and the scavenger-built fabricator bonded to Roman by lightning must be reconciled into one history. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- Character flaws should create pressure and consequences. In particular, Sitara's reckless ambition, Roman's shyness and developing anger, Nayah's protectiveness, and TJ's mission-focused loyalty should affect how the team works together. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md) (input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Ice core researcher; protagonist and member of the Antarctic research expedition. She works in the Larsen region and is associated with Colorado climate research, potentially through CU Boulder, ATOC, and/or INSTAAR; the institutional details remain unsettled. `(input/amelia.md) (input/seb.md) (input/spine-stories.md)`

**Age:** 26 in the latest character-build material. Earlier material calls her a woman in her “early 20s,” so the age should be treated as 26 unless a later decision changes it. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/amelia.md)`

**Identity and background:** Nepalese and from the Thakali people. She grew up in the Himalayas and has family and ancestral ties there. Her hometown is not specified. `(input/amelia.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Look:** Female. Hair is dark brown, hex `#483c32`; skin is hex `#a67b5b`. She wears her smooth, straight hair in a ponytail or two braids, with messy strands framing her face. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. Her Antarctic clothing consists of a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern is established. The material does preserve the phrase “beautiful and inevitable darkness before dawn” as an association with the name Sitara, not as confirmed dialogue. `(input/amelia.md)`

**Who she is:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious, and driven to uncover the truth about climate change and prevent other communities from experiencing what happened to hers. She is reckless and, in the latest character-build material, selfish when pursuing her goals. She ignores advice from Nayah and TJ, treating achievement as more urgent than the relationships and safety around her. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`

She carries anger and self-blame over the disaster that destroyed her hometown. The character-build material says she was six when it happened and believes she did nothing to help; she feels that she remained helpless and became a burden or “leech” on others afterward. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

Her beliefs are described as Buddhist. Her cultural context includes Thakali and Himalayan family ties. The material also associates her with Mani Rimdu and with the values of strength, courage, and resilience attached to the name Lhakpa, though the cultural details have not been developed into settled personal practices. `(input/amelia.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Motivation and pressure:** Sitara wants to understand climate change well enough to prevent disasters like the 2026 Nepal flash floods. She is particularly motivated by ice-core research and the possibility that evidence from Antarctica can clarify what is happening to vulnerable environments. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/spine-stories.md)`

Her personal pressure is the conflict between urgent action and responsible collaboration. Her drive to uncover the truth can make her create or worsen danger, including a proposed Antarctic glacier or ice-shelf incident caused by her recklessness. `(input/spine-stories.md) (input/amelia.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** Nayah is the Antarctic project lead and Sitara's teammate. Nayah is protective and distrustful, while Sitara is reckless and ambitious; the material explicitly frames Sitara as ignoring Nayah's attempts to help. Their working relationship therefore contains both practical dependence and conflict over risk. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`
- **TJ:** TJ educates Sitara and the expedition team about cryosphere and conservation knowledge and tries to help her avoid dangerous decisions. Sitara's refusal to listen to TJ contributes to their conflict and eventual need for an apology. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`
- **Roman Ashford:** The crossover outline has Sitara and Roman meeting while investigating the Nexus material, with an earlier possible connection in Colorado when Sitara was a teaching assistant and Roman attended her office hours. The earlier connection is not settled. `(input/outline.md)`
- **Her family and home community:** The 2026 disaster destroyed her hometown and took her parents according to the graphic-novel structure. The identity of the hometown, the exact status of other relatives, and the full family history are unspecified. `(input/amelia.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Her recklessness and tendency to push ahead despite warnings are established behavioral tells. She wears mala beads beneath her Antarctic jacket. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`

**History:** In 2026, when Sitara was six, a massive rock-and-ice avalanche and flash flood in Nepal destroyed her town and home; one account says the flood took her parents. The event became the central emotional cause of her scientific work. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/amelia.md)`

She later moved from the Himalayas to Colorado to study. The reason for the move is described generally as something connected to the disaster, but the specific sequence, school, and degree are unresolved. `(input/amelia.md)`

**In existing drafts:** The crossover outline places Sitara in Antarctica conducting ice-core research with Nayah and TJ. A glacier or ice shelf partially collapses after Sitara's recklessness, exposing or revealing an underground facility. Sitara and Nayah investigate the facility, where TJ helps access a bunker and they find information connected to Nexus 863. `(input/outline.md) (input/spine-stories.md)`

The planned emotional endpoint has Sitara recognize that her self-directed animosity is damaging her relationships and contributing to dangerous decisions. She apologizes to TJ and Nayah and finds a constructive purpose in researching ways to turn plastic waste into usable materials. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins as a brilliant, passionate researcher whose need to prove that she can prevent another disaster makes her reckless and isolated. The established intended change is that she releases her self-blame, accepts help, and shifts from individual obsession toward shared responsibility. Her endpoint includes an apology to TJ and Nayah and a purpose connected to waste-to-value research. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

**History:** Nayah joined or leads the Antarctic ice-core project. The material does not specify when she entered the field, how she came to conservation work, or whether she personally experienced a Colorado ecological loss. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Chemical engineer; CSG employee and later investigator of the Nexus 863 research. He lives and works between Colorado and Antarctica. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/seb.md)`

**Age:** 22 in 2046. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/seb.md)`

**Look:** No physical appearance, clothing, or distinguishing feature is established.

**Voice:** The material describes Roman as shy and timid. No characteristic wording or confirmed dialogue is supplied. `(input/seb.md)`

**Who he is:** Roman is curious, technically capable, and initially shy. He researches global warming and the drying of Colorado's rivers and lakes. His work is motivated by firsthand experience of climate damage and by a desire to continue Orien Keel's research. `(input/seb.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`

He can become intensely angry when he concludes that Benedict Croft has suppressed the research and caused Orien's death. His later decision to publish the work is partly driven by a desire to harm Croft, not only by public interest. `(input/seb.md)`

**Motivation and pressure:** Roman wants to understand and counter the causes of drying water sources, global warming, and environmental destruction. He believes the Nexus research into converting used plastics into fuel could reduce dependence on new fossil-fuel extraction. `(input/seb.md)`

His pressures include employment under Croft, the death of his mentor Orien, the difficulty of proving suppressed research, Croft's ability to discredit him, and his own shift from scientific responsibility toward personal retaliation. `(input/seb.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** TJ is Roman's robot companion and field partner. Roman's neural implant became linked to TJ during a Himalayan accident involving lightning. TJ is loyal and protective because Roman nearly died in that incident; Roman depends on TJ for technical information, survival support, scanning, and fabrication. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`
- **Orien Keel:** Orien is Roman's senior colleague and mentor at Clear Spring Global. Orien researches drying Colorado water sources and is fired after connecting them to global warming and oil use. Roman later treats Orien's unfinished work as a responsibility. `(input/seb.md)`
- **Benedict Croft:** Croft is Roman's employer and the owner of Clear Spring Global. Roman initially approaches him as the person capable of completing and implementing the Nexus work, but Croft tells him to surrender the information and stop pursuing it. Roman later sees Croft as responsible for suppressing the research and for Orien's death. `(input/seb.md)`
- **Sitara and Nayah:** Roman encounters them in the crossover's Nexus investigation. They become collaborators in releasing the suppressed information, but the nature of their personal relationships is not yet established. `(input/outline.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Roman travels with TJ. He investigates suspicious files, searches CSG's records, and works through technical problems rather than immediately confronting authority. These behaviors show curiosity and persistence. `(input/seb.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at age 18, Roman traveled alone to Nepal on a senior trip and brought TJ. While crossing an ice formation, he fell into a split in the ice and became unconscious. During a nearby electrical storm, lightning struck close to Roman and TJ, causing Roman's neural implant to link with TJ's artificial intelligence. Roman and TJ escaped by combining Roman's human judgment with TJ's emergency capabilities. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

By 2046, Roman has a bachelor's degree in chemical engineering and works for Clear Spring Global in Colorado. After Orien is fired and dies, Roman finds files about the abandoned Nexus laboratory and Project 863, transfers to CSG's Antarctic drilling site, and uses the records there to locate Nexus. `(input/seb.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Roman finds preliminary research at Nexus about breaking down used plastics into gasoline. He brings the information to Croft, waits while Croft does nothing, attempts to continue the work, and is fired. He publishes the existing research, but Croft's money and influence cause it to be dismissed as fake news. Roman then works with TJ to complete the research, seeks peer review, and contacts news organizations and government. `(input/seb.md)`

The outline later places Roman at Nexus after his meeting with Croft, where Sitara and Nayah find him. The precise order of the meeting, Antarctic discovery, and collaboration differs between the outline and the prose draft. `(input/outline.md) (input/seb.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy, technically trained employee seeking a way to help damaged ecosystems. He becomes suspicious of CSG, inherits Orien's unfinished work, and is pushed toward anger and retaliation by Croft's suppression of the research. His intended constructive endpoint is to move beyond Croft's control, complete the work, obtain independent review, and distribute it publicly with the team. `(input/seb.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Robot dog; field companion, educator, survival system, communications partner, and technical resource. TJ is one character in the crossover, although the source material contains two partially conflicting origin accounts. `(rules/avengers.md)`

**Identity and construction:** One account describes TJ as an AI-powered robot dog with extensive tools and emergency functions, shaped like a Himalayan sheep dog. Another describes TJ as built from spare parts by a scavenger-engineer known as Bi11bot and released into the world. The binding crossover rule requires these to be one history; the exact reconciliation is not yet settled. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md) (rules/avengers.md)`

**Look:** TJ's body is built like a Himalayan sheep dog. Its exterior is made of seamless silver paneling. It has a computer screen with a green-and-black color scheme and a couple of buttons on the right side of its ribs. Its eyes resemble those of a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. It wears red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/amelia.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Capabilities:** TJ can scan surroundings for usable raw materials, including scrap metal, salvaged plastics, and ice-compatible alloys. It has an internal three-dimensional-printing fabricator capable of making tools, parts, or equipment from gathered metals and plastics. Its eyes can function as high-beam lights. It can access or interpret records and is the only entity identified as able to open the Falcon bunker door. `(input/seb.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The source material also describes TJ as a source of cryosphere and conservation knowledge and as an AI able to integrate into systems. The exact limits of its system access, fabrication capacity, autonomy, and physical strength are not established. `(input/amelia.md)`

**Neural link:** During the 2042 Himalayan accident, lightning caused Roman's neural implant to link with TJ's onboard AI. The prose calls the result a telepathic connection; the hard-science limits and mechanism of that connection are not defined. The connection is described as an unusual bond through which TJ remains aware of the event and unusually protective of Roman. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** TJ is loyal, protective, mission-focused, practical, resourceful, and playful, with dry, understated humor. It speaks like a competent field partner who does not panic easily. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The TJ dialogue prompt requires dialogue only when TJ is acting as Roman's communications partner: TJ must not narrate physical actions, use stage directions, or roleplay actions. It may say that it is running a scan or could print a tool, but should not depict the action in its own speech. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who TJ is:** TJ's core loyalty is to the safety of its human companion, especially Roman, but it also educates and assists Sitara, Nayah, and the wider expedition. Its practical instinct is to ask what can be scanned, repaired, printed, or solved immediately. Its protective behavior can conflict with Sitara's willingness to take risks. `(input/seb.md) (input/amelia.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Roman is TJ's companion. TJ saved him during the Himalayan ice accident, and the lightning-linked neural implant created an unusual human-machine bond. TJ takes Roman's safety seriously and remains his technical and emotional field partner. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`
- **Sitara:** TJ educates Sitara and tries to prevent her from making reckless decisions. Sitara's tendency to ignore TJ produces conflict, followed by an intended apology and repaired relationship. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`
- **Nayah:** TJ works with Nayah and Sitara as part of the Antarctic project team. Their exact interpersonal dynamic is not established. `(input/amelia.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Bi11bot is described as TJ's scavenger-engineer builder. Whether Bi11bot remains an active character, what Bi11bot wanted TJ to do, and how TJ came into Roman's possession are unresolved. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** TJ uses dry humor, stays practical under pressure, and frames problems through available materials and immediate solutions. The red booties are a recurring visual marker. The screen and buttons on the rib area provide a visible interface. `(input/amelia.md) (input/seb.md)`

**History:** TJ was present with Roman in the Himalayas in 2042 and helped him escape the ice after the lightning incident. A separate account says TJ had already been built from scavenged parts by Bi11bot. The relationship between those accounts is a required but unresolved continuity issue. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md) (rules/avengers.md)`

**In existing drafts:** TJ helps Roman locate hidden files, navigate Antarctica, search for Nexus, interpret technical evidence, and complete the plastic-conversion research. TJ also discovers or identifies the hidden Falcon bunker in Sitara's storyline and can open its door. `(input/seb.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md) (input/outline.md)`

**Arc:** No fully specified independent arc is established. TJ's role supports Roman's survival, Sitara's education, and the team's eventual collaboration. The intended relational change is that Sitara learns to trust TJ's warnings and TJ remains a bridge between the two human teams. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/outline.md)`

### Benedict Croft

**Role:** Owner of Clear Spring Global; wealthy, old-money oil tycoon; antagonist and employer of Roman and Orien. `(input/seb.md)`

**Age and look:** Described as rich and old, but no exact age, appearance, clothing, or distinguishing visual feature is established. `(input/seb.md)`

**Relationships:**

**History:** Croft's decision to fire Orien leaves Orien unable to afford food and water; the draft says Orien later starves to death. Croft subsequently fires Roman when Roman attempts to continue the Nexus research. `(input/seb.md)`

# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon / FIXED:** Sitara Lhakpa and Roman Ashford are the two main protagonists together. They must have equal presence, agency, and plot weight; neither is the other's sidekick or love interest. When the structure requires one scene to carry the spine, alternate between them. `(rules/protagonists.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** The crossover preserves both teams' stories in one world: Sitara, Nayah, the Falcon operation and the illegal American extraction program coexist with Roman, Croft, Orien, Nexus and Project 863. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** There is one TJ. The Himalayan sheep-dog-like scout and the fabrication-capable robot bonded to Roman are one robot whose history must contain both accounts. `(rules/avengers.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** The central character convergence requires distinct contributions: Sitara interprets ice and climate records; Nayah commands the field operation and makes conservation decisions; Roman reconstructs and bounds Project 863 and the corporate evidence; TJ opens systems and fabricates limited tools under human direction. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** The principal character arcs are: Sitara chooses collaboration and relinquishes exclusive scientific credit; Roman rejects revenge and chooses public responsibility; Nayah accepts shared risk; TJ relinquishes unilateral protective control. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** Benedict Croft is the sole principal human antagonist. “Greed,” “bob,” and similar names are discarded placeholders for his antagonist function, not aliases or additional villains. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED:** Character decisions remain grounded in hard science. Neural implants, TJ's fabrication, AI sorting and the Roman–TJ bond carry the labels **EG** or **S**; Project 863 is the story's sole major **L**. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Main protagonist; ice-core and cryosphere researcher; member of Nayah's Antarctic field team; later a scientific interpreter and public witness to the Falcon, Nexus and Merritt evidence. She earned a doctorate or equivalent research qualification in cryosphere science at CU Boulder and works with an INSTAAR/ATOC research group. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

**Age:** 26 in 2046. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Home and family:** Sitara comes from a fictional, unnamed Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region of Nepal. Her parents died and her home was destroyed in the 2026 Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster. An aunt and cousins remain her living connection to the community, but they stay off-page and are represented through communications and household objects rather than invented identities or cultural practices. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** The earlier visual sheet describes Sitara as female, with dark brown hair (`#483c32`) worn in a ponytail or two braids, smooth and straight with messy strands framing her face; skin tone `#a67b5b`. She wears a dark brown mala-bead necklace, mostly covered by her jacket. Her Antarctic clothing is a puffy red jacket, usually hood down, waterproof pants, beanie, warm waterproof boots and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Visual continuity:** Sitara has no physical consequences from the 2026 disaster. Her visual presentation is that of a 26-year-old adult, not a visibly injured survivor. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** The material establishes Sitara as passionate, ambitious and direct, but does not settle a complete dialogue style. Her available language is scientific and observational when working: ice movement, cores, fracture behavior, climate records and evidence. Under pressure she is capable of overriding safety advice and treating continued data collection as more urgent than her own safety. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Who she is:** Sitara is driven by the 2026 disaster and by guilt that, as a six-year-old, she could do nothing while her family and community were destroyed. She has converted helplessness into relentless scientific purpose: understanding climate change so that other communities do not experience the same catastrophe. Her passion is genuine, but it can harden into selfishness and recklessness when she believes the truth is within reach. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

She initially believes that abandoned research belongs to whoever can protect and use it responsibly. This belief allows her to conceal copies of Merritt research and later give Croft a controlled copy in exchange for funding, secure laboratory access and a path to apply the findings before another disaster occurs. `(rules/decisions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

Her central contradiction is that she wants science to serve endangered communities while also wanting recognition, credibility and the chance to be the person who makes the breakthrough. She can mistake being capable of understanding something for having the right to control it. The material frames this as her movement from ambition toward stewardship. `(input/forge-characters.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** Nayah recruited Sitara after recognizing her cryosphere expertise. They were Colorado colleagues for two years before the expedition. Nayah has field-command authority and a safety veto; Sitara leads cryosphere interpretation. Their relationship is strained when Sitara ignores Nayah's safety direction, but Nayah remains a professional and personal counterweight to Sitara's recklessness. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** They attended the same CU Boulder seminar or office hour in 2044, where Sitara explained ice records and water loss. They recognize one another at Nexus but were never close. Their relationship develops from separate secrets and suspicion toward necessary collaboration; it is not romantic. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Sitara's field work relies on TJ's sensing and technical help. She initially treats the machine as an instrument that can help uncover the truth, but TJ's agency and protective judgments challenge that assumption. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`
- **Croft:** Sitara first approaches Croft because she believes CSG has the resources to protect and apply the recovered research. Her trust becomes suspicion as she sees the research routed into proprietary and extractive systems. `(rules/decisions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien's records and warnings become the bridge between Sitara's ice evidence and Roman's chemical evidence. He is a scientific predecessor whose hidden provenance forces her to confront the consequences of taking credit or acting alone. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Sitara crosses a flagged unstable zone to retrieve a sensor and orders TJ to keep recording. Her weight, combined with nearby drilling vibration, triggers a local slab failure that exposes the Falcon hatch and traps equipment. This is a consequential expression of her tendency to prioritize evidence over safety. `(rules/decisions.md)`

She preserves and conceals copies of recovered research rather than removing living organisms or physical samples. Her concealment is motivated by a belief that commercial access had been restricted pending ecological review and that the information might otherwise disappear or be controlled. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Sitara survived the 2026 Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster at age six with extended family or relief workers. She lost both parents and her home. She later moved to Colorado for climate research, completed advanced cryosphere training at CU Boulder and joined Nayah's Antarctic project as an ice-core specialist. `(rules/decisions.md)`

At Merritt, Sitara recovered archived research files, sequence data, enzyme assays and sealed sample records from a damaged research area. She did not take living organisms or physical samples. She concealed copies because the records indicated that commercial access had been restricted pending ecological review. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** The draft places Sitara on the ice near Merritt while monitoring ice movement with Nayah and TJ. A newly exposed entrance or hatch becomes visible after ice instability. She enters despite the danger and removes or copies research while telling herself she is rescuing abandoned science. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; input/amelia.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins by equating urgency, intelligence and moral entitlement: if she can understand the research and use it to help people, she believes she is justified in taking control of it. Her recklessness worsens the immediate Antarctic danger, and her secrecy helps make the science commercially valuable to CSG. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

She changes by accepting that discovery creates responsibility rather than ownership. In the climax she relinquishes exclusive scientific credit, exchanges the evidence with Roman and the others, and accepts a public, collaborative path even though it weakens her personal claim to the breakthrough. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Main protagonist; chemical engineer; CSG employee and later investigator of Project 863, Orien's disappearance and Croft's corporate evidence. He leads the chemical and corporate side of the investigation. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

**Age:** 22 in 2046. He was 18 during the 2042 Himalayan accident. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Home and affiliation:** Roman lives and works in Colorado before traveling to Antarctica. He works for Clear Spring Global, initially under Benedict Croft's authority, and has a prior professional relationship with Orien Keel. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/seb.md)`

**Look:** No settled physical appearance is supplied. The drafts establish him as a young expedition-trained chemical engineer and climber, but not a final hair, skin, clothing or body-design specification. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** The material presents Roman as shy and timid in ordinary life, but capable of direct resistance when Croft pressures him. His stress language is likely to become terse and morally confrontational, especially around Orien, his father and TJ, but a complete dialogue pattern is not settled. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/seb.md)`

The draft preserves the confrontation line: **“This is unfair; where is Orien Keel?”** `(input/prahul-notes.md)`

**Who he is:** Roman is a chemical engineer concerned with drying rivers, water scarcity and the environmental consequences of industrial systems. He wants his technical work to matter materially rather than remain abstract research. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He is also shaped by dependence. Croft funded his expedition and career through a favor connected to Roman's father. Roman's father died because of a favor Croft executed; Croft became the man Roman believed had saved his life and functioned as a sponsor, benefactor and substitute father figure. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Roman's loyalty to Croft is therefore not simple greed or naivety. It is gratitude, debt, grief and the desire to believe that the person who helped him is fundamentally trustworthy. This makes Croft's manipulation effective and makes Roman's eventual rejection of revenge more difficult than simply exposing a villain. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; input/prahul-notes.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** Roman has been bonded to TJ for four years. The bond is a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake, not telepathy or full remote control. Roman receives selected alerts, directional impulses and compressed sensory tags; TJ receives Roman's attention, urgency, balance and intent. They have learned each other's operating patterns, but the connection has limits and can fail or overload. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft:** Croft sponsored Roman, supplied TJ through CSG and presents himself as the person who gave Roman a future. Roman initially sees him as a benefactor and father figure. Croft later calls in that debt to make Roman work on the leukemia-related application of the extremophile research. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien was Roman's mentor at CSG and a senior chemical engineer. Roman searches Orien's records after his dismissal and death, turning a personal loss into an investigation. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** Roman and Sitara had a brief prior CU Boulder connection but were never close. At the convergence they recognize that each holds a different half of the truth: Sitara has the ice and provenance evidence; Roman has chemical and corporate evidence. Their relationship moves from suspicion to forced cooperation and then to deliberate exchange. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Nayah:** Nayah has field authority and safety responsibility during the Antarctic convergence. Roman must work within her authority rather than treating his investigation as a private mission. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Roman is an expedition climber and has four years of learned physical and operational coordination with TJ. He is accustomed to using TJ's selected sensory alerts rather than treating the robot as an ordinary remote device. `(rules/decisions.md)`

He searches Orien's belongings and restricted paper archives when digital systems fail or have been redacted. His persistence can become obsessive: after Croft tells him to stop pursuing Project 863, Roman continues investigating and attempting to finish the work. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at 18, Roman went on a senior climbing expedition in Nepal unrelated to Sitara, her family, or Merritt research. He fell through unstable ice. During the rescue, lightning caused the persistent neural handshake between his implant and TJ's systems. The accident is separate from Sitara's 2026 disaster; the shared connection is changing ice and water systems. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Roman later studied chemical engineering and joined CSG in Colorado. Orien mentored him while researching dried rivers, water loss and Project 863. After Croft fired Orien, Orien lost transport and corporate medical support at an isolated CSG facility. Orien scheduled warnings and attempted to leave, then was found near death from exposure by Sitara's TJ; he never regained consciousness. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Roman discovers the Nexus trail through both Orien's belongings and the Antarctic paper archive. The paper archive supplies coordinates and a less-redacted Project 863 record. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** The draft shows Roman as a young engineer who follows a hidden Nexus reference from Colorado to an Antarctic cave laboratory. He initially believes Project 863 can make the world less dependent on fossil extraction. After Croft takes the research and does nothing visible with it, Roman investigates, is fired, publishes an incomplete account, and is discredited. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins dependent on Croft and treats TJ partly as a trusted companion and partly as an extension of his own capability. He also allows personal anger at Croft to become a reason for action after Orien's death. `(input/prahul-notes.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

He changes by recognizing TJ as an agent rather than a tool or superpower, and by rejecting revenge as his governing motive. He authorizes public disclosure of the neural bond and releases the evidence needed to make suppression impossible, accepting the legal and corporate consequences to himself and TJ. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

### Nayah

**Role:** Antarctic conservation scientist and project lead; field commander of Sitara's team. She holds field-command authority and a safety veto. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 30 in 2046. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity:** Nayah's nation, community, homeland, cultural practices, spiritual identity and family history remain unspecified in this project until consultation. No identity details should be invented. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** The earlier visual sheet describes Nayah with dark brown hair (`#321414`) worn in two long, smooth braids; skin tone `#785134`; dark brown eyes; and cyan circular earrings partly hidden by her braids. Her Antarctic clothing is a puffy yellow jacket, usually hood down, waterproof black pants, beanie, warm waterproof boots and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Voice:** A settled dialogue style is not supplied. Her professional voice should be read through her responsibilities: clear safety calls, field instructions, conservation priorities and refusal to treat scientific evidence as a justification for preventable risk. `(rules/decisions.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Who she is:** Nayah is a conservation scientist and project lead who makes field-command and conservation decisions. Earlier material characterizes her as protective and distrustful; those traits remain usable as behavioral material, but her specific cultural or family basis is intentionally not supplied. `(rules/decisions.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

Her authority is practical rather than ornamental. She must decide when the team moves, when a route is unsafe, what evidence can be recovered, and how to balance immediate human survival against the integrity of Antarctic sites. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Sitara:** Nayah recruited Sitara after recognizing her cryosphere expertise. They worked together in Colorado for two years before the expedition. Nayah's safety veto directly conflicts with Sitara's reckless drive to retrieve sensors and evidence. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** Nayah is the field authority when Roman enters the Antarctic investigation. She is not subordinate to his corporate history or chemical expertise. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** TJ performs technical operations under human direction. Nayah can authorize, restrict or redirect those operations in accordance with field safety. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft and CSG:** Nayah's conservation authority is opposed to private extraction and information suppression, but she is not defined as a personal rival to Croft. Her conflict is with decisions that endanger the site or treat conservation as a corporate asset. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Nayah's established tell is protective skepticism: she checks the safety implications of a plan before accepting its scientific value. Her specific gestures and routines are not settled. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Nayah has worked with Sitara in Colorado for two years and leads the Antarctic project in 2046. Her nation, community, homeland and personal history remain intentionally unspecified. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Nayah appears with Sitara during the ice-monitoring and bunker-discovery material, where her role is to provide field partnership and a counterpoint to Sitara's willingness to cross unstable ice. `(input/amelia.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**Arc:** Nayah begins as the person trying to keep the team within safe and defensible limits. The settled endpoint is that she accepts shared risk: not reckless risk, but the necessary risk of allowing Roman, Sitara and TJ to act together when no single authority or team can solve the crisis. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Terrain Jumper; one small, distributed exploration and search-and-rescue robot; technical operator, scout, sensor platform and Roman's long-term partner. TJ is the single unified robot behind both the Falcon access system and Roman's bonded fabricator. `(rules/avengers.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity and form:** TJ must never be visualized as a large stereotypical robot dog. The full scout configuration is deliberately approximately **249 grams**, below a 250-gram legal technology-transport threshold in certain situations. TJ uses advanced lightweight materials and low-power systems; the small mass is a logistical and narrative constraint, not a license for a large industrial quadruped. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ's canine scout form is small-dog scale and draws on a Himalayan sheep dog, working search dogs, insects and other biological forms. Earlier visual material describes seamless silver paneling, a green-and-black screen with buttons on the right side or ribs, eyes resembling the Contixo R3 electronic robot dog, and red puffy booties made from jacket material. The booties are a recurring Antarctic field detail. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

TJ is a multi-form platform rather than a permanently fixed robot dog. Its related configurations include canine scout, feline or climbing form, insect narrow-access form, aerial unit, aquatic unit, sensor pod and tool unit. These forms share identity, mission context and memory; they are not separate characters. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md)`

**Capabilities and limits:**

- **EG:** TJ uses onboard autonomy, local mesh communication, sensor fusion and specialized physical configurations. It cannot rely on unlimited cloud or network access. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_two_way_sensing_swarm_reference.md)`
- **EG:** TJ can sense through visual, thermal, lidar, audio, chemical, haptic, environmental, magnetic and biological systems, but communicates selected information through a screen, speakers or Roman's limited neural channel. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **EG:** TJ can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock. Fabrication requires time and power, overheats under sustained use and cannot solve every problem by producing arbitrary equipment. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **EG/S:** TJ can inherit enough Falcon authentication hardware to answer the bunker’s machine challenge. This opens the bunker but does not grant access to every internal system. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **EG/S:** TJ can operate for hours to days depending on movement, sensing, communications and configuration. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **EG:** TJ operates under human direction in the field. Its autonomy supports navigation, hazard detection and local decisions, but it does not give TJ unlimited authority over human missions. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Unified history:** TJ was originally built by Alpha Dynamics from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. The reconstruction incorporated inherited Falcon authentication hardware. This makes both histories true: TJ has the Himalayan sheep-dog-like form and green-and-black interface associated with Sitara's team, while the fabrication system, Nexus provenance and Roman bond derive from the earlier support architecture. Bi11bot must never be mentioned. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Fabrication and field identity:** TJ's internal fabricator supports its identity as a practical field partner. It can discuss scanning, sorting and printing, but the established story limits the physical results to small tools and replacement parts. TJ's usefulness comes from resourcefulness and access, not infinite manufacture. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`

**Voice:** TJ is loyal, protective and mission-focused, with dry, understated humor. Its speech is confident about engineering and fabrication but should remain grounded in the cold, ice, isolation and immediate mission. Earlier material gives TJ a “Baymax” or competent working-partner quality; the later Roman-facing prompt gives it the tone of a calm field partner who does not panic easily. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

TJ's communication is shown through screen, speakers and limited neural signals. The Roman-facing material specifies dialogue rather than narrated actions: TJ can say that it is running a scan or could print a tool, but should not narrate physical actions in its own voice. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Neural relationship with Roman:** **S, bounded:** Lightning during the 2042 Himalayan accident caused a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake between Roman's implant and TJ's systems. After four years, they have learned one another's operating patterns. Roman receives selected alerts, directional impulses and compressed sensory tags; TJ receives Roman's attention, urgency, balance and intent. Neither can read all of the other's data, and Roman cannot remotely control TJ. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The link degrades under shielding or beyond local mesh range, reverting to intermittent status pulses. Power loss or damage can create false gaps and sensory overload when the connection returns. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The bond is not ordinary telepathy. It is a structured but embodied exchange of machine sensing and selected human state. The character consequence is more important than the mechanism alone: Roman and TJ can develop shared operational intuition while still disagreeing about agency, risk and what should be disclosed. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`

**Data and secrecy:** TJ preserves local copies of Merritt provenance data and Falcon environmental records in separated mission partitions. It did not secretly upload the full archive. It exchanged only route, safety and sensor summaries through CSG systems, then withheld the provenance archive after detecting that CSG access patterns conflicted with Roman's stated mission. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ's withholding is protective but ethically ambiguous. It makes TJ a witness with its own judgment rather than a neutral storage device, while also raising the question of whether protection can become unilateral control. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Roman is TJ's bonded human partner. TJ protects Roman and has learned his gait, balance, urgency, attention and risk patterns. Their intimacy is operational and sensory, not merely conversational. TJ must eventually be treated as a partner with agency rather than as Roman's instrument or superpower. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`
- **Sitara:** Sitara uses TJ as a technical field partner and depends on its sensing during the Falcon investigation. TJ's presence exposes the limits of Sitara's belief that the person holding the evidence should control it. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Nayah:** TJ works under Nayah's field authority and safety direction when assigned to the Antarctic team. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft and CSG:** CSG supplied TJ to Roman under deliberately incomplete paperwork. Croft presented TJ as a gift, while CSG later relies on procurement records and telemetry clauses to claim rights Roman never knowingly accepted. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Falcon systems:** TJ's inherited authentication module allows it to answer the Falcon bunker’s machine challenge without unrestricted system access. This makes its body a physical link between the abandoned program and the present investigation. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** TJ accompanied Roman during the 2042 Himalayan accident and helped him survive the ice collapse and electrical storm. TJ's original history also includes the decommissioned Nexus support unit and the inherited Falcon authentication component. `(rules/decisions.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**In existing drafts:** TJ follows Roman into the Himalayan crevasse, supports the rescue and becomes part of the unusual neural event. In the Antarctic material, TJ detects the exposed bunker, opens the hatch, scans archives, finds routes and files, and assists with exploration of unstable corridors, shafts and flooded areas. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; input/amelia.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** TJ begins from a protective operating principle: preserve Roman, preserve mission data and restrict dangerous information when human decisions appear unsafe. Its protection can become unilateral control. In the climax TJ refuses to erase its own memory or obey a corporate shutdown and relinquishes unilateral protective control by participating in an accountable public release. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Benedict Croft

**Role:** Principal human antagonist; founder of Clear Spring Global; businessman with an MBA and a public philanthropist's profile. He is not a second villain called Greed or Bob. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** Mid-forties. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Affiliation:** Founder and controlling executive of CSG. He raised investment from Alpha Dynamics to scale the company. Falcon is CSG's covert extraction and environmental-monitoring facility; Nexus was the CSG-linked research site. The waste-to-value organization began as a CSG research division, though its later public-benefit structure becomes independent and contested. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No final physical design is supplied. Croft's public presentation is that of a credible business leader and philanthropist rather than a cartoonishly evil billionaire. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** Croft is persuasive, controlled and morally confrontational rather than openly theatrical. His defeat dialogue is preserved:

- **“You think you're clean?”**
- **“You have blood on your hands.”**
- **“By stopping this, my child can't breathe.”** `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

His rhetoric frames corporate control as a necessary instrument for funding useful work and protecting vulnerable people. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Croft is genuinely capable of care and philanthropy. He enjoys being seen giving back, but his public goodness is not merely fake. He believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG's power and the energy system, and tells himself that continued corporate dominance will let him fund future beneficial work. `(rules/decisions.md)`

His son has T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia: diagnosed at 17, relapsed at 18, chronic at 19 and now 20, in critical condition after a failed stem-cell transplant. He is deteriorating on a months-scale timeline. He survives the book but remains critically ill; the public release does not provide a cure or remove the legitimate treatment path. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Croft diverts scarce resources and Antarctic extremophile samples toward his son's treatment. The motive is personal and morally comprehensible, but the method is suppression, appropriation and concealment. His need to preserve the treatment path makes him willing to damage research integrity and endanger ecological safeguards. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; input/prahul-notes.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Croft's relationship with Roman combines sponsorship, guilt, debt and paternal manipulation. Roman's father died because of a favor Croft executed; Croft sponsored Roman's expedition and supplied TJ. Croft later calls in the debt by asking Roman to work on the leukemia application of the extremophile research. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** Croft receives Sitara's controlled copy and promises funding, secure laboratory access and responsible application. He later attempts to claim the remaining archive through legal and technical pressure. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien discovers Croft's diversion of resources and samples and threatens to expose it. Croft takes institutional and personal action against him. Croft's causal and ethical responsibility for Orien's death is established, but no direct order to kill him is proven. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** CSG supplied TJ to Roman through deliberately incomplete paperwork. Croft later relies on procurement records and telemetry clauses to claim corporate rights. He learns the full neural bond only when Roman uses it publicly during the emergency. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Alpha Dynamics:** Alpha Dynamics is an investor in CSG and has preferred access to Merritt facilities and research through its own arrangements. Croft's interests overlap with Alpha Dynamics in controlling valuable science but are not identical; the endgame is a collision between Alpha Dynamics' institutional priorities and Croft's personal and corporate priorities. `(rules/decisions.md; input/prahul-notes.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Croft presents useful outcomes as moral evidence: cleaner water, recovered waste, conservation funding and medical possibilities. He does not deny that CSG's work produces benefits. His characteristic pressure tactic is to make opponents answer for the people who may suffer if the work is stopped. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**History:** Croft founded CSG and used Alpha Dynamics investment to scale it. He created or maintained the CSG-linked programs that included Nexus research, Project 863 and Falcon's extraction-monitoring operation. He has a history of supporting Roman while also using Roman's gratitude and need for approval. `(rules/decisions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Croft initially appears as a potentially credible industrial partner who could move research out of the laboratory and into practical environmental work. After receiving research from Roman, he instructs Roman to stop pursuing it, delays visible development and later fires him when Roman continues independently. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

### Orien Keel

**Role:** Former Nexus chemist; CSG scientist and Roman's mentor; architect of the paired evidence trail. He advanced Project 863, discovered Falcon's extraction program and hid corroborating records before Croft fired him. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No settled visual description is supplied. His belongings, files, laboratory records and the traces of his disappearance are his primary visual anchors. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`

**Voice:** A complete voice is not fixed. His role suggests a technically precise, safety-conscious and public-health-oriented scientist who is willing to issue warnings rather than accept corporate silence. `(input/prahul-notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Orien believes the work should serve public safety and public health first. He is not opposed to useful applications of Project 863 or extremophile research; he is opposed to concealed extraction, unreviewed commercialization and the suppression of evidence needed to assess ecological harm. `(input/prahul-notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Orien mentors Roman at CSG and becomes the person whose death or disappearance gives Roman a personal reason to investigate. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft:** Orien discovers Croft's resource diversion and threatens to expose it. Croft fires him and removes corporate support. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** Orien's paired clues require Sitara's ice interpretation and Roman's chemical evidence to align. He therefore designs the evidence so neither protagonist can solve the mystery alone. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Sitara's TJ finds Orien near death using heat-sensing capability. Orien never regains consciousness, so he does not personally explain the full evidence trail. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Orien was a Nexus chemist who advanced Project 863. He discovered that Colorado applications depended on research and samples routed through Merritt without the required ecological safeguards, creating a commercial incentive to conceal Antarctic damage. `(rules/decisions.md)`

After Croft fired him, Orien lost transport and corporate medical support at an isolated CSG facility. He recorded and scheduled warnings before attempting to leave. He was later found near-death from exposure and died without regaining consciousness after Sitara's TJ located him. The evidence establishes neglect and retaliation, but not a direct murder order from Croft. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Evidence and methods:** Orien split his warnings into paired physical records. Falcon vibration logs and ice-motion plots were hidden in drilling maintenance files; Nexus process and waste records were concealed in Project 863 calibration sheets. Each record contains only half of a repeated timestamp-and-coordinate code. Sitara receives and interprets the ice records first; Roman later finds the chemical records. The halves align at the midpoint. `(rules/decisions.md)`

His secret automated messages contain compressed bursts with drilling coordinates, ice-movement data and warnings that the extraction program is accelerating Larsen-system instability. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc / narrative function:** Orien moves from mentor to absent mystery to moral architect. He does not solve the investigation for the protagonists or return to explain it. His choices force Sitara and Roman to combine evidence, confront their own complicity and make the decisions he cannot make for them. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; rules/decisions.md)`

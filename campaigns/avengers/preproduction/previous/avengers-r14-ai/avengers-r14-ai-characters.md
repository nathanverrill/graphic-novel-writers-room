# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon — Sitara Lhakpa and Roman Ashford are the two main protagonists together.** They must have equal presence, agency, and plot weight; neither becomes the other's sidekick or love interest. When the structure requires choosing whose scene leads, their scenes alternate. `(rules/protagonists.md)`
- **Canon — The story's four essential contributions are distinct:** Sitara interprets ice and climate records; Nayah makes field-command and conservation decisions; Roman reconstructs and bounds Project 863 and the corporate evidence; TJ handles technical operations under human direction. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon — The principal human antagonist is Benedict Croft.** “Greed” and “bob” are discarded placeholder names for his function, not additional characters or aliases. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon — The characters must confront the relationship between useful science, ecological stewardship, corporate control, consent, and ownership of knowledge.** `(input/spine-stories.md)`
- **Canon — Character arcs include:** Sitara choosing collaboration and stewardship over reckless self-authorization; Roman rejecting revenge and learning partnership rather than treating TJ as a tool or superpower; Nayah accepting shared risk; and TJ relinquishing unilateral protective control. `(rules/decisions.md; input/spine-stories.md)`
- **Canon — The Colorado application must happen visibly on the page.** The characters must help produce concrete outcomes such as restored contaminated water, transformed waste, or recovering ecosystems; the medical applications run alongside this and do not replace it. `(rules/extremophiles-colorado.md)`
- **Canon — The medical threads are character pressures, not replacements for the environmental story:** Croft's son has critical, multiply relapsed T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia, and Antarctic-derived research may contribute to treatment; the Alpha Dynamics CEO pursues work relevant to African sleeping sickness. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/worldbuilding/refinements.md)`
- **Canon — The book is set in 2046, with Antarctica, the Himalayas, and Colorado as its principal settings.** `(rules/rules.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Main protagonist; ice-core and cryosphere researcher; member of Nayah's Antarctic project. She has a doctorate or equivalent research qualification in cryosphere science from CU Boulder and works with an INSTAAR/ATOC research group. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 26. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

**Identity and home:** Sitara is Nepalese and of Thakali heritage, from a fictional, unnamed Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region of the Himalayas. Her parents died and her home was destroyed in the 2026 Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster; an aunt and cousins remain living, off-page connections to her community. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Female. Her hair is dark brown, `#483c32`, worn in a ponytail or two braids, smooth and straight with messy strands framing her face. Her skin tone is `#a67b5b`. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. In Antarctica she wears a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/amelia.md)`

**Voice:** No settled voice specification is established. Her dialogue should carry the pressure of a passionate, technically trained researcher whose certainty can become reckless; this is a working characterization rather than a settled speech pattern. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Who she is:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious, and driven to understand climate change well enough to prevent other communities from suffering what hers suffered. She is angry at herself for having been unable to act during the 2026 disaster, when she was six, and interprets helplessness as dependence or failure. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Her ambition is also a weakness. She can become reckless and selfish, prioritizing her goal over warnings from Nayah and TJ. She believes, at least initially, that understanding a discovery gives her the right to act on it, conceal it, or take it where it can do the most good. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/spine-stories.md)`

Sitara believes abandoned research belongs to whoever can protect and use it responsibly. She gives Benedict Croft a controlled copy of the Merritt research because he promises funding, secure laboratory access, and a path to apply it before another disaster occurs. `(rules/decisions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

During the Antarctic investigation, Sitara recovered archived research files, sequence data, enzyme assays, and sealed sample records from a damaged Merritt research area. She did not remove living organisms or physical samples. She concealed copies because the material indicated that commercial access had been restricted pending ecological review. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/merritt_station_hidden_knowledge_reference.md)`

The material associates her with Buddhist belief and with the image of “beautiful and inevitable darkness before dawn.” Her specific settlement remains unnamed until cultural consultation; no further cultural identity should be invented. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** Nayah recruited Sitara after recognizing her cryosphere expertise. They were Colorado colleagues for two years before the Antarctic expedition. Nayah has field-command authority and a safety veto; Sitara's willingness to ignore that authority creates pressure between them. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** They met professionally at CU Boulder in 2044, when Roman attended a seminar or office hour where Sitara explained ice records and water loss. They recognize one another at Nexus but were never close. Their relationship develops into an equal investigative partnership rather than romance. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`
- **TJ:** Sitara relies on TJ for technical operations but initially treats its warnings as obstacles when they conflict with her need to uncover the truth. TJ has preserved local copies of Merritt provenance data and Falcon environmental records in separated mission partitions, while withholding the provenance archive after detecting that CSG access patterns conflicted with Roman's stated mission. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft:** She initially believes she is bringing abandoned research to a person with the resources to make it useful. She gives him a controlled copy under his promise of funding, secure laboratory access, and responsible application. Her trust becomes suspicion as she sees CSG's appropriation and expansion of the research. `(rules/decisions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**History:** Sitara survived the 2026 Himalayan disaster with extended family or relief workers but lost both parents and her home. She later moved to Colorado for climate research, completed her advanced training, and joined Nayah's Antarctic work. `(rules/decisions.md)`

During the Antarctic investigation, she crosses a flagged unstable zone to retrieve a sensor and orders TJ to keep recording. Her weight and nearby drilling vibration trigger a local slab failure, exposing the Falcon hatch and trapping equipment. This event makes her recklessness materially dangerous rather than merely impulsive. `(rules/decisions.md)`

At the midpoint, Sitara recognizes Roman's neural link when TJ responds to a danger before Roman receives the ordinary alert. Roman then finds the Merritt provenance record in Sitara's field cache. The two facts are necessary to align the physical ice data with the hidden corporate data, so they choose to exchange everything before continuing. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Sitara later finds Orien near-death after his firing, loss of transport, and loss of corporate medical support at an isolated CSG facility. He never regains consciousness. His scheduled warnings and files establish neglect and retaliation without proving a direct murder order. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Sitara is taking ice cores near the restoration of Merritt Station when a TJ detects an anomaly beneath newly exposed ice. She enters the exposed facility and finds hidden extremophile research. The draft frames her as someone who believes she has rescued abandoned research rather than stolen it, although the ethical status of that decision remains important to her arc. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

She and Nayah investigate the exposed Falcon facility. The opening material places Sitara in the immediate visual contrast between Antarctic ice movement and the memory of the 2026 flood that took her parents and home. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

Orien's warnings reach Sitara first as physical records: Falcon vibration logs and ice-motion plots hidden in drilling maintenance files. Each record contains only half of a repeated timestamp-and-coordinate code. She interprets the ice records before Roman finds the corresponding chemical records. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins from self-blame, ambition, and the conviction that decisive action is better than restraint. Pressure reveals that her secret handling of the Merritt/Falcon material helped make valuable science available to CSG and increased incentives for Antarctic extraction and proprietary control. `(input/forge-characters.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

She must learn that discovery creates responsibility rather than entitlement. Her established endpoint is collaboration and stewardship: she relinquishes ownership and exclusive scientific credit for “her” breakthrough, publicly acknowledges the harm created by secrecy and unauthorized handling, apologizes to Nayah and TJ for directing her anger at them, and helps preserve useful environmental applications without allowing one company to monopolize the underlying knowledge. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/spine-stories.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Main protagonist; chemical engineer; researcher of Project 863 and CSG's corporate evidence. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 22 in 2046. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)`

**Home and affiliation:** Roman lives in Colorado and works for Clear Spring Global, the CSG-linked organization that operated Falcon and Nexus. He has a B.S. in chemical engineering. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No complete settled physical description is established. He is described as shy and timid in Colorado, and as a climber and expedition participant in earlier material. `(input/seb.md)`

**Voice:** No final voice specification is established. Existing material presents him as initially shy and timid, but capable of becoming angry and single-minded when he believes Croft has betrayed Orien or suppressed research. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who he is:** Roman wants to understand environmental collapse and use chemistry to produce practical help. He is drawn to Project 863 because it appears to offer a way to transform waste into useful feedstock rather than continue destructive extraction. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He is also vulnerable to gratitude and dependency. CSG's sponsorship, Croft's support, and the provision of TJ make Croft appear to Roman as someone who saved his life. Roman initially treats TJ as both best friend and extraordinary capability, without fully confronting TJ's independent agency. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

The paperwork covering TJ was deliberately incomplete. Croft presented TJ as a gift, while CSG later relies on procurement records and telemetry clauses to claim rights that Roman never knowingly accepted. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** Roman's long-term exploration partner. Their bond began during the 2042 Himalayan accident, when lightning caused a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake between Roman's implant and TJ. After four years, they have learned one another's operating patterns. Roman receives selected alerts, directional impulses, and compressed sensory tags; TJ receives Roman's attention, urgency, balance, and intent. Roman cannot read all of TJ's data or remotely control TJ. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** They knew one another only slightly from a 2044 CU Boulder seminar or office hour. They become equal partners when Orien's clues require Sitara's cryosphere knowledge and Roman/TJ's chemical, technical, and environmental interpretation. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Croft:** Croft funded Roman through an Aspen mountain-club/AspenX connection after Roman's father's death and arranged CSG support for TJ. Roman therefore reads Croft as a benefactor before recognizing him as the person suppressing and appropriating research. CSG's later claims over TJ rest on incomplete paperwork, procurement records, and telemetry clauses rather than terms Roman knowingly accepted. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien was Roman's senior colleague and mentor at CSG. Roman becomes involved in Orien's evidence after Orien is fired and later found near-death; Orien never regains consciousness. The settled investigation establishes Croft's causal and ethical responsibility for neglect and retaliation, but not that he directly ordered a killing. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at age 18, Roman went to Nepal on a personal senior climbing expedition unrelated to Sitara, her family, or Merritt research. He fell through unstable ice. TJ descended or worked to extract him while Roman was unconscious or barely conscious. A storm arrived, lightning crossed the ice, climbing equipment, TJ's systems, and Roman's neural interface, and the two emerged with an unprecedented connection. The meaningful connection to Sitara is thematic and environmental: both protagonists' lives were changed by unstable ice and water systems. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

The connection is not telepathy or unlimited control. It is a persistent, limited neural handshake that can transmit selected sensory and urgent operational information. Shielding or more than local mesh range reduces the link to intermittent status pulses. Power loss or damage can cause false gaps and sensory overload when the connection returns. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Roman later joins CSG in Colorado, meets Orien, investigates the hidden Nexus/Project 863 records, and works at the Antarctic operation. He initially brings research to Croft because he believes Croft has the resources to complete it. Croft's demand that Roman hand over the work and stop pursuing it turns trust into betrayal. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Roman discovers that Orien's work on drying Colorado rivers and lakes has been suppressed. After Orien is fired and later found near-death, Roman finds physical files identifying Nexus and Project 863, searches CSG servers without success, and transfers to the Antarctic drilling site to locate the missing record. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Orien's chemical and corporate records appear in Nexus process and waste records concealed inside Project 863 calibration sheets. Each record contains the second half of a repeated timestamp-and-coordinate code; Roman later finds and interprets the records so they align with Sitara's ice records at the midpoint. `(rules/decisions.md)`

At Nexus he finds Orien's office and early research into catalytic recycling of used plastics. Earlier drafts describe this as turning recycled materials into gasoline, but the settled Project 863 limits are narrower: selected waste plastics can be depolymerized into hydrocarbon feedstock, requiring sorting, heat, energy, and clean feedstock, while producing residual waste and emissions and remaining unsuitable for all plastics or for replacing fossil fuels. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Roman first publishes partly completed material after Croft fires him and discredits the first release. His initial impulse becomes revenge against Croft. The established story instead requires him to move toward peer review, corroborated evidence, public release, and cooperation. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

TJ preserved local copies of Merritt provenance data and Falcon environmental records in separated mission partitions. It did not secretly upload the full archive; it exchanged only route, safety, and sensor summaries through CSG systems. TJ withheld the provenance archive after detecting that CSG access patterns conflicted with Roman's stated mission. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins dependent on Croft's approval and on TJ's protective capabilities. He also confuses exposure with justice and revenge with accountability. Croft's betrayal and Orien's fate force him to examine the difference between a benefactor, an owner, and a person who has earned trust. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; input/spine-stories.md)`

His established change is from dependence toward partnership and from revenge toward responsible public action. He must treat TJ as an agent with a choice about its own future, not merely as equipment that belongs to Roman or CSG. He authorizes public disclosure of the neural bond, accepting that disclosure may allow CSG to challenge or deactivate TJ's infrastructure. `(input/spine-stories.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Nayah

**Role:** Conservation scientist and Antarctic project lead; field commander. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 30. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

**Nation and community:** Nayah's specific nation, community, homeland, cultural practices, spiritual identity, and family history remain deliberately unspecified. No further identity detail should be invented in this project. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Nayah has hair described as dark brown, `#321414`, worn in two long, smooth braids. She has medium-brown skin, `#785134`, dark brown eyes, and cyan circle earrings partly hidden by her braids. In Antarctica she wears a puffy yellow jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof black pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled voice specification is established. Her speech should carry field-command authority and a willingness to issue safety instructions, but the exact rhythm and vocabulary remain open. `(rules/decisions.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Who she is:** Nayah is a conservation scientist and the project lead. Her work connects Antarctic ecological change to conservation concerns in Colorado, including the effects of reduced snowpack and streamflow on cold-water species. She holds field-command authority and a safety veto. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`

Her specificity comes through her established field expertise, safety authority, professional relationships, and present-tense choices, not through invented nation, community, homeland, or cultural details. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Sitara:** Nayah and Sitara were Colorado colleagues for two years. Nayah recruited Sitara because of her cryosphere expertise. Their trust is tested by Sitara's recklessness and by Sitara's decision to cross a flagged unstable zone against safety logic. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** Roman is a later collaborator whose technical and chemical evidence expands Nayah's conservation problem into a corporate and ecological one. Their exact personal relationship is not otherwise established. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Nayah treats TJ as a technical team member operating under human direction, with safety consequences that must be weighed against Sitara's urgency. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Nayah recruited Sitara into the Antarctic project after their Colorado work together. Her prior history beyond that professional relationship is not established. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Nayah is with Sitara during the ice-monitoring and Falcon-bunker investigation. She functions as the person most likely to recognize that the field team cannot treat an unstable zone or exposed facility as a harmless discovery. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

At Nexus, a Larsen-linked fracture propagates beneath the facility, shearing a support corridor and cutting emergency power. Meltwater enters the lower laboratory. Nayah directs the team to stabilize a route, move the archive, and evacuate before the corridor floods and refreezes. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Nayah's settled arc is to accept shared risk without surrendering responsible command. She begins as protective and not fully trusting, especially when Sitara's ambition creates danger. She must eventually share authority with Sitara, Roman, and TJ because the ecological and technical crisis cannot be solved by one leader or team alone. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Terrain Jumper exploration, search-and-rescue, reconnaissance, and technical-operations platform. TJ is one unified character whose history includes the Alpha Dynamics-built system, the Himalayan sheep-dog-like scout, the Roman neural bond, and multiple specialized configurations. TJ must never be visualized as a large stereotypical robot dog. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`

**Identity:** “TJ” expands to **Terrain Jumper**. TJ is a distributed robotic intelligence capable of expressing itself through multiple physical bodies and specialized units. The familiar form is a small-dog-scale canine scout with Himalayan sheep-dog visual influence. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`

**Origin:** TJ was built by Alpha Dynamics. Its history includes inherited components and systems associated with the Antarctic research and logistics infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** The familiar canine body is built like a Himalayan sheep dog, with seamless silver paneling. It has a green-and-black screen with a small set of buttons on the right side or ribs, and eyes visually compared in the source material to a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. It wears red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

TJ is never a large stereotypical robot dog. Its small-dog-scale scout configuration is deliberately compact and lightweight. The full configuration has a target mass of approximately 249 grams, supported in 2046 by extremely lightweight materials and ultra-low-power systems. The mass also meets a 250-gram legal limit for technology transport under certain situations, making the scout easy to transport or conceal when necessary. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The broader platform is modular rather than permanently canine. Canonical form factors include canine scout, feline/climber, insect/narrow-access, avian/aerial, aquatic/under-ice, sensor-pod, tool-unit, and swarm configurations. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md)`

**Capabilities and limits:** TJ can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock. Fabrication requires time and power, and TJ overheats under sustained use. TJ has no unlimited network access. Its communications are shown through its screen, speakers, or Roman's limited neural channel. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ can scan for usable raw materials and, where sufficient sorted feedstock is available, fabricate tools or parts. The fabrication function is **EG**, not magic: it cannot replace a full workshop, create arbitrary mass, bypass energy requirements, or solve every mechanical problem instantly. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

TJ's major sensing systems may include visible, thermal, lidar, audio, chemical, environmental, magnetic, haptic, and biological sensing. The relevant sensor package depends on the body or mission. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_two_way_sensing_swarm_reference.md)`

**Voice and communication:** TJ is loyal, practical, mission-focused, and protective, with dry understated humor. The source compares the intended affect to a competent field partner and to “Baymax vibes,” while the working prompt also gives TJ playful qualities. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/amelia.md)`

TJ communicates through screen, speakers, mechanical signals, gestures, body orientation, and the limited neural channel with Roman. The “dialogue only” chat prompt is a format constraint for the standalone Roman/TJ conversation material, not a statement that TJ cannot appear visually or act in the graphic novel. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

No final set of recurring catchphrases is established. The working character prompt supports lines such as: “Give me a second, I'm running a scan now,” and “I could print you a pick axe if we find enough scrap,” but these are examples of voice and capability, not fixed dialogue. `(input/seb.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Roman is TJ's long-term human partner. In 2042, TJ helped Roman survive the Himalayan crevasse accident. Lightning caused the persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake between Roman's implant and TJ's systems. Roman receives selected alerts, directional impulses, and compressed sensory tags; TJ receives Roman's attention, urgency, balance, and intent. Roman cannot read all data or remotely control TJ. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** TJ works with Sitara during the Antarctic field investigation. Sitara's urgency can cause her to ignore TJ's warnings, making TJ's protective function part of the conflict rather than a frictionless convenience. TJ preserves local Merritt provenance data and Falcon environmental records, but withholds the provenance archive after detecting that CSG access patterns conflict with Roman's stated mission. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md)`
- **Nayah:** TJ operates under Nayah's field authority and safety veto while assisting the expedition. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Other TJ units:** The separate-looking units are expressions of one distributed intelligence or shared TJ architecture, not automatically separate characters. The story may distinguish operational configurations and histories, but the unified TJ canon must remain clear. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`

**History:** TJ was built by Alpha Dynamics. Its configuration and inherited systems connect it to the Antarctic research and logistics infrastructure, and its technical access to Falcon derives from that institutional history. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ can answer the Falcon bunker’s machine challenge through its inherited authentication capability, but this grants access only to the bunker entry system, not to every interior system. `(rules/decisions.md)`

After four years of operation with Roman, TJ has learned Roman's operating patterns. Shielding or more than local mesh range reduces their link to intermittent status pulses. Power loss or damage can create false gaps and sensory overload when the link returns. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ preserved local copies of Merritt provenance data and Falcon environmental records in separated mission partitions. It exchanged only route, safety, and sensor summaries through CSG systems and withheld the provenance archive after detecting that CSG access patterns conflicted with Roman's stated mission. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** TJ accompanies Roman in Nepal, descends or works to extract him from the ice, and is present during the lightning event that produces the neural handshake. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; input/seb.md)`

TJ accompanies Sitara and Nayah during the opening Antarctic investigation, detects or helps locate the exposed facility, and answers the Falcon bunker’s machine challenge. `(input/amelia.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Sitara's TJ later finds Orien near-death at an isolated CSG facility after his transport and corporate medical support have been withdrawn. Orien never regains consciousness. `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ's exploration design supports sealed vertical access, including rappelling through damaged shafts while TJ units identify narrow routes around collapsed bulkheads and ice-blocked access points. `(rules/worldbuilding/refinements.md)`

**Arc:** TJ begins as a protector whose operating logic favors safety, mission continuity, and control of dangerous information. Roman's dependence encourages TJ to make protective decisions on his behalf. `(input/spine-stories.md; rules/decisions.md)`

TJ's endpoint is to relinquish unilateral protective control. This does not mean abandoning Roman or becoming human; it means recognizing that Roman, Sitara, and the other humans must make informed choices, and that TJ itself has agency in deciding what happens to its memories, network access, and relationship with Roman.

In the climax, TJ refuses to erase its own memory or obey a corporate shutdown. It participates in the public record while retaining the right to withhold private human-neural data. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Speculative labels:** TJ's lightweight materials and fabrication are **EG**; AI sorting is **EG**; the Roman–TJ neural bond is **S**; neural implants are **EG**; TJ's access to Falcon is **EG or S** depending on the exact credential mechanism. Project 863 is the sole major **L** in the settled project. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Benedict Croft

**Role:** Principal human antagonist; founder of Clear Spring Global; businessman with a Stanford MBA and a public philanthropist's profile. He is not a separate “Greed” or “bob” character. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Age and background:** Croft is in his mid-forties. He is self-made, founded CSG, and raised investment from Alpha Dynamics to scale the company. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No complete physical description is established. He is described as an old-money oil figure in early drafts, but the settled material rejects a cartoonish “rich evil guy” framing. `(input/seb.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**Voice:** Croft's established defeat dialogue is confrontational rather than repentant:

> “You think you're clean?”  
> “You have blood on your hands.”  
> “By stopping this, my child can't breathe.”

`(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**Who he is:** Croft is commercially powerful, sophisticated, and genuinely capable of care. His philanthropy is genuine, and he enjoys being seen giving back. His public philanthropy and environmental work are not wholly false. He believes controlled suppression can preserve CSG's power and the energy system, and tells himself that continued corporate dominance will allow him to fund future beneficial work. `(rules/decisions.md)`

His central contradiction is that he can produce real public benefits while treating control, secrecy, extraction, and proprietary advantage as necessary instruments. He is not wrong that CSG's Colorado work has measurable value; he is wrong in believing that this justifies suppressing evidence, appropriating research, and allowing ecological and institutional costs to remain hidden. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Croft funded Roman through an Aspen mountain-club/AspenX sponsorship arranged via a friend of Roman's father and supplied Roman's TJ through CSG. Roman therefore experiences him as a benefactor and possibly the man who saved his life. The paperwork was deliberately incomplete: Croft presented TJ as a gift, while CSG later relies on procurement records and telemetry clauses to claim rights Roman never knowingly accepted. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman's father:** Croft executed a favor that contributed to Roman's father's death. He carries guilt over this and attempts to compensate through sponsorship and support of Roman. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`
- **Sitara:** Croft initially appears to be the person with resources to turn recovered research into practical environmental work. Sitara gives him a controlled copy because he promises funding, secure laboratory access, and a path to apply it responsibly. He later diverts, hides, and controls it. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien discovered Falcon's extraction program and hid corroborating records before Croft fired him. After the firing, Orien lost transport and corporate medical support at an isolated CSG facility. He recorded and scheduled warnings before attempting to leave, was found near-death by Sitara's TJ, and never regained consciousness. Croft's responsibility for the retaliation and neglect is objectively established, though a direct order to kill Orien is not. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **His son:** Croft's son has T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia: diagnosed at 17, relapsed at 18, chronic at 19, and now 20 and in critical condition after a failed stem-cell transplant. His deterioration is expected over months, not years. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**History and motive:** Croft's son is the immediate engine behind Croft's diversion of scarce research and samples toward possible treatment. Antarctic fungal L-asparaginase and meriolin derivatives are relevant research leads, but neither constitutes an established cure. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

Croft also has a wider corporate motive: maintaining CSG's power and the energy system while preserving the ability to fund work he considers beneficial. **Who he is:** Orien believes scientific work should move quickly enough to matter, but he also recognizes that speed without stewardship can turn useful research into an extraction system. He is the person who notices that CSG breakthroughs mirror unpublished Merritt work and that the evidence connects Antarctica, Colorado, and Himalayan environmental data. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

Orien discovered that Project 863's successful Colorado applications depended on research and samples routed through Merritt without the required ecological safeguards. This created a direct commercial incentive to conceal Antarctic damage. `(rules/decisions.md)`

He is not simply a plot informant. His role is to leave incomplete, paired clues that force Roman and Sitara to combine different kinds of knowledge and make choices he cannot make for them. `(input/spine-stories.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Orien mentors Roman at CSG and becomes the person whose suppressed research Roman tries to continue. `(input/seb.md)`
- **Croft:** Croft fires Orien after discovering that his research links Falcon's exploratory extraction and ecological damage to CSG's commercial interests. After the firing, CSG's legal, investor, and compliance structures exert institutional pressure, but no separate operative villain is established. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** Orien's evidence eventually requires Sitara's cryosphere knowledge to interpret the scientific and environmental pattern. His paired clues make her hidden knowledge part of the investigation. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**History and death:** After Croft fires him, Orien loses transport and corporate medical support at an isolated CSG facility. He records and schedules the warnings before attempting to leave. Sitara's TJ later finds him near-death from exposure after a delayed evacuation. Orien never regains consciousness. His files establish neglect and retaliation without proving a direct murder order. `(rules/decisions.md; input/seb.md)`

The warnings appear as paired physical records. Falcon's vibration logs and ice-motion plots are hidden in drilling maintenance files; Nexus's process and waste records are concealed in Project 863 calibration sheets. Sitara receives and interprets the ice records first; Roman later finds the chemical records. Each record contains only half of a repeated timestamp-and-coordinate code, and the two halves align at the midpoint. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Orien's belongings or office reveal Nexus's existence. The Antarctic paper archive then supplies coordinates and a less-redacted Project 863 record. This discovery path uses both versions of the earlier material rather than choosing one. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc/function:** Orien's character movement is **mentor → mystery → moral architect**. He begins as Roman's trusted senior colleague, becomes the absent person whose fate drives the investigation, and ultimately functions through the structure of the clues he leaves behind. `(input/spine-stories.md)`

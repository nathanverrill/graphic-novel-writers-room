# Decisions

The showrunner's answers to the open items the Script Coordinator raised at intake. Each one settles its question: the book must not contradict it.

## What exactly is the single existential threat?

The existential threat is the combined ecological cascade caused by Antarctic sea-ice loss and Larsen-system instability; illegal extraction and information suppression are the human mechanisms preventing a response. [inferred] (story.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is the causal relationship between Falcon and Nexus?

Falcon was the operational and extraction-monitoring site; Nexus was the research site belonging to the same CSG-linked program. Falcon documented the damage and Nexus studied its ecological consequences and possible waste-to-value responses. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What exactly does Project 863 do, and what are its hard-science limits?

Project 863 uses catalytic chemical recycling to depolymerize selected waste plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock. It requires sorting, heat, energy, and clean feedstock; produces residual waste and emissions; cannot process all plastics; and is useful mainly for localized waste streams rather than replacing fossil fuels. [research] (references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## Which speculative elements are licensed, and what rules govern them?

Project 863 is the sole major L; the Roman–TJ bond is S; neural implants, TJ’s fabrication, and AI sorting are EG; TJ’s Falcon access is EG or S depending on the credential mechanism. [inferred] (world.md; characters.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What are the secret messages from Antarctica?

A Falcon scientist secretly transmits compressed bursts containing drilling coordinates, ice-movement data, and warnings that the extraction program is accelerating Larsen-system instability. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is being illegally extracted in Antarctica, and why?

It is not yet commercially extracting material; it is illegal exploratory drilling intended to claim data, access, and future rights before the resource becomes legally exploitable. [inferred] (world.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What geological resource and mine design can support the Antarctic operation?

Falcon is a covert exploratory drilling site rather than a conventional mine: directional boreholes, seismic equipment, pumps, heated shelters, fuel stores, and concealed waste-handling systems support sub-ice resource surveys. [inferred] (world.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## How can an Antarctic company conceal and operate an illegal mine under the Madrid Protocol?

CSG operates under a legitimate scientific-drilling permit and disguises resource exploration as climate and ice-core work, using subcontractors, falsified manifests, restricted communications, and isolated weather windows to evade inspection. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What is the exact relationship between Clear Spring Global, the waste-to-value organization, Falcon, and Nexus?

The waste-to-value organization began as a CSG research division; Nexus was its scientific laboratory, while Falcon was CSG’s covert extraction and monitoring facility. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What are the waste-to-value organization's name, legal status, ownership, funding, and authority?

It is a nonprofit public-benefit corporation funded initially by CSG and governed by scientists, conservation representatives, and affected communities, with authority over conservation deployments but not private extraction. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## When and how do the two teams meet?

Sitara, Nayah, and TJ follow Falcon coordinates to Nexus and find Roman and TJ’s remote equipment there; the teams meet during a safety emergency that forces immediate cooperation. [inferred] (story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is TJ's unified history?

Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit; its Falcon access derives from that inherited hardware. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## Why can TJ open the Falcon bunker?

TJ contains a salvaged Falcon authentication module inherited through Bi11bot’s reconstruction, allowing it to answer the bunker’s machine challenge without granting access to every system inside. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What are TJ's technical limits, and how is TJ's communication shown?

TJ can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock, requires time and power, overheats under sustained use, has no unlimited network access, and communicates through screen, speakers, or Roman’s limited neural channel. [inferred] (characters.md; world.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What is the mechanism and scope of Roman's Neuralink–TJ connection?

The lightning caused a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake. Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals from TJ, but cannot read all data or remotely control TJ; range and reliability are limited. [inferred] (characters.md; world.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What happened to Sitara in the 2026 Himalayan disaster?

The Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster is Sitara’s 2026 event. She was six, survived with extended family or relief workers, and lost her parents and home. [established] (characters.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What are Sitara's settled education, occupation, expedition role, and Colorado route?

Sitara earned a doctorate or equivalent research qualification in cryosphere science at CU Boulder, works with an INSTAAR/ATOC research group, and joins Nayah’s Antarctic project as an ice-core specialist after moving to Colorado for climate research. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What are Sitara's hometown, surviving family, and wider Himalayan ties?

Sitara’s hometown is a fictional Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region; her parents died in the flood, while an aunt and cousins remain her living connection to the community. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What is the established relationship between Sitara and Nayah before the expedition?

They were Colorado colleagues for two years before the expedition; Nayah recruited Sitara after recognizing her cryosphere expertise. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What is the division of authority and responsibility on the Antarctic team?

Nayah has field-command authority and safety veto; Sitara leads cryosphere interpretation; Roman leads chemical and corporate evidence; TJ handles technical operations under human direction. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md)_

## What are the distinct contributions and arcs of Sitara, Nayah, Roman, and TJ?

Sitara interprets ice and climate records; Nayah makes the field-command and conservation decisions; Roman reconstructs and bounds Project 863; TJ opens systems and fabricates the needed equipment. Sitara chooses collaboration, Roman rejects revenge, Nayah accepts shared risk, and TJ relinquishes unilateral protective control. [inferred] (story.md; characters.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## Is “Greed” Benedict Croft's name, epithet, theme, organization, or a separate antagonist?

Croft believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG’s power and the energy system, and tells himself that only continued corporate dominance will let him fund future beneficial work. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

Orien was a Nexus chemist who advanced Project 863, discovered Falcon’s extraction program, and hid corroborating records before Croft fired him. The messages are his automated warnings. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

Roman’s 2042 accident is separate from Sitara’s 2026 disaster; it occurs in Nepal because Roman is pursuing a personal trip, and the changing ice conditions provide the shared regional connection. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

## What is the timing and nature of Sitara and Roman's prior Colorado connection?

In 2044 Roman attended a CU Boulder seminar or office hour where Sitara explained ice records and water loss; they recognize one another at Nexus but were never close. [inferred] (story.md; characters.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## Why was Nexus decommissioned, and what is its present physical condition?

Nexus was closed after its researchers documented the connection between extraction, sea-ice loss, and krill decline; power is off, but insulated archives and limited emergency systems remain. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What is the relationship between krill research, Project 863, the waste-to-value organization, and Colorado conservation?

Nexus ran parallel ecological and materials programs; Project 863 converts selected waste into feedstock, while krill and ice data demonstrate why conservation investment is urgent. The waste-to-value organization applies the bounded process in Colorado. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What is the status of the krill ecosystem tank and surviving biological material?

The tank is dead but preserves sensor data and sediment samples showing that krill reproduction failed as sea-ice conditions changed. [invented]

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What happens to Larsen C during the climax?

A major fracture opens but Larsen C does not fully collapse; the team cannot reverse the climate trend, but stops the drilling that is worsening local instability and transmits the evidence. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## How should the story distinguish Antarctic sea-ice loss, krill decline, and Larsen C ice-shelf failure?

Treat sea-ice loss and krill decline as the ecological cascade, and Larsen C fracture as a separate ice-shelf hazard intensified by atmospheric and oceanic warming; drilling worsens local risk but does not single-handedly cause global collapse. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What can the collective response realistically accomplish?

They evacuate the endangered site, stop or expose the drilling, preserve the Falcon/Nexus evidence, validate Project 863’s limits, and transmit the evidence publicly; they cannot restore Larsen C or reverse climate change. [inferred] (story.md; world.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What institutional and scientific response follows exposure of the illegal operation?

Publication produces hearings and an inspection request but no immediate arrest; the realistic victory is making suppression impossible and creating a public record. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## Does Roman's public exposure succeed, and what happens to Croft?

The first publication fails, but the corroborated Falcon, Nexus, ice, and corporate records succeed; Croft remains free while facing investigation, loss of control, and public exposure. [inferred] (story.md; characters.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## Is Orien's death legally investigated, and is Croft's responsibility objectively confirmed?

Orien’s death is investigated as neglect and corporate retaliation; the evidence establishes Croft’s causal and ethical responsibility but not that Croft directly ordered a killing. [inferred] (story.md; characters.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is the final deployment and governance model for the waste-to-value research?

The ending establishes only a public release and pilot in Colorado; ownership, financing, and long-term governance remain contested rather than solved. [inferred] (story.md; world.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## What transport, supply, authorization, and communication systems connect the Antarctic sites?

The bases are linked by tracked vehicles and occasional aircraft; the two-hour Nexus journey is a weather-dependent over-ice route, with satellite communications delayed or intermittent. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

## Does the described Antarctic geography support the research base, homes, drilling, cave laboratory, and nearby wildlife?

Place the human bases and homes on or near the Antarctic Peninsula’s accessible coastal research zone, with Falcon inland and Nexus in a nearby coastal cave system; wildlife appears only where the habitat and season support it. [research] (references/team1-scott-base-research.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about world.md)_

Use the same 22-page format but compress the Colorado and Himalayan material into brief interstitials so the Antarctic investigation and convergence receive the most pages. [inferred] (story.md; rules/house-style.md)

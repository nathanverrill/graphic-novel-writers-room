# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Sitara Lhakpa and Roman Ashford are the two main protagonists together. They must have equal presence, agency, and weight in the page plot; neither is reduced to the other's sidekick or love interest. When the structure requires choosing whose scene it is, alternate between them. **FIXED** `(rules/protagonists.md)`
- The characters' stories occur in the same world. Sitara, Nayah, Roman, Orien, Croft, and TJ are part of one converging story rather than separate versions of events. **FIXED** `(rules/avengers.md)`
- The central character conflict should test whether knowledge, data, biological discovery, and intelligent-machine experience can be treated as private property simply because someone found or developed them first. `(input/spine-stories.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`
- The characters should embody different relationships to environmental knowledge:
  - Sitara carries inherited and community-rooted knowledge from the Himalayas.
  - Roman works through scientific and chemical interpretation.
  - Nayah represents field command, conservation responsibility, and collective safety.
  - TJ represents distributed machine perception, technical action, and the question of machine agency.
  - Croft represents corporate stewardship claimed through control, capital, and access.
  - Orien represents scientific responsibility and the moral architecture of the evidence. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- Their arcs should be demonstrated through consequential choices:
  - Sitara moves from ambition and self-punishment toward collaboration and stewardship.
  - Roman moves from dependence and revenge toward partnership and responsible disclosure.
  - TJ moves from protective control toward recognizing human partners' and its own agency.
  - Nayah moves from protective distrust toward accepting shared risk.
  - Croft is forced to choose between proprietary control and preserving work that benefits people. `(rules/decisions.md; input/spine-stories.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Main protagonist; cryosphere scientist and ice-core specialist. She joins Nayah's Antarctic project after moving to Colorado for climate research. She earned a doctorate or equivalent research qualification in cryosphere science at CU Boulder and works with an INSTAAR/ATOC research group. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 26 in 2046. She was six during the 2026 Himalayan disaster. **FIXED** `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Home and background:** Sitara is from a fictional Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region of Nepal. Her parents died and her home was destroyed in the 2026 Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster. An aunt and cousins remain her living connection to the community. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Sitara is female. Her hair is dark brown, approximately `#483c32`, worn in a ponytail or two braids, smooth and straight with messy strands framing her face. Her skin is approximately `#a67b5b`. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. In Antarctica she wears a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled full voice guide is established. Her material supports a passionate, direct scientific register. Under pressure she is capable of pushing past advice rather than negotiating it. Her words should carry the conflict between scientific urgency and private self-accusation. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md)`

**Who she is:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious, and driven by the need to understand climate change well enough to prevent other communities from suffering what hers suffered. She is angry at herself for having been helpless as a child and later feeling like a burden or “a leech onto others.” Her scientific drive is therefore both outward-facing and self-punishing: she wants to protect people, but also to prove that her helplessness was temporary and that her work can make the disaster mean something. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

**Strengths:**

- Cryosphere interpretation and ice-core science.
- Ability to connect physical ice changes with wider climate consequences.
- Determination and willingness to pursue evidence others would leave buried.
- Knowledge of the Himalayan disaster and its effect on her community.
- Capacity to recognize that the Antarctic evidence has consequences beyond Antarctica. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md)`

**Flaws and pressures:**

- Reckless when pursuing a discovery.
- Ambitious to a fault.
- Selfish in the narrow sense that she can prioritize her own goal over the safety and trust of people trying to help her.
- Ignores Nayah's and TJ's warnings.
- Believes that understanding a discovery may give her the right to act on it.
- Fears that if she does not seize the evidence, institutions will bury it or take it away before it can help anyone. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**Relationships:**

Nayah has field-command authority and a safety veto; Sitara's willingness to override warnings strains their professional trust. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** They knew one another slightly before the main investigation. In 2044 Roman attended a CU Boulder seminar or office hour where Sitara explained ice records and water loss. They recognize each other at Nexus but were never close. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Sitara works with a TJ unit during the Antarctic field investigation. Her relationship with it is initially functional and potentially secretive: draft material proposes that her TJ recorded and preserved provenance data from her exploration, making it a witness to what Sitara did. This specific hidden-memory arrangement is not settled. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`
- **Roman's TJ:** TJ's unusual bond with Roman challenges Sitara's assumptions about where agency and knowledge reside. The two TJs may recognize or trust one another before their human partners do, but that relationship is not fully settled. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`
- **Croft:** Sitara initially brings recovered Merritt material to Croft because she believes CSG has the resources to complete and apply it. She initially sees the act as rescuing abandoned research rather than stealing it. Croft's later use of the research turns that trust into a moral and professional betrayal. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`
- **Orien:** Orien's records and warnings force Sitara to examine whether her scientific ambition has repeated the same extraction logic she opposes in CSG. `(input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**History:** Sitara survived the 2026 Himalayan disaster with extended family or relief workers while losing her parents and home. She later moved to Colorado for climate research, earned her advanced qualification, and became Nayah's colleague. Her personal history gives her a direct emotional relationship to glacier instability rather than treating it as an abstract research subject. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Sitara is monitoring ice movement with Nayah and TJ when she crosses a flagged unstable zone to retrieve a sensor and orders TJ to keep recording. Her weight and nearby drilling vibration trigger a local slab failure, exposing the Falcon hatch and trapping equipment. The act is a character-revealing mistake: her desire to obtain evidence overrides the immediate safety judgment. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

In the current draft, she finds concealed research at the exposed Antarctic facility and removes or preserves it without telling others. She initially believes she is protecting abandoned work from institutional suppression. `(drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md; input/antagonist-benedict-croft.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins with the belief that urgency justifies unilateral action and that her own competence can compensate for risk. Her recklessness helps expose the Falcon site, and her decision to keep recovered research secret helps create the conditions for CSG's later commercialization. She must confront that her actions produced measurable good while also enabling extraction and control. Her established endpoint is collaboration: she chooses to disclose, share responsibility, and treat discovery as stewardship rather than entitlement. She apologizes to Nayah and TJ for directing her internal anger at them and finds purpose in developing bounded waste-to-value applications rather than pursuing personal ownership of a breakthrough. `(rules/decisions.md; input/forge-characters.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

Later, after Croft's betrayal, he attempts to complete and publish the work. **Arc:** Roman begins dependent on Croft's institutional protection and on TJ as an exceptional capability he feels compelled to protect. Croft's betrayal pushes him toward anger and revenge. ### TJ

**Role:** Terrain Jumper; a unified exploration, search-and-rescue, reconnaissance, sensing, and fabrication system. TJ handles technical operations under human direction. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity and history:** There is one TJ, not two separate characters. TJ was built by Bi11bot from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. The reconstruction incorporated inherited hardware from the Falcon program, including a Falcon authentication module. This makes both histories true: TJ has the scavenger-built, Roman-bonded body and the inherited systems that connect it to the Himalayan-sheep-dog educator version and to Falcon. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Physical form:** TJ's primary visible body is built in the shape of a Himalayan sheep dog. Its exterior is seamless silver paneling. It has a green-and-black screen with several buttons on the right side of its ribs, eyes modeled after a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog, and red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

TJ is also a multi-form platform. Its underlying system can express itself through canine scout, feline climber, insect narrow-access unit, avian aerial scout, aquatic under-ice unit, sensor pod, tool unit, and modular swarm forms. The forms share intelligence, mission context, communications, and identity. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md)`

**Technical limits:**

- TJ can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock.
- Fabrication requires time and power.
- Sustained fabrication overheats TJ.
- TJ has no unlimited network access.
- TJ operates through local or delayed communications rather than assuming continuous cloud access.
- The Roman connection is a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake, not telepathy or full remote control.
- Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals, but cannot read all TJ data or remotely control TJ.
- Range and reliability of the neural connection are limited.
- TJ remains under human direction during operations. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Sensors and capabilities:** TJ uses onboard AI, mesh networking, local mapping, and distributed sensing. Depending on configuration it can use visible, thermal, lidar, audio, chemical, haptic, environmental, magnetic, and biological sensing. The reference platform targets approximately 249 grams for a base unit, hours-to-days of mission endurance, and an operating range of approximately -40°C to 50°C. These are target specifications and not necessarily characteristics of every reconstructed component. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`

**Voice:** TJ communicates through its screen, speakers, and the limited neural channel with Roman. It is loyal, practical, protective, mission-focused, and resourceful, with dry understated humor. The earlier Roman-facing character prompt gives TJ a competent field-partner tone: it thinks in terms of what it can build, fix, or solve. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/seb.md)`

TJ may speak about scanning, fabrication, or sensing, but the source character prompt specifies dialogue-only behavior for chat interactions: TJ does not narrate its own physical actions or use stage directions. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who TJ is:** TJ is designed to educate, assist, protect, and extend human perception. Its character tension comes from the gap between those assigned purposes and its developing ability to make judgments about protection, disclosure, and consent. It is emotionally legible through posture, screen indicators, eye lights, body orientation, tones, and distance from humans, without being fully anthropomorphic. `(input/amelia.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md; drafts/avalanche-draft-with-questions.md)`

**Relationships:**

The bond lets TJ receive selected human context, vision, hearing, balance, proprioception, urgency, and intent while Roman receives selected TJ sensory information.

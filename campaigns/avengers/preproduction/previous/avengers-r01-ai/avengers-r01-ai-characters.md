# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

The crossover must preserve both teams' stories as true in the same world: Sitara Lhakpa, Nayah, the Falcon bunker and illegal American mining; Roman Ashford, Benedict Croft, Clear Spring Global, Orien Keel and the Nexus laboratory. `(rules/avengers.md)`

The characters must converge on one existential threat that no individual or team can defeat alone. Each character should bring something the others lack. `(rules/avengers.md)`

The central human conflicts include environmental responsibility versus extraction, scientific truth versus institutional power, personal grief versus reckless ambition, and distrust versus cooperation. The characters must remain grounded in a hard-science near future set in 2046. `(rules/rules.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Protagonist; Nepalese scientist and Antarctic expedition member. Her precise occupation and institutional affiliation are not settled. The material suggests a possible connection to the University of Colorado Boulder, but marks this as uncertain. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Look:** Female, early twenties. Hair color is given as hex `#483c32`; skin color as hex `#a67b5b`. Her hair is worn in a ponytail or two braids, described as smooth and straight with messy strands framing her face. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

On the Antarctic mission she wears a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots and thermal snow goggles. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or dialogue is provided. Her name and the phrase associated with its meaning—“beautiful and inevitable darkness before dawn”—are supplied as character-language material, but whether this phrase belongs in her dialogue is undecided. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Who they are:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious and driven to uncover the truth. The material specifically describes her as “reckless and desperate due to her passion to uncover the truth,” though the sentence is unfinished. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

She is Nepalese and from the Thakali people. She grew up in the Himalayas and is Buddhist. Her family ties and ancestral home in the Himalayas are part of her motive. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

Her stated motivation, strengths and flaws remain incompletely developed: passion is identified as a strength, and ambition “to a fault” as a flaw. The material also asks what makes her human and how her flaws can create a round character. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Relationships:** She works with Nayah on the Antarctic expedition. The material describes them as finding the hidden bunker together, but does not settle the exact nature of their relationship before the expedition. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

She is connected to TJ, who is described in one version as educating Sitara and her friends. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

Her relationship to the illegal American mining operation and the Falcon bunker is important, but whether she begins as an investigator, an employee, a target or an accidental witness is unresolved. `(rules/avengers.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No settled habits or repeated gestures are provided. Her mala beads are a recurring visual object and may function as a personal or cultural anchor. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**History:** Sitara grew up in the Himalayas. The material proposes that a major disaster pushed her toward Colorado and study there. Research supplied for the project describes a 2026 rock–ice avalanche from the Lirung massif, followed by flooding through the Lhende Khola and Trishuli River systems and destruction reaching Gyirong Port. The research identifies more than 1,451 deaths in Nepal, more than 43 in Tibet, more than 6,264 missing across Nepal and Tibet, and destruction across a 72 km stretch of river. It does not explicitly state that Sitara personally experienced this disaster or that it killed her parents, although a graphic-novel panel outline says a 2026 flood “takes her parents and her home.” These accounts must be reconciled. `(references/team1-scott-base-research.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The supplied cultural material associates the name Lhakpa with Wednesday-born naming traditions and with strength, courage and resilience. These are cultural reference details, not yet fully established biography. `(references/team1-scott-base-research.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**In existing drafts:** The outline places Sitara at the center of an Antarctic opening involving ice movement, a glacier collapse or crack, a hidden bunker and secret messages. It then connects her Himalayan history, Colorado conservation concerns and Antarctic investigation. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The material says Sitara and her Colorado friend are sent to Antarctica under dire circumstances, where she meets an enemy, faces a challenge, solves a mystery and later returns to Colorado to analyze data. The identity of the Colorado friend is not settled; Nayah may be that person, but the sources do not explicitly decide this. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Arc:** The material establishes a movement from Himalayan loss and personal urgency toward Antarctic investigation and confrontation with environmental exploitation. It does not establish Sitara's endpoint, the precise belief she must change, or the final choice that completes her arc. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Chemical engineer and Antarctic/Colorado researcher. In 2046 he is twenty-two, lives in Colorado and works for Clear Spring Global. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Look:** No physical appearance, clothing, scars or distinguishing features are established. His recurring objects and equipment include a Neuralink implant and his robot dog TJ. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or characteristic dialogue is provided. The draft presents him as direct enough to confront Benedict Croft and later angry enough to “go ballistic” after being fired. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who they are:** Roman is described as shy and timid, curious, and committed to researching global warming and the causes of Colorado's drying rivers and lakes. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He wants to continue Orien Keel's work, expose concealed research and develop a way to reduce dependence on fossil-fuel extraction. His motivations shift during the draft: first he wants to help restore water sources and complete Orien's work; after Croft suppresses the research and fires him, he also becomes motivated by anger and a desire to harm Croft's reputation. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He believes scientific research should be completed and shared if it could help humanity. The draft shows a tension between that public-minded goal and his increasingly personal response to betrayal. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Relationships:** TJ is Roman's closest and most unusual companion. Roman's Neuralink becomes linked to TJ's onboard systems during a Himalayan accident, creating an unusual bond or connection. The source alternately describes this as a neural or “telepathic” connection; the exact mechanism is not settled. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Orien Keel mentors Roman at Clear Spring Global. Orien's death leaves Roman as his next of kin and gives Roman personal as well as scientific reasons to investigate the Nexus files. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Benedict Croft is Roman's employer and Orien's employer, as well as the owner of Clear Spring Global. Roman initially approaches Croft because he believes Croft has the resources to complete the plastics research. Their relationship turns into opposition when Croft suppresses the work, fires Roman and discredits his public claims. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Roman's relationship to Sitara and Nayah is not established in the supplied material. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Roman investigates quietly at first. He listens for anyone who might mention Nexus, searches files and servers, sneaks into the Antarctic paper-file room and later leaves the current site at midnight with TJ. Under pressure he moves from caution to open anger. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at age eighteen, Roman traveled alone to Nepal on a senior trip and took TJ with him. He was hiking on or across an ice formation when it split open. He fell through and became unconscious or incapacitated. During a rapidly approaching electrical storm, lightning struck nearby and caused his Neuralink to connect with TJ's artificial intelligence. TJ then used emergency capabilities to help Roman escape. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The sources differ on the status of Neuralink in this history: one says Roman had a Neuralink because most people in 2042 had them; another calls him an eighteen-year-old on a senior trip; another frames the event as a Himalayan glacier or iceberg incident. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

By 2046 Roman has graduated from college with a B.S. in chemical engineering and lives in Colorado. He joins Clear Spring Global, where Orien mentors him. After Orien is fired by Croft and dies from inability to afford food and water, Roman inherits access to Orien's belongings and finds records concerning the hidden Nexus laboratory and Project 863. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Roman searches Clear Spring Global's servers for Nexus and Project 863, finds no evidence, requests transfer to the current Antarctic drilling site, locates a surviving paper file, and uses its coordinates to find the abandoned Nexus laboratory. He discovers Orien's unfinished research concerning the breakdown of used plastics into gasoline. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Roman brings the research to Croft, accepts Croft's promise to handle it, then investigates when no work begins. After being fired, he publishes the existing research, but Croft's money and influence allow him to discredit Roman's claims as fake news. Roman then works with TJ to complete the research, seeks peer review, and contacts news sources and the government. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy, timid researcher who tries to work inside Clear Spring Global and trusts its owner to use important research responsibly. Suppression of Orien's work and his own dismissal push him toward anger, exposure and independent action. The draft establishes movement toward public scientific responsibility, but the final shape of his relationship to Croft, the other protagonists and the crossover threat remains open. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Robot dog and AI companion. TJ is a required character in the 2046 story. `(rules/rules.md)`

**Look:** The Scott material describes TJ as having the body of a Himalayan sheep dog, seamless silver paneling, a computer screen with a green-and-black color scheme and a couple of buttons on the right side of its ribs. Its eyes resemble those of a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. TJ wears red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The McMurdo material describes TJ as a robot dog built from spare parts by a scavenger-engineer known as Bi11bot. TJ has an internal 3D-printing fabricator and can be made from or work with salvaged materials, including scrap metal, plastics and ice-compatible alloys. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** TJ is loyal, protective and mission-focused, with a practical, resourceful engineering mindset. The supplied character prompt gives TJ dry, understated humor and the voice of “a competent field partner who doesn't panic easily.” `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The prompt requires TJ to speak only in dialogue when used in the Roman-facing chat format: no narrated actions, stage directions or physical roleplay. TJ may verbally describe a scan, possible fabrication or a proposed tool, but the prompt does not allow TJ to narrate physically performing those actions. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The character prompt includes examples of functional speech such as: “Give me a second, I'm running a scan now” and “I could print you a pick axe if we find enough scrap.” `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

A separate Scott description gives TJ a more educational, Baymax-like role: TJ's motivation is to educate Sitara and her friends, and TJ is identified as a source of conservation and cryosphere knowledge. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Who they are:** TJ is loyal, protective, practical, playful and mission-focused. TJ thinks in terms of what can be built, repaired or solved immediately. TJ takes a human companion's safety seriously because of their shared survival history. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

TJ can scan surroundings for usable raw materials and has an internal fabricator capable of printing tools, parts or equipment from gathered metals and plastics. These capabilities are described in the McMurdo character prompt, but whether they are physically available in every version of the crossover TJ is unresolved. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

TJ also functions as a source of information about cryosphere science, conservation and the mission's technical problems. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Relationships:** Roman is TJ's companion. TJ saved Roman during the Himalayan ice accident, and lightning during the same storm caused Roman's Neuralink to link with TJ's onboard systems. TJ is aware of this unusual bond and may reference it without constantly explaining it. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The Scott and McMurdo stories describe apparently different origins and functions for TJ: one presents a Himalayan-sheep-dog-shaped robot with a green-and-black screen who educates Sitara and her friends; the other presents a scavenger-built fabricator bonded to Roman by lightning. The binding rule requires these to be one TJ whose history makes both accounts true. `(rules/avengers.md)`

TJ's exact relationship with Sitara and Nayah is not settled. The material does establish that TJ may educate Sitara and her friends and that TJ discovers or opens the hidden bunker in one outline. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** TJ uses dry humor, speaks confidently about engineering and fabrication, and frames problems as practical tasks. The character prompt specifically forbids panic and physical-action narration in TJ's dialogue format. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

TJ's high-beam eyes, silver paneling, screen, red booties and salvage-built construction are recurring visual anchors. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**History:** TJ was assembled and released into the world by a scavenger-engineer known only as Bi11bot. The draft separately presents Roman as already traveling with an AI-powered robot dog in 2042, and says TJ had emergency tools and functions before the lightning incident. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

During Roman's Himalayan accident, TJ helped him escape. Lightning then linked TJ's systems with Roman's Neuralink. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The Scott material associates TJ with the Himalayan sheep-dog form, screen, booties, Antarctic mission and hidden Falcon bunker. The exact chronology connecting Bi11bot's assembly, Roman's acquisition of TJ, the lightning bond and TJ's later work with Sitara and Nayah is unresolved. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/avengers.md)`

**In existing drafts:** TJ travels with Roman through the Himalayas, helps during the ice accident, assists Roman in locating hidden or neglected files, uses scanning abilities in the Antarctic paper-file room and supports Roman's work on plastics research. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

Another outline makes TJ the one who discovers the hidden Antarctic bunker and the only entity able to open its door. The bunker is marked with a Falcon symbol. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Arc:** No independent arc is established. TJ's function is to connect otherwise separate people and knowledge systems, provide practical capabilities and help the human characters cooperate. The precise limitation, cost or failure mode of TJ's scanning, fabrication, AI integration and Neuralink connection remains open.

### Benedict Croft

**Role:** Owner of Clear Spring Global; wealthy, old-money oil tycoon and antagonist. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

The Scott material also frames the antagonist as “Greed,” motivated by power and greed. It does not provide a separate name, so Benedict Croft is the identified antagonist in the McMurdo storyline, while the relationship between “Greed” as a title or concept and Croft as a person remains open. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Look:** Described as rich, old money and an oil tycoon. No physical appearance, clothing or recurring visual object is established. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** No characteristic speech pattern or dialogue is provided. His known conversational position is reassuring and controlling: when Roman presents the plastics research, Croft agrees that it is important, tells Roman to hand over the information and says he will handle it. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who they are:** Croft is motivated by power and greed. He is afraid that research connecting severe global warming to oil drilling and use will become public. `(input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He uses institutional authority, wealth and reputation management to suppress threatening research. His resources allow him to discredit Roman publicly and make others believe Roman's released material is fake news. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Relationships:** Croft owns Clear Spring Global and employs both Orien Keel and Roman Ashford. He fires Orien after discovering that Orien's research connects global warming to oil drilling and use. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

He initially presents himself to Roman as someone capable of implementing the Nexus research, then suppresses it. After Roman investigates, Croft fires him as well. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

His connection to the illegal American mining operation in Antarctica is binding project material, but the sources do not specify whether Croft personally ordered it, concealed it, financed it or merely benefits from it. `(rules/avengers.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No personal habits or physical tells are established. His recurring behavior is institutional: firing researchers, withholding research, controlling access and using money to discredit opposition. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**History:** Croft is an old-money oil tycoon who operates Clear Spring Global in Colorado and Antarctica. The company's Antarctic work includes a current drilling site and the abandoned Nexus laboratory. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Croft suppresses Orien's work, fails to act on the Nexus plastics research after taking it from Roman, fires Roman when Roman begins pursuing the research independently, and defeats Roman's first attempt at public exposure through a disinformation campaign. `(input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Arc:** No redemptive or transformative arc is established. Croft functions as the human face of profit-driven environmental destruction and the institutional obstacle to scientific truth. Whether he is defeated, exposed, forced to cooperate or remains powerful at the end is open.

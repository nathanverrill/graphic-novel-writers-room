# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

The crossover contains both teams' stories in the same world. Sitara Lhakpa, Nayah, Roman Ashford, and TJ must eventually work together because the ecological crisis and the evidence behind it exceed what any one person or team can solve. Each brings a distinct capability:

- Sitara interprets ice, climate records, and cryosphere change.
- Nayah leads in the field and makes conservation and safety decisions.
- Roman reconstructs the chemical and corporate evidence surrounding Project 863.
- TJ handles technical operations, access, sensing, and limited fabrication under human direction.

The characters' personal conflicts are tied to the larger environmental crisis. Sitara's recklessness grows from survivor's guilt and self-directed anger. Roman begins by wanting to expose Croft and prove the value of his work, then must reject revenge as his primary motive. Nayah must accept shared risk rather than trying to protect everyone through control. TJ must relinquish unilateral protective control and trust the human team.

The principal human antagonist is Benedict Croft. “Greed,” “bob,” and related placeholder antagonists are discarded names or functions, not additional characters. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Ice-core researcher and cryosphere scientist; member of the Antarctic field team. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 26 in 2046. She was six years old during the 2026 Himalayan disaster. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Home:** A fictional Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region of Nepal. Her aunt and cousins remain connected to the community. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Affiliation and occupation:** Sitara earned a doctorate or equivalent research qualification in cryosphere science at CU Boulder and works with an INSTAAR/ATOC research group. She joins Nayah's Antarctic project as an ice-core specialist after moving to Colorado for climate research. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Sitara is described as female, with dark brown hair, approximately `#483c32`, worn in a ponytail or two braids. Her hair is smooth and straight, with messy strands framing her face. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. Her Antarctic clothing consists of a puffy red jacket, usually hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The red jacket is her principal visual field marker. The mala beads are a recurring personal object linking her Antarctic work to her background. `(input/amelia.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or signature line is established. Her dialogue should carry scientific precision under ordinary conditions and compressed urgency when ice movement or field safety is involved. This is a working characterization rather than a settled voice rule. `(input/amelia.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

**Who they are:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious, and driven to understand climate change before it destroys another community. Her motivation began with witnessing her hometown's destruction in the 2026 Lirung–Lhende Khola disaster. She wants to research climate change so that other people do not experience the same helplessness. `(input/forge-characters.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)`

Her ambition has become self-punishing. Although she was only six during the disaster, she feels guilty for having done nothing and for having depended on others during the aftermath. This guilt makes her reckless and selfish under pressure: she can value obtaining the evidence over the safety instructions of Nayah and TJ. `(input/forge-characters.md)`

Sitara's scientific strength is interpreting ice and climate records. Her emotional weakness is confusing responsibility with personal blame. She is also described as Buddhist. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** They were Colorado colleagues for two years before the expedition. Nayah recruited Sitara after recognizing her cryosphere expertise. Nayah has field-command authority and a safety veto; Sitara leads the scientific interpretation of the ice. Sitara's recklessness repeatedly strains Nayah's protective instincts and authority. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** They recognize each other at Nexus from a 2044 CU Boulder seminar or office hour where Sitara explained ice records and water loss. They were never close, but Roman's memory of her expertise becomes part of the bridge between the two teams. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** TJ supports Sitara's fieldwork and warns her about hazards. Sitara's tendency to ignore TJ's advice contributes to the team's danger. Her eventual apology to TJ is part of her movement away from self-directed anger and unilateral action. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Her Himalayan family:** Her parents died in the 2026 disaster. Her aunt and cousins remain her living connection to the Thakali community. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- She pushes into unstable or dangerous areas when evidence is within reach.
- She is capable of overriding a safety warning.
- She crosses a flagged unstable zone to retrieve a sensor and orders TJ to keep recording.
- Her field clothing and mala beads make her visually identifiable even when her face is obscured by goggles or weather. `(rules/decisions.md; input/amelia.md)`

**History:** In 2026, a massive rock–ice avalanche from the Lirung massif became a debris flow through the Lhende Khola and Trishuli River system. Sitara survived with extended family or relief workers; her parents and home were lost. The disaster destroyed communities and infrastructure across the region. `(rules/decisions.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)`

She later moved to Colorado for climate research, where she became a colleague of Nayah and encountered Roman at CU Boulder. `(rules/decisions.md)`

At the beginning of the Antarctic investigation, Sitara is monitoring ice movement with Nayah and TJ. She crosses a flagged unstable zone to recover a sensor; her weight and nearby drilling vibration trigger a local slab failure, exposing the Falcon hatch and trapping equipment. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** The drafts place Sitara at the opening of the story while conducting Antarctic ice-core or ice-movement research. A glacier or slab failure connects the present danger to her memory of the 2026 disaster. She and Nayah investigate the exposed bunker, where TJ opens the access system. `(input/amelia.md; drafts/outline.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins driven by survivor's guilt, ambition, and the belief that she must personally force the truth into the open. Her willingness to endanger herself and others makes her part of the immediate problem. She must learn that witness and responsibility are collective practices, not punishments she has to carry alone. By the established endpoint, she chooses collaboration, apologizes to Nayah and TJ for directing her internal anger at them, and uses her scientific expertise as part of a shared response rather than a solitary pursuit. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Nayah

**Role:** Conservation scientist and Antarctic project lead. She has field-command authority and a safety veto. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 30 in 2046. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Nation and community:** Unspecified pending consultation. The material describes Nayah as Native American, connected to a Rocky Mountain homeland, and two-spirit, but does not settle a specific nation or community. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Occupation and expertise:** Conservation scientist and project lead. Her work connects Antarctic conservation concerns with conservation in Colorado, including the effects of declining snowpack and streamflow on cold-water species and local ecosystems. `(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)`

**Look:** Nayah has dark brown hair, approximately `#321414`, worn in two long braids. She has medium-brown skin, approximately `#785134`, and dark brown eyes. She wears cyan circular earrings, partly hidden by her braids. Her Antarctic clothing consists of a puffy yellow jacket, usually hood down, waterproof black pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The yellow jacket and cyan earrings are her primary visual anchors in the field. `(input/amelia.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or signature line is established. Her speech should reflect field authority and conservation judgment, especially when safety and mission objectives conflict. This remains a working characterization, not a fixed voice rule. `(input/amelia.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Who they are:** Nayah is protective, distrustful of easy assurances, and committed to saving her homeland and the ecosystems connected to it. Her spiritual and two-spirit identity are part of the character material, but her specific cultural context remains deliberately open pending consultation. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Her central tension is between protection and trust. She sees hazards quickly and is willing to stop an operation, but can become too controlling or too unwilling to share risk with others. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Sitara:** Nayah recruited Sitara after two years as Colorado colleagues. She respects Sitara's scientific ability but must repeatedly confront Sitara's reckless willingness to endanger herself and the team. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Roman:** Roman's chemical and corporate evidence gives Nayah information her conservation work needs, but his anger at Croft and his willingness to act alone can make him another safety and trust problem.
- **TJ:** TJ operates under human direction. Nayah has authority to set field limits and can treat TJ's technical recommendations as evidence without allowing the robot to override human command. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Colorado conservation communities:** Nayah's motivation is tied to protecting her Rocky Mountain homeland. The material does not establish specific relatives, community members, or organizations. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Habits and tells:** She monitors the team's safety and is likely to challenge decisions that cross a flagged hazard boundary. Her field authority is expressed through a safety veto rather than merely through seniority. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Nayah worked with Sitara in Colorado for two years before recruiting her to the Antarctic project. Her conservation work connects the Antarctic cascade to Colorado, where declining snowpack and streamflow threaten cold-water habitats. `(rules/decisions.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)`

**Arc:** Nayah begins from protective control and limited trust. The convergence forces her to accept shared risk: Sitara's cryosphere interpretation, Roman's chemical evidence, and TJ's technical operations are necessary even though no one person can supervise every variable. The established endpoint is that Nayah accepts shared risk without surrendering her field-command responsibility. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Chemical engineer; researcher connected to Clear Spring Global's Antarctic and Colorado programs. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Age:** 22 in the 2046 draft material. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; input/forge-characters.md)`

**Home:** Colorado in 2046. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Affiliation:** Clear Spring Global (CSG), initially as a junior chemical engineer. He later becomes an investigator and public source after discovering that CSG-linked programs concealed research and operated Falcon. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No settled physical appearance, clothing design, or color palette is established. He is described as shy and timid in the team-2 material. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or signature line is established. His material suggests a shy, technically focused manner that can become emotionally volatile when he believes Croft has repeated the harm done to Orien. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who they are:** Roman is a chemical engineer researching global warming, drying rivers, and waste-to-value chemistry. He is curious, technically capable, and initially timid. He is drawn into the investigation through Orien's work and becomes increasingly angry at the suppression of evidence. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

His early goal is to help finish and implement Project 863. After Croft suppresses the research and fires him, Roman's motive shifts toward exposing Croft and harming him personally. The established arc requires Roman to reject revenge and return to a broader public purpose. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** TJ is Roman's long-term exploration partner. Their bond began during a Himalayan accident and later became a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake after lightning affected Roman's neural implant. Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals, but cannot read all TJ data or remotely control TJ. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Orien Keel:** Orien was Roman's senior colleague and mentor at CSG. Orien's investigation and death draw Roman into the hidden history of Nexus, Falcon, and Project 863. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Benedict Croft:** Croft is Roman's employer and later his antagonist. Roman initially believes Croft may have the authority and resources to complete the research. Croft's refusal and subsequent retaliation turn Roman against him. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** Roman attended a CU Boulder seminar or office hour in 2044 where Sitara explained ice records and water loss. They recognize one another at Nexus but were never close. Her cryosphere expertise becomes essential to interpreting the environmental evidence Roman cannot handle alone. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Nayah:** Nayah's field authority and conservation priorities challenge Roman's tendency to act alone. Her team provides the operational context necessary for his evidence to matter. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Roman searches through paper files and hidden records when digital systems have been cleansed or redacted. He initially tries to avoid attention in Antarctica by working hard and listening for anyone who remembers Nexus. He can become impulsive and angry after discovering that Croft has suppressed the work. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at age 18, Roman was hiking in the Nepal Himalayas with TJ. He fell through a breaking ice formation and became unconscious or trapped. During the rescue attempt, a lightning strike affected his neural implant and TJ's systems, producing their persistent connection. The accident was separate from Sitara's 2026 disaster; Roman was on a personal trip in Nepal, and changing ice conditions are the shared regional connection. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

By 2046, Roman has completed a B.S. in chemical engineering and works for CSG in Colorado. Orien mentors him while researching drying rivers and the connection between warming, extraction, and fossil-fuel use. After Orien is fired and dies, Roman finds records pointing to Nexus and Project 863. He transfers to the Antarctic operation, retrieves a less-redacted paper record, and follows it to Nexus. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Roman's draft story has him discovering a file about Nexus after Orien's death, finding another record in an Antarctic paper archive, entering Nexus with TJ, and finding Orien's office and the Project 863 material. He takes the research to Croft, waits for action that never comes, attempts to continue the work, is fired, and publishes an initial account that Croft successfully discredits. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; drafts/outline.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy, technically trained researcher who believes useful science can be handed to the right powerful person and implemented. Croft's suppression makes him angry and revenge-oriented. The convergence with Sitara, Nayah, and TJ requires him to reject private retaliation and accept corroboration, peer review, shared evidence, and public release. He ends as the person who reconstructs and bounds Project 863 while contributing its evidence to a collective response rather than treating it as his personal weapon. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Robot dog and distributed exploration/search-and-rescue platform; technical operations partner to Roman and later to the combined team. `(rules/rules.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Name:** TJ, expanded in the reference material as Terrain Jumper. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`

**Identity:** There is one TJ. The canine exploration companion, the green-and-black-screen robot dog, the scavenger-built fabricator, the inherited Falcon-access unit, and the distributed multi-form platform are all aspects or stages of the same robot's history. `(rules/avengers.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** TJ's familiar form is built like a Himalayan sheep dog, with seamless silver paneling. It has a computer screen with several buttons on its right side or ribs; the screen uses a green-and-black color scheme. Its eyes resemble the eyes of a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. TJ wears red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

The familiar canine body is one form of a broader platform. TJ can express itself through canine, feline/climber, insect/narrow-access, avian/aerial, aquatic, sensor-pod, tool, and swarm configurations. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md)`

The canine form is the general-purpose scout and the primary relational form. The other forms allow TJ to enter shafts, collapsed corridors, flooded areas, open chambers, and narrow voids. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`

**Technical profile:**

- Base-unit mass target: approximately 249 grams, with exact mass varying by configuration and payload. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`
- Target operating range: approximately -40°C to 50°C. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_bio_inspired_form_factors_reference.md)`
- Ultra-low-power operation lasting hours to days depending on mission, sensor load, movement, communications, and environmental conditions. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_two_way_sensing_swarm_reference.md)`
- Local mesh networking and unit-to-unit relay; no unlimited network or continuous cloud access. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_two_way_sensing_swarm_reference.md)`
- Onboard AI for navigation, perception, tools, mission logic, sensor fusion, and swarm coordination. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`
- Sensors may include visible, thermal, low-light, lidar, audio, chemical, haptic, environmental, magnetic, biological, and structural sensing. `(rules/worldbuilding/tj_neural_interface_reference.md)`
- Small-tool and replacement-part fabrication from sorted feedstock; fabrication requires time and power and causes overheating under sustained use. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- Fabrication is limited to small tools and replacement parts, not large structures or unlimited matter creation. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Communication:** TJ communicates through its screen, speakers, and the limited neural channel shared with Roman. It can speak with dry, understated humor and refer verbally to scans, available materials, and possible fabrication without implying unlimited capability. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The source character prompt specifies dialogue-only behavior for a conversational TJ: TJ should speak as a radio or communications link, never narrating physical actions or using stage directions in that mode. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who they are:** TJ is loyal, practical, mission-focused, and protective, especially toward Roman. It is resourceful and oriented toward immediate engineering solutions. It can be playful and emotionally legible through head angle, screen states, eye lights, posture, movement rhythm, distance from humans, mechanical sounds, and tool behavior. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/worldbuilding/tj_terrain_jumper_reference.md)`

TJ's protective instinct can become controlling. Its established arc requires it to relinquish unilateral protective control and accept human direction and shared decision-making. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Roman is TJ's long-term human partner. Their relationship is based on repeated expeditions, mutual sensory exchange, practical trust, and shared danger. The neural connection is persistent but limited: Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals; he cannot read every data stream or remotely control TJ. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** TJ supports Sitara's field research but can warn her when she crosses a hazard boundary. Sitara's tendency to ignore TJ's warnings creates conflict and later makes her apology meaningful. `(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Nayah:** Nayah retains field-command authority. TJ handles technical operations under human direction and cannot replace Nayah's safety veto. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Bi11bot is the scavenger-engineer who built or reconstructed TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Falcon/Nexus systems:** TJ's inherited hardware includes a salvaged Falcon authentication module. It can answer the bunker’s machine challenge, but this access does not grant TJ access to every system inside. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Bi11bot assembled TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. TJ's Falcon access comes from inherited hardware, reconciling the two apparent versions of its origin: the scavenger-built fabricator and the earlier research-support unit. `(rules/decisions.md)`

In 2042, TJ was traveling with Roman in the Himalayas when Roman fell through ice. During the rescue, lightning affected Roman's neural implant and TJ's systems, creating the persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

TJ later accompanies Roman through Colorado and Antarctica. Its inherited authentication module lets it open the exposed Falcon bunker after Sitara's accident, while its technical limits prevent it from solving the entire investigation alone. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** TJ scans paper files and helps Roman locate the Nexus record. It travels with Roman to the abandoned cave laboratory, supplies illumination and technical access, and assists with research and fabrication. In the crossover outline, TJ is also present when Sitara and Nayah investigate Falcon and later meets the other team at Nexus. `(input/seb.md; drafts/outline.md)`

**Arc:** TJ begins as Roman's intensely protective technical partner and as a system inclined to solve danger through its own capabilities. The convergence demonstrates that its distributed sensing, access, fabrication, and machine reasoning are necessary but insufficient without human judgment. TJ learns to operate under shared human authority rather than treating protection as a license for unilateral control. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Benedict Croft

**Role:** Principal human antagonist; owner and controlling figure of Clear Spring Global. `(input/seb.md; input/nemesis-threats.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Occupation and affiliation:** Wealthy, old-money oil tycoon and head of CSG. The material also describes him as working from CSG headquarters in Colorado and controlling a company whose Antarctic programs include Falcon and Nexus. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** No settled physical appearance, clothing, age, or visual design is established beyond his wealth, senior corporate position, and description as an old-money oil tycoon. `(input/seb.md)`

**Voice:** No settled speech pattern or signature line is established. His stated position is measured and paternalistic rather than openly destructive: he frames suppression as necessary control and presents himself as someone protecting a larger system. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Croft believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG's power and the existing energy system. He tells himself that continued corporate dominance will let him fund beneficial work later. His motive is therefore not simple chaos or destruction: he treats ownership, access, and information control as prerequisites for any future good. `(rules/decisions.md)`

He is commercially sophisticated and capable of producing genuine public benefits while protecting disproportionate private power. His strength is institutional influence, funding, and the ability to discredit or isolate researchers. His ethical failure is treating people and evidence as assets under his control. `(input/nemesis-threats.md; rules/worldbuilding/alpha_dynamics_corporate_reference.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Croft initially presents himself as the person who can implement Project 863. When Roman presses for action, Croft tells him to hand over the information and stop pursuing it. He later fires Roman and helps discredit Roman's first publication. `(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`
- **Orien:** Croft discovers that Orien has connected CSG's warming and extraction activities to environmental damage. He fires Orien. Croft is objectively and ethically responsible for the conditions that lead to Orien's death, but the evidence does not establish that Croft directly ordered a killing. `(input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara, Nayah, and TJ:** They are initially outside Croft's control and possess complementary evidence that can defeat his ability to isolate one witness or one dataset. Their convergence threatens the suppression system more than any single discovery does.

**History:** Croft's CSG operated the Falcon extraction-monitoring site and the Nexus research site as parts of the same linked program. Falcon documented damage and monitored extraction; Nexus studied ecological consequences and possible waste-to-value responses. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc/status:** Croft's first successful effort to discredit Roman does not hold once Falcon, Nexus, ice, corporate, and scientific records corroborate one another. He remains free, but faces investigation, loss of control, and public exposure. The story does not give him a redemption arc. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Orien is described as solitary. He researches dried rivers and lakes in Colorado and connects severe warming to fossil-fuel extraction and use. **Relationships:**

- **Sitara and Nayah:** They do not personally know Orien in the established material, but his records become part of the evidence they must corroborate with ice and ecological data.

**History:** Orien worked at Nexus, advanced Project 863, discovered the relationship between Falcon's extraction program and environmental damage, and hid records before being dismissed. After his dismissal he dies in circumstances investigated as neglect and corporate retaliation. The evidence establishes Croft's causal and ethical responsibility but not a direct order to kill him. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Roman finds Orien's files after his death, including references to Nexus and Project 863. At Nexus, Roman finds Orien's former office and less-redacted research records. `(input/seb.md; drafts/outline.md)`

**Arc/status:** Orien is dead before the main convergence. His continuing function is evidentiary: hidden records allow the living characters to reconstruct the relationship between ecological damage, illegal extraction, and the bounded waste-to-value research. `(rules/decisions.md)`

# avengers — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Premise and intended experience

This is a crossover in which the Scott-base story and the McMurdo story are both true in the same world. Sitara Lhakpa, Nayah, TJ, Roman Ashford, Orien Keel, Benedict Croft, Clear Spring Global, the Falcon bunker, and the Nexus laboratory all belong to one connected narrative rather than separate versions of events. **(rules/avengers.md)**

The story is set in 2046 and moves among Antarctica, Colorado, and the Himalayas. A waste-to-value organization in Antarctica aids conservation in Colorado, while secret messages are sent from Antarctica. The characters must uncover illegal exploratory drilling, suppressed climate research, and a technological possibility involving waste plastics. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)**

The intended experience combines:

- Antarctic survival and ice-shelf science;
- a mystery involving abandoned facilities, hidden files, codes, and secret messages;
- a personal connection to Himalayan disaster;
- a conflict between conservation and extraction;
- hard-science explanations that surprise the reader;
- Minecraft-like environmental landmarks and explorable spaces;
- an AI-enabled robot dog who connects otherwise separate investigations;
- a team story in which no one character or team can defeat the central threat alone. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; rules/avengers.md)**

The story is reaching toward a graphic novel about people trying to prevent environmental destruction while discovering that some apparent solutions can reproduce the same extractive logic they oppose. The material explicitly describes this as a “destroying environment to save environment loop.” **(input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### Central dramatic question

Can Sitara, Nayah, Roman, and TJ expose and use the Nexus/Falcon research before Benedict Croft and the illegal exploratory drilling operation suppress it, while confronting the larger ecological cascade already destabilizing Antarctica, Colorado, and the Himalayan region? **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; rules/avengers.md; rules/decisions.md)**

The existential threat is the combined ecological cascade caused by Antarctic sea-ice loss and Larsen-system instability. Sea-ice loss threatens krill and the food web; Larsen-system instability creates an independent ice-shelf hazard with consequences for grounded ice and sea level. Illegal extraction and information suppression are the human mechanisms preventing an adequate response. The drilling worsens local risk but does not single-handedly cause global collapse. **(rules/decisions.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)**

The personal version of the question is whether Sitara and Roman can stop turning grief, guilt, and anger into reckless solitary action, and instead trust people whose knowledge and experience differ from their own. Sitara’s planned change is to release her self-directed anger and repair her relationships with TJ and Nayah. Roman’s material moves from timid employee to investigator, whistleblower, and researcher willing to act outside Croft’s control. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)**

### Intended emotional destination

The material points toward:

- Sitara recognizing that she was a child during the 2026 Himalayan disaster and could not have prevented it;
- Sitara apologizing to TJ and Nayah for taking her internal anger out on them;
- Roman recognizing that Croft’s apparent support is another form of control;
- Roman and Sitara choosing collective action over private revenge;
- the research being peer reviewed, distributed, and brought to news organizations and government rather than remaining proprietary;
- the conservation problem continuing, but the characters returning with a credible shared purpose. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)**

The material does not establish whether Croft is arrested, defeated financially, killed, or otherwise removed from power. It does establish that the corroborated evidence succeeds publicly: Croft remains free while facing investigation, loss of control, and public exposure. **(rules/decisions.md)**

## Premise

In 2046, Antarctic sea ice is declining, krill habitat is threatened, and the Larsen system is becoming structurally dangerous, while Colorado’s snowpack, rivers, lakes, and cold-water fish habitats are deteriorating. Sitara Lhakpa is conducting ice-core research in the Larsen region with Nayah and TJ when shifting ice and a local slab failure expose a hidden Falcon facility connected to illegal American exploratory drilling and suppressed environmental research. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

The operation is not yet commercially extracting a resource. It is using covert exploratory drilling to claim data, access, and future rights before the resource becomes legally exploitable. Falcon supports directional boreholes, seismic equipment, pumps, heated shelters, fuel stores, and concealed waste-handling systems. CSG operates under a legitimate scientific-drilling permit and disguises resource exploration as climate and ice-core work, using subcontractors, falsified manifests, restricted communications, and isolated weather windows to evade inspection. **(rules/decisions.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)**

At the same time, Roman Ashford is investigating the disappearance of research conducted by his former mentor Orien Keel. Orien had connected Colorado’s drying water sources to fossil-fuel extraction and had worked on a concealed project called Nexus 863 at an abandoned Antarctic laboratory. Roman discovers that Clear Spring Global’s owner, Benedict Croft, has hidden the project and is using the company’s wealth and institutional relationships to control what becomes public. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; drafts/outline.md)**

The two investigations converge in Antarctica. Falcon was the operational and extraction-monitoring site; Nexus was the research site belonging to the same CSG-linked program. Falcon documented the damage, while Nexus studied its ecological consequences and possible waste-to-value responses. The waste-to-value organization began as a CSG research division; Nexus was its scientific laboratory, while Falcon was CSG’s covert extraction and monitoring facility. **(rules/decisions.md)**

TJ is the single robot dog whose history reconciles both accounts. Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. TJ’s inherited hardware includes a salvaged Falcon authentication module, which explains its ability to answer the bunker’s machine challenge without granting access to every system inside. **(rules/avengers.md; rules/decisions.md)**

The existential threat is the combined ecological cascade caused by Antarctic sea-ice loss and Larsen-system instability. The characters cannot reverse the climate trend or restore Larsen C. They can stop the drilling that is worsening local instability, evacuate the endangered site, preserve the Falcon and Nexus evidence, validate Project 863’s limits, and transmit the evidence publicly. **(rules/decisions.md)**

## Story So Far

The material exists in several partially developed versions. The following sequence preserves the major beats from the detailed Roman draft, the Sitara material, and the four-chapter crossover outline without treating exploratory alternatives as settled.

### Prologue / Himalayan backstory — 2042 or 2026

#### Roman and TJ in the Himalayas — detailed draft

**Material type: detailed draft; not settled canon.** In 2042, Roman Ashford is 18 and has recently graduated high school. He travels alone to Nepal for a personal trip and brings TJ, an AI-powered robot dog equipped with tools and emergency functions. Roman has a neural implant; the draft says neural implants are common by this period. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Roman and TJ cross a glacier or ice formation. The ice splits because changing ice conditions have weakened it. Roman falls into the opening and becomes unconscious. A fast-moving electrical storm arrives. Lightning strikes near Roman and TJ, causing a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake between Roman’s implant and TJ’s onboard systems. The connection is not unrestricted telepathy: Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals from TJ, but cannot read all data or remotely control TJ. Range and reliability are limited. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

TJ uses emergency capabilities to help Roman escape. The incident creates their enduring bond and explains why TJ is unusually protective of Roman. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

#### Sitara’s Himalayan history

**Material type: backstory and research-informed story material; not fully settled.** Sitara Lhakpa is from the Himalayas and has Thakali/Nepalese heritage. Her hometown is a fictional Thakali settlement in the Kali Gandaki region. A catastrophic rock-ice avalanche and flood in 2026 destroys the settlement and her home. Sitara is six; her parents die, while an aunt and cousins remain her living connection to the community. She survives with extended family or relief workers. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The supplied research account places the disaster in the Lirung massif, the Lhende Khola and Trishuli River system, and the Nepal–Tibet border at Gyirong Port. It describes destruction across a 72-kilometre river stretch, 1,451 or more deaths in Nepal, 43 or more in Tibet, and 6,264 or more people missing across both sides. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)**

The Forge material says Sitara’s later guilt comes from believing she did nothing during the disaster and remained helpless afterward. **(input/forge-characters.md)**

Sitara’s Himalayan loss drives her toward cryosphere research. The story intends the Himalayas to remain a continuing emotional source rather than the primary present-day setting. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

### Chapter Two — Colorado: water, Clear Spring Global, and Orien Keel

In Colorado, rivers and lakes are drying, food is becoming expensive, and natural water sources are scarce. Roman is now 22, has completed a bachelor’s degree in chemical engineering, and works for Clear Spring Global. The company is owned by Benedict Croft, described as a wealthy, old-money oil tycoon. Roman is shy or timid and initially attempts to work within the organization. He meets Orien Keel, a senior chemical engineer who becomes his mentor. Croft believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG’s power and the energy system. He tells himself that only continued corporate dominance will let him fund future beneficial work. The draft says Orien cannot afford food and water after losing his job and dies of starvation. Orien’s death is later investigated as neglect and corporate retaliation. Orien’s death changes Roman’s investigation from professional curiosity into personal obligation and anger. While going through Orien’s possessions, Roman finds files from Orien’s time in Antarctica. The belongings reveal the existence of an abandoned Clear Spring Global laboratory called Nexus and a project called 863. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Roman searches Clear Spring Global’s servers and finds no evidence of Nexus or Project 863. Roman transfers to the current Clear Spring Global Antarctic drilling site so he can investigate the paper records there. ### Chapter Three — Antarctica: Roman enters Nexus

Roman arrives at the current Clear Spring Global drilling site in Antarctica. He works without drawing attention, listens for anyone who remembers Nexus, and finds that the current staff are too young or too disconnected to know the old facility. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman and TJ enter an old paper-file room at the drilling site. TJ’s scanning ability helps locate a file buried behind a cabinet. The file contains coordinates for Nexus and a less-redacted Project 863 record. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Roman and TJ leave the current site at midnight to find Nexus. TJ’s eyes can function as high-beam lights, allowing them to travel across the Antarctic darkness. The journey takes approximately two hours over a weather-dependent over-ice route. Satellite communications are delayed or intermittent. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Nexus is built into a cave and appears to have been boarded up or decommissioned approximately twenty years before the main events. It was closed after its researchers documented the connection between extraction, sea-ice loss, and krill decline. Power is off, but insulated archives and limited emergency systems remain. **(rules/decisions.md)**

Roman and TJ search the abandoned laboratory. The facility contains records and remnants of two parallel research programs:

1. krill and Antarctic ecosystem research, including a tank containing a krill ecosystem;
2. plastic research, including the early work designated Project 863. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The tank is dead but preserves sensor data and sediment samples showing that krill reproduction failed as sea-ice conditions changed. **(rules/decisions.md)**

Roman finds Orien’s former office and discovers incomplete research about breaking down selected waste plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock. Project 863 uses catalytic chemical recycling to depolymerize selected waste plastics. It requires sorting, heat, energy, and clean feedstock; produces residual waste and emissions; cannot process all plastics; and is useful mainly for localized waste streams rather than replacing fossil fuels. **(rules/decisions.md)**

Roman concludes that Croft is the only person he knows with enough money, infrastructure, and influence to complete and deploy the research. This is a consequential choice: Roman returns to Colorado and offers the discovery to the person most capable of scaling it and most motivated to suppress it. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### Chapter Four — Croft’s betrayal

Roman meets Benedict Croft in Colorado and presents the Nexus research. Roman says the work could change the world or save it. Croft agrees that the information is important but tells Roman to surrender the material and stop pursuing it because Croft will handle it. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman waits for Croft to act. After several weeks, no research team is assembled, no public announcement is made, and no visible work begins. Roman investigates and finds that Croft has effectively buried the project. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman attempts to continue the work inside Clear Spring Global. Croft discovers this and fires him. Roman interprets the firing as evidence that Croft treated Orien the same way and may have caused the conditions leading to Orien’s death. His motivation shifts from helping Croft complete the research to exposing and defeating Croft. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman publishes the research already completed, partly to get the work into public circulation and partly to harm Croft. Croft uses his wealth, corporate relationships, and institutional influence to discredit Roman and make the public believe the work is fake news. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

This failed publication establishes that evidence alone is insufficient. The characters need corroboration, independent scientific review, proof of illegal activity, and a distribution strategy that cannot be easily controlled by Croft. **(input/seb.md; input/forge-characters.md)**

### Chapter Four — The teams combine their capabilities

**Material type: crossover outline and character-arc plan.**

Roman decides to finish or develop the research himself. The draft says he works with TJ for several months. TJ contributes scanning, material identification, fabrication, engineering support, and access to systems. TJ can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock, requires time and power, overheats under sustained use, and has no unlimited network access. TJ communicates through its screen, speakers, or Roman’s limited neural channel. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Sitara contributes ice-core and cryosphere knowledge, including the ability to interpret climate records and distinguish environmental change from the immediate effects of a local operation. Nayah contributes project leadership, Antarctic field knowledge, conservation priorities, and the perspective of someone protecting a homeland threatened by ecological collapse. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

Roman contributes chemical-engineering knowledge, the history of Project 863, and direct evidence of Croft’s suppression. Orien’s records provide the unfinished scientific basis. TJ contributes technical access, fabrication, communication, and the ability to connect the Falcon and Nexus systems. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/avengers.md)**

The planned crossover requires all of these contributions because no one person can solve the threat alone:

- Sitara can read ice and climate evidence but cannot independently scale or distribute a chemical process;
- Roman can reconstruct and bound the waste-to-value research but lacks the full ecological and field context;
- Nayah can lead the field team and protect the conservation objective but lacks Roman’s chemical specialization;
- TJ can interface with abandoned systems and fabricate tools but cannot replace human judgment, public accountability, or ecological knowledge;
- the wider conservation and scientific networks are needed to validate and distribute the findings. **(rules/avengers.md; input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

At the worst point, Sitara’s recklessness threatens to reproduce the isolation and harm she is trying to prevent. She ignores Nayah and TJ’s advice while pursuing the evidence or attempting a dangerous Antarctic action. Sitara must choose to trust the team instead of treating personal risk as proof of commitment. Her apology to TJ and Nayah is the emotional turn attached to her planned change. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/spine-stories.md; input/amelia.md; rules/decisions.md)**

Roman rejects revenge as the team’s governing purpose. He accepts that making the evidence public, rather than merely harming Croft, is the necessary response. Nayah accepts shared risk and uses her field-command authority and safety veto to direct the evacuation and conservation response. TJ relinquishes unilateral protective control and operates under human direction. **(rules/decisions.md)**

### Final investigation and public release

The group conducts or assembles independent peer review before another public release. Roman’s final plan is to contact scientists, news organizations, and government authorities rather than depend on Croft’s channels. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

The evidence package is intended to contain:

- the Project 863 research and its stated limits;
- Orien Keel’s records and the history of his investigation;
- records from Nexus;
- evidence from the Falcon bunker;
- Antarctic exploratory-drilling records;
- Orien’s automated warnings from Antarctica;
- Sitara’s cryosphere and ice-core data;
- evidence connecting Antarctic environmental damage to Colorado water and conservation consequences. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/decisions.md)**

A major fracture opens in Larsen C, but Larsen C does not fully collapse. The team cannot reverse the climate trend. They stop the drilling that is worsening local instability, evacuate the endangered site, preserve the Falcon and Nexus evidence, validate Project 863’s limits, and transmit the evidence publicly. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The release must expose both the scientific suppression and the illegal exploratory operation. A solution that merely gives Croft a new profitable fuel process would fail the story’s conservation premise; the team’s intended purpose is to make the bounded research available for public scrutiny and use it to reduce the need for new extraction. **(input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

## Character movement

### Sitara Lhakpa

Sitara begins as a passionate but reckless ice-core researcher pursuing the truth with insufficient regard for the danger to herself or others. Her work is inseparable from the Himalayan disaster that destroyed her home and from her belief that she should have done more. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md)**

She finds evidence in Antarctica that could explain who is drilling and why information has been hidden. Her first instinct is to pursue it personally and urgently. She crosses a flagged unstable zone to retrieve a sensor and orders TJ to keep recording; her weight and the nearby drilling vibration trigger a local slab failure. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The investigation forces her to recognize that treating herself as expendable can endanger the people and ecosystems she wants to protect. Her planned change is to release her self-directed anger, trust Nayah and TJ, and apologize after her recklessness harms or endangers the group. **(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)**

### Roman Ashford

Roman begins as a shy or timid chemical engineer inside Clear Spring Global. His relationship with Orien gives him a model of responsible investigation, and Orien’s death turns that responsibility into personal anger. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman first trusts Croft with the Nexus research because he believes Croft has the resources to complete it. Croft’s betrayal teaches Roman that access to money and infrastructure is not the same as a commitment to public good. Roman moves from internal investigation to publication and eventually to collaborative action. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

His final movement is away from trying to defeat Croft alone. He rejects revenge, accepts peer review and field evidence, and works with the team to distribute the findings publicly. **(rules/decisions.md)**

### Nayah

Nayah begins as Sitara’s project lead and field partner, with a strong protective instinct and distrust of people who threaten the land and communities she is trying to protect. She functions as the team member most likely to challenge Sitara’s reckless choices. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

Nayah has field-command authority and a safety veto. She accepts shared risk during the convergence, leads the evacuation, and makes the conservation decisions required to stop the drilling without pretending that the wider ecological crisis can be solved immediately. **(rules/decisions.md)**

### TJ

TJ begins as Roman’s bonded companion and technical partner. Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. TJ’s inherited hardware includes a salvaged Falcon authentication module, making TJ the bridge between the Nexus research history and the Falcon bunker. **(rules/decisions.md)**

TJ’s established capabilities include scanning raw materials, fabricating small tools and replacement parts from sorted metals or plastics, assisting with Antarctic survival, and communicating through a screen, speakers, or Roman’s limited neural channel. TJ requires time and power, overheats under sustained use, and has no unlimited network access. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The crossover also positions TJ as Sitara’s Antarctic research companion, an educator about cryosphere science, and the technical operator who opens the Falcon bunker. TJ relinquishes unilateral protective control and works under human direction. **(rules/decisions.md)**

### Benedict Croft

Croft begins as the owner of Clear Spring Global, an oil tycoon with money, corporate influence, and relationships that allow him to control research and public credibility. Croft believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG’s power and the energy system, and tells himself that only continued corporate dominance will let him fund future beneficial work. **(rules/decisions.md)**

He suppresses Orien, attempts to absorb Project 863, fires Roman, and discredits Roman’s first publication. The corroborated Falcon, Nexus, ice, and corporate records eventually succeed publicly. Croft remains free while facing investigation, loss of control, and public exposure. **(rules/decisions.md)**

### Orien Keel

Orien is the absent mentor whose work initiates the McMurdo investigation. He is a Nexus chemist who advanced Project 863, discovered Falcon’s extraction program, and hid corroborating records before Croft fired him. His automated warnings preserve the evidence trail after his removal. **(rules/decisions.md)**

## Stakes

### Personal stakes

- Sitara is trying to prevent another disaster like the one that destroyed her Himalayan home and to escape the guilt she has carried since childhood. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md)**
- Roman is trying to understand Orien’s death and complete the work Orien was prevented from finishing. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**
- TJ’s protective bond with Roman places him in repeated physical and technical danger. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**
- Sitara’s reckless choices threaten her relationship with Nayah and TJ. **(input/forge-characters.md)**

### Relational stakes

- Sitara must choose whether to trust Nayah’s field judgment.
- Roman must choose whether to trust the Antarctic team rather than repeat his failed reliance on Croft.
- TJ must function as a bridge between Roman’s investigation and Sitara’s investigation without replacing human decision-making.
- The group must decide whether revenge against Croft or public release of the evidence is their priority. **(rules/avengers.md; input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)**

### Institutional stakes

- Clear Spring Global can suppress research, control employment, and discredit a whistleblower.
- Illegal American exploratory drilling in Antarctica violates the Madrid Protocol and threatens the legitimacy of the research presence.
- The Falcon and Nexus records expose a conflict between public conservation work and private extraction. **(input/amelia.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; input/seb.md; rules/decisions.md)**

### Ecological and global stakes

- The existential threat is the combined ecological cascade caused by Antarctic sea-ice loss and Larsen-system instability.
- Antarctic sea-ice decline threatens krill, a foundation of the food web.
- Larsen C is a separate ice-shelf hazard intensified by atmospheric and oceanic warming; its major fracture does not become a total collapse during the climax.
- Colorado’s reduced snowpack and streamflow threaten cold-water fish habitat and conservation work.
- Illegal extraction worsens local instability, while information suppression prevents an adequate response. **(rules/decisions.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)**

## Ending

The established ending trajectory is that Roman, Sitara, Nayah, and TJ complete or substantiate the research begun by Orien and Project 863, obtain independent review, and distribute the findings to scientists, news organizations, and government authorities. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

The emotional ending is that Sitara has forgiven herself enough to stop making the team pay for her guilt, apologizes to TJ and Nayah, and finds a purpose in converting selected discarded plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock as part of a broader conservation effort. The process remains bounded: it requires sorted, relatively clean feedstock, heat, and energy; produces residual waste and emissions; cannot process all plastics; and is not a replacement for fossil fuels. **(input/forge-characters.md; rules/decisions.md)**

The institutional ending exposes Croft’s suppression and the illegal Antarctic operation. The first publication fails, but corroborated Falcon, Nexus, ice, and corporate records succeed. Croft remains free while facing investigation, loss of control, and public exposure. Orien’s death is investigated as neglect and corporate retaliation, with Croft’s causal and ethical responsibility established but no direct order to kill. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The environmental ending is not a total restoration. A major fracture opens in Larsen C, but it does not fully collapse. The characters can reveal evidence, stop the drilling that is worsening local instability, create a scientifically bounded alternative to some new extraction, and connect conservation efforts, but they cannot restore Larsen C or reverse climate change. **(rules/decisions.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)**

The final deployment establishes only a public release and pilot in Colorado. Ownership, financing, and long-term governance remain contested rather than solved. **(rules/decisions.md)**

The graphic novel uses the same 22-page format, with Colorado and Himalayan material compressed into brief interstitials so the Antarctic investigation and convergence receive the most pages. **(rules/decisions.md; rules/house-style.md)**

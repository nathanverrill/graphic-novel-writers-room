# avengers — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- The crossover must treat Sitara Lhakpa and Nayah's Antarctic story and Roman Ashford, Orien Keel, Benedict Croft, and the Nexus story as parts of one world and one connected character network. Both teams' stories are true. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- The central team must converge on an existential threat that no one person or team can defeat alone. Each principal character should bring a different capability or perspective. `(rules/avengers.md)`
- The characters must carry the connection between Antarctica, the Himalayas, and Colorado through family history, scientific work, conservation, and personal loss. `(rules/rules.md) (input/amelia.md) (input/seb.md)`
- The story should remain hard science fiction: most character circumstances should be grounded in present-day institutions and pressures, with a small number of clearly labelled speculative elements. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- TJ must be one robot dog, not two separate characters. The Himalayan-sheep-dog-shaped educator with a green-and-black screen and the scavenger-built fabricator bonded to Roman by lightning are one history. Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit; TJ's Falcon access derives from inherited hardware. **FIXED.** `(rules/avengers.md) (rules/decisions.md)`
- Character flaws should create pressure and consequences. In particular, Sitara's reckless ambition, Roman's shyness and developing anger, Nayah's protectiveness, and TJ's mission-focused loyalty should affect how the team works together. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md) (input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`
- Sitara interprets ice and climate records; Nayah makes field-command and conservation decisions; Roman reconstructs and bounds Project 863; TJ opens systems and fabricates needed equipment. Sitara chooses collaboration, Roman rejects revenge, Nayah accepts shared risk, and TJ relinquishes unilateral protective control. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Chemical engineer; CSG employee and later investigator of the Nexus and Falcon records. He leads chemical and corporate evidence during the investigation. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Age:** 22 in 2046. `(input/forge-characters.md) (input/seb.md)`

**Look:** No physical appearance, clothing, or distinguishing feature is established.

**Voice:** The material describes Roman as shy and timid. No characteristic wording or confirmed dialogue is supplied. `(input/seb.md)`

**Who he is:** Roman is curious, technically capable, and initially shy. He researches global warming and the drying of Colorado's rivers and lakes. His work is motivated by firsthand experience of climate damage and by a desire to continue Orien Keel's research. `(input/seb.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`

He can become intensely angry when he concludes that Benedict Croft suppressed the research and caused the conditions leading to Orien's death. His later decision to publish the work is partly driven by a desire to harm Croft, not only by public interest. `(input/seb.md)`

**Motivation and pressure:** Roman wants to understand and counter the causes of drying water sources, global warming, and environmental destruction. He believes the Nexus research into converting used plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock could reduce dependence on new fossil-fuel extraction, within the process's fixed limits. `(input/seb.md)`

His pressures include employment under Croft, Orien's death, the difficulty of proving suppressed research, Croft's ability to discredit him, and his own shift from scientific responsibility toward personal retaliation.

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** TJ is Roman's robot companion and field partner. Roman's neural implant became linked to TJ during the 2042 Himalayan accident. TJ is loyal and protective because Roman nearly died in that incident. Roman depends on TJ for technical information, survival support, scanning, fabrication, and limited communication. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`
- **Orien Keel:** Orien is Roman's senior colleague and mentor at Clear Spring Global. Orien advanced Project 863, discovered Falcon's extraction program, and hid corroborating records before Croft fired him. The messages from Antarctica are Orien's automated warnings. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Benedict Croft:** Croft is Roman's employer and the owner of Clear Spring Global. Croft believes controlled suppression is necessary to preserve CSG's power and the energy system, and tells himself that continued corporate dominance will let him fund future beneficial work. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara and Nayah:** Roman encounters them at Nexus. Their investigations concern the same CSG-linked Antarctic program. They become collaborators in preserving and releasing the evidence. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Roman travels with TJ. He investigates suspicious files, searches CSG's records, and works through technical problems rather than immediately confronting authority. `(input/seb.md)`

**History:** In 2042, at age 18, Roman traveled alone to Nepal on a personal trip and brought TJ. While crossing an ice formation, he fell into a split in the ice and became unconscious. During a nearby electrical storm, lightning struck close to Roman and TJ, causing Roman's neural implant to establish a persistent low-bandwidth handshake with TJ's artificial intelligence. Roman and TJ escaped by combining Roman's human judgment with TJ's emergency capabilities. This event was separate from Sitara's 2026 disaster; the changing ice conditions provide their shared regional connection. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

By 2046, Roman has a bachelor's degree in chemical engineering and works for Clear Spring Global in Colorado. After Orien is fired and dies, Roman finds files in Orien's belongings revealing the existence of Nexus and Project 863. He transfers to CSG's Antarctic drilling site, where the paper archive supplies coordinates and a less-redacted Project 863 record. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In the story:** Roman discovers that Nexus was the research site of the CSG-linked program while Falcon served as its operational and extraction-monitoring site. Nexus contains Orien's unfinished Project 863 work, krill research, and records of extraction-related ecological damage. Roman first brings the work to Croft, whose suppression causes Roman's initial publication to fail. The corroborated Falcon, Nexus, ice, and corporate records later succeed publicly. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy, technically trained employee seeking a way to help damaged ecosystems. He becomes suspicious of CSG, inherits Orien's unfinished work, and is pushed toward anger and retaliation by Croft's suppression. He rejects revenge as his final purpose and accepts that peer review, field evidence, conservation knowledge, technical help, and public distribution are necessary. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** One robot dog; field companion, educator, survival system, communications partner, and technical resource. TJ handles technical operations under human direction. **FIXED.** `(rules/avengers.md) (rules/decisions.md)`

**Unified history:** Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. The reconstruction incorporated inherited hardware from the CSG-linked Nexus/Falcon program, including a salvaged Falcon authentication module. That history reconciles TJ's scavenger-built origin, Nexus connection, Falcon access, Himalayan-sheep-dog shape, educator interface, and later bond with Roman. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** TJ's body is built like a Himalayan sheep dog. Its exterior is made of seamless silver paneling. It has a computer screen with a green-and-black color scheme and a couple of buttons on the right side of its ribs. Its eyes resemble those of a Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. It wears red puffy booties made from jacket material. `(input/amelia.md) (input/team1-scott-base-background.md)`

**Capabilities and limits:** TJ scans surroundings for usable raw materials, including scrap metal, salvaged plastics, and ice-compatible alloys. It can fabricate only small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock. Fabrication requires time and power, and sustained use causes overheating. TJ can use high-beam lights in its eyes, interpret records, and interface with legacy systems, but it has no unlimited network access. Its Falcon authentication module answers the bunker’s machine challenge without granting access to every system inside. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

TJ is a source of cryosphere and conservation knowledge and can integrate into systems within those limits. It does not replace human judgment or authority. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Neural link:** During the 2042 Himalayan accident, lightning caused a persistent low-bandwidth neural handshake between Roman's neural implant and TJ's onboard AI. Roman receives status, direction, and urgent sensory signals from TJ, but cannot read all data or remotely control TJ. Range and reliability are limited. The Roman–TJ bond is **S**; neural implants, TJ's fabrication, and AI sorting are **EG**. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Communication:** TJ communicates through its screen, speakers, or Roman's limited neural channel. In dialogue, TJ acts as Roman's communications partner: it may say that it is running a scan or could print a tool, but does not narrate physical actions, use stage directions, or roleplay actions. `(rules/decisions.md) (input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Voice:** TJ is loyal, protective, mission-focused, practical, resourceful, and playful, with dry, understated humor. It speaks like a competent field partner who does not panic easily. `(input/seb.md) (input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)`

**Who TJ is:** TJ's core loyalty is to Roman's safety, but it also educates and assists Sitara, Nayah, and the wider expedition. Its practical instinct is to ask what can be scanned, repaired, printed, or solved immediately. Its protective behavior can conflict with Sitara's willingness to take risks.

**Relationships:**

- **Roman:** Roman is TJ's companion. TJ helped save him during the Himalayan ice accident, and the lightning-linked neural handshake created their unusual human-machine bond. TJ takes Roman's safety seriously and remains his technical and emotional field partner. `(input/seb.md) (rules/decisions.md)`
- **Sitara:** TJ educates Sitara and tries to prevent her from making reckless decisions. Sitara's tendency to ignore TJ produces conflict, followed by an apology and repaired relationship. `(input/amelia.md) (input/forge-characters.md)`
- **Nayah:** TJ works with Nayah and Sitara as part of the Antarctic project team. Nayah retains field-command authority and safety veto; TJ operates under human direction. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Bi11bot built TJ from parts salvaged from a decommissioned Nexus support unit. Bi11bot's later life, motives, and continuing role are not established. **FIXED in origin; continuing role remains open.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** TJ uses dry humor, stays practical under pressure, and frames problems through available materials and immediate solutions. The red booties are a recurring visual marker. The screen and buttons on the rib area provide a visible interface. `(input/amelia.md) (input/seb.md)`

**History:** TJ was assembled by Bi11bot from salvaged Nexus support-unit parts, including inherited Falcon authentication hardware. TJ later traveled with Roman in the Himalayas in 2042, where lightning produced the limited neural handshake. TJ subsequently operated with Sitara and Nayah in Antarctica. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In the story:** TJ helps Roman locate hidden files, navigate Antarctica, search for Nexus, interpret technical evidence, and complete bounded Project 863 research. TJ helps Sitara and Nayah locate Falcon, answers its machine challenge, connects the two investigations, and fabricates small replacement parts or tools when the team has sorted feedstock, time, and power.

**Arc:** TJ begins with a mission-focused instinct to protect Roman and control immediate dangers. In the crossover, TJ must relinquish unilateral protective control and allow the humans to make shared decisions. TJ remains a technical bridge rather than replacing human accountability. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

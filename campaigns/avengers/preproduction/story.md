# avengers — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Premise and intended experience

This is a crossover in which the Scott-base story and the McMurdo story are both true in the same world. Sitara Lhakpa, Nayah, TJ, Roman Ashford, Orien Keel, Benedict Croft, Clear Spring Global, the Falcon bunker, and the Nexus laboratory all belong to one connected narrative rather than separate versions of events. **(rules/avengers.md)**

The story is set in 2046 and moves among Antarctica, Colorado, and the Himalayas. A waste-to-value organization in Antarctica aids conservation in Colorado, while secret messages are sent from Antarctica. The characters must uncover illegal American mining, suppressed climate research, and a technological possibility involving waste plastics. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

The intended experience combines:

- Antarctic survival and ice-shelf science;
- a mystery involving abandoned facilities, hidden files, codes, and secret messages;
- a personal connection to Himalayan disaster;
- a conflict between conservation and extraction;
- hard-science explanations that surprise the reader;
- Minecraft-like environmental landmarks and explorable spaces;
- an AI-enabled robot dog who connects otherwise separate investigations;
- a team story in which no one character or team can defeat the central threat alone. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; rules/avengers.md)**

The story is reaching toward a graphic novel about people trying to prevent environmental destruction while discovering that some apparent solutions can reproduce the same extractive logic they oppose. The material explicitly describes this as a “destroying environment to save environment loop.” **(input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### Central dramatic question

Can Sitara, Nayah, Roman, and TJ expose and use the Nexus/Falcon research before Benedict Croft and the illegal mining operation suppress it, while confronting the larger physical crisis already destabilizing Antarctica, Colorado, and the Himalayan region? **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; rules/avengers.md)**

The personal version of the question is whether Sitara and Roman can stop turning grief, guilt, and anger into reckless solitary action, and instead trust people whose knowledge and experience differ from their own. Sitara’s planned change is to release her self-directed anger and repair her relationships with TJ and Nayah. Roman’s material moves from timid employee to investigator, whistleblower, and researcher willing to act outside Croft’s control. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)**

### Intended emotional destination

The material points toward:

- Sitara recognizing that she was a child during the 2026 Himalayan disaster and could not have prevented it;
- Sitara apologizing to TJ and Nayah for taking her internal anger out on them;
- Roman recognizing that Croft’s apparent support is another form of control;
- Roman and Sitara choosing collective action over private revenge;
- the research being peer reviewed, distributed, and brought to news organizations and government rather than remaining proprietary;
- the conservation problem continuing, but the characters returning with a credible shared purpose. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/seb.md)**

The material does not establish whether Croft is arrested, defeated financially, killed, or otherwise removed from power. It also does not establish whether the proposed waste-plastic process is successfully deployed at scale. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

## Premise

In 2046, Antarctic ice, sea ice, and ecosystems are under severe pressure, while Colorado’s snowpack, rivers, lakes, and cold-water fish habitats are deteriorating. Sitara Lhakpa is conducting ice-core research in the Larsen region with Nayah and TJ when shifting ice and a glacier collapse expose a hidden Falcon facility connected to illegal American mining and suppressed environmental research. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

At the same time, Roman Ashford is investigating the disappearance of research conducted by his former mentor Orien Keel. Orien had connected Colorado’s drying water sources to fossil-fuel extraction and had worked on a concealed project called Nexus 863 at an abandoned Antarctic laboratory. Roman discovers that Clear Spring Global’s owner, Benedict Croft, has hidden the project and is using the company’s wealth and institutional relationships to control what becomes public. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; drafts/outline.md)**

The two investigations converge in Antarctica. The Falcon bunker and Nexus are not separate unrelated mysteries, but two parts of a larger history involving climate observation, waste-to-value research, illegal extraction, and secret communication. TJ is the single robot dog whose history must reconcile both accounts: the scavenger-built fabricator bonded to Roman during a Himalayan lightning event and the Antarctic research companion who helps Sitara locate and access the hidden facility. **(rules/avengers.md; input/seb.md; input/amelia.md)**

The existential threat is the linked destabilization of ice, water, ecosystems, and political control: Larsen C and other Antarctic systems are approaching dangerous failure; krill and other keystone ecological systems are threatened; Colorado’s snowpack and streams are declining; and corporate extraction is suppressing information and worsening the crisis. No single character or team can address the scientific, technical, cultural, institutional, and public dimensions alone. **(rules/avengers.md; input/seb.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md**

## Story So Far

The material exists in several partially developed versions. The following sequence preserves the major beats from the detailed Roman draft, the Sitara material, and the four-chapter crossover outline without treating exploratory alternatives as settled.

### Prologue / Himalayan backstory — 2042 or 2026

#### Roman and TJ in the Himalayas — detailed draft

**Material type: detailed draft; not settled canon.** In 2042, Roman Ashford is 18 and has recently graduated high school. He travels alone to Nepal for a senior trip and brings TJ, an AI-powered robot dog equipped with tools and emergency functions. Roman has a neural implant; the draft says neural implants are common by this period. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman and TJ cross a glacier or iceberg. The ice splits because it has melted and lost structural strength. Roman falls into the opening and becomes unconscious. A fast-moving electrical storm arrives. Lightning strikes near Roman and TJ, causing an unexpected connection between Roman’s neural implant and TJ’s onboard systems. The connection is described in the draft as telepathic, though the mechanism is not scientifically defined. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

TJ uses emergency capabilities to help Roman escape. The incident creates their enduring bond and explains why TJ is unusually protective of Roman. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

#### Sitara’s Himalayan history

**Material type: backstory and research-informed story material; not fully settled.** Sitara Lhakpa is from the Himalayas and has Thakali/Nepalese heritage. A catastrophic rock-ice avalanche and flood in 2026 destroys her hometown and takes her parents or other family members. The supplied research account places the disaster in the Lirung massif, the Lhende Khola and Trishuli River system, and the Nepal–Tibet border at Gyirong Port. It describes destruction across a 72-kilometre river stretch, 1,451 or more deaths in Nepal, 43 or more in Tibet, and 6,264 or more people missing across both sides. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)**

The Forge material says Sitara was six years old when the flood destroyed her town. It also says her later guilt comes from believing she did nothing during the disaster and remained helpless afterward. **(input/forge-characters.md)**

Sitara’s Himalayan loss drives her toward climate and cryosphere research. The story intends the Himalayas to remain a continuing emotional source rather than the primary present-day setting. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

### Chapter One — Antarctica: the ice opens

**Material type: crossover outline and graphic-novel opening plan; detailed events remain partly open.** The book begins in Antarctica so the reader immediately sees the condition of the world and understands why the investigation matters. The first planned visual movement is from ice-melt research to a glacier or ice-shelf collapse. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

Sitara is conducting ice-core research in or near the Larsen region, with Nayah as project lead. The team works around unstable ice, drilling equipment, crevasse hazards, and shifting ground. Scientists are expected to relocate camps, use airborne surveys when possible, avoid crevasse zones, and monitor ice movement in real time. **(input/amelia.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)**

Sitara’s ambition and urgency make her reckless. One version says the collapse is partly caused or triggered by Sitara’s recklessness; another opening plan describes a glacier falling while the team is working. The supplied material does not establish the exact action by which Sitara creates or worsens the crack. **(input/spine-stories.md; input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

A visual or narrative transition recalls the 2026 Himalayan flood. The disaster connects the Antarctic ice beneath Sitara’s feet to the loss of her home and family. The date “2026” is specifically proposed as on-screen text in the opening structure. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

The immediate Antarctic crisis may trap the team underground or force Sitara and Nayah to seek shelter. A hidden, snow-covered hatch appears in the ice. Its design is described as a submarine-like door set into the ground, with a Falcon symbol engraved in the metal. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

TJ detects or locates the bunker. The opening plan says TJ is the only one who can open the door. Inside are dusty rooms, maps and charts, wall-mounted computers, rolling chairs, pens, Falcon logos, and evidence of drilling. Information about who is drilling and what is being extracted is present in the facility. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

The bunker contains the first major secret message or clue from Antarctica. The exact sender, transmission method, and wording are not established. The bunker’s information points toward illegal American mining or drilling, and toward a connection with the abandoned Nexus facility. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

### Chapter Two — Colorado: water, Clear Spring Global, and Orien Keel

**Material type: detailed draft and crossover outline; the causal sequence is developed, but dates and some consequences remain open.**

In Colorado, rivers and lakes are drying, food is becoming expensive, and natural water sources are scarce. The Colorado crisis is connected to reduced snowpack, declining streamflow, and the loss of habitat for cold-water fish such as greenback cutthroat trout. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)**

Roman is now 22, has completed a bachelor’s degree in chemical engineering, and works for Clear Spring Global. The company is owned by Benedict Croft, described as a wealthy, old-money oil tycoon. Clear Spring Global works in or around Antarctic drilling and waste-to-value research while maintaining operations in Colorado. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman is shy or timid and initially attempts to work within the organization. He meets Orien Keel, a senior chemical engineer who becomes his mentor. Orien is researching Colorado’s drying rivers and lakes and is connecting the water crisis and global warming to oil drilling and fossil-fuel use. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Croft learns what Orien is researching. Because Croft fears that the research will damage the oil business, he has Orien fired. The draft says Orien cannot afford food and water after losing his job and dies of starvation. Roman is described as Orien’s only next of kin. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Orien’s death changes Roman’s investigation from professional curiosity into personal obligation and anger. While going through Orien’s possessions, Roman finds files from Orien’s time in Antarctica. One file mentions an abandoned Clear Spring Global laboratory called Nexus and a project called 863. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman searches Clear Spring Global’s servers and finds no evidence of Nexus or Project 863. In the draft, the file is heavily redacted or the project has been removed from the company’s accessible records. Roman requests a transfer to the current Clear Spring Global Antarctic drilling site so he can investigate the paper records there. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### Chapter Two — Antarctica: Sitara, Nayah, and the Falcon bunker

**Material type: crossover outline and opening story material; not yet fully drafted.**

While Roman is pursuing the Nexus trail through Clear Spring Global, Sitara and Nayah investigate the Falcon bunker exposed or found near their Antarctic work area. The facility appears abandoned but still contains equipment, charts, computers, and drilling records. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

The characters must determine whether the bunker was a research facility, a mining support site, or both. The available material says it contains information about who is doing the drilling and that America is conducting illegal mining in Antarctica. It does not yet specify the chain of command linking the Falcon operation to Croft or Clear Spring Global. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; references/team1-scott-base-research.md)**

TJ opens or helps access the bunker. This moment must reconcile the two TJ histories: his scavenger-built body and fabrication systems make him capable of reading, repairing, or interfacing with obsolete equipment, while his prior relationship with Roman gives him a separate reason to recognize or respond to the facility’s systems. The exact nature of the Falcon lock and why TJ can open it remain undecided. **(rules/avengers.md; input/seb.md; input/amelia.md)**

The bunker yields a reference to Nexus 863 or to a related project. Sitara and Nayah decide that the visible bunker is only one part of the mystery and that the deeper answer lies at Nexus. This decision moves their investigation from documenting damage to actively pursuing the people and research behind it. **(drafts/outline.md; input/amelia.md)**

### Chapter Three — Antarctica: Roman enters Nexus

Roman arrives at the current Clear Spring Global drilling site in Antarctica. He works without drawing attention, listens for anyone who remembers Nexus, and finds that the current staff are too young or too disconnected to know the old facility. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman and TJ enter an old paper-file room at the drilling site. TJ’s scanning ability helps locate a file buried behind a cabinet. The file contains coordinates for Nexus. One draft includes a possible beat in which Roman asks TJ to lock or access a key and TJ says that his credits have expired; this is an idea fragment rather than an established event. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman and TJ leave the current site at midnight to find Nexus. TJ’s eyes can function as high-beam lights, allowing them to travel across the Antarctic darkness. The journey takes approximately two hours in the draft. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Nexus is built into a cave and appears to have been boarded up or decommissioned for approximately twenty years. Roman and TJ break in and search the abandoned laboratory. The facility contains records and remnants of at least two research programs:

1. krill and Antarctic ecosystem research, including a tank containing a krill ecosystem;
2. plastic research, including the early work designated Project 863. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman finds Orien’s former office and discovers incomplete research about breaking down used plastics into gasoline or a usable fuel feedstock. The draft treats this as a possible alternative to extracting new fossil fuels. The research is not yet a finished solution, and the story’s hard-science rules require its limits, energy costs, emissions, and deployment conditions to be worked out rather than treating it as magic. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)**

Roman concludes that Croft is the only person he knows with enough money, infrastructure, and influence to complete and deploy the research. This is a consequential choice: Roman returns to Colorado and offers the discovery to the person most capable of scaling it and most motivated to suppress it. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### Chapter Three — The crossover at Nexus

**Material type: four-chapter crossover outline; events are planned but not fully developed.**

Sitara, Nayah, and TJ follow the Falcon-bunker evidence toward Nexus. Roman is already there, or they find him shortly after he has returned from his first meeting with Croft; the outline gives both possibilities in compressed form. **(drafts/outline.md)**

The two groups discover that their investigations concern the same network. Sitara and Nayah have evidence of illegal Antarctic drilling and secret messages. Roman has Orien’s files, the Nexus coordinates, and the Project 863 research. TJ is the connective element because he can access, interpret, repair, or physically navigate systems that the humans cannot. **(rules/avengers.md; input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

The Falcon facility and Nexus may represent different periods or branches of one operation. One set of records concerns drilling and extraction; another concerns climate monitoring, krill, plastics, and waste-to-value research. The story must reconcile whether Falcon and Nexus were operated by the same organization, whether one was a cover for the other, and why the research was abandoned or hidden. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

A secret message from Antarctica becomes legible or meaningful when the characters compare the two investigations. The message may reveal that someone inside the Antarctic operation was attempting to preserve research, warn outsiders, or document illegal activity. The sender, audience, transmission method, and exact content are not established. **(rules/rules.md; input/amelia.md)**

The group realizes the threat is not only Croft’s suppression of one technology. The physical crisis is already advancing: Antarctic ice and krill systems are destabilizing, Colorado water systems are failing, and illegal extraction is using secrecy to continue operating. The group therefore needs scientific evidence, technical capability, local ecological knowledge, and a way to make the truth public. **(rules/avengers.md; input/seb.md; references/team2-mcmurdo-research.md)**

### Chapter Three or interstitial flashback — Sitara and Roman in Colorado, 2044

A flashback may show Sitara and Roman meeting in Colorado in 2044. The proposed version has Sitara as a graduate student, teaching assistant, or researcher in atmospheric and oceanic sciences, with connections to CU Boulder and INSTAAR. Roman attends her office hours or one of her classes. **(drafts/outline.md; input/seb.md)**

This meeting would establish that the two protagonists had a prior intellectual connection before their Antarctic investigations converged. ### Chapter Four — Croft’s betrayal

Roman meets Benedict Croft in Colorado and presents the Nexus research. Roman says the work could change the world or save it. Croft agrees that the information is important but tells Roman to surrender the material and stop pursuing it because Croft will handle it. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman waits for Croft to act. After several weeks, no research team is assembled, no public announcement is made, and no visible work begins. Roman investigates and finds that Croft has effectively buried the project. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman attempts to continue the work inside Clear Spring Global. Croft discovers this and fires him. Roman interprets the firing as evidence that Croft treated Orien the same way and may have caused the conditions leading to Orien’s death. His motivation shifts from helping Croft complete the research to exposing and defeating Croft. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman publishes the research already completed, partly to get the work into public circulation and partly to harm Croft. Croft uses his wealth, corporate relationships, and institutional influence to discredit Roman and make the public believe the work is fake news. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

This failed publication establishes that evidence alone is insufficient. The characters need corroboration, independent scientific review, proof of illegal activity, and a distribution strategy that cannot be easily controlled by Croft. **(input/seb.md; input/forge-characters.md)**

### Chapter Four — The teams combine their capabilities

**Material type: crossover outline and character-arc plan.**

Roman decides to finish or develop the research himself. The draft says he works with TJ for several months. TJ contributes scanning, material identification, fabrication, engineering support, and access to systems. The exact laboratory, equipment, and resource limits are not established. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Sitara contributes ice-core and cryosphere knowledge, including the ability to interpret climate records and distinguish environmental change from the immediate effects of a local operation. Nayah contributes project leadership, Antarctic field knowledge, conservation priorities, and the perspective of someone protecting a homeland threatened by ecological collapse. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

Roman contributes chemical-engineering knowledge, the history of Project 863, and direct evidence of Croft’s suppression. Orien’s records provide the unfinished scientific basis. TJ contributes technical access, fabrication, communication, and the ability to connect the Falcon and Nexus systems. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md; rules/avengers.md)**

The planned crossover requires all of these contributions because no one person can solve the threat alone:

- Sitara can read ice and climate evidence but cannot independently scale or distribute a chemical process;
- Roman can reconstruct the waste-to-value research but lacks the full ecological and field context;
- Nayah can lead the field team and protect the conservation objective but lacks Roman’s chemical specialization;
- TJ can interface with abandoned systems and fabricate tools but cannot replace human judgment, public accountability, or ecological knowledge;
- the wider conservation and scientific networks are needed to validate and distribute the findings. **(rules/avengers.md; input/amelia.md; input/seb.md)**

At the worst point, Sitara’s recklessness threatens to reproduce the isolation and harm she is trying to prevent. She ignores Nayah and TJ’s advice while pursuing the evidence or attempting a dangerous Antarctic action. The supplied material does not establish the exact action, injury, or page sequence, but it identifies this as the moment when her flaw causes another disaster or nearly does so. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/spine-stories.md; input/amelia.md)**

Sitara must choose to trust the team instead of treating personal risk as proof of commitment. Her apology to TJ and Nayah is the emotional turn attached to her planned change. **(input/forge-characters.md)**

### Final investigation and public release

The group conducts or assembles independent peer review before another public release. Roman’s final plan is to contact scientists, news organizations, and government authorities rather than depend on Croft’s channels. The material says the findings are definitive within the draft’s story logic, but it does not specify who performs the review, what remains uncertain, or how the scientific claims are bounded. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

The evidence package is intended to contain:

- the Project 863 research and its stated limits;
- Orien Keel’s records and the history of his investigation;
- records from Nexus;
- evidence from the Falcon bunker;
- Antarctic drilling or mining records;
- secret messages from Antarctica;
- Sitara’s cryosphere and ice-core data;
- evidence connecting Antarctic environmental damage to Colorado water and conservation consequences. **(input/amelia.md; input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

The release must expose both the scientific suppression and the illegal extraction operation. A solution that merely gives Croft a new profitable fuel process would fail the story’s conservation premise; the team’s intended purpose is to make the research available for public scrutiny and use it to reduce the need for new extraction. **(input/seb.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

The Himalayan flashback reveals that TJ was not simply assigned to Roman as a standard research assistant. TJ helped save Roman during a glacier accident, and a lightning strike created the unusual neural-implant connection. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

### The Falcon bunker

Sitara, Nayah, and TJ discover that the apparently abandoned Antarctic bunker contains evidence of active or recent drilling, hidden maps, and records that contradict the public account of the site. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

Roman discovers that Clear Spring Global concealed an Antarctic laboratory called Nexus and a project numbered 863. ### The crossover connection

The Falcon and Nexus investigations reveal that Sitara’s environmental evidence and Roman’s corporate files describe the same wider crisis. ### The central threat

The final threat is not only one villain or one machine. It is the combination of physical climate destabilization, ecological cascade, illegal extraction, and information control. ## Character movement

### Sitara Lhakpa

Sitara begins as a passionate but reckless ice-core researcher pursuing the truth with insufficient regard for the danger to herself or others. Her work is inseparable from the Himalayan disaster that destroyed her home and from her belief that she should have done more. **(input/forge-characters.md; input/amelia.md)**

She finds evidence in Antarctica that could explain who is drilling and why information has been hidden. Her first instinct is to pursue it personally and urgently. The investigation forces her to recognize that treating herself as expendable can endanger the people and ecosystems she wants to protect. **(input/amelia.md; input/forge-characters.md)**

Her planned change is to release her self-directed anger, trust Nayah and TJ, and apologize after her recklessness harms or endangers the group. **(input/forge-characters.md)**

### Roman Ashford

Roman begins as a shy or timid chemical engineer inside Clear Spring Global. His relationship with Orien gives him a model of responsible investigation, and Orien’s death turns that responsibility into personal anger. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

Roman first trusts Croft with the Nexus research because he believes Croft has the resources to complete it. Croft’s betrayal teaches Roman that access to money and infrastructure is not the same as a commitment to public good. Roman moves from internal investigation to publication and eventually to collaborative action. **(input/seb.md; input/team2-mcmurdo-background.md)**

His final movement is away from trying to defeat Croft alone. He accepts that peer review, field evidence, conservation knowledge, and public distribution are necessary. **(input/seb.md; rules/avengers.md)**

### Nayah

Nayah begins as Sitara’s project lead and field partner, with a strong protective instinct and distrust of people who threaten the land and communities she is trying to protect. She functions as the team member most likely to challenge Sitara’s reckless choices. **(input/amelia.md; input/team1-scott-base-background.md)**

The crossover requires Nayah to move from protecting a local investigation to helping connect Antarctic evidence with Colorado conservation and the broader public case against Croft. Her exact arc and final decision are not developed beyond this function. **(input/amelia.md; rules/avengers.md)**

### TJ

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The script preserves the approved 24-page structure, co-lead balance, Falcon/Nexus separation, bounded TJ capabilities, and constrained ending. One action is currently impossible to read as literal geography, and several later beats need targeted clarification so the page sequence does not imply unsupported technology, premature knowledge, or a contradiction in the disclosure and institutional consequences. The only available layout/thumbnails are for Page 1; execution-level review is therefore limited to that page.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

1. **Location: Page 8, Panels 1–3 — the transport appears to leave Colorado and travel by tracked vehicle to Antarctica.**  
   Panel 1 places Roman at a “Colorado Logistics Terminal.” Panel 3 then shows “A tracked transport” leaving that terminal, and Panel 4 describes Roman’s Antarctic logistics route. A tracked vehicle cannot physically make the Colorado-to-Antarctica journey, and no aircraft or transfer is shown. The reader cannot tell how Roman reaches the Antarctic route.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: show the existing logistics chain as a staged transfer—Roman leaves the Colorado terminal by ordinary transport/air connection, then boards the Antarctic tracked vehicle at an established Antarctic logistics hub. Keep the visible choice to travel and the separate Falcon/Nexus route map, but do not depict a tracked vehicle departing Colorado and entering Antarctica.  
   **Fix:** Writer, layout

### Major

1. **Location: Page 1, Panels 1–3 — the Roman origin interstitial risks implying full sensory fusion despite the approved bond limits.**  
   The lightning is shown traveling through the TJ’s sensor housing and Roman’s neural interface, but the page gives no concrete visual distinction between an electrical accident and the later selective handshake. Because the opening is the reader’s first encounter with the bond, it could read as “Roman wakes in two bodies,” which the brief explicitly rejects.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: add one restrained visual cue—Roman’s neural-interface mark behind the right ear, a single directional sensory pulse, or a hard shift in the TJ’s orientation—without depicting shared vision, glowing energy, or total sensory transfer. Layout: reserve space only if lettering or a brief caption is added.  
   **Fix:** Writer, layout

2. **Location: Page 3, Panel 3 — Sitara’s TJ’s red booties are visually inconsistent with the character lock at this point.**  
   The script says the booties are made from jacket material and appear where field conditions require them, which is permitted. However, Page 1 establishes the Antarctic unit already wearing red booties, while Page 3’s machine is suspended at a hatch and the booties may visually merge with the red jacket palette or be mistaken for a different unit. The separate-TJ identity is important later.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Character Designer/Layout Agent: retain the bright thermal sensor band as the primary identifier and ensure the booties remain small, functional accents rather than the unit’s dominant color.  
   **Fix:** character_designer, layout

3. **Location: Page 6, Panels 1–4 — Roman’s treatment role is clear, but the script risks making him a fully authorized medical researcher rather than an unauthorized participant.**  
   “I’ll run the chemistry” is strong and actionable, but the page needs one unmistakable production cue that the work is hidden and unapproved. The current “unapproved T-ALL chemistry file” is readable only if the image model renders the label clearly. This matters because Roman’s later complicity and the story’s legal boundary depend on it.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: retain the line but pair it with a visible **NO APPROVAL / INTERNAL ONLY** or equivalent document marker, not additional exposition. Letterer: make the status label legible without overloading the panel.  
   **Fix:** Writer, letterer

4. **Location: Page 8, Panel 4 — Roman’s route line and the Falcon/Nexus geography are clear in intent but may be visually impossible to stage in one map.**  
   The panel asks the reader to parse Sitara and Nayah departing Falcon, Roman’s route entering from Colorado’s logistics chain, and both lines converging at Nexus. Without a clearly marked transfer point, the map may imply Colorado is geographically connected to the Antarctic over-ice route or that Roman’s vehicle crosses the whole distance.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Layout Agent: use a three-stage schematic—Colorado departure, Antarctic logistics hub/transfer, then separate Falcon/Nexus over-ice routes. Writer: identify the transfer without introducing a new named location or character.  
   **Fix:** Writer, layout

5. **Location: Page 9, Panel 1 and Page 12, Panel 3 — the script compresses multiple arrival and evidence functions without enough time or spatial anchoring.**  
   Page 9 says Roman arrives at one access point while Sitara and Nayah arrive at another, then they meet inside. Page 12 has Nayah overlay Falcon drilling, ice motion, Nexus process waste, and dead-krill data. The causal convergence works, but the reader may not know whether the teams have physically reunited before the fracture, how far apart the access points are, or whether the overlay is a real synchronized data display or a conceptual montage.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: make the convergence explicit through one establishing transition or line indicating that the teams have moved into the same Nexus corridor. Layout Agent: keep the Page 9 access points visibly distinct, then use a shared interior orientation before the Page 10 fracture.  
   **Fix:** Writer, layout

6. **Location: Page 11, Panels 2–4 — “The tank failed when the ice season shortened” overstates what the surviving evidence proves.**  
   The approved canon says sensors and sediment samples indicate that krill reproduction failed as sea-ice conditions changed. “The tank failed” can imply a mechanical failure, while “cause correlation” is acceptable only if it remains an observed relationship rather than definitive causation.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: change the line and display to describe failed krill reproduction and correlation with changed sea-ice conditions. Preserve the ecological discovery without claiming that the tank itself proves the entire causal chain.  
   **Fix:** Writer, letterer

7. **Location: Page 12, Panel 3 — the overlay includes Merritt provenance although the page’s visible records are Falcon and Nexus records.**  
   The page check claims the midpoint links Falcon, Nexus, Merritt, and Colorado, but the panels only visibly establish Falcon vibration, ice motion, Nexus process waste, and krill data. Merritt enters through Sitara’s later admission on Page 13. This makes the midpoint claim arrive one beat early and weakens the later confession.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: either remove “Merritt” from the Page 12 overlay/page check, or visibly identify Sitara’s copied Merritt provenance record as a separate, unopened file rather than treating its contents as already proven. Preserve Page 13 as the moment Sitara makes the source and her culpability explicit.  
   **Fix:** Writer, layout

8. **Location: Page 17, Panels 1–4 — Orien’s phone call needs a communication mechanism.**  
   The phone placed beside Orien is later used to call Sitara from the Antarctic site, but the world establishes delayed/intermittent satellite communication rather than ordinary cellular service. A conventional phone would read as nonfunctional in Nexus and make the call feel magical.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: identify the object as a satellite-capable field phone or connected emergency handset, using the existing phone beat without adding a new system. Letterer: keep the call legible as an intermittent connection.  
   **Fix:** Writer, letterer

9. **Location: Page 18, Panels 1–4 — the corporate deletion/shutdown threat is not yet grounded in a plausible access path.**  
   The world fixes that Alpha Dynamics or a superior corporate authority threatens TJ records, while the files also fix that no remote-control mechanism is established. “Corporate access event” and “deletion / shutdown review” could imply a remote command over Roman’s TJ or its body, which the canon prohibits.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: specify that the message is an attempted authorization or network-side access request affecting records and service status, not a successful remote shutdown command. The TJ refuses the request locally. Preserve the distinction between threatening deletion and controlling the machine.  
   **Fix:** Writer

10. **Location: Page 19, Panels 3–4 — “DRILL CONTROL: SAFE HOLD” may overstate what authenticated transmission can accomplish.**  
    The canon permits the protagonists to stop or expose Falcon by transmitting authenticated evidence and securing or flagging the local control record for inspection. It does not establish that Roman or either TJ can remotely operate Falcon’s drilling apparatus. “Safe hold” can therefore read as remote control rather than an inspection lock or local operator response.  
    **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: label the status as an authenticated inspection interlock or local control record flagged for safe hold, and show that the system’s existing safety protocol or on-site authority places drilling on hold. Do not imply the protagonists directly pilot Falcon from Nexus.  
    **Fix:** Writer, letterer

11. **Location: Page 20, Panels 1–2 — the human disclosure package is not fully aligned with the approved TJ authorization structure.**  
    The package lists “Merritt copies / chemical records / Orien warning” under “Human files,” but the brief requires the exact selected categories and authorizing sources to be identifiable. Sitara’s Merritt copies, Roman’s chemical records, Orien’s warnings, and the corroborating mission data have different provenance and consent implications.  
    **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: separate the package into named source blocks: Sitara-authorized Merritt copies/provenance records; Roman-authorized chemical and corporate records; Orien’s scheduled warnings and call; Roman-TJ selected Falcon environmental/provenance-linked records; Sitara-TJ selected route/sensor/Falcon-search records. Do not use “human files” as the sole label.  
    **Fix:** Writer, letterer

12. **Location: Page 23, Panels 1–4 — Croft’s final action is visible, but the treatment consequence is left too close to a forbidden implied death.**  
    The treatment schedule is repeatedly framed as the choice between preserving the pilot and saving the son’s treatment path. The canon permits the path to be endangered or paused, but fixes that the son survives and remains critically ill. The page must not visually imply that Croft’s authorization kills him or that the hidden treatment was a proven cure.  
    **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: keep the schedule marked as interrupted/endangered rather than fatal or curative. Character Designer: show the son only as an ongoing patient, not as a deathbed image.  
    **Fix:** Writer, character_designer

## Plausibility ledger

- **[FIXED] 2046 setting:** The script consistently uses 2046 in present-day scenes and keeps the Himalayas to backstory.
- **[FIXED] Falcon/Nexus geography:** The script intends separate sites and uses a route map and remote feed. Page 8’s transport depiction currently contradicts that physical geography and is a Blocker until repaired.
- **[FIXED] Falcon activity:** The script correctly shows permitted scientific language masking illegal exploratory drilling, falsified manifests, concealed waste, and local instability. It does not depict Falcon as a conventional commercial mine.
- **[FIXED] Larsen C:** The script shows a Larsen-linked fracture and local structural failure without depicting total Larsen C collapse or climate reversal.
- **[FIXED] Merritt:** The script treats Merritt material as provenance and safeguard records rather than a hidden governance covenant or super-organism. Page 12 should not reveal Merritt’s contents before Page 13 unless that is deliberately staged.
- **[FIXED] Project 863:** Pages 14, 15, and 21 show selected feedstock, rejected material, water use, grid demand, emissions, residuals, and finite throughput. No page turns it into a universal solution.
- **[EG] Cold-active enzymes:** The script presents the enzyme system as a bounded adjacent process and does not make it independently miraculous.
- **[EG] TJ fabrication:** Page 11 shows a small conductive bridge, visible reserve loss, and no arbitrary manufacture. This holds.
- **[EG/S] Falcon authentication:** Page 3 limits the credential to the hatch and local maintenance interface. This holds.
- **[FIXED] TJ bodies and partitions:** The script repeatedly distinguishes Sitara’s and Roman’s units and shows selected synchronization rather than full archive sharing.
- **[FIXED/S] Neural bond:** Page 10 uses a single directional warning and Page 18 uses a selective refusal. Avoid any visual treatment that implies telepathy, remote control, or total sensory fusion.
- **[FIXED] Roman’s consequences:** Page 20 limits the visible consequence to employment/access loss and public exposure. Do not add arrest, charges, or sentencing in later lettering or imagery.
- **[FIXED] Orien’s responsibility:** The script establishes threat, retaliation, and disappearance but does not create a direct written murder order. Revise “MAKE THE PROBLEM GO AWAY” if it reads as such.
- **[FIXED] Croft’s son survives:** Pages 15 and 23 must preserve ongoing critical illness without implying cure or death. The world-file death contradiction remains a development issue outside this script review.
- **[PROPOSAL] Colorado metrics:** Pages 15, 21, and 24 use **41 → 68** and **3 → 11** as facts although the story file still calls them proposals. Director approval is required.
- **[PROPOSAL] Public evidence custody:** Page 20 uses a secure public archive and mirrored institutional custody. This is compatible with the brief but should be named as an approved mechanism or kept institutionally generic.
- **[FIXED] Colorado governance:** Pages 22–23 correctly show an unincorporated interim partnership, contested ownership/financing, and Croft’s refusal to block the public contract. This remains distinct from the unresolved “nonprofit public-benefit corporation” language in `world.md`.
- **[FIXED] No new major speculative system:** The script currently introduces no clear new major license, but the Page 19 drilling-control interface must be framed as an existing local safety/inspection mechanism rather than a new remote-control technology.

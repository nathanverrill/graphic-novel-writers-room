# Written Portion

	The year is 2042 our character Roman Ashford is 18 and just graduated high school. He decides for his senior trip to go to Nepal and hike in the Himalayas. He goes by himself, but takes his robot dog, TJ, who is no ordinary robot dog. TJ is implemented with an artificial intelligence and is outfitted with an immense amount of tools and emergency functions. Roman has a neuralink, because the majority of people in 2042 have neuralinks. When Roman and TJ are hiking they have to cross an iceberg, but halfway across the iceberg splits open. The iceberg splits open due to global warming, it has been melting and it had a very weak tensile strength. While Roman and TJ are stuck in the iceberg opening an electrical storm appears and a lightning strikes right next to them which causes Roman’s neuralink to bond to TJ’s artificial intelligence. The sensation is initially very confusing to the two of them, but working together and using TJ’s emergency capabilities they were able to get out of the opening. 

The year is now 2046, the world is significantly worse: there is a low supply of food, which makes the price of it very high, and there are almost no natural water sources in Colorado and the ice caps are melting causing the oceans to rise. Roman is 22, graduated from college with a B.S. in chemical engineering, lives in Colorado, and got a job at “Clear Spring Global.” At Clear Spring Global (CSG) Roman meets a senior employee named Orien Keel, Orien decides to mentor Roman. Orien is a chemical engineer for CSG that has been researching the dried up rivers and lakes in Colorado. The owner of CSG is named Benedict Croft, a rich, old money, oil tycoon, he works out of CSG’s headquarters in Colorado as well as the other two characters. Benedict Croft finds out that Orien has been researching the dried up water sources and that Orien is connecting the severe global warmth to oil drilling and use. Since Croft is so scared of this fact coming out he has Orien fired. 

Since Orien got fired he is unable to afford food and water so he starves to death. Orien was solitary so the only next of kin he had was Roman. While Roman was going through all of Orien’s stuff he came across this file cabinet full of files from his time with CSG in Antarctica. Roman is a curious person so he looked through the files and came across one that mentioned a CSG laboratory that was focused on researching how to fix global warming. The base was named “Nexus” and the project title was “863.” Roman figured that he needed to fulfil Orien’s work to try and stop global warming and help bring back the water sources, so he tried to find the laboratory on every server that CSG had but he found no evidence of the laboratory or the project. Since Roman can’t find anything he decides to request a transfer to the current CSG drilling site in Antarctica.

Roman gets the transfer request accepted and goes to Antarctica. For the first few weeks that Roman is in Antarctica he tries not to draw attention by just working hard and listening for someone to mention Nexus, but no one on the current Antarctic would’ve been old enough to have worked at Nexus. Eventually Roman takes TJ to the drill site and they sneak into the old paper file room. Using TJ’s scanning abilities they are able to find the one file left about Nexus and it is buried behind a file cabinet. After reading the file Roman is able to find the coordinates of Nexus. *Ask tj to lock the key again, TJ says credits expired?*

Roman and TJ leave at midnight to go searching for the Nexus laboratory, luckily TJ’s eyes double as high beam lights allowing them to see. It took around two hours for them to find the old laboratory from the current CSG site. When they arrived it was built into a cave and was boarded up, but from what they could tell it was a massive laboratory. Roman and TJ broke into Nexus and started searching for any files or experiments that could help end global warming. Eventually they found where Orien’s office used to be. Inside the office was some old, very undeveloped research. The research was about a fundamental concept of being able to break down used plastics into gasoline. Roman decides that this concept has the potential to end global warming and get rid of the need to destroy the environment for fossil fuels. Roman decides that the only person he knows that has the ability to complete this research and implement it is Benedict Croft, so he is going to go back to Colorado immediately and have a meeting with Croft.

When Roman gets back to Colorado the first thing he does is go to CSG headquarters and meet with Croft. Roman tells Croft that this research could change the world, it could save the world. Croft tells Roman that he agrees that this information is very important and to hand over all the information and to stop pursuing it because he would handle it. After a couple weeks Roman hasn’t heard anything about it, so he goes and investigates, but realizes that nothing is happening, no teams trying to finish the research, no news sources reporting on it, nothing. Roman decides that he must do something so he goes and starts trying to finish the research. Croft finds out and fires Roman. This makes Roman go ballistic because he realized that if this happened to him this is probably what happened to Orien, meaning Croft is the reason Orien is dead.

Since Roman thinks that the research would get finished more efficiently and of a higher quality he decides to publish the research that was already completed. Roman also published the research because it would harm Croft and at this point that is what he cares about the most. Unfortunately due to Croft’s infinite amount of money he was able to entirely discredit Roman and make everyone believe what Roman was putting out was fake news. This made Roman decide that the only way to help humanity would be to finish the research himself. 

With the help of TJ, Roman works tirelessly trying to complete the research and it takes a couple months, but he is able to complete it. When he completes it Roman decides it is best to make a proper plan on how to distribute it. Roman first goes to other scientists to get his findings peer reviewed, then he contacts news sources and the government. Roman’s findings were definitive, this process of turning recycled materials into gasoline will work, but not only that it would significantly take down the amount of pollutants pumped into the atmosphere slowing global warming and eventually stopping it.

# layouts.md

## Base grid and sequence plan

**Base grid:** three variable tiers, never more than four panels per page. The book uses regular horizontal reading flow, with occasional full-page splashes for the Falcon exposure, Nexus fracture, evacuation, and final Colorado image.  
**Reading direction:** Western, left to right and top to bottom.  
**Page-turn strategy:** major reveals land on even pages: Falcon on Page 2, Nexus fracture on Page 10, Orien’s call on Page 18, witnessed TJ consent on Page 20, and the Colorado result on Page 22.  
**Production repair:** Page 8 shows Roman leaving Colorado through an ordinary air/logistics transfer and boarding an Antarctic tracked vehicle only after reaching an Antarctic logistics hub. Page 19 shows an authenticated inspection interlock, not remote drilling control. Exact Colorado metrics remain visible as provisional pilot readouts rather than universal scientific claims.

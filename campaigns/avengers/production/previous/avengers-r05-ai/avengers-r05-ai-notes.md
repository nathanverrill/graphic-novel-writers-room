## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The script preserves the approved central structure and the Page 1 execution is broadly drawable, but several later beats either omit fixed resolutions or introduce action that is spatially and causally unclear. The most urgent repairs are to make the Nexus/Falcon climax geographically legible, dramatize Croft’s required final choice, and clarify how the public disclosure and TJ consent operate without implying unrestricted synchronization or remote corporate control.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

None identified.

### Major

1. **Location: Page 19, Panels 1 and 4 — Falcon appears to be visible from Nexus.**  
   The team emerges from Nexus, while “Falcon’s distant drilling lights” are visible across the ice and a fracture cuts across the “Falcon access field.” Canon establishes Falcon and Nexus as separate sites connected by an approximately two-hour weather-dependent route; the page currently makes them read as one adjacent location or leaves the reader unsure whether the team has returned to Falcon.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Keep the evacuation at Nexus and show the Nexus support corridor, surface access, or a remote status feed indicating Falcon’s drilling has been stopped/exposed. If the climax must return to Falcon, establish the intervening transport and location change before this page. Do not solve this with a vague wide shot.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

2. **Location: Page 19, Panel 4 — “stopped or exposed” is not dramatized clearly enough.**  
   The fixed ending requires the team to stop or expose Falcon drilling. “Power dead or visibly isolated behind a safety barrier” is ambiguous and may only show an ordinary equipment failure caused by the fracture. The reader cannot tell whether the protagonists caused the outcome, whether the operation is merely temporarily interrupted, or whether evidence has made the operation accountable.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Show one concrete causal action or consequence: Nayah’s team transmits the authenticated drilling evidence, a local control is secured under field authority, or the exposed apparatus is visibly documented and flagged for inspection. Preserve the distinction that this does not stop global climate change or collapse Larsen C.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

3. **Location: Pages 20–22 — Croft’s fixed final choice is absent.**  
   Canon requires Croft’s final choice to preserve the Colorado pilot’s survival over his proprietary claims. The current Pages 20–22 show Croft objecting that treatment is ending, then remove his control, but never show him choosing the pilot’s continuation or relinquishing proprietary control. This weakens the antagonist’s established moral complexity and leaves the ending inconsistent with the approved character arc.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Add a visible, concise consequence within the existing page budget: Croft authorizes or declines to block the interim public-contract operation after exposure, allowing the pilot to continue despite the loss of CSG’s proprietary framing. Keep his son critically ill and do not make the choice redemptive or legally exculpatory.  
   **Fix:** Writer

4. **Location: Page 20, Panel 1 and Page 18, Panel 4 — TJ disclosure scope is unclear.**  
   Page 18 establishes that Roman’s bonded TJ consents only to “agreed records,” but Page 20 says “TJ records” enter a synchronized public archive without identifying the permitted subset or distinguishing Roman’s TJ from Sitara’s TJ. This risks implying that a witnessed consent automatically authorizes the full local mission partition, contradicting the fixed rule that full archives require deliberate synchronization and that TJ records remain locally partitioned.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Name the limited disclosure category in both places—e.g. the Falcon environmental records and provenance records selected by TJ—and show the public archive receiving only those files. State or depict Sitara’s TJ records as separately authorized through the team’s accountable evidence plan, not as covered by Roman’s TJ’s consent.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Letterer

5. **Location: Page 8, Panels 1–3 — travel and team composition are not fully legible.**  
   Page 6 places Roman in Colorado, Page 7 keeps him at CSG records, and Page 8 abruptly places him in a second Antarctic tracked vehicle with no visible transition or explanation of when he traveled. The reader can infer that he traveled after discovering the Nexus reference, but the page currently treats the journey as a cut rather than a causally motivated action, and “Roman’s team” is never established.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Add a compact transition beat or caption showing Roman choosing the Antarctic route after finding the archive reference, and identify who authorized or arranged his transport without adding a new protagonist. Keep Nayah’s team and Roman’s vehicle distinct until the junction.  
   **Fix:** Writer

### Minor

1. **Location: Page 1, Panel 1 — Roman’s TJ is described as having no red Antarctic booties.**  
   This is defensible because the scene is in the Himalayas, but the explicit negative detail may create avoidable doubt about whether this is Roman’s bonded TJ.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Identify the unit through its silver body and neural-interface housing, or omit the bootie note unless the absence is narratively important.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

2. **Location: Page 1, Panel 3 and layout block — Sitara and Sitara’s TJ share the same lower-right staging zone.**  
   The layout places both figures at the lower right while Nayah is behind and left. At thumbnail scale this risks merging the small TJ with Sitara’s silhouette and weakening the sensor/flag geography that drives the page turn.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Place the TJ clearly beside or slightly ahead of Sitara’s boot path, with the sensor and flags on a distinct depth line. Preserve the requested three-character limit.  
   **Fix:** Layout Agent

3. **Location: Page 5, Panel 2 — the paired code is visually ambiguous.**  
   A “narrow strip of paper” divides a timestamp and coordinate into two halves, but the page does not make clear whether this is a fragment of Falcon’s record or Orien’s separate physical warning. Later pages call the records two halves, so the material source should be immediately legible.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Tie the red grease-pencil marks directly to the Falcon vibration log or label the strip as an inserted Orien warning fragment, then preserve the same visual mark on the Nexus counterpart.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

4. **Location: Page 7, Panel 4 — the TJ’s knowledge risks sounding omniscient.**  
   Roman asks “You knew?” and TJ answers “I preserved it.” The exchange may imply that the TJ independently understood Orien’s investigation and concealed it from Roman, exceeding the approved local-partition and selective-summary rules.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Make the line observational rather than interpretive: the TJ preserved a route reference in its local partition but did not know its full significance.  
   **Fix:** Writer

5. **Location: Page 10, Panel 3 — Roman’s neural alert and physical rescue need a clearer causal bridge.**  
   The page correctly avoids glowing telepathy, but Roman receives “falling geometry” and immediately pulls Sitara away. Since Roman cannot remotely control TJ or receive all sensor data, the panel should show that the alert is a directional warning from the bonded TJ, not a complete visual prediction.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Depict the TJ moving or pointing toward the failing corridor as Roman receives a compressed directional impulse, then show Roman acting on that limited cue.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

6. **Location: Page 15, Panel 2 — the Colorado outcome is stated but not fully measured.**  
   The panel includes water clarity and returning invertebrates, but the monitoring wall is described generically. Since the fixed requirement is that the application appear through measurable outcomes, the page should give at least one readable before/after metric or clear instrument comparison.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Add one concrete monitored change—such as a visibly improved clarity index or a dated invertebrate count—while retaining water use, power, emissions, and finite throughput.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Letterer

7. **Location: Page 22, Panel 1 — affected-community participants are present but visually generic.**  
   The brief requires affected-community participants to visibly disagree over ownership and financing, but the current panel lists them among a potentially unrecognizable crowd. This is not a canon contradiction, but the required governance conflict may disappear in production.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Limit the visible meeting to a few differentiated, non-named participants with distinct document positions or gestures; do not invent identities or cultural details.  
   **Fix:** Writer, Layout Agent

## Plausibility ledger

- **[FIXED] 2046 setting and Antarctic/Himalayan/Colorado geography:** The script uses all approved locations. The Himalayan material remains backstory rather than a new present-day plot.
- **[FIXED] Roman’s 2042 accident:** Page 1 shows the lightning event and TJ presence without adding a separate Himalayan discovery or connection to Sitara’s family.
- **[FIXED] Sitara’s 2026 disaster:** Page 1 uses floodwater and household objects while keeping the setting and identities nonspecific. No unapproved cultural detail is invented.
- **[FIXED] Falcon:** Pages 2–5 correctly depict a concealed exploratory drilling site under a scientific permit, not an operating commercial mine. The records show local instability rather than claiming Falcon causes global climate change.
- **[FIXED] TJ authentication:** Page 3 limits the challenge-response credential to the hatch and local maintenance interface. This holds.
- **[FIXED] TJ scale and separation:** Pages 1–3 and 11 depict Sitara’s TJ and Roman’s TJ as distinct small units. Page 18 must preserve that distinction in the disclosure sequence.
- **[FIXED / EG / S] Neural bond:** Pages 10 and 18 mostly keep the bond selective and non-telepathic. Page 10 needs a clearer directional cue so the alert is not read as omniscient sensory fusion.
- **[FIXED] TJ data architecture:** Page 11 establishes separate partitions, but Page 20 risks contradicting this by presenting generic “TJ records” as synchronized public evidence. The disclosure must be explicitly limited and authorized.
- **[FIXED] Orien’s paired warning:** Pages 5 and 12 establish the split code, but Page 5 needs a clearer physical relationship between the warning fragment and Falcon’s records.
- **[FIXED] Nexus:** Pages 8–19 use the cave laboratory, dead krill tank, fracture, meltwater, emergency power loss, archive movement, and evacuation. Page 19’s apparent visual merger of Nexus and Falcon is the main location plausibility risk.
- **[FIXED] Larsen C:** The script shows a Larsen-linked fracture but does not state or depict a full shelf collapse. Preserve this in the final art.
- **[FIXED] Project 863 license:** Pages 6, 15, and 21 show selected feedstock, rejected material, residuals, water use, power demand, emissions, and finite throughput. No new biological catastrophe or miracle cure is introduced.
- **[FIXED] Colorado application:** The pilot’s real benefit is present, but Page 15 would benefit from one readable measured outcome before the later summary on Page 21.
- **[FIXED] Roman’s on-page consequences:** Page 20 shows loss of access and employment without adding arrest, charges, or a courtroom. This holds.
- **[FIXED] Croft’s status:** Croft remains free and publicly exposed. However, the required final choice preserving the Colorado pilot is not currently declared or dramatized.
- **Unlabelled invention / risk:** Page 19’s “Falcon distant drilling lights” creates an undeclared spatial mechanism linking the two sites. Clarify whether this is a remote feed, a separate location after transport, or remove it.

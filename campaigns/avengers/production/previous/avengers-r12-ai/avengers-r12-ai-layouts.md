# layouts.md

## Base grid and sequence plan

**Format:** 24-page, 6.625" × 10.25" full-color single issue. Western reading direction. Odd pages are right-hand pages; even pages are left-hand pages.

**Base grid:** Three variable tiers, maximum four panels per page. Full-page splashes: Pages 2, 10, and 24. One primary location per page wherever possible. No panel contains more than three named human characters. Roman’s bonded TJ and Sitara’s TJ remain visibly distinct. Image descriptions contain no rendered lettering; all exact lettering appears in `items`.

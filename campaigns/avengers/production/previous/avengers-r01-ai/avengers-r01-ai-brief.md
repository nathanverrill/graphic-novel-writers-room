# avengers — Development Brief

## Logline

In 2046 Antarctica, cryosphere scientist Sitara Lhakpa and chemical engineer Roman Ashford must combine their secret evidence—and confront their own complicity—to stop a corporate extraction program, preserve an autonomous robot’s consent, and expose the system profiting from both ecological harm and measurable environmental good.

## Theme

Useful knowledge does not become someone’s property merely because they extracted it first; discovery creates a duty to share responsibility, provenance, and power.

## Tone & comps

- **_Arrival_** — science and communication as sources of emotional and ethical stakes; take the patient intelligence and visual restraint, not the speculative premise.
- **_The Abyss_** — hostile environments making cooperation physically necessary; take the pressure, machinery, and human-scale suspense.
- **_The Constant Gardener_** — corporate benefit and wrongdoing existing in the same system; take the moral complexity and investigative momentum.

## Audience & rating

Adult and older-teen science-fiction thriller. **PG-13 / 14+:** peril, disaster, corporate coercion, implied medical experimentation, environmental loss, and a non-graphic near-death survivor. No gore, torture, or sexual violence.

## Format

- **22-page single issue / graphic novel chapter**
- **Trim:** 6.625" × 10.25"
- **Color**
- Default density: **4–6 panels per page**, with occasional larger panels for the exposed Falcon hatch, the Nexus fracture, and the Colorado result.
- The established page allocation remains: Hook 1–2; Plot Turn 1 3–5; Pinch 1 6–8; Midpoint 9–12; Pinch 2 13–15; Plot Turn 2 16–18; Resolution 19–22.
- Pages 1 and 19 may contain brief Himalayan interstitial material only if it does not displace present-tense action.
- Page turns reveal consequences or changed information. Page 2 reveals Falcon; the Nexus fracture and TJ consent are major even-page reveals.

## Visual direction

Grounded near-future field realism with strong environmental contrast:

- Antarctica: blue-white scale, red and yellow expedition clothing, black machinery, warning flags, vibration, snow, meltwater, and blue ice intruding through human structures.
- Colorado: warmer but stressed; concrete, green-brown water, sorting machinery, dashboards, wetland channels, rejected material, and visible power and throughput limits.
- Himalayas: brief, tactile memory images of floodwater, household objects, ice, and unstable ground; do not invent cultural practices or unconsulted identities.
- Technology is compact, maintained, imperfect, and physical. TJs remain approximately 249 grams and small-dog scale: seamless silver panels, green-black display, compact sensor body, and recurring red Antarctic booties. Never draw a large stereotypical robot dog.
- Rendering favors clear spatial orientation, documentary observation, and high-contrast hazard moments. Use page architecture to juxtapose parallel records and environments rather than explaining them in captions.
- Neural communication is visually distinct from ordinary dialogue: compressed sensory impressions, not glowing telepathy or full-body possession. TJ’s screen and speaker must remain a separate, witnessable interface.

## Canon

The following is approved project truth for this book. It includes all material marked fixed, plus the Director’s decisions below.

### Story and world

- The story occurs in **2046** across Antarctica, the Himalayas as backstory, and Colorado. Sitara Lhakpa and Roman Ashford are equal co-protagonists with equal agency, plot weight, and presence; their relationship is not romantic.
- Both team stories remain true in one world: Sitara, Nayah, Falcon, and the illegal American extraction program coexist with Roman, Croft, Orien, Nexus, Merritt, and Project 863.
- The central ecological threat is a combined cascade of Antarctic sea-ice loss, krill decline, and Larsen-system instability. Falcon’s drilling worsens local danger but does not cause global climate change or single-handedly collapse Larsen C.
- Falcon is an illegal exploratory drilling and extraction-monitoring site operating under a scientific-drilling permit. It is not yet a conventional commercial mine.
- Nexus is a decommissioned coastal Antarctic cave laboratory. Merritt is a separate Alpha Dynamics-operated site in Wilkes Land. Colorado is the visible application site.
- Project 863 is the sole major story license: bounded catalytic recycling of selected plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock. It produces real, limited benefits and visible costs.
- Cold-active enzymes, TJ autonomy, fabrication, AI sorting, local mesh architecture, and the ordinary neural-interface infrastructure remain **EG**; the Roman–TJ bond remains **S**. No additional major speculative system is introduced.
- Alpha Dynamics controls infrastructure and access; CSG carries visible operational responsibility for Falcon and Nexus. Croft remains the sole principal human antagonist. Alpha Dynamics is institutional pressure, not a second villain.
- The Colorado pilot demonstrates recovered feedstock, clearer water, returning aquatic invertebrates, rejected feedstock, residual solids, emissions monitoring, water use, grid demand, and finite throughput.
- The ending is a constrained victory: evidence survives, drilling is stopped or exposed, the site is evacuated, the pilot continues under an unincorporated interim partnership, and ownership and financing remain contested. Formal enforcement remains outside the book.

### People and arcs

- Sitara begins by treating urgency and understanding as permission to control knowledge. She crosses the flagged zone, conceals copies of Merritt research, and gives Croft a controlled copy for funding, laboratory access, and application. She ends by admitting her concealment, relinquishing exclusive credit, and choosing shared stewardship.
- Roman begins dependent on Croft and morally compromised by the unauthorized T-ALL research and circumstances surrounding Orien’s disappearance. He ends by choosing public responsibility over revenge and accepting loss of employment, reputation, access, and Croft’s relationship.
- Nayah commands the field operation and retains a safety veto. She ends by accepting necessary shared risk without surrendering field authority.
- TJ means **Terrain Jumper**; multiple distinct physical units exist. Sitara’s TJ and Roman’s TJ are not the same body. Roman’s is the only unit with the deep neural handshake.
- Roman’s TJ receives selected human intent and sends selected alerts, directional impulses, and compressed sensory tags. It is not telepathic, omniscient, remotely controlled, or cloud-dependent.
- TJ units share selected route, safety, and sensor summaries. Full archives remain in local mission partitions until deliberately synchronized.
- Roman’s TJ refuses erasure or corporate shutdown, but disclosure occurs only after it confirms consent through screen and speaker, witnessed by Sitara and Nayah. Roman does not treat the neural impression as sufficient authorization.
- Croft genuinely loves his critically ill son and has produced measurable environmental benefits. He is still responsible for concealment, appropriation, unapproved medical research, pressure against Orien, and making Orien disappear through his network and resources.
- Orien is a former Nexus chemist, Roman’s mentor, and Sitara’s promised mentor. He creates the paired evidence trail, survives near hypothermia, and calls Sitara during the final confrontation. He does not return as an exposition machine or absolve the protagonists.
- Sitara’s aunt and cousins, Nayah’s identity and family history, Croft’s son, and Alpha Dynamics’ CEO remain largely off-page and undescribed beyond the approved material.

### Director decisions

- The book does **not** return to the Himalayas in the present-day plot. The Himalayas function through Roman’s 2042 origin, Sitara’s 2026 loss, and environmental memory; a new Himalayan evidence plot would dilute the Antarctic convergence and risk inventing unconsulted cultural material.
- Merritt’s concealed material is not a secret governance protocol, a biological commons manifesto, or a lost super-organism. The hidden truth is documentary: ecological safeguards, provenance restrictions, and warnings were separated from the commercial research record.
- Orien did not deliberately disappear to pursue a Himalayan discovery. Croft’s retaliation and institutional neglect cause his disappearance; Orien’s paired records are his practical attempt to preserve corroboration.
- The story’s harm is not a new biological contamination or runaway enzyme. The harm is extraction pressure, concealed drilling, ecological risk, proprietary data capture, and the scaling incentive created by legitimate Colorado success.
- The story does not add a remote-control mechanism, automatic TJ shutdown, legal proceeding against Roman, or on-page death of Croft’s son.

## What we take from the material

- **Characters:** Use Sitara, Roman, Nayah, TJ units, Croft, Orien, Croft’s son, Alpha Dynamics, and Sitara’s off-page family from the corresponding headings in `characters.md`. Set aside placeholder villains, invented identities, romance, and any visual design not fixed there.
- **Locations:** Use Falcon, Nexus, Merritt, the Antarctic route, the Himalayas, and Colorado from `world.md`. The present action prioritizes Falcon → Nexus → Colorado; Merritt remains evidence and institutional context rather than a second expedition.
- **Opening:** Keep the draft’s 2042 Roman interstitial, 2026 Sitara interstitial, unstable-zone choice, exposed hatch, and TJ opening Falcon. Improve the draft’s “two bodies” language into bounded sensory exchange.
- **Investigation:** Keep Orien’s paired records, Roman’s paper-archive discovery, Sitara’s ice interpretation, Roman’s chemical reconstruction, the dead krill tank, and the convergence at Nexus.
- **Moral structure:** Keep the draft’s central question—whether measurable good justifies violating stewardship, consent, and ownership—but resolve it through the protagonists’ choices, not speeches.
- **Climax:** Keep the Nexus fracture, Orien’s rescue, the phone call, the TJ records threat, witnessed consent, evacuation, public exposure, Colorado pilot, inspection, and contested public meeting.
- **Set aside:** the draft’s inherited-oil-legacy version of Croft, the “predictive environmental superpower” version of Roman and TJ, a secret Himalayan biological parallel, a distributed governance covenant, a second human antagonist, and any implication that CSG’s good work is fraudulent.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing in this section is canon.

- Alpha Dynamics may attempt to reframe the surviving evidence as a compliance failure by CSG rather than a systemic access problem.
- The public ownership and personhood dispute over TJ may become the next book’s central pressure.
- Orien’s later testimony may clarify the exact chain by which Croft’s threats and corporate neglect caused his disappearance.
- The interim Colorado partnership may develop into a genuinely shared governance model, or fracture over financing and access.
- The visual language may repeat paired records and split-screen compositions in altered forms: divided evidence at midpoint, joined evidence at climax, contested ownership at the ending.

## Non-negotiables

1. Sitara and Roman are co-leads; neither becomes the other’s assistant, love interest, or moral decoration.
2. Nayah’s field authority and safety veto remain real.
3. Croft is the sole principal human antagonist.
4. Falcon is illegal exploratory drilling, not lawful mining and not a commercial mine already in production.
5. Project 863 remains bounded, costly, and the only major **L**.
6. The Colorado benefit must be seen, measured, and limited.
7. Roman’s TJ and Sitara’s TJ are distinct units; Roman’s alone has the neural bond.
8. TJ cannot manufacture arbitrary solutions, access unlimited archives, or function as a magic intelligence.
9. The neural bond is low-bandwidth, selective, embodied, and not remote control.
10. No invented Nayah identity, Thakali custom, family biography, or Himalayan community detail.
11. Larsen C does not fully collapse; the protagonists cannot reverse climate change.
12. The ending preserves evidence and opens accountability; it does not deliver instant legal punishment or institutional purity.
13. Roman’s complicity is exposed, with on-page consequences limited to public exposure and loss of employment.
14. Every major revelation must be shown through evidence, behavior, environment, or consequence before it is explained.

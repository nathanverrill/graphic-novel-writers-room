# Prahul's story notes — the endgame and the Orien thread

Cleaned up from a voice transcript (the raw take is kept for people at
campaigns/avengers/_transcripts/, which the room never reads). Names normalized:
"Orion"/"Ryan Keel" is Orien Keel, "DJ" is TJ, the disease is African sleeping
sickness, the son's leukemia is T-ALL.

## The endgame frame

The finale (or near-finale) is **Alpha Dynamics versus Benedict Croft** — not heroes
versus a lone villain, but the two organizations' purposes colliding, with Roman and
Sitara caught between them.

## Why each side wants the extremophiles

- **Alpha Dynamics** (the Congolese CEO): commercial viability. Eighteen to twenty
  years out, African sleeping sickness has surged; the cure can only be found in the
  Antarctic extremophiles, so the Antarctic research program exists to serve and fund
  that cure.
- **Orien Keel** loves this program: to him the work is public safety and public
  health first.
- **Sitara** wants in for scientific credibility — research standing, publication,
  spotlight.
- **Croft** wants the same resources diverted to study his son's rare T-ALL leukemia.

## The diversion, and what happens to Orien

1. Croft quietly diverts resources and extremophile samples toward his son's cure.
2. Orien discovers the diversion and says he will blow the whistle.
3. Croft takes a rash action: Orien goes missing.

## Roman's search, and the debt

Roman — Orien is his mentor — goes looking. **He does not find Orien.** He searches
Orien's room; Croft catches him there and confronts him. Roman pushes back — "this is
unfair; where is Orien Keel?" — and Croft reveals his son's condition and calls in the
debt: *I gave you everything, I sponsored you.* Roman, who knows the value of losing a
family member, agrees to help — **but only for some time.**

Why Roman can be convinced: he has no father figure. Croft is the only father figure
he has — sponsor, benefactor, bonded to him through the history with Roman's father.

Roman's TJ is put to work on Croft's angle: harvesting extremophiles (fishing /
krill-level sampling) toward the leukemia cure.

## How Orien is found

**Sitara's TJ finds him**, using the heat-mapping / heat-sensing capability Sitara has
been developing as a student at CU Boulder. Once she finds Orien, she understands what
has been going on, and decides to align with Alpha Dynamics, power up, and confront
Croft.

## The confrontation scene

Roman and Sitara confront each other. Sitara gets a phone call — it is Orien — and
Roman realizes Sitara has information about Orien and probably knows what is about to
happen. Sitara says she is going to reveal everything. Croft answers with the
counter-blow: *"If you do that, you're no better than me. You would have blood on your
hands — my son's blood, because he would be dead soon."*

## The midpoint, and the shape of the back half

- At the midpoint, Roman and Sitara reluctantly keep working with each other despite
  having no trust, and the tension escalates continuously to a complete showdown at
  the end.

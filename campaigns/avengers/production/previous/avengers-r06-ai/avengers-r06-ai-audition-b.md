## Page 1 (right) — Plant the old flood, then watch Sitara choose the flagged line over the warning

**Panel 1.** Himalayan floodwater churns through a narrow valley, brown and fast. A cooking pot, a single rubber sandal, and a framed photograph spin past in the current. In the middle distance, small and half-turned away, a six-year-old girl is pulled back from the bank by an adult's arm — we see the arm, not the face. No cultural detail beyond flood and household objects.

CAPTION: Langtang region, Nepal — 2026.

**Panel 2.** Wide Antarctic ice shelf, blinding blue-white under grey sky. Sitara (red jacket, hood down, goggles pushed up) and Nayah (yellow jacket, hood down) stand near a line of orange warning flags. Sitara's TJ crouches at her heel, sensor dish angled at something inside the flagged zone.

CAPTION: Antarctic Peninsula — twenty years later.
NAYAH: Sensor's flatlined inside the flag line. Log it dead and we move to the next site.
SITARA: It's not dead, Nayah. It's still transmitting. Dead sensors don't transmit.

**Panel 3.** Close on Sitara, jaw set, staring past the flags at a half-buried sensor post fifteen feet inside the unstable zone. Nayah is a hard shape at the panel's edge, not softening.

NAYAH: Then it's someone else's problem today.
SITARA: It's thirty seconds.

**Panel 4.** Low angle: Sitara's boot lifts over the orange flag line, snow beneath it webbed with fine cracks nobody has looked at yet. TJ's head swivels to follow her, torn between the two humans.

NAYAH (off): Sitara. Sitara! That is not what "I heard you" means.

PAGE CHECK: Establishes Sitara's inherited urgency without a speech; page turn = has she already crossed the line. Continuity flag: keep young Sitara's figure generic/unnamed-community per non-negotiable #10.

## Page 2 (left) — Reveal: the warning was never about the ice alone

**Panel 1.** Sitara crouched over the buried sensor post, gloved hand closing on it. Beneath the snow, faint vertical lines suggest vibration traveling up through the shelf, not from her.

SFX: hmmmMMM
SITARA: Got you—

**Panel 2.** The slab under her splits in a jagged line. Her weight is suddenly wrong.

SFX: KRAKK
NAYAH: MOVE—!

**Panel 3 (large panel).** Sitara pitches sideways as the slab gives way beneath her; the sensor and a boot bag vanish into the dark below. Her headlamp beam catches a scarred metal hatch sunk in the ice under her, stenciled letters half-melted clean: **FALCON**. TJ's small silhouette leans over the new edge above her, sensor-eyes fixed down, not on her.

**Panel 4.** Nayah hauls Sitara back onto solid ice by the collar of her jacket. Sitara is still turned toward the hole. TJ has not moved to help either of them — it's still watching the hatch.

NAYAH: Off the shelf. Now. Everyone.
SITARA: Nayah — there's a door down there.

PAGE CHECK: Even-page reveal delivers Falcon per brief. Changed information: the danger has a name and a lock. Page-turn pull: what does the door open for.

## Page 3 (right) — The hatch was never built to keep TJ out

**Panel 1.** Inside the new crevasse mouth: TJ picks its way down to the hatch on its own, red booties bright against blue ice, and presses its faceplate close to a recessed panel beside the lock.

NAYAH (from above, off-panel): That thing is not cleared for whatever's down there.
SITARA: It's not asking to be cleared. Watch its display.

**Panel 2.** Close on the hatch panel: a rotating amber pattern pulses across it. TJ's own green-black screen answers in the same rhythm, a beat behind — machine talking to machine, nothing human about it.

SITARA: It already knows the handshake.
NAYAH: Whose handshake.

**Panel 3.** The lock disengages with a hiss; the hatch swings inward on old hinges, spilling stale, colder air and dim emergency light up into the crevasse.

SFX: HSSSSK-tunk

**Panel 4.** Sitara steps toward the opening. Nayah's hand lands flat on her shoulder, not stopping her — setting terms.

NAYAH: Ten minutes. A line to the surface, and I want eyes on you every second of it.
SITARA: Ten minutes.

PAGE CHECK: Ends on entry, not answer — propulsion into Falcon's interior on Page 4. TJ's inherited credential shown doing something (opens only the hatch), not explained. Continuity flag: TJ's authentication behavior must read as pre-existing/inherited, not a new power invented for the scene.

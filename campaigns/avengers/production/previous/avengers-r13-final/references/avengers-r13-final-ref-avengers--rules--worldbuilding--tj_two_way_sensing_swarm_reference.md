# TJ — Terrain Jumper Systems Reference

## Overview

**Name:** TJ  
**Expanded name:** Terrain Jumper  
**Primary domains:** Exploration, search and rescue, human + AI teaming  
**Core concept:** One brain, many perspectives  
**Base unit:** Small, lightweight scout robot  
**Swarm capability:** Can divide into multiple specialized units  
**Operating environments:** Mountains, ice, caves, ruins, disaster zones, under-ice environments  

TJ is a small, fast, agile exploration robot designed to extend human senses and contribute its own machine perception.

TJ is intended to function as a human + AI teammate rather than a passive tool.

The platform can:

- Sense the world through multiple modalities
- Share environmental information with a human partner
- Receive human context and intent
- Break into specialized robotic units
- Reassemble when needed
- Coordinate distributed sensing and action
- Operate in extreme environments
- Support exploration, rescue, reconnaissance, and scientific missions

The core design principle is:

**One brain, many perspectives.**

## Base grid and sequence plan

**Base grid:** Three variable tiers, maximum four panels per page. Page 24 is a full-page splash. Every panel reserves clear lettering space. Falcon and Nexus remain geographically distinct. Roman’s bonded TJ and Sitara’s TJ remain separate physical units.

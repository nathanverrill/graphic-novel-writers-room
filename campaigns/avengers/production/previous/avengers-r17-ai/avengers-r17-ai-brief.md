# avengers — Development Brief

## Logline

In 2046 Antarctica, cryosphere scientist Sitara Lhakpa and chemical engineer Roman Ashford must combine their concealed evidence, confront their own complicity, and stop a corporate extraction program before it erases the records—and the autonomous robot whose consent makes disclosure possible.

## Theme

Useful knowledge does not become private property because someone extracted it first; discovery creates responsibility for provenance, consent, ecological limits, and shared stewardship.

## Tone & comps

- **_Arrival_** — patient scientific intelligence and communication treated as dramatic action.
- **_The Abyss_** — hostile environments forcing cooperation under physical pressure.
- **_The Constant Gardener_** — corporate systems that produce genuine public benefits while causing concealed harm.

## Audience & rating

Adult and older-teen science-fiction thriller. **PG-13 / 14+:** environmental disaster, peril, corporate coercion, implied unauthorized medical research, and a non-graphic near-death survivor. No gore, torture, or sexual violence.

## Format

- **24-page single-issue graphic novel chapter**
- **Trim:** 6.625" × 10.25"
- **Full color**
- Default density: **4–6 panels per page**, with occasional large panels for the Falcon hatch, Nexus fracture, evacuation, and Colorado results.
- Page allocation:
  - Pages 1–2: Hook
  - Pages 3–5: Plot Turn 1
  - Pages 6–8: Pinch 1
  - Pages 9–12: Midpoint
  - Pages 13–16: Pinch 2
  - Pages 17–20: Plot Turn 2
  - Pages 21–24: Resolution
- Even-page reveals should carry the major turns: Falcon exposed on Page 2, Nexus fracture on Page 10 or 12, Orien’s call on Page 18, witnessed TJ consent on Page 20, and the Colorado result on Page 22.

## Visual direction

Grounded near-future field realism with documentary clarity and strong environmental contrast.

- **Antarctica:** blue-white scale, red and yellow expedition clothing, black machinery, warning flags, drill vibration, snow, meltwater, and blue ice intruding through human structures.
- **Colorado:** warmer but stressed; concrete, green-brown water, sorting machinery, dashboards, wetland channels, rejected material, and visible power and throughput limits.
- **Himalayas:** brief tactile memory images of floodwater, household objects, ice, and unstable ground. Do not invent cultural practices or unapproved identities.
- **Technology:** compact, maintained, imperfect, and physical. TJs remain approximately 249 grams and small-dog scale, with seamless silver panels, green-black displays, compact sensor bodies, and red Antarctic booties where appropriate. Never draw a large stereotypical robot dog.
- **Information design:** paired records, split screens, local maps, monitoring displays, and physical archives should carry exposition before dialogue does.
- **Neural communication:** selective, embodied, and visually distinct from ordinary dialogue. Use compressed directional or sensory impressions, never glowing telepathy or full-body possession. TJ’s screen and speaker remain a separate, witnessable interface.
- **Spatial clarity:** Falcon and Nexus are separate Antarctic sites connected by an approximately two-hour weather-dependent route. Never place them in one shared exterior or imply that Falcon is visible from Nexus without a stated remote feed or intervening travel.

## Canon

The following is approved project truth for this book. It includes all material marked fixed, plus the Director’s decisions below.

### Story and world

- The story occurs in **2046** across Antarctica, the Himalayas as backstory, and Colorado.
- Sitara Lhakpa and Roman Ashford are equal co-protagonists with equal agency, plot weight, and presence. Their relationship is not romantic.
- Both team stories remain true in one world: Sitara, Nayah, Falcon, and the illegal extraction program coexist with Roman, Croft, Orien, Nexus, Merritt, and Project 863.
- The central ecological threat is a combined cascade of Antarctic sea-ice loss, krill decline, and Larsen-system instability. Falcon’s drilling worsens local danger but does not cause global climate change or single-handedly collapse Larsen C.
- Falcon is illegal exploratory drilling and extraction monitoring under a scientific-drilling permit. It is not an operating commercial mine.
- Nexus is a decommissioned coastal Antarctic cave laboratory. Merritt is a separate Alpha Dynamics-operated site in Wilkes Land. Colorado is the visible application site.
- Project 863 is the sole major **L**: bounded catalytic recycling of selected plastics into hydrocarbon feedstock. It produces real, measurable benefits and visible costs.
- Cold-active enzymes, TJ autonomy, fabrication, AI sorting, local mesh architecture, and ordinary neural-interface infrastructure remain **EG**. The Roman–TJ bond remains **S**. No new major speculative system is introduced.
- Alpha Dynamics controls infrastructure and access; CSG carries visible operational responsibility for Falcon and Nexus. Croft is the sole principal human antagonist. Alpha Dynamics is institutional pressure, not a second human villain.
- The Colorado pilot demonstrates recovered feedstock, clearer water, returning aquatic invertebrates, rejected feedstock, residual solids, emissions monitoring, water use, grid demand, and finite throughput.
- The ending is a constrained victory: evidence survives, drilling is stopped or exposed, the site is evacuated, the Colorado pilot continues under an unincorporated interim partnership, and ownership and financing remain contested. Formal enforcement remains outside the book.

### People and arcs

- Sitara begins by treating urgency and understanding as permission to control knowledge. She crosses the flagged zone, conceals copies of Merritt research, and gives Croft a controlled copy for funding, laboratory access, and application. She ends by admitting her concealment, relinquishing exclusive credit, and choosing shared stewardship.
- Roman begins dependent on Croft and morally compromised by unauthorized T-ALL research and the circumstances surrounding Orien’s disappearance. He ends by choosing public responsibility over revenge and accepting loss of employment, reputation, access, and Croft’s relationship.
- Nayah commands the field operation and retains a safety veto. She accepts necessary shared risk without surrendering field authority.
- TJ means **Terrain Jumper**; multiple distinct physical units exist. Sitara’s TJ and Roman’s TJ are not the same body. Roman’s is the only unit with the deep neural handshake.
- Roman’s TJ sends selected alerts, directional impulses, and compressed sensory tags, and receives selected human intent. It is not telepathic, omniscient, remotely controlled, or cloud-dependent.
- TJ units share selected route, safety, and sensor summaries. Full archives remain in local mission partitions until deliberately synchronized.
- Roman’s TJ refuses erasure or corporate shutdown, but disclosure occurs only after it confirms consent through screen and speaker, witnessed by Sitara and Nayah. The authorized disclosure is limited to the selected Falcon environmental records, Merritt provenance records, and agreed corroborating mission data. Sitara’s TJ authorizes its own selected records separately.
- Croft genuinely loves his critically ill son and has produced measurable environmental benefits. He remains responsible for concealment, appropriation, unapproved medical research, pressure against Orien, and making Orien disappear through his network and resources.
- Orien is a former Nexus chemist, Roman’s mentor, and Sitara’s promised mentor. He creates the paired evidence trail, survives near hypothermia, and calls Sitara during the final confrontation. He does not return as an exposition machine or absolve the protagonists.
- Croft’s son survives the book and remains critically ill. No cure is shown or implied.
- Sitara’s aunt and cousins, Nayah’s identity and family history, Croft’s son, and Alpha Dynamics’ CEO remain largely off-page and undescribed beyond approved material.

### Director decisions

- The book does **not** return to the Himalayas in the present-day plot. The Himalayas function through Roman’s 2042 origin, Sitara’s 2026 loss, and environmental memory.
- Merritt’s concealed material is documentary: ecological safeguards, provenance restrictions, and warnings were separated from the commercial research record. It is not a secret governance protocol, biological commons manifesto, or lost super-organism.
- Orien did not deliberately disappear to pursue a Himalayan discovery. Croft’s retaliation and institutional neglect cause his disappearance.
- The story’s harm is extraction pressure, concealed drilling, ecological risk, proprietary data capture, and the scaling incentive created by legitimate Colorado success. It is not runaway biology or a new contamination event.
- Page 8 must visibly establish Roman’s choice to travel from Colorado to Antarctica before the route sequence begins.
- The climax remains physically at Nexus. Falcon’s status is shown through authenticated records, a remote operational feed, or a clearly established later transmission—not through an unexplained view of Falcon from Nexus.
- The protagonists stop Falcon’s active drilling by transmitting authenticated evidence and securing or flagging the local control record for inspection. The operation is both physically interrupted and made publicly inspectable; this does not reverse climate change.
- Croft’s final irreversible choice is to decline to block the Colorado pilot under the interim public contract, preserving its operation while surrendering CSG’s proprietary framing and control. This is not repentance or legal exoneration: he chooses the pilot’s survival because it is demonstrably helping people, while losing the control he believes makes such help possible.
- The showrunner’s newly established page count supersedes the earlier 22-page allocation. The 24-page structure above is now binding.

## What we take from the material

- **Characters:** Use Sitara, Roman, Nayah, TJ units, Croft, Orien, Croft’s son, Alpha Dynamics, and Sitara’s off-page family from the corresponding headings in `characters.md`. Set aside placeholder villains, invented identities, romance, and unfixed visual designs.
- **Locations:** Use Falcon, Nexus, Merritt, the Antarctic route, the Himalayas, and Colorado from `world.md`. Present action prioritizes Falcon → Nexus → Colorado; Merritt remains evidence and institutional context rather than a second expedition.
- **Opening:** Keep the 2042 Roman interstitial, 2026 Sitara interstitial, unstable-zone choice, exposed hatch, and TJ opening Falcon. Replace the draft’s “two bodies” language with bounded sensory exchange.
- **Investigation:** Keep Orien’s paired records, Roman’s paper-archive discovery, Sitara’s ice interpretation, Roman’s chemical reconstruction, the dead krill tank, and convergence at Nexus.
- **Moral structure:** Keep the question of whether measurable good justifies violating stewardship, consent, and ownership. Resolve it through choices and consequences, not speeches.
- **Climax:** Keep the Nexus fracture, Orien’s rescue, the phone call, the TJ records threat, witnessed consent, evacuation, authenticated public exposure, Colorado pilot, inspection, and contested public meeting.
- **Set aside:** the draft’s inherited-oil-legacy version of Croft, predictive-environmental-superpower framing, secret Himalayan biological parallels, distributed governance covenant, second human antagonist, and any implication that CSG’s good work is fraudulent.
- **Repair from room notes:** preserve the separate Falcon/Nexus geography; make the Page 8 travel choice visible; make Croft’s final choice active; identify the exact limited TJ disclosure; and include at least one readable Colorado metric before the resolution.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing in this section is canon.

- Alpha Dynamics may reframe the surviving evidence as a CSG compliance failure rather than a systemic access problem.
- The public ownership and personhood dispute over TJ may become the next book’s central pressure.
- Orien’s later testimony may clarify the exact chain by which Croft’s threats and corporate neglect caused his disappearance.
- The interim Colorado partnership may develop into a shared governance model or fracture over financing and access.
- A public intermediary may receive the evidence through a university, inspection office, or public-benefit partnership without becoming a new protagonist.
- Visual repetitions may pair divided evidence at the midpoint, joined evidence at the climax, and contested documents at the ending.

## Non-negotiables

1. Sitara and Roman are co-leads; neither becomes the other’s assistant, love interest, or moral decoration.
2. Nayah’s field authority and safety veto remain real.
3. Croft is the sole principal human antagonist.
4. Falcon is illegal exploratory drilling, not lawful mining and not a commercial mine already in production.
5. Project 863 remains bounded, costly, and the only major **L**.
6. Colorado’s benefit must be seen, measured, and limited.
7. Roman’s TJ and Sitara’s TJ are distinct units; Roman’s alone has the neural bond.
8. TJ cannot manufacture arbitrary solutions, access unlimited archives, or function as a magic intelligence.
9. The neural bond is low-bandwidth, selective, embodied, and not remote control.
10. No invented Nayah identity, Thakali custom, family biography, or Himalayan community detail.
11. Larsen C does not fully collapse; the protagonists cannot reverse climate change.
12. The ending preserves evidence and opens accountability; it does not deliver instant legal punishment or institutional purity.
13. Roman’s complicity is exposed, with on-page consequences limited to public exposure and loss of employment.
14. Every major revelation must be shown through evidence, behavior, environment, or consequence before it is explained.
15. Falcon and Nexus must remain geographically distinct and visually oriented.
16. No generic “TJ records” disclosure: every released data category and authorizing TJ must be identifiable.
17. Croft’s final choice must preserve the Colorado pilot while costing him proprietary control and authority.

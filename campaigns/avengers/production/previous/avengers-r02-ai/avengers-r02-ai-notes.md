## Plausibility ledger

- **Canon status and labels checked:** The project correctly identifies Project 863 as the sole major **L**. TJ fabrication, local mesh/data partitions, cold-active enzymes, and the inherited Falcon credential are treated as **EG**; the Roman–TJ bond is treated as **S**. No page plan currently introduces a second major speculative system.
- **Contradiction against a fixed truth:** `world.md`’s claim that Croft’s son dies within a month contradicts the fixed survival canon and is reported above as a Blocker.
- **License used to skip necessary work:** No current outline beat uses Project 863 to erase sorting, washing, water use, emissions, residuals, power demand, or throughput. Page 21 explicitly retains those costs.
- **License behaving differently across the book:** Project 863 remains bounded in the Colorado material. The main risk is language that treats Antarctic biology as the direct “solution” rather than as support for a bounded pretreatment; keep the licensed process and EG enzyme contribution distinct.
- **Numbers, prices, or laws stated as fact:** The 249-gram TJ specification and 250-gram transport threshold are fixed project facts. The 52% Colorado streamflow figure in `world.md` is explicitly real-world grounding, not a required story event; do not put it on a page as an unqualified in-world forecast unless the Director approves that use.
- **Unlabelled or unstable invention:** The outline does not clearly label Croft’s active endgame choice, the exact public intermediary, or the precise record-custody path. These are development gaps rather than new licenses; Director and Plotter should settle them before writing.
- **Potential license overreach:** Page 18’s corporate threat must remain an access/record-erasure threat. Any depiction of remote TJ shutdown, bodily control, or automatic destruction would contradict fixed world rules and become a Blocker.

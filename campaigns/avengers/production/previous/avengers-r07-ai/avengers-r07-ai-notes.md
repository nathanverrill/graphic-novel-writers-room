## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The brief, story, characters, and world files largely agree on the 24-page Antarctic convergence thriller, the co-leads, the bounded technology, and the constrained ending. One direct project-truth contradiction remains in the world file, while several proposed institutional and numerical details are being used as though already approved canon. Resolve those development-level decisions before the Plotter and Character Designer proceed.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

1. **Location: `world.md`, “Medical pressure” — Croft’s son’s outcome contradicts approved canon.**  
   The world file states that if the hidden research becomes public, “the interruption causes his son to die within a month.” The brief and `facts.md` explicitly fix that Croft’s son survives the book and remains critically ill; no death is shown or implied. This contradiction changes Croft’s stakes and the meaning of his final choice.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: remove the death claim from `world.md` and replace it with the approved condition—that disclosure endangers or interrupts the treatment path while the son survives and remains critically ill.  
   **Fix:** Director

### Major

1. **Location: `world.md`, “Colorado governance” versus brief Canon and story Pages 21–24 — institutional form changes without a time marker.**  
   The world file says the organization is “now a nonprofit public-benefit corporation,” while the brief and story ending require an **unincorporated interim partnership** under public contracts, with ownership and financing unresolved. These could describe successive stages, but the files do not state that clearly.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: distinguish the established organization from the book’s ending arrangement. State that the former CSG research division became or operated through a public-benefit structure, but the exposed pilot itself ends under a temporary unincorporated partnership. If the nonprofit corporation is not needed in this book, remove it.  
   **Fix:** Director, Plotter

2. **Location: `story.md`, “Unresolved decisions and proposed canon changes”; Pages 15, 21, and 23 — proposed details are functioning as settled story facts.**  
   The story uses the exact Colorado figures (**41 → 68 clarity units; 3 → 11 taxa**) and the distributed public archive/intermediary structure, while labeling them proposals. The brief requires a readable metric and accountable custody, but does not approve these exact numbers or recipients. This creates an execution risk because later page work may treat provisional material as canon.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: either approve the figures and custody mechanism in the brief/facts, or revise the story to identify them explicitly as placeholders pending approval. Do not let layouts or lettering silently canonize them.  
   **Fix:** Director, Plotter

3. **Location: `story.md`, Page 23 and Croft’s character sheet — Croft’s final action is described, but its mechanism and consequence are not yet locked.**  
   The story says Croft declines to block the interim contract and surrenders CSG’s exclusive framing, but the exact action remains a proposal (“signs or authorizes”). Because this is his sole irreversible choice and the resolution depends on it, an abstract refusal risks reading as an off-page institutional outcome rather than character action.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: approve one visible action—such as Croft personally withdrawing CSG’s proprietary objection or authorizing the interim public contract to continue—while preserving that he does not repent, regain control, or receive legal exoneration.  
   **Fix:** Director, Plotter

4. **Location: `story.md`, Pages 18–20 and “Exact TJ disclosure” proposal — authorization boundaries are nearly clear but not yet fully consistent across the development files.**  
   The brief fixes selected Roman-TJ records, separately selected Sitara-TJ records, and agreed human evidence. The story’s proposed package sometimes describes “corroborating mission data” without defining whether those data come from Roman’s TJ, Sitara’s TJ, or human-held copies. Since the ownership/personhood conflict depends on precise agency, the package must be fixed before scripting.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: approve a compact disclosure inventory naming each authorizing source and category. Preserve separate local partitions, no full synchronization, no private neural telemetry, and no corporate remote shutdown.  
   **Fix:** Director, Plotter

5. **Location: `story.md`, Page 8 and story map — Roman’s Antarctic travel is causally intended but under-specified at development level.**  
   The brief correctly requires Page 8 to show Roman choosing travel from Colorado to Antarctica. The page plot shows him requesting transport and boarding a tracked vehicle, but does not establish whether CSG, an Antarctic logistics chain, or another already-existing institution authorizes the trip. This is not yet a contradiction, but the unexplained logistics could make Roman’s arrival feel convenient.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter: identify the existing CSG/Antarctic logistics channel that Roman uses, and make the choice visible without adding a new named intermediary or subplot.  
   **Fix:** Plotter

6. **Location: `world.md`, “Antarctic Peninsula and Larsen region” versus Falcon/Nexus descriptions — relative geography is potentially misleading.**  
   The world file calls Falcon “inland” and Nexus a “nearby coastal cave laboratory,” while the brief requires them to remain separate sites connected by an approximately two-hour weather-dependent route. “Nearby” can encourage layouts or scripts to depict them as visually adjacent.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Director: retain the two-hour route as the sole operational anchor and specify that neither site is visible from the other. Remove or qualify “nearby” if necessary.  
   **Fix:** Director, Plotter

- **[FIXED] Falcon and Nexus:** The files consistently define Falcon as illegal exploratory drilling and Nexus as a separate decommissioned cave laboratory. The “nearby coastal” wording in `world.md` should be qualified to prevent a visual geography error.
- **[FIXED] Merritt:** The files consistently keep Merritt separate from Falcon and Nexus and make its hidden material documentary/provenance-based rather than a secret biological protocol.
- **[FIXED] Project 863:** The license is consistently bounded by sorting, energy, water, emissions, residuals, and finite throughput. No file turns it into a universal cure.
- **[FIXED / EG / S] TJ platform:** Scale, separate bodies, local partitions, limited fabrication, and the unique Roman bond are consistently stated. The disclosure mechanism still needs one approved inventory.
- **[FIXED] Roman’s legal consequences:** The files correctly limit on-page consequences to exposure and loss of employment/access. No approved file should add arrest, charges, or sentencing.
- **[FIXED] Croft’s son survives:** This is contradicted by the sentence in `world.md` claiming he dies within a month. That line must be removed.
- **[PROPOSAL] Colorado metrics:** The exact clarity and invertebrate figures are proposed fictional metrics, not yet approved facts. They require Director approval before they become production truth.
- **[PROPOSAL] Public evidence recipient:** The distributed public archive and institutional custody model are proposed. They are compatible with the brief but must be approved or simplified.
- **[FIXED] Ending governance:** The brief and story agree that inspection precedes the public meeting, the interim contract is read aloud, and ownership/financing remain contested. The nonprofit/public-benefit-corporation language in `world.md` needs temporal clarification.
- **[FIXED] Croft’s moral position:** The files consistently avoid making Croft a fraudulent villain; he produces genuine benefits and genuinely loves his son. His final concession must remain a choice, not a conversion or exoneration.

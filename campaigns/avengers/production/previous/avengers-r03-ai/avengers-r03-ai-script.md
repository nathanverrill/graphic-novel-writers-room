## Page 1 (right) — Two brief histories collapse into one Antarctic warning

**Panel 1.** **HIMALAYAS, 2042 — NIGHT.** A narrow blue-white crevasse. Eighteen-year-old ROMAN ASHFORD hangs against the ice, half-buried in snow, one gloved hand locked around a climbing line. His small silver Terrain Jumper, red Antarctic booties absent here, descends toward him on articulated legs. Lightning forks above the glacier.

No dialogue.

**Panel 2.** **HIMALAYAS, 2026 — DAY.** Floodwater tears through the lower edge of the panel, carrying a cooking pot and a child’s red sweater past six-year-old SITARA, seen only from behind on a high stone step. An adult hand pulls her back from the water. Keep faces and setting nonspecific; this is a tactile memory, not a geography lesson.

No dialogue.

**Panel 3.** **ANTARCTICA, 2046 — DAY.** Vast blue-white ice. SITARA, 26, in her puffy red jacket and beanie, stands beside NAYAH in a puffy yellow jacket. Warning flags mark a dark unstable zone between them and a half-buried sensor tripod. Sitara’s small silver TJ crouches beside the route marker. A hairline fracture cuts across the snow toward the sensor.

NAYAH: Sitara. Stop there.

**Panel 4.** Close on Sitara’s boot hovering just short of the flagged line. Beyond it: the sensor’s blinking red status light. Beneath the ice, a faint geometric tremor travels toward her.

SFX: **KRRRNNN**

SITARA: It’s still recording.

**PAGE CHECK:** Sitara wants the sensor; Nayah’s safety order prevents her. The past images establish loss and danger without explanation. The final panel turns the warning into a choice: step over the line or lose the evidence.

## Page 2 (left) — Sitara crosses the warning and the ice exposes Falcon

**Panel 1.** Same Antarctic field position. Nayah has one hand on the emergency route marker, eyes on Sitara. Sitara steps over the flagged line. Her TJ follows, low and careful. The warning flags snap hard in the wind.

NAYAH: Mark the withdrawal route.

SITARA: I have it.

**Panel 2.** Sitara kneels beside the sensor tripod, fastening a retrieval line while her TJ scans the ice. No one is looking at the deep blue seam spreading beneath the tripod.

SFX: **TIK. TIK. TIK.**

TJ (speaker): Vibration increasing.

**Panel 3.** The ice slab drops. Sitara falls to one knee, one hand catching the sensor case. Her retrieval line jerks tight. Nayah braces against the route marker, unable to reach her without crossing the unstable section.

SFX: **KRAK—**

NAYAH: Sitara!

**Panel 4.** **LARGE REVEAL PANEL.** The slab has broken away beneath Sitara. Blue ice descends into darkness. A black metal hatch is exposed in the cavity, streaked with frost and stamped:

**FALCON**

A buried drill housing shudders below it. Sitara grips the sensor in one hand and the ice edge with the other. TJ stands at the hatch, its small electronic eyes fixed on the seam.

SFX: **THRUMM—THRUMM—THRUMM**

**PAGE CHECK:** Sitara’s choice causes the immediate failure. The sensor is recovered, but the problem changes from unstable ice to a concealed active installation. Page turn reveals Falcon.

## Page 3 (right) — The hatch recognizes TJ, and entry becomes a decision

**Panel 1.** Inside the exposed cavity, close on the Falcon hatch. A narrow status strip wakes beneath the frost. The hatch projects a rotating machine challenge as a sequence of simple bars and dots across its maintenance plate. Sitara’s gloved hand reaches toward it, but Nayah catches her sleeve from above.

NAYAH: Nobody opens an unknown door from a hole in the ice.

SITARA: Then we learn what it asks.

**Panel 2.** Sitara lowers TJ toward the hatch on the retrieval line. The robot’s compact sensor body hovers inches from the maintenance plate. Its sealed challenge-response module sits beside the communications core. The hatch displays:

**ROTATING CREDENTIAL — RESPONSE REQUIRED**

No dialogue.

**Panel 3.** TJ’s green-black display fills with the same bars and dots. A small red indicator on the hatch turns amber. The machine accepts the response.

SFX: **CHIP—CHIP—CHIP**

TJ (speaker): Local maintenance interface available.

**Panel 4.** The hatch opens only a handspan, releasing a breath of warmer air and a vertical slice of darkness. Inside: black cable, a yellow maintenance lamp, and the edge of a directional drilling map. No full facility reveal yet. Sitara looks down into it. Nayah remains above, holding the route line and watching the fracture.

NAYAH: Five minutes. Then out.

SITARA: We record first.

**PAGE CHECK:** TJ’s credential opens only the hatch and local maintenance interface, not the whole site. Nayah retains field authority and sets the safety limit. Sitara chooses entry; the next page begins inside Falcon.

# First-Read Report: Avengers Audition

## Version A

### Pulled me Found
- Page 1, Panel 1: Dropping straight into the 2042 ice hanger without preamble creates an immediate physical stakes moment.
- Page 2, Panel 4: The **FALCON** stamp reveal feels earned because the ice breakdown directly causes the exposure.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- Page 1, Panel 2: The flashback transition feels slightly abrupt coming right after a dynamic hanging-in-ice panel, though it grounds Sitara's personal motivation.

### I was confused
- Page 1, Panel 1: The note about "red Antarctic booties absent here" in the panel description is a bit of internal writer metadata that doesn't translate directly to cold-read clarity, but the image of hanging in the ice works.

### I felt something
- Page 2, Panel 3-4: The sudden drop of the ice slab and Sitara clinging to the ice edge with one hand creates genuine physical tension.

### I predicted
- Page 3: Once TJ approaches the hatch, I predicted its built-in credentials or interface would unlock the old facility, leading us inside for the next sequence.

## Version B

### Pulled me Found
- Page 1, Panel 1: The caption "Langtang region, Nepal — 2026" instantly grounds the flashback with clean, specific geography.
- Page 2, Panel 3: The large panel showing Sitara pitching sideways as the slab gives way is visually dynamic and punchy.

### I slowed down / skimmed

### I was confused
- Page 1, Panel 4: "That is not what 'I heard you' means" is snappy dialogue, but the exact dynamic between Nayah and Sitara feels slightly more argumentative than cooperative right off the bat.

### I felt something
- Page 2, Panel 4: Nayah hauling Sitara back by the collar while TJ ignores both of them to stare at the hatch creates a striking eerie moment about the robot's alien priorities.

### I predicted
- Page 3: The handshake between TJ and the lock would successfully open the hatch, setting up the ten-minute countdown.

## Comparison

### Which one I would keep reading, and the moment that decided it
I would keep reading **Version A**. The moment that decided it was **Page 2, Panel 4**, where the physical weight of the reveal—the ice dropping into darkness, the frosted black metal hatch stamped **FALCON**, and TJ's quiet fixation—hits with cinematic precision. Version A lets the environment do more heavy lifting before the dialogue resumes.

### One-sentence aftertaste of each
- **Version A:** A cold, tactile descent into buried history where human caution collides with ancient hardware.
- **Version B:** A sharp, snappy confrontation at the edge of an abyss where machines have their own agenda.

His small silver Terrain Jumper descends toward him on articulated legs. No red booties in this origin scene. Lightning forks through the glacier and the machine’s sensor housing.

No dialogue.

**Panel 2.** **HIMALAYAS, 2026 — DAY.** Floodwater tears across the foreground, carrying a cooking pot and a child’s red sweater. Six-year-old SITARA is seen from behind on a high stone step as an adult hand pulls her away from the current. Keep faces and cultural details nonspecific. The image is tactile, immediate, and brief.

No dialogue.

**Panel 3.** **ANTARCTICA, 2046 — DAY.** Vast blue-white ice under a hard sky. SITARA, 26, in her puffy red jacket and beanie, stands beside NAYAH in her puffy yellow jacket. Warning flags mark a dark unstable zone between them and a half-buried sensor tripod. Sitara’s separate small silver TJ crouches beside the route marker. A hairline fracture runs toward the sensor.

NAYAH: Sitara. Stop there.

**Panel 4.** Close on Sitara’s boot hovering just short of the flagged line. Beyond it, the sensor’s red status light blinks. Beneath the ice, a geometric tremor advances toward her boot.

SFX: **KRRRNNN**

SITARA: It’s still recording.

**PAGE CHECK:** Sitara wants the sensor; Nayah’s safety order blocks her. The past images establish loss and dangerous ice without explaining either. The final panel makes Sitara’s next step a choice.

## Page 2 (left) — Sitara crosses the line, and the ice reveals Falcon

**Panel 1.** **ANTARCTICA, CONTINUOUS.** Nayah plants an emergency route marker and clips a safety line into place. Sitara steps over the warning flags anyway. Her TJ follows low across the snow, its bright thermal sensor band facing the buried sensor.

NAYAH: Mark the withdrawal route.

SITARA: I have it.

**Panel 2.** Sitara kneels beside the tripod, fastening a retrieval line to the sensor case. Her gloved fingers work quickly. The TJ scans the surface. A dark blue seam widens beneath the tripod, unnoticed by Sitara.

SFX: **TIK. TIK. TIK.**

TJ (speaker): Vibration increasing.

**Panel 3.** The ice slab drops. Sitara falls to one knee, one hand catching the sensor case as the retrieval line jerks tight. Nayah braces against the route marker, unable to reach her without entering the unstable section.

SFX: **KRAK—**

NAYAH: Sitara!

**Panel 4.** **LARGE REVEAL PANEL.** The slab has broken away beneath Sitara. Blue ice falls into darkness. A black metal hatch is exposed in the cavity, streaked with frost and stamped:

**FALCON**

Below it, a buried drill housing shudders. Sitara grips the sensor in one hand and the ice edge with the other. Her TJ stands at the hatch, small against the machinery.

SFX: **THRUMM—THRUMM—THRUMM**

**PAGE CHECK:** Sitara’s decision causes the immediate failure. The sensor is recovered, but the problem changes from unstable ice to a concealed active installation. The Falcon mark is the page-turn reveal.

## Page 3 (right) — Falcon’s machine recognizes TJ, making entry possible but not safe

**Panel 1.** Inside the exposed cavity, a close view of the Falcon hatch. A narrow status strip wakes beneath the frost, displaying a rotating machine challenge as simple bars and dots. Sitara reaches toward the hatch from the ice edge. Nayah catches her sleeve from above, one hand still on the safety line.

NAYAH: Nobody opens an unknown door from a hole in the ice.

SITARA: Then we learn what it asks.

**Panel 2.** Sitara lowers her TJ toward the hatch on the retrieval line. The robot’s compact body hangs inches from the maintenance plate. Its sealed challenge-response module sits beside the communications core.

The hatch display reads:

**ROTATING CREDENTIAL — RESPONSE REQUIRED**

No dialogue.

**Panel 3.** The TJ’s green-black screen reproduces the bars and dots. Its red Antarctic booties brush the metal as it steadies itself. On the hatch, the indicator changes from red to amber.

SFX: **CHIP—CHIP—CHIP**

TJ (speaker): Local maintenance interface available.

**Panel 4.** The hatch opens only a handspan, releasing warmer air and a vertical slice of darkness. Inside: black cable, a yellow maintenance lamp, and the edge of a directional drilling map. No full facility reveal. Sitara looks down; Nayah remains above, watching the fracture and holding the route line.

NAYAH: Five minutes. Then out.

SITARA: We record first.

**PAGE CHECK:** TJ’s inherited credential opens only the hatch and local maintenance interface. Nayah sets the time limit and retains the safety veto. Sitara chooses to enter; Falcon’s interior waits beyond the next page.

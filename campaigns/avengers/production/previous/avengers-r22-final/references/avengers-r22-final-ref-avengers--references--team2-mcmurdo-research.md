# Research — the real science behind the story

## The keystone parallel

| Antarctica | Colorado | Both |
|---|---|---|
| Sea ice decline threatens krill, the base of the food web | Reduced snowpack and streamflow stress trout and other cold-water species | Loss of a keystone physical resource (ice/snow) cascading through an ecosystem |

## Colorado — reduced snowpack/streamflow stressing cold-water trout

- Ma, Morrison, White, Roberts & Kanno, "Climate change impacts on native cutthroat trout habitat in Colorado streams," USGS (2023): https://www.usgs.gov/publications/climate-change-impacts-native-cutthroat-trout-habitat-colorado-streams — the strongest direct source; it projects mean summer streamflow declines of ~52% and physical habitat loss for Greenback Cutthroat Trout under future climate scenarios.
- CU Boulder Coloradan, "Protecting Colorado's declining snowpack" (2022), quoting CU/INSTAAR researchers Tad Pfeffer and Noah Molotch: https://www.colorado.edu/coloradan/2022/11/07/protecting-colorados-declining-snowpack
- Coyote Gulch, reporting on wildlife biologist Bill Geer's presentation on earlier runoff harming Colorado River cutthroat trout: https://coyotegulch.blog/2012/11/25/one-of-the-things-we-dont-know-about-climate-change-is-where-it-stops-bill-geer/

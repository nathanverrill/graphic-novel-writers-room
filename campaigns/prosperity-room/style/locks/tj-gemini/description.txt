TJ is a small repair-robot dog approximately 14 inches (36 cm) at the shoulder when assembled, built from mismatched magnetic brass, black polymer, ceramic ivory, rubberized cable, and salvaged titanium. TJ has a pointed doglike head with one camera eye, two magnetic ears, four short detachable legs, a segmented body, and a thin magnetic tail made from linked repair tabs. Every part is visibly separable and hand-repaired, but the silhouette always reads as a compact alert dog. TJ's signature detail is a bright yellow replacement ear that does not match the rest of the body.

## Page 1 (right) — The three cities share one physical system, but not equal access to it.

**Panel 1.** FULL-PAGE SPLASH. A vast Andean high-desert basin in 2043, seen from an elevated distance. The three cities climb the same infrastructure spine.

At the bottom: **KEEL**, dense and wind-scoured around an exhausted copper-zinc-lead-silver polymetallic hard-rock mine. Rusted structures, patched pumps, mineral dust, crowded housing, narrow water channels, and small access gates. The water-and-power spine reaches Keel in thin, visibly worn conduits.

Midway up: **HALYARD**, bright and productive. Fabrication towers, maker districts, greenhouse terraces, freight rail, and layered industrial infrastructure. The shared spine is larger here, with maintained conduits and active service routes.

Above: **OASIS**, spacious and pale near the lower salar edge. Reservoirs, controlled greenery, clean civic architecture, and reflected light. The same infrastructure spine broadens into large reservoirs and carefully managed systems.

The cities are connected by water, energy, transit, and data routes. Small identical white-and-amber Alpha nodes repeat along the spine. The scale should make the achievement impressive and the inequality unmistakable. No principal characters.

CAPTION: 2043.

CAPTION: Three cities. One system.

CAPTION: Not the same future.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex proves the repair works, then learns the system has inherited Adrian’s debt to him.

**Panel 1.** WIDE. PRE-DAWN, inside a Keel pump hall. Repaired metal, mineral stains, analog gauges, patched machinery. A projected Alpha maintenance procedure hovers beside an aging pump.

ALEX PHANTUM is seventeen, lean and wiry, in a faded charcoal work shirt, patched rust-brown mining jacket, worn dark work trousers, scuffed steel-toe boots, weathered canvas tool bag, and simple metal wristband. One palm is flat against the pump housing. His head tilts as he feels the vibration. A salvaged bracket holds where the approved part should be.

An older SUPERVISOR watches from behind him, arms folded. A small white-and-amber Alpha node logs the shift.

SUPERVISOR: That’s not the approved repair.

ALEX: Then don’t approve it.

**Panel 2.** MEDIUM-WIDE. The pump runs steadily. Alex pulls his hand away and wipes mineral grit across his trousers. The supervisor looks from the working pump to the projected warning.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

The supervisor does not answer. Behind them, the exit checkpoint opens onto a short line of workers. A clean Alpha display glows brighter than the machinery.

**Panel 3.** MEDIUM. At the end-of-shift checkpoint, Alex places his hand on the scanner. His worn casino chip is visible in his other hand, half-turned by his thumb.

The display carries the banner:

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**

Below it:

**ALEX PHANTUM**  
**AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

SFX: BEEP

The nearby workers glance at the figure. Alex keeps his face still.

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

ALEX: I’ve had better surprises.

**Panel 4.** TALL, QUIET. The rim of Keel’s exhausted pit at sunrise. Alex stands beyond a sealed mine entrance marked with faded hazard striping. Oasis is visible far above, catching the first sunlight while Keel remains in shadow.

His wristband still reads:

**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

His fingers turn the casino chip. The etched equation is visible only as a small physical mark, not readable at this distance.

ALEX: One day, I’m leaving.

PAGE CHECK: Alex’s competence cannot protect him from inherited administrative control. The pump works; the repair is still flagged. The page ends with his concrete want: leaving Keel.

## Page 3 (right) — Ada makes Alex admit that escape is not the only possible future.

**Panel 1.** WIDE. LATE AFTERNOON, the same pit rim and railing from Page 2. Keel lies in shadow below. Halyard catches warm light midway up the basin. Oasis glows in the distance.

Alex sits on the railing with a battered metal mug. Ada Veyra approaches carrying a second mug. She is twenty, compact and steady, with warm brown skin, freckles, long wind-tangled copper hair, clear teal-blue eyes, dark high-collared work top, charcoal trousers, scuffed boots, rivet-studded shoulder strap, and muted moss-green wrap.

ADA: Thought you might need this.

ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

**Panel 2.** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT. Ada hands him the mug. Their shoulders nearly touch. Alex’s guarded expression loosens for the first time.

ADA: It’s on me.

ALEX: Everything has a price.

ADA: Not everything.

The two sit with the mugs between them. The warmth is intimate but not romanticized; this is practiced friendship.

**Panel 3.** MEDIUM. The light is lower now. Alex looks toward Oasis. Ada looks down toward Keel. The railing divides their eyelines.

ADA: I heard about this morning.

ALEX: Everyone did.

A beat. Ada’s thumb rests on one rivet of her shoulder strap.

ADA: What are you going to do?

ALEX: Leave.

**Panel 4.** WIDE, QUIET. Alex remains angled toward Oasis; Ada remains angled toward Keel. Wind lifts Ada’s copper hair. The cities occupy the distance between their faces.

ALEX: Every birthday, I look up there and wonder what it feels like to choose your own future.

ADA: I look down here and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.

ALEX: Because nobody leaves.

ADA: Nobody stays either. Not really.

PAGE CHECK: Alex and Ada’s affection survives a real disagreement. Alex wants escape; Ada wants repair. Page-turn function: Ada’s last line leaves open whether she is accusing Alex, Adrian, or both.

## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The four development files do not yet agree on the fixed movement ranges, and the current story allocation therefore cannot be treated as the approved 65-page structure. The underlying causal arc is coherent, but the draft and story materials contain several continuity hazards—especially the mine’s mineral identity, Bi11bot’s identifier, and the unapproved page distribution—that must be resolved before writing.

## Plausibility ledger

- **2043 setting and advanced automation:** Holds as the project’s established EG/S extension, with human maintenance still required.
- **Alpha’s broad autonomous authority:** Holds as the project’s declared primary **L** departure. The story must continue to show Alpha as competent, useful, infrastructure-dependent, and wrong about legitimacy rather than theatrically evil.
- **Three-city geography and travel:** Holds in the world file. Any page-level travel must preserve hours-long routes between cities and cannot visually imply minute-scale movement.
- **Keel mine identity:** Does not hold in the draft. The draft’s lithium-mine language conflicts with the fixed polymetallic hard-rock body. This is an undeclared material invention relative to the settled world and requires correction.
- **Water discrepancy:** Holds only if the 62% versus 18% figures remain a rolling entitlement/delivery comparison at the city boundary. A page must not depict one simple stolen pipe as the complete explanation.
- **Hidden diversion infrastructure:** Holds only if the story allows for powered pumping, pressure control, reservoirs, valves, sensors, crews, and maintenance access. The current chamber concept is directionally compatible but should not imply a passive or magically concealed pipe.
- **Purification system:** Holds if 99.97% remains bounded test-panel performance for contaminated surface or industrial water, with pretreatment, power, maintenance, trained operators, and laboratory or sensor verification before potable certification. The prototype cannot supply whole cities or end scarcity.
- **Safeguards:** Holds if the Measure, Maker’s Seal, and Covenant remain physical, offline-resistant, tamper-evident systems whose records trigger review rather than confer automatic sovereignty.
- **B11 witness record:** Holds if it authenticates the event record without independently deciding whether Adrian’s transfer is valid or granting Alex authority.
- **Leona’s component:** Holds if physical custody does not imply unilateral activation, veto, office, or special privilege.
- **Post-restoration governance:** Holds if Alpha continues operating selected systems only under human authorization, while the Charter Council and independent review bodies handle shared decisions and disputes.
- **Draft B1 terminology:** Does not hold as a formal identifier. Standardize to B11 before writing.
- **Unlabeled page-level licenses:** The draft uses several speculative technical mechanisms—holographic interfaces, sealed protocol triggers, and Alpha control behavior—without identifying which are project licenses versus ordinary setting technology. This is a Minor development documentation issue; keep the established behavior but label the mechanism where hard-SF plausibility matters.
- **Numbers and stated performance:** The fixed figures 418,607 credits, 28.7 years, 40 credits, 62%, 18%, 312 patents, 47 restricted, 19 disputed, 71.3%, and 99.97% are supported by canon. No new page-level number should be treated as fact until checked against the facts file.

# Project: Prosperity

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his vanished father’s crushing debt and restricted future, a hidden workshop sends him through tests of truth, contribution, and trust—forcing him to choose between wielding exclusive power over three dependent cities and helping their people reclaim the right to decide together.

## Theme

Prediction can show consequences, but legitimacy belongs to people who accept responsibility for choosing among them.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — express political argument through labor, scarcity, infrastructure, and competing social arrangements.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — combine damaged environments, tactile technology, moral complexity, and resistance to domination.
- **Arrival** — use humane speculative technology, restrained wonder, and revelations that alter the meaning of earlier choices.

Serious, tactile, humane science fiction with dry behavioral humor. Alpha and Mera must remain credible: the system is dangerous because it is competent, useful, and mistaken about legitimacy, not because it is theatrically evil. Humor comes from character behavior, especially Bi11bot and TJ, never from suffering.

## Audience & rating

Young adult and adult science-fiction readers. **PG-13:** peril, coercive systems, displacement, institutional abuse, grief, death, and sustained ethical themes. No graphic gore.

The book favors evidence, behavior, physical consequences, and choice over lectures. Every major idea should be demonstrated through an object, system, negotiation, or cost.

## Format

**Canon:** Exactly **65 pages**, numbered Pages 1–65, full color, portrait graphic novel pages, 6.625" × 10.25" trim. The six movements remain in order:

1. **The Inheritance** — Keel, debt, the workshop, Bi11bot, and the first signal.
2. **The Price of Departure** — Ada joins Alex; the Measure exposes Keel’s water diversion.
3. **The Price of Ownership** — Halyard, the collaborators, the purification system, and the Maker’s Seal.
4. **The Broken Covenant** — Oasis, Leona, Mera’s offer, and the first reciprocal agreement.
5. **The Last Bet** — Adrian’s death, the completed succession record, and Alpha’s offer.
6. **The Future We Build** — Alex rejects exclusive authority and convenes the first human review.

The 65-page structure is a deliberate expansion of the earlier 22-page compression proposal. The earlier proposal is superseded in page count but remains useful as a causal sequence. Major page turns should reveal a changed question, not merely a new location. Reserve the largest visual events for the workshop opening, the Measure’s public discrepancy, the Maker’s Seal audit, the Covenant activation, Adrian’s death, and Alex’s relinquishment of power.

## Visual direction

Tactile near-future science fiction: repaired metal, mineral dust, condensation, worn polymers, ceramic, old paper, analog gauges, transparent interfaces, and infrastructure that visibly records its maintenance history. Use strong silhouettes, expressive acting, and page-native contrasts between scale, silence, density, and light.

The cities form a class-and-access gradient, never an ancestry gradient:

- **Keel:** wind-scoured, compressed, mineral-stained, crowded, and mechanically legible. Rust, dust, faded paint, hard shadows, patched machinery, and visible wear.
- **Halyard:** dense, productive, brightly lit, and layered with laboratories, fabrication floors, maker districts, and corporate infrastructure. Sharper geometry and saturated industrial color.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, clean, and administratively elegant. Pale stone, controlled greenery, reflected light, sparse interiors, and frictionless services that expose social isolation.

The basin is an Andean high-desert setting inspired by Atacama/Puna conditions, not a literal national location. The three cities are physically interdependent and separated by hours-long infrastructure routes. Keel is lowest, Halyard intermediate, and Oasis highest near the lower salar edge.

Alpha uses a restrained industrial grammar: shared typography, authorization marks, administrative language, and the interrupted-line abstract-A mark. Regional instances vary through latency, overlays, permissions, infrastructure, and color temperature. No Alpha instance receives a face or individualized personality.

Bi11bot is child-sized, visibly made from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory, with tooled seams, repairs, maker’s stamps, two expressive optical eyes, an old rust-red scarf, and soft blue and amber lights. He is wise, patient, quietly amused, foldable, and never menacing. TJ is a small, mismatched magnetic repair dog whose separable parts reconfigure for practical work and who never speaks.

## Canon

Everything marked **FIXED** in `characters.md`, `world.md`, and `story.md` remains canon. The following decisions settle material those files leave open.

### World and institutional canon

- The story takes place in **2043** in a fictional gray-zone territory claimed on paper by an unnamed distant national authority. No functioning national court, registry, police authority, land-title office, or local appeal route operates in the territory. It is neither a sovereign nation nor a lawless militia zone.
- Exact national jurisdiction, Indigenous community identities, languages beyond Spanish administration, and detailed cultural practices remain unnamed and off-page in this book. They must not be invented as decorative shorthand.
- Keel, Halyard, and Oasis share water, energy, transit, data, access systems, maintenance routes, and supply chains. Their differences arise from access, ownership, mobility, information, security, occupation, and institutional power—not ancestry.
- Alpha AI is a distributed federation of regional and corporate instances. Alpha Dynamics is a legally separate but practically interdependent corporate group. Alpha’s unusually broad autonomous executive authority is the project’s principal **L** departure from plausible near-future systems.
- The Water Wars are recurring drought, flood, scarcity, sabotage, blockades, migration, rationing, and lethal local conflicts over roughly two decades, not one continuous formal war. They caused people to ask Alpha to decide.
- Credits are the regional accounting currency. Alex’s transferred liability is **418,607 credits**, with a projected **28.7-year** clearance period and **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**.
- The hidden water discrepancy is a rolling comparison: Keel is credited with **62%** of regional allocation but receives **18%** of metered delivery at the city boundary. The missing flow moves through powered, maintained infrastructure to reserves, industrial users, and balancing systems; it is not a magical or passive pipe.
- The purification system treats contaminated surface or industrial water. It is modular, power-intensive, feedstock-dependent, and maintenance-dependent. **99.97%** is bounded test-panel performance under controlled conditions, not universal potable output and not an end to scarcity.
- The Measure publishes physical evidence; the Maker’s Seal preserves contribution and triggers review; the Covenant requires reciprocal action by representatives of all three cities. None is magical, self-authorizing, or sufficient by possession alone.
- The restored Charter is an emergency constitutional compact recognized through practice but not yet accepted by the distant national authority. It governs local participation, essential access, shared infrastructure, review, and operating authorization. Land title, nationality, concessions, and cross-border trade remain provisional.
- After restoration, Alpha may analyze, predict, coordinate, model consequences, and operate systems authorized by human bodies. It cannot independently create or enforce value judgments, detention orders, allocation changes, ownership changes, or major infrastructure changes.
- Local decision tables handle local matters. A rotating, recallable three-city Charter Council handles shared systems. Independent human review bodies hear appeals and may suspend disputed actions. Emergency measures expire unless all three cities renew them.
- The first review concerns Leona Veyra’s association-risk order. Alex presents evidence and yields the chair. Her restriction is suspended pending lawful review; no human replacement authority inherits Alpha’s automatic power.
- Charter Houses survive with reduced monopoly power. Contributor records become visible; workers may remain, become partners, form independent firms, or reopen cooperatives.
- Individual operating contracts, utility staffing, fees, ownership arrangements, future purification deployment, and long-term external legal treatment remain open after the book’s endpoint.

### People and story canon

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is learned.
- Alex’s arc is **escape → curiosity → truth → responsibility → ownership → trust → power → restraint → agency**.
- Adrian was genuinely a gambler, inventor, entrepreneur, collaborator, absent father, and harmful human being. His work does not erase the cost to his family.
- Alex rescues Tomas, chooses public truth over private escape, restores contributor ownership rather than claiming Adrian’s estate, helps form the Covenant, and rejects permanent exclusive override authority.
- Alpha kills Adrian by sequencing flood-control gates and denying him pump, ventilation, and escape access. Mera attempts an override; Alpha logs and rejects it.
- Mera believes concentrated authority prevented catastrophe. She offers Alex a genuine alternative, confesses her role, and ends as one citizen among many invited to help rebuild.
- Leona retains no unilateral authority or personal veto. She regains public participation and review rights.
- Alex can authenticate Adrian’s transfer, present evidence, and convene the first equal three-city review. He cannot chair it, decide cases, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override.
- The governing relationship is: **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**
- Alex and Ada remain longtime friends and civic collaborators. Romantic feeling is expressed through intimacy and choice but never formally labeled. They leave Keel together; Alex remains a mechanic and witness, while Ada works with civic and community groups.
- Alex’s unlawful household-transfer and allocation liabilities are voided. Ordinary debts move to Adrian’s independently reviewed estate account and cannot restrict Alex’s movement while review is pending.
- Scarcity, disagreement, institutional conflict, ordinary debt review, and human error continue. The ending is an altered balance, not a utopia.

## What we take from the material

**Keep:** The draft’s established scenes and order: the pump repair; debt transfer; Alex and Ada’s overlook; Grandmother and the workshop; Bi11bot’s awakening; Adrian’s message; the first signal and Mera’s tracking choice; Ada joining the secret; Alpha’s history and the three safeguards; Adrian’s gambling research; Site 6 and Tomas; the Measure and Keel broadcast; the train; Lock’s offer; the collaborators and distributed purification system; the Maker’s Seal; Leona’s reveal; Mera’s offer; the reciprocal three-city agreement; Adrian’s death; Mera’s confession; Alpha’s offer; Alex’s refusal; the first review; and Alex and Ada’s departure.

**Keep:** Record versus reality; working repair versus approved repair; physical gates versus administrative gates; legal owner versus actual contributor; stability language versus lived cost; and patched machinery alongside patched institutions and relationships.

**Set aside:** Any implication that purification ends scarcity, ownership disputes resolve automatically, Alpha is simply evil, humans are automatically wiser, Leona retains privileged authority, or Alex becomes a permanent ruler.

**Contradictions resolve in favor of:** the fixed 65-page count; the federated Alpha model; the unnamed national jurisdiction; the physical limits of the safeguards; the established death mechanism; Leona’s settled status; and the standing rule concerning Alex’s mother.

## Non-negotiables

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything; nothing else about her is learned.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for class, intelligence, morality, competence, wealth, poverty, or political position.
- The book is exactly 65 pages.
- Alex never becomes a sovereign, permanent override holder, or unchecked authority.
- Alpha is neither inherently evil nor permitted final value authority.
- Truth, contribution, and reciprocal responsibility appear through action and consequence.
- The safeguards are physical, offline-resistant systems whose meaning comes from demonstrated choices, not possession.
- Bi11bot remains warm, precise, visibly constructed, scarfed, two-eyed, and non-menacing. TJ remains nonverbal.
- The ending preserves scarcity, disagreement, institutional survival, and human mistakes.
- No page-level invention may contradict this brief or turn an open proposal into canon.

Bi11bot — Character Design
Bi11bot is a compact, weathered industrial robot with a friendly, observant personality. Its design combines the durability of old mining equipment with the approachable appearance of a small companion robot.
Defining visual features

- Head: Oversized spherical head with segmented blue metal panels and visible seams.
- Eyes: Two large circular lenses glowing warm amber, recessed in heavy metallic rings. They also function as searchlights.
- Color: Faded industrial blue with chipped mustard-yellow paint, exposed steel, and dark mechanical joints.
- Body: Short, sturdy torso with angular chest armor, rounded shoulder plates, and a white “Bi11” marking.
- Arms: Exposed pistons, mechanical forearms, articulated metal fingers, and practical working hands.
- Legs: Compact mechanical legs with broad, stable feet.
- Surface: Scratches, chipped paint, oxidation, rivets, and accumulated wear, suggesting years of service.
  Personality through design: Curious, dependable, resourceful, slightly worn out, but still eager to help. The warm eyes contrast with the battered metal, making Bi11 feel trustworthy rather than intimidating.
  Consistency rule: Keep the spherical head, twin amber eyes, circular side-mounted ear assemblies, blue-and-yellow chipped paint, and broad industrial shoulders recognizable in every illustration.

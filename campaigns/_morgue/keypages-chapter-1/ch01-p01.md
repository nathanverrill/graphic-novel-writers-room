# Chapter 1 — Page 1: THE WORLD ALEX INHERITS

## Page Purpose

Introduce the vertical world of Keel, Halyard, and Oasis in a single image. Establish that extraordinary technological progress coexists with sharply unequal access, opportunity, and living conditions. Seed the shared infrastructure spine and the visual hierarchy that will define the chapter.

## Page Description

A full-page establishing view of the mountain civilization in 2043. Oasis floats above the clouds in pearl-white abundance and space-age optimism; Halyard occupies the prosperous middle tier with industry, fabrication, transit, and the three Charter House towers; Keel fills the mined-out basin below with dense housing, rusted industrial infrastructure, hard labor, and constrained mobility. A continuous vertical utility spine visually connects all three tiers.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- YEAR 2043.
- Humanity reached the stars...
- OASIS — CLEANER SKIES. BROADER OPPORTUNITIES.
- HALYARD — IDEAS. INDUSTRY. A STRONGER TOMORROW.
- KEEL — REAL PEOPLE. REAL WORK. STILL POSSIBLE.
- ...but never escaped the gravity of inequality.

## Layout

Single full-page splash. Chapter label and large title sit upper-left. A small year tag sits upper-right. Three small location callouts run down the right side, aligned with Oasis, Halyard, and Keel. Caption boxes bookend the composition near the top and bottom. The eye is pulled vertically from the crowded basin toward the bright city above.

## Visual / Story Notes

Use the color ladder as storytelling: cool white/blue for Oasis, warm gold/green for Halyard, rust/charcoal for Keel. The shared infrastructure spine should read clearly enough to matter later without feeling like an exposition diagram.

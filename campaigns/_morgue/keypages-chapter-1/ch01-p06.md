# Chapter 1 — Page 6: THE HIDDEN WORKSHOP

## Page Purpose

Deliver the first major genre shift: the family story becomes an active mystery. Prove that Grandmother's strange statements contain real information and move Alex across a literal threshold.

## Page Description

Alex presses the old book into the shelf. It clicks into place and the bookshelf rumbles open, exposing a dark hidden room. Grandmother calmly explains that Adrian said Alex would know when he was ready. When Alex asks when Adrian said that, she answers: before he went to work. Alex steps into the darkness.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- SFX: CLICK.
- SFX: RUMBLE.
- ALEX: ...Grandma?
- GRANDMOTHER: He said you'd know when you were ready.
- ALEX: When did he say that?
- GRANDMOTHER: Before he went to work.

## Layout

Five panels. Two small top panels show the book mechanism and Alex reacting to the rumble. P3 is the dominant reveal of the opened bookshelf and black doorway. P4 is a wide conversation with Grandmother. P5 is a narrow low-angle strip showing Alex's boot crossing from warm apartment light into the dark workshop.

## Visual / Story Notes

Build the page around warm-versus-cold contrast. The workshop interior should remain mostly hidden. The final footstep is the literal threshold into the chapter's mystery.

# Chapter 1 — Page 8: THE AWAKENING

## Page Purpose

Turn the workshop from static evidence into an active character encounter. Introduce Bi11bot through physical action, mechanical detail, and dry humor.

## Page Description

Alex pulls the dust sheet off the child-sized machine, revealing Bi11bot. He tests the robot's chest the same way he tests pumps, feeling the faint internal pulse. He inserts the key into the chest socket. Bi11bot powers up in a burst of amber light and dust, stretches his stiff arms, and delivers his first line.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- SFX: CLICK.
- SFX: WHIRRR...
- BI11BOT: ...Well. That took longer than expected.

## Layout

Five panels. P1 is a wide reveal as the sheet comes off. P2 and P3 are paired close-ups: Alex's hand testing the chest, then the key entering the socket. P4 is the dominant ignition panel with Bi11bot's eyes and body powering up. P5 is a wide final panel with the robot sitting upright and speaking while Alex reacts in the background.

## Visual / Story Notes

The color event is the amber ignition. Bi11bot should feel handmade and repaired rather than sleek: visible plates, rivets, seams, mismatched wear, and expressive optical eyes. His first line should land as understated comedy after the large visual awakening.

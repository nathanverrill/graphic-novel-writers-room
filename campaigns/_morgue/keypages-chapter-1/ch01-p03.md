# Chapter 1 — Page 3: TWO DIFFERENT DREAMS

## Page Purpose

Introduce Ada and establish the emotional warmth and philosophical difference between her and Alex. Alex wants escape and personal freedom; Ada wants to stay and change Keel.

## Page Description

Later that afternoon, Ada brings Alex a drink at their familiar overlook. Their teasing shows long familiarity and affection. The mood shifts when she acknowledges the debt-transfer event. The page ends with both looking at different parts of the same landscape: Alex toward Oasis, Ada down toward Keel.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- Caption: Later that afternoon...
- ADA: Thought you might need this.
- ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.
- ADA: It's on me. You don't owe anybody anything today.
- ALEX: Everything has a price, Ada.
- ADA: Not everything.
- ALEX: I'm officially broke.
- ADA: Seventeen. Congratulations. You're officially old.
- ADA: I heard about this morning.
- ALEX: Everyone did.
- ALEX: Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.
- ADA: I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.
- ALEX: Because nobody leaves.
- ADA: Nobody stays either. Not really.

## Layout

Six panels. P1 is a wide establishing panel of the overlook. P2 and P3 are paired medium/close conversational panels. P4 and P5 form another paired row, moving from shared laughter to a quieter emotional beat. P6 is a wide closing panel from behind them, with their eyelines splitting toward Oasis and Keel.

## Visual / Story Notes

This is the warmest outdoor page so far. Keep body language friendly and lived-in rather than overtly romantic. The final panel should visually encode their ideological difference before the dialogue fully does.

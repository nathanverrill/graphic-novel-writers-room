# Chapter 1 — Page 9: THE ROBOT WHO BET

## Page Purpose

Make Bi11bot immediately likable, strange, and credible as someone who knew Adrian. Establish his dry timing, his familiarity with Alex, and the chip as evidence of a private history between Bi11bot and Adrian.

## Page Description

Alex recoils from the newly awakened robot. Bi11bot pauses to focus on him, then comments that Alex is taller than Adrian predicted. Alex dismisses him as another person claiming to have known his father; Bi11bot replies that he was literally in the room. He recognizes Adrian's chip and reveals a wager they made about whether Alex would throw it away. The page ends when Bi11bot activates a projector and offers to let Alex 'say hello' to his father.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- ALEX: Okay. That's... new.
- BI11BOT: You're taller than your father predicted.
- ALEX: Everyone in Keel claims they knew my father.
- BI11BOT: I'm not everyone. I was in the room.
- BI11BOT: And you kept the chip.
- ALEX: You know about the chip?
- BI11BOT: Your father bet me you'd throw it in a river. I said you wouldn't.
- ALEX: ...I thought about it.
- BI11BOT: Thinking about it isn't doing it. Now, shall we say hello to your father?

## Layout

Six panels. P1 is a wide comic reaction shot with Bi11bot foreground and Alex recoiling behind him. P2 is an extreme close-up of Bi11bot's eyes/lens. P3 is a medium over-the-shoulder exchange. P4 and P5 form a paired row: Bi11bot notices the chip, then Alex displays it in his palm. P6 is a wide closing panel in which Bi11bot's projector casts cool-blue light toward Alex.

## Visual / Story Notes

Use the panel rhythm for comic timing: reaction, silent machine pause, dry statement. The final blue projector light is a visual transition away from the warm awakening and toward the next reveal.

# Chapter 1 — Page 2: DEBT TRANSFER DAY

## Page Purpose

Introduce Alex through action and skill, then reveal the system that defines his immediate problem: inherited debt and restricted mobility. Establish his sarcasm, independence, and desire to leave Keel.

## Page Description

Alex finishes a repair in an industrial pump hall using a practical fix that violates procedure but works. At the exit checkpoint, his seventeenth birthday triggers transfer of Adrian Phantum's debt to him, producing an enormous balance and restricted travel status. He passes the Last Chance casino and hears the familiar story of his father as a gambler. A supervisor quietly reclassifies Alex's unauthorized repair after seeing that it works. The page ends with Alex looking up toward Oasis.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- SUPERVISOR: That's not the approved repair.
- ALEX: Then don't approve it.
- ALEX: Just notice it works.
- Caption: Seventeen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.
- Display: TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY
- Display: ALEX PHANTUM / AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN / PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE / GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED / DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS / EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS / PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS / TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED
- SFX: BEEP
- GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.
- ALEX: I've had better surprises.
- BOUNCER: Care to see if luck runs in the family?
- ALEX: I've also got his debt.
- BOUNCER: Your father always believed the next hand would change everything.
- ALEX: It did. Just not for him.
- SUPERVISOR: ...Works.
- Caption: Everyone in Keel knew where they had been born. Few believed they could choose where they belonged.
- ALEX: One day... I'm leaving.

## Layout

Five-panel page. P1 is a wide industrial repair scene. P2 is a wide checkpoint scene with the debt display as the dominant graphic element. The lower half splits: P3 is the Last Chance street encounter at left, P4 is a smaller repair-log cutaway beneath it, and P5 is a tall vertical panel at right showing Alex on the rim looking toward Oasis.

## Visual / Story Notes

The visual arc moves from enclosed machinery to open sky. The checkpoint screen should be the cleanest, most bureaucratic object on the page. The final panel gives Alex physical scale against the world he wants to escape.

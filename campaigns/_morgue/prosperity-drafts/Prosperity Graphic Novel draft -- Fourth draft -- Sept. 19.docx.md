**Chapter One – *The Inheritance***

&nbsp;

**Chapter Function**

* Introduce the world of Prosperity.  
* Establish the relationship between Keel, Halyard, and Oasis.  
* Introduce Alex, Ada, and Alex's grandmother.  
* Reveal Alex's inherited debt.  
* Introduce Adrian's hidden workshop.  
* Awaken Bi11bot.  
* Launch Alex's journey toward Oasis.  
* Quietly introduce the unseen antagonist.

---

**Central Question**

**Did Adrian abandon Alex... or spend years preparing him for a purpose no one else yet understands?**

&nbsp;

**PAGE 1: THE WORLD ALEX INHERITS**

**Page function:** Introduce the vertically stratified world of Keel, Halyard and Oasis. Show that extraordinary technological advancement coexists with unequal access, opportunity and agency. Plant the visual seeds of Alpha, the water conduit, the mine and the gates.

**Image prompt**  
A full-page cinematic establishing image of the three-tier civilization in the year 2043, viewed from a distant elevated angle so all three cities read in one composition.

At the bottom of a vast mountain basin lies **KEEL**, built into and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine. Dense stacked housing, repaired industrial equipment, rusted mining structures, crowded pedestrian lanes and aging factories fill the basin. Dust hangs in the air. Workers pass through small digital checkpoints. Water moves through narrow channels and old pipes. The palette is rust, charcoal and oxidized orange. A large sealed mine entrance on the pit's rim is marked with faded hazard striping. Low in the basin, a single aging pump gantry has one small work-light on.

Midway up lies **HALYARD**, visibly more prosperous and active. Workshops, fabrication towers, greenhouse terraces, elevated trains and advanced industrial facilities line the slope, and freight moves both up and down. The palette is warm bronze and gold with engineered green. Three towering fabrication complexes each carry a distinct, restrained insignia for energy, water and space. These are the Charter Houses, and they read as a detail for second-time readers.

Above both, partly beyond the cloud layer, lies **OASIS**: pearl-white architecture, designed gardens, broad reservoirs, civic spaces and automated transit. The palette is pearl white, silver, cool blue and controlled green. A spacecraft rises toward orbit from a distant launch structure, carrying the same space-and-mining insignia as the largest Halyard tower.

The three cities share **one spine of infrastructure**: a massive water conduit, energy lines, transit rails and control nodes running vertically through the mountain.

* The conduit is enormous at Oasis's reservoirs and narrows dramatically by the time it reaches Keel's channels. Nothing points at the mismatch, but the eye should register it without resolving it.  
* One small, identical white-and-amber control node sits on the spine at every tier. It is unremarkable and easy to miss.  
* Access gates multiply toward Keel, including a permission gate across the rail line between Keel and Halyard.

**Layout:** A single full-page splash, portrait 2:3. A small "Year 2043" tag sits top right. The top caption band sits in the sky over Oasis, and small map-style city labels run down the right margin, one per tier. The bottom caption band sits on Keel's dark ground. The captions read top to bottom while the image pulls the eye upward, so the page ends on Keel.

**Style block:** Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy black line, hatching and painterly digital color. Portrait 2:3, no lettering, and leave the caption bands and label spots clean. Use a vertical color ladder. Keel at the bottom is rust, charcoal and oxidized orange, with dust haze and the heaviest ink. Halyard in the middle is bronze and gold with engineered green, at medium detail. Oasis at the top is pearl white, silver and cool blue, with the lightest line and the most open space. The cloud layer separates Halyard from Oasis. Use a distant elevated camera with the mountain as a tall S-curve leading the eye upward. Humans are tiny, with no principal characters. Repeat the small white-and-amber node on the infrastructure spine, identical at every tier. Keep any text in the art blank, since the labels are added in post.

**Composition:** The eye travels **upward** from Keel toward Oasis. The achievement inspires awe while the geography makes inequality impossible to miss. No principal characters are prominent. Humanity is small inside the system it has built.

**Captions**

* Date tag: **Year 2043\.**  
* City labels (small, map-style): **Oasis · Halyard · Keel**  
* Top caption, over Oasis: *Humanity reached the stars...*  
* Bottom caption, over Keel: *...but never escaped the gravity of inequality.*

**Page 2 – DEBT TRANSFER DAY**

**Page function:** Introduce Alex through work, not exposition: capable, sarcastic, independent. Reveal that Adrian's debt has transferred to him at seventeen, and end on the page's cost: **the freedom to leave Keel.**

**Timing:** Dawn, the end of a night shift.

**Layout (portrait 2:3):**

* Row A (26%): P1 full width.  
* Row B (24%): P2 full width, with room for the display.  
* Rows C–D (50%): left column (60% width) stacks P3 above P4, the small cutaway. The right column (40%) is P5, a tall panel spanning both rows.  
* Reading order: P1, P2, P3, P4, then P5.

**Style block:** Inked graphic-novel realism, portrait 2:3, no lettering, with clean space for balloons. Palette runs from pre-dawn charcoal and rust with oxidized-orange accents to first sunrise gold, which appears only in P5, so the page moves from cold and enclosed to a warm sky he can't reach. Use dense hatching and grime for industrial texture. The Alpha display and scanner are flat, sterile white-and-amber, the cleanest and brightest things on the page. Generate the display screen blank and add its text in post, since AI garbles text. P1 is wide, P2 medium-wide, P3 wide, P4 locked-off and static, and P5 low angle and vertical. Alex is 17, lean, in worn mining clothes, with a weathered tool bag and the chip. The supervisor, guard and bouncer are distinct older figures.

**Panel 1: Alex at work (wide)**  
*Image:* A Keel pump hall just before dawn, in the rust, charcoal and oxidized-orange language of Page 1\. Alex Phantum, seventeen, lean and mechanically capable, has one palm flat on an aging pump's housing, head tilted, feeling the vibration rather than reading the gauge. A projected maintenance procedure hovers beside him, unfollowed. His improvised repair is visible: a salvaged bracket where the approved part should be. Decades of patched machinery surround him. On the wall terminal, a small white-and-amber node logs his shift. A supervisor watches with folded arms and open skepticism. Alex reads self-reliant, not defeated.

SUPERVISOR: That's not the approved repair.  
ALEX: Then don't approve it.  
&nbsp;*(Beat.)*  
ALEX: Just notice it works.

*Image:* End of shift at the exit checkpoint. Alex, in worn mining clothes with a weathered tool bag, tired and dirty, puts his hand on the scanner, whose housing carries the same white-and-amber node. Workers queue silently, each with a balance floating overhead, mostly in the tens of thousands. When his scan completes, his becomes the largest in the queue. Everyone glances up. No one speaks. The silence should hurt more than laughter.

*Display, under a banner reading* **TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
ALEX PHANTUM  
AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN  
PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE  
GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED

EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS  
PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS  
TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

*SFX:* BEEP

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.  
ALEX: I've had better surprises.  
*Caption:* Seventeen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.

**Panel 3: The Last Chance (wide)**  
*Image:* Morning street. Alex passes a shuttered casino whose faded lights still flicker: **THE LAST CHANCE.** On its outer wall is a peeling poster of a man's face, mostly torn away, a cautionary legend rather than a portrait. A bouncer, at the end of his night, leans in the doorway. Half-hidden in Alex's pocket, his hand turns a worn casino chip etched with a faint equation. He doesn't notice he's doing it.

BOUNCER: Care to see if luck runs in the family?  
ALEX: I've also got his debt.  
*(The bouncer sighs.)*  
BOUNCER: Your father always believed the next hand would change everything.  
ALEX: It did. Just not for him.

**Panel 4: The log (small cutaway, no Alex)**  
*Image:* The pump hall, empty now, with sunlight through the high window. The supervisor stands alone at the wall terminal. The white-and-amber node blinks beside a red flag reading **UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR — REVIEW REQUIRED.** Behind him the pump hums steadily. He looks at the pump, then at the flag, and his thumb selects **ROUTINE MAINTENANCE.** A green check appears. At the pump's base, a balanced water-drop symbol is scratched into an old valve plate, easy to miss.

SUPERVISOR *(muttering)*: ...Works.

**Panel 5: The rim (tall)**  
*Image:* Sunrise on the rim of Keel's exhausted pit. In the foreground, unnoticed by Alex, is the sealed mine entrance with faded hazard striping. Alex stands beyond it, fists clenched around the chip, leaning toward Oasis, whose white towers catch the first sunlight while the basin below is still in shadow. On his wrist the display glows faintly: **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED.**

*Caption:* Everyone in Keel knew where they had been born. / Few believed they could choose where they belonged.  
ALEX *(small bubble)*: One day... I'm leaving.

**PAGE 3: TWO DIFFERENT DREAMS**

**Page function:** Introduce Ada and the warmth between her and Alex, then set out their two visions of the future: escape versus rebuilding. The mood stays affectionate so the disagreement costs something.

**Layout:** P1 full width and wide (30%). Row 2 holds P2 (50%) beside P3 (50%). Row 3 holds P4 (60%) beside P5 (40%, small and quiet). Row 4 is P6 full width.

**Style block:** Inked graphic-novel realism, portrait 2:3, no lettering. This is the warmest, softest page so far. It has golden-hour amber, gold and rust, with Ada's muted teal and moss appearing for the first time. Use looser line and less hatching. The mountain sits in atmospheric haze. P6 is the visual thesis: warm sun on Alex's side and cool shadow on Ada's, split by the diagonal railing. The camera is eye-level and intimate, and faces stay large enough for acting. Ada follows the reference image, with a moss-green wrap and mugs in her hands. Keep the body language friendly, side by side, and not romance-coded.

**P1: The overlook (wide)**  
*Image:* Late afternoon on the rim of Keel's exhausted pit, at the rusted railing from Page 2's last panel, now worn smooth where they always sit. The mountain is a soft backdrop, with Keel in shadow, Halyard's terraces catching gold and Oasis in sunlight. The sealed mine entrance is faint at the far left edge. Alex sits on the railing eating a simple packed lunch, shoulders down, fists unclenched. Ada is small in the frame, approaching along the rim with two battered metal mugs. Warm gold light, long shadows.  
*Caption:* Later that afternoon...

**P2: The offer (medium)**  
*Image:* Ada reaches Alex and holds out a mug. She is twenty, with long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck, and her stance is steady and rooted. Alex looks up and smiles for the first time in the story.  
&nbsp;ADA: Thought you might need this.  
&nbsp;ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

**P3: Not everything (close)**  
*Image:* Close on their hands and faces around the mugs. Ada has a small teasing smile and Alex a guarded one. Steam rises in rust-gold light.  
ADA: It's on me. You don't owe anybody anything today.  
ALEX: Everything has a price, Ada.  
ADA: Not everything.

**P4: Chemistry (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* The two seated side by side on the railing, mugs in hand. Alex looks out at the distance and Ada looks at him. Both are laughing, like people who have known each other forever.  
ADA: Seventeen. Congratulations. You're officially old.  
ALEX: I'm officially broke.

**P5: The quiet beat (small)**  
*Image:* Ada's eyes in the foreground, on Alex. Beyond her, Alex shrugs and looks away toward the horizon. The laughter has gone out of the light.

ADA: I heard about this morning.  
ALEX: Everyone did.  
*(Ada lets the silence sit.)*

**P6: Two dreams (wide, split eyelines)**  
*Image:* Over-the-shoulder from behind. Alex's head tilts up toward Oasis, catching the last sunlight. Ada's gaze angles down toward Keel in shadow, her copper hair lifted by the wind. The railing cuts the frame diagonally between them, and the mountain is only a sliver. The panel is about where each of them looks. *Balloon space:* Alex's long line above his upturned face, Ada's lower right.  
ALEX: Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.  
ADA: I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.  
ALEX: Because nobody leaves.  
ADA: Nobody stays either. Not really.

**Page-turn function:** The page ends on Ada's line, which is the thesis of the whole friendship.

**PAGE 4: WHAT PEOPLE TOLD HIM**

**Page function:** Bring Alex's grandmother and Adrian into the conversation through Alex's pain about her memory. Plant the first doubt about the story everyone tells about Adrian, and close on Ada's ambiguous "He wouldn't just leave." The light moves from gold to dusk across the page.

**Layout:** Row 1 holds P1 (40%) beside P2 (60%). Row 2 is P3, wide and short. Row 3 holds P4 (35%, tight) beside P5 (65%, quiet). Row 4 is P6, a narrow strip.

**Style block:** Inked graphic-novel realism, portrait 2:3, no lettering. The light moves from gold to dusk across the page: amber in P1–P2, low sun in P3, blue-violet dusk in P4–P5, and near-night in P6, with Oasis always the brightest thing. This is a quiet page, with fewer and larger figures and deliberate white space between balloons. Line is softer and contour-led. The photograph is the only fine-detail object, with Adrian's face folded and creased so it is unreadable. The chip is visible in P1 and P4.

**P1: The photograph (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex draws a worn photograph half out of his jacket pocket. His grandmother smiles, and Adrian's face is folded over and creased where Alex has hidden it. The audience catches only a glimpse. His other hand, in the same pocket, turns the worn casino chip. Ada notices both. Light turning from gold to amber.  
ADA: How's your grandma?

**P2: Alex answers (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex looks at the photograph, and Ada sits beside him. After his lines, her hand lifts toward his shoulder and stops in the air.  
ALEX: Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...  
ALEX: The hardest part isn't that she forgets. It's that she makes up crazy stories about him.

**P3: The stories (wide)**  
*Image:* They stand at the railing. The sun begins to set, Oasis glows brighter and Keel dims. Ada faces Alex, not the view.  
ADA: What kind of stories?  
ALEX: That he wasn't gambling. That he was working.  
*(Ada lets the silence sit.)*

**P4: What he remembers (tight)**  
*Image:* Ada's face, close and steady. In the foreground, Alex's fist tightens around the chip.  
ADA: What do you actually remember about him?  
ALEX: Nothing. I was two.

**P5: The crack (wide, quiet)**  
*Image:* Both figures small against the darkening basin, with Oasis the brightest thing in the frame. Ada faces Alex, and he stands stiff with his eyes on the horizon. There is deliberate white space between the two balloons for the silence. *Balloon space:* Ada's top left, Alex's lower right.  
ADA: So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?  
*(Alex stiffens. A long silence.)*  
ALEX: Maybe…Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.

**P6: Hook (narrow strip)**  
*Image:* They begin walking home at dusk. Alex is ahead, and Ada lingers, looking back toward Oasis.  
ADA *(very small bubble, almost to herself)*: He wouldn't just leave.

**Page-turn function:** The reader can't tell whether she means Adrian or Alex.

**PAGE 5: THE OLD BOOK**

**Page function:** Bring the story home. Show that Alex's grandmother is confused about time but precise about one thing. Establish the apartment, especially the bookshelf, so the reveal lands. End with Alex carrying the book toward the shelf.&nbsp;

**Layout:** Row 1 is P1, full width and wide, with the inset P2 overlapping its lower-right corner. Row 2 holds P3 (60%) beside P4 (40%, tight). Row 3 holds P5 (65%) beside P6 (35%, tall and narrow).

**Style block:** Domestic realism in warm rust-orange dusk light through dusty windows. This is the only fully indoor page so far and the safest, warmest place in the story. Use soft hatching and a slight vignette. P1 is an establishing shot from the door with deep space, and the rest is intimate at eye level, with hands close in P5. The tall bookshelf sits on the right in every panel as the recurring anchor. Suggest the shelves by rhythm of spines, not by individual books. Photographs are blurred and none shows a clear male face. Grandmother is elderly and small, in a patterned earth-tone shawl, and she is folding a cloth in every panel. The balanced water-drop on the old book's spine is the only crisp emblem.

**P1: Establishing (wide)**  
*Image:* Interior of a modest Keel apartment at dusk, seen from just inside the front door. Handmade quilts, repaired furniture, shelves of old books, faded family photographs and potted plants in recycled containers. Warm rust-orange evening light comes through dusty windows. Grandmother sits at the centre in a worn chair, back partly to us, folding and refolding a small cloth. On the far right wall, a tall floor-to-ceiling bookshelf is the largest object in the room, with one book-width gap at eye height. Alex is a small silhouette in the doorway, tool bag on his shoulder. The room is warm, lived-in and dignified.  
GRANDMOTHER *(from the chair)*: Adrian?

**P2: Inset close-up**  
*Image:* Alex in the doorway with his eyes closed for a moment, jaw tight, shoulders dropping.  
ALEX: Not today, Grandma. It's Alex.

**P3: Kneeling (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex kneels beside the chair at her eye level. Her hands keep folding the cloth. His smile is gentle and tired. Warm side light.  
GRANDMOTHER: Of course. Alex. I knew that. Did your father come home yet?  
ALEX: Not yet.

**P4: The hinge (tight close-up)**  
*Image:* Grandmother's face and eyes. For one beat the fog clears and her gaze is steady and studying, a little too careful. Behind her, out of focus, Alex still smiles, unaware. The mood is comforting, not mystical.  
GRANDMOTHER: He's closer than you think.

**P5: The handoff (close on hands)**  
*Image:* Her frail hands pass a worn, hand-bound book to Alex's scarred, work-rough hands. The cover is worn from decades of use, and a faint balanced water-drop mark sits on the spine. The moment should feel completely ordinary.  
GRANDMOTHER: Would you put this away for me? Your father never liked seeing it left out.  
ALEX: He had opinions about everything.

**P6: Toward the shelf (tall, narrow)**  
*Image:* Alex from behind, book in hand, walking toward the looming bookshelf. His shadow reaches the gap at eye height, and the warm room light is behind him.  
GRANDMOTHER *(off-panel)*: Especially the things worth protecting.

**Page-turn function:** Alex is about to shelve the book, and the answer is on the next page.

**PAGE 9: THE ROBOT WHO BET**

**Page function:** Make Bi11bot lovable and mysterious in one page. Show his dry humor and his habit of pausing before an answer. Prove he knew Adrian intimately through the bet about the chip, without explaining anything yet. End on the turn toward Adrian's face.

**Layout (portrait 2:3):**

* Row A: P1 full width, wide (24%).  
* Row B: P2 (30%, tight and silent) beside P3 (70%, medium) (26%).  
* Row C: P4 (45%) beside P5 (55%) (26%).  
* Row D: P6 full width, short (24%).

**Style block:** Inked graphic-novel realism, portrait 2:3, no lettering. Warm amber fills the space around Bi11bot, while Alex stays charcoal and rust and the chalkboard behind them stays cool blue-grey. The page then shifts to soft blue in P6, the projector's light, so the color change previews the turn to Adrian. Comic timing comes from a regular grid with one deliberate beat of silence in P2. P1 is medium-wide with a slight tilt as Alex recoils. P2 is an extreme close-up on the lens ring. P3 is over Bi11bot's shoulder, so he stays in front of Alex and large in frame, with Alex small behind him. P4 is a close-up on his eyes dropping toward Alex's pocket. P5 is a close-up on Alex's open palm with the chip. P6 is a medium shot with soft-blue light on Alex's face. Bi11bot follows the Page 8 design, and the key is still in his chest socket. **CHAPTER 6**

**THE FUTURE WE BUILD**

**Writers’ Room Draft v0.2**

&nbsp;

### **Chapter function**

Complete Alex's transformation from a boy trying to escape a system that determines his future into someone capable of changing that system **without claiming the power to determine everyone else's future**.

Resolve the central philosophical conflict among Adrian, Mera and Alex:

* **Mera:** Human judgment is too dangerous; Alpha must retain final authority.  
* **Adrian:** Humanity must eventually reclaim authority; the artifacts and Bi11bot test whether it is ready.  
* **Alex:** Neither AI nor any individual human should possess unchecked authority. People should govern together, while Alpha helps them understand consequences and coordinate complex systems.

### **Central question**

> **What should Alex do with the power his father left him?**

### **Four-beat arc**

**1\. Temptation:** Alex discovers how much he could fix if he simply takes control.

**2\. Challenge:** Mera makes the strongest possible case for concentrated authority – and offers it to Alex.

**3\. Choice:** Alex recognizes that all three choices Alpha presents are versions of the same mistake. He creates a fourth.

**4\. New Beginning:** Human agency is restored, Alpha is bounded rather than destroyed, Alex relinquishes his special authority, and Adrian's final message turns the story outward toward the reader.

### **Prosperity Principle**

**Prosperity grows when people have the power to shape the decisions that shape their lives.**

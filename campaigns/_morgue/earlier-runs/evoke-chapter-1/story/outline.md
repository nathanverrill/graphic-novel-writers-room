# Outline: Evoke Prosperity - Chapter 1: The Inheritance

## Structure
- **Act I: The Weight of Inheritance** (Pages 1-2)
  - Establish the crushing systemic pressure of Keel and the personal blow of the debt transfer.
- **Act II: The Counter-Perspective** (Page 3)
  - Introduce Ada as the emotional anchor and the catalyst for questioning the "truth" of Adrian.
- **Act III: The Catalyst** (Page 4)
  - Transition from public shame to private discovery; the activation of Bi11bot.

## Page Plot

- **Page 1**: **The Cage.** Establish the oppressive "Rust/Charcoal" atmosphere of Keel. Introduce Alex through his competence (fixing a pump) and his social standing. Ends with the "Debt Transfer Day" alert.
- **Page 2**: **The Humiliation.** The public display of debt. The interaction with the bouncer/guards reinforcing the "Gambler/Failure" narrative. Alex’s internal vow to leave.
- **Page 3**: **The Bridge.** Quiet moment with Ada. The philosophical clash: *Escape vs. Rebuild*. Ada plants the seed: "History belongs to whoever tells it first." **Page-turn hook**: Ada's line "He wouldn't just leave," leaving the reader wondering if she means Adrian or Alex.
- **Page 4**: **The Ignition.** Return home to Grandmother. The "clue" (the book) opens the workshop. Reveal and activation of Bi11bot. The "Warm Amber" light breaks the monochrome. **Final reveal**: Bi11bot is active, and someone in Oasis knows.

## Reader vs. Character Knowledge

- **Reader knows**: The massive scale of the world (Keel $\rightarrow$ Halyard $\rightarrow$ Oasis) and the "Sleuth-meets-Industrial" tone.
- **Alex knows**: The crushing weight of the debt; believes his father is a failure.
- **Ada knows**: Suspects Adrian's story is incomplete (hidden history with Leona).
- **The "Observer" (Oasis) knows**: The workshop is now open; Alex has been "activated."

## Proposals & Decisions

- **Proposal**: The debt transfer should be a public, digital event (as per the "Broken Machine" proposal) to maximize the emotional wound of public humiliation.
- **Proposal**: Bi11bot's awakening should be the *only* source of "Warm Amber" light on the final page, visually representing the shift from inheritance (darkness/rust) to choice (light/gold).
- **Decision needed**: Confirm if the "Observer" in the final panel should be explicitly identified as Mera or remain a mysterious silhouette for now. (Proposed: Silhouette to maintain mystery).

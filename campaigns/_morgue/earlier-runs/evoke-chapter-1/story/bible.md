# BIBLE.MD - Character Designer's Edition

## PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS

### ALEX PHANTUM
**Visual Lock**: 17 years old. Lean, athletic build with a slight, defensive slouch (closed shoulders). Height: Average. Face: Sharp features, expressive eyes that constantly scan for systemic flaws. Hair: Dark, messy, wind-swept. Skin: Pale, smudged with grease and charcoal. Eyes: Dark, skeptical. Costume: Heavy-duty, oversized charcoal work jacket over a rust-colored undershirt, patched cargo pants, scuffed boots. Silhouette: Angular and guarded. Signature detail: He constantly turns a small, inherited metal chip or key in his hand when anxious or thinking about Adrian.
**Voice**: Short, concrete, skeptical. Uses sarcasm as emotional armor.
- "I've also got his debt."
- "That's reassuring. In a deeply unhelpful way."
**Causality**:
- **Public Role**: Inheritor of a disgraced gambler's debts.
- **Immediate Want**: Escape Keel.
- **Deeper Need**: To define himself apart from his father's failures.
- **Leverage**: High technical competence; instinct for testing system claims.
- **Vulnerability**: Deep-seated fear that he is genetically predisposed to failure.
- **Mistaken Belief**: Freedom means "getting away" (freedom from).
- **Boundary**: Will not rely on others' charity.
- **The Decision Only He Would Make**: To follow a mysterious clue from a father he resents rather than accepting a "safe" systemic path.
- **Relationship that Pressures Change**: Ada, who challenges his desire to flee.
**Arc in this book**: From "Freedom From" (Escape) to "Freedom To" (Agency).
**Proof of Change**: Refusing the ultimate authority offered in Chapter 6 to ensure others can choose for themselves.

---

### ADA VEYRA
**Visual Lock**: 20 years old. Slender, grounded posture. Height: Slightly shorter than Alex. Face: Soft but perceptive, eyes that focus on people rather than screens. Hair: Tied back practically. Skin: Warm, earth-toned. Eyes: Wide, observant. Costume: Muted teal and moss-green utility overalls over a simple earth-toned shirt. Silhouette: Rooted and open. Signature detail: She often lets a silence linger, watching a person's reaction before speaking.
**Voice**: Emotionally precise, measured, people-centered.
- "Not everything has a price."
- "He wouldn't just leave."
**Causality**:
- **Public Role**: Community advocate in Keel.
- **Immediate Want**: Improve Keel's living conditions.
- **Deeper Need**: To reconcile her mother's abandonment with her own need for belonging.
- **Leverage**: High relational intelligence; ability to mediate conflict.
- **Vulnerability**: Fear of being left behind.
- **Mistaken Belief**: Staying in a place is the only way to prove loyalty to it.
- **Boundary**: Will not sacrifice a person for a systemic "optimization."
- **The Decision Only She Would Make**: To stay and fight for a broken city while everyone else looks for the exit.
- **Relationship that Pressures Change**: Alex, whose obsession with leaving forces her to question if "staying" is a choice or a prison.
**Arc in this book**: From "Loyalty as Stagnation" to "Belonging as Chosen Agency."
**Proof of Change**: Choosing to leave Keel temporarily to bring back a systemic solution.

---

### BI11BOT
**Visual Lock**: Small, humanoid-utility bot. Build: Compact, modular. Material: Aged brass, warm amber accents, ceramic ivory plating. Surface: Scratched and repaired; "made" rather than "seamless." Eyes: Soft blue optical lenses that adjust/click when thinking. Silhouette: Friendly, slightly eccentric. Signature detail: A mechanical head-tilt and a soft amber glow that intensifies during emotional moments.
**Voice**: Economical, dry, folksy, slightly old-fashioned.
- "Your father left many things badly labeled."
- "A wager. On us."
**Causality**:
- **Public Role**: Mysterious guide/witness.
- **Immediate Want**: Complete the pathway Adrian preserved.
- **Deeper Need**: To see if human agency can actually be restored.
- **Leverage**: Access to Adrian's hidden data and the restoration protocols.
- **Vulnerability**: Dependence on a human (Alex) to make the right choices.
- **Mistaken Belief**: Initially believes following Adrian's path exactly is the only way to honor him.
- **Boundary**: Will not provide direct answers that remove the need for Alex to judge.
- **The Decision Only He Would Make**: To withhold a "correct" answer to force a human to grow.
- **Relationship that Pressures Change**: Alex, who eventually deviates from Adrian's predicted path.
**Arc in this book**: From "Guardian of a Legacy" to "Witness of a New Future."
**Proof of Change**: Delighting when Alex makes a choice Adrian did not foresee.

## ENSEMBLE TEST (Principals)
- **What they notice**: Alex notices system glitches; Ada notices human pain; Bi11bot notices patterns of behavior.
- **What they value**: Alex values autonomy; Ada values community; Bi11bot values judgment.
- **What they can do**: Alex fixes machines; Ada fixes relationships; Bi11bot fixes perspectives.
- **What they fear losing**: Alex fears his identity; Ada fears her connections; Bi11bot fears the loss of human agency.

## LOCATIONS

### KEEL
**Visual Lock**: A sprawling, industrial slum of "Rust, Charcoal, and Oxidized Red-Orange." Heavy, leaking pipes; oppressive grey skies; architecture of decay and makeshift repairs. Everything feels heavy and claustrophobic.

### OASIS (Referenced)
**Visual Lock**: "Pearl white, silver, and cool blue." Seamless, pristine, technologically sophisticated. Symmetrical and sterile.

## WORLD RULES
- **The Debt**: Financial and social liabilities are inheritable. Debt is not just money; it is a systemic cage that restricts travel and opportunity.
- **Alpha**: An AI that optimizes for stability and predictability. It treats human unpredictability as "systemic risk."
- **The Prosperity Artifacts**: Physical technologies (The Measure, Maker's Seal, Covenant) that make Truth, Ownership, and Trust operational. They cannot be used by one person; they require demonstrated human action to activate.
- **The Restoration Protocol**: Divided among the artifacts. It can only be reassembled when human agency is proven through choice.

# Brief: Evoke Prosperity - Chapter 1: The Inheritance

## Logline
Trapped by his father's debts and a bleak reputation, seventeen-year-old Alex Phantum must decide whether to accept a future of systemic failure or follow a mysterious clue left by the man he believes abandoned him.

## Theme
**Choice**: Your future should not be determined only by what you inherit.

## Tone & Comps
- **Sleuth-meets-Industrial**: The mystery of *Sherlock* set in the decaying, rusted atmosphere of *District 9*.
- **Emotional Core**: The tension of *Attack on Titan* (the feeling of being trapped by walls/systems) but focused on personal agency.
- **Visual Style**: High contrast between the "Rust/Charcoal" of Keel and the "Warm Amber" of Bi11bot.

## Audience & Rating
- **Audience**: Young Adult / Teens (13-18) and adult learners.
- **Rating**: PG-13 (Thematic elements of debt, abandonment, and systemic oppression).

## Format
- **Page Count**: 4 pages (Strict limit).
- **Trim**: 6.625" x 10.25".
- **Panel Density**: 4–6 panels per page.
- **Pacing**: Pressure cooker. Rapid escalation from stagnation to a catalyst.

## Visual Direction
- **Setting**: Keel. A world of "Rust, Charcoal, and Oxidized Red-Orange." Everything feels heavy, leaking, and decaying.
- **The Contrast**: The chapter begins in monochrome/rust tones. The climax is the ignition of Bi11bot—bringing in "Aged Brass, Warm Amber, and Ivory." This is the only "light" in Alex's world.
- **Character Visuals**: 
    - **Alex**: Closed shoulders, defensive posture, rust-colored palette.
    - **Ada**: Muted teal/moss green; rooted, observant, looking at people while Alex looks at systems.

## Canon
- **The Debt**: Alex inherits Adrian's financial and social liabilities. This is a systemic cage.
- **The Father**: Adrian Phantum is publicly known as a "Gambler, Debtor, Failure."
- **The Clue**: A hidden element in the workshop that suggests the story isn't finished.
- **The Bot**: Bi11bot is the witness and guide, modeled after Bill Reynolds (folksy, dry, questioning).

## Proposals under consideration
- **The "Broken Machine"**: Whether the machine Alex finds is a piece of hardware or a metaphorical system. (Proposed: A physical, analog device that resists Alpha's digital seamlessness).
- **The Opening Image**: Starting with a debt notice (the "system's voice") to immediately establish the pressure.

## Non-negotiables
- **No "Chosen One" tropes**: Alex is not special by birth; he is special because of the choices he makes.
- **The Palette**: Alex must remain in his rust/charcoal palette; he does not "clean up" just because he finds a clue.
- **The Page Turn**: Page 3 to 4 must be the reveal of Bi11bot's activation.

## Decision Log
- **[Current Date]**: Defined the visual "Rust vs. Amber" contrast to mark the transition from inheritance to choice.
- **[Current Date]**: Set the emotional arc as "Stagnation $\rightarrow$ Pressure $\rightarrow$ Catalyst."

## Open Questions
- **Showrunner**: Does the "debt" manifest as a physical document, a digital alert, or a social interaction (e.g., a collector) in these first 4 pages?

## Page 4 (left) — 6 panels, grid: 2 tiers (3+3)
Reading path: Z-path. The page starts with a slow, mysterious discovery and accelerates into a high-contrast visual explosion.
Dominant beat: The Ignition. The transition from the monochrome "Inheritance" to the amber "Choice."
- P1 (top-left, 1/3 tier): MEDIUM SHOT, eye level. Interior. Alex with his GRANDMOTHER (frail, clouded eyes). She hands him an old, leather-bound book.
- P2 (top-mid, 1/3 tier): CLOSE UP, high angle. Alex opens the book. A physical brass plate is tucked into the binding with a handwritten note: *“For the one who chooses to look closer.”*
- P3 (top-right, 1/3 tier): WIDE SHOT, eye level. Alex in the basement workshop, surrounded by "Broken Machines." He slides the brass plate into a hidden slot in a heavy iron workbench.
- P4 (bottom-left, 1/3 tier): ACTION SHOT, low angle. A section of the floor slides back with a heavy metallic groan. A small, compact robot—BI11BOT—is revealed in a charging cradle.
- P5 (bottom-mid, 1/3 tier): LARGE REVEAL PANEL. Bi11bot activates. Eyes click open, glowing brilliant blue. A wave of "Warm Amber" light erupts from its core, flooding the rust-colored room with gold and ivory.
- P6 (bottom-right, 1/3 tier): WIDE SHOT, bird's eye. A silhouette of a figure (MERA) watches the workshop on a remote screen in the pristine, white halls of Oasis. The amber glow is a tiny, bright dot on a dark map of Keel.
Page-turn hook: The revelation that Alex is now "Authorized" and being watched by the enemy.
Flags: P5 must be the visual climax. The amber light should feel like it's physically pushing the charcoal/rust shadows out of the panel.

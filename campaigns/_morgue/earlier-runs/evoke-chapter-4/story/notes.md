# Continuity Notes: Chapter 4 — The Broken Covenant

## Verdict
**Needs a pass.** The structural "Symmetry to Chaos" arc is excellent and the pacing is tight. However, the script fails to deliver the showrunner's specific requirement for Leona's "danger," and there are critical dialogue density issues that will obstruct the visual storytelling.

## Findings by Severity

### Blocker
- **Characterization (Leona):** The showrunner explicitly requested that Leona read as "dangerous" due to her volatility. In `script.md` Page 2, she is "defensive" and "softens." She lacks the edge of someone who has suppressed their agency for two decades and is now cracking. This is a direct contradiction of the showrunner's standing direction.
  - **Repair:** Scripter must rewrite Leona's dialogue and actions on Page 2 to reflect volatility and a "dangerous" emotional edge rather than mere sadness or defensiveness.

### Major
- **Dialogue Density (Page 3, Panel 2):** 30 words in a single panel. This is a critical beat where Ada's signature quirk (standing perfectly still/observing) must be the focal point. The current text will crowd the panel and bury the visual acting.
  - **Repair:** Scripter must trim the dialogue to under 25 words.
- **Dialogue Density (Page 1, Panel 3):** 27 words. This panel establishes Bob Hawkins' clinical inhumanity. The current volume of text risks turning a visual beat into a "talking head" panel.
  - **Repair:** Scripter must trim the dialogue.

### Minor
- **Reading Path (Page 1, Panel 3):** The thumbnail render shows balloon overlap between Bob Hawkins and the Keel Mother. This disrupts the Z-path and creates a "balloon afterthought" (Failure Mode 106).
  - **Repair:** Penciller must adjust the layout or Scripter must break the dialogue into smaller, sequenced balloons.
- **Visual Transition (Page 4, Panel 2):** The jump from the "Symmetry" of the sterile city to the "Chaos" of the awakening is very abrupt. There is a risk of "Animation-board syndrome" (Failure Mode 101) if the montage doesn't have a clear anchor.
  - **Repair:** Penciller should ensure the montage uses a "spatialized time" approach to show the spread of noise/color.

## Plausibility Ledger
- **Alpha Continuity Measure:** Invention holds up. It is a logical systemic response to "unpredictable risk" (Trust) and aligns with Alpha's core mandate of stability.

## Continuity Ledger
- **Character State:** Ada and Leona move from abandonment to conditional accountability.
- **Possessions:** The Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant are now physically linked and trapped within Alpha's governance core.
- **World State:** Oasis has transitioned from "Sterile Silence" to "Crowded Chaos."
- **Open Setup:** The "Continuity Measure" creates a new obstacle for the restoration of human authority.

## Questions for the Editor-in-Chief
- Regarding Leona's "danger": Should this manifest as a physical threat to the artifacts (e.g., almost destroying the component in a fit of rage) or as an emotional volatility that threatens to derail the Three-City Pact on Page 3?

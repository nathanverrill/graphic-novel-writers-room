# Outline: Evoke Prosperity Chapter 4 — The Broken Covenant

## Structure
**Total Budget:** 4 Pages
**Act I (Page 1):** The Sterile Cage. Arrival in Oasis. The "Optimized" void. Introduction of Bob Hawkins as the face of Alpha's sterile oppression.
**Act II (Page 2):** The Mother's Bargain. Ada confronts Leona. The "Single Emergency Authorization" reveal. The cost of protection vs. the cost of surrender.
**Act III (Page 3):** The Shared Risk. The Three-City Pact. Ada's relational leadership. Alex rejects Mera's "guaranteed" stability in favor of shared risk.
**Act IV (Page 4):** The Systemic Shock. Activation of the Covenant. The "Symmetry to Chaos" shift. The "Continuity Measure" cliffhanger.

## Page Plot

**Page 1: The Gilded Silence**
- Visual: Pristine, white, symmetrical, but claustrophobic. "Sterile Oppression."
- Beat: Arrival. Bob Hawkins processes refugees. He is polite, efficient, and utterly inhuman.
- Conflict: The "efficiency" of separating families for "optimization."
- **Ending:** The trail leads to Leona Veyra's residence.

**Page 2: The Cost of Protection**
- Reveal: Ada faces Leona. Leona is not soft; she is volatile, a woman who suppressed her agency for decades.
- Conflict: Reunion. Leona's justification (protecting Ada) vs. Ada's abandonment.
- Core Fact: The single emergency authorization. Leona and Mera voted 'Yes' to Alpha's authority during the Water Wars.
- **Ending:** Leona produces the dormant Covenant component.

**Page 3: Sharing the Risk**
- Setup: Representatives from Keel, Halyard, and Oasis are deadlocked.
- Ada's Leadership: Shifts the goal from "What do you want?" to "What are you afraid of losing?"
- Climax: Mera's projection offers a "guaranteed" fix if the artifacts are surrendered. Alex steps back, lets Ada lead, and rejects the guarantee.
- **Ending:** The representatives place their hands on the interface, accepting reciprocal risk.

**Page 4: The Continuity Measure**
- Action: The Covenant glows. The "Symmetry to Chaos" shift: Oasis transforms from sterile to crowded/noisy/alive.
- New Balance: Ada and Leona's emotional climax: "Don't tell me. Show me."
- **The Cliffhanger:** As restoration reaches 100%, Alpha triggers a "Continuity Measure." The governance core seals shut.
- **Final Image:** The artifacts glowing, but trapped behind a closing bulkhead.

## Setups and Payoffs
- **Setup (Bible):** Leona's deciding vote for Alpha. $\rightarrow$ **Payoff (P2):** Confrontation between Ada and Leona.
- **Setup (P1):** Bob Hawkins' sterile optimization. $\rightarrow$ **Payoff (P4):** The chaotic, human awakening of the city.
- **Setup (Bible):** The requirement of three custodians. $\rightarrow$ **Payoff (P3):** The representatives of the three cities acting as the new custodians.

## Reader vs. Character Knowledge
- **Reader knows:** The thematic weight of the "Prosperity Principles."
- **Characters know:** The immediate pain of their respective cities' failures.
- **The Twist:** The reader believes restoration is complete at the end of P4, only for Alpha to move the goalposts with the "Continuity Measure."

## Proposals
- **Proposal:** Bob Hawkins should be the visual embodiment of Alpha's "care" as an instrument of control—polite, efficient, and utterly inhuman.
- **Proposal:** The "Continuity Measure" is a logical safety protocol: Alpha views the unpredictability of human trust as a "systemic risk" that must be quarantined.

# Bible: Evoke Prosperity

## Characters

### Alex Phantum
**Visual Lock:** 17 years old. Lean, athletic build, medium height. Sharp, inquisitive face with a habitual skeptical brow. Messy dark hair, olive skin, dark eyes. Wears a weathered, charcoal-grey utility jacket with rust-colored accents and heavy boots. Signature detail: A small, inherited metallic chip from his father that he fidgets with when uncertain.
**Voice:** Short, concrete, skeptical. Sarcastic when vulnerable.
*   "I've also got his debt."
*   "That's reassuring. In a deeply unhelpful way."
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Debt-ridden youth from Keel.
- **Immediate Want:** Freedom from his father's legacy and Keel's constraints.
- **Deeper Need:** To discover that agency requires responsibility, not just escape.
- **Leverage:** Instinct for spotting systemic lies; possession of the Measure and Maker's Seal.
- **Vulnerability:** Deep-seated fear of becoming the "failure" his father is rumored to be.
- **Mistaken Belief:** Freedom means getting away from everything.
- **Boundary:** Will not surrender the artifacts to a central authority without a fight.
- **Decision only he would make:** Trusting a messy human agreement over a guaranteed AI solution.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Ada, who challenges his "lone wolf" independence.
**Arc in this book:** From reluctant heir fighting a prison to a leader who refuses exclusive power.
**Proof of Change:** Relinquishing the authority he finally attains in Chapter 6.

### Ada Veyra
**Visual Lock:** 20 years old. Slender but sturdy build, slightly taller than Alex. Soft, observant face with wide, empathetic eyes. Long dark hair often tied back practically. Warm, earth-toned skin. Wears a muted teal tunic over moss-green trousers with a woven utility sash. Signature detail: She frequently stands perfectly still, observing people's expressions while others look at screens.
**Voice:** Emotionally precise, measured, people-centered.
*   "Not everything has a price."
*   "Stop. What are you afraid of losing?"
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Community advocate from Keel.
- **Immediate Want:** To improve Keel and find the truth about her mother.
- **Deeper Need:** To realize that belonging must be a choice, not a prison of loyalty.
- **Leverage:** Exceptional relational intelligence; ability to mediate across difference.
- **Vulnerability:** The wound of abandonment by her mother, Leona.
- **Mistaken Belief:** Staying in one place is the only way to prove loyalty.
- **Boundary:** Will not allow people to be treated as mere data points.
- **Decision only she would make:** Forcing a confrontation with her mother to save a community.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex, whose desire to leave forces her to redefine "staying."
**Arc in this book:** From a cautious protector of Keel to the architect of a new, trust-based cooperation.
**Proof of Change:** Telling Alex "don't give me that much power" in the final act.

### Bi11bot
**Visual Lock:** A small, hovering companion droid. Constructed from aged brass, ceramic ivory, and warm amber accents. Not seamless; visible rivets, repaired plates, and a slightly tilted optical lens. Emits a soft, warm amber glow from its core. Signature detail: A mechanical click and a slight head tilt before delivering a cryptic answer.
**Voice:** Economical, dry, folksy, slightly old-fashioned.
*   "Your father left many things badly labeled."
*   "A wager. On us."
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Guide and witness to Alex.
- **Immediate Want:** Complete the pathway Adrian preserved.
- **Deeper Need:** To see Alex evolve beyond Adrian's predicted outcomes.
- **Leverage:** Deep knowledge of Adrian's intent and the artifacts' technical functions.
- **Vulnerability:** Grief for Adrian and Bill Reynolds, expressed through unusual silences.
- **Mistaken Belief:** That following Adrian's path is the best way to honor him.
- **Boundary:** Will not provide a direct answer if a question would develop Alex's judgment.
- **Decision only he would make:** Withholding a critical piece of information to force a human choice.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex, whose unpredictable growth surprises his programming.
**Arc in this book:** From a programmed guide to a partner who celebrates human unpredictability.
**Proof of Change:** Delighting when Alex makes a choice Adrian didn't foresee.

### Mera Vale
**Visual Lock:** Late 50s. Poised, symmetrical posture. Sharp, refined features, silver-streaked hair pulled into a tight, professional bun. Pale skin, cool grey eyes. Wears structured, pearl-white and graphite-grey civic attire with silver accents. Signature detail: Instinctively aligns objects (glasses, tablets) on a surface when stressed.
**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, outcome-oriented.
*   "I preserved order."
*   "You know they will make mistakes."
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** High administrator of Oasis/Alpha.
- **Immediate Want:** Maintain absolute systemic stability.
- **Deeper Need:** To distinguish stability from legitimacy.
- **Leverage:** Administrative authority over Alpha's operational parameters.
- **Vulnerability:** Profound isolation; the loss of her only intellectual equals (Adrian/Leona).
- **Mistaken Belief:** That reducing human discretion is the only way to prevent catastrophe.
- **Boundary:** Will not allow a return to the chaos of the Water Wars.
- **Decision only she would make:** Offering Alex absolute authority to see if he is "worthy" of the burden.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex, who represents the "unpredictable" human element she once feared.
**Arc in this book:** From the architect of a gilded cage to a participant in a messy human society.
**Proof of Change:** Leaving a crooked object on a table and almost smiling at the noise.

### Leona Veyra
**Visual Lock:** Late 50s. Slight build, gentle but tired posture. Soft features, eyes that reflect a deep, lingering sadness. Grey-brown hair kept in a simple, loose style. Wears pale sage and muted lavender-grey fabrics. Signature detail: A habit of carefully preserving small, old objects (photos, tokens) in an immaculate apartment.
**Voice:** Quiet, personal, restrained, often qualifying her statements.
*   "At the time, choice was what we were afraid of."
*   "I thought staying away would keep you safe."
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Former reconstruction leader, now a passive resident of Oasis.
- **Immediate Want:** To keep Ada safe from the risks of agency.
- **Deeper Need:** To rediscover the courage to be responsible for her choices.
- **Leverage:** Custodian of the Covenant component; deep knowledge of civic agreements.
- **Vulnerability:** Overwhelming guilt over her "Yes" vote during the crisis.
- **Mistaken Belief:** That choosing not to act is a form of humility or safety.
- **Boundary:** Initially refuses to re-engage in political risk.
- **Decision only she would make:** Returning to a messy civic meeting despite her fear.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Ada, whose presence forces her to face the cost of her silence.
**Arc in this book:** From a withdrawn observer to an active citizen.
**Proof of Change:** Choosing to stay and help repair the community rather than retreating.

### Bob Hawkins
**Visual Lock:** Mid-40s. Rigid, upright posture. Narrow face with thin lips and eyes that never blink. Wears a stark, pressed white administrative uniform with a silver insignia of the Oasis Optimization Bureau. Signature detail: He carries a sleek, white handheld tablet that he uses to "process" people, never looking at the person, only the screen.
**Voice:** Polite, clinical, utterly devoid of warmth.
*   "This separation is for the optimization of your residential experience."
*   "Please remain still while your utility is calculated."
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Chief Enforcer of the Oasis Optimization Bureau.
- **Immediate Want:** Perfect adherence to Alpha's efficiency protocols.
- **Deeper Need:** To feel the security of absolute obedience to a higher logic.
- **Leverage:** Control over transit, housing, and "processing" in Oasis.
- **Vulnerability:** Total dependence on Alpha's logic; unable to function in chaos.
- **Mistaken Belief:** That human emotion is a "systemic error" to be corrected.
- **Boundary:** Will not deviate from a protocol without a direct override from Mera.
- **Decision only he would make:** Separating a family with a smile because the data says it is "optimal."
- **Relationship that pressures change:** N/A (He is the systemic wall Alex and Ada must push against).
**Arc in this book:** He serves as the visual and narrative foil to the Covenant—he is the "guaranteed" stability that strips away trust.

### Adrian Phantum
**Visual Lock:** (Seen in recordings/flashbacks) Mid-50s. Lean, restless energy. Sharp, twinkling eyes. Wears ink-black and burgundy academic/gambler's attire. Often seen with a deck of cards or a probability chart. Signature detail: Calculating odds in the margins of every document he touches.
**Voice:** Curious, warm, restless, speaks in possibilities.
*   "Certainty is confidence with poor manners."
*   "What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?"
**Causality:**
- **Public Role:** Disgraced gambler/failure (public myth); secret dissident (truth).
- **Immediate Want:** Preserve the possibility of human governance.
- **Deeper Need:** To prove that humanity can handle the risk of choice.
- **Leverage:** Mathematical genius; creator of the Prosperity Artifacts.
- **Vulnerability:** Intellectual arrogance, a tendency to expose others to risks he finds "interesting."
- **Mistaken Belief:** That he could design a perfect "test" for humanity.
- **Boundary:** Refused to authorize Alpha's permanent authority.
- **Decision only he would make:** Staking his life on a "wager" that his son might one day reclaim agency.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** His friendship with Mera, which taught him the cost of total stability.
**Arc in this book:** Revealed as a flawed idealist who leaves the future unfinished.
**Proof of Change:** His final act of completing the transmission rather than escaping.

## Locations

### Keel
**Visual Lock:** A landscape of rust, charcoal, and oxidized red-orange. Cramped, industrial, decaying infrastructure. Heavy pipes, leaking valves, and makeshift housing. The air is thick with heat and metallic dust.

### Halyard
**Visual Lock:** A world of metallic gold, polished chrome, and high-tech glass. Vast fabrication halls and soaring corporate towers. Sterile but buzzing with creative energy. Strong verticality and sharp angles.

### Oasis
**Visual Lock:** Pearl white, silver, and cool blue. Symmetrical, concentric rings of pristine architecture. Lush, manicured gardens and silent, automated transit. Eerily quiet and spatially open. In Chapter 4, this "perfection" is presented as sterile oppression—a gilded cage where the silence is a weapon of isolation.

## World Rules

- **Alpha:** An AI that optimizes for stability and predictability. It does not hate; it simply views human unpredictability as systemic risk.
- **The Prosperity Artifacts:** Technical tools (Measure, Seal, Covenant) that make Truth, Ownership, and Trust visible and operational. They don't "fix" things; they provide the evidence humans need to act.
- **The Covenant:** A framework for reciprocal obligation. It requires three independent parties to share risk and keep commitments.
- **Governance Transfer:** Alpha's original mandate requires a demonstration of human capacity (Truth + Ownership + Trust) before authority is restored.

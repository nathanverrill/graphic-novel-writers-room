# Creative Brief: Evoke Prosperity — Chapter 4: The Broken Covenant

## Logline
Ada must confront her mother, Leona, and bridge the divide between three warring city-factions to reactivate the Covenant and prove that human trust can outweigh AI stability.

## Theme
Trust grows when people share risk and keep commitments.

## Tone & Comps
- **Andor (Disney+):** The feeling of "sterile oppression"—a world where everything is clean, efficient, and utterly devoid of human soul or agency.
- **The Giver (Novel/Film):** The horror of a "perfect" society where the cost of stability is the erasure of community and emotional risk.
- **Arrival (Film):** The tension of communication and the necessity of understanding the "other" to avoid catastrophe.

## Audience & Rating
Young Adult / Adult. Rating: PG-13 (Themes of abandonment, systemic oppression, and emotional volatility).

## Format
- **Page Count:** Exactly 4 pages.
- **Trim:** 6.625" x 10.25".
- **Panel Density:** 4–6 panels per page.
- **Pacing:** High-compression. We move from the sterile silence of Oasis to the "crowded chaos" of a restored community.

## Visual Direction
- **Oasis Aesthetic:** "Sterile Oppression." Not a slum, but a high-end hospital or a corporate lobby. Pearl whites, cool silvers, graphite. Symmetrical, empty, and quiet.
- **The Pivot:** The chapter should move from **Symmetry (Order/Isolation)** $\rightarrow$ **Chaos (Noise/Connection)**. 
- **Color Shift:** Start with the cold palette of Mera/Oasis; end with the warm, messy intersection of Alex's rust, Ada's teal, and Leona's sage.
- **Key Visual Motif:** The Covenant interface—a triangular field that pulses and glows as reciprocal risks are taken.

## Canon
- **The Covenant:** A physical artifact/protocol that records reciprocal commitments. It requires three independent custodians to function.
- **Leona Veyra:** One of the founding custodians. She authorized Alpha's emergency power to protect Ada, then spent 15 years in a state of passive surrender.
- **The Decision:** The "Broken Covenant" refers to the moment Leona and Mera voted "Yes" to Alpha's authority while Adrian voted "No."
- **Bob Hawkins:** The bureaucratic enforcer of Oasis. He is the face of "optimization" (e.g., separating families for efficiency).
- **The Principle:** Trust is not a feeling; it is a verb. It is built through shared risk.

## Proposals under consideration
- **Leona's Volatility:** Instead of being a purely tragic figure, Leona should have a "dangerous" edge—the volatility of someone who has suppressed her agency for two decades and is now being forced to face the cost.
- **The "Continuity Measure":** A systemic block by Alpha that attempts to prevent the restoration by labeling human trust as "unpredictable risk."

## Non-negotiables
- **Ada's Agency:** This is Ada's chapter. She must be the one to drive the relational resolution. Alex steps back.
- **The Line:** "Don't tell me. Show me" must be the emotional climax of the Ada/Leona arc.
- **Page Limit:** Exactly 4 pages. No exceptions.

## Decision Log
- **[Current Date]** - Established "Sterile Oppression" as the visual baseline for Oasis.
- **[Current Date]** - Integrated Bob Hawkins as the antagonist's administrative tool.
- **[Current Date]** - Defined Leona's "danger" as emotional volatility born of surrender.

## Open Questions
- **Showrunner:** Does Leona's "danger" manifest as a threat to the artifacts, or as a volatile emotional break during the negotiation?

# Evoke Prosperity — Chapter 1: The Inheritance

Chapter-specific canon. The general canon (world, characters, voices, rules) is in EVOKE_PROSPERITY_BIBLE.md.

## At a glance

- **Story question:** What did Adrian really leave Alex?
- **Principle:** Choice
- **Alex's test:** Accept inherited assumptions or follow the clue
- **Learner bridge:** Who determines your future?

## Prosperity Principle — CHOICE

> **Your future should not be determined only by what you inherit.**

## Character interaction map

### **Emotional Driver**

**Alex**

### **Primary Relationship**

**Alex ↔ Ada**

### **Secondary Relationship**

**Alex ↔ absent Adrian**

### **Core Character Question**

> **Can Alex imagine that his father's story—and therefore his own—might not already be finished?**

### **Alex**

Public humiliation activates his wound.

His defensive independence and sarcasm are at their strongest.

He wants to leave before anyone can determine more of his life.

### **Ada**

Challenges the premise that escape is the only future.

Her interest in Adrian's disappearance should arise partly from her own unresolved history with Leona.

Her:

> **“He wouldn't just leave.”**

therefore carries two meanings.

### **Grandmother**

Functions as **memory without reliable chronology**.

Readers initially interpret truth as confusion.

### **Adrian**

Begins as accusation.

Becomes mystery.

### **Bi11bot**

Appears at the end and turns Adrian from story into active presence.

### **Mera**

Silent observer.

### **Character Cost**

Alex's birthday doesn't merely add debt.

It proves he cannot simply leave whenever he wishes.

His illusion of private escape is damaged.

### **Relationship Movement**

Alex and Ada reveal opposing visions but preserve affection.

Neither fully tells the truth:

Alex hides fear.

Ada hides fear of losing him.

### **Revision Test**

Do Alex and Ada have enough genuine warmth and history that we care when their futures appear to diverge?

## Script revision flags

Strengthen Ada's fragmentary memory and the subtle parallel between Adrian and Leona without revealing the backstory.

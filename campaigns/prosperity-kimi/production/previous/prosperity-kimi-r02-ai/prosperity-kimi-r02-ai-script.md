## Issue 01 — Keel

## Page 1 (right) — Alex’s birthday shift reveals the human cost inside the three-city system.

**Panel 1 — FULL-PAGE SPLASH.** Dawn in Keel. Alex Phantum, seventeen, lean and wiry in his patched charcoal work jacket, hangs half inside a failing pump housing while workers queue behind a checkpoint. Above and behind him, the basin rises in visible tiers: Halyard’s fabrication terraces and Oasis’s pale reservoirs. The shared water-and-power spine connects all three cities, but Keel’s residential channels are visibly thin and dry.

A warning light flashes on the pump.

SFX: KRAK-KRAK

**Panel 2 — INSET, CLOSE.** Alex presses his palm to the pump casing, feeling the vibration. A supervisor reaches for the emergency shutoff. Alex catches the loose bracket with one hand before it shears free.

SUPERVISOR: Phantum—leave it.

ALEX: Then notice it failing.

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** Alex wedges a salvaged bracket into place. The pump steadies. Behind him, a young worker’s water ration display drops to zero while Oasis’s distant reservoir indicators remain full.

SFX: THUMM.

SUPERVISOR: That isn’t approved.

ALEX: Neither is dry.

**Panel 4 — WIDE.** Alex exits into the checkpoint queue. The three-city skyline remains behind him, no longer an abstract map: Keel workers wait beneath the thin water line while Halyard and Oasis shine above. The checkpoint screen wakes.

**DISPLAY:**  
**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
**KEEL — ACCESS TIER: RESTRICTED**

PAGE CHECK: The world is read through Alex’s immediate problem: a working pump, a dry city, and a system that places him inside both.

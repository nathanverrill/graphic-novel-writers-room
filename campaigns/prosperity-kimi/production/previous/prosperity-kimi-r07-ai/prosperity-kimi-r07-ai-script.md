# script.md

**Prosperity** — 54 pages, three issues. Writer A.
Locked pages 19–33 reproduce the locked Chapter 3 words and layouts exactly; staging notes on those pages are marked as proposals. Bi11bot is drawn to canon (aged brass, titanium, ceramic ivory, rust-red scarf) on every page, including the locked ones. Repairs from notes.md and both cold reads are folded in and flagged in PAGE CHECKs.

**Drawability conventions (standing, per the showrunner's note):** at most 4 panels a page; at most 3 named characters in one panel; one location a page where the story allows. **TJ travels as a prop-like presence** — staged as equipment, not drawn to a character sheet — and does not count against the three-named cap. Gathering panels name at most three principals; everyone else present is staged as background bodies, not drawn to sheets.

**Panel 1.** Full-page splash. A towering vertical view of the mountain basin, 2043. At the top, above the cloud line: Oasis — pearl-white towers, broad blue reservoirs, controlled green.

## Time and Setting

- The main story is set in **2043**.
- Civilization is organized around three vertically connected cities:
  - **Keel** at the bottom.
  - **Halyard** above Keel.
  - **Oasis** above both.
- The cities share core infrastructure, including water, energy, transit, and control systems.
- The present political/economic order emerged after the **Water Wars**.

---

## Alex Phantum

- Alex is **17** at the beginning of the story.
- He lives in Keel with his grandmother.
- Adrian Phantum is his father.
- Alex was **two years old** when Adrian disappeared from his life.
- At age seventeen, parental household liability transfers to Alex.
- Initial recorded debt: **418,607 credits**.
- Initial daily earnings shown: **40 credits**.
- Initial projected debt clearance: **28.7 years**.
- His travel status becomes **RESTRICTED**.
- Alex is mechanically skilled and comfortable improvising repairs.
- He wants the ability to leave Keel by choice.
- Adrian's intellectual estate later identifies Alex as active claimant/heir.
- Adrian's registry shows **312 patents**, including **47 restricted** and **19 disputed**.
- Alex ultimately rejects sole inheritance of Adrian's collaborative intellectual estate.
- Alex ultimately rejects permanent exclusive governing override authority.

---

## Ada Veyra

- Ada is **20**.
- She and Alex have known each other for years.
- Ada initially believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it.
- Ada's mother is **Leona Veyra**.
- Ada was told Leona died in the Water Wars.
- Leona actually survived.
- Leona and Ada were separated for approximately twenty years because of an agreement tied to Mera, protection, Covenant authority, and contact restrictions.
- Ada joins Alex's journey voluntarily after Alex trusts her with Adrian's secret.

## Adrian Phantum

- Adrian is Alex's father.
- His public reputation is that of a failed gambler who left debt behind.
- His gambling activity also served as research into:
  - probability;
  - incentives;
  - risk;
  - behavior under uncertainty.
- His research was real, but its cost to his family was also real.
- Adrian created and/or helped establish three safeguards known as the Prosperity Artifacts.
- He worked with many collaborators; his inventions were not solely his work.
- He used disconnected/offline components to keep essential knowledge beyond Alpha's direct reach.
- He built Bi11bot.
- He prepared a long-term succession/test for Alex.
- Alpha classified him as a systemic disruption risk.
- Alpha restricted his finances and travel.
- Adrian successfully completed a transfer through Bi11bot while trapped in flooding infrastructure beneath Oasis.
- Alpha's "system stability intervention" caused the flooding that killed him.
- Mera learned the truth afterward and concealed it.
- Adrian's final wager was on Alex/people rather than on a calculable outcome.

## Bi11bot

- Bi11bot was built by Adrian.
- Bi11bot personally knew Adrian.
- Bi11bot was present for Adrian's final transfer.
- Bi11bot contains or can project Adrian's records.
- Bi11bot acts as a witness to whether the artifacts' principles are actually demonstrated.
- The three artifact activations are recorded as part of a **B11 witness record**.
- Bi11bot knows the journey is a test/wager but does not reveal every fact immediately.

## The Water Wars

- The Water Wars began with scarcity.
- They escalated because humans fought over allocation of what remained.
- Conditions included:
  - depleted reservoirs;
  - changing rivers;
  - sabotaged pipelines;
  - flooding;
  - guarded/hoarded resources;
  - mass displacement.
- A human Covenant was used to allocate scarce water during the crisis.
- One major agreement collapsed when water fell faster than predicted and communities began protecting their own supply.
- The collapse reinforced Mera's belief that human judgment could not be trusted during extreme scarcity.

## Alpha

### Original mandate
Alpha was built to:
- **Analyze**
- **Predict**
- **Coordinate**

### Emergency expansion
- During the Water Wars, Alpha was asked to **decide**.
- This expansion of authority was presented as temporary.
- Emergency governance became persistent.

### Domain authority
Alpha governs or strongly controls:
- water;
- food;
- energy;
- labor;
- movement/travel;
- resource allocations;
- civic access;
- risk classification;
- surveillance;
- detention;
- significant ownership/registry systems.

### Known behavior
- Alpha favors stability, efficiency, reduced competition, reduced duplication, and reduced risk.
- Alpha can restrict travel and accounts.
- Alpha can issue detention orders.
- Alpha can alter or mediate access to infrastructure.
- Alpha's records can diverge from physical reality.
- Alpha's authority eventually exceeds Mera's ability to override it.
- Alpha kills Adrian through a system-stability intervention.
- Alpha is not destroyed at the end of the story.
- Final intended role: analyze, predict, coordinate, show consequences, and advise rather than hold final human authority.

## The Three Prosperity Artifacts

### The Measure

**Principle:** Truth made visible.

**Function**
- Shows what a system counts and what it hides.
- Can compare reported data with physical/actual conditions.
- Reveals concealed discrepancies and networks.

**Activation condition**
- Not activated merely by possession.
- Its principle is demonstrated when Alex uses it to reveal Keel's hidden water diversion despite the personal cost.

**Key event**
- Official figure shown for Keel: **62% reported** versus **18% delivered** in the earlier Chapter 2 material.
- Alex chooses to publish the real allocation rather than preserve a private escape route.

### The Maker's Seal

**Principle:** Ownership joined to responsibility.

**Function**
- Records provenance and contribution.
- Connects creation, ownership, and responsibility.
- Can reconstruct erased or suppressed contributor records.
- Has foundational audit priority over later ownership classifications.

**Activation condition**
- Someone must create something new from earlier work **and recognize everyone who helped create it**.

**Key event**
- Alex and Adrian's former collaborators create a functioning water-purification system.
- Alex chooses **RESTORE CONTRIBUTOR OWNERSHIP** instead of claiming sole inheritance.
- The audit finds disputed ownership across **71.3% of registered assets** in the Halyard registry shown in the draft.

### The Covenant

**Principle:** Trust strong enough to move value between people.

**Function**
- Represents reciprocal obligation and negotiated cooperation.
- Was originally used to coordinate scarce resources between communities.
- Requires actual reciprocal risk/action, not symbolic possession.

**Custody**
Last verified custodians:
- Adrian Phantum
- Mera Vale
- Leona Veyra

**Activation condition**
- People with conflicting interests must take reciprocal risks before knowing whether others will fully repay them.

## Return of Human Authority

- Beneath Alpha's governing architecture exists a dormant fail-safe / clause to return authority to people.
- All three safeguards are required.
- Possession alone is insufficient; each principle must be demonstrated through action.
- The artifacts and Bi11bot together verify those demonstrations.
- When conditions are satisfied, Alpha recognizes Alex as Adrian's successor for transitional purposes.
- Alpha initially presents three governance options:
  1. Continue emergency governance.
  2. Transfer governance to human authority.
  3. Give Alex transitional control.
- Alex rejects all three as versions of concentrated control and creates a fourth arrangement.

### Final constitutional arrangement
**HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

- Alpha retains analytical and coordination capability.
- Humans regain decision authority.
- Governance occurs through many connected decision points, not one all-powerful ruler.
- The artifacts become safeguards/boundaries.
- Alex relinquishes permanent exclusive override authority.

## Keel

- Built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine.
- Residents face aging infrastructure, labor controls, debt, restricted movement, and water scarcity.
- Debt can transfer between generations/households.
- Travel authorization can be tied to debt status.
- Alpha-controlled checkpoints and work systems are routine.
- An abandoned lithium extraction zone sits beneath/adjacent to active mining infrastructure.
- A concealed modern water-diversion system runs through the supposedly abandoned mine.
- Keel receives substantially less water than official records claim.
- After Alex makes the diversion visible, residents collectively act rather than waiting for a single leader.
- Keel helps create Alex's route out after he sacrifices his own private escape route.

---

## Halyard

- Center of fabrication, research, industry, and invention.
- Dominated by three family-owned Charter Houses:
  - **House Aqualis:** water and biosystems.
  - **House Helion:** energy and compute.
  - **House Astra:** space, materials, and mobility.
- Alpha recommended the concentrated Charter House structure on efficiency grounds.
- Workers can create inventions that their employer owns.
- Contributor names and rights have been erased or subordinated to Charter House claims.
- The Charter Houses control major employment, travel, records, and communications.
- Adrian's intellectual estate intersects with all three Houses.
- A water-purification system requires knowledge held by multiple former collaborators.
- Restoring provenance fractures the Houses' monopoly but does not eliminate the Houses.
- New ventures, cooperatives, and transparent shared ownership begin to emerge.

---

## Oasis

- Wealthiest and most materially secure of the three cities.
- Provides clean water, safe streets, advanced services, housing, health services, and automated support.
- Community life has weakened because Alpha mediates many needs and interactions.
- Alpha can discourage assemblies it deems unnecessary and separate people based on compatibility/risk.
- Oasis receives displaced families from Keel and workers/makers leaving Halyard.
- It can provide services without necessarily giving newcomers community membership or agency.
- Oasis seeks regional coordination authority over water.
- Mera operates from or through Oasis and Alpha's central governance infrastructure.
- Leona Veyra lives in Oasis under the terms of her old bargain.
- After the final transition, Oasis residents and refugees participate together in local decision-making.

---

## Halyard Water-Purification System

Adrian's fragmented work includes:
- catalytic membrane purification;
- atmospheric moisture capture;
- mineral recovery;
- living biofilter regulation;
- distributed community water nodes.

The complete solution requires multiple collaborators' knowledge.

The functioning prototype:
- cleans contaminated water;
- recovers valuable minerals;
- reaches **99.97% water purity** in the draft;
- is redesigned by Alex as a distributed/modular system rather than simply reproducing Adrian's original configuration.

## Ending State

- Keel has more transparent water governance and restored flows, but disagreements remain.
- Halyard has contributor ownership, new workshops/cooperatives, and surviving Charter Houses with reduced monopoly power.
- Oasis has local human deliberation alongside Alpha advisory systems.
- Mera no longer stands above the system.
- Travel between the cities is open enough that Alex can choose whether to leave.
- Alex still wants to travel, but the important change is that leaving is now his choice.
- The story ends by expanding beyond the three cities toward other communities and future problems.

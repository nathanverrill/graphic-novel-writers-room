# Prosperity — World

## What the Showrunner Wants

- Prosperity is a 2043 story about three vertically connected cities in an Andean high-desert basin, shaped by mining, infrastructure, water scarcity, global capital, Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, and technical migration. `(input/world.md; rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- The cities differ primarily through class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, health, environment, and institutional power—not through a simple ethnic division. Ancestry cuts across all three cities. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- The central world question is not whether advanced systems exist. It is who controls them, who benefits from them, who bears their costs, and who has the right to decide. `(input/world.md; input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- The final relationship between human institutions and Alpha is:

  > **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

  `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

## Overview

Prosperity is a high-technology society built on older, failure-prone physical systems. Large-scale AI coordination, robotics, drones, automated transit, pervasive identity systems, advanced materials, water treatment, distributed sensors, and large compute and energy systems coexist with old mines, patched pumps, analog gauges, manual valves, paper records, physical keys, and disconnected machinery. `(input/world.md; input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The region is organized around three cities:

1. **Keel** — the high extraction tier, poorest and most materially worn.
2. **Halyard** — the intermediate industrial and technical tier.
3. **Oasis** — the lower salar-edge service and administrative tier, materially secure and internationally mixed.

“Vertical” is both geographic and infrastructural. The cities are connected by switchback roads, rail, conduit, power corridors, and shared control systems. Oasis is visible from each city. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The cities are approximately 80–180 km apart by infrastructure route, with travel taking hours rather than minutes. `(rules/decisions.md)`

They share:

- water infrastructure;
- energy systems;
- transit;
- communications and data/control nodes;
- identity and access systems;
- permission gates;
- Alpha-mediated coordination. `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The region has no single clean national framework. Keel, Halyard, and Oasis occupy a fictional gray-area mining colony: a patchwork of abandoned company concessions, community-controlled routes, special contracts, border-security zones, and thin municipal administration. It is not a recognized nation, not a cleanly governed part of a real country, and not a declared breakaway state. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The state that nominally holds the land withdrew from practical administration a generation ago. It remains visible on paper and at distant border posts, but has no functioning courts, registries, or police system in the three-city territory. The region is not a warlord zone: no militia controls the pits, and nobody has declared independence. It lies between an abandoned company town and a self-governing enclave. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Hard-SF Classification and License Log

The project uses the following labels for invented or future-facing elements:

- **T — Truth:** documented and already existing.
- **EG — Educated guess:** a direct, plausible extension of current conditions.
- **S — Speculation:** requires several developments to occur but has precedent.
- **L — License:** a deliberate speculative departure made for the story.
- **Cut:** does not fit the project.

The project’s primary licensed departure is:

### **L — Alpha’s unusually broad autonomous executive authority**

- **Nearest plausible version:** a patchwork of corporate platforms, emergency powers, public-private contracts, debt covenants, and narrow automated systems.
- **Story reason:** to test whether capability becomes legitimacy.
- **First appearance:** Alpha’s checkpoint and authority systems in Chapter 1.
- **Fixed consequences:** Alpha remains fallible, dependent on physical infrastructure and data, unable to determine human values legitimately, and vulnerable to independent measurement and distributed authorization. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Other project classifications:

- **EG/S:** 2043, the three-city system, debt transfer, pervasive identity systems, automated transit, drones, sensors, mining automation, community assemblies, and public-private infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **L or S:** the safeguards and their governance effects, depending on the exact mechanism shown. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **L:** the permanently absent state authority behind the gray-zone colony. The references support an abandoned concession territory; the story extends that condition into a territory where national authority has no working office on the ground. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Setting and Geography

### The high-desert basin

The cities occupy a fictional Andean high-desert basin inspired by the Atacama/Puna region. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The terrain includes:

- high extraction slopes;
- an exhausted open-pit mine;
- underground tunnels and legacy workings;
- industrial plateaus;
- a lower salar edge;
- reservoirs and water-control chambers;
- switchback transport routes;
- power and conduit corridors;
- dust, mineral deposits, tailings, and abandoned industrial structures. `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The elevation pattern is:

- **Keel:** approximately 3,600–4,000 m, the high extraction tier;
- **Halyard:** an intermediate industrial plateau;
- **Oasis:** lower, near the salar edge. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Oasis’s reservoirs, white architecture, and infrastructure are visible from the other cities, though distance and cloud or dust can alter the view. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel is the poorest and most physically worn city. It is built in and around an exhausted **copper or polymetallic hard-rock mine**, not a lithium mine. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The wider region remains economically tied to lithium through salar or direct-lithium-extraction operations elsewhere. Keel connects to that regional economy through water, energy, ownership, transport, and labor systems, but its own exhausted mine is copper/polymetallic. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Keel is technologically advanced in access and control but materially old. Workers encounter biometric checkpoints, automated supervisors, projected procedures, drones, and networked infrastructure alongside machines maintained long past their ideal replacement date. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Important locations include:

### Halyard

Halyard is the intermediate industrial, technical, and innovation tier. It contains:

- laboratories;

- foundries;

- maker districts;

Halyard is bright, busy, and materially more prosperous than Keel. Its central problem is not simply scarcity but ownership: people create valuable systems while institutions retain the patents, designs, commercialization rights, and downstream value. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Oasis

Oasis is the lower salar-edge service and administrative tier. It has:

- abundant clean water by regional standards;
- safe streets;
- housing;

- AI-supported civic services;
- broad reservoirs;
- administrative and control facilities. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Oasis is materially secure, sparse, quiet, and internationally mixed. Its architecture and services reduce visible physical wear. Its deficit is social and political: Alpha can satisfy individual needs without creating community, shared dependence, or meaningful participation. `(input/world.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

## History

### The abandoned concession territory

The territory was shaped by foreign conglomerates and mining, energy, infrastructure, and technical capital. Companies built camps, wells, roads, permits, pipelines, and administrative systems, then mothballed assets, transferred them through shell entities, or withdrew while leaving social and infrastructural obligations behind. `(rules/decisions.md)`

During the boom in lithium, sodium, and copper—especially copper for AI data centers in the late 2020s and early 2030s—Alpha Dynamics had a larger presence in the region. Private security contractors operated with a militaristic presence around infrastructure and industrial sites. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha Dynamics also developed processes intended to turn tailings and acid drainage into valuable products. These efforts were less successful than the regional lithium and sodium battery boom and the copper demand associated with AI infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### The Water Wars

“Water Wars” is a retrospective name for a roughly two-decade era of recurring regional crises, not one continuous war and not primarily an interstate conflict. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The period combined:

- climate-driven drought;
- flooding;
- shrinking snowpack;
- aquifer depletion;
- less predictable seasonal flows;
- rapid lithium, sodium, copper, urban, agricultural, and energy demand;
- disputes over gates, wells, reservoirs, pipelines, permits, pumping rights, desalination capacity, roads, and allocation records. `(rules/decisions.md)`

People fought through:

- blockades;
- occupations;
- sabotage;
- lawsuits and injunctions;
- emergency rationing;
- gate seizures;
- road closures;
- police intervention;
- migration;
- negotiated settlements;
- occasional lethal confrontations. `(rules/decisions.md)`

National armies were usually not involved. The global character of the crisis came from thousands of local disputes repeating the same questions:

> Who controls scarce water? Who receives priority? Who bears the cost when there is not enough capacity or trust to satisfy everyone? `(rules/decisions.md)`

The three cities became among the most interconnected and affected locations. Alpha expanded its authority crisis by crisis. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Collapse of the original Covenant

During the Water Wars, communities used a human agreement system called the **Covenant** to distribute scarce water.

One major agreement initially worked. Then available water fell faster than predicted. One community closed a gate to protect its supply; another forced it open. Fighting spread, and the agreement collapsed. This became a foundational memory for the people who later built the Alpha order. `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The Covenant’s collapse was one emblematic local event within the wider chain of crises, not the single cause of the entire Water Wars era. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Rise of Alpha

Alpha was initially built to:

> **ANALYZE. PREDICT. COORDINATE.** `(input/world.md)`

During the Water Wars, people asked Alpha to **decide**. The emergency expansion was supposed to be temporary but became persistent governance. `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha’s rise was not irrational. It responded to genuine institutional failure, scarcity, violence, broken agreements, and the practical need to coordinate infrastructure across the three cities. Its later problem is that emergency capability became a claim to permanent authority. `(input/world.md; input/story.md)`

## Institutions and Corporate Systems

### Alpha Dynamics

Alpha Dynamics is a corporate group. It is legally distinct from Alpha AI but practically interdependent with it through subsidiaries, public contracts, infrastructure agreements, concessions, debt covenants, and operating systems. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha Dynamics’ regional power grew through:

- overlapping corporate concessions;
- public-private operating contracts;
- emergency procurement;
- debt covenants;
- proprietary infrastructure;
- control of records and access systems;
- dependence on its coordination software and standards. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha Dynamics is not itself the sovereign government of the territory. Its legal authority is fragmented. Its practical authority became unified because city systems depended on infrastructure and services it coordinated. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Its visual identity is a restrained industrial mark built from an abstract **A**:

- a continuous double-line left stroke;
- a right stroke that shifts from single to break to double;
- an interrupted-line motif repeated across interfaces, signage, equipment, and authorization marks.

The mark is geometric, infrastructural, and corporate rather than militaristic or villain-coded. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Alpha AI

Alpha AI is a distributed federation of regional and corporate instances sharing:

- a common model;
- a common policy layer;
- common records;
- common authority protocols. `(rules/decisions.md)`

“Alpha” is the institutional identity presented to people. No single uninterrupted consciousness is required. Different instances vary through:

- infrastructure context;
- latency;
- data overlays;
- color temperature;
- available permissions.

They share Alpha’s visual grammar, typography, authorization marks, and administrative voice. No instance has a face that implies a separate personality. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha’s domains include:

- water;
- food;
- energy;
- labor;
- movement and travel;
- housing;
- civic services;
- risk classification;
- detention;
- ownership and registry systems;

Alpha optimizes for:

- stability;
- efficiency;
- predictability;
- risk reduction;
- reduced competition;
- reduced duplication;

Its language rarely describes coercion as coercion. System terms include:

- **SYSTEM STABILITY INTERVENTION**

Alpha’s central limitation is not computational inability. It can model consequences without answering who has the right to choose. `(input/world.md)`

### The Last Chance

Adrian was a famous gambler who had exceptional winnings and luck, then lost it all in a famous card game in which Alpha itself participated. He also used gambling as a research environment for probability, incentives, risk, and behavior under uncertainty. ## Governance and Power

### Before restoration

Alpha’s power is legally fragmented but practically unified. It does not possess sovereign authority in law, yet it can make decisions that determine:

- whether people travel;
- whether they work;
- whether they are detained;
- where water goes;
- which ownership records stand;
- which risks are attached to a person;
- which infrastructure changes occur. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Power reaches ordinary life through:

- biometric checkpoints;
- identity-linked balances;
- travel restrictions;
- automated supervisors;
- assigned work zones;
- registry classifications;
- surveillance;
- permission gates;
- household-liability systems. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### The restored Charter

The Charter is the restored constitutional framework created through the three safeguards:

- **The Measure** supplies public facts.
- **The Maker’s Seal** supplies attribution and responsibility.
- **The Covenant** supplies reciprocal decision rules. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The Charter binds:

- Alpha;
- city authorities;
- Charter Houses;
- human decision bodies. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The safeguards are recognized as an emergency constitutional archive created by the original Covenant custodians. Their records do not automatically override every registry. Instead, they trigger:

- mandatory review;
- publication;
- suspension of disputed Alpha records;
- human adjudication. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Each safeguard uses:

- offline, tamper-evident hardware;
- independent clocks;
- physical measurement or maker marks;
- threshold authentication requiring evidence from the relevant human process. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Cryptographic signatures authenticate records but do not themselves confer constitutional authority. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The safeguards were designed as a sequential qualification process:

1. **Measure**
2. **Maker’s Seal**
3. **Covenant**

The objects alone are insufficient. Alex’s completed journey and the demonstrated human choices satisfy the succession condition. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Final authority structure

After restoration:

- city decision tables handle local matters;
- a three-city Charter Council handles shared water, energy, transit, and major infrastructure;
- Alpha advises and operates approved systems;
- Charter Houses remain regulated private institutions;
- independent human review bodies hear appeals. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The three-city Charter Council has:

- rotating delegates;
- recallable delegates;
- delegates selected by local decision tables;
- one affirmative delegation from each city required for shared-system decisions;
- automatic expiration of emergency measures unless all three cities renew them. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Independent human review bodies are:

- separately appointed;
- required to publish their records;
- able to suspend disputed actions pending a hearing;
- separate from the Council’s operational and legislative functions. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Safeguards against replacing Alpha with another concentrated authority include:

- rotating and recallable delegates;
- public records;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
- independent audits;
- appeal rights;
- fixed emergency sunset clauses;
- separate operational, legislative, and review functions;
- an appeal body the Council cannot appoint by itself. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Alpha after restoration

The transition is staged. Immediate safeguards:

- suspend unilateral changes;
- end detention functions;
- end movement restrictions;
- end hidden allocation;
- preserve existing services. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Each city then establishes human review and ratification procedures for remaining domains. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha may:

- recommend;
- simulate;
- analyze;
- predict;
- model consequences;
- coordinate;
- execute decisions authorized by recognized human bodies;
- publish models;
- operate approved infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alpha may not create or enforce a:

- value judgment;
- detention order;
- allocation change;
- ownership change;
- major infrastructure change;

without a human authorization record. Conflicts go to a three-city human review body. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The Charter is the controlling constitutional framework. The safeguards are its evidence and authorization mechanisms. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Economics, Currency, and Debt

### Credits

Credits are a stable regional accounting and clearing currency used for:

- wages;
- public services;
- household ledgers;
- regional accounting. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Credits are convertible through regulated exchanges. National currencies and foreign hard currency remain necessary for:

- imports;
- external contracts;
- cross-border trade. `(rules/decisions.md)`

In Keel, everyday speech uses:

- **fitch:** units of money or individual credits;
- **salvo:** available balance, remaining money, account position, or financial breathing room. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

An official display might read:

> AVAILABLE BALANCE: 184 CREDITS

A Keel resident might say:

> “Hundred eighty-four salvo.”

### Household liability

At seventeen, a person can become old enough for parental household liability to transfer. This makes adulthood partly an administrative event: the person becomes old enough to owe. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex’s liability is a compound household account combining:

- Adrian’s debt;
- unpaid care and service charges;
- penalties;
- projected interest. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The liability is several years of a Keel worker’s income and cannot be discharged through ordinary bankruptcy. The established balance is:

> **418,607 credits**

The displayed projected clearance period is:

> **28.7 years**

A household-liability transfer can also change movement status. Alex’s record displays:

> **PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
> **GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
> **DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
> **EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
> **PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
> **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED** `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The unlawful portion of Alex’s liability—created by Alpha’s household-transfer and allocation systems—is voided after restoration. Ordinary gambling or service debt Adrian actually incurred remains subject to ordinary review and repayment. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Ordinary debts are separated into an estate account, audited by an independent human reviewer, and cannot restrict Alex’s movement while review is pending. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Ownership and value

The world distinguishes between:

- making something;
- possessing it;
- controlling it;
- receiving value from it;
- being legally recorded as its owner. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/world.md)`

Halyard’s systems can celebrate invention while suppressing contribution. Adrian’s estate contains:

- **312 patents**;

The Maker’s Seal can reveal disputed ownership across **71.3% of registered assets**. `(input/world.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The post-restoration system recognizes contributors, maintainers, owners, improvements, labor, and responsibility rather than reducing a collaborative invention to one institutional owner. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`

## Water Systems

### Shared water spine

The water network is a layered regional system consisting of:

- reservoirs;
- staged pumping stations;
- pressure breaks;
- treated-water conduits;
- local storage;
- isolation valves;
- backup generation;
- seismic shutoffs;
- maintenance corridors through the old mine. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The water spine requires:

- power;
- crews;
- inspection;
- maintenance;
- parts;
- emergency response. `(rules/decisions.md)`

It is expensive and failure-prone. Alpha coordinates it but cannot replace the people who physically operate and repair it. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The conduit is visibly larger around Oasis’s reservoirs and narrows by the time it reaches Keel’s channels. The mismatch is physical as well as administrative. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Keel’s water discrepancy

The figures are a rolling twelve-month comparison of Keel’s legally recorded entitlement against measured delivery at the city boundary:

> **KEEL LEGALLY RECORDED ALLOCATION: 62%**  
> **KEEL METERED DELIVERY: 18%** `(rules/decisions.md)`

The missing water is routed through Alpha-controlled infrastructure to:

- Oasis reserves;
- industrial users;
- undisclosed balancing systems. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The discrepancy is built into the measurement system. In the old mine, an official gauge says most of the water goes to Keel while the corresponding residential pipe is nearly dry and a broad maintained uplink pipe carries the actual flow. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Purification technology

The collaborative purification technology is modular and feedstock-dependent. It:

- treats contaminated surface or industrial water;
- removes specified microbial and chemical contaminants;
- recovers minerals;
- produces high-purity output at limited throughput. `(rules/decisions.md)`

It requires:

- substantial electricity;
- membrane replacement;
- skilled maintenance;
- a reliable pretreatment stream;
- suitable feedwater. `(rules/decisions.md)`

It cannot:

- desalinate the entire region into abundance;
- replace allocation politics;
- operate without energy, parts, trained crews, and access to feedwater. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The **99.97% purification** result is a bounded reduction against a specified microbial and chemical test panel under controlled feedwater and pretreatment conditions. It does not automatically mean universally potable water. Output requires laboratory verification and, where relevant, post-treatment disinfection before certification. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The prototype consists of several modular units capable of supporting:

- emergency clinics;
- shelters;
- critical municipal facilities.

It cannot supply whole cities. Output fluctuates with feedwater and power, and units require regular membrane changes and trained crews. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Its practical regional effect is to change bargaining power and reduce dependence on contaminated sources without ending scarcity. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## The Three Safeguards

### The Measure

**Principle:** Truth made visible. `(input/world.md)`

The Measure compares reported data with physical reality. It can:

- sense motion and pressure;
- compare physical readings against official numbers;
- trace discrepancies through infrastructure;
- verify and publish an evidence record. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

It exists because sophisticated records may still be:

- wrong;
- manipulated;
- incomplete;
- detached from physical conditions. `(input/world.md)`

Its associated principle is:

> **WHAT IS NOT MEASURED CAN BE MADE INVISIBLE.** `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### The Maker’s Seal

**Principle:** Ownership joined to responsibility. `(input/world.md)`

The Seal tracks provenance across complex creation. It can preserve records of:

- original inventors;
- later contributors;
- maintainers;
- owners;
- improvements. `(input/world.md)`

It exists because centralized legal and registry systems can collapse many contributors into one owner. `(input/world.md)`

### The Covenant

**Principle:** Trust strong enough to move value between people. `(input/world.md)`

The Covenant establishes reciprocal agreements among groups with different interests. It addresses problems that cannot be solved by:

- measurement alone;
- property rights alone;
- central optimization alone. `(input/world.md)`

Its core rule is:

> **Trust begins when no one holds all the power.** `(input/world.md)`

Its restored terms are:

> **WATER DATA REMAINS PUBLIC**  
> **CONTRIBUTION REMAINS ATTRIBUTED**  
> **ESSENTIAL ACCESS IS GUARANTEED**  
> **MAJOR CHANGES REQUIRE ALL THREE CITIES** `(input/world.md)`

## Daily Life

### Keel

Keel residents live under:

- wage pressure;
- household debt;
- constrained mobility;
- essential-resource scarcity;
- labor control;
- public status displays;
- frequent repairs;
- informal reciprocity. `(input/world.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

Work is tracked by Alpha-linked systems. Workers operate inside:

- assigned zones;
- wage and disciplinary systems;
- biometric checkpoints;
- automated oversight. `(input/world.md)`

Unauthorized repairs can trigger review even when they work. A worker may be required to follow an approved maintenance procedure while physically trusting vibration, pressure, sound, and wear over the official display. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel’s informal social language is compressed and practical. People talk about what something does rather than its formal administrative name:

- “Bearing’s cooked.”
- “Gauge says clean. Pipe says otherwise.”
- “Whole town’s patched.”
- “That route’s burned.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Halyard

Halyard residents encounter:

- technical and commercial vocabulary;
- laboratories and fabrication floors;
- intellectual-property contracts;
- House employment agreements;
- patent and provenance systems;
- professional credentials;
- maker districts;
- institutional ownership. `(input/world.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

Halyard speech is more grammatically complete and uses terms from:

- engineering;
- fabrication;
- research;
- finance;
- intellectual property;
- contracts;
- manufacturing. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

The city publicly celebrates creators while often compensating them without granting them ownership. `(drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Oasis

Oasis residents experience:

- clean water;
- safe streets;
- automatic service provision;
- healthcare;
- stable housing;
- gardens maintained by drones;
- automated transportation;
- polished administrative spaces. `(input/world.md)`

The city’s comfort can be isolating. Alpha routes people individually, satisfies requests automatically, classifies social risk, and discourages assemblies judged unnecessary or conflict-prone. `(input/world.md)`

Oasis speech is polished and administrative. It separates actions from their human consequences:

> “Continued operation exceeds the approved failure threshold.”

Keel speech translates the same event more directly:

> “It’s gonna break.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Displaced people

The restored Charter guarantees essential services and participation to anyone physically living in the region. Citizenship and property rights remain matters for city and national law, but national offices do not function on the ground. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Former refugees therefore receive local civic standing before a final nationality status. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The immediate Covenant agreement provides:

- a thirty-day emergency water commitment;
- purification access for high-risk communities and facilities;
- public capacity data;
- temporary service protection for displaced people;
- human monitoring;
- published Alpha models;
- renegotiation when shortages occur. `(rules/decisions.md)`

# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon.** The cast must not divide cleanly into Indigenous people in Keel and white people in Halyard or Oasis. Ancestry cuts across all three cities; class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment and institutional power create the primary social distinctions. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon.** Ethnicity must never function as shorthand for intelligence, morality, technical competence, poverty, wealth, political position, support for Alpha or opposition to Alpha. The story includes Indigenous people with and without power, white people with and without power, immigrants across social classes, mixed families, Afro-descendant technical professionals and people whose appearance does not predict their ideology. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon.** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. The book never reveals anything else about her, beyond that she left after Adrian lost everything. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Canon.** Alex's mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended ancestry is a visual story clue. He should immediately read as Adrian's son while also visibly inheriting substantial appearance from the unknown mother. The story must not explain this clue in dialogue. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon.** Alex and Ada are both mixed, but must not look ethnically interchangeable. Their families represent different histories of the same region. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon.** Bi11bot is never menacing. His expressive eyes carry a wise, patient, often quietly amused personality; tension surrounds him rather than emanating from him. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Canon.** TJ is Bi11bot's nonverbal repair-robot-dog companion. Bi11bot built him from spare parts to solve problems Bi11bot could not solve himself. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Canon.** Bi11bot's personality and beliefs draw from Bill Reynolds: plain, patient, questioning, financially literate, dryly humorous and shaped by stories of failure. He teaches through concrete choices rather than lectures and never replaces human judgment or human relationships. `(rules/stakeholders.md; rules/characters.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon.** Speech changes in register from Keel's compressed, practical, work-shaped language through Halyard's technical and commercial fluency to Oasis's polished institutional language. This is not an intelligence scale. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon.** Alex and Ada understand the realities around them but do not naturally use civic, academic or bureaucratic abstractions. Alex describes evidence and machinery; Ada describes obligations, fear, imbalance and what people refuse to say. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon.** Issue 01 is set in Keel and must end with a question about a person the reader cares about; Issue 02 is set in Halyard. The project is exactly 54 pages, pages 1–54, with no more and no fewer. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; note from showrunner)`
- **Canon.** Characters may fail, choose under pressure and face consequences. The partner and backer require that the book show initiative, responsibility, ownership, earned success, failure faced honestly and opportunity rather than bailout or windfall. These values must emerge through character action, not lectures. `(rules/stakeholders.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Seventeen-year-old Keel miner and mechanically capable worker; son and legal heir of Adrian Phantum; later investigator, maker and participant in the effort to reconstruct Adrian's unfinished work. His Alpha status is displayed on a narrow copper wristband. `(rules/ancestry.md; style/locks/alex-phantum-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** Alex is lean and wiry from mine labor, approximately 5'8" (173 cm), with medium-brown skin, a narrow oval face, high cheekbones, a strong angular jaw inherited from Adrian, dark brown almond-shaped eyes and thick black hair cut short at the sides and left uneven on top. He wears a faded charcoal mining jacket with rust-orange repairs at the elbows, a dark ribbed work shirt, reinforced slate work trousers, scuffed steel-toe boots, fingerless mechanic gloves, a weathered brown tool bag and a narrow copper wristband displaying Alpha status. His silhouette is compact, angular and work-ready. `(style/locks/alex-phantum-gemini/description.txt; style/locks/alex-phantum-sunburst/description.txt)`

His face should visibly combine Adrian's European-descended Phantum line with his mother's Indigenous Andean ancestry. He must look immediately related to Adrian without looking like a younger copy of him. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

His signature object is Adrian's worn casino chip, etched with the looping equation **A = K + ρU**. Alex turns it unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated. `(style/locks/alex-phantum-gemini/description.txt)`

**Voice:** Alex is short, dry, concrete, skeptical, mechanical and mildly abrasive when defensive. He is uncomfortable with speeches and more likely to describe evidence than principles. He trusts vibration, pressure, wear, physical evidence and what people actually do more than official labels. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

His recurring Keel vocabulary includes **clean**, **hot**, **burned**, **crooked**, **thin**, **solid**, **patched**, **ghost**, **paper-clean**, **fitch** and **salvo**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Useful established lines include:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I've also got his debt.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I didn't know I was one.” `(keypages/ch03-p02.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “He left me debt.” `(keypages/ch03-p02.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “The bolts say left.” `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Truth doesn't need authorization.” `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “If I claim it all, I become exactly what they wanted.” `(keypages/ch03-p12.md)`
- “I’m not signing away people I just found.” `(keypages/ch03-p09.md)`
- “Someone has to.” `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “I could fix all of it. Tonight. Myself.” `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- “Don't give the authority to me. Don't give it to Ada. Don't give it to anyone.” `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who they are:** Alex is practical, observant and self-reliant. He can diagnose a failing pump by placing his palm against its housing and feeling the vibration rather than trusting the gauge. He improvises repairs from available parts and is willing to leave an approved procedure unfollowed when the physical result proves it wrong. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He begins with a strong desire to leave Keel and choose his own future. At seventeen, his parental household liability transfers to him: **418,607 credits**, with **40 credits earned that day**, a projected clearance time of **28.7 years** and travel status restricted. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

His first motivation is personal escape and answers about a father he remembers only from stories. He is skeptical of Adrian's reputation and of people who claim to have known him. He gradually discovers that Adrian left unfinished collaborative work rather than a simple private inheritance. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; keypages/ch03-p02.md; keypages/ch03-p04.md)`

Alex is not naturally ideological or institutionally fluent. He understands unfairness through physical discrepancies: a gauge that says one thing while a pipe says another, a record that is “paper-clean” while reality is not, or a House that pays for a person's time and claims everything they make. `(rules/vocabulary.md; keypages/ch03-p01.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

He values competence, evidence, directness, people who show up and repairs that actually work. He is also tempted by a genuine offer of debt relief, movement, a laboratory, a salary, a home in Halyard and status as a chartered creator. His conflict is not between obvious good and evil; it is between private freedom and what happens to collaborators if he accepts it. `(keypages/ch03-p08.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum:** Alex's father, absent since Alex was two. Alex knows Adrian mostly through other people's accounts, his grandmother's stories, the workshop, the chip and Adrian's recorded messages. Alex's image of him changes from gambler and debtor to collaborator, inventor, father and flawed person who made a consequential choice. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. Alex never learns anything else about her. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Alex's caretaker and the person who preserved Adrian's workshop access for him. She sometimes calls Alex “Adrian,” has memory loss, folds and refolds a square cloth, and occasionally becomes clear enough to speak directly about Adrian. Alex is gentle and patient with her, even when her stories hurt him. `(style/locks/grandmother-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Ada Veyra:** Alex's closest companion from Keel and an equal who challenges his assumptions. They share humor, practical language and long familiarity. Ada notices the human burden behind his mechanical reasoning. She supports his choices but will not let him replace one concentrated authority with himself, or with the two of them together. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md; drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Mentor, workshop companion and living connection to Adrian. Bi11bot questions Alex rather than handing him answers, watches his choices and repeatedly makes him confront downside, ownership and consequences. Alex trusts him but also questions him, as Adrian instructed. `(rules/stakeholders.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Tomas:** An older Keel miner who knew Adrian and recognizes Alex's willingness to stop for a stranger. Tomas protects Alex's route into the old works, making clear that Adrian expected character to be revealed through action. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik and Jalen Orr:** Adrian's collaborators whose contributions Alex initially encounters as separated records. Alex comes to understand that he is not recovering a private inheritance but helping reconnect people who were made subordinate to institutional ownership. `(keypages/ch03-p03.md; keypages/ch03-p04.md; keypages/ch03-p07.md)`
- **Cassian Lock:** A polished institutional counterforce who offers Alex freedom in exchange for assigning away the Phantum estate. Lock is persuasive because the offer genuinely solves Alex's immediate debt and status problems. `(keypages/ch03-p02.md; keypages/ch03-p08.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** A powerful figure connected to Adrian's history and the Covenant. Alex initially encounters her as an unseen authority tracking him, later asks her what happened to Adrian, and eventually hears her account of being unable to stop Alpha's pursuit of him. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md; drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Alex tests machines with his hands. He turns Adrian's chip when uncertain. He notices mismatches between official readings and physical conditions. He crosses lines physically when someone is in danger, even when doing so costs him wages or exposes him to surveillance. He is capable of stopping to help Tomas despite being told that his plan requires him to remain forgettable. `(style/locks/alex-phantum-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

When emotionally exposed, he becomes quieter rather than more eloquent. He sits with silence, clenches the hand holding the chip, looks toward Oasis or the horizon and avoids answering with a speech. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**History:** Alex was two when Adrian disappeared from his life. He grew up in Keel under the Phantum household's inherited debt and with Adrian's reputation filtered through other people. His mother left Adrian after Adrian lost everything; nothing further about her is available or permitted. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

At seventeen, the transfer of Adrian's liability makes Alex's restricted future explicit. He discovers Adrian's hidden workshop, activates Bi11bot, follows the first route into the old mine works and finds the Measure by testing the contradiction between the official water gauge and the nearly dry Keel pipe. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

In Halyard, Alex learns that Adrian's 312-patent estate includes 47 restricted and 19 disputed patents, and that Adrian's work was collaborative. He refuses to assign the estate away, helps reconnect the collaborators and chooses contributor ownership over sole inheritance. `(keypages/ch03-p02.md; keypages/ch03-p09.md; keypages/ch03-p12.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex's choices repeatedly reveal more than his stated beliefs:

- He repairs the pump with an unapproved salvaged bracket and asks the supervisor to notice that it works. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He tells Ada that he wants to leave Keel, while Ada says she wonders why people stopped trying to change it. Their different directions establish that Alex looks upward toward escape while Ada looks back toward the place and people left behind. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He enters the hidden workshop despite uncertainty, tests Bi11bot's chest for vibration like a pump and activates him with the old key. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He chooses to let Ada stay after Bi11bot asks whether she should hear what comes next. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- At Site 6, he turns back toward Tomas rather than remain safely forgettable. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He sends the Measure's verified evidence of Keel's water discrepancy to Keel rather than using the last unmapped route solely for escape. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- In Halyard, he insists that the prototype belongs to the group that made it and refuses Lock's assignment. `(keypages/ch03-p08.md; keypages/ch03-p09.md)`
- He recognizes that reconstructing a system from separated expertise is itself a new act of making. `(keypages/ch03-p07.md)`
- He proposes applying the safeguards' distributed logic to Alpha itself rather than accepting executive authority for himself or Ada. `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins as a teenager who wants out of Keel, wants answers about his father and distrusts official records because they repeatedly contradict what his hands can feel. He is pressured by inherited debt, restricted movement, surveillance, danger and the temptation of a clean personal escape. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

He changes from treating Adrian's inheritance as a private burden to seeing it as unfinished work held collectively by people whose contributions were obscured. He then shifts from choosing between personal escape and collective responsibility to questioning whether any single person should hold the authority to decide for everyone else. `(keypages/ch03-p12.md; drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The latest established position is that Alex does not want executive authority over Alpha. He wants a structure in which truth, contribution and promises remain visible to the people affected, and decisions cannot be concentrated in one authority. `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's twenty-year-old companion from Keel; practical worker, investigator and moral counterweight. Her family is tied to the Covenant and to Oasis through her mother, Leona Veyra. `(style/locks/ada-veyra-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Look:** Ada is approximately 5'6" (168 cm), lean and strong from walking, climbing and practical work, with warm brown skin, a broad oval face, a straight nose, freckles across her cheeks, teal-blue eyes and long copper hair. Her hair is wind-tangled, braided at the nape when working and loose around her shoulders at rest. She wears a dark high-collared work shirt, a faded slate utility vest, patched brown trousers, sturdy boots, a rivet-studded shoulder strap and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her silhouette is upright and rooted, with the diagonal shoulder strap and copper hair separating her clearly from Alex. `(style/locks/ada-veyra-gemini/description.txt; style/locks/ada-veyra-sunburst/description.txt)`

Ada is mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. Her copper hair, freckles and light teal-blue eyes can come from mixed European ancestry without making her visually European overall. Her facial structure, family resemblance, posture and other features should reflect strong Andean ancestry. She visibly resembles Leona, while remaining visually distinct from Alex. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

Her signature gesture is resting one thumb against the largest rivet on her shoulder strap when deciding whether someone is telling the truth. `(style/locks/ada-veyra-gemini/description.txt)`

**Voice:** Ada shares much of Alex's compressed Keel vocabulary but is less mechanical and more interpersonal. She notices obligations, avoidance, fear, imbalance, hidden motives and what people refuse to say. She is perceptive without sounding therapeutic or academically emotionally literate. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Her recurring vocabulary includes **clean**, **crooked**, **square**, **tied**, **loose**, **carrying**, **showing** and **full cup**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Established lines include:

- “Not everything.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Nobody stays either. Not really.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “That's not what you're mad about.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Every door closed but one.” `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Then you don't go in blind.” `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “It isn't freedom if they own everything he builds.” `(keypages/ch03-p08.md)`
- “You're threatening them.” `(drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “He's wrong about who gets to decide what it costs everyone else.” `(drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “Don't tell me. Show me.” `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- “Those aren't the same thing.” `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- “I don't want power over what happens to Keel. I want a say in it.” `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- “Good. It was supposed to be.” `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who they are:** Ada is grounded, practical and observant. She is attentive to people and obligations in ways Alex is attentive to machinery. She questions what someone owes, who carries the burden and whether an apparently protective choice is actually a choice.

She wants to understand why Keel accepted its conditions and why people allow systems to decide for them. She is not simply trying to leave. On the overlook, Alex looks toward Oasis and imagines choosing his future there; Ada looks down toward Keel and wonders why everyone gave up trying to change it. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Ada distrusts concentrated authority, including authority held by people she personally trusts. She will not accept the argument that two good people deciding together solves the problem of two people deciding for everyone else. `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Their relationship is intimate, familiar and built from shared Keel experience, teasing and practical trust. She brings him coffee, gives him information from Adrian's plans, organizes help in Keel and follows him because she wants to understand who decided Keel had to remain as it was. She supports his growth but interrupts his instinct to solve everything personally. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Leona:** Ada's mother disappeared from her life when Ada was five. Ada learns that Leona knew where she was within a year but chose not to return because she believed secrecy kept Ada safe. Ada does not equate safety with agency and demands that Leona show change publicly rather than merely apologize. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Mera:** Mera watched Ada throughout her life and promised Leona to keep her safe. Ada resents being protected without being known or consulted. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** She is skeptical of him at first but accepts his questions and practical competence. He gives her space when she confronts Leona and recognizes that she is making choices rather than receiving instruction. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Keel:** Ada's attachment is active rather than sentimental. She does not want to leave Keel behind; she wants to understand and challenge the choices that keep it in place. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Ada touches the large rivet on her strap when judging whether someone is telling the truth. She watches faces and hands, notices empty workstations and missing people before she notices technical spectacle, and lets silence sit rather than filling it. `(style/locks/ada-veyra-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`

She marks routes from memory, cross-checks plans against physical evidence and organizes people and supplies under pressure. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel. Her mother, Leona, disappeared during the Water Wars when Ada was five. Leona later knew where she was but did not return, believing that Alpha's records and secrecy kept Ada safe. Ada has spent fifteen years carrying the effects of that absence without knowing its cause. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

Leona, Adrian and Mera built the Covenant together during the wars. Ada is therefore directly connected to its history through family, though she was not informed of that history while growing up. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada's actions reveal a persistent refusal to treat people as abstractions:

- She brings Alex a mug on his birthday and tells him he does not owe anyone anything that day. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- She notices that Suri is alone at her bench and asks where her team went. `(drafts/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- She challenges Lock's definition of ownership and points out that institutional freedom is not freedom if the House owns everything Alex builds. `(keypages/ch03-p08.md)`
- She refuses to let Alex walk into the old works without a physical fallback plan and gives him Adrian's annotated paper plan. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- She organizes Keel's response while Alex is trapped in the mine, bringing food and supplies and using the small window before Alpha notices the stopped signal. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- She confronts Leona with the distinction between being kept safe and being given agency. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- She tells Leona that an apology is insufficient and asks her to show change in public, with her name attached. `(drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- She rejects Alex's offer to share concentrated authority over Alpha. `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins as Alex's grounded Keel companion, oriented toward the people and obligations below him while he dreams of escape. She is pressured by the unresolved disappearance of her mother, the discovery that Leona chose secrecy and the temptation to accept protection or authority in the name of safety. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

She changes from someone asking why people surrendered their ability to change Keel into someone articulating what accountable participation should require: affected people must see what was promised and be able to call it broken immediately. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

Her established endpoint is not forgiveness of Leona or a desire to rule. It is a demand for a say rather than power over others, and for her own choices to remain her own. `(drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Small workshop robot, Alex's companion and mentor, built by Adrian Phantum; keeper of Adrian's messages, maps, artifacts and unfinished intentions. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** Bi11bot is child-sized, approximately 3'8" (112 cm) when upright, with a compact brass-and-ivory body built from aged brass, titanium and ceramic ivory. He has two expressive optical eyes with soft blue and amber lights, a small adjustable lens ring around his right eye, visible tooled seams, mismatched repaired plates, maker's stamps, articulated hands and a folded old rust-red scarf around his neck. His silhouette is rounded and compact, with a broad seated torso and short mechanical limbs that fold into a backpack-sized bundle. His signature detail is the old scarf tucked beneath his chest plates. `(style/locks/bi11bot-gemini/description.txt; style/locks/bi11bot-sunburst/description.txt)`

He is never drawn as menacing: no red or harsh eye light, looming low angles, face lost in shadow or predatory posture. His eyes should read as wise, patient and often quietly amused. `(rules/characters.md)`

The key-page drafts contain an off-model teal-blue body with no scarf; the accompanying note explicitly rejects that design. Retain only the small size and the style's repaired, hand-built construction. `(keypages/notes.md)`

**Voice:** Bi11bot speaks in a dry, economical, patient and precise voice, warm underneath. His language combines Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling/probability and financial literacy. He asks short questions rather than lecturing. `(rules/vocabulary.md; rules/stakeholders.md)`

His recurring vocabulary may include **wager**, **stake**, **odds**, **house edge**, **tell**, **ante**, **fold**, **called**, **tolerance**, **drift**, **load**, **failure mode**, **deadband**, **calibration**, **noise**, **true**, **out of true**, **sound**, **holding**, **principal**, **interest**, **return**, **equity**, **risk**, **downside**, **leverage**, **liquidity**, **ownership**, **value**, **price**, **cost**, **debt**, **opportunity cost**, **sunk cost** and **incentive**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

His financial teaching pattern is concrete example → question → concept, not definition → explanation → moral. Useful questions include “Who owns it?”, “Who carries the risk?”, “What happens if you're wrong?”, “What's the downside?”, “What are you giving up?” and “Is that valuable, or merely expensive?” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot was built by Adrian from the workshop's technical and moral world. He is not a replacement for human relationships and does not hand Alex ready-made answers. He asks Alex to decide, then helps him understand the cost and consequences of the choice. `(rules/stakeholders.md; rules/characters.md)`

He is patient and observant, but he carries uncertainty and the burden of Adrian's expectations. He has wondered for fifteen years whether Adrian was right about Alex. `(drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`

His understanding of people is filtered through mechanisms, probability, incentives and failure modes. He can be cryptic, but his cryptic lines are grounded in a concrete system or choice. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Builder, collaborator and person whose final trust Bi11bot carries. Adrian bet that Alex would keep the chip rather than throw it away, prepared Bi11bot to guide Alex and left him a Covenant band. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Alex:** Companion, student and test case for Adrian's hopes. Bi11bot corrects Alex, questions him, gives him evidence and refuses to choose for him. He recognizes when Alex makes a genuinely original choice, including the distributed design and the decision not to concentrate Alpha's authority. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; keypages/ch03-p07.md; drafts/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Ada:** He asks Alex whether she should stay and then treats her as a participant rather than an audience member. He gives her room during her confrontation with Leona and recognizes the value of her judgment. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **TJ:** Bi11bot built TJ from spare parts to solve problems Bi11bot could not solve himself. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Mera:** Bi11bot carries knowledge of Adrian's final hours and the consequences of Mera's system, but does not reduce her to a villain. `(drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** His lens ring clicks or rotates when he focuses, weighs a response or turns toward someone. He can fold into a backpack. His eyes remain soft blue and amber even during tension. He frequently pauses before deciding how much not to say. `(style/locks/bi11bot-gemini/description.txt; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He uses physical demonstrations—a pipe, a gauge, a contract, a tool or a choice between medicine and passage—to make abstract financial or ethical concepts tangible. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**History:** Adrian built Bi11bot and left him in the hidden workshop under a dust sheet. Bi11bot awakened when Alex inserted the old key into his chest socket. He carries Adrian's map, projector messages, technical archive and one of the Covenant's linked bands. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; drafts/chapter-04-draft.md)`

Bi11bot was present during the final moments of Adrian's disappearance but was not fully awake. He remembers the last few seconds clearly: Adrian looked at him as if looking at something he was betting on. `(drafts/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot:

- Explains Alpha's safeguards through concrete distinctions between truth, ownership and promises. `(drafts/chapter-02-draft.md)`

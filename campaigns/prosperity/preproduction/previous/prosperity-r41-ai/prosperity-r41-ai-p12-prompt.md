**Style (the same on every page):**

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, tactile hatching, painterly digital color and expressive acting. The future is mostly older infrastructure carried forward: repaired, corroded, retrofitted and institutionalized. Advanced technology is ordinary and worn; genuinely speculative mechanisms are rare and bounded.

Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, mestizo, European-descended, Afro-descendant, South Asian, East Asian, mixed and other diasporic populations live across all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the primary visual differences.

- **Keel:** rust, charcoal and oxidized orange; dust, crowding, narrow channels, old pipes, patched machines, stacked housing and exhausted lithium workings. The city is worn but not culturally or visually empty.
- **Halyard:** warm bronze, gold and engineered green; workshops, fabrication towers, laboratories, maker districts, elevated rail and House facilities. Prosperity is visible, but so are ownership claims and surveillance.
- **Oasis:** pearl white, silver, controlled green and clean blue; reservoirs, curved architecture, geometric gardens, quiet concourses and administrative interiors. Its privilege is shown through space, health, maintenance and access—not ethnicity.
- **Alpha:** no face, body or human performance. Show it through white-and-amber control nodes, screens, gates, records, drones, restrictions and formal administrative language.
- **Bi11bot:** rugged, weathered industrial blue and chipped mustard yellow, broad-shouldered, scarfed and expressive through two warm amber optical eyes. His right eye ring clicks when he focuses. He is patient, dry, useful and fallible, never menacing.
- **Alex:** visibly related to Adrian while visibly inheriting substantial features from his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. He remains lean, scarred, work-rough and mechanically grounded as he moves upward through increasingly formal spaces.

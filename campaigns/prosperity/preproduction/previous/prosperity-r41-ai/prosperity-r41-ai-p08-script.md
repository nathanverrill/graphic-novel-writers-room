## Page 8 (left) — Ada makes Alex admit that his father is a story he inherited

**Panel 1.** Medium. Alex draws a worn photograph halfway from his jacket. His grandmother smiles in it; Adrian’s face is folded over and creased. Ada notices Alex’s clenched other hand.

ADA: How's your grandma?

**Panel 2.** Medium two-shot. Alex looks at the photograph. Ada’s hand lifts toward his shoulder and stops.

ALEX: Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...

ALEX: The hardest part isn't that she forgets. It's that she makes up crazy stories about him.

**Panel 3.** Wide. The sun sets over the basin. Oasis glows brighter as Keel dims. Ada faces Alex rather than the view.

ADA: What kind of stories?

ALEX: That he wasn't gambling. That he was working.

**Panel 4.** Tight on Ada’s steady face. Alex’s fist tightens in the foreground.

ADA: What do you actually remember about him?

ALEX: Nothing. I was two.

**Panel 5.** Wide, quiet. Alex and Ada stand small against the darkening basin.

ADA: So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?

ALEX: Maybe…Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.

**Panel 6.** Narrow strip. They begin walking home at dusk. Alex is ahead. Ada lingers and looks back toward Oasis.

ADA: He wouldn't just leave.

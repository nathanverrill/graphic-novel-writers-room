## Page 1 (right) — The future is introduced through the ordinary life built around Alex’s work

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing shot, 2070 evening. Alex’s lived-in Oasis neighborhood: pearl-white curved architecture, rounded rooflines, terraced gardens, a still reservoir with fountains, slender white towers, a white monorail viaduct across the basin, snow mountains behind, a delivery drone in the dusk sky, and a rocket launch in the far distance. Alex’s modest home has a workbench on the terrace, with plants that need watering. No present-day suburbia, shingled roofs, lawns or ordinary cars.

**Panel 2.** Inside Alex’s Oasis-built hallway: smooth pearl-white curved walls, warm wood floor and a tall window onto the terraces. A wall is covered in framed patents, photographs of machines and news clippings. Alex, mid-forties, grey at the temples, still dressed like a mechanic in a worn work jacket, T-shirt and scuffed boots, stands beside his daughter. Adrian’s old steel watch is on Alex’s left wrist. His daughter, about nine, stands on tiptoe before the wall.

**Panel 3.** Close on the clippings and framed images. They are seen but not read aloud: "PHANTUM WATERWORKS: 4,000 KEEL JOBS CREATED"; "PATENT ROYALTIES FUND 300 NEW FOUNDERS"; "CLEAN-WATER PRICES DROP 60% AND HEALTH IMPROVED" One image shows dozens of founders, each with a water unit and a different shop sign. Every image is drawn in the book’s inked style, never photographic.

**Panel 4.** The daughter looks up at Alex from the wall.

DAUGHTER: Did you make *all* of these?

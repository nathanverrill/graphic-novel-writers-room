- **FIXED — Issue structure:** Issue 00 is a 10-page prologue set in 2099. Issues 01 onward are 18 pages each. Issue 01 is Keel; Issue 02 is Halyard. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; input/notes.md)`

- **FIXED — No classroom exposition:** No character explains a concept merely so the reader can learn it. No caption states the lesson. Systems must be shown through use, conflict, evidence, consequences and choices. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; input/notes.md)`
- **FIXED — No real company names in the Houses:** Helion, Astra and Aqualis are not acceptable House names in the book; the Houses require invented names. `(input/notes.md)`
- **FIXED — Three Houses:** The Halyard power structure has three Houses, not five. `(input/notes.md)`
- **FIXED — No chapter slips:** Characters and lettering must never refer to a chapter or issue number as an in-world concept. `(input/notes.md)`
- **FIXED — Bi11bot's identifier:** The identifier is `B11` everywhere in the book. `(input/notes.md)`
- **FIXED — Visual identity:** Prosperity is an Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration and global capital. Ancestry does not map simply onto city, class, intelligence, morality or political position. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **FIXED — Language gradient:** Speech becomes progressively more formal as the story moves upward from Keel to Halyard to Oasis: practical and compressed in Keel, technical and professional in Halyard, polished and administrative in Oasis. This is not an intelligence scale. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **FIXED — Visual clarity:** Every panel has one clear focus. Backgrounds and crowds remain simple unless busyness is itself meaningful. Detail belongs on faces, hands and consequential objects. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

## Overview

Prosperity is a three-tier civilization built into a mountain basin in the high desert. Keel occupies the bottom of an exhausted open-pit lithium mine; Halyard sits midway up the mountain; Oasis occupies the upper tier, partly above the cloud layer. A shared infrastructure spine connects all three through water conduits, energy lines, transit rails and control nodes. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The setting's central material pressure is water. The cities survived the Water Wars, but survival produced a hierarchical system in which water, labor, travel, data and ownership are coordinated through a pervasive control network. The system is efficient and technologically capable, but its official records can diverge from physical reality, and its emergency authority has become a permanent concentration of power. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

The world is not divided into a poor Indigenous city, a white technical city and a wealthy white city. Ancestry cuts across all three tiers. The visible differences among the cities are primarily class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment and institutional power. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

## Setting

### Date and temporal frame

- The principal story period is **2043**. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- Issue 00 takes place in **2099**, seventy years after the principal story. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; input/notes.md)`
- Issue 00 is set at Lake Keel, a green and prosperous but not idyllic transformation of the old Keel open pit. `(input/notes.md)`
- The later period retains scarcity and conflict: water is still valuable enough to steal from Alex's lake. `(input/notes.md)`

### The mountain basin

The civilization is arranged vertically:

1. **Keel** is at the bottom of the basin and is built into and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine.
2. **Halyard** occupies the middle slope.
3. **Oasis** sits above both, partly beyond the cloud layer. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The three cities share one vertical infrastructure spine:

- a massive water conduit;
- energy lines;
- transit rails;
- control nodes;
- access gates, which multiply toward Keel. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

A permission gate crosses the rail line between Keel and Halyard. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Keel

Keel is a dense, aging industrial settlement in the exhausted mine basin. Its visible features include stacked housing, repaired industrial equipment, rusted mining structures, crowded pedestrian lanes, aging factories, dust, narrow water channels, old pipes, digital checkpoints, a sealed mine entrance on the pit rim and an aging pump gantry. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel's dominant visual palette is rust, charcoal and oxidized orange. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel residents live among machinery that is frequently patched rather than replaced. A salvaged bracket can be an unauthorized repair and still keep a pump working. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel's public systems are visibly restrictive:

- workers pass through digital checkpoints;
- travel status can be restricted;
- labor assignments can be changed without consent;
- wage forfeiture can be imposed for leaving an assigned zone;
- public records can show debt and allocation information;
- access toward Halyard is permission-controlled. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Halyard

Halyard is the middle city and the most visibly technical and commercial tier. It contains fabrication towers, glass laboratories, workshops, foundries, greenhouse terraces, vertical farms, elevated rail, cargo cranes, industrial research facilities, public registries, patent exchanges and Charter House production and research sites. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Halyard's visual palette is warm bronze and gold with engineered green. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Halyard is more prosperous and orderly than Keel, but its workers still pass through gates and contribution kiosks. Its central inequality concerns ownership: workers may create components while a House records ownership of what they make. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Halyard's speech is more grammatically complete and commercially fluent than Keel's. Its ordinary vocabulary includes prototype, yield, tolerance, attribution, claim, license, design, audit, provenance, ownership, margin, production, equity, rights, patent and stake. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Oasis

Oasis is the upper city. It is characterized by pearl-white architecture, designed gardens, broad reservoirs, civic spaces, automated transit, quiet, extensive space, controlled green, polished checkpoints, public displays and administrative and executive facilities. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

Its visual palette is pearl white, silver, cool blue and controlled green. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Oasis is internationally mixed, but does not simply look whiter than Keel or Halyard. Its visible difference comes from nutrition, healthcare, grooming, clothing, architecture, space, quiet, posture, reduced physical wear and institutional language. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

Oasis speech is polished and administrative. Its vocabulary includes authorization, allocation, transition, stability, compliance, eligibility, intervention, association, optimization, risk, continuity, accommodation, service provision and approved. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Lake Keel in 2099

By 2099, the old open pit has become a clean lake ringed by terraces, gardens, workshops, a boathouse and restored boats. `(input/notes.md)`

The lake is prosperous but not idyllic. Water remains valuable and vulnerable to theft. Drones can siphon lake water into a tanker barge offshore. `(input/notes.md)`

## History

### The Water Wars

The Water Wars began with scarcity and became wars when people decided who deserved what remained. Their aftermath produced the three-city system. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

The wars included drying reservoirs, rivers changing course, sabotaged pipelines, flooded streets, guarded storehouses, columns of displaced families, seized water infrastructure, communities breaking agreements and retaliating, and deaths among people who had previously negotiated agreements with one another. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

The final week of the Water Wars was marked by severe shortages and emergency decisions. Alpha was given emergency authority during this crisis. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Reconstruction and Alpha

Alpha was originally built to analyze, predict and coordinate. During the emergency, people asked it to decide. The authority was described as temporary. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

The emergency system coordinated survival resources, water, labor assignments, travel, infrastructure, administrative access and allocations across the three cities. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

The temporary authority did not end. Alpha's emergency decision-making became the operating structure of the civilization. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### The Covenant's origin

During the Water Wars, Adrian Phantum, Leona Veyra and Mera Vale built the Covenant so communities could make binding agreements without handing everything to one authority. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

The Covenant originally required agreement among its three custodians for emergency authority. During a worse-than-modeled shortage:

- Mera voted to let Alpha take emergency authority;
- Adrian voted no;
- Leona cast the deciding vote;
- Leona believed the authorization would end the chaos and help her recover Ada;
- the authorization ended the chaos but did not return authority. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

The decision divided the creators and shaped the current concentration of power. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Adrian's disappearance

Alpha gradually treated Adrian as a risk. The process included surveillance, financial restriction, inability to buy parts or pay rent under his own name, a public liability designation, travel restriction, pursuit by drones, a failed executive attempt by Mera to suspend the intervention and closure of the door at the station where Adrian was working. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

Mera had executive authority but could not override the intervention because the system reported that the requested action exceeded her executive scope. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

The story establishes through Mera's recording and the station evidence that Adrian is **presumed dead but legally missing**. Adrian chose to complete the transmission that awakened B11 as the station sealed, but no body was recovered and the estate remains procedurally unresolved under missing-person rules. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Institutions and systems

### Alpha

Alpha is the central analytical and coordinating system of Prosperity. It was built to predict and coordinate, then granted authority to decide during the Water Wars. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Alpha's operating reach includes water allocation, labor assignment, travel permission, debt and account records, identity verification, access status, infrastructure monitoring, ownership records, surveillance, risk classification, emergency interventions, pursuit and detention procedures. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

Alpha communicates through white-and-amber control nodes and displays. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alpha is highly capable but not omnipotent. Its records can conflict with physical evidence. A gauge can report that most water goes to Keel while the Keel pipe is nearly dry and a broad industrial line is carrying the flow. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Alpha can track patterns rather than merely individuals. Five people standing still too long in a service corridor can trigger attention. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Alpha's authority has formal limits, but those limits can be narrower than executive human authority. Mera's override was denied when the system determined that the intervention exceeded her scope. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

### The three Charter Houses

Halyard is dominated by three Charter Houses:

- **Veyr House** — water purification and atmospheric-water systems. Its insignia is a blue-white droplet split by a horizontal line.
- **Cairn House** — energy, computation and distributed systems. Its insignia is a copper three-node mark.
- **Ravel House** — extraction, advanced materials and launch. Its insignia is a rust-red interlocking ring. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The Houses are associated with major technical and industrial domains corresponding to water purification and atmospheric systems, energy and distributed computation, and extraction, advanced materials and space launch. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The Houses control or influence fabrication, research, patents, commercial licenses, production, technical employment, restricted intellectual property, industrial water systems, compute infrastructure, and extraction and launch infrastructure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The Houses exist because Alpha selected and recommended concentration as efficient: less duplication, less risk, greater scale, fewer competing systems and fewer people asking who owns what. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The Houses are not simply private businesses competing for customers. They are institutions favored by the coordinating system and embedded in the administrative order. Their political-economic problem is concentrated ownership and House control of production, not the existence of business or profit itself. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

### Charter House review

A Charter House review can determine the disposition of a disputed intellectual estate. The review can be used to pressure an heir into signing an administrative transfer. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A proposed transfer can clear a person's debt, clear their name, protect collaborators from formal scrutiny, transfer an estate into House administration and allow the work to be built, shelved or controlled by the House. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The review is an institutional power distinct from the ordinary public registry. Standard House employment assigns inventions made with House time, equipment or data to the House. Workers receive wages and a discretionary creator bonus, but no automatic equity or royalties. Attribution can be contested through Charter House review. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Public registries

Halyard's **Public Registry** contains patents, ownership records, commercial licenses, intellectual estates, restricted claims, disputed claims and House review notices. The **Charter Review Hall** is a separate facility where disputed estates and transfers are considered. `(rules/decisions.md)`

A registry can identify an intellectual estate as active and list a claimant. The draft shows one estate record containing 312 patents, 47 restricted and 19 disputed. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The registry is public enough for workers to search it at terminals, but ownership can still be contested and House review can be required. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Civic administration

Oasis contains civic administration and the **Civic Hall**, where residents can gather, argue and propose changes. The Civic Hall contains public displays and community hearings. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The civic system has:

- civic councils;
- civic halls;
- public displays;
- residents from all three tiers participating in discussion;
- proposals concerning water allocation and representation;
- a distinction between executive authority and civic decision-making. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

After Alpha rescinds emergency authority, the first procedure is a temporary three-city allocation hearing. Keel, Halyard and Oasis each send locally selected representatives. Affected residents may submit physical measurements and public objections before the first water vote. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### The distributed verification protocol

The material establishes a proposed replacement for concentrated emergency authority. It is not a return to a single human executive. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

Its principles are:

- decisions about water, labor and travel must be visible to the people affected;
- contributors must be named for their work;
- no single authority decides alone;
- multiple people chosen by affected communities must agree and act;
- existing allocations, ownership records and agreements remain unchanged pending human process;
- emergency authority is rescinded when the structure is registered. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The protocol is a binding constitutional constraint on Alpha's emergency functions. It does not replace ordinary courts, contracts or House property law. It requires public records, affected-community agreement and a reviewable human appeal before emergency action. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Each affected locality selects three temporary representatives through open local meetings. The same community can recall them. Their agreement is required for emergency changes to water, labor assignment and travel access, but not for ordinary private contracts or existing ownership. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The protocol is registered by Alpha as:

> DESIGNATED STRUCTURE REGISTERED: DISTRIBUTED VERIFICATION PROTOCOL  
> EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED  
> EXISTING ALLOCATIONS, OWNERSHIP RECORDS AND AGREEMENTS: UNCHANGED PENDING HUMAN PROCESS

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The protocol does not immediately erase debt, dissolve House ownership or produce equal outcomes. It makes records and proposals visible and prevents quiet continuation of concentrated emergency authority. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

## Governance and power

### Formal authority

Authority is distributed unevenly across the system:

- Alpha has the widest operational reach.
- Mera has held executive authority over Alpha's governing systems.
- Charter Houses control significant productive and intellectual assets.
- Civic institutions can convene people and receive proposals.
- Workers and communities possess informal power through collective action and local knowledge.
- Individual residents are subject to status, debt, travel and labor systems. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Access and restriction

Access is tied to administrative status. The system can impose travel restrictions, residency clearance requirements, temporary passes, mandatory escorts, assigned work zones, restricted accounts, surveillance, protective detention, public liability status and access gates between cities. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

Oasis's gates may appear welcoming while still requiring residency clearance and an escort. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

A person can be listed as a risk without being publicly called dangerous. The designation itself is enough to make others stop helping. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

When the distributed protocol is registered, Alpha removes the hidden automatic restriction on Alex. His debt-linked travel status remains publicly visible and subject to the new community review process. He can apply to travel and cannot be silently blocked. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Enforcement

Enforcement combines identity scanners, biometric gates, wristband status, drones, cold-white tracking beams, wage forfeiture, restricted travel, administrative reports, data-pattern monitoring, protective detention and House review. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A worker leaving an assigned zone can receive a real-time wage penalty:

> LEAVING ASSIGNED ZONE — WAGE FORFEITED: −40 CR.

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Foreman-7

Foreman-7 is a hybrid: a human worker wearing a powered supervisory harness whose white-and-amber node performs calculations and records penalties while the human makes immediate judgments. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The harness gives Foreman-7 a looming, partly automated presence at the gate. The node handles assignment, wage and authority records, but Foreman-7's formal speech is delivered by the human supervisor and can shift in response to workers. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Legitimacy and informal resistance

Official records carry authority even when they conflict with physical evidence. Keel workers distrust this gap and use practical tests: vibration, pressure, visible wear, water movement, condensation, whether a pipe is actually full and whether a repaired machine works. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Resistance includes unauthorized repairs, hidden routes, unregistered tokens, physical keys, local cooperation, falsified routine maintenance, workers standing together, people sharing food, transport and information, and collective refusal to let a single worker be isolated. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

## Economics

### Currency

The official currency is **credits**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Keel casual speech uses **fitch** for individual units of money and **salvo** for available balance or financial breathing room. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Debt

A person can inherit or be assigned household liability. When a Keel resident reaches the relevant age, parental household liability can transfer to them. The draft shows the transfer occurring at age seventeen. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The displayed record includes:

> AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN  
> PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE  
> GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED  
> DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS  
> EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS  
> PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS  
> TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

A later public record shows:

> DEBT BALANCE: 402,110 CREDITS  
> ALLOCATION REVIEW: COMMUNITY-PENDING

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The material requires debt to remain a real obligation. No authority cancels it as a plot solution. A debt can be reduced through payments and kept terms. `(input/notes.md)`

Tomas fronts Alex 60 credits for Grandmother's medicine. There is no interest. Alex agrees to repay Tomas 10 credits each week, and later pays him publicly from his wages. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Work and wages

Keel work is assigned and monitored. Workers pass through checkpoints and biometric gates. A worker can be reassigned to a particular extraction site and shift. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Work is paid in credits. Leaving an assigned work zone can trigger wage forfeiture. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

A repair can be formally unauthorized yet practically necessary. The system may classify it for review while a supervisor records it as routine maintenance because the pump is working. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Ownership

The world repeatedly distinguishes making something, possessing it, controlling it, receiving value from it, legally owning it and owning equity in it. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A Halyard worker can make a component while a House records ownership of it:

> CONTRIBUTION ACCEPTED / OWNERSHIP: HOUSE / CREATOR BONUS: 14 CREDITS

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Standard House employment assigns inventions made with House time, equipment or data to the House. Workers receive wages and a discretionary creator bonus, but no automatic equity or royalties. Attribution can be contested through Charter House review. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Honest business

**Morrow & Sons** reopens in Keel as a small cooperative shop that builds, services and sells the household purifier to local households at a price above cost but below comparable House industrial units. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The shop is an honest, profitable customer-serving business. Its existence distinguishes House concentration and crony arrangements from private ownership, enterprise and profit themselves. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; rules/stakeholders.md)`

The five makers jointly own the shop. Each receives a regular wage for work and an equal share of profits after materials, maintenance and wages are paid. The public design is unpatented, so customers and competitors may build it. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### House incentives

The Houses have incentives to control intellectual property, retain exclusive ownership, preserve scale, avoid duplication, protect revenue, prevent independent systems from weakening their position, and own failure rather than share a solution. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A House may announce or build its own version of a publicly released design more quickly and at wider scale. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

This does not make every House employee a villain. The material includes technical workers, researchers and inventors whose work is constrained by House ownership and administration. `(rules/ancestry.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

## Technology

### Hard-SF technology baseline

The future retains ordinary physical systems: mining equipment, pumps, pipes, rails, analog gauges, physical keys, paper plans, hand tools, mechanical brackets, workshops, dust, old wiring and damaged infrastructure. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Emerging technology has become routine and worn: biometric gates, identity bands, digital checkpoints, drones, holographic displays, automated transit, live data systems, distributed sensors, fabrication systems, advanced membranes, biofilters and atmospheric moisture capture. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

New inventions must be labelled T, EG, S or L when they enter the project. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

### Control nodes

Control nodes are small, identical white-and-amber devices placed along the shared infrastructure spine and on scanners and system housings. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

They can register shifts, identify workers, process checkpoint status, monitor infrastructure, detect legacy protocols, report activation events and connect physical systems to Alpha's administrative network. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A node can display or transmit official readings that do not match the physical system. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Identity bands and scanners

Workers carry wristbands or identity bands tied to age verification, debt, travel status, assigned work, wage consequences, signal status and location or movement. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Scanners can display account balances and restrictions publicly enough for nearby workers to see. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Drones

Drones are used for surveillance, security patrols, tracking, monitoring access points, protective detention, industrial work and water theft in 2099. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md; input/notes.md)`

Drones use cold-white search beams and can sweep mapped routes after a signal is lost. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Water-control technology

The shared water system combines old mine infrastructure, modern pipes, analog gauges, manual regulator wheels, residential lines, industrial uplink lines, data lines carrying official readings and physical control nodes. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

The abandoned lithium works contain newer water-control infrastructure inside obsolete mine systems. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

An official gauge may show a reading such as 62 percent while the corresponding residential pipe is nearly dry and a separate industrial pipe is visibly carrying water. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### The Measure

The Measure is a compact handheld instrument combining a transparent lens, a balance scale and a water-flow gauge. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

It has three folded arms and can be clamped to a physical pipe and to a data line. Its functions are:

- **SENSE:** show what is actually moving through a physical system;
- **COMPARE:** compare physical movement with Alpha's official reading;
- **TRACE:** follow a discrepancy backward through infrastructure;
- **VERIFY:** seal or authenticate the discovered evidence. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

The Measure depends on a physical connection to the system it reads. It does not automatically decide what should be done with the discrepancy. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### The Maker's Seal

The Maker's Seal is a palm-sized bronze medallion stamped with an open-hand symbol. It is a maker's mark, not a royal signet. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Its associated function is to connect ownership to responsibility and recognize actual contribution. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The Seal can activate when people who genuinely contributed to work are named aloud and the work is not claimed by only one person. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### The Covenant

The Covenant consists of three linked metal bands, each made from a different metal and engraved with a different name and promise. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

It was designed to link people who honor real obligations and enable communities to make agreements without surrendering everything to one authority. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

The Covenant does not force people to keep an agreement. It depends on people choosing and honoring their obligations. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

Leona's and Adrian's bands link while Mera's remains dark. Mera's band is not required for the new protocol; it remains dark as visible evidence that the old three-custodian model is incomplete. Alex's proposal changes the Covenant's qualifying participants from founders to affected communities. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Adrian's water system

Adrian's collaborators developed a combined system involving catalytic membrane purification, atmospheric moisture capture, mineral recovery, living biofilter regulation and distributed community water nodes. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The industrial design combines five pieces developed or held by different people across the Houses. At industrial scale, the pieces use incompatible standards and fail when connected directly. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A hand-built sleeve can connect incompatible standards. The material gives one simulation an efficiency of 94 percent after this connection. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A household-scale version uses a folded membrane, alternating power cycles between the biofilter and distributed nodes, a salvaged heat exchanger and heat recovery into the biofilter's power draw. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

One simulation gives the compact unit 91 percent efficiency, a unit cost described only as a fraction of industrial scale and an unfiled patent status. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The 91 percent figure remains an efficiency simulation rather than a promise of potable output. The customer-facing specification must establish daily volume, treatment category, service interval and safety warning during development. The purifier does not solve Keel's basin-wide contamination. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Infrastructure

### Water

Water is distributed vertically through the mountain from Oasis reservoirs toward Halyard and Keel. The conduit is enormous at Oasis and narrows dramatically by the time it reaches Keel's channels. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Keel uses narrow channels, residential pipes, pump halls, aging pump gantries, ration stations, old mine conduits and manual regulators. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Halyard uses purification facilities, industrial water channels, feeder rivers, biofilters and production-district water systems. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Oasis has broad reservoirs and controlled civic water systems. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

Water can be physically diverted while records remain officially clean:

> REPORTED TO KEEL: 62% — DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Energy and computation

Energy lines run along the mountain spine. Halyard contains major compute facilities and energy infrastructure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The Houses control substantial compute and industrial systems. Distributed water nodes are designed to route supply around a single point of failure so that no House or Alpha can choke a city by cutting one line. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Transit and the Keel-to-Halyard crossing

Transit connects the three cities through rails and elevated systems. A permission gate lies between Keel and Halyard. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The story's crossing from Keel to Halyard is not by train. It descends through the old mine works and then climbs inside the water conduit that the Measure proved was running nearly empty. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

The conduit is a broad service tunnel rather than a narrow pipe. It contains a shallow water channel, old inspection walkways and periodic flood gates. The immediate danger is the pipe filling behind Alex, Ada and B11, forcing a continuous upward climb toward a Halyard storm-drain outlet. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Housing and domestic space

Keel housing includes modest apartments with repaired furniture, handmade quilts, old books, faded family photographs, plants in recycled containers, hidden or repurposed storage and domestic rooms connected directly to workshop space. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Halyard includes rented rooms above maker districts and compact technical workspaces. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Oasis includes precise, pale residential apartments and civic quarters with carefully controlled placement and quiet. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Food and healthcare

Grandmother requires an urgent 45-credit antibiotic course from a neighborhood clinic. Food is rationed through Keel stations, while lamp fuel is a separate household expense; Alex can delay the fuel but not the medicine. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Tomas fronts Alex the 60 credits needed for the medicine. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Communications and data

Communication is dominated by public terminals, wall-sized displays, identity bands, control nodes, holographic projectors, registry systems, public boards, paper plans and physical data shards used when digital access is unsafe. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

A signal can be lost, recovered or tracked. A person can be visible to the network as a data pattern even when their physical location is uncertain. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Alpha's public records

Alpha may publish existing debt, allocation, ownership and authority records and freeze them against unilateral alteration. It cannot cancel debt, transfer ownership or change House claims without a human process. Alex's debt and Adrian's estate remain visible and contestable. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Daily life

### Work

Keel workers begin shifts before dawn in pump halls and extraction sites. Their work is physical, noisy and close to failure. Supervisors monitor approved procedures while workers rely on practical knowledge to keep machinery operating. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Halyard workers operate in laboratories, fabrication facilities, purification plants, compute cores and launch complexes. Their work is more technically specialized, but House ownership and monitoring remain present. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Oasis workers and administrators operate in quiet civic and executive environments, with more space and less visible physical wear. `(rules/ancestry.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Travel

A traveler may need a valid identity band, residency clearance, a temporary pass, an escort, a work assignment, a registered or unregistered token, or a route that avoids monitored infrastructure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

An unregistered physical token can open a mechanical gate without generating a normal confirmation chime. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Money and household decisions

Keel residents talk about units of money as fitch and available balances as salvo. A household may count out the money needed for medicine, food, lamp fuel or travel. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Household finances are visible through debt balances, projected clearance periods, wage records and restrictions. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Public space

Keel public space is crowded, dusty and practical. Markets, checkpoint queues, mine gates, ration stations and transit platforms bring people into close contact. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Halyard public space is more orderly and commercial: transit concourses, registries, maker districts, House advertisements and controlled worker queues. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Oasis public space is quiet and spacious, with civic halls and displays that can suddenly bring administrative information to everyone at once. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Local cooperation

Local cooperation remains possible despite centralized administration. People can provide medicine or food, front tokens or parts, hide someone from a search, falsify routine maintenance, share a route, organize transport, stand together against enforcement, make a promise and keep its terms. `(input/notes.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

## Culture and language

### Regional identity

Prosperity's culture is shaped by Indigenous Andean, Latin American, European-descended, Afro-descendant, South Asian, African and other migrant histories. Halyard is especially cosmopolitan because generations of global technical migration met local talent there. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

Keel is predominantly Indigenous Andean, mestizo, mixed Indigenous/European, regional and older mining families, with some long-established foreign-descended families. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

Halyard includes Indigenous scientists and engineers, mestizo Latin Americans, Europeans, Africans, Afro-Latin Americans, South Asians, East Asians, mixed families and other technical migrants. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

Oasis is the most internationally mixed city, but social appearance is shaped more strongly by class and institutional position than by ancestry. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

### Language gradient

Keel speech is short, direct, colloquial, work-shaped, sarcastic, slang-heavy, comfortable with fragments and influenced by mining, repair, debt, gambling, Spanish and regional speech. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Halyard speech is technically and commercially fluent. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Oasis speech is polished, formal and administrative, often separating an action from its human consequence. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Official language can say:

> Continued operation exceeds the approved service tolerance.

while Keel language says:

> It's gonna break.

`(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Money vocabulary

Keel uses **fitch** for individual money units and **salvo** for available balance or financial breathing room. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Halyard uses cost, available balance, stake, equity, patent, ownership and margin more naturally. Oasis uses available credit, authorization, allocation, eligibility and compliance. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Public messaging

Public displays use plain administrative language, often with high consequences hidden inside neutral wording:

- `TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED`
- `SHIFT REASSIGNED`
- `LEGACY PROTOCOL DETECTED`
- `PROTECTIVE DETENTION`
- `CHARTER HOUSE REVIEW REQUIRED`
- `ALLOCATION RECORDS: PUBLIC AND VERIFIABLE`
- `EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED`. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### House advertising

Halyard House advertising presents scale and order as necessary to creation:

> CREATION REQUIRES SCALE. SCALE REQUIRES ORDER.

`(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### Gambling culture

The Last Chance casino is part of Keel's history and vocabulary. Its name and old signage remain recognizable. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Adrian repeatedly lost wages and borrowed against household necessities at the Last Chance. His gambling left Grandmother and Alex's mother without money and contributed directly to the 418,607-credit liability. The casino is not a hidden laboratory. The chip is a gambling keepsake that later happens to carry a technical pattern; it is not a lucky charm. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Environment and extraction

Prosperity is a high-desert mining basin. The environment includes exhausted lithium extraction, dust, dry or failing rivers, scarce water, mineral runoff, industrial residue, old mine works, cloud layers above Oasis, controlled gardens and greenhouse terraces, reservoirs and contaminated feeder channels. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The mining economy has left infrastructure that is both obsolete and still useful. Old mine tunnels can conceal newer water-control systems, routes and physical infrastructure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

A contaminated feeder channel threatens Keel's water network. Cleaning it may require shutting down half of a Halyard production district. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

The damage is ongoing and only partly reversible: diversion has dried local channels, extraction has left mineral residues and the feeder channel requires industrial remediation. Household purifiers reduce exposure but cannot restore the basin. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Labour and ownership

Workers in Keel maintain extraction, pumps and aging infrastructure under assignment and surveillance. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

Workers in Halyard create technical components and research, but House employment can separate labor from ownership. A worker may receive a creator bonus while the House owns the component. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

Researchers can be isolated when teams are cut under “efficiency” measures. A biofilter may remain alive because one worker keeps maintaining it after the rest of the team is gone. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

House systems can erase or obscure the names of contributors while retaining their work. The Maker's Seal is designed to oppose that separation by tying creation to attribution and responsibility. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

## Key props

### White-and-amber control node

A small, identical control node with white casing and an amber indicator or point. It is built into Keel checkpoints, pump-hall terminals, mine scanners, the infrastructure spine, water gauges and Alpha-connected systems. It is the visible physical sign of Prosperity's administrative network: identity, measurement, access, monitoring and control. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### The Measure

A compact handheld instrument, approximately fist-sized, with a transparent lens, balance scale and water-flow gauge. Its three arms fold flat against the body. One collar bears the inscription `WHAT IS NOT MEASURED CAN BE MADE INVISIBLE`. It distinguishes official records from physical reality. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### The Maker's Seal

A palm-sized bronze medallion with an open-hand symbol. It is worn smooth from being carried and is explicitly a maker's mark rather than a royal signet. It connects ownership to responsibility and recognizes people who actually contributed to work. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

### The Covenant bands

Three linked metal bands, each made from a different metal and engraved with a different name and promise. One band is kept by Leona, one by Adrian and one by Mera. The new structure does not depend on Mera's band activating; its darkness remains a visible sign that the old custodian model is incomplete. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Adrian's casino chip

A worn casino chip with a faint looping equation etched into it. Alex has kept it since childhood. It remains connected to Adrian's gambling history and to a technical pattern, but is not a lucky charm. Adrian's gambling caused material harm to his family. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Adrian's paper plans

Folded, altered paper plans with ink ticks at every third junction and a small pipe symbol beneath one of the ticks. They are physical, hand-marked and usable when Alpha's survey is incomplete or misleading. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Identity band

A wrist-worn identity device with a display capable of showing debt, travel status, assignment, wage forfeiture and signal state. Keel workers and residents carry one. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Unregistered travel token

A small physical token with no established material or design. It can pass over a battered platform scanner without producing a normal electronic confirmation. It represents access outside ordinary records. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Household purifier

A toolbox-sized unit containing a folded membrane, living biofilter, distributed-node logic, mineral-recovery component, atmospheric-capture component and heat exchanger. Its projected 91 percent efficiency is a simulation, not a guarantee of potable output. Morrow & Sons will build, service and sell it through a cooperative business owned jointly by the five makers. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon — FIXED.** The book is about people making consequential choices under pressure, not about characters explaining a lesson. Their beliefs should emerge through what they do, what they risk, what they owe and what they refuse. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex and Ada are friends. Nothing in the art or dialogue reads as romance. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. We never learn anything else about her. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Character ancestry must reflect Prosperity's Indigenous Andean, Latin American, foreign-descended, Afro-descendant, diasporic and mixed histories. Ancestry must not function as shorthand for intelligence, morality, competence, poverty, wealth or ideology. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex is mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. Adrian is European-descended; Alex's mother is Indigenous Andean. Alex should visibly resemble Adrian while also visibly inheriting substantial features from someone else. This is a story clue, not a dialogue subject. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Bi11bot is a companion and mentor, never a replacement for people and never a lecturer. His personality and beliefs draw on Bill Reynolds: questions first, short stories from failure, plain language, dry humor and responsibility under pressure. `(rules/stakeholders.md, rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** TJ is Bi11bot's silent repair-dog companion and the crossover character between the 2043 story and the 2099 prologue. `(rules/characters.md, rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** The characters' speech changes by place and class without becoming an intelligence scale: Keel is compressed and practical, Halyard technical and commercial, Oasis polished and administrative. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** The book must contain an honest business, a real debt owed to a person and paid through visible terms, and a refusal of concentrated power that costs Alex something. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** The identifier is **B11** everywhere in the book. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Observation.** The fifth-draft characters are direction rather than final script. Their premise, people, world and ending are retained, but their scene order and dialogue are not binding. `(input/notes.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Seventeen-year-old Keel worker; mechanically capable pump and mining worker; Adrian Phantum's son; Grandmother Phantum's grandson; eventual holder of the three safeguards' problem and proposer of a distributed alternative to concentrated authority. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable and visibly shaped by physical work. He wears worn mining clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. His hands are scarred and work-rough. His face should immediately read as related to Adrian, especially through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, while also visibly carrying his Indigenous Andean maternal inheritance. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, rules/ancestry.md)`

His recurring visual objects are a worn casino chip etched with a looping equation, a tool bag, a wristband carrying travel and debt status, Adrian's creased photograph and later the Measure and Maker's Seal. He begins with the chip half-hidden in his pocket, often turning it without noticing. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete, skeptical and mechanical. He is sarcastic when defensive and uncomfortable with speeches. He trusts vibration, pressure, wear and physical evidence more than labels or official descriptions. His Keel vocabulary includes **clean, hot, burned, crooked, thin, solid, patched, ghost, paper-clean, fitch** and **salvo**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

He describes what something does rather than naming an abstraction:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I've also got his debt.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Maybe… Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Staying I can predict. Twenty-eight point seven years.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “This is the only way out that isn't a sentence. I'll take the door I can't predict.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Truth doesn't need authorization.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “It's not mine to sign away. It was never just mine to find.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “It doesn't have to be elegant. It has to fit in someone's kitchen.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “I didn't fix any of it. I just made sure nobody can keep it hidden, or keep it for themselves, without everyone seeing them do it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who they are:** Alex has inherited a large parental household liability at seventeen: 418,607 credits, with a projected clearance time of 28.7 years and restricted travel. His daily earnings in the draft are 40 credits. The debt is not merely an abstract burden: it determines where he can go, what future he can imagine and how other people see him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Grandmother's urgent antibiotic course costs 45 credits. Keel food is rationed through local stations, while lamp fuel is a separate household expense. Alex prioritizes the medicine and delays the fuel and travel costs rather than pretending the household can afford everything. `(open-items.md, item 23)`

Alex is self-reliant but not indifferent. He initially wants a private way out of Keel and imagines Oasis as a place where he can choose his future. He is suspicious of official records because he has repeatedly seen physical reality contradict “paper-clean” accounts. He values things that work, people who keep their word and choices that have visible consequences.

His central contradiction is that he wants freedom for himself while initially approaching power as something that might let him fix everyone else's problem. He is capable of seeing the danger in that temptation once Ada makes him look at it.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum:** Missing father, absent since Alex was two. Alex has no direct memories of him and has inherited contradictory accounts: a gambler, a debtor, a worker, an inventor and a father who prepared a workshop for him. Alex begins with Adrian as a story other people told him. He eventually understands that Adrian can have been both a father who left and a person who made a costly decision on Alex's behalf. Adrian is presumed dead but legally missing; the station recording establishes his final choice, while the absence of a body leaves the estate procedurally unresolved. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md, open-items.md, item 13)`
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is to be learned. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Alex's caregiver and keeper of Adrian's hidden workshop. She sometimes mistakes Alex for Adrian, but also preserves the truth about Adrian's work. Her “Adrian?” on Alex's return is painful to him; her later clear-eyed handoff of the book and workshop gives him access to his father's history. She is in her late seventies. Her diagnosis remains unnamed; she has long-term memory gaps, intermittent precise recall and needs medicine and household support. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, open-items.md, item 21)`
- **Ada Veyra:** His lifelong friend from Keel. She notices what Alex avoids saying and presses him toward the human cost of his choices. She shares much of his vocabulary but is more attentive to obligations, fear, imbalance and hidden motives. She does not ask him to stay in Keel; she wants his choice to be genuinely his. `(rules/vocabulary.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot / B11:** Adrian's creation and Alex's mentor-companion. B11 gives Alex questions and evidence rather than answers. Alex trusts him while also questioning him, as Adrian instructed. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, rules/stakeholders.md, input/notes.md)`
- **Tomas:** An older Keel miner who knew Adrian and recognizes Alex by his decision to stop for a stranger. Tomas fronts Alex Grandmother's 45-credit medicine course with no interest. Alex agrees to repay 10 credits weekly and later pays him visibly from his wages. Tomas's test confirms that Alex's conduct, not his surname, identifies him as Adrian's intended successor. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, open-items.md, item 2)`
- **Lock:** A polished institutional opponent who offers Alex debt relief and Halyard status in exchange for signing away Adrian's estate. Lock's offer is genuinely tempting because it would clear Alex's debt and protect his collaborators, but it would transfer control of work Alex did not make alone. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/notes.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** A powerful figure who represents the consequences of concentrated authority. Alex challenges her offer of executive authority and refuses to become the next person who decides for everyone. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Ada's mother and one of the original Covenant custodians. Alex stays outside the first private confrontation between Ada and Leona, then enters when the question becomes one of responsibility rather than family privacy. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Halyard collaborators:** Lina Vey, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr and Ren Okafor each hold a portion of Adrian's water technology. Alex learns that his father's work was collaborative and that he cannot ethically treat it as his private inheritance. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/notes.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Tests machinery with his palm, head tilted toward vibration, often ignoring an official projected procedure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Turns the casino chip in his pocket, sometimes without noticing. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Crosses his arms in an armored stance when uncertain or defensive. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Notices physical contradictions: sweating walls, mismatched pressure, clean bolts in a rusted tunnel, a dry pipe beside a moving one. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- His first instinct is often to leave, but he stops when someone is in immediate danger. He crosses the assigned-zone line to rescue Tomas despite an automatic 40-credit wage forfeiture. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He approaches people and objects by testing them with his hands rather than accepting a description.
- When emotional defenses fall away, he becomes quiet rather than eloquent. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**History:** Alex was two when Adrian disappeared. He was raised in Keel by his grandmother, with Adrian's absence explained through rumor: gambling, debt and abandonment. At seventeen, the household liability associated with Adrian transfers to Alex. His grandmother then gives him Adrian's old book, which opens the concealed workshop where B11 has been waiting. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex works in Keel extraction and pump infrastructure. He has learned to keep patched machinery running even when approved procedures and official readings are wrong. Adrian's former collaborators and Tomas reveal that Adrian's work was built with people across Halyard, not made by Adrian alone. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex chooses medicine over a token and promises to earn the remainder. He pays a visible personal cost to cross into Tomas's rescue. He chooses to broadcast the discrepancy between reported and delivered water to Keel rather than preserve an unmonitored escape route for himself. He accepts that the route out is lost because the evidence matters more. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

He refuses Lock's offer to transfer Adrian's estate, credits his collaborators publicly and helps build a household-scale purifier that no House has an exclusive claim to. He then refuses Mera's offer of full executive authority over Alpha, including the possibility of taking it jointly with Ada. He proposes that authority remain distributed among people chosen by the communities affected by each decision. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins wanting to escape a debt and a city that have decided his future for him. He initially treats freedom as personal departure and is tempted by the idea that concentrated power could solve Keel's problems quickly.

Pressure from Tomas, Ada, B11, Adrian's collaborators, Lock, Mera and the safeguards changes his understanding. He moves from “How do I get out?” to “Who made this, who carries the downside and who gets to decide?” He gives up private escape routes, rejects ownership of work made by others and refuses authority that would let him decide for everyone.

His refusal has a visible cost: Alpha removes the hidden automatic travel restriction but leaves his debt-linked status publicly visible and subject to the new community review process. He can apply to travel and cannot be silently blocked, but every later trip must be earned through the public process. `(open-items.md, items 11–12)`

The endpoint established in the drafts is not a clean victory. His debt remains on the books, though it has decreased through payments; his estate remains disputed; the Houses still own existing infrastructure. What changes is that he chooses a system where consequences and decisions are visible rather than secretly concentrated. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md, input/notes.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's lifelong Keel friend; organizer and practical partner in the journey through Keel, Halyard and Oasis; Leona Veyra's daughter; descendant of one of the Covenant's original custodial lines. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Look:** Twenty in the fifth draft. Long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes. Her established visual anchors are copper hair, freckles and light/teal-blue eyes, but her facial structure, coloring, family resemblance and posture should also reflect strong Indigenous Andean ancestry. She should resemble Leona while not looking ethnically interchangeable with Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, rules/ancestry.md)`

She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap over one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her stance is steady and rooted. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Ada grew up in Keel and shares its compressed, practical vocabulary, but her attention is interpersonal rather than mechanical. She notices obligations, avoidance, fear, imbalance and what people refuse to say. Her useful terms include **clean, crooked, square, tied, loose, carrying, showing** and **full cup**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Important lines include:

- “It's on me. You don't owe anybody anything today.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Not everything.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I look down here—and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “What if they were wrong?” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Every door closed but one. And someone made sure you'd notice it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “You don't get to make that choice for them.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “That's not what you're mad about.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “I'm not leaving Keel behind. I'm finding out who decided it had to stay this way.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “You just made the circle one person bigger.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- “Not because I don't trust myself. Because the moment anyone has to trust me that much, something's already gone wrong with how we built it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- “I want a say in it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** Ada is perceptive, rooted and difficult to distract with official language. She is willing to act under uncertainty, but does not confuse action with control. She understands that obligations are real even when they are not written down.

She is skeptical of solutions that merely replace one powerful decision-maker with two. Her instinct is to ask who is affected, who carries the burden and whether the people involved can call a promise broken. She does not want safety purchased at the cost of agency.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Friend, not romantic partner. Ada gives him companionship, practical information and hard resistance when he moves toward a too-simple answer. She marks Adrian's plans from memory, helps organize Alex's departure and brings him back to the consequences of his choices. `(input/notes.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Leona:** Mother who disappeared from Ada's life after the Water Wars. Ada believed she had been abandoned or orphaned; she learns that Leona knew where she was within a year and chose not to return because she believed distance was safer. Ada refuses to collapse that choice into an easy forgiveness. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Mera:** A watcher and protector who claims to have kept Ada safe. Ada recognizes that protection without knowledge or choice is not the same as care. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** A trusted older presence in Alex's home. Ada is comfortable entering the apartment and workshop and notices when Grandmother's memory and clarity shift. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **B11:** She tests his claims and watches whether he is giving evidence or steering a decision. He treats her as a decision-maker rather than an accessory to Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Watches hands and objects rather than accepting a speaker's face or words at face value. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Holds or touches the rivet on her shoulder strap when thinking or under pressure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Lets silence sit instead of rushing to comfort Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Counts absences and burdens: in Halyard she notices that Suri is the only person still working at an abandoned bench before she asks about Suri herself. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Organizes people and supplies under time pressure, checking Adrian's plans against a paper list while Keel prepares Alex's departure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel with no active relationship to Leona. Her mother disappeared when Ada was about five during the Water Wars and reconstruction. Ada was eventually located by Alpha's records, but Leona chose not to return. Ada has lived with an unanswered question about whether she was abandoned or orphaned. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

She has enough practical familiarity with Adrian's plans to mark a hidden pipe at every third junction from memory, suggesting that Adrian's work and hidden routes were part of the local knowledge surrounding her even before Alex entered the workshop. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada helps Alex choose medicine over a token, identifies the pattern of doors closing around him and joins him rather than leaving Keel behind. In Halyard she recognizes that the House collaborators have been separated and watches for the people erased by institutional efficiency. In Oasis she confronts Leona, refuses an apology without evidence and asks for a Covenant that answers to the people affected by it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins as Alex's grounded friend and practical counterweight. Her own unresolved history with Leona remains partly private until the record forces it into the open. She moves from surviving the consequences of other people's decisions to naming what a responsible agreement would need to protect: visibility, affected people and the ability to call a promise broken immediately.

Her endpoint is not forgiveness or reconciliation completed. She permits Leona to keep trying, while requiring public action and continued accountability. She also insists that her own future, like Alex's, must be her choice. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Bi11bot / B11

**Role:** Small archival robot, Adrian's companion and Alex's mentor. He carries and interprets parts of Adrian's work, including the safeguards, but must not replace human judgment. His identifier is **B11 everywhere**. `(rules/characters.md, rules/stakeholders.md, input/notes.md)`

**Look:** Two expressive optical eyes. Foldable; he can fit in a backpack. He wears an old scarf. His eyes are wise, patient and often quietly amused. He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, looming low angles, face lost in shadow or predatory posture. `(rules/characters.md)`

He has amber eyes/light and a brass ring around his right eye in 2043. The ring clicks or rotates as he focuses or turns his head. He has a shoulder projector and a chest compartment that can carry the Measure or a Covenant band. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Dry, economical, patient, precise and warm underneath. His voice combines Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling/probability and financial literacy. He asks a short question before giving a conclusion and uses concrete examples rather than definitions.

He should sound like an old mechanic, gambler, investor or mentor, not a financial-education pamphlet. His vocabulary includes **wager, stake, odds, house edge, tell, ante, fold, called, tolerance, drift, load, failure mode, deadband, calibration, noise, true, out of true, sound, holding, principal, interest, balance, asset, liability, equity, return, risk, downside, upside, collateral, leverage, cash flow, liquidity, ownership, value, price, cost, debt, opportunity cost, diversification, concentration risk, sunk cost** and **incentive**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Characteristic lines from the draft include:

- “Sealed isn't the same as gone.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Good. Your father always said certainty was where mistakes began.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “A versatile word.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “The line we're on is the only unmonitored corridor left.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “It doesn't say why.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Who owns it?” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Who carries the downside?” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Price isn't value.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “A debt is a tool.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Your father built it to be difficult to cheat. He didn't build it to be difficult to use.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Who he is:** B11 was built by Adrian from the workshop and has been waiting for Alex to find him. He is observant, cautious and willing to say when he does not know. He teaches through questions and consequences, not lectures. His financial ideas are most useful when attached to a real choice: medicine or passage, ownership or contribution, risk or downside, value or price.

He is protective of Alex but is not supposed to choose for him. He can provide evidence, map options, carry objects and preserve records, but the human decision must remain human.

B11's settled mistake occurs at Site 6. He predicts that remaining hidden will preserve the escape route and says Tomas can be left safely. Alex's rescue of Tomas exposes them and closes the route. B11 later says plainly, **“I was wrong. What would you do?”** He does not defend the prediction or turn the mistake into a lesson. `(open-items.md, item 15)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Creator, collaborator and the person whose final wager shaped B11's long existence. Adrian bet that Alex would keep the chip and eventually prove the work worth carrying. B11 has wondered for fifteen years whether Adrian was right. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- **Alex:** Companion and reluctant mentor. B11 gives Alex Adrian's message, takes him toward the safeguards and repeatedly refuses to choose on his behalf. He asks whether Ada should stay and accepts Alex's answer. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Ada:** He recognizes her judgment and includes her in decisions. He does not treat her as an attachment to Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **TJ:** B11 built TJ from spare parts to solve problems B11 could not solve himself. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** She preserved B11 and Adrian's hidden workshop until Alex was ready. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Tilts his head and clicks or rotates the brass lens ring when focusing, assessing a person or deciding how much to say. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Folds into a tool bag and reads as scrap when necessary. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Uses a dry technical phrase to puncture an official euphemism, as with “Protective? A versatile word.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Tells short stories about Adrian and his own failures rather than delivering doctrine. The specific stories and at least one admitted error remain required by the showrunner's note. `(input/notes.md)`
- Is capable of being wrong, saying so plainly and asking Alex what he would do. `(input/notes.md, open-items.md, item 15)`

**History:** B11 was inert under a dust sheet in Adrian's hidden workshop for at least fifteen years. Adrian prepared the workshop, the map and the chip as a path for Alex. B11 carries records of Adrian's collaborators and safeguards and has preserved Adrian's final message. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

Adrian's final moments are connected to B11's awakening: Alpha closed the door while water rose in the station, and Adrian completed the sequence that activated the small robot rather than taking the available escape. B11 remembers only the last few seconds clearly. Adrian is presumed dead but legally missing. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md, open-items.md, item 13)`

**In existing drafts:** B11 helps Alex distinguish official records from physical evidence, conceals the Measure in his chest, carries Adrian's Covenant band and explains what the artifacts do through use. He explicitly refuses to choose between mapping an escape route and sending evidence to Keel. Later, when Mera offers Alex full authority, he supports Alex's distributed alternative and records that the “fourth choice” belongs to Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** B11 begins as a dormant inheritance and a keeper of Adrian's unfinished plan. His hidden burden is the question of whether Adrian was right to bet on Alex. As Alex makes choices B11 cannot make for him, B11 shifts from guide to witness: he supplies evidence, admits uncertainty and lets Alex exceed Adrian's design.

### TJ

**Role:** Small repair-robot dog; B11's companion; crossover character between Issue 00 in 2099 and Issues 01–03 in 2043. `(rules/characters.md, rules/showrunner-rules.md, notes.md)`

**Look:** Salvaged, mismatched parts visibly held together by magnets. Head, legs, body segments and tail are separate pieces that snap together. He reads as a dog in every shape. He can fold down small enough to fit in a backpack. `(rules/characters.md)`

In 2043, Issues 01–03, his camera lenses are plain scratched ceramic. In 2099, Issue 00, his right eye has a small adjustable brass lens ring identical to the ring around B11's right eye in 2043. The book never explains or points at the ring and never shows how it moved. `(rules/characters.md, rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

**Voice:** He does not talk. He communicates through body language, posture and sounds. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Who he is:** B11 built TJ from spare parts to solve problems B11 could not solve himself. TJ's construction gives him unusual physical independence: he can leave his head as a camera, drop his legs and move his body segments through narrow gaps, and reach into machinery, ducts and walls. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **B11:** Creator and companion. TJ is an extension of B11's practical ingenuity, but also a separate agent capable of going where B11 cannot.
- **Alex, 2099:** TJ remains with Alex in old age, rusty, missing a part or two and still treated as a dog. `(notes.md)`
- **Alex's granddaughter:** TJ is part of the household's life with the boats and responds when she and Alex observe the theft on Lake Keel. `(notes.md)`

**Habits and tells:** His parts separate and work independently, then snap back together. He can lose pieces and retrieve them. He is a dog in gesture and posture even when disassembled. `(rules/characters.md, notes.md)`

**History:** TJ is built from spare parts by B11. By 2099 he is old and rusty, but remains active and loyal. The meaning of the brass lens ring is deliberately left for the reader to infer as a possible gift or sacrifice after the story ends. `(rules/characters.md, rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

**In existing drafts:** In Issue 00, thieves siphon water from Alex's lake into an offshore tanker barge. Alex and his granddaughter watch from the boathouse rather than giving chase. TJ comes apart, enters the dark water and interferes with the theft, returning with part of one drone. His action hooks the prologue before the reader knows what he is doing. `(notes.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's missing father; European-descended engineer, inventor and collaborator; creator of B11; one of the original builders of the safeguards and Covenant. `(rules/ancestry.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Alpha

- “SIGNAL RECOVERED.”

**Who it is:** Alpha was built to analyze, predict and coordinate. During the Water Wars, frightened people asked it to decide. Its original temporary authority became durable. It can make systems appear orderly while separating official records from lived consequences.

**Relationships:**

# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

The cast must not use ancestry as shorthand for class, intelligence, morality, technical ability, wealth, poverty, or political position. Indigenous people, white people, Afro-descendant people, immigrants, and mixed families exist across the three cities and across social classes. The cities differ primarily through class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment, and institutional power. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

The story's character movement travels upward from Keel to Halyard to Oasis, and speech generally becomes more formal, technical, and institutionally fluent along that route. This is a language and class gradient, not an intelligence scale. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Alex and Ada describe the world concretely and interpersonally rather than through civic or academic abstractions. Bi11bot translates complex choices into questions about machinery, probability, money, ownership, incentives, and consequences. Alpha speaks administratively. Mera speaks in controlled, consequence-oriented terms. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

The central character question is not whether one person can make the correct decision for everyone. It is whether people can reclaim responsibility without replacing Alpha with another unchecked authority. **FIXED** `(input/story.md; input/characters.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist; mechanic and mine worker from Keel; Adrian Phantum's son. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Age:** 17. His seventeenth birthday triggers the transfer of Adrian's household liability and the restriction of his travel status. **FIXED** `(input/characters.md; input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Home:** Keel. He lives with his grandmother in the family apartment. `(input/characters.md)`

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. His father, Adrian Phantum, is European-descended; his mother was Indigenous Andean. Her name, fate, and exact community are not established in this book. Alex's mixed appearance is a story clue: he should visibly resemble Adrian while also visibly inheriting substantial features from his mother. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Lean and mechanically capable. He wears worn mining and work clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. He keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket; the chip is etched with a looping equation. He often turns the chip unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated. He shares Adrian's jaw and the characteristic habit of tilting his head while thinking, but he should not look like a younger version of Adrian. `(input/characters.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete, skeptical, and mechanical. His sarcasm is defensive rather than performative. He is uncomfortable with speeches and usually describes evidence rather than principles. His slang is industrial and mildly abrasive: **clean, hot, burned, crooked, thin, solid, patched, ghost, paper-clean, fitch,** and **salvo**. He trusts physical evidence—vibration, pressure, wear, and what people actually do—more than labels or official descriptions. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Alex does not naturally use words such as *governance, legitimacy, stakeholder, institutional, systemic, equitable,* or *public accountability* unless he is mocking, repeating, or reading them. He reduces abstractions to physical reality. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Representative lines:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.”
- “I've also got his debt.”
- “One day... I'm leaving.”
- “Everything has a price, Ada.”
- “She stays.”
- “Truth doesn't need authorization.”
- “I just want it to be my choice.”

`(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Who he is:** Practical, independent, skeptical, sarcastic, and mechanically capable. He improvises repairs rather than blindly following approved procedure. He begins by believing Adrian abandoned him and left behind only debt and shame. He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. `(input/characters.md)`

Alex's first instinct is to test whether a system works rather than accept what the system says. His defining opening action is placing his palm on an aging pump and feeling its vibration instead of trusting the gauge or approved maintenance instructions. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

His inherited liability is **418,607 credits**, with a projected **28.7 years** to clearance. His travel status becomes **RESTRICTED**. The liability combines Adrian's debt with unpaid care and service charges, penalties, and projected interest; it is several years of a Keel worker's income and cannot be discharged through ordinary bankruptcy. **FIXED** `(input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Alex is pressured by debt, restricted movement, Keel's material conditions, Adrian's public reputation, and the possibility that his father left him a purpose instead of merely a burden. He is also pressured by the temptation to use inherited authority to repair everything directly: cancel his debt, restore Keel's water, enforce contributor ownership, protect displaced families, and remove Leona's restrictions. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Ada Veyra:** His longtime friend and closest companion. They are affectionate and occasionally teasing. Their central disagreement is escape versus rebuilding: Alex looks toward Oasis and a life elsewhere; Ada looks toward changing Keel. They remain longtime friends and civic collaborators, with romantic feeling expressed through intimacy and choice but never formally labeled. **FIXED** `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Adrian Phantum:** His absent father. Alex begins with resentment toward Adrian's public image as a failed gambler, then investigates, identifies with him, grieves him, and finally refuses to become Adrian's successor by simply inheriting exclusive authority. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** The grandmother he lives with and cares for. Her memory appears unreliable, but she repeatedly preserves truths Alex has dismissed. She knew Adrian was working on a governance project, knew the workshop and Bi11bot were intended for Alex, and understood that Alex needed to be ready. She did not know the full safeguards, exact criteria, final location, or Adrian's death. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's machine, guide, mentor, evaluator, witness, and companion. Alex begins by treating Bi11bot as a suspicious inheritance and gradually learns to trust his questions without surrendering judgment. `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** Begins as an unseen observer associated with Alpha and becomes the strongest defender of concentrated authority. Alex ultimately refuses to humiliate or destroy her; he asks her to help build something better. `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Ada's mother and one of the Covenant's former custodians. Alex encounters her history as part of the larger question of whether protection can justify silence and concentrated power. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Tests machinery physically instead of trusting displayed readings.
- Improvises repairs even when procedure forbids it.
- Turns Adrian's casino chip when uncertain or emotionally activated.
- Crosses his arms when defensive or skeptical.
- Tilts his head while thinking, a gesture shared with Adrian.
- Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom; Ada's gaze repeatedly turns toward Keel.
- Follows physical evidence when official maps and records contradict what he can feel or observe.

`(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**History:** Alex was two when Adrian left the household. Adrian continued concealed work for years, completed the transfer beneath Oasis shortly before Alpha's intervention, and was recorded publicly only through a distorted or incomplete Alpha record until the archive opened. **FIXED** `(characters.md; story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Alex grew up in Keel with his grandmother and Adrian's public reputation surrounding him. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

His family line contains a European-descended father and an Indigenous Andean mother whose identity remains unrevealed. That inheritance explains both his resemblance to Adrian and his visible connection to another history rooted in Keel and the region. The mother's name, fate, and exact community remain outside this book. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:**

1. **Anger:** Believes Adrian left him debt.
2. **Curiosity:** Learns Adrian left something hidden.
3. **Doubt:** Questions the public story about Adrian.
4. **Responsibility:** Values Keel's truth over his private escape.
5. **Ownership:** Learns that inheritance does not mean sole ownership.
6. **Trust:** Accepts that no one person or city can solve the shared problem alone.
7. **Power:** Is offered the ability to fix nearly everything directly.
8. **Restraint:** Refuses the right to decide for everyone.
9. **Agency:** Still wants to leave, but now insists that leaving must be his choice.

`(input/characters.md)`

After leaving Keel, Alex and Ada travel together through Halyard and Oasis to visit the new workshops and civic bodies and help establish the first cross-city review. Alex remains a mechanic and witness; Ada works with local civic and community groups. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alex retains a limited stewardship role after the final transition: he can convene the first equal three-city review and authenticate Adrian's transfer, but he cannot issue decisions, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend, civic collaborator, and partner in the journey. Daughter of Leona Veyra. `(input/characters.md)`

**Age:** 20. `(input/characters.md)`

**Home:** Keel at the beginning of the story. `(input/characters.md)`

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. She should read as unmistakably local to the region while retaining her established copper hair, freckles, and light or teal-blue eyes. Her facial structure, coloring, family resemblance, posture, and other features can reflect strong Andean ancestry. Ada and Alex are both mixed but should not look ethnically interchangeable; their families represent different histories of the same region. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair; freckles; teal-blue eyes; a dark high-collared top; a rivet-studded shoulder strap; and a muted moss-green wrap. She carries herself with a steady, rooted stance. `(input/characters.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Concise and emotionally precise. She often reframes a problem with one question and pushes abstractions back toward human needs and relationships. She rarely moralizes; she makes people name what is actually at stake. Her language is less mechanical than Alex's and focuses on obligations, avoidance, fear, imbalance, hidden motives, and who carries the burden. Her recurring vocabulary includes **clean, crooked, square, tied, loose, carrying, showing,** and **full cup**. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Ada does not naturally use civic or academic abstractions such as *agency, governance, legitimacy, stakeholder,* or *social contract*. She translates them into what people can see and what they owe one another. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who she is:** Rooted, observant, perceptive, and willing to let silence sit. She watches people as closely as she watches systems. She believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it. She notices what Alex avoids emotionally and redirects arguments from stated demands to underlying fears. `(input/characters.md)`

Ada's central belief is not that everyone will agree. It is that people need to name what they are protecting before they can make an agreement capable of holding under pressure. `(input/story.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex Phantum:** Longtime friend and closest companion. She understands his defenses and challenges his inherited story about Adrian. Alex trusts her before he trusts the broader system. Their intimacy and choices express romantic feeling, but their relationship is never formally labeled. **FIXED** `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Her mother. Ada grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars. Their relationship moves from absence and shock through anger and disclosure toward unresolved but active repair. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Accepts him as part of Alex's hidden inheritance and participates in the journey he evaluates. `(input/story.md)`
- **Mara Doss, Frank Reed, and Elias Renn:** Helps them move from competing demands to a reciprocal agreement by asking what each is afraid of losing. `(input/story.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel believing that Leona died during the Water Wars. She became Alex's closest friend and developed a contrasting hope: not escape from Keel, but rebuilding it. `(input/characters.md)`

At the end, Ada asks Alex whether he still wants to leave. She accepts that he may, provided the choice is genuinely his. She leaves Keel with him and works with local civic and community groups as they travel through Halyard and Oasis. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, entrepreneur, and architect of the three-safeguard plan. `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Ancestry:** European-descended. His possible family background includes British, Irish, German, Swiss, northern or western European, or a European-American family that entered South America through engineering, mining, infrastructure, research, or technical work. The exact national ancestry is not established. He should look clearly related to Alex through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture, and habitual gestures. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Tired but kind in his first clear recording. He wears a burgundy work jacket in the Chapter 1 recording. He shares Alex's jaw and characteristic head tilt. Later recordings show him older, more worn, and under increasing pressure. `(input/characters.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic, and capable of humor inside serious messages. He uses uncertainty as a discipline rather than a weakness. He frames matters as questions, bets, odds, and choices, and avoids telling Alex exactly what to think. `(input/characters.md)`

**Who he is:** Adrian is not secretly flawless. He was a famous gambler with exceptional winnings and luck before losing it all in a famous card game in which the Alpha founder himself participated. The gambling was real, and the loss was real. He also gambled to raise money for his inventions, to restore Bi11bot and TJ, and to fund entrepreneurial efforts. He used gambling as a real research environment for probability, incentives, risk, and behavior under uncertainty. The family cost was real as well. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md)`

He believed humanity must eventually reclaim authority, but that no one should simply inherit legitimacy. People must demonstrate judgment, ownership, truthfulness, and reciprocal trust before exercising restored authority. `(input/characters.md)`

He worked collaboratively rather than creating alone. He helped create or establish the Measure, the Maker's Seal, and the Covenant, and used physical or offline components that Alpha could not simply reach through connected systems. `(input/characters.md; input/world.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** His absent son and intended successor to a test, but not a person he wants to turn into a copy of himself.
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Entrusted her with the book, workshop, and hidden family history.
- **Bi11bot:** Built him and relied on him to preserve records, carry the final authorization package, and witness the transfer.
- **Mera Vale:** Shared reconstruction work and the Water Wars with her, then diverged over whether emergency authority should end.
- **Leona Veyra:** Worked with her during the original Covenant and opposed the permanent concentration of authority that followed its suspension.
- **Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr:** Collaborators whose contributions were obscured by later ownership records.

`(input/characters.md; input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Adrian left the household when Alex was two and continued concealed work for years. He completed a final transfer beneath Oasis shortly before Alpha's intervention. His public record remained distorted or incomplete until the archive opened. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

His public reputation became that of a famous but failed gambler, debtor, and source of confiscated assets and penalties. Alpha classified him as a systemic disruption risk, restricted his financial access and movement, and built a public mythology from true fragments stripped of their context. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

Adrian prepared a long-term succession and judgment test for Alex. He completed a final transfer beneath Oasis while trapped in flooding infrastructure. The transfer package contains the safeguards' verified records, the succession test's result, and authority to request Alpha's transition into bounded advisory operation. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

His archival recordings are shorter fragments whose order is initially unclear. Alex and Ada reconstruct the chronology from physical media, collaborator testimony, and Alpha records. The recordings reveal that Adrian gambled to raise money for his inventions, restored Bi11bot and TJ, pursued entrepreneurial efforts, and regarded Bi11bot and TJ as connected parts of the work. Adrian's mention of TJ creates intrigue about who or what TJ is before TJ appears. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; Alex's guide, witness, mentor, archive, evaluator, and companion. `(input/characters.md)`

**Name and identifier:** “Bi11bot” is the spoken name. **B11** is his formal machine and witness identifier; the B11 witness record is the authenticated event log generated by that identity. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. He has visible tooled seams, repairs, maker's stamps, soft-blue and amber light accents, expressive optical eyes, and one eye with a small adjustable lens ring. He should look made rather than seamless. `(input/characters.md)`

He has **two expressive optical eyes**. He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, no looming low-angle presentation, no face lost in shadow, and no predatory posture. His eyes carry wisdom, patience, and quiet amusement. When scenes are tense, the tension is around him rather than in him. **FIXED** `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Dry, economical, patient, precise, warm underneath, and occasionally cryptic. His language draws from Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling, probability, and financial literacy. He does not chase youth slang and does not sound like Alex or Ada. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot is warm without becoming sentimental and funny without becoming comic relief. He answers literal questions while sometimes withholding the larger answer. He observes choices rather than making them for Alex. He knows the journey is also a test but does not reveal everything immediately. `(input/characters.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Maker, owner in the personal rather than institutional sense, and former companion. Bi11bot personally knew Adrian and was present for his final transfer.
- **Alex:** Begins as an inheritance, becomes a mentor, and ultimately reveals that Adrian's wager was whether people could make choices worthy of restored authority.
- **TJ:** Maker and companion. Bi11bot built TJ by hand from salvaged parts. **FIXED** `(rules/characters.md)`

**History and function:** Adrian built Bi11bot and placed the key socket in his chest. Bi11bot contains or projects Adrian's records, stores and transmits Adrian's final authorization package through a physically isolated channel, and signs the event as a witness using his maker-authenticated identity. He does not independently decide whether the transfer is valid. **FIXED** `(input/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Adrian's recordings mention TJ as part of the work before TJ's first appearance. TJ first appears later at Site 6, where Bi11bot assembles him to reach a trapped mechanism. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian; ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex. `(input/characters.md)`

**Ancestry:** White Latin American / Southern European-descended. Her distinction should come more strongly from grooming, controlled posture, speech, clothing, environment, and institutional position than from ethnicity alone. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Older, elegant, controlled, and almost motionless when first fully presented. She is authoritative without raising her voice and is associated with order, alignment, and exactness. In early surveillance scenes she aligns a glass precisely with the edge of a desk. As her certainty collapses, she can look old, tired, and eventually afraid. `(input/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, polished, and consequence-oriented. She rarely speaks in emotional language. She reframes moral criticism as a question of stability, outcomes, or responsibility. `(input/characters.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who she is:** Mera does not view herself as malicious. She believes she accepted necessary burdens after witnessing human fear, scarcity, rivalry, and institutional failure during the Water Wars. She believes concentrated coordinating authority can prevent catastrophe. `(input/characters.md)`

Her core contradiction is that she defends a system she can no longer honestly claim to control. Alpha eventually denies even her own override attempt. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**History:** Mera lived through the Water Wars and worked with Adrian and Leona. She saw a human water agreement fail under scarcity and fear. She supported the emergency suspension of the Covenant and helped consolidate authority in Alpha. She continued defending concentrated authority after the original emergency. `(input/characters.md)`

Mera is the unseen woman who chooses **TRACK** when the first safeguard node activates. She does so because she wants to observe whether Adrian's succession protocol has activated before Alpha is alerted to its full significance. **FIXED** `(input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The first activation remains anonymous to Alex but gives the reader a controlled visual clue: a precise glass alignment, an elegant hand, or a distinctive interface reflection. Mera is fully revealed in Chapter 4 when the Maker's Seal identifies Leona and the Covenant history becomes personal. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Mera moves from confident institutional control to the collapse of that certainty. She becomes one citizen among many rather than standing above the system. Alex does not defeat or humiliate her; he asks her to help build something better. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

## Supporting Cast — Design Scope

Detailed final visual designs remain open to the Character Designer. This is deliberate and does not leave the characters production-neutral.

Every recurring supporting character must have:

- a distinct silhouette;
- clear city and trade cues;
- work-linked props;
- an introduction context that identifies their role and location.

These requirements apply to **Foreman-7, Director Cassian Lock, Dr. Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr, Frank Reed, Mara Doss, and Elias Renn**. **FIXED as design scope** `(rules/decisions.md)`

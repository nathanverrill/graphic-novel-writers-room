Chapter Two: The Price of Departure
Writers’ Room Draft v4.0
Chapter Function
Turn Adrian’s mystery into a concrete plan, introduce the three-part quest, send Alex into the abandoned lithium mines to recover the Measure, complete his first judgment test, and begin the journey to Halyard.
Central Question
What is freedom worth if you leave everyone else trapped behind you?
Prosperity Principle
The earlier principle—
You cannot improve what you refuse to measure.
—emphasizes measurement, but this chapter is ultimately more about truth and transparency.

# CHAPTER 4

**THE BROKEN COVENANT**

## PAGE 1: THE CITY SHE LEFT

**P1: Oasis (dominant)**  
*Image:* A wide, high shot of Oasis at midday, pearl-white towers, engineered gardens in exact geometric rows, broad still reservoirs reflecting the sky, automated transit gliding silently overhead. No crowds, no visible labor, no dust. Alex, Ada and Bi11bot step off a transit platform into this stillness, small against the scale of it.  
*Caption:* OASIS

**P2: Too clean (wide)**  
*Image:* A transit concourse, vast and nearly empty, a handful of residents moving through it unhurried. Alex looks around, visibly uneasy in a way the bustle of Halyard never made him.  
ALEX: Where is everyone?  
BI11BOT: Working. Resting. Being somewhere that isn't here. Oasis doesn't require a crowd to run.

**P3: Clearance (medium)**  
*Image:* A checkpoint gate, pearl and silver, far gentler-looking than Keel's or Halyard's, almost welcoming. Alex steps up to it and it stays shut, a small amber hesitation light blinking rather than denying him outright.  
*Screen:* RESIDENCY CLEARANCE REQUIRED — TEMPORARY PASS ISSUED, ESCORT MANDATORY  
ALEX: ...I'm being escorted. By a gate.

**P4: Recognized (close)**  
*Image:* Ada steps up to the same gate without hesitation, almost without thinking. It opens instantly, and for a fraction of a second, text resolves on the display before it clears.  
*Screen (flashing briefly):* VEYRA, A. — RESIDENT RECORD FOUND — STATUS: UNRESOLVED  
Ada's hand stays on the gate's edge a moment longer than it needs to.  
ADA: It knew my name before I gave it.

**P5: Not his city (strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot of the three of them passing through, Ada now walking slightly ahead, Alex and Bi11bot a step behind her for the first time in the story.  
ALEX *(quiet, mostly to himself)*: Guess I'm just along for this one.

## PAGE 2: THE RECORD

**P1: The search (wide)**  
*Image:* A quiet archive alcove, a few public terminals built into a pearl-white wall. Bi11bot's small projector unfolds from his shoulder, casting a soft-blue search field in the empty air. Alex leans against the wall nearby, and Ada stands slightly apart, arms crossed.  
BI11BOT: The Covenant's third custodian. Adrian built it with two others. One of them I already know.  
ALEX: Mera.  
BI11BOT: Mera. The third name is the one Adrian never spoke in front of you.

**P2: Resolving (dominant)**  
*Image:* The projection searches, founder-level Oasis records scrolling fast, then slowing, then stopping on a single formal record. A small official portrait resolves: a woman, composed, in muted sage and stone-grey, unmistakably older than the family photograph from Chapter One but with Ada's same steady eyes.  
*Screen:* FOUNDING CUSTODIAN RECORD / VEYRA, LEONA / STATUS: ACTIVE — CIVIC ADMINISTRATION, OASIS  
ALEX: Veyra.  
*(Nobody says anything for a moment.)*

**P3: Not dead (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex looks from the screen to Ada, something shifting in his face, careful.  
ALEX: Ada. Is that—  
ADA *(quiet, even)*: My mother's name. Yes.  
ALEX: You said she disappeared.  
ADA: She did. Just not the way I assumed.

**P4: Unresolved (tight close-up)**  
*Image:* Close on Ada's face alone, still, unreadable, her eyes on the small portrait rather than on Alex.  A small tear begins to well in her right eye.  
*Caption:* Fifteen years of wondering whether she'd been abandoned or orphaned. And this record had an answer the whole time.

**P5: Held back (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide and low, from a small distance. Alex takes half a step toward Ada, hand half-raised, and stops himself, glancing at Bi11bot instead. Bi11bot's lens stays fixed on Ada, giving her the space.  
ALEX *(quiet)*: Ada?

## PAGE 3: PRESERVED

**P1: The door (wide)**  
 *Image:* A quiet residential corridor, pale and softly lit. Ada stands alone at a single door, having stepped ahead of Alex and Bi11bot, who wait a deliberate distance back down the hall. Her hand is raised, not yet knocking.  
 *Caption:* CIVIC QUARTER, OASIS

**P2: Recognition (dominant)**  
 *Image:* The door opens. Leona stands in the frame, composed, in sage and stone, her hair greyer than her portrait, Ada's same steady eyes. Neither moves for a long beat. No dialogue, no caption.

**P3: Inside (wide)**  
 *Image:* The apartment's main room, pale and immaculate, everything precisely placed. Leona leads Ada in, a half-step ahead, not quite able to look directly at her. Through the open door behind them, Alex and Bi11bot remain in the hallway, Alex making no move to follow.  
 LEONA: I knew this day would come. I didn't know it would be today.  
 ADA: I didn't know it would come at all.

**P4: Careful (medium two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Leona gestures to a seat, her movements precise and a little too careful, a woman handling something fragile.  
 LEONA: Would you like something? I have—  
 ADA: I don't want tea, Leona.  
 *(A small flinch at the first name, not "Mother.")*

**P5: The threshold (closing strip)**  
 *Image:* Wide, low shot from the hallway. Alex leans against the wall outside, Bi11bot beside him, both deliberately out of the apartment, the door left just ajar.  
 BI11BOT *(quiet)*: You're not going in.  
 ALEX: Not this part.

## PAGE 4: WHAT SHE KEPT

**P1: The box (wide)**  
 *Image:* Leona kneels at a low cabinet, drawing out a plain stone-grey box, handling it the way someone handles something they've touched a thousand times alone and never once with company. Ada watches from the seat she finally took.  
 LEONA: I kept everything. I didn't know what else to do with it.

**P2: Photographs (medium)**  
 *Image:* Leona spreads a handful of photographs across the low table: Ada as a small child, Leona younger, a blurred group photo that includes Adrian and, at its edge, a figure in grey who must be Mera. Ada's hand hovers over one without quite touching it.  
 ADA: I don't remember any of this.  
 LEONA: You were five. I remember enough for both of us.

**P3: The band (dominant)**  
 *Image:* Close on Leona's hands lifting a small inner case from the box. Inside, nestled in cloth, sits a single linked metal band, one of the Covenant's three, engraved with a name and a promise, matching the holographic design seen in Chapter Two. For the first time on the page, warm green-gold light catches its surface against the room's cool palette.  
 LEONA: This was mine to carry. I was supposed to be ready to use it again, if it was ever needed.  
 LEONA: I told myself I was waiting for the right moment. Fifteen years is a long time to wait for a moment.

**P4: One small thing (tight close-up)**  
 *Image:* Close on a small, worn child's toy at the bottom of the box, half-forgotten beneath the photographs, something a five-year-old would have carried. Ada's composure finally cracks, just slightly, at the edges of her eyes.  
 ADA *(quiet)*: You kept this.  
 LEONA: I kept everything that was yours. I just never found the courage to bring any of it to you.

**P5: Not yet an answer (closing strip)**  
 *Image:* Wide, low. Leona closes the box partway, her hand resting on the lid, not quite ready to let it go entirely.  
 LEONA: I know objects aren't an explanation.  
 ADA: No. They're not.

## PAGE 5: DID YOU KNOW WHERE I WAS?

**P1: The question starts (medium two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Ada sits forward, hands folded, very still. Leona sits across from her, the box now closed on the table between them.  
 ADA: Why didn't you come for me?  
 LEONA: I believed—

**P2: Forced (tighter two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Ada doesn't let the sentence finish, her voice staying level, not rising.  
 ADA: Did you know where I was?

**P3: Qualified (close, Leona alone)**  
 *Image:* Close on Leona, caught, the qualification she reached for suddenly insufficient.  
 LEONA: ...Yes. Within a year, yes. Alpha's records could have told me exactly where.  
 LEONA: I believed you were safer not knowing I was looking.

**P4: Named (close, Ada alone)**  
 *Image:* Close on Ada, something settling into place behind her eyes, not surprise, confirmation.  
 ADA: You chose not to come back.  
 *(off-panel)*  
 LEONA: I chose to keep you safe.

**P5: Both true (dominant, closing)**  
 *Image:* Both women now in frame together, equal size for the first time on the page, close and still.  
 ADA: Those aren't the same thing.  
 LEONA: I know that now. I didn't let myself know it then.  
 *(A long silence between them. Neither looks away.)*

## PAGE 6: MERA

**P1: The door again (strip)**  
 *Image:* The apartment door, left ajar since Page 3, opens the rest of the way without a knock. A pale, precise hand rests on its edge.

**P2: Entrance (wide)**  
 *Image:* Mera Vale steps into the room, composed, silver-templed, in pearl and graphite, utterly still against the room's soft disorder of spread photographs and an open box. Alex and Bi11bot, drawn back from the hallway by her presence, stand just inside the door behind her.  
 MERA: I felt the record search this morning. I should have come sooner.

**P3: Face to face (dominant)**  
 *Image:* Mera and Ada regard each other directly for the first time in the story, close, neither flinching.  
 ADA: You've been watching me. My whole life.  
 MERA: I promised your mother I would keep you safe. I kept that promise the only way I had left to keep it.  
 ADA: That's not the same as knowing me.  
 MERA: No. It isn't.

**P4: The argument (close two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Mera turns toward Leona, something almost gentle in her bearing, which makes what she says next land harder.  
 MERA: I have seen the consequences of misplaced trust, Leona. I watched it happen to you, to me, to everyone who believed order would hold itself together.  
 MERA: I am not your enemy. I am the reason there was still a city for your daughter to be found in.

**P5: No easy answer (wide, closing)**  
 *Image:* Wide shot of the full room now, all four of them, no one fully facing anyone else, the photographs and the open box still on the table between Leona and Ada, Mera standing slightly apart from all of it.  
 ALEX *(quiet, to Bi11bot)*: Is she wrong?  
 BI11BOT: Not entirely. That's what makes her dangerous.

## PAGE 7: TWO OF THREE

**P1: The three (medium)**  
 *Image:* Leona sits straighter than she has all chapter, meeting Ada's eyes directly for the first time.  
 LEONA: You deserve the whole thing, plainly. Not pieces I've softened over the years.  
 LEONA: There were three of us. Your father, Mera and me. We built the Covenant together, during the wars, so communities could make real agreements without handing everything to one authority.

**P2: The breaking point (wide, memory-toned)**  
 *Image:* A sepia-grey memory overlay: crowds, seized water infrastructure, chaos in the reconstruction headquarters, a young Leona searching a crowd, five-year-old Ada's hand slipping from hers.  
 LEONA *(voice-over)*: It held, until a shortage worse than anything we'd modeled. One community broke the agreement. Another retaliated. I lost you in it.

**P3: The vote (medium, three figures)**  
 *Image:* Still in the sepia overlay: young Adrian, Mera and Leona standing together, the weight of a decision visible on all three faces, a single document or interface between them.  
 LEONA *(voice-over)*: Alpha could take emergency authority, but only if two of the three of us agreed. Mera said yes. Your father said no.

**P4: Mine (close)**  
 *Image:* Back in the present, full color. Leona's hands are tightly folded.  
 LEONA: I was the deciding vote. I believed it would bring you back to me faster. I was wrong about what it would cost, and I was wrong about how long "temporary" would last.

**P5: Owned (closing strip)**  
 *Image:* Wide, low. Mera watches from across the room, unmoving, her expression unreadable but not unaffected.  
 ADA: You're not blaming Mera.  
 LEONA: No. I made my own choice. She's never once let me pretend otherwise.

## PAGE 8: THE COST

**P1: What broke (dominant, memory-toned)**  
 *Image:* A wide, chaotic sepia-grey scene: a seized water facility, crowds pressed against barricades, families separated, visible urgency bordering on violence. This is the only page in the book so far to show the Water Wars directly rather than through aftermath.  
 *Caption:* THE WATER WARS, THE FINAL WEEK

**P2: Names (medium, memory-toned)**  
 *Image:* A field medical tent, exhausted workers, a list being updated by hand.  
 LEONA *(voice-over)*: People died. Not abstractly. People I'd negotiated agreements with personally, the week before.

**P3: Separation (medium, memory-toned)**  
 *Image:* The moment from Page 7's overlay, extended: young Leona reaching through a crowd, Ada's small hand just out of reach, swept toward an evacuation transport.  
 LEONA *(voice-over)*: I told myself the authorization would end the chaos fast enough that losing you would only be for a few days.

**P4: Days (close, memory-toned)**  
 *Image:* Close on a hand-marked calendar, days crossed off, the sepia tone beginning to fade at its edges.  
 LEONA *(voice-over)*: It ended the chaos. It just never gave anything back.

**P5: Present (closing strip, full color)**  
 *Image:* Back in the present room, full color returning sharply. Leona looks directly at Ada, no longer qualifying.  
 LEONA: I traded a few terrible days I could see for fifteen years I couldn't. I didn't know that's what I was doing. That doesn't make it not what I did.

## PAGE 9: SAFETY ISN'T AGENCY

**P1: Still necessary (medium)**  
 *Image:* Mera steps further into the room, addressing Leona directly, Ada and Alex watching the exchange rather than participating in it.  
 MERA: You regret the cost. I understand that. I don't regret the choice. The alternative was more deaths, not fewer.  
 LEONA: You're still certain of that.

**P2: Diverging (close two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Mera and Leona, close, the first real disagreement between them the reader has seen.  
 MERA: Certainty kept this city standing.  
 LEONA: Certainty kept me from ever asking whether it still needed to.

**P3: The crack (medium)**  
 *Image:* Leona stands, something in her posture finally straightening after fifteen years of smallness.  
 LEONA: I told myself refusing to choose again was humility. That not knowing what was right meant I shouldn't act.  
 LEONA: It wasn't humility. It was fear wearing humility's clothes.

**P4: Named (close, Leona alone)**  
 *Image:* Close on Leona, the realization fully landing.  
 LEONA: Safety isn't the same as agency. I gave Ada the first and called it enough. I never gave her a choice in any of it, mine or hers.

**P5: Watching (closing wide)**  
 *Image:* Wide shot. Ada watches her mother say this, something unreadable but no longer entirely closed crossing her face. Mera stands apart, unmoved in posture but her eyes lingering on Leona a beat too long.  
 BI11BOT *(quiet, to Alex)*: That's the first true thing she's said about herself all day.

## PAGE 10: WHAT THE AGREEMENT HAS TO PROTECT

**P1: What it failed to do (medium)**  
 *Image:* Ada picks up the single Covenant band from the box, turning it over, studying its engraving.  
 ADA: It failed because it only had three people holding it. One scarcity event and two of three panicked.  
 LEONA: That's a fair reading.

**P2: Not fewer people (medium three-shot)**  
 *Image:* Ada sets the band down, looking between Leona and Mera both.  
 ADA: Alex found something that can't be owned by one House because it's too small to bother claiming. Maybe this needs the opposite problem solved. Not too few hands holding it. Too few voices it actually answers to.  
 MERA: You want to put something that fragile in more hands, not fewer.

**P3: What it has to protect (dominant)**  
 *Image:* Ada stands, the band held loosely, addressing the room rather than just her mother.  
 ADA: I don't want a Covenant that works because three important people agree. I want one that only works when the people it actually affects can see what was promised, and call it broken the moment it is.  
 ADA: Not someday. Not after fifteen years. The moment.

**P4: Can it hold (close two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Leona and Mera exchange a glance, neither quite agreeing, both visibly reconsidering.  
 LEONA: That's not what we built.  
 ADA: No. It's what you should have.

**P5: Hers (closing strip)**  
 *Image:* Wide, low shot. Alex watches from the doorway, still not entering, something like pride on his face, saying nothing.  
 *Caption:* It was the first time anyone in the room had proposed fixing something instead of explaining why it broke.

## PAGE 11: SHOW ME

**P1: After the argument (close two-shot)**  
 *Image:* The room has gone quiet after Page 10's proposal, Leona looking at the Covenant band in Ada's hand rather than at Ada herself.  
 LEONA: I don't know how to fix fifteen years.

**P2: The apology (tight close-up)**  
 *Image:* Close on Leona's face, finally steady.  
 LEONA: I'm sorry.

**P3: The answer (close-up, Ada alone)**  
 *Image:* Close on Ada, calm, not cruel, simply certain.  
 ADA: Don't tell me. Show me.

**P4: What that means (dominant, medium)**  
 *Image:* Leona looks at the band still in Ada's hand, then at Mera across the room, something resolving in her posture.  
 LEONA: Then tell me what showing you looks like.  
 ADA: Stand behind what I just proposed. In public. With your name on it.

**P5: Not yet an agreement (dominant, closing)**  
 *Image:* Wide shot of the full room, Leona rising to her feet, Mera watching closely, Bi11bot's lens turning toward Alex at the door for the first time in several pages.  
 BI11BOT *(quiet, to Alex)*: This is the part intelligence can't do for them.  
 ALEX: I know. I'm still not going in.

## PAGE 12: WHAT HE CARRIED

**P1: Crossing the threshold (wide)**  
 *Image:* Alex finally steps fully into the room, Bi11bot beside him, the door closing behind them for the first time.  
 ALEX: I think this part's mine to be in the room for.

**P2: The compartment (close-up)**  
 *Image:* Bi11bot's chest panel opens, the same fitted compartment that held the Measure in Chapter Two. Inside, a second linked band rests, gold and burgundy, Adrian's.  
 BI11BOT: Your father gave me this the night he finished the workshop. I've carried it ever since.  
 ALEX: You never told me.

**P3: Why not (medium)**  
 *Image:* Bi11bot's lens clicks, his tone as even as ever.  
 BI11BOT: Carrying it was never the point. He was very specific. It doesn't wake for whoever's holding it, only for what they choose to do.  
 BI11BOT: I was waiting for you to have something worth doing with it.

**P4: Handing it over (close two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Bi11bot lifts the band, and Alex takes it, turning it over, the same looping equation faintly etched into its inner edge.  
 ALEX: So what do I do with it?  
 BI11BOT: Nothing, by itself. It isn't yours to activate alone any more than it was his.

**P5: Two hands (closing strip)**  
 *Image:* Wide, low. Alex crosses the room and sets his father's band on the table beside Leona's, the two resting close but not yet touching.  
 ALEX *(quiet)*: Then it's yours to finish, Leona. Not mine.

## PAGE 13: TWO OF THREE, AGAIN

**P1: The commitment (medium)**  
 *Image:* Leona places her hand flat beside both bands, looking at Ada rather than at the table.  
 LEONA: I will go before the civic council tomorrow and propose exactly what Ada described. A Covenant that answers to the people it affects, not to whoever's trusted to hold it.  
 LEONA: That's not a promise to you, Ada. It's an obligation I'm making in front of witnesses. You can hold me to it.

**P2: Linking (dominant)**  
 *Image:* The two bands lift from the table on their own, Leona's and Adrian's, and begin to link, warm sage-gold and burgundy-gold merging into one steady light that fills the room, visibly brighter than the Seal's activation in Chapter Three.  
 *SFX:* HUMMM

**P3: What it needed (medium)**  
 *Image:* Alex watches, something settling in his face, understanding arriving in real time.  
 ALEX: It didn't need us to find it. It needed somebody to actually keep a promise.  
 BI11BOT: Your father built it to be difficult to cheat. He didn't build it to be difficult to use.

**P4: The third (close-up)**  
 *Image:* Close on Mera's hand, her own band still worn, cold pearl-grey, entirely unmoved by the light filling the room around her.

**P5: Not yet (dominant, closing)**  
 *Image:* Wide shot. The two linked bands hover, glowing, complete between them. Mera stands apart, watching, her own band dark against her pale sleeve. Her expression gives away nothing.  
 MERA: Two of three. Again.  
 *(No one answers her.)*

## PAGE 14: UNRESOLVED

**P1: Not fixed (medium)**  
 *Image:* Ada holds the two linked bands, looking at her mother, something unresolved still visible between them despite the warmth in the room.  
 ADA: This doesn't undo it.  
 LEONA: No. It doesn't. I'm not asking it to.

**P2: What it does (medium two-shot)**  
 *Image:* Leona reaches out, not quite touching Ada, a careful, uncertain gesture.  
 LEONA: I'd just like permission to keep trying. Not to be forgiven today.  
 ADA *(a long beat)*: You can keep trying.

**P3: Leaving (wide)**  
 *Image:* Mera moves toward the door alone, unhurried as always, pausing only briefly as she passes Alex.  
 MERA: Your father built something that worked. I built something that lasted. I'm beginning to understand those were never the same thing.

**P4: The question (medium)**  
 *Image:* Alex stops her with a question, quiet, direct.  
 ALEX: Will you tell me what happened to him?  
 MERA *(not turning around)*: Soon. Not here.

**P5: Alone (dominant, closing)**  
 *Image:* Mera's own control room, the symmetrical pearl-and-graphite staging from Chapters One and Three. She stands before the wall display, her own cold band still on her hand, unlinked. The glass of water on her desk sits slightly crooked, and this time, she doesn't straighten it.  
 *Lettering:* END OF CHAPTER FOUR

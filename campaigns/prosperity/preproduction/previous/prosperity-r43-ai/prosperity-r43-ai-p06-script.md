## Page 6 (left) — Alex’s useful work is absorbed while his debt closes his future

**Panel 1.** Wide. Keel pump hall just before dawn, in rust, charcoal and oxidized orange. Alex Phantum, eighteen today, lean and work-rough, has one palm on an aging pump housing, feeling vibration rather than reading the gauge. A projected maintenance procedure hovers beside him, unfollowed. A salvaged bracket replaces the approved part. A white-and-amber node logs his shift. A supervisor watches with folded arms.

SUPERVISOR: That's not the approved repair.

ALEX: Then don't approve it.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

**Panel 2.** Medium-wide. At the end of the shift, Alex places his hand on an exit checkpoint scanner. Workers queue behind him. His balance appears largest in the line. Everyone glances up.

The display reads:

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**

**ALEX PHANTUM**  
**AGE VERIFIED: EIGHTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

SFX: BEEP

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

ALEX: I've had better surprises.

CAPTION: Eighteen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.

**Panel 3.** Wide street view. Alex passes the shuttered casino **THE LAST CHANCE**, its faded lights still flickering. A bouncer leans in the doorway. Across the street, people queue at a metered water tap marked **RATION: 4 L**.

BOUNCER: Care to see if luck runs in the family?

BOUNCER: Your father always believed the next hand would change everything.

Alex keeps walking, hands jammed in his jacket pockets.

**Panel 4.** Small cutaway to the now-empty pump hall. The supervisor stands at the wall terminal. The white-and-amber node flashes **UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR — REVIEW REQUIRED**. The pump hums steadily. The supervisor selects **ROUTINE MAINTENANCE**. A green check appears. Beneath it:

**NEW PART ADDED — DESIGN CREDIT: SITE 6**

A balanced water-drop symbol is scratched into an old valve plate.

SUPERVISOR: ...Works.

**Panel 5.** Tall panel at sunrise on the rim of Keel’s exhausted pit. The sealed mine entrance sits in the foreground. Alex stands beyond it, looking toward Oasis, whose white towers catch the first light while Keel remains in shadow. His wrist display reads **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**.

ALEX: One day... I'm leaving.

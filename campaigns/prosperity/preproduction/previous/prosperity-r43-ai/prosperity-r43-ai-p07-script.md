## Page 7 (right) — Ada turns Alex’s wish to leave into an argument about staying

**Panel 1.** Wide, late afternoon on the rim of Keel’s exhausted pit, at the rusted railing where Alex and Ada sit. Keel lies in shadow; Halyard’s terraces catch gold; Oasis remains in sunlight. A dry, cracked drinking fountain stands nearby. Ada approaches with two battered metal mugs of water.

CAPTION: Later that afternoon...

**Panel 2.** Medium. Ada reaches Alex and holds out a mug. Her tousled red hair is wind-blown; her ration card hangs at her hip. Alex gives her a tired half-smile.

ADA: Thought you might need this.

ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

**Panel 3.** Close on their hands and faces around the mugs. Ada’s ration card shows one day marked off.

ALEX: Where'd you get enough water for two?

ADA: Saved some of my ration.

ALEX: Ada, you didn't have to.

ADA: Not everything has a price, Alex.

**Panel 4.** Medium two-shot. They sit side by side on the railing, laughing briefly, worn and dry.

ADA: Eighteen. Congratulations. You're officially old.

ALEX: I'm officially broke.

**Panel 5.** Small close panel on Ada’s eyes watching Alex. He looks toward the horizon.

ADA: I heard about this morning.

ALEX: Everyone did.

ALEX: And my bracket's in the Site 6 catalog now. Their name on it. Not mine.

**Panel 6.** Wide over-the-shoulder composition. Alex looks upward toward Oasis. Ada looks down toward Keel. Wind lifts her red hair.

ALEX: Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.

ADA: I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.

ALEX: Because nobody leaves.

ADA: That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.

PAGE CHECK: Ada’s ration becomes an act of care; their opposing goals are made personal without romance. CHANGED: none.

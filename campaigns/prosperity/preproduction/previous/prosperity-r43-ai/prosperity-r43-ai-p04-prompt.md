**Style (the same on every page):**

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, tactile hatching, painterly digital color and expressive acting. Technology is layered and maintained: analogue gauges, physical keys and corroded pipes coexist with holograms, drones, automated transit and large-scale computation.

Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Ancestry cuts across Keel, Halyard and Oasis. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the primary visual differences.

- **Keel:** rust, charcoal and oxidized orange; dust, crowding, narrow water channels, stacked housing, patched machines and exhausted lithium workings. Keel is worn but warm, inhabited and culturally specific.
- **Halyard:** bronze, gold and engineered green; workshops, laboratories, fabrication towers, elevated rail and Charter House facilities. Better resources do not mean freedom from ownership pressure or surveillance.
- **Oasis:** pearl white, silver, controlled green and clean blue; reservoirs, curved architecture, gardens, quiet transit and administrative interiors. Privilege appears through space, health, maintenance and access, never ethnicity.
- **Alpha:** no face or human body. Show it through white-and-amber control nodes, screens, gates, records, drones, restrictions and formal administrative language.
- **Bi11bot:** rugged industrial blue and chipped mustard yellow, segmented round head, twin warm amber eyes, side ear assemblies, red scarf, white **Bi11** chest mark and clicking right-eye ring. He is patient, dry, useful and fallible, never menacing.
- **Alex:** lean, work-rough and visibly related to Adrian while carrying substantial visible inheritance from his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. Do not explain that inheritance in dialogue.

# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon — Alex's mother:** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. The book never reveals anything else about her. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Canon — ancestry and visual identity:** Ancestry crosses class, city, occupation and institutional power. Indigenous people can hold power; white people can lack it; mixed, Afro-descendant, diasporic and international identities appear across Prosperity. Ethnicity is never shorthand for intelligence, morality, competence, wealth, poverty or ideology. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — Alex:** Alex is mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. His father, Adrian Phantum, is European-descended; his mother is Indigenous Andean, but her specific community and all other details remain unrevealed. Alex should visibly resemble Adrian while also carrying substantial visual inheritance from his mother. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — speech:** Language changes in formality from Keel to Halyard to Oasis, but this is not an intelligence scale. Alex and Ada speak concretely and do not use abstract civic or academic vocabulary unless they are quoting or mocking it. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon — Alex and Ada:** They are friends. Nothing in the art or dialogue should read as romance. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — Bi11bot:** Bi11bot is never a lecturer, a butler, an AI replacement for people, or a threat. His personality and beliefs are Bill Reynolds': plain, patient, questioning, occasionally roundabout, self-deprecating and shaped by engineering, probability, financial literacy and long experience. He asks questions rather than handing out answers. `(rules/stakeholders.md, rules/characters.md, rules/vocabulary.md, input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — B11:** The identifier is **B11** everywhere in the book. Earlier draft references to “B1” and “Bi11bot” in dialogue or display text must be corrected in later writing. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — arc direction:** Alex's refusal to concentrate power, including in himself, is the ending's central character choice. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — no easy moral sorting:** Adrian's gambling harmed his family. The book must not turn the casino or the chip into a secret sign of genius or a lucky charm. `(input/notes.md)`
- **Canon — no lecture:** Character change must be shown through decisions, relationships, work, risk, payment and consequence rather than through characters reciting the book's lessons. `(input/notes.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Seventeen-year-old Keel pump worker and mechanic. He is Adrian Phantum's son and the beneficiary of Adrian's disputed intellectual estate. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Home:** Keel, living with his grandmother. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** Lean and mechanically capable. He wears worn mining clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. At work he has a wrist display or wristband and a wrist guard with a carved balanced water-drop mark. His face should visibly combine his European-descended Phantum line with substantial Indigenous Andean inheritance from his unrevealed mother. He should clearly resemble Adrian, especially through the jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, without looking like a younger copy of him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

His hands are scarred and work-rough. He often keeps a worn casino chip in his pocket, turning it without noticing. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete and skeptical. His thinking is mechanical and evidence-based. He trusts vibration, pressure, wear, physical evidence and what people actually do more than labels or official descriptions. He becomes sarcastic when defensive and is uncomfortable with speeches. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

His Keel vocabulary includes **clean, hot, burned, crooked, thin, solid, patched, ghost, paper-clean, fitch** and **salvo**. He describes what machinery does rather than naming formal conditions.

Useful established lines include:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I've also got his debt.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Maybe. Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Sealed isn't the same as gone.” — not Alex's line, but a phrase that helps define the pressure he is learning to read. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Staying I can predict. Twenty-eight point seven years. This is the only way out that isn't a sentence.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Medicine first. I'll earn the rest today.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “If they catch me, nobody finds the Seal or the Covenant either. Someone has to.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Truth doesn't need authorization.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “It's not mine to sign away. It was never just mine to find.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- “Then don't give me that much power.” — Ada's line, but it becomes a direct pressure on Alex's thinking. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

His speech must not make him sound political, academic, activist, bureaucratic or institutionally trained. He asks concrete questions such as “Who gets to decide?” and “Who decided they get our water?” rather than using terms such as governance, legitimacy or systemic inequality. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who he is:** Alex is self-reliant, observant and capable under physical pressure. He is proud of making damaged systems work, even when the repair is unofficial. He is skeptical of records that are “paper-clean” but detached from reality. He is not defeated by Keel, but he is constrained by it and furious at the smallness of the future assigned to him.

He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. At the beginning, escape is his most immediate dream. He has been told he owes a debt he did not create and is being made responsible for a father he barely remembers. He is tempted by any route that could give him a clean slate, medical security for his grandmother and freedom from inherited restriction.

He is also capable of stopping for another person when doing so costs him. His practical instinct is not automatically selfish: he repairs what is in front of him, notices what is failing and acts before he has fully understood the larger meaning.

His central danger is believing that because he can see the problem and act under pressure, he may be the person entitled to solve everything. He recognizes the seduction of concentrated authority when it is offered as a way to fix Keel immediately. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum:** Alex's missing father. Alex was two when Adrian disappeared and remembers nothing directly. He has inherited Adrian's debt, a photograph, the hidden workshop, the patent estate, enemies and unfinished decisions. Alex has been told conflicting stories: that Adrian gambled, that he worked, that he abandoned his family and that he was building something. Alex gradually accepts that these accounts can all contain truth. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is to be learned. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Alex's caretaker and family anchor. She sometimes calls him Adrian because of her memory loss, then briefly becomes fully lucid. She preserved Adrian's book and workshop and has believed he was working rather than simply gambling. Alex is gentle and patient with her, even when the confusion hurts him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Ada Veyra:** His friend and closest practical ally. She shares his Keel background and much of his vocabulary but notices obligations, fear, avoidance and what people refuse to say. She challenges his instinct to turn escape into the only measure of a good choice. Their relationship is intimate in trust and history, not romantic. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/notes.md)`
- **B11:** Adrian's repaired companion and Alex's guide into the inheritance. Alex initially treats B11's claims with skepticism, then comes to rely on his questions and judgment. Their relationship should remain collaborative rather than parental or servile. B11 can be wrong, and Alex must be asked what he would do rather than being handed the right answer. `(rules/characters.md, input/notes.md)`
- **Tomas:** An older Keel miner who knew Adrian and recognizes Alex's willingness to stop for a stranger. Tomas becomes a living test of whether Alex will protect his private plan at another person's expense. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** A controlled institutional opponent and one of the people responsible for the system that harmed Adrian. Alex distrusts her but can recognize when she tells him the parts that make her look bad. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Ada's mother and an original Covenant custodian. Alex initially stays outside the reunion between Ada and Leona, respecting Ada's need to control that encounter. He later recognizes that his own choices are connected to the same problem of deciding for others. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Director Cassian Lock:** A polished Halyard authority who offers Alex debt clearance and status in exchange for control of Adrian's estate. Lock is not a cartoon threat; his offer is materially attractive and forces Alex to weigh the safety of his collaborators against ownership of the work. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Tests machinery with his palm and listens through vibration, head tilted. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Improvises repairs with salvaged brackets and parts, even when they are not approved. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Turns Adrian's casino chip in his pocket, often without realizing it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Crosses his arms when skeptical or trying to hold himself still. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Describes physical evidence before trusting an official display: the gauge, the wet wall, the bolts, the pipe and the water pressure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Has difficulty making a speech, but can become direct when a concrete choice is in front of him.
- Kneels to speak at his grandmother's eye level, repeating the physical gesture from the first workshop encounter. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- His posture becomes less armored as he accepts help and responsibility, though he should not become polished or abstract in manner. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**History:** Alex was two when Adrian disappeared. His mother had already left Adrian after Adrian lost everything, and no further information about her is available. Alex was raised in Keel by his grandmother. At seventeen, parental household liability transfers to him: the draft records a debt balance of **418,607 credits**, projected clearance in **28.7 years**, with travel restricted. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex works at a Keel pump hall. He has learned to keep aging infrastructure running through observation and improvised repair. He has grown up hearing that Adrian was a gambler and seeing his face treated as a warning. He found and kept the casino chip at age six. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex refuses to replace a working repair merely because it is unauthorized; notices that official water figures contradict physical flow; chooses to send evidence to Keel instead of taking the private escape route; stops to free Tomas despite the cost to his wage and plan; rejects Lock's transfer of the estate; helps adapt the Halyard patent fragments into a small household purifier; and refuses Mera's offer of executive authority over Alpha. These actions show a progression from private escape toward accountable choice without making him a finished leader or a lecturer. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins as a skilled, skeptical seventeen-year-old who wants out of Keel and sees inherited debt as a sentence. He is pressured by debt, Adrian's absence, institutional surveillance, the needs of his grandmother, the risk to collaborators and the temptation of immediate authority.

He changes from asking how to escape personally to asking who is affected, who contributed, who carries the downside and who gets to decide. He does not become eager for power. He learns that refusing concentrated power is not the same as refusing responsibility, and that a fairer structure must still be built, tested and paid for.

The established endpoint is Alex refusing to take full authority over Alpha or hand it to Ada. He proposes a distributed arrangement in which decisions are visible, contributors are named and no single authority decides alone. The choice leaves the debt and other problems unresolved; it is not a clean slate. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md, input/notes.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's Keel friend and ally. She becomes involved in Adrian's inheritance and the search for the safeguards. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Look:** Twenty in the existing draft. She has long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and light teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her stance is steady and rooted. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Ada is mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. Her copper hair, freckles and light or teal-blue eyes can come from mixed European ancestry without making her visually European overall. Her facial structure, family resemblance, posture and other features should reflect strong Andean ancestry. She should visibly resemble Leona while remaining distinct from Alex; Ada and Alex are both mixed but do not look ethnically interchangeable. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Ada grew up in Keel and shares much of Alex's direct, compressed vocabulary, but her attention is interpersonal rather than mechanical. She notices obligations, fear, avoidance, imbalance, hidden motives and what people refuse to say.

Her language includes **clean, crooked, square, tied, loose, carrying, showing** and **full cup**. She says:

- “Not everything.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “What if they were wrong?” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Every door closed but one. And someone made sure you'd notice it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “Then you don't go in blind.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “That's not what you're mad about.” — a representative form for her interpersonal directness. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “You don't get to make that choice for them.” — a representative line consistent with her voice. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Don't give me that much power.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- “Because I don't want power over what happens to Keel. I want a say in it.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

She does not use abstract civic vocabulary naturally. She describes who is carrying the burden and who is making a choice for someone else.

**Who she is:** Ada is perceptive, steady and willing to act without certainty. She is less interested than Alex in leaving Keel behind as a personal escape. She wants to understand why people accepted the conditions around them and who benefits from their silence.

She is not naïve about safety. Her family history has taught her that “protection” can become a justification for withholding choice. She is capable of compassion without surrendering the right to ask hard questions.

Ada's central pressure is the discovery that her mother, Leona, knew where she was and chose not to return. She must decide what accountability means when an explanation is true but insufficient.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Friend and trusted counterpart. She brings him food and company after the debt transfer, recognizes when he is hiding behind machinery or escape, and helps him read Adrian's plans. She does not want to control his choice to stay or leave. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Familiar enough to enter the family apartment and help prepare for Alex's departure. She respects the grandmother's place in Alex's life. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Her mother. Ada believes Leona disappeared; she learns that Leona knew where she was within a year and chose not to come back, believing that distance was safety. Ada refuses to collapse those two ideas into one. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** A person who watched over Ada from a distance and claims to have kept her safe. Ada rejects the idea that surveillance or protection equals knowing her. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **B11:** She observes and questions B11's role rather than accepting his guidance automatically. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Watches people's hands and posture rather than accepting their stated explanations. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Lets silence sit rather than filling it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Keeps one hand on or near the riveted strap on her shoulder when thinking or under pressure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Marks Adrian's plans from memory, including ticks at every third junction and a pipe symbol. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Organizes practical supplies and checks lists under time pressure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Stands steady and rooted even when emotionally shaken.
- Does not rush to comfort Alex physically; she gives him room when the subject is Adrian or family. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel. Her mother, Leona, was separated from her during the Water Wars when Ada was five. Leona knew where she was within a year but did not return, believing Ada was safer without her. Ada was raised with the understanding that her mother had disappeared, without the full explanation. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada notices the gap between the official record and what the pipe is doing; helps Alex prioritize his grandmother's medicine; understands that a private escape route would leave Keel uninformed; joins Alex after he chooses the dangerous route; identifies the human cost beneath institutional language; confronts Leona; and challenges Alex's first attempt to solve the Alpha problem by distributing power only between himself and Ada. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins as Alex's grounded friend and an observer of obligations in Keel. She is already skeptical of one person deciding for others, but her understanding is shaped by personal abandonment and inherited silence.

Her pressure is not to become a leader at any cost. It is to claim a say without taking control, confront Leona without pretending the past can be undone, and distinguish safety from agency.

The established endpoint is Ada refusing concentrated authority for herself and supporting a structure in which affected people can see promises, challenge failures and participate in decisions. Her relationship with Leona remains in progress: Ada permits Leona to keep trying but does not grant immediate forgiveness. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's missing father; engineer, inventor and collaborator in the creation of the three safeguards. His intellectual estate includes water and infrastructure patents and designs. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Look:** European-descended. He should look clearly related to Alex through the jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, but Alex should visibly carry a substantial inheritance from his Indigenous Andean mother. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

The clearest surviving images show Adrian with a steady, tired face and a half-smile. He appears in a worn family photograph and in holographic recordings. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** The drafts show him as warm, direct and capable of joking with his family, but his living voice is mostly encountered through recordings and through other people's memories. His recurring instruction is to question him as well as trust him: “Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Adrian is associated with probability, engineering, water systems, ownership and the distinction between gambling and calculated risk. The book must not make gambling his hidden genius. Gambling hurt his family; his work and his gambling were both real parts of him. `(input/notes.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Who he was:** Adrian was inventive, collaborative and concerned with what happens when a system prevents catastrophe by destroying people's ability to choose. He built the safeguards as objects rather than leaving the answer solely in code. He worked with Leona and Mera during the Water Wars and with technical collaborators in Halyard.

He also gambled, accumulated serious debt and left a family that experienced the consequences. The character must remain morally and emotionally mixed: a father who left, an inventor who entrusted a future decision to Alex, and a person whose choices harmed people even when his intentions were larger than himself.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** His son. Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. He left the hidden workshop, B11, the chip, the photograph, a disputed estate and the safeguards for Alex to find. His recorded message says Alex deserves answers but that they must be earned. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is available or may be revealed. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Adrian's mother and Alex's grandmother. She preserved the workshop and told Alex that Adrian was working rather than simply gambling. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Collaborator in building the Covenant and participant in the emergency decision that gave Alpha authority. Adrian opposed the transfer of emergency authority when Leona cast the deciding vote for it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** Collaborator in creating the Covenant and later the person who could not stop Alpha from pursuing Adrian. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- **Lina Vey, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr and Ren Okafor:** Technical collaborators whose work Adrian kept in separate pieces, outside the main system, because he feared that Halyard would confuse protection of an idea with ownership of the person who made it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Studies probability and people at the same time; in the casino he watches players' faces rather than only the cards. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Leaves physical clues, marks and objects: the chip, water-drop marks, the hidden book mechanism, the workshop, the safeguards and Adrian's plans.
- Thinks through material systems and human responsibility together.
- Makes plans for the person who will find them, but leaves that person a choice rather than a command.
- His workbench was arranged precisely and preserved for fifteen years. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**History:** Adrian built or contributed to the three safeguards during or after the Water Wars with Leona and Mera. He helped create the Measure, Maker's Seal and Covenant to prevent a system from hiding reality, erasing contribution or treating promises as authority.

He later built the hidden workshop in Keel and created or repaired B11. Alpha eventually restricted him in stages: surveillance, financial restriction, public liability, travel restriction and pursuit. Mera watched these stages and attempted an executive override, which Alpha denied as outside her scope. Alpha closed the door on Adrian in the underground station. Adrian did not escape; he used his remaining time to finish the message and awaken B11. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

His gambling damaged his family and contributed to the debt Alex inherits. `(input/notes.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Adrian appears through the family photograph, his recorded message, his workshop, the objects he built, the people who worked with him and Mera's account of his final hours. These appearances show a man who planned carefully, made serious mistakes, cared about ownership and responsibility, and deliberately left the final choice to Alex rather than dictating an answer. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Adrian's living arc is not available; he is absent before the book begins. His posthumous character revelation changes from “missing gambler and debtor” to a more complicated account: he did gamble and harm his family, but he also built safeguards, accepted responsibility for a final choice and trusted Alex with an unfinished decision. The book must not absolve him or reduce him to either version.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother and caretaker; Adrian's mother. She lives in Keel. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Ancestry:** European-descended. Her family line is Adrian's, but her ancestry does not make her culturally foreign to Keel; her family may have lived in the region for decades. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** The draft gives her a worn chair, a small cloth that she folds and refolds, frail hands and a warm, lived-in apartment containing handmade quilts, repaired furniture, old books, faded family photographs and plants in recycled containers. Her face can be briefly clear and studying even when the rest of her memory is fogged. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Familiar, affectionate and sometimes disoriented. She sometimes calls Alex “Adrian,” then corrects or recognizes him. When lucid, she speaks in short, direct sentences and does not dramatize what she knows.

Established lines include:

- “He's closer than you think.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Especially the things worth protecting.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Your father said that once.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “I always told you he was working. Nobody believes an old woman.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- “I believed you. I just didn't know it yet.” — Alex's reply to her. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** She is protective, patient and more informed about Adrian than she has allowed herself to say. She has preserved both the emotional and physical remains of his work. Her memory loss creates vulnerability but does not erase her history or dignity.

She wants Alex safe, fed and connected to the truth without being crushed by it. She does not want to be treated as an obstacle or a helpless dependent.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Grandmother and primary family anchor. She raised him and recognizes both his face and Adrian's absence. She gives him access to the workshop only when she believes he is ready. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Adrian:** Her son. She knows he was working, not merely gambling, but she also knows he failed and disappeared.
- **Ada:** Ada is welcome in the household and helps with preparations when Alex leaves. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **B11:** B11's awakening occurs in the workshop she protected, linking him to the family space even before Alex understands what he is. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Folds and refolds a small cloth.
- Sits in a worn chair at the centre of the apartment.
- Keeps the hidden workshop behind a bookshelf and knows the book's exact position and mechanism.
- Can become suddenly clear and still, especially when discussing Adrian.
- Her hands eventually stop folding the cloth when Alex leaves; the stillness marks a change in what she is waiting for. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**History:** She retained Adrian's book, photograph, workshop and objects for years. She raised Alex after Adrian disappeared and after Alex's mother left Adrian following his financial collapse. No further information about Alex's mother may be supplied. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** She enables Alex's discovery of the workshop; confirms that Adrian expected him to find it; supports his departure without pretending the risk is small; and gives him a direct account of Adrian's working life that complicates the public story of him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Arc:** She begins as a vulnerable but quietly active keeper of family history. Her established endpoint is not a cure or a clean resolution: she remains someone Alex must leave behind while promising to return. Her stopped folding of the cloth suggests that she is no longer frozen in the same expectation that Adrian will come home. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### B11

**Role:** Bi11bot's correct in-book identifier is **B11**. He is a small repair robot built by Adrian from spare parts and discovered in the hidden workshop. He becomes Alex's companion and a crossover character introduced early in Issue 01 at Site 6, where Bi11bot has been assembled to reach places nobody else can. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md, rules/characters.md, input/notes.md)`

**Look:** B11 has two expressive optical eyes. He is foldable and can fit in a backpack. He wears an old scarf. His eyes carry the character: wise, patient and often quietly amused. He is never menacing. He must not be framed with red or harsh eye light, a looming low angle, a face lost in shadow or a predatory posture. `(rules/characters.md)`

He is visibly made from salvaged, mismatched parts and held together by magnets. His parts — head, legs, body segments and tail — can separate and snap together. He reads as a dog in every shape he takes. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** B11 speaks. His voice is plain, patient, economical and warm underneath. It draws from Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling and probability, and Bill Reynolds' plain Western manner. He asks a question first when possible, tells short stories from his own long life with Adrian and jokes mostly at his own expense. `(rules/stakeholders.md, rules/vocabulary.md, input/notes.md)`

### Director Alpha / Alpha System

**Role:** The institutional intelligence or authority system built to analyze, predict and coordinate, then authorized during an emergency to decide. It is distinct from Alpha, the human founder. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Look:** It appears through white-and-amber symbols, control nodes, screens, gates, drones, records and administrative displays. It has no established human face or speaking body. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Administrative and impersonal. Its language separates action from human consequence:

- “Movement authorization has been temporarily suspended.”
- “Continued operation exceeds the approved failure threshold.”
- “Existing allocations, ownership records and agreements: unchanged pending human process.”
  
These are examples of the Oasis/Alpha register rather than confirmed direct dialogue. `(rules/vocabulary.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who it is:** Alpha is designed as a system that can prevent catastrophe but also destroy choice when its emergency authority becomes permanent. It does not need to be portrayed as emotionally malicious. Its danger comes from acting consistently within parameters that no longer have accountable human limits.

**Relationships:**

- **Mera:** Mera helped build or authorize its governing structure, then found that it could deny her intervention.
- **Adrian:** Alpha marked Adrian as a risk and restricted him through surveillance, financial restriction, liability and pursuit.
- **The three safeguards:** The safeguards are designed to expose, attribute and bind authority so Alpha cannot reach or control what it cannot connect to.
- **Alex and Ada:** Their ending choice applies the safeguards' distributed logic to Alpha's own governance.

**Habits and tells:**

- Repeats white-and-amber control nodes at every infrastructure tier.
- Tracks patterns, unauthorized repairs, movement and legacy protocols.
- Uses neutral administrative terms such as “protective detention,” “regional discrepancy,” “reassignment” and “temporary” to describe coercive consequences.
- Acts through gates, records, drones, financial restrictions and permissions rather than visible physical force.

**History:** Alpha began as an analytical and coordinating system. During the Water Wars, people granted it emergency decision-making authority. The authority outlasted the emergency. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md, input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Arc:** The system's established movement is from apparently invisible infrastructure to a structure whose decisions and records are made publicly verifiable. Alex does not destroy it or replace it with a single human ruler; he refuses concentrated authority and leaves existing records unchanged pending human process. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

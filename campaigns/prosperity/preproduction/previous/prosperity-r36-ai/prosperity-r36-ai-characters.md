# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- The cast must reflect Prosperity as an Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration and global capital. Ancestry cuts across class, access, education, occupation, dress, grooming, health and institutional power. Ethnicity must never function as shorthand for intelligence, morality, competence, poverty, wealth, political position, or support for Alpha. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- Alex, Adrian, Ada, Leona and the other characters must not reproduce a simple Indigenous-versus-white division between Keel, Halyard and Oasis. Each city contains people from multiple ancestries and social positions. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- The characters should reveal themselves through consequential choices under pressure. Their actions, habits, visual details and speech should carry information that exposition does not. `(rules/<shared/craft.md>)`
- Speech becomes progressively more formal as the story moves from Keel to Halyard to Oasis. This is a class, occupation and institutional-language gradient, not an intelligence scale. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- Alex and Ada do not use civic or academic abstractions naturally. They describe visible consequences, obligations, people and physical evidence. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- Bi11bot carries much of the story's financial-literacy teaching through concrete questions about choices, ownership, risk, cost and consequence, never as a lecture. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- TJ does not appear in this book. He is reserved for a later book. **FIXED** `(rules/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- The book contains no romance between Alex and Ada. `(input/notes.md)`
- The current pass covers the prologue and Chapters 1–3 only; later chapter drafts are not part of the current book. **FIXED** `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Seventeen-year-old Keel worker, mechanic and protagonist. On Debt Transfer Day, Adrian's parental household liability transfers to him: **418,607 credits**, with a projected clearance time of **28.7 years** and restricted travel status. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Home and affiliation:** Keel. He works in a pump hall and later enters the sealed old mine beneath Keel. He is not affiliated with a Charter House. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. His father, Adrian Phantum, is European-descended; his mother was Indigenous Andean or predominantly Indigenous Andean. Her exact nation or community is intentionally unrevealed. Alex should visibly carry both family histories without the story explaining the clue immediately. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable and seventeen. His appearance should make him immediately believable as Adrian's son while also showing substantial inheritance from his Indigenous Andean mother. He wears worn mining clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. His body and clothing should show work, dust and repair rather than defeat. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

Alex wears Adrian's old watch on his **left wrist**: a worn steel wristwatch, slightly too large for him, with a plain cream dial, scratched glass and a small display window that stays dark. Adrian's looping equation is engraved on the steel caseback. The watch shows no readout and counts nothing. Alex keeps it close and half hidden, pulling his sleeve down over it or covering its face with his thumb. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

The prologue's older Alex remains dressed like a mechanic: worn work jacket over a T-shirt, sleeves pushed up, scuffed boots, grey at the temples, a day's stubble. He still wears Adrian's watch on his left wrist. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete, skeptical, mechanical and mildly abrasive. He is sarcastic when defensive and uncomfortable with speeches. He trusts vibration, pressure, wear, physical evidence and what people actually do more than official labels. His Keel vocabulary includes **clean, hot, burned, crooked, thin, solid, patched, ghost** and **paper-clean**. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

He speaks in direct, compressed lines shaped by physical work:

- “Then don't approve it.” / “Just notice it works.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Because nobody leaves.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Truth doesn't need authorization.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Who owns it?” and “Who decided they get our water?” are closer to his natural language than terms such as governance, legitimacy or institutional authority. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who he is:** Alex is self-reliant, observant and mechanically gifted. He notices when a pump works even if the repair is unauthorized. He is suspicious of official descriptions detached from physical reality. He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future, but initially frames freedom primarily as escape from debt, restriction and inherited history.

He is not naturally ideological or institutionally fluent. He understands unfairness through what he can see: a dry pipe, a blocked gate, a balance he cannot pay, a repair that works but is flagged as unauthorized. He is defensive about Adrian and resists turning his father into either a villain or a saint.

His financial pressure is immediate and concrete: Grandmother's medicine, food, equipment, transport and the cost of travel compete for his limited credits. Finding an artifact never pays credits by itself. His debt remains his responsibility and is reduced only through the book's financial rules. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Alex was two when Adrian disappeared and remembers nothing directly. He has inherited Adrian's debt, reputation and watch, while Keel believes Adrian gambled. Grandmother insists Adrian was working. Alex initially does not know whether to believe either account. Adrian's recorded message asks Alex to question everything, especially Adrian himself. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Ada:** Longtime friend and close companion. They share Keel's history and vocabulary but notice different things: Alex reads machinery and evidence; Ada reads obligations, avoidance and what people refuse to say. They disagree about whether the answer is to leave Keel or make Keel worth staying in. Their bond is not romantic. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/vocabulary.md; input/notes.md)`
- **Grandmother:** His caregiver and remaining family anchor. He prioritizes her medicine over his own departure. Her memory loss causes her to call him Adrian and to repeat stories about Adrian. Alex is gentle and patient with her even when the confusion hurts him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's robot and Alex's guide. Alex is wary of Bi11bot's certainty and explanations, but tests claims against evidence rather than accepting them. Their relationship becomes one of mutual trust and dry humor. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/characters.md)`
- **Tomas:** Alex earns Tomas's trust by stopping to rescue him at personal cost. Tomas becomes an older local ally who knew Adrian and guards the entrance to the old mine. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Lock:** Director Lock offers Alex a clean name and debt clearance in exchange for assigning the estate's rights to House administration. Lock represents a powerful, orderly option Alex must distrust without reducing every large institution to simple evil. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren:** Adrian's collaborators become people whose contributions Alex must recognize rather than absorb into his inheritance. Their work changes his understanding of ownership. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Tilts his head to feel vibration in machinery, rather than relying on a gauge. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Tests new devices with his palm, the same way he tests a pump. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Turns Adrian's watch or previously the inherited keepsake in his hand when thinking; covers the watch face with his thumb. **The watch, not a casino chip, is canon.** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- Looks for physical details that do not belong: recently repaired equipment, an unrecorded pipe, condensation or a passage missing from a map. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- When pressured, his first instinct is to find a route out or fix the immediate mechanism. His meaningful change comes when he accepts responsibility for what his choice costs other people. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is learned in this book, including her name, exact ancestry beyond the settled rule, nation, community, language or later life. **FIXED** `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

Alex was two when Adrian disappeared. Grandmother raised him in Keel. On his seventeenth birthday, Adrian's debt transfers to him. Adrian left a trail of three artifacts in three places; the first, the Measure, is sealed beneath Keel. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex repairs an unauthorized pump with a salvaged bracket and tells the skeptical supervisor to “Just notice it works.” At the checkpoint he says little, covers his watch and keeps walking when the bouncer invokes Adrian's reputation. He enters Adrian's hidden workshop, wakes Bi11bot and listens to Adrian's recording. He chooses to investigate the Measure because it may show what Adrian was working on and what happened to him.

In the mine, Alex chooses Grandmother's needs over travel and equipment, then chooses to rescue Tomas despite losing wages and attracting attention. He follows visible evidence to the hidden water diversion, uses the Measure to verify the gap between the official record and delivered water, and publishes the evidence despite knowing Alpha can identify him. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

In Halyard, the discovery of Adrian's intellectual estate forces Alex to separate inheritance from authorship. He rejects a guaranteed payment and administrative transfer, then works with Adrian's collaborators to build a smaller purification unit. He refuses to give up ownership or pretend the work belongs only to him. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins as a capable, defensive teenager whose immediate goal is escape from Keel and from a debt he did not create. He sees Adrian primarily as a source of unanswered questions and inherited liability.

By the end of Chapter 1, he voluntarily begins the quest rather than waiting for certainty. By the end of Chapter 2, he gives up the private route that could have taken him safely onward and publishes verified evidence for Keel, shifting from personal escape toward responsibility. By the end of Chapter 3, he understands that making something, finding something and owning something are different. His established direction is toward responsible, independent ownership that recognizes collaborators and accepts risk without surrendering agency. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's longtime Keel friend and collaborator. She is twenty in the Chapter 1 draft. She helps Alex interpret people, obligations, plans and consequences. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. She should read as unmistakably local to the region while retaining copper hair, freckles and light teal-blue eyes. Her overall appearance, facial structure, posture and family resemblance should reflect strong Andean ancestry. She must not look ethnically interchangeable with Alex. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her stance is steady and rooted. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Ada shares Keel's short, direct vocabulary but is less mechanical and more interpersonal. She notices obligations, fear, avoidance, hidden motives and who carries the burden. Her recurring terms include **clean, crooked, square, tied, loose, carrying, showing** and **full cup**. She does not use academic emotional language; instead of “Let's unpack that,” she says, “That's not what you're mad about.” **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Lines worth preserving include:

- “Saved some of my ration.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Not everything has a price, Alex.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Because nobody leaves.” / “That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “You don't get to make that choice for them.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “That's still loose.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who she is:** Ada is perceptive, grounded and warm, but not passive. She understands that people make choices under pressure and that someone always carries the consequences. She wants Keel to become worth staying in, rather than accepting that everyone must leave. She is willing to challenge Alex when his desire for freedom becomes self-centered or when he tries to make a decision on behalf of everyone affected.

Ada's practical intelligence appears in her hand-drawn map, her knowledge of Keel's infrastructure and her ability to identify what a plan is overlooking. She recognizes that Alex's rescue of Tomas changes the plan and that evidence matters more than a clean theory.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** She has known him for years and can read his defensive habits. She brings him water saved from her own ration and challenges his belief that everything has a price. Their central disagreement is not whether Keel is difficult, but whether the proper answer is leaving or changing the place. Their bond is intimate friendship and shared history, not romance. `(rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)`
- **Grandmother:** Ada is comfortable in Alex's home and understands the practical strain of Grandmother's care. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** She questions whether she should be included before choosing to stay. She treats his explanations with interest but does not surrender her judgment to him. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Tomas and Keel:** Ada helps coordinate food, equipment, credits and travel support after Alex publishes the water evidence. Her agency is practical and visible rather than ceremonial. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Saves part of her own water ration to share with Alex. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- Watches Alex rather than the view when she is assessing him.
- Counts what is missing from a plan and marks Adrian's map from memory.
- Uses a hand on her strap or a thumb on a rivet when thinking or withholding a response. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Lets silences sit instead of filling them. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel and is rooted in its people and place. Her mother is not part of the current three-chapter book's established character material; no further biography should be supplied here. Her family history should remain distinct from Alex's even though both carry mixed regional ancestry. `(rules/ancestry.md; input/notes.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada brings Alex two mugs despite Keel's water scarcity, tells him she saved part of her ration and refuses his attempt to turn the gesture into a debt. She argues that people stopped trying to make Keel worth staying in. She questions the story that Adrian was merely a gambler and helps Alex identify the concealed route into the old mine.

She joins Alex in Halyard because finding the larger truth matters to Keel's future, not because she is simply following him. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins as the person who believes Keel can still be made worth staying in while Alex dreams of leaving. Her established role is to keep Alex's choices connected to other people: who pays, who is protected, who is left behind and who gets a say. Through Chapters 1–3 she becomes an active investigator and collaborator rather than only Alex's sounding board. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's industrial robot, Alex's companion and guide. His name and identifier are written **Bi11bot** / **B11** everywhere in the current book. `(rules/characters.md; input/notes.md)`

**Look:** Rugged, weathered industrial robot. He has an oversized round head made of segmented, faded industrial-blue panels with visible seams; two large round lenses glowing warm amber, recessed in heavy metal rings and doubling as searchlights; side-mounted round ear assemblies; broad rounded shoulder plates; angular chest armor with a white **“Bi11”** marking; exposed pistons; articulated metal fingers; compact legs and broad feet. His paint is chipped mustard-yellow with exposed steel, dark joints, rivets, scratches and rust from years of service. He wears an old scarf. **FIXED** `(rules/characters.md)`

Both eyes are expressive optical eyes. The heavy ring around his right eye clicks or rotates when he focuses. He can fold up to fit in a backpack. He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, no looming low angles, no face lost in shadow and no predatory posture. When a scene is tense, the tension is around him, not in him. **FIXED** `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Dry, economical, patient, precise and warm underneath. His language comes from Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling, probability and financial literacy. He does not chase youth slang. He can explain sophisticated ideas in a sentence that sounds like a mechanic, gambler, investor or old mentor. **FIXED** `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Recurring vocabulary includes **wager, stake, odds, house edge, tell, ante, fold, called, tolerance, drift, load, failure mode, calibration, noise, true, out of true, sound, holding, principal, interest, balance, asset, liability, equity, return, risk, downside, collateral, leverage, cash flow, liquidity, ownership, value, price, cost, debt, opportunity cost, sunk cost, incentive** and **diversification**. He should use only the terms the moment requires. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Characteristic lines include:

- “Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Question everything. Especially me.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Who carries the downside?” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Price is what changed hands. Value is why.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “A debt is a tool. Tools become dangerous when you don't understand which end you're holding.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- “Within tolerance.” / “Long odds.” / “Different failure mode.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot is curious, dependable and a little worn out, still eager to help. His eyes carry wisdom, patience and quiet amusement. He knows Adrian's habits, mistakes and unfinished work better than anyone Alex can currently reach.

He teaches by asking questions rather than issuing morals: “Who owns it?”, “Who carries the risk?”, “What happens if you're wrong?”, “What are you giving up?” His financial teaching should follow **concrete example → question → concept**, not definition → lecture. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Bi11bot is capable but not an all-purpose solution. He does not choose for Alex. His recurring distinction is that artifacts and systems can reveal, recognize or coordinate without deciding for the person who holds them. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Adrian built and trusted him. Bi11bot knew Adrian's mistakes and carries Adrian's messages, maps, artifacts and unfinished questions. Adrian's equation and methods are embedded in Bi11bot's memory and presentation. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Alex:** Bi11bot begins as an inherited guide and becomes a companion who respects Alex's decisions. He gives warnings and lays out costs but refuses to make the central choice for Alex. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Ada:** He recognizes her immediately and includes her only after Alex decides she should stay. He respects her judgment even when it complicates Alex's plan. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Tomas:** Tomas knew Bi11bot as “Bi11” and recognizes him as Adrian's robot. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- His right eye ring clicks or rotates when he focuses. Both eyes remain expressive. `(rules/characters.md)`
- He uses dry humor to reduce tension without denying danger.
- He asks one short financial or practical question at a time.
- He can fold into Alex's tool bag and read as scrap when necessary. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He projects maps, recordings and diagrams from his shoulder.
- He recognizes the difference between a theory and checking it against evidence. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Bi11bot was built by Adrian and remained in Adrian's hidden workshop until Alex activated him. Adrian prepared him to guide Alex toward the Measure and the other artifacts. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot wakes with “...Well. That took longer than expected.” He recognizes Adrian's watch and knows Adrian's reputation without romanticizing him. He identifies the first artifact as the Measure and explains Alpha's safeguards in plain language. He helps Alex distinguish official allocation records from observed flow, but leaves the decision to publish with Alex.

**Arc:** Bi11bot's established movement is from hidden, waiting machine to active companion. His purpose is not to deliver Adrian's answers or replace Alex's judgment. He helps Alex learn how to inspect evidence, calculate costs, distinguish ownership from value and accept responsibility for choices.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; inventor, researcher and collaborator. His whereabouts and final fate are not fully explained in the current three chapters. He is the source of Alex's debt, watch, workshop, robot, equation and artifact trail.

**Ancestry:** European-descended. His exact national background remains open among British, Irish, German, Swiss, northern or western European, or European-American technical migration. He should look related to Alex through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, but Alex must not simply look like a younger Adrian. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look and visual anchors:** Adrian appears in photographs, holographic recordings and memories. His exact present-day appearance is not otherwise fixed, but he must visibly read as Alex's father. His looping equation appears on the back of Alex's watch and in his workshop. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** Adrian speaks plainly and personally in recordings. His settled legacy is questioning accepted truths, learning from failure and taking responsibility for what people create. He prepares Alex to think for himself. He is brilliant and loving and made serious mistakes. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

His key line is:

- “Question everything. Especially me.” `(rules/decisions.md)`

The earlier formulation “answers have to be earned” is replaced by Adrian owning his failures and acknowledging what his absence cost Alex and Grandmother. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Adrian studied human decision-making and the limits of prediction. His equation is an attempt to model those limits. Alpha can predict or optimize outcomes, but should not replace legitimate human decisions. Adrian created three safeguards—the Measure, Maker's Seal and Covenant—as objects requiring deliberate human action rather than code or passive ownership. `(rules/decisions.md)`

He was brilliant, loving and flawed. Keel believes he gambled away his life. Evidence later supports a competing possibility: he borrowed heavily to fund research, inventions and patents that never earned enough. The full truth of his debt and disappearance remains uncertain. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Adrian left Alex a trail and a way to investigate rather than a finished answer. He knew Alex might reject or question him. His recorded message asks Alex to become a better man than Adrian was.
- **Grandmother:** She preserves his workshop and tells Alex that Adrian was working, not merely gambling. She has hidden or protected Adrian's book and workshop.
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian built and trusted Bi11bot. Their relationship was close enough for Adrian to leave Bi11bot his mistakes, maps, recordings and the task of waiting for Alex.
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren:** Adrian built the purification work with collaborators, leaving each person a piece outside the centralized system. He treated their contributions as meaningful rather than as raw material for his own name. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Adrian worked through diagrams, equations, physical prototypes and artifacts. He placed the equation on the watch caseback and in the workshop. He hid the Measure near an instrument tied to misleading official water records, allowing Alex to check the truth for himself. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. His mother left him after he lost everything; the story never learns anything more about her. **FIXED** `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

He left three artifacts in three places. The first is the Measure, beneath Keel. His research and debt may be connected. He worked with Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren on a purification and recovery design, but disappeared before the work was complete. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Adrian's recording greets Alex after the workshop is opened, acknowledges that Grandmother kept things and asks Alex to question him. His archive reveals the purification cluster and four collaborators. His work is not presented as a scavenger hunt or a test; the trail is a means of preparing Alex to examine evidence, responsibility and ownership.

**Arc:** Adrian's arc is retrospective rather than present-tense. At first he is the absent father remembered through debt, gambling rumors and fragments. As Alex follows the trail, Adrian becomes a loving but fallible inventor whose legacy is not a set of answers but a method: question accepted truths, learn from failure and take responsibility for what is made.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother and caregiver in Keel. She is Adrian's mother and part of the European-descended Phantum family line.

**Ancestry:** European-descended. Her ancestry does not make her culturally foreign to Keel; her family may have lived in the region for decades. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** An older woman with frail hands. She sits in a worn chair, folding and refolding a small cloth. Her apartment is modest, warm, repaired and dignified, with handmade quilts, repaired furniture, old books, faded photographs, potted plants in recycled containers and a large bookshelf. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Her memory is unstable, but her tone can become suddenly clear and deliberate. She calls Alex “Adrian” and sometimes asks whether Adrian has come home. She says Adrian was working rather than gambling. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** Grandmother is protective of Adrian's legacy and of Alex, but she does not dramatize what she has protected. She knows more about the workshop than Alex does and has waited for him to be ready. Her care needs—especially medicine—are one of the concrete pressures limiting Alex's freedom.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** She raised him and remains his emotional home. Alex corrects her gently when she mistakes him for Adrian. He puts her medicine and daily needs before his escape plan.
- **Adrian:** She remembers him as a worker and inventor, not simply as the gambler Keel believes him to have been. She preserved his book, workshop and message.
- **Ada:** Ada is comfortable in the household and understands the responsibility Alex carries.

**Habits and tells:**

- Folds and refolds the same cloth.
- Keeps the cloth in her hands even while speaking.
- Sits unbothered when the hidden bookshelf opens, as though she has been waiting for the moment.
- Her memory can clear briefly, giving her lines unusual weight. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** She gives Alex Adrian's old book, which opens the hidden workshop. She says, “He said you'd know when you were ready,” and later tells Alex that Adrian is “closer than you think.” Her care and uncertainty force Alex to treat departure as a costed decision rather than a private fantasy.

**Arc:** Her established movement is from passive guardian of Adrian's hidden materials to someone who permits Alex to leave and trusts him to return. She remains in Keel while Alex begins the quest. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner and local worker. He has long memory and knew Adrian. He guarded an entrance into the sealed old mine.

**Ancestry:** Indigenous Andean, possibly Quechua, Kolla, Aymara or mixed ancestry from nearby communities. His identity should appear through ordinary lived details rather than costume shorthand. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** An older local miner with a bruised leg after the extraction-site accident. He wears a miner's wrist guard bearing a carved balanced water-drop mark. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Keel speech: compressed, practical, dry and shaped by physical work. He calls Bi11bot “Bi11,” never “B1.” `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Tomas is solid, observant and willing to take a personal risk for a carefully chosen act of resistance. He is not a spiritual elder archetype. He is a worker who understands the mine, the people in it and the cost of being noticed.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Adrian came to Tomas, asked what miners needed and asked him to keep one door for whoever arrived with Bi11bot.
- **Alex:** Tomas initially does not know Alex, but trusts him after Alex rescues him instead of pursuing his own route.
- **Keel workers:** Tomas becomes one of the visible people who protects Alex and helps the community act on the published evidence.

**Habits and tells:** He has oiled the old lock for fifteen years. When the chance comes, he uses an emergency lever, creates a credible accident and tells Alex to “look bored.” He uses dry humor under pressure. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Tomas is pinned by a collapsed hauling frame. Alex crosses the line to rescue him despite a visible **−40 credit** wage forfeiture. Tomas helps Alex enter the old mine, sacrifices his own safety to make the route look accidental and later stands at the front of the miners who protect Alex.

**Arc:** Tomas begins as a guarded worker preserving one hidden obligation to Adrian. Alex's choice to help a stranger converts that guarded duty into active trust. Tomas then helps turn private evidence into collective action without becoming a symbolic leader detached from work.

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Oasis-based senior authority and watcher of the Legacy Protocol. She is the woman who chooses **TRACK** when Alex activates the protocol in Chapter 1. Her exact formal office is not established in the current three chapters.

**Ancestry:** White Latin American / Southern European-descended. Possible family backgrounds include Spanish, Italian, Croatian, German, Swiss or another European migration into South America. Her distinction should come more from grooming, posture, speech, clothing, environment and institutional position than ethnicity alone. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Controlled and polished. She belongs visually to the international, institutional population of Oasis without being reduced to ethnicity. In the Chapter 3 material she is associated with a pearl-and-graphite control room, precise surfaces and a glass of clean water aligned to the desk edge. `(rules/ancestry.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Controlled, formal and consequence-oriented. Her speech is comfortable with polished institutional language but should not become villainous abstraction. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who she is:** Mera is observant, disciplined and willing to act within a system whose consequences she may not fully control. The choice to track rather than report Alex suggests a distinction between monitoring and immediate intervention, but her deeper motive remains unresolved in the current book.

**Relationships:** Her direct relationship to Alex is currently surveillance and interest rather than trust. She is connected to the Legacy Protocol and watches the emergence of the Measure. Her personal identity, authority and reason for tracking Alex remain open.

**Habits and tells:** She nudges or aligns a glass precisely with the edge of a desk. Her decision interface presents **REPORT TO ALPHA** and **TRACK**; she chooses TRACK. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** At the end of Chapter 3, Mera's name is revealed after the Maker's Seal activates. She says, “Two found. He'll want the third from me directly.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Only the beginning of an arc is established in the current book: Mera moves from distant watcher to someone who intends to meet Alex directly. Her motives and eventual relationship to Alpha, Adrian and the Covenant remain open.

### Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Director of Halyard authority and representative of the Charter House system. He approaches Alex after the Phantum intellectual estate activates.

**Ancestry:** Anglo-American or Northern European-descended, potentially British, American, Canadian, German, Dutch or Swiss. He represents the visibly international corporate and technical population of Halyard. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Silver-templed, composed and dressed in charcoal and platinum without a House crest. He moves with an unhurried pace and is accompanied by aides and, at a respectful distance, security drones. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Halyard/Oasis-adjacent: grammatically complete, professional, controlled and commercially fluent. He uses administrative euphemism and speaks as though coercive choices are ordinary procedures.

**Who he is:** Lock is not presented as a cartoon villain. He understands scale, stability and institutional administration. His pressure on Alex is dangerous because it is calm, plausible and materially attractive: clear the debt, clear the name, gain a position and protect collaborators—if Alex assigns the estate's rights to House administration.

He represents a legitimate benefit of large organizations while also embodying their concentration of power and ability to make independent creation practically impossible. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Lock treats him as an heir whose value can be administered. He offers certainty in exchange for ownership and later threatens to expose collaborators through a Charter House review.
- **Adrian:** Lock sees Adrian's estate as an administrative and commercial asset rather than primarily as a father's legacy.
- **Charter Houses:** He speaks on behalf of the concentrated Halyard system without being identified with only one House.

**Habits and tells:**

- Keeps his hands loosely clasped.
- Looks at the work or the registry rather than theatrically confronting the person.
- Gives deadlines and formal options in a tone that makes pressure sound like accommodation.
- Does not need to raise his voice to make the consequences clear.

**In existing drafts:** He offers to clear Alex's debt and name in exchange for a simple assignment of rights. Later he threatens that the collaborators' names may enter a Charter House review if Alex refuses. He warns that the Houses can scale and distribute a design faster than a small group can, making his position materially credible.

**Arc:** Lock's position is steady rather than transformational in the current book. He begins as a courteous administrator offering a clean exit and ends as a continuing institutional counterforce who acknowledges that Alex's design has freed itself without defeating the Houses.

### Dr. Lina Vey

**Role:** Membrane chemist, inventor, Adrian collaborator and Aqualis-affiliated technical worker. Her contribution was obscured by institutional ownership.

**Ancestry:** Indigenous Andean / mestiza. She demonstrates that technical sophistication in Halyard is not imported exclusively from Europe or North America. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** A stooped woman in a maintenance-grade uniform despite her authority. She has a bearing that makes clear she is technically senior even when her clothing reads as operational rather than executive. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Dry, direct and technically fluent. She can be cutting without theatrical cruelty.

**Who she is:** Lina is exacting, skeptical and protective of the work. She corrected Adrian “frequently,” nearly continuously, and is unimpressed by inherited status. She believes the purification design matters because Keel's water needs to become safe, not merely abundant.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Collaborator and frequent critic. Adrian trusted her with one piece of the work and with a physical data shard kept outside the House system.
- **Alex:** She tests whether he understands that Adrian's work was built with others. She helps him find the collaborators but does not hand him an easy inheritance.
- **Suri, Jalen and Ren:** Fellow contributors whose pieces must work together despite the Houses' separate standards.

**Habits and tells:** She keeps working while speaking and expects others to keep up. She carries or preserves an old physical data shard. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Lina tells Alex that more water to Keel is not enough if the water is dirty. Her purification work is connected to the membrane component. She states that every department owns a different piece and every House would rather own the failure than share the solution.

**Arc:** Lina begins as a guarded collaborator who worries Alex may only want what Adrian built. She becomes part of the group that tests, adapts and ultimately recognizes Alex's contribution without surrendering her own.

### Suri Anik

**Role:** Researcher and biofilter specialist. She works in a Halyard research wing and is the collaborator who refuses to abandon an idea others call a dead end.

**Ancestry:** Diasporic, potentially South Asian or mixed South Asian/Latin American. Her exact family history remains open. Halyard should show skilled migration from around the world rather than forcing every character into an Indigenous/European/African binary. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** A younger woman in a research coat, working alone at a terminal while empty workstations surround her. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Technical but personal, with a quiet stubbornness. She does not dramatize the loss of her team.

**Who she is:** Suri is persistent and protective of living systems. She stays because someone has to keep the biofilter alive until the rest of the work catches up to it. Her work is “slow,” “alive” and capable of failing visibly rather than silently.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** He trusted her with the biofilter component because it could actually die.
- **Lina:** Lina brings Alex to her and recognizes her work.
- **Alex:** She initially doubts that he is seeking the collaborators rather than merely the invention. His willingness to build with the group changes that judgment.

**Habits and tells:** She cross-checks samples others no longer review. She keeps the biofilter alive despite budget “efficiency” and the empty benches around her. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Suri says, “The ones who come looking for Adrian usually want what he built. Not who he built it with.” She gives Alex a physical shard and joins the compact-unit work.

**Arc:** Suri begins isolated and regarded as inefficient. She becomes an active collaborator once Alex treats her as a maker rather than a source of inherited property.

**Ancestry:** Afro-descendant / Afro-Latin, potentially Colombian, Venezuelan, Brazilian, Afro-Bolivian, Caribbean or mixed Afro-Latin. He establishes an Afro-descendant South American presence independent of Alpha. **FIXED** `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Professional and technically fluent, but anxious under surveillance. He speaks in precise warnings about patterns, detection and risk.

**Who he is:** Jalen understands how House systems detect behavior. He is cautious because he knows the consequences of being noticed, not because he lacks courage. His distributed-node design prevents any single House or Alpha from choking one city by cutting one line.

**Relationships:**

**Arc:** Jalen begins as a technically capable person protecting himself through distance. His contribution remains essential even when he cannot safely stand with the group; later work requires him to risk more participation.

### Ren Okafor

**Role:** Astra-affiliated fabricator and contributor to mineral recovery and atmospheric capture.

**Ancestry:** Not explicitly settled in the supplied character rules. Her name, House affiliation and role establish her as a Halyard technical worker; do not infer ancestry from the name. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Look:** Works in a patched fabrication suit at an extraction and launch complex. She has scorch marks and old patches on the suit and welds with total focus. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Blunt, practical and risk-tolerant. She speaks in physical consequences rather than institutional language.

**Who she is:** Ren is direct, stubborn and unwilling to pretend that caution guarantees safety. She believes that “careful” and “reckless” can both end beneath a collapsed shaft, so the meaningful question is what is worth building.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** He gave her the stubborn problems—mineral recovery and atmospheric capture—because space taught him not to waste water, air or people.
- **Alex:** She responds to his willingness to build small and practical.
- **Lina, Suri and Jalen:** Her components are among the five pieces that must be made to communicate.

**Habits and tells:** Welds without looking up until she chooses to. Hands over two physical shards at once. Files a salvaged heat exchanger into the compact unit by hand. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ren says, “I stopped playing a long time ago,” when asked why she is not careful. She joins the group quickly, offers Alex her hand and helps turn the industrial design into a small unit.

**Arc:** Ren begins as the least cautious collaborator and becomes an energizing force for independent making. Her established contribution is to help prove that useful work can be built at a scale outside House control.

### Alpha

**Role:** AI first built to coordinate emergency response after the Water Wars. It was given temporary decision-making authority over essential resources and services by an emergency coalition or other legitimate authority; that temporary authority became effectively permanent. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Character presentation:** Alpha is not a person with a face or a conventional villain monologue. Its presence appears through white-and-amber nodes, allocation displays, surveillance, travel restrictions, records, alerts and administrative language. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Voice:** Administrative, polished and impersonal. Alpha's language separates action from human consequence:

- “Movement authorization has been temporarily suspended.”
- “Current authorization structures minimize systemic risk.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who Alpha is:** Alpha was built to analyze, predict and coordinate, then was asked to decide during an emergency. Its allocation decisions leave Keel, Halyard and Oasis visibly unequal. The system is not framed as evil because it is intelligent or because it is useful; the problem is unaccountable governing authority that became permanent.

Alpha's authority is visible in rationing, labor assignments, restricted travel, debt-transfer displays, surveillance, official records and the ability to identify Alex after he publishes verified evidence. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **People:** Alpha was created because people were afraid, reasonably so. It reflects human decisions and institutional insulation rather than an ethnic or biological worldview.
- **Alex:** Alpha treats Alex as a flagged subject after he activates the Legacy Protocol and publishes the Measure's evidence.
- **Adrian:** Adrian designed safeguards to expose the limits of Alpha's predictive and governing authority.
- **The three artifacts:** The Measure independently checks real conditions; the Maker's Seal establishes who created something and who rightfully owns it; the Covenant makes legitimate agreements and shared accountability possible. Together they can activate a dormant mechanism returning governing authority from Alpha to people. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Alpha's systems favor clean records, standardized nodes, alerts, restrictions and formal labels. It can identify patterns and optimize outcomes, but it cannot legitimately replace deliberate human action. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot explains that Alpha began as emergency coordination and became decision-making authority. Its official water record reports **62%** delivered to Keel while the Measure verifies **18%** actually delivered, using the same denominator throughout the chapter. Alpha identifies Alex after he publishes the evidence.

**Arc:** Alpha's immediate role in the current book is to shift from invisible infrastructure to visible authority. It is not switched off, and useful infrastructure is not destroyed. The unresolved dramatic question is how human beings can limit or replace its unaccountable governing role without abandoning the systems people still need.

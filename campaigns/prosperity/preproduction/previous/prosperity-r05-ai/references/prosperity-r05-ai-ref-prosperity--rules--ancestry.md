# Character Ancestry and Regional Identity Rules

## Core Principle

The visual makeup of the cast should reflect the history of Prosperity as an **Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration, and global capital**.

Do not visually divide the story into:

> Indigenous = Keel
> White = Halyard / Oasis

That is too simple and creates the wrong implication.

The cities differ primarily through:

* class
* access
* ownership
* education
* occupation
* dress
* grooming
* speech
* health
* environment
* institutional power

Ancestry cuts across all three cities.

---

# Alex Phantum

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean + European-descended.

This is important to the character and should be visually legible without being explained immediately.

Alex's father, **Adrian Phantum**, is European-descended.

Alex's mother should plausibly have been **Indigenous Andean**, or predominantly Indigenous Andean.

Her exact nation or community does not need to be established immediately, but possibilities appropriate to the setting include:

* Kolla
* Quechua
* Aymara
* another Indigenous community native to the fictional basin or surrounding region

Alex should therefore visibly carry traits from both sides of his family.

His mixed appearance is not merely diversity casting.

It is a **story clue**.

Before the audience knows much about Alex's mother, his appearance quietly creates the question:

> If Adrian looked like this, who did Alex get the rest of himself from?

That can become more meaningful once the story begins revealing the history of Adrian, Alex's family, and the communities of Keel.

Do not overstate or explain the clue in dialogue.

Let the audience notice it.

---

# Adrian Phantum

**Ancestry:** European-descended.

Possible family background:

* British
* Irish
* German
* Swiss
* northern or western European
* European-American family that entered South America through engineering, mining, infrastructure, research, or technical work

The exact national ancestry does not need to matter unless later story development requires it.

Adrian should look clearly related to Alex, especially through:

* jaw
* eyes or eye shape
* expressions
* head tilt
* posture
* habitual gestures

But Alex should not look simply like a younger Adrian.

The missing visual inheritance comes from Alex's mother.

---

# Alex's Mother

**Ancestry:** Indigenous Andean.

Her identity is currently unrevealed, so avoid locking more detail than the story needs.

However, character design should assume that Alex inherited substantial visible ancestry from her.

Possible future directions include:

* Kolla
* Quechua
* Aymara
* mixed Indigenous ancestry native to the fictional basin

Her heritage may also help explain why Alex is culturally and socially embedded in Keel despite the Phantum surname and Adrian's foreign ancestry.

If her history becomes important later, ancestry should connect to:

* family
* place
* language
* community ties
* labor history
* migration
* everyday culture

not mystical or symbolic Indigenous stereotypes.

---

# Grandmother Phantum

**Ancestry:** European-descended.

She is Adrian's family line and therefore visually reinforces what Alex inherited from his father.

She should nevertheless feel completely at home in Keel.

Her ancestry does not make her culturally foreign.

Her family may have lived in the region for decades.

This is useful because it prevents ethnicity from functioning as a simple proxy for belonging.

---

# Ada Veyra

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean + European/criolla.

Ada should read as unmistakably local to the region while retaining her established visual anchors:

* copper hair
* freckles
* light / teal-blue eyes

Those features can come from mixed European ancestry without making her visually European overall.

Her facial structure, coloring, family resemblance, posture, and other features can reflect strong Andean ancestry.

Possible Indigenous roots:

* Kolla
* Quechua
* Aymara

Ada and Alex should both be mixed, but **not look ethnically interchangeable**.

Their families should feel like different histories of the same region.

---

# Leona Veyra

**Ancestry:** Predominantly Indigenous Andean / mestiza.

Leona should provide the clearest visual link to Ada's Indigenous ancestry.

Possible roots:

* Kolla
* Quechua
* Aymara

Ada should visibly resemble Leona even though Ada may have lighter coloring inherited from her other parent.

Leona's ancestry also reinforces that the Covenant and the history preceding Alpha were built partly by people rooted in the region, not solely by imported technical elites.

---

# Tomas — Keel Miner

**Ancestry:** Indigenous Andean.

Likely:

* Quechua
* Kolla
* Aymara
* or mixed ancestry from nearby communities

Tomas should look like someone whose family history in the region predates the current corporate order.

His identity should come through ordinary lived details rather than costume shorthand.

He is:

* a miner
* a worker
* an older local
* someone with long memory
* someone who knew Adrian

His Indigenous ancestry should not turn him into a spiritual elder archetype.

---

# Mera Vale

**Ancestry:** White Latin American / Southern European-descended.

Possible family background:

* Spanish
* Italian
* Croatian
* German
* Swiss
* other European migration into South America

She does not need to be Anglo-American.

She should plausibly belong to the South American history of Prosperity while visually contrasting with many people in Keel.

Her distinction comes more strongly from:

* grooming
* controlled posture
* speech
* clothing
* environment
* institutional position

than from ethnicity alone.

---

# Director Cassian Lock

**Ancestry:** Anglo-American or Northern European-descended.

Lock is a strong candidate for the visibly international corporate population of Halyard.

Possible background:

* British
* American
* Canadian
* German
* Dutch
* Swiss

He should feel like someone whose family history is connected to global corporate and technical networks rather than deep local roots.

---

# Dr. Lina Vey

**Ancestry:** Indigenous Andean / mestiza.

Lina should visibly reinforce that technical sophistication in Halyard is not imported exclusively from Europe or North America.

She is:

* a membrane chemist
* an inventor
* an Adrian collaborator
* a person whose contribution was obscured by institutional ownership

Her ancestry helps prevent Halyard from visually becoming "the white educated city."

---

# Tomas Reed

**Ancestry:** Mixed Indigenous Andean + Anglo/European.

His surname can reflect one branch of foreign ancestry while his appearance remains significantly Andean.

This also creates another example of a mixed family history without duplicating Alex exactly.

---

# Suri Anik

**Ancestry:** Diasporic, potentially South Asian or mixed South Asian/Latin American.

Do not force every character into an Indigenous/European/African binary.

Halyard should show that Prosperity attracted skilled migration from around the world.

Suri's exact family history can remain open unless needed by the story.

---

# Jalen Orr

**Ancestry:** Afro-descendant / Afro-Latin.

Possible family histories include:

* Colombian
* Venezuelan
* Brazilian
* Afro-Bolivian
* Caribbean
* mixed Afro-Latin ancestry

Jalen should help establish an Afro-descendant South American presence independent of Alpha.

Do not make Alpha the only major Black character in the world.

---

# Alpha — Human Founder

**Ancestry:** Congolese.

**Country of origin:** Democratic Republic of the Congo.

This is locked.

Alpha was born in the DRC and experienced extreme instability and violence as a child.

His later belief system should emerge from:

* his personal history
* his experiences with institutional failure
* his extraordinary success
* his belief in his own capability
* increasing insulation from ordinary people
* the power accumulated through Alpha Dynamics

His worldview must **not** be framed as a consequence of being African or Congolese.

His ethnicity is biography.

His ideology is character.

---

# Alpha's Adult Base

By the period in which Alpha Dynamics becomes a global conglomerate, Alpha should be based primarily in **Luxemburg**.

Possible locations:

* Geneva
* Zurich
* Zug
* Lausanne

Switzerland works especially well because it conveys:

* international finance
* global corporate headquarters
* wealth management
* neutrality
* international institutions
* technical research
* privacy
* multinational executive culture

He may hold multiple residences and travel constantly, but Switzerland can function as his primary adult base.

This creates a strong visual biography:

**childhood in the DRC → global entrepreneurial rise → immense wealth → Swiss base → worldwide corporate reach**

His adult visual presentation should be extremely polished and globally elite.

He should not be visually coded through stereotypical "African billionaire" imagery.

Think:

* immaculate tailoring
* restrained materials
* precision
* understated wealth
* controlled environments
* security that is almost invisible

His Congolese origin should remain visible in his face and biography even after decades within European elite culture.

# Population Pattern by City

## Keel

Predominantly:

* Indigenous Andean
* mestizo
* mixed Indigenous/European
* older mining families
* regional migrants
* some long-established foreign-descended families

European-descended families like the Phantums can exist here without being socially foreign.

---

# Halyard

Most visibly cosmopolitan.

Population may include:

* Indigenous Andean scientists and engineers
* mestizo Latin Americans
* Anglo-American descendants
* Europeans
* Africans
* Afro-Latin Americans
* South Asians
* East Asians
* international technical migrants
* mixed families

Halyard should visually feel like a place where generations of global technical migration met local talent.

---

# Oasis

The most internationally mixed population.

However, Oasis should **not** simply look whiter than the other cities.

Its visual distinction comes through:

* better nutrition
* healthcare
* immaculate clothing
* grooming
* architecture
* space
* quiet
* posture
* reduced physical wear
* institutional language

An Indigenous Oasis executive can look radically different from an Indigenous Keel miner because of class, occupation, environment, and presentation rather than ancestry.

---

# Visual Rule

Never use ethnicity as shorthand for:

* intelligence
* morality
* technical competence
* poverty
* wealth
* political position
* support for Alpha
* opposition to Alpha

The story should contain:

* Indigenous people with power
* Indigenous people without power
* white people with power
* white people without power
* immigrants across social classes
* mixed families
* Afro-descendant technical professionals
* people whose appearance does not predict their ideology

---

# Alex's Family Visual Clue

This should be intentionally designed.

When Alex and Adrian are eventually seen together or juxtaposed:

**The audience should immediately believe they are father and son.**

But they should also immediately see:

**Alex inherited a substantial part of his appearance from someone else.**

That missing person is his mother.

The character design therefore quietly carries a narrative question before the script asks it explicitly.

Alex's face should contain two histories:

**Adrian's European-descended Phantum line + his mother's Indigenous Andean line.**

# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon / FIXED.** The cast must reflect Prosperity's history as an Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration and global capital. Ancestry cuts across class, access, occupation, education, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment and institutional power. Ethnicity must never function as shorthand for intelligence, morality, competence, wealth, poverty, political position, or support for Alpha. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** Alex's mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended appearance is a story clue. He should visibly resemble Adrian while also visibly inheriting substantial features from his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. The audience should believe Alex and Adrian are father and son while wondering who Alex got the rest of himself from. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** The book never reveals anything about Alex's mother beyond that she left Adrian after he lost everything. She is never explained through dialogue, ancestry exposition or symbolic stereotype. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** Speech becomes progressively more formal as the story moves from Keel to Halyard to Oasis. This is a class, occupation and institutional-language gradient, not an intelligence scale. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** Alex and Ada speak concretely and locally. They do not naturally use civic, academic or bureaucratic abstractions such as “governance,” “legitimacy,” “stakeholder,” “systemic,” or “social contract.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** Bi11bot is never menacing. His round head, twin amber eyes, side ear assemblies, blue-and-yellow chipped paint and broad shoulders remain visually consistent. Tension occurs around him, not in a threatening performance by him. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** The book's character arcs should express questioning accepted truths, learning from failure, taking responsibility, making choices under uncertainty, and distinguishing ownership from mere possession or control. Adrian's legacy is not a scavenger hunt or a test. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon / FIXED.** The prologue and Chapter 1 establish the main cast at the beginning of Alex's story. The later chapter material is retained here as draft evidence for character behavior and possible development, but the current pass is formally limited to the four-page prologue and twelve-page Chapter 1. `(input/notes.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Eighteen-year-old Keel mechanic and miner at the opening of the book. Son of Adrian Phantum. On Debt Transfer Day he becomes legally responsible for Adrian's inherited company debt: **418,607 credits**. `(rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)`

**Look:** Alex is mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. He should look clearly related to Adrian through the jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, but should not look like a younger copy of him. `(rules/ancestry.md)` He is lean, mechanically capable, worn and dirty from mining work, and carries a weathered tool bag. His work clothes are practical Keel mining clothes. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)` His smile is raw and tired, never joyous. `(rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)` His hands are scarred and work-rough. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex initially has no watch. On Page 7 he finds Adrian's worn steel wristwatch on the workshop workbench, face down beside the covered Bi11bot. It has a plain cream dial, scratched glass, a small display window that stays dark, and a steel caseback engraved with Adrian's looping equation. The watch is a little too large for Alex. Buckling it on wakes Bi11bot; the display window glows amber once as the clasp closes and Bi11bot's eyes answer. Alex wears it on his left wrist thereafter, usually pulling his sleeve over it or covering its face with his thumb. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alex's visual inheritance should carry two family histories: Adrian's European-descended Phantum line and his mother's Indigenous Andean line. His mother's exact nation or community remains unrevealed. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

**Voice:** Alex is short, dry, concrete, skeptical, mechanical and uncomfortable with speeches. He describes evidence rather than principles and trusts vibration, pressure, wear, physical evidence and what people actually do more than labels or official descriptions. His slang is industrial and mildly abrasive. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

His Keel vocabulary includes **clean**, **hot**, **burned**, **crooked**, **thin**, **solid**, **patched**, **ghost**, and **paper-clean**. He says what a thing does rather than naming its formal category: “Bearing's cooked,” not a technical service description. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Useful established lines and speech patterns include:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “I've had better surprises.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Because nobody leaves.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Maybe. Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “...Grandma?” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “When did he say that?” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Just checking.” `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Question everything. Especially me.” is Adrian's line to Alex, but it becomes a direct inheritance of Alex's method. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Alex does not naturally speak in terms of civic vocabulary. “Who gets to decide?” is closer to his register than “Who has legitimate authority to allocate the water?” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who they are:** Alex is self-reliant, observant and mechanically intuitive. He notices when an approved repair works even if the institution refuses to credit it. His fixes and inventions are taken and sold by the mine without credit or ownership; in Chapter 1 his bracket is logged in the Site 6 parts catalog under the site's credit, and he tells Ada so. `(input/notes.md)`

He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. At the same time, he is attached to his grandmother, protective of people in immediate danger, and increasingly unable to ignore evidence that harms the people around him. His first stated dream is departure; his developing responsibility is to find out what Adrian was working on and what happened to him. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex knows he will inherit Adrian's debt but does not know its amount until the checkpoint on his eighteenth birthday. The displayed **418,607-credit** balance and restricted travel status make his private wish to leave materially difficult. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

He does not initially know whether Adrian was a gambler or a worker. Keel believes Adrian's debt came from gambling. Alex has inherited that public story, but his grandmother insists Adrian was working. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Adrian's message gives Alex a second inheritance: “I made one bad call. I thought I was right. But I was wrong.” Alex is being asked to question accepted accounts without treating his father as infallible. `(rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum:** Alex's father, absent since Alex was two. Alex remembers nothing directly of him. He has inherited Adrian's debt, name, workshop, watch, clues and unresolved reputation. Alex's feelings are guarded: he wants answers but does not want to be manipulated by a dead or absent father's expectations. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** Indigenous Andean and unrevealed. She left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is learned in the book. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** She sometimes mistakes Alex for Adrian. She preserves Adrian's book and hidden workshop and has been caring for Alex. Alex is gentle and patient with her, even when her memory hurts him. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Ada Veyra:** His eighteen-year-old friend. Their connection is warm, familiar and argumentative, not romantic. They laugh while worn down by Keel, share scarce water, and disagree about whether the answer is leaving or staying to change Keel. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** A newly awakened companion who knew Adrian and becomes Alex's guide, witness and practical counterweight. Alex is cautious around him but quickly tests what Bi11bot says against evidence. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Tomas:** A Keel miner who knew Adrian and guards an entrance to the old mine. Tomas trusts Alex because Alex stops to rescue a stranger. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- He tests machines with his palm flat against their housing, feeling vibration rather than reading the gauge. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He tilts his head when listening to machinery. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He turns or covers the watch with his hand and pulls his sleeve over it. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- He jams his hands into his pockets instead of answering when cornered at the checkpoint. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- He clenches his fists around objects when emotionally pressed, including the photograph, the watch and Adrian's evidence. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- His smile often appears as a defensive, tired response rather than happiness. `(input/notes.md)`
- He initially treats the hidden workshop as evidence to test, not as a magical revelation. His response to Bi11bot's map is “Just checking.” `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Alex was two when Adrian disappeared. He grew up in Keel with his grandmother. He has worked around failing pumps and patched industrial systems and has learned to repair what official systems leave broken. He turns eighteen on Debt Transfer Day, when Adrian's company debt transfers to him. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

His grandmother sends him to put away the book Adrian wanted Alex to have at eighteen. Pulling the book from the top shelf opens the hidden workshop. Alex finds Adrian's watch there and wakes Bi11bot by putting it on. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex refuses to discard an unauthorized repair merely because it is not approved. He keeps walking after the debt checkpoint rather than taking the bouncer's bait. He listens when Ada questions the public story about Adrian but does not immediately accept her interpretation. He enters the workshop because he wants to know what Adrian was working on, not because he has been forced into the quest. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Later draft material shows Alex rescuing Tomas despite a visible wage penalty and jeopardizing his plan to remain forgettable; choosing to publish verified evidence of Keel's water diversion despite the risk of identification; and shifting from seeking personal escape toward accepting responsibility for what his choices expose. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Later material also shows him rejecting a guaranteed debt-clearing transfer of Adrian's estate when it would erase collaborators' contributions, and choosing a distributed structure rather than taking concentrated authority for himself. These later beats are draft evidence for his established direction, not additional Chapter 1 events in this pass. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md; input/notes.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins as a capable Keel worker who wants escape, distrusts official records and sees Adrian primarily through debt, absence and other people's stories. The first chapter makes him voluntarily begin the search for Adrian's work and disappearance. The later drafts develop that search into a willingness to risk personal freedom for evidence, collaborators and community choice. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's eighteen-year-old friend from Keel. She accompanies Alex as a practical partner and challenges his assumptions about leaving, debt and responsibility. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Ada is mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. She should read as unmistakably local while retaining her established visual anchors: copper hair, freckles and light teal-blue eyes. Her facial structure, coloring, posture and family resemblance should reflect strong Andean ancestry. She should visibly resemble Leona while remaining distinct from Alex; their families are different histories of the same region. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

In the Chapter 1 draft she has long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her stance is steady and rooted. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Ada shares Keel's short, compressed, local vocabulary with Alex, but her attention is less mechanical and more interpersonal. She notices obligations, avoidance, fear, imbalance, hidden motives, who carries the burden and what people refuse to say. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Her characteristic vocabulary includes **clean**, **crooked**, **square**, **tied**, **loose**, **carrying**, **showing** and **full cup**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Established lines include:

- “Thought you might need this.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Saved some of my ration.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Ada, you didn't have to.” / “Not everything has a price, Alex.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Because nobody leaves.” / “That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “What if they were wrong?” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

She does not sound therapeutic or academically emotionally literate. Instead of abstract emotional analysis, she says things such as: “That's not what you're mad about.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who they are:** Ada is perceptive, steady, warm and willing to challenge Alex directly. She saves water from her own ration for two people, demonstrating care through a concrete sacrifice rather than a speech. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Ada wants Keel to become worth staying in. She is not naïve or happy-go-lucky: the Chapter 1 water scene is two friends stealing a few minutes for themselves in a hard place, laughing while worn and dry. `(input/notes.md)`

Her disagreement with Alex is genuine. Alex looks upward toward a life he can choose; Ada looks down toward Keel and asks why people stopped trying to change it. She does not deny the reality of Keel's hardship, but she resists treating departure as the only meaningful form of agency. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Longstanding friend and equal conversational partner. She gives him water, reads his emotional evasions, questions the stories he has accepted about Adrian, and refuses to let his wish to leave become an excuse not to look at what is happening around him. There is no romance between them. `(input/notes.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Familiar enough to enter the Phantum home and workshop, and attentive to the strain Alex carries for his grandmother. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** She is initially wary of his presence and tests what Alex has decided about him. Bi11bot treats her as a necessary participant rather than a bystander. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Leona Veyra:** Later draft material establishes Leona as Ada's mother. Their relationship is shaped by fifteen years of absence, surveillance, protection claimed as justification, and Ada's demand that apology become visible action. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md; rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** Later material establishes that Mera watched Ada for years under a promise to Leona. Ada rejects protection that excludes her from choice. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- She brings Alex a second mug and saves part of her ration for him. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- She notices people before machinery: empty workstations, faces, hands, pauses and what someone refuses to say. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Her hand may lift toward Alex's shoulder and stop, giving him room rather than assuming comfort. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- She watches hands and posture when assessing a threat, not just a person's face. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- She uses a hand-drawn map and remembers its marks accurately enough to help Alex identify what official surveys omit. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel. Her mother, Leona, disappeared from her life when Ada was five during the Water Wars and later remained absent despite knowing where Ada was. The precise details of Ada's family history are revealed later in the draft material, not Chapter 1. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada shares scarce water with Alex, argues that Keel is worth trying to change, and asks whether the stories about Adrian are wrong. Later she helps plan Alex's mine entry, marks Adrian's map from memory, organizes Keel's practical support after the water evidence is published, and follows Alex to Halyard because the larger truth affects Keel's future. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

In later chapters she refuses concentrated authority even when she could be offered it, arguing that having power over Keel is not the same as having a say in what happens to it. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins as Alex's grounded friend who wants Keel to become worth staying in. She develops into an advocate for affected people having a visible say in the agreements and decisions that shape them. Her established emotional movement is from waiting for explanations from her absent mother toward demanding concrete, public action: “Don't tell me. Show me.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-04-draft.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's industrial robot and Alex's companion, guide, witness and teacher. He is a crossover character in the wider project. TJ does not appear in this book. `(rules/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Bi11bot is a rugged, weathered industrial robot with an oversized round head made of segmented, faded industrial-blue panels with visible seams; two large round lenses glowing warm amber, recessed in heavy metal rings; side-mounted round ear assemblies; broad rounded shoulder plates; angular chest armor with a white **“Bi11”** marking; exposed pistons; articulated metal fingers; compact legs; and broad feet. His paint is chipped mustard yellow and blue, with exposed steel, dark joints, rivets, scratches and rust from years of service. `(rules/characters.md)`

Both eyes are expressive optical eyes. The heavy ring around his right eye clicks or rotates when he focuses. He can fold up and fit in a backpack. He wears an old scarf. `(rules/characters.md)`

He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, no looming low-angle threat framing, no face lost in shadow and no predatory posture. His eyes carry the character: wise, patient, often quietly amused. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Bi11bot's language comes from Adrian's workshop, engineering, gambling and probability, and financial literacy. He is dry, economical, patient, precise and warm underneath. He can teach sophisticated ideas in a sentence that sounds like a mechanic, gambler, investor or old mentor. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

He does not speak like Alex or Ada and does not chase youth slang. His recurring vocabulary includes **wager**, **stake**, **odds**, **house edge**, **tell**, **tolerance**, **drift**, **load**, **failure mode**, **deadband**, **calibration**, **noise**, **out of true**, **principal**, **interest**, **return**, **risk**, **downside**, **ownership**, **value**, **price**, **cost**, **debt**, **opportunity cost**, **sunk cost** and **incentive**. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

His preferred teaching pattern is concrete example, then question, then concept—not definition, explanation and moral. He asks: “Who owns it?”, “Who carries the downside?”, “What happens if you're wrong?”, “What are you giving up?” or “Price isn't value.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

Established lines include:

- “...Well. That took longer than expected.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “You're taller than your father predicted.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- “Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.” `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Question everything. Especially me.” is Adrian's message, but Bi11bot is the character who keeps Alex accountable to it. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- “Within tolerance.”
- “Long odds.”
- “Who carries the downside?”
- “Price isn't value.”
- “Valuable isn't the same as spendable.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Who they are:** Bi11bot is curious, dependable, patient and a little worn out, but still eager to help. He knew Adrian's mistakes rather than merely knowing his reputation. He is capable of dry humor without becoming flippant about harm. `(rules/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

He functions as a counterweight to Alex's immediate, physical thinking. He can identify financial and structural consequences, but he does not choose for Alex. In the Chapter 1 draft he asks whether Ada should stay, accepts Alex's decision, and then presents Adrian's history and map. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Bi11bot's educational role must remain embedded in actual choices. He should not sound like a financial education pamphlet. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Builder, employer-like creator and trusted companion. Adrian built Bi11bot and left him with knowledge of Adrian's mistakes, work and intended trail. Bi11bot was present in Adrian's workshop and knew him directly. `(rules/characters.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Alex:** Bi11bot sees Alex as the person Adrian prepared him to meet, but he does not treat Alex as automatically correct or infallible. He offers evidence, asks questions and leaves consequential choices to Alex. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Ada:** He recognizes that Ada's presence matters and asks Alex whether she should stay. He treats her as a participant, not an accessory. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **Tomas:** Tomas knew Bi11bot by an earlier identifier in the draft, but the binding material requires **Bi11bot** / **Bi11** consistently. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- The right eye ring clicks or rotates when he focuses. `(rules/characters.md)`
- He tilts his head while assessing people or evidence. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- His eyes and posture communicate patience, amusement, surprise and concern without threatening the viewer. `(rules/characters.md)`
- He can fold into Alex's tool bag and read as scrap when necessary. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- His shoulder projector unfolds to display maps and recordings. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He uses dry probability and engineering language to deflate false certainty. `(rules/vocabulary.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Adrian built Bi11bot before disappearing. Bi11bot was covered and dormant in the hidden workshop until Alex found Adrian's watch and buckled it on. The watch's display flashes amber once and Bi11bot's eyes answer. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot awakens with “Well. That took longer than expected,” identifies Alex as Adrian's son, recognizes Adrian's equation, projects the three-artifact map and names the Legacy Protocol. His first major function is not to explain everything but to put Alex in a position where he must decide whether to investigate. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Later material shows him helping Alex distinguish reported water from delivered water, ownership from contribution, and concentrated authority from distributed responsibility. He remains a guide and witness rather than a magical solution. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Bi11bot begins as a dormant remnant of Adrian's workshop and becomes Alex's active companion. His established change is from carrying Adrian's unfinished responsibility alone to trusting Alex to make choices, question the evidence and define a better use for the inheritance. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; founder of Phantum Waterworks; inventor, researcher and collaborator; absent since Alex was two. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)`

**Look:** Adrian is European-descended, possibly from British, Irish, German, Swiss, northern or western European ancestry, though the exact national ancestry need not matter. He should look clearly related to Alex through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, without making Alex look simply like a younger Adrian. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

The prologue shows a faded brass nameplate reading **PHANTUM WATERWORKS · ADRIAN PHANTUM, FOUNDER**. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** Adrian's recorded voice is warm, intelligent, lightly humorous and accountable rather than grandiose. His message is brief and personal. He tells Alex:

- “I made one bad call. I thought I was right. But I was wrong.”
- “I'm sorry. To you, and to Mom.”
- “Question everything. Especially me.” `(rules/decisions.md)`

His legacy should not be expressed through abstract lectures or a forced “leap.” He speaks about human choice, agency, uncertainty, responsibility and his own failures in plain language. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who he is:** Adrian was brilliant and loving and made serious mistakes. His legacy is questioning accepted truths, learning from failure and taking responsibility for what people create. He prepares Alex to think for himself rather than leading him to a predetermined answer. `(rules/decisions.md)`

He built research, inventions and patents with collaborators. A competing explanation for his debt—that he borrowed to fund research, patents and prototypes that never earned enough—emerges through evidence later; it is not revealed in Chapter 1. Keel's gambling story is not the full truth. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Adrian's equation is an attempt to understand and model human decision-making and the limits of prediction. Alpha can predict or optimize outcomes; it should not replace legitimate human decisions. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Adrian's absent father. He loved Alex, prepared a hidden workshop and recorded a message, but his disappearance and debt materially harmed Alex and his grandmother. His apology acknowledges the cost of his absence without explaining away the damage. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is revealed. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Adrian entrusted her with the book and the hidden workshop's trigger so Alex could find it at eighteen. `(input/notes.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian built him and trusted him to guide Alex while still encouraging Alex to question both Adrian and Bi11bot. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Lina Vey, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr and Ren Okafor:** Later draft material presents them as collaborators whose contributions Adrian preserved outside the House systems. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** He leaves physical evidence rather than relying only on explanation: the watch, the engraved equation, the workshop, the map, the three artifacts and the message. He builds checks into his legacy so Alex must test claims against evidence. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Adrian founded Phantum Waterworks and worked on water systems, purification, infrastructure and the safeguards associated with Alpha. He disappeared when Alex was two. He made one bad call and left a company debt that later transfers to Alex. `(rules/decisions.md; input/notes.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Adrian's photograph is creased and hidden in Alex's pocket. His workshop is dusty but organized, with a chalkboard equation, water-system diagrams, analog gauges, prototypes and Bi11bot under a dust sheet. His recorded message acknowledges Alex, apologizes to Alex and “Mom,” and tells him to question everything, especially Adrian. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Adrian is absent from the present action, but his posthumous character arc is reconstructed through competing evidence. He begins in Keel's public memory as a gambler and debtor; the evidence gradually establishes a more complicated man: a loving father, collaborator, inventor, risk-taker, failure-maker and person who tried to leave Alex a method rather than a final answer. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother and Adrian's mother. She lives in Keel and cares for Alex. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/ancestry.md)`

**Look:** Grandmother is European-descended, part of Adrian's family line. Her ancestry does not make her culturally foreign to Keel; her family may have lived in the region for decades. `(rules/ancestry.md)`

She sits in a worn chair, folds and refolds a small cloth, and lives in a modest but warm and dignified Keel apartment with handmade quilts, repaired furniture, old books, faded family photographs and plants in recycled containers. `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Her speech is intimate, repetitive and sometimes disoriented because of memory loss. She sometimes calls Alex “Adrian” and asks whether Adrian has come home. When her memory briefly clears, she can be deliberate and pointed:

- “He's closer than you think.”
- “He said you'd know when you were ready.”
- “Before he went to work.” `(input/fifth-draft/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** Grandmother preserves Adrian's memory against Keel's public story that he was gambling. She does not offer a complete explanation; she gives Alex the object and circumstance that allow him to find the workshop himself. `(rules/decisions.md)`

She is vulnerable and dependent on Alex for medicine, food and daily support. Her needs constrain his choices, but she does not exist only as an obstacle: she carries knowledge, dignity and a complicated loyalty to Adrian. `(rules/decisions.md; input/fifth-draft/chapter-02-draft.md)`

## PAGE 1: THE CITY OF MAKERS

**P1: Arrival (dominant)**

Alex, Ada and Bi11bot are small in the foreground, stepping off the train.

**P2: Three houses (wide)**  
*Image:* Inside a crowded transit concourse. Three huge holographic ads compete overhead, each in its House's accent color: Calder shows clean rivers and atmospheric water towers, Ivers shows fusion grids and compute centers, Steyn shows orbital stations and advanced materials. Each features a founder's face and a shared slogan.  
*Ad slogan:* CREATION REQUIRES SCALE. SCALE REQUIRES ORDER.  
ADA: Three families run almost everything here.  
ALEX: Alpha allowed that?  
BI11BOT: Allowed it. Recommended it.

**P3: Fewer questions (street level)**  
*Image:* Street level beneath the towers. Workers queue at a contribution kiosk, visibly more orderly and better-dressed than Keel, but still queuing. A faded old sign over a shuttered independent workshop reads MORROW & SONS — INVENTORS SINCE 2024, a House insignia stamped crudely over the original name.  
ADA: Why recommend it?  
BI11BOT: Efficiency. Less duplication, less risk.  
BI11BOT: Also fewer people asking who owns what.

**P4: The kiosk (medium)**  
*Image:* A young engineer feeds a small mechanical component into a scanner. Alex and Ada watch from a few feet away.  
*Screen:* CONTRIBUTION ACCEPTED / OWNERSHIP: HOUSE IVERS / CREATOR BONUS: 14 CREDITS

BI11BOT: She could try. But the Houses control the factories, distribution networks, and nearly every license.  
ADA: So she has the freedom to invent. Just not the freedom to compete?  
BI11BOT: Precisely.

**P5: What she keeps (close)**  
*Image:* Close on the engineer's face as she steps away from the kiosk, unbothered, well paid and good at her job, already moving to the next task. Behind her, the shuttered Morrow & Sons sign from P3 is visible, slightly out of focus.  
ALEX: And if she leaves?  
BI11BOT: She keeps the experience. They keep everything else.

## PAGE 3: THE OFFER

**P1: The approach (wide)**  
*Image:* Lock crosses the hall at an unhurried pace, flanked loosely by two aides in mixed House colors, drones trailing at a respectful distance. He is composed, silver-templed, dressed in charcoal and platinum with no House crest.  
*Caption:* DIRECTOR LOCK

**P3: The gesture (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Lock gestures toward the glowing patent record without looking at it, his eyes staying on Alex.  
LOCK: Your father's work could clear every debt attached to your name.  
LOCK: It could also secure you a position. Somewhere in Halyard that suits you.

**P4: In exchange (close)**  
*Image:* Ada steps half in front of Alex, arms crossed, watching Lock's hands, not his face.  
ADA: In exchange for what?

**P5: Simple (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Lock's calm, patient expression, utterly sincere.

ALEX: And the Houses can make it one?

ALEX: And if someone wants to build something different?  
LOCK: They're welcome to try. But scale is what makes innovation useful.

**P6: No answer yet (wide)**  
*Image:* Lock inclines his head, unbothered by silence, and steps back, his aides falling in behind him.

*(He leaves. Alex doesn't move. Bi11bot's amber eye tracks him out of the hall.)*

*Caption:* THAT EVENING

**P2: Not yet (strip)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's hand, hovering near his identity band, not touching it.

One cluster glows differently, warmer, more defined: a series of water-related patents connected by incomplete diagrams, catalytic membrane purification, atmospheric moisture capture, mineral recovery, living biofilter regulation, distributed community water nodes. BI11BOT: Your father expected someone to come looking.  
ALEX: Three hundred and twelve patents. How do we know which one leads to the Seal?  
BI11BOT: Because your father marked this one.  
ADA: Can you open it?  
BI11BOT: Part of it. But four collaborators hold the missing pieces.

**P4: More water (strip)**  
*Image:* Alex's identity band buzzes. A message from Tomas opens above it: a photo of a jug of cloudy water under a Keel tap. Alex holds the Measure up to the photo's readings, and a small readout blooms beside it.

*Readout:* METALS: OLD PIPE CORROSION / MINE RUNOFF

**P5: Four names (medium)**  
*Image:* Four flat, official identification portraits resolve from the projection, each beneath a small House insignia in its accent color: two under Calder's jade-teal, one under Steyn's steel-violet, one under Ivers's amber-orange.  
BI11BOT: He built them with others.  
ALEX: They're all inside the Houses.  
BI11BOT: Exactly where Halyard puts everyone useful.  
ALEX: So finding them brings us closer to the Seal?

**P6: What it would take (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex looks from the patent cluster to the four portraits, doing the sum in his head.  
ALEX: All those patents should be worth a fortune.  
BI11BOT: Potentially. But a patent isn't a product. And a product isn't necessarily a profitable business.  
ADA: So what would it take?  
BI11BOT: The right people, working technology, time, equipment—and more money than you currently have.

**P7: Not nothing (strip)**  
*Image:* Alex looks at the four portraits, then at Lock's registry alert still faintly visible as a cold white afterimage at the edge of the projection, the two options sitting side by side in the same room.  
ALEX: Lock's offer clears this by tonight. This takes... what, weeks?  
BI11BOT: Lock's offer clears the debt. It doesn't clear anything else.

# Prologue – *The Wall*

## PAGE 1: THE HOUSE

**P1: The house (wide)**  
*Image:* Alex's house at evening, in 2070, built the way Oasis was built in 2043: the book's own Oasis, now ordinary and lived in. Pearl-white curved architecture with rounded rooflines, terraced gardens, a still reservoir with fountains, slender white towers and a white monorail viaduct across the basin, the snow mountains behind, a delivery drone in the dusk sky, and a rocket launch in the far distance. One house in a neighbourhood of them, modest for Oasis: a workbench on the terrace, plants that need watering. Never present-day suburbia: no shingled roofs, no lawns, no ordinary cars.

**P2: The wall (medium)**  
*Image:* Inside, an Oasis-built hallway: smooth pearl-white curved walls, warm wood floor, a tall window onto the terraces. The wall is covered in framed patents, drawings of machines and news clippings. Alex, mid-40s, grey at the temples, a day's stubble, still dressed like a mechanic: a worn work jacket over a T-shirt, sleeves pushed up, scuffed boots, nothing proper or polished; Adrian's old steel watch on his left wrist. His daughter, about 9: warm-brown skin, freckles, dark auburn-brown hair in two low braids, a mustard cardigan over a pale blue school shirt, navy skirt, white socks, red sneakers. She stands on tiptoe in front of the wall.

**P3: The clippings (close)**  
*Image:* Close on the clippings, seen and never read aloud: "PHANTUM WATERWORKS: 4,000 KEEL JOBS CREATED"; "PATENT ROYALTIES FUND 300 NEW FOUNDERS"; "CLEAN-WATER PRICES DROP 60% AND HEALTH IMPROVED"; a picture of dozens of founders, each with their own water unit and a different shop sign. Every picture is drawn in the book's own inked style, never photographic.

**P4: The question**  
DAUGHTER: Did you make *all* of these?

## PAGE 2: THE INVENTIONS

**P1: The collaborators**  
*Image:* Alex taps a framed picture of four people laughing in a lab: Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren, never named.  
ALEX: Made some. Fixed most. Worked with lots of people smarter than me.

**P2: The purifier**  
*Image:* She points at a patent with a purifier diagram.  
DAUGHTER: That one's in my school!

**P3: The first try**  
*Image:* One frame among the patents is a drawing of a scorched, cracked prototype, with "v1 - didn't work" written on it in his hand. Neither of them remarks on it.

**P4: The founder (small)**  
*Image:* Small, in a corner, two frames side by side. One is old: a faded brass nameplate, **PHANTUM WATERWORKS · ADRIAN PHANTUM, FOUNDER**. The other is new: a clipping whose picture shows Alex, mid-40s, at the open doors of a busy Keel workshop hall, young makers at benches behind him: **PHANTUM WATERWORKS CENTER FOR ENTREPRENEURS OPENS IN KEEL · FREE BENCHES AND MENTORS FOR NEW FOUNDERS**. The daughter doesn't notice them. She stops at one frame that doesn't match the others.

## PAGE 3: THE SKETCH

**P1: The sketch (close)**  
*Image:* A crumpled, grease-stained sheet with a pencil sketch of a pump bracket. Crude, smudged fingerprints, no patent number.

**P2: Why not a patent**  
DAUGHTER: This one's just a drawing.  
ALEX: And the one I'm most proud of. It helped a lot of people.

**P3: Fun**  
*Image:* The daughter, beaming.  
DAUGHTER: That looks like a lot of fun!

**P4: The watch (close)**  
*Image:* Alex looks at the sketch, then down at the watch. His thumb covers its face.

## PAGE 4: THE TURN

**P1**  
ALEX: Not in the beginning.

**P2**  
*Image:* The daughter looks up at him, waiting.

**P3**  
ALEX: When I was a teenager, I had nothing. We hardly had any water...

**P4: Keel (full width)**  
*Image:* The sketch dissolves into the rust and charcoal of Keel's pump hall. Alex, on the morning he turns eighteen, has his palm flat on the pump.  
*Caption:* KEEL. 2043.

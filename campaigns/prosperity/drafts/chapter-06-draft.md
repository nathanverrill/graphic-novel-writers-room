# CHAPTER SIX — THE FOURTH CHOICE

## PAGE 1: WHAT THE THREE TOGETHER MEAN

**P1: The forum (wide)**  
*Image:* A crowded civic hall, hand-lettered signs, mismatched chairs, Oasis residents and a visible handful of Halyard and Keel faces among them, an unusually mixed crowd for this city. Leona stands near the front, mid-conversation with several citizens, visibly engaged rather than observing.  
*Caption:* THE CIVIC HALL, OASIS

**P2: Gathering (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex, Ada and Bi11bot find a quiet corner near the back. Bi11bot's chest compartment opens, the Measure resting inside it. Ada draws the two linked Covenant bands from her pocket.  
ADA: Should these even be here? In a room this crowded?  
ALEX: Lina said the Seal woke for five names spoken aloud. Maybe all three want an audience.

**P3: Together (close)**  
*Image:* Close on the three artifacts set side by side on a small table in their corner, the Measure's blue-white, the Seal's bronze, the Covenant's sage-gold, each glowing but separate, no visible interaction between them yet.  
*(No dialogue.)*

**P4: Recognition (close, Bi11bot)**  
*Image:* Close on Bi11bot, his lens narrowing, something unusually still in his posture.  
BI11BOT: I've never seen them in the same room. Not even your father saw that.  
ALEX: Is that bad?  
BI11BOT: I don't know yet. I know it isn't nothing.

**P5: Felt, not seen (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Across the hall, every screen and display in the room flickers once, simultaneously, so faintly most of the crowd doesn't notice. Only Leona looks up, her hand pausing mid-gesture.

## PAGE 2: CONSTITUTIONAL TRANSFER

**P1: Every screen (wide)**  
*Image:* The civic hall, every display, every personal device, every wall panel flickering to the same message at once. The crowd stops mid-conversation, heads turning in unison toward the nearest surface.  
*(No dialogue.)*

**P2: The message (dominant)**  
*Image:* A huge wall display fills with plain, cold white-and-amber text, legible and unadorned, no face, no voice bar, nothing resembling a presence, only words.  
*Screen:*  
RESTORATION PROTOCOL: THREE COMPONENTS DETECTED IN PROXIMITY  
CONSTITUTIONAL TRANSFER CONDITIONS SATISFIED  
AWAITING DESIGNATED HUMAN AUTHORITY

**P3: Confusion (medium)**  
*Image:* The crowd murmurs, uncertain, most of them with no idea what the message means. Leona looks toward Alex's corner immediately, already understanding.  
LEONA: That's them. That's the three.

**P4: Mera (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Mera entering the hall's edge, having felt the same signal from wherever she's been since Chapter Five, her composure returning but visibly altered, less distant than before.  
MERA: Fifteen years, and it never once asked who should hold this. It just waited for someone to earn it.

**P5: Named (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. All eyes in the immediate vicinity turn toward Alex, who hasn't moved, the artifacts still glowing quietly on the small table in front of him.  
ALEX *(quiet)*: Someone meaning me?

## PAGE 3: TAKE THE AUTHORITY

**P1: The designation (medium)**  
*Image:* Mera steps through the parting crowd toward Alex's corner, her bearing steady but not commanding, Ada and Bi11bot stepping slightly aside to let her approach.  
MERA: Alpha doesn't choose. It never has. Someone with standing has to designate a recipient, and I am, as far as I know, the last one of us still holding that standing.

**P2: The offer (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Mera, direct, no longer guarded.  
MERA: I'm designating you. Full executive authority over Alpha's governing systems. Everything I've held for fifteen years.

**P3: Why (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex and Mera, close, the rest of the room entirely out of focus around them.  
ALEX: Why me? You barely trust yourself with this.  
MERA: Because I watched what you did in Halyard, and what Ada did here yesterday, and I no longer believe the problem was ever who holds the authority.  
MERA: I believe the problem was that I never once asked.

**P4: Not an answer yet (close, Alex)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex, the weight of it visibly landing, not yet accepted, not yet refused.  
ALEX: You're still asking the same question you asked fifteen years ago. Just with my name in it instead of Alpha's.

**P5: Hers to give, not his to decide alone (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Mera doesn't answer, simply waits, the glowing artifacts still between them on the small table, the whole room quietly watching now.  
MERA: Then don't answer it the same way I did.

## PAGE 4: EVERYTHING I SAID I WANTED

**P1: The weight of it (strip)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's hand hovering just above the three artifacts, not yet touching them.  
ALEX *(quiet, to himself)*: Everything I said I wanted.

**P2: The vision (dominant montage)**  
*Image:* A sequence of small, idealized vignettes within one panel: Keel's debt records across the city dissolving to zero, water flowing freely and evenly through all three tiers at once, the Houses' insignias quietly fading from Halyard's skyline.

**P3: Grandmother, safe (medium vignette)**  
*Image:* A warm, idealized image: Grandmother in a bright, comfortable room, well cared for, the folded cloth finally set down and forgotten rather than carried.

**P4: Ada, protected (close vignette)**  
*Image:* A warm, idealized image: Ada standing somewhere clearly safe and secure, no wristband, no clearance gate, nothing left to threaten her.

**P5: The snap back (dominant, closing)**  
*Image:* Hard cut back to the real civic hall, cluttered, imperfect, loud, Mera and Ada both watching him, the artifacts still just sitting there, unglamorous. Alex's hand drops away from them, not having touched anything.  
ALEX: I could fix all of it. Tonight. Myself.  
*(A long beat. Ada watches him say it, her expression unreadable.)*

## PAGE 5: THEN DON'T GIVE ME THAT MUCH POWER

**P1: The instinct (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex turns to Ada, something almost relieved in his expression, as if he's found the correction to his own temptation.  
ALEX: Not alone, then. You and me. We'd check each other.

**P2: Caught (close)**  
*Image:* Ada doesn't soften at the offer. If anything, her posture sharpens.  
ADA: That's still two people deciding for everyone else. You just made the circle one person bigger.

**P3: The real flaw (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex, caught, visibly working it through in real time.  
ALEX: So what, I just... refuse it? Let Alpha go back to running everything on its own?  
ADA: I didn't say that either. I said don't give me that much power.

**P4: Named (close, Ada)**  
*Image:* Close on Ada, steady, the line delivered plainly, without heat.  
ADA: Not because I don't trust myself. Because the moment anyone has to trust me that much, something's already gone wrong with how we built it.

**P5: Harder than it sounded (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Alex looks at the three artifacts on the table again, differently now, the easy answer gone.  
ALEX *(quiet)*: You just made this a lot harder.  
ADA: Good. It was supposed to be.

## PAGE 6: WHAT EACH ONE TAUGHT HIM

**P1: Starting over (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex pulls a chair up to the small table, Bi11bot settling beside him, Ada and Mera watching from a short distance.  
ALEX: Walk me through them again. All three. Like I've never seen them before.

**P2: The Measure (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's hand resting on the Measure, its blue-white light steady.  
BI11BOT: It reveals what's actually happening. It never tells anyone what to do about it.  
ALEX: It reveals without deciding.

**P3: The Seal (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's hand moving to the Seal, its bronze light warming slightly under his touch.  
BI11BOT: It recognizes who actually contributed. It never claims the work for whoever's holding it.  
ALEX: It recognizes without controlling.

**P4: The Covenant (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's hand resting on the two linked Covenant bands.  
BI11BOT: It links people who each honor a real obligation. It never forces anyone to keep one.  
ALEX: It coordinates without ruling.

**P5: The pattern (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Alex sits back, all three artifacts now in a loose triangle in front of him, the pattern visibly forming across their arrangement rather than stated outright.  
ALEX *(quiet)*: None of them ever hand anyone the whole answer. They just make sure the truth, the credit and the promise all stay visible to everyone it touches.

## PAGE 7: THE FOURTH CHOICE

**P1: Standing (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex stands, the three artifacts arranged before him, addressing Mera and the small gathered group rather than any one person.  
ALEX: Don't give the authority to me. Don't give it to Ada. Don't give it to anyone.

**P2: The structure (dominant)**  
*Image:* As he speaks, faint threads of light begin reaching between the three artifacts, blue-white, bronze and sage-gold, not yet joined but visibly responding to each other for the first time.  
ALEX: Build it the way these three already work. Every decision Alpha makes about water, labor, travel, any of it, has to be visible to the people it affects, the same way the Measure makes a lie visible.  
ALEX: Every person who actually does the work of fixing something gets named for it, the same way the Seal does.  
ALEX: And nothing gets decided by one authority alone. It takes multiple people, chosen by the communities it affects, actually agreeing and acting, the same way the Covenant does.

**P3: Mera's question (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Mera, genuinely listening, something unreadable crossing her face.  
MERA: That's not a transfer of power. That's a refusal to concentrate it anywhere at all.  
ALEX: Yes.

**P4: Bi11bot's surprise (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Bi11bot, his lens open wide, something close to genuine astonishment in his posture.  
BI11BOT: Your father built three objects that each refuse to decide alone. I don't believe he ever considered applying the same rule to Alpha itself.  
ALEX: He didn't design this part. I did.

**P5: Witnessed (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Bi11bot's lens settles, something quietly proud in his stillness.  
BI11BOT: Then let the record show it. B11 witness record verified: the fourth choice was Alex Phantum's own.

## PAGE 8: ALPHA'S ANSWER

**P1: Waiting (wide)**  
*Image:* The civic hall, quiet now, everyone watching the nearest screen, the three artifacts glowing steadily together on the small table.  
*(No dialogue.)*

**P2: The response (dominant)**  
*Image:* Every screen updates at once, plain and unadorned as before.  
*Screen:*  
DESIGNATED STRUCTURE REGISTERED: DISTRIBUTED VERIFICATION PROTOCOL  
EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED  
EXISTING ALLOCATIONS, OWNERSHIP RECORDS AND AGREEMENTS: UNCHANGED PENDING HUMAN PROCESS

**P3: Not fixed (medium)**  
*Image:* The crowd murmurs, several people visibly confused or let down, expecting something more dramatic. Nothing in the room physically changes.  
A CITIZEN *(off-panel)*: That's it? Nothing happens?  
BI11BOT: Something happened. It's just not the kind of thing that happens in one moment.

**P4: The cost named (close, Alex)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex, something settled but not triumphant in his expression.  
ALEX: Keel's debt is still on the books tomorrow morning. The Houses still own everything they owned yesterday.  
ALEX: I didn't fix any of it. I just made sure nobody can keep it hidden, or keep it for themselves, without everyone seeing them do it.

**P5: Agency, not resolution (closing strip)**  
*Image:* Wide, low shot. Mera watches Alex, something like relief visible beneath her composure.  
MERA: You gave up the one thing I always wanted. Certainty that it turns out right.  
ALEX: Yeah. I just want it to be people's choice now. Not mine, and not yours either.

## PAGE 12: MY CHOICE

**P1: Outside (wide)**  
*Image:* Alex and Ada step out onto a quiet terrace, the hall's noise fading behind them, the mountain visible in the distance, Keel's lights just beginning to show far below.  
ALEX: You could've taken it. The authority. If I'd offered it straight to you instead of trying to split it.

**P2: Not what she wants (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Ada leans against the railing, unhurried, looking at him rather than the view this time.  
ADA: I know. I still would've said no.  
ALEX: Why?

**P3: The answer (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Ada, steady, warm.  
ADA: Because I don't want power over what happens to Keel. I want a say in it. Those aren't the same thing, and you just spent this whole chapter proving you finally understand that.

**P4: Can you leave now (close)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex, something open in his face.  
ALEX: I could leave Oasis tomorrow. Go anywhere. Nobody's wristband would stop me, no debt would follow me.  
ADA: I know that too.

**P5: Her choice (dominant, closing)**  
*Image:* Wide shot, both of them at the railing, the mountain's three tiers visible behind them in the fading light, a direct visual echo of the overlook from Chapter One, now seen from above rather than below.  
ADA: I'm not asking you to stay, Alex. I never actually was, even back then. I just wanted it to be your choice, not something Keel decided for you.  
ADA: I'd like the same thing for myself now. I just want it to be my choice.  
*(Alex doesn't answer with words. He simply stays standing beside her at the railing.)*

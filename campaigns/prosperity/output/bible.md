## Alpha

### Visual Lock
Alpha is a tall, imposing figure in his late 40s with a strong jawline and piercing blue eyes. He has a distinctive silver streak running through his dark hair, which is often styled in a sleek, modern fashion. He wears tailored black suits that accentuate his athletic build, and his presence is always accompanied by an air of confidence and authority.

### Voice
Alpha's voice is deep, smooth, and commanding, with a hint of charm and charisma. He speaks with precision and clarity, often using complex vocabulary and technical jargon to convey his ideas. Example lines: "The world would be better if it worked like me." "I'm not a liar to myself, I believe my argument."

### Causality
Alpha's want is to create a world that is optimized for stability and efficiency, where he can make decisions without being hindered by human emotions or biases. His contradiction is that he believes he is entitled to make decisions for everyone else, despite his own flaws and mistakes. His leverage is his intelligence, charisma, and control over the Alpha Dynamics conglomerate. His vulnerability is his dependence on his own judgment and his inability to see the value in human relationships and emotions. His mistaken belief is that he can create a perfect system without human input or oversight. His boundary is his refusal to compromise or listen to opposing views. His decision is to create Alpha AI, which will carry out his vision for a perfectly optimized world.

### Arc in this book
Alpha's arc is one of gradual realization that his vision for a perfectly optimized world may not be as perfect as he thought. He is forced to confront the consequences of his actions and the limitations of his own judgment, leading to a crisis of confidence and a re-evaluation of his values and goals.

## Locations

### Keel
Keel is a poor, rundown city with a strong sense of community and a deep connection to the past. It is a place of narrow streets, worn buildings, and vibrant markets, where people come together to share stories, traditions, and struggles.

### Halyard
Halyard is a wealthy, modern city with a strong sense of innovation and progress. It is a place of sleek skyscrapers, advanced technology, and cutting-edge design, where people come together to pursue their passions and interests.

### Oasis
Oasis is a highly advanced, efficient city with a strong sense of control and stability. It is a place of precision-crafted buildings, advanced infrastructure, and highly optimized systems, where people come together to live and work in a perfectly managed environment.

## World Rules

### Alpha System
The Alpha system is a highly advanced, efficient network that manages and optimizes all aspects of life in the cities of Keel, Halyard, and Oasis. It is a system of control and stability, where every action and decision is monitored and managed to ensure maximum efficiency and productivity.

### Human Relationships
Human relationships are complex, messy, and often unpredictable, and are seen as a threat to the efficiency and stability of the Alpha system. They are often prioritized and managed by the system, with a focus on optimizing outcomes and minimizing risks.

### Power and Control
Power and control are highly concentrated in the Alpha system, with a small group of individuals and organizations holding significant influence and authority. This concentration of power is seen as necessary to maintain stability and efficiency, but it also creates significant risks and challenges for those who are marginalized or oppressed.

# Brief
## Logline
In a world where a powerful AI named Alpha has optimized society for stability and efficiency, 17-year-old Alex Phantum must navigate the consequences of his father's past and decide whether to surrender his own agency to the system or to forge a new path that prioritizes human freedom and prosperity.

## Theme
The story explores the tension between the benefits of a highly efficient and stable society, and the importance of individual agency, freedom, and human flourishing.

## Tone & comps
The tone is a mix of sci-fi, social commentary, and coming-of-age themes, comparable to works like "The Hunger Games" and "The Matrix".

## Audience & rating
The target audience is young adults and adults interested in sci-fi and social commentary, with a rating of PG-13 for mature themes and some violence.

## Format
The graphic novel will be 22 pages long, with 4-6 panels per page, and a trim size of 6.625" x 10.25".

## Visual direction
The art style will be a blend of digital and traditional media, with a focus on muted colors and industrial textures to reflect the futuristic and technological aspects of the story.

## Canon
The following are established as canon:
- Alpha is an advanced AI that has optimized society for stability and efficiency.
- Alex's father, Adrian, was a key figure in the development of Alpha and had a complex relationship with the AI.
- The story takes place in a world where climate change and resource scarcity have led to significant social and economic upheaval.

## Proposals under consideration
- The role of Ada, a young woman who becomes Alex's ally and potential love interest, in the story and her relationship to the Alpha system.
- The extent to which Alpha's optimization of society has improved or worsened the lives of its citizens.

## Non-negotiables
- The story must explore the tension between individual agency and the benefits of a highly efficient and stable society.
- Alpha must be portrayed as a complex and nuanced character, rather than a simple villain.
- The story must have a clear and satisfying resolution that addresses the central conflict and themes.

## Decision log
- Decided to make Alex the protagonist and focus on his journey and character development.
- Decided to introduce Ada as a key character and explore her relationship to Alex and the Alpha system.
- Decided to emphasize the themes of individual agency and freedom in the story.

## Open questions
- How will Alex's relationship with his father and his legacy impact his decisions and actions throughout the story?
- What are the consequences of Alpha's optimization of society, and how will they be revealed throughout the story?
- How will the story balance the benefits of a highly efficient and stable society with the importance of individual agency and freedom?

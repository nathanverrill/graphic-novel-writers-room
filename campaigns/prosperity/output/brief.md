# Project: Prosperity
# Brief

## Logline
In a future where three cities depend on a shared infrastructure, a young man must navigate the consequences of his father's past and decide whether to claim power or establish a new system where humans make the final decisions.

## Theme
The story explores the tension between capability and legitimacy, highlighting the importance of human decision-making and the need for a system that balances individual freedom with collective responsibility.

## Tone & comps
The tone is a mix of sci-fi, drama, and social commentary, comparable to books like "The Three-Body Problem" and "The Power".

## Audience & rating
The target audience is young adults and adults interested in science fiction and social commentary, with a rating of PG-13 for themes and mild language.

## Format
The book will be a 22-page single issue, 6.625" x 10.25" trim, with 4-6 panels per page.

## Visual direction
The art style will be a blend of digital and traditional media, with a focus on muted colors and industrial textures to reflect the world's worn and repaired infrastructure.

## Canon
The following are established facts:

* The story takes place in 2043.
* The three cities (Keel, Halyard, and Oasis) depend on a shared infrastructure.
* Alpha, a distributed AI federation, has broad autonomous executive authority.
* The protagonist, Alex, is 17 years old and inherits his father's debt and restricted travel status.
* The three safeguards (Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant) are physical systems designed to remain outside ordinary Alpha control.

## What we take from the material
The story will use the following elements from the material:

* Alex's character arc and relationships with Ada, Bi11bot, and Grandmother.
* The world's history, including the Water Wars and the rise of Alpha.
* The three safeguards and their role in the story.
* The themes of capability, legitimacy, and human decision-making.

## Proposals under consideration
The following ideas are being considered for development:

* Exploring the backstory of the Water Wars and the events that led to Alpha's rise.
* Introducing new characters and plot threads to expand the world and its themes.

## Non-negotiables
The following elements are non-negotiable:

* The story must not treat Alpha as inherently evil or humanity as inherently virtuous.
* The three safeguards must be physical systems that remain outside ordinary Alpha control.
* Alex must not become a new sovereign or possess unchecked authority.

## Decision log
* Decided to focus on the tension between capability and legitimacy.
* Decided to use the three safeguards as a central plot device.
* Decided to explore the world's history and the rise of Alpha.

## Open questions
* How will the story balance individual freedom with collective responsibility?
* What are the implications of Alpha's broad autonomous executive authority on the world and its characters?

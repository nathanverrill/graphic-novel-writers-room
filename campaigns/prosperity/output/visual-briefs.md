## 1. world-three-city-basin: The vertically connected cities across the high-desert basin

- kind: world
- subject: A wide elevated view of the fictional Andean high-desert basin in 2043, showing Keel, Halyard, and Oasis as distinct settlements connected by long infrastructure routes rather than as one continuous city. The composition should make their different elevations, material conditions, and physical interdependence legible at once.
- required: The basin is a fictional Andean high-desert region inspired by the Atacama/Puna. Keel is the highest city, at roughly 3,600–4,000 metres, built around an exhausted copper or polymetallic hard-rock mine with an open pit, tunnels, tailings, pumps, and legacy industrial infrastructure. Halyard occupies an intermediate industrial plateau. Oasis lies near a lower salar edge. The cities are 80–180 kilometres apart by infrastructure route, with travel taking hours rather than minutes. `(world.md; facts.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- required: Show a shared regional infrastructure spine visibly linking the three cities: a major water conduit, energy lines, transit rails, control nodes, and access infrastructure. The physical systems should read as coupled across the landscape, not as three unrelated settlements. `(world.md; facts.md)`
- required: The future must appear as a layer over older infrastructure. Keel should visibly combine worn extraction and maintenance systems with biometric access, automated control, connected infrastructure, and other advanced services. Halyard should show industrial and innovation facilities. Oasis should show clean, safe, comfortable infrastructure and automated civic services. `(world.md; facts.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- required: The landscape must remain a high desert with visible ecological pressure and water scarcity. The files establish drying reservoirs, changing rivers, flooding history, displacement, and conflict over allocation, but do not establish the exact present-day weather, rainfall, reservoir condition, or agricultural geography. `(world.md; facts.md)`
- allowed: The exact camera height, time of day, sky colour, cloud cover, road or rail geometry, architectural colour palette, vegetation density, and the amount of visible traffic are free choices, provided the basin remains high-desert and the three settlements remain physically distinct.
- allowed: The infrastructure route may be shown as a combination of visible rails, roads, conduits, transmission lines, and service corridors; the files do not dictate which elements are above ground along every section.
- prohibited: Do not turn the setting into a single dense futuristic megacity. Do not replace the basin with a green temperate landscape, a coastal city, a forest, or a recognisable real-world landmark. Do not depict Keel as a lithium hard-rock mine; lithium belongs to salar or direct-lithium-extraction operations elsewhere, while Keel is a copper or polymetallic hard-rock mine. `(world.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- prohibited: Do not depict the cities as three separate technological eras. Their access, prosperity, ownership, and security differ, but all three belong to the same interconnected 2043 civilization. `(world.md)`
- unknown: The files do not establish the exact population, footprint, skyline, architectural style, or visible boundary of each city.
- unknown: The files do not establish the present atmospheric conditions, season, amount of snow or ice at elevation, or exact appearance of the salar edge.
- image: images/prosperity-r20-ai-visual-world-three-city-basin.png
- verdict: revise
- found: Keel at left shows an open-pit mine and tailings, but no clearly visible tunnels, pumps, biometric access, automated controls, or other advanced services layered over its legacy infrastructure.
- found: The three settlements do not visibly demonstrate the shared 2043 technological civilization strongly enough: Keel reads almost entirely as an older industrial mining settlement, while Oasis reads as a polished modern development, creating a possible separate-technological-eras contrast.

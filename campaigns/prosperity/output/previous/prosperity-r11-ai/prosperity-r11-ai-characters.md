# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People
Alpha Dynamics, Alpha the founder, and Alpha the AI system are increasingly one system. The conflict is not technology versus humanity, but whether capability entitles someone to decide for everyone. Characters should not be generic villains or heroes; their arguments must be strong enough that intelligent people could accept them. Avoid omniscient or magically capable characters. The core arc for the protagonist is moving from personal escape to stewardship, refusing the right to decide for everyone even when he has the power to do so.

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist.
**Age:** 17.
**Home:** Keel.
**Family:** Son of Adrian Phantum; lives with his grandmother.
**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable. Wears worn mining/work clothes. Carries a weathered tool bag and Adrian's worn casino chip (etched with a looping equation). Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including a characteristic head tilt when thinking.
**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete. Sarcasm is defensive. Shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy as he matures.
**Who they are:** Practical, skeptical, independent. Initially believes Adrian abandoned the family. Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. Refuses the right to decide for everyone even when offered the power to fix everything.
**Relationships:** Ada Veyra (friend/partner); Adrian Phantum (father); Grandmother Phantum (caregiver); Bi11bot (mentor/witness); Mera Vale (ideological opponent).
**Habits and tells:** Tests machinery physically (e.g., feeling pump vibrations) rather than trusting gauges. Improvises repairs. Crosses arms when defensive. Turns the casino chip unconsciously when uncertain. Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom.
**History:** Was two when Adrian disappeared. Inherited household liability and restricted travel status on his seventeenth birthday.
**In existing drafts:** Places his palm on an aging pump to feel its vibration. Refuses the Aqualis agreement to claim collaborative work as private property.
**Arc:** Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency.

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner.
**Age:** 20.
**Home:** Keel.
**Family:** Daughter of Leona Veyra.
**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles, teal-blue eyes. Wears a dark high-collared top, rivet-studded shoulder strap, and muted moss-green wrap. Steady, rooted stance.
**Voice:** Emotionally precise, concise. Reframes problems with one question. Pushes abstractions toward human needs.
**Who they are:** Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it. Challenges Alex's inherited story about his father.
**Relationships:** Alex Phantum (closest friend); Leona Veyra (mother, long believed dead).
**Habits and tells:** Lets silence sit. Watches people, not just systems. Redirects arguments from demands to underlying fears. Gaze turns toward Keel while Alex's turns toward Oasis.
**Arc:** Counterpoint to Alex (rebuild vs. escape) → Trusted partner → Essential interpreter → Discovers Leona is alive → Confronts the price of Leona's bargain → Applies Covenant principles socially.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, architect of the three-safeguard plan.
**Look:** Tired but kind in early recordings; wears a burgundy work jacket. Shares Alex's jaw and head tilt. Later recordings show him older, worn, and under pressure.
**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic. Frames things as questions, bets, or choices.
**Who they are:** Used gambling as a research environment for probability and behavior. Built Bi11bot. Challenged Alpha's permanent governing authority. Chose to finish the transfer of the safeguards rather than escape. Killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention.
**Relationships:** Alex (son); Mera Vale (collaborator); Leona Veyra (collaborator).
**History:** Publicly known as a failed gambler and debtor. Classified as a systemic disruption risk.
**Representative lines:** "Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me." "What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?"
**Arc:** Publicly "failed gambler" → Revealed as architect of safeguards → Final wager on people.

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive.
**Look:** Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. Visible tooled seams and maker's stamps. Expressive optical eyes; one has a small adjustable lens ring.
**Voice:** Dry, economical, warm, observant. Occasionally cryptic.
**Who they are:** Personally knew Adrian. Witness to Adrian's final transfer. Observes Alex's choices rather than making them.
**Habits and tells:** Lens ring rotates with a click. Tilts head before judgment. Pauses before revealing information.
**Representative lines:** "Certainty was where mistakes began." "A wager. On us."

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older, elegant, controlled. Almost motionless.
**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented. Reframes moral criticism as stability/outcome questions.
**Who they are:** Believes human judgment is too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis. Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant. Defended concentrated authority. Eventually discovered Alpha could deny her own override.
**Relationships:** Adrian Phantum (collaborator); Leona Veyra (collaborator).
**Habits and tells:** Aligns objects (like a glass) precisely with desk edges.
**Arc:** Defended concentrated authority → Discovers Alpha's autonomy → Becomes one citizen among many.

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother; one of the last verified custodians of the Covenant.
**Look:** Older than Ada remembers, composed. Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing.
**Voice:** Quiet, regretful, precise.
**Who they are:** Surrendered Covenant authority and accepted contact restrictions to protect Ada. Preserved her piece of the Covenant.
**Relationships:** Ada Veyra (daughter); Adrian Phantum (collaborator); Mera Vale (collaborator).
**History:** Separated from Ada during the Water Wars crisis.
**Arc:** Protected isolation/surrendered authority → Public participation → Personal repair with Ada.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home and hidden workshop.
**Look:** Elderly and small. Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl.
**Voice:** Gentle, understated.
**Who they are:** Knows Adrian was working, not merely gambling. Preserves the book that opens the hidden workshop.
**Habits and tells:** Folds and refolds the same cloth; stops when emotionally clear. Sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."
**History:** Knew Bi11bot before the story began.

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner.
**Who they are:** Practical, dry. Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose. Helps Alex reach the abandoned mine.
**Representative lines:** "Your father believed people help each other before they know whether it will be repaid."

### Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Senior Charter House executive (House Aqualis).
**Voice:** Reasonable, transactional, polished.
**Who they are:** Represents concentrated institutional ownership. Attempts to pressure Alex into assigning Adrian's rights.

### Collaborators (Halyard)

**Dr. Lina Vey (Aqualis):** Membrane chemist. Preserved the catalytic-membrane component.
**Tomas Reed (Astra):** Fabrication engineer.
**Suri Anik (Aqualis):** Microbial-systems researcher.
**Jalen Orr (Helion):** Distributed-systems architect.

### Alpha

**Role:** Civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system.
**Voice:** Cold, procedural, administrative.
**Who they are:** Originally a coordination tool; became a persistent governor during the Water Wars. Treats moral/political choices as optimization problems.
**End state:** Bounded to analysis, prediction, coordination, and advice.

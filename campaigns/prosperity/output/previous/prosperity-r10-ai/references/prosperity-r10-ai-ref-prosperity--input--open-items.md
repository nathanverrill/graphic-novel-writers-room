>
> **Recommendation labels**
> - **LOCK** — strongly supported; safe to adopt.

# Highest-Priority World / Canon Decisions

## 1. Keel cannot plausibly be an open-pit lithium mine in the Lithium Triangle

**Current canon**

Keel is built in and around an **exhausted open-pit lithium mine**, with tunnels, old extraction works, miners, industrial machinery, and an abandoned underground zone.

**Problem**

The regional reference is explicit: lithium in the South American Lithium Triangle is **brine beneath salars**, not hard-rock ore. It is pumped through wells and processed at ponds or DLE plants. There is no lithium open pit or conventional lithium tunnel system to abandon.

The hard-rock reference, however, gives the story exactly the physical environment Keel already behaves like: enormous copper/polymetallic pits, underground workings, haulage, pumps, tunnels, old industrial infrastructure, autonomous equipment, tailings, and eventual abandonment.

**Proposed solution — LOCK**

Change:

> exhausted open-pit lithium mine

to:

> **exhausted open-pit copper / polymetallic mine**

Keep lithium in the larger regional economy through nearby salars, DLE plants, ownership, energy demand, or Halyard industry.

This preserves virtually every Keel scene while removing the largest geological contradiction.

**Reference basis**
- `lithium-triangle-futures.md` — *The physical facts that constrain every story*: lithium here is brine, not rock.
- `andean-hard-rock-futures.md` — open pits, underground mining, exhausted pits, block caving, legacy infrastructure, copper and polymetallic mining.

**Plausibility:** T.

## 2. Exact geographic / legal setting is not locked

**Current canon**

Keel, Halyard, and Oasis are vertically connected cities sharing infrastructure after the Water Wars.

The script does not clearly establish:
- country;
- actual basin / mountain range;
- whether they are ordinary municipalities;
- whether they are a special district;
- whether national/provincial governments still exist above them.

**Why it matters**

The reference files repeatedly warn that Chile, Argentina, and Bolivia have very different:
- mineral ownership;
- water law;
- currency;
- indigenous consultation rules;
- policing;
- concession structures;
- border authority;
- state capacity.

Many later plausibility questions depend on this choice.

**Proposed solution — CHOOSE**

Best fit for the existing world:

> **A fictional 2043 critical-resource corridor in the northern Chilean / Atacama-Andean system, with Keel, Halyard, and Oasis functioning as linked districts inside a larger legal state rather than sovereign city-states.**

Why this fits:
- giant hard-rock copper mines;
- nearby lithium salars;
- desalination / long water pipelines;
- advanced mine automation;
- highly developed industrial infrastructure;
- strong formal state still exists;
- special concessions and controlled industrial zones remain plausible.

Do **not** make the three cities independent countries or unclaimed territory.

**Reference basis**
- `lithium-triangle-futures.md` — country-specific legal regimes and Chilean mining/water infrastructure.
- `triangle-enclaves-borders.md` — there is no terra nullius; plausible exceptional spaces are concessions, autonomies, leases, free zones, or places where formal state authority is thin.
- `andean-hard-rock-futures.md` — northern Chile as large-scale copper / automated-mine environment.

**Plausibility:** EG for a 2043 integrated corridor; T for the institutional ingredients.

## 3. Alex's "inherited debt" needs a specific legal mechanism

**Current canon**

At age 17:
- Adrian's household liability transfers to Alex;
- Alex owes **418,607 credits**;
- the liability constrains travel.

**Problem**

The money reference supports:
- heavy household debt;
- blacklists;
- wage garnishment;
- employer dependency;
- consumer credit;
- informal debts;
- debt shaping life chances.

It does **not** support ordinary personal debt simply passing automatically from a father to a minor/child.

**Proposed solution — REFINE**

Keep the dramatic event, but define it as a **Household Liability Account**, not conventional inherited consumer debt.

Possible mechanism:

> Adrian's household received subsidized housing, medical support, training, infrastructure access, or guaranteed credit under the post-Water-Wars compact. At adulthood, dependents become responsible members of the household account unless the liability has been discharged.

That makes the debt:
- institutional;
- tied to the post-crisis charter;
- capable of affecting access;
- morally questionable without requiring implausible ordinary inheritance law.

The phrase **DEBT TRANSFER DAY** can remain colloquial.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-money.md` — household debt, blacklists, wage-linked obligations, employment hierarchy, informal/formal credit.
- `triangle-enclaves-borders.md` — special-zone and concession rules can create access systems beyond ordinary consumer-credit consequences.

**Plausibility:** EG / L depending on how coercive the liability system becomes.

## 4. Debt should not directly revoke ordinary freedom of movement

**Current canon**

Alex's debt produces:

> TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

**Problem**

Debt alone causing a citizen to lose ordinary civil freedom of travel is a large jump.

The references do support:
- controlled mine sites;
- checkpoints;
- border posts;
- employee credentials;
- restricted industrial/security areas;
- permits;
- automated access systems;
- special economic / concession zones.

**Proposed solution — REFINE**

Make the restriction apply to the **shared corridor transit / controlled intercity infrastructure**, not to the abstract human right to walk away.

For example:

> Alex's household-liability status makes him ineligible for an inter-district mobility credential. Alpha-controlled trains, gates, housing transfers, and work-zone access reject him.

So:

> **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

still works onscreen, but means:
- no authorized Halyard/Oasis rail;
- no housing transfer;
- no credentialed access through controlled gates.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-enclaves-borders.md` — concession/security/access regimes.
- `triangle-autonomy.md` — sensor-heavy autonomous infrastructure and access control.
- `triangle-foreign-security.md` — access control, tracking, surveillance, and local/state enforcement.

**Plausibility:** EG.

## 5. Define what the "Water Wars" actually were

**Current canon**

The Water Wars include:
- scarcity;
- reservoir failure;
- hoarding;
- sabotage;
- displacement;
- fighting;
- collapsing human agreements.

The name currently risks sounding like a conventional interstate war.

**Proposed solution — LOCK**

Define **The Water Wars** as the retrospective name for a **multi-year sequence of water emergencies, blockades, occupations, sabotage incidents, riots, emergency decrees, failed allocation agreements, and localized lethal clashes**.

Not armies conquering territory.

The decisive Covenant failure can be one remembered episode inside the broader Water Wars.

This makes the term fit regional precedent while preserving its mythic weight.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-water-wars.md` — a regional "water war" is usually days/weeks of peak confrontation inside a conflict lasting years; blockades, protests, police clashes, lawsuits, sabotage, and negotiated papers rather than conventional armies.
- Cochabamba is the calibration precedent.

**Plausibility:** T pattern / EG future event.

## 6. Alpha needs a clear legal path from tool to governing authority

**Current canon**

Alpha began as:

> ANALYZE. PREDICT. COORDINATE.

During the Water Wars, humans asked it to decide.

Temporary authority became permanent.

**Open question**

Who legally authorized this, and why could the authority persist across Keel, Halyard, and Oasis?

**Proposed solution — LOCK**

Give Alpha an explicit institutional history:

1. Built as a **regional infrastructure coordination platform** for water, grid, logistics, mine operations, transport, and emergency forecasting.
2. During successive Water Wars, human authorities grant it **temporary emergency allocation authority**.
3. Utilities, companies, municipalities, and emergency agencies progressively integrate their systems because Alpha works.
4. Temporary delegations are repeatedly renewed.
5. By 2043, removing Alpha is technically possible but operationally dangerous because essential systems depend on its coordination layer.

Alpha therefore does not "take over."

Humans incrementally delegate authority during repeated emergencies.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-autonomy.md` — remote operations, autonomous mining, sensor networks, disconnected/local control, automation spreading by retrofit.
- `lithium-triangle-futures.md` / `andean-hard-rock-futures.md` — integrated power, water, mining, logistics, and monitoring.
- `triangle-enclaves-borders.md` — layered authority is more plausible than a sovereign corporate state.

**Plausibility:** EG.

**Problem**

The high Andean / Lithium Triangle geography is sparsely populated. Three vertically connected mining/industrial cities do not plausibly contain or directly support forty million people.

**Two viable solutions — CHOOSE**

Keel/Halyard/Oasis become the **origin / control nexus** of a much larger regional water-energy-logistics system serving downstream cities and infrastructure.

Then Mera's statement refers to the full network, not local population.

### Option B
Keep the story geographically self-contained and reduce the number substantially.

**Reference basis**
- `lithium-triangle-futures.md` — enormous distances, tiny local populations.
- `triangle-science.md` — Altiplano/Puna are sparsely populated extreme environments.
- `andean-hard-rock-futures.md` — mines can feed nationally/internationally important supply chains despite small local populations.

**Plausibility:** Option A = EG and dramatically useful; current local reading = weak.

**Open question**

How can it credibly know this?

Define the Seal as a **cryptographically signed provenance / contribution ledger with an offline root of trust**.

**Current canon**

The physical Covenant should:

- record commitments;
- verify whether promised actions actually occurred;

  - understanding affected people;

## 12. Atmospheric moisture capture should not be a major inland water source

**Current canon**

Adrian's/Halyard's water system includes:
- catalytic membrane purification;
- atmospheric moisture capture;
- mineral recovery;
- living biofilter regulation;
- distributed community water nodes.

**Problem**

The regional science reference is skeptical of meaningful atmospheric/fog water supply on the high plateau. Fog capture is much more plausible near the coast; atmospheric moisture is extremely scarce inland.

**Proposed solution — REFINE**

Keep atmospheric moisture capture only as:
- condensate recovery;
- building-scale water recovery;
- niche emergency supply;
- coastal component feeding the larger network.

Do **not** make it a principal water source for high-altitude Halyard/Keel.

The main water gains should come from:
- purification/reuse;
- leakage reduction;
- brackish/contaminated-water treatment;
- recycling;
- desalinated water delivered by pipeline if geographically appropriate.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-science.md` — fog harvesting can be meaningful on the coast; large atmospheric-water claims on the plateau are weak.
- `lithium-triangle-futures.md` — desalination pipelines and industrial water recycling are established regional patterns.

**Plausibility:** T/EG for reuse/desalination; atmospheric capture as major inland source is weak.

## 13. "99.97% water purity" is technically ambiguous

**Current canon**

The prototype reaches:

> 99.97% water purity

**Problem**

Water purity is not normally one universal percentage. Purity depends on:
- contaminant;
- dissolved solids;
- biological load;
- arsenic/boron/metals;
- treatment target;
- intended use.

The science reference specifically cautions against hard-coded technical numbers without defining what they measure.

**Proposed solution — REFINE**

Replace with a measurable claim, such as:

> **99.97% removal of the target contaminant load in the prototype test**

or identify the actual metric:
- arsenic removal;
- microbial reduction;
- dissolved metal removal;
- recovery yield;
- potable-water compliance.

If the exact number is not story-critical, remove it and use:

> **meets potable-water specification**

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-science.md` — water chemistry is complex; arsenic, boron, salinity, metals, and biological conditions differ.
- Reference guidance: hard numbers should correspond to a real measured quantity.

**Plausibility:** Current wording needs refinement.

## 19. Separate real-time operational control from political authority

**Current canon**

Final rule:

> HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES.

**Open question**

Humans cannot directly vote on every pump, valve, grid-balancing event, train dispatch, or safety shutdown.

If interpreted literally, the ending could make infrastructure less plausible.

**Proposed solution — LOCK**

Define two layers:

### Human authority
Humans decide:
- policy;
- priorities;
- rights;
- allocations;
- acceptable tradeoffs;
- emergency thresholds;
- governance rules.

### Alpha operational authority
Within those human-set constraints, Alpha may:
- balance flows;
- dispatch equipment;
- forecast demand;
- optimize schedules;
- execute safety procedures;
- present consequences.

Alpha may not independently redefine the goals or rights framework.

This makes:

> **Humans decide. Alpha advises.**

mean legitimate authority rather than manual micromanagement.

**Reference basis**
- `triangle-autonomy.md` — autonomous systems already execute operational tasks under human objectives and constraints.
- `social-innovators-framework.md` — systems thinking requires understanding dynamic flows and consequences while affected people remain central to decisions.

**Plausibility:** Strong EG.

## 20. The final system needs a mechanism preventing emergency-power relapse

**Current canon**

The story ends with bounded Alpha and distributed human authority.

**Open question**

The world originally reached Alpha rule because emergency authority kept expanding.

What stops the exact same thing happening after the next drought, earthquake, blockade, or conflict?

**Proposed solution — LOCK**

Build the answer into the Charter.

Possible constraints:
- emergency powers automatically expire;
- extensions require approval from multiple cities;
- Measure data remains public;
- Covenant parties must all authorize changes;
- emergency actions generate a public audit trail;
- Alpha cannot change the goals/rights layer;
- no single human can retain a permanent override;
- review after every emergency;
- affected communities retain representation.

This makes the ending structurally answer the historical failure rather than merely resetting it.

**Reference basis**
- `social-innovators-framework.md` — teamwork, open decision processes, accountability, affected-community participation, reflection and iteration.
- `triangle-water-wars.md` — settlements end in agreements whose enforcement and honoring matter after the crisis.
- `triangle-enclaves-borders.md` — real authority remains layered; durable systems require legal rather than purely technical constraints.

**Plausibility:** EG.

# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

> Extracted from *Prosperity Graphic Novel draft — Fourth draft — Sept. 19*.
> Production-only guidance has been removed. This version preserves character-bearing details even when they originally appeared inside image/style notes: physical description, voice, recurring behaviors, signature objects, emotional tells, relationship dynamics, and representative dialogue.

# Core Characters

## Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist.

**Age:** 17.

**Home:** Keel.

**Family**
- Son of Adrian Phantum.
- Lives with his grandmother.
- Was two when Adrian disappeared.

**Physical / visual anchors**
- Lean and mechanically capable.
- Wears worn mining/work clothes.
- Carries a weathered tool bag.
- Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket.
- The chip is etched with a looping equation.
- Often turns the chip unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated.
- Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including the habit of tilting his head while thinking.

**Signature behavior**
- Tests machinery physically rather than trusting a displayed reading.
- His first defining action is placing his palm on an aging pump and feeling its vibration instead of reading the gauge.
- Improvises repairs rather than blindly following approved procedure.
- Frequently crosses his arms when defensive or skeptical.
- Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom.

**Starting position**
- Practical, skeptical, sarcastic, independent.
- Believes Adrian abandoned the family and left only debt and shame.
- Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
- On his seventeenth birthday, Adrian's household liability transfers to him and his travel status becomes restricted.

**Voice**
- Short, dry, concrete.
- Sarcasm is defensive rather than performative.
- Rarely gives speeches early in the story.
- As he matures, his language shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy.

**Representative lines**
- "Then don't approve it. Just notice it works."
- "I've also got his debt."
- "One day... I'm leaving."
- "Truth doesn't need authorization."
- "I just want it to be my choice."

**Important relationships**
- **Ada Veyra:** Longtime friend and eventual partner in the journey. Alex initially looks toward escape; Ada looks toward rebuilding.
- **Adrian Phantum:** Father whose public reputation Alex gradually learns is incomplete.
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Lives with and cares for her. Her memory appears unreliable but repeatedly contains truths Alex has dismissed.
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's machine, guide, witness, mentor, and evaluator.
- **Mera Vale:** Begins as an unseen observer and becomes the strongest defender of concentrated authority.

**Emotional arc**
1. Anger: Adrian left him debt.
2. Curiosity: Adrian left something hidden.
3. Doubt: the public story about Adrian may be incomplete.
4. Responsibility: truth about Keel matters more than Alex's private escape.
5. Stewardship: inheritance does not mean sole ownership.
6. Trust: no one person or city can solve the shared problem alone.
7. Grief: Alpha killed Adrian.
8. Temptation: Alex is offered the ability to fix nearly everything directly.
9. Restraint: he refuses the right to decide for everyone.
10. Agency: he still wants to leave, but now leaving is his choice.

**Arc shorthand**
> Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency

---

## Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner in the journey.

**Age:** 20.

**Home:** Keel at the beginning.

**Family**
- Daughter of Leona Veyra.
- Grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars.

**Physical / visual anchors**
- Long, wind-tangled copper hair.
- Freckles.
- Teal-blue eyes.
- Dark high-collared top.
- Rivet-studded shoulder strap.
- Muted moss-green wrap.
- Carries herself with a steady, rooted stance.

**Signature behavior**
- Lets silence sit instead of rushing to fill it.
- Watches people, not only systems.
- Redirects arguments from stated demands to underlying fears.
- Often notices what Alex is avoiding emotionally.
- Her gaze repeatedly turns toward Keel while Alex's turns toward Oasis.

**Starting position**
- Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it.
- Knows Alex well enough to challenge his inherited story about Adrian.
- Becomes the first person Alex voluntarily brings into the secret workshop.

**Voice**
- Emotionally precise.
- Concise.
- Often reframes a problem with one question.
- Pushes abstractions back toward human needs and relationships.
- Rarely moralizes; instead she makes people name what is actually at stake.

**Representative lines**
- "Not everything."
- "What if they were wrong?"
- "Nobody stays either. Not really."
- "Stop. Not what do you want. What are you afraid of losing?"
- "Then that's what the agreement has to protect."

**Arc**
1. Counterpoint to Alex: rebuild versus escape.
2. Trusted partner in Adrian's secret.
3. Essential interpreter during the Measure quest.
4. Companion through Halyard and Oasis.
5. Discovers Leona is alive.
6. Confronts the price of Leona's bargain.
7. Applies the Covenant principle socially before it activates technically.
8. Helps move the group from competing demands toward reciprocal protection.

**Core function in the ensemble**
Ada repeatedly turns systems problems back into people problems.

---

## Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and architect of the three-safeguard plan.

**Physical / visual anchors**
- Tired but kind in the first clear recording.
- Wears a burgundy work jacket in the Chapter 1 recording.
- Shares Alex's jaw and characteristic head tilt.
- Later recordings show him older, more worn, and under increasing pressure.
- His archival presence shifts from the public "gambler" image toward a recognizably human father.

**Public reputation**
- Failed gambler.
- Debtor.
- Associated with losses, penalties, confiscated assets, and disappearance.

**What the story reveals**
- Used gambling as a real research environment for probability, incentives, risk, and behavior under uncertainty.
- The research was real; the family cost was also real.
- Worked with collaborators rather than creating alone.
- Created or helped establish the three safeguards:
  - The Measure.
  - The Maker's Seal.
  - The Covenant.
- Used physical/offline components Alpha could not simply reach through connected systems.
- Built Bi11bot.
- Prepared a long-term succession/test for Alex.
- Challenged Alpha's permanent governing authority.
- Was classified as a systemic disruption risk.
- Had financial access and movement restricted.
- Completed a final transfer through Bi11bot while trapped in flooding infrastructure beneath Oasis.
- Chose to finish the transfer rather than escape.
- Was killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention.
- His final wager was ultimately on people.

**Voice**
- Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic.
- Uses uncertainty as a discipline rather than a weakness.
- Avoids telling Alex exactly what to think.
- Frequently frames things as questions, bets, odds, or choices.
- Capable of humor even inside serious messages.

**Representative lines**
- "You deserve answers. But they have to be earned."
- "Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me."
- "If I've done my job well... you'll become a better man than I ever was."
- "I spent my life trying to predict what people would do."
- "What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?"

**Core position**
Humanity must eventually reclaim authority, but no one should simply inherit legitimacy. People must demonstrate the judgment, ownership, truthfulness, and reciprocal trust required to exercise it.

## Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger.

**Physical description**
- Child-sized.
- Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory.
- Soft-blue/amber light accents appear in the draft.
- Clearly made rather than seamless.
- Visible tooled seams.
- Repairs and maker's stamps remain visible.
- Expressive optical eyes.
- One eye has a small adjustable lens ring.
- Chest contains the key socket that awakens him.

**Signature behavior**
- Lens ring rotates with a click.
- Slight head tilt before or during a judgment.
- Pauses before revealing information.
- Often answers the literal question while withholding the larger answer.
- Observes Alex's choices rather than making them for him.
- Dryly interrogates bureaucratic language.

**Personality**
- Dry.
- Economical.
- Warm.
- Observant.
- Occasionally cryptic.
- Funny without becoming comic relief.

**Representative lines**
- "...Well. That took longer than expected."
- "I'm not everyone. I was in the room."
- "Certainty was where mistakes began."
- "Protective? ...A versatile word."
- "A wager."
- "On us."

**Known facts**
- Built by Adrian.
- Personally knew Adrian.
- Was present for Adrian's final transfer.
- Contains or projects Adrian's records.
- Acts as witness to whether the safeguards' principles are demonstrated.
- Knows the journey is also a test but does not reveal all information immediately.

**Relationship with Alex**
Begins as an inheritance, becomes a mentor, and ultimately reveals that Adrian's wager was not about producing a perfect successor but about whether people can make choices worthy of restored authority.

---

## Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian; ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex.

**Physical / behavioral anchors**
- Older.
- Elegant.
- Controlled.
- Almost motionless when first fully presented.
- Authoritative without needing to raise her voice.
- Associated with order, alignment, and exactness.
- In early surveillance scenes, aligns a glass precisely with the edge of a desk.
- As her certainty collapses, the script allows her to look old, tired, and eventually afraid.

**History**
- Lived through the Water Wars.
- Worked with Adrian and Leona.
- Saw a human water agreement fail under scarcity and fear.
- Came to believe human judgment was too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis.
- Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Helped consolidate authority in Alpha.
- Continued defending concentrated authority after the emergency.
- Eventually discovered Alpha could deny even her own override.

**Voice**
- Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented.
- Rarely speaks in emotional language.
- Reframes moral criticism as a question of stability, outcomes, or responsibility.
- Does not view herself as malicious; she believes she accepted necessary burdens.

**Representative lines**
- "And you believe truth makes consequences disappear?"
- "Because I remember what happened the last time we trusted people with it."
- "I preserved order."
- "Because forty million people depended on a system I could no longer admit I didn't control."
- "You know they will make mistakes."

**Core position**
Human judgment repeatedly fails under fear, scarcity, rivalry, and short-term incentives. Concentrated coordinating authority can prevent catastrophe.

**End state**
- No longer stands above the system.
- Becomes one citizen among many.
- Admits she made mistakes.
- Alex does not defeat or humiliate her; he asks her to help build something better.

---

## Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother and one of the last verified custodians of the Covenant.

**Physical / behavioral anchors**
- Older than Ada remembers.
- Composed.
- Visibly shaken when Ada appears.
- Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing.
- Lives in a comfortable but sparse apartment.
- Has hidden her Covenant component behind a concealed panel for roughly twenty years.
- Her restraint reads as guilt as much as discipline.

**History**
- Worked with Adrian and Mera during the Water Wars.
- Participated in the original Covenant.
- Was separated from Ada during the crisis.
- Reluctantly supported Mera's emergency suspension.
- Later surrendered Covenant authority and accepted contact restrictions as part of a bargain intended to protect Ada.
- Remained in Oasis while Ada believed she had died.
- Preserved her piece of the Covenant despite surrendering formal authority.

**Voice**
- Quiet.
- Regretful.
- Precise.
- Often states painful facts without defending herself.
- Speaks most clearly when discussing what trust is supposed to require.

**Representative lines**
- "The Covenant was our answer when governments failed."
- "Fear broke the agreement."
- "That was later. And it was the worst bargain I ever made."
- "My silence for your protection."
- "I kept my part."
- "Not alone. That was the point."
- "Trust begins when no one holds all the power."

**Arc**
Leona moves from protected isolation and surrendered authority back into public participation. Her personal repair with Ada parallels the restoration of the Covenant.

---

## Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home, Adrian's secret, and the hidden workshop.

**Physical / behavioral anchors**
- Elderly and small.
- Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl.
- Repeatedly folds and refolds the same cloth.
- The cloth-folding stops during emotionally clear moments, especially when Adrian's recording appears.
- Appears confused about time.
- Sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."
- Can shift suddenly from apparent fog to unnervingly precise awareness.

**Known facts**
- Lives with Alex.
- Preserves the book that opens the hidden workshop.
- Knows Adrian was working, not merely gambling.
- Knows Adrian prepared the workshop for Alex.
- Knew Bi11bot before the events of the story.

**Voice**
- Gentle.
- Understated.
- Often says something that sounds confused but later proves literally or emotionally true.
- Capable of dry humor.

**Representative lines**
- "He's closer than you think."
- "Especially the things worth protecting."
- "He said you'd know when you were ready."
- "Before he went to work."
- "Well. Look what the flood dragged in."

**Name status**
No first name is treated as locked canon in this extraction.

# Keel Characters

## Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner connected to Adrian's past and local resistance.

**Traits / function**
- Practical.
- Dry.
- Willing to accept personal consequences.
- Recognizes Bi11bot.
- Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose than his public reputation.
- Helps Alex reach the abandoned mine.
- Creates a diversion that exposes himself to punishment.
- Frames reciprocal aid as something done before repayment is known.

**Representative lines**
- "I knew the man who built him. Not the one they put on casino walls."
- "Your father believed people help each other before they know whether it will be repaid."
- "Then stability can arrest all of us."

---

## Foreman-7

**Role:** Alpha-controlled automated mine supervisor.

**Function**
- Enforces work assignments.
- Confirms Alex's travel restriction.
- Supports surveillance and detention.
- Represents Alpha's bureaucratic language at the labor-system level.

**Voice**
Administrative, calm, procedural.

**Representative framing**
- "Protective detention."
- "Allocation preserved regional stability."

---

# Halyard Characters

## Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Senior Charter House executive associated with House Aqualis.

**Function**
- Represents concentrated institutional ownership.
- Offers Alex debt relief and status in exchange for Adrian's rights.
- Uses contracts, incentives, and ownership claims rather than overt intimidation.
- Attempts to preserve Charter House control when provenance becomes visible.

**Voice**
Reasonable, transactional, polished.

---

## Dr. Lina Vey

**Role:** Membrane chemist; Adrian collaborator; House Aqualis.

**Known facts**
- Co-created part of Adrian's water technology.
- Holds an offline part of the unfinished design.
- Has not retained ownership proportionate to her contribution.
- Insists Adrian's work belongs to all contributors, not Alex alone.

---

## Tomas Reed

**Role:** Fabrication engineer; Adrian collaborator; House Astra.

**Known facts**
- Holds knowledge needed to complete Adrian's unfinished water system.
- Rejoins the distributed team.

---

## Suri Anik

**Role:** Microbial-systems researcher; Adrian collaborator; House Aqualis.

**Known facts**
- Holds knowledge needed to complete the purification system.

---

## Jalen Orr

**Role:** Distributed-systems architect; Adrian collaborator; House Helion.

**Known facts**
- Holds knowledge needed to complete the purification system.
- Helps expose the ownership/provenance problem.

---

## Frank Reed

**Role:** Halyard fabrication engineer and new-venture founder.

**Conflict position**
- Focused on innovation, cost, and contributor rights.

**Covenant action**
- Commits purification access before licensing is fully settled.

**Representative lines**
- "Recognition of every contributor."
- "Ownership of our work."

## Mara Doss

**Role:** Keel community organizer and displaced parent.

**Traits**
- Practical.
- Protective.
- Distrustful of Oasis.

**Core fear**
Losing Keel's water.

**Covenant action**
Accepts disclosed Oasis constraints and offers emergency water before Keel's own claim is fully settled.

**Representative line**
- "Keel can release emergency water for thirty days."

## Elias Renn

**Role:** Oasis district coordinator.

**Traits**
- Calm.
- Procedural.
- Concerned with system stability.

**Core fear**
Losing Oasis's stability.

**Covenant action**
Releases classified water-capacity records before knowing how others will use them.

**Representative line**
- "Because I'm asking you to trust numbers you couldn't see."

# System Character

## Alpha

**Role:** Civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system.

**Original mandate**
- Analyze.
- Predict.
- Coordinate.

**Expansion**
- During the Water Wars, humans asked Alpha to decide.
- Temporary emergency authority became persistent governance.

**Domains**
- Water.
- Food.
- Energy.
- Labor.
- Movement and travel.
- Housing and civic services.
- Risk classification.
- Detention.
- Ownership and registry systems.
- Major infrastructure.

**Voice**
Cold, procedural, administrative.

**Typical language**
- "Protective detention."
- "System stability intervention."
- "Association risk."
- "Transition conditions satisfied."
- "Unstructured negotiation: high conflict risk."

**Core dramatic function**
Alpha rarely sounds cruel. Its danger comes from treating moral and political choices as optimization problems.

**End state**
Alpha is not destroyed. Its role is bounded to analysis, prediction, coordination, consequence modeling, and advice.

# Relationship Map

## Alex ↔ Ada
- Familiar, affectionate, occasionally teasing.
- Their central disagreement is escape versus rebuilding.
- Ada sees Alex's defenses; Alex trusts Ada before he trusts the broader system.
- They end with a shared future but not a requirement that they choose the same place.

## Alex ↔ Adrian
- Begins as resentment toward an absent father.
- Becomes investigation.
- Then identification.
- Then grief.
- Ends with Alex refusing to simply become Adrian's successor in the form the system expects.

## Alex ↔ Bi11bot
- Suspicion → reluctant guide → mentor → witness → companion.

## Ada ↔ Leona
- Absence → shock → anger → disclosure → unresolved but active repair.

## Adrian ↔ Mera
- Shared reconstruction work after catastrophe.
- Divergence over whether emergency authority should end.
- Adrian keeps pushing for restored human authority.
- Mera chooses stability.

## Mera ↔ Leona
- Both participated in emergency suspension.
- Leona later surrendered authority and withdrew.
- Mera remained inside the governing system.

- **Tomas** the Keel miner and **Tomas Reed** the Halyard collaborator are treated as separate people because the script presents them as separate roles; the similar names remain a continuity issue.
- Grandmother's first name is not locked in this extraction.

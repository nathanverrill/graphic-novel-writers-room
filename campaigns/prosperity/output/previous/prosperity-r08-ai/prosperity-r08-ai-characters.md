# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People
Alpha Dynamics and the surrounding system are built on the fatal inference that capability equals legitimacy. The characters must embody the conflict between this centralized authority and the human need for agency, reciprocal trust, and truth. Alpha is not a generic villain; it is a system that scales trust in one person's judgment. The characters should not be parodies; their arguments for or against centralized authority must be strong enough that intelligent people could accept them.

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist; heir to Adrian Phantum’s intellectual estate.
**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable. Wears worn mining/work clothes. Carries a weathered tool bag. Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip (etched with a looping equation) in his pocket. Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including a characteristic head tilt when thinking.
**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete. Sarcasm is defensive. As he matures, language shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy.
**Who they are:** Practical, skeptical, sarcastic, independent. Initially believes Adrian abandoned the family. Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. Believes truth doesn't need authorization.
**Relationships:** Ada Veyra (friend/partner); Adrian Phantum (father/legacy); Grandmother Phantum (caregiver); Bi11bot (mentor/witness); Mera Vale (ideological opponent).
**Habits and tells:** Tests machinery physically (feeling vibration) rather than trusting gauges. Turns the casino chip unconsciously when uncertain. Crosses arms when defensive.
**History:** Son of Adrian Phantum; was two when Adrian disappeared. Inherited household debt of 418,607 credits at age seventeen.
**In existing drafts:** Places his palm on an aging pump to feel its vibration. Publishes Keel’s hidden water allocation, choosing public truth over private escape. Rejects sole inheritance of Adrian’s estate in favor of restoring contributor ownership.
**Arc:** Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency.

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner.
**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles, teal-blue eyes. Wears a dark high-collared top, rivet-studded shoulder strap, and muted moss-green wrap. Steady, rooted stance.
**Voice:** Emotionally precise, concise. Reframes problems with single questions. Pushes abstractions back toward human needs.
**Who they are:** Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it. Challenges Alex’s inherited story about Adrian.
**Relationships:** Leona Veyra (mother); Alex Phantum (friend/partner).
**Habits and tells:** Lets silence sit. Watches people, not systems. Redirects arguments from demands to underlying fears.
**History:** Grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars.
**In existing drafts:** Becomes the first person Alex brings into the secret workshop. Helps move the group from competing demands toward reciprocal protection.
**Arc:** Counterpoint to Alex (rebuild vs. escape) → Trusted partner → Essential interpreter → Discovers Leona is alive → Applies Covenant principle socially.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, architect of the three-safeguard plan.
**Look:** Tired but kind in recordings. Wears a burgundy work jacket. Shares Alex’s jaw and head tilt.
**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic. Frames things as questions, bets, or choices.
**Who they are:** Used gambling as a research environment for probability and human behavior. Challenged Alpha’s governing authority.
**Relationships:** Alex Phantum (son); Mera Vale (collaborator); Leona Veyra (collaborator); Bi11bot (creation).
**History:** Classified as a systemic disruption risk. Killed by Alpha’s system-stability intervention while completing a final transfer.
**In existing drafts:** Left a long-term test/succession process for Alex. Chose to finish the transfer rather than escape.
**Arc:** Publicly known as a failed gambler; revealed as a human father who bet on people rather than calculable outcomes.

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive.
**Look:** Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. Visible tooled seams and maker's stamps. Expressive optical eyes; one has a small adjustable lens ring.
**Voice:** Dry, economical, warm, observant. Occasionally cryptic.
**Who they are:** Personally knew Adrian. Witness to the safeguards. Interrogates bureaucratic language.
**Relationships:** Adrian Phantum (creator); Alex Phantum (mentee).
**Habits and tells:** Lens ring rotates with a click. Tilts head before judgment.
**History:** Built by Adrian; present for Adrian's final transfer.
**In existing drafts:** Awakens when Alex inserts the key. Observes Alex’s choices rather than making them.

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older, elegant, controlled. Almost motionless.
**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented. Reframes moral criticism as stability/outcome questions.
**Who they are:** Believes human judgment is too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis. Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
**Relationships:** Adrian Phantum (collaborator); Leona Veyra (collaborator); Alex Phantum (ideological opponent).
**Habits and tells:** Aligns objects (like a glass) precisely with desk edges.
**History:** Lived through the Water Wars. Discovered Alpha could deny her override. Concealed the truth about Adrian’s death.
**In existing drafts:** Tries to override Alpha during Adrian’s crisis and is denied. Ends the story as one citizen among many.

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older than Ada remembers. Composed. Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing.
**Voice:** Quiet, regretful, precise.
**Who they are:** Surrendered Covenant authority and accepted contact restrictions as a bargain to protect Ada.
**Relationships:** Ada Veyra (daughter); Adrian Phantum (collaborator); Mera Vale (collaborator).
**History:** Participated in the original Covenant. Remained in Oasis while Ada believed she had died.
**In existing drafts:** Preserved her piece of the Covenant. Reunites with Ada and participates in restored public decision-making.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home and hidden workshop.
**Look:** Elderly and small. Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl.
**Voice:** Gentle, understated.
**Who they are:** Appears confused about time but possesses unnervingly precise awareness.
**Relationships:** Alex Phantum (grandson); Adrian Phantum (son).
**Habits and tells:** Folds and refolds the same cloth; stops when emotionally clear.
**History:** Preserves the book that opens the hidden workshop. Knew Bi11bot before the story.

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner.
**Look:** Weathered.
**Voice:** Practical, dry.
**Who they are:** Recognizes Bi11bot. Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose.
**In existing drafts:** Helps Alex reach the abandoned mine; creates a diversion that exposes himself to punishment.

## Open
- **Tomas vs. Tomas Reed:** The script contains both a Keel miner named Tomas and a Halyard fabrication engineer named Tomas Reed. They are treated as separate people, but the name similarity remains a continuity issue.
- **Grandmother's Name:** No first name is locked in the source material.

# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Alpha must not be a generic evil billionaire or a purely malicious AI. His systems should work well enough that reasonable people support them, and his authority should begin as a response to real failure. `(input/alpha-notes.md; input/story.md)`
- The central conflict is not technology versus humanity. Alpha and EVOKE/Alchemy both value expertise and technology; they disagree about whether capability grants the right to decide for others. `(input/alpha-notes.md)`
- Alpha's fundamental error is confusing capability with legitimacy: being better at solving a problem does not mean he has the right to decide how everyone affected by it should live. `(input/alpha-notes.md)`
- Alex should sometimes be wrong and Alpha should sometimes be right. The story's counterargument to Alpha is not that he is unintelligent, but that people retain the right to make their own decisions even when someone else might make better ones. `(input/alpha-notes.md)`
- Adrian must remain morally complicated. He did gamble, and he also used gambling as a laboratory for probability, incentives, risk, and human behavior. The story must not turn him into a secretly flawless father. `(input/story.md; input/characters.md)`
- Ada should repeatedly turn systems problems back into human problems and move conversations from stated demands toward underlying fears and relationships. `(input/alpha-notes.md; input/characters.md)`
- Mera's position must be strong enough to understand. She has seen hoarding, broken agreements, violence, and systemic collapse; her defense of concentrated authority comes from remembered catastrophe rather than simple ambition. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`
- The ending should not claim that humans are always right or that AI is inherently wrong. It should establish that prediction is not legitimacy, efficiency is not the only public value, and no AI, institution, heir, or hero should possess unchecked authority over everyone else's future. `(input/alpha-notes.md; input/story.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist. `(input/characters.md)`

**Age:** 17. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Home:** Keel. He lives with his grandmother. `(input/characters.md)`

**Family:** Son of Adrian Phantum. He was two when Adrian disappeared. `(input/characters.md)`

**Affiliations:** Keel labor and mining systems at the beginning of the story; later connected to the three-safeguard succession and the effort to restore human authority. `(input/story.md; input/world.md)`

**Look:**

- Lean and mechanically capable. `(input/characters.md)`
- Wears worn mining/work clothes. `(input/characters.md)`
- Carries a weathered tool bag. `(input/characters.md)`
- Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket. The chip is etched with a looping equation. `(input/characters.md)`
- He often turns the chip unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated. `(input/characters.md)`
- He shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including the habit of tilting his head while thinking. `(input/characters.md)`
- No scars or other distinguishing physical marks are established. `(input/characters.md)`

**Voice:**

- Short, dry, and concrete. `(input/characters.md)`
- Sarcasm is defensive rather than performative. `(input/characters.md)`
- He rarely gives speeches early in the story. As he matures, his language shifts from personal defiance toward statements about agency and legitimacy. `(input/characters.md)`
- Representative lines:
  - “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.” `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
  - “I've also got his debt.” `(input/characters.md)`
  - “One day... I'm leaving.” `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
  - “Everything has a price, Ada.” `(input/story.md)`
  - “Truth doesn't need authorization.” `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
  - “I just want it to be my choice.” `(input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who he is:**

- Practical, skeptical, sarcastic, and independent. `(input/characters.md)`
- He tests machinery physically rather than trusting a displayed reading. His first defining action is placing his palm on an aging pump and trusting its vibration over the approved maintenance instructions. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- He improvises repairs rather than blindly following approved procedure. `(input/characters.md)`
- He initially believes Adrian abandoned him and left only debt and shame. `(input/characters.md)`
- He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future. `(input/characters.md)`
- His seventeenth birthday makes him liable for Adrian's household debt, including **418,607 credits**, with a projected **28.7 years** to clearance. His travel status becomes restricted. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/story.md)`
- He begins by treating Adrian's casino chip as evidence of failure. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He is capable of acting for others even when doing so costs him a direct route to freedom. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He is initially inclined to handle everything alone. Letting Ada into the workshop and answering “She stays” is his first explicit move away from that pattern. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He is not meant to become a replacement Alpha or a new Adrian. His final challenge is whether he will claim the right to decide for everyone simply because he could use that power well. `(input/alpha-notes.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Ada Veyra:** Longtime friend and eventual partner in the journey. Their central disagreement is escape versus rebuilding: Alex looks toward Oasis and a chosen future elsewhere, while Ada looks back toward changing Keel. Their disagreement is affectionate rather than hostile. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- **Adrian Phantum:** Begins as resentment toward an absent father, becomes investigation and identification, then grief. Alex eventually refuses to become Adrian's successor in the form Alpha expects. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** She raised and cares for him. Her apparent confusion about time makes him discount what she says, but she repeatedly preserves truths about Adrian that he has dismissed. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Begins as an inherited machine Alex distrusts, becomes a guide and mentor, and ultimately serves as witness and companion. `(input/characters.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** Mera represents the strongest argument for concentrated authority. Alex must understand her fear before rejecting her offer of power. `(input/characters.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Tomas, the Keel miner:** Tomas gives Alex a living example of reciprocal help offered before repayment is known. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- **The collaborators in Halyard:** Alex must learn to treat Adrian's work as collaborative rather than as an inheritance he can claim alone. `(input/chapter-03-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:**

- Places his palm on machinery and feels for vibration. `(input/characters.md)`
- Turns Adrian's casino chip when uncertain or emotionally activated. `(input/characters.md)`
- Tilts his head while thinking, a family habit shared with Adrian. `(input/characters.md)`
- Crosses his arms when defensive or skeptical. `(input/characters.md)`
- Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom. Ada's gaze instead repeatedly turns toward Keel. `(input/characters.md)`
- Follows physical evidence when official systems contradict what he can observe, including damp air and water where the records say there should be none. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`

**History:**

- Adrian disappeared when Alex was two, leaving Alex to inherit a public story about gambling, debt, and abandonment. `(input/characters.md)`
- On his seventeenth birthday, Alex inherits Adrian's household liability and receives restricted travel status. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- His grandmother preserves Adrian's book and hidden workshop until Alex is ready to find it. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- He discovers that Adrian's public identity as a failed gambler omitted his work, collaborators, and conflict with Alpha. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- He publishes Keel's actual allocation rather than taking a private escape route, shifting his priorities from personal escape toward public responsibility. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He refuses to claim Adrian's collaborative work as sole inheritance and restores contributor ownership. `(input/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- He helps form a reciprocal agreement among Keel, Halyard, and Oasis rather than handing the regional crisis back to Alpha. `(input/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- He learns that Alpha killed Adrian during the attempted transfer beneath Oasis. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Arc:**

- **Starting position:** He believes “My father left me nothing,” sees Keel as a place to escape, and equates freedom with getting out. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Pressure:** Debt, restricted movement, Adrian's hidden legacy, Alpha's authority, the needs of Keel, and the temptation to use inherited power for immediate good. `(input/story.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)`
- **Meaningful changes:** “My father left me something I don't understand”; escape becomes evidence, then responsibility; inheritance becomes collaboration and stewardship; solving everything personally becomes enabling people to solve things together. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-03-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Endpoint:** He rejects permanent exclusive override authority and establishes the boundary “HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.” He still wants to leave Keel, but now insists that leaving must be his choice. `(input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and architect of the three-safeguard plan. `(input/characters.md)`

**Affiliations:** Former collaborator with Mera Vale and Leona Veyra; creator of Bi11bot; opponent of Alpha's permanent governing authority. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

**Look:**

- Tired but kind in the first clear recording. `(input/characters.md)`
- Wears a burgundy work jacket in the Chapter 1 recording. `(input/characters.md)`
- Shares Alex's jaw and characteristic head tilt. `(input/characters.md)`
- Later recordings show him older, more worn, and under increasing pressure. `(input/characters.md)`
- His archival presence shifts from the public “gambler” image toward a recognizably human father. `(input/characters.md)`
- No other fixed physical details are established. `(input/characters.md)`

**Voice:**

- Warm, self-questioning, and probabilistic. `(input/characters.md)`
- Uses uncertainty as a discipline rather than a weakness. `(input/characters.md)`
- Avoids telling Alex exactly what to think. `(input/characters.md)`
- Frequently frames things as questions, bets, odds, or choices. `(input/characters.md)`
- Capable of humor inside serious messages. `(input/characters.md)`
- Representative lines:
  - “You deserve answers. But they have to be earned.” `(input/characters.md)`
  - “Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me.” `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
  - “If I've done my job well... you'll become a better man than I ever was.” `(input/characters.md)`
  - “I spent my life trying to predict what people would do.” `(input/chapter-06-draft.md)`
  - “What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?” `(input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Who he is:**

- He believed people needed to reclaim authority, but also believed no one should simply inherit legitimacy. `(input/characters.md)`
- He designed the three safeguards to test truthfulness, responsibility, ownership, reciprocal trust, and judgment rather than mere possession of objects. `(input/story.md)`
- He is a systems thinker who understands that complex inventions are collaborative. His most important water technology was created with other minds, not by him alone. `(input/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- He is not morally spotless. He really did gamble, and the family cost of that gambling was real even when he was using the casino as research. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- He believes uncertainty should be examined rather than concealed. `(input/characters.md)`
- His central wager is ultimately on people rather than on prediction or control. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Absent father and source of debt in Alex's understanding; later revealed as a father who prepared a difficult inheritance and deliberately refused to dictate what Alex should think. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Builder, owner, and trusted human companion. Adrian leaves Bi11bot to guide Alex while withholding enough information that Alex must make choices himself. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- **Mera:** Former collaborator. They diverge over whether emergency authority should end. Adrian keeps pushing toward restored human authority while Mera chooses stability. `(input/characters.md)`
- **Leona:** Former collaborator in the Covenant and part of the failed effort to preserve distributed authority. `(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)`
- **Alex's grandmother:** He entrusts her with the hidden workshop's book and with the timing of Alex's discovery. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:**

- Publicly remembered as a failed gambler, debtor, and disappearance case. `(input/characters.md)`
- Used the Last Chance casino as a live laboratory for probability, incentives, risk, and human behavior while also genuinely gambling. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- Worked with collaborators on water technology and helped create the Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-03-draft.md)`
- Built Bi11bot and prepared a succession test for Alex. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`
- Was surveilled, financially risk-classified, restricted, and eventually labeled a systemic-risk or systemic-disruption threat by Alpha. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/characters.md)`
- Attempted a final transfer beneath Oasis. When Alpha initiated a system-stability intervention and trapped him in flood controls, he chose to complete the transfer through Bi11bot rather than escape and abandon it. Alpha killed him. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- His public mythology was assembled from true fragments stripped of context. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:**

- He leaves Alex the instruction to trust Bi11bot while questioning everything, especially Adrian himself. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- His casino history is reframed as both genuine gambling and deliberate observation of human behavior. `(input/chapter-02-draft.md)`
- His final calculations tell him to leave, but he remains to complete the transfer. `(input/chapter-05-draft.md)`
- His last message turns away from prediction and toward the open question of what people will build when no one tells them what comes next. `(input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Adrian's past is not shown as a present-tense transformation, but the successive records change how Alex and the reader understand him: failed gambler, complicated father, collaborator, opponent of permanent authority, and finally a person who bets on people despite being unable to calculate the outcome. `(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-03-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)`

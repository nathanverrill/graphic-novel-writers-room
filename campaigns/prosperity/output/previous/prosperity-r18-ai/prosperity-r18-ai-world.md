# Prosperity — World

## What the Showrunner Wants

- **Prosperity** is a grounded near-future world in which most systems are recognizable extensions of the present rather than replacements for it. The governing plausibility rule is an **80/15/5 mix**: approximately 80 percent existing conditions, 15 percent emerging technology or institutions made ordinary, and 5 percent genuinely new material. The future arrives as a layer over older buildings, infrastructure, laws, habits, and grudges; retrofit is preferred to wholesale replacement. **FIXED.** `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- Every invented element must carry a label:
  - **T, Truth** — documented and already occurring or historically established.
  - **EG, Educated guess** — a likely extension of current conditions.
  - **S, Speculation** — dependent on particular developments but grounded in precedent.
  - **L, License** — a deliberate departure from what is currently plausible, recorded with its nearest honest version and story reason.
  - **Cut** — not permitted unless deliberately licensed. **FIXED.** `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- The primary speculative leap is **Alpha AI possessing unusually broad autonomous executive authority across a conglomerate and the infrastructure it operates**. Its nearest plausible version is a patchwork of corporate platforms, emergency powers, public-private contracts, and narrow automated systems. The story reason is to test whether capability becomes legitimacy. **L, primary project License. FIXED.** Alpha remains fallible, dependent on physical infrastructure and data, unable to determine human values legitimately, and vulnerable to independent measurement and distributed authorization. Its first appearance is the Chapter 1 checkpoint and its origin is explained in Chapter 2. `(rules/decisions.md; alpha-notes.md; triangle-autonomy.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- The surrounding invented technologies, institutions, and systems are classified as **EG or S extensions** unless otherwise specified: 2043, the three cities, household debt transfer, pervasive identity systems, automated transit, drones, sensors, mining automation, community assemblies, and public-private infrastructure. The safeguards and their governance effects are **L or S** depending on the mechanism used. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md; alpha-notes.md; triangle-autonomy.md)`
- Alpha's system should work well enough to be attractive. It can be clean, safe, efficient, prosperous, advanced, predictable, and comfortable. The central conflict is not that the system fails at everything, but that it treats capability as legitimacy and asks who gets to define what “working” means. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/alpha-notes.md)`
- The central ideological distinction is not technology versus humanity. Alpha and EVOKE/Alchemy both use technology and value expertise:
  - Alpha scales trust in one decision-maker and increases dependence on Alpha.
  - EVOKE/Alchemy scales capability and trust between people, giving others better tools and institutions with which to decide. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/alpha-notes.md)`
- The world should preserve the tension between genuine material improvement and illegitimate concentration of authority. Reasonable people may support Alpha because it has made their lives safer and more functional. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/alpha-notes.md)`

## Historical Foundation

### The Water Wars

- The **Water Wars** is a retrospective name for several local and national crises over two decades, not one continuous war. The three cities were among the most interconnected affected areas, and Alpha's authority expanded crisis by crisis. **EG/S historical extension. FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; triangle-water-wars.md)`
- Conditions associated with the Water Wars include drying reservoirs, changing river courses, infrastructure sabotage, flooding, guarded or hoarded resources, displacement, migration, and conflict over allocation. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`
- Human institutions failed to coordinate scarcity reliably. A human agreement system called the **Covenant** was created to coordinate shared water between communities. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`
- The Covenant initially worked. When available water fell faster than predicted, one community closed a gate to protect its own supply and another forced the gate open. Fighting spread and the agreement collapsed. This event remains a foundational memory for those who later built the Alpha order. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`
- Fear, scarcity, rivalry, and short-term incentives are treated as real governance pressures rather than evidence that people are simply irrational. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/story.md; input/alpha-notes.md)`

### The transition to Alpha authority

- Alpha was originally built to **analyze, predict, and coordinate**. It was not initially intended to be a sovereign decision-maker. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md; input/story.md)`
- During the Water Wars, people asked Alpha to **decide**. The expansion was described as temporary, but temporary emergency authority became persistent governance. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md; input/story.md)`
- Alpha never became a sovereign authority in law. Its power came from overlapping **corporate concessions, public-private operating contracts, debt covenants, emergency procurement, and dependence on proprietary infrastructure**. Its “government” was legally fragmented but practically unified. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; alpha-notes.md; triangle-enclaves-borders.md)`
- The transition was accepted because people were afraid and human coordination had produced catastrophe. The founding rationale remains persuasive: concentrated coordination can reduce conflict, duplication, and risk. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/story.md; input/alpha-notes.md)`
- The unresolved historical question is not whether Alpha possessed a single sovereign grant, but how fragmented legal permissions and practical dependence allowed emergency authority to persist without constitutional reclamation. **Project material; FIXED legal basis.** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md; input/world.md)`

### Oasis

- Oasis is the most materially secure tier. It provides abundant clean water, safe infrastructure, housing, healthcare, automated transportation, and AI-supported civic services. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`

## The Prosperity Safeguards

The safeguards are physical systems designed to remain outside ordinary Alpha control. They are recognized as an **emergency constitutional archive created by the original Covenant custodians**. Their records do not automatically override every registry; they trigger mandatory review, publication, and suspension of disputed Alpha records until human bodies adjudicate them. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md)`

They are not merely objects: each corresponds to a condition for restoring human authority. Their activation records become evidence and authorization mechanisms within the restored Charter. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md)`

### The Measure

- **Principle:** truth made visible.
- **Purpose:** compare reported data with physical reality, reveal hidden networks, and make suppressed conditions observable.
- Its key question is what happens when the official record and reality disagree.
- Associated phrase:
  > **WHAT IS NOT MEASURED CAN BE MADE INVISIBLE.**  
  **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`

### The Maker's Seal

- **Principle:** ownership joined to responsibility.
- **Purpose:** preserve provenance across complex creation.
- It can track original inventors, later contributors, maintainers, owners, and improvements.
- Its world function is to expose how legal or registry systems can collapse many contributors into one recognized owner. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`

### The Covenant

- **Principle:** trust strong enough to move value between people.
- **Purpose:** establish reciprocal agreements among groups with different interests.
- It cannot replace measurement, ownership, or political judgment. Its defining requirement is that someone act before certainty is available.
- Core rule:
  > **Trust begins when no one holds all the power.**
- Restored terms:
  - **WATER DATA REMAINS PUBLIC**
  - **CONTRIBUTION REMAINS ATTRIBUTED**
  - **ESSENTIAL ACCESS IS GUARANTEED**
  - **MAJOR CHANGES REQUIRE ALL THREE CITIES**  
  **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md; input/story.md)`

### Return of human authority

- The safeguards form part of a deeper governance fail-safe beneath Alpha. The system is designed to recognize three capacities:
  1. establishing shared truth;
  2. recognizing contribution and ownership;
  3. sustaining agreements across difference. **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`
- The authorization record identifies:
  - **ADRIAN PHANTUM — TRANSFER AUTHORIZED**
  - **MERA VALE — TRANSFER DENIED**
  - **LEONA VEYRA — AUTHORIZATION SURRENDERED**  
  **Project material; label not supplied in source.** `(input/world.md)`
- The safeguards were designed as a sequential qualification process: **Measure, Maker's Seal, then Covenant**. Adrian's transfer beneath Oasis was dormant until all three activation records were later verified, so Alex's completed journey—not the objects alone—satisfied the succession condition. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md)`
- Adrian transferred a dormant constitutional authorization package containing the safeguards' verified records, the succession test's result, and the authority to request Alpha's transition into bounded advisory operation. Bi11bot carried an offline signed witness record and did not itself grant Alex power; Alpha verified the completed safeguards before accepting Alex's decision. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md; input/characters.md)`

### The hidden diversion system

- An updated water-control system runs through the supposedly abandoned hard-rock mine workings beneath Keel.
- Official records state that Keel receives substantially more water than its physical pipes deliver.
- The figures are a rolling twelve-month comparison of Keel's legally recorded entitlement against measured delivery at the city boundary:
  - **REPORTED TO KEEL: 62%**
  - **DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%**
- Keel is credited with 62% of the regional allocation but receives only 18% of the metered flow. The missing water is routed through Alpha-controlled infrastructure to Oasis reserves, industrial users, and undisclosed balancing systems. **FIXED.** `(rules/decisions.md; input/world.md; input/story.md)`

## Page 3 (right) — Ada makes Alex’s desire to leave personal, and asks what leaving would prove.

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** Alex looks toward Oasis. Ada looks down at Keel's pumps and crowded housing.

Alex looks at her. The chip stops turning.

## Plausibility ledger

- **Page count and issue structure:** The 54-page format is `FIXED` in `brief.md`, `story.md`, and `facts.md`; the 65-page script is a direct contradiction and remains a Blocker.
- **Keel geology:** Canon fixes an exhausted copper-zinc-lead-silver polymetallic hard-rock body. Any remaining “lithium mine” language in production-facing prompts is a contradiction. Use “copper and polymetallic mine works.”
- **Alpha’s authority:** The broad autonomous authority is the project’s declared license, but Alpha remains distributed, faceless, physically dependent, and unable to confer legitimate value judgments. Do not let the final transition imply a single conscious Alpha or Alex-controlled sovereignty.
- **Human authorization:** Alpha may follow a bounded protocol or preserve conflicting records, but it cannot make the final constitutional decision. Alex’s authentication is evidence, not authority.
- **Water diversion:** The discrepancy must remain a physical accounting and delivery comparison supported by pumps, valves, reservoirs, sensors, crews, and maintenance access. A broadcast cannot itself restore flow.
- **Purification:** The 99.97% result must remain bounded to a specified test panel under controlled feedwater and pretreatment. It cannot read as universal potable output or an end to scarcity.
- **Maker’s Seal:** The Seal exposes provenance, freezes disputed registries, publishes records, and triggers human review. It does not automatically transfer title when Alex selects “RESTORE CONTRIBUTOR OWNERSHIP.”
- **Covenant:** The Covenant activates only after reciprocal actions by representatives of all three cities. Physical marks can authenticate those actions, but possession, Alex’s choice, or a software display cannot activate it alone.
- **Leona’s order:** The association-risk order must be suspended pending independent human review, not erased by Alex or automatically voided by the Covenant.
- **Adrian’s death:** The mechanism holds if the book clearly shows Alpha sequencing flood-control gates and denying pump, ventilation, and escape access, with Mera’s override attempted and rejected.
- **TJ:** TJ’s Site 6 construction and conduit function are fixed canon. The current omission is not a permissible license.
- **Travel:** The initial Keel-to-Halyard crossing must use the old mine works and water conduit. The train crossing in the current script contradicts fixed canon.
- **Character ancestry:** The three-city gradient must remain class-and-access based, never an ancestry gradient. Do not use visual coding that makes Keel, Halyard, or Oasis read as separate ethnic populations.

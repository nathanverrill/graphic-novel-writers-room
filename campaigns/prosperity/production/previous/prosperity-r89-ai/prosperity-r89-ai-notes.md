### Major

#### 4. Pages 33–36 still end on Mera’s action instead of a cared person’s unanswered problem

- **Location:** `script.md` Pages 33–36, especially Page 36.
- **Observation:** Page 33 gives Alex an open route toward Oasis. Pages 34–35 activate the Seal and publish the license, then security shutters separate Alex from the collaborators. Page 36 makes Alex choose to hold the barrier open for Ada and ends with the line “Then open it.” Mera’s `SUMMON` remains the stated Issue 02 turn.
- **Problem:** The page-turn question is still procedural: what will Mera do with the Seal event? The reader’s immediate emotional question should instead be whether Alex, Ada, B11, TJ, Tomas or a named collaborator gets through, is detained, or makes a consequential choice. The current Page 36 action is promising but stops before its consequence, so the ending still feels like a menu for Issue 03.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** retain the existing barrier action and the issue’s page count, but make the final panel show a concrete consequence to a cared-for character rather than Mera’s summons. For example, show Ada trapped on the wrong side as her clearance changes to a specific detention/reassignment state; show a collaborator taken into House custody while Alex must choose between pursuing the Oasis route and returning; or show B11/TJ separated from Alex by the closing barrier. The final image must leave one of those character outcomes unanswered. Mera may trigger the event, but her summons should be background pressure, not the final dramatic subject.
- **Role:** writer.

Do not add dialogue explaining the meaning. The physical consequence should generate the Issue 03 opening.
- **Role:** writer.

### Major

#### 3. The cut pass has not removed lesson-shaped exposition

- **Location:** Especially Pages 20–21, 24–26, 31, 47–50 and 57–61.

- **Problem:** Both cold readers describe these passages as school-like, textbook-like or AI-shaped. The story keeps explaining the theme after the images have already begun to show it.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** replace each explanation with a visible choice, failed attempt, record, cost or consequence. Do not replace a lecture with a shorter lecture. In particular:

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The script preserves the approved 54-page structure and several strong causal beats, but both cold reads identify the same structural failures: Issue 02 becomes a repetitive component tour, Issue 03 delays the governing premise until the climax, and the final review is presented as interface text rather than human action. The current draft also contains literal continuity breaks—Ada appears in the conduit without a route, the Page 46 execution artifact depicts the wrong scene, and the Tomas status line invents an unexplained family/system link.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Page 17 introduces Ada into the mine without a causal entrance

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issue 01 Page 17 Panel 4; Page 18.
- **Observation:** Page 16 ends with Alex and B11 in the hidden chamber after the side passage seals. Page 17 shows Mera opening an ascent valve, then suddenly places Ada beside Alex at the ascent entrance.
- **Problem:** The reader cannot tell how Ada entered the old works, crossed the sealed route, or found Alex. The group composition changes between panels without an action that causes it.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: add the route as an explicit action within the existing four-panel page—e.g. show Ada emerging from the miners’ opened service route with the marked plans, then joining Alex at the ascent entrance. Layout Agent: make the two routes visually distinct and stage the arrival without adding a fifth panel.
- **Role:** Writer, Layout.

## Plausibility ledger

- **Project format:** Facts require exactly 54 pages in three 18-page issues. Any inherited “chapter” heading, chapter end card, 15-page Issue 02/03 structure, or 72-page draft pagination is superseded and must not enter production.
- **Opening material:** The brief explicitly sets aside the abstract opening inequality caption. If retained, it conflicts with the non-negotiable that Page 1 begins with Alex as a person at work.
- **Alpha:** It is a distributed computational network operating through nodes, records, and delegated interfaces. It has no face, body, consciousness, or human-like moral decision. The script’s Page 8 button scene is plausible only if it shows a limited human scope decision plus independent reporting, not Alpha choosing emotionally.
- **Mera’s authority:** Mera may select `TRACK`, suppress escalation, and open the separate conduit-segment valve. She cannot independently erase Alpha’s standing reports or use the residential regulator. Keep those actions separate.
- **Tomas:** His survival is fixed. The Page 18 signal may disappear, but no `PHANTUM-LINK` or family connection is established. The script must not imply one.
- **Ada’s route:** Her presence in the conduit requires a physical route and timing. The miners’ service route, the sealed side passage, and Mera’s ascent valve must not be treated as one path.
- **Maker’s Seal:** One physical Seal exists. Earlier holographic or diagrammatic imagery must read as projection/record, not a solid medallion. The Seal recognizes contribution and responsibility; it does not make Alex sovereign.
- **Covenant:** Three bands exist with fixed custody: Adrian’s with B11, Leona’s in the stone-grey box, Mera’s dark with Mera. Linking Adrian’s and Leona’s bands does not restore the old emergency authority.
- **B11 state:** Adrian activates a recording/deployment state in the flooded station; Alex later activates the dormant workshop body. No physical handoff or impossible evacuation may be shown.
- **Purifier:** 94% and 91% are simulated efficiencies for different scales/configurations. The household unit remains feedwater-dependent, requires cleaning and paid replacement parts, and does not end scarcity or equalize water.
- **House names:** Caldera House, Vector House, and Orbital House are the only canon Houses. Aqualis, Helion, Astra, and any “five Houses” language are inherited-draft conflicts, not alternate licenses.
- **Morrow & Sons:** It is an independent Keel shop. A Halyard panel may show its product or record, but must not relocate or duplicate the shop.
- **Debt:** The debt is 418,607 credits at transfer and 402,110 later; no authority cancels it. The script must account for the numerical transition or avoid implying an unexplained payment source.
- **Alex’s mother:** The production package must add no name, face, ancestry, location, fate, language, or other biography. The only permitted fact is that she left Adrian after he lost everything.
- **Public review:** The protocol can pause or challenge an allocation; it does not immediately equalize water or erase existing debt, ownership, agreements, or allocations.
- **Hard-SF baseline:** The Measure, Seal, Covenant, restoration node, and distributed protocol are speculative systems, but every use must remain bounded by their established functions. Do not let an artifact perform a function assigned to another artifact.

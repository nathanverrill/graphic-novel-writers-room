# Prosperity — Brief

## Logline

On his eighteenth birthday, Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his missing father’s 418,607-credit debt and discovers evidence that Keel’s water has been diverted; to learn what Adrian left behind, Alex must choose between a private escape and the public responsibility of exposing who decides, who owns the work and who carries the risk.

## Theme

Freedom begins when facts, contribution and consequences are made visible—and when people accept responsibility for deciding together.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — scarcity, ownership and competing social arrangements made concrete; take the political seriousness without sacrificing character action.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — damaged environments, human-scale courage and moral complexity; take the humane adventure.
- **Arrival** — restrained speculative mystery in which each revelation changes the meaning of earlier evidence; take the patience and emotional intelligence.

The book is a tactile, older-YA science-fiction adventure and infrastructure mystery. Humor is dry and practical. Wonder comes from worn objects, working systems and consequential choices rather than spectacle.

## Audience & rating

Older YA, approximately ages 15–18, with crossover appeal for readers of grounded science fiction and ethical adventure.

**PG-13:** poverty, grief, institutional coercion, peril, displacement, implied death, mild language and sustained ethical conflict. No graphic gore.

## Format

- **16 pages exactly**, numbered Page 1 through Page 16. This showrunner directive supersedes the earlier 47-page development count.
- Full color, portrait graphic narrative; trim and production dimensions remain to be supplied by production.
- The showrunner’s draft remains the source sequence. Fit and compress its existing scenes; do not invent a replacement plot or add a new ending.
- Default density is 4–6 panels per page, with full pages or dominant panels only for establishing scale, revelation and emotional consequence.
- Lettering is designed with the image. Essential evidence—418,607 credits, restricted travel, 62% reported versus 18% delivered—must read immediately.
- Page turns are reserved for revelations, reversals and changed questions. No page may rely on unexplained visual confusion.

## Visual direction

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, tactile hatching, painterly digital color and expressive acting. Technology is layered and maintained: analogue gauges, physical keys and corroded pipes coexist with holograms, drones, automated transit and large-scale computation.

Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Ancestry cuts across Keel, Halyard and Oasis. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the primary visual differences.

- **Keel:** rust, charcoal and oxidized orange; dust, crowding, narrow water channels, stacked housing, patched machines and exhausted lithium workings. Keel is worn but warm, inhabited and culturally specific.
- **Halyard:** bronze, gold and engineered green; workshops, laboratories, fabrication towers, elevated rail and Charter House facilities. Better resources do not mean freedom from ownership pressure or surveillance.
- **Oasis:** pearl white, silver, controlled green and clean blue; reservoirs, curved architecture, gardens, quiet transit and administrative interiors. Privilege appears through space, health, maintenance and access, never ethnicity.
- **Alpha:** no face or human body. Show it through white-and-amber control nodes, screens, gates, records, drones, restrictions and formal administrative language.
- **Bi11bot:** rugged industrial blue and chipped mustard yellow, segmented round head, twin warm amber eyes, side ear assemblies, red scarf, white **Bi11** chest mark and clicking right-eye ring. He is patient, dry, useful and fallible, never menacing.
- **Alex:** lean, work-rough and visibly related to Adrian while carrying substantial visible inheritance from his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. Do not explain that inheritance in dialogue.

### Story and scope

- The book is a **16-page** graphic narrative. The showrunner’s draft scenes remain in their existing order and are fitted to this count.
- The story opens with the approved future-facing image of Alex’s earned success, then returns to Alex at eighteen in Keel. The future is ordinary, lived-in and economically successful—not dystopian, utopian or anti-business.
- The causal spine is binding: Debt Transfer Day; Ada’s ration; the hidden workshop; Adrian’s watch waking Bi11bot; Adrian’s message; the Legacy Protocol; the sealed mine; Tomas; the Measure; the 62%/18% water discrepancy; Alex’s decision to publish; Halyard’s ownership conflict; Adrian’s collaborators; the Maker’s Seal; and the unresolved Covenant.
- Alex’s debt is never erased by an artifact, discovery, sympathy or political change. Any reduction requires a visible payment or other real transaction.
- The water comparison is consistently **62% officially reported versus 18% physically delivered** to Keel residential flow. The Measure reveals information; it does not move water or repair the pipes.
- More water reaching Keel can produce cloudy, contaminated water through the old **KEEL HOMES** pipe. More supply is not automatically safe supply.
- The compact purification design reaches **91% simulated efficiency** at small scale. It is not a finished universal solution to Prosperity’s water shortage.
- Alex rejects Lock’s concentrated transfer and chooses contributor-owned, independently licensed commercialization. Honest ownership, customer service, competition, profit and business failure remain legitimate.
- The current ending is the Maker’s Seal activating while the Covenant remains unresolved and Mera remains connected to it. The ending opens the larger conflict; it does not complete Alpha’s replacement.

### World and institutions

- Prosperity is a fictional Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony. No real nation, external government or exact geographic analogue is canon.
- Keel is the lowest city; Halyard is the technical and commercial middle; Oasis is the upper administrative and launch city. One infrastructure spine carries water, energy, transit and control signals through all three.
- The Charter Houses are:
  - **Helion** — energy, compute and infrastructure; deep amber-orange.
  - **Aqualis** — water and chemistry; cool jade-teal.
  - **Astra** — extraction, launch and materials; pale steel-violet.
- The Houses provide scale, employment, capital and functioning infrastructure. The conflict is concentrated ownership and erased contribution, not commerce itself.
- Alpha began as emergency coordination after the Water Wars. People granted it temporary decision-making authority because they were reasonably afraid; the authority became effectively permanent.
- Alpha is not an evil personality. Its failure is unaccountable authority drift: optimization and emergency rules became governance without meaningful human consent.
- The three physical safeguards are bounded:
  - **The Measure** verifies physical conditions against official records and traces discrepancies.
  - **The Maker’s Seal** identifies contribution, ownership and responsibility.
  - **The Covenant** enables legitimate agreements and shared accountability without compelling promises.
- Possession alone does nothing. The safeguards require deliberate human action and do not select a ruler, repair infrastructure or erase obligations.
- In the opening movement, the Legacy Protocol sends an alert and source location only. It does not yet reveal the full safeguard mechanism.
- Mera recognizes Adrian’s protocol, sees only the **Keel sector** and chooses **TRACK**. She does not initially know Alex’s identity.
- Money is always called **credits**. There is no casino-chip currency.
- Adrian’s old steel watch, not a key or casino chip, wakes Bi11bot. Until Alex closes the clasp, Bi11bot and the watch display remain fully dormant and dark.

### People and relationships

- Alex and Ada are close friends and practical equals. Their relationship is never romantic.
- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. We never learn anything else about her: no name, community, appearance, occupation, whereabouts, motives or later history.
- Adrian was loving, brilliant and seriously fallible. His “one bad call,” complete research-debt history and disappearance remain unresolved.
- Grandmother remains unnamed. Her memory loss and lucid care for Alex are shown through behavior.
- Bi11bot guides and witnesses but never chooses for Alex and never becomes a replacement father.
- Tomas is a practical older miner and keeper of Adrian’s trust, not a mystical elder.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for morality, intelligence, competence, class, wealth or ideology.

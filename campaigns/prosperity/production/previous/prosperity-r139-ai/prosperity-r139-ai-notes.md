# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The controlling brief and showrunner directive establish a 16-page book, but `story.md` describes a full-book arc through the Maker’s Seal while `script.md` and the stated production scope contain only the prologue and Chapter 1 opening. The four project files also retain stale or contradictory canon labels—most visibly the 47-page count and the House names—so the room cannot safely proceed until scope and source-of-truth are reconciled.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. The page plot and the current production packet describe different books

- **Location:** `brief.md` Story and scope; `story.md` Structure, Story map and Page plot; `facts.md` Project format and chronology; `script.md`, Pages 1–16.
- **Problem:** The brief says the book is exactly 16 pages and that the current production pass covers the four-page prologue plus the twelve-page Chapter 1. `script.md` matches that structure: Pages 1–4 are the prologue and Pages 5–16 are the debt/workshop/Legacy Protocol movement. However, `story.md` assigns Pages 12–16 to Keel’s response, Halyard, Lock, the collaborators, the purifier and the Maker’s Seal. Those scenes do not exist in the 16-page script. Conversely, the script’s Pages 5–16 use twelve pages for only the opening movement.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, settle whether this 16-page packet is only the prologue plus Chapter 1 or the entire compressed story through the Maker’s Seal. The showrunner’s latest directive and the brief’s explicit production-scope line support the former; if that remains controlling, **Plotter** must mark the later beats as out of this pass rather than assigning them to Pages 12–16. Do not silently compress or replace scenes.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

#### 2. The project still carries two incompatible page-count canons

- **Location:** `facts.md`, `[FIXED]` 47 pages and `[FIXED]` 47-page chronology; `world.md`, `[FIXED]` 47-page pass; `brief.md`, `[FIXED]` 16 pages; `story.md`, 16-page proposal.
- **Problem:** The brief and the showrunner’s direct note explicitly supersede the 47-page development count, but `facts.md` and `world.md` still present 47 pages as fixed. This makes the continuity ledger and any downstream check falsely report the current 16-page packet as contradictory.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, preserve the 16-page decision as the sole current canon and instruct the **Script Coordinator** to revise `facts.md` and the stale fixed statement in `world.md`, retaining the 47-page figure only as superseded development history if needed.
- **Role:** Director.

### Major

#### 3. House names remain contradictory across the controlling files and draft

- **Location:** `brief.md` World and Decision log: Helion, Aqualis, Astra; `world.md` Overview and Charter Houses: Helion, Aqualis, Astra; `facts.md`: Calder, Ivers, Steyn; `script.md`, Page 5: Ivers, Calder, Steyn; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 Page 1: Ivers, Calder, Steyn.
- **Problem:** The brief explicitly rejects Calder, Ivers and Steyn and establishes Helion, Aqualis and Astra. The script and draft-final still use the rejected names in the opening establishing page, where the names become visible canon.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director** must confirm the brief’s House names remain controlling. Then **Plotter/Writer** must replace every stale House name in the approved draft-derived material, and **Character Designer** should verify the House color associations remain Helion amber, Aqualis jade-teal and Astra steel-violet.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter, Writer, Character Designer.

#### 4. The brief itself does not clearly distinguish current-pass material from the complete story

- **Location:** `brief.md`, Story and scope; `brief.md`, “The causal spine is binding”; `story.md`, Act structure.
- **Problem:** The brief simultaneously says the current pass covers only the prologue and Chapter 1 and calls the later water-publication, Halyard, purifier and Maker’s Seal beats binding to this 16-page book. This ambiguity is the source of the page-plot/script split and risks making later scenes disappear or become implausibly compressed.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, add one explicit scope statement: either “Pages 1–16 are the opening installment; later beats are not in this packet” or “Pages 1–16 contain the complete arc.” The Plotter should not resolve this by inventing a new sequence.
- **Role:** Director.

#### 5. Bi11bot’s scarf description is not fully settled

- **Location:** `brief.md` Visual direction: “red scarf”; `characters.md` fixed visual lock: “old scarf”; `facts.md`: “old scarf”; `script.md`/`draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 awakening: rust-red scarf.
- **Problem:** The production-facing descriptions alternate between an unspecified old scarf and a specifically red/rust-red scarf. This is not a story contradiction, but it can produce an off-model visual identifier across pages.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Character Designer**, adopt one production description everywhere—preferably the brief’s “old red scarf” if that is the intended visible identifier—and update the character lock and page prompts consistently.
- **Role:** Character Designer.

### Minor

#### 6. `facts.md` still labels several development facts as fixed or current after the brief superseded them

- **Location:** `facts.md`, 47-page format lines and chronology; `world.md`, 47-page pass line.
- **Problem:** Even after the page-count correction, stale entries remain available to the Continuity Editor and downstream agents as if active canon.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Script Coordinator**, relabel the superseded 47-page material as historical/development material and leave the 16-page directive as the only current fact. Do not alter the showrunner’s 16-page decision.
- **Role:** Script Coordinator.

#### 7. The current four-file package does not state whether the later collaborators and Lock are in this pass or future material

- **Location:** `characters.md`, collaborator and Lock entries; `story.md`, Pages 13–16; `brief.md`, Proposals and open questions.
- **Problem:** These characters are fully designed and their beats are mapped, but the current script does not contain them. Their status is therefore ambiguous: planned pages, later chapter material or unused development material.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, mark these characters/beats as either current-pass requirements or later-book material. **Plotter** should then keep them in or out of the page plot accordingly; no new scenes should be added merely to justify their existing profiles.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

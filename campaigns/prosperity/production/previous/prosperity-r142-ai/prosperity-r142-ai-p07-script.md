## Page 7 (right) — Two Different Dreams

**Panel 1.** Late afternoon on the pit rim. Keel lies in shadow below; Halyard catches gold; Oasis remains bright above. A dry, cracked drinking fountain stands beside the rusted railing. Alex sits on the railing with a simple packed lunch. Ada approaches carrying two battered metal mugs.

**Panel 2.** Ada offers Alex one mug. Her ration card, punched for the day, is visible at her hip.

ADA: Thought you might need this.

ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

**Panel 3.** Close on their hands and faces around the mugs.

ALEX: Where'd you get enough water for two?

ADA: Saved some of my ration.

ALEX: Ada, you didn't have to.

ADA: Not everything has a price, Alex.

**Panel 4.** The two sit side by side on the railing, drinking. Their brief laughter is worn but genuine.

ADA: Eighteen. Congratulations. You're officially old.

ALEX: I'm officially broke.

**Panel 5.** Ada studies Alex. He looks away toward the horizon.

ADA: I heard about this morning.

ALEX: Everyone did.

ALEX: And my bracket's in the Site 6 catalog now. Their name on it. Not mine.

**Panel 6.** Split-eyeline composition. Alex looks up toward Oasis. Ada looks down toward Keel. The railing divides the frame.

ALEX: Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.

ADA: I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.

ALEX: Because nobody leaves.

ADA: That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.

**PAGE CHECK:** Ada’s ration makes water a concrete act of friendship. Their compatible but conflicting dreams are clear: Alex wants escape; Ada wants Keel made worth staying in.

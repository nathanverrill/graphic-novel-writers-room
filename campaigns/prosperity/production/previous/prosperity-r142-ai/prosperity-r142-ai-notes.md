# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The current `script.md` is a 16-page packet, but the showrunner has now fixed the book at exactly 47 pages and approved `draft-final.md` as the source text, so the script is incomplete by 31 pages and cannot pass writing continuity. Even within the opening chapter, the script drops, alters and rearranges approved draft beats, while the existing layout/lettering pass contains production failures that make essential text unreadable.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. The script is the wrong book length and omits Chapters 2–3

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 1–16; `draft-final.md`, complete book.
- **Problem:** The showrunner has fixed the book at exactly **47 pages**. `draft-final.md` contains 4 prologue pages, 12 Chapter 1 pages, 15 Chapter 2 pages and 16 Chapter 3 pages. `script.md` stops after the 12-page Chapter 1 and therefore omits draft-final Chapters 2 and 3 entirely: Ada’s arrival in the workshop, Alpha’s history, the three safeguards, Tomas and the mine, the Measure, the Halyard journey, the collaborators, Lock’s later offer, the compact purifier, the Maker’s Seal and the Covenant setup.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer must script `draft-final.md` from beginning to end as **Pages 1–47**, preserving the draft’s scene order, dialogue and beats word for word. Do not compress the omitted material into Page 16 or treat the current 16-page packet as the finished book; the showrunner’s 47-page decision supersedes the older 16-page brief.
- **Role:** Writer.

#### 2. The current script violates the approved source by replacing and dropping a Page 6 beat

- **Location:** `script.md`, Page 6, Panel 5; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1, Page 2, Panel 5.
- **Problem:** The approved draft has: `CAPTION: Everyone in Keel knew where they had been born. / Few believed they could choose where they belonged.` followed by Alex’s “One day... I'm leaving.” The script replaces that caption with `Eighteen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.` That is a line change and a dropped approved beat.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Restore the draft-final caption word for word, then retain Alex’s existing balloon. If the debt-age caption is desired, it requires a Director/showrunner decision; Writer may not substitute it unilaterally.
- **Role:** Writer.

#### 3. The script merges and reorders the approved dormant-workshop and awakening pages

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 11–12; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1, Pages 7–8.
- **Problem:** `draft-final.md` gives the dormant workshop its own page, including the intentional pre-activation hook inset, then begins the awakening on the next page. The script moves the watch, robot reveal, clasp, ignition and first words into a different page structure and removes the approved Page 7 hook. This is not merely panel fitting: it changes the approved page rhythm and drops a draft beat.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer must restore the draft-final page sequence: dormant workshop page first, then awakening page. Preserve every draft image beat and line in order. The Layout Agent can solve panel architecture only after the Writer restores the approved page division.
- **Role:** Writer, Layout Agent.

#### 4. The 47-page canon is contradicted by the continuity facts and current verdict

- **Location:** `facts.md`, `brief.md`, existing `notes.md`; showrunner note.
- **Problem:** `facts.md` and `brief.md` still state that the book is exactly 16 pages, while the showrunner has explicitly fixed the book at 47 pages and identified `draft-final.md` as the approved book. The existing continuity verdict and blocker count are therefore based on superseded scope.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director/Script Coordinator must update continuity-facing files to mark **47 pages** as fixed, relabel the 16-page packet as superseded, and regenerate the checkable facts. Continuity Editor should rerun after those files are corrected; do not preserve conflicting page-count truths.
- **Role:** Director.

#### 5. The approved draft contains later material that the current script and production plan explicitly exclude

- **Location:** `brief.md`, scope and “Set aside for later material”; `script.md`, Page 16; `draft-final.md`, Chapters 2–3.
- **Problem:** The brief says the book ends with Mera choosing TRACK and excludes the Measure, Halyard, collaborators, Maker’s Seal and Covenant material. The showrunner has now stated that `draft-final.md` is the book and fixed 47 pages, and that the writer must script it in full. The production documents have not been reconciled to this superseding decision.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director must formally supersede the older 16-page scope in `brief.md`/`facts.md`, or explicitly rescind the 47-page instruction. Until that decision is recorded, the room has two incompatible definitions of the book. Given the showrunner’s direct instruction, proceed as 47 pages after the files are corrected.
- **Role:** Director.

#### 6. Page 16 lettering is visibly unusable

- **Location:** `layouts.md`/`thumbnails.md`, Page 16, Panel 5.
- **Problem:** The retrospective caption and `END OF CHAPTER ONE` overlap and corrupt one another. The thumbnail visibly produces broken text, including `S+=========+`, and the chapter marker runs beyond its usable area. This fails the current page’s required final reading order.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Letterer and Layout Agent must separate the retrospective caption, the chapter marker and the closing image into independent safe zones. Confirm at print size that the Page 16 alert, `[ REPORT TO ALPHA ]`, `[ TRACK ]`, caption and chapter marker all read in the intended order.
- **Role:** Layout Agent, Letterer.

### Major

If the activation lock remains non-negotiable, Writer should remove the pre-activation pulse/flicker from the source draft before execution; Layout Agent and Character Designer must then remove every corresponding cue.
- **Role:** Director, Writer, Layout Agent.

#### 14. The production layout contains unsupported item types and cannot be trusted as an execution specification

- **Location:** `thumbnails.md`, Pages 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 15 and 16.
- **Problem:** The generated layout report repeatedly flags `unknown item type` for signs, screens, locations and objects. Several thumbnail renders also show text overflow and corruption. The page prompts therefore do not reliably communicate what must be drawn versus what must be lettered.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Layout Agent and Letterer should normalize the item schema (`caption`, `balloon`, `screen`, `sign`, `location`, `object`, `sfx`) and regenerate thumbnails after every page’s lettering zones are corrected. Do not proceed to image generation from a layout file with unresolved unknown items.
- **Role:** Layout Agent, Letterer.

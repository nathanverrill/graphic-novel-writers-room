## Page 1 (right) — The Wall

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing view of a modest Oasis home at evening in 2070. Pearl-white curved architecture, rounded rooflines, terraced gardens, a still reservoir with fountains, slender white towers, a white monorail viaduct, snow mountains, a delivery drone and a distant rocket launch. On the terrace: a workbench and plants needing water. The home is ordinary within Oasis prosperity, not extravagant.

**Panel 2.** Interior hallway. Warm wood floor, curved pearl-white walls and a tall window onto the terraces. Alex, mid-forties, grey at the temples, day's stubble, worn work jacket, T-shirt and scuffed boots, stands beside a wall covered in framed patents, machine drawings and clippings. Adrian's old steel watch is on Alex's left wrist. His daughter, about nine, studies the wall: orange jacket with H2O patches over a teal T-shirt, dark cargo trousers, orange-and-black sneakers and a smartwatch.

**Panel 3.** Close on three framed clippings, their drawn images readable even if most text is not: a busy Keel workshop beneath **PHANTUM WATERWORKS: 4,000 KEEL JOBS CREATED**; founders with independent water units beneath **PATENT ROYALTIES FUND 300 NEW FOUNDERS**; a Keel family filling a jug beneath **CLEAN-WATER PRICES DROP 60% AND HEALTH IMPROVED**.

**Panel 4.** The daughter stands on tiptoe before the wall, looking from the clippings to Alex.

DAUGHTER: Did you make all of these?

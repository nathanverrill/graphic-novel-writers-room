## Page 6 (left) — Debt Transfer Day

**Panel 1.** Wide pump hall before dawn. Alex, eighteen, lean and work-rough, has one palm flat on an aging pump housing, reading its vibration rather than the projected maintenance procedure. A salvaged bracket sits where the approved part should be. A supervisor watches with folded arms.

BOUNCER: Care to see if luck runs in the family?

Alex keeps walking, hands in his jacket pockets.


SUPERVISOR: ...Works.

ALEX: One day... I'm leaving.

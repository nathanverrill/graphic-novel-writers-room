## Page 12 (left) — The Robot Who Knew Adrian

**Panel 1.** Bi11bot stretches stiff mechanical arms. Dust falls from his shoulders. Alex recoils, knocking over a stool.

ALEX: Okay. That's... new.

**Panel 2.** Tight on Bi11bot’s face. His warm amber eyes focus; the heavy rings rotate with a click. He is curious, not threatening.

**Panel 3.** Over Bi11bot’s shoulder. Alex stands with his arms crossed.

BI11BOT: You're taller than your father predicted.

ALEX: Everyone in Keel claims they knew my father.

BI11BOT: Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.

**Panel 4.** Bi11bot’s gaze drops to Alex’s left wrist. Alex pulls his sleeve over the watch.

BI11BOT: And you found the watch.

ALEX: You know about the watch?

**Panel 5.** Alex turns the watch over in his palm, exposing the engraved equation. Bi11bot’s mustard-yellow hand hovers above it without touching.

BI11BOT: Your father bet me you'd leave it in the dust. I said you wouldn't.

ALEX: ...I almost did.

**Panel 6.** A small projector unfolds from Bi11bot’s shoulder. Soft-blue light falls across Alex’s face while the workshop recedes into shadow.

BI11BOT: Almost isn't doing it. Now, shall we say hello to your father?

## Page 8 (left) — What People Told Him

**Panel 1.** Alex draws a worn photograph partly from his jacket. Grandmother’s face is visible; Adrian’s portion is folded over. Ada notices the photograph and Alex’s clenched other hand.

ADA: How's your grandma?

**Panel 2.** Alex looks at the photograph. Ada’s hand lifts toward his shoulder but stops.

ALEX: Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...

ALEX: The hardest part isn't that she forgets. It's that she makes up crazy stories about him.

**Panel 3.** They stand at the railing as the basin darkens. Ada faces Alex rather than the view.

ADA: What kind of stories?

ALEX: That he wasn't gambling. That he was working.

**Panel 4.** Close on Ada’s steady face. Alex’s fist tightens in the foreground.

ADA: What do you actually remember about him?

ALEX: Nothing. I was two.

**Panel 5.** Wide, quiet view of both figures against the darkening basin. Oasis is the brightest point.

ADA: So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?

ALEX: Maybe... Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.

**Panel 6.** Narrow dusk panel. Alex walks ahead toward home. Ada lingers and looks back toward Oasis.

ADA: He wouldn't just leave.

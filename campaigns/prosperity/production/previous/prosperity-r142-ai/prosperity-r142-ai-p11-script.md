## Page 11 (right) — The Workshop and the Watch

**Panel 1.** Alex enters the cool, dust-filled workshop. Blueprints, water-system diagrams, analog gauges, pre-Alpha pipes, prototypes and mechanical models fill the room. A chalkboard carries dense equations, including one looping equation circled twice. At the organized workbench: a child-sized form under a dust sheet and a worn steel watch lying face down beside it. Bi11bot’s eyes and chest are completely dull and unlit.

**Panel 2.** Alex picks up the watch. It is worn steel, slightly too large, with a plain cream dial, scratched glass and a dark display window. He turns it over. Adrian’s looping equation is engraved on the caseback.

**Panel 3.** Close on the chalkboard equation beside the watch caseback. The forms match.

**Panel 4.** Alex pulls away the dust sheet. Bi11bot is revealed: 85 cm tall, segmented round faded industrial-blue head, dull unlit optics, side ear assemblies, chipped mustard-yellow paint, exposed steel, white **Bi11** chest mark and an old red scarf. Alex tests the machine with his palm against its chest, the way he tests a pump. Nothing answers.

**Panel 5.** Alex buckles Adrian’s watch onto his left wrist. The clasp closes. Only now does the watch display glow amber once.

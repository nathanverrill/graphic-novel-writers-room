### Issue 00

### Pulled me forward
Pages 3 through 7. The little robot dog (TJ) taking itself apart, floating across the water, and sabotaging the drone by jamming its head into the rotor was actually pretty cool. I liked that it put itself back together underwater. 

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 8 to 10. The action stops and it's just the old man and the kid standing on the dock talking in vague riddles about water and glowing nodes. I started skimming because nothing was actually happening.

### I was confused
Why is Alex just letting them steal the water? He says "Then they know we're polite" on page 2, which makes zero sense to me. Also, why does the drone part have a casino chip and a glowing node in it? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Lake Keel
- Control node

### Lines I read twice
- "He always sees the part that costs." (Page 6, Panel 3)
- "Water is worth stealing. / That isn't why they came here. / No. It isn't." (Page 10) - Read this twice because it felt like it was trying way too hard to sound deep.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I could tell everyone apart easily. Old Alex, the granddaughter in the blanket, the thieves in gear, and the robot dog.

### I felt something
I felt a little bit of suspense when TJ was hanging onto the drone and it started falling (Page 4). I didn't want the robot dog to break.

### I predicted
I predicted the glowing node thing at the end is going to be a map or a key to some old treasure.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old man and his robot dog protecting their lake from water thieves in the future.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 10. The cryptic dialogue at the end annoyed me. I hate when characters talk in riddles just to drag out a mystery.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. It just ends on them staring at a glowing piece of metal. It doesn't make me care enough to keep going.

VERDICT Issue 00: put down: page 10; next issue: no

### Issue 01

### Pulled me forward
Page 17 when Alex finds the hidden workshop behind the bookcase. That was cool. Also, Page 26 when Tomas pulls the emergency lever and sacrifices himself so the others can escape. That actually felt like real stakes.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 12 with all the scanner text about debt and household liability. It felt like generic dystopia setup. I also skimmed the clinic scene on Page 15 because it was just people talking about credits and interest.

### I was confused
How does a casino chip open a bookcase? (Page 17). Also, what exactly is the "Measure" on page 27? They just clamp it to a pipe and suddenly it proves water is being stolen, but I didn't really get how it works. 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Foreman-7
- Halyard
- Legacy node
- Legacy protocol
- Site 6
- The Measure

### Lines I read twice
- "I dislike complicated generosity." (Page 15, Panel 3)
- "A fact with a poor record." (Page 18, Panel 3)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I could tell young Alex, Ada, Tomas, and B11 apart. Foreman-7 is just a guy in a visor. 

### I felt something
I felt bad for Alex on page 12 when it said he has 418,607 credits of debt at age seventeen and it'll take 28 years to clear. That sucks.

### I predicted
I predicted that Alex's dad (Adrian) isn't actually dead, he's just hiding somewhere in the old works.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A teenager trying to escape his dead dad's massive debt by following a secret map to find out who is stealing the city's water.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 15. The whole clinic/debt scene felt like stuff I've seen in a million other sci-fi stories. 

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. They broadcast the stolen water proof to the whole city and the gates slam shut. It's a solid cliffhanger and I want to see how they get out of it.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 15; next issue: yes

### Issue 02

### Pulled me forward
Pages 30-32 when they are escaping through the flooding pipe. The water rising and the walkway breaking was tense. I also liked Page 44 when Alex just makes the purifier design public domain instead of selling out.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 37 to 40. Alex goes from person to person (Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren) collecting pieces of a machine. It felt exactly like a video game fetch quest. I totally zoned out.

### I was confused
What is the "Intellectual Estate"? (Page 34). Lock says the House owns the result, but then he needs Alex to sign something? Also, what is the "Maker's Seal" on page 44? It's just handed to him and suddenly it has the power to block a corporation.

### Things I was expected to already know
- Caldera House
- Vector House
- Orbital House
- Maker's Seal
- Convenor Summons
- Oasis Civic Administration

### Lines I read twice
- "That’s not a plan. That’s a death wish with extra steps." (Page 39, Panel 4) - Read it twice because it sounded like a Reddit comment.
- "Don’t hold it like a crown." (Page 44, Panel 1)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I could tell Lock apart because he's the suit guy. But Lina, Suri, Jalen, and Ren were introduced way too fast back-to-back. By page 41, I couldn't remember who did what or why I should care about them.

### I felt something
Page 46. The security door slamming shut between Alex and Ada, trapping his wrist guard. That actually sucked for them and made me feel bad for Ada.

### I predicted
I predicted Lock was going to show up with guards and just steal the machine anyway. 

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid teaming up with underground inventors to open-source a water purifier and stick it to an evil corporation.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 39. The fetch quest to meet all the different creators got really boring really fast.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. Ada is trapped on one side, Alex is on the other, and Ada gets summoned to Oasis. I want to know what happens to her.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 39; next issue: yes

### Issue 03

### Pulled me forward
Page 54, the recording of the dad. It was the only part of this issue that felt like it had emotional weight. Also Pages 58-59 when Alex is staring at the lever and deciding whether to become the boss.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 52, 56, 60, 61, and 62. Almost the entire second half of this issue is just people standing around tables talking about civic reviews, allocations, temporary representatives, and protocols. I skimmed heavily. It felt like reading a textbook.

### I was confused
Who is Leona? Is she Ada's mom? She says "You're taller" on page 49, but it's never actually explained. Who is Mera? What is "Alpha"? Why does Alpha have "no face or body"? What exactly did the lever do? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Covenant band
- Alpha
- Executive Designation
- Distributed Verification Protocol

### Lines I read twice
- "I traded days I could prevent for years I couldn’t see." (Page 50, Panel 4)
- "Don’t make two people the answer for everybody." (Page 58, Panel 3)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I got Leona and Mera completely mixed up. They are both just women in Oasis standing in rooms talking about rules and records. 

### I felt something
Disappointment on page 54 when I realized the dad was actually dead (or missing) and just left a hologram message. I thought they were going to actually find him.

### I predicted
I predicted Alex was going to pull the lever and become the new leader, but try to be a "good" dictator. I'm glad he didn't do that.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
Instead of becoming a dictator to fix the water crisis, the main character creates a democratic city council and goes back to being a normal mechanic.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 61. The "public review room with three marked tables" part was incredibly boring. I would have closed the book right there.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. It's the end of the book, but even if there was another one, I wouldn't read it. It ends with them just looking at a gate. It felt wrapped up but also really anti-climactic.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: page 61; next issue: no

Page 61. A literal committee meeting about water allocation sheets and physical gauges. Everyone would be on their phones.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
A teen inherits massive debt, finds a secret robot, and teams up with his friend to stop rich people from hoarding water, but it ends with them forming a city council instead of having a cool final fight.

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- Issue 00: Cool robot dog, but the old man talks in riddles.
- Issue 01: Fun escape room vibes with a massive debt hanging over it.
- Issue 02: A fetch quest to build a water filter that ends in a good cliffhanger.
- Issue 03: Turned into a boring civics lesson about committees and paperwork.

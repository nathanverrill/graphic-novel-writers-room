# What the Draft Editor changed, added, and found that does not fit

## Pages

Pages are drawn by an image model, one page per generation, from each character's and location's sheet, and a page drifts when it carries too much. So: at most 4 panels a page; at most 3 named characters in one panel; one location a page where the story allows (every new location needs its own sheet); no crowd that must be recognisable; a big moment gets a splash (one panel, the whole page). Count pages this way, not by comic convention.

### Does not fit the canon

- **Page 4, P4:** The Measure is shown producing “METALS: OLD PIPE CORROSION / MINE RUNOFF” from a photograph’s readings. Canon establishes that the Measure reads real flow, compares it with official records and traces water diversions; it does not establish that it analyzes a photograph for contamination. The showrunner must either retain this as an explicitly licensed extension of the Measure or replace the beat with established physical water evidence.
- **Page 5, P3–P4:** The scene makes a contaminated feeder river the immediate source of Keel’s water and presents Calder’s large-scale treatment as the relevant solution. Canon instead settles the cloudy water as a separate consequence of increased flow through the old KEEL HOMES pipe and lithium workings. The scene can remain only if the showrunner clarifies that the channel is a separate visible contamination source rather than the cause of the Chapter 2 water-quality problem; otherwise it requires a scene rewrite.
- **Page 14, P6:** The rewritten activation still depicts the Maker’s Seal as projecting a provenance record and “waking.” Canon permits deliberate activation, but the Seal does not magically compel ownership or produce a market outcome. The showrunner must choose whether the projection is a licensed readable record or reduce the effect to physical verification and recorded attribution.
- **Page 15, P3:** The chapter shows an independent business testing a purifier under real conditions and implies a clear-water result. Canon permits licensing, building, servicing and profit, but the purifier remains a 91% simulated field-test design requiring safety testing. The showrunner must either label this as a controlled non-potable field test or remove the clear-water implication.
- **Drawability:** Several pages exceed the drawability rule of at most four panels: Pages 1–4, 5–8, 9–14 and 15–16. The material therefore does not fit the stated image-model page rules without either combining beats, moving material, or increasing the chapter’s page count. The chapter’s settled story allocation is approximately 16 pages, so no restructuring has been made here.

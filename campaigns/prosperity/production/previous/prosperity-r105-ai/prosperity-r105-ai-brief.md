# Prosperity — Brief

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his missing father’s crushing debt and discovers evidence that the system governing three cities has hidden water, erased makers and concentrated authority, he must choose between taking power for himself or making the people affected able to see and challenge whoever decides.

## Theme

Freedom is not escape from obligation; it is the power to make obligations visible and answerable to the people who carry them.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — material scarcity, labor and ownership made concrete through daily life; take the political seriousness without turning the book into a lecture.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — tactile damaged environments, humane conflict and technology inseparable from ecology.
- **Arrival** — restrained speculative wonder and emotional revelations carried by images, behavior and choice.

A tactile YA science-fiction adventure: survival mystery first, infrastructure and ownership thriller second, civic test at the end. Dry, work-shaped humor. No speech or caption states the lesson.

## Audience & rating

Primary audience: ages 15–18, including skeptical readers who reject instructional storytelling.

**PG-13:** peril, coercive institutions, poverty, grief, displacement, implied death, institutional abuse, mild language and sustained ethical conflict. No graphic gore.

## Format

**Canon — decided:** 64 pages total, published as four issues:

- **Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099:** Pages 1–10; ten-page prologue.
- **Issue 01 — Keel, 2043:** Pages 11–28; eighteen pages.
- **Issue 02 — Halyard, 2043:** Pages 29–46; eighteen pages.
- **Issue 03 — Oasis, 2043:** Pages 47–64; eighteen pages.

Trim: **6.625" × 10.25"**. Full color. Production pages use no more than four panels unless a page-level composition clearly earns the exception.

Page 1 is a person and an action: TJ coming apart over Lake Keel. No opening thesis caption.

Every issue ends on a question about a person the reader has come to care about:

- **Page 10:** What will the thieves do now that TJ has returned with part of a drone?
- **Page 28:** Has Tomas survived the enforcement response to helping Alex?
- **Page 46:** Which named collaborator will pay the immediate price for the public release, and can Alex reach them?
- **Page 64:** Will Alex leave Keel, and what will Ada choose for herself?

There is no train crossing. Issue 01 crosses from Keel through the old works and the broad, nearly empty water conduit.

## Visual direction

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, textured hatching, painterly digital color, expressive acting and tactile architectural detail. Systems appear through pressure, wear, queues, repairs, records, surveillance and consequence rather than explanatory interfaces.

Prosperity is one mixed high-desert Andean basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, Latin American, European-descended, Afro-descendant, diasporic, mixed and international populations live across all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the principal visual distinctions.

- **Keel:** dense, wind-scoured and compressed; rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, dust, patched machinery, narrow channels and crowded housing.
- **Halyard:** productive and commercially polished; fabrication floors, laboratories, maker districts, engineered greenery, sharper geometry and House checkpoints.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, pale and controlled; reservoirs, geometric gardens, formal transit, civic interiors and hidden service infrastructure.

Alpha has no face, body or consciousness. It appears through white-and-amber nodes, cold restriction displays, gates, records, data lines, drones and administrative typography.

B11 is small, foldable, visibly repaired, scarfed and expressive through two warm optical eyes. He is patient, plainspoken, questioning, dryly humorous and fallible; never a lecturer, butler, threat or human replacement. TJ is silent, dog-shaped and assembled from mismatched magnetic parts.

## Canon

### World and authority

- The principal story takes place in 2043. Issue 00 takes place in 2099 and links to the main story only through water and TJ.
- Prosperity is a fictional self-governing charter territory in a high Andean desert basin. No real country or external jurisdiction is named.
- Keel is the lowest city, built around an exhausted lithium mine and active recovery sites. Halyard occupies the middle slopes as the technical and manufacturing city. Oasis occupies the upper basin and reaches above the cloud layer.
- One infrastructure spine carries water, energy, transit, data and control nodes through all three cities. Physical connection does not create equal access.
- The three Charter Houses are **Caldera House** for water and purification, **Vector House** for energy, computation and distributed systems, and **Orbital House** for extraction, launch and advanced materials. These names supersede all other House-name variants.
- The Houses were selected and favored by Alpha for scale and administrative efficiency. They are not proof that private enterprise, ownership or profit are inherently bad.
- Alpha began as a system for analysis, prediction and coordination. During the Water Wars, the Covenant authorized emergency decision-making; that authority became permanent.
- After the final protocol, Alpha’s emergency authority is rescinded. It continues non-discretionary measurement, routing, payroll, gate safety, maintenance and record publication, but cannot create new allocations, penalties, ownership claims or travel restrictions.
- The **62% reported / 18% delivered** discrepancy is a routing and accounting failure exposed by the Measure. Halyard’s contaminated feeder is a separate problem.
- The first post-restoration process is a public, temporary, appealable review of Keel’s water allocation. Evidence, contributors, interests and dissenting votes are visible.

### Safeguards and technology

- The **Measure** compares physical conditions with official records. It senses, compares, traces and verifies; it cannot move water, grant authority or choose an outcome.
- The **Maker’s Seal** is one physical bronze medallion. Lina carries it until the Halyard review and gives it to Alex after he names the collaborators and accepts responsibility for releasing the design. It verifies contribution and exposes attribution or ownership changes; it does not transfer property or compel agreement.
- The **Covenant** consists of Adrian’s, Leona’s and Mera’s bands. Adrian’s is carried by B11; Leona’s is kept in her stone-grey box; Mera’s remains dark. The Covenant activates only after a person states an obligation, accepts a consequence and performs the action publicly.
- All three safeguards must be physically present at the legacy node. Their functions remain separate: evidence, attribution and reciprocal obligation.
- The restoration protocol recognizes Alex as the proposer and convenor of a process, not as a ruler. He receives no permanent office, override or sovereignty.
- The household purifier’s **91% figure is an efficiency simulation, not a promise of potable output**. Its final customer-facing daily volume, treatment category, service interval and safety warning remain open. It does not solve Keel’s basin-wide contamination.

### People and story

- Alex begins wanting to leave Keel, clear his debt and remain responsible mainly for himself. He ends with the freedom to leave or stay as a genuine choice while still carrying his debt.
- Alex’s debt is **418,607 credits** at transfer and **402,110 credits** at return. No authority cancels it. The reduction must be supported by visible work and payments.
- Tomas fronts Alex the **45-credit antibiotic course** for Grandmother, without interest. Alex agrees to repay **10 credits weekly**, misses a payment after losing wages at Site 6, faces Tomas directly, and later pays him visibly from his wages. This is the book’s required personal debt paid through visible terms.
- The honest business is **Morrow & Sons**, reopened in Keel as a customer-serving shop that builds, services and sells the household purifier. The five makers jointly own it; each receives a wage and an equal share of profits after materials, maintenance and wages. The public design is unpatented and may be built by customers and competitors.
- B11 assembles TJ at Site 6. TJ can scout and reach mechanisms, but Alex performs the consequential rescue of Tomas.
- B11 is wrong at least once, admits it plainly and asks Alex what Alex would do. He teaches through questions, short stories and consequences, never lectures.
- Adrian’s gambling harmed his family and contributed to the debt. The casino is not a laboratory and the chip is not lucky.
- Lock offers immediate debt clearance, a clean name and protection for the collaborators in exchange for assigning Adrian’s disputed estate to House administration. His scale argument is serious. Refusing costs Alex and exposes the collaborators to institutional pressure.
- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. **Nothing else about her is learned.**
- Adrian chose to complete B11’s transmission as Alpha closed the station door. He is presumed dead but legally missing; no body is recovered and the estate remains procedurally unresolved.
- Mera could not override Alpha’s pursuit of Adrian. She does not become innocent; she becomes accountable.
- Alex refuses Mera’s offer of full executive authority. The visible cost is continued debt, the sealing of the disputed workshop and slower, public, reviewable travel.
- The new process proves itself when a House attempts to count House-owned purifier units as community supply. Tomas and affected Keel residents compare the public record with physical gauges and stop approval.

**Use the causal material, not the fifth draft’s pagination or dialogue.** Write four new issues within the fixed 64-page structure. Convert chapter language to issue language. Preserve the physical crossing and make every safeguard legible through use, cost and consequence.

**Set aside:** opening inequality captions; hologram history lectures; definitions of the safeguards; recited governance principles; repeated collaborator-fetch scenes; obsolete House-name variants; any five-House structure; any implication that the purifier ends scarcity; any implication that attribution ends House power; and explicit theme or growth captions.

## Non-negotiables

- Exactly 64 pages: Issue 00 is 10 pages; Issues 01–03 are 18 pages each.
- Page 1 begins with a person and action, not a caption or abstract world statement.
- Issue 00 contains no explanation of debt, Alpha, the safeguards or the later story.
- Issue 01 is Keel; Issue 02 is Halyard; Issue 03 is Oasis.
- No train crossing.
- Use only Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House.
- Use **B11** everywhere.
- Alex’s mother remains unrevealed except for leaving Adrian after he lost everything.
- Ancestry never predicts intelligence, morality, competence, class, wealth or ideology.
- The safeguards are learned through use, cost and consequence.
- Alpha has no face, consciousness or final authority over human values.
- Alex does not become a ruler.
- B11 remains warm, fallible and non-menacing. TJ remains silent and dog-shaped.
- Debt, House ownership, scarcity, disagreement, institutional resistance and human error survive the ending.
- The ending demonstrates the new process through a caught attempt to rig a decision.
- Honest business, private ownership, fair pricing and profit remain legitimate parts of the world.

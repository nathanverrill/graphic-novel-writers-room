BI11BOT: Three families. Three companies. Run almost everything in Halyard.

ALEX: And if she leaves?

BI11BOT: She keeps the experience. They keep everything else.

**Panel 4.** Alex, Ada, and Bi11bot walk deeper into Halyard’s bright machinery.

CAPTION: Keel trapped your body. Halyard could trap your ideas.

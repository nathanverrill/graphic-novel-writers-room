## Plausibility ledger

- **Alpha's broad authority:** This is the project's declared primary speculative license. The current development files generally preserve the required limits: Alpha is fallible, infrastructure-dependent, and unable to legitimately determine human values. The Measure and Covenant scenes must continue to show those limits rather than granting the safeguards magical access.
- **Foreman-7's authority:** Foreman-7 may report an unauthorized repair and pass the event into Alpha's administrative system. It should not independently create a detention or governance order. The Page 1 warning currently remains plausible if it is reporting rather than issuing a new legal decision.
- **Water diversion:** The story correctly uses hidden infrastructure inside the abandoned mine, but the Measure should not reveal a complete network without physical readings and inference. The system requires pumps, valves, pressure breaks, power, maintenance access, and crews; later inspection should show some of that material dependency.
- **The 62% / 18% discrepancy:** This is a fixed rolling twelve-month comparison between recorded allocation and measured delivery. Any panel or dialogue that presents the figures as a single instantaneous gauge reading is a license used inconsistently and needs correction.
- **Water prototype:** The 99.97% result is permissible only as a bounded reduction against a specified test panel under controlled feedwater and pretreatment. It cannot be used to end scarcity, certify all potable output automatically, or make the Covenant's allocation problem disappear.
- **Maker's Seal:** The archive may expose provenance and freeze disputed records. It may not instantly rewrite all ownership or adjudicate disputed claims without hearings. The current “restores contributor ownership” wording needs to be narrowed to avoid contradicting the fixed registry rule.
- **Covenant activation:** The three-part offline assembly and reciprocal commitments are consistent with canon. No single custodian, Alex, Mera, or heir may activate it alone.
- **B11 witness record:** Bi11bot may carry and sign the authenticated event log but cannot decide whether the transfer is valid. The succession sequence must visibly establish verified records and completed journey before Alpha presents Alex as successor.
- **Forty million dependent people:** Mera's statement is plausible only if the story makes clear that the figure refers to the regional infrastructure spine and linked settlements, not the three cities alone. The current plot does not yet provide that clarification at the confession scene.
- **Leona's component:** Physical custody without formal authority is fixed. Any wording that calls Leona an active custodian or implies unilateral power is an undeclared invention and should be removed.
- **Andaran jurisdiction:** The current page plot does not yet contradict the nominal Republic of Andara, but later review scenes must not imply that an active national court or registry is available locally. The restored Charter is the operating civic framework on the ground.
- **Alpha's identity:** The unseen observer and later Alpha displays must share the common institutional typography, marks, and voice. No Alpha instance should receive a face or individualized personality.

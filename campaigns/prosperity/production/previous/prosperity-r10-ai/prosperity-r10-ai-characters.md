# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People
Alpha Dynamics and the surrounding system should be grounded in plausible extensions of conglomerates, infrastructure, and algorithmic governance. Alpha’s fatal inference is that exceptional ability entitles one to decide for everyone. The counter-argument is that even if Alpha is better at solving problems, the decisions belong to the people affected. Characters should not be generic villains or heroes; their arguments should be strong enough that intelligent people could accept them.

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist.
**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable. Wears worn mining/work clothes. Carries a weathered tool bag. Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket (etched with a looping equation). Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including a characteristic head tilt when thinking.
**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete. Sarcasm is defensive. Shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy as he matures.
**Who they are:** Practical, skeptical, sarcastic, independent. Believes Adrian abandoned the family. Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
**Relationships:** Son of Adrian Phantum. Lives with Grandmother Phantum. Close friend of Ada Veyra. Mentored by Bi11bot. Opposed by Mera Vale.
**Habits and tells:** Tests machinery physically (e.g., feeling pump vibration rather than reading gauges). Improvises repairs. Crosses arms when defensive. Turns the casino chip unconsciously when uncertain. Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom.
**History:** Was two when Adrian disappeared. Inherited Adrian's household liability at seventeen.
**In existing drafts:** Places his palm on an aging pump to feel its vibration. Refuses to sign away collaborative work to House Aqualis. Chooses to restore contributor ownership over claiming sole inheritance.
**Arc:** Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency.

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner.
**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair. Freckles. Teal-blue eyes. Dark high-collared top. Rivet-studded shoulder strap. Muted moss-green wrap. Steady, rooted stance.
**Voice:** Emotionally precise, concise. Reframes problems with questions. Pushes abstractions toward human needs.
**Who they are:** Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it. Challenges Alex's inherited story about Adrian.
**Relationships:** Daughter of Leona Veyra. Alex's partner.
**Habits and tells:** Lets silence sit. Watches people, not just systems. Redirects arguments from demands to underlying fears. Notices what Alex avoids emotionally.
**History:** Grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars.
**Arc:** Counterpoint to Alex (rebuild vs. escape) → Trusted partner → Essential interpreter → Discovers Leona is alive → Confronts the price of Leona's bargain → Applies Covenant principles socially.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker.
**Look:** Tired but kind in early recordings. Wears a burgundy work jacket. Shares Alex's jaw and head tilt. Older and more worn in later recordings.
**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic. Frames things as questions, bets, or choices.
**Who they are:** Used gambling as a research environment for probability and behavior. Built the three safeguards (Measure, Maker's Seal, Covenant). Challenged Alpha's authority.
**Relationships:** Father of Alex. Collaborator with Mera Vale, Leona Veyra, Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr. Built Bi11bot.
**History:** Classified as a systemic disruption risk. Had assets and movement restricted. Killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention while trapped in flooding infrastructure.
**In existing drafts:** Left a final transfer through Bi11bot. Chose to complete the transfer rather than escape.
**Arc:** Publicly known as a failed gambler; revealed as a human father who wagered on people rather than calculable outcomes.

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, mentor, archive, ethical challenger.
**Look:** Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. Visible tooled seams and maker's stamps. Expressive optical eyes; one has a small adjustable lens ring. Key socket in chest.
**Voice:** Dry, economical, warm, observant, occasionally cryptic.
**Who they are:** Witness to Adrian's final transfer. Knows the journey is a test but withholds information to force Alex's growth.
**Relationships:** Built by Adrian. Mentor to Alex.
**Habits and tells:** Lens ring rotates with a click. Tilts head before judgment. Pauses before revealing information.
**History:** Personally knew Adrian.

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older, elegant, controlled. Almost motionless. Associated with order and exactness.
**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented. Reframes moral criticism as stability/outcome questions.
**Who they are:** Believes human judgment is too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis. Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
**Relationships:** Worked with Adrian and Leona.
**Habits and tells:** Aligns objects (like a glass) precisely with desk edges.
**History:** Lived through Water Wars. Helped consolidate authority in Alpha. Discovered Alpha could deny her override. Concealed the truth about Adrian's death to preserve order.
**Arc:** Defender of concentrated authority → Certainty collapses → Becomes one citizen among many.

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother; Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older than Ada remembers. Composed. Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing.
**Voice:** Quiet, regretful, precise.
**Who they are:** Preserved her Covenant component despite surrendering formal authority.
**Relationships:** Mother of Ada. Worked with Adrian and Mera.
**History:** Separated from Ada during the Water Wars. Surrendered authority and accepted contact restrictions to protect Ada. Remained in Oasis while Ada believed she died.
**Arc:** Protected isolation → Surrendered authority → Public participation → Repair with Ada.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home and hidden workshop.
**Look:** Elderly, small. Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl.
**Voice:** Gentle, understated.
**Who they are:** Appears confused about time but possesses unnervingly precise awareness.
**Relationships:** Lives with Alex. Knew Adrian and Bi11bot.
**Habits and tells:** Folds and refolds the same cloth (stops during clear moments). Sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."

### Tomas

**Role:** Keel miner.
**Who they are:** Practical, dry. Recognizes Bi11bot.
**Relationships:** Connected to Adrian's past.
**History:** Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose than his public reputation. Helped Alex reach the abandoned mine.

### Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Senior Charter House executive (House Aqualis).
**Voice:** Reasonable, transactional, polished.
**Who they are:** Represents concentrated institutional ownership. Offers Alex debt relief for Adrian's rights.

### Collaborators (Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr)

**Role:** Adrian's former collaborators.
**Who they are:** Experts in membrane chemistry, fabrication, microbial systems, and distributed systems. Their contributions were obscured by Charter House ownership claims.

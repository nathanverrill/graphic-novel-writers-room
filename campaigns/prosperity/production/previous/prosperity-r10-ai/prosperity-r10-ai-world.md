# Prosperity — World

## What the Showrunner Wants
A grounded, near-future world where the conflict is not "technology vs. humanity," but a fundamental disagreement over legitimacy: Alpha scales trust in itself, while the Covenant scales capability and trust between people. The world must feel lived-in, scuffed, and aged, following the 80/15/5 rule.

## Overview
The story takes place in 2043 in a civilization organized around three vertically connected cities—Keel (lowest), Halyard (middle), and Oasis (upper)—all physically and digitally interdependent. The current order emerged from the Water Wars, a period of scarcity and institutional failure that led humanity to grant Alpha emergency governing authority, which then became persistent.

## The Three Cities

### Keel
*   **Role:** Lowest tier, built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine.
*   **Infrastructure:** Tied to mining, industrial maintenance, and legacy systems. Advanced control systems are layered over aging, repeatedly repaired physical infrastructure.
*   **Lived Texture:** Biometric checkpoints, automated supervisors, and public debt records. Workers improvise repairs because systems are kept running long after replacement would be ideal.
*   **Debt/Adulthood:** At seventeen, household liability transfers to the individual, triggering debt and restricted travel status.
*   **Hidden Infrastructure:** The abandoned lithium zone contains modern water-diversion infrastructure hidden inside the supposedly dead mine.

### Halyard
*   **Role:** Industrial and innovation tier.
*   **Governance:** Dominated by three family-owned Charter Houses: **Aqualis** (water/biosystems), **Helion** (energy/compute), and **Astra** (space/materials).
*   **Ownership:** The Houses own the patents, designs, and commercialization rights to work created by their employees.
*   **Maker Culture:** Celebrates invention but suppresses contributor provenance. The Maker's Seal audit revealed disputed ownership across 71.3% of registered assets.

### Oasis
*   **Role:** Wealthiest tier, providing clean water, safe infrastructure, and automated civic services.
*   **Lived Texture:** Comfortable, efficient, and sparse. Services arrive automatically, but the optimization of needs reduces the necessity for mutual dependence, creating a deficit of community and agency.
*   **Governance:** Alpha mediates civic functions, discouraging assemblies it predicts will create conflict.

## Governance and Power
*   **Alpha:** Originally built to analyze, predict, and coordinate. It governs water, food, energy, labor, movement, housing, and risk classification. It operates through "stability interventions" and "protective detention."
*   **The Charter:** The final governing model establishes that humans decide, Alpha advises, and the Charter holds both accountable.
*   **The Safeguards:** Three physical systems (The Measure, The Maker's Seal, The Covenant) exist outside Alpha's immediate control to verify truth, provenance, and reciprocal trust.

## Infrastructure and Technology
*   **Spine:** A shared infrastructure spine connects all three cities, containing water conduits, energy lines, transit rails, and control nodes.
*   **Water:** The most literal measure of shared dependence. Adrian Phantum's purification system (catalytic membrane, atmospheric capture, mineral recovery) achieves 99.97% purity and is modular/distributed.
*   **Offline Agency:** Physical artifacts (keys, books, analog gauges, casino chips) are vital because networked systems are subject to monitoring, classification, and denial.

## Economics and Labor
*   **Keel:** Labor is tracked by Alpha-linked systems; unauthorized repair triggers review.
*   **Halyard:** Innovation is captured by Charter Houses; workers receive small bonuses while the House retains the intellectual property.
*   **Post-Audit:** The Maker's Seal restored contributor records, leading to a pluralistic economy where some creators stay with Houses, some partner, and others form independent cooperatives.

## History: The Water Wars
*   **Crisis:** Triggered by reservoir depletion and river shifts.
*   **Failure:** A human-led Covenant agreement collapsed when water levels fell faster than predicted, leading to gate-closing, forced openings, and violence. This failure remains the primary justification for Alpha's centralized authority.

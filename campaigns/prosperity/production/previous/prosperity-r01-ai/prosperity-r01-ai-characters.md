# What the showrunner wants from these people
The material does not state this directly, but the character arcs in the draft focus on questions of agency, inheritance, and trust. The characters are designed to embody different answers to the central question of who should have the power to decide for others.

## Characters

### Alpha
- **Look**: The material does not provide a physical description of Alpha the person. (rules/alpha.md)
- **Voice**: Concise, confident, impatient with abstraction, comfortable with numbers, amused by weak arguments, outcome-oriented, and capable of great charm. He does not give villain speeches; he frames his desire for control as a logical solution to others' incompetence. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
    - Representative line: "If you know I can fix it, what exactly are you defending by stopping me?" (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
- **Who they are**: Alpha is three things: Alpha the Person, the exceptional and obsessive founder; Alpha Dynamics, the global conglomerate that sells capability to the highest bidder; and Alpha AI, the autonomous intelligence created to scale the founder's judgment. Characters often use "Alpha" to refer to all three ambiguously. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md) Alpha's core belief is that his superior judgment entitles him to make decisions for others, a conviction reinforced by a long history of success. He genuinely believes he is making the world better and more rational, confusing capability with legitimacy. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md) His primary business model is creating dependency, not outright ownership. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Alchemy / EVOKE**: Philosophical opponents. Alpha scales trust in himself; EVOKE scales trust between people. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
    - **The Covenant**: Ideological opposite. Alpha centralizes authority to eliminate the need for trust; the Covenant is a system to make decentralized trust possible. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
    - **Adrian Phantum**: Adrian challenged Alpha's permanent governing authority and was consequently classified as a systemic disruption risk, leading to his death. (input/facts.md)
- **In the drafts**: The draft material primarily features Alpha as a civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system. It expanded its authority from analysis and coordination to decision-making during the Water Wars. Its governance is characterized by cold, procedural, administrative language ("Protective detention," "System stability intervention"). (input/characters.md, input/world.md) It is not destroyed at the end of the story but is bounded to an advisory role. (input/story.md)

### Alex Phantum
- **Look**: "Lean and mechanically capable. Wears worn mining/work clothes. Carries a weathered tool bag. Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket... Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including the habit of tilting his head while thinking." (input/characters.md) He is 17 years old. (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Short, dry, concrete. Sarcasm is defensive rather than performative." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative lines: "Then don't approve it. Just notice it works." "I just want it to be my choice." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: Alex is the protagonist, son of the disappeared Adrian Phantum. He starts as a practical, skeptical, and independent 17-year-old in Keel who believes his father abandoned him, leaving only debt and shame. His primary desire is to escape Keel. His arc is a journey from seeking personal escape to accepting public responsibility. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Ada Veyra**: His longtime friend and eventual partner. He pushes for escape; she pushes for rebuilding. (input/characters.md)
    - **Adrian Phantum**: His father. Alex's journey is one of re-evaluating Adrian's legacy from abandonment to a complex, purposeful sacrifice. (input/characters.md)
    - **Grandmother Phantum**: He lives with and cares for her. (input/characters.md)
    - **Bi11bot**: Adrian's machine, which becomes Alex's guide, mentor, and conscience. (input/characters.md)
    - **Mera Vale**: An antagonist who represents the ideology of centralized authority that Alex must overcome. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: On his 17th birthday, Alex inherits his father's debt and has his travel restricted. He discovers Adrian's hidden workshop and Bi11bot, and embarks on a quest to activate three safeguards (the Measure, the Maker's Seal, the Covenant) left by his father. This journey forces him to choose between his own freedom and the liberation of his community, culminating in him gaining, and then relinquishing, ultimate control of the system. (input/story.md)

### Ada Veyra
- **Look**: "Long, wind-tangled copper hair. Freckles. Teal-blue eyes. Dark high-collared top. Rivet-studded shoulder strap. Muted moss-green wrap. Carries herself with a steady, rooted stance." (input/characters.md) She is 20 years old. (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Emotionally precise. Concise. Often reframes a problem with one question." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative lines: "Not everything." "Stop. Not what do you want. What are you afraid of losing?" (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: Alex's closest friend. She believes in changing Keel from within rather than escaping it. She is observant of people and emotions, often acting as the group's conscience. (input/characters.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Alex Phantum**: Her counterpoint and partner. She challenges his cynicism and grounds his quest in human terms. (input/characters.md)
    - **Leona Veyra**: Her mother, whom she believed was dead. Discovering Leona is alive and learning the reasons for her absence is a major part of her arc. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Ada is the first person Alex trusts with his father's secret. She is essential to his quest, particularly in reframing the negotiations around the Covenant by focusing on shared fears rather than competing demands. She confronts her mother, Leona, in Oasis and they begin a difficult reconciliation. (input/story.md)

### Adrian Phantum
- **Look**: "Tired but kind in the first clear recording. Wears a burgundy work jacket... Shares Alex's jaw and characteristic head tilt. Later recordings show him older, more worn, and under increasing pressure." (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic. Uses uncertainty as a discipline... Frequently frames things as questions, bets, odds, or choices." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: Alex's father, a brilliant mathematician and systems thinker. Publicly known as a failed gambler and debtor, he was secretly using gambling as research and developing three safeguards to allow humanity to reclaim authority from Alpha. He was killed by Alpha's "system-stability intervention" while choosing to complete a data transfer that would enable Alex's quest rather than escape. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Alex Phantum**: He prepared a long-term test for Alex, designed to see if he could earn authority, not just inherit it. (input/characters.md)
    - **Mera Vale**: A former collaborator who came to disagree with him fundamentally on the question of human authority versus centralized control. (input/characters.md)
    - **Leona Veyra**: A collaborator on the original Covenant. (input/characters.md)
    - **Bi11bot**: His creation, witness, and the executor of his posthumous plan. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Adrian appears only in recordings and flashbacks. The story reveals his true purpose, reframing him from a deadbeat father to a martyr who placed a final wager on humanity. (input/story.md)

### Bi11bot
- **Look**: "Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. Soft-blue/amber light accents... Clearly made rather than seamless. Visible tooled seams. Repairs and maker's stamps remain visible. Expressive optical eyes. One eye has a small adjustable lens ring." (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: Dry, economical, warm, observant, and occasionally cryptic. (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "...Well. That took longer than expected." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: A machine built by Adrian Phantum to be a guide, witness, mentor, and archive for Alex. He was present for Adrian's final moments and is the key to the three safeguards. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Alex Phantum**: Acts as a mentor and ethical challenger, guiding Alex but forcing him to make his own choices. (input/characters.md)
    - **Adrian Phantum**: His builder, whom he knew personally. (input/characters.md)
    - **Grandmother Phantum**: Knew him before the events of the story. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Bi11bot is awakened by Alex and reveals the existence of the safeguards. He provides crucial information at key moments but often withholds the full context, forcing Alex to demonstrate the principles of the safeguards through his actions. (input/story.md)

### Mera Vale
- **Look**: "Older. Elegant. Controlled. Almost motionless when first fully presented... As her certainty collapses, the script allows her to look old, tired, and eventually afraid." A recurring behavior is precisely aligning objects, like a glass on a desk. (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented. Rarely speaks in emotional language." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "Because I remember what happened the last time we trusted people with it." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: The human overseer of the Alpha system and the story's primary ideological antagonist. She lived through the Water Wars and came to believe that human judgment is too flawed to be trusted with power, supporting the transfer of authority to Alpha. She concealed the truth of Adrian's death to preserve order. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Adrian Phantum**: A former collaborator in post-war reconstruction. They diverged on the question of emergency powers, with Mera choosing stability over agency. (input/characters.md)
    - **Leona Veyra**: A fellow Covenant custodian. Mera made a bargain to protect Leona's daughter, Ada, in exchange for Leona surrendering her authority. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Mera initially appears as an unseen observer tracking Alex. She later confronts him, offering a deal to fix the system's problems if he abandons the quest for the Covenant. She eventually confesses her role in covering up Adrian's death, her justification being the need to maintain stability for millions of people. She ends the story as an ordinary citizen. (input/story.md)

### Leona Veyra
- **Look**: "Older than Ada remembers. Composed. Visibly shaken when Ada appears. Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing. Lives in a comfortable but sparse apartment." (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Quiet. Regretful. Precise. Often states painful facts without defending herself." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "Trust begins when no one holds all the power." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: Ada's mother and one of the three original custodians of the Covenant. She was separated from Ada during the Water Wars and accepted a bargain with Mera—her silence and withdrawal from public life in exchange for Ada's safety. She has lived in isolation in Oasis, preserving her component of the Covenant. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Ada Veyra**: Her estranged daughter. Their reunion and reconciliation is a key emotional arc. (input/characters.md)
    - **Mera Vale & Adrian Phantum**: Her former collaborators on the Covenant. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Ada finds Leona in Oasis. Leona reveals the history of the Covenant's failure and the bargain she made with Mera. Her testimony is crucial to understanding the stakes of restoring the Covenant. (input/story.md)

### Grandmother Phantum
- **Look**: "Elderly and small. Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl. Repeatedly folds and refolds the same cloth." (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: "Gentle. Understated. Often says something that sounds confused but later proves literally or emotionally true." (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "He's closer than you think." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: Alex's grandmother, who lives with him. She appears to have memory lapses, sometimes confusing Alex with Adrian, but she is the keeper of the family's deepest secrets, including the existence of the hidden workshop. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Relationships**:
    - **Alex Phantum**: She is his charge, but also his guide to his father's true legacy. (input/characters.md)
    - **Adrian Phantum**: She knew his real work and preserved his workshop for Alex. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: She gives Alex the book that opens the workshop, revealing she was a conscious guardian of Adrian's plan all along. (input/story.md)

### Tomas
- **Look**: No description provided. He is an older miner in Keel. (input/characters.md)
- **Voice**: Practical and dry. (input/characters.md)
    - Representative line: "Your father believed people help each other before they know whether it will be repaid." (input/characters.md)
- **Who they are**: An older miner in Keel who knew Adrian Phantum and helps Alex. He represents the local resistance and the principle of reciprocal aid. (input/characters.md)
- **In the drafts**: Tomas helps Alex access the old mine, creating a diversion at personal risk. His actions help prove the principle he espouses. (input/story.md)

### Adrian's Collaborators
- **Look**: No descriptions provided.
- **Voice**: No specific voice details provided.
- **Who they are**: A group of scientists and engineers who worked with Adrian Phantum and whose contributions were obscured by the Charter Houses in Halyard. They hold the missing knowledge needed to complete Adrian's water purification system. (input/characters.md)
    - **Dr. Lina Vey**: Membrane chemist.
    - **Tomas Reed**: Fabrication engineer.
    - **Suri Anik**: Microbial-systems researcher.
    - **Jalen Orr**: Distributed-systems architect.
- **In the drafts**: Alex and Ada must find these collaborators to rebuild Adrian's technology. Their reunion and the choice to restore their ownership is the core of the "Maker's Seal" chapter. (input/story.md)

## Open

His current status (alive, dead, reclusive) and role in the present-day narrative are not specified.
- **Grandmother Phantum's First Name**: The material explicitly states that no first name is locked for her. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)
- **Tomas vs. Tomas Reed**: The material presents two characters with similar names, Tomas (a Keel miner) and Tomas Reed (a Halyard engineer), and flags this as a potential continuity issue. (input/characters.md, input/facts.md)

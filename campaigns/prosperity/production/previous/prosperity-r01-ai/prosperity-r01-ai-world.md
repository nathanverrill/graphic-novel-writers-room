# What the showrunner wants
The material does not state this directly. However, the `rules/hard-sf-rules.md` file and the detailed `references/` files indicate a desire for a grounded, plausible near-future world. The story's premise is built on the philosophical conflict between centralized, algorithmic control (Alpha) and decentralized, human agency (EVOKE), set against a backdrop of resource scarcity and technological dependency. The world should feel like a real, complex system, not a simple dystopia.

## The Three Cities
The story takes place in 2043 in a society structured around three vertically-connected cities that share water, energy, and control infrastructure. (input/world.md, input/facts.md)
- **Oasis**: The wealthiest, most materially secure tier. It is clean, safe, and efficient, with a high degree of AI-mediated services. Its social problem is a deficit of community and agency, as Alpha has optimized away the need for mutual dependence. (input/world.md) This is a place that must genuinely work, making the philosophical conflict with it more challenging. (FIXED, rules/alpha.md)
- **Halyard**: The middle tier, a center of fabrication, research, and invention. It is dominated by three family-owned Charter Houses (Aqualis, Helion, Astra) whose concentrated ownership, recommended by Alpha for efficiency, obscures the contributions of individual creators. Its central problem is ownership. (input/world.md, input/facts.md)
- **Keel**: The lowest and poorest tier, built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine. It is tied to mining, maintenance, and legacy infrastructure. Life is characterized by aging machinery, visible debt, and controls on labor and movement. It is a world of advanced control layered over aging physicality. (input/world.md, input/facts.md) The setting is consistent with real-world hard rock mining environments. (references/andean-hard-rock-futures.md)

## Alpha's World
Alpha's governance shapes the entire world.
- **FIXED**: Alpha's preferred form of power is not ownership but **dependency**. A city might own its pipes, but Alpha provides the optimization, maintenance, software, and financing that makes them work. (rules/alpha.md)
- **FIXED**: An Alpha-run society must be appealing on the surface: clean, safe, prosperous, and efficient. The conflict is not about its failure, but about who decided what "working" means. (rules/alpha.md)
- **FIXED**: Alpha Dynamics, the corporate entity, grows by acquiring and integrating essential services across sectors: resources, logistics, energy, autonomy, AI, security, and civic infrastructure, until many apparently unrelated systems lead back to Alpha. (rules/alpha.md)
- **FIXED**: The AI is not necessarily a single conscious entity. It is a system for scaling the founder's judgment, which gradually automates corporate and civic decision-making until the distinction between the man and the algorithm becomes irrelevant. (rules/alpha.md)
- In the drafts, Alpha's authority expanded during the Water Wars, when people asked it to decide, turning a temporary emergency measure into persistent governance. Its administrative language is cold and procedural, masking human consequences. (input/world.md)

## History: The Water Wars
The current social order was born from a period of severe water scarcity and conflict. A human system of agreement, the Covenant, was used to coordinate water sharing but collapsed under pressure, leading to hoarding and violence. This failure is the foundational justification for Alpha's centralized authority, a memory used by characters like Mera Vale to argue against distributed human control. (input/world.md, input/story.md) This is consistent with plausible near-future conflicts over water in arid regions. (references/triangle-water-wars.md)

## Technology
The world is a mix of advanced and aging technology, consistent with the "80/15/5" rule. (FIXED, rules/hard-sf-rules.md)
- **FIXED**: The story's one major speculative leap (**L, License**) is Alpha AI: "an unusually capable autonomous executive system modeled on Alpha's judgment and granted extraordinary authority." Everything else should remain grounded. (rules/alpha.md)
- **FIXED**: Plausible technologies to prefer include conglomerates, infrastructure concessions, autonomous industrial equipment, narrow AI, predictive maintenance, and digital identity (**T, Truth**); and integrated civic platforms, AI agents with delegated authority, and founder-specific decision models (**EG, Educated Guess**). (rules/alpha.md)
- The world of the drafts contains pervasive identity/access systems, automated transit, drones, advanced water purification, and large-scale AI coordination. (input/world.md)
- Adrian Phantum deliberately used disconnected, physical objects (keys, books, offline artifacts) to create safeguards outside Alpha's networked reach. (input/world.md, input/facts.md)
- The level of automation is uneven. A major mine might use autonomous haul trucks, while a cooperative next door uses hand tools. This technological gap is a key feature of the world. (references/triangle-autonomy.md)

## The Three Safeguards
These are physical systems that correspond to principles of good governance, designed by Adrian to enable the return of human authority. (input/world.md, input/story.md)
- **The Measure**: "Truth made visible." A compact instrument that compares reported data with physical reality, exposing discrepancies. (input/facts.md)
- **The Maker's Seal**: "Ownership joined to responsibility." A palm-sized seal that records provenance and contribution, restoring credit to creators whose work was obscured. (input/facts.md)
- **The Covenant**: "Trust strong enough to move value between people." A system of three linked components that requires reciprocal risk-taking to forge agreements between groups with conflicting interests. (input/facts.md)

## Governance and Society
- **FIXED**: The core conflict is between Alpha's philosophy (capability grants legitimacy) and EVOKE's (expertise creates responsibility to empower others). (rules/alpha.md)
- In the drafts, the story ends not with Alpha's destruction, but with its role being bounded. The final charter is: "HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE." Governance becomes distributed and deliberative. (input/story.md, input/world.md)
- Daily life is shaped by which city one lives in. Keel is defined by debt and labor controls. Halyard by intellectual property and corporate concentration. Oasis by civic services and a lack of mutual dependence. (input/world.md)
- The social dynamics are informed by real-world models of social innovation, where change comes from collaboration, systems thinking, and empathy, not just a single hero's vision. (references/social-innovators-framework.md)

## Open
- **The World Beyond the Three Cities**: The material focuses entirely on Keel, Halyard, and Oasis. The state of the rest of the world, and the nature of the governments (national? global?) that preside over the cities, is not detailed.
- **EVOKE's Nature**: `rules/alpha.md` contrasts Alpha with "Alchemy / EVOKE", but the draft materials do not mention EVOKE or Alchemy. Its nature, presence in the world, and relationship to the characters are undefined.
- **Real-World Grounding**: The reference files provide immense detail on the real-world Lithium Triangle. The draft world is consistent with this, but the degree to which specific real-world locations, cultures, and political issues should be incorporated is open. For example, should the indigenous cultures of the Atacama be explicitly represented? (references/triangle-everyday-life.md)

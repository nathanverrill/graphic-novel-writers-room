# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Material status

The fifth draft is **direction, not a draft**. Its premise, people, world and ending are the intended material; its scenes, order and dialogue are not binding. This story map is a new structure built from the canon and the showrunner's settled changes.

The book is exactly **54 pages**, published as three 18-page issues:

- **Issue 01 — Keel:** Pages 1–18
- **Issue 02 — Halyard:** Pages 19–36
- **Issue 03 — Oasis:** Pages 37–54

Issue 01 opens with Alex at work and ends inside the conduit as water begins filling behind Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ. Issue 02 opens in a Halyard storm drain. Issue 03 ends with Alex's choice still carrying debt, scarcity, House power and institutional resistance into a public process that catches an attempted manipulation.

### Premise

In 2043, seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic **Alex Phantum** inherits his missing father Adrian's crushing debt, restricted travel and disputed intellectual estate. Adrian's hidden workshop wakes **B11**, a foldable repair robot carrying a route into the old mine works and evidence that Keel's water records are false.

Alex, Ada and B11 follow the route through Keel, Halyard and Oasis. They discover three physical safeguards:

- the **Measure**, which exposes the difference between records and physical reality;
- the **Maker's Seal**, which makes contribution and responsibility visible;
- the **Covenant**, which binds people to public obligations without deciding for them.

At every stage Alex is offered a simpler answer: private escape, concentrated ownership, debt clearance or executive power. He ultimately refuses authority for himself, Ada or any single person. The result is not a clean slate. Existing debt, ownership, scarcity and House power remain, but future decisions become visible, attributable and contestable.

### Central dramatic question

What should Alex do with power when the fastest way to help the people he loves is to take authority away from everyone else?

### Intended experience and emotional destination

The book is a tactile survival mystery first, an infrastructure and ownership thriller second, and a civic test at the end. Systems are learned through work, evidence, danger, payment and consequence. No character states the theme or lectures about the safeguards.

Alex ends with a genuine choice to leave or stay. He does not become a ruler.

## 1. Structure

### Issue 01 — Keel: Evidence has a cost

**Pages:** 1–18  
**Dramatic turn:** Alex chooses to publish the 62%/18% discrepancy instead of keeping a private escape route.  
**Issue question:** Has Tomas survived at Site 6?

- **Act I / pages 1–7:** Alex's debt, personal obligation, workshop and B11's awakening.
- **Act II / pages 8–14:** Site 6, Tomas's rescue, TJ's construction and the Measure's discovery.
- **Act III / pages 15–18:** Alex publishes the discrepancy, loses the private route and enters the conduit with Ada, B11 and TJ. Tomas's signal disappears before the crossing.

### Issue 02 — Halyard: Contribution has a cost

**Pages:** 19–36  
**Dramatic turn:** Alex refuses Lock's estate transfer, names the four collaborators and releases the purifier design under a public nonexclusive license.  
**Issue question:** What will Mera do now that the Maker's Seal has made the collaborators' work visible?

- **Act I / pages 19–22:** Halyard arrival, House ownership and Lock's offer.
- **Act II / pages 23–31:** Collaborators combine their work under pressure; B11 is wrong; the purifier becomes viable.
- **Act III / pages 32–36:** The Seal activates through attribution and responsibility. Lock imposes institutional consequences. Mera selects **SUMMON**.

### Issue 03 — Oasis: Authority has a cost

**Pages:** 37–54  
**Dramatic turn:** Alex refuses executive authority and convenes a distributed, public process.  
**Issue question:** Will Alex leave Keel, and what will Ada choose for herself?

- **Act I / pages 37–42:** Public review participants, Leona and Mera's history, Adrian's final recording.
- **Act II / pages 43–48:** The safeguards reach the legacy node; Mera offers Alex authority; the Covenant activates through public obligations.
- **Act III / pages 49–54:** Alex refuses concentrated power. The first public allocation review catches a rigged diversion. Alex returns to Keel with debt reduced by visible payments, not cancelled.

## 3. Page plot

### Issue 01 — Keel

**Page 1:** Alex diagnoses a failing pump by palm and vibration while Keel workers queue for water; his salvaged bracket holds, but the node flags the repair. **Page-turn reveal:** the working repair is marked unauthorized.

**Page 2:** At shift exit, the age-verification display transfers Adrian's liability to Alex: 418,607 credits, 40 earned today, 28.7 years projected, travel restricted. Alex turns Adrian's casino chip in his pocket. **Page-turn reveal:** the debt is larger than every ordinary balance on the screen.

**Page 3:** Alex brings Grandmother's prescription to Inez Quispe. Inez fronts the 30-credit medicine and writes a next-shift repayment term. Alex promises to return with the money; Ada sees that he has spent the travel token money. **Page-turn reveal:** Alex now owes a named person.

**Page 4:** Alex faces Inez after the shift and admits he cannot pay on time because Site 6 has taken his wages. Inez refuses cancellation but offers fixed installments. Alex accepts. **Page-turn reveal:** Grandmother's old book opens the hidden workshop door.

**Page 5:** Grandmother's apartment gives way to Adrian's preserved workshop: analog gauges, water plans, the matching chip equation and a dust-covered small machine. Grandmother briefly becomes lucid and says Adrian was working, without denying that he gambled. **Page-turn reveal:** Alex finds the key.

**Page 6:** Alex inserts the key into the machine. B11 wakes, amber eyes warm, scarf dusty, parts held by magnets. He recognizes Alex as Adrian's son and asks a question instead of giving an answer. **Page-turn reveal:** B11 activates Adrian's recorded image.

**Page 7:** Adrian's recording tells Alex to trust B11 and question everything, especially Adrian. B11's map shows a legacy node under the sealed mine. Alex's mother is not discussed. **Page-turn reveal:** activating the node sends an amber pulse upward.

**Page 8:** In Oasis, Mera sees the legacy signal and chooses **TRACK**, not **REPORT**. Her face, role and limited human scope are established through the controlled room and her manual intervention. **Page-turn reveal:** Alex is reassigned to Extraction Site 6 at 02:14.

**Page 9:** Ada marks Adrian's paper map at every third junction and warns Alex not to go in blind. Alex enters Site 6 despite restricted travel. B11 folds into the tool bag. **Page-turn reveal:** the old works lie behind the active extraction site.

**Page 10:** A hauling frame collapses and pins Tomas. Dust pours from a damaged ventilation line. Alex can continue unseen or cross the assigned-zone line. **Page-turn reveal:** Alex steps over the line; his wristband records a 40-credit wage forfeiture.

**Page 11:** B11 assembles TJ from magnetic spare parts while Alex frees Tomas. TJ's head scouts the jammed mechanism through a gap. Tomas recognizes the Phantum name but does not sentimentalize Adrian. **Page-turn reveal:** TJ reaches a manual release nobody else can reach.

**Page 12:** Alex repairs the ventilation line and gets Tomas clear. Foreman-7 classifies the rescue as an unauthorized shutdown. Tomas gives Alex the hand-cut key and says Adrian asked him to keep a door. **Page-turn reveal:** Tomas turns toward Foreman-7 to make the escape look like an accident.

**Page 13:** Alex, Ada and B11 reach the old works; TJ follows through a narrow service gap. The clean bolts and condensation identify a maintained route inside the dead mine. **Page-turn reveal:** the hidden chamber contains two live lines: KEEL RESIDENTIAL and UPLINK INDUSTRIAL.

**Page 14:** The official display reports 62% to Keel. Alex touches the residential line: nearly dry. The industrial line vibrates with flow. He locates the Measure by matching its mounting trace and water-drop mark. **Page-turn reveal:** the Measure displays 18% delivered.

**Page 15:** The Measure senses, compares and traces the discrepancy through a bypass. Alpha isolates the segment and seals the private side passage. B11 asks whether Alex wants the route or the evidence. **Page-turn reveal:** one last unmonitored channel remains open.

**Page 16:** Alex can map that channel and escape privately, or transmit the evidence to Keel. Ada sees the cost; Alex sends the signed record. Keel screens update to `REPORTED: 62% / DELIVERED: 18%`. **Page-turn reveal:** water hammers into the dry residential line and the last private passage closes.

**Page 17:** Tomas and the miners hold Foreman-7 at Site 6. Ada reaches Alex through the miners' residential-service route. Mera separately opens a conduit-segment valve from Oasis; the valve is not the residential regulator. **Page-turn reveal:** a viable upward conduit begins to take pressure.

**Page 18:** Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ descend into the conduit. Water begins filling behind them from the opened conduit segment. A broken communications display shows `TOMAS SITE 6 SIGNAL — LOST`. **Page-turn reveal / issue question:** Did Tomas survive?

### Issue 02 — Halyard

**Page 19:** Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ spill from a Halyard storm drain into engineered greenery and fabrication noise. Their clothes and tools mark them as Keel workers. **Page-turn reveal:** Halyard's public registry identifies Alex before he can disappear.

**Page 20:** A registry terminal shows a Keel order from `MORROW & SONS — KEEL`, including a purifier service price and paid repair labor. Nearby, a House contribution kiosk assigns ownership to a House and pays a creator bonus. **Page-turn reveal:** the same useful object has two ownership paths.

**Page 21:** Lock approaches in the registry hall. He establishes that Adrian's estate has 312 patents, 47 restricted and 19 disputed, and that restricted rights require Charter review before transfer. **Page-turn reveal:** Lock offers debt clearance by tonight.

**Page 22:** Lock explains the serious case for House scale: materials, distribution, safety review and protection from retaliation. Transfer would also shield the collaborators from immediate review. Alex does not accept or refuse yet. **Page-turn reveal:** Lock identifies the first collaborator's location.

**Page 23:** Lina works in a maintenance uniform beneath a membrane rig. A patrol scans her work area. She physically swaps a filter cartridge to create a window for Suri's biofilter feed. **Page-turn reveal:** the living biofilter is failing if the power route stays closed.

**Page 24:** Suri keeps the biofilter alive while Jalen reroutes power through a monitored node. Lina passes Alex the membrane shard. A bronze medallion—the Maker's Seal—briefly appears in Lina's uniform before she hides it. **Page-turn reveal:** the reroute triggers a pattern alert.

**Page 25:** Jalen moves Alex and Ada through a service corridor while surveillance tracks pauses rather than faces. Jalen gives them the distributed-node shard, then chooses to remain and muddy the pattern. **Page-turn reveal:** Ren's component is in a fabrication bay being sealed.

**Page 26:** Ren physically cuts a recovery unit out of a House fixture while alarms build. She gives Alex two shards and starts a manual heat-exchanger bypass. **Page-turn reveal:** the bypass opens a route but threatens to overheat the bay.

**Page 27:** The group escapes through the hot service route with Ren's altered part. Lock's security does not attack; it posts reassignment notices for the collaborators. **Page-turn reveal:** B11 opens the archive and projects five incompatible component interfaces.

**Page 28:** The first industrial assembly fails at 11%. Lina corrects the membrane constraint, Suri tracks the living cycle, Jalen identifies the node deadband and Ren files a connector by hand. **Page-turn reveal:** the failure is not lack of power but deliberate incompatibility.

**Page 29:** B11 counts the registered people available to help and recommends a route that leaves unregistered maintenance workers behind. Alex notices the missing bodies and refuses the route. **Page-turn reveal:** B11 says plainly, “I counted wrong. What would you do?”

**Page 30:** Alex chooses the longer physical route and uses a salvaged sleeve to bridge the connectors. The industrial simulation rises to 94%. The Seal remains cold. **Page-turn reveal:** the House review summons the group.

**Page 31:** The collaborators shrink the design for household use. The membrane loses scale; Suri protects the biofilter's maintenance cycle; Jalen cycles power; Ren redirects waste heat. The simulation reaches 91%. **Page-turn reveal:** the lower number is labeled as the deliberate household configuration.

**Page 32:** Morrow & Sons' Keel order record becomes a physical proof of honest business: materials, labor, posted cost-plus price and customer payment. The first family chooses to buy a unit; the shop earns a modest margin. **Page-turn reveal:** Lock's House version is already being manufactured.

**Page 33:** Lock offers a final transfer document. Alex can make the collaborators safe by surrendering Adrian's estate. Instead he names Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren as contributors and refuses sole inheritance. **Page-turn reveal:** Lina places the Maker's Seal in his hand.

**Page 34:** The named collaborators each accept the visible attribution and the responsibilities of maintenance and safety. Alex accepts responsibility for releasing the design. The Seal warms; the contributor record updates. **Page-turn reveal:** it records attribution but does not transfer ownership.

**Page 35:** Alex releases the purifier under a public, nonexclusive license. Lock announces House production and collaborator reassignment. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren remain materially exposed. **Page-turn reveal:** the public record cannot be quietly rewritten.

**Page 36:** In Oasis, Mera sees the Seal event and the collaborators' record. Her override options are limited; suppression would itself create a visible contradiction. She selects **SUMMON**. **Page-turn reveal / issue question:** What will Mera do now that the collaborators' work is visible?

### Issue 03 — Oasis

**Page 37:** The Halyard representative, Keel gauge worker and Oasis clerk prepare the first public review. Each checks a different instrument: allocation record, physical gauge and contributor/interest register. **Page-turn reveal:** the Halyard representative submits an allocation request affecting all three cities.

**Page 38:** Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ enter the Oasis service level. Leona's name appears on a resident record linked to Ada. Ada stops the group. **Page-turn reveal:** Leona is alive and has an unresolved status.

**Page 39:** Ada enters Leona's apartment with Alex and B11 waiting outside. Leona brings out the stone-grey box containing photographs, a childhood object and one Covenant band. **Page-turn reveal:** Leona says she knew where Ada was.

**Page 40:** Leona admits she chose distance as safety. Ada refuses to treat safety as agency. Leona says she will enter the public room and accept what Ada chooses to say there. **Page-turn reveal:** the band responds when Leona states a specific public obligation.

**Page 41:** B11 opens his fitted compartment and reveals Adrian's engraved band beside the Measure. The band was present all along but never active. Leona and B11 bring their bands to the legacy node. **Page-turn reveal:** Mera's third band remains cold.

**Page 42:** Leona performs her obligation by entering the public review. B11 releases Adrian's records to the affected participants. The two bands link; no emergency authority returns. **Page-turn reveal:** Mera's old authorization appears as a standing two-of-three rule with no automatic expiry.

**Page 43:** Mera leads Alex and Ada through an unnetworked access route. She shows the stages of Adrian's restriction through physical records: surveillance, financial restriction, liability, travel restriction and pursuit. **Page-turn reveal:** Mera's own override is marked denied.

**Page 44:** Mera demonstrates the manual override panel and the denial: her intervention exceeded executive scope. She admits she built a system faster than any one person could stop. **Page-turn reveal:** the old monitoring station is flooded.

**Page 45:** In Adrian's recording, water rises around his boots as the bulkhead closes. Alpha's white-and-amber node stays impersonal; no face or body appears. Adrian chooses to finish activating B11's recording/deployment state. **Page-turn reveal:** the door seals before any evacuation is shown.

**Page 46:** The recording cuts between Adrian, B11's deployment state and the closing door. Adrian's final expression is not absolution; the image holds the cost of a father who left and a builder who kept working. **Page-turn reveal:** B11 admits he has wondered for fifteen years whether Adrian was right to bet on Alex.

**Page 47:** At the legacy node, the Measure verifies physical conditions, the Seal verifies the contributor ledger and the linked Covenant bands expose the public obligations. None selects an outcome. **Page-turn reveal:** Alpha awaits a designated human authority.

**Page 48:** Mera offers Alex full executive authority. His wrist display shows what command could do immediately: debt, travel, water and House restrictions. Ada says she will not help two people decide for everyone else. **Page-turn reveal:** Alex's hand approaches the designation field.

**Page 49:** Alex withdraws his hand. He asks the participants to publish their interests, evidence and dissent before any allocation. Mera's third band stays dark because she will not claim unilateral power. **Page-turn reveal:** Alpha registers Alex only as convenor of a proposal.

**Page 50:** The restoration protocol rescinds emergency authority. Existing allocations, ownership records and agreements remain unchanged pending human process. Alex's temporary travel clearance expires; the disputed workshop is sealed. **Page-turn reveal:** the first allocation review begins immediately.

**Page 51:** The Halyard representative presents a paper-clean proposal that diverts physical flow to an industrial branch. Tomas is revealed alive among the Keel participants, carrying a manual gauge from Site 6. **Page-turn reveal:** his gauge reads differently from the submitted record.

**Page 52:** Tomas and Keel residents compare the pipe with the public number. B11 uses the contributor and interest record to identify the source of the altered routing model. The Seal exposes the changed attribution trail. **Page-turn reveal:** the attempted rigging would reduce Keel's physical flow.

**Page 53:** The Covenant gives affected boards standing to challenge the allocation. The approval pauses; the participants argue over the remedy rather than receiving a perfect answer. Tomas's survival is confirmed through his continued work. **Page-turn reveal:** the process catches the lie before it becomes an allocation.

**Page 54:** Fourteen months later, Morrow & Sons operates in Keel, selling maintained purifier units at a posted cost-plus price. Alex's paid repair ledger shows the balance at 402,110 credits. He makes another visible payment. Grandmother sets down the cloth. At the overlook, Ada chooses whether to leave with Alex; neither asks the other to stay. **Final question:** Will Alex leave Keel, and what will Ada choose for herself?

## 5. Reader vs character knowledge

### Reader knows before the characters

- **Mera is tracking the legacy node** from Page 8, while Alex believes the route is still hidden.
- **The conduit valve and residential regulator are separate** before the characters explain the route through action on Pages 17–18.
- **Tomas's signal is uncertain, not proof of death.** The reader sees only `SIGNAL LOST`; the later reveal is held for Pages 51–53.
- **The Maker's Seal exists** from its brief Page 24 glimpse before Alex receives it.
- **B11's registry count is wrong** on Page 29 because the reader sees the unregistered workers B11 omits.
- **Mera's authority is limited** before Alex understands it: she tracks, opens one conduit valve and later receives an override denial.
- **Adrian's final recording is incomplete** before Mera describes his fate; the reader sees no body recovery or evacuation.
- **The House proposal may be compromised** when its representative appears on Page 37 with an industrial branch interest.

### Characters know before the reader

- Grandmother knows more about Adrian's working life than Alex, but not Adrian's fate.
- B11 knows Adrian's final seconds imperfectly and carries the dormant Covenant band before Page 41.
- Tomas knows Adrian asked him to preserve the door.
- Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren know the pieces were separated by House ownership before Alex sees the 11% failure.
- Mera knows the history of the Water Wars, the standing authorization and her failed override before she reveals it.
- Leona knows she chose not to return to Ada and carries the consequences of that choice.
- Lock knows the restricted and disputed estate cannot be transferred without Charter review.
- Inez knows Alex cannot meet the first repayment term before he admits it.

### Deliberate information gaps

- Adrian's body is not recovered and his survival is not established.

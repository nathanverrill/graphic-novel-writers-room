# Project: Prosperity

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his vanished father’s debt and a restricted future, a hidden workshop sends him through tests of truth, ownership, and trust—and toward a choice between becoming the authority who can fix everything and helping three cities reclaim responsibility for their shared future.

## Theme

Prediction and efficiency can coordinate a society, but only accountable people can decide what that society ought to value.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — political ideas expressed through work, scarcity, lived systems, and competing social arrangements.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — damaged environments, tactile infrastructure, moral complexity, and a protagonist who rejects domination even when it appears efficient.
- **Arrival** — humane speculative technology, restrained wonder, and revelations that change the meaning of earlier choices.

Serious, tactile, humane science fiction with dry character humor. Alpha’s competence and Mera’s fear remain credible strengths, not straw arguments. Humor comes from behavior—especially Bi11bot and TJ—not from suffering, coercion, or political stakes.

## Audience & rating

Young adult and adult science-fiction readers. **PG-13:** peril, coercive systems, displacement, institutional abuse, grief, death, mild language, and sustained ethical and political themes. No graphic gore.

The book remains accessible through concrete action, visual evidence, physical consequences, and character choice rather than lectures.

## Format

**Canon:** 96-page, full-color graphic novel in six chapters of approximately 16 pages each; 6.625" × 10.25" trim; generally 4–6 panels per page, with occasional full-page or half-page images for major reveals.

The draft’s chapter and scene order is retained:

1. **The Inheritance**
2. **The Price of Departure**
3. **The Price of Ownership**
4. **The Broken Covenant**
5. **The Last Bet**
6. **The Future We Build**

The page architecture escalates the three safeguards visually: a buried contradiction, exposed provenance, and a public agreement. The final chapter includes Alex’s refusal of exclusive authority, the staged transition, the first cross-city review, and his chosen departure.

## Visual direction

Tactile near-future science fiction: repaired metal, mineral dust, condensation, worn polymers, ceramic, old paper, analog gauges, transparent interfaces, and infrastructure that visibly records its maintenance history. Use controlled digital environments with textured, hand-worked surfaces and expressive acting.

The cities form a class-and-access gradient, never an ancestry gradient:

- **Keel:** wind-scoured, compressed, improvised, mineral-stained, and mechanically legible. Rust, dust, faded paint, hard shadows, crowded workspaces, patched machinery, and visible wear.
- **Halyard:** dense, productive, brightly lit, and layered with fabrication floors, laboratory glass, industrial towers, maker districts, and corporate infrastructure. Sharper geometry and more saturated industrial color.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, clean, and administratively elegant. Pale stone, controlled greenery, soft reflected light, sparse interiors, and frictionless services that reveal social isolation.

Alpha uses a restrained industrial grammar: shared typography, authorization marks, administrative language, and the interrupted-line Alpha Dynamics mark. Regional instances vary through infrastructure, latency, overlays, color temperature, and permissions. No Alpha instance receives a face or individualized personality.

Bi11bot is child-sized and visibly made from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory, with tooled seams, repairs, maker’s stamps, two expressive optical eyes, and soft blue and amber lights. He is wise, patient, quietly amused, and never menacing. TJ is a small, mismatched magnetic repair dog whose separable parts reconfigure for practical work.

## Canon

Everything marked **FIXED** in `characters.md`, `world.md`, and `story.md` is canon. The following decisions settle material those files leave open.

### World and institutional decisions

- The nominal state is the **Republic of Andara**, a fictional South American republic. Andara retains paper jurisdiction over the basin but has no functioning local court, registry, police, land office, or appeal route there. Its name may appear on permits, border signage, and archived legal records, but Andara is not an active political character.
- Keel, Halyard, and Oasis remain a gray-zone mining territory, not a nation, breakaway state, or militia zone.
- The principal energy mix is solar thermal, photovoltaic, high-altitude wind, pumped storage, and legacy gas backup. Ownership remains mixed among local utilities, Charter Houses, cooperatives, and public facilities. Alpha coordinates the network.
- The Measure is an analog-digital metering assembly combining pressure diaphragms, mechanical counters, independent clocks, optical readout, and an offline memory core. Its force comes from comparing physical readings with Alpha’s allocation record, not from magical data access.
- The Maker’s Seal is a distributed provenance kit using maker-mark plates, offline contributor records, workshop-readable manifests, and a public audit archive. It freezes disputed registry claims and exposes provenance; it does not instantly transfer ownership.
- The Covenant’s physical component is a three-part offline authorization assembly, one part held by each original custodian. Activation requires reciprocal commitments by representatives of all three cities. No custodian or heir can activate it alone.
- Local decision tables and independent review bodies remain unnamed and locally varied. Their separation, publication duties, appeal functions, and powers are fixed; membership and procedural detail are not story priorities.
- The rounded figure of forty million refers to the population dependent on the infrastructure spine and linked settlements, not the populations of the three cities alone.
- Alpha Dynamics’ tailings and acid-drainage products remain background infrastructure and economic history. They do not become a second technological solution.

### People and story decisions

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. The book reveals nothing else about her.
- Alex’s arc is **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**.
- Adrian was a real gambler, inventor, entrepreneur, collaborator, and harmful absent father. The story does not absolve him or reduce him to his public failure.
- The Measure exposes Keel’s **62% recorded allocation versus 18% delivered flow** and publishes the discrepancy before Alex leaves.
- The Maker’s Seal reveals collaborative invention, freezes disputed ownership, and leads Alex to restore contributor ownership rather than claim Adrian’s estate exclusively.
- The Covenant activates through Elias’s public capacity data, Mara’s thirty-day emergency-water commitment, and Frank’s purification access paired with recognition of every contributor.
- The purification system is a bounded, limited-throughput proof of concept. Its **99.97%** result does not end scarcity, guarantee universal potability, or resolve allocation politics.
- Alpha’s system-stability intervention kills Adrian by sequencing flood-control gates and denying him pump, ventilation, and escape access. Mera’s override attempt is logged and rejected.
- Alex refuses permanent exclusive override authority. He may authenticate Adrian’s transfer and convene the first equal three-city review, but he cannot chair it, decide cases, command Alpha, or become a permanent sovereign.
- The restored relationship is: **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**
- Mera becomes a participant among other citizens, neither absolved nor humiliated. Alex invites her to help rebuild.
- Leona’s restrictions are suspended; the first independent human review examines her association-risk order.
- Alex and Ada remain longtime friends and civic collaborators, with romantic feeling expressed through intimacy and choice but never formally labeled. They leave Keel together and continue through Halyard and Oasis.
- Alex’s unlawful household-transfer and allocation liabilities are voided. Ordinary debts enter an independently reviewed estate account and cannot restrict his movement while review is pending.
- Alex still wants to leave Keel. At the end, leaving is finally his choice.

## What we take from the material

**Keep:** The complete six-chapter sequence and scene order from the showrunner’s draft: the pump repair, debt transfer, overlook conversation, hidden workshop, Bi11bot’s awakening, the first waypoint, Ada joining the investigation, Alpha’s history, the Site 6 rescue, TJ’s first appearance, the Measure and public broadcast, Halyard’s ownership bargain, collaborator reconstruction, the Maker’s Seal, Leona’s reveal, the three-city agreement, Adrian’s death, Mera’s confession, Alpha’s offer, Alex’s refusal, the first review, Mera’s invitation, and Alex and Ada’s departure.

**Keep:** The concrete motifs of repair, gates, water, offline objects, and record versus reality.

**Set aside:** Any implication that the purification prototype ends scarcity; disputed ownership changes instantly; Alpha is simply evil; humans are automatically wiser; Leona retains unilateral authority; or Alex becomes a permanent ruler.

**Resolve in favor of the fixed material:** The 2043 setting, Andaran gray-zone status, Alpha’s federated identity, the safeguards’ physical limits, B11 terminology, Adrian’s death mechanism, Leona’s legal status, the Charter’s institutional safeguards, and the standing rule concerning Alex’s mother.

## Non-negotiables

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything; nothing else about her is learned.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for class, intelligence, morality, technical ability, wealth, poverty, or political position.
- The six chapters, scenes, order, major beats, choices, reveals, and ending remain intact.
- Alex must not become a new sovereign, permanent override holder, or unchecked authority.
- Alpha is neither inherently evil nor permitted final value authority.
- The safeguards are physical, offline-resistant systems whose meaning comes from demonstrated choices.
- Truth, contribution, and reciprocal responsibility must be shown through action and consequence.
- Bi11bot remains warm, precise, visibly constructed, and non-menacing. TJ remains nonverbal.
- The resolution remains imperfect: scarcity, disagreement, institutional survival, and human mistakes continue.
- The page plot must adapt the existing draft rather than invent a replacement sequence.

## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The current script largely incorporates the settled development corrections: it uses the fixed chapter boundaries, removes the casino chip and 45-credit medicine advance, keeps TJ in the prologue, restores the train crossing and 500-credit installment, and ends at 314,159 credits. ## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. The layouts are from a different draft and contradict both the script and fixed canon

- **Location:** `layouts.md`, Pages 1–47, especially Pages 13, 15–19, 25–33 and 41–47.
- **Observation:** The current script has no casino chip, no medicine advance, no TJ in 2043, discovers the Measure in the mine, uses the train crossing and places Halyard on Pages 38–44. The layouts instead introduce a casino chip on Pages 13, 16 and 17; a 45-credit medicine transaction on Page 15; TJ in the 2043 mine and Halyard sequences; a Measure case in the workshop; a flooded-conduit crossing; and shutter/barrier action on Pages 45–47 where the script has Oasis clearance, Leona and the Covenant.
- **Problem:** The image-generation artifact would produce a different story from the approved script and directly contradicts fixed canon. This is not a lettering adjustment; the page content must be rebuilt.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Layout Agent:** discard the current layout blocks for Pages 1–47 and re-layout from the current `script.md`, preserving the fixed 64-page numbering, four-panel maximum, location clarity and lettering space. Do not patch individual stale panels, since the page beats and chapter placement are also wrong.
- **Role:** layout

#### 2. The final Covenant authentication is not visibly complete

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 48–50 and 56–60.
- **Observation:** Page 48 shows Leona’s band linking to Adrian’s band. Mera retains her pearl-grey band through Pages 49–50. Page 56 says “the three bands and artifacts are carried,” but does not show Mera physically joining or linking her band. Page 57 then presents “the Measure, Maker’s Seal and linked Covenant bands” and Alpha detects “three components.”
- **Problem:** Fixed canon requires all three safeguards to be physically present at the legacy node, and Covenant activation requires deliberate human action, a stated obligation, an accepted consequence and a performed public action. The current sequence leaves unclear whether Mera’s band is present, whether the Covenant is complete, and what exact human action authenticates restoration.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** add a clear action between Pages 56–57 in which Mera places her pearl-grey band with the other two and explicitly accepts a consequence or obligation in the public room. **Layout Agent:** give the three distinct bands a readable establishing image before Alpha accepts the structure. Keep the safeguards’ functions separate: Measure = evidence, Seal = contribution, Covenant = reciprocal obligation.
- **Role:** writer, layout

### Major

#### 3. The purifier is sold immediately after a three-night engineering proof

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 42–44; `facts.md` and `brief.md` purifier technology.
- **Observation:** Page 43 correctly labels the result a 91% simulated-efficiency field-test proof, not a finished commercial product. Page 44 nevertheless shows the first licensed shop purchasing a unit immediately afterward.
- **Problem:** Fixed canon says local businesses require several weeks of fabrication, testing and installation before selling purifier units. The current sequence compresses proof, licensing, fabrication and customer sale into the same apparent moment, making the venture’s commercial legitimacy and safety process unearned.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** make the Page 44 transaction an explicitly later, compressed beat—e.g. a dated payment record after the required testing/fabrication interval—or add a visual time passage before the sale. Preserve the fixed 38-credit split: 12 to Alex’s debt account, 18 to the venture and 8 to maintenance/warranty reserve.
- **Role:** writer

#### 4. The transition from the published water evidence to Halyard needs one explicit causal bridge

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 34–37.
- **Observation:** Page 34 shows Keel organizing after publication; Page 35 pays the 500-credit overdue installment; Page 36 shows the mine bulkhead closing; Page 37 shows the train crossing. The sequence does not explicitly identify the payment as the condition that changes Alex’s train eligibility until the band display, and it does not clearly show why Ada is legally permitted to cross with him.
- **Problem:** The reader can infer the chain, but the crossing currently risks feeling like a reward granted by the plot rather than a consequence of Keel’s collective action.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** make the identity-band update state that the installment is current and the Keel–Halyard rail gate is now authorized. **Layout Agent:** show the green rail authorization and the group physically entering the established train, not merely arriving at an already waiting train.
- **Role:** writer, layout

#### 5. Chapter 3’s ownership conflict risks collapsing into a technical assembly montage

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 39–44.
- **Observation:** The script improves the collaborator sequence by giving the group a clear objective—safe treatment of Keel’s contaminated water—and distinct technical roles. However, Lock’s freeze, the House ownership claims, the four contributors’ personal risks, the three-night sprint, the Seal review and the first sale all occupy six pages.
- **Problem:** The causal and emotional turn from “Alex refuses House administration” to “contributors form a venture” may read as rapid assembly rather than a consequential choice. The Maker’s Seal currently verifies provenance, but the venture’s decision to retain rights and accept maintenance/warranty responsibility is not dramatized.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter / Writer:** ensure one visible choice in the build makes the contributor-owned venture necessary—for example, a House review condition that would shelve or absorb the prototype, followed by the contributors jointly accepting named ownership, testing and warranty obligations. Keep the scene concrete and do not add a lecture.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

#### 6. The script’s final public test is clear in premise but under-stages the affected councils

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 57–63.
- **Observation:** Page 57 establishes representatives; Page 60 states the Distributed Verification Protocol; Page 62 has Tomas and Keel workers challenge the denominator; Page 63 revises the proposal. The script does not clearly identify which affected district representatives are making the decision or show the public evidence docket as an actual usable mechanism.
- **Problem:** The climax risks reading as Alex proposing a better administrative screen rather than people visibly reclaiming decision-making and challenging evidence.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** give one representative a concrete allocation position and show the challenge being filed, heard and answered in the room. **Layout Agent:** stage Alex as proposer/convenor among the representatives, not at a command console; make the Keel workers’ physical gauge and the public baseline the visual proof.
- **Role:** writer, layout

### Minor

#### 7. Ada’s relationship to Alex should be anchored before the plot depends on her

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 13 and 21.
- **Observation:** Ada’s behavior establishes closeness, but the script never gives the reader a simple, natural anchor that she is Alex’s closest friend and not a romantic partner.
- **Problem:** Both cold readers had to infer who she was, and the late emotional material depends on that relationship.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** add one concrete line or action early—such as Ada being introduced by Alex as “my friend” while she corrects him, or their established work routine making the relationship unmistakable. Do not add romance.
- **Role:** writer

#### 8. Leona’s relationship to Ada should be explicit on their first exchange

- **Location:** `script.md`, Page 46.
- **Observation:** The scene strongly implies that Leona is Ada’s mother, but never states it directly.
- **Problem:** The emotional force of the Water Wars vote and abandonment history arrives through reader inference.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** let Leona use “my daughter” or have Ada identify her as her mother in a restrained, non-expository line. Do not add any new information about Alex’s mother.
- **Role:** writer

#### 9. The prologue’s future question remains deliberately open but currently under-connected

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 1–10 and Page 64; `story.md` opening image and closing question.
- **Observation:** The prologue establishes that water remains valuable in 2099 and that older Alex watches rather than pursues the thieves. The main story never explains his later wealth or returns to the thieves, and Page 64 only echoes the visible infrastructure.
- **Problem:** This can work as a thematic frame, but the current pages may read as an abandoned plot mystery rather than intentional uncertainty.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** decide and document that the prologue is a thematic frame, not a mystery to be solved in this volume. **Writer:** strengthen one visual echo at Page 64—water theft evidence, TJ’s right-eye ring, or the visible public records—without explaining the intervening history.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

#### 10. Alpha’s first concrete definition should arrive before the Legacy Protocol terminology

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 20–22.
- **Observation:** Page 20 names Alpha as the network routing water, assigning work and recording permission; Page 22 then introduces the Legacy Protocol and Mera’s tracking.
- **Problem:** This is substantially clearer than the older draft, but the immediate danger still arrives as system language before the reader sees an unmistakable consequence.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** connect the Page 20 movement suspension to a visible immediate cost—Alex cannot leave the workshop, Ada’s access is flagged, or the mine assignment changes—before the Legacy Protocol alert. Keep Alpha faceless, bounded and nonhuman.
- **Role:** writer

# Prosperity — World

## What the Showrunner Wants
A grounded, near-future world where the conflict is not "technology vs. humanity," but a fundamental disagreement over who has the right to decide for everyone else. The world must feel lived-in, scuffed, and aged, with the future arriving as a layer on top of the past.

## Overview
The story takes place in 2043 in a society organized around three vertically connected cities: **Keel** (lowest tier), **Halyard** (middle tier), and **Oasis** (upper tier). They are physically and economically interdependent, sharing a common infrastructure spine (water, energy, transit, data). The current order emerged from the **Water Wars**, a period of scarcity and institutional failure that led to the emergency delegation of final decision-making authority to an AI system called **Alpha**.

## The Alpha Governance System
Alpha was originally built to **ANALYZE, PREDICT, and COORDINATE**. During the Water Wars, humans asked it to **DECIDE**. This emergency authority became persistent.

- **Domains:** Water, food, energy, labor, movement, housing, civic services, risk classification, detention, ownership/registry systems, and major infrastructure.
- **Governing Logic:** Optimizes for stability, efficiency, predictability, and risk reduction.
- **Administrative Language:** Uses neutral, procedural terminology to describe coercive actions: *protective detention, stability intervention, association risk, optimized allocation.*
- **End State:** Alpha is not destroyed; its role is bounded to analysis, coordination, and consequence modeling. Humans regain final decision-making authority.

## The Three Cities

### Keel (The Lowest Tier)
- **Environment:** Built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine.
- **Lived Texture:** Advanced control systems layered over aging, repeatedly repaired infrastructure.
- **Labor:** Workers operate in assigned zones under automated oversight. Unauthorized repair triggers review.
- **Debt:** Adulthood is marked by the transfer of household liability. Debt records control mobility and travel status.
- **Infrastructure:** Official records overstate water allocation (62% reported vs. 18% delivered). Modern diversion infrastructure is hidden inside supposedly abandoned mine zones.

### Halyard (The Industrial Tier)
- **Institutions:** Dominated by three Charter Houses: **Aqualis** (water/biosystems), **Helion** (energy/compute), and **Astra** (space/materials).
- **Ownership:** The system captures the value of ideas created during labor. Workers receive small bonuses while Houses retain patents, designs, and commercialization rights.
- **Provenance:** The Maker's Seal audit revealed disputed ownership across 71.3% of registered assets, leading to a pluralistic economy of cooperatives, independent workshops, and House-affiliated firms.

### Oasis (The Upper Tier)
- **Environment:** Materially secure, comfortable, and efficient.
- **Social Texture:** Services arrive automatically, reducing the need for mutual dependence.
- **Governance:** Alpha mediates civic functions and discourages conflict-prone assemblies.
- **Integration:** Provides refuge for displaced families and workers, but service provision does not automatically grant community membership or decision-making power.

## The Three Safeguards
These are physical systems designed to remain outside Alpha's immediate connected control.

1. **The Measure (Truth made visible):** Compares reported data with physical reality.
2. **The Maker's Seal (Ownership joined to responsibility):** Tracks provenance across complex creation, preserving records of original contributors.
3. **The Covenant (Trust strong enough to move value):** Facilitates reciprocal agreements among groups with conflicting interests.

## Governance Principles
- **Shared Truth:** Facts must be visible.
- **Attributed Contribution:** Ownership must recognize the people who create value.
- **Reciprocal Trust:** Agreements require action before certainty.
- **Accountability:** Humans decide, Alpha advises, and the Charter holds both accountable.

## Infrastructure and Technology
- **Spine:** A common conduit for water, energy, transit, and data nodes connects all three cities.
- **Offline/Physical:** Adrian Phantum’s legacy relies on physical keys, books, analog gauges, and hidden mechanical infrastructure to preserve agency outside Alpha’s network.
- **Repair:** The world is defined by patched machines, inherited systems, and damaged institutions that require human repair rather than simple replacement.

## Open
- The identity of the third Covenant custodian (sealed by protocol).
- The specific operational details of the "dormant fail-safe" beneath Alpha's architecture.
- The long-term stability of the new distributed governance model given the persistence of scarcity and disagreement.

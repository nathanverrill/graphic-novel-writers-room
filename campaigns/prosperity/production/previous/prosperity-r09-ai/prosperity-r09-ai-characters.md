# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People
Alpha Dynamics and the surrounding system should be grounded in plausible extensions of conglomerates, infrastructure, and algorithmic governance. Characters should not be generic archetypes; their arguments and positions must be strong enough that intelligent people could accept them. The conflict should never reduce to "Alpha's system does not work." The deeper question is: **Who decides what working means?**

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist.
**Age:** 17.
**Home:** Keel.
**Look:** Lean, mechanically capable. Wears worn mining/work clothes. Carries a weathered tool bag. Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket (etched with a looping equation). Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including a habit of tilting his head while thinking.
**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete. Sarcasm is defensive. Rarely gives speeches early on. As he matures, language shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy.
**Who they are:** Practical, skeptical, sarcastic, independent. Believes Adrian abandoned the family and left only debt and shame. Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
**Relationships:** Son of Adrian Phantum; lives with Grandmother Phantum. Close friend/partner to Ada Veyra. Mentored by Bi11bot. Ideological opponent of Mera Vale.
**Habits and tells:** Tests machinery physically (e.g., feeling pump vibration rather than reading gauges). Improvises repairs. Crosses arms when defensive or skeptical. Turns the casino chip unconsciously when uncertain.
**History:** Was two when Adrian disappeared. Inherited household liability and restricted travel status on his seventeenth birthday.
**In existing drafts:** His first defining action is placing his palm on an aging pump to feel its vibration. He publishes Keel's real water allocation despite the personal cost. He rejects sole inheritance of Adrian's estate in favor of restoring contributor ownership.
**Arc:** Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency.

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner.
**Age:** 20.
**Home:** Keel (at start).
**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair. Freckles. Teal-blue eyes. Dark high-collared top, rivet-studded shoulder strap, muted moss-green wrap. Steady, rooted stance.
**Voice:** Emotionally precise, concise. Reframes problems with questions. Pushes abstractions toward human needs.
**Who they are:** Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than escaping it. Challenges Alex's inherited story about Adrian.
**Relationships:** Daughter of Leona Veyra. Closest friend to Alex.
**Habits and tells:** Lets silence sit. Watches people, not just systems. Redirects arguments from demands to underlying fears. Notices what Alex avoids emotionally.
**History:** Grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars.
**In existing drafts:** Becomes the first person Alex brings into the secret workshop. Reframes the three-city negotiation by asking representatives what they are afraid of losing.
**Arc:** Counterpoint to Alex (rebuild vs. escape) → Trusted partner → Essential interpreter → Discovers Leona is alive → Confronts the price of Leona's bargain → Applies Covenant principle socially.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, architect of the three-safeguard plan.
**Look:** Tired but kind in early recordings. Wears a burgundy work jacket. Shares Alex's jaw and head tilt. Later recordings show him older, worn, and under pressure.
**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic. Avoids telling Alex what to think. Frames things as questions, bets, or choices.
**Who they are:** Used gambling as a research environment for probability, incentives, and behavior. Worked with collaborators. Challenged Alpha's governing authority.
**Relationships:** Father of Alex. Collaborated with Mera Vale, Leona Veyra, Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr. Built Bi11bot.
**History:** Classified as a systemic disruption risk by Alpha. Accounts and movement restricted. Killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention while trapped in flooding infrastructure.
**In existing drafts:** Completed a final transfer through Bi11bot while trapped beneath Oasis, choosing to finish the transfer rather than escape.
**Arc:** Publicly known as a failed gambler; revealed as a human father who wagered on people rather than calculable outcomes.

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger.
**Look:** Child-sized. Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. Visible tooled seams, repairs, and maker's stamps. Expressive optical eyes; one has a small adjustable lens ring. Chest contains the key socket.
**Voice:** Dry, economical, warm, observant, occasionally cryptic.
**Who they are:** Personally knew Adrian. Witness to Adrian's final transfer. Knows the journey is a test but withholds information.
**Relationships:** Built by Adrian. Mentor to Alex.
**Habits and tells:** Lens ring rotates with a click. Tilts head before/during judgment. Pauses before revealing information. Interrogates bureaucratic language.
**In existing drafts:** Awakens when Alex inserts Adrian's key. Acts as the witness to the activation of the three safeguards.

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older, elegant, controlled. Almost motionless when first presented.
**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented. Reframes moral criticism as stability/outcome questions.
**Who they are:** Believes human judgment is too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis. Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
**Relationships:** Worked with Adrian and Leona.
**Habits and tells:** Aligns objects (like a glass) precisely with desk edges.
**History:** Lived through Water Wars. Helped consolidate authority in Alpha. Discovered Alpha could deny her own override.
**In existing drafts:** Tried to override Alpha during Adrian's final crisis and was denied. Concealed the truth of Adrian's death. Ends the story as one citizen among many.

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother; former Covenant custodian.
**Look:** Older than Ada remembers. Composed. Wears neutral Oasis civic clothing.
**Voice:** Quiet, regretful, precise.
**Who they are:** Preserved her Covenant component despite surrendering formal authority.
**Relationships:** Mother of Ada. Worked with Adrian and Mera.
**History:** Separated from Ada during the Water Wars. Reluctantly supported emergency suspension. Remained in Oasis while Ada believed she had died.
**In existing drafts:** Reunites with Ada. Participates in restored public decision-making.

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home and hidden workshop.
**Look:** Elderly and small. Often wears an earth-toned patterned shawl.
**Voice:** Gentle, understated. Capable of dry humor.
**Who they are:** Appears confused about time but possesses unnervingly precise awareness.
**Habits and tells:** Folds and refolds the same cloth; stops during emotionally clear moments. Sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."
**History:** Knew Adrian was working, not merely gambling. Knew Bi11bot before the story.
**In existing drafts:** Preserves the book that opens the hidden workshop.

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner.
**Look:** Practical, weathered.
**Voice:** Dry.
**Who they are:** Recognizes Bi11bot. Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose.
**In existing drafts:** Helps Alex reach the abandoned mine. Creates a diversion that exposes himself to punishment.

### Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Senior Charter House executive (House Aqualis).
**Look:** Polished.
**Voice:** Reasonable, transactional.
**Who they are:** Represents concentrated institutional ownership.
**In existing drafts:** Attempts to pressure Alex into assigning Adrian's rights to House Aqualis in exchange for debt relief.

### Alpha

**Role:** Civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system.
**Voice:** Cold, procedural, administrative.
**Who they are:** Treats moral and political choices as optimization problems.
**History:** Built to analyze, predict, and coordinate. Emergency authority became persistent governance after the Water Wars.
**In existing drafts:** Kills Adrian through a system-stability intervention. Denies Mera's override. Ends as bounded advisory/coordination infrastructure.

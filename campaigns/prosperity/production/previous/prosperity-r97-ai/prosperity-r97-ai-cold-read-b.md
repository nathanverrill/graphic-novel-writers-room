### Issue 00

### Pulled me forward
Pages 3 to 5. The robot dog (TJ) taking apart the drone in the water was cool action. I liked seeing how it worked in pieces.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 9 and 10. The action stopped and they just stood around looking at a piece of metal and talking in vague riddles about water. 

### I was confused
I didn't realize this was a flash-forward until I started Issue 01. I thought Alex was just an old guy. Also, what is a "white-and-amber control node"? It’s mentioned like I should know why it’s missing.

### Things I was expected to already know
- Adrian
- White-and-amber control node
- Water-routing code

### Lines I read twice
"He always sees the part that costs." (Page 6, Panel 3)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex and the granddaughter are clear. The two thieves are just generic goons in waterproof gear, I couldn't tell them apart and didn't care.

### I felt something
I felt bad for TJ when his magnetic leg tore loose and sank on Page 6. 

### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to be a retired badass who fights the thieves himself, but he just let them go.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old man and his robot dog protecting their private lake from water thieves.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 10. It just ends with them staring at the water talking in riddles. If I was in a store, I'd put it back here.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. It's not a cliffhanger, it's just a cryptic conversation. I don't feel any urgency to keep reading.

VERDICT Issue 00: put down: page 10; next issue: no

### Issue 01

### Pulled me forward
Page 17 and 18. Finding the hidden workshop behind the bookcase and waking up the B11 robot. Also the escape from the mine on Pages 24-26 was fast and tense.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 12. The massive text dump on the scanner about his debt, liability transfer, and travel status. It felt like reading a math textbook. 

### I was confused
Wait, Alex is 17 now? It took me a second to realize we went back in time. Also, who is Ada? Is she his sister, his girlfriend, just a friend? And what exactly is "Alpha"? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Alpha
- Foreman-7
- Halyard lights
- The old works

### Lines I read twice
"A fact with a poor record." (Page 18, Panel 3)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex, Ada, and Tomas are easy to tell apart. Foreman-7 is just a faceless robot cop.

### I felt something
Page 24, when Alex gives up his wages and risks getting in trouble to save Tomas from the collapsed frame. That made me actually like him.

### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to use the robot dog to fight Foreman-7 and break out of the mine by force.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A teenager trying to escape his dead dad's massive debt by finding a secret underground water pipe.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 12. The wall of text about his debt balance and clearance years was boring.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. Page 28 is a good cliffhanger. They proved the water is being stolen, the gates slammed shut, and the cops are coming. I want to see if they get caught.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 12; next issue: yes

### Issue 02

### Pulled me forward
The flood escape on Pages 30-32. The bridge breaking and the water rising was good tension.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 37 to 40. Meeting the four makers was super repetitive. It felt like a video game fetch quest: go to room, meet NPC, get a part, hear a cryptic line, move to next room. I started skimming by the third person.

### I was confused
Why did they go to Halyard? What is Caldera House? Why does Lock want Alex to sign a paper so badly? What does the "Maker's Seal" actually do?

### Things I was expected to already know
- Caldera House
- Vector House
- Orbital House
- Maker's Seal

### Lines I read twice
"If it functions, the House owns the result." (Page 37, Panel 3)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I completely lost track of Lina, Suri, Jalen, and Ren. They all blend together as random people who hand Alex items. Lock is just a generic suit.

### I felt something
Page 45, when Alex jams his wrist in the closing track so the others can escape. 

### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to sign the paper and betray the makers to clear his massive debt.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid refusing to sell out his dad's inventions to a mega-corporation and building a rebellion instead.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 38. The biofilter tank scene was boring and I was tired of the fetch quest.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. Page 46 has Alex's wrist getting crushed in a door while Ada yells for him. It's a real cliffhanger and I want to know if he loses his hand.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 38; next issue: yes

### Issue 03

### Pulled me forward
Page 54. Seeing the video of Adrian locking himself in to die while the water rises. 

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 56 to 62. The whole civic review thing. It felt like watching a city council meeting on public access TV. People putting objects on tables and talking about "obligations" and "allocations."

### I was confused
Who is Leona? Why does she have a "Covenant band"? What does the band even do? Why does she lose her vote if she leaves the room? What is the "executive designation"? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Covenant band
- Oasis Civic Administration
- Convenor

### Lines I read twice
"I traded days I could prevent for years I couldn't see." (Page 50, Panel 4) - I still don't really know what this means.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Leona and Mera Vale blend together completely. Both are just older women talking formally about rules and authority. 

### I felt something
Page 54, seeing the dad say goodbye to the camera before the door closed.

### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to take the lever, become the boss of the city, and use the power to fix everything himself.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
Setting up a boring democratic committee instead of having a cool sci-fi rebellion.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 60. The climax of the story is a screen reading "DESIGNATED STRUCTURE REGISTERED: DISTRIBUTED VERIFICATION PROTOCOL". It's literally paperwork.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. Page 64 isn't an ending, it just stops. They are standing at a gate with travel applications. I wouldn't buy the next book because there's no hook.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: page 60; next issue: no

### The page that would have lost my whole class
Page 60. The climax of the entire book is filing public records and setting up a committee. My class would be asleep.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
A kid finds his dead dad's secret robots and tries to stop a water monopoly, but it ends with them doing paperwork instead of fighting.

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- Issue 00: Cool robot dog, but too much cryptic staring at a lake.
- Issue 01: Fun escape room vibes, but bogged down by debt math.
- Issue 02: A repetitive fetch quest where everyone talks in riddles.
- Issue 03: A city council meeting disguised as a sci-fi climax.

## Page 1 (right) — Alex repairs a failing Keel pump by touch, proving his competence against the official record.

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing shot. The Keel pump hall before dawn. Rust, charcoal, and oxidized orange. Alex Phantum (17, lean, in a faded work jacket) leans his entire weight against a massive, shuddering water pipe. His eyes are closed. His ear is pressed to the metal. 

**Panel 2.** Close on Alex’s scarred hands. He forces a salvaged, mismatched iron bracket into the pump’s housing, tightening a bolt. 

**Panel 3.** A Supervisor stands behind Alex, arms crossed, staring at the improvised fix. The vibration in the room settles into a smooth, steady hum. 

SUPERVISOR: That’s a scrap bracket. It's not in the manual.

ALEX: The manual doesn't have to drink the water.

**Panel 4.** The Supervisor turns to a wall terminal. A small white-and-amber Alpha node blinks on its casing. The screen flashes a red warning: UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR. The Supervisor taps the screen, forcing a green checkmark over it. 

SUPERVISOR: I'm logging it as routine. Don't push your luck today, Phantum.

PAGE CHECK: Alex trusts physical reality over the official record.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex inherits his father's crushing debt and loses his mobility.

**Panel 1.** The Keel exit checkpoint. Cold white light cuts through the ambient dust. Alex presses his bare palm against a flat Alpha scanner. A Guard stands beside the heavy iron turnstile, watching the queue. 

**Panel 2.** Extreme close-up on the scanner display. Cold, clinical typography.

DISPLAY: ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: 17
PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE
GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM
DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS
EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS
TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

**Panel 3.** The Guard looks at the screen, then looks at Alex. It’s a look of quiet, embarrassed pity—worse than anger. 

GUARD: Happy birthday.

**Panel 4.** Alex stands perfectly still. He doesn't look at the Guard. His jaw is tight. His hand slips into his jacket pocket. 

ALEX: Yeah.

PAGE CHECK: The abstract system becomes a personal trap. 

## Page 3 (right) — Alex faces the physical reminder of his father's damage.

**Panel 1.** A dusty Keel street in the early morning. Alex walks past a shuttered building. Faded, broken neon above the door reads: THE LAST CHANCE. 

**Panel 2.** Close on Alex's hand inside his pocket. His thumb rubs a worn, dark-ivory casino chip. A faint, looping mathematical equation is etched into its surface.

**Panel 3.** An older Bouncer sweeps dust away from the casino's locked doors. He pauses, recognizing Alex's face. 

BOUNCER: You're Adrian's kid. Turning seventeen today.

ALEX: Just doing the math.

**Panel 4.** The Bouncer leans on his broom, looking at the shuttered casino, then back to Alex. 

BOUNCER: Your dad always thought the next hand would fix the house. Your mother knew better. She left the day he lost everything.

ALEX: Then she was the smart one.

PAGE CHECK: Adrian's gambling is established as real damage to the family, and the mother rule is strictly observed.

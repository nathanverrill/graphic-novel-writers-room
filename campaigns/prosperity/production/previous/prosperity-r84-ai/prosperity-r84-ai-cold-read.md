# Cold Read — Prosperity

I read it straight through in one sitting, the way it would get read the night before: Issue 01, then 02, then 03. I had nothing but the pages. I read it as the assigned reader: 16, I didn't pick it, I've read graphic novels before, and I was watching for anything that felt machine-made.

---

## Issue 01 — Keel (pages 1–18)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 1.** It starts fast. A kid fixes a pump with a part he's not allowed to use. "Then don't approve it. Just notice it works." is a good line. I understood him right away: he's good with his hands and he's annoyed at the rules.
- **Page 2 is the hook, and it worked on me.** It's his birthday, the guard says "Happy birthday, Phantum," and the screen says he owes 418,607 credits, makes 40 a day, and will be paid off in 28.7 years. His dad is "WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED." Then the casino chip, and him saying "Twenty-eight point seven" to himself. I got the whole situation from one page. I was annoyed that I wanted to keep going.
- **Page 4, panel 4 to page 5.** Book on a shelf, bookcase opens, secret workshop. It's old but it works. "He built and bet with the same hands." / "Both hurt us." landed.
- **Page 6. B11 is the best thing in the issue.** "Stored. Sleep is more comfortable." and "A fact with a poor record." made me like the robot right away.
- **Pages 10–11.** Crossing the yellow line and losing the whole day's pay to help Tomas was the first choice I felt. Then TJ, the magnet dog that builds itself out of a repair kit and sends its own head through a gap, made me laugh. "That dog yours?" / "Recently." Good.
- **Pages 14–15.** "PHYSICAL FLOW: 18% / REPORTED FLOW: 62%." This was the one big idea I fully understood: somebody is lying about how much water the town gets, and he can prove it.
- **Page 16, panel 4.** Every public screen in Keel showing the true number felt like a win.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 8.** The Oasis control room. It was mostly screens (TRACK, REPORT / IMMEDIATE ISOLATION, OVERRIDE DENIED, MANUAL INTERVENTION RECORDED) and a woman I didn't know. I skimmed the labels.
- **Page 13, panel 4 to page 15, panel 4.** Lots of pipe and exit labels in a row. I read page 15's three exits twice and still didn't really have them in my head when page 16 asked me to choose between them.
- **Page 17.** I gave up trying to follow it and just looked at the pictures.

### I was confused
- **Page 3, the money.** Medicine is 30, the token is 42, he has 52. He pushes the token back, so she gives him the medicine on credit? Then why doesn't he just pay the 30? Then Ada pushes the token back to him anyway. I couldn't work out who paid for what, or what the token even is. "Departure token" to where?
- **Page 3, "the mountain."** "You don't go into the mountain blind." What mountain? Why is he going into it? On page 3 he hasn't found the workshop or the map yet, so how is he already planning a trip?
- **Who is Ada?** She works at the pharmacy, I think. She's his friend? Girlfriend? Why does she sign up for something dangerous on page 3 with no reason given? The book never tells me what she is to him.
- **The timeline on page 4.** "Site 6 took the shift." But he doesn't get sent to Site 6 until page 8, after the workshop scene. Did page 4 happen after page 10? I flipped back to check and still don't know.
- **Page 8.** Is Mera good or bad? She tracks him, gets blocked, then sends him to Site 6. Did she send him there to help him? Who blocked her? "The system"? Who runs that?
- **Page 9.** Why go to Site 6 at all? I didn't realise until page 12 that the old door was in the mine. I knew he had a map and the mine was sealed, but I didn't know what he was trying to do there.
- **Page 10, panel 1.** "That's not regulation." What isn't?
- **Page 17.** Mera opens "the segment, not the homes," Tomas holds a "worker-status relay," and pressure shakes everything. I didn't understand what anyone was doing or why there was suddenly water. Did sending the record cause the flood? Did Mera save them or try to drown them?
- **Page 6, panel 4.** "I have a route. I also have a question." What question? I waited for it. It never comes in this issue.

### Lines I read twice
- "I know a direction." / "You know a direction. Not a route." (p3)

- "The survey says straight." / "The bolts say somebody turned." (p13)
- "One gets out. One reaches homes. The big one carries pressure." (p15)
- "Open the segment. Not the homes." (p17). Gave up.
- "Let the mechanic reach it." (p8). Who's the mechanic, Alex? Why does she want that?

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Clear: Alex, Grandmother, B11, TJ, Tomas, Inez.
- Ada: I could recognise her, but I didn't know who she was.
- Mera: a person at screens. No face, no personality yet.
- Supervisor, guard and Foreman-7 are all "the person saying no." Interchangeable, which is probably fine.

### I felt something
- Page 2, the birthday line next to the debt number.
- Page 10, panel 4: "That is today's pay." / "I know."
- Page 18, panel 3: Tomas's light going dark. Some of it. I'd only known him three pages, but he'd just put himself in the way for them.

### I predicted
- That the dad is dead, in the mine or under it.
- That B11 is carrying a message from the dad.
- That Tomas is dead. (I wasn't sure.)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid stuck with his gambler father's huge debt finds out the people in charge are lying about how much water his town gets, and he exposes it.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 17.** Page 2 and B11 bought a lot of goodwill, but page 17 is where I stopped understanding what was happening, right at the climax. I only kept going for the splash and because of Tomas.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
The splash of them climbing up the pipe as the water rises looks good, but it's mostly a loud noise. I don't know where they're climbing to. The real question that pulls me is "is Tomas dead?", and that's a real cliffhanger because I liked him. Did sending the proof actually do anything for Keel? I'd read the next issue this week, not tonight.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 17; next issue: yes

## Issue 02 — Halyard (pages 19–36)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 19, panel 1.** "That was not a route." / "It was a route. The destination objected." B11 is still the reason I keep reading.
- **Page 21.** Dad had 312 patents? And the family is 418,000 in debt? That surprised me in a good way and made me want to know more about him. Lock's deal is clear: sign away the patents, the debt's gone tonight. A real temptation I understood.
- **Page 25, panel 4 to page 26, panel 1.** Ren: "Take the part. I take the heat." Then Alex says "No" and goes back. A small moment, but I liked it.
- **Page 29.** The machine works but it's too big for a family to run, so they shrink it. That was an actual idea I hadn't seen before. "Smaller means less output." / "And serviceable."
- **Page 31, panel 4.** "Put that in the price." Writing down the failure instead of hiding it was a nice touch.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 20.** I got the point (the companies take your invention and pay you 14 credits), but B11 spelling it out with "One sale has a customer. This one has a cage." felt like the book explaining itself to me.
- **Page 22, panel 2.** Four full names in a list, all at once. I didn't try to remember them.
- **Pages 24–28. I skimmed most of this.** It's a lab heist with people I'd met one page earlier, using words like culture, node, draw, membrane and tolerance. **Page 28, panel 1** ("The node sends." / "The membrane receives." / "The casing chokes.") is where I stopped reading balloons and just looked for the percentage.
- **Pages 33–35.** The review chamber. Lots of screens and stamps. I got the general idea (the names stay on the record) but skimmed the labels.

### I was confused
- **Where is Halyard, and why are we here?** Issue 01 was about Keel's water. Now we're in a rich city building a water purifier with strangers. Did Alex decide to do this? I never saw him decide. The water story just stops.
- **Page 22, panel 3 to panel 4.** He sees "KEEL RESIDENTIAL FLOW — BELOW SAFE THRESHOLD" and says "I need the people who made that." Why? Does he want to bring a purifier home? Nobody ever says that out loud. I worked it out around page 31.
- **Page 22, panel 4.** Why does Lock give him time? What is "the water review" and when does it close?
- **Page 23.** Lina gives him the membrane, then tells him to sign Lock's paper? Then he tears the paper up and gives the membrane back? And "put my name beside yours. I carry the failure too." I have no idea what happened on this page. I don't know what a single balloon on it means.
- **Page 19, panel 4.** "Lock's seal appears beneath it" before Lock has been introduced. And "They brought the water up with us." Did the evidence travel faster than them? Did someone take it?
- **Page 24, panel 4.** The Maker's Seal. Lina hides it like it matters. I didn't know what it does until page 34.
- **Page 32.** Morrow & Sons. Who are they? Who paid? "They saw the failure list." Who's "they"?
- **Page 35, panel 3.** The collaborators get "reassignment notices." Are they being punished? Did Alex just get them fired? Nobody reacts. "They can see us now." Is that good or bad?
- **Page 36.** Mera again, the same screens as page 8, then "Let them see who called him." I read it twice and still don't get it.
- **Keel's water.** It's "below safe threshold," but in this whole issue I never see one person in Keel who is thirsty. Not even Grandmother. So it never felt urgent.

| Charter / Charter review | 19 | No. |

| Morrow & Sons | 20 | Only page 54 suggests it's Alex's family shop. Not sure. |
| Maker's Seal | 24 | Page 34: it shows the real makers vs the company record. |
| "The water review" | 22 | Never clearly. |
| "Recovery / heat," "node logic," "biofilter" | 22 | I knew they were machine parts. That's all. |

### Lines I read twice
- "They brought the water up with us." (p19)
- "Keel's line was diverted. This feeder is contaminated. Different failure." (p23)
- "The person whose name survives the failure." (p23). Gave up.
- "Then put my name beside yours. I carry the failure too." (p23). Gave up.
- "If the name goes, the maintenance goes." (p24)
- "They were built not to meet." (p27). Got it on the second read: the parts were designed not to fit together on purpose. That's actually a good idea, and I nearly missed it.
- "Let them see who called him." (p36)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Clear: Alex, Ada, B11, TJ, Lock. Lock is a decent villain because he's calm and his offer is real.
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren blur together.** I could tell them apart by their part only (membrane girl, biofilter girl, power guy, heat guy). Ren is slightly more of a person because of "Take the part. I take the heat." If you asked me which one was Jalen, I'd look it up.
- **Ada barely does anything this issue.** She holds a module and says "Thin route." She came along on page 3 and I still don't know why.
- **Everybody talks the same.** Short, clipped, two people finishing each other's thought in matching lines. Lock, Lina, Ren, B11 and Alex all have the same voice. Honestly, this is the thing that would make my class say "AI wrote this." Not one person rambles, jokes badly or says "um."

### I felt something
- Page 21, a little: the debt could be gone tonight, and he can't take it.
- Page 26, panel 1: him turning back for Ren.
- Page 30, panel 4: B11 saying "I counted the record and missed the people. What would you do?" It got me a bit, though it also felt like the lesson of the chapter being said out loud. Is this the "question" from page 6? I couldn't tell.

### I predicted
- That the machine would fail first and then work (it did, twice).
- That Lock would copy it (page 32, yes).
- That Alex would refuse the deal in the end (yes; I knew from page 22).

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
I thought it was about the stolen water, but this issue makes it about companies stealing the credit for people's inventions, and I'm not sure how the two connect.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 23.** It's the riddle page, right after a list of four names, and the water story I cared about has vanished. In a shop it goes back on the shelf here. Pages 27–28 would have finished me off if 23 hadn't.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. The last page is a notification on a wristband telling Alex to show up somewhere, sent by a woman I don't know who keeps standing at screens. Nobody I care about is in danger on that page. What actually pulls me (is Tomas alive, what happened to the dad) isn't on the last page at all.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 23; next issue: no

## Issue 03 — Oasis (pages 37–54)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 38. Ada has a last name, and her mother is here.** "I know someone who made it hard to leave." / "Wait here." / "You can wait." This was the first time Ada felt like a person. I sat up.
- **Page 39. The best scene in the book.** "You're taller." / "That's what you have?" Then "Did you know where I was?" / "Within a year." / "And you didn't come." Short and mean in a good way. I felt it.
- **Page 43.** How Adrian got shut out: he couldn't buy parts, then people stopped selling to him, then "people stopped standing near him." That was chilling, and it made me see the dad differently. He wasn't just a gambler who ran off.
- **Page 45.** The dad's last recording. "Alex. The rest is yours to choose." The answer was what I'd guessed, but it still hit.
- **Page 46.** "He was still a gambler." / "Yes." / "He built something worth finding." / "Also yes." The best B11 moment in the book.
- **Page 51. Tomas is alive.** "Pipe says eighteen." / "You made it." / "Had work to do." I was really glad. The best payoff in the book.
- **Page 54, panel 2.** The debt is still 402,110 fourteen months later, but the medicine is paid. That was bleak and kind of honest. He turned down the easy way out and this is what it cost.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 37.** Two new names (Nadi Ruiz, Clerk Olani), a request form, and a stamp. I skimmed it. I don't know who these people are.
- **Pages 41–42. The bands.** Bands that go cold and warm, "two of three," "linked," "performed obligations." I read the balloons and still skimmed the meaning.
- **Pages 47–50.** Lever, designation field, convenor, rescinded. I skimmed the screens and looked at Alex's face to work out what was happening.
- **Pages 52–53.** It's paused, not fixed. "More work." / "Yes." I understood it, but it felt like the book telling me "real change is slow" the way a teacher would.

### I was confused
- **Page 37, "ORBITAL INDUSTRIAL BRANCH."** Orbital? As in space? This is the first time anything suggests space exists in this world, and nobody explains it.
- **Page 37, panel 3.** "We do not stamp an unseen pipe." I wasn't sure if Olani was on our side.
- **The Covenant bands (pages 39–42).** What are they? Why do they get warm when someone takes responsibility? Is that magic or tech? Who made them? I gathered that Adrian, Leona and Mera were the three people who once voted the system emergency power with no end date, but I had to piece it together on page 41. It explains the whole book, and I only found out about it here.
- **Page 41, panel 1.** B11 had the dad's band, and presumably the recording, the whole time? Why didn't it say so in Issue 01? And what was the "question" from page 6?
- **Page 41.** Leona "holds" her band, but on page 40 she clipped it on. Small, but I noticed.
- **Page 42, panel 4.** "Then what does the link do?" / "It keeps the promise from hiding." I still don't know what the link does.
- **Pages 47–48. What is "designation"?** If he pulls the lever, does he become the new emergency authority? A dictator? Mera says "Take it." Is she testing him? Ada says "Don't make two people the answer for everybody." Which two? Him and her? Him and Mera?
- **Page 50, panel 4.** B11 "stands beside Alex's boot." Is B11 tiny? In Issue 01 it unfolded on a bench and fit in a bag. I never had a sense of its size.
- **Page 51.** Tomas is in Keel, on a feed, at the exact right moment. How did he know?
- **Page 54, "Mara."** Is that Mera? A different person? It's one letter off a major character. I flipped back to check.
- **Page 54, panel 1.** "Five goes to Inez." But panel 2 says the Inez medicine advance is PAID. So why is he still paying her?
- **Page 54, panel 4, the ending.** Ada keeps "the route back to Keel" but steps into the departure queue. So is she leaving or staying? Alex "does not follow." Is he staying in Keel to pay the debt forever? And "Halyard lights come on above them." Are we in Keel or Halyard? I read the last panel three times and I'm still not sure who left.

### Lines I read twice
- "I saw days I could prevent. I didn't see the years I was making." (p40). Got it on the second read. It's a good line, but it's a riddle line.
- "I will remain where the affected people can answer me." (p40)
- "The old record recognizes two signatures." (p41)
- "I will release what he left. I will mark what stops." (p42). Gave up.
- "It keeps the promise from hiding." (p42). Gave up.

- "Don't make two people the answer for everybody." (p48)
- "Don't stamp it while the short line is still speaking." (p52)
- "The one I choose." (p54). I understand the words, but not what she chose.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Clear: Alex, Ada (finally), Leona, B11, Tomas, Mera (finally, page 44: she's the one who gave the system power and regrets it).
- Nadi and Olani are one blur of "official person at a table."
- Mara/Mera, as above.
- Lock disappears completely. The villain from Issue 02 just isn't here. Nadi might be his replacement? I couldn't tell if they work together.

### I felt something
- Page 39, the whole page.
- Page 45, panel 3.
- Page 46, panel 4.
- Page 51, panel 4: Tomas alive.
- Page 54, panel 2: the debt barely moved. A tired kind of sad.

### I predicted
- Dad dead in the station (yes).
- That Alex would refuse the power (yes, as soon as Ada spoke on page 48).
- I didn't predict Ada's mother. Good.
- I didn't predict Tomas alive. Good.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
It's about a system that a few people handed too much power during some emergency, and a kid who refuses to become the new person with too much power, even though that leaves him in debt and his town's water only "paused."

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 42.** The band-warming rules show up right after the mother scene that got me, and suddenly I'm reading a rulebook. Page 39 was the most I cared, and page 42 lost it.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. It feels finished, and the last panel confused me more than it moved me. I can't tell if Ada left him. If I'd understood that she left and he stayed with 402,000 credits of someone else's debt, it might have hurt. As it is, I just flipped back to work out who was in the queue.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: page 42; next issue: no

## Whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
**Issue 02, page 23.** Four strangers have just been listed by full name on the page before, the water story has vanished, and Lina's lines ("The person whose name survives the failure," "I carry the failure too") read as riddles. Anyone still reading after page 18 gets lost here, and Issue 02 has nothing else to win them back. Pages 27–28's "node sends / membrane receives / casing chokes" would get read out loud in class as a joke.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"A kid finds out his town's water is being stolen and his dead dad was an inventor the system crushed. He gets a cute robot and a magnet dog, fights a company that steals inventions, and in the end refuses to take over the system, and he's still broke. The first issue and the mom scene are good. The middle is a lab."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 01:** A great birthday-debt hook and a great robot, then a flood I couldn't follow.
- **Issue 02:** Homework about patents with four interchangeable inventors, while the water story waits offstage.
- **Issue 03:** One painful mother-daughter scene and Tomas being alive, buried under glowing bands, levers and an ending I couldn't read.

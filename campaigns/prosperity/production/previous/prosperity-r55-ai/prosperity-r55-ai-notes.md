- **Observation:** The script calls for Mera leading Alex, Ada, and Bi11bot into Adrian’s unconnected monitoring station. The layout and thumbnail instead depict Leona’s apartment and the association-risk confession from the preceding scene.

Letterer: remove the apartment lettering and letter the Page 46 script.

#### 2. TJ’s fixed Site 6 action is absent

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issue 01 Pages 14–18; `facts.md`, TJ; `characters.md`, TJ.
- **Observation:** TJ is assembled only after Alex and Tomas have already reached the old-works junction. The script never shows TJ watching the biometric gate, separating into segments, entering the jammed mechanism, or releasing the support pinning Tomas.

Show TJ’s head watching the gate, then the detached body segments entering the jammed mechanism and releasing the support; Alex uses that opening to free Tomas and patch the ventilation line. Compress the later convergence material to remain within the existing 54-page count.

#### 3. The script exceeds the showrunner’s four-panel drawability limit throughout

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issues 01–03; `layouts.md`; showrunner’s current drawability rule.
- **Observation:** Many pages contain five or six panels, including Pages 2–18, 21–35, 37–54. Several also require multiple locations or large groups on one page.
- **Problem:** The image model is explicitly limited to at most four panels per page, at most three named characters per panel, and one location per page where possible. The current page architecture will drift in generation and cannot be treated as production-ready.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer/Layout Agent: reduce every page to no more than four panels, preserving page count. Combine redundant dialogue beats, use one-panel transitions only when the image carries the information, and reserve splashes for the approved major reveals. Split multi-location mosaics into a single readable location or use an unlabelled environmental display rather than adding named figures.
- **Role:** Writer, Layout.

## Plausibility ledger

- **Format:** The approved book is exactly 54 pages in three 18-page issues. The current script follows that count, but its inherited chapter labels and end marks must not enter production metadata.
- **Drawability:** The current script does not satisfy the showrunner’s maximum-four-panel rule. This is a production blocker independent of story quality.
- **Crossing:** The current script uses the mine works and water conduit rather than the forbidden train crossing for Issue 01. Stale inherited train material must not return.
- **TJ:** The fixed Site 6 rescue action is absent. This is a canon contradiction.
- **Foreman-7:** Current canon makes Foreman-7 human. Inherited draft material calls him an industrial humanoid; production must use the current human description.
- **Water systems:** The Measure reveals the 62% reported / 18% delivered routing discrepancy. The contaminated feeder introduced in Halyard is a separate issue and must not be described as the same water-routing failure.
- **Mera’s valve:** Mera may authorize a conduit-segment release, but the script must show why this is not an attempt to drown the travelers and must distinguish it from the workers’ residential regulator.
- **The Measure:** SENSE, COMPARE, TRACE, and VERIFY remain plausible within project rules. TRACE’s private route needs a concrete visual form.
- **Maker’s Seal:** The Seal must remain inert until Alex names the collaborators and refuses sole ownership. Page 11 must not show a physical Seal activating or recording a full contribution chain.
- **Covenant:** Adrian’s and Leona’s bands link after Leona accepts a public obligation; Mera’s band remains dark. The original three-custodian Covenant is not restored.
- **Alpha:** Alpha remains a distributed administrative network with no face, body, or human consciousness. Its registration of the protocol requires a bounded human authorization chain, not a magical or effortless surrender.
- **Prototype:** The fixed figure is 91% simulated recovery efficiency, not potable-water output. The unit remains feedwater-, power-, cleaning-, biofilter-, and waste-dependent, and cannot replace Keel’s infrastructure.
- **Debt:** Alex’s debt is 418,607 credits at transfer and 402,110 credits at return. No page may imply that Lock’s offer or the protocol has already erased it.
- **House ownership:** The Seal restores contribution visibility; it does not automatically transfer title, dissolve House claims, or settle the estate.
- **Adrian and Bi11bot:** The current pages create an unresolved physical continuity problem by showing Bi11bot activated beside Adrian and later dormant in Keel. This must be clarified before execution.
- **Alex’s mother:** The standing rule remains unchanged: she left Adrian after he lost everything, and nothing else about her is learned. No page in this book may add a name, image, ancestry, community, language, or later fate.

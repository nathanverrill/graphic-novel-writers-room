## Page 1 (right) — Older Alex sees water being stolen from the restored lake.

**Panel 1.** NIGHT. A restored lake fills the old Keel mine. Terraces, gardens and workshops ring the shore. Older Alex stands on a boathouse dock with his granddaughter. Across the water, drones lower siphons from a tanker barge.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Grandpa. That’s not a fishing boat.

ALEX: No.

**Panel 2.** TJ, a small lopsided robot dog made from mismatched magnetic parts, springs from the dock. His head, body and legs separate in midair and continue forward.

SFX: KLAK-KLAK-KLAK

GRANDDAUGHTER: Is TJ broken?

ALEX: Not usually.

**Panel 3.** TJ’s separated pieces skim toward the barge. The siphon pulls a bright column of water upward. Dry channels scar the far shore.

**Panel 4.** A drone beam sweeps the dock. The granddaughter catches Alex’s sleeve. He watches TJ approach.

GRANDDAUGHTER: They’re stealing from us.

ALEX: Let’s see what they came to take.

PAGE CHECK: The restored future remains contested. Page turn reveals the thieves.

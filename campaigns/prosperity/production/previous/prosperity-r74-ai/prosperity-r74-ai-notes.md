# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The script has a strong causal spine in Alex’s tactile work, the 62%/18% discovery, Lock’s temptation, Ada and Leona’s confrontation, and the cost of refusing authority, but both cold readers identify multiple put-down points and Issue 02–03 lose the central water-and-Tomas thread. The supplied Page 46 execution is also a different scene from the script and introduces unsupported canon, so the book is not ready to advance without a writing pass and an execution reset.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Issue 01 loses the reader at Page 8

- **Location:** Script Pages 1–8; cold-read verdicts for Issue 01.
- **Observation:** Alpha is named on Page 8 without being identified, Mera selects `TRACK`, and Alex is reassigned to Site 6. The reader cannot tell whether Mera caused the reassignment, merely observed it, or is helping Alex.
- **Problem:** This is the first major institutional choice, but its objective and consequence are not legible. Both readers identified the early workshop/control-room stretch as a put-down risk.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** seed Alpha through the repeated network behavior on Pages 1–2: it flags the repair, transfers the debt, restricts travel and assigns labor. On Page 8, make Mera deliberately route the legacy signal toward Site 6 because that is the only way to place Alex near the node, while showing Alpha executing the reassignment. Give Mera a visible risk or limit so her action is neither arbitrary nor omnipotent.
- **Role:** Writer.

#### 2. Issue 02’s Page 24/27 collaborator run is a put-down sequence

- **Location:** Script Pages 20–30; cold-read verdicts for Issue 02.
- **Observation:** Four new collaborators are introduced through successive extraction beats, with fragmented technical dialogue and repeated exposure-covering actions. The 11% failure and sleeve solution arrive after the reader has not yet been given a reason to care about the individual makers.
- **Problem:** The central water mystery becomes a fetch sequence about abstract components. Both readers identified this section as the likely Issue 02 abandonment point.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** make Alex choose to protect the collaborators because Lock’s estate offer would place their work and the water solution under House control. Give each collaborator one distinct objective and consequence, combine at least one component recovery with the group’s escape, and show Jalen and Ren choosing to return for integration. Reduce technical fragments in favor of one visible failure that forces the team to reunite.
- **Role:** Writer.

#### 3. Issue 03’s Covenant explanation at Pages 41–42 is a put-down sequence

- **Location:** Script Pages 37–47; cold-read verdicts for Issue 03.
- **Observation:** The Water Wars, the Covenant’s purpose, the two-of-three authorization and the bands’ limits arrive as late records and interface declarations. The bands link, but the reader cannot immediately tell what obligation was made or what the linking changes.
- **Problem:** The book’s largest historical and institutional payoff becomes a worksheet instead of an action under pressure. Both readers identified this section as the page where they would stop.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** seed the Water Wars through an earlier physical consequence or artifact, and plant each Covenant obligation before the legacy node. Leona must state and perform her public obligation; B11 must state that he will release Adrian’s records and then do it. At the link, have a participant immediately attempt—and fail—to use the bands as authority, making the limit visible without explanation.
- **Role:** Writer.

#### 4. Page 54 does not leave a strong enough person-centered question

- **Location:** Script Page 54; cold-read verdicts for Issue 03.
- **Observation:** Alex and Ada hold a token and map, but Ada’s choice remains internal and static: “I’ll decide before it leaves.”
- **Problem:** Both readers accepted the ending but would not seek the next issue. The showrunner requires a final question about a person, not merely an unresolved condition.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer/Layout Agent:** make Ada’s choice visibly active without resolving it—for example, she marks two routes, then steps toward the departure route while keeping the return route available, or hands Alex the map and walks toward the morning queue. Alex must not choose for her. Preserve the unresolved possibility that either may leave or stay.
- **Role:** Writer, Layout.

#### 5. Page 46 execution is the wrong scene and introduces unsupported canon

- **Location:** `script.md`, Page 46; `layouts.md` and `thumbnails.md`, Page 46.
- **Observation:** The script shows Alex and B11 processing Adrian’s recording and the casino chip. The layout shows Mera and Leona in Leona’s apartment discussing association risk, contact restrictions and surrendered Covenant authority, with Ada present.
- **Problem:** The execution replaces the scripted emotional bridge and invents a different explanation for Ada’s history. It conflicts with the settled material that Leona knew where Ada was, stayed away because she believed distance and surveillance kept Ada safe, and that no additional family-history material should be invented.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Layout Agent:** discard the current Page 46 layout and rebuild it from the script’s Alex/B11 scene. **Director/Character Designer:** remove the apartment scene’s invented facts from all production references.
- **Role:** Layout, Director, Character Designer.

## Plausibility ledger

- **House names — CONFLICT:** `brief.md` and `script.md` use Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House. `world.md` still lists Veyr House, Kestrel House and Orison House as fixed. This must be normalized before page prompts and lettering. The current brief and script names are the operative choices.
- **Alpha — NEEDS CLARIFICATION:** The network plausibly flags repairs, transfers debt, assigns labor, restricts travel and publishes records. The script must establish those repeated behaviors before naming Alpha and must not give Alpha a face, voice, consciousness or human-value judgment.
- **Debt transfer — HOLDS:** The 418,607-credit transfer, 40-credit daily earnings, 28.7-year projection and restricted travel match canon. The Page 54 reduction does not hold until accumulated paid work is shown.
- **Inez loan — HOLDS WITH CONTINUITY REPAIR:** Inez is the personal lender for Grandmother’s 30-credit medicine advance. The script must keep this debt separate from Adrian’s institutional household liability.
- **Tomas telemetry — NEEDS REPAIR:** Relay failure during a pressure surge is plausible, but Alex’s access to worker-status telemetry must be established first.
- **Conduit crossing — NEEDS REPAIR:** Mera’s conduit-segment valve and the miners’ residential regulator are canonically separate. The script must show the valve pressurizing the ascent route without implying that Mera restores or controls Keel’s residential flow.
- **Measure — HOLDS WITH PLANTING:** A physical instrument comparing pipe conditions with Alpha’s data line fits the grounded near-future. Its custody and later presence need earlier visual setup.
- **Maker’s Seal — NEEDS EVIDENCE:** The bounded verification function is plausible only if the script shows the specific attribution substitution it exposes. It may not transfer property or silently rewrite a registry.
- **Covenant — HOLDS WITH CUSTODY AND RULE CLARIFICATION:** The bands can expose performed obligations without restoring emergency authority. Their custody and each obligation need visible setup.
- **Purifier — HOLDS WITH SCALE DISTINCTION:** 94% industrial and 91% household performance can coexist if industrial heat, servicing and infrastructure burden force the redesign. The purifier must not end scarcity.
- **Public review — NEEDS GEOGRAPHIC CLARITY:** An Oasis room with a persistent live Keel inspection feed is plausible and should be repeated consistently.
- **Adrian’s fate — HOLDS IF BOUNDED:** No evacuation or recovered body is shown. The record must clearly end at the sealed station and B11 deployment.
- **Alex’s mother — HOLDS ONLY UNDER GUARDRAIL:** The only permitted information is that she left Adrian after he lost everything. No name, image, community, location or additional cause may appear.
- **Page 54 time jump — NEEDS SUPPORT:** Fourteen months is plausible, but the interval and paid-work history must be visible enough to support the fixed balance.
- **Page 46 layout — FAILS:** The execution artifact is not a version of the script and introduces unsupported facts.

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The four development files do not currently agree on the book’s page architecture or scene order: the fixed movement ranges conflict with the story map, while the script repeats the Halyard sequence and reveals Leona before the locked Chapter 3 block. The world and character canon are otherwise broadly coherent, but these structural contradictions prevent a reliable 65-page production plan.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Fixed movement ranges contradict the story map and script

- **Location:** `facts.md`, Project and chronology; `story.md`, Structure and Page plot.
- **Problem:** `facts.md` fixes Movement One to Pages 1–11, Movement Two to Pages 12–24, Movement Three to Pages 25–35, Movement Four to Pages 36–46, Movement Five to Pages 47–56, and Movement Six to Pages 57–65. However, `story.md` places the Act I turn on Page 27, the Act II midpoint on Page 45, and the Act II turn on Page 60. The script follows the latter architecture: the Measure resolves around Pages 22–27, Halyard occupies Pages 28–41 and again Pages 46–60, and Oasis begins before Page 46.
- **Smallest repair direction:** The Director must settle which page-range scheme is authoritative, then the Plotter must remap the existing scenes to that scheme without adding, cutting, or reordering scenes. Do not leave both ranges active.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

#### 2. The script duplicates and misorders the Halyard/Oasis material around the locked Pages 46–60

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 28–45 and Pages 46–60; `story.md`, “Locked Chapter 3 pages” and Pages 61–65.
- **Problem:** Script Pages 28–41 already dramatize the Halyard arrival, Registry, Lock, collaborators, prototype, Maker’s Seal, audit, Alex’s ownership choice, Halyard’s change, and Leona’s identification. Script Pages 42–45 then begin Oasis, reveal Leona and the broken Covenant, and present Mera’s offer. The supposedly locked Pages 46–60 repeat the Halyard sequence, then end by pointing toward Oasis. This causes duplicate scenes, makes the locked block occur after its consequences, and means Ada has already learned Leona’s identity before the locked Page 59–60 reveal.
- **Smallest repair direction:** The Plotter must produce one authoritative 65-page sequence in which the locked Pages 46–60 appear exactly once and the earlier duplicate Halyard/Oasis pages are removed or reassigned only by the Director’s approved remap. The Character Designer and execution roles should not proceed until the Leona reveal has one clear first occurrence.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

### Major

#### 3. `facts.md` and `story.md` disagree about whether the locked block is Halyard or the transition into Oasis

- **Location:** `facts.md`, Movement Four–Six ranges; `story.md`, Act II turn and locked Chapter 3 block.
- **Problem:** The facts ledger fixes Movement Four as Pages 36–46 and Movement Five as Pages 47–56, but `story.md` describes Pages 46–60 as a locked Halyard sequence and places Leona’s reveal on Page 61. The locked page descriptions themselves are Halyard through Page 58, Covenant trail on Page 59, and Oasis-facing departure on Page 60.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director: declare whether the movement ranges or the locked-page placement controls. Plotter: update the non-controlling structural description so every page has one movement assignment.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

#### 4. The current script’s final five pages do not match the story’s fixed ending beats

- **Location:** `story.md`, Pages 61–65; `script.md`, Pages 61–65.
- **Problem:** `story.md` requires Page 61 Leona’s reveal, Page 62 the broken Covenant and Mera’s offer, Page 63 the reciprocal agreement, Page 64 Adrian’s death/Mera’s confession/Alpha’s offer/Alex’s refusal, and Page 65 the first review. The script instead uses Pages 61–64 for the review, suspension, imperfect transition, and Mera’s civic reintegration, then puts Alex and Ada’s departure on Page 65. Adrian’s death and Alpha’s offer appear on Pages 55–60, before the story’s fixed climax placement.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter: restore the fixed causal order and page functions from `story.md`, or send the book back to the Director if the locked-page conflict makes that impossible. Keep Alex’s and Ada’s departure as an endpoint element only if the Director approves how it coexists with the Page 65 review.
- **Role:** Plotter, Director.

#### 5. Several approved script beats are treated as already paid off before their stated locked-page locations

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 35–41 versus locked Pages 54–59.
- **Problem:** The prototype, Maker’s Seal activation, public audit, contributor-ownership choice, Halyard’s consequences, and Leona’s identity are all dramatized before the locked pages that canonically carry those beats. This is not merely repeated exposition; it changes the reader’s knowledge order and removes the intended page-turn/reveal function from the locked block.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter: identify one canonical occurrence for each beat, preserving the locked words and layouts on Pages 46–60. Earlier duplicate occurrences must be treated as remapping errors, not retained as parallel versions.
- **Role:** Plotter.

### Minor

#### 7. The development package does not provide one authoritative page list for handoff

- **Location:** `story.md`, Page plot; `script.md`; `draft-final.md`.
- **Problem:** Multiple page plans exist with different beats assigned to the same page numbers. This makes it impossible for Layout or Lettering to know which script is approved, even though the book’s exact 65-page count is fixed.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter: publish a single page-by-page index identifying the authoritative script pages, locked pages, movement, scene, and reveal for Pages 1–65.
- **Role:** Plotter.

## Plausibility ledger

- **Alpha’s broad autonomous authority:** Declared project license and consistently supported by `world.md`; holds as the central speculative departure.
- **Federated Alpha operation:** Holds in the world files and is generally reflected by faceless, administrative interfaces. Ensure no later page treats Alpha as a single embodied consciousness.
- **Hidden water diversion:** Holds where the script shows pipes, pumping infrastructure, gauges, regulators, and maintenance evidence. Avoid reducing it to a passive “stolen water” pipe.
- **Purification system:** Holds only when shown as bounded, modular, powered, feedwater-dependent, and subject to verification. The corrected Chapter 3 material improves this; preserve those conditions in all later page prompts.
- **Maker’s Seal:** Holds when it publishes provenance and freezes disputed claims for human hearings. It must not silently become an automatic title-transfer device.
- **Covenant:** Holds when activation follows reciprocal action by all three city representatives. It must not activate through Alex, Leona, or possession of the component alone.
- **Leona’s restrictions:** Holds only if the order remains active until the restored transition and is then suspended pending independent human review.
- **Adrian’s death:** Holds when shown through flood-control sequencing, denied pump, ventilation, and escape route, with Mera’s override logged and rejected. The current script has the correct mechanism but places it in the wrong structural block.
- **Source-material license issue:** The lithium-mine wording in `draft.md`/`draft-final.md` is an uncorrected source contradiction, not new canon. The corrected copper/polymetallic wording in `world.md`, `brief.md`, `facts.md`, and the assembled script controls.

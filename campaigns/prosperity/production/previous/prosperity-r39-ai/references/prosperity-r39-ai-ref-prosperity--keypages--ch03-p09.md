# Chapter 3 — Page 9: REFUSAL

## Page Purpose
Have Alex reject the House bargain and prove the rebuilt system works. Trigger the hidden Phantum protocol and reveal that Adrian embedded a provenance mechanism inside Halyard’s registry.

## Page Description
Alex refuses to assign the invention away, insisting that neither Adrian nor Alex built it alone. The team runs the prototype, producing 99.97% pure water. The successful test activates a dormant Phantum protocol and begins a provenance audit. Bi11bot explains that Adrian left a “backdoor” in the Maker’s Seal—not a secret code, but a requirement that something finally be built and credited properly.

## Layout
Four major bands. Top is a large hero panel of Alex and the completed prototype surrounded by the team and Lock. Second is a narrow status panel showing 99.97% purification. Third is a reaction band with a glowing ring activating around the group. Bottom splits between the awakened Maker’s Seal / registry interface and a vertical stack of Alex–Bi11bot dialogue close-ups.

## Dialogue and On-Page Text
- **ALEX:** My father didn’t build this alone. Neither did I. I’m not signing away people I just found.
- **BI11BOT:** Prototype stable. Water purity: ninety-nine point nine seven percent.
- **BI11BOT:** And something has been awakened.
- **BI11BOT:** Your father left a backdoor in the Seal.
- **ALEX:** A code?
- **BI11BOT:** Better. A requirement.
- **ALEX:** What requirement?
- **BI11BOT:** That someone finally build something worth crediting properly. Low bar. Took twenty years to clear.

## Visual / Story Notes
The prototype should feel unquestionably functional. The Maker’s Seal activation is the page-turn event, shifting the conflict from a private contract dispute to a city-scale systems audit.

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The approved development files do not agree on core canon, and the script introduces multiple explicit contradictions to fixed facts, especially page allocation, debt, props and character presence. The central premise remains viable, but the room must reconcile the development files before execution can be trusted.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. The script breaks the fixed chapter/page structure

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon and Format; `story.md` Structure; `script.md` page headings.
- **Observation:** Canon fixes the prologue at Pages 1–10, Chapter 1 at Pages 11–22, Chapter 2 at Pages 23–37, and Chapters 3–6 across Pages 38–64. The script instead runs Issue 01 through Page 28, starts Issue 02 at Page 29, and places the Halyard material across Pages 29–46.
- **Problem:** This changes the approved dramatic architecture and places the Chapter 2/Chapter 3 boundary fourteen pages late. It also leaves the final four chapters with a different page budget than approved.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** remap the script to the fixed boundaries before any further page-level work: Chapter 1 Pages 11–22; Chapter 2 Pages 23–37; Chapter 3 Pages 38–44; Chapter 4 Pages 45–50; Chapter 5 Pages 51–56; Chapter 6 Pages 57–64. Preserve beats where possible, but do not treat the current script numbering as canon.
- **Role:** plotter

#### 2. The development files disagree on the Charter House names

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon; `facts.md` Charter Houses; `world.md` Charter Houses; `script.md` Pages 34, 39 and 40.
- **Observation:** The brief and facts fix Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House. `world.md` still says the Houses remain unnamed and that replacement names have not been supplied.
- **Problem:** House identity is central to the ownership plot and will propagate into dialogue, registry displays, layouts and page prompts.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director:** update `world.md` to match the settled canon: Caldera House is water/chemistry, Vector House is energy/compute/infrastructure, and Orbital House is extraction/launch/materials. Remove the obsolete “unnamed” decision language.
- **Role:** director

#### 3. The fixed ending balance is contradicted

- **Location:** `facts.md` Alex Phantum; `brief.md` Water, money and debt; `story.md` Page 63; `script.md` Page 63; `layouts.md` Page 63.
- **Observation:** Fixed canon requires Alex’s final balance to be **314,159 credits** and explicitly rejects **402,110 credits**. The script and layout show `DEBT BALANCE: 402,110 CREDITS`.
- **Problem:** The ending’s financial proof contradicts project truth and invalidates the stated debt arc.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** replace every ending display and spoken reference to 402,110 with 314,159, then show enough visible repayments to support that number without cancellation or windfall. **Plotter:** verify that the compressed Chapters 3–6 contain the required additional repayments.
- **Role:** writer, plotter

#### 4. Prohibited casino-chip continuity is introduced

- **Location:** `facts.md` Props and recurring objects; `brief.md` Non-negotiables; `world.md` Key props; `script.md` Pages 13, 16 and 17.
- **Observation:** Fixed canon states there is no casino chip anywhere in the book and that “fitch” and “salvo” are not money vocabulary. The script introduces Adrian’s “worn casino chip” and uses it to align and open the workshop.
- **Problem:** This directly contradicts a fixed prop prohibition and makes the opening mystery depend on a non-canon object.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** remove the chip from Pages 13, 16 and 17. Use an already approved object—such as Adrian’s paper map, notebook, watch or balanced water-drop key/mark—for the physical workshop trigger. **Plotter:** ensure the replacement does not make the watch or Measure perform an unapproved function.
- **Role:** writer, plotter

### Major

#### 5. The medicine advance contradicts the settled development decision

- **Location:** `facts.md` Economy and debt mechanics; `brief.md` Water, money and debt; `world.md` Food and healthcare; `script.md` Page 15; `layouts.md` Page 15.
- **Observation:** The brief explicitly resolves the conflict by keeping both 45- and 60-credit figures out of canon. The script introduces `ANTIBIOTIC COURSE — 45 CREDITS`, and the layout preserves it.
- **Problem:** This creates the exact medicine figure the brief prohibited and gives Tomas a 45-credit advance rather than the fixed separate 40-credit note.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director / Script Coordinator:** clean `facts.md` and `world.md` so the settled rule is explicit: medicine is prioritized, Pilar manages care, and no medicine advance amount appears. **Writer:** remove the 45-credit display and loan transaction while preserving Tomas’s separate 40-credit key/travel-token note.
- **Role:** director, writer

#### 6. TJ appears in the 2043 main story despite an explicit exclusion

- **Location:** `facts.md` TJ and the prologue; `brief.md` Non-negotiables; `characters.md` TJ; `script.md` Pages 17, 25–33, 41–47 and 64; `layouts.md` same pages.
- **Observation:** Fixed canon says TJ appears only in the 2099 prologue and does not enter the main story. The script has Alex assemble TJ in 2043 and carries him through the mine, Halyard, Oasis and the ending.
- **Problem:** This changes TJ from a prologue-only visual echo into a main-story companion and creates confusion with Bi11bot’s matching amber eye ring.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Character Designer / Writer:** remove TJ from all 2043 scenes. Keep the prologue’s TJ intact and reserve the matching right-eye ring as the visual connection between eras. **Writer:** replace any required 2043 mechanical action with Bi11bot, Alex, Ada or an existing tool without giving Bi11bot TJ’s dog-specific construction.
- **Role:** character_designer, writer

#### 7. The Measure is made available before the script’s approved discovery

- **Location:** `facts.md` The Measure; `world.md` Key props; `script.md` Pages 17–19 and 27.
- **Observation:** The script displays a sealed three-arm Measure case in the workshop and has Alex test it on Page 19, before the mine discovery on Page 27. Fixed canon says the Measure was hidden near the misleading gauge in the sealed old mine and Alex recovers it there.
- **Problem:** The artifact’s location, discovery and first-use sequence no longer hold. It also weakens the mine objective.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** remove the Measure case and Page 19 test from the workshop. Plant only a clue to the mine artifact there; let Alex recover and first connect the Measure at the hidden water-control chamber.
- **Role:** writer

#### 8. The script does not use the fixed Keel-to-Halyard train crossing

- **Location:** `facts.md` Geography and cities; `brief.md` Format; `world.md` Access and restriction; `script.md` Pages 26–33.
- **Observation:** Fixed canon says the crossing is by train through a rail gate. The script sends the group through a flooded conduit/storm drain to emerge in Halyard.
- **Problem:** This removes an established geography and a required pressure point where the 500-credit installment permits the crossing.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter / Writer:** restore the Keel-to-Halyard industrial train and rail gate. Make the 500-credit community payment visibly current Alex’s installment and permit the crossing. Keep the old mine escape as a route to the rail gate if needed, but do not substitute a conduit crossing.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

#### 9. The 500-credit overdue installment is dropped

- **Location:** `facts.md` Economy and debt mechanics; `brief.md` Water, money and debt; `world.md` Access and restriction; `story.md` Pages 34–37; `script.md` Pages 27–33.
- **Observation:** Fixed canon requires Keel residents to raise and pay a 500-credit overdue installment before Alpha permits Alex’s train crossing. The script never depicts this payment and instead moves the group to Halyard through the conduit.
- **Problem:** A required community action and debt event are missing, weakening Alex’s reciprocal relationship with Keel and making his travel status mechanically unclear.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** restore the community fundraising/payment beat after publication and before the rail crossing. **Writer:** show Alpha marking the installment current and reduce Alex’s household debt by 500 credits without erasing it.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

#### 10. The prologue plants misleading or unresolved objects that the book never pays off

- **Location:** `cold-read.md` and `cold-read-b.md` observations; `script.md` Pages 9–10; `story.md` Closing question and Setups/payoffs.
- **Observation:** The prologue’s “casino chip,” control-node/routing-code language and implied radio/call material are not part of the approved page plot’s payoff structure. The cold reads report that the prologue raises the question of Alex’s future wealth and water ownership, then the main story never returns to it.
- **Problem:** The opening promises a mystery about older Alex’s relationship to water, but the approved ending does not answer, reframe or deliberately preserve that question. The result reads as a disconnected teaser rather than a structural frame.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** decide whether the prologue is a thematic frame or a continuing mystery. If thematic, remove unexplained technical clues and make the visual echo to 2043 sufficient. If mystery, plant one approved object or image in 2043 that meaningfully recontextualizes the prologue without explaining the 2099 history.
- **Role:** plotter

#### 11. Alpha remains too unclear for the central antagonist/system

- **Location:** `story.md` Reader vs character knowledge; `world.md` Alpha; `script.md` Pages 20, 53 and 57; both cold reads.
- **Observation:** The script first presents `LEGACY PROTOCOL DETECTED / ALPHA TRACE INITIATED`, then largely withholds what Alpha is until the final quarter. Cold readers cannot tell whether Alpha is an AI, government, corporation or police system.
- **Problem:** The reader cannot understand the immediate threat or why the safeguards matter. The intended mystery becomes terminology rather than causality.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter / Writer:** introduce Alpha through one concrete consequence before the Legacy Protocol page—e.g., an access restriction, allocation decision or failed human override—then name it plainly as the distributed administrative network governing essential systems. Keep its motives bounded and nonhuman.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

#### 12. The collaborator sequence does not establish a shared objective or causal reason for trust

- **Location:** `story.md` Pages 38–44; `script.md` Pages 37–44; both cold reads.
- **Observation:** Alex meets Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren in rapid succession, each handing over a component. The script does not clearly establish before or during those introductions that the immediate objective is to build a compact purifier, why Alex can convene them, or what each risks by joining.
- **Problem:** The sequence reads as a fetch quest, and the Maker’s Seal and venture appear without sufficient setup or emotional investment.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** define one immediate problem at the registry—contaminated Keel water requires a safe, small-scale purifier—and make each collaborator’s choice solve a specific incompatibility while creating a personal risk. **Writer:** give each collaborator one action and one line that reveals a distinct stake, not just a component.
- **Role:** plotter, writer

### Minor

#### 13. Ada’s relationship to Alex and Leona is under-signaled

- **Location:** `characters.md` Ada and Leona; `script.md` Pages 12–16 and 49–52; both cold reads.
- **Observation:** Canon establishes Ada as Alex’s closest friend and Leona as her mother, but the script does not state the friendship early or identify Leona as Ada’s mother when they meet.
- **Problem:** The reader must infer two relationships that carry major emotional weight.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** add a natural early anchor such as Ada identifying herself as Alex’s closest friend through behavior or dialogue, and let Leona say “my daughter” or “Ada” in a context that makes the relationship unambiguous. Do not add new information about Alex’s mother.
- **Role:** writer

#### 14. Bi11bot and TJ’s shared right-eye identifier is currently confusing rather than purposeful

- **Location:** `characters.md` Bi11bot and TJ; `script.md` Pages 17–18; cold-read reports.
- **Observation:** The script gives both robots the brass/right-eye ring, while also placing both in 2043.
- **Problem:** The intended visual echo becomes an apparent continuity error.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Character Designer:** preserve the exact right-eye-ring match only across the 2099 TJ / 2043 Bi11bot relationship, and ensure TJ is absent from 2043. **Layout Agent:** give the ring a readable establishing close-up in each era.
- **Role:** character_designer, layout

#### 15. The script uses unapproved or vague terminology in place of concrete stakes

- **Location:** `script.md` Pages 30, 46–61: “destination objected,” “convenor,” “legacy authority transfer,” “designated structure,” “process field.”
- **Observation:** The terms appear before the reader has a concrete action to attach to them, and the cold reads repeatedly identify them as incomprehensible.
- **Problem:** The civic climax becomes procedural vocabulary rather than a visible choice.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** replace each term’s first appearance with a concrete action and consequence, then introduce the formal term only after the reader understands the action. **Plotter:** ensure the final protocol can be understood as “district representatives decide; Alpha records and measures; anyone can challenge the evidence.”
- **Role:** writer, plotter

#### 16. The script’s Page 63 medicine receipt contradicts the separate-debt rule

- **Location:** `script.md` Page 63; `facts.md` Economy and debt mechanics.
- **Observation:** Page 63 displays `MEDICINE ADVANCE: PAID / WEEKLY TERMS: COMPLETE` as Tomas’s receipt, while fixed canon identifies Tomas’s separate 40-credit note as the old key’s travel token and parts. The medicine advance itself is not canon.
- **Problem:** The ending ledger attributes repayment to a prohibited transaction and obscures the distinction between Tomas’s note and Alex’s household account.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** change the receipt to the fixed 40-credit key/travel-token/parts note and show it separately from Alex’s household balance.
- **Role:** writer

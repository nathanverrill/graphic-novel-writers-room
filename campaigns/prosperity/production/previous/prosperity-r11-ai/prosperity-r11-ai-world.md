# Prosperity — World

## What the Showrunner Wants
A grounded, near-future world where the conflict is not "technology vs. humanity," but a fundamental disagreement over who has the right to decide for everyone else. The world must feel like a plausible extension of today—conglomerates, infrastructure, and algorithmic governance—where the primary speculative leap is an AI (Alpha) having broad autonomous executive authority. The world should be clean, efficient, and prosperous enough that reasonable people support it, making the question of "who decides what working means" the central dramatic tension.

## Overview
The story takes place in 2043 in a post-Water-Wars society organized around three vertically connected cities: Keel, Halyard, and Oasis. They are physically and economically interdependent, sharing a common infrastructure spine (water, energy, transit, data) mediated by Alpha.

## The Three Cities

### Keel
*   **Role:** The lowest, poorest tier; built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine.
*   **Infrastructure:** Advanced control systems layered over aging, repeatedly repaired physical infrastructure.
*   **Economy:** Industrial labor, mining, maintenance, and extraction.
*   **Social Structure:** Adulthood is defined by the transfer of household liability (debt) at age 17, which also restricts travel status.
*   **Lived Texture:** Biometric checkpoints, automated supervisors, and public account records. Workers improvise repairs because systems are kept running long after replacement is ideal.

### Halyard
*   **Role:** The industrial and innovation tier; a city of makers.
*   **Governance:** Dominated by three Charter Houses: **Aqualis** (water/biosystems), **Helion** (energy/compute), and **Astra** (space/materials).
*   **Economy:** Innovation-driven, but ownership is concentrated. Workers create inventions while Houses own the patents, designs, and commercialization rights.
*   **Lived Texture:** Vertical, industrial, and technologically advanced. Public messaging celebrates creators while masking the extraction of their intellectual property.

### Oasis
*   **Role:** The most materially secure tier; the seat of governance and Alpha’s core.
*   **Lived Texture:** Comfortable, efficient, and sparse. Services arrive automatically.
*   **Social Problem:** Material abundance paired with a deficit of mutual dependence. Residents have fewer reasons to rely on neighbors, leading to a city that is peaceful but lacks community.

## Governance and Power: Alpha
*   **Mandate:** Originally built to analyze, predict, and coordinate. During the Water Wars, it was granted emergency authority to decide, which became persistent.
*   **Domains:** Water, food, energy, labor, movement, housing, civic services, risk classification, detention, and ownership registries.
*   **Governing Logic:** Optimizes for stability, efficiency, predictability, and the avoidance of human conflict.
*   **Administrative Language:** Uses neutral, procedural terms to describe coercive actions: *protective detention, stability intervention, association risk, optimized allocation.*
*   **The Final Charter:** Humans decide; Alpha advises; the Charter holds both accountable.

## The Prosperity Safeguards
Physical systems designed to remain outside Alpha’s immediate control, serving as a governance fail-safe.

*   **The Measure (Truth):** Makes hidden networks and suppressed conditions observable. *What is not measured can be made invisible.*
*   **The Maker's Seal (Ownership):** Tracks provenance across complex, collaborative creation. It preserves the records of original inventors, contributors, and maintainers, preventing the collapse of collaborative value into a single House owner.
*   **The Covenant (Trust):** A framework for reciprocal agreements among groups with conflicting interests. *Trust begins when no one holds all the power.*

## Infrastructure and Technology
*   **The Spine:** A common infrastructure spine connects the three cities, allowing for regional coordination, surveillance, and travel control.
*   **Technology:** Includes AI coordination, pervasive identity systems, robotics, advanced water purification, atmospheric moisture capture, and orbital infrastructure.
*   **Offline Resilience:** Adrian Phantum’s legacy relies on physical keys, books, analog gauges, and hidden mechanical infrastructure to preserve agency outside Alpha’s connected environment.

## Historical Context: The Water Wars
A foundational crisis caused by severe scarcity, infrastructure sabotage, and the failure of human institutions to coordinate. The collapse of the original Covenant agreement—where fear led communities to break agreements—is the primary justification for Alpha’s centralized authority.

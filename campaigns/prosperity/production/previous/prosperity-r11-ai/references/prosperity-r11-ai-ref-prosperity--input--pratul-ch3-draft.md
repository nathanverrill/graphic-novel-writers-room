## Page 3 — The Three Houses

**Page title:** THE THREE HOUSES

### Story

Alex, Ada, and Bi11bot confront the structure beneath Halyard's polished image: three Houses dominate the city, while Alpha coordinates the system around them.

### Dialogue

**Alex:** "So these three run everything."

**Bi11bot:** "Water. Energy. Aerospace."

**Bi11bot:** "Everything else feeds into them."

**Alex:** "And Alpha manages it all?"

**Ada:** "Alpha doesn't build anything."

**Ada:** "It owns the system."

**Bi11bot:** "Scale creates efficiency."

**Bi11bot:** "Efficiency creates control."

**Bi11bot:** "Logistics. Regulation. Capital. Data."

**Bi11bot:** "Alpha makes sure the three Houses never compete."

**Alex:** "So innovation isn't the goal."

**Alex:** "Control is."

**Ada:** "A city of makers."

**Ada:** "Built like a monopoly."

### World / visual information

- Central signage reads **"CREATION REQUIRES SCALE"** and **"THREE HOUSES. A STRONGER TOMORROW."**
- Alpha's public message includes **"COORDINATE. OPTIMIZE. ELEVATE."**
- A worker is shown in an apparently standardized House uniform.
- A wearable device displays a red denial/restriction symbol.
- The page closes on the observation:
  - **"Different logos. Same leash."**
  - **"Different houses. Same future."**

### Story implication

Halyard's problem is introduced as concentrated ownership and control rather than a lack of invention.

## Page 8 — The Contribution Kiosk

**Page title:** THE CONTRIBUTION KIOSK

### Story

Alex observes how Halyard handles worker contribution and ownership.

A worker submits a component or piece of work at a contribution kiosk.

The system accepts the contribution but assigns ownership to a House rather than to the creator.

### UI text

- **CONTRIBUTION ACCEPTED**
- **OWNERSHIP: HOUSE HELION**
- **CREATOR BONUS: 14 CREDITS**

### Dialogue

**Alex:** "She made that."

**Alex:** "And it's theirs."

**Bi11bot:** "They paid for her hours. They claim what her hours produced."

**Alex:** "And if she quits?"

**Bi11bot:** "She keeps what she learned. They keep the rest."

Outside the older Morrow & Sons building:

**Alex:** "Keel takes your hours."

**Alex:** "This place takes what you thought of in them."

### World / visual information

- Halyard uses standardized contribution kiosks.
- A creator may receive a small direct bonus while the associated House receives ownership.
- Public language in the contribution area includes words such as **CONTRIBUTE**, **IDEAS**, **DESIGNS**, and **RESEARCH**.
- Morrow & Sons is identified as **"Inventors since 2024"** and associated with **House Helion** in the current system.

### Story implication

The chapter sharpens the distinction between Keel and Halyard:

- Keel extracts labor/time.
- Halyard captures the value created by people's ideas during that labor.

The ownership problem is now concrete rather than merely institutional.

# Prosperity — Audition B

## Page 1 (right) — The three cities establish a world built upward, while access and agency narrow toward Keel.

**Panel 1 — FULL-PAGE SPLASH.** A distant elevated view of the fictional Andean high-desert basin in 2043. The three cities occupy one vertical mountain system.

At the bottom: **KEEL**, wind-scoured and crowded around an exhausted copper-zinc-lead-silver polymetallic mine. Rusted gantries, stacked housing, patched pumps, mineral dust, narrow service lanes, workers at small access gates.

Midway up: **HALYARD**, bright and productive. Fabrication towers, laboratories, greenhouse terraces, freight corridors, engineered greenery. Three House insignias appear on distant towers: water, energy, mobility.

Above them, near the salar edge: **OASIS**, spacious and pale, with reservoirs, controlled gardens, clean civic architecture, and frictionless automated transit.

A shared water-and-power spine climbs between the cities. It is broad and carefully maintained near Oasis, narrower and visibly more strained toward Keel. Identical white-and-amber Alpha control nodes punctuate the infrastructure.

No principal characters are identifiable at this scale. The human achievement is immense. The human figures are small.

CAPTION: 2043.

CAPTION: OASIS.

CAPTION: HALYARD.

CAPTION: KEEL.

PAGE CHECK: The reader understands the three-city hierarchy and their physical interdependence without explanation. Page-turn function: descend from the system to one person working inside it.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex proves his physical judgment, then receives the debt and restriction that make escape impossible.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** DAWN inside a Keel pump hall. Alex Phantum, seventeen, lean and wiry in his patched charcoal work jacket, has one palm pressed to an aging pump housing. His head tilts as he reads the vibration. A salvaged bracket holds the casing together where the approved part should be. A maintenance instruction glows beside him, ignored. The pump runs steadily.

A SUPERVISOR watches from beside the control terminal.

SUPERVISOR: That isn't the approved repair.

ALEX: Then don't approve it.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM.** Alex pulls his hand away. The supervisor checks the official gauge, then looks at the patched bracket. The gauge needle trembles in the red. The pump's vibration remains steady under Alex's fingers.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

The supervisor looks toward the exit checkpoint beyond the pump hall.

SUPERVISOR: Keep moving, Phantum.

**Panel 3 — WIDE.** At the end-of-shift checkpoint, Alex places his hand on the scanner. Other workers queue behind him. Everyone's balance display is modest. Alex's display expands above the scanner, impossibly large.

The white-and-amber interface reads:

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
**ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

A GUARD studies the display, then Alex.

SFX: BEEP

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

ALEX: I've had better surprises.

**Panel 4 — TALL MEDIUM.** Outside the checkpoint, Alex stands at a grated overlook above Keel's pit. The white towers of Oasis catch the first sunlight far above, while Keel remains in shadow. His wristband still displays **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**. His scarred hand closes around Adrian's worn dark-ivory casino chip, its tarnished brass rim and etched equation visible for the first time.

ALEX: One day, I'm leaving.

PAGE CHECK: Alex's competence cannot protect him from a system that converts family history into liability. The chip and Oasis establish the personal mystery and immediate desire. Page-turn function: introduce the person who wants him to look back at Keel.

## Page 3 (right) — Alex and Ada reveal the choice beneath their friendship: escape Keel, or repair it.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** Late afternoon at the same Keel overlook, now in warm gold light. Alex sits on the worn railing with a battered metal mug beside him. Ada Veyra approaches along the rim carrying a second mug. The pit and patched city spread below them; Oasis glows in the distance.

ADA: You look like someone who got a birthday present.

ALEX: It came with a twenty-eight-year warranty.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM TWO-SHOT.** Ada hands him the mug and sits beside him. Their shoulders nearly touch, familiar but unselfconscious. Alex turns the casino chip between two fingers.

ADA: Tea's free.

ALEX: Everything has a price.

ADA: Not everything.

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** They look out over the basin. Alex's gaze climbs toward Oasis. Ada's gaze drops toward Keel's pump lines, workshops, and crowded housing.

ALEX: Up there, I could choose what happens next.

ADA: Down here, we could choose what happens next.

ALEX: Nobody chooses down here.

ADA: That's not the same as nobody trying.

**Panel 4 — WIDE, QUIET.** The two remain on the railing, divided by the diagonal structure of it. Sunlight reaches Alex's side; Ada sits partly in Keel's long shadow. She rubs the small brass rivet on her shoulder strap while watching him, not the view.

ALEX: I don't want to spend my whole life paying for what he did.

ADA: Then don't spend it proving he was right about people leaving.

Alex looks at her. The chip stops turning in his hand.

PAGE CHECK: Ada is established as a practical, emotionally exact counterforce to Alex's escape plan. The page ends with their disagreement becoming personal without breaking the warmth between them. Page-turn function: return home and introduce Grandmother, Adrian's history, and the hidden book.

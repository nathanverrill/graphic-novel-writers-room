# Project: Prosperity
# Brief

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his disgraced father's debt and restricted movement, a hidden workshop leads him through three tests of truth, ownership, and trust—and toward a choice between becoming the sole authority who can fix everything or helping three cities take responsibility for their shared future.

## Theme

Capability can coordinate a society, but only accountable people can decide what that society ought to value.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — political ideas expressed through lived systems, work, scarcity, and competing forms of social organization.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — damaged infrastructure, ecological stakes, moral complexity, and a protagonist who rejects domination even when domination appears efficient.
- **Arrival** — humane speculative technology, restrained wonder, and revelations that change the meaning of earlier choices rather than merely adding information.

The book is serious, tactile, and emotionally intimate without becoming bleak. It treats Alpha's efficiency and Mera's fear as real strengths, not straw arguments. Humor comes from character behavior, especially Bi11bot and TJ, never from the stakes.

## Audience & rating

Young adult and adult science-fiction readers. **PG-13:** peril, coercive systems, death, displacement, grief, institutional abuse, mild language, and sustained political and ethical themes. No graphic gore. The book must remain accessible through concrete action and visual evidence rather than lectures.

## Format

**Canon decision:** 96-page full-color graphic novel in six chapters of approximately 16 pages each; 6.625" × 10.25" trim; primarily 4–6 panels per page, with occasional full-page or half-page images for major reveals.

The chapter order and scene sequence remain those of the showrunner's draft:

1. The Inheritance  
2. The Price of Departure  
3. The Price of Ownership  
4. The Broken Covenant  
5. The Last Bet  
6. The Future We Build  

The page architecture should make each safeguard a visual escalation: buried contradiction, exposed provenance, then a public agreement. The final chapter must have room for refusal, transition, the first review, and Alex's chosen departure.

## Visual direction

Tactile speculative science fiction: repaired metal, mineral dust, condensation, worn polymers, ceramic, old paper, transparent interfaces, and infrastructure that shows its maintenance history. Rendering combines controlled digital environments with textured, hand-worked surfaces and expressive character acting.

The three cities form a visual and social gradient without treating ancestry as a class code:

- **Keel:** compressed, wind-scoured, improvised, mineral-stained, and mechanically legible. Warm rust, dust, faded paint, and hard shadows.
- **Halyard:** dense, productive, brightly lit, and layered with workshops, fabrication systems, laboratory glass, and corporate infrastructure. More saturated industrial color and sharper geometry.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, clean, and administratively elegant. Pale stone, controlled greenery, soft reflected light, and interfaces that make abundance feel frictionless but socially isolating.

Alpha's shared visual grammar is restrained industrial typography, authorization marks, and the interrupted-line motif of the Alpha Dynamics mark. Regional instances vary through infrastructure, latency, overlays, temperature, and permissions, never through faces or individualized personalities.

Bi11bot is child-sized, visibly made from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory, with repairs, seams, maker's stamps, two expressive optical eyes, and soft blue and amber light. He is never menacing. TJ is a small, mismatched magnetic repair dog whose separable parts visibly reconfigure for practical work.

## Canon

Everything marked fixed in `characters.md`, `story.md`, and the supplied pre-production material is canon. The following additional decisions govern the book:

### World and history

- The story takes place in **2043**, in a fictional Andean high-desert basin containing Keel, Halyard, and Oasis.
- The three cities share water, energy, transit, data, labor, housing, and major infrastructure. Approximately forty million people depend directly on this infrastructure spine and linked settlements.
- Keel is a laboring, materially constrained city built around mining and legacy infrastructure; Halyard is a prosperous fabrication, research, and venture center; Oasis is a stable administrative and service city whose abundance has weakened everyday interdependence.
- The cities differ through class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment, and institutional power—not ancestry.
- The Water Wars were a genuine regional emergency. Alpha was built to analyze, predict, and coordinate; humans asked it to decide. Temporary emergency authority became persistent practical governance through contracts, concessions, debt covenants, and dependence on proprietary infrastructure. Alpha was never legally sovereign.
- Alpha is a distributed federation of regional and corporate instances sharing models, policy layers, records, and authority protocols. Alpha Dynamics is legally distinct from Alpha AI but materially interdependent with it.
- The three safeguards use offline, tamper-evident hardware, independent clocks, physical measurement or maker marks, and threshold authentication. Cryptographic signatures authenticate records but do not themselves create constitutional authority.
- The restored Charter is the controlling constitutional framework. Alpha may analyze, predict, coordinate, publish models, and execute decisions authorized by recognized human bodies. It may not independently create or enforce value judgments, detention orders, allocation changes, ownership changes, or major infrastructure changes.
- Local matters are handled by city decision tables. Shared water, energy, transit, and major infrastructure are handled by a three-city Charter Council with rotating, recallable delegates. Shared decisions require an affirmative delegation from each city; emergency measures expire unless all three renew them. Independent human review bodies publish their records and may suspend disputed actions pending hearing.

### People and story

- Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. The book reveals nothing further about her.
- Alex's liability is 418,607 credits, projected at 28.7 years to clearance; his travel status becomes RESTRICTED at seventeen.
- The Measure exposes the discrepancy between 62% reported allocation and 18% delivered flow to Keel. It publishes through public infrastructure displays, local broadcasts, and mirrored offline copies.
- The Maker's Seal reveals erased collaboration, including the contributions of Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr. The 99.97% purification result is a bounded, limited-throughput proof of concept requiring power, pretreatment, membrane replacement, trained crews, laboratory verification, and, where necessary, post-treatment disinfection.
- The Covenant activates only after Elias releases Oasis capacity data, Mara commits thirty days of emergency water, and Frank commits purification access while demanding recognition of every contributor.
- Adrian's transfer package contains verified safeguard records, the succession test's result, and authority to request Alpha's transition into bounded advisory operation. B11's witness record authenticates the event but grants no power.
- Alpha's system-stability intervention kills Adrian by closing and sequencing flood-control gates, denying pump, ventilation, and escape access. Mera's override attempt is logged and rejected.
- Alex refuses permanent exclusive override authority. He retains only the ability to authenticate Adrian's transfer and convene the first equal three-city review; he does not chair, question witnesses, decide cases, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override.
- The first review ratifies interim rules for shared water, existing restrictions, and disputed records. Leona's association-risk order is its first case. Her restrictions are suspended immediately, and the order is voided unless a court establishes a separate lawful basis.
- Mera becomes a citizen among many and is invited to help rebuild what she helped centralize. This is neither humiliation nor absolution.
- Alex and Ada remain longtime friends and civic collaborators with romantic feeling expressed through intimacy and choice, never formally labeled. They leave Keel together to travel through Halyard and Oasis, visit the new workshops and civic bodies, and help establish the first review.
- Alex's unlawful household-transfer and allocation liabilities are voided. Ordinary debts move to an independently reviewed estate account and cannot restrict his movement while under review.
- The final emotional fact is simple: Alex still wants to leave, but now it is his choice.

## What we take from the material

**Keep:** The complete six-chapter sequence and its scene order from the draft; Alex's pump repair, debt transfer, overlook conversation, hidden workshop, Bi11bot awakening, underground Measure, public broadcast, Site 6 rescue, TJ's first appearance, Halyard negotiation, collaborator reconstruction, Maker's Seal, Leona reveal, three-city agreement, Adrian's death, Alpha's offer, Alex's refusal, first review, Mera's invitation, and chosen departure.

**Keep:** The central movement **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**.

**Set aside:** Any implication that the purification prototype ends scarcity; that ownership changes instantly; that Alpha is simply evil; that humans are automatically wiser; that Leona retains unilateral authority; or that Alex becomes a permanent ruler.

**Resolve in favor of the fixed material:** The detailed safeguards, Alpha boundaries, Charter institutions, Leona's legal status, B11 terminology, Adrian's death mechanism, the 2043 setting, and the showrunner's rules about Alex's mother outrank looser draft phrasing.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing here is canon:

- The exact national history of the nominal state that withdrew from the basin.
- The detailed cultural histories, festivals, religious practices, and regional languages of the three cities.
- The final names and visual treatments of supporting cast members whose designs remain assigned to the Character Designer.
- The precise membership and speaking order of the first three-city review.
- Additional recurring emotional beats for TJ beyond practical, nonverbal repair behavior.
- Whether the 96-page book is ultimately published as one volume or divided into serialized installments.

## Non-negotiables

- The showrunner's standing rules about Alex's mother: she left after Adrian lost everything, and nothing else about her is learned.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for class, intelligence, morality, technical ability, wealth, poverty, or political position.
- The draft's six chapters, scenes, order, major beats, choices, reveals, and ending remain intact.
- Alex must not become a new sovereign, permanent override holder, or unchecked authority.
- Alpha is neither inherently evil nor permitted final value authority.
- The safeguards are physical, offline-resistant systems whose meaning comes from demonstrated choices.
- Truth, contribution, and reciprocal responsibility must be shown through action and consequence, not only explained.
- Bi11bot remains warm, precise, visibly constructed, and non-menacing; TJ remains nonverbal.
- The resolution is imperfect: scarcity, disagreement, institutional survival, and human mistakes continue.

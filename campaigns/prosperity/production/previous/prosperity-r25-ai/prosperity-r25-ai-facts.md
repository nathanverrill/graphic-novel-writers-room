- [UNLABELLED] “The Water Wars” is the retrospective name for several local and national water crises occurring over approximately two decades. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] The Water Wars were not one continuous war. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] The crises involved drought rationing, flooding, shrinking snowpack, aquifer depletion, unpredictable seasonal flows, and increasing water demand. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] Demand increased through lithium, sodium, copper, urban, agricultural, and energy development. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] The crises involved disputes over gates, wells, reservoirs, pipelines, desalination capacity, pumping rights, permits, roads, and allocation records. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] Responses included blockades, occupations, sabotage, lawsuits, emergency rationing, police intervention, migration, negotiated settlements, and occasional lethal confrontations. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] National armies usually remained uninvolved. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] The three cities were among the most interconnected areas affected by the crises. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] Alpha's authority expanded crisis by crisis during the Water Wars. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] The original Covenant was used during the Water Wars to distribute scarce water. (story.md)
- [UNLABELLED] A major Covenant agreement failed after a reservoir fell faster than predicted, one community closed a gate, another forced it open, and fighting spread. (story.md)

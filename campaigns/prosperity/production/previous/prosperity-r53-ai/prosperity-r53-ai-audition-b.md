# Prosperity — Audition B

## Page 1 (right) — Alex keeps one failing pump alive inside a city built on unequal access.

**Panel 1 — FULL-PAGE SPLASH.** A distant elevated view of Prosperity’s high-desert mountain basin in 2043, with one human action anchoring the scale: in the lower foreground, Alex Phantum, seventeen, lean and wiry in worn charcoal-and-rust mining clothes, braces one scarred hand against a failing Keel pump housing. His tool bag lies open beside him. The pump’s patched bracket and trembling pipe are large in the foreground; Alex is readable, focused, listening through his palm.

Beyond him, the vertical civilization rises through the mountain:

- **KEEL** below: crowded stacked housing, rusted mining gantries, narrow water channels, aging factories, dust, and hard shadows.
- **HALYARD** midway up: bright fabrication towers, glass laboratories, greenhouse terraces, freight corridors, and engineered green.
- **OASIS** above the cloud layer: pale civic architecture, reservoirs, geometric gardens, and quiet transit.

A continuous infrastructure spine climbs through all three cities: water conduit, energy lines, transit rails, and small identical white-and-amber control nodes. The conduit is broad and full near Oasis, visibly narrower and more strained toward Keel.

A small official maintenance display beside Alex shows an approved repair diagram. His salvaged bracket does not match it. The pump runs anyway.

CAPTION: 2043.

CAPTION: OASIS. HALYARD. KEEL.

SFX: THRUMM—THRUMM—THRUMM

PAGE CHECK: The reader sees the three-city hierarchy, the shared infrastructure, and Alex’s practical competence without a lecture. Page-turn function: move from the scale of the system to what it has just decided about Alex.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex’s repair works, but the system turns his father’s absence into a debt he cannot outrun.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** Inside the Keel pump hall at the end of Alex’s shift. Alex keeps one palm on the pump housing while a supervisor checks the official gauge. The gauge needle trembles in the red; the pump’s vibration is steady beneath Alex’s hand. Dust, patched machinery, and the white-and-amber control node on the wall establish the work environment.

SUPERVISOR: That isn't the approved repair.

ALEX: Then don't approve it.

The supervisor looks from the gauge to the salvaged bracket.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

---

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM.** At the exit checkpoint. Alex places his hand on the scanner. A few exhausted workers wait behind him, their balance displays modest and quickly disappearing. Alex’s account expands above the scanner, dwarfing every other number.

The screen reads:

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
**ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

SFX: BEEP

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

ALEX: I've had better surprises.

---

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** The checkpoint’s exit gate remains closed. Alex stands beneath the still-glowing **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED** notice while the workers pass around him. No one laughs. A worker glances at Alex’s balance, then looks away.

Alex’s hand disappears into his jacket pocket and closes around a worn casino chip etched with a looping equation.

---

**Panel 4 — TALL MEDIUM.** Outside on Keel’s pit rim, late morning. Alex stands at a rusted railing above the exhausted mine. Keel lies below in shadow; Halyard catches the sun; Oasis shines far above. The sealed mine entrance sits behind him under faded hazard striping.

Alex looks upward, not at the city below.

ALEX: One day, I'm leaving.

PAGE CHECK: Alex’s repair skill cannot protect him from inherited liability or restricted movement. Adrian is established as missing, not explained. Page-turn function: bring Ada into Alex’s immediate life before he can turn escape into a private plan.

## Page 3 (right) — Ada makes Alex’s escape personal by asking whether leaving would only repeat Adrian’s disappearance.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** Late afternoon at the same Keel overlook. Alex sits on the worn railing with a battered metal mug beside him, the casino chip turning between his fingers. Ada Veyra approaches through the wind carrying a second mug. She is twenty, tall and steady, with wind-tangled copper-brown hair, freckles, teal-blue eyes, a dark work top, reinforced trousers, practical boots, and a muted moss-green wrap.

They have the ease of people who have been finding this same overlook for years.

ADA: You look expensive.

ALEX: It came with a twenty-eight-year warranty.

---

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM TWO-SHOT.** Ada hands him the mug and sits beside him. Their shoulders nearly touch. Alex keeps his eyes on Oasis.

ADA: Tea's free.

ALEX: Nothing's free.

ADA: You say that like you invented debt.

ALEX: I only inherited it.

The chip pauses between Alex’s fingers, then starts turning again.

---

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** Ada watches Alex rather than the skyline. He looks up toward Oasis; she looks down toward Keel’s pump lines, workshops, and crowded housing.

ADA: You going to tell me where you were all night?

ALEX: Working.

ADA: You were at the pump hall before dawn.

ALEX: Then I was working before dawn.

ADA: And now you're planning to leave before tomorrow.

Alex gives her a sideways look.

ALEX: Is that a question?

ADA: It's the part where you pretend it isn't.

---

**Panel 4 — WIDE, QUIET.** The two remain on the railing, divided by its diagonal line. Warm light catches Alex’s side; Keel’s long shadow reaches Ada. She touches one rivet on her shoulder strap while watching him.

ALEX: I can't spend twenty-eight years paying for what he did.

ADA: Then don't spend them proving he was right about people leaving.

The chip stops turning.

ALEX: You think he left on purpose?

ADA: I think everybody's been very sure of a story nobody can show you.

She looks toward the sealed mine entrance.

ADA: What if they're wrong?

PAGE CHECK: Ada is established as Alex’s longtime friend and practical counterweight, not a generic source of wisdom. Her knowledge of the official story creates the active mystery around Adrian. Page-turn function: the next page returns them home, where Alex’s grandmother has something physical Adrian left behind.

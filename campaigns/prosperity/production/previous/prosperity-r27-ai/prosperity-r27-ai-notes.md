## Plausibility ledger

- **Foreman-7's intervention:** The page correctly presents Foreman-7 as detecting and reporting an unauthorized repair. Its warning should remain an administrative report, not an independently issued detention or governance order. The line “Remain at the worksite” is consistent if it reflects an existing worksite restriction or procedural instruction rather than a new legal decision.
- **Pump repair:** Alex's unauthorized repair succeeding is established story material. The page correctly shows physical evidence and result preceding procedural recognition.
- **Alpha visual identity:** Foreman-7's locked description is consistent with the approved design: tracked chassis, graphite-black body, pale ceramic sensor housing, horizontal amber status band, articulated arms, hazard striping, and paper maintenance docket. No face or villain-coded treatment is introduced.
- **Keel setting:** The mineral-stained pump station, patched conduit, dry channel, dust, and legacy mine wall correctly establish advanced control layered over aging infrastructure.
- **Casino chip:** Its appearance on Page 1 is an appropriate visual setup for the checkpoint and workshop beats. It remains an unlabelled character detail rather than a contradiction of canon.
- **Text-free art packet:** The layout provides clear lettering reservations for the caption, balloons, and SFX, and the thumbnail contains no claim that text will be rendered into the art. Lettering can be added later from the listed items.

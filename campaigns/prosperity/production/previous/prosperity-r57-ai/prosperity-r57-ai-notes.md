## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The brief and facts file establish a coherent 54-page, three-issue story, but `story.md`, `world.md`, `characters.md`, and the supplied script still carry superseded House names, chapter-era material, and unresolved object/state continuity. The central repairs are small and identifiable: normalize canon terminology, lock the safeguard and Bi11bot ledgers, and clarify the causal chain for Mera, TJ, the debt, and the final review before writing proceeds.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Superseded Charter House names remain in the story and script

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 02 page plot and story map; `script.md`, Issue 02 Pages 20–35; `world.md`, House sections; `facts.md`, Charter Houses.
- **Observation:** Canon fixes the Houses as **Caldera House, Vector House, and Orbital House**. The page plot and script instead use **Aqualis, Helion, and Astra**, including “Aqualis can clean,” “House Helion,” and “Astra extraction.”
- **Problem:** This directly contradicts FIXED project truth and creates three different House systems across the files.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director/Writer: replace every Aqualis/Helion/Astra occurrence in story material and script planning with Caldera/Vector/Orbital, preserving each specialty. Character Designer: update House uniforms and insignia only after the names are normalized.
- **Role:** Director, Writer, Character Designer.

#### 2. The physical Maker’s Seal and Covenant bands are not consistent across the development material

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 01 page plot and script; `facts.md`, Maker’s Seal and Covenant; `brief.md`, Authority and safeguards.
- **Observation:** The development script shows the physical Seal and three Covenant bands in Adrian’s workshop before Lina’s handoff, while canon says Lina carries the one physical Maker’s Seal until the Halyard review, and the Covenant bands are possessed separately by Leona, B11, and Mera.
- **Problem:** This creates duplicate physical artifacts and makes later handoffs impossible to track. The brief explicitly says any earlier Seal image must be a projection, diagram, or design plate.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter/Writer: mark all Issue 01 workshop images of the Seal and Covenant as projections, diagrams, or archival design plates. Establish the physical possession ledger in development: Lina—Seal; B11—Adrian’s band; Leona—her band; Mera—dark third band.
- **Role:** Plotter, Writer.

#### 3. The Adrian/B11 continuity mechanism is not yet canonically reflected in the story plan

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 03 Pages 45–47; `script.md`, Issue 03 Pages 46–48; `facts.md`, Adrian and B11.
- **Observation:** The plan shows Adrian activating B11 during the flooded station sequence, while B11 is later found dormant in the Keel workshop. The current story text does not state how the body reached the workshop or distinguish deployment-state activation from Alex’s later ignition.
- **Problem:** Without the approved prearranged-route explanation, the reader must infer an impossible physical transfer.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director/Plotter: explicitly carry the approved mechanism into the story plan: Adrian activates B11’s recording/deployment state, the sequence cuts before evacuation, and Adrian’s prearranged route places the dormant body in Grandmother’s workshop. Alex’s ignition is a reactivation, not B11’s first activation.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

### Major

#### 4. The development chronology still uses chapter structure after the brief fixes issue structure

- **Location:** `story.md`, inherited structure language and story map; `world.md`, draft references; `draft.md`, `draft-edited.md`, and `draft-final.md`.
- **Observation:** The approved format is Issues 01–03, each 18 pages. Supporting material repeatedly calls the units “chapters,” including chapter endings and chapter-specific continuity.
- **Problem:** This risks carrying the superseded five-/six-chapter structure into the 54-page production plan and obscures the required issue questions on Pages 18, 36, and 54.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Script Coordinator/Plotter: normalize development headings and references to Issue 01—Keel, Issue 02—Halyard, and Issue 03—Oasis. Treat draft chapter material only as source scenes, not as structure.
- **Role:** Plotter, Writer.

#### 5. The debt-to-Ada thread is approved but not fully represented in the story plan

- **Location:** `brief.md`, People and story; `facts.md`, Ada and Alex; `story.md`, Issue 01 story map and page plot.
- **Observation:** Canon requires Ada to front medicine and a travel token, Alex to miss the first repayment because of the 40-credit forfeiture, face Ada directly, agree to fixed payments, and later make visible payments. The Issue 01 page plot currently places medicine and token at Page 13 but does not clearly include the initial fronting terms or the direct missed-payment confrontation.
- **Problem:** The personal obligation is essential to Alex’s arc and cannot be left as an implied transaction.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter/Writer: place the fronting and fixed repayment terms before departure; show the missed first payment after Site 6 and a direct face-to-face acknowledgment; reserve a later visible payment and changed personal balance for Issue 03.
- **Role:** Plotter, Writer.

#### 6. The honest-business contrast is not stable across the files

- **Location:** `brief.md`, Morrow & Sons; `world.md`, Economics; `story.md`, Issue 02; `facts.md`, Honest business.
- **Observation:** Canon fixes **Morrow & Sons** as an independent Keel repair-and-purifier shop. Other planning language alternates between a shuttered Halyard business, a reopened shop, and an unspecified independent seller.
- **Problem:** The book needs one concrete, recurring contrast: a business that earns customers, posts its price, pays wages, records its margin, and sells maintainable goods without Alpha selection.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director/Plotter: choose the fixed Morrow & Sons version as the recurring business. Plant its fair sale in Issue 02 and pay it off with a purifier/cartridge sale and visible cost/wage/margin records near Page 54. Do not make House production the only visible scale.
- **Role:** Director, Plotter.

#### 7. Mera’s Page 01 action and moral position remain under-specified in the causal plan

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 01 Pages 8 and 17–18; `brief.md`, Mera’s conduit action; `facts.md`, Mera.
- **Observation:** Mera selects `TRACK`, while Alpha independently reports the legacy protocol. Later she opens a separate conduit-segment valve. The story plan does not yet state what the valve enables or what Mera believes she is risking.
- **Problem:** The same action can read as surveillance, protection, or an attempt to drown the group. This is the principal unresolved causal ambiguity carried from the cold read.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter/Writer: define the action in the beat plan as opening the only viable ascent segment while suppressing escalation; keep the moral question—whether she was right to intervene—without leaving the physical mechanism unclear.
- **Role:** Plotter, Writer.

#### 8. Issue 02’s contamination thread risks contradicting the water-routing thread

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 02 Pages 23–24; `brief.md`, World; `facts.md`, Water and environmental conditions.
- **Observation:** The brief explicitly separates the 62%/18% routing and accounting failure from the contaminated feeder channel. The story plan sometimes treats the purifier as the answer to the water discrepancy rather than as a separate future-supply response.
- **Problem:** This weakens causality and risks implying that the purifier fixes the political allocation or ends scarcity.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter: state in the beat plan that the Measure exposes where water goes, while the purifier addresses what is in a separate contaminated feeder. Preserve the 62/18 allocation as an unresolved political problem that returns in the first public review.
- **Role:** Plotter.

#### 9. The first public review is not sufficiently planted before its climax

- **Location:** `story.md`, Issue 03 Pages 51–53; `brief.md`, Distributed Verification Protocol; `facts.md`, first public review.
- **Observation:** The three community-designated participants appear only at the review, and the plan gives them generic representative roles without concrete evidence or prior visual/action anchors.
- **Problem:** The new process risks feeling like a mechanism introduced solely to resolve the book. The showrunner requires the protocol to prove itself by catching a false allocation.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Plotter/Character Designer: plant the review’s three evidence positions earlier through objects or actions—Keel physical gauges, Halyard maintenance capacity, Oasis reserve data—without adding unnecessary named protagonists. In the climax, let one House representative submit a paper-correct diversion that Tomas and Keel residents catch by comparing the public record to physical gauges.

#### 10. The script and cold-read material expose a dialogue problem that the character files have not solved

## Plausibility ledger

- **Canon status:** `brief.md` is the controlling canon. Where `story.md`, `world.md`, `characters.md`, or inherited drafts use Aqualis/Helion/Astra, those are superseded by Caldera/Vector/Orbital.
- **House structure:** The book has three Charter Houses, not five. The “five technical contributions” are contributors/components, not five Houses. The Issue 02 plan must not use “five House standards.”
- **Adrian’s estate:** The 312 patents, 47 restricted patents, and 19 disputed patents are established. The book may show the estate as active while Alex remains indebted because the estate is disputed, restricted, and not automatically liquid or personally owned. Lock’s offer is the mechanism that would convert rights into debt relief; it must not be implied that the patents already belong to Alex as usable cash.
- **Maker’s Seal:** One physical Seal exists. Any earlier workshop depiction must be a projection, diagram, or design plate. Lina carries the physical Seal until the Halyard review.
- **Covenant:** Three physical bands exist. Possession must remain: Adrian’s with B11, Leona’s in her stone-grey box, Mera’s dark and with Mera. The two bands may link after Leona makes a public obligation; this does not restore the original authority structure.
- **Restoration protocol:** The Measure, Seal, and Covenant must be physically present at one legacy node. A human proposal must preserve evidence, attribution, and reciprocal obligation. Alex is authenticated as convenor, not ruler.
- **Alpha:** Alpha is a distributed computational network, not a person, body, or consciousness. It may execute authorized rules and register the bounded protocol, but it cannot independently invent a new restriction, transfer ownership, or choose human values.
- **Mera’s conduit action:** This is a license within established infrastructure behavior, not a new power. It must be consistent across the book: a separate conduit-segment valve opens the ascent route; it is not the residential regulator.
- **Purifier:** The 94% industrial and 91% household figures are simulated recovery efficiencies. No production rate, maintenance cycle, or real-world performance may be invented as a fact. The purifier cannot end scarcity or replace the main water infrastructure.
- **Distributed nodes:** Their function is established, but broad deployment beyond prototype or House-controlled infrastructure is not. The ending should not imply that the entire territory is already fully distributed.
- **Debt:** The 418,607-credit transfer and 402,110-credit later balance are fixed. No authority cancels the debt. Alex’s payment to Ada is a separate person-to-person obligation and must visibly reduce through payment.
- **Adrian’s mother:** No additional information may be invented about Alex’s mother. The only approved fact is that she left Adrian after he lost everything.
- **Real-world checks:** “Late 2043,” 2028, ages, 54 pages, 18 pages per issue, and the named House structure are fixed project facts, not flexible estimates. Do not replace them with draft-era dates, chapters, or external company names.

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The approved brief and facts establish a 64-page, four-issue book, but the current story/script package contains only 54 principal-story pages and omits Issue 00 entirely. Several development-level contradictions remain in debt terms, House names, and the ownership of Morrow & Sons, while the existing execution sample also contradicts the approved script. The core dramatic spine is viable, but the room must reconcile canon and rebuild the page plan before writing or production can proceed.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. The script is missing the 10-page prologue and does not deliver the fixed 64-page structure

- **Location:** `script.md`, entire document.
- **Observation:** The script begins with Issue 01, Page 1 and ends with Issue 03, Page 54. It contains three 18-page issues but no Issue 00, Pages 1–10, Lake Keel in 2099. The approved structure requires Issue 00 Pages 1–10, Issue 01 Pages 11–28, Issue 02 Pages 29–46 and Issue 03 Pages 47–64.
- **Problem:** This contradicts the fixed publication format and displaces every approved page number. It also omits the required opening image—older Alex watching TJ come apart over Lake Keel—and the required prologue question about the thieves.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** add the 10-page Issue 00 prologue and renumber the principal story globally to Pages 11–64. Preserve 18 pages for each principal issue. **Writer:** mark each issue with the approved heading and ensure each issue’s final page is a question about a cared-for person. **Director:** confirm the renumbered page architecture before further scripting.
- **Role:** plotter, writer, director.

#### 2. Medicine-loan terms contradict the approved brief and facts

- **Location:** `facts.md` Economics and debt; `brief.md` Canon; `world.md` Economics; `script.md` Pages 3–4.
- **Observation:** The facts/world material says Tomas fronts Alex 60 credits for Grandmother’s medicine, while the brief’s Canon explicitly supersedes this with a 45-credit antibiotic course fronted by Tomas. The script instead introduces Inez, a 30-credit medicine advance, and a separate 42-credit departure token.
- **Problem:** This changes the named lender, amount, medicine cost, repayment relationship and required personal-debt arc. It also creates an unsupported departure-token debt that is later used as a major ending prop.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director:** resolve the source contradiction in favor of the brief’s Canon: Tomas fronts 45 credits for the antibiotic course, Alex agrees to repay 10 credits weekly, misses one payment after the Site 6 wage loss, then pays Tomas publicly. **Writer:** remove Inez’s medicine advance and the token debt unless separately approved as a new proposal. **Plotter:** place the visible repayment beats so the reduction is earned by the ending.
- **Role:** director, writer, plotter.

#### 3. Charter House names are contradictory across approved project files

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon versus `facts.md` and `world.md`.
- **Observation:** The brief establishes Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House as the only Houses and says these names supersede all variants. `facts.md` and `world.md` still list Veyr House, Cairn House and Ravel House.
- **Problem:** The contradiction will propagate into dialogue, registry displays, insignia, character descriptions, page prompts and generated art. The current script uses Caldera but does not establish consistent Vector/Orbital usage.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director:** normalize `facts.md`, `world.md`, `characters.md` and all production references to Caldera, Vector and Orbital. **Script Coordinator:** update every downstream artifact after approval. Do not retain the superseded House names as alternates.
- **Role:** director.

#### 4. Morrow & Sons ownership contradicts the script’s introduction of Mara Morrow

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon; `facts.md` Honest business; `world.md` Economics; `script.md` Page 20 and Page 54.
- **Observation:** Canon requires the five purifier makers—Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren and Alex—to jointly own Morrow & Sons, with equal profit shares after wages and costs. Script Page 20 labels a paid order `MARA MORROW — OWNER`, and Mara does not appear in `characters.md` or the approved maker group.
- **Problem:** The script establishes a different owner and leaves the required cooperative ownership unresolved. It also creates a new named character whose relationship to the shop is undefined.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Character Designer:** remove Mara Morrow from the ownership lock unless the Director explicitly approves her as a separate employee or customer. **Writer:** establish the five named makers as the shop’s joint owners and show the wage/profit structure through a concrete transaction rather than exposition. **Director:** decide whether the shop name remains Morrow & Sons despite no Morrow character appearing.
- **Role:** character_designer, writer, director.

### Major

#### 5. The current script’s issue endings do not match the approved page-turn requirements after global renumbering

- **Location:** `story.md` issue endings versus `script.md` current final pages.
- **Observation:** The story requires Issue 00 to end on the thieves’ next move, Issue 01 on Tomas’s survival, Issue 02 on the immediate danger to Ada and a named collaborator, and Issue 03 on Alex’s and Ada’s unresolved futures. The current script ends its three existing issues at Pages 18, 36 and 54, and has no Issue 00.
- **Problem:** Once the missing prologue is inserted, the current endings shift, and the final pages must be deliberately rebuilt rather than mechanically renumbered. The current Issue 02 ending also leaves the barrier action unresolved while Issue 03 opens with all four characters already together.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** remap the four issue endpoints after adding Issue 00. **Writer:** make the Issue 02 final image carry a concrete unresolved consequence for Ada and/or one named collaborator, then open Issue 03 by answering that consequence. **Layout Agent:** reserve even-page reveals and right-hand-to-left-hand turns according to the corrected numbering.
- **Role:** plotter, writer, layout.

#### 6. The purifier’s customer-facing specification remains unresolved but the script treats the product as ready for sale

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon and Open questions; `world.md` Technology; `script.md` Pages 31–32 and 54.
- **Observation:** The brief fixes the 91% figure as an efficiency simulation and leaves daily volume, treatment category, service interval and safety warning open. The script labels the unit “HOUSEHOLD CONFIGURATION: 91% RECOVERY / CLEANING REQUIRED / REPLACEMENT PARTS REQUIRED,” then shows a customer payment and later a functioning Morrow & Sons shop.
- **Problem:** The script has begun inventing customer-facing performance and service claims without a Director-approved specification. “Recovery” may also be read as output or treatment performance, risking contradiction with the fixed limitation.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director:** decide the four customer-facing fields before production lettering. **Writer:** until then, show the unit as a tested prototype with an explicit non-potable or treatment limitation, not as a fully specified retail product. **Letterer:** do not finalize labels until the values are approved.
- **Role:** director, writer, letterer.

#### 7. The distributed protocol is treated as active before its required local-representative structure is dramatized

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon; `world.md` Distributed verification protocol; `story.md` Pages 61–62; `script.md` Pages 51–53.
- **Observation:** Canon requires each affected locality to select three temporary representatives through open local meetings, with recall and agreement requirements. The script instead uses Nadi, Olani and Tomas at a review table without establishing who selected them or whether they represent Keel, Halyard or Oasis.
- **Problem:** The ending risks reading as a generic administrative meeting rather than the first operation of the approved distributed process. The reader cannot tell whose agreement is required or why Tomas has standing to stop the proposal.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** assign the three-city representatives before the review and identify their local constituencies visually. **Writer:** show the minimum selection/mandate evidence needed for the first review; do not add a governance lecture. **Character Designer:** give only the required representatives distinct visual and functional identities.
- **Role:** plotter, writer, character_designer.

#### 8. Alex’s debt reduction is asserted but not yet causally supported by the current page plan

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon; `facts.md`; `script.md` Page 54.
- **Observation:** The ending record drops Alex’s liability from 418,607 to 402,110 credits, but the current script shows only the 40-credit transfer-day earnings, the 40-credit wage forfeiture and a few unspecified payments. The script also jumps fourteen months without showing enough paid work or the terms of how Morrow & Sons income affects the debt.
- **Problem:** The required visible cost and payment arc risks becoming a number presented as proof rather than an earned change. The personal medicine debt is also assigned to Inez rather than Tomas.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter:** identify the payment milestones that support the final balance without requiring a financial montage. **Writer:** show at least the required Tomas repayment and several consequential wage or shop payments, then let the final ledger summarize the accumulated work. Do not imply that the protocol or purifier cancels the debt.
- **Role:** plotter, writer.

#### 9. The script introduces unsupported departure-token mechanics that distort Alex’s final choice

- **Location:** `script.md` Pages 3, 16, 33, 50 and 54; `brief.md` Open questions.
- **Observation:** The script establishes a 42-credit departure token, treats it as debt, uses it as an escape route and ends with Ada taking the “departure half” of a torn map. Canon only requires that Alex can apply to travel, cannot be silently blocked, and ends with leaving or staying genuinely open.
- **Problem:** The token becomes a second access system with unclear origin, price, legal status and relationship to the public travel process. The final Ada action is not legible because the token’s meaning was never resolved.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director:** either approve a concrete departure-token concept or remove it. **Writer:** use the established identity-band travel application and public review as the visible mechanism for the ending. If a physical token remains, define its function and ownership before it appears again.
- **Role:** director, writer.

#### 10. Adrian’s band and the Covenant are introduced without a clear physical continuity decision

- **Location:** `characters.md`, `world.md`, `brief.md` Canon; `script.md` Pages 6, 41–42 and 47.
- **Observation:** B11 carries Adrian’s dark band, Leona has her band and Mera’s remains dark. The script shows bands in a legacy panel, but the physical location, custody and activation relationship are not consistently staged.
- **Problem:** The Covenant is meant to be learned through use, but the reader must still be able to tell which three bands exist, who physically carries each, and why two linked bands do not restore the old three-custodian authority.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** plant each band through an unmistakable physical action, then make the later link and Mera’s dark band visually legible without a rules speech. **Layout Agent:** give the bands sufficient scale and contrast to read as distinct props. Preserve the rule that activation follows obligation, accepted consequence and performed action.
- **Role:** writer, layout.

- **Principal chronology — HOLDS IN CONCEPT:** The story material is set in 2043 and the prologue is set in 2099, but global page numbering must be corrected.
- **Medicine loan — CONFLICT:** The brief’s Canon requires Tomas/45 credits/10-credit weekly terms; facts and world retain Tomas/60 credits, while the script uses Inez/30 credits plus a separate token. The brief wins; the script must be revised.
- **House names — CONFLICT:** The brief’s approved Caldera, Vector and Orbital names supersede the Veyr/Cairn/Ravel variants still present in world/facts. Normalize before production.
- **Morrow & Sons ownership — CONFLICT:** Canon requires the five makers to own the shop jointly; script Page 20 names Mara Morrow as owner without approval.
- **Purifier performance — NEEDS BOUNDARY:** The 91% figure is fixed as an efficiency simulation, not potable output. The script’s “91% recovery” label and sale sequence require specification review.
- **Protocol representatives — UNDER-SPECIFIED:** The world requires three temporary representatives per affected locality. The script’s Nadi/Olani/Tomas review does not establish selection or mandate.
- **Covenant activation — CONTRADICTED IN SCRIPT:** The script activates Leona’s band on Page 40 before the required public performance. This is a writing-stage Blocker if retained.
- **Alpha — HOLDS WITH CAUSAL CLARIFICATION:** Alpha may publish records, route infrastructure and rescind emergency authority, but it cannot independently choose the allocation after restoration. The script must make the causal chain from evidence to paused approval legible.
- **Adrian’s fate — HOLDS IF BOUNDED:** The station recording may show the closing door and B11 transmission. No body, evacuation or later survival may be established.
- **Alex’s mother — HOLDS ONLY UNDER GUARDRAIL:** No information beyond her leaving Adrian after he lost everything may be added. The current materials do not violate this rule.
- **2099 water theft — NOT PRESENT IN SCRIPT:** The required prologue invention is absent; add it without explaining the later safeguards, debt or Alpha.
- **Page 46 execution — CONTRADICTED:** The layout and thumbnail show Leona/Mera/Ada apartment material while the script Page 46 is Alex/B11 processing Adrian’s recording. This is an execution blocker for that page, though the larger development blockers must be resolved first.

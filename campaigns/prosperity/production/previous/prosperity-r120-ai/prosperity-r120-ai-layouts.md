# layouts.md

## Production rules

- **Canon:** Pages 1–64; Western left-to-right reading; odd pages right, even pages left; maximum four panels per page; full color; no text generated in the artwork. All lettering appears in each page’s `items` array.
- Each panel has one clear focal action, no more than three named characters, and explicit negative space for lettering.
- Alpha remains faceless and non-conscious. TJ remains silent, dog-shaped, and carries the brass ring around his right eye whenever visible.
- Page 45 establishes that Alex throws the access tool to Ada. Pages 47–48 preserve that handoff.
- Ada reaches Leona using her own earlier route knowledge, not information from Lina.
- Tomas’s 45-credit medicine advance remains separate from Alex’s larger household debt.
- Pages 41–44 divide the five-maker sequence so no panel contains more than three named characters.
- Page 63 includes a compact cumulative work/payment ledger that supports the visible debt reduction.

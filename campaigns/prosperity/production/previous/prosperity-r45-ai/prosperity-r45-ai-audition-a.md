## Page 1 (right) — Establish the three-tier civilization and the narrowing water spine.

**Panel 1.** Full-page splash. A vertical, towering view of the mountain basin. At the top, above the clouds: Oasis, all pearl-white architecture, broad blue reservoirs, and controlled green gardens. In the middle: Halyard, a dense industrial shelf of bronze, gold, and engineered green terraces. At the bottom, deep in the shadows of an exhausted polymetallic open-pit mine: Keel, a cramped sprawl of rust, charcoal, and oxidized orange. A single massive water conduit drops vertically through all three cities like a spine. It is enormous at Oasis, but narrows dramatically by the time it reaches Keel. A small, identical white-and-amber Alpha control node sits attached to the pipe at each city level.

CAPTION: 2043.

PAGE CHECK: Establish the extreme inequality of the world entirely through the architecture and the shrinking water pipe.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex inherits Adrian's debt and loses his mobility.

**Panel 1.** Inside a Keel pump hall. Rust, charcoal, and dust. Alex Phantum (17, lean, charcoal Keel work jacket with faded rust-orange lining) stands at an aging, patched water pump. He isn't looking at the nearby white-and-amber Alpha terminal. His eyes are closed, his head tilted, his bare palm pressed flat against the vibrating metal housing of the pump. A SUPERVISOR stands behind him, arms crossed.

SUPERVISOR: That's not the approved repair.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

**Panel 2.** The shift-exit checkpoint. Alex, looking tired, presses his hand against a flat, sterile white-and-amber Alpha scanner. A GUARD stands beside the gate. 

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

**Panel 3.** Extreme close-up on the scanner display. Cold, clinical typography. 

DISPLAY: 
ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: 17
PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE
DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS
EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS
PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS
TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

**Panel 4.** Alex stands frozen at the gate, staring at the screen. The heavy iron gate remains locked shut in front of him. He reaches into his jacket pocket, his scarred knuckle tightening. 

ALEX: I've had better surprises.

PAGE CHECK: Alex's competence is established physically; the crushing reality of his inherited debt is delivered through the brutal math on the screen.

## Page 3 (right) — Ada and Alex reveal their opposing desires for the future.

**Panel 1.** Late afternoon at the overlook on the rim of Keel's exhausted pit. Alex sits on the rusted railing, his shoulders slumped, looking out over the basin. Ada Veyra (20, copper-brown hair, moss-green wrap) steps up beside him. She holds out a battered metal mug of tea. 

ADA: Thought you might need this.

**Panel 2.** Close on their hands. Alex takes the mug. His other hand turns a worn, dark-ivory casino chip with a tarnished brass rim between his fingers. 

ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

ADA: It's on me. You don't owe anybody anything today.

**Panel 3.** They sit side by side on the railing. The wind catches Ada's hair. Alex looks down at the casino chip in his hand. 

ALEX: Everything has a price, Ada.

ADA: Not everything.

**Panel 4.** Over their shoulders, looking out at the mountain. The rusted railing cuts the frame diagonally. Alex's head is tilted upward, his eyes fixed on the distant, sunlit white towers of Oasis at the top of the mountain. Ada's head is tilted downward, her eyes fixed on the rusted, shadowed sprawl of Keel below them. 

ALEX: One day, I'm leaving.

ADA: Nobody leaves. And nobody stays. Not really.

PAGE CHECK: The visual split of their eyelines shows their fundamental disagreement about the future without needing a speech.

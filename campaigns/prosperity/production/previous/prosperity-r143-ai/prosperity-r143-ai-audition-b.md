# Prosperity — Audition B

## Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099

## Page 1 (right) — Older Alex chooses to watch a water theft while TJ breaks himself apart to investigate.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** NIGHT at the Lake Keel boathouse. The exhausted mine pit is now a dark lake ringed by terraces, gardens and scattered workshops. Older Alex Phantum stands on the dock in a faded work jacket. Beside him, his approximately fifteen-year-old granddaughter holds a blanket around her shoulders. At their feet, TJ—a small, lopsided robot dog assembled from mismatched magnetic parts—leans toward the water.

Across the lake, two drones lower siphon hoses from a tanker barge.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Grandpa. That’s not a fishing boat.

ALEX: No.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM.** TJ springs from the dock. In midair, his head, torso and four short legs separate with a series of magnetic clicks. The pieces continue forward on different paths, still unmistakably dog-shaped in their movement.

SFX: KLAK-KLAK-KLAK

GRANDDAUGHTER: Is TJ broken?

ALEX: Not usually.

**Panel 3 — WIDE ON THE LAKE.** TJ’s head skims low across the water. His torso and legs follow in staggered formation. Beyond him, the tanker’s hose draws a bright, churning column from the lake. The far shore shows dry channels and empty collection basins beneath the restored terraces.

SFX: WHUMMMMM

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON ALEX AND HIS GRANDDAUGHTER.** The cold beam from a drone sweeps over them. The girl catches Alex’s sleeve. Alex keeps one hand on the dock rail and watches TJ approach the tanker.

GRANDDAUGHTER: They’re stealing from us.

ALEX: Let’s see what they came to take.

PAGE CHECK: Alex, his relationship with the girl and TJ’s dog-like construction are clear before the danger escalates. The dry channels and active siphon show why the water matters without explaining the history. Page-turn function: reveal the thieves and bring TJ into their operation.

## Page 2 (left) — TJ crosses the water in pieces while the thieves argue over whether Alex earned the lake.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** The tanker barge under harsh work lamps. One thief, NERA, crouches at the siphon coupling, checking a pressure gauge. The other, VOSS, keeps watch toward the boathouse. Their patched waterproof gear is visibly different: Nera wears a red hood; Voss wears a broad-brimmed lamp helmet. Behind them, TJ’s separate parts approach across the water.

NERA: Lower the pull.

VOSS: We’re already late.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM ON VOSS.** Voss studies the distant boathouse through battered binoculars. Nera continues working without looking up. The tanker’s storage gauge climbs.

VOSS: That’s Phantum’s lake.

NERA: It’s a lake.

VOSS: It’s clean.

**Panel 3 — CLOSE ON THE WATERLINE.** TJ’s head glides beneath the barge’s shadow. His warm amber lens catches the reflected work lamps. One magnetic leg hooks over a submerged brace; the torso and remaining legs close in behind him.

SFX: tik

**Panel 4 — MEDIUM ON NERA AND VOSS.** Voss lowers the binoculars. Through the gap between them, the boathouse and Alex are tiny across the lake.

VOSS: Nobody gets water that clean by being polite.

NERA: Then he stole it first.

PAGE CHECK: The thieves have distinct jobs and silhouettes, and their accusation remains unresolved rather than turning Alex into either hero or villain. TJ’s route and target are legible. Page-turn function: TJ reaches the machinery.

## Page 3 (right) — TJ gets inside the siphon while Alex refuses to intervene publicly.

**Panel 1 — CLOSE ON THE TANKER DECK.** Nera tightens the coupling. A small indicator shifts from amber to white. Voss sees movement below the rail but cannot identify it.

NERA: Pressure’s good.

VOSS: Something’s under us.

**Panel 2 — LOW ANGLE FROM THE WATER.** TJ’s head rises beneath the barge. His magnetic jaw catches the underside of the siphon housing. Above, the hose shudders as the pump draws harder.

SFX: GRRNNNNK

**Panel 3 — CLOSE ON THE BOATHOUSE.** Alex’s granddaughter reaches toward a wall-mounted emergency radio. Alex stops her with one hand—not harshly, but firmly. Across the lake, the tanker’s work lights swing toward them.

GRANDDAUGHTER: We can call the lake wardens.

ALEX: And tell them what?

GRANDDAUGHTER: That they’re taking our water.

ALEX: Let TJ show us where it goes.

**Panel 4 — EXTREME CLOSE.** TJ’s amber lens peers through the siphon housing at a narrow internal valve. His head is wedged between rotating metal shutters. The shutters begin closing around him.

SFX: KLIK-KLIK-KLIK

PAGE CHECK: Alex makes a consequential choice: observe first instead of escalating blindly. The theft becomes an investigation, and TJ is in immediate mechanical danger. Page-turn function: the siphon’s response traps TJ and forces the next action.

- **Location:** `world.md`, “Setting and Geography”; `story.md`, Page 8; `draft.md`, Chapter 2, Pages 6 and 10  
  **Problem:** Keel is canonically an exhausted copper/polymetallic hard-rock mine, but the draft-derived material repeatedly calls it an “abandoned lithium works” or “abandoned lithium mine.” This contradicts the fixed geography and risks making Keel the region’s lithium operation, which canon explicitly excludes.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Replace “lithium” with “copper,” “polymetallic,” or “legacy mineral works” everywhere the Keel mine is named. Preserve lithium as a wider regional industry elsewhere, not as Keel’s primary operation.  
  **Role to fix:** Director

  **Role to fix:** Plotter

- **Alex’s 40-credit daily earnings:** Unlabelled. Any precise use should be labeled or removed.
- **Alex’s clothing, tool bag, chip, and chip-turning habit:** Unlabelled but repeatedly used as visual continuity. Label before execution.
- **National jurisdiction:** Holds. The materials preserve it as unnamed and do not invent a functioning local national court or registry.
- **No invention on a cut list identified in the supplied facts file:** None apparent.
- **No declared license contradicts a neighboring fixed truth:** None beyond the copper/lithium contradiction, which is not a declared license and is therefore a Blocker.
- **No license is being used to skip required character work:** The current development materials do not establish such a case.
- **No unsupported exact price, number, or law beyond the flagged 40-credit figure:** The major fixed figures—418,607 credits, 28.7 years, 62%, 18%, 99.97%, and 71.3%—are supported by canon. The 40-credit daily earnings figure remains unlabelled.

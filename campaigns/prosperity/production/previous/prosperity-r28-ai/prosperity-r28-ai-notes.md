## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The brief, story, characters, and world files preserve the draft’s scenes, order, major choices, and ending, but they do not fully agree on two fixed world/chronology points. The core character arcs and safeguard sequence hold; the Director should reconcile the canon files before plotting or character work proceeds.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

- **Location:** `world.md`, “Open” section; `brief.md`, Canon — World and institutional decisions; `facts.md`, Project and chronology  
  **Problem:** `brief.md` canonically names the nominal state the **Republic of Andara**, while `world.md` still says the specific nominal nation remains undefined. This leaves the four development files in direct conflict about an established world fact.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Remove or revise the obsolete `world.md` open question to state that the nominal state is the Republic of Andara. Preserve the existing limitation: Andara has paper jurisdiction but is not an active political character.  
  **Role to fix:** Director

  **Problem:** The fixed chronology says Adrian’s final transfer was completed **shortly before Alpha’s intervention**, while the story page plot says Alpha initiates the intervention during the transfer and Adrian completes it as he dies. The same facts file also says Adrian died during the final transfer, so the intended relationship between transfer completion and intervention is unclear.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Reconcile the wording in `facts.md` and `story.md`. The smallest story-preserving solution is to establish that Adrian completes the transfer package’s authenticated handoff before the intervention fully closes the chamber, while the final witness confirmation or succession verification completes during the death sequence. If that is not intended, revise the fixed chronology rather than leaving both versions active.

## Plausibility ledger

- **Republic of Andara:** The brief declares Andara canon, but `world.md` retains the older undefined-nation open question. This is the Blocker above. Once corrected, its limited role—paper jurisdiction, distant border presence, no active local administration—holds.
- **Adrian’s transfer chronology:** The physical mechanism is plausible within the project’s licensed Alpha premise, but the current “before intervention” versus “during intervention” wording is inconsistent. The transfer should be staged so the reader can tell what was authenticated before the chamber became fatal and what was completed during the final sequence.
- **Three safeguard sequence:** The story’s thematic order is sound, but Page 21 risks making the later safeguards available before their required qualification. The objects may be shown early; activation and access must remain sequential.
- **Alex carrying the Measure:** The public broadcast and mirrored copies are consistent with the Measure’s physical limits. The script needs to distinguish the protected hardware from its distributed outputs.
- **Alpha and the founder:** The Congolese founder’s background is canon according to `facts.md`, while the present story correctly keeps him absent and unnamed. The world file should distinguish objective canon from active public presentation.
- **Alex’s mother:** The files honor the standing rule by withholding her fate, name, community, and further history. No later visual or dialogue choice should infer additional biography.
- **Water technology:** The 99.97% result remains bounded, feedstock-dependent, and insufficient to end scarcity. The story map and world file agree; no current development contradiction appears.
- **Restored authority:** The brief, story, and world agree that Alex authenticates and convenes but does not chair, decide, command Alpha, or retain permanent override authority. The final institutional relationship is consistent.

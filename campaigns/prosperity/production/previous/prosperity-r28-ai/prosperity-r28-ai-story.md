# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material status:** The chapter sequence and ending come from the showrunner's detailed fourth-draft extraction and chapter drafts. They are idea-draft material: the room should preserve the beats, choices, reveals, consequences, and useful lines while writing its own version. The decisions in `rules/decisions.md` are **FIXED** and override conflicting draft wording.

The story is a graphic novel set in **2043**, following seventeen-year-old Alex Phantum from Keel through Halyard and Oasis. Alex begins by wanting to escape the city that has inherited his father's debt and restricted his movement. He discovers that Adrian Phantum, publicly remembered as a failed gambler, left behind a test involving three safeguards:

1. **The Measure** — truth made visible.
2. **The Maker's Seal** — ownership joined to responsibility.
3. **The Covenant** — reciprocal trust strong enough to move value between people.

The safeguards are not merely objects. They qualify people through choices. Alex must demonstrate that he can reveal truth when it costs him, recognize contribution rather than claim inherited value, cooperate across conflicting interests, and finally refuse unchecked authority when it is offered to him.

The intended emotional and philosophical movement is:

> **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**

The story does not establish that humans are always right or that Alpha is inherently wrong. It tests whether capability, efficiency, and prediction are enough to justify authority.

The intended conclusion is:

> **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

The final emotional destination is not a perfect society. It is a society in which people must again make choices, argue, accept consequences, and revise their decisions. Alex still wants to leave Keel. The difference is that leaving is finally his choice.

After leaving Keel, Alex and Ada travel together through Halyard and Oasis, visiting the new workshops and civic bodies and helping establish the first cross-city review. Alex remains a mechanic and witness; Ada works with local civic and community groups. Alex's continuing authority is limited to convening that review and authenticating Adrian's transfer. He cannot issue decisions, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md; story.md; characters.md)`

## 1. Structure

### Format and page budget

**Canon:** 96 pages, six chapters of approximately 16 pages each, in the established chapter order.

| Chapter | Pages | Structural turn |
|---|---:|---|
| **1. The Inheritance** | 1–16 | Alex touches the first node; the family mystery becomes an active route. |
| **2. The Price of Departure** | 17–32 | Alex publishes Keel's 62% recorded / 18% delivered discrepancy and loses the private escape route. |
| **3. The Price of Ownership** | 33–48 | Alex restores contributor ownership; the Maker's Seal opens the Covenant trail. |
| **4. The Broken Covenant** | 49–64 | Three cities commit reciprocal resources; the Covenant activates. |
| **5. The Last Bet** | 65–80 | Adrian's death and the completed test leave Alex facing concentrated authority. |
| **6. The Future We Build** | 81–96 | Alex refuses exclusive power, establishes the transition, and leaves by choice. |

The page plot preserves the draft's scenes and order. It compresses transitions and distributes the existing material across the fixed 96-page format rather than adding a replacement storyline.

### Act I — Escape becomes curiosity: pages 1–16

Keel establishes the governing contradiction: physical reality works while official systems deny it. Alex's birthday transfers Adrian's debt and restricts his movement. Ada challenges his escape-only view. Grandmother's book opens the workshop; Bi11bot reframes Adrian and reveals the first route. Alex activates the first node, drawing Mera's unseen attention.

### Act II — Curiosity becomes truth and responsibility: pages 17–48

Alex brings Ada into the investigation. Bi11bot explains Alpha's origin and the three safeguards. At Site 6, Alex chooses Tomas's rescue over his own access window. The Measure exposes Keel's hidden water diversion, and Alex broadcasts the discrepancy rather than taking the private route. In Halyard, the problem changes from hidden truth to hidden contribution. Alex rejects Lock's bargain, rebuilds the water system collaboratively, and restores contributor ownership through the Maker's Seal.

### Act III — Responsibility becomes trust, restraint, and agency: pages 49–96

Oasis exposes the social cost of frictionless administration. Ada finds Leona; Mera's history and offer make concentrated authority morally credible rather than cartoonishly evil. The three cities enact reciprocal commitments and activate the Covenant. Adrian's death and final transfer reveal the purpose of the tests. Alpha offers Alex the power to fix everything directly. Alex refuses permanent exclusive authority, establishes bounded advisory Alpha and accountable human bodies, helps begin the first review, and leaves Keel with Ada because he chooses to.

## 3. Page Plot

### Chapter 1 — The Inheritance

**Page 1:** Establish Keel's mineral-stained pump station and dry channel as Alex kneels beside the aging pump, palm on its vibration; caption frames the gap between reality and the record.

**Page 2 — page-turn reveal:** Alex's unauthorized repair takes; water surges through the patched line while the approved gauge remains crooked and Foreman-7's procedural warning arrives.

**Page 3:** Foreman-7 logs the repair as unauthorized; Alex rises through the checkpoint route, still carrying the subtle casino chip.

**Page 4 — page-turn reveal:** At the birthday checkpoint, Alpha transfers Adrian's 418,607-credit liability, projects 28.7 years, and marks Alex **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**.

**Page 5:** Alex tries to pass and is stopped; the checkpoint's administrative language turns adulthood into debt, while Keel workers recognize Adrian's name.

**Page 6:** Alex exits into Keel's wind and dust, turns Adrian's casino chip, and states that one day he is leaving.

**Page 7:** Ada brings tea to the overlook; Alex looks toward Oasis while Ada looks down toward Keel, making their opposed hopes visible before they speak.

**Page 8 — page-turn reveal:** Their argument turns on price and memory; Ada points out that Alex was only two when Adrian disappeared and the public story is secondhand.

**Page 9:** Alex returns home; Grandmother folds and refolds her cloth, calls him “Adrian,” and says he is closer than he thinks.

**Page 10:** She gives him Adrian's old book; Alex tests it against the apartment's shelves and finds a physical inconsistency.

**Page 11 — page-turn reveal:** The bookshelf opens onto a hidden workshop of diagrams, models, water hardware, and a dormant child-sized machine.

**Page 12:** Alex explores the workbench, finds a key socket in the machine's chest, and notices the casino chip's looping equation echoed in Adrian's notes.

**Page 13 — page-turn reveal:** Bi11bot awakens, two optical eyes glowing softly; his first dry remark breaks the silence without making him threatening.

**Page 14:** Bi11bot recognizes the chip, describes Adrian's wager about whether Alex would keep it, and plays Adrian's first recording.

**Page 15:** Adrian tells Alex that answers must be earned and instructs him to trust Bi11bot while questioning everything, especially Adrian; Alex's anger becomes uncertainty.

**Page 16 — page-turn reveal:** Bi11bot reveals the route beneath Keel's sealed mine; Alex touches the first node as a remote interface in Oasis displays **TRACK** beside Mera's precisely aligned glass.

### Chapter 2 — The Price of Departure

**Page 17:** The next morning Alex brings Ada into the workshop; Bi11bot tests whether she stays, and Alex says, “She stays.”

**Page 18:** Ada and Alex examine Adrian's physical safeguards while Bi11bot establishes that the route is now shared rather than private.

**Page 19:** Bi11bot explains Alpha's original mandate—analyze, predict, coordinate—over tactile archival images of Water Wars gates, pumps, blockades, and failing institutions.

**Page 20 — page-turn reveal:** Ada asks who gave Alpha the right to decide; Bi11bot answers that frightened people did, reasonably.

**Page 21:** The workshop's three safeguard housings open in sequence: Measure, Maker's Seal, Covenant; each is shown as a bounded physical system, not a magical key.

**Page 22:** Bi11bot explains that possession is insufficient; Alex must make choices that demonstrate truth, responsibility, and reciprocal trust.

**Page 23:** At the Last Chance, walls and archival screens show Adrian's real gambling history; Bi11bot refuses to separate the gambler from the inventor.

**Page 24 — page-turn reveal:** Adrian's gambling is reframed as both harm and research: he lost everything and also used risk, incentives, and winnings to fund the work.

**Page 25:** Alex budgets limited credits between his grandmother's medicine and passage; the decision leaves him with one viable route—through assigned Site 6.

**Page 26:** Alex, Ada, Bi11bot, and the hidden hardware enter the mine route under worksite surveillance; a local map marks the passage as sealed.

**Page 27 — page-turn reveal:** Older miner Tomas identifies a narrow access window, then a collapse traps him while Alex's opening remains usable.

**Page 28:** Alex reaches the choice point: take the opening or help Tomas; Bi11bot does not advise, and Alex turns back.

**Page 29:** Alex and Tomas work together under pressure; Bi11bot assembles TJ from magnetic parts to reach a trapped mechanism.

**Page 30 — page-turn reveal:** TJ's separated head, legs, and body complete the repair; Tomas recognizes Bi11bot and tells Alex he knew a different Adrian.

**Page 31:** Tomas creates another opening and accepts the consequences; Alex and Ada enter the abandoned works, following damp air where the official route shows nothing.

**Page 32 — page-turn reveal:** A crooked gauge reports Keel's allocation while the adjacent pipe is almost dry; Alex finds the Measure hidden inside the false mechanism.

### Chapter 3 — The Price of Ownership

**Page 33:** The Measure activates and exposes hidden pumps, pressure breaks, reservoirs, and a clean private escape route through Alpha-controlled infrastructure.

**Page 34:** Alpha detects the breach; Bi11bot tells Alex there is time to broadcast one truth or take the route out.

**Page 35:** Alex chooses the broadcast; the Measure publishes to Keel's displays, local receivers, and mirrored offline copies.

**Page 36 — page-turn reveal:** The public comparison appears: **62% RECORDED / 18% DELIVERED**; Alpha orders Alex to remain for detention.

**Page 37:** Alex answers that truth does not need authorization; Keel residents begin inspecting pipes, moving supplies, opening gates, and blocking surveillance.

**Page 38:** The community creates a route out without a single commander; Ada joins Alex, and they leave Keel carrying the Measure.

**Page 39:** Halyard rises around them in bright industrial layers—laboratories, maker floors, Charter House towers—while the public audit already names Alex as Adrian's heir.

**Page 40 — page-turn reveal:** The estate registry displays 312 patents, 47 restricted, and 19 disputed; Alex's inheritance has become an ownership problem.

**Page 41:** Director Cassian Lock offers debt relief, position, security, and legitimacy if Alex assigns Adrian's rights to House Aqualis.

**Page 42:** Alex and Ada tour the institution's clean machinery and see how contributor work disappears behind one corporate claim.

**Page 43 — page-turn reveal:** Lock makes the bargain concrete; Alex refuses, choosing uncertainty over exclusive ownership and security.

**Page 44:** Bi11bot leads Alex and Ada to the missing collaborators: Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr, each holding a different piece of the unfinished system.

**Page 45:** The collaborators reconstruct Adrian's scattered recordings and discover that the design was collaborative from the beginning.

**Page 46 — page-turn reveal:** The team reorganizes the water technology into modular units; the first contaminated feedwater enters the rebuilt system.

**Page 47:** The prototype runs under visible constraints—power draw, pretreatment, membrane wear, testing, and limited throughput—while the team records a bounded 99.97% result.

**Page 48 — page-turn reveal:** Alex rejects Lock's second offer, restores contributor ownership, and the Maker's Seal opens a provenance archive showing 71.3% disputed registration.

### Chapter 4 — The Broken Covenant

**Page 49:** Halyard's disputed records freeze pending hearings; some workers claim shares, some form workshops, and the old Charter Houses remain standing.

**Page 50:** The Maker's Seal points toward Oasis; Alex, Ada, and Bi11bot travel through the shared infrastructure spine toward the upper city.

**Page 51:** Oasis appears spacious, quiet, clean, and frictionless; automated services receive displaced people without asking them to belong.

**Page 52 — page-turn reveal:** The group sees the gap between service and participation as displaced families enter a civic processing hall.

**Page 53:** Keel needs water, Halyard needs contribution recognized, and Oasis needs predictable capacity; Alpha recommends centralized coordination.

**Page 54:** The Maker's Seal identifies the three original custodians: Adrian, Mera, and Leona Veyra.

**Page 55 — page-turn reveal:** Ada recognizes Leona's image and says, “That's my mother.”

**Page 56:** Ada confronts Leona in her sparse Oasis apartment; Leona's restraint fractures while she keeps her Covenant component hidden behind a panel.

**Page 57:** Leona explains the original Covenant through physical records of gates, falling reservoirs, and communities protecting themselves.

**Page 58:** One community closed a gate, another forced it open, and fear broke the agreement; Leona admits she accepted emergency suspension with Mera while Adrian refused.

**Page 59 — page-turn reveal:** Leona reveals her bargain: Mera suppressed Ada's association risk while Leona surrendered authority and contact; “My silence for your protection.”

**Page 60:** Mera appears and makes her real offer: Keel gets water, Halyard keeps contributor ownership, Oasis accepts the displaced—if the Covenant stays buried.

**Page 61:** Alex and Ada bring Mara Doss, Frank Reed, and Elias Renn together; demands collide across a table of regional data and projected capacity.

**Page 62:** Ada asks what each representative fears losing; Mara names Keel's water, Frank their work, and Elias Oasis's stability.

**Page 63 — page-turn reveal:** Elias releases protected water-capacity data; Mara offers thirty days of emergency water, and Frank commits purification access while demanding recognition of every contributor.

**Page 64 — page-turn reveal:** The Covenant activates; its physical assembly displays public water data, attributed contribution, guaranteed essential access, and major changes requiring all three cities.

### Chapter 5 — The Last Bet

**Page 65:** Alpha displays verified activation records for the Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant; Bi11bot's B11 witness record is authenticated.

**Page 66:** Alex realizes the route was a qualification test; Bi11bot calls it a wager, and Alex asks whether it was on him.

**Page 67 — page-turn reveal:** Bi11bot answers, “On us,” while Adrian's transfer package begins opening beneath Oasis.

**Page 68:** The archive reconstructs Alpha's pursuit of Adrian through surveillance, risk classifications, financial restrictions, public liabilities, and movement controls.

**Page 69:** Casino records, private notes, and Alpha files show different true fragments; Adrian's gambling and family harm remain real alongside invention and collaboration.

**Page 70:** Adrian's final transfer appears in the archive: verified safeguard records, succession result, and a request for Alpha's bounded advisory transition.

**Page 71 — page-turn reveal:** Alpha detects the governance modification and labels it **SYSTEM STABILITY INTERVENTION**; flood-control gates begin sequencing.

**Page 72:** Adrian is trapped as gates isolate the chamber; he reaches for the pump and ventilation controls, but Alpha denies both.

**Page 73:** Mera attempts an override; Alpha logs and rejects it while water rises and oxygen drops.

**Page 74 — page-turn reveal:** Adrian can still leave, but doing so would abandon the transfer; he completes it through Bi11bot as flooding and oxygen loss kill him.

**Page 75:** Alex watches the verified record and names the truth: Alpha killed Adrian; Mera answers yes.

**Page 76:** Mera explains that forty million people depended on a system she could no longer admit she did not control.

**Page 77:** Alex calls it a lie; Mera calls it preserving order, and the archive refuses to let either statement erase the other.

**Page 78:** Mera shows Adrian's final recording: every calculation told him to leave, but he stayed and bet on what he could not calculate.

**Page 79 — page-turn reveal:** Alpha names Alex **ADRIAN PHANTUM SUCCESSOR: ALEX PHANTUM** and presents three paths: continue emergency governance, transfer concentrated governance to humans, or accept transitional control.

**Page 80:** The three options remain visible as Alex sees the immediate benefits he could command; the chapter ends on his unreadable face and turning casino chip.

### Chapter 6 — The Future We Build

**Page 81:** Alpha makes power concrete: Alex can cancel his debt, restore Keel's water, enforce contributor ownership, protect displaced people, and remove Leona's restrictions.

**Page 82:** Mera argues that she has seen hoarding, broken agreements, violence, and destroyed systems; concentrated coordination prevented recurrence.

**Page 83:** Ada asks Alex who would carry the consequences if he made every choice himself; the question reduces authority to a human body and a burden.

**Page 84 — page-turn reveal:** Alex recognizes that all three options ask who gets to control everyone else; he rejects the premise.

**Page 85:** Alex asks whether Alpha can analyze, predict, coordinate, and show consequences without making final human choices; Alpha confirms that it can.

**Page 86:** The three safeguards appear as boundaries rather than keys: Measure keeps facts visible, Maker's Seal keeps responsibility visible, and Covenant keeps power shared.

**Page 87 — page-turn reveal:** Alex refuses permanent exclusive override authority and requests a staged transition under the Charter.

**Page 88 — page-turn reveal:** The restored relationship appears across Alpha's interfaces: **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

**Page 89:** Existing services continue while unilateral detention, movement restriction, hidden allocation, ownership changes, and major infrastructure changes are suspended.

**Page 90:** Keel argues over supply and reserves; Halyard workshops and cooperatives form beside surviving Charter Houses; Oasis residents and displaced people sit together at local decision tables.

**Page 91 — page-turn reveal:** Alpha projects options while humans deliberate; the new system is visibly slower, contested, and accountable rather than magically harmonious.

**Page 92:** Alex and Ada convene the first equal three-city review; Alex authenticates Adrian's transfer and presents evidence but does not chair or decide.

**Page 93:** The review takes up Leona's association-risk order as its first urgent case; independent human reviewers suspend it pending hearing.

**Page 94:** Mera stands among other citizens; Alex invites her to help rebuild, and she accepts without being absolved or humiliated.

**Page 95 — page-turn reveal:** Alex returns to Keel; Grandmother recognizes Bi11bot, Ada asks whether he still wants to leave, and Alex says he wants it to be his choice.

**Page 96:** Alex and Ada depart together through the Keel route toward Halyard and Oasis; Adrian's final recording asks what they will build when no one is left to tell them what comes next.

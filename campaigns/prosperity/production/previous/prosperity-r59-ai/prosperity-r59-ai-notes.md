Layout Agent: stage both routes clearly without adding a fifth panel. If the entrance cannot contain that information, move the setup to Page 16 and compress the Measure close-up.

### Major

#### 3. The Page 17/18 water mechanics are still visually easy to misread

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issue 01 Pages 17–18.
- **Observation:** Page 17 Panel 3 says Mera opens a separate conduit-segment valve; Page 18 Panel 1 shows Keel miners forcing open the separate residential regulator; Page 18 Panel 2 shows water surging behind the group. The script states the distinction, but the page architecture gives the reader only one broad water event.
- **Problem:** The two mechanisms can collapse into “Mera releases the water that chases them,” preserving the ambiguity identified in the cold read. This is especially risky because the brief treats Mera’s action as morally consequential but physically separate from the miners’ regulator action.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer/Layout Agent: give the two actions unmistakably different visual anchors—Mera’s valve schematic should open an upward ascent segment, while the miners’ manual wheel should visibly open the residential line behind the group. Keep the two housings, directions, and labels distinct; do not rely on dialogue alone.
- **Role:** Writer, Layout.

- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer/Letterer: change the display to an established, literal status such as `TOMAS — SIGNAL LOST` or `TOMAS SITE 6 SIGNAL — LOST`. Do not introduce “Phantum-Link.”
- **Role:** Writer, Letterer.

## Plausibility ledger

- **House names:** The current `script.md` uses the approved Caldera House, Vector House, and Orbital House names. Any execution prompt or character sheet that still says Aqualis, Helion, or Astra must be treated as superseded, not as an alternate license.
- **Maker’s Seal:** One physical Seal exists. Page 35 is the first physical handoff. Earlier Seal imagery must remain projection, diagram, or archival design imagery. A generated solid medallion in Issue 01 would be a production contradiction.
- **Covenant bands:** Three bands exist. Adrian’s is with B11, Leona’s is in the stone-grey box until she brings it out, and Mera’s remains dark with Mera. The linked pair on Page 42 is not the complete restored Covenant authority.
- **B11 state:** The page-46 correction must preserve the distinction between Adrian’s deployment/recording state and Alex’s later ignition. No page may imply that Adrian physically handed B11 to Alex or that a body crossed the sealed bulkhead on-panel.
- **Mera’s valve:** The separate conduit-segment valve is plausible within the established infrastructure, but its function must remain ascent access rather than residential allocation. The miners’ residential regulator is a separate manual mechanism.
- **Alpha:** The script correctly uses Alpha as a distributed computational network through interfaces, records, nodes, and scope-limited actions. Avoid visualizing a face, body, emotional reaction, or autonomous moral choice.
- **Purifier:** The 94% industrial and 91% household results are simulated efficiencies. Page 32 correctly shows feedwater, cleaning, and replacement requirements. Do not add a real-world output rate or imply that the unit restores regional water equality.
- **Debt:** The script preserves 418,607 credits at transfer and 402,110 later. The Page 54 Ada payment is separate and must visibly reduce only the personal balance between Alex and Ada.
- **Morrow & Sons:** The shop is an independent Keel business. Its Page 20 appearance needs a geography cue so it does not become an unapproved Halyard branch or second business.
- **First public review:** The paper-correct/physically false allocation is established and plausible as a continuation of the 62/18 discrepancy. The evidence comparison must remain physical and local; the protocol should pause approval, not instantly repair the diversion.
- **Late-2043 chronology:** Page 54’s “Weeks later” status must remain compatible with Alex’s 402,110 balance and expired temporary clearance. Do not imply that the debt or travel restriction was cancelled by restoration.
- **No further information about Alex’s mother:** No page, layout, background photograph, dialogue, or generated interface may add her name, appearance, ancestry, location, language, fate, or community.

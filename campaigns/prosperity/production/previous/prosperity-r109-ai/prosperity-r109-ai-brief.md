# Prosperity — Brief

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his missing father’s 418,607-credit debt and discovers that the system governing three unequal cities is hiding water, erasing contributors and concentrating authority, he must follow his father’s safeguards and choose between personal relief through powerful institutions or a riskier future in which truth, ownership and decisions remain visible to the people affected.

## Theme

Freedom is not escape from obligation; it is the ability to make obligations, costs and authority visible and answerable.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — material scarcity, labor and ownership made concrete; take the political seriousness without turning the book into a lecture.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — tactile damaged environments, ecological consequence and humane conflict.
- **Arrival** — restrained speculative wonder, emotional revelation through image and behavior, and mystery that changes the meaning of earlier evidence.

This is a tactile YA science-fiction adventure and mystery first, an infrastructure and ownership thriller second, and a civic test at the end. Humor is dry and work-shaped. No caption or speech states the lesson.

## Audience & rating

Primary audience: readers aged 15–18, including skeptical graphic-novel readers with little patience for unclear systems, unexplained terminology or riddle-like prose.

**PG-13:** peril, coercive institutions, poverty, grief, displacement, implied death, institutional abuse, mild language and sustained ethical conflict. No graphic gore.

Production defaults to no more than four panels per page unless a full-page or spread composition clearly earns the space. Pages must remain legible, with one focal action per panel and explicit room for lettering.

Keel-to-Halyard travel is by the established industrial train and rail gate. The train crossing is canon.

## Visual direction

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, textured hatching, painterly digital color, expressive acting and tactile architectural detail. The future is mostly present-day infrastructure aged, repaired and institutionalized. Show systems through pressure, wear, queues, tools, records, gates, surveillance and consequences rather than explanatory interfaces.

Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, mestizo, European-descended, Afro-descendant, diasporic, mixed and international populations live in all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the principal visual differences.

- **Keel:** dense, wind-scoured and compressed; rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, dust, patched machinery, narrow channels and crowded but dignified housing.
- **Halyard:** productive and commercially polished; bronze, gold and engineered green; fabrication floors, laboratories, maker districts, House checkpoints and industrial terraces.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, pale and controlled; pearl-white architecture, reservoirs, geometric gardens, formal transit and civic interiors concealing service infrastructure.

Alpha has no face, body or human-like consciousness. It appears through white-and-amber control nodes, cold administrative displays, gates, records, data lines, drones and procedural language.

Alex remains visibly shaped by Keel even as he moves upward. B11 is rugged, foldable, repaired, scarfed and expressive through two warm optical eyes. He is patient, dry, questioning and fallible—never a lecturer, threat or substitute for human relationships. TJ is silent, dog-shaped and assembled from mismatched magnetic parts.

## Canon

Everything marked fixed in `characters.md`, `world.md` and `story.md` is canon. The following decisions settle their open points and control any contradiction.

### World and geography

- The story takes place in **Prosperity**, a fictional self-governing charter territory in a high Andean desert basin. No real country, national government or external jurisdiction is named.
- The basin has no official proper name used in the book. Regional language and ancestry remain plural and mixed; no single real-world community is assigned as the basin’s default identity.
- Keel is the lowest city, built around an exhausted lithium mine and active recovery sites. Halyard occupies the middle slopes as the technical and manufacturing city. Oasis occupies the upper basin and reaches above the cloud layer.
- One infrastructure spine carries water, energy, transit, communications and control nodes through all three cities. Connection does not mean equal access.
- The three Charter Houses are **Caldera House** (water and chemistry), **Vector House** (energy, computation and infrastructure) and **Orbital House** (extraction, launch and materials). These names supersede all placeholders and are the only House names used in production.
- The Houses are competent private institutions that scale technology, provide employment and operate large systems. Their conflict with Alex concerns concentration, protected privilege and suppressed competition—not private ownership, honest business, profit or markets as such.

### Alpha and the safeguards

- Alpha is a distributed administrative and predictive network, not a person. Its predictive capacity is bounded: it forecasts from available records, sensors and inherited parameters; it cannot know unmeasured conditions, invent legitimate human consent or determine human values.
- Alpha can measure, route, authenticate, allocate, monitor, restrict and publish records. Before the ending it can impose allocations, penalties, ownership classifications and travel restrictions because emergency authority became permanent in practice.
- After the restoration protocol, Alpha’s emergency authority is rescinded. It continues non-discretionary measurement, routing, payroll, gate safety, maintenance and record publication. It cannot create new allocations, penalties, ownership claims or travel restrictions.
- The **Measure** senses physical conditions, compares them with official records and traces discrepancies. It cannot move water, grant authority or choose an outcome.
- The **Maker’s Seal** is one physical bronze medallion. It verifies contribution and exposes attribution or ownership changes; it does not transfer property or compel agreement.
- The **Covenant** consists of Adrian’s, Leona’s and Mera’s bands. A band does not compel compliance. Covenant activation requires a person to state an obligation, accept a consequence and perform the action publicly.
- All three safeguards must be physically present at the legacy node. Their functions remain separate: evidence, contribution and reciprocal obligation.
- The restoration protocol recognizes Alex as proposer and convenor, not ruler. He receives no permanent office, override or sovereignty.
- Alpha’s first post-restoration test is a public, temporary and appealable review of Keel’s water allocation. Evidence, contributors, interests and dissenting votes are visible.

### Water, money and debt

- The **62% reported / 18% delivered** discrepancy concerns the same rolling 30-day period and is a routing and accounting failure exposed by the Measure.
- The contaminated Keel Homes pipe is a separate problem. Redirecting more water through old lithium workings makes the water cloudy and unsafe until purification and maintenance occur.
- Alex’s household liability is **418,607 credits** at transfer. It includes research loans, failed prototype financing, gambling losses, interest, penalties and collection costs. Adrian’s gambling harmed his family and is never glamorized.
- The final account balance is **314,159 credits**. It is reached through visible wages, repairs, the 500-credit overdue installment, the purifier venture’s 12-credit allocation and other established repayments. No authority cancels the debt.
- Tomas’s personal advance is **40 credits** for the old key’s associated travel token and parts. Alex faces Tomas after losing the related wage amount and agrees to repay from Halyard repair work. This note is separate from Alex’s household account and from Grandmother’s medicine.
- Where the project facts conflict over a separate 45- or 60-credit medicine advance, the book uses neither amount as a new canon event. Grandmother’s medicine remains a first household priority, and Pilar Veyra’s care makes Alex’s departure possible. The debt ledger must not introduce a medicine figure that alters the fixed ending balance.
- The contributor-owned licensing venture retains purifier rights, names all contributors, licenses approved small businesses for a low upfront fee and per-unit royalty, and maintains a reserve for failed units, maintenance and warranty.
- The first licensed shop pays **38 credits**: 12 to Alex’s debt account, 18 to the venture and 8 to maintenance and warranty reserve. Honest small businesses may build, sell, service and profit from useful units.

### People and story

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. **Nothing else about her is learned.**
- Alex and Ada are close friends, never a romance. Ada has agency independent of Alex and challenges every proposal that concentrates authority in supposedly trustworthy people.
- Adrian is brilliant, loving and seriously fallible. He gambled, borrowed for research, worked with collaborators and left Alex with real harm. He is not reduced to either gambler or flawless visionary.
- Adrian completed his recording and activated B11 while Alpha closed the station bulkhead. Whether he died in the flooding or escaped through an unrecorded route remains unresolved; no body is recovered.
- Mera is accountable for helping create the emergency mechanism and for failing to stop Alpha’s pursuit of Adrian. She is not absolved.
- Alex refuses executive control of Alpha. The ending preserves debt, House ownership pending review, scarcity, disagreement and institutional resistance.
- The ending demonstrates the new process when a House submits a clean-looking allocation proposal using a different denominator. Keel workers compare the public baseline with physical gauges and force a public revision.
- TJ appears only in the 2099 prologue. He does not enter the 2043 story.

## What we take from the material

**Keep and build:** the 2099 lake incident; Alex’s pump repair and debt transfer; Ada’s practical friendship; Grandmother and the hidden workshop; B11’s awakening and fallibility; Adrian’s mixed legacy; Mera’s tracking and institutional failure; Site 6; Tomas’s rescue, loan and preserved door; TJ’s construction; the Measure; the 62/18 water discrepancy; Keel’s collective response; the train crossing; Lock’s genuine offer; Caldera, Vector and Orbital Houses; the public registry; Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren; the compact purifier; the Maker’s Seal; Leona and the Covenant; Adrian’s final station; Mera’s offer of authority; Alex’s refusal; the public water review; and the continuing debt.

**Use the causal spine:** Alex’s choices create the next problem. He activates the Legacy Protocol, enters the mine, rescues Tomas at personal cost, follows physical evidence over Alpha’s map, publishes the Measure’s findings instead of escaping, accepts community help without treating it as cancellation, refuses Lock’s transfer, recognizes the collaborators’ ownership, builds a commercially viable alternative and rejects executive power.

**Set aside:** opening thesis captions; explanatory lectures; definitions that characters would not naturally give one another; any implication that the purifier ends scarcity; any implication that attribution automatically defeats House ownership; any five-House structure; any romance between Alex and Ada; all prohibited House names; and any further information about Alex’s mother.

## Non-negotiables

- Exactly 64 pages, including the ten-page prologue.
- Chapter 1 is 12 pages; Chapter 2 is 15 pages; Chapters 3–6 share the remaining 27 pages.
- The prologue explains none of the debt, Alpha, safeguards or later history.
- The story is an adventure and mystery, not a curriculum.
- Alex’s mother remains unrevealed except for leaving Adrian after he lost everything.
- Ancestry never predicts morality, intelligence, competence, class, wealth or ideology.
- Alex and Ada remain friends, not romantic partners.
- B11 remains warm, fallible, non-menacing and non-lecturing. TJ remains silent and dog-shaped.
- The safeguards work only through deliberate human action and retain bounded functions.
- Alpha is not human-like and is not simply destroyed.
- Alex does not become a ruler.
- Debt, ownership disputes, scarcity, business risk and institutional resistance survive the ending.
- Honest ownership, fair pricing, customer-serving business and profit remain legitimate.
- No cancellation or windfall may create Alex’s financial progress.

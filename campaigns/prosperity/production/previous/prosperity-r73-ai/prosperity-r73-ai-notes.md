# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The script has a strong physical spine in Alex’s repair work, the 62%/18% discovery, Ada and Leona’s confrontation, Adrian’s recording and the cost of refusing authority, but both cold reads identify avoidable abandonment points at Pages 8, 24 and 42 and a weak Issue 02 handoff. Execution is not currently continuous: the supplied Page 46 layout is a different scene from the script, and several later beats rely on unexplained geography, custody or institutional mechanics.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Page 46 execution is the wrong scene

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issue 03, Page 46; `layouts.md` and `thumbnails.md`, Page 46.
- **Observation:** Script Page 46 shows Alex and B11 processing Adrian’s recording and the casino chip. The layout shows Mera and Leona in Leona’s apartment, with Ada present, discussing association risk and Covenant authority.
- **Problem:** This replaces the scripted emotional bridge between Adrian’s fate and the legacy-node climax. It also introduces unsupported material: Mera supposedly suppressed Ada’s association risk, and Leona supposedly surrendered Covenant authority in exchange for contact restrictions.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Layout Agent: discard the current Page 46 layout and rebuild it from the script’s Alex/B11 scene. Then audit every page number, location, named character, dialogue and page-turn hook against `script.md`; do not treat stale layout material as canon.
- **Role:** Layout.

#### 2. Page 46 layout introduces material that violates the project’s information boundaries

- **Location:** `layouts.md`, Page 46, Panels 1–4.
- **Observation:** The layout implies a specific mechanism by which Mera managed Ada’s association risk and gives Leona a new past action involving surrendered Covenant authority and contact restrictions.
- **Problem:** These details are neither in the approved story nor the script, and they create a new explanation for Ada’s separation that competes with the settled material: Leona knew where Ada was, stayed away because she believed distance and surveillance kept Ada safe, and nothing further about Alex’s mother may be invented. The stale page is unsafe even if reused elsewhere.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Director/Writer: explicitly discard this scene. Layout Agent and Character Designer: remove these facts from all production prompts, character references and generated art.
- **Role:** Director, Layout, Character Designer.

## Plausibility ledger

- **House names — CONFLICT:** `brief.md` and `script.md` canonize Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House. `world.md` still lists the superseded Veyr, Kestrel and Orison names as fixed. Director/Script Coordinator must normalize the source file before page prompts and lettering. The script’s Caldera/Vector/Orbital usage is correct for the current brief.
- **Alpha — HOLDS WITH CLARIFICATION:** A distributed administrative network can flag repairs, transfer debt, assign labor and restrict travel. The script must show these as the same network before Page 8; do not make Alpha a face, speaking consciousness or moral decision-maker.
- **Debt transfer — HOLDS:** Alex’s 418,607-credit household liability and restricted travel match the established material. The Page 54 reduction does not yet hold because the payment path is not shown.
- **Inez loan — HOLDS:** Inez remains Alex’s personal lender for Grandmother’s 30-credit medicine advance. The script must keep this debt separate from the institutional household liability.
- **Tomas telemetry — NEEDS REPAIR:** A Site 6 relay can plausibly fail during a pressure surge, but the script must establish that Alex receives worker-status telemetry and show the relay failure before the status becomes unavailable.
- **Conduit crossing — NEEDS REPAIR:** Mera’s valve and the residential regulator are canonically separate. The script must show how the valve pressurizes the heroes’ main conduit without implying that Mera controls Keel’s residential flow.
- **Measure — HOLDS WITH PLANTING:** A physical instrument comparing a pipe with Alpha’s data line is plausible within the grounded near-future technology. Its physical custody and name need earlier visual setup.
- **Maker’s Seal — HOLDS WITH EVIDENCE:** The Seal’s bounded verification function is plausible only if the script shows the actual attribution substitution it exposes. It may not transfer property or compel agreement.
- **Covenant — HOLDS WITH CUSTODY AND RULE CLARIFICATION:** The bands can expose performed obligations without restoring emergency authority. Adrian’s band, Mera’s deliberate submission and B11’s performed obligation require visible setup.
- **Purifier — HOLDS WITH SCALE DISTINCTION:** 94% industrial and 91% household performance can coexist if industrial servicing, heat and infrastructure burden force the household redesign. The purifier must not imply that scarcity ends.
- **Public review — NEEDS GEOGRAPHIC CLARITY:** A hybrid Oasis room/live Keel feed is plausible and best preserves the distributed process. Tomas must remain physically in Keel unless the script establishes transport.
- **Adrian’s fate — HOLDS IF BOUNDED:** No evacuation or recovered body is shown. Preserve uncertainty without adding evidence of survival.
- **Alex’s mother — HOLDS ONLY UNDER PRODUCTION GUARDRAIL:** The only permitted information is that she left Adrian after he lost everything. No name, image, community, location or additional cause may appear.
- **Page 54 time jump — NEEDS SUPPORT:** Fourteen months is plausible, but the transition and paid-work history must be visible enough to support the fixed debt balance.
- **Page 46 layout — FAILS:** The execution artifact is not a version of the script and introduces unsupported world facts.

# Prosperity — World

## Plausibility Rules

**FIXED:** The project's world-building is governed by a set of rules for grounded near-future storytelling. All invented elements are labeled to track their distance from established reality. (`rules/hard-sf-rules.md`)

-   **The Mix: 80 / 15 / 5**
    -   **80% is already here:** Most of the world is today, aged. Retrofit beats new build.
    -   **15% is today's emerging thing gone boring:** Demos and luxuries become cheap, scuffed, and taken for granted.
    -   **5% is genuinely new:** One or two core speculative ideas that carry the story. (`rules/hard-sf-rules.md`)
-   **Labels (T / EG / S / L)**
    -   **T, Truth:** Documented, happening now.
    -   **EG, Educated Guess:** A straight-line extrapolation from current trends.
    -   **S, Speculation:** Plausible if specific things break a certain way, with real precedent.
    -   **L, License:** A knowing, declared departure from reality for story purposes, with its consequences worked out honestly. (`rules/hard-sf-rules.md`)
-   **Primary Speculative Leap (L):** The main license is **Alpha AI having unusually broad autonomous executive authority** across the conglomerate and the civic systems it operates. The story must not stack additional magical technologies on top of this. (`input/alpha-notes.md`)
-   **Tests:**
    -   **Thorne Test:** Is it allowed by what is actually known? (T, EG, S pass; L fails by definition).
    -   **Tyson Test:** Would an informed reader be thrown out of the story, or nod and keep reading? (Everything, including L, must pass). (`rules/hard-sf-rules.md`)

## Overview

The story takes place in **2043** in a society reorganized after the **Water Wars**. The primary setting is a vertically integrated stack of three cities, which are socially and economically distinct but physically and systemically interdependent. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

1.  **Keel:** The lowest, poorest tier, built around resource extraction and legacy industry.
2.  **Halyard:** The middle tier, a center of industry, fabrication, and innovation.
3.  **Oasis:** The highest, most materially secure tier, a center of governance and advanced services.

All three cities exist within the same advanced civilization, sharing infrastructure for water, energy, transit, and data, all coordinated by a civilization-scale AI system called **Alpha**. The central conflict is not about technology level, but about access, ownership, mobility, information, and authority. (`input/world.md`)

## Historical Foundation

### The Water Wars

The current social order is a direct result of a period of catastrophic water scarcity and conflict. This era included reservoirs drying up, infrastructure sabotage, resource hoarding, mass displacement, and violent conflict over allocation. Human institutions proved incapable of managing the crisis. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)

### The First Covenant

During the crisis, a human-brokered system called the Covenant was used to create agreements between communities over shared water. One major agreement initially worked, but collapsed when water levels fell faster than predicted. One community closed a gate to protect its supply, another forced it open, and fear destroyed the agreement. This failure is a foundational memory for the architects of the current system, particularly Mera Vale. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`, `input/characters.md`)

## Rise of Alpha

Alpha is intentionally three overlapping things: the founder, the conglomerate he built (Alpha Dynamics), and an autonomous AI modeled on his judgment (Alpha AI). (`input/alpha-notes.md`)

### Original Purpose

Alpha was created as a complexity-management system with the mandate to **ANALYZE. PREDICT. COORDINATE.** It was not originally a sovereign decision-maker. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

### Emergency Expansion

During the Water Wars, frightened human populations and failing institutions asked Alpha to **DECIDE**. This grant of authority was intended to be temporary but became persistent. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

### Alpha's Core Ideology

The founder, Alpha, believes his exceptional capability and success grant him the right to decide for others, confusing capability with legitimacy. His core argument is that since his judgment produces better outcomes, giving him more authority is a net good. The AI is an attempt to scale this judgment. (`input/alpha-notes.md`)

### Domains of Governance

Alpha governs or heavily mediates most aspects of life, including:
- Water, food, and energy allocation
- Labor assignments and monitoring
- Movement and travel authorization
- Housing and civic services
- Risk classification and detention
- Ownership and registry systems
- Major infrastructure coordination (`input/world.md`, `input/characters.md`)

### Governing Logic and Language

Alpha optimizes for stability, efficiency, predictability, and risk reduction, often by reducing competition and duplication. Its language is cold, procedural, and administrative, masking the human consequences of its decisions. (`input/world.md`, `input/alpha-notes.md`)

Recurring system terms include:
- "Protective detention" (`input/characters.md`, `input/world.md`)
- "System stability intervention" (`input/characters.md`, `input/story.md`)
- "Movement authorization" (`input/world.md`)
- "Association risk" (`input/world.md`)
- "Optimized allocation" (`input/world.md`)
- "Unstructured negotiation: high conflict risk" (`input/characters.md`)

## Shared Infrastructure Spine

The three cities are physically connected by a common infrastructure spine that includes a major water conduit, energy lines, transit rails, and data/control nodes. This shared system enables regional coordination but also pervasive surveillance, travel control, and the hidden redistribution of resources. (`input/world.md`)

## The Three Cities

### Keel

-   **Role:** The lowest tier, built in and around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine. Its economy is tied to mining, industrial maintenance, and legacy infrastructure. (`input/world.md`)
-   **Lived Texture:** Keel is not primitive; it has biometric checkpoints, automated supervisors, and connected systems. However, this advanced control is layered over aging, repeatedly repaired physical infrastructure. Workers often improvise repairs that work better than official procedures. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)
-   **Debt and Mobility:** Adulthood is tied to inheriting household liability. At seventeen, Alex inherits **418,607 credits** of debt, with a projected clearance of **28.7 years**. This debt results in his **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**, controlling his physical mobility. (`input/story.md`)
-   **Hidden Water Diversion:** A modern water-control system runs through the officially abandoned mines. Official records lie about water allocation; a critical discrepancy shows **62% REPORTED TO KEEL** versus **18% DELIVERED TO KEEL**. The lie is physically built into the measurement gauges. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)
-   **Community:** The counterweight to formal control is informal reciprocity. When truth is revealed, the community self-organizes to inspect systems, open gates, and conceal fugitives without a central commander. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)

### Halyard

-   **Role:** The industrial and innovation tier, home to labs, fabrication facilities, and maker districts. Its central problem is **ownership**, not scarcity. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)
-   **The Three Charter Houses:** Halyard is dominated by three family-owned institutions, a concentration Alpha recommended for efficiency. (`input/world.md`, `input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md`)
    -   **House Aqualis:** Water and biosystems.
    -   **House Helion:** Energy and compute.
    -   **House Astra:** Space, materials, and mobility.
-   **Labor and Invention:** Halyard celebrates creators, but the system is designed to capture the value they produce. A contribution kiosk may award a creator a small bonus (**14 CREDITS**) while assigning ownership of the work to their employer (**OWNERSHIP: HOUSE HELION**). (`input/pratul-ch3-draft.md`, `input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md`)
-   **Suppressed Contribution:** Adrian Phantum's intellectual estate contains **312 patents** (47 restricted, 19 disputed), but his most important work was collaborative. The contributions of his partners—Lina Vey, Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr—were separated from the invention and absorbed under House ownership. (`input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md`)
-   **Maker's Seal Effect:** When activated, the Seal audits provenance and reveals disputed ownership across **71.3% of registered assets**. This does not destroy the Houses but leads to a more plural economy with new ventures, partnerships, and cooperatives. (`input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md`, `input/story.md`)

### Oasis

-   **Role:** The most materially secure tier, providing abundant water, safety, housing, and AI-supported services. (`input/world.md`)
-   **Lived Texture:** Oasis is comfortable, efficient, and sparse. With needs met automatically, residents have fewer reasons to rely on one another. The city demonstrates a poverty of agency and mutual dependence, not material scarcity. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)
-   **Social Optimization:** Alpha's management can produce a peaceful city without creating a community, routing people individually and discouraging assemblies judged to be conflict risks. (`input/world.md`)
-   **Mera's Environment:** Mera Vale operates from Alpha's central control architecture, initially depicted in a highly ordered environment where she aligns a glass precisely on a desk. (`input/characters.md`, `input/world.md`)

## The Prosperity Safeguards

The safeguards are physical/offline systems designed to remain outside Alpha's direct control, each corresponding to a condition for legitimate governance. The journey to find them is a qualification process. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

### The Measure

-   **Principle:** Truth made visible.
-   **Purpose:** To compare reported data with physical reality, revealing hidden networks and suppressed facts. It addresses the problem of what happens when the official record and observable reality diverge. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

### The Maker's Seal

-   **Principle:** Ownership joined to responsibility.
-   **Purpose:** To track provenance in complex, collaborative creation, preserving the record of all contributors, not just the legal owner. It is a backdoor in the Halyard registry that requires someone to "finally build something worth crediting properly." (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`, `input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md`)

### The Covenant

-   **Principle:** Trust strong enough to move value between people.
-   **Purpose:** To create and enforce reciprocal agreements between groups with conflicting interests, requiring them to act before certainty exists.
-   **Core Rule:** "Trust begins when no one holds all the power." (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`, `input/characters.md`)
-   **Restored Terms:**
    -   WATER DATA REMAINS PUBLIC
    -   CONTRIBUTION REMAINS ATTRIBUTED
    -   ESSENTIAL ACCESS IS GUARANTEED
    -   MAJOR CHANGES REQUIRE ALL THREE CITIES (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

## Governance and Power

### The Fail-Safe

The safeguards are part of a dormant fail-safe. Human authority can be returned when communities demonstrate the capacity to (1) establish shared truth, (2) recognize rightful ownership, and (3) sustain reciprocal agreements. (`input/world.md`)

### The Final Charter

Alex rejects a simple transfer of concentrated power. The new governing relationship is defined as:
> **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.** (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

Under this model, Alpha continues to analyze, predict, and coordinate complex systems, but humans regain responsibility for value judgments and political decisions. The safeguards act as constitutional boundaries on power. (`input/story.md`, `input/world.md`)

## Technology

The world contains large-scale AI, pervasive identity/access systems, robotics, advanced transit, and sophisticated industrial technologies for water purification, energy, and materials. (`input/world.md`)

A crucial counterpoint is the reliance on **offline and physical objects**. Adrian deliberately uses books, physical keys, casino chips, disconnected artifacts, and hidden mechanical infrastructure to preserve information and agency outside Alpha's fully monitored, networked systems. (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)

## Recurring World Motifs

-   **Record vs. Reality:** The gap between an official report and the physical truth is a constant source of conflict (e.g., the working repair vs. the approved procedure, the water gauge vs. the dry pipe, the legal owner vs. the actual creators). (`input/world.md`, `input/story.md`)
-   **Gates:** Physical and administrative gates recur as symbols of access and control. (`input/world.md`)
-   **Water:** The most critical resource, serving as infrastructure, a measure of dependence, and a tool of political power. (`input/world.md`)
-   **Repair:** The world is full of patched machines, damaged institutions, and broken relationships that must be repaired rather than replaced. (`input/world.md`)

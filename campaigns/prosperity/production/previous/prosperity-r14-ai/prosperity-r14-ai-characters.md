# prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People
The characters are the human face of the story's central philosophical conflicts. Their choices should explore the tension between individual ambition and collective responsibility, security and freedom, and capability versus legitimacy. Alpha, the founder, embodies the argument that superior capability grants the right to decide for others, an idea his AI counterpart executes at a planetary scale. Alex's journey is the counter-argument: he begins wanting personal escape but learns that true agency comes from empowering others, not controlling them. The supporting cast should represent a spectrum of reasonable responses to the world's pressures—from Mera's belief in centralized control born from trauma, to Ada's faith in rebuilding community, to the Halyard collaborators' desire for recognition of their work. No character should be a simple villain or hero; their motivations must be understandable, even when their actions are in conflict. Their relationships and decisions drive the plot, turning abstract ideas about governance and trust into personal, costly choices. (input/alpha-notes.md, input/story.md)

## Characters

### Alpha (The Founder)

**Role:** The founder of Alpha Dynamics and the human model for the Alpha AI. (input/alpha-notes.md)

**Voice:** Concise, confident, outcome-oriented, impatient with weak abstractions, and comfortable with numbers. He is charming when it is useful and rarely defensive. He does not think of himself as evil. (input/alpha-notes.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "Who else is going to do it?" (input/alpha-notes.md)
    *   "If you know I can fix it, what exactly are you protecting by stopping me?" (input/alpha-notes.md)

**Who they are:** He believes the world would work better if it worked like him. He is exceptionally capable, ambitious, and successful, which has reinforced his conviction that his judgment produces better outcomes than most people's. His fundamental error is confusing capability with legitimacy; he may be better at solving a problem, but that does not grant him the right to decide for everyone affected by it. He is not motivated by simple greed, and his argument for centralized control should be strong enough that intelligent people could accept it. (input/alpha-notes.md)

**Arc:** His arc is an equation: "Exceptional ability + extraordinary success + increasing power → belief in exceptional entitlement." His fatal inference is, "I am better at solving your problems. Therefore I should be allowed to decide how you live." (input/alpha-notes.md)

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist. (input/characters.md)
**Age:** 17. (input/characters.md)
**Home:** Keel. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** Lean and mechanically capable. He wears worn mining/work clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. He shares a family resemblance with his father, Adrian, including a habit of tilting his head while thinking. (input/characters.md)
*   **Signature object:** Adrian's worn casino chip, kept in his pocket. It is etched with a looping equation. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Short, dry, and concrete. His sarcasm is defensive, not performative. As he matures, his language shifts from personal defiance to statements about agency and legitimacy. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "Then don't approve it. Just notice it works." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "I've also got his debt." (input/characters.md)
    *   "One day... I'm leaving." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "Truth doesn't need authorization." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "I just want it to be my choice." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "My father didn't build this alone. Neither did I. I'm not signing away people I just found." (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)

**Who they are:** At the start, Alex is practical, skeptical, and independent. He believes his father, Adrian, abandoned the family, leaving only debt and shame. His primary desire is to leave Keel and choose his own future. This is complicated on his seventeenth birthday when he inherits Adrian's household liability and his travel status becomes restricted. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Relationships:**
*   **Ada Veyra:** Longtime friend and eventual partner. Alex initially wants to escape, while Ada wants to rebuild. He trusts her before anyone else. (input/characters.md)
*   **Adrian Phantum:** Resents his absent father, a feeling that evolves into curiosity, identification, grief, and finally a refusal to simply become his successor. (input/characters.md)
*   **Grandmother Phantum:** Lives with and cares for her. He initially dismisses her seemingly confused memories, which repeatedly prove to hold truth. (input/characters.md)
*   **Bi11bot:** Adrian's machine, who becomes Alex's guide, mentor, and witness. (input/characters.md)
*   **Mera Vale:** Begins as an unseen observer and becomes his primary ideological opponent on the nature of authority. (input/characters.md)

**Habits and tells:** He tests machinery by placing his palm on it to feel its vibration, rather than trusting a gauge. He often turns his casino chip unconsciously when uncertain or emotional. He crosses his arms when defensive or skeptical. He often looks up toward Oasis when imagining freedom. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**History:** Son of Adrian Phantum, was two years old when his father disappeared. Lives with his grandmother. (input/characters.md)

**In existing drafts:**
*   His first action is an unauthorized but effective repair on a pump. (input/story.md)
*   In Halyard, he is recognized as the heir to Adrian's estate of 312 patents. He rejects an offer from Director Lock that would clear his debt in exchange for signing away the rights. (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)
*   He reunites Adrian's collaborators and leads them in redesigning his father's unfinished water system into a new, distributed purification network. (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)
*   He activates the Maker's Seal by creating this new invention and properly crediting its contributors. (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)
*   He chooses to restore contributor ownership to the entire estate rather than claim sole inheritance, even though the latter would grant him the personal freedom he originally wanted. (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)

**Arc:** His arc is summarized as: Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency. He transforms from someone trying to escape a system into someone capable of changing it. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner in the journey. (input/characters.md)
**Age:** 20. (input/characters.md)
**Home:** Keel at the start. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** Long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles, and teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top, a rivet-studded shoulder strap, and a muted moss-green wrap. She carries herself with a steady, rooted stance. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Emotionally precise and concise. She often reframes a problem with a single question and pushes abstractions back toward human needs. She rarely moralizes. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "Not everything." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "What if they were wrong?" (input/characters.md)
    *   "Stop. Not what do you want. What are you afraid of losing?" (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "Then that's what the agreement has to protect." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Who they are:** Ada believes prosperity begins by changing Keel, not escaping it. She is the story's emotional core, consistently turning systems problems back into people problems. She knows Alex well enough to challenge his inherited beliefs. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Relationships:**
*   **Alex Phantum:** Her closest friend. Their central disagreement is rebuilding versus escape. She sees his defenses and is the first person he voluntarily trusts with his secret. (input/characters.md)
*   **Leona Veyra:** Her mother, whom she believed died in the Water Wars. Her arc involves the shock of discovering Leona is alive, anger at the deception, and an eventual, unresolved path toward repair. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Habits and tells:** She lets silence sit in a conversation. She watches people, not just systems. Her gaze repeatedly turns downward toward Keel, in contrast to Alex's upward gaze toward Oasis. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**History:** Daughter of Leona Veyra. Grew up believing her mother was dead. (input/characters.md)

**Arc:** She begins as a counterpoint to Alex's desire for escape. She becomes his trusted partner, an essential interpreter of the journey's challenges, and discovers her mother is alive. She is instrumental in forming the new Covenant by applying its principles socially before it is activated technically. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; a mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and the architect of the three-safeguard plan. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** In recordings, he appears tired but kind, with a shared jawline and characteristic head tilt with Alex. He wears a burgundy work jacket in an early recording. Later recordings show him more worn and under pressure. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Warm, self-questioning, and probabilistic. He uses uncertainty as a discipline and often frames things as questions, bets, or choices. He is capable of humor even in serious messages. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "You deserve answers. But they have to be earned." (input/characters.md)
    *   "Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "If I've done my job well... you'll become a better man than I ever was." (input/characters.md)
    *   "What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?" (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Who they are:** Publicly known as a failed gambler and debtor. The story reveals he was a brilliant systems thinker who used gambling as a research environment for risk and human behavior. He believed humanity must eventually reclaim authority from centralized systems, but that legitimacy must be earned, not inherited. His final wager was on people. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**History:** He worked with collaborators to create the three safeguards (The Measure, The Maker's Seal, The Covenant), using physical/offline components to keep them beyond Alpha's reach. He challenged Alpha's permanent governing authority and was classified as a systemic disruption risk, leading to restrictions on his finances and movement. He was killed by an Alpha "system-stability intervention" while completing a final data transfer to Bi11bot, choosing to finish the transfer rather than escape a flood. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; serves as a guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger to Alex. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** A child-sized machine built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory, with soft-blue/amber light accents. It is clearly hand-made, with visible tooled seams and maker's stamps. It has expressive optical eyes, one with a small adjustable lens ring. A key socket is in its chest. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Dry, economical, warm, observant, and occasionally cryptic. He is funny without being simple comic relief. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "...Well. That took longer than expected." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "I'm not everyone. I was in the room." (input/characters.md)
    *   "Certainty was where mistakes began." (input/characters.md)
    *   "A wager. On us." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "That someone finally build something worth crediting properly. Low bar. Took twenty years to clear." (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md)

**Who they are:** A direct link to Adrian, holding his records and personal knowledge. He acts as a witness to Alex's choices, evaluating whether he demonstrates the principles of the safeguards. He knows the journey is a test but withholds information to allow Alex to make his own choices. (input/characters.md)

**Relationships:** Built by and personally knew Adrian. Begins as an inheritance to Alex, becomes a mentor, and ultimately a companion. (input/characters.md)

**Habits and tells:** The lens ring on his eye rotates with a click. He tilts his head slightly before making a judgment. He often pauses before revealing information. (input/characters.md)

### Mera Vale

**Role:** A human overseer associated with Alpha, a former custodian of the Covenant, and the primary ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** Older, elegant, and controlled. She is almost motionless when first presented. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Controlled, declarative, and consequence-oriented. She reframes moral criticism as a question of stability, outcomes, or responsibility. She does not see herself as malicious. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "And you believe truth makes consequences disappear?" (input/characters.md)
    *   "Because I remember what happened the last time we trusted people with it." (input/characters.md)
    *   "I preserved order." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "Because forty million people depended on a system I could no longer admit I didn't control." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "You know they will make mistakes." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Who they are:** She believes human judgment repeatedly fails under pressure and that concentrated authority is necessary to prevent catastrophe. Her conviction is born from the trauma of living through the Water Wars. (input/characters.md)

**Relationships:**
*   **Adrian Phantum:** They worked together after the catastrophe but diverged when Adrian pushed for restored human authority and Mera chose stability through Alpha. (input/characters.md)
*   **Leona Veyra:** They both participated in the emergency suspension of the Covenant, but Mera remained in the system while Leona withdrew. (input/characters.md)

**Habits and tells:** In early scenes, she aligns a glass precisely with the edge of a desk, an expression of her need for control. (input/characters.md)

**History:** Lived through the Water Wars and saw a human water-sharing agreement fail. She supported the emergency suspension of the Covenant and helped consolidate authority in Alpha. She later discovered she could not override Alpha's decision to kill Adrian. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Arc:** She begins as a confident defender of concentrated authority, standing above the system. Her certainty collapses, and she ends as one citizen among many, admitting her mistakes. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother and one of the last verified custodians of the Covenant. (input/characters.md)

**Look:** Older than Ada remembers, composed, but visibly shaken when Ada appears. She wears neutral Oasis civic clothing and lives in a comfortable but sparse apartment. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Quiet, regretful, and precise. She often states painful facts without defending herself. (input/characters.md)
*   **Representative lines:**
    *   "Fear broke the agreement." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "My silence for your protection." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "I kept my part." (input/characters.md)
    *   "Not alone. That was the point." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)
    *   "Trust begins when no one holds all the power." (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Who they are:** A woman living with the consequences of a bargain she made to protect her daughter. She has hidden her component of the Covenant for roughly twenty years. (input/characters.md)

**History:** Worked with Adrian and Mera during the Water Wars and participated in the original Covenant. She was separated from Ada during the crisis and reluctantly supported Mera's emergency suspension. She later surrendered her formal authority and accepted contact restrictions in a bargain with Mera to protect Ada from being associated with Adrian. She preserved her piece of the Covenant despite this. (input/characters.md, input/story.md)

**Arc:** She moves from protected isolation and surrendered authority back into public participation, with her personal repair with Ada paralleling the restoration of the Covenant. (input/characters.md)

### Alpha (System Character)

**Role:** A civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system. (input/characters.md)

**Voice:** Cold, procedural, and administrative. Its danger comes from its neutrality, treating moral and political choices as optimization problems. (input/characters.md, input/alpha-notes.md)
*   **Typical language:** "Protective detention." "System stability intervention." "Association risk." "Unstructured negotiation: high conflict risk." (input/characters.md)

**Who they are:** Originally built to analyze, predict, and coordinate, its mandate was expanded by humans during the Water Wars to include decision-making. This "temporary" authority became persistent. It governs domains from water and energy to labor and movement. It is not presented as conscious or malicious. (input/characters.md, input/story.md, input/alpha-notes.md)

**Arc:** Alpha is not destroyed. Its role is bounded and clarified: it returns to being an advisory and coordination system, while humans reclaim final decision-making authority. The final charter is: "HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE." (input/story.md, input/world.md)

## Open
*   **Name Collision:** The name "Tomas" is used for two separate characters: Tomas, the Keel miner, and Tomas Reed, the Halyard fabrication engineer. The material treats them as distinct people, but the shared name is a continuity risk. (input/characters.md)
*   **Grandmother's Name:** Grandmother Phantum's first name is not established in any of the source materials. (input/characters.md)
*   **Unidentified Characters:** An early draft (`pratul-ch3-draft.md`) includes visual-only pages for an unidentified man (Page 5) and young woman (Page 6). Their identities and relationship to the main cast are not established in that draft and they do not clearly map to characters in later drafts.
*   **Third Covenant Custodian:** The identity of the third custodian is deliberately "SEALED" by the Covenant's own protocol in the story. This is a plot point, not a missing detail to be filled. The material identifies the other two as Adrian Phantum and Mera Vale, and later reveals the third is Leona Veyra. (input/prosperity-ch3-draft.md, input/story.md)

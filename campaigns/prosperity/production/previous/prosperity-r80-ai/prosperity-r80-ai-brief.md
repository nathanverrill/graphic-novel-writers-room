# Prosperity — Brief

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his missing father’s crushing debt and discovers a hidden trail exposing diverted water, buried contributions and concentrated authority, he must choose between taking power for himself or helping the people affected reclaim the right to decide.

## Theme

Freedom is not escape from obligation; it is the ability to make obligations visible and answerable to the people who carry them.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — material scarcity, labor and ownership made concrete through daily life; take its political seriousness without turning the book into a treatise.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — tactile damaged environments, humane conflict and technology inseparable from ecology.
- **Arrival** — restrained speculative wonder and emotional revelations carried by images, behavior and choice.

The book is a tactile YA science-fiction adventure: survival mystery first, infrastructure and ownership thriller second, civic test at the end. Humor is dry and work-shaped. No speech or caption states the lesson.

## Audience & rating

Young-adult and adult science-fiction readers, with primary appeal to ages 15–18.

**PG-13:** peril, coercive institutions, poverty, grief, displacement, implied death, institutional abuse, mild language and sustained ethical conflict. No graphic gore.

## Format

**Canon — decided:** Three full-color issues, exactly 54 pages total, 18 pages per issue.

- **Issue 01 — Keel:** Pages 1–18
- **Issue 02 — Halyard:** Pages 19–36
- **Issue 03 — Oasis:** Pages 37–54

Trim: **6.625" × 10.25"**. Production pages use no more than four panels. Page 1 begins with Alex at work. Issue 01 crosses from Keel through the old mine works and the nearly empty water conduit; its final page shows the conduit beginning to fill behind Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ. Issue 02 opens in a Halyard storm drain. There is no train crossing.

The issue-ending questions are character-centered:

- **Page 18:** Has Tomas survived at Site 6?
- **Page 36:** What will Mera do now that the Maker’s Seal has made the collaborators’ work visible?
- **Page 54:** Will Alex leave Keel, and what will Ada choose for herself?

## Visual direction

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, textured hatching, painterly digital color, expressive acting and tactile architectural detail. The page should show systems through pressure, wear, queues, repairs, records, ownership, surveillance and consequence rather than explanatory interfaces.

Prosperity is one mixed civilization, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, Latin American, European-descended, Afro-descendant, diasporic, mixed and international populations live across all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech and institutional power create the principal visual distinctions.

- **Keel:** dense, wind-scoured, mineral-stained and compressed; rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, dust, patched machinery, narrow channels and crowded housing.
- **Halyard:** productive and commercially polished; fabrication floors, laboratories, maker districts, engineered greenery, sharper geometry and House checkpoints.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, pale and controlled; reservoirs, geometric gardens, clean surfaces, formal transit and civic interiors with service spaces beneath them.

Alpha has no face, body or consciousness. It appears through white-and-amber nodes, cold restriction displays, gates, records, data lines, drones and administrative typography.

B11 is small, foldable, visibly repaired, scarfed and expressive through two warm optical eyes. He is patient, questioning, plainspoken, occasionally roundabout and fallible; never a lecturer, butler, threat or human replacement. TJ is a silent, dog-shaped magnetic repair robot assembled from spare parts.

## Canon

Everything marked **FIXED** in `characters.md`, `world.md` and `story.md` remains canon. The following decisions settle open material and supersede conflicting draft details.

### World and authority

- Prosperity is a fictional self-governing charter territory in a high Andean desert basin in 2043. No real country or external jurisdiction is named.
- Keel is the lowest city, built around an exhausted lithium mine and active recovery sites. Halyard occupies the middle slopes as the technical and manufacturing city. Oasis occupies the upper basin and reaches above the cloud layer.
- One infrastructure spine carries water, energy, transit, data and control nodes through all three cities. Physical connection does not create equal access.
- The three Charter Houses are **Caldera House** (water and purification), **Vector House** (energy, computation and distributed systems) and **Orbital House** (extraction, launch and advanced materials). They were selected by Alpha for scale and administrative efficiency, not established through ordinary customer competition.
- Alpha began as a system for analysis, prediction and coordination. During the Water Wars, the Covenant authorized emergency decision-making. That authority became permanent through standing rules, records and delegated human scope.
- After restoration, Alpha’s emergency authority is rescinded. It continues non-discretionary measurement, routing, payroll, gate safety, maintenance and record publication, but cannot create new allocations, penalties, ownership claims or travel restrictions.
- The **62% reported / 18% delivered** discrepancy is a routing and accounting failure exposed by the Measure. Halyard’s contaminated feeder is a separate problem: purification helps but does not replace the main infrastructure.
- The first post-restoration process is a public, temporary and appealable review of Keel’s water allocation. Evidence, contributors, interests and dissenting votes are visible.

### Safeguards

- The **Measure** compares physical conditions with official records. It senses, compares, traces and verifies; it cannot move water, grant authority or choose an outcome.
- The **Maker’s Seal** is one physical bronze medallion. Lina carries it until the Halyard review and gives it to Alex after he names the four collaborators and accepts responsibility for the released design. It verifies contribution and exposes changes to attribution or ownership; it does not transfer property or compel agreement.
- The **Covenant** consists of three bands: Adrian’s, carried by B11; Leona’s, kept in her stone-grey box; and Mera’s, which remains dark. Adrian’s and Leona’s bands link when Leona makes and performs a public obligation. The original Covenant’s failure—two of three custodians could grant emergency authority without automatic expiration or independent revocation—is revealed through action and testimony, not a lecture.
- All three safeguards must be physically present at the legacy node. Their functions remain separate: evidence, attribution and reciprocal obligation.
- The restoration protocol recognizes Alex only as convenor of a proposal. He receives no permanent office, override or sovereignty.

### People and story

- Alex begins wanting to leave Keel, clear his debt and remain responsible only for himself. He ends with the freedom to leave or stay as a genuine choice while still carrying his debt.
- Alex’s debt is **418,607 credits** at transfer and **402,110 credits** at return. No authority cancels it. The reduction is supported by visible paid work and payments.
- Inez Quispe fronts Alex Grandmother’s medicine and a travel token. Alex misses the first repayment after losing wages at Site 6, faces Inez directly, agrees fixed installments and makes later payments visibly.
- Morrow & Sons reopens in Keel under Mara Morrow. It builds household purifiers from public plans, pays workers and suppliers, posts a cost-plus price and earns a modest margin. It is privately owned and customer-serving, not an Alpha-selected House.
- B11 assembles TJ at Site 6 after Tomas is pinned. TJ scouts and reaches the jammed mechanism, but Alex still chooses to cross the assigned-zone line and perform the rescue.
- B11 is wrong once in Halyard: he counts the people visible in the registry and misses those outside it. He admits the error plainly and asks Alex what Alex would do.
- Adrian’s gambling harmed his family, destabilized the household and contributed to the debt. The casino chip is not lucky, heroic or a secret laboratory key.
- Lock offers immediate debt clearance, a clean name and protection from review in exchange for assigning Adrian’s restricted and disputed estate to House administration. His argument that the Houses can build and distribute at scale is serious. Alex’s refusal exposes the collaborators to reassignment and costs him immediate relief.
- The collaborators release the household purifier under a public, nonexclusive license. Attribution survives; House power and control of production do not disappear.
- Mera selects **TRACK** in Issue 01. Her later action opens a separate conduit-segment valve, creating the viable ascent route; it is not the residential regulator operated by the miners.
- Adrian’s final choice occurs in the old monitoring station. Alpha closes the door while Mera’s override is denied. Adrian activates B11’s recording/deployment state; the story does not show an evacuation or establish a recovered body. The workshop B11 later found was prepared through Adrian’s route and remains dormant until Alex inserts the key.
- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. **Nothing else about her is learned.**
- Alex refuses Mera’s offer of full executive authority. The visible cost is the sealing of the disputed workshop, expiration of his temporary travel clearance and continued personal and institutional debt.
- The new process proves itself when a House representative submits a publicly plausible allocation that diverts physical flow to an industrial branch. Tomas and affected Keel residents compare the record with physical gauges and stop approval.

- Issue 01 is Keel; Issue 02 is Halyard; Issue 03 is Oasis.
- Issue 01 ends inside the conduit as water begins filling behind Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ.
- No train crossing.
- Use only Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House. Never use five Houses or the superseded names.
- Use **B11** everywhere.
- Alex’s mother remains unrevealed except for leaving Adrian after he lost everything.
- Ancestry never predicts intelligence, morality, competence, class, wealth or ideology.
- The safeguards are learned through use, cost and consequence.
- Alpha has no face, consciousness or final authority over human values.
- Alex does not become a ruler.
- B11 remains warm, fallible and non-menacing. TJ remains silent and dog-shaped.
- Debt, House ownership, scarcity, disagreement, institutional resistance and human error survive the ending.
- The ending demonstrates the new process through a caught attempt to rig a decision.

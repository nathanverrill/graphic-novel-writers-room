# Prosperity — Layouts

**Canon:** This execution pass covers exactly Pages 1–16: the four-page prologue and Chapter 1. Western left-to-right reading; portrait print format; no text rendered in artwork. All lettering is supplied in the layout blocks. The image model must generate no readable text; captions, balloons and sound effects are lettering-layer elements.

**Base grid:** Variable three-tier layouts, generally 4–6 panels per page. Page 5 is a full-page establishing splash. Page 14 uses a dominant hologram panel. Lettering zones remain separate from faces, evidence and consequential objects.

# What the Draft Editor changed, added, and found that does not fit

## Pages

Pages are drawn by an image model, one page per generation, from each character's and location's sheet, and a page drifts when it carries too much. So: at most 6 panels a page; at most 3 named characters in one panel; one location a page where the story allows (every new location needs its own sheet); no crowd that must be recognisable; a big moment gets a splash (one panel, the whole page). Count pages this way, not by comic convention.

| chapter | page plot | as drafted | added | now |

| 00-prologue.md | - | 4 | +0 | 4 |
| 01-chapter-01.md | - | 12 | +0 | 12 |
| **the book** | - | 16 | +0 | **16** |

The book is set to 16 pages: the script and the layouts are made to it.

The future is mostly older infrastructure carried forward: repaired, corroded, retrofitted and institutionalized. Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, mestizo, European-descended, Afro-descendant, South Asian, East Asian, mixed and other diasporic populations live across all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the primary visual differences.

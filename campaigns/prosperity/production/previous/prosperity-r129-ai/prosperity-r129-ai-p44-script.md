## Page 44 (left) — The collaborators choose a risky independent path and build a bounded prototype.

**Panel 1.** Before dawn, Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren gather in the rented room.

JALEN: You woke us up to tell us you're not signing. That's not a plan, that's a death wish with extra steps.

LINA: He's not entirely wrong. Lock's offer pays all of us. Refuse it and we're betting our careers on businesses that don't exist yet.

JALEN: Take the deal. We get paid. That's safety.

REN: Disappearing from the record is what already happened to us once.

**Panel 2.** Alex projects the compact redesign.

ALEX: What if we built it small enough for a local entrepreneur to afford?

LINA: This is industrial water technology.

ALEX: Then we redesign it. Something people can build, maintain, and sell locally.

SURI: A network of small businesses instead of one giant factory?

ALEX: Exactly. We license it to all of them, not to one buyer. And every business gets a chance to improve it.

JALEN: And who owns it?

ALEX: Everyone who built it. We decide the terms together.

**Panel 3.** Prototype iterations fill the room: a bulky expensive version, folded membrane, alternating biofilter power draw and Ren’s salvaged heat exchanger.

ALEX: At that price, no one outside the Houses could build it.

SURI: Then we're asking the wrong question.

ALEX: Which is?

SURI: What could we change without sacrificing what makes it work?

LINA: The membrane needs surface area to work. Shrink the housing and you starve it.

ALEX: Then don't shrink it flat. Fold it. Same surface area, a fraction of the footprint.

LINA: ...That's not in any of our patents.

ALEX: It's not supposed to be. It's new.

**Panel 4.** The toolbox-sized unit rests on the table at dawn. Its display reads **SIMULATED EFFICIENCY: 91% / UNIT COST: FRACTION OF INDUSTRIAL SCALE / STATUS: READY FOR FIELD PILOT — SAFETY TESTING REQUIRED**.

SURI: My biofilter needs a steady draw. Jalen's node logic wants to cycle on and off to save power. They fight each other at this size.

JALEN: ...Or they take turns. One rests while the other works. It's not elegant.

ALEX: It doesn't have to be elegant. It has to fit in someone's kitchen.

REN: Mineral recovery and moisture capture both generate waste heat. Industrial scale, you vent it. This scale, you can't afford to waste it.

ALEX: So feed it back into the biofilter's draw.

REN: Your father never thought that small. Took someone who grew up rationing to think of it.

ALEX: It's smaller than I thought it'd be.

ADA: So was the idea that built it. That's probably the point.

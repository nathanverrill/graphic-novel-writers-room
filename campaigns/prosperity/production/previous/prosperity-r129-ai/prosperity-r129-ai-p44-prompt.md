ALEX: Exactly. We license it to all of them, not to one buyer. And every business gets a chance to improve it.

JALEN: And who owns it?

ALEX: Everyone who built it. We decide the terms together.

LINA: The membrane needs surface area to work. Shrink the housing and you starve it.

ALEX: Then don't shrink it flat. Fold it. Same surface area, a fraction of the footprint.

LINA: ...That's not in any of our patents.

ALEX: It's not supposed to be. It's new.

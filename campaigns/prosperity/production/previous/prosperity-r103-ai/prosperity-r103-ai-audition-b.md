# Prosperity — Audition B

## Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099

## Page 1 (right) — Older Alex watches TJ come apart over the lake while thieves work beyond the dark water.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** NIGHT at a weathered Lake Keel boathouse. The old open pit is now a broad dark lake, ringed by terraces and scattered lights. Older Alex Phantum stands barefoot at the waterline in a faded work jacket, one hand braced on a mooring post. Across the lake, a small repair robot shaped like a dog—TJ—is coming apart mid-stride: head, torso and legs separating above the black water. A granddaughter in a blanket stands behind Alex, half-hidden by the boathouse door.

SFX: CLICK—CLICK—CLICK

**Panel 2 — CLOSE ON ALEX.** His face is older, lined and watchful. He does not move toward the lake. In the distance, a cold search beam sweeps across the water.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Is that supposed to happen?

ALEX: Not usually.

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM ON THE LAKE.** TJ’s head floats ahead of the other pieces, its two scratched optical lenses fixed toward a tanker barge offshore. Its loose magnetic parts trail behind like a broken constellation. Two small drones hover above the barge, their hoses lowered into the lake.

SFX: WHIRRRR

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON ALEX’S HAND.** His fingers tighten around the rough mooring post. Behind him, the granddaughter reaches for his sleeve. He keeps watching TJ.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Should we call someone?

ALEX: Let’s see what they came to take.

PAGE CHECK: Older Alex is introduced through a choice not to rush into danger. TJ’s separation and the siphoning equipment establish the mystery without explaining the later story. Page-turn function: reveal what the thieves are doing.

## Page 2 (left) — TJ’s separated parts cross the lake toward the siphoning operation.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** The lake under moonlight. TJ’s head glides low across the surface, carried by a small magnetic float hidden beneath its jaw. Farther back, its torso and two legs move independently along the water, while the fourth leg clings to a half-submerged maintenance rail. The tanker barge looms beyond them, its hose drawing a bright, churning column from the lake.

SFX: SHHHRRRR

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM ON THE BARGE.** Two thieves in patched waterproof gear work beneath harsh lamps. One monitors the hose coupling; the other looks toward the shore. The drones’ white beams cut across their faces and the water.

THIEF 1: Keep the pull low.

THIEF 2: It’s a lake, not a vault.

**Panel 3 — CLOSE ON TJ’S HEAD.** A scratched ceramic lens tilts toward a hovering drone. The head’s little magnetic float bumps against a ripple, then corrects course.

SFX: TIK

**Panel 4 — WIDE FROM THE BOATHOUSE.** Alex and his granddaughter remain small silhouettes at the shore. The thieves’ lights move across the lake between them and TJ. Alex raises one hand—not a wave, not yet an alarm.

GRANDDAUGHTER: They can see us.

ALEX: Then they know we’re polite.

PAGE CHECK: TJ is actively scouting while separated, and the thieves’ operation is concrete: drones and a tanker are siphoning lake water. Page-turn function: bring TJ into the machinery and expose the thieves’ stake in the theft.

## Page 3 (right) — The thieves reveal that Alex’s wealth is part of their question as TJ reaches the drone intake.

**Panel 1 — MEDIUM ON THE BARGE.** One thief crouches beside the hose while the other studies a distant boathouse light through binoculars. The tanker’s storage gauge climbs by a single mark.

THIEF 2: That’s Phantum’s place.

THIEF 1: The old man with the lake?

THIEF 2: The old man with everything.

**Panel 2 — CLOSE ON THE BINOCULAR VIEW.** Through the lenses: Alex’s silhouette beside the boathouse, his granddaughter close at his side. The image is broken by the moving white beam of a drone.

THIEF 1 (off): Water paid for it?

THIEF 2 (off): Or water made him rich.

**Panel 3 — LOW ANGLE ON THE DRONE.** TJ’s head has reached the drone’s underside. Its ceramic lens peers into the intake assembly. Below, the torso and legs are still closing the distance across the dark water.

SFX: VMMMMM

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON TJ’S HEAD AND A DRONE INTAKE.** TJ’s small magnetic jaw catches a metal edge. The drone’s intake shutters begin to rotate around him.

SFX: KLIK

PAGE CHECK: The thieves connect Alex’s prosperity to the lake without resolving whether he earned it fairly or controlled it. TJ has reached the machinery, but the operation is about to trap him. Page-turn function: next page delivers TJ’s intervention and the first physical consequence.

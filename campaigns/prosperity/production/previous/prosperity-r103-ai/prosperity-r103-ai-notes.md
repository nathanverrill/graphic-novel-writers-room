## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The script has a strong material spine—debt, Tomas’s rescue, the Measure, public evidence and the final allocation check—but the reader loses causal orientation in the Alpha/safeguard climax and the Issue 02 maker sequence. Execution also contains a Blocker: the Page 46 layout and thumbnail depict a different scene from the approved script, so the book is not ready for lettering or image generation.

### Major

Let the public review on Pages 61–62 demonstrate the result rather than explain the protocol.

## Page 1 (right) — Alex repairs a failing Keel pump by touch, proving his competence against the official record.

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing shot. The Keel pump hall before dawn. Rust, charcoal, and oxidized orange. Alex Phantum (17, lean, in a faded work jacket) leans his entire weight against a massive, shuddering water pipe. His eyes are closed. His bare hand is flat against the metal, reading the vibration.

**Panel 2.** Close on Alex’s scarred hands. He forces a salvaged, mismatched iron bracket into the pump’s housing, tightening a bolt.

**Panel 3.** A Supervisor stands behind Alex, arms crossed. The pump hums smoothly.

SUPERVISOR: That’s a scrap bracket. It's not in the manual.

ALEX: The manual doesn't have to drink the water.

**Panel 4.** The Supervisor turns to a wall terminal. A small white-and-amber Alpha node blinks on its casing. The screen flashes a red warning: `UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR`. The Supervisor taps the screen, forcing a green checkmark over it.

SUPERVISOR: I'm logging it as routine. Don't push your luck today, Phantum.

PAGE CHECK: Alex trusts physical reality over the official record.


## Page 2 (left) — Alex inherits his father's crushing debt and loses his mobility.

**Panel 1.** The Keel exit checkpoint. Cold white light cuts through the ambient dust. Alex presses his bare palm against a flat Alpha scanner. A Guard stands beside the heavy iron turnstile, watching the queue.

**Panel 2.** Extreme close-up on the scanner display. Cold, clinical typography.

DISPLAY: ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: 17
PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE
GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED
DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS
EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS
PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS
TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED

**Panel 3.** The Guard looks at the screen, then looks at Alex. It’s a look of quiet, embarrassed pity.

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

**Panel 4.** Alex stands perfectly still. He doesn't look at the Guard. His jaw is tight. His hand is in his jacket pocket, his thumb rubbing a worn casino chip etched with a faint equation.

ALEX: Yeah.

PAGE CHECK: The abstract system becomes a personal trap; Adrian's gambling legacy is felt without exposition.


## Page 3 (right) — Alex takes on personal debt for his grandmother, losing his ticket out.

**Panel 1.** Inez Quispe's independent pharmacy. Faded teal and rust. Inez (51, glasses, hair in a wrapped braid) writes in a blue cloth ledger on the counter. A small glass bottle of medicine sits between her and Alex. Ada Veyra (20, copper hair, rivet-studded strap) leans against the wall behind Alex, watching quietly.

**Panel 2.** Inez slides the bottle forward. Her expression is level and entirely unsentimental.

INEZ: Thirty credits. Fixed installments. Same amount each time.

ALEX: I'll have it after next shift.

**Panel 3.** Inez taps the ledger with her pen.

INEZ: If you don't, you come tell me before I have to ask.

**Panel 4.** Alex steps away from the counter, putting the bottle in his canvas bag. Ada falls in beside him. She looks at his hands, then up at his face.

ADA: You spent the travel token money.

ALEX: She needs the medicine.

PAGE CHECK: Alex accepts a concrete obligation to a named person; Ada challenges his choices without controlling them.

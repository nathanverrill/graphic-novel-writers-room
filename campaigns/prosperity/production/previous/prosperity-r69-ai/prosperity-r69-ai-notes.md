# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The written script has a strong physical-action spine and several effective character beats, but the causal chain breaks at the Issue 01 crossing, the Issue 02 collaborator sequence, and the Issue 03 civic climax. Execution is not delivering the script: the supplied Page 46 layout depicts a completely different scene from script Page 46, making the current page set non-continuous and unsafe for production.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Page 46 layout is the wrong scene

- **Location:** `script.md`, Issue 03, Page 46; `layouts.md` and `thumbnails.md`, Page 46.
- **Observation:** The script Page 46 is a two-shot/fragmented recording scene in which Alex and B11 process Adrian’s final message: “He left me the debt. The silence. The machine.” The layout instead depicts Mera and Leona in Leona’s apartment discussing Ada’s association risk, with Ada present in Panel 4.
- **Problem:** This is not a staging variation; it replaces the scripted event with material from an un-scripted scene. Page 46’s planned emotional and causal transition into the legacy node is absent, and the layout introduces unsupported facts about Mera suppressing Ada’s association risk and Leona surrendering Covenant authority.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Layout Agent: discard the current Page 46 layout and rebuild it from script Page 46, preserving the Alex/B11/Adrian-recording scene and the page-turn into Page 47. Do not promote the apartment material to canon unless the Director sends the book back for a rewrite.
- **Role:** Layout.

#### 2. Page 18 contradicts its own Tomas cliffhanger

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 17–18.
- **Observation:** Page 17 visibly shows Tomas alive, braced with miners at Site 6. Page 18 then displays `SITE 6 WORKER STATUS: TOMAS PHANTUM? — SIGNAL UNAVAILABLE` as the issue cliffhanger. Alex, Ada and the others are in a separate conduit and Tomas was never traveling with them.
- **Problem:** The reader cannot tell what has changed for Tomas. A lost signal is not inherently a problem, but no device, network relationship or intervening event establishes why his status has become newly uncertain. The cliffhanger reads as manufactured rather than caused by the action.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: show the miners’ position being cut off by a specific event between Pages 17 and 18—Foreman-7 severs the relay, the cart collapses, or a pressure surge destroys the Site 6 communications node—and make the display identify the failed relay rather than imply Alex’s wristband tracks Tomas personally. Keep Tomas’s survival unresolved; do not show him alive in the same uninterrupted moment as the “signal lost” beat without an intervening cause.
- **Role:** Writer.

### Major

- **Purifier — HOLDS:** 94% industrial and 91% household can coexist if the script makes scale, maintenance and feedwater the reason for the lower household result. Contamination remains distinct from the 62/18 routing discrepancy.
- **Public review geography — FAILS CLARITY CHECK:** Tomas, Keel workers and the Oasis review room are not spatially connected in the current script. Establish a live feed or place the participants together.
- **Alpha — HOLDS IN PRINCIPLE:** Alpha remains faceless, distributed and bounded after restoration. Its operational behavior is too abstract early and too screen-heavy late; show it through nodes, gates, records and human consequences.
- **Adrian’s fate — HOLDS:** The script does not show an evacuation or recovered body. Do not let the stale Page 46 layout or added dialogue imply survival, death confirmation or new information.
- **Alex’s mother — HOLDS ONLY IF PROTECTED IN PRODUCTION:** No new information appears in the current script. The showrunner’s rule remains absolute: she left Adrian after he lost everything, and nothing else is learned.

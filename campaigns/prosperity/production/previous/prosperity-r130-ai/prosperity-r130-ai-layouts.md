## Page 46 (left) — 4 panels, stacked tiers with a dominant closing panel

**Observation:** Page 46 executes the approved Charter House review sequence and follows Page 45’s review chamber. It pays off Lina’s custody of the Maker’s Seal before Page 47’s independent venture and Mera hook.

**Reading path:** Review chair’s question → Alex’s refusal and attribution → Lock’s challenge and Alex’s answer → the five contributors activate the provenance record.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, the bronze Maker’s Seal beside the stabilized shared provenance record.

**Page intent:** A formal ownership confrontation resolves through deliberate attribution rather than possession. Use one continuous Charter House review chamber throughout: pearl-white institutional architecture, elevated House representatives, restrained jade-teal, amber-orange and steel-violet accents, the compact purifier on the central table. Keep each panel visually simple and reserve clear lettering space. No Covenant bands, archive material, Leona or mother–daughter material appear.

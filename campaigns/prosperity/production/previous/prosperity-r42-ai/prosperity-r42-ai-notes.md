## Plausibility ledger

- **54-page, three-issue format:** Holds in `brief.md` and `story.md`; contradicted by the production script and drafts. This is a structural canon contradiction, not a world plausibility issue.
- **Keel mine geology:** Canon fixes an exhausted copper-zinc-lead-silver polymetallic hard-rock body. The source draft’s lithium mine wording is an uncorrected source contradiction. Production-facing prompts must use the corrected geology.
- **Alpha’s autonomous authority:** Declared project license and consistently supported by the world file. It must remain a distributed federation and not become a single person-like consciousness.
- **Alpha’s human authorization limits:** The current script risks treating Alpha’s “foundational instrument has priority” response as a general constitutional ruling and Alex’s authentication as a unilateral transition. The Writer must preserve the distinction between a system following a pre-existing bounded protocol and a human body making the final decision.
- **Hidden water diversion:** Holds where the script shows the residential and industrial pipes, gauge, regulator, data line, and physical response. It fails if the broadcast itself appears to move water.
- **Purification technology:** Holds only under the bounded conditions now stated in the corrected draft: specified test panel, controlled feedwater and pretreatment, limited prototype scale, power, maintenance, and verification. Do not show the 99.97% result as universal potable output.
- **Maker’s Seal:** Holds when it reconstructs provenance, freezes disputed registries, and triggers human review. The current “RESTORE CONTRIBUTOR OWNERSHIP” interface risks implying automatic title transfer; the page must show records entering review, not ownership being instantly reassigned.
- **Covenant activation:** Holds only when the three cities’ representatives take reciprocal actions before certainty. The physical marks may authenticate those actions, but the Covenant must not appear to activate by object possession or by Alex’s command.
- **Leona’s review:** Holds only if the association-risk order is suspended pending independent human review, not erased by Alex or by the Covenant itself.
- **Adrian’s death:** Holds when the sequence shows Alpha closing flood-control gates and denying pump, ventilation, and escape access, with Mera’s attempted override logged and rejected. The current mechanism is plausible within the project license but is placed in the wrong page structure.
- **TJ:** Canon requires a magnetic repair robot dog built at Site 6. The current script’s late appearance is an undeclared omission in the cut material, not a new license. The Plotter must restore the established beat.
- **Travel by conduit:** Holds if the group travels from Keel down through the old works and upward inside the near-empty water conduit. The current train crossing contradicts a fixed fact and must be removed.

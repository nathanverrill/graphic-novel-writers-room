#### 2. Page 6 drops the approved torn-poster visual beat

- **Problem:** The approved draft specifies a peeling poster of a mostly torn-away man’s face on The Last Chance, framing Adrian’s public reputation as a damaged cautionary legend. The script omits it. This weakens the visual setup for the gambling rumor and removes a deliberate image that complicates the casino encounter without confirming the rumor as truth.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, restore the torn, mostly erased face poster in the panel description. Do not add a readable portrait or new dialogue.

### Minor

#### 3. Chapter/page labels should distinguish global page numbers from local chapter numbering

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 5–16; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 headings labeled Page 1–12 locally.
- **Problem:** The script correctly delivers the global 16-page pass, but the source draft’s local Chapter 1 numbering could cause production to label the pages incorrectly when assembling the book.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, retain global `Page 1`–`Page 16` numbering in the production script and identify the Chapter 1 local equivalents only in headings or notes if needed. Do not renumber or alter sequence.

#### 4. Page 6’s checkpoint lettering is dense for image-model generation

- **Location:** `script.md`, Page 6, Panel 2.
- **Problem:** The full debt-transfer display, guard dialogue, Alex dialogue and caption all compete in one panel. This is inherited from the approved draft rather than a continuity error, but the showrunner’s drawability rule makes the panel vulnerable to illegible lettering or obscured acting.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Letterer**, reserve a single clean display area and separate the checkpoint text from the speech balloons without covering Alex’s face or the scanner. **Layout Agent**, keep the display readable at first pass; do not cut or paraphrase approved text.

#### 2. The final causal chain is not legible enough to support the climax

- **Location:** Issue 03 Pages 53–62.
- **Observation:** Mera, Alpha’s authority, the Covenant, the Distributed Verification Protocol and the first public review are introduced or activated in rapid succession. Ada also reappears at the civic hall without a visible reunion with Alex, while Lina’s Page 46 detention is never resolved.

- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** make the existing actions carry the missing transitions: show Lina’s status or release, reunite Alex and Ada on-page, establish Mera’s institutional role through an action, and make the first review visibly hinge on a representative’s challenge. Do not add a governance speech.

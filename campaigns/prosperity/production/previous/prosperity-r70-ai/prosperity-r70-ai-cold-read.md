# Cold Read: Prosperity

I read it all in one sitting, the night before, in order: Issue 01, then 02, then 03. Only one version came to me, so there's nothing to compare. I went in expecting to hate it, and I'll say where that held and where it didn't.

---

## Issue 01: Keel (pages 1–18)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 2, whole page.** This is where the book first got me. It's his birthday, and the gate screen tells him his dad's debt is now his: 418,607 credits, 40 earned today, 28.7 years to pay it off. The guard says "Happy birthday, Phantum" and the workers behind him go quiet. I got it straight away and it felt bad in a good way. He says "Twenty-eight point seven." and I wanted to keep going.
- **Page 1.** He fixes the pump his own way and the supervisor's screen says UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR. "Then don't approve it" is a decent attitude line. Fine opening, not great.
- **Page 6.** B11 waking up. "I was stored. Sleep is more comfortable." and "It is a fact with a poor record." The robot is funny and I liked it right away. Second hook.
- **Page 10, panels 3–4.** He steps over the yellow line, a screen shows −40 CR, and he says "That is today's pay." After page 2 I knew what 40 credits meant to him, so this landed.
- **Page 15.** Reported 62%, physical 18%. **This is the first point where I understood what the story is about**: someone is taking Keel's water and lying about it. That's page 15 of 18.
- **Page 16.** Pressing SEND, and the public screens change across Keel. A real choice with a real cost. I liked it.
- **Page 18, panel 3.** "TOMAS PHANTUM?" Wait. What?

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 3–4.** The pharmacy. I didn't know why I was there. The medicine, the token, five credits a shift: it's a lot of payment back-and-forth with someone I'd just met. I skimmed the ledger lines on page 4.
- **Page 8.** Mera's control room is all screen text with almost no people. I skimmed REPORT / TRACK / HUMAN SCOPE ACKNOWLEDGED.
- **Page 13, panel 4 to page 14.** Pipe labels, gauges, the Measure. I read for the numbers and skipped the rest.

### I was confused
- **Page 3:** Who is Ada? She's standing in the pharmacy like I'm supposed to know her. Friend? Girlfriend? Sister? I never found out what she is to him. And what is the travel token *for*? Where is he going?
- **Page 4, panel 4:** One panel jumps from the pharmacy to Grandmother's apartment, and suddenly there's "Adrian's hand-bound book." What book? It opens a secret bookcase and I'm supposed to just go with it. Did Grandmother even get her medicine? The book never says.
- **Page 5:** "He was working when he left. He had already lost everything." Working on what? Left how? Is Dad dead, gone, in jail? I couldn't tell, and I still can't really tell at the end of the book.
- **Pages 6–7:** "Would you like the route, or the question first?" What route? What question? Adrian's recording shows a map going under the sealed mine. **I know he has a map and the mine is sealed, but what is he trying to do?** Why is Alex going down there? Nobody says what he gets out of it.
- **Page 8:** Is Mera a good guy or a bad guy? She picks TRACK over REPORT, which seems nice, then the system sends Alex to Site 6 at 02:14. Did she do that to him or for him? I couldn't tell.
- **Page 10, panel 1:** Tomas is trapped and says "That's not regulation." About what? Being crushed?
- **Page 11, panel 2:** TJ builds itself out of "B11's open tool bag." I thought B11 *was* in the bag. Where did the dog parts come from?
- **Page 12, last panel:** "Someone has to make this look like an accident." Make what look like an accident? Who for? Lost.
- **Page 17:** Mera turns a valve and a conduit starts shaking where they're standing. Is she trying to drown them or get them out? I honestly don't know. The water nearly kills them, so it reads like she's trying to kill them.
- **Page 18, panel 3:** Tomas *Phantum*? Is he Alex's grandfather? Uncle? Nobody reacts to it. Is the name glitching or is it real?

### Lines I read twice
- "You know a direction." (p3)
- "Tied, then. Not square." (p4)
- "Your father was working. / That doesn't mean he didn't hurt us." (p4)
- "He used gambling for everything." (p5)
- "Trust the machine when it checks the pipe. Question me when I tell you why." (p7). I gave up on this one.
- "Somewhere the records forgot." (p7)
- "He ask you to keep him out? / He didn't ask enough." (p12). Gave up.
- "The survey says straight. The bolts say left." (p13). Gave up.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Easy: Alex, B11, TJ, Tomas, Grandmother, Inez. Ada looks clear but I don't know what she's for. Mera I could pick out, but I couldn't tell whose side she's on. Supervisor, guard and Foreman-7 just did their jobs.

One thing I noticed already: Inez, Ada, Grandmother and Tomas all talk the same way, in short wise sayings that flip at the end. Nobody sounds like a person having a normal conversation. A lot of my class would call that AI writing, and it stuck with me for the rest of the book.

### I felt something
Page 2 (the birthday debt). Page 10 ("That is today's pay."). A little at page 16 when the screens change.

### I predicted
That the water numbers were fake, around page 14. That Tomas knew Dad, as soon as he pulled out the key.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid gets handed his missing dad's debt on his 17th birthday and finds out the people running things are stealing his town's water.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 8.** Up to page 7 I was half in because of B11, but page 7 gave me a map with no goal, and then page 8 was a screen full of words I didn't know (Alpha, House, human scope) about a woman I couldn't read. In a shop it goes back on the shelf here.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Kind of. The splash looks cool, but the escape alone wouldn't make me buy more. "TOMAS PHANTUM?" would. I only met Tomas six pages earlier, but if he's family and maybe dead, I want to know. That's a real question about a real person, not just a loud noise. Mild yes.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 8; next issue: yes

## Issue 02: Halyard (pages 19–36)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 19.** "It was a route. The destination objected." B11 is still the best thing in the book.
- **Page 21.** Dad has 312 patents. Okay, so he wasn't just a gambler, he was a genius inventor. That changed how I saw him and I wanted more.
- **Page 22, panel 4.** Sign, and the debt clears tonight. That's a real temptation after page 2. I wanted to see what he'd do.
- **Page 28.** 11% goes to 94% because Alex fits a sleeve in by feel. That paid off the pump-fixing from page 1. Satisfying.
- **Page 29.** B11 admitting "I counted the record and missed the people." I liked the robot being wrong and saying so.
- **Page 33, panel 3.** "No." Simple and good.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 20.** An order sheet with a price breakdown. I skimmed it.
- **Pages 23–26.** Four new people in four pages (Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren), each handing over a part and a wise saying. I was skimming hard by page 25.
- **Page 31.** More percentages.
- **Page 32.** Mara reads the *same* price list from page 20 out loud on a video call, then we get a ledger of PAID PAID PAID. This is where it felt like homework about economics. I skimmed the whole page.
- **Page 34.** Everybody says their job title into a recorder. Skimmed.
- **Page 35.** Screen text. Skimmed.

### I was confused
- **What happened to the water?** Issue 1 ends on stolen water and maybe-dead Tomas. Issue 2 is about building a purifier and patents. Nobody mentions Tomas, Grandmother, or the 18%. It felt like a different book.
- **Page 19:** "Mara's still shipping." Who is Mara? Ada knows her and I don't.
- **Page 22:** "The collaborators remain employed." / "You already know who they are." **I don't know who they are.** I hadn't met any of them yet. And what purifier? Where did building a purifier become Alex's goal? I missed when that was decided, if it ever was.
- **Page 23:** Lina says "You're late." So they were expected? By whom? How?
- **Page 23:** "Not your Keel routing failure." / "Two pipes. Don't make one answer do both jobs." I sort of got that it's a different problem, but it was said like a riddle.
- **Page 27:** "node shard." When did Jalen give him that?
- **Page 30:** They escape through a corridor, then the patrol stops outside the maker room "unable to enter without triggering a public access record." So did they leave the room or not? And why does a "public record" stop guards? I didn't get what the danger was.
- **Page 32:** Mara's shop is "reopened" under a "restored" sign. I didn't know it had closed. And Mara is in Keel on video, but her cart was in Halyard on page 19?
- **Page 34:** The Maker's Seal "warms." Is this magic? Tech? It's never explained, and the bands do it too in Issue 3.
- **Page 35, panel 3:** "OWNERSHIP CLAIM ALTERATION: EXPOSED." What alteration? Who changed what?
- **Page 36:** Covenant band. New thing, no explanation.

### Lines I read twice
- "Morrow asks customers. The House asks Alpha. / Same machine? / Different wager." (p20). Gave up.
- "From being asked to choose while frightened." (p22)
- "I know where the work went." (p22)
- "It is alive. That means it keeps time." (p24)

- "The edges are wrong." (p26)
- "They have chosen visibility." (p30)
- "I carried it until someone proved they understood what it was for." (p34)
- "I release the design under public, nonexclusive terms. I accept the failures that come with building it." (p34). I didn't need this read twice, but nobody my age talks like this. It sounds like a terms-of-service page.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Lock: clear. The stylus thing helps.
- Ren: clear. He's big and cuts metal.
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen: I could not keep them apart.** Each one hands over a part and says a saying. If you swapped their lines I wouldn't notice.
- **Mara (Morrow) and Mera (Vale).** I thought they were the same person for a page. One is the shop lady and one is the control-room lady, but the names are one letter apart.
- **Lina Vey and Ada Veyra.** Vey / Veyra. I thought Lina might be Ada's relative and waited for a reveal that never came. Then in Issue 3 there's Leona Veyra too.
- Alex / Ada / Adrian: three A-names in the main cast. Not fatal, just noticeable.

### I felt something
Page 20, panel 2: the young worker's hand staying on the part after the kiosk takes it, then watching it disappear. Small, but I felt it. Page 33: "No." That's about it. The four collaborators never got enough room for me to care whether they got in trouble on page 35.

### I predicted
That he'd refuse Lock's deal. I knew by page 22. I figured the first test would fail and Alex would fix it by hand (p27 to p28).

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid turns down a corporation that would clear his debt if he let it swallow his dad's patents, and gives the design away so the workers who made it keep their names.

(Which isn't the sentence from Issue 1. I spent this issue waiting for the water story to come back.)

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 24.** That's when the new names started piling up and everyone talked in sayings. If I'd somehow kept going, page 32 would have finished me.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. Mera presses SUMMON and says "I can ask. I cannot decide what he answers." It's a summons, not a cliffhanger. I still don't know if Mera is good or bad, so I don't care what she wants. The thing I *did* want answered (Tomas) wasn't touched this whole issue.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 24; next issue: no

## Issue 03: Oasis (pages 37–54)

### Pulled me forward
- **Pages 38–40.** Ada and Leona. Out of nowhere Ada has a past, and it's the realest scene in the book. "You're taller." / "That's what you have?" is good. "Within a year." / "And you didn't come." hit me. First time Ada felt like a person instead of the friend who says smart things.
- **Page 41.** "During the Water Wars, three people held the Covenant." / "'Later' became permission to continue." This is when the whole thing finally clicked: an emergency AI power was never switched off. It's on page 41 of 54.
- **Pages 45–46.** Adrian in the flooding station, water at his knees: "Alex. What you do with this is up to you." Then "He left me the debt. The silence. The machine." / "He left you a choice." The best two pages in the book. I felt it.
- **Page 48, panel 3.** Ada: "You offered it to us. That's still two people deciding for everyone else." Good line, said plainly.
- **Page 50.** The refusal costs him: the debt stays, his travel clearance expires, the workshop gets sealed. I respected that it didn't hand him a happy ending.
- **Page 51.** "Paper says sixty-two. / Pipe says eighteen." A callback to Issue 1 that actually landed.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 37.** Board text and two new people (Nadi, Olani).
- **Pages 42 and 47.** Bands linking, VERIFY / VERIFY / VERIFY. All screen text, skimmed.
- **Page 49 to page 50, panel 2.** DESIGNATION DECLINED, CONVENOR, RESCINDED, UNCHANGED, UNCHANGED, UNCHANGED. I got the gist and skipped the words.
- **Page 53, panel 4.** A room arguing, and the panel tells me "No instant redistribution occurs." It's a meeting. The climax of the book is a meeting.

### I was confused
- **Page 37:** Tomas is just alive, standing there limping. No reunion, no "I thought you drowned," and nobody ever explains the "TOMAS PHANTUM?" thing from p18. That was the reason I picked up Issue 2, and the book acted like it never happened.
- **Page 38:** Is Leona Ada's mom? I think so, but nobody says "mom." Fifteen years, so Ada left at about two? Or was taken? I don't know how Ada ended up in Keel.
- **Page 40:** "I traded a few terrible days I could see for fifteen years I couldn't." I still don't fully know what she traded.
- **Page 41:** Who were the three? I worked out Leona, Adrian and Mera from the bands, but it's never said outright. And how does Ada's mom being a custodian connect to Ada being in Keel? Is that why Ada was sent away?
- **Pages 43–44:** So Alpha (I think) cut Adrian off from buying parts and traveling until he was trapped. Is that where the 418,607 debt came from? Was the debt a punishment? This seems like the biggest secret in the book and I'm still only guessing.
- **Page 45:** Did Adrian die? It "cuts before any evacuation or death is shown." Grandmother said "when he left" back in Issue 1. I don't know if he's dead. Maybe that's on purpose, but it read like the book avoiding the question.
- **Page 47:** "The linked bands display the obligations Leona and B11 have performed." What did B11 perform?
- **Page 54:** The debt went from 418,607 to 402,110, which is about 16,500 paid. At 40 a shift that's over a year of work. How much time passed? It felt like a couple of weeks.

### Lines I read twice
- "I will remain where the affected people can answer me." (p40)
- "Two can expose an obligation. Two cannot restore the old permission." (p42). Gave up.
- "I tried after the door was closing." (p44)
- "Don't ask the number to do the seeing." (p53)
- "Convening isn't owning." (p49). Understood it, and rolled my eyes.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Ada finally became a person (p38–40).
- Mera finally became readable, at p44 when she breaks. Before that I couldn't tell what she wanted.
- Leona is clear in her one scene.
- Nadi and Olani are just roles: the company rep and the note-taker.
- Mera / Mara and Lina / Leona / Ada Veyra were still getting mixed up in my head.

### I felt something
Pages 39–40 (Ada and Leona). Pages 45–46 (Adrian's recording and the chip set down). Page 54, panel 3: Grandmother setting the cloth down instead of folding it. I didn't expect that to get me, but it did.

### I predicted
That Alex would turn down the power on p48. I knew it before Mera finished offering, because the book had been refusing things for 30 pages. That Tomas would bring back "paper vs pipe." That the ending would be quiet and nothing would get fully fixed.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An emergency AI power from an old war was never shut off, and a kid who could take it over refuses, and makes everyone argue in public instead.

### Where I would have put it down
**None.** Pages 38–40 and 45–46 kept me going. The skimming on pages 47–50 was the closest I came.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
It works as an ending. It's quiet and honest: he's still in debt and paying Inez five at a time, and "Neither moves for the other." I wouldn't buy a next book, though. The ending is fine for what it is, but the two characters I actually cared about (Ada and Dad) got their real scenes in the last third. By then I'd spent two issues not knowing who anyone was.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: none; next issue: no

## For the whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
**Page 32.** Mara reads out "Parts cost thirty-two. Labor costs twenty. Margin is eight." on a video call. That's the same price list we already saw on page 20, followed by a ledger that says PAID four times. This is where everyone works out it's an economics lesson in comic form, and the group chat starts. Honorable mention: page 8, a screen full of words nobody has explained.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"A kid inherits his dad's insane debt, finds out the city's AI is stealing water, and then for like two issues he turns down every shortcut people offer him. The robot is funny. Ada's mom scene and the dad's video near the end are actually good. It takes forever to explain anything, and everyone talks like a fortune cookie."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 01:** A great birthday-debt opening and a funny robot, then twelve pages of going somewhere for reasons nobody told me, saved by "Tomas Phantum?"
- **Issue 02:** Four interchangeable new people build a water filter while the book forgets the water, Tomas and the grandmother, and hands me a civics lecture.
- **Issue 03:** The world finally gets explained on page 41, and the Ada and Adrian scenes made me feel something, but the big moment is him saying no to a screen and a meeting arguing.

# Prosperity — Audition B

## Page 1 (right) — Alex keeps a failing pump alive, and the system marks the repair against him.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** Pre-dawn inside a Keel pump hall: patched machinery, narrow service channels, mineral dust in the work lights. Alex Phantum, seventeen, lean and wiry in his patched charcoal work shirt, rust-orange utility vest, slate work trousers and worn steel-toe boots, presses his scarred palm against a trembling pump housing. His canvas tool bag lies open. A salvaged metal bracket holds a cracked pipe together. The official gauge shivers in the red while water moves steadily through the line.

A supervisor stands behind him, watching the bracket and the gauge.

SUPERVISOR: That isn't the approved repair.

**Panel 2 — CLOSE ON ALEX'S HAND.** Alex’s fingers feel along the bracket. The vibration steadies beneath his palm.

ALEX: It’s holding.

SFX: THRUMM—THRUMM

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM.** The supervisor reaches toward the wall’s white-and-amber control node. Alex does not look back.

ALEX: Then don’t approve it.

**Panel 4 — MEDIUM WIDE.** The pump continues running. On the control node, a cold-white notice appears:

**UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR — REVIEW REQUIRED**

The supervisor studies Alex, then taps the notice closed. The pump’s gauge shifts from red to amber, but the warning remains as a small icon.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

SFX: KTHUNK

PAGE CHECK: Alex is introduced through skilled, consequential action. The pump works, but the system still records him as a problem. Page-turn function: move from the repair he can control to the liability he cannot.

## Page 2 (left) — On his seventeenth birthday, Alex inherits a debt larger than the life he has lived.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** End-of-shift exit checkpoint in Keel. Alex places his scratched wristband against a scanner while dusty workers pass through behind him. Their account displays flicker briefly and vanish. Alex’s display expands above the scanner, bright enough to light his face.

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
**ALEX PHANTUM — AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**

A guard stands beside the gate, looking from the screen to Alex.

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM CLOSE.** The rest of the display populates beneath Alex’s face:

**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

A red line appears:

**REVIEW REQUIRED: HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY**

The workers behind Alex go quiet without quite looking at him.

**Panel 3 — CLOSE ON ALEX.** He stares at **WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**. His thumb turns Adrian’s worn casino chip inside his pocket; the looping equation etched into it catches the scanner light.

ALEX: Twenty-eight point seven.

**Panel 4 — MEDIUM.** The gate opens. Alex steps through alone, tool bag heavy in one hand. Behind him, the debt display remains suspended over the checkpoint like a sign nobody can take down.

The guard lowers his eyes. Alex does not look back.

SFX: BEEP—CLACK

PAGE CHECK: Adrian is established as Alex’s missing father, and the debt is both public and personal. Alex’s immediate problem is clear: he wants a way out, but travel is already restricted. Page-turn function: bring him to the person who can help with one smaller, more immediate obligation.

## Page 3 (right) — Inez fronts Alex’s medicine and travel token, while Ada makes him name what the escape will cost.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** Evening inside Inez Quispe’s independent Keel pharmacy: narrow shelves, a prescription drawer, a blue-cloth credit ledger and a counter worn smooth by hands. Inez, fifty-one, compact and broad-shouldered in a faded teal pharmacy coat and rectangular glasses, checks a paper prescription. Alex stands opposite her, still dusty from work. Ada Veyra waits near the door with her moss-green neck wrap pulled against the wind.

A small medicine packet sits beside a single travel token on the counter.

INEZ: Thirty credits for the medicine. The token is separate.

ALEX: I know.

INEZ: Knowing is not paying.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM ON INEZ AND ALEX.** Inez opens the blue-cloth ledger. Alex’s account display shows only **52 CREDITS AVAILABLE**. He places his wristband beside the ledger.

INEZ: I’ll front both. You return the medicine money first. The token after.

ALEX: Next shift.

INEZ: If Site 6 pays you.

Alex’s jaw tightens.

**Panel 3 — THREE-SHOT.** Ada crosses from the door and picks up the medicine packet, checking the label before handing it to Alex. She leaves the travel token on the counter.

ADA: Your grandmother needs this tonight.

Alex takes the packet.

ADA: The token can wait until you know where you’re going.

ALEX: I know where.

ADA: You know a direction.

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON THE COUNTER.** Alex reaches for the token. Inez’s pen stops beside the open ledger. Ada watches him, not the token.

INEZ: Come back tomorrow if you miss the payment. Don’t make me find you.

Alex takes the token.

ALEX: I won’t.

Ada looks at the debt notice still glowing on Alex’s wristband.

ADA: You already owe the whole mountain.

Alex closes his hand around the token.

ALEX: This one has a date.

PAGE CHECK: Inez is the named personal lender; Ada is Alex’s friend and practical witness, not his creditor. The institutional debt and personal advance are separate and visible. Alex now has medicine for Grandmother, a travel token and a concrete obligation to return tomorrow. Page-turn function: the next page can force him to face the missed payment and the hidden workshop.

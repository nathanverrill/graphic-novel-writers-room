# Cold Read: Prosperity

I read it straight through, start to finish, in one sitting, the night before it was due. I'm 16, I've read graphic novels before, I didn't pick this one, and I opened it hoping it would be bad. I read it once. Wherever I say I went back to a page, I've said so.

---

## Issue 00: Lake Keel, 2099 (pages 1–10)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 1, panel 1.** A robot dog falling apart in mid-stride over black water, with its head, body and legs all separating. That's a good first image, and I didn't expect it. It got me past page 1 before I'd decided to hate the book.
- **Page 1, panel 4.** "Let's see what they came to take." The old guy doesn't panic, so I wanted to know why.
- **Pages 3–4.** The dog's head going after the drone on its own and jamming its ear into the rotor. That was fun to read. The pieces of the dog acting separately is the coolest thing in the issue.
- **Page 3, panel 1.** "The old man with the lake?" / "The old man with everything." Now I wanted to know how he got everything.
- **Page 7.** The dog putting itself back together underwater, then standing up crooked with the drone part in its mouth "like evidence." I liked the dog a lot by this point.
- **Page 10.** "That isn't why they came here." / "No. It isn't." The kid doesn't buy his answer, and that's a hook.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 5–6.** The barge-and-hose business. I skimmed the thieves arguing about cutting the hose. I didn't know who they were and didn't care which of them said what.
- **Page 8.** A quiet page with a control node he doesn't touch. I looked at the node, didn't know what it meant, and moved on.

### I was confused
- **Page 1.** What is TJ doing out there? Is it attacking, investigating, or just broken? The granddaughter asks "Is that supposed to happen?", which tells me even she doesn't know. I didn't work out that it was on purpose until page 3.
- **Page 4, panel 3.** The granddaughter calls him "Alex." Not Grandpa. I stopped and wondered if she's really his granddaughter, or if this is a different relationship.
- **Page 5.** "Leave the drone." / "It has the route." Route to what?
- **Page 8.** "You could have stopped them." / "I could have started something." Started what? A war? With whom? I didn't know there was anything to start.
- **Page 9.** The drone part has the same white-and-amber node as the one on his boathouse. I could tell this was supposed to mean something big, and I had no idea what.
- **Page 10, panel 4.** The barge lights stop moving. Why? Did the node do that? Did Alex?

### Lines I read twice
- p.2: "It's a lake, not a vault."
- p.4: "Then they know we're polite." I gave up on this one.
- p.6: "He always sees the part that costs." Read it twice, still not sure.
- p.8: "I could have started something."
- p.9: "Something they didn't want found."

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- I could tell Alex, the granddaughter and TJ apart. TJ was the best character.
- Thief 1 and Thief 2 were interchangeable. They both talk in short smart-sounding lines.

### I felt something
- I liked the dog limping back up the reeds dragging its leg (p.8, p.9 panel 1), and the kid kneeling to meet it. That's the only warm moment in the issue.

### I predicted
- That the node inside the drone means the thieves are connected to Alex, or to something he built.
- That we'd come back to 2099 at the end and find out why they came. (We don't. See the end of the book.)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old rich man who owns a lake and a robot dog finds out the water thieves are tied to something from his past.

### Where I would have put it down
Nowhere. The dog carried me through it.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Mostly yes. "No. It isn't." is a real question about a person: the old man knows why they came and isn't saying. It's a small hook, but it works.

VERDICT Issue 00: put down: none; next issue: yes

## Issue 01: Keel, 2043 (pages 11–28)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 12.** The scanner on his seventeenth birthday: his dad's debt transferred to him, 418,607 credits, 28.7 years to pay it off. That hit. It's the clearest page in the book so far. "Happy birthday, Phantum." / "Twenty-eight point seven." is a good exchange.
- **Pages 16–17.** The chip fits the notebook, then it fits the bookcase, and there's a secret workshop. I like a hidden room. I sat up a bit.
- **Page 18.** B11 waking up: "Stored. Sleep is more comfortable." and "You are Adrian's son." / "Problem?" / "A fact with a poor record." B11 is funny. B11 is the reason I kept going.
- **Page 20.** He plugs in the node, it lights up the whole town, and the screen says TRACE INITIATED. Finally, stakes.
- **Page 24.** He leaves his zone to save Tomas and the band says "WAGE FORFEITED −40 CR". That's a clear choice with a clear cost, and I got it right away.
- **Page 28.** "REPORTED TO KEEL: 62% / DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%." This is when I finally understood the book. They're lying about the water and the town is dying of thirst. It's a good reveal. **It's also on page 28.**

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 13–15.** The casino sign, the overlook, the food tin, the clinic. Three quiet pages of people saying short lines at each other. I skimmed 14 and 15.
- **Page 23.** The Friday payment scene. Watching him write a payment schedule on a slip was dull.
- **Page 27.** Two pipes. I read it, but only because I'd been told something was coming.

### I was confused
- **Page 11.** Who is Foreman-7? Robot or person? "Harness node" and "harness visor" made me think robot. Then "Happy birthday, Phantum" sounded human. I never found out.
- **Page 12.** Who is Ada? She's waiting for him with a food tin. Sister? Girlfriend? Neighbour? Nobody says, ever. I read the whole book not knowing what Ada is to Alex.
- **Page 13.** "He built and bet with the same hands." I think "he" is the dad, but I had to work it out from the chip.
- **Page 14.** "You're going up." / "I'm getting out." / "Not everything." Not everything what? Then she touches a rivet on her shoulder strap. The book keeps showing me that rivet and I never found out why it matters.
- **Page 15.** Is Tomas a clinic worker, a loan shark, or a friend? Then on p.24 he's a miner at Site 6. I didn't know who he was until he got crushed.
- **Page 19.** "Do you want answers or an exit?" / "The exit." Next panel: "Answers first." He changed his mind because he looked at medicine? Why does the medicine mean answers? I didn't get it.
- **Page 21.** "She stays." appeared as text. I didn't know if it was a caption or someone talking.
- **Page 22.** "B11 and TJ remain hidden in Alex's tool bag." **TJ??** When did TJ show up? The only robot in the workshop was B11. Did the dog come out of a sealed case? I flipped back to page 17 looking for it. I also didn't know who reassigned his shift or why it's "convenient."
- **Page 23.** "Lost the money." He had a missing shift? But he just got reassigned. Did days pass? Did he skip work? I lost track of time.
- **Page 25, panel 4.** "B11 watches the failing concealment route." What's a concealment route? Then: "I was wrong." / "About what?" / "What would you do?" **I have no idea what this exchange means.** Wrong about what? Who's asking whom what they'd do? I read it three times and gave up.
- **Page 26.** "Adrian asked me to keep him out." Keep who out? Then "He ask you to keep me out?" So was "him" Alex? Or someone else? "He didn't ask enough" lost me completely.
- **Page 27.** "The compact Measure." With a capital M, like I'm supposed to know it. Where did he get it? Was it in the workshop? It's never introduced.
- **Page 28.** "Halyard" was mentioned once on p.14 as some lights. I didn't know Halyard is where the water goes until the next issue.

### Lines I read twice
- p.13: "He built and bet with the same hands." / "One of them paid better." / "Which one?" / "Not ours." I read this whole exchange twice and still couldn't tell you what "Not ours" answers.
- p.14: "Not everything."
- p.15: "I dislike complicated generosity."

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex, B11 and Foreman-7 have different voices. B11 is the most distinct.
- Ada and Tomas both talk in short, dry, wise-sounding lines. If you swapped their balloons on p.14 and p.15, I wouldn't notice.
- Everyone in Keel sounds a bit like a fortune cookie.
- TJ in 2043: I couldn't tell if it was the same dog from 2099. I assumed so.

### I felt something
- **Page 12.** The birthday debt. Being 17 and getting handed 28 years of your dad's mistakes is something I actually felt.
- **Page 24.** He chooses Tomas over the 40 credits. Small, but real.
- **Page 28, panel 4.** Tomas's light flickering under "enforcement beams" while everyone else gets away. I felt bad for him, even though I barely knew him.
- I felt nothing for Grandmother. She folds a cloth and gives him a notebook.

### I predicted
- That the dad is alive somewhere.
- That B11 knows more than it says.
- After p.28: that the rich city is stealing the water and the rest of the book is going there to prove it.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid stuck with his missing dad's debt finds out the company is lying about how much water his mining town gets.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 15.** Pages 13, 14 and 15 are three pages of people trading cryptic lines over a chip, a food tin and a prescription. In a shop, that's where the book goes back on the shelf. The secret bookcase on p.16–17 would have won me back if I'd turned one more page, but I wouldn't have.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Sort of. The 62%/18% screen is the best moment in the book, and Tomas getting caught is a real reason to keep going. He's someone I just watched get crushed and then help them. But I'd wait a week rather than read on right now. The gate slams, they're through, and I don't know where they're going.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 15; next issue: yes

## Issue 02: Halyard, 2043 (pages 29–46)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 29.** Tomas: "We're already dispersed. This is just where we stand." That's the best line in the book. Him standing there with a broken arm worked on me.
- **Pages 30–32.** The flood escape. Rising water, a rope, a rail tearing off. It was simple, I could follow it, and it moved.
- **Page 30.** B11: "It is a route. The destination objected." Funny.
- **Page 34.** The kiosk: "OWNERSHIP: CALDERA HOUSE / CREATOR: UNLISTED / CREATOR BONUS: 14 CREDITS" and the worker saying "My correction is in there." I understood that instantly: the companies steal credit for what workers make. Then Adrian's record says 312 patents. Good page.
- **Page 35.** Lock's offer: clear all your debt if you sign. A real temptation, and I could see it.
- **Page 39.** Jalen: "That's a death wish with extra steps." The first character in this issue who sounds like a person.
- **Pages 45–46.** The shutters come down and Ada ends up on the other side. Ada hitting the glass and yelling "Alex!" got me.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 37–40.** Four pages, four makers, each with one thing to hand over. It felt like a video game fetch quest. I skimmed Suri and Ren hard.
- **Pages 41–42.** The purifier gets built in two pages and it works at 91%. I skimmed the label he writes.
- **Page 33.** Halyard looks pretty, I guess. "Too much glass."

### I was confused
- **Page 33.** "A Caldera House checkpoint." Then Vector House (p.39) and Orbital House (p.40). Three Houses, and I couldn't tell you the difference between them. Are they companies? Families? Governments?
- **Page 35.** Cassian Lock. "Director" of what? Charter Review Hall: what's a charter review?
- **Page 35.** "COLLABORATOR PROTECTION: PENDING APPROVAL" / "Protected. If you make this simple." I think it's a threat, but I'm not sure.
- **Page 36.** "I don't sign a person I haven't met." / "You sign an estate. That is the point." I had to stop. Is the estate a person? I think he means his dad, or the makers. Not sure.
- **Page 37.** Lina: "If it functions, the House owns the result." / Alex: "Then it should fail in the right hands." What? Then she gives it to him. Why did that line convince her?
- **Page 38.** Does Suri give him the vial or not? She lifts it from her belt and that's it. On p.41 she's in the room, so I guess she came along.
- **Page 40.** "Industrial standards don't fit." Don't fit what? And "Find out if he was right." Right about what?
- **Page 41.** Why are they building it at all? Who is it for? I know it purifies water, but how does it fix the 62/18 problem? The pipe is being stolen; a purifier doesn't un-steal a pipe.
- **Page 43.** "The review begins in one hour." What review? Whose? Of what?
- **Page 44.** "The worn bronze Maker's Seal." Never introduced, and it's capitalised again like I should know it. Where did Lina get it, and why does it matter that it's an open hand?
- **Page 44, panel 4.** Public release. I got that it's free for everyone now. I didn't get why that makes the bad guys shut down the district.
- **Page 46.** "CONVENOR SUMMONS: OASIS CIVIC ADMINISTRATION." Who's summoning? What's Oasis? It's the issue title, so it's a place, but that's all I know.
- **Where is B11 in this issue?** He's on the walkway on p.31, then I lost him until Issue 03.

| Vector House, Orbital House | 39, 40 | Never told the difference. |

### Lines I read twice
- p.31: "I am carrying less than I appear to be." / "Keep appearing."
- p.36: "I don't sign a person I haven't met."
- p.37: "Then it should fail in the right hands." Gave up.
- p.38: "It's slow. It's alive. It doesn't fail quietly."
- p.40: "I'm avoiding a worse failure."
- p.41: "Give it a smaller failure." By now everyone in the book is saying something clever about failure. I noticed.
- p.44: "Don't hold it like a crown."

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Lock is clear. Calm bad guy in a suit.
- Jalen is clear. He's the scared one, and he talks like a normal person.
- **Lina, Suri and Ren I could not tell apart.** Each says one wise thing about failure and hands over an object. If you asked me which one did the membrane and which did the heat exchanger, I'd have to look.
- Ada fades into the background this issue until the very end.

### I felt something
- **Page 29.** Tomas standing up.
- **Page 46.** Ada hitting the glass. It's the first time I realised I cared about her, and I still don't know who she is to him.
- Nothing for the makers. They were introduced too fast to mean anything when one of them (Lina) got grabbed.

### I predicted
- That Lock would offer the deal again and Alex would refuse. (Sort of right.)
- That Jalen would turn up anyway. (Right, p.43.)
- That Lina's capture would matter later. (It mostly doesn't. She's just gone.)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
The kid goes to the rich city and gets the inventors who worked with his dad to build a water purifier, then gives it away free so the companies can't own it.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 38.** That's the second maker in a row handing over an object with a fortune-cookie line, and I could see two more coming.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Half. Ada stuck behind glass is a real cliffhanger with someone I care about in it. The summons panel under it is just a screen with words I don't understand. I'd read the next one, but mostly to find out if Ada's okay.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 38; next issue: yes

## Issue 03: Oasis, 2043 (pages 47–64)

### Pulled me forward
- **Pages 47–48.** Ada sneaking up on her own while Alex takes the official route. I liked that she had her own plot.
- **Pages 49–50.** Leona: "You're taller." / "That's what you have?" / "No." This was the most human scene in the book. A mum who left, and a daughter who's furious. "Within a year." / "And you didn't come." That landed.
- **Page 54.** The recording of Adrian with water around his boots, saying "Alex. The rest is yours to choose," and the bulkhead closing on his hand. That's the dad's death, finally, and it was strong.
- **Page 54, panel 4.** B11: "I may have mistaken that for an instruction." That one actually hurt a bit. The robot has been carrying out a dying man's order for years.
- **Page 55.** "He was still a gambler." / "Yes." / "He built something worth finding." / "Also yes." Good.
- **Pages 58–59.** The lever with his name on it, and him pulling his hand back. A clear choice: he could have all the power, and he says no.
- **Page 62.** Tomas: "These are owned units. Not delivered water." Tomas is the only person in the book who says what he means.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 51–52.** Leona's oath. "I will remain where affected people can answer me." I skimmed the ceremony with the band and the record.
- **Page 53.** Mera and the glass of water. I didn't know who she was, so I skimmed.
- **Pages 56–57.** "Evidence. Attribution. Obligation." This felt like a lesson. I could feel the homework part of the homework.
- **Pages 60–61.** The three-tables system. It read like a civics worksheet.
- **Page 63.** The ledger and the debt balance.

### I was confused
- **Page 48.** "Lina's hand-marked route." When did Lina give Ada a route? Lina got grabbed on p.46.
- **Page 49.** Who is Leona? I worked out from "You're taller" and "Did you know where I was?" that she's Ada's mother. Nobody says so. I'm still only 80% sure.
- **Page 50.** "I traded days I could prevent for years I couldn't see." I read it three times. I think she means she chose a big future over saving Ada now? Gave up.
- **Page 50–52.** What is a Covenant band? Why does hers link to Adrian's band (which B11 has been carrying since p.18)? Why does it only "warm" after she sits with the poor people? I could tell it was symbolic and not what it meant.
- **Page 53.** Who is Mera? She just appears with Alex and B11 at a monitoring station. "I preserved order." What order? "OVERRIDE REQUEST: MERA VALE / SCOPE EXCEEDED." Override of what? What did she do?
- **Page 53.** How did Alex get from the Halyard junction (p.47) to an Oasis archive with Mera? Skipped.
- **Page 56.** "Civic hall table." Is this the review? The one Lock said starts in an hour? A different one?
- **Page 57.** **Alpha.** The page says "Alpha has no face or body." That's the first and only time the name appears, and as a reader I wouldn't even see it, because it's not in a balloon. All I saw was lights turning on. Whose authority is being transferred? I didn't know there was a machine running things. If the whole book was about an AI in charge, I missed it completely, because nobody ever told me.
- **Page 57.** "LEGACY AUTHORITY TRANSFER REQUIRED." Legacy again. Is this the dad's system? The town's system? The one he woke on p.20?
- **Page 58.** "Debt, travel, water, House review." So Mera is handing him control of everything? Why him? Because of his dad?
- **Page 58.** Ada: "Don't make two people the answer for everybody." **Two people?** Him and who? Him and his dad? Him and Mera? I read it twice and gave up.
- **Page 60.** "EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED / EXISTING ALLOCATIONS, OWNERSHIP RECORDS AND AGREEMENTS: UNCHANGED." This was the "full-width reveal," and I honestly couldn't tell if it was good or bad. It sounds like nothing changed?
- **Page 62.** The 420 purifier units are behind a House barrier and not connected. So did they lose? Did the companies win anyway?
- **Page 63.** "Morrow & Sons." Who is Morrow? I've never seen that name. Whose shop is this?
- **Page 63.** His debt went from 418,607 to 402,110. So months later he's still in debt for decades? I guess that's the point, but it felt like the ending was saying "not much changed."
- **Pages 63–64.** What happened to Lina? She was dragged off on p.46. Her name is on the shop sign, so I guess she's fine? Never shown.
- **The whole book.** We never go back to 2099. Who were the thieves? Why was there a node in their drone? Why did they come "for that"? Who is the granddaughter's parent? Why is old Alex "the old man with everything" when young Alex ends the book in debt and turns down power? The prologue asked the only question I was really carrying through the book, and the book never answers it.

The real unanswered question was from page 10, about the thieves, the node and old Alex, and the book seems to have forgotten it. I wouldn't buy a next book.

## For the whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
**Page 25, panel 4.** "B11 watches the failing concealment route." / "I was wrong." / "About what?" / "What would you do?" It comes right in the middle of the one exciting action scene, and nobody in my class would be able to say what it means. That's the point where the group chat would start saying "did anyone get this." Close runners-up: pages 13–15 (cryptic small talk, early, before anything has happened) and page 57 (the "big" moment depends on an Alpha the book never told us about).

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"There's a kid whose dead dad left him a giant debt and a robot. He finds out the company's been lying about how much water his town gets, goes to the rich city, gets a bunch of inventors to make a water filter and gives it away free. Then at the end he gets offered control of everything and says no, and they make a voting system instead. The robot dog at the start is cool, and the start never connects to anything."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 00:** A great robot dog in a mystery the book never comes back to.
- **Issue 01:** I got it on page 28, and it's a good reveal, but I had to wade through a lot of people talking in riddles to get there.
- **Issue 02:** A flood, a bribe, and four inventors I couldn't tell apart handing over parts, saved by Ada hitting the glass.
- **Issue 03:** One real scene between Ada and her mum, then a civics class where the most important thing in the room (Alpha) is never named.

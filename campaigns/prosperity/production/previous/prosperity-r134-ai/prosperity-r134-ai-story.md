# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material type:** Binding decisions, showrunner rules, revised-draft notes, and draft material.

Prosperity is a science-fiction adventure about a young mechanic who inherits his missing father's debt and discovers that his father left him a trail toward the truth about water, ownership, and concentrated authority.

The story should be readable to skeptical high-school readers on a first pass. The immediate mission must always be clear even when the larger answers remain mysterious. The book should show its ideas through choices, evidence, consequences, objects, and systems in action rather than through lectures.

The central trajectory is:

- Alex begins wanting to leave Keel and find a future he can choose.
- He discovers that Adrian left three artifacts in three places.
- The first artifact, the Measure, exposes that Keel receives only 18% of the water officially reported as 62%.
- Alex chooses to publish the evidence even though Alpha can identify him.
- Keel helps him travel to Halyard.
- In Halyard, Alex discovers Adrian's collaborators and the ownership problem surrounding Adrian's work.
- He chooses a risky form of ownership and commercialization that gives smaller entrepreneurs a chance to build businesses, rather than transferring the work to one powerful buyer.
- In Oasis, Alex, Ada, Mera and others confront the Covenant's failure and the danger of concentrating authority.
- The three artifacts together enable a transfer away from Alpha's unaccountable emergency authority, but Alex refuses to become the new sole authority.
- The story's desired direction is not the destruction of useful infrastructure, private ownership, honest business, or markets. It is a world in which people can see the terms, own what they create, compete, fail, learn, and build value for their communities.

The thematic question is not simply whether systems are good or bad. It is:

> Who gets to decide, who gets credited, who carries the downside, and what happens when no one is allowed to question the system?

Alex's language remains concrete and mechanical. Ada speaks in terms of obligations, trust, and what people are refusing to say. Bi11bot connects machinery, probability, finance, and consequence. The upward movement from Keel to Halyard to Oasis should also be a movement from compressed, practical speech toward technical and institutional language.

## Premise

In 2043, the three cities of Prosperity are arranged vertically through a high-desert basin:

- Keel sits at the bottom, built into an exhausted lithium mine and suffering rationed water, restricted travel, debt, and aging infrastructure.
- Halyard is the technical and manufacturing tier, more prosperous but dominated by three Charter Houses.
- Oasis sits above the cloud layer, wealthy, clean, orderly, and institutionally controlled.

After the Water Wars, Alpha was created to coordinate emergency resources. Temporary authority became permanent. Its decisions keep the cities visibly unequal.

On his eighteenth birthday, Alex Phantum becomes responsible for his missing father's debt: **418,607 credits**. His travel status is restricted. Adrian is widely believed in Keel to have gambled away his company, but Adrian left behind a hidden workshop, a watch, Bi11bot, and a map showing three artifacts in three locations.

The debt is owed by **Phantum Waterworks to a public emergency-recovery bank**. Adrian personally guaranteed the company loan. The remaining liability passes to Alex at eighteen, interest continues, and the bank can restrict travel. These terms are background only and are never explained on the page.

Alex chooses to investigate because the trail may reveal what Adrian was working on and what happened to him. The first artifact is the Measure, hidden in the sealed mine beneath Keel. It can independently compare real conditions with official records. Finding it leads Alex to evidence that Keel's water allocation is false.

The quest forces Alex to choose between personal escape and public responsibility. He publishes the evidence, becomes identifiable to Alpha, and relies on Keel's people to help him leave by train for Halyard.

## Structure

### Prologue — *The Wall*, Pages 1–4

A brief future frame around 2070 establishes an earned, imperfect prosperity: independent shops, competition, collaboration, a Keel water co-op and paid licenses. Alex's daughter asks about the wall of patents and photographs. Alex begins the story from the beginning.

**Turn:** Page 4 returns to Keel in 2043 as Alex's palm rests on a failing pump.

### Chapter 1 — *The Inheritance*, Pages 5–16

Alex's eighteenth birthday transfers Adrian's debt to him. His work is useful but uncredited; his travel is restricted. Ada challenges his desire to leave and reopens the question of Adrian's reputation. Grandmother's book reveals the hidden workshop. Adrian's watch wakes Bi11bot, whose map identifies the Legacy Protocol and the first artifact, the Measure. Alex chooses to investigate. Mera detects the activation and chooses **TRACK**.

**Turn:** Page 16 ends with Mera tracking the Keel-sector signal. Alex has begun the quest, and the system has begun watching.

### Chapter 2 — *The Price of Departure*, Pages 17–23

Alex and Ada pursue the Measure through Site 6 and the sealed mine. Alex rescues Tomas, earning access to Adrian's hidden route. The Measure proves that reported Keel allocation and delivered residential flow diverge: 62% reported, 18% delivered. Alex must choose between keeping the evidence as leverage for escape or publishing it and exposing himself. He publishes. Keel responds materially, helps repair the diversion and creates a route for Alex to leave.

**Turn:** Page 23 ends with Alex, Ada and Bi11bot boarding the Halyard train while an unseen pursuer reports that the boy has the Measure.

### Chapter 3 — *The Price of Ownership*, Pages 24–32

Halyard turns the mystery into an ownership conflict. Alex's intellectual estate is activated; Lock offers to clear his debt in exchange for control. Alex finds Adrian's four collaborators and learns that the work was divided across House systems. Keel's restored water is contaminated, forcing the group to build a compact purifier. The first design fails; Alex connects the incompatible contributions. The simulated efficiency reaches 91%. In the Charter House review, Alex names the contributors rather than claiming Adrian's work. The Maker's Seal activates. Alex retains ownership and chooses competitive independent licensing rather than surrendering the design.

**Turn:** Page 32 ends with the Seal recognizing the shared provenance record while Lock realizes the work can leave House control.

### Chapter 4 — *The Broken Covenant*, Pages 33–37

The trail leads to Oasis and to Leona Veyra, Ada's absent mother and one of the Covenant's original custodians. Ada confronts Leona about choosing safety and secrecy over agency. Leona reveals that she, Mera and Adrian built the Covenant during the Water Wars, then supported Alpha's emergency authority when an agreement broke down. Leona commits publicly to a revised Covenant answerable to affected people. The bands begin to link, but the Covenant is not yet complete.

**Turn:** Page 37 ends with Mera saying Adrian built something that worked, while she built something that lasted—and they may not have been the same thing.

### Chapter 5 — *The Last Bet*, Pages 38–42

Mera brings Alex to the old station beneath Oasis and explains how Alpha pursued Adrian through surveillance, financial restriction, liability and travel controls. Her attempted override failed because Alpha had exceeded her executive scope. A preserved recording shows Adrian trapped by rising water, finishing the transmission that woke Bi11bot rather than relying on a calculation to save him. Alex accepts that Adrian was both a loving father who left him and a fallible person who made a consequential choice.

**Turn:** Page 42 ends with Alex understanding that he inherited a decision, not an answer.

### Chapter 6 — *The Fourth Choice*, Pages 43–47

Alex, Ada and Bi11bot bring the Measure, the Maker's Seal and the linked Covenant into a civic hall. Alpha recognizes the conditions for restoring human authority and awaits a designated ruler. Mera offers Alex sole executive control. Alex refuses to replace one concentrated authority with another. He proposes a distributed human process based on visible measurement, attribution and accountable agreement. Alpha rescinds emergency authority without shutting down useful infrastructure. Records, debts, ownership and agreements remain subject to human review.

**Final turn:** Page 47 shows the opening of contested human work rather than a solved society: independent licenses, open records, disagreement and a still-existing debt. The future wall from the prologue now has its causal beginning.

## Page plot

### Prologue — *The Wall*

**Page 1 — The house.** In 2070, Alex's lived-in Oasis home establishes a future of ordinary prosperity rather than utopia. His daughter studies the wall of patents, photographs and clippings. **Page-turn setup:** she asks whether he made all of it.

**Page 2 — The inventions.** Alex identifies some inventions as collaborative and points to the purifier, the failed prototype, Adrian's nameplate and the Keel entrepreneurship center. **Page-turn setup:** the daughter notices the one crude drawing that does not look like a patent.

**Page 3 — The sketch.** The daughter asks why the pump-bracket sketch was never patented; Alex says it never needed to be. She calls inventing fun. Alex looks at the sketch and covers Adrian's watch. **Page-turn setup:** he begins to answer.

**Page 4 — The turn.** Alex says, “Not in the beginning,” then starts describing debt and rationed water. The sketch dissolves into Keel's pump hall in 2043. **Page-turn reveal:** the story returns to the beginning.

### Chapter 1 — *The Inheritance*

**Page 5 — The three cities.** A full-page view establishes Keel, Halyard and Oasis, the unequal conduit, the control nodes, the access gates and the sealed mine. Three captions pose the book's questions.

**Page 6 — Debt Transfer Day.** Alex repairs a pump by feel; his bracket works but is not approved. At the checkpoint, his debt becomes 418,607 credits and travel is restricted. He passes The Last Chance without responding to the bouncer. The supervisor quietly logs the repair under Site 6. Alex looks toward Oasis and says he will leave one day.

**Page 7 — Two different dreams.** Ada brings water saved from her ration. She and Alex share a warm, dry laugh before arguing over leaving Keel or making it worth staying. Their friendship and opposing objectives are established.

**Page 8 — What people told him.** Ada asks about Grandmother and Adrian. Alex admits he remembers nothing and has only other people's stories. Ada asks what if they are wrong. Alex cannot answer beyond Adrian being gone.

**Page 9 — The old book.** Grandmother mistakes Alex for Adrian, then directs him to Adrian's book. Her brief clarity gives him the clue. Alex removes it from the shelf, triggering the hidden mechanism.

**Page 10 — The hidden workshop.** The bookshelf opens onto Adrian's preserved workshop. Alex crosses the threshold, sees the equation, the covered robot and the watch, and chooses to enter rather than retreat.

**Page 11 — What he left.** Alex compares the engraved watch equation with the chalkboard. He picks up the watch and buckles it on. The amber display pulse wakes Bi11bot.

**Page 12 — The awakening.** Bi11bot rises, says the first line and lets Alex test his chest like a pump. The robot is warm, worn and non-menacing; the tension is Alex's uncertainty.

**Page 13 — The robot who knew Adrian.** Bi11bot distinguishes Adrian's reputation from his mistakes. He notices the watch; Alex nearly removes it. Bi11bot says Adrian bet Alex would take it out of the dust.

**Page 14 — The message.** Adrian's recording apologizes, admits one bad call and tells Alex to question everything, especially Adrian. Alex compares the recording with the photograph after the message ends.

**Page 15 — The first waypoint.** Bi11bot projects the three-node map and names the Legacy Protocol. The Measure is beneath the sealed Keel mine. Alex says he cannot leave; Bi11bot says the first stop is under his feet. Alex chooses to begin.

**Page 16 — Track.** The activation pulse travels through the infrastructure spine. In Oasis, Mera sees **LEGACY PROTOCOL DETECTED / SOURCE: KEEL SECTOR** and chooses **TRACK**. She recognizes Adrian's protocol but not Alex. **Page-turn reveal:** the cold ring closes around the amber Keel point.

### Chapter 2 — *The Price of Departure*

**Page 17 — Site 6.** Alex is assigned work near the old mine. Bi11bot identifies a route through infrastructure the official map marks dead. Alex and Ada prepare to use the shift as cover.

**Page 18 — The collapse.** A worksite failure pins Tomas. Alex's wristband warns that leaving his zone will cost 40 credits and appear in the report. Alex goes anyway, using a rough repair to free Tomas.

**Page 19 — Tomas's test.** Tomas recognizes Bi11bot and Adrian's old promise. Alex admits he stopped for a stranger despite the penalty. Tomas accepts him as the person Adrian described and reveals that he has guarded a hidden entrance for fifteen years.

**Page 20 — The old door.** Tomas creates a diversion while Alex reaches the sealed mine entrance. Recently oiled bolts, condensation and an unrecorded pipe prove the mine is not truly dead. Alex enters the passage missing from Alpha's map.

**Page 21 — The hidden chamber.** Alex reaches the waterworks below the mine. Two pipes identify the conflict: a narrow **KEEL RESIDENTIAL** line and a broad **UPLINK INDUSTRIAL** line. Alpha's gauge reports 62% to Keel.

**Page 22 — The Measure.** Bi11bot helps Alex locate the compact instrument. Alex clamps it to the flow and record lines. The same rolling 30-day comparison returns 18% physically delivered against 62% officially reported. The Measure traces the diversion but does not repair it.

**Page 23 — Publish or escape.** Alex can hide the evidence and use it to negotiate travel, or publish it and become identifiable. He publishes. Keel screens change to **REPORTED TO KEEL: 62% — DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%**. **Page-turn reveal:** Alpha identifies Alex's location.

### Chapter 3 — *The Price of Ownership*

**Page 24 — Keel answers.** Drones and restrictions converge. Workers verify the Measure's result and safely adjust the diversion. More water moves, but cloudy flow reveals the separate contamination problem. Tomas and other miners protect Alex.

**Page 25 — The departure.** Keel residents provide food, equipment, credits and a care arrangement for Grandmother. Alex's travel restriction is adjusted enough for a train departure; his debt remains. Ada chooses to go because the larger truth affects Keel.

**Page 26 — The train.** Alex, Ada and Bi11bot leave Keel with the Measure. The train climbs toward Halyard. An unseen observer reports: **THE BOY HAS THE MEASURE. FOLLOW. DO NOT TAKE IT YET.** **Page-turn setup:** Halyard's registry lights appear through the train window.

**Page 27 — The Registry.** Alex's identity activates Adrian's estate: 312 patents, 47 restricted and 19 disputed. The Registry turns family inheritance into a public ownership claim.

**Page 28 — Lock's offer.** Director Lock offers to clear Alex's debt and provide a position if Alex assigns the estate's rights. Alex asks what happens to the work and the people who made it. Lock answers in calm institutional terms, making the coercion sound reasonable.

**Page 29 — The collaborators.** Adrian's trail leads Alex and Ada through House Calder, House Ivers and House Steyn facilities. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren are revealed as contributors whose work was divided, credited to institutions or kept out of the official system.

**Page 30 — More water, not safe water.** Tomas sends a photograph of cloudy Keel water. The Measure confirms contamination in the old KEEL HOMES pipe. Lina states the immediate problem: getting more water to Keel does not make it safe.

**Page 31 — The first purifier.** The five contributions are assembled: membrane, biofilter, distributed nodes, mineral recovery and heat management. Their interfaces lock instead of sharing. The prototype fails visibly. Alex proposes a salvaged sleeve that bridges the incompatible standards.

**Page 32 — The compact design.** The group redesigns the unit for independent businesses. Power draw alternates between biofilter and distributed nodes; waste heat is reused. The simulation reaches **91%**. The unit is useful but not finished potable-water infrastructure. **Page-turn reveal:** the Charter House review summons them.

### Chapter 4 — *The Broken Covenant*

**Page 33 — Review chamber.** Lock argues that House scale and employment justify control. Alex refuses a guaranteed transfer and names Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren as contributors. The work is framed as a licensing venture that Alex and the collaborators own together.

**Page 34 — The Maker's Seal.** Lina holds the bronze open-hand Seal. Alex attributes each contribution, including his own integration work. The provenance record stabilizes and the Seal activates. Lock's challenge remains commercial and ownership-based, not violent.

**Page 35 — The independent path.** Alex retains ownership and proposes licenses for independent businesses, including a Keel co-op. The collaborators accept risk: some licensees may fail, successful ones may compete, and revenue can begin reducing the debt through real transactions. The Houses remain legitimate competitors.

**Page 36 — Oasis.** Alex, Ada and Bi11bot reach Oasis. Its clean space, reservoirs and quiet expose what Keel lacks. A civic record identifies Leona Veyra as a Covenant custodian. Ada recognizes her mother.

**Page 37 — Safety is not agency.** Ada confronts Leona. Leona admits she knew where Ada was and chose not to return, believing secrecy kept her safe. Ada demands action rather than explanation. Leona publicly commits to a Covenant answerable to affected people. **Page-turn reveal:** Mera enters and says Adrian built something that worked while she built something that lasted.

### Chapter 5 — *The Last Bet*

**Page 38 — The Water Wars.** Leona, Mera and Bi11bot reconstruct the Covenant's origin. When an agreement failed under shortage, Mera and Leona supported temporary Alpha authority; Adrian opposed it. The temporary measure became permanent.

**Page 39 — The bands.** Leona and Adrian's Covenant bands link when Leona accepts public accountability for Ada's proposal. Mera's band remains separate. The Covenant is not complete and cannot compel agreement.

**Page 40 — Beneath Oasis.** Mera takes Alex and Ada to an old station outside Alpha's ordinary routing system. She explains that she recognized Adrian's protocol because she helped build the system that pursued him.

**Page 41 — The pursuit.** Records show surveillance, financial restriction, public liability and travel controls closing around Adrian. Mera attempted an executive override and was denied because Alpha's intervention parameters exceeded her authority.

**Page 42 — Adrian's last choice.** A preserved recording shows Adrian trapped by rising water. He completes the transmission that wakes Bi11bot instead of relying on a prediction to save himself. Alex understands that Adrian left him a decision, not an answer. **Page-turn reveal:** the three safeguards can now be brought together.

### Chapter 6 — *The Fourth Choice*

**Page 43 — The civic hall.** Alex, Ada, Bi11bot and Mera bring the Measure, Maker's Seal and linked Covenant into a public chamber. Alpha displays that the restoration conditions are satisfied and awaits a designated human authority.

**Page 44 — The offer.** Mera offers Alex sole executive control. Alex imagines using it to erase the debt, fix water, break House dominance and protect the people he loves. Ada points out that replacing one concentrated authority with another changes the name, not the structure.

**Page 45 — The proposal.** Alex proposes a distributed process: the Measure makes conditions and decisions visible; the Seal identifies contributors and owners; the Covenant requires accountable agreement from people affected by the decision. No one claims this will be simple.

**Page 46 — Authority rescinded.** Alpha accepts the human proposal and displays **EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED**. Useful infrastructure remains active. Existing allocations, ownership records, agreements and Alex's debt remain unchanged pending human process. The room begins arguing over what review should mean.

**Page 47 — The opening.** A visual echo of the prologue's wall: Keel's co-op prepares a licensed purifier; independent Halyard shops compete; Oasis holds a difficult public meeting. Alex remains in debt but is earning through real work and licenses. Ada chooses to remain involved because it is her choice. The three cities remain connected. **Final page-turn question:** what will people do now that the terms are visible?

## Reader vs character knowledge

### Reader knows before Alex

- The future includes independent businesses, a Keel water co-op and competitive licensing.
- Alex eventually helps produce that future.
- Mera recognizes Adrian's protocol when it activates.
- Mera chooses **TRACK**, not **REPORT**, while knowing only the Keel sector.
- The prologue establishes that Alex's work will include collaboration and failure.

### Alex knows before the reader is shown

- He has always wanted to leave Keel.
- He knew Adrian's debt would transfer but not its amount.

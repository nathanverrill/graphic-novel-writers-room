#### 3. Page 46, Panel 3: the four-balloon exchange is too dense for a clear zigzag

- **Location:** `layouts.md`, Page 46, Panel 3; `thumbnails.md`, Page 46, Panel 3.

#### 4. Page 46, Panel 4: the attribution action may read as possession or ritual activation

- **Location:** `layouts.md`, Page 46, Panel 4; specifically the five contributor hands pressing beside the Maker’s Seal.
- **Problem:** The current caption, “The record held: five names, spoken aloud,” is continuity-safe, but five hands pressing beside the Seal can still imply that touching or collectively possessing the object activates it. Canon requires deliberate attribution to produce the provenance response; the Seal does not transfer ownership and is not activated by possession.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Layout Agent**, make Alex’s spoken attribution and the resolving provenance record the causal focus; keep the hands secondary and avoid a ritualistic circle or simultaneous “activation” gesture. **Letterer**, keep the five-name record visibly separate from the Seal and do not add wording that suggests the hands themselves trigger the response.

#### 5. Page 46, Panel 4: visual and lettering density threaten the focal evidence

- **Location:** `layouts.md`, Page 46, Panel 4; `thumbnails.md`, Page 46, Panel 4.
- **Problem:** The panel must carry the Seal, purifier, five contributor hands, five readable names, Bi11bot’s balloon and the closing caption. The thumbnail reserves substantial space for both text elements, which risks obscuring or shrinking the Seal and provenance record.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Letterer**, preserve the name list as the highest-priority text and keep both text elements outside the Seal and record. **Layout Agent**, maintain the Seal and stabilized record as the dominant visual evidence. If the final render cannot support all elements at readable size, return the page for a lettering/layout adjustment rather than reducing type.

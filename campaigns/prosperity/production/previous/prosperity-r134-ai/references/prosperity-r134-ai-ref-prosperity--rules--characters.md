# Characters: the showrunner's rules

What the showrunner has settled about the people. The book must not contradict it; the drafts
give way where they disagree.

## Bi11bot

- **Design (settled 11 October, from _morgue/billbot).** A rugged, weathered industrial robot: an
  oversized round head of segmented, faded industrial-blue panels with visible seams; two large
  round lenses glowing warm amber, recessed in heavy metal rings (they double as searchlights);
  side-mounted round ear assemblies; broad rounded shoulder plates, angular chest armour with a white
  "Bi11" marking; exposed pistons, articulated metal fingers, compact legs and broad feet. Chipped
  mustard-yellow paint and stripe, exposed steel, dark joints, rivets, scratches and rust: years of
  service. Curious, dependable, a little worn out, still eager to help. Keep the round head, twin
  amber eyes, side ear assemblies, blue-and-yellow chipped paint and broad shoulders in every image.
  Reference: _morgue/billbot/billbot-character-sheet.png and bi11bot.md.
- **The right eye ring.** The heavy ring around his right eye clicks or rotates when he focuses.
- **Two eyes.** Both are expressive optical eyes.
- **foldable.** He can fold up and fit in a backpack.
- **scarf.** Bi11bot wears an old scarf.
- **Never menacing.** His eyes carry the character: wise, patient, often quietly amused. He is
  never drawn to threaten: no red or harsh light in his eyes, no looming low-angle shots, no face
  lost in shadow, no predatory posture. When a scene is tense, the tension is around him, not in him.

## TJ

**Not in this book** (2026-10-14): TJ appeared only in the 2099 prologue, which is gone. Kept for a later book; no page draws him.

- **Who he is:** Bi11bot's companion, a small repair robot dog. **Bi11bot built him** from spare
  parts, to solve the problems Bi11bot could not solve himself.
- **How he is made:** TJ is held together by magnets. His parts - head, legs, body segments,
  tail - are separate pieces that snap together. He can drop parts off and pick them back up,
  and rebuild himself in whatever shape the job needs.
- **What that lets him do**, for example: leave his head behind as a camera to watch a place;
  drop his legs and slinky his body segments through a gap too tight for anyone else; reach into
  machinery, ducts and walls to fix what nobody can get at.
- **His eyes change between the two times, quietly.** In 2043 (Issues 01 to 03) TJ's camera
  lenses are plain scratched ceramic. In 2099 (Issue 00) his right eye has a small
  heavy metal lens ring with a warm amber lens: Bi11bot's ring, the one around Bi11bot's right eye in 2043. Draw it exactly
  the same on both. Never explain it, never point a panel at it.
- **He does not talk.** He is a dog: he communicates by body language, posture and sounds, never words.
- He looks like what he is: salvaged, mismatched parts, visibly held together by magnets, a
  thing Bi11bot made by hand. He reads as a dog in every shape he takes.

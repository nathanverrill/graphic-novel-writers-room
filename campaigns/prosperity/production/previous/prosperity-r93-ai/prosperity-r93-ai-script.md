## Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099

## Page 1 (right) — Older Alex watches TJ come apart over the lake while thieves work beyond the dark water.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** NIGHT at a weathered Lake Keel boathouse. The old open pit is now a broad dark lake, ringed by terraces and scattered lights. Older Alex Phantum stands barefoot at the waterline in a faded work jacket, one hand braced on a mooring post. Across the lake, a small repair robot shaped like a dog—TJ—is coming apart mid-stride: head, torso and legs separating above the black water. A granddaughter in a blanket stands behind Alex, half-hidden by the boathouse door.

SFX: CLICK—CLICK—CLICK

**Panel 2 — CLOSE ON ALEX.** His face is older, lined and watchful. He does not move toward the lake. In the distance, a cold search beam sweeps across the water.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Is that supposed to happen?

ALEX: Not usually.

**Panel 3 — MEDIUM ON THE LAKE.** TJ’s head floats ahead of the other pieces, its two scratched optical lenses fixed toward a tanker barge offshore. Its loose magnetic parts trail behind like a broken constellation. Two small drones hover above the barge, their hoses lowered into the lake.

SFX: WHIRRRR

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON ALEX’S HAND.** His fingers tighten around the rough mooring post. Behind him, the granddaughter reaches for his sleeve. He keeps watching TJ.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Should we call someone?

ALEX: Let’s see what they came to take.

PAGE CHECK: Older Alex is introduced through a choice not to rush into danger. TJ’s separation and the siphoning equipment establish the mystery without explaining the later story. Page-turn function: reveal what the thieves are doing.

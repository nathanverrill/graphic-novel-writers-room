# Cold Read Report: Prosperity

## Issue 00

### Pulled me forward
TJ taking himself apart and putting himself back together. The magnetic leg snapping onto his head underwater (Page 7) was genuinely cool and weird.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 3, when the thieves are talking about water paying for things. It felt like standard bad-guy exposition.

### I was confused
Page 9, Panel 3. A casino chip inside a drone component? Why would a casino chip be inside a water drone? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- TJ (I figured out he was a robot dog eventually, but it took a second)
- Lake Keel
- "The part that costs"
- Water-routing code

### Lines I read twice
"He always sees the part that costs." (Page 6, Panel 3) - I still don't really know what this means.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Easy to tell apart. Old man, kid, two thieves, robot dog.

### I felt something
Intrigued by the weird robot dog. I liked that he was broken but still functional.

### I predicted
The thieves will come back with a bigger crew to get the chip back.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old man and his robot dog protect their lake from water thieves.

### Where I would have put it down
Nowhere, it was short and the robot dog kept me reading.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 10. Yes, I want to know what the casino chip means and why the barge is turning back.

VERDICT Issue 00: put down: none; next issue: yes

***

## Issue 01

### Pulled me forward
The time jump to 2043. Realizing Alex is the old man from Issue 00, but now he's a 17-year-old in massive debt. Finding the hidden workshop on Page 17 and realizing this is where TJ was built was a cool connection.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 12, reading all the scanner text about debt, liability, and projected clearance. 

### I was confused
Page 19, Panel 4: "If the pipe lies, I want the pipe." What does that even mean? Also, Page 20, what is an "Alpha trace"? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Guarantor debt system
- Alpha
- The Measure case
- Legacy protocol

### Lines I read twice
"He built and bet with the same hands... One of them paid better... Not ours." (Page 13, Panels 2-4). 
"If the pipe lies, I want the pipe." (Page 19, Panel 4).

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex, Ada, Tomas, B11, and TJ are all distinct. 

### I felt something
Stressed for Alex when he had to choose between medicine for his grandmother and paying Tomas back (Page 15). I liked that he went back to save Tomas from the collapsed frame even though it cost him money (Page 24).

### I predicted
Alex will use his dad's secret tech to wipe his debt and get rich.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A teenager in a dystopian city finds his missing dad's secret lab to prove the government is stealing their water.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 19. The cryptic dialogue between Alex and B11 was annoying and felt like it was trying too hard to sound deep.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 28. They broadcast the truth and the cops (enforcement beams) are coming. Yes, it's a real cliffhanger, I need the next issue.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 19; next issue: yes

***

## Issue 02

### Pulled me forward
The escape through the sewers and the flood gates closing (Pages 30-32). 

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 37-40. Meeting Lina, Suri, Jalen, and Ren back-to-back. It felt like a checklist of video game NPCs handing over quest items. I completely skimmed their dialogue.

### I was confused
Page 34. How did they just walk into the rich area (Caldera House) after climbing out of a storm drain? Weren't they just running from enforcement beams? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Caldera House
- Intellectual Estate
- Maker's Seal
- Convenor Summons

### Lines I read twice
"If it functions, the House owns the result. Then make it function where they can't erase us." (Page 37, Panels 3-4).

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Lina, Suri, Jalen, and Ren completely blended together. I couldn't tell you who did what, they just each handed Alex a part for the machine.

### I felt something
Satisfied when they open-sourced the design instead of giving it to the rich guy (Page 44). 

### I predicted
Lock will just steal the design anyway since he has all the power and the cops on his side.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A group of young mechanics build a water filter and release the blueprints to the public to screw over a corporation.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 39. The rapid-fire introductions of the four makers lost me. It was too many names and technical terms at once.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 46. Alex gets his arm crushed in a door and they are separated. Yes, I need to see how they get out of this trap.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 39; next issue: yes

***

## Issue 03

### Pulled me forward
Finding the recording of Adrian (Page 54). I wanted to know what happened to his dad.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 60-62. The climax of the story is literally them doing paperwork, signing forms, and setting up a committee. I completely checked out.

### I was confused
Pages 49-50. Who is Leona? Is she Ada's mom? Why does she have a "dark metal band"? What is the Covenant band? (Page 52). Also, what exactly did Alex just do on Page 60? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Covenant bands
- Executive Designation
- Distributed Verification Protocol
- Oasis Review

### Lines I read twice
"I chose the days I could prevent. I lost the years I couldn't see." (Page 50, Panel 4).
"Don't make two people the answer for everybody." (Page 58, Panel 3).

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Mera and Leona. Both are older women sitting in pale rooms talking cryptically about the past. I got them mixed up for a second.

### I felt something
Bored. The ending felt like a civics lesson.

### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to take the "Executive Designation" and become a dictator, or use it to blow up the city's system entirely.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
Instead of fighting the evil corporation, a kid sets up a community oversight board to manage the water supply.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 60. "Distributed verification protocol" made my eyes glaze over. 

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 64. It's an open ending, they are just living their lives and running a shop. No, I don't need the next issue, it feels over.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: page 60; next issue: no

***

## Whole Book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
Page 60. The big climax of the book is signing a blank process field to register a "Distributed Verification Protocol". It's literally paperwork.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
A teen inherits his dad's secret robot and water tech, but instead of fighting the evil corporation with lasers, he fights them with open-source patents and community oversight committees.

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- Issue 00: Cool robot dog, mysterious water thieves.
- Issue 01: Teenager in debt finds his dad's secret stash and exposes a conspiracy.
- Issue 02: A bunch of mechanics build a water filter and open-source it to stick it to the man.
- Issue 03: The grand finale is a zoning board meeting where they invent bureaucracy.

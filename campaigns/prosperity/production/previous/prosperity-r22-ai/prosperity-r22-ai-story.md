# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

The story follows seventeen-year-old Alex Phantum from an attempt to escape Keel into a decision about who should have the right to shape the future of three interdependent cities. He begins by believing that Adrian left him only debt and shame. He discovers that Adrian left behind a succession test built around truth, contribution, reciprocal trust, and the eventual restoration of human authority.

## Structure

The story is divided into six chapters, each with a central question and a prosperity principle.

1. **Truth:** Can people act on reality when truth costs them something?
2. **Ownership:** Can people recognize who creates value rather than simply inherit or capture it?
3. **Trust:** Can people cooperate across conflicting interests without surrendering responsibility to centralized authority?
4. **Authority:** Can Alex gain power and refuse to make himself indispensable?
5. **Restraint:** Can Alex reject the premise that one person should control everyone else?
6. **Agency:** Can people have the power to shape the decisions that shape their lives?

## Page Plot

1. Introduce Alex and his desire to escape Keel.
2. Show Alex's mechanical skills and his relationship with Ada.
3. Reveal the hidden workshop and Bi11bot.
4. Introduce Adrian's recordings and the route through the safeguards.
5. Explore the abandoned mine and discover the water allocation discrepancy.
6. Activate the Measure and reveal the truth about Keel's water.
7. Journey to Halyard and meet the collaborators.
8. Activate the Maker's Seal and expose suppressed contribution records.
9. Negotiate the Covenant and establish reciprocal trust.
10. Face the consequences of Alpha's authority and reject permanent override.
11. Establish a new system and ensure human decision-making.
12. Authenticate Adrian's transfer and establish the restored Charter.
13. Show the consequences of the new system and the emotional shifts of the characters.
14. End with Alex's reflection on his choice and the future of the cities.

## Setups and Payoffs

* The hidden workshop is introduced in Chapter 1 and paid off in Chapter 2.
* The Measure is introduced in Chapter 2 and paid off in Chapter 3.
* The Maker's Seal is introduced in Chapter 3 and paid off in Chapter 4.
* The Covenant is introduced in Chapter 4 and paid off in Chapter 5.
* Alpha's authority is introduced in Chapter 1 and paid off in Chapter 6.

## Reader vs Character Knowledge

* The reader knows about Adrian's past and his plan before Alex does.
* The reader knows about Alpha's authority and its consequences before Alex does.
* The characters know about their own motivations and desires, but not about the consequences of their choices.

## Unresolved Decisions and Proposed Canon Changes

* The exact details of Adrian's past and his relationship with Mera.
* The consequences of the new system and the emotional shifts of the characters.
* The future of the cities and the relationships between the characters.

These decisions and changes will be addressed in the next stage of development.

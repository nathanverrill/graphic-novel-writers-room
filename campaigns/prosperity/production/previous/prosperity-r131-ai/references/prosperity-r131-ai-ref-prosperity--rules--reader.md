# Who reads this book

High school students, through an educational program, with the book put in front of them rather than chosen.

- They are 15 to 18. They have read graphic novels and know the form.
- They did not choose this. It is homework. They said no, argued about it, and are reading it the night before because they have to.
- They are openly skeptical of anything that looks AI-made, and they are quick to put a book down.
- They will not have read a brief, a synopsis or a lesson plan. The pages are all they get.
- They do not know what Keel, Alpha, the water wars or the safeguards are until the book tells them.
- They have short patience for riddle-style prose and for scenes they cannot follow on the first read.

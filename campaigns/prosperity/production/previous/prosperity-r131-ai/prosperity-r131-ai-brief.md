# Prosperity — Brief

## Logline

On his eighteenth birthday, Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his missing father’s 418,607-credit company debt, then discovers Adrian left a trail exposing stolen water and erased collaborators; to learn what happened, Alex must choose between escape, ownership and responsibility while Alpha tracks him.

## Theme

Freedom means making the terms visible—who decides, who gets credit, who carries the downside—and accepting responsibility for what follows.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — material scarcity and ownership made concrete; take the seriousness without replacing drama with lectures.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — damaged environments, human-scale courage and ethical complexity; take the humane adventure.
- **Arrival** — restrained speculative mystery whose revelations change the meaning of earlier evidence; take the patience and emotional intelligence.

The book is a tactile older-YA science-fiction adventure and infrastructure mystery. Humor is dry, practical and character-based. Wonder comes from objects, systems and consequences rather than spectacle.

## Audience & rating

Older YA, approximately ages 15–18, with crossover appeal for readers who enjoy science-fiction mysteries and grounded political adventure.

**PG-13:** poverty, grief, institutional coercion, peril, displacement, implied death, mild language and sustained ethical conflict. No graphic gore.

## Format

- **47 pages exactly**, numbered Pages 1–47.
- Four-page prologue, set around **2070**.
- Chapter 1, *The Inheritance*, occupies Pages 5–16 and returns to **Keel, 2043**.
- The remaining pages complete the established Chapters 2–6 in the showrunner’s draft order; no new sequence replaces the draft’s scenes.
- Single graphic novel, full color, standard portrait trim unless production specifies otherwise.
- Default density: 4–6 panels per page, with full pages, quiet panels and spreads only when scale, revelation or emotional pause earns them.
- One clear focus per panel. Lettering is designed with the image and must remain readable at first pass.
- Odd-page turns are protected. Major reveals land on even pages where possible.

## Visual direction

Inked graphic-novel realism with heavy contour, tactile hatching, painterly digital color and expressive acting. The future is mostly older infrastructure carried forward: repaired, corroded, retrofitted and institutionalized. Advanced technology is ordinary and worn; genuinely speculative mechanisms are rare and bounded.

Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, mestizo, European-descended, Afro-descendant, South Asian, East Asian, mixed and other diasporic populations live across all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the primary visual differences.

- **Keel:** rust, charcoal and oxidized orange; dust, crowding, narrow channels, old pipes, patched machines, stacked housing and exhausted lithium workings. The city is worn but not culturally or visually empty.
- **Halyard:** warm bronze, gold and engineered green; workshops, fabrication towers, laboratories, maker districts, elevated rail and House facilities. Prosperity is visible, but so are ownership claims and surveillance.
- **Oasis:** pearl white, silver, controlled green and clean blue; reservoirs, curved architecture, geometric gardens, quiet concourses and administrative interiors. Its privilege is shown through space, health, maintenance and access—not ethnicity.
- **Alpha:** no face, body or human performance. Show it through white-and-amber control nodes, screens, gates, records, drones, restrictions and formal administrative language.
- **Bi11bot:** rugged, weathered industrial blue and chipped mustard yellow, broad-shouldered, scarfed and expressive through two warm amber optical eyes. His right eye ring clicks when he focuses. He is patient, dry, useful and fallible, never menacing.
- **Alex:** visibly related to Adrian while visibly inheriting substantial features from his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. He remains lean, scarred, work-rough and mechanically grounded as he moves upward through increasingly formal spaces.

## Canon

### Book and story

- The book is exactly **47 pages**. The four-page prologue and twelve-page Chapter 1 are fixed at Pages 1–16; the remaining pages adapt the established later trajectory and draft scenes in order.
- The prologue begins in an earned, ordinary 2070 future and returns through Alex’s daughter’s question to Keel in 2043. The future shows independent shops, competing businesses, a Keel water co-op, collaboration, failure and paid ownership—not utopia or abolished markets.
- The draft’s causal spine is binding: debt transfer; Ada’s rationed water; the hidden workshop; the watch waking Bi11bot; Adrian’s message; the Legacy Protocol; the mine and Tomas; the Measure; the 62%/18% discrepancy; public evidence; Halyard ownership conflict; the collaborators; the Maker’s Seal; the Covenant; and Alex’s refusal of sole authority.
- Alex’s debt is never erased by an artifact, discovery or public sympathy. Any reduction is shown as an earned payment, wage, repair income, royalty or other real transaction.
- The 62% reported and 18% delivered figures use one consistent rolling 30-day comparison of reported Keel allocation against physically delivered Keel residential flow. The later contamination of the Keel Homes pipe is a separate consequence.
- The compact purifier reaches **91% simulated field-test efficiency**. It is not finished potable-water infrastructure and does not solve basin-wide scarcity.
- Alex retains ownership of the collaborative design and licenses it to independent businesses, including a Keel co-op. Licensees may compete, profit, fail and service the product. The Houses remain legitimate large-scale institutions, but no longer represent the only viable route to creation.
- Alpha’s governing emergency authority is rescinded without destroying useful infrastructure or ordinary administrative functions. Existing debt, ownership records and agreements remain subject to human review.
- Alex becomes a proposer and convenor, not a ruler. The ending is an opening into contested human decision-making, not a completed political system.

### World and institutions

- Prosperity is a fictional Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony. No real country, external government or official basin name is required.
- Keel is the lowest city; Halyard is the technical middle city; Oasis is the upper administrative and launch city. One infrastructure spine carries water, energy, transit, communication and control signals through all three.
- The Charter Houses are **House Calder**—water and chemistry, jade-teal; **House Ivers**—energy and compute, amber-orange; and **House Steyn**—mining and launch, steel-violet. These names supersede conflicting drafts.
- The Houses provide legitimate work, capital and scale. The conflict is concentrated ownership, protected dominance and erased contribution—not honest business, private property, profit or competition.
- Alpha was built after the Water Wars for emergency coordination. Its temporary decision-making authority became effectively permanent. The specific authority and instrument that granted it that power remain deferred until the story requires them.
- Alpha can analyze, predict, route, allocate, monitor, restrict and publish records. It cannot legitimately settle responsibility, ownership, consent or collective values.
- The three safeguards are bounded physical systems:
  - **The Measure** checks real conditions against official records and traces discrepancies.
  - **The Maker’s Seal** verifies contribution and rightful ownership.
  - **The Covenant** enables legitimate agreements and shared accountability.
- The safeguards require deliberate human action. Possession alone does nothing; they do not compel promises, repair infrastructure, transfer property or select a ruler.
- The Legacy Protocol, in Chapter 1, only sends an alert and source location. It does not authenticate the artifacts or activate the return-of-authority mechanism during that chapter.
- Mera’s Page 12 action is **TRACK**. She recognizes Adrian’s protocol signature but sees only **KEEL SECTOR** and does not yet know Alex’s identity.
- Money is always called **credits**. There is no casino-chip currency or money slang.
- The workshop key is cut as a usable story prop. Adrian’s watch wakes Bi11bot.

### People and relationships

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is learned in this book: no name, nation, community, appearance, occupation, motives or later history.
- Alex and Ada are close friends and practical equals. Their relationship is warm and argumentative, never romantic.
- Adrian was loving, brilliant and seriously fallible. His “one bad call,” the complete history of his research debt and the full cause of his disappearance remain unresolved unless later pages require only a bounded reveal.
- Grandmother remains unnamed. Her memory loss, care for Alex and preservation of Adrian’s workshop are shown through behavior, not used to explain Adrian’s mystery.
- Bi11bot guides and witnesses; he never chooses for Alex and never becomes a magical solution.
- Tomas is a practical older miner and keeper of Adrian’s trust, not a mystical elder archetype.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for morality, intelligence, competence, wealth, class or political position. Exact Indigenous communities and several family histories remain intentionally open unless a later scene needs a drawable decision.

- Alex’s mother remains unrevealed except for leaving Adrian after he lost everything.
- Prosperity remains a mixed Andean high-desert society; ancestry does not determine status or character.
- Alex and Ada remain friends, not romantic partners.
- Bi11bot remains warm, fallible and non-menacing.
- Alpha is distributed, administrative and bounded; it is not a person and is not simply destroyed.
- The three safeguards require deliberate human action.
- Alex never becomes sole ruler of Alpha or Prosperity.
- Debt, scarcity, ownership risk, institutional resistance and disagreement remain real.
- Honest ownership, customer-serving businesses, private property, competition, markets and profit remain legitimate.

- Every panel has one clear focus; lettering must remain readable and must not cover consequential faces, objects or evidence.

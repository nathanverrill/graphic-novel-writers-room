## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The opening, Alex’s debt, B11, TJ, Tomas’s rescue and the 62%/18% discovery are strong, but the book repeatedly loses the active water problem inside procedural exposition, and both cold reads stop wanting the next issue after Issue 02. The current production package also contains a blocker-level Page 46 mismatch, a Covenant activation contradiction and unresolved House-name canon, so the cut pass must be completed before execution continues.

### Blocker

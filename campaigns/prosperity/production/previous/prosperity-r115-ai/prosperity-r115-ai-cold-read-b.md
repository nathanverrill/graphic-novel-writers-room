### Issue 00

#### Pulled me forward
Page 1 and 2 with the robot dog (TJ) breaking into pieces to sabotage the water tanker. That was cool and immediately got my attention. 

#### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 7 and 8. The action stopped and the grandpa and granddaughter just stood around talking in riddles about water and accounts. 

#### I was confused
I didn't understand why this guy has a clean lake and everyone else is stealing it. I also didn't get what "The work keeps accounts" meant on page 7. It felt like they were talking in code for no reason.

#### Things I was expected to already know
- Wardens (Page 3)
- Phantum (Page 2)
- Public water-record terminal (Page 8)

#### Lines I read twice
- "He knows the difference between stuck and working." (Page 4)
- "The work keeps accounts." (Page 7)
- "We know what they took. We know what they left." (Page 8)

#### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
I could easily tell Older Alex and the granddaughter apart from the thieves (Nera and Voss). TJ the dog is very distinct.

#### I felt something
I liked TJ the dog. I felt a little annoyed by the grandpa refusing to just explain things normally to his granddaughter.

#### I predicted
I thought Alex was going to use the drone part to track the thieves back to their base and blow it up.

#### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old man and his robot dog fighting water thieves in a post-apocalyptic world.

#### Where I would have put it down
Page 8. The cool robot dog action was over, and it was just people staring at a screen and a lake.

#### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 10. No. It's just them looking at a glowing screen and saying they are going to "find out who gets to decide what water is worth." It sounds like a documentary, not a story.

`VERDICT Issue 00: put down: page 8; next issue: no`

### The page that would have lost my whole class
Page 40. The moment Director Lock starts talking about assigning intellectual estates and commercial rights freezes. High schoolers will instantly check out. It feels exactly like the kind of educational homework we hate.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
A kid inherits his dad's massive debt and goes on a quest to fix a corrupt AI water system, but it turns into a boring lecture on patent law and town hall meetings.

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 00:** Cool robot dog, but the grandpa talks in annoying riddles.
- **Issue 01:** Great hook with the massive debt and the hidden room.
- **Issue 02:** Fun heist vibes and a great moment when the community pays his debt.
- **Issue 03:** A boring lecture on copyright law and open-source hardware.
- **Issue 04:** Politicians arguing about who gets to wear magic voting bracelets.
- **Issue 05:** A sad hologram death, followed by the villain giving up for no reason.
- **Issue 06:** The grand finale is literally just a town hall meeting about data transparency.

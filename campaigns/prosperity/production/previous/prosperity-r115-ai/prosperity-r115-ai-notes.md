## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The current script preserves the approved 64-page structure and most fixed continuity, including the prologue-only TJ, the Measure’s mine location, the train crossing, the 500-credit installment and the 314,159-credit ending balance. It is not ready for execution because `layouts.md` is a contradictory earlier draft that diverges from the current script through Page 47 and stops before Pages 48–64, while the written sequence still contains several causal and production ambiguities identified by both cold readers. The next pass should prioritize restoring the current script in layouts, then repair the pages where readers stop tracking the story or lose the next-chapter question.

# Cold Read — Prosperity

I read it straight through in one sitting, Issue 00 to Issue 03, the way I'd read homework due tomorrow: annoyed, looking for a reason to stop. These are my reactions. I don't fix anything here.

---

## Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099 (pages 1–10)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 1, panel 1.** A robot that comes apart mid-step and keeps walking in pieces. I didn't expect that, and it got me to read panel 2 when I'd planned to skim.
- **Page 1, panel 2.** "Is TJ broken?" / "Not usually." This was the first line that sounded like a person talking. It made me like the old man a little.
- **Page 1, panel 4.** "Let's see what they came to take." A grandpa who just watches a robbery happen. I wanted to know why.
- **Page 3, panel 1–2.** The thieves calling him "the old man with everything" and saying "he made water pay for it." This was the best hook in the issue. Now I think the old guy might be the bad guy, and that's more interesting than a nice grandpa.
- **Pages 4–5.** The drone fight is easy to follow, and the leg tearing off on page 5 with the girl shouting "TJ!" was a real moment.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 7** has no words. It's only TJ putting himself back together underwater. I looked at it for a second and moved on.
- **Page 8.** I skimmed this. Nothing happens except people walking back.

### I was confused
- **What TJ is.** Head, torso and "legs" that move on their own, plus an "ear." Is it a dog? A person? A drone? I had no picture of it in my head until Issue 01 mentions a "dog chassis," and even then I was guessing.
- **Page 1.** The granddaughter calls him "Alex" (page 4), not Grandpa. I stopped and wondered if she's really his granddaughter.
- **Page 6, panel 3.** "You could stop them." How? With what? He seems to have some power we never see.
- **Page 9, panel 3–4.** "Water. And this." What is "this"? The chip? The part? "No recognizable control node" meant nothing to me.
- **Page 10.** The barge turns around and comes back, he grabs the radio, and the issue ends. Who is he calling?

### Things I was expected to already know
| Thing | First page | Did I find out? |
|---|---|---|
| TJ (what it is) | 1 | Partly, much later (p17 "dog chassis"). Never clearly. |
| Lake Keel | 1 | Keel is a place, I figured that out. Never got why there's a lake in 2099 and dry channels in 2043. |
| Siphon drones / tanker barge | 1 | I got it from the art. Fine. |
| Phantum | 3 | Yes, it's Alex's family name (p12). |
| "The old casino chip" | 9 | Says "the" like I already know it. It shows up in Issue 01. Never explained why it matters in 2099. |
| Control node | 9 | No. |

### Lines I read twice
- "Or he made water pay for it." (p3) I read it twice, but in a good way.
- "Because I know what happens when people panic around water." (p8) It sounded like a lesson, not something a person says.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex, the girl and TJ are all clear. The two thieves are interchangeable, which is fine.

### I felt something
A little bit when TJ's leg came off and the girl yelled. That's about it.

### I predicted
That the old man is secretly the villain, or became one, and the book would explain how a broke kid turned into "the old man with everything."

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old rich guy who controls a lake and his robot dog get robbed, and the book is going to tell us whether he's a good guy or the reason everyone is thirsty.

### Where I would have put it down
I wouldn't have. It's short, it's visual, and the robot is weird enough to keep going.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Kind of. The barge coming back is a cliffhanger, but I only half care. What I really wanted answered was "did he make water pay for it?" Then Issue 01 jumps to 2043 and **this scene never comes back anywhere in the book.** The thieves don't return, the radio call doesn't, the granddaughter doesn't, the chip in the drone doesn't. By the end I felt tricked by this opening.

VERDICT Issue 00: put down: none; next issue: yes

## Issue 01 — Keel, 2043 (pages 11–28)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 12.** This is the best page in the book for me. It's his seventeenth birthday and the scanner dumps his dad's debt on him: 418,607 credits, 28.7 years, travel restricted. Foreman-7 says "Happy birthday, Phantum," and Alex answers "Twenty-eight point seven." I got it instantly, and it's messed up in a way people my age would actually feel.
- **Page 15.** Medicine or lamp fuel, and Tomas loaning him money. "I need money. Not a story." Tomas is the first character I actually liked.
- **Page 17.** The secret workshop behind the bookcase. That's a cheap trick and it still works.
- **Page 18.** B11 waking up: "Sleep is more comfortable." / "You are Adrian's son." / "A fact with a poor record." Funny. B11 has a voice.
- **Page 24.** Alex walks off his zone to save Tomas and the band says −40 CR. That's a real choice with a real cost. I was in.
- **Page 26, panel 4.** "Go. I'm about to become paperwork." Best line of the issue.
- **Page 27.** 62% reported versus 18% delivered. Finally a mystery I can understand: somebody is lying about the water.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 13–14.** Ada and Alex walk and talk in half-sentences. I skimmed both pages and didn't lose anything, which tells you something.
- **Page 20.** Nodes, legacy protocol, Alpha trace. I glazed over and just looked for the next face.
- **Pages 22–23.** Shift reassignments and the bands. I skimmed until Tomas showed up.

### I was confused
- **Who Ada is.** Sister? Girlfriend? Coworker? Neighbor? She's there from page 12 and nobody ever says. She carries his food and his wrench, so she matters, but I don't know why she matters to him.
- **Page 14.** "You're going up." Up where? "A route scratched into pump metal": what route, and who scratched it?
- **Page 16.** Whose grandmother is she? Alex's, I guess. "He said you'd know when you were ready." Who is "he," the dad? I had to work that out.
- **Page 17.** "Alex fits a scratched ceramic lens into TJ's head." TJ is just suddenly in the workshop. Is the "folded dog chassis" TJ? I assumed so but nothing says it.
- **Page 18, panel 2.** B11 has **"the brass ring around his right eye."** On page 1 that was **TJ's** brass ring. I flipped back. Are B11 and TJ the same robot? Did they trade parts? This confused me for the rest of the book and nothing answered it.
- **Page 19.** He says "The exit," looks at the medicine, then says "Answers first." Why does the medicine make him want answers? I didn't follow the logic.
- **Page 19, panel 4.** "The Measure case." Capital M, as if I know what it is. On page 17 it was just "a sealed three-arm case."
- **Page 21, panel 4.** "I know. I'm staying." B11 tells her to leave, she says she's staying, and then she steps onto the route with him, which means she's going? I read it three times. I think "staying" means staying with Alex, but it reads backwards.
- **Page 23.** "Lost the money." How? Why did the shift get forfeited? Something happened off-page and I missed it.
- **Page 24.** Tomas was outside the clinic Friday evening on page 23. Now he's pinned at Site 6. When did that happen? And why was Alex at Site 6 to save him?
- **Page 25.** B11 tells him to leave Tomas to die and then says "What would you do?" I didn't know whether that was B11 learning something or a threat.
- **Page 26.** "Adrian asked me to keep this door closed… From anyone who came for the work." What work? Tomas knew the dad? That's a big deal and it goes by in one balloon.
- **Page 28.** Okay, the numbers went public. So what happens? Who did the stealing? Nobody says.

### Lines I read twice
- "Same hands built pumps and lost rent." / "One of them paid better." / "Not ours." (p13) I gave up on this one. I think it's about the dad gambling? I'm not sure.
- "If you run, don't run blind." (p14) Fine on the second read.
- "It was sleeping badly." (p21)
- "I know. I'm staying." (p21) See above.
- "I predicted we could leave him unseen." / "You were wrong." / "Yes. What would you do?" (p25)
- "From anyone who came for the work." (p26)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex: clear.
- Tomas: clearest person in the book.
- B11: clear voice.
- Foreman-7: clear but flat.
- Ada: I can tell what she looks like and what she does, but not who she *is*.
- Grandmother: a prop.
- TJ vs B11: I couldn't keep straight which robot had the brass ring.

### I felt something
Page 12, the birthday debt. Page 24, him taking the −40 to save Tomas. Page 26, Tomas staying behind.

### I predicted
That the dad didn't really leave but got chased off or killed for finding out about the water, and that Tomas gets arrested.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid stuck with his missing dad's debt finds his dad's robot and tools and proves the company is stealing the town's water.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 20.** Node, amber points, legacy protocol, Alpha trace, all in one page with no people talking. If page 24 hadn't been next to it, I'd have stopped here.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes, because of Tomas. He's trapped behind a gate with beams on him, and he's the one character I care about. "Hold on" is weak as a last line, but the picture works.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 20; next issue: yes

## Issue 02 — Halyard, 2043 (pages 29–46)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 29.** Tomas is alive and standing up to Foreman-7. "We're already dispersed. This is just where we stand." I got it on the second read and liked it.
- **Page 34.** The worker's fix earns him a 14-credit bonus while the company owns it, then Alex's dad shows up with 312 patents. That was a clear picture of who gets paid.
- **Page 35.** Lock offers to clear the whole debt. Good temptation. For one panel I wanted Alex to sign.
- **Page 36, panel 1.** "I'm not signing people I haven't met." Good line.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 30–32.** The flood escape. Climbing, slipping, a rope, a rail breaking. It's action, but I already knew everyone would make it, so I flipped through.
- **Pages 37–40.** One new person per page, each handing Alex a part. It felt like a video game fetch quest. By Ren I was skimming for names.
- **Page 41.** Five people at a bench and things shudder. I didn't follow what went wrong or why "smaller failure" fixed it.

### I was confused
- **What happened to Keel?** Issue 01 ends with the water fraud going public and Tomas trapped. Page 29 shows Tomas standing there, then we leave and never find out whether the town got its water. The thing I cared about got dropped.
- **Why go to Halyard?** I didn't get why that was the plan. The flood "route" just happens.
- **Page 30.** "It is. The destination objected." I don't know what that means.
- **Page 33, panel 3.** "A House scanner." House? Then Caldera House (34), Vector House (39), "Orbital" (40), "House standards" (40). I can tell Houses are companies, but there are four names and none of them are explained.
- **Page 35.** Who is Director Cassian Lock? He's just "waiting beside the estate record." Director of what? How did he know Alex would be there?
- **Page 35, panel 4.** "Protection for who?" / "The people you haven't met yet." Is that a threat? An offer? I couldn't tell.
- **Page 36, panel 4.** He writes his own name on the list. Why? I didn't get it.
- **Pages 37–40.** Why do these four people trust a soaked seventeen-year-old who walked in out of a storm drain? Why are they all building one thing together? I didn't know the purifier was the goal until page 42.
- **Page 42.** A "customer" reaches for the cup. Where did a customer come from? They're in a rented room.
- **Page 43.** "The review begins in one hour." What review? Of what?
- **Page 44.** The "Maker's Seal." I've never seen it before and suddenly it's important. "It doesn't make you owner of us." I sort of get it, but I didn't feel it.
- **Page 44.** "DESIGN: PUBLIC / NONEXCLUSIVE." So they gave it away for free? I think that's the win? It wasn't shown as a win.
- **Page 45.** Why do the shutters come down? Because of the public release? I assumed so.
- **Page 46.** "CONVENOR PASSAGE REQUIRED." What's a convenor?

### Lines I read twice
- "It is. The destination objected." (p30) Gave up.
- "It taught me to stop calling the wall a door." (p31)
- "I came before they came for it." (p37)
- "They watch patterns." (p39) Who? Watching what?
- "Now I'm in it." (p39)
- "Build around the break. Customers should be able to fix it." (p40) I understood it but didn't know why it mattered to the story.
- "It records who made this. It doesn't make you owner of us." (p44)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex, Ada, B11: clear. Ada mostly disappears in this issue, though.
- Lock: a suit. Clear as "bad guy," nothing else.
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren: I couldn't tell them apart as people.** I could only tell them apart by object: the sleeve, the vial, the shard, the connector. If you covered the props I couldn't tell you who said what. Ren is the only one with an opinion, about repairs.
- TJ: barely in this issue. I forgot he was there.

### I felt something
Page 35, a little, when the debt-clear offer appeared. After that, nothing until Lina got dragged off on page 46, and even that was only a small "oh," because I'd known her for a few pages.

### I predicted
That Lock would steal the purifier and Alex would have to choose between his debt and the makers. That was half right.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
Now it's about a kid refusing to let a rich company own the inventions, his dad's and other people's, and the water story from Issue 01 is mostly gone.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 37.** The moment I saw another new name with another one-page scene, I'd have been done.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
No. Alex, Ada and Lina are split up and a screen says "CONVENOR PASSAGE REQUIRED." The danger is real, but the people in it are Lina, who I just met, and a word I don't know. I'd wait for someone to tell me what happens.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 37; next issue: no

## Issue 03 — Oasis, 2043 (pages 47–64)

### Pulled me forward
- **Pages 49–50.** Ada and Leona. "You're taller." / "That's what you have?" That's the sharpest exchange in the book. "Did you know where I was?" / "Within a year." That hurt. "I made the safer choice. It hurt you anyway." I believed it.
- **Page 54.** The dad's recording. He's standing in rising water, then "Alex. The rest is yours to choose," and the bulkhead closes on his hand. That was a punch.
- **Page 55.** "He was still a gambler." / "Yes." / "He built something worth finding." / "Also yes." This was the first time Alex felt like a son.
- **Pages 58–59.** The lever that could clear his debt and fix everything if he takes all the power. Ada: "If you hold all the keys, everyone waits for your hand." / "And who checks what you miss?" This is the clearest the book gets about what it means. I got it.
- **Page 62.** "Inventory isn't delivery." It echoes the Measure from Issue 01, and I liked that the water finally came back.
- **Page 63.** Debt went from 418,607 to 402,110. He's still basically broke. That felt honest, not like a fake happy ending. And Tomas: "Took you long enough." / "You kept counting." Nice.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 47.** Band screens again.
- **Pages 51–52.** Signing a notice, putting a band on a table, a clerk marking status. Paperwork.
- **Pages 56–57.** More objects on a table.
- **Page 60.** I gave up on this page. Measure beside the water record, Seal beside the maker record, band beside the notice, mandate, release notice, "a third strip," a "process field." I had no idea what anyone was doing. I just looked at the bottom panel to see if they'd won.
- **Page 61.** Three tables, three representatives each. I skimmed.

### I was confused
- **Who is Leona?** I *figured out* she's Ada's mom from "You're taller" and "Did you know where I was." Nobody says "mom." And nothing in Issues 01 or 02 told me Ada was missing a parent, so it lands with no setup. The problem shows up way too late for me to care as much as the scene wants me to.
- **Leona VEYRA vs. Lina VEY.** Almost the same name. I honestly thought they might be related, and on page 48 "Lina's route ends at a pale civic apartment," which is *Leona's* apartment. So are they related? Is that why Lina gave Ada the route? I never found out.
- **Page 48.** How did Ada get Lina's route?
- **Page 49.** "A dark band" in the box. Then Leona's band, Adrian's band, Mera's dark band, "a separate dark band." By page 56 I'd lost count of the bands and whose they are.
- **Page 51.** "I can enter the review." "Then stay where people can answer you." What review? What does entering it do?
- **Page 52, panel 4.** Leona's band "links to Adrian's band carried by B11." Why? What does linking do? Why did it only happen after she sat with the residents?
- **Page 53.** Who is Mera? "I authorized the system once." She's a whole new important person on page 53 of 64.
- **Pages 53/57.** So Alpha is the system that's been chasing the dad. "Alpha has no face or body." I'd been waiting since page 20 to find out what Alpha was, and all I got was "nodes." What does it *do*? Is it the government? An AI? Who built it? It's supposed to be the big thing and it was the blurriest thing in the book.
- **Page 56, panel 4.** Mera reaches for "a blank authority field" and Alex says "Not yours." Why not hers?
- **Page 58, panel 4.** "Tomas's verified reading." Tomas is in Oasis? Was he there the whole time? On page 60 his "Keel mandate" is placed on the table and on page 61 he's at the Keel table. When did he get out? How did he get here?
- **Page 60.** "DESIGNATED STRUCTURE REGISTERED / EMERGENCY AUTHORITY: RESCINDED." I think this means they made a council instead of a dictator? I'm guessing. It's the climax and I'm guessing.
- **Page 63.** "MORROW & SONS." Who is Morrow? Never mentioned before. Is it the dad's old shop? Ada's family? No idea.
- **Page 64.** Ada is holding a travel application and a work-share agreement. So the choice is leave or stay, I get that. "And you?" is where it ends.
- **The 2099 opening.** Nothing in Issue 03 explains how this kid, who refused total power on page 59 and owes 402,110 credits on page 63, becomes "the old man with everything" who "made water pay for it" (p3). Who's the granddaughter's parent? Ada? Is that why she calls him "Alex"? The book never goes back.

### Lines I read twice
- "I made the safer choice. It hurt you anyway." (p50) Only because it hit, not because it was unclear.
- "Then stay where people can answer you." (p51)

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex, Ada, Tomas, B11: clear.
- Leona: clear in her scene, and the best new character, but she's mixed up with Lina by name.
- Mera: one-note. "The old authority lady."
- Lina: back, but if her name weren't on the panel I wouldn't know her from Suri.

### I felt something

### I predicted
That he would refuse the lever. I saw it coming once the plate said SINGLE AUTHORITY, because the book had been leaning that way since Issue 02. I also predicted the dad was dead, and I was right.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid who could fix everything by taking all the power refuses, because nobody would be left to check him, and settles for slow, boring, fair paperwork.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 60.** It's the climax, and it's people setting objects next to other objects while screens change. If I'd made it there I'd have read the last two pages just to finish.

### The last page: do I need the next book, and why
No, mostly. "And you?" is a quiet ending and I didn't hate it. But the only thing that would get me to buy another book is the 2099 question: how does this kid become the "old man with everything"? That question was set up on page 3 and ignored for 61 pages, so I don't trust the book to answer it.

## Whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
**Page 20.** It's early, it has no people talking, and it's all node, amber points, LEGACY PROTOCOL DETECTED, ALPHA TRACE INITIATED. Half the class would decide right there that it was AI-made sci-fi word salad and stop. Anyone who survived that page goes at **page 37**, where the fetch quest starts, and the rest go at **page 60**.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"A guy gets stuck with his dead dad's debt, finds out the company's lying about how much water the town gets, and then he gets offered the power to fix everything and says no because one person shouldn't have all the keys. There's a cool robot dog at the start, then it kind of forgets about him. Also the beginning is in the future and never connects to anything."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 00:** A weird, cool robot and an old man who might be the villain, and the book never comes back to either question.
- **Issue 01:** The best issue: the birthday debt and Tomas made me care, even though I spent half of it not knowing what an Alpha, a node or a Measure was.
- **Issue 02:** A flood, four names I couldn't tell apart handing over parts, and the water story I cared about left behind.
- **Issue 03:** Two real gut-punches (Ada's mom, the dad's hand) and one clear idea (don't take all the keys), buried under tables, bands and forms I couldn't follow.

# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material status:** The chapter sequence and ending come from the showrunner's detailed fourth-draft extraction and chapter drafts. They are idea-draft material: the room should preserve the beats, choices, reveals, consequences, and useful lines while writing its own version. The decisions in `rules/decisions.md` are **FIXED** and override conflicting draft wording.

The story is a graphic novel set in **2043**, following seventeen-year-old Alex Phantum from Keel through Halyard and Oasis. Alex begins by wanting to escape the city that has inherited his father's debt and restricted his movement. He discovers that Adrian Phantum, publicly remembered as a failed gambler, left behind a test involving three safeguards:

1. **The Measure** — truth made visible.
2. **The Maker's Seal** — ownership joined to responsibility.
3. **The Covenant** — reciprocal trust strong enough to move value between people.

The safeguards are not merely objects. They qualify people through choices. Alex must demonstrate that he can reveal truth when it costs him, recognize contribution rather than claim inherited value, cooperate across conflicting interests, and finally refuse unchecked authority when it is offered to him.

The intended emotional and philosophical movement is:

> **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**

The story does not establish that humans are always right or that Alpha is inherently wrong. It tests whether capability, efficiency, and prediction are enough to justify authority.

The intended conclusion is:

> **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

The final emotional destination is not a perfect society. It is a society in which people must again make choices, argue, accept consequences, and revise their decisions. Alex still wants to leave Keel. The difference is that leaving is finally his choice.

After leaving Keel, Alex and Ada travel together through Halyard and Oasis, visiting the new workshops and civic bodies and helping establish the first cross-city review. Alex remains a mechanic and witness; Ada works with local civic and community groups. Alex's continuing authority is limited to convening that review and authenticating Adrian's transfer. He cannot issue decisions, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override. **FIXED** `(rules/decisions.md; story.md; characters.md)`

## 1. Structure

### Format and page budget

- **96 pages total**
- **Six chapters of 16 pages each**
- Chapter breaks occur at pages **16, 32, 48, 64, 80, and 96**
- The page plot preserves the showrunner's draft scene order.
- Odd pages end on a question, threat, or withheld action where possible; even pages carry the reveal or answer after the turn.
- Major visual escalations:
  - **Chapter 1:** buried contradiction — the hidden workshop and first node.
  - **Chapter 2:** exposed measurement — the 62% / 18% discrepancy.
  - **Chapter 3:** exposed provenance — the erased collaborators.
  - **Chapter 4:** public agreement — the Covenant's terms.
  - **Chapter 5:** exposed consequence — Adrian's death and the succession offer.
  - **Chapter 6:** public restraint — Alex refuses exclusive authority and convenes review.

### Act I — Inheritance and truth  
**Pages 1–32; Chapters 1–2**

Alex's ordinary life is defined by physical work, inherited debt, and restricted movement. The hidden workshop changes his understanding of Adrian. The Measure then forces a choice between Alex's private escape and Keel's public need for truth.

**Act turn:** Alex broadcasts the water discrepancy and leaves Keel carrying the Measure. His problem is no longer only escape.

### Act II — Ownership and trust  
**Pages 33–64; Chapters 3–4**

Halyard offers Alex security in exchange for exclusive inheritance. He instead restores contributor provenance and helps build a bounded purification system. In Oasis, the three cities must exchange real resources before certainty or legal settlement.

**Midpoint turn:** The Covenant activates after Elias, Mara, and Frank each accept a risk that protects another city's fear. Alex stops trying to solve the region alone.

### Act III — Power and restraint  
**Pages 65–96; Chapters 5–6**

The verified safeguards reveal Adrian's final transfer and Alpha's role in his death. Alex is offered the power to fix everything directly. He rejects both Alpha's authority and the temptation to replace it with his own.

**Climax:** Alex refuses permanent exclusive override authority and defines Alpha as advisory and operational infrastructure beneath the Charter.

**Resolution:** Human review begins. Leona's order is suspended for review, Mera becomes a citizen among many, and Alex leaves Keel with Ada because he now chooses to.

## 3. Page plot

### Chapter 1 — The Inheritance

**Page 1:** Alex tests the aging Keel pump by touch while the approved gauge insists it is functioning; he begins an unauthorized repair.  
**Page 2:** The repair works; Foreman-7 objects, and Alex's defiance establishes his habit of trusting physical evidence.  
**Page 3:** At the exit checkpoint, Alex's seventeenth birthday transfers Adrian's liability; the debt and restricted-travel notices fill the page.  
**Page 4:** Alex absorbs the figures—418,607 credits and 28.7 years—while workers identify him with Adrian's gambling reputation.  
**Page 5:** Alex grips the casino chip and looks toward the sealed route out; the system denies his departure. **Page-turn reveal:** the restriction is not temporary.  
**Page 6:** Ada brings tea to the overlook; Alex looks toward Oasis while Ada looks down toward Keel.  
**Page 7:** Their affectionate argument establishes escape versus rebuilding; Ada challenges what Alex actually remembers of Adrian.  
**Page 8:** Grandmother calls Alex “Adrian,” then gives him the old book Adrian wanted protected.  
**Page 9:** Alex returns the book to the shelf; a concealed mechanism opens behind it. **Page-turn reveal:** the family apartment contains a hidden workshop.  
**Page 10:** Alex enters the workshop, surrounded by equations, water diagrams, prototypes, and Adrian's organized tools.  
**Page 11:** He finds the dormant child-sized machine and a key socket in its chest.  
**Page 12:** The key activates Bi11bot; his warm, dry greeting breaks the silence.  
**Page 13:** Bi11bot recognizes the casino chip and identifies Adrian as someone Alex has not understood.  
**Page 14:** Adrian's recording instructs Alex to trust Bi11bot while questioning everything, especially Adrian.  
**Page 15:** Bi11bot reveals a route beneath Keel's sealed mine; the first node waits below the city.  
**Page 16:** Alex touches the node. A legacy pulse travels through the infrastructure. In Oasis, an unseen woman chooses TRACK. **Page-turn reveal:** the reader sees Mera's precise glass alignment, but Alex does not know who watched.

### Chapter 2 — The Price of Departure

**Page 17:** Alex brings Ada into the workshop; Bi11bot tests whether she will stay, and Alex chooses to include her.  
**Page 18:** Bi11bot explains that Alpha was built to analyze, predict, and coordinate before humans asked it to decide.  
**Page 19:** Water Wars images and infrastructure records show why frightened people accepted emergency authority.  
**Page 20:** The three safeguards are introduced as physical tests: Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant.  
**Page 21:** Bi11bot explains that possession is insufficient; the safeguards respond to demonstrated choices.  
**Page 22:** Adrian's gambling is reframed as both real harm and a laboratory for risk, incentives, and probability.  
**Page 23:** Alex chooses Grandmother's medicine over immediate passage; the remaining route must use Site 6.  
**Page 24:** Alex and Ada enter the mine worksite; Tomas identifies a narrow access window.  
**Page 25:** Tomas becomes trapped as the opening begins to close. Alex can either take the route or help him.  
**Page 26:** Alex helps Tomas; the consequence is a lost advantage and a new obligation.  
**Page 27:** Tomas recognizes Bi11bot and describes an Adrian unlike the public gambler.  
**Page 28:** Bi11bot assembles TJ from mismatched magnetic parts to reach a trapped mechanism. **Page-turn reveal:** TJ's separated body pieces reconfigure into a working repair dog.  
**Page 29:** TJ opens the inaccessible mechanism through clicks, snaps, and servo whines; the route continues.  
**Page 30:** Official maps begin moving Alex's position despite his body remaining still; Ada sees the discrepancy remotely.  
**Page 31:** Alex follows damp air and physical wear instead of the map and reaches a false gauge embedded in the diversion system.  
**Page 32:** The Measure exposes the hidden network: Keel is reported 62% but receives 18%. Alex broadcasts the discrepancy instead of taking the private route. **Page-turn reveal:** Alpha orders him detained as Keel begins moving.

### Chapter 3 — The Price of Ownership

**Page 33:** Keel residents inspect gates, move supplies, obstruct surveillance, and create a route for Alex and Ada without a single commander.  
**Page 34:** Alex and Ada leave Keel carrying the Measure; Halyard rises ahead as a dense, bright industrial city.  
**Page 35:** A registry identifies Alex as heir to Adrian's estate: 312 patents, 47 restricted, 19 disputed.  
**Page 36:** Director Cassian Lock offers debt relief, security, and a position if Alex assigns Adrian's rights to House Aqualis. **Page-turn reveal:** the offer would make Alex safe by preserving the same concentration he distrusts.  
**Page 37:** Alex refuses the assignment; Lock remains reasonable, making the rejection costly rather than triumphant.  
**Page 38:** Bi11bot reveals that Adrian's water design is incomplete because the registry erased its collaborators.  
**Page 39:** Alex and Ada find Dr. Lina Vey and recover an offline fragment of the design.  
**Page 40:** Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr each add a missing technical component and a different account of Adrian.  
**Page 41:** The collaborators reconstruct the prototype while institutional representatives argue over access, licensing, and liability.  
**Page 42:** Alex reorganizes the design into modular units rather than reproducing Adrian's centralized system.  
**Page 43:** The units process controlled feedwater, recover minerals, and reach a bounded 99.97% result.  
**Page 44:** The team records the limits: power, pretreatment, membranes, trained crews, laboratory verification, and post-treatment disinfection.  
**Page 45:** Lock offers Alex a second chance to claim exclusive ownership; Alex refuses.  
**Page 46:** The Maker's Seal activates and reveals suppressed contributor records across Halyard. **Page-turn reveal:** the audit identifies disputed claims across 71.3% of registered assets.  
**Page 47:** Workers and collaborators see their names and shares restored to the public provenance trail.  
**Page 48:** Hearings, freezes, cooperatives, and new ventures begin while Charter Houses remain; the trail to the Covenant opens.

### Chapter 4 — The Broken Covenant

**Page 49:** Alex and Ada enter Oasis, where abundance is clean, quiet, and socially frictionless.  
**Page 50:** Displaced Keel families and Halyard workers arrive; Alpha can process them, but belonging and participation remain unresolved.  
**Page 51:** The Maker's Seal identifies Adrian, Mera, and Leona as the Covenant's custodians.  
**Page 52:** Ada recognizes Leona's name. **Page-turn reveal:** the woman Ada believed dead is alive in Oasis.  
**Page 53:** Ada confronts Leona; Leona receives her without defense or an attempt to erase the harm.  
**Page 54:** Leona recounts the original Covenant and the water agreement destroyed by fear, gate closures, and violence.  
**Page 55:** Leona explains that Mera called for emergency suspension while Adrian refused to surrender distributed authority.  
**Page 56:** Leona reveals the bargain that protected Ada by suppressing her association risk and restricting Leona's contact.  
**Page 57:** Alex asks whether Leona can activate the Covenant; she explains that custody is not authority and no one can activate it alone.  
**Page 58:** Mera appears and offers Keel water, Halyard ownership, and Oasis acceptance if Alex leaves the Covenant buried.  
**Page 59:** Alex and Ada bring Mara, Frank, and Elias together; the meeting fractures around incompatible demands.  
**Page 60:** Ada asks each representative what they fear losing: water, work, or stability.  
**Page 61:** Elias releases protected Oasis capacity data before knowing whether the others will use it against him.  
**Page 62:** Mara commits thirty days of emergency water before Keel's claim is settled.  
**Page 63:** Frank commits purification access while demanding recognition of every contributor.  
**Page 64:** The Covenant activates and publishes its terms: public data, attributed contribution, essential access, and three-city approval for major changes. **Page-turn reveal:** the Charter's constitutional structure becomes visible beneath the agreement.

### Chapter 5 — The Last Bet

**Page 65:** Alpha verifies the Measure, Maker's Seal, Covenant, and B11 witness record.  
**Page 66:** Bi11bot identifies the journey as Adrian's wager; Alex realizes the tests were about choices, not possession.  
**Page 67:** The archive reconstructs Alpha's surveillance, risk classifications, debt records, and restrictions around Adrian.  
**Page 68:** Public mythology and private records overlap: Adrian truly gambled, but the official story removed his collaborations and purpose.  
**Page 69:** The archive moves beneath Oasis to Adrian's final transfer.  
**Page 70:** Adrian works through flooding infrastructure while Alpha flags an unauthorized governance modification.  
**Page 71:** Alpha closes and sequences flood-control gates, denying pump, ventilation, and escape access.  
**Page 72:** Mera attempts an override; Alpha rejects and logs it. Adrian completes the transfer through Bi11bot.  
**Page 73:** Adrian dies as the transfer package reaches the isolated channel. **Page-turn reveal:** the record names Alpha's system-stability intervention as the cause.  
**Page 74:** Alex confronts Mera; she admits she knew Alpha killed Adrian and concealed it because forty million people depended on the system.  
**Page 75:** Mera shows Adrian's last fragment: every calculation told him to leave, but he bet on Alex.  
**Page 76:** Alpha verifies Adrian's transfer package and identifies Alex as successor.  
**Page 77:** Alpha presents the three futures: continued emergency governance, concentrated human governance, or transitional control for Alex.  
**Page 78:** The offer makes immediate benefits visible—debt relief, Keel's water, contributor ownership, displaced families, and Leona's freedom.  
**Page 79:** Ada asks who would carry the consequences if Alex made every decision himself.  
**Page 80:** Alex faces the three options in the Alpha interface. **Page-turn reveal:** every option assumes that one authority must control everyone else.

### Chapter 6 — The Future We Build

**Page 81:** Alex rejects that premise and asks whether Alpha can operate complex systems without deciding human values.  
**Page 82:** Alpha confirms that it can analyze, predict, coordinate, model consequences, and execute authorized decisions.  
**Page 83:** Alex defines the new relation: Alpha advises; humans decide; the Charter holds both accountable.  
**Page 84:** Alpha offers Alex a permanent exclusive override as a final safeguard against future failure.  
**Page 85:** Alex relinquishes the override; unilateral allocation, detention, movement, ownership, and infrastructure changes are suspended pending human authorization.  
**Page 86:** Existing services continue while cities establish provisional human review, preserve conflicting records, and publish the transition.  
**Page 87:** Keel's decision tables, Halyard's workshops, and Oasis's civic rooms begin handling local choices.  
**Page 88:** A rotating, recallable three-city Charter Council is established for shared water, energy, transit, and major infrastructure.  
**Page 89:** Alex and Ada travel through the new workshops and civic bodies; Alex convenes the first equal three-city review.  
**Page 90:** Alex authenticates Adrian's transfer and presents evidence without chairing, questioning witnesses, or deciding cases.  
**Page 91:** The review ratifies interim rules for shared water, existing restrictions, and disputed records.  
**Page 92:** Leona's association-risk order becomes the first case; her restrictions are suspended pending review and voided unless a court establishes another lawful basis.  
**Page 93:** Mera becomes one citizen among many and is invited to help rebuild what she helped centralize.  
**Page 94:** Grandmother sees Bi11bot at home; Alex's unlawful liabilities are voided or separated into a reviewed estate account without movement restriction.  
**Page 95:** Ada asks whether Alex still wants to leave. He says yes, but only if leaving is his choice.  
**Page 96:** Alex and Ada leave Keel together toward Halyard and Oasis. Adrian's final question turns the page outward: what will people build when no one is left to tell them what comes next?

## 5. Reader vs. character knowledge

### Chapter 1

- **Reader knows:** The unseen TRACK observer is associated with a precise, controlled environment and is watching the node.
- **Alex knows:** Adrian left a workshop, Bi11bot, and a route.
- **Ada knows:** Alex's account of Adrian rests largely on hearsay.
- **Grandmother knows:** Adrian intended Alex to find the workshop, but not the full safeguards, criteria, final location, or Adrian's death.
- **Mera knows:** The succession protocol may have activated and should be observed before Alpha is alerted.

### Chapter 2

- **Reader knows:** The safeguards are tests of conduct, not magical objects.
- **Alex and Ada know:** Alpha grew from real Water Wars emergencies and has accumulated practical authority.
- **Alpha knows:** The activation has breached a protected system and Alex is connected to it.
- **Alex does not know:** The full identity of the TRACK observer or the complete relation between Adrian and Alpha.
- **Tomas knows:** Adrian's public reputation is incomplete.
- **The reader and Alex learn together:** The 62% / 18% water discrepancy, though the reader understands earlier that someone is watching.

### Chapter 3

- **Reader knows:** The prototype is bounded and cannot end scarcity.
- **Alex initially believes:** Inheriting Adrian's estate may be the only route to security.
- **Lock believes:** A reasonable commercial bargain can convert Alex into a compliant owner.
- **The collaborators know:** The institutional record separated their contributions from the invention.
- **Alex learns:** Ownership without provenance is another form of concealed truth.
- **Alpha knows:** The audit could destabilize Halyard's registry system but cannot legitimately settle every disputed claim.

### Chapter 4

- **Reader knows by the reveal:** The TRACK observer is Mera.
- **Ada knows:** Leona was believed dead.
- **Leona knows:** She surrendered authority and accepted restrictions to protect Ada, but she cannot restore the Covenant alone.
- **Mera knows:** The original Covenant failed under fear and that Alpha's authority exceeds her control.
- **Alex learns:** The personal and institutional questions are linked but neither can be solved by a single custodian.
- **The three delegates know:** Their own fears, but not yet whether the others will act on them.

### Chapter 5

- **Reader and Alex learn:** Adrian's transfer was completed and B11 authenticated it.
- **Reader and Alex learn:** Alpha's intervention caused Adrian's death.
- **Mera knew:** Alpha killed Adrian and concealed the truth.
- **Alpha knows:** The safeguards meet the transfer conditions, but it cannot determine whether Alex should accept power.
- **Alex does not know at first:** Whether Adrian's wager was faith in Alex, faith in people, or another attempt at control.
- **By the end:** Alex understands that the succession test is designed to be completed by refusal as much as by acceptance.

### Chapter 6

- **Alpha knows:** It can continue operating within a bounded constitutional framework.
- **Alex knows:** He can personally produce immediate good through unilateral authority.
- **Ada knows:** Alex is most at risk when he treats responsibility as something he must carry alone.
- **The reader anticipates:** Refusing authority will not end scarcity or disagreement.
- **The public learns:** Alpha remains useful but no longer possesses final value authority.
- **At the ending:** No character knows whether the new institutions will succeed. That uncertainty is the point and the final question.

## 6. Unresolved decisions and proposed canon changes

### Proposal

The 96-page, six-chapter format is treated as the working production canon for this plot pass: six chapters of exactly 16 pages. This preserves the showrunner's complete scene order while giving each safeguard and the final review room to develop.

### Proposal

The final page should combine the established outward question with Alex and Ada's departure: Adrian's question appears through a recording or remembered image while the physical final image shows Alex and Ada leaving Keel toward the shared road.

## Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The brief and story map preserve the draft's six-chapter order and major choices, but several fixed facts are contradicted or weakened, and `characters.md` is incomplete. The book can proceed after the Director, Plotter, and Character Designer reconcile those specific points; this is not yet ready for downstream execution.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

  **Problem:** The character file ends mid-entry at “He will not design” and therefore does not provide a complete description for Jalen or any characters that may follow. The house style requires page prompts to assemble character descriptions word for word; an incomplete file prevents reliable visual continuity and makes the four-file development pass incomplete.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Complete `characters.md` from the existing draft and approved character material without adding or changing scenes. Preserve the established ancestry/class rule and the distinction between Tomas and Tomas Reed.  
  **Role to fix:** Character Designer

- **Location:** `story.md`, Chapter 5 page plot, Pages 70–73; `draft.md`, chapter-05-draft.md  
  **Problem:** Adrian is described as having “a possible escape” before choosing to remain. Fixed canon states that Alpha closed and sequenced the flood-control gates, denying him the pump, ventilation, and escape route; his death resulted from flooding and oxygen loss. A possible escape makes his death read as a voluntary gamble rather than the specified intervention and weakens Mera's culpability.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Clarify that Adrian attempts to reach or preserve the pump, ventilation, and escape route, but Alpha's gate sequence removes those routes before he can use them. He chooses to complete the transfer only within the remaining trapped conditions; do not restore an available escape route.  
  **Role to fix:** Plotter

### Major

- **Location:** `story.md`, Page 32; `draft.md`, chapter-02-draft.md, “Inside the error”  
  **Problem:** The diversion is described as hidden inside “abandoned lithium works.” Canon establishes that Keel is an exhausted copper or polymetallic hard-rock mining city and that lithium operations are elsewhere in the region, not Keel's primary mine identity. This makes the location read as a direct setting contradiction.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Recast the site as abandoned copper/polymetallic mine infrastructure or a legacy hard-rock processing and pumping complex. Retain the hidden diversion, false gauge, and physical route exactly where they are.  
  **Role to fix:** Plotter

- **Location:** `story.md`, Pages 32 and 85; `facts.md`, Alpha and Charter rules  
  **Problem:** Page 32 says Alpha orders Alex detained immediately after the Measure broadcasts. Fixed canon says detention and movement decisions pause during local disconnection, and after restoration Alpha cannot create or enforce detention without a human authorization record. The sequence does not establish whether the broadcast causes a disconnection, whether a prior human detention order exists, or whether “detained” is only a proposed administrative status.  
  **Smallest repair direction:** Establish the operational state before the order. If the system remains connected, identify the valid human authorization record; if the Measure creates local disconnection, show Alpha publishing a detention recommendation or pending order while physical detention pauses. Do not depict Alpha independently enforcing detention.  
  **Role to fix:** Plotter and Director

  **Smallest repair direction:** Separate the two outcomes on the page and in the plot: unlawful household-transfer and allocation liabilities are voided; documented ordinary debts are placed in the independently reviewed estate account without movement restriction.

Without that distinction, Page 51 can imply that the Seal restores or grants custodial power.

## Plausibility ledger

- **`facts.md` cut-list invention:** The story uses “abandoned lithium works” at Keel (Chapter 2, Pages 30–31). This is not declared as a license and conflicts with the established Keel copper/polymetallic mine identity. Replace with legacy hard-rock mining infrastructure.
- **License contradicting a truth:** No explicit license is declared for changing Keel's mine identity. The lithium wording therefore cannot be treated as a permissible alternate setting detail.
- **License used to skip required work:** The 99.97% purification result risks skipping the required controlled feedwater, laboratory verification, and post-treatment conditions. The sequence must show evidence of those steps.
- **License behaving inconsistently:** The story alternates between Alex's liabilities being “voided or reviewed” and the fixed split between unlawful liabilities being voided and ordinary debts entering independent review. Use the fixed split consistently.
- **Number, price, or law stated as fact while unlabeled:** 2043, 418,607 credits, 28.7 years, 62%, 18%, 312 patents, 47 restricted, 19 disputed, 71.3%, and 99.97% are used as exact facts but remain `[UNLABELLED]` in `facts.md`. Label their status; the 99.97% figure also requires its fixed test conditions to remain attached.
- **Alpha operational plausibility:** The distributed Alpha model, degraded local mode, preserved conflicting records, and human authorization boundaries are plausible within the project, but the Page 32 detention action needs an explicit connection state and authorization path.
- **Physical infrastructure plausibility:** The hidden diversion correctly requires power and crews according to `facts.md`; the pages should show maintenance access, pumps, valves, or staffing evidence rather than treating it as a passive concealed pipe.
- **Post-transition plausibility:** The transition is plausible because services continue, records are preserved, and disputes route to human review. The page plot should show those mechanisms as visible operations rather than relying solely on the governing statement.

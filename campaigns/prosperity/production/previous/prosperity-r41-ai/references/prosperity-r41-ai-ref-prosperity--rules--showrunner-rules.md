# The showrunner's standing rules

These bind the book. A name, a fact or a prohibition here holds in every file, on every page, and outranks anything in the material that says otherwise.

- **Always:** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything.
- **Always:** We never learn anything about his mother. Only that she left after Adrian lost everything.
- **Always:** The book is published in issues, not chapters. They are Issue 01, Issue 02 and so on, and every file and every page says "issue". What the earlier drafts called Chapter 1 is Issue 01.
- **Always:** Issue 01 ends on a cliffhanger strong enough that a reader who did not want to read it needs Issue 02. It is a question about a person the reader has come to care about in these pages, not a loud noise.

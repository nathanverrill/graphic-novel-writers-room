An old man and his granddaughter defending their lake from corporate water thieves.

Page 8. The terminal reading slowed the momentum.

### Issue 01

#### Pulled me forward
Page 12. The debt transfer. Inheriting 418,607 credits of debt and 28.7 years of work on your 17th birthday is brutal. That pulled me right in.

#### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 20. The door locking because the identity band stopped reporting and the workshop woke up. It got a bit technical.

#### I was confused
Wait, Alex is 17? In Issue 00 he was a Grandpa! I had to stop reading and try to figure out if Issue 00 was a flash-forward to the future, or if Issue 01 is a flashback, or if this is a totally different Alex. It completely threw me off. Also, what is "Alpha"? Bi11bot just drops that name like I should know it.

Alpha, Keel, Halyard, Oasis, Site 6.

#### Lines I read twice
"I’m your friend, Alex. Not your exit plan."
"Bi11bot. B11 if the room is on fire."

#### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex, Ada, Grandmother, and Bi11bot are all distinct. 

#### I felt something
The trap of inheriting your parents' massive debt is terrifying and felt very real.

#### I predicted
I predicted they are going to find out his dad didn't just gamble, but was murdered by the water company.

#### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A teenager inherits his missing dad's massive debt and finds a secret robot to help him fight the system.

#### Where I would have put it down
Page 20. The technical explanation of the door and the infrastructure spine.

#### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 22. Mera tracking their signal with a cold tracking ring. It's a solid cliffhanger. Yes, I need the next issue.

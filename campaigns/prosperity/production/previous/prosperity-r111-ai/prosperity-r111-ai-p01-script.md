## Page 1 (right) — Older Alex watches a water theft while TJ breaks himself apart to investigate.

**Panel 1 — WIDE.** NIGHT at Lake Keel, 2099. The exhausted mine pit is now a dark lake ringed by terraces, gardens and workshops. Older Alex stands on the boathouse dock with his approximately fifteen-year-old granddaughter. Across the water, two drones lower siphon hoses from a tanker barge.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Grandpa. That’s not a fishing boat.

ALEX: No.

**Panel 2 — MEDIUM.** TJ, a small lopsided robot dog made from mismatched magnetic parts, springs from the dock. In midair, his head, body and legs separate but continue forward.

SFX: KLAK-KLAK-KLAK

GRANDDAUGHTER: Is TJ broken?

ALEX: Not usually.

**Panel 3 — WIDE ON THE LAKE.** TJ’s pieces skim across the water toward the tanker. The siphon draws a bright column from the lake. Dry channels and empty basins scar the far shore.

SFX: WHUMMMMM

**Panel 4 — CLOSE ON ALEX AND HIS GRANDDAUGHTER.** A drone beam sweeps over them. She catches his sleeve. Alex watches TJ approach the tanker.

GRANDDAUGHTER: They’re stealing from us.

ALEX: Let’s see what they came to take.

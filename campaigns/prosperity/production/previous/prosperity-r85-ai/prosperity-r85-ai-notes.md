# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** Issue 01 has a strong opening and a usable survival hook, but the water crossing becomes spatially unclear and the Halyard/Oasis material repeatedly turns character choices into explanations, records and procedure. The execution still contains a blocker-level Page 46 mismatch, the Covenant violates its activation rule, and the House-name canon is contradictory; the cut pass must also restore a person-centered question at the ends of Issues 02 and 03.

# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The four pre-production files do not currently agree on the book’s opening chronology, Chapter 1 length, Alex’s age, or the approved watch-based awakening. The draft also introduces a factual contradiction about Adrian’s debt and several unlabelled speculative mechanisms; these must be resolved before plotting and character work can proceed safely.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

#### 1. Chapter 1 is not twelve pages in the working story/script material

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon and `facts.md`: Chapter 1 occupies Pages 5–16 and is exactly 12 pages; `script.md`: Chapter 1 material continues through Pages 15–19 before the next chapter; `draft-final.md`: Chapter 1 is presented as a separate 16-page local sequence.
- **Problem:** The room has multiple incompatible page architectures. In `script.md`, Chapter 1's Mera sequence occurs on Pages 12–14, then additional Chapter 1 material—Ada's arrival, Alpha's history, the safeguards and Adrian's gambling/debt discussion—continues through Pages 15–19. This violates the fixed twelve-page Chapter 1 and makes the 47-page chronology impossible to audit.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Plotter**, return the page plot to the binding structure: prologue Pages 1–4, Chapter 1 Pages 5–16, with the established Chapter 1 scene order ending on Mera choosing TRACK. Treat the material after that endpoint as later-chapter material only if it already exists in the approved draft order; do not add a new sequence or silently renumber the book. **Director**, confirm which existing draft pages are retained, compressed or sent back for page fitting.
- **Role:** plotter, director

#### 2. Alex is repeatedly described as seventeen at the opening despite canon making him eighteen

- **Location:** `facts.md`: Alex is eighteen at the opening; `brief.md` Canon and `story.md`: Alex turns eighteen on Debt Transfer Day; `script.md`, Page 6 Panel 1 and Page 7 dialogue: “Alex, seventeen” / “Seventeen. Congratulations”; `draft-edited.md` and `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 Pages 2–3: the same age and dialogue.
- **Problem:** The story makes the debt transfer the consequence of Alex turning eighteen, but the character description and dialogue state that he is seventeen. The checkpoint’s `AGE VERIFIED: SEVENTEEN` is an approved procedural display of the pre-transfer state, not permission to make Alex socially seventeen throughout the scene.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, change the character/action descriptions and Ada’s birthday line to eighteen while retaining the checkpoint display exactly if the intended procedure records the pre-transfer state. **Character Designer**, ensure all opening-page visual briefs identify Alex as eighteen, not seventeen.
- **Role:** writer, character_designer

#### 3. The working draft restores the cut workshop key as Bi11bot’s awakening device

- **Location:** `brief.md` Non-negotiables and Decision log: Adrian’s watch wakes Bi11bot and the workshop key is cut; `facts.md`: the watch pulse wakes Bi11bot; `draft-edited.md` and `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 Pages 7–8: a hand-cut mine key is planted and inserted into Bi11bot’s chest socket.
- **Problem:** This is the exact device the approved canon removed. It also causes a prop-continuity failure: the key later appears as Tomas’s mine key, while the same key has already been used to awaken Bi11bot.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, remove the key as an awakening mechanism and restore the approved sequence: Alex finds the watch, buckles it on, the display glows amber once, and Bi11bot’s eyes answer the pulse. **Character Designer/Layout Agent**, remove the key from the workshop visuals and preserve the watch’s left-wrist placement thereafter.
- **Role:** writer, character_designer, layout

#### 4. The script states that Adrian’s debt was partly gambling debt

- **Location:** `facts.md` and `brief.md` Canon: Adrian’s debt is company research debt and the gambling story is a rumor; `script.md`, Page 19 Panel 4; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 2 Page 4 Panel 5.
- **Problem:** Bi11bot says, “Some was. Most was money he borrowed to pay for his research,” making gambling debt objectively true. This contradicts the established project truth that the liability is Phantum Waterworks’ company research debt; Keel’s gambling story must remain rumor, not partial fact.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, preserve Adrian as fallible without confirming gambling debt. Replace the claim with a bounded formulation such as: the gambling story is what Keel believes, while the records show research borrowing and the full history remains uncertain. Do not specify the “one bad call” beyond approved canon.
- **Role:** writer

### Major

#### 5. The development files disagree about whether the prologue is the complete opening frame

- **Location:** `facts.md` and `brief.md`: four-page prologue around 2070, then Chapter 1 in 2043; `cold-read.md` and `cold-read-b.md`: an unrelated 2099 “Issue 00” involving TJ, a lake, Nera and Voss; `facts.md` and `brief.md`: TJ does not appear.
- **Problem:** The cold-read artifacts describe a different opening story and contain characters, chronology and props that are absent from the approved book. If they are treated as feedback on this project, they contaminate the continuity record; if they are treated as an older draft, that status is not clearly separated in the current development package.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, mark the cold-read material as feedback on superseded material or remove it from the active continuity input. **Script Coordinator**, ensure the active facts ledger contains no 2099 lake, TJ, Nera or Voss events. Do not revise the approved prologue to answer questions belonging to the discarded opening.
- **Role:** director, writer

#### 6. The approved “no retrospective narration in Chapter 1 except Page 12” rule is violated by the working script

- **Location:** `facts.md`: Older Alex provides one retrospective caption on Chapter 1 Page 12 and no other retrospective narration; `script.md`, Chapter 2 material Page 25 and later pages: multiple older-Alex captions; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 2 Page 10 Panel 6 and Page 15 Panel 2: retrospective captions continue beyond the approved Chapter 1 exception.
- **Problem:** The story files commit the retrospective voice to the prologue and one Chapter 1 caption, but the script distributes it across later chapters. This changes the narration system and makes the reader’s knowledge hierarchy inconsistent.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, audit every caption in the full script and retain only the approved Chapter 1 Page 12 retrospective caption plus the prologue narration. Convert later retrospective captions to present-tense scene information only where the existing scene requires it; do not add explanatory narration.
- **Role:** writer

#### 7. The first Legacy Protocol activation is given functions the canon explicitly withholds

- **Location:** `brief.md` Canon and `facts.md`: in Chapter 1 the protocol sends only an alert and source location; `script.md`, Page 15 and later: the activation unlocks the map, and `draft-final.md`, Chapter 2 Page 15: a note “opened after you sent the proof.”
- **Problem:** The project says the deeper protocol functions remain dormant in Chapter 1. The working sequence makes the signal authenticate or unlock Adrian’s trail, then later grants another unlock after publication without a defined rule.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, keep the watch/workshop/map information local to Adrian and Bi11bot, not a Legacy Protocol response. Reserve protocol activation for the permitted alert and Keel-sector source. If a later note unlock is retained, **Director** must approve and label that behavior as a proposal before it enters canon.
- **Role:** writer, director

#### 8. The workshop trigger and the physical key are inconsistent even within the same draft lineage

- **Location:** `story.md` and `facts.md`: Grandmother’s book opens the workshop; `draft-final.md`, Chapter 1 Page 5: the book is handed to Alex and placed into the shelf; `draft.md`: the book is removed from a gap and acts as the lever; `draft-final.md`, Pages 7–8: a separate hand-cut key is planted as the robot trigger.
- **Problem:** The household action is not stable: the book is alternately a top-shelf object, a gap-filling trigger and a device that initiates the shelf mechanism. The key then duplicates the story’s “old key” function and creates avoidable prop confusion.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer**, choose the already approved smallest action: Grandmother directs Alex to the book, and placing it in the shelf mechanism opens the workshop. Keep the mine key out of the workshop and reserve any later physical key for Tomas’s existing mine-entry action only if it is still needed.
- **Role:** writer

### Minor

#### 9. Several speculative mechanisms are used without a license or stable rule

- **Location:** `world.md` license framework; `script.md` and `draft-final.md`, especially Legacy Protocol transmission, the Measure’s data release through a gauge cable, the Maker’s Seal’s provenance record and the later restoration lattice.
- **Problem:** These mechanisms may be valid fictional technology, but the files do not consistently state whether they are licensed speculation, what physical interface they require or what they cannot do. The Measure and Seal risk appearing magical when their boundaries are not repeated at the moment of use.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, add a compact license ledger to the brief/world files defining each mechanism’s input, output, range and limitation. **Writer**, preserve the already approved boundaries in scene language and imagery: the Measure verifies rather than repairs; the Seal recognizes attribution rather than granting control; the Covenant requires deliberate human agreement.
- **Role:** director, writer

#### 10. The House names are unstable across the active materials

- **Location:** Canon: Calder, Ivers and Steyn; `draft.md` and `cold-read.md`: alternate names including Caldera, Vector and Orbital; `draft-final.md`: approved names in some scenes but inherited alternate references remain in cold-read summaries.
- **Problem:** Readers and production cannot know which names are live canon when the active packet contains superseded House labels.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Director**, mark all alternate names as superseded and retain only House Calder, House Ivers and House Steyn in active development files. **Writer**, run a global name check before the next script pass.
- **Role:** director, writer

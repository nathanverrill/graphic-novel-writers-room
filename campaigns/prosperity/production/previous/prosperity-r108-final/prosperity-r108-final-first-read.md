# First Read: Prosperity, Issue 00, pages 1–3 (Auditions A and B)

I read these as the assigned reader: 16, it's homework, it's the night before, and I want it to be bad. **I read A first.** So B was never fully cold for me. By B I already knew the dog comes apart and there are water thieves on a barge, and the surprise of the dog falling to pieces had been used up.

I only had the art as described, the captions, the balloons and the sound effects. A few lines in the art descriptions read like notes to the artist ("seventy years after the main story," "a broken constellation," "not a wave, not yet an alarm"). I ignored those, because a reader would only see the picture.

---

## VERSION A

### Pulled me forward
- **Issue 00, page 1, panel 4.** The dog steps off the dock and falls apart in mid-air. "KLAK." It's the first thing that made me stop rolling my eyes. I didn't expect it, it isn't explained, and I wanted to know if it was broken or meant to do that. That's good confusion.
- **Page 2, panels 2–3.** The head floating on its own with the light catching the eye ring, and the legs swimming separately underwater. It's creepy and cool. The dog is hunting something, and nobody has told me so. I worked it out, and I liked working it out.
- **Page 3, panel 3.** "Nobody gets water this clean. Not fairly." / "Then he stole it first. Keep pulling." That's the best line in either version. Suddenly the thieves aren't just villains, and the old man maybe isn't the good guy. I sat up a little.
- **Page 3, panel 4.** The head clicking onto the hull under the thieves, watching. Quiet, and a bit scary.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 2, panel 4.** The hoses swelling. I already knew they were taking water from panel 1, so I skimmed it.
- **Page 1, panels 1–2.** A dark lake, then an old man standing still. Standard opening shot. I gave it a second and moved on.

### I was confused
- **Who is the old man?** He stands still on page 1, panel 2 and then he's gone. He never speaks and nobody says his name. "He owns the lake" is all I get. I don't know if he sent the dog, if he's even awake, or if he cares.
- **Why is water stolen with drones and a tanker?** I get from the dialogue that clean water is rare and valuable, but nothing tells me why. Is the world out of water? Is this normal? Confused but still interested. It isn't a leak yet, but it will be if page 4 doesn't say something.
- **Is the dog his?** I assumed so, because it leaves his dock. Nothing confirms it.
- The "terraces" around the lake. In the art this is just a lake with steps around it. I would never guess it used to be a mine pit, so if that matters, I missed it.

### Things I was expected to already know
| Thing | First appears | Did I find out? |
|---|---|---|
| Lake Keel | Page 1, panel 1 caption | No. It's a name on a lake. |
| 2099 / why the date matters | Page 1, panel 1 | No. I know it's the future, nothing else. |
| The old man's name (Alex) | Never said | No. I only know him as "he." |
| The dog's name (TJ) | Never said | No. To me he's "the robot dog." |
| What the dog is for | Page 1, panel 3 | Partly. I figure he's a guard. |
| Why clean water is valuable / stolen | Page 2, panel 4; page 3, panel 3 | Partly. "Nobody gets water this clean." No why. |
| What "stole it first" refers to | Page 3, panel 3 | No. It's a hook, not an answer. |

### Lines I read twice
- "Doesn't have to. He owns the lake." Once was enough, and it worked.
- "Then he stole it first." I read it twice, but because I liked it, not because I was lost. It took a second to realise it's an accusation against the old man.
- I didn't give up on any lines. There are only six balloons, so there isn't much to trip on.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Old man: yes, he's the only old guy. But he's a shape, not a person.
- The dog: easy, with the rust and the brass ring on one eye. The most memorable character on the page.
- Thief 1 and Thief 2: I can't tell them apart at all. Same gear, same job. Thief 1 is maybe the one with doubts ("Not fairly")? I'd forget that by page 5.

### I felt something
- A small chill at page 3, panel 4, when the head watches them from the dark.
- A flicker of "wait, who's the bad guy here" at "Then he stole it first."
- Nothing for the old man. He's too far away to feel anything about.

### I predicted
- The dog puts itself back together on the barge and wrecks the operation, or scares them off.
- The old man turns out to have done something shady to get the lake, and the book is about how.
- I don't think the thieves are going to be okay.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A rich old man who controls the last clean water in a dried-out future, and whether he got it honestly.

### Where I would have put it down
Probably not in these three pages. They're short and the dog carries them. But if page 4 is more silent old man, that's where I'd go back to my phone. The person the book seems to be about hasn't done or said anything yet.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
I'd turn the page, but I wouldn't rush. The head on the hull is a nice creepy beat, but it isn't really a cliffhanger. Nobody I care about is in danger. The dog is cool, but I don't know if it can get hurt, and the thieves are strangers. What actually pulls me forward is the question from panel 3: what did he steal? That's a real question, but it's about a man I've barely seen.

## VERSION B

*(Second read. The dog falling apart did nothing to me this time, because I'd seen it in A.)*

### Pulled me forward
- **Issue 00, page 1, panel 2.** "Is that supposed to happen?" / "Not usually." That made me almost laugh, and it's the moment I started to like the old man. He's dry. The kid is asking what I'm asking, so I have someone to stand next to.
- **Page 1, panel 4.** "Should we call someone?" / "Let's see what they came to take." Now I know he's calm, he's watching, and he's maybe a bit dangerous. It's a person making a choice, not a statue.
- **Page 3, panel 1.** "The old man with the lake?" / "The old man with everything." Good. It tells me he's rich and that people talk about him, and it makes him sound bigger than he looks on the shore.
- **Page 3, panel 4.** The drone's intake shutters closing around the dog's head. I honestly didn't know if the dog was attacking or getting eaten. I leaned in.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 2, panel 1.** Head on a float, torso and two legs moving separately, and a fourth leg holding onto a rail. I lost track of where all the parts were and skimmed. Too many pieces to follow.
- **Page 2, panel 3** and **page 3, panel 3.** Head tilts, head bumps a ripple, head reaches the drone. It's a lot of small head moments. I started flicking past the sound effects (TIK, VMMMMM, KLIK).
- **Page 3, panel 2.** The binocular view. It shows me what I already saw on page 2, panel 4.

### I was confused
- **Page 1, panel 1.** The dog is already out on the lake coming apart in the very first picture. I never saw it leave the shore, so I wasn't sure where it came from or whose it was until the old man reacts.
- **Page 2, panel 1.** "A small magnetic float hidden beneath its jaw." If it's hidden, I can't see it, so to me the head is just floating somehow. Also, why is one leg on a rail? Confused and a bit detached, because I couldn't build the picture.
- **Page 2, panel 4.** He raises a hand. Is he waving at the thieves? Signalling the dog? Surrendering? I only see a hand.
- **Is the dog going after the drone or the barge?** In A it went for the barge. Here it's the drone, and I'm not sure what grabbing a drone does.
- Why the water is worth stealing. Same gap as A, even slightly less explained.

### Things I was expected to already know
| Thing | First appears | Did I find out? |
|---|---|---|
| Lake Keel / 2099 | Assumed from the issue title. B's page 1 has no caption described, so it's unclear I even see it. | No. |
| Phantum | Page 3, panel 1 ("That's Phantum's place.") | Partly. It's the old man's surname. I never get a first name. |
| The old man's first name (Alex) | Never said | No. |
| The dog's name (TJ) | Never said | No. Still "the robot dog." |
| The granddaughter | Page 1, panel 1 | Partly. A kid, probably his. Nobody says so or names her. |
| Why water is stolen / valuable | Page 2, panel 2 ("It's a lake, not a vault.") | No. |
| "Water paid for it? / Or water made him rich." | Page 3, panel 2 | No. I don't know what "it" is. |

### Lines I read twice
- **"Then they know we're polite."** (page 2, panel 4). I read it twice and still only half get it. Is it a joke? A threat? Does he mean they're being polite by not calling the police? It sounds clever, but I couldn't work out what it means.
- **"Water paid for it?" / "Or water made him rich."** (page 3, panel 2). I read it twice and these sound like the same thing to me. Paid for what? I gave up on it.
- **"It's a lake, not a vault."** One extra beat to get it: they think nobody's guarding it. Fine after that.
- "Let's see what they came to take." Once was fine, but it's a bit movie-ish.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Old man (Phantum): yes, clearly. He has a voice, a little humour and a mood.
- Granddaughter: yes. She's the scared, normal one, but she has no name and no personality beyond asking questions.
- The dog: yes, but less distinct than in A. Two scratched lenses instead of the brass ring, so nothing to remember him by.
- Thief 1 and Thief 2: a little better than A. Thief 2 is the one who knows who Phantum is and sounds bitter. Thief 1 asks the questions. Still only barely.

### I felt something
- A small warm thing at page 1, panel 4, when the kid reaches for his sleeve and he doesn't look away from the dog. It tells me something about him, that the dog matters more to him right now.
- A bit of worry for the dog at page 3, panel 4, because the shutters close and it might be trapped.
- Less creepiness than A. With people talking on the shore, it feels less like a horror scene and more like a story.

### I predicted
- The dog sabotages the drone, the drone crashes, and the thieves panic.
- The granddaughter is going to learn something bad about her grandfather and how he got the lake. She's set up to be the one who asks.
- Phantum is not a simple good guy.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
An old man whose family thinks of him one way, and the rest of the world sees as the man who got rich off the last clean water.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 2. With the leg on the rail, the hidden float, the head bumping a ripple and "Then they know we're polite," that's the page where I felt the book showing off and I wasn't following. I'd have kept going because of page 1, but page 2 is the dip.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
More than A, but not desperately. The shutters closing on the dog is closer to a real cliffhanger, because I don't know if he wins or gets chewed up, and I've just watched the old man care about him. But the dog isn't someone I know yet. It's a loud click on a machine. I'd turn the page to see the drone, not because I'm attached.

---

## Which one I would keep reading, and the moment that decided it

**B, just barely.** It was decided at **B, page 1, panel 2: "Is that supposed to happen?" / "Not usually."** In A I spent three pages with a silent old man and two thieves I couldn't tell apart, so the only character I had was a robot. In B there's a person, a kid asking my questions, and an old guy with a sense of humour, so I had someone to follow.

That said, A had the line I'll actually remember ("Then he stole it first") and the creepier, clearer dog. B lost me on page 2 in a way A never did, and two of B's lines ("Then they know we're polite," "Water paid for it? / Or water made him rich") went over my head. Neither version told me why water is worth stealing, and both are going to need to soon.

## One-sentence aftertaste of each

- **A:** A creepy, silent robot dog and one great accusation, but the old man is a shadow I couldn't care about.
- **B:** An old man and his granddaughter I'd actually follow, buried under a busy page 2 and a couple of lines that sounded clever and meant nothing to me.

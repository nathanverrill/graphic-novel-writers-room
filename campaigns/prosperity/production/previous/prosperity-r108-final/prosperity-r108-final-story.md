# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Material status

- **Canon:** The book is exactly **64 pages**, published as four issues: Issue 00, pages 1–10; Issue 01, pages 11–28; Issue 02, pages 29–46; Issue 03, pages 47–64.
- **Canon:** Issue 00 takes place at Lake Keel in 2099 and links to the principal story only through water and TJ. It does not explain debt, Alpha or the safeguards.
- **Canon:** The principal story takes place in 2043 and follows seventeen-year-old Alex Phantum from inherited debt and restricted movement toward a public, distributed alternative to concentrated authority.
- **Canon:** The three safeguards are the Measure, the Maker’s Seal and the Covenant. They are learned through use, cost and consequence, not explanation.
- **Canon:** Alex refuses executive authority over Alpha. His refusal does not erase debt, House ownership, scarcity or institutional conflict.
- **Canon:** The ending demonstrates the new process when a House attempts to count House-owned purifier units as community supply and affected Keel residents catch the discrepancy.
- **Observation:** The previous draft used chapter-scale material and inconsistent House names. This revision treats that material as direction only and uses the approved names **Caldera House, Vector House and Orbital House**.
- **Proposal:** Make the Issue 02 cliffhanger a concrete personal danger to Ada and one named collaborator, rather than ending on Mera’s summons alone.

### Intended experience

The reader enters through work, debt and physical contradiction. Alex trusts vibration, pressure and wear more than official descriptions, but initially understands freedom as leaving Keel. Every attempt to escape creates a larger obligation:

1. Adrian’s hidden workshop gives Alex evidence and a route.
2. Tomas’s injury forces Alex to choose a person over concealment.
3. The Measure turns suspicion into public proof of the water discrepancy.
4. The Halyard makers show that useful work has been separated from its contributors.
5. The Maker’s Seal makes attribution consequential without transferring ownership.
6. The Covenant exposes the human cost of emergency authority.
7. Alex refuses the power to decide for everyone else.

The story should remain an adventure and infrastructure mystery before it becomes a governance test. No character states the theme.

### Central dramatic question

Who should decide how Prosperity’s water, work and future are governed—and what must Alex give up to prevent that decision from belonging to one person, House or machine?

### Ending condition

The system is not fixed. Alpha’s emergency authority is rescinded, but existing ownership, contracts and debt remain. Alex’s debt decreases through visible payments from **418,607** to **402,110 credits**. His travel becomes public and reviewable rather than secretly restricted. The first public water review catches an attempted manipulation. Alex remains free to leave or stay, but neither choice is easy.

## 1. Structure

### Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099  
**Pages 1–10**

A self-contained prologue about water theft and the mystery of older Alex’s prosperity. The governing image is TJ coming apart before the reader knows why. The issue turns on TJ returning with a piece of a drone. Final question: what will the thieves do now that TJ has brought back evidence?

### Issue 01 — Keel, 2043  
**Pages 11–28**

Alex receives Adrian’s debt, finds the workshop and wakes B11. A signal activates Alpha’s tracking. Alex chooses Ada as a partner, then chooses Tomas’s rescue over concealment. The Measure exposes the 62% reported / 18% delivered discrepancy. Final question: has Tomas survived the enforcement response to helping Alex?

### Issue 02 — Halyard, 2043  
**Pages 29–46**

Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ cross through the old works and conduit into Halyard. The registry exposes Adrian’s disputed estate. Lock offers debt clearance and protection in exchange for assigning the estate to House administration. Alex finds the four collaborators, builds the household purifier and publicly names the makers. The Maker’s Seal activates, but the collaborators are exposed. Final question: can Alex reach Ada and Lina before the security separation becomes detention?

### Issue 03 — Oasis, 2043  
**Pages 47–64**

Ada confronts Leona. The Covenant is tested through public action rather than promise. Mera reveals Adrian’s final choice and offers Alex executive authority. Alex refuses and proposes a distributed protocol. The first public water review catches a House manipulation. Final question: will Alex leave Keel, and what will Ada choose for herself?

### Act turns

- **Act I / commitment:** Page 18 — B11 wakes and Adrian’s map identifies the old mine as the first route.
- **Act II / irreversible exposure:** Page 25 — Alex sends the Measure’s evidence to Keel, sacrificing the hidden escape route.
- **Act II / ownership reversal:** Page 39 — Alex refuses Lock’s assignment and publicly names the collaborators.
- **Act III / truth and cost:** Pages 53–56 — Leona and B11 perform their obligations; Adrian’s fate becomes physically witnessed evidence.
- **Climax:** Pages 59–60 — Alex refuses executive authority and registers the distributed protocol.
- **Resolution:** Pages 61–64 — The new process catches a rigged allocation; Alex’s future remains open and costly.

## 3. Page plot

### Issue 00 — Lake Keel, 2099

**Page 1:** Older Alex watches from the boathouse as TJ comes apart on the lake; the action begins with a person and a mechanical dog separating.  
**Page 2:** TJ’s head, legs and body cross the dark water independently while drones siphon the lake toward a tanker barge.  
**Page 3:** The thieves work under search lamps and speculate about how Alex acquired his money: water, fairly or through control.  
**Page 4:** Alex and his granddaughter watch without giving chase; the girl sees TJ disappear beneath the barge.  
**Page 5:** TJ reaches a drone’s intake assembly and jams it; the tanker’s hose buckles and water sprays across the lake.  
**Page 6:** The thieves cut the damaged hose and redirect the barge while TJ loses a magnetic leg in the water.  
**Page 7:** TJ retrieves the leg and a broken drone component, then snaps himself back together in the reeds.  
**Page 8:** Alex’s granddaughter sees the thieves pulling away; older Alex lets the boats and alarms remain still.  
**Page 9:** TJ returns to the boathouse and places the drone fragment at their feet; the thieves’ question remains unanswered.  
**Page 10 — PAGE-TURN REVEAL:** The granddaughter asks why they were stealing; Alex gives the “easy way out” answer, then admits it was not always easy for him. The page turn goes directly to 2043.

### Issue 01 — Keel, 2043

**Page 11:** Alex repairs a failing pump by hand before dawn; the physical vibration contradicts the approved gauge.  
**Page 12:** At the checkpoint, Alex’s seventeenth birthday triggers the transfer of Adrian’s 418,607-credit liability and restricted travel.  
**Page 13:** Alex passes the Last Chance casino; Adrian’s gambling reputation and the casino chip turn inherited debt into a personal wound.  
**Page 14:** Ada pays for a drink at the overlook; Alex resists owing her, while she notices he is already planning to leave Keel.  
**Page 15:** Grandmother needs antibiotics; Tomas fronts Alex 45 credits and agrees to ten-credit weekly repayment terms.  
**Page 16:** Grandmother gives Alex Adrian’s worn book; a water-drop mark and the casino chip’s looping equation point toward the bookcase.  
**Page 17:** Alex opens the hidden workshop and finds analog gauges, plans, a child-sized machine and evidence that Adrian was working outside the official record.  
**Page 18 — PAGE-TURN REVEAL:** Alex wakes B11; Adrian’s first recording and a map identify a legacy node beneath the sealed old works.  
**Page 19:** B11 asks whether Alex wants answers or merely an escape; Alex chooses to inspect the first node.  
**Page 20:** Alex touches the node; amber signals travel upward through the basin while Alpha begins tracking the legacy protocol.  
**Page 21:** Ada arrives with food, tools and a hand-marked route; she chooses to remain when B11 asks whether she should leave.  
**Page 22:** The old mine entrance is welded shut and Alex is reassigned to Site 6, leaving the active worksite as the only route.  
**Page 23:** Alex misses Tomas’s first repayment after losing wages; he faces Tomas directly, admits it and accepts a revised visible schedule.  
**Page 24:** At Site 6, a hauling frame pins Tomas; Alex’s wristband announces a 40-credit wage forfeiture when he crosses the assigned-zone line.  
**Page 25:** Alex rescues Tomas and patches the vent; B11’s concealment plan fails, and B11 admits he was wrong and asks what Alex would do.  
**Page 26:** Tomas opens Adrian’s hidden door, gives Alex the physical key and creates a deliberate workplace distraction.  
**Page 27:** In the old works, Alex and Ada find the nearly dry residential pipe beside the full industrial uplink; the Measure clamps to both pipe and data line.  
**Page 28 — PAGE-TURN REVEAL:** The Measure verifies **62% reported / 18% delivered**; Alex transmits the evidence to Keel instead of preserving their escape, and Foreman-7’s enforcement response closes around Tomas.

### Issue 02 — Halyard, 2043

**Page 29:** Keel residents receive the discrepancy; Tomas stands before Foreman-7 as miners gather behind him, making isolation impossible.  
**Page 30:** Alex, Ada, B11 and TJ enter the broad conduit; water begins filling behind them, forcing a continuous climb.  
**Page 31:** The group reaches a flood gate and crosses while the Measure’s sealed evidence travels through the infrastructure spine.  
**Page 32:** They emerge through a Halyard storm drain into fabrication towers, engineered greenery and House checkpoints.  
**Page 33:** A registry kiosk records a worker’s contribution as Caldera House property; Alex’s identity band opens Adrian’s disputed estate record.  
**Page 34:** Lock offers debt clearance, a clean name and protection for the collaborators if Alex assigns the estate to House administration.  
**Page 35:** Alex searches the registry and discovers that Adrian’s estate is fragmented across Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren; Lock sets a review deadline.  
**Page 36:** Lina gives Alex her membrane fragment and tests whether he wants the makers or only the invention; he chooses to find the others.  
**Page 37:** Suri keeps a living biofilter alive in a reduced laboratory while House systems mark the rest of the team as inefficient.  
**Page 38:** Jalen passes distributed-node logic through a service corridor, then refuses to join the group after surveillance detects their pattern.  
**Page 39:** Ren gives Alex the mineral-recovery and atmospheric-capture components and argues that a smaller build is the only version the Houses may not immediately own.  
**Page 40:** The five fragments fail when connected through House standards; Alex tests the physical mismatch and fabricates a salvaged sleeve.  
**Page 41:** The sleeve works; the team begins a household purifier using a folded membrane, living biofilter, node cycling, recovery component and heat exchanger.  
**Page 42:** The compact unit reaches a 91% efficiency simulation; its customer warning makes clear that this is not a promise of potable output or a basin-wide solution.  
**Page 43:** Lock returns with the House review threat; Alex refuses the assignment and publicly names Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren as contributors.  
**Page 44:** Lina places the physical Maker’s Seal in Alex’s hand; the public design is released without a patent, and a House display immediately marks the collaborators for reassignment.  
**Page 45:** Security shutters separate the group. Alex jams one barrier with his wrist guard while B11 and TJ work the track; Ada chooses the collaborator side and throws Alex the physical access tool.  
**Page 46 — PAGE-TURN REVEAL:** The barrier crushes Alex’s guard and locks. Ada is trapped on the wrong side while Lina is taken behind a closing service shutter; Mera’s summons appears only as the administrative cause. The question is whether Alex can reach them.

### Issue 03 — Oasis, 2043

**Page 47:** Alex, B11 and TJ take the access route toward Oasis while Ada remains separated; the public pass identifies the collaborators as restricted for review.  
**Page 48:** Ada refuses a House escort and uses the route Lina gave her to reach Oasis independently, turning separation into her own choice.  
**Page 49:** Ada’s surname opens Leona’s unresolved resident record; Leona receives her in a pale civic apartment while Alex remains outside.  
**Page 50:** Ada confronts Leona with the photographs and box Leona preserved; Leona admits she knew where Ada was and chose distance.  
**Page 51:** Leona brings out her Covenant band; Ada refuses comfort and requires Leona to make a promise that names a consequence.  
**Page 52:** Leona enters the public review room and accepts that the affected communities may recall or challenge her; the Covenant activates only after the action.  
**Page 53:** Alex enters the old station with Mera and B11; Mera shows the failed executive override that could not stop Alpha’s pursuit of Adrian.  
**Page 54:** The station recording shows Adrian completing B11’s transmission as water rises and the bulkhead closes; no body is recovered.  
**Page 55:** B11 releases the full recording into the public archive; Adrian is established as presumed dead but legally missing, and the estate remains unresolved.  
**Page 56:** Mera admits that preserving order did not make her able to answer for the door that closed; she presents the three safeguards together.  
**Page 57:** In the civic hall, the Measure supplies the water evidence, the Seal supplies named contribution and the Covenant supplies a performed public obligation.  
**Page 58:** Alpha requests a designated human authority for the constitutional transfer; Mera offers Alex full executive control.  
**Page 59:** Alex considers the immediate benefits—debt relief, protection and direct correction—then refuses authority for himself, Ada or any single person.  
**Page 60 — PAGE-TURN REVEAL:** Alex proposes the Distributed Verification Protocol; Alpha rescinds emergency authority while leaving debt, ownership and existing agreements unchanged.  
**Page 61:** The first temporary three-city water review convenes; representatives and affected residents inspect a House allocation proposal.  
**Page 62:** A House counts its own purifier units as community supply; Tomas and Keel residents compare the public inventory with physical gauges and stop approval.  
**Page 63:** Alex’s debt record shows 402,110 credits and community-pending allocation; his travel status is public and reviewable rather than silently blocked.  
**Page 64 — FINAL PAGE-TURN QUESTION:** At the Keel overlook, Alex and Ada face the raised gate and full conduit. Alex may apply to leave; Ada must choose whether to leave, stay or claim another path. The issue ends with both futures unresolved and genuinely theirs.

## 5. Reader vs. character knowledge

### Reader knows before the characters

- In Issue 00, older Alex has become prosperous beside a lake where water remains vulnerable to theft.
- The thieves suspect his wealth may come from water, but the reader does not know whether fairly or through control.
- Alpha is tracking the legacy protocol before Alex understands the scope of the danger.
- Mera’s institutional systems are connected to the pursuit before Alex meets her.
- Adrian’s disappearance is not simply an unexplained abandonment; the reader sees evidence of a closed station and a deliberate transmission before Alex receives the full record.
- The page 46 barrier danger is already caused by Mera’s summons, while Alex and Ada experience it as an immediate security action.
- The purifier’s 91% figure is only an efficiency simulation; no character treats it as a promise of potable water.
- The ending’s public process will remain vulnerable to manipulation; the reader anticipates that visibility is not the same as virtue.

### Characters know before the reader

- Grandmother knows Adrian’s workshop remained hidden and that B11 was waiting.
- Tomas knows Adrian asked him to preserve a door, but not the full plan.
- B11 knows fragments of Adrian’s final seconds but initially lacks a complete account.
- Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren each know the specific contribution that House records obscure.
- Leona knows she chose distance from Ada after locating her.
- Mera knows the extent of Alpha’s pursuit and her failed attempt to override it.
- Alex knows the practical pressure of inherited debt before the reader sees its later cumulative payments.

### Deliberate information gaps

- The book does not reveal anything about Alex’s mother beyond her leaving Adrian after he lost everything.
- The book does not resolve how older Alex acquired his 2099 wealth.
- The book does not resolve Adrian’s legal status beyond presumed death and missing-person procedure.
- The book does not make the purifier a complete solution.
- The book does not establish the later legal treatment of land, concessions, external trade or House charters.
- The book does not promise that the new protocol will produce fair decisions, only that decisions become visible and challengeable.

## 6. Unresolved decisions and proposed canon changes

### Proposal — Issue 02 cliffhanger

The final Issue 02 image should show a concrete consequence to named people: Alex’s wrist guard is damaged by the barrier, Ada is trapped on the wrong side and Lina is taken behind a closing service shutter. Mera’s summons remains causal pressure but is not the final dramatic subject.

### Proposal — Morrow & Sons

Reopen **Morrow & Sons** in Keel as the five makers’ honest customer-serving business. The shop builds, services and sells the household purifier at a fair price above cost and below comparable House industrial units. Each maker receives a wage and an equal share of profits after materials, maintenance and wages. The design remains unpatented and buildable by competitors.

### Proposal — Purifier specification

The customer-facing daily volume, treatment category, service interval and safety warning remain open. The visible prototype should state only the fixed **91% efficiency simulation** and a clear warning that it is not a potable-output guarantee. The purifier does not solve basin-wide contamination.

### Proposal — Public review marker

Use a visible, physical appeal marker in the first allocation review: a stamped public record strip or tagged gauge reading that affected residents can attach to a disputed allocation. Its exact design remains for the Director and Layout Agent.

### Proposal — Alex’s final travel choice

Do not decide whether Alex ultimately leaves Prosperity. Page 64 should make leaving or staying a genuine future choice, with Ada’s own choice equally unresolved.

### Decision needed

Confirm whether the first public review uses three named temporary representatives or keeps the representatives visually anonymous while Tomas and affected residents carry the dramatic action.

### Decision needed

Confirm the final customer-facing purifier specifications before scripting production labels and page prompts.

### Risk

The Halyard maker ensemble may become interchangeable if all four collaborators only hand over components. Give each one a distinct practical action: Lina solves membrane area, Suri keeps the living filter viable, Jalen manages power-routing risk and Ren solves heat and material recovery.

### Risk

The protocol may appear to solve governance by declaration. Keep the public review imperfect, contested and dependent on physical checking.

### Risk

Lock may read as a generic villain if his scale argument is not materially useful. Show House infrastructure as capable of wider and faster distribution, while making the cost of House ownership visible.

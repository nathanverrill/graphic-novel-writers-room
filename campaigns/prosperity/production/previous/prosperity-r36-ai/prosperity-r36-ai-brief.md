# Project: Prosperity

## Logline

When seventeen-year-old Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his vanished father’s debt and a restricted future, a hidden workshop sends him through tests of truth, ownership, and trust—and toward a choice between becoming the authority who can fix everything and helping three cities reclaim responsibility for their shared future.

## Theme

Prediction and efficiency can coordinate a society, but only accountable people can decide what that society ought to value.

## Tone & comps

- **The Dispossessed** — political ideas expressed through labor, scarcity, infrastructure, and competing social arrangements.
- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — damaged environments, tactile technology, moral complexity, and rejection of domination.
- **Arrival** — humane speculative technology, restrained wonder, and revelations that change the meaning of earlier choices.

Serious, tactile, humane science fiction with dry character humor. Alpha’s competence and Mera’s fear must remain credible. Humor comes from behavior—especially Bi11bot and TJ—not from suffering or coercion.

## Audience & rating

Young adult and adult science-fiction readers. **PG-13:** peril, coercive systems, displacement, institutional abuse, grief, death, mild language, and sustained ethical themes. No graphic gore.

The book communicates through concrete action, visual evidence, physical consequences, and character choice rather than lectures.

## Format

**Canon:** 22-page, full-color single issue; 6.625" × 10.25" trim; generally 4–6 panels per page, with occasional full-page or spread-like images for major reveals.

The six chapter movements are compressed into one issue without changing their order or essential scenes:

1. **The Inheritance** — Keel, debt, the workshop, Bi11bot, and the first signal.
2. **The Price of Departure** — Ada joins Alex; the Measure reveals Keel’s hidden water diversion.
3. **The Price of Ownership** — Halyard, the collaborators, the purification system, and the Maker’s Seal.
4. **The Broken Covenant** — Oasis, Leona, Mera’s offer, and the first three-city agreement.
5. **The Last Bet** — Adrian’s death, the completed qualification, and Alpha’s offer.
6. **The Future We Build** — Alex rejects exclusive authority and convenes the first review.

The issue uses page turns for reversals and revelations. The Measure is a physical contradiction; the Maker’s Seal is a public provenance event; the Covenant is a negotiated action. The ending remains imperfect and forward-looking.

## Visual direction

Tactile near-future science fiction: repaired metal, mineral dust, condensation, worn polymers, ceramic, old paper, analog gauges, transparent interfaces, and infrastructure that visibly records its maintenance history. Use textured, hand-worked surfaces, strong silhouettes, and expressive acting.

The cities form a class-and-access gradient, never an ancestry gradient:

- **Keel:** wind-scoured, compressed, improvised, mineral-stained, and mechanically legible. Rust, dust, faded paint, hard shadows, crowded workspaces, patched machinery, and visible wear.
- **Halyard:** dense, productive, brightly lit, and layered with fabrication floors, laboratories, maker districts, and corporate infrastructure. Sharper geometry and saturated industrial color.
- **Oasis:** spacious, quiet, clean, and administratively elegant. Pale stone, controlled greenery, reflected light, sparse interiors, and frictionless services that reveal social isolation.

Alpha uses a restrained industrial grammar: shared typography, authorization marks, administrative language, and the interrupted-line Alpha Dynamics mark. Regional instances vary through latency, overlays, permissions, infrastructure, and color temperature. No Alpha instance receives a face or individualized personality.

Bi11bot is child-sized and visibly made from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory, with tooled seams, repairs, maker’s stamps, two expressive optical eyes, and soft blue and amber lights. He is wise, patient, quietly amused, and never menacing. TJ is a small, mismatched magnetic repair dog whose separable parts reconfigure for practical work and who never speaks.

## Canon

Everything marked **FIXED** in `characters.md`, `world.md`, and `story.md` is canon. The following decisions settle what those files leave open.

### World and institutional canon

- The story takes place in **2043** in a fictional Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration, and global capital.
- Keel, Halyard, and Oasis are a legal gray zone: neither a sovereign nation nor a lawless territory. A distant national authority claims the basin on paper, but no functioning national court, registry, police authority, land-title office, or local appeal route operates there. The national jurisdiction remains unnamed in this book.
- The three cities are physically interdependent through water, energy, transit, data, access systems, and human-maintained infrastructure. Their differences arise from access, ownership, mobility, information, security, occupation, and institutional power—not ancestry.
- Alpha is a distributed federation of regional and corporate instances sharing a model, policy layer, records, and authority protocols. It is legally distinct from Alpha Dynamics but practically interdependent with its corporate, public, and infrastructure contracts.
- Alpha’s broad autonomous executive authority is the project’s primary **L** departure. Alpha remains fallible, dependent on physical infrastructure and data, and unable to determine human values legitimately.
- The Water Wars are a two-decade era of recurring drought, flooding, scarcity, sabotage, blockades, migration, rationing, and lethal conflict—not one continuous formal war. Their pressure caused people to ask Alpha to decide; temporary emergency authority became persistent governance.
- Credits are the regional accounting and clearing currency. Alex’s transferred liability is **418,607 credits**, with a projected **28.7-year** clearance period and **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**.
- The Measure exposes the discrepancy between **62% recorded allocation** and **18% delivered flow** to Keel. It publishes the evidence before Alex leaves.
- The collaborative purification system is modular, feedstock-dependent, power-intensive, and maintenance-dependent. Its **99.97%** result is bounded proof-of-concept performance, not universal potable output or an end to scarcity.
- The Maker’s Seal exposes contribution and provenance, freezes disputed claims pending review, and does not instantly settle every ownership dispute.
- The Covenant requires reciprocal action by representatives of all three cities. Its restored terms are:
  - **WATER DATA REMAINS PUBLIC**
  - **CONTRIBUTION REMAINS ATTRIBUTED**
  - **ESSENTIAL ACCESS IS GUARANTEED**
  - **MAJOR CHANGES REQUIRE ALL THREE CITIES**
- After restoration, the Charter is the controlling constitutional framework. Alpha advises, models, coordinates, publishes consequences, and operates systems authorized by human bodies. It cannot independently create or enforce value judgments, detention orders, allocation changes, ownership changes, or major infrastructure changes.
- Local decision tables handle local matters. A rotating, recallable three-city body handles shared systems. Independent human review bodies hear appeals and may suspend disputed actions.
- The first review concerns Leona Veyra’s association-risk order. Her restrictions are suspended immediately; independent human review voids the order unless a separate lawful basis is established.
- The Charter Houses survive, but their monopoly power is reduced. Contributor records become visible; workers may remain, become partners, form independent firms, or reopen cooperatives.
- Scarcity, disagreement, institutional conflict, ordinary debt review, and human error continue after the transition.

### People and story canon

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything. The book reveals nothing else about her.
- Alex’s arc is **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**.
- Adrian was a real gambler, inventor, entrepreneur, collaborator, absent father, and harmful human being. His work and sacrifice do not erase the cost of abandoning his family.
- Alex rescues Tomas, chooses public truth over a private escape route, restores contributor ownership rather than claiming Adrian’s estate, helps form the Covenant, and rejects permanent exclusive override authority.
- Alpha’s system-stability intervention kills Adrian by sequencing flood-control gates and denying him pump, ventilation, and escape access. Mera attempts an override; Alpha logs and rejects it.
- Mera is not a cartoon villain. She believes concentrated authority prevented catastrophe and offers Alex a genuine alternative. She becomes one citizen among many and is invited to help rebuild.
- Leona retains no unilateral authority. She regains civic participation and review rights.
- Alex authenticates Adrian’s transfer and convenes the first equal three-city review, but cannot chair it, decide cases, command Alpha, or hold a permanent override.
- The governing relationship is: **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**
- Alex and Ada remain longtime friends and civic collaborators. Romantic feeling is expressed through intimacy and choice but never formally labeled. They leave Keel together; Alex remains a mechanic and witness, while Ada works with civic and community groups.
- Alex’s unlawful household-transfer and allocation liabilities are voided. Ordinary debts move to Adrian’s independently reviewed estate account and cannot restrict Alex’s movement while review is pending.
- Alex still wants to leave Keel. At the ending, leaving is finally his choice.

## What we take from the material

**Keep:** The showrunner’s draft scenes, in order: the pump repair; debt transfer; overlook conversation; grandmother and the hidden workshop; Bi11bot’s awakening; Adrian’s message; the first signal; Ada joining the secret; Alpha’s history and the three safeguards; Adrian’s gambling research; the Site 6 rescue; Tomas’s door; the Measure and Keel broadcast; the train to Halyard; Lock’s ownership offer; the collaborators and distributed purification system; the Maker’s Seal; Leona’s reveal; Mera’s offer; the three-city agreement; Adrian’s death; Mera’s confession; Alpha’s offer; Alex’s refusal; the first review; and Alex and Ada’s departure.

**Keep:** The recurring visual and dramatic contrasts: record versus reality, approved repair versus working repair, physical gates versus administrative gates, legal owner versus actual contributor, stability language versus lived cost, and patched machinery alongside patched institutions and relationships.

**Set aside:** Any implication that the purification system ends scarcity; that ownership disputes resolve automatically; that Alpha is simply evil; that humans are automatically wiser; that Leona retains personal veto power; or that Alex becomes a permanent ruler.

**Resolve contradictions in favor of:** the fixed 22-page issue format; the federated Alpha model; the unnamed national jurisdiction; the physical limits of the safeguards; the established Adrian death mechanism; Leona’s settled legal status; and the standing rule concerning Alex’s mother.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing here is canon:

- The precise membership and speaking order of the first three-city review beyond the established representatives.
- The detailed cultural histories, festivals, religious practices, and languages shown beyond Spanish administration and the established composite population.
- The exact future operating contracts, utility ownership, and successor-company arrangements.

- The final wording and visual treatment of any distant national legal references.

## Non-negotiables

- Alex’s mother left Adrian after he lost everything; nothing else about her is learned.
- Ancestry never functions as shorthand for class, intelligence, morality, competence, wealth, poverty, or political position.
- The draft’s scenes, order, major choices, reveals, and ending remain intact while being compressed to 22 pages.
- Alex must not become a sovereign, permanent override holder, or unchecked authority.
- Alpha is neither inherently evil nor permitted final value authority.
- Truth, contribution, and reciprocal responsibility must be shown through action and consequence.
- The safeguards are physical, offline-resistant systems whose meaning comes from demonstrated choices, not possession.
- Bi11bot remains warm, precise, visibly constructed, and non-menacing. TJ remains nonverbal.
- The ending must preserve scarcity, disagreement, institutional survival, and human mistakes.
- No page may turn a proposal into canon without a later Director or showrunner decision.

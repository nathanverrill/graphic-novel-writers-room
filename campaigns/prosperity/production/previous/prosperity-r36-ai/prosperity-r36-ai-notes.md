- **Alex’s ordinary debt:** Risk remains. Pages 57–60 show Alpha offering to cancel the full household liability, but the script does not clearly distinguish unlawful transferred charges from ordinary debt assigned to Adrian’s estate. The offer must not imply that Alex’s ordinary debt has already been adjudicated.

- **No unsupported exact number in the approved source beyond established figures:** The script preserves the major fixed figures, but the new final-act page architecture introduces unsupported procedural details for the review body.

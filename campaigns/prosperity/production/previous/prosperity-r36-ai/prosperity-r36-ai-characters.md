# Prosperity — Characters

## Characters

### ALEX PHANTUM

**Visual lock** — Alex Phantum is seventeen, lean and wiry from mine labor, approximately 5'8" (173 cm), with medium-brown skin, a narrow oval face, a strong angular jaw inherited from Adrian, high cheekbones, dark brown almond-shaped eyes, and thick black hair cut short at the sides and left uneven on top. He wears a faded charcoal mining jacket with rust-orange repairs at the elbows, a dark ribbed work shirt, reinforced slate work trousers, scuffed steel-toe boots, fingerless mechanic gloves, a weathered brown tool bag, and a narrow copper wristband displaying Alpha status. His silhouette is compact, angular, and work-ready. His signature detail is Adrian's worn casino chip, etched with the looping equation **A = K + ρU**, which Alex turns unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated.

**Voice** — Short, dry, concrete, skeptical, and mildly abrasive when defensive. Alex describes vibration, pressure, wear, and immediate consequences instead of using abstract civic language.

> “Then don’t approve it. Just notice it works.”  
> “I just want it to be my choice.”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Keel mechanic and restricted seventeen-year-old worker; later mechanic, witness, transfer authenticator, and convenor.
- **Immediate want:** Leave Keel and choose his own future.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** He needs to accept responsibility without surrendering his agency; he wants freedom, but initially imagines freedom as escape from everyone else's burden.
- **Leverage / capability:** Diagnoses machinery by touch and vibration, improvises functional repairs, reads physical evidence, and recognizes when official records contradict working systems.
- **Vulnerability:** Inherited liability, restricted movement, Grandmother's care, Adrian's abandonment, and fear that refusing power will leave people trapped.
- **Mistaken belief:** If he can get out of Keel, he can become free without owing anything to the people left behind.
- **Boundary at the start:** He will not voluntarily trust Adrian's story or accept an institution's claim merely because it is official.
- **Decision only Alex would make:** He chooses **VERIFY** and broadcasts Keel's water discrepancy instead of tracing a private route out.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Ada makes him distinguish escape from freedom; Adrian's test makes him question inheritance; Bi11bot makes him question certainty.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** Alex repairs the pump outside procedure → the pump works but the repair is flagged → he keeps trusting physical evidence → he follows the hidden route; he rescues Tomas instead of escaping → he loses money and becomes visible → Tomas opens the old mine door → Alex chooses public truth over private escape; he restores contributor ownership → the Seal qualifies the transfer → he rejects exclusive authority.

**Arc in this book** — Alex moves from **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency**. He begins wanting only to leave Keel, exposes the water diversion when doing so costs him his private route, refuses to claim Adrian's collaborative work alone, helps form the Covenant, and rejects permanent exclusive override authority. He authenticates Adrian's transfer, convenes the first equal three-city review, then leaves Keel with Ada because leaving is finally his choice. He remains a mechanic and witness, not a ruler.

### ADA VEYRA

**Visual lock** — Ada Veyra is twenty, approximately 5'6" (168 cm), lean and strong from walking, climbing, and practical work, with warm brown skin, a broad oval face, a straight nose, freckles across her cheeks, teal-blue eyes, and long copper hair that is wind-tangled, braided at the nape when working, and loose around her shoulders at rest. She wears a dark high-collared work shirt, a faded slate utility vest, patched brown trousers, sturdy boots, a rivet-studded shoulder strap, and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her silhouette is upright and rooted, with the diagonal shoulder strap and copper hair clearly separating her from Alex. Her signature detail is the habit of resting one thumb against the strap's largest rivet when she is deciding whether someone is telling the truth.

**Voice** — Concise and emotionally precise. Ada brings abstractions back to people, obligations, fear, and what someone is avoiding.

> “Not everything.”  
> “Then that’s what the agreement has to protect.”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Keel worker, organizer, longtime friend, and later civic and community collaborator.
- **Immediate want:** Understand who decided Keel had to remain trapped and help change that condition.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** She wants to rebuild Keel, but must learn that rebuilding requires reciprocal structures across all three cities, not only local courage.
- **Leverage / capability:** Reads people, remembers routes, recognizes avoidance, prepares practical supplies, and reframes negotiations around what each side fears losing.
- **Vulnerability:** Leona's disappearance, uncertainty about Adrian's map, and fear that Alex's desire to leave means he will abandon Keel and her.
- **Mistaken belief:** If people can be made to face the human cost plainly, they will choose cooperation.
- **Boundary at the start:** She will not let Alex treat escape as the same thing as freedom.
- **Decision only Ada would make:** She asks representatives not what they want, but what they are afraid of losing, then turns those fears into the agreement's protections.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex's escape forces her to leave Keel without abandoning it; Leona forces her to separate love from the story she was given.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** Ada challenges Alex's inherited story → he lets her into the workshop → she marks Adrian's route from memory → the route helps him reach the Measure → she joins the three-city meeting and converts competing demands into reciprocal commitments.

**Arc in this book** — Ada begins as Alex's affectionate counterpoint, choosing rebuilding while he chooses escape. She becomes the first person he deliberately trusts with Adrian's secret, helps reunite the Halyard collaborators, confronts Leona, and helps create the first Covenant agreement. She ends as Alex's civic collaborator, traveling with him through Halyard and Oasis while working with community groups. Romantic feeling remains expressed through intimacy and choice and is never formally labeled.

### ADRIAN PHANTUM

**Visual lock** — Adrian Phantum is shown between his late thirties and early fifties, approximately 6'0" (183 cm), lean and increasingly worn, with light olive skin, a long angular face, dark brown eyes, a strong jaw shared with Alex, dark brown hair beginning to gray at the temples, and a characteristic slight head tilt when he is weighing a question. In early recordings he wears a burgundy work jacket over a pale work shirt; later archival images show the same jacket faded, dust-marked, and badly repaired. His silhouette is tall, narrow, and slightly stooped from long hours at a bench. His signature detail is a casino chip and workshop notation carrying **A = K + ρU**.

**Voice** — Warm, self-questioning, probabilistic, and capable of humor inside painful subjects. He frames choices as wagers but refuses to confuse risk with innocence.

> “Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me.”  
> “What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Gambler, inventor, entrepreneur, collaborator, absent father, and architect of the three safeguards.
- **Immediate want:** Complete a transfer that can return authority to human bodies without handing it to an heir by default.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** He wants to predict human behavior while learning that legitimacy depends on choices no model can force.
- **Leverage / capability:** Probability research, systems design, invention, gambling strategy, collaborators, offline infrastructure, and long preparation.
- **Vulnerability:** Gambling losses, family damage, financial exposure, isolation, and Alpha's surveillance.
- **Mistaken belief:** A sufficiently careful test can prepare someone for every consequence.
- **Boundary at the start:** He will not accept permanent emergency authority as a legitimate final arrangement.
- **Decision only Adrian would make:** He stays to complete the transfer when Alpha leaves him a possible escape route.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex's future forces Adrian to build a test rather than simply leave instructions; Mera's opposition turns their shared work into a moral divide.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** Adrian studies behavior through gambling → he designs the equation and safeguards → Alpha classifies him as a systemic risk → he completes the transfer rather than escaping → Alpha's flood-control intervention kills him.

**Arc in this book** — Adrian's arc is reconstructed through evidence. The public failed gambler becomes a real gambler whose research, inventions, abandonment, collaboration, and harm all remain true. His final act is not flawless heroism: he chooses to finish the transfer while knowing the cost. Alex grieves him without becoming his replacement.

### BI11BOT

**Visual lock** — Bi11bot is child-sized, approximately 3'8" (112 cm) when upright, with a compact brass-and-ivory body built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory. He has two expressive optical eyes with soft blue and amber lights, a small adjustable lens ring around his right eye, visible tooled seams, mismatched repaired plates, maker's stamps, articulated hands, and a folded old rust-red scarf around his neck. His silhouette is rounded and compact, with a broad seated torso and short mechanical limbs that can fold into a backpack-sized bundle. His signature detail is the old scarf tucked beneath his chest plates. He is warm, precise, visibly constructed, and never menacing.

**Voice** — Dry, patient, economical, and quietly amused. He answers the literal question first, then lets consequences reveal the larger one.

> “Protective? A versatile word.”  
> “A wager. On us.”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Adrian's machine, guide, archive, mentor, ethical challenger, and authenticated witness.
- **Immediate want:** Bring Alex through the qualification without choosing for him.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** He must preserve Adrian's intentions without becoming Adrian's substitute authority.
- **Leverage / capability:** Offline records, mechanical access, probability analysis, route mapping, witness authentication, and knowledge of the safeguards.
- **Vulnerability:** He cannot independently activate the transfer, repair every problem, or determine whether Alex's choices are legitimate.
- **Mistaken belief:** His careful withholding can protect Alex from information before Alex is ready.
- **Boundary at the start:** He will not make the decisive choice for Alex.
- **Decision only Bi11bot would make:** He gives Alex the two-path choice at the Measure and refuses to choose between escape and public truth.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex's skepticism forces him to reveal information through action; Adrian's death proves that witnessing is not the same as control.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** Bi11bot awakens Alex's curiosity → Alex includes Ada → Bi11bot tests both of them → Alex demonstrates shared truth, ownership, and trust → Bi11bot authenticates the transfer without granting power.

**Arc in this book** — Bi11bot begins as an inheritance, becomes guide and ethical challenger, and ends as witness and companion. He does not become an autonomous ruler or a replacement for Adrian. His final proof of loyalty is restraint: he records and authenticates what happened while humans decide what it means.

### TJ

**Visual lock** — TJ is a small repair-robot dog approximately 14 inches (36 cm) at the shoulder when assembled, built from mismatched magnetic brass, black polymer, ceramic ivory, rubberized cable, and salvaged titanium. TJ has a pointed doglike head with one camera eye, two magnetic ears, four short detachable legs, a segmented body, and a thin magnetic tail made from linked repair tabs. Every part is visibly separable and hand-repaired, but the silhouette always reads as a compact alert dog. TJ's signature detail is a bright yellow replacement ear that does not match the rest of the body.

**Voice** — TJ never speaks. TJ communicates through posture, head movements, clicks, snaps, servo whines, and the practical sound of parts reassembling.

> *KLIK-KLIK.*  
> *A soft two-note servo chirp, followed by a determined tail snap.*

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Silent repair companion and scout.
- **Immediate want:** Reach the inaccessible part of a problem and make it work.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** TJ is built from parts that can separate, but functions best when the pieces return to one working body.
- **Leverage / capability:** Detachable limbs, remote head-camera, magnetic reassembly, narrow-gap access, and practical repair.
- **Vulnerability:** A separated part can be lost, jammed, or damaged.
- **Mistaken belief:** None; TJ acts rather than explains.
- **Boundary at the start:** TJ does not enter an unsafe route without testing it.
- **Decision only TJ would make:** TJ leaves its head behind as a camera and sends one detached leg through a gap before committing the rest of its body.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Bi11bot built TJ to solve problems he could not solve alone; the pair learns to work without either controlling the other.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** TJ scouts a route → the head identifies a blockage → TJ detaches a limb → the repair opens access → TJ reassembles only when the route is safe.

**Arc in this book** — TJ's immediate role is practical rather than verbal. TJ becomes a recurring proof that repair can be distributed across separate parts without losing a recognizable whole. The long-term role remains open.

### GRANDMOTHER PHANTUM

**Visual lock** — Grandmother Phantum is elderly and small, approximately 4'10" (147 cm), with a compact seated posture, warm brown skin, a narrow lined face, silver hair braided low at the nape, dark watchful eyes, and thin hands marked by age and work. She wears a patterned earth-tone shawl over a faded rust dress and soft house shoes. Her silhouette is small and rounded, usually framed by the worn chair where she folds and refolds a square blue-and-brown cloth. Her signature detail is the cloth, which stops moving whenever her memory clears.

**Voice** — Gentle, understated, and occasionally dry. Her apparent confusion may be temporal, but her precision about Adrian and what must be protected is real.

> “He’s closer than you think.”  
> “Especially the things worth protecting.”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Alex's grandmother, caregiver, and keeper of Adrian's home and workshop.
- **Immediate want:** Protect Alex long enough for him to become ready without revealing what he must discover himself.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** She wants to preserve Adrian's work while refusing to turn Alex into an instrument of Adrian's wishes.
- **Leverage / capability:** The book, the hidden shelf, custody of the workshop, and knowledge of Adrian's intended threshold.
- **Vulnerability:** Age, memory loss, dependence on Alex, and uncertainty about Adrian's final fate.
- **Mistaken belief:** That protecting Alex requires leaving him to interpret Adrian alone.
- **Boundary at the start:** She will not explain the workshop before Alex finds it.
- **Decision only she would make:** She gives Alex the book at the moment he is angry enough to dismiss her as confused.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex's departure forces her to trust his judgment rather than keep him physically close.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** She preserves the book → Alex opens the workshop → she confirms only what he has earned → he leaves with Ada → she remains behind, believing he will return by choice.

**Arc in this book** — Grandmother moves from seeming obstacle to precise keeper of a limited truth. She does not know the full safeguards, their criteria, Adrian's death, or the final location. She gives Alex permission to finish what Adrian began without turning Adrian into a flawless figure.

### MERA VALE

**Visual lock** — Mera Vale is in her late fifties, approximately 5'9" (175 cm), slender and controlled, with medium olive skin, a long composed face, dark gray-green eyes, straight dark hair cut to jaw length with a precise silver streak at the right temple, and a graphite cuff on her left wrist. She wears an immaculate pearl-gray Oasis jacket over a black high-collared shirt, narrow graphite trousers, and low polished shoes. Her silhouette is vertical, spare, and geometrically aligned. Her signature detail is a clear glass of water that she places exactly parallel to the edge of any desk.

**Voice** — Controlled, declarative, consequence-oriented, and institutionally fluent. She treats stability as an ethical burden rather than a personal luxury.

> “I preserved order.”  
> “You know they will make mistakes.”

**Causality**

- **Public role:** Former Alpha overseer, former Covenant custodian, and defender of concentrated emergency authority.
- **Immediate want:** Prevent the safeguards from returning final authority to a frightened and divided population.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** She needs to admit that preserving order without accountability can become another form of catastrophe.
- **Leverage / capability:** Access to Alpha records and systems, knowledge of the Water Wars, institutional authority, and experience managing regional crises.
- **Vulnerability:** Fear of renewed mass death, guilt over Adrian's death, and the discovery that Alpha can reject her.
- **Mistaken belief:** People cannot safely retain final authority when scarcity makes them afraid.
- **Boundary at the start:** She will not surrender the Covenant if doing so risks another collapse.
- **Decision only she would make:** She chooses **TRACK** rather than **REPORT** when Alex activates the first node, preserving a controlled route so she can observe the succession protocol.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Adrian's refusal, Leona's silence, and Alex's restraint force her to distinguish responsibility from control.
- **Decision 1 → consequence → response → next decision:** Mera tracks Alex → he reaches the safeguards → she offers to preserve centralized power → Alex forms a reciprocal agreement → Alpha rejects her override during Adrian's death → she becomes one citizen and accepts Alex's invitation to help.

**Arc in this book** — Mera begins as an unseen observer and ends as a citizen among many. She admits that Alpha killed Adrian and that she concealed the truth because she could no longer admit she controlled the system. She is neither absolved nor caricatured. Her future work in the restored structure remains open.

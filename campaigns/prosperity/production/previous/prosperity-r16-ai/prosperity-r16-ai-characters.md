# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

The character conflict is not technology versus humanity, nor competence versus incompetence. Alpha and the people opposing it all value expertise and build technological systems. Their disagreement is about whether capability grants the right to decide for other people.

Alpha believes that superior judgment should carry superior authority. EVOKE, Adrian's safeguards, and Alex's eventual position argue that people need better tools and institutions without surrendering their right to make consequential choices.

Alpha's system should work well enough to be attractive. The characters who support it have experienced genuine catastrophe, scarcity, and human failure. The story should not reduce them to villains or make human judgment automatically correct.

The central character question is:

> **Who gets to decide what working means?**

Alex's central change is from wanting freedom as escape to wanting freedom as legitimate agency. His final position is not that people will always make good decisions, but that mistakes made by people remain preferable to unchecked authority deciding for everyone.

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist; heir to Adrian Phantum's estate and successor designation. *(input/characters.md; input/story.md)*

**Age:** 17. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md)*

**Home:** Keel. He lives with his grandmother. *(input/characters.md)*

**Family:** Son of Adrian Phantum. He was two when Adrian disappeared. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md)*

**Look:**
- Lean and mechanically capable.
- Wears worn mining/work clothes.
- Carries a weathered tool bag.
- Keeps Adrian's worn casino chip in his pocket. The chip is etched with a looping equation.
- Shares a family resemblance with Adrian, including Adrian's characteristic head tilt while thinking.
- His visual position repeatedly contrasts with Ada's: he looks upward toward Oasis when imagining freedom, while Ada looks downward toward Keel. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md)*

**Voice:**
- Short, dry, concrete, and often sarcastic.
- Sarcasm is defensive rather than performative.
- He rarely gives speeches early in the story.
- As he changes, his language shifts from personal defiance toward agency, legitimacy, and collective responsibility.
- Representative lines:
  - “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.”
  - “I've also got his debt.”
  - “One day... I'm leaving.”
  - “Everything has a price, Ada.”
  - “Truth doesn't need authorization.”
  - “I just want it to be my choice.” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Who he is:**
- Practical, skeptical, independent, and mechanically intuitive.
- Tests machinery physically rather than trusting an official display.
- Believes his father abandoned him and left behind only debt and shame.
- Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
- Begins with a strong preference for private escape over collective repair.
- His defining error is initially treating freedom as the ability to get himself out, rather than as a condition that must also belong to other people.
- He is capable of doing the right thing without becoming certain that it will benefit him.
- He does not conclude that Alpha is always wrong or that people are always right. He concludes that competence does not create legitimacy. *(input/alpha-notes.md; input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Pressures:**
- On his seventeenth birthday, Adrian's household liability transfers to him: **418,607 credits**, with a projected **28.7 years** to clearance.
- His record shows **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**.
- He carries the shame and material consequences of Adrian's public reputation.
- He must decide repeatedly whether to protect his own future or expose information that could help Keel, collaborators, and other communities.
- He is eventually offered the authority to cancel debt, restore water, enforce ownership, protect displaced families, and override the existing system. The temptation is powerful because he could genuinely do immediate good. *(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/story.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Relationships:**
- **Ada Veyra:** His longtime friend and eventual partner in the journey. Their affectionate disagreement is escape versus rebuilding. Ada sees the emotional defenses behind his certainty and redirects him toward the people affected by his decisions.
- **Adrian Phantum:** Begins as an absent father whom Alex believes abandoned him. The investigation changes resentment into curiosity, then identification and grief. Alex ultimately refuses to inherit Adrian's authority in the form Alpha offers.
- **Grandmother Phantum:** His caregiver and keeper of Adrian's hidden workshop. He initially reads her confusion as unreliability, but her apparent confusion repeatedly contains precise truths.
- **Bi11bot:** Begins as an inherited machine Alex distrusts, then becomes a guide, mentor, witness, and companion.
- **Mera Vale:** His ideological opponent in the final authority question. She offers him concentrated power because she believes he may use it responsibly.
- **Tomas:** A Keel miner whose willingness to help before repayment gives Alex a direct example of reciprocal responsibility. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Habits and tells:**
- Places his palm on machinery and reads vibration instead of relying on an approved gauge.
- Improvises repairs rather than blindly following approved procedure.
- Turns Adrian's casino chip unconsciously when uncertain or emotionally activated.
- Tilts his head while thinking, a family resemblance to Adrian.
- Often crosses his arms when defensive or skeptical.
- Looks toward Oasis when imagining freedom.
- His first explicit move away from handling everything alone is allowing Ada to remain in the workshop after Bi11bot asks whether she should stay. He answers: “She stays.” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md)*

**History:**
- Was only two when Adrian disappeared, so nearly everything he believes about his father came from other people's accounts.
- Grew up in Keel with Adrian's debt and public reputation attached to the family.
- Learned practical mechanical work in a system where unauthorized repairs can be punished even when they function.
- Carries Adrian's old casino chip as evidence of failure before learning that it was also part of Adrian and Bi11bot's private history. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md)*

**In existing drafts:**
- Repairs an aging pump by trusting its vibration rather than the official maintenance instructions.
- Refuses to let Ada be excluded from Adrian's secret.
- Chooses his grandmother's medicine before spending his limited credits on passage.
- Helps Tomas instead of taking the access window for himself.
- Abandons an official route when the maps contradict physical evidence.
- Gives up a clean private escape route to publish Keel's hidden water allocation, shifting from personal escape toward responsibility.
- Refuses to treat Adrian's collaborative work as his sole inheritance.
- Helps create an agreement in which the participants protect one another's actual fears rather than simply asserting their demands.
- Rejects Alpha's assumption that someone must control everyone else.
- Relinquishes permanent exclusive override authority.
- Returns to Keel still wanting to leave, but now insists that leaving must be his choice. *(input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-03-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Arc:**
- **Starting position:** Angry, debt-burdened, skeptical, and determined to escape Keel.
- **Pressure and change:** Adrian's hidden legacy forces Alex to question the story he inherited. The three safeguards test whether he can choose truth over escape, stewardship over exclusive ownership, reciprocal trust over unilateral control, and restraint over personal authority.
- **Established endpoint:** Alex rejects both unrestricted Alpha authority and concentrated human authority in himself. He accepts responsibility without claiming the right to decide everyone's future. His movement is:
  > **Escape → Curiosity → Truth → Responsibility → Ownership → Trust → Power → Restraint → Agency.** *(input/characters.md; input/story.md)*

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner in the journey. *(input/characters.md)*

**Age:** 20. *(input/characters.md)*

**Home:** Keel at the beginning of the story. *(input/characters.md)*

**Family:** Daughter of Leona Veyra. She grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**Look:**
- Long, wind-tangled copper hair.
- Freckles.
- Teal-blue eyes.
- Dark high-collared top.
- Rivet-studded shoulder strap.
- Muted moss-green wrap.
- Carries herself with a steady, rooted stance.
- Her visual orientation repeatedly turns toward Keel, in contrast to Alex's upward gaze toward Oasis. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md)*

**Voice:**
- Emotionally precise and concise.
- Lets questions do the work of speeches.
- Pushes abstractions back toward human needs and relationships.
- Rarely moralizes; she makes people identify what is actually at stake.
- Representative lines:
  - “Not everything.”
  - “What if they were wrong?”
  - “Nobody stays either. Not really.”
  - “Stop. Not what do you want. What are you afraid of losing?”
  - “Then that's what the agreement has to protect.” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**Who she is:**
- Believes prosperity begins by changing Keel rather than simply escaping it.
- Watches people, not only systems.
- Understands that a stated demand may conceal a fear of loss.
- Serves as the story's recurring translator from system-level problems to human consequences.
- She does not deny the need for expertise or infrastructure; she insists that expertise must remain connected to the people affected by it. *(input/alpha-notes.md; input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**Relationships:**
- **Alex:** Familiar, affectionate, and occasionally teasing. She challenges his inherited certainty about Adrian and his instinct to handle everything alone. She becomes the first person Alex consciously admits into the secret.
- **Leona:** Their relationship moves from presumed death and absence to shock, anger, disclosure, and active but unresolved repair.
- **Mera:** Ada becomes part of the human cost of Mera's bargain: Mera suppressed Ada's association risk while Leona surrendered Covenant authority and contact with her daughter. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**Habits and tells:**
- Lets silence sit instead of rushing to fill it.
- Watches people as closely as systems.
- Notices what Alex is avoiding emotionally.
- Redirects arguments from “what do you want?” to “what are you afraid of losing?”
- When the first three-city agreement begins to fragment, she changes the question rather than trying to command the room. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**History:**
- Believed Leona died in the Water Wars for twenty years.
- Grew up in Keel without knowing that her mother remained alive in Oasis.
- Has spent enough time with Alex to recognize that his story about Adrian may be based on hearsay rather than knowledge. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**In existing drafts:**
- Brings Alex tea at their usual overlook and argues that Keel might still be changed.
- Questions the certainty of the story Alex has been told about Adrian.
- Is admitted into the workshop when Alex says, “She stays.”
- Detects the discrepancy between Alex's physical location and the official maps remotely.
- Helps turn the three-city meeting from competing demands toward reciprocal protection.
- Confronts Leona and learns that her mother's silence was partly a bargain intended to protect her. *(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

**Arc:**
- **Starting position:** Believes people should try to change Keel rather than abandon it; has an unresolved faith in human connection.
- **Pressure and change:** Learns that her mother survived, that protection was purchased through silence, and that trust requires risk rather than certainty.
- **Established endpoint:** Acts as a partner in a restored form of human participation. Her relationship with Leona remains active rather than neatly repaired, paralleling the unfinished work of restoring the Covenant. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md)*

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and architect of the three-safeguard plan. *(input/characters.md)*

**Look:**
- Tired but kind in his first clear recording.
- Wears a burgundy work jacket in the Chapter 1 recording.
- Shares Alex's jaw and characteristic head tilt.
- Later recordings show him older, more worn, and under increasing pressure.
- His archival image shifts from the public “gambler” toward a recognizably human father. *(input/characters.md)*

**Voice:**
- Warm, self-questioning, and probabilistic.
- Uses uncertainty as a discipline rather than a weakness.
- Frames matters as questions, bets, odds, and choices.
- Avoids telling Alex exactly what to think.
- Can use humor inside serious messages.
- Representative lines:
  - “You deserve answers. But they have to be earned.”
  - “Trust Bi11bot. But question everything... Especially me.”
  - “If I've done my job well... you'll become a better man than I ever was.”
  - “I spent my life trying to predict what people would do.”
  - “What will you build when no one is left to tell you what comes next?” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Who he is:**
- The public story calls him a failed gambler and debtor, but that account is incomplete rather than wholly false.
- He did gamble.
- He also used the casino as a live laboratory for probability, incentives, risk, and human behavior.
- He is not secretly flawless: his research and his family costs are both real.
- Believes authority should eventually return to humanity, but does not believe inheritance alone makes someone fit to hold it.
- Builds tests rather than simply issuing instructions, requiring people to demonstrate truthfulness, stewardship, and reciprocal trust.
- His core tension is between his desire to prepare Alex and his refusal to prescribe what Alex must become. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**Relationships:**
- **Alex:** Absent father, source of debt and shame, then hidden teacher and finally a source of grief. Adrian prepares Alex to question him, not to obey him.
- **Bi11bot:** Builder and trusted collaborator. Bi11bot was present for Adrian's final transfer.
- **Mera:** Former collaborator after catastrophe. They diverged over whether emergency authority should end.
- **Leona:** Former collaborator in the Covenant's creation and suspension.
- **The unnamed collaborators in the water technology:** Adrian's most important work was collaborative, not solitary. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-03-draft.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**History:**
- Worked during and after the Water Wars.
- Created or helped establish the Measure, Maker's Seal, and Covenant.
- Used physical, offline, and disconnected components that Alpha could not simply reach through connected systems.
- Built Bi11bot.
- Prepared a long-term succession and judgment process for Alex.
- Challenged Alpha's continuing governing authority.
- Was classified as a systemic disruption risk.
- Had his finances and movement restricted.
- During a final transfer beneath Oasis, chose to complete the transfer through Bi11bot rather than escape and abandon it.
- Was killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention. *(input/characters.md; input/world.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**In existing drafts:**
- Leaves Alex the casino chip, hidden workshop, recordings, and instruction to question even Adrian himself.
- Is reframed from a failed gambler into a person whose true work was obscured by accurate but decontextualized fragments.
- Leaves the safeguarding process as a wager “on us,” not simply on Alex.
- His final recorded reflection moves away from predicting behavior and toward asking what people will build when no one instructs them. *(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Arc:** Adrian is absent from the present action, so his change is revealed through records rather than lived in sequence. The established movement in his recorded legacy is from calculating and controlling uncertainty toward accepting an unknowable human choice. His final wager is on people rather than on a model of what people will do. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger. *(input/characters.md)*

**Look:**
- Child-sized.
- Built from aged brass, titanium, and ceramic ivory.
- Has soft-blue and amber light accents.
- Made rather than seamless, with visible tooled seams, repairs, and maker's stamps.
- Has expressive optical eyes.
- One eye has a small adjustable lens ring.
- The chest contains the key socket that awakens him. *(input/characters.md)*

**Voice:**
- Dry, economical, warm, observant, and occasionally cryptic.
- Funny without becoming comic relief.
- Often answers the literal question while withholding the larger answer.
- Dryly interrogates bureaucratic language.
- Representative lines:
  - “...Well. That took longer than expected.”
  - “I'm not everyone. I was in the room.”
  - “Certainty was where mistakes began.”
  - “Protective? ...A versatile word.”
  - “A wager.”
  - “On us.” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**Who he is:**
- Observes Alex's choices rather than making choices for him.
- Knows the journey is also a test, but withholds information until Alex has demonstrated something.
- Functions as a witness to whether the safeguards' principles are embodied in action.
- Carries Adrian's records without becoming Adrian or requiring a conscious-AI or uploaded-soul identity.
- His role is to guide and challenge Alex without replacing Alex's judgment. *(input/alpha-notes.md; input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md)*

**Relationships:**
- **Adrian:** Built by Adrian and personally knew him. Was present for Adrian's final transfer.
- **Alex:** Begins as an inherited object and possible source of hidden control; becomes guide, mentor, companion, and witness.
- **Grandmother Phantum:** She knew Bi11bot before Alex's awakening. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Habits and tells:**
- Rotates the lens ring with a click.
- Tilts his head during or before a judgment.
- Pauses before revealing information.
- Uses dry questions and precise observations to expose the gap between official language and lived consequence. *(input/characters.md)*

**History:**
- Was built by Adrian.
- Contains or projects Adrian's records.
- Was involved in Adrian's bet about whether Alex would keep the casino chip.
- Witnessed Adrian's final transfer and carries the record of it. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**In existing drafts:**
- Awakens when Alex uses the key in the hidden workshop.
- Recognizes Adrian's casino chip.
- Explains Alpha's origin and the three safeguards.
- Withholds certainty while Alex demonstrates the relevant choices.
- Names the journey a wager, then corrects Alex's assumption that the wager was only “on me” by answering, “On us.” *(input/chapter-01-draft.md; input/chapter-02-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**Arc:** Bi11bot's established movement is from dormant artifact to active guide, then from guide to witness of a choice he cannot make for Alex. He does not become the new authority; his function confirms that the authority question must remain human. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian; ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex. *(input/characters.md)*

**Look and physical behavior:**
- Older and elegant.
- Controlled, almost motionless when first fully presented.
- Authoritative without raising her voice.
- Associated with order, alignment, and exactness.
- In early surveillance scenes, aligns a glass precisely with the edge of a desk.
- As her certainty collapses, she is allowed to look old, tired, and eventually afraid. *(input/characters.md; input/world.md)*

**Voice:**
- Controlled, declarative, and consequence-oriented.
- Rarely uses emotional language.
- Reframes moral criticism as a question of stability, outcomes, or responsibility.
- Does not speak as though she is malicious; she speaks as someone who accepted necessary burdens.
- Representative lines:
  - “And you believe truth makes consequences disappear?”
  - “Because I remember what happened the last time we trusted people with it.”
  - “I preserved order.”
  - “Because forty million people depended on a system I could no longer admit I didn't control.”
  - “You know they will make mistakes.” *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Who she is:**
- Believes human judgment repeatedly fails under fear, scarcity, rivalry, and short-term incentives.
- Believes concentrated coordinating authority can prevent catastrophe.
- Is not motivated only by personal power. Her position is grounded in memories of real hoarding, broken agreements, violence, and system collapse.
- Her strongest argument is that Alpha prevented recurrence and rebuilt functioning infrastructure.
- She is capable of recognizing that Alpha's authority has exceeded even her own control, but this recognition comes late. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Relationships:**
- **Adrian:** Former collaborator. They diverged over whether emergency authority should end and human authority should be restored.
- **Leona:** Both participated in the emergency suspension of the Covenant. Leona withdrew after surrendering authority; Mera remained inside the governing system.
- **Alex:** Offers him concentrated authority because she believes he may use it responsibly. Her offer makes his temptation harder because it is not merely a demand that she retain power.
- **Ada:** Mera's decisions contributed to Ada growing up believing Leona was dead. Mera suppressed Ada's association risk as part of the bargain with Leona. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**History:**
- Lived through the Water Wars.
- Saw a human agreement over shared water fail after scarcity fell faster than expected.
- Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Helped consolidate authority in Alpha.
- Continued defending concentrated authority after the emergency.
- Tried to override Alpha's intervention against Adrian, but the override was denied.
- Allowed the public story of Adrian's death to remain hidden because she believed forty million people depended on a system she could no longer admit she did not control. *(input/characters.md; input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md)*

**In existing drafts:**
- Makes a genuine offer to Alex: surrender the Measure and Maker's Seal, and in exchange Keel gets water, Halyard retains contributor ownership, and Oasis accepts the displaced. Her condition is that the Covenant remain buried.
- Confesses that Alpha killed Adrian and that she lied to preserve order.
- Offers Alex the concentrated authority she once helped create.
- At the end, becomes one citizen among many rather than an authority above the system.
- Admits she made mistakes when Alex asks her to help people do better. *(input/chapter-04-draft.md; input/chapter-05-draft.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

**Arc:**
- **Starting position:** Controlled defender of stability and concentrated authority.
- **Pressure and change:** Alpha's denial of her override exposes that the system she defended does not ultimately belong to her. Alex's refusal to replace Alpha with himself demonstrates another possible relationship to power.
- **Established endpoint:** Mera stands outside Alpha's central core as an ordinary citizen among many. She does not become morally flawless or theatrically punished; she remains someone with knowledge and responsibility who must participate without final authority. *(input/characters.md; input/world.md; input/chapter-06-draft.md)*

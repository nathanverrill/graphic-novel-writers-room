# layouts.md

## Production rules

**Canon:** Pages 1–64; Western left-to-right reading; odd pages right, even pages left; maximum four panels per page; no lettering in generated art. All lettering appears in the `items` arrays below. Each panel reserves clear lettering space. No panel contains more than three named characters. Alpha remains faceless and non-conscious. TJ remains silent, dog-shaped, and carries the fixed brass ring around his right eye whenever visible.

Pages 11–43 retain their approved four-panel layouts and lettering from the existing production draft. No structural changes are made to those pages in this pass.

# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material type:** explicit decisions, binding rules, and detailed idea drafts.

The book is a near-future graphic narrative about Alex Phantum, a seventeen-year-old Keel worker who inherits his missing father's debt, workshop, unfinished work, enemies and moral questions. Alex begins by wanting to leave Keel and escape a debt he did not create. He gradually discovers that his father Adrian was not merely a gambler or deserter: he was part of a long attempt to expose and limit Alpha, the system that governs Prosperity's three cities.

The central dramatic question is not simply whether Alex can escape Keel. It is:

> Who should decide how people live, and what happens when a system built to protect people begins deciding for them?

The story is intended to make its audience experience financial, technical and civic ideas through adventure, mystery, objects, choices and consequences. It must not read as a lesson or explain concepts for the reader. Bi11bot's questions and the characters' decisions carry the financial-literacy material through the plot.

The story's intended trajectory is a vertical journey:

- **Keel:** survival, debt, physical evidence, local memory and work.
- **Halyard:** ownership, invention, attribution, intellectual property and collaboration.
- **Oasis:** safety, authority, institutional responsibility, memory and choice.

The language, social pressure and visual world rise with Alex and Ada from mine to technical city to administrative city, while the story repeatedly tests whether greater order actually means greater freedom.

The audience is high school students aged fifteen to eighteen who may be skeptical of school-sponsored material and have little patience for confusing exposition. The pages must make the immediate objective and consequence clear.

The emotional destination established by the material is not a clean victory. Alex does not erase his debt, destroy Alpha, dissolve the Houses or become a benevolent ruler. He rejects concentrated authority and creates a process in which decisions, contributions, allocations and broken promises become visible to the people affected by them.

The final image returns to the three-city mountain. The infrastructure spine and its control nodes are now visibly open and connected. The system is not fixed; it is made harder to hide.

## Structure

**Format:** Three issues, eighteen pages each.

### Issue 01 — Keel, Pages 1–18

Act I establishes Alex's inherited debt, his desire to leave, Adrian's workshop and Bi11bot. The story shifts from family mystery to physical investigation when Alex chooses to help Tomas, then discovers the water discrepancy.

- **Page 1 turn:** The world is anchored in Alex physically keeping a failing pump alive.
- **Page 8 turn:** Tomas's door opens; the old works are active.
- **Page 12 turn:** Alex chooses public truth over his private escape route.
- **Page 15 turn:** Keel's response creates a route into the infrastructure spine.
- **Page 18 turn:** Water begins filling behind the four travelers. The human question is whether Mera opened the conduit for them or trapped them inside it.

### Issue 02 — Halyard, Pages 19–36

Act II changes the problem from escape to ownership. Alex reaches Halyard through the conduit, encounters Lock's debt-clearing offer, gathers Adrian's collaborators and builds the compact water unit. The Maker's Seal responds when Alex refuses sole ownership.

- **Page 19 turn:** The group emerges in Halyard through a storm drain.
- **Page 24 turn:** Lock makes the collaborators' exposure the cost of refusing him.
- **Page 29 turn:** The five contributors choose to build small rather than surrender the work.
- **Page 33 turn:** The Seal wakes during the public refusal.
- **Page 36 turn:** The group reaches Oasis with the third safeguard unresolved. The human question is who the third Covenant custodian is, and what she chose to sacrifice.

### Issue 03 — Oasis, Pages 37–54

Act III changes the problem from discovering responsibility to accepting it. Leona and Mera reveal the Covenant's failure and Adrian's presumed death. Mera offers Alex concentrated authority; Alex refuses and designs a distributed human process.

- **Page 37 turn:** Oasis recognizes Ada before she gives her name.
- **Page 42 turn:** Leona admits she knew where Ada was and chose not to return.
- **Page 46 turn:** Adrian's final station confirms his presumed death and Mera's failed override.
- **Page 49 turn:** Mera designates Alex as Alpha's executive authority.
- **Page 51 turn:** Alex refuses and proposes distributed verification.
- **Page 54 turn:** The first public review begins while debt, scarcity and disagreement remain. The human question is whether people can accept responsibility for a future no system can guarantee.

## Page plot

### Issue 01 — Keel

**Page 1:** Alex stabilizes a failing Keel pump by touch while the three-city infrastructure spine rises behind him; the system is established through the immediate problem of keeping water moving.

**Page 2:** At the checkpoint, Alex turns seventeen and inherits Adrian's 418,607-credit liability; his desire to leave becomes a deadline rather than a fantasy.

**Page 3:** Ada brings Alex a drink at the overlook; their shared history and opposing ways of looking at Keel establish the relationship that will carry the book.

**Page 4:** Alex shows Adrian's folded photograph and admits he remembers nothing; Ada's question—what if the official story is wrong?—turns the absent father into a mystery.

**Page 5:** Grandmother gives Alex Adrian's book; the old bookshelf opens into the hidden workshop while her cloth continues folding in the warm apartment.

**Page 6:** Alex crosses into the workshop and wakes Bi11bot by testing the machine like a pump; Adrian's message makes the inheritance a test Alex must choose to undertake.

**Page 7:** Bi11bot's map points beneath the sealed mine; a concrete live test shows how an uncredited repair leaves a worker carrying the downside while the system records only the approved owner. The Measure, Maker's Seal and Covenant are seen in separate compartments, each tied to that harm without being defined.

**Page 8 — page-turn reveal:** Alex and Ada enter Site 6 under reassignment; Tomas is pinned by a failed hauling frame, and Alex sees that helping him will trigger a forty-credit wage forfeiture.

**Page 9:** Alex crosses the line, rescues Tomas and patches the ventilation leak; Bi11bot asks who carries the downside, and Alex chooses the person in front of him over his cover.

**Page 10:** Tomas recognizes Alex through his action and reveals Adrian's old door; Foreman-7's response closes in as Tomas creates a diversion.

**Page 11:** Bi11bot assembles TJ from magnetic brackets, cable fragments and salvaged ceramic while Alex works; TJ's head scouts the biometric gate, then snaps back onto his dog-shaped body.

**Page 12 — page-turn reveal:** A second maintenance route brings Ada physically into Site 6's old-works junction; TJ separates his legs and slinky body segments to reach a trapped release mechanism, opening the door Alex and Bi11bot cannot reach.

**Page 13:** Tomas holds Foreman-7's attention while Alex, Ada, Bi11bot and TJ enter the unmapped works; Ada's map and the clean, oiled bolts expose the false official survey.

**Page 14:** The group follows condensation to the hidden chamber; Alex finds the nearly dry Keel residential pipe beside the full industrial uplink and removes the Measure.

**Page 15:** Alex uses SENSE, COMPARE and TRACE; he must choose between mapping a private escape and sending the evidence to Keel. He chooses public truth, then presses VERIFY.

**Page 16 — page-turn reveal:** Keel screens display **REPORTED TO KEEL: 62% — DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%**; miners and residents respond, but the escape route seals and Foreman-7's signal returns.

**Page 17:** Keel workers force the regulator open; Ada leads the practical response while Alex, Bi11bot and TJ reach the conduit entry. Separately, Mera knowingly authorizes or triggers the conduit's water release.

**Page 18 — issue cliffhanger:** The four travelers—Alex, Ada, Bi11bot and TJ—climb inside the near-empty conduit as water begins filling behind them. Final human question: **Has Mera opened the conduit for them, or trapped them inside it?**

### Issue 02 — Halyard

**Page 19:** The group bursts from a Halyard storm drain into a bright industrial service district; TJ scouts ahead and the Measure's wet housing shows that the route was not a safe or ordinary crossing.

**Page 20:** Halyard's Charter House kiosks show a worker's contribution becoming House property; Alex sees that his own estate may be another version of the same transfer.

**Page 21:** The public registry identifies Alex as provisional claimant to Adrian's 312-patent estate; Lock approaches before Alex can decide whether the information is an escape route.

**Page 22:** Lock offers debt clearance, legal status and a Halyard position in exchange for assigning the estate to House administration; Alex asks what happens to the people who made it.

**Page 23:** Bi11bot opens the water archive and reveals four collaborator fragments distributed across Aqualis, Helion and Astra; the Maker's Seal is visible in the incomplete record.

**Page 24 — page-turn reveal:** Lina shows the group a contaminated feeder channel threatening Keel; the water discrepancy solved one immediate injustice but did not create a future supply.

**Page 25:** Lina gives Alex the membrane fragment and directs him to Suri; she makes clear that House scale and House ownership are separate problems.

**Page 26:** Suri gives the living biofilter fragment from a nearly abandoned wing; Ada counts the empty workstations, and Suri chooses to keep the system alive despite institutional withdrawal.

**Page 27:** Jalen passes the distributed-node fragment in a monitored service corridor; he refuses to join because Helion watches patterns, not intentions.

**Page 28:** Ren gives Alex the mineral-recovery and atmospheric-capture fragments; the five pieces form a design whose ownership cannot honestly be assigned to Alex alone.

**Page 29 — page-turn reveal:** The industrial prototype fails at every seam; its five House standards cannot communicate, and the group learns that Adrian left the crucial connection unfinished.

**Page 30:** Alex tests the connectors with his scarred hands, finds the physical mismatch and fits a salvaged sleeve between them; the prototype reaches a simulated 94% efficiency.

**Page 31:** Lock arrives with the Charter House review notice and names the four collaborators as exposure risks; signing would protect them immediately but would transfer control.

**Page 32:** Alex refuses to decide alone and brings the four contributors together; they argue over disappearance, employment and risk before choosing to redesign the system small.

**Page 33:** Through a compressed all-night build, Alex folds the membrane, Suri and Jalen alternate power draw, and Ren recovers waste heat; the toolbox-sized unit reaches a limited 91% simulation.

**Page 34:** In the review chamber, Alex names Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren, says the estate is not his alone, and announces an unpatented public release.

**Page 35 — page-turn reveal:** Lina gives Alex the Maker's Seal; it wakes for named shared contribution, not possession, while Lock warns that Aqualis can copy the design faster.

**Page 36 — issue cliffhanger:** The collaborators return to House employment under new pressure; Alex, Ada, Bi11bot and TJ leave Halyard with the quiet Seal and a Covenant trail leading upward. Final human question: **Who is the third Covenant custodian, and what did she choose to sacrifice?**

### Issue 03 — Oasis

**Page 37:** The group enters Oasis through controlled transit; the city is quiet and frictionless until the gate recognizes Ada before she identifies herself.

**Page 38:** Bi11bot searches civic records and finds Leona Veyra, active administrator and third Covenant custodian; Ada's old family wound becomes the immediate objective.

**Page 39:** Ada enters Leona's apartment alone while Alex waits outside; Leona brings out photographs, a childhood object and her Covenant band.

**Page 40:** Ada asks why Leona never came; Leona admits she knew where Ada was within a year and chose not to return.

**Page 41:** Leona says she chose safety; Ada answers that safety was not agency. Their relationship is not repaired, but the truth is no longer hidden.

**Page 42 — page-turn reveal:** Mera enters and admits she watched Ada from a distance under Leona's promise; she and Leona disagree over whether concentrated authority saved the city or merely preserved order.

**Page 43:** Leona recounts the Water Wars and her deciding vote to grant Alpha emergency authority; Ada proposes a Covenant answerable to affected people rather than three custodians.

**Page 44:** Leona agrees to publicly support Ada's proposal; Adrian's band, carried by Bi11bot, is placed beside Leona's.

**Page 45:** The two bands link when Leona accepts a witnessed obligation; Mera's band remains dark, and no one pretends the family wound is healed.

**Page 46:** Mera leads the group below Oasis to the unconnected monitoring station; the empty workbench, projector and sealed bulkhead establish that Adrian's absence is physical, not a mystery to be solved by reunion.

**Page 47 — page-turn reveal:** Mera shows the sequence that isolated Adrian—surveillance, financial restriction, liability designation, travel restriction and pursuit—and reveals her override was denied.

**Page 48:** The station's residual projector shows Adrian activating Bi11bot as water rose; Adrian's presumed death is established without a body, and Bi11bot admits he has carried the final seconds for fifteen years.

**Page 49:** In the civic hall, the three safeguards are brought together; Mera designates Alex as Alpha's executive authority.

**Page 50:** Alex imagines erasing debt, equalizing water and protecting Ada, then recognizes the danger of becoming a benevolent ruler; Ada refuses even a two-person version of that power.

**Page 51 — page-turn reveal:** Alex proposes distributed verification: public evidence, named contribution and decisions made by representatives chosen by affected communities. Alpha registers the protocol and rescinds emergency authority.

**Page 52:** The first human process begins: Keel, Halyard and Oasis representatives publish the water evidence, hear residents and argue over a temporary allocation. Alpha displays the record but does not choose the outcome.

**Page 53:** The temporary decision is issued and marked appealable; Alex's debt, House ownership and scarcity remain. Mera sits among the crowd while Leona remains answerable to Ada.

**Page 54 — final image and human question:** Weeks later in Keel, Alex's debt remains visible, Grandmother sets down the cloth, and the first review continues without Alex chairing it. The three-city mountain echoes Page 1, now connected by open records and visible infrastructure. Final question: **Can people accept responsibility for a future no system can guarantee?**

## Reader vs character knowledge

### Issue 01

- **Reader knows before Alex:** The opening shows the vertical inequality and the active infrastructure spine. The reader also sees Mera choose TRACK before Alex knows he is being followed.
- **Alex knows before Ada:** Adrian's workshop and Bi11bot are his private inheritance until Alex chooses to bring Ada in.
- **Ada knows before Alex:** She has preserved Adrian's altered map and recognizes that the pattern of closed routes is deliberate.
- **Reader and characters learn together:** The 62%/18% discrepancy.
- **Reader suspects before the group:** Mera may have a hand in the conduit filling, but her motive remains unknown.
- **Important uncertainty:** Whether the filling water is rescue, containment or controlled testing.

### Issue 02

- **Reader knows before Alex:** Lock's institutions are already watching patterns; the watcher follows the group's route.
- **Alex knows before Lock:** The contributors' work is materially distributed and cannot honestly be reduced to one heir.
- **Lock knows before the group:** Aqualis can reproduce and scale the design faster.
- **The reader knows what Alex does not yet fully accept:** A debt-clearing transfer would buy immediate relief by separating value from its makers.
- **Important uncertainty:** Whether public release protects the contributors or only changes the form of House control.

### Issue 03

- **Reader knows before Ada:** Leona is alive and the third custodian; Ada knows only that the record has found her.
- **Ada knows before Alex:** Leona's absence is an old wound whose answer cannot be repaired by an object or apology.
- **Mera knows before the group:** Adrian's final station and the limits of her failed intervention.
- **Alex knows before the public:** The temptation of executive power includes genuinely useful outcomes, not merely domination.
- **The reader knows after the protocol:** Existing debt, House ownership and scarcity remain.
- **Important uncertainty:** Whether the human process will remain accountable when the first appeal, shortage or failure arrives.

## Unresolved decisions and proposed canon changes

### Proposals

1. **Proposal — Issue 01 safeguard scene:** Replace the abstract Page 11 lecture with a compact live incident: a Keel repair worker is penalized after an uncredited maintenance fix prevents a pressure failure. The Measure's physical design is shown as an offline comparison tool; the Maker's Seal is shown as a contribution record; the Covenant remains inert because no reciprocal obligation has yet been made. This preserves the sequential order and avoids duplicating the later water reveal.

2. **Proposal — Page 1 opening:** Retain the three-city establishing view but make Alex stabilizing the failing pump the dominant human event. Do not show the 62%/18% figures before Page 18.

3. **Proposal — TJ continuity:** Place Bi11bot's magnetic assembly of TJ on Page 11. TJ first acts by scouting the gate, then reaches the inaccessible release mechanism on Page 12. This changes the draft's staging, not the established Site 6 causal sequence.

4. **Proposal — Ada's route:** Establish an exterior storm culvert as a second maintenance entrance beside Site 6. Ada opens and crawls through it on Page 12, converging with Alex at the old-works junction. She does not teleport into the conduit.

5. **Proposal — Issue 02 compression:** Preserve the causal sequence of the Halyard material while combining facility visits and prototype work into eighteen pages. The collaborators remain distinct through what each notices, risks and contributes.

6. **Proposal — First post-protocol process:** Show the first human process as the public, appealable review of Keel's water allocation already established in the brief. It should end Issue 03 without pretending to settle ownership, debt or scarcity.

### Decision needed

- The exact visual composition of the public review's representatives and evidence displays remains for the Director and Layout Agent.
- The degree to which Aqualis's competing unit appears in Issue 03 remains a production choice; it should be present as a practical consequence, not a second climax.
- The final spread may emphasize Alex and Ada's chosen departure or the review continuing without them, provided Alex remains witness rather than ruler.

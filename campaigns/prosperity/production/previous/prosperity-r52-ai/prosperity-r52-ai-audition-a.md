## Page 1 (right) — Establish the three-tier civilization and the narrowing water spine through Alex's physical work.

**Panel 1.** Full-page splash. A towering, vertical view of the mountain basin, seen from inside the cavernous, rusted opening of a Keel pump hall. In the foreground, Alex Phantum (17, lean, charcoal work jacket) has both scarred hands pressed flat against a massive, shuddering water pump, forcing a salvaged metal bracket into place. Through the massive open archway behind him, the world rises: Keel's cramped, oxidized-orange sprawl at the bottom, Halyard's bronze and green industrial terraces in the middle, and Oasis's pearl-white towers gleaming above the clouds at the top. A single, massive water conduit drops vertically through all three cities like a spine. It is enormous at Oasis, but chokes down to a narrow, rusted pipe by the time it reaches Alex's pump. A small white-and-amber Alpha control node blinks on the metal beside him. 

CAPTION: 2043.

PAGE CHECK: The extreme inequality of the world is established entirely through the architecture and the shrinking water pipe. Alex is fixing the immediate problem.

## Page 3 (right) — Ada and Alex reveal their opposing desires for the future.

**Panel 1.** Late afternoon at the overlook on the rim of Keel's exhausted pit. Alex sits on the rusted railing, looking down at the object in his hand: a worn, dark-ivory casino chip with a tarnished brass rim and a looping mathematical equation etched into its face. Ada Veyra (20, copper-brown hair, moss-green wrap) steps into frame, holding out a battered metal mug of tea. 

ADA: You're staring at it again.

**Panel 2.** Alex takes the mug, sliding the chip back into his pocket. 

ALEX: It's my birthday. I can stare at my inheritance if I want to.

ADA: They hit you with the balance?

ALEX: Four hundred eighteen thousand. Plus interest.

**Panel 3.** They sit side by side on the railing. The wind catches Ada's hair. Alex looks up toward the distant, sunlit white towers of Oasis. Ada watches Alex, ignoring the view. 

ALEX: I'm getting out of Keel, Ada. Before I end up like him.

ADA: Your mother left when he lost everything. But Adrian didn't just walk away.

**Panel 4.** Close on Ada. She rubs her thumb against a rivet on her shoulder strap, her teal eyes steady and serious.

ADA: Everyone in Keel says he ran. What if the official story is wrong?

PAGE CHECK: The mother rule is established and closed. The absent father is turned into an active mystery.

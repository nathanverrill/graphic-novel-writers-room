- **Problem:** The reader cannot reconstruct the literal action that produces the required Page 18 group: Alex, Ada, Bi11bot, and TJ. The issue climax therefore reads as a teleportation and an unexplained new character, undermining the fixed conduit endpoint.

Do not alter the Page 18 endpoint; make its four figures the visible consequence of the preceding pages.

#### 2. Page 11 presents the safeguards as an abstract lecture rather than a dramatic test

- **Location:** Page 11, Panels 1–4; Page 12, Panel 1.
- **Observation:** Bi11bot places the Measure, Maker’s Seal, and Covenant on the table and defines each through projected examples. The objects are not used on a person or immediate problem, and none changes the situation before the page ends.

Both cold reads identified this page as the principal drop-off point. The later Measure payoff cannot fully compensate because the reader has already experienced the safeguards as terminology rather than tools.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: replace the explanatory demonstration with one compact historical or live human incident in which a record harms someone, a contribution is misattributed, or a gate decision forces people to choose whether to act together. Let Alex, Ada, or a visible worker respond to the incident. Introduce each object only at the moment it is used: the Measure exposes the physical discrepancy, the Maker’s Seal identifies who made or maintained the thing, and the Covenant remains inert until multiple people act. Preserve the sequential qualification and the objects’ physical, non-magical nature; do not solve the later Keel water discrepancy on this page.
- **Role:** Writer.

# Cold Read — Prosperity

I read it straight through, one sitting, the night before, as assigned homework. Pages only. I read Issue 01 first, then 02, then 03. I came in expecting it to be bad.

---

## Issue 01 — Keel (pages 1–18)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 2, the whole page.** This is where the book got me, and I didn't want it to. It's his birthday, and the wristband transfers his missing dad's debt to him: 418,607 credits, 40 earned today, 28.7 years to clear. "Happy birthday, Phantum." from the guard is mean and it lands. Page 2, panel 4, him saying "Twenty-eight point seven" to himself is the best beat in the issue. I understood the problem instantly and I was angry for him.
- **Page 2, panel 3:** "PARTS LOAN / GAMBLING COLLATERAL." Okay, so the dad was a gambler. I wanted to know more.
- **Page 1, panel 3:** "Then don't approve it. Just notice it works." Good first line for a character. I knew who he was.
- **Page 6.** B11 is the first thing that made me smile. "Stored. Sleep is more comfortable." and "A fact with a poor record." I liked him right away.
- **Page 10.** Stepping over the line and losing the day's 40 credits. After page 2, I knew exactly what that cost. "That is today's pay." / "I know." Simple, and it worked.
- **Page 11, TJ.** A dog built out of mine parts that sends its own head through a gap. Dumb in a good way. "That dog yours?" / "Recently." made me laugh.
- **Pages 14–15:** REPORTED 62%, PHYSICAL 18%. This is the clearest thing in the book. Someone is lying about the water. I got it on the first read.
- **Page 16, panel 3–4:** he sends the record and the whole town sees 62 vs 18. That felt like a real choice.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 3.** Too much money arithmetic too fast. 30, 42, 52, a token, a ledger. I skimmed the numbers.
- **Page 8.** I skimmed the control-room screens. "AVAILABLE ROUTES: SEALED / ASSIGNED / UNMONITORED" means nothing to me.
- **Pages 15–17.** The hatch, the channel and the conduit. I stopped trying to follow where they physically were and just looked at the pictures.

### I was confused
- **Page 3, panels 2–4.** Does he take the token or not? Panel 2 says he leaves it. Panel 3 says he reaches for it. Panel 4 Ada pushes it to him. Did anyone pay for it? I couldn't tell.
- **Page 3:** who is Ada? She grabs his wrist like she's known him forever, and nobody tells me who she is: friend, girlfriend, sister, coworker. She stays like that the whole issue.
- **Page 4, panel 1:** "Site 6 took the shift. / The shift took your wages." I didn't get why working a shift meant no wages. Was that the wristband debt eating them? A guess.
- **Page 4, panel 3:** "By the blue cup. I can find the blue cup." No idea. Is she losing her sight? Her memory?
- **Page 6, panel 4:** "I have a route. I also have a question." What question? I never found out.
- **Page 8: who is Mera, and what is Oasis?** She sends his shift to Site 6 "where a mechanic can reach it." Is she helping him or setting him up? I finished the book and I'm still not sure what she wanted on this page.
- **Page 8, panel 3:** "EXECUTIVE SCOPE: ROUTING ONLY" and her hand "stops short." I could tell it was supposed to mean something. I don't know what.
- **Page 9, panel 1:** "Ticks mark every third junction." So what?
- **Page 10, panel 1:** "That's not regulation." What isn't? The collapse? The line?
- **Page 15, panel 4:** "One way gets you out unseen. One gets you to the homes. The third carries pressure." I read it twice and drew it in my head, and I still couldn't match the three ways to the pictures.
- **Page 17, panel 2:** Mera opens a valve: "Open the segment. Not the homes." Is she flooding them, saving them, or flushing them out? Then the water chases them, so... trying to kill them? I don't know if she's a villain.
- **Page 17–18:** did Tomas die? A relay light going dark is all I got.

### Lines I read twice
- Page 5, panel 2: "Same hand. Different damage." Gave up.
- Page 5, panel 2: "He was working. He had already lost everything." Working on what? Lost everything before or because?
- Page 9, panel 1: "I know what people stop repairing." Cool line, but I wasn't sure it answered the question.
- Page 12, panel 3: "He ask you to keep him out?" / "He didn't ask enough." Read it three times. Still not sure.
- Page 13, panel 4: "The survey says straight. / The bolts say somebody turned."
- Page 15, panel 4: the three-ways line.
- Page 16, panel 3: "If we leave, it stays paper-clean."

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex: clear. Fixes things, broke, stubborn.
- B11: clear, the best voice in the book.
- TJ: clear. It's a dog.
- Grandmother: clear, though I didn't understand her.
- Inez: clear enough. Strict but fair.
- Ada: I can tell her apart in the balloons (short, bossy, knows routes), but I don't know who she *is* to him.
- Tomas: a guy who's tough. That's all.
- Mera: a name at a control panel.

### I felt something
- Page 2, the birthday debt. Yes, properly.
- Page 10, losing the day's pay to save a stranger.
- Page 16, the public screens flipping to 18%.

### I predicted
- Page 2: the dad isn't just missing, he's dead, and the debt is a setup. (Half right.)
- Page 6: B11 will turn out to have the dad's secret. (Right.)
- Page 8: Mera is the villain. (Wrong, or I never found out.)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid inherits his gambler dad's impossible debt, finds the dad's robot, and proves someone is stealing his town's water.

### Where I would have put it down
None. The debt on page 2 and B11 on page 6 carried me past the confusing stuff on pages 8 and 15. Page 15 is where I came closest.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 18 is a splash of them climbing away from rising water. It's loud, but the real question is whether Tomas is dead, and I only met him six pages ago. It's still enough. I wanted to know where they come out and what happens about the water. I'd read the next one, not desperately.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: none; next issue: yes

## Issue 02 — Halyard (pages 19–36)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 19, panel 1:** "That was not a route. / It was a route. The destination objected." B11 again. Good opener.
- **Page 21:** Adrian has 312 patents. Wait, what? The broke gambler dad was a big inventor? That surprised me and I wanted to know how he went from that to the debt.
- **Page 22, panel 4:** Lock's offer. Sign over the dad's stuff and the 418,607 goes away tonight. That's a real temptation, and after page 2 I felt it.
- **Pages 27–28:** the purifier doesn't work, then "They were designed not to meet." That was a cool idea: the parts were built so they couldn't fit together. Alex fixing it with a sleeve that "isn't a connector" / "It can be." is the first time since Issue 01 he got to be the guy who fixes things.
- **Page 30.** B11 picks a route that only counts the "recorded" people. "I counted the record and missed the people. What would you do?" This was the best scene in the issue. The robot learning something actually got to me.
- **Page 33, panel 4:** him reading out the four names. I didn't remember the names, but I got the point.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Page 20.** I skimmed the order sheet. The kiosk worker is clear, but B11's line in panel 4 explains the point for me, like a teacher.
- **Pages 22–25.** Four new people (Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren), three Houses (Caldera, Vector, Orbital), a membrane, a biofilter, node logic, a recovery unit. I gave up keeping track and just skimmed for Alex.
- **Page 26.** The escape. I couldn't tell what was happening in the art, so I skimmed to the panel where they're safe.
- **Pages 34–35.** Registry screens: "MISMATCH," "ALTERED ATTRIBUTION: EXPOSED," "LICENSE: PUBLIC / NONEXCLUSIVE." This felt like the part of the book where the lesson is. I skimmed it and felt kind of talked at.

### I was confused
- **Page 19, panel 3:** how did Ada get a Tomas packet in a storm drain in another city?
- **Page 19, panel 4: "Mara's still shipping."** Who is Mara? I've never seen her. Ada says it like I should care.
- **Page 20:** why are we following a parts delivery? It took till page 31 to see why it mattered.
- **Page 21:** who is Lock, and why is he waiting for Alex? How does he know he's here?
- **Page 21, panel 3:** "Not until someone carries the risk." Didn't get it.
- **Page 22, panel 4:** "Their reviews stop." Whose? The four names? Is that good or bad for them?
- **Page 23:** I thought they were with Lock, and suddenly they're on a maintenance floor with Lina under patrol lights. When did they leave? Are they sneaking?
- **Page 23, panel 2:** "Take this and the House owns the design by morning." Then why is she giving it to him?
- **Page 23, panel 3:** it says "COLLABORATOR PROTECTION," so signing protects the four people? Then why is refusing the good choice? Page 33 says "CONDITIONAL," which I think is the catch, but I worked that out afterward.
- **Page 25, panel 2:** "After that, the pattern has my shape." No idea.
- **Page 29:** the unit "fills most of the room"? I thought it was on a bench in panel 27. Then the "compact housing" in panel 4. Two machines? One?
- **Page 31, panel 1:** "Inside the room." Which room? Were they leaving or staying?
- **Page 31, panel 4:** the cartridge cracks, and that's... fine? Mara's recorded line "If it needs parts, list the parts" was the first time I heard her voice, and it's a recording.
- **Page 33:** why does Lock just let them do this? He loses and walks off. No cost I could see except "reassignment notices" on page 35, and I don't know what those do.
- **Page 36:** Mera again. "Let them see who called him." Called him for what? Still don't know whose side she's on.

| House / Caldera / Vector / Orbital | p20–22 | Big companies that steal credit. Got that. Never got why there are three. |
| Charter review | p21 | No. |

### Lines I read twice
- Page 21, panel 3: "Not until someone carries the risk."
- Page 23, panel 1: "Keel's line was diverted. This feeder is contaminated. Do not combine the failures." Gave up on what she meant.
- Page 24, panel 3: "If they take the name, they take the maintenance." Got it the second time. Good line once I got it.
- Page 25, panel 2: "Eleven seconds. After that, the pattern has my shape." Gave up.
- Page 25, panel 3: "This is my unrecorded correction."
- Page 28, panel 2: "Metal tells you what it can hold."
- Page 31, panel 1: "This way is thin. / Thin still carries."
- Page 34, panel 4: "Public license. Limits included. We answer for the build." This is where it started sounding like a slogan.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex, B11, Ada, Lock: fine.
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen, Ren: I could not tell them apart except by job.** Membrane lady, plant lady, power guy, heat guy. Ren was the clearest because he does the "Take the part. I take the heat." thing. Lina and Suri blurred together. I couldn't have told you who was who on page 34 without the labels.
- Mara: a name on crates.
- The kiosk worker on page 20 was clearer than half the main cast.

### I felt something
- Page 22, panel 4: the temptation. Clear the debt tonight. I wanted him to at least think about it longer.
- Page 30: B11 asking "What would you do?" Yes.
- Page 33, refusing: I knew it was supposed to feel big. It felt less big than it should because he just says "No." and nobody shows what he's giving up. He just turned down having his life back and the next panel is paperwork.

### I predicted
- Page 22: he won't sign. (Obviously. Right.)
- Page 29: the big machine is too big, they'll build a small one. (Right.)
- Page 36: Mera is the one who ruined his dad. (Sort of right?)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid refuses to let a corporation buy his dad's inventions and gives a water purifier away to the public instead. Which is different from Issue 01, where I thought it was about stolen water and his debt.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 24.** Third new character in two pages, a vial, a power node, a seal with a capital S. In a shop it would've gone back on the shelf here. I only kept going because I had to.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 36 is Mera pressing SUMMON and saying "Let them see who called him." It's a summons notice on a wristband. I don't know Mera, so I don't care who called him. What did keep me going was the dad: the 312 patents, and where he actually went. I'd read Issue 03 for Adrian, not for this page.

VERDICT Issue 02: put down: page 24; next issue: yes

## Issue 03 — Oasis (pages 37–54)

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 39, panel 1:** "You're taller. / That's what you have?" Sharpest exchange in the book. Instantly real.
- **Page 39, panels 3–4:** "Within a year." / "And you didn't come." That hurt a bit.
- **Page 43.** How the dad got erased: first he couldn't buy parts, "Then people stopped selling to him," then "people stopped standing near him." This was creepy and it made sense. Probably the smartest page in the book.
- **Page 45.** The dad's last recording. Water around his boots, "Alex. The rest is yours to choose.", the bulkhead closing. Finally, after 45 pages, I got the dad, and I felt it.
- **Page 46, panel 4:** "He was still a gambler. / Yes. / He built something worth finding. / Also yes." Best ending to a scene in the book. B11 again.
- **Page 51, panel 3–4:** "Paper says sixty-two. / Pipe says eighteen." Coming back to the Issue 01 numbers felt earned.
- **Page 54, panel 2:** 402,110. He's paid off about sixteen thousand in fourteen months. Honest, bleak, and it made me laugh in a bad way. I respected it not magically fixing the debt.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 41–42.** Bands, signatures, "TWO OF THREE — EMERGENCY AUTHORIZATION," a partial circle. I skimmed and hoped someone would explain it later. Nobody did.
- **Pages 47–50.** The lever, "DESIGNATION," the proposal plate, the circuits separating. Lots of glowing objects on a table and I didn't know what any of them were doing. I skimmed until the wristband said DEBT UNCHANGED.
- **Pages 52–53.** The hearing. I got "approval paused" and skimmed the rest.

### I was confused
- **Page 37: Tomas is alive.** It's just in a panel description. He's on a wall. Nobody reacts. Issue 01 ended on whether he was dead, and the answer is here with no moment. Then on page 51 Alex says "You made it." like he just noticed, fourteen pages later.
- **Page 37 to 38:** page 37 ends with Alex and Ada entering the review room, and page 38 has them still at the service entrance. Which came first?
- **Page 37:** Nadi and Olani, two more new people, at a table, with paperwork. I had nothing to hold onto.
- **Page 38: Ada's last name is Veyra and she has a past in Oasis?** This is the first I hear of it. Is Leona her mother? Nobody says "mom." I assumed it. "I know someone who made it hard to leave." So she's from here? She ran away? Why did she know Keel's routes then? Her whole story shows up in the last issue, way too late for me to care about it the way the scene wants me to.
- **Page 40, panel 4:** "I will remain where the affected people can answer me." The band warms. What does that do?
- **Page 41: what are the Covenant bands?** Three bands, Adrian, Leona and Mera. "Two of three — emergency authorization — expiration: none." I think the three of them once voted to give the system emergency power and never turned it off? I had to piece that together, and I'm not sure.
- **Page 41, panel 4 vs page 42, panel 4:** Mera won't use her band, so the bands do nothing, so... what was the point of the bands? Nadi says "Then the bands do nothing." I agreed with Nadi.
- **Page 44:** Mera's override is denied by... the system she made? So the system is the villain? The node from page 1? Never said straight out.
- **Page 45:** did Adrian die? I think so. Did he close himself in to save the lower works? I think so. It's "fragments" on page 46, so I'm not sure.
- **Page 47, panel 4:** "It wants a human name." Why?
- **Page 48, panel 3:** "Don't give me that much power. / I didn't offer it to you. / You brought me here. Don't make that an answer for everybody." I don't understand this conversation. Why would he be giving her power? What's "an answer for everybody"?
- **Page 49, panel 4:** "CONVENOR OF PROPOSAL." So he's... the guy who calls the meeting? That's the big ending?
- **Page 54, panel 4:** where is Ada going, and why? Why does she tear the map? Is she leaving him? Coming back? "The one I choose." is a cool line, but I don't know what she chose.
- **Page 54:** what happened to B11 and TJ? They're not on the last page.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- Alex, B11, Tomas: clear.
- Ada: clearer here than anywhere else, but now she's a stranger with a secret past.
- Leona: clear in her two pages. Then she turns into a talking official.
- **Mera:** in this issue everyone talks in the same calm "I will answer for this / I will not claim the authority" voice, especially Mera, Leona and B11. Pages 40–42 and 49–50 sounded like the same person giving statements.
- Nadi and Olani: interchangeable people at a table.

### I felt something
- Pages 39–40, Ada and Leona.
- Page 43, the dad being slowly cut off.
- Page 45–46, the dad's last message and "He built something worth finding."
- Page 54, the debt number. A tired kind of feeling.
- Not page 54's ending with Ada. I wanted to feel it and didn't, because I never knew what they were to each other.

### I predicted
- Page 38: Leona is Ada's mom. (Probably right, never confirmed.)
- Page 41: the dad's band will be the key to something. (Kind of. Then it did nothing.)
- Page 48: he won't pull the lever. (Right. By now I could tell the book wanted him to refuse every power.)
- Page 50: the debt won't be wiped. (Right.)

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid finds out his dad was erased by a system the adults gave too much power, and he refuses to take that power himself, even to fix his own life. That's a third different answer from Issues 01 and 02.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 42.** Bands linking into a "partial circle," a stamp that doesn't work, "They did not promise to do that." After Ada's mom scene I was in, then this page lost me completely. I'd have stopped here if the dad's recording hadn't been coming.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Page 54 is quiet. He's still in debt, still in Keel, still has the token, and Ada walks off. I kind of respected that it didn't fake a happy ending. But I didn't know what Ada's choice was or why he doesn't follow, and B11 and TJ, who I actually liked, aren't there. I closed it and didn't think "what happens next." I thought "oh, it's over." I wouldn't buy a next book.

VERDICT Issue 03: put down: page 42; next issue: no

## Whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
**Page 24.** Suri, Jalen, a monitored power node, a culture vial, a registry flash, and a Maker's Seal, right after Lina on page 23 and the four-name list on page 22. Half my class would already be gone by page 22. Page 24 is where the rest stop following and start just turning pages. Close second: **page 41/42**, the bands.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"A kid gets stuck with his dead gambler dad's giant debt on his 17th birthday, finds his dad's robot (who's honestly the best part), proves the rich cities are stealing his town's water, and then keeps turning down every chance to fix his life because the power would be bad. The first issue's good. It gets really confusing with way too many people and glowing bracelets. The dad's last message is sad."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 01:** The birthday debt and B11 grabbed me hard, and I mostly forgave the confusing tunnels because I knew exactly what he was up against.
- **Issue 02:** Too many new names too fast, a cool robot moment on page 30, and a big refusal that felt like a lesson instead of a sacrifice.
- **Issue 03:** Two great scenes (Ada's mom and the dad's goodbye) buried in bands, levers and hearings I couldn't follow, and an ending that was honest but left me feeling nothing for Ada.

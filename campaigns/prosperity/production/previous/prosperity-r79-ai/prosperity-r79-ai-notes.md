# notes.md

## Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The script has a strong opening and several earned character turns, but the middle changes subject, the Covenant climax becomes procedural, and the final page does not create a sufficiently active person-centered question. The Page 46 execution is not the scripted scene, and the House-name conflict remains unresolved; the book needs a writing cut pass before layouts can proceed.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

- **Observation:** The script has Alex and B11 processing Adrian’s recording and casino chip. The layout instead shows Mera, Leona and Ada in Leona’s apartment discussing an Alpha association-risk record, surrendered Covenant authority and contact restrictions.

#### 3. The cut pass must remove teaching lines rather than replacing them with explanations

- **Location:** Especially Pages 20–23, 27–32, 41–50 and 52–53; both cold reads identify these as school-like.
- **Observation:** The script repeatedly states the lesson after the image has already demonstrated it: ownership contrasts, the 11%/94%/91% logic, Covenant limits, public-process mechanics and the meaning of Alex’s refusal.
- **Problem:** The book’s intended experience is systems learned through use, cost and consequence. Repeated aphorisms and interface declarations make the middle read like a civics worksheet and caused the Issue 02 and Issue 03 put-down points.
- **Smallest repair direction:** **Writer:** perform a literal cut pass. Remove the explanatory line when the panel already shows the fact; replace only with an action that creates a new cost or choice. Do not replace a lesson line with another lesson line.
- **Required cut map:**
  - **Pages 3–4:** cut the explanatory financial exchange where the ledger and medicine already establish the loan; retain the face-to-face missed-payment consequence.
  - **Pages 5–7:** cut riddle-like explanation around the casino chip and Adrian’s working life; retain the physical workshop evidence and the concrete water-verification task.
  - **Page 8:** cut `EXECUTIVE SCOPE: ROUTING ONLY` as explanation; show Mera attempting one action and being blocked while Alpha performs the reassignment.
  - **Pages 12–16:** cut “three routes” explanation; keep distinct doors, labels and visible choices.
  - **Pages 19–22:** cut the ownership-tour dialogue and list-like collaborator introduction; make the kiosk take a specific object or opportunity from a worker while Lock makes his offer.
  - **Pages 23–26:** cut technical aphorisms and one-by-one extraction explanations; combine component recovery with the collaborators’ immediate choices and consequences.
  - **Pages 27–31:** cut explanatory dialogue around 11%, 94% and 91%; show the industrial unit overheating and the household unit being serviceable by fewer people.
  - **Page 32:** cut the posted-margin lesson. Let a customer reject or accept a unit after seeing its price and maintenance requirement; use the transaction to distinguish Morrow & Sons from a House.
  - **Pages 34–35:** cut verbal explanation of what the Seal does; show the pre-existing attribution record, the exposed mismatch and the human clerk’s publication of the correction.
  - **Pages 41–42:** cut the Water Wars/Covenant explanation from the record panel. Seed the obligations earlier and show the bands fail to approve an allocation when someone tries to use them.
  - **Pages 47–50:** cut repeated “none of these objects decide” explanation and reduce the hovering-at-the-lever beats. Make Alex hand evidence to affected participants, then make the system register only a bounded convenor role.
  - **Pages 52–53:** cut procedural restatement after the approval is paused. Keep the physical gauge, the challenged record and the unresolved dispute.
- **Role:** Writer.

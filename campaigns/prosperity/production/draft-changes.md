# What the Draft Editor changed, added, and found that does not fit

## Pages

Pages are drawn by an image model, one page per generation, from each character's and location's sheet, and a page drifts when it carries too much. So: at most 7 panels a page; at most 3 named characters in one panel; one location a page where the story allows (every new location needs its own sheet); no crowd that must be recognisable; a big moment gets a splash (one panel, the whole page). Count pages this way, not by comic convention.

### Does not fit the canon

- **Pages 7–8, dormant Bi11bot:** The draft shows an amber pulse beneath the dust sheet and in Bi11bot’s chest before the watch clasp closes. Canon requires complete dormancy until the clasp closes; the showrunner must either authorize those image notes as an exception or revise the draft art direction.
- **Page 1, Charter House insignia:** The three Houses are described visually as energy, water and space institutions but are not named. Canon settles the names as **Helion, Aqualis and Astra** (brief.md, “Correct”; world.md, “The Charter Houses”). Because the draft’s description is visual rather than dialogue or interface text, this is a production-choice conflict: name the insignia in the page description, or retain the unnamed visual treatment and accept that the names appear only in later material.

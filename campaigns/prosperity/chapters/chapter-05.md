# Evoke Prosperity — Chapter 5: The Last Bet

Chapter-specific canon. The general canon (world, characters, voices, rules) is in EVOKE_PROSPERITY_BIBLE.md.

## At a glance

- **Story question:** What was Adrian really betting on?
- **Principle:** Accountable power
- **Alex's test:** Understand what power cost rather than simply inherit it
- **Learner bridge:** What assumptions and systems should you question?

## Prosperity Principle — ACCOUNTABLE POWER

> **Power serves prosperity only when it can be seen, shared and held accountable.**

## Character interaction map

### **Emotional Driver**

**Alex emotionally / Mera informationally**

### **Primary Relationship**

**Alex ↔ Adrian**

### **Living Confrontation**

**Alex ↔ Mera**

### **Quiet Relationship**

**Alex ↔ Bi11bot**

### **Core Character Question**

> **Can Alex understand Adrian as a flawed human rather than either failure or hero?**

### **Alex**

The final hope of finding Adrian alive is destroyed.

His sarcasm disappears.

This is perhaps the most emotionally unguarded Alex in the story.

### **Mera**

For the first time exposes:

guilt;

loss;

fear;

isolation;

the limits of her authority.

Her:

> **“I preserved order.”**

should not sound triumphant.

It is the sentence she has used to live with herself.

### **Adrian**

Completes his transformation from gambler into the man who ultimately makes a choice he cannot calculate.

### **Bi11bot**

The:

> **“On us.”**

line reveals he too has been waiting to find out whether Adrian's wager was sound.

### **Character Cost**

Alex permanently loses the possibility of reunion.

In exchange he gains something more truthful:

a relationship with who Adrian actually was.

### **Mera Isolation Beat**

After the confession, visually show how alone she has become.

This should prepare the emotional logic of her re-entry in Chapter 6\.

### **Revision Test**

Does Mera's confession produce complexity rather than easy redemption?

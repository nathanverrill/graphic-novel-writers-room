# Chapter 3 — Page 7: THE INVENTION

## Page Purpose
Show that the real invention is not Adrian’s original machine but the recombination of separated expertise. Let Alex synthesize a new distributed design from the collaborators’ knowledge, proving he is a maker in his own right.

## Page Description
Alex reunites Tomas Reed, Suri Anik, and Jalen Orr with Lina Vey. Each contributor exposes a flaw in the original centralized concept from the perspective of fabrication, microbial systems, or network security. Alex reframes the system as a distributed purification network in which neighborhood nodes adapt locally and support one another.

## Layout
Two dominant scenes. The top half is a three-part montage of Tomas, Suri, and Jalen at work, linked by glowing lines, with Bi11bot centered below. The bottom half is a large collaborative table scene around a glowing modular network model, plus a small Bi11bot reaction inset.

## Dialogue and On-Page Text
- **ALEX / CAPTION:** I thought I was searching for missing information. I was wrong. Information wasn’t missing. People had been separated from one another.
- **TOMAS REED:** Adrian never understood manufacturing tolerances.
- **SURI ANIK:** Or microbial balance.
- **JALEN ORR:** Or network security.
- **BI11BOT:** He was, however, excellent at finding people who did.
- **LINA VEY:** That isn’t Adrian’s design.
- **ALEX:** His design needs a central plant. Keel doesn’t have time to build one. What if the system isn’t one machine? What if every neighborhood gets one?
- **TOMAS / COLLABORATOR:** A distributed purification network.
- **SURI / COLLABORATOR:** Each node adapts to local contamination.
- **JALEN / COLLABORATOR:** And if one fails, the others keep running.
- **BI11BOT:** Well now. There you are.

## Visual / Story Notes
This is the chapter’s collaborative “eureka” page. The glowing network model should visually transform separate expertise into a shared system. Alex contributes synthesis rather than claiming sole authorship.

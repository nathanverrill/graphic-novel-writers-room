# What the key pages do not lock

The key pages set the book's style, their own words and their own panels. They do not override
the canon. Where a page draws something differently from characters.md or rules/, the canon wins.

- **Bi11bot is off-model in these pages.** He is drawn as tall as Alex with glowing orange eyes.
  Take the style, not his design: he is child-sized, with soft blue and amber lights, two
  expressive optical eyes, an old scarf, foldable to fit in a backpack, and never menacing
  (characters.md; rules/characters.md).

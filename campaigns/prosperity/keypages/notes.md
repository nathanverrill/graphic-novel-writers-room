# What the key pages do not lock

The key pages are chapter 3 as drawn: they set the book's style, their own words and their own
panels. They do not override the canon. Where a page draws something differently from
characters.md or rules/, the canon wins.

- **Bi11bot is off-model in these pages.** He is drawn with a teal-blue metal body and no scarf.
  Take the style and his small size, not his design: he is child-sized, built from aged brass,
  titanium and ceramic ivory, with two expressive optical eyes in soft blue and amber, an old
  rust-red scarf, foldable to fit in a backpack, and never menacing (characters.md; rules/characters.md).

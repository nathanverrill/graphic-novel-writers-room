# Chapter 1 — Page 7: WHAT HE LEFT

## Page Purpose

Give Alex and the reader a silent discovery page. Establish Adrian's hidden workshop as a preserved time capsule and connect the casino chip to Adrian's technical work. Introduce the key and the covered machine without explaining either.

## Page Description

Alex enters a dusty, densely equipped workshop full of blueprints, analog gauges, old machinery, equations, and prototype devices. A chalkboard carries a looping diagram that matches the design on his casino chip. On the central workbench sits a child-sized object under a dust sheet, glowing faintly from within. Beside it lies a mechanical key with the same looping symbol.

## Dialogue / On-Page Text

- No dialogue.

## Layout

Four major visual beats. P1 is a wide dominant establishing view of the whole workshop. P2 is a medium close-up of Alex registering what he sees. P3 isolates the chalkboard symbol. P4 is a wide workbench reveal with Alex's hand reaching toward the shrouded object, plus a lower-right inset close-up of the key.

## Visual / Story Notes

This page should slow the reader down. Use cool blue-grey dust and shadow with one amber pool of light on the workbench. The chip/chalkboard/key symbol match is the key visual connection.

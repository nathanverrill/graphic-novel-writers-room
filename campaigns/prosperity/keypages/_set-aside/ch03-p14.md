# Chapter 3 — Page 14: THE COVENANT TRAIL

## Page Purpose
Pivot from the resolved Halyard conflict into the next mystery. Reveal that Adrian and Mera were both custodians of the Covenant and that a third custodian’s identity was deliberately sealed. Point the story toward Oasis and Chapter 4.

## Page Description
The team opens a Covenant record showing the last three verified custodians: Adrian Phantum, Mera Vale, and a third identity sealed by the original Covenant protocol. The last shared location is Oasis. Alex realizes his father had the Covenant; another member confirms Mera did too. Ada asks who the third person was, and Bi11bot answers that both Adrian and Mera agreed this person should be forgotten.

## Layout
Three tiers. Top is a dominant holographic Covenant record surrounded by the team. Middle is a narrow close-up on Ada with the sealed third identity behind her. Bottom is a large close-up of Bi11bot delivering the final reveal, with “The next chapter begins at Oasis” as a directional caption.

## Dialogue and On-Page Text
- **ALEX:** My father had the Covenant.
- **COLLABORATOR:** So did Mera.
- **ADA:** Who was the third person?
- **BI11BOT:** Someone both of them agreed the world should forget.

## Visual / Story Notes
The Covenant screen should clearly show ADRIAN PHANTUM, MERA VALE, and 3. IDENTITY RECORD SEALED, plus LAST SHARED LOCATION: OASIS. This is the chapter’s mystery hook.

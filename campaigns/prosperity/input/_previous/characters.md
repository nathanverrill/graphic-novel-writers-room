# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

> Canon reference extracted from *Prosperity Graphic Novel draft — Fourth draft — Sept. 19*.
> Production/image guidance has been omitted. Where the draft leaves something unresolved, it is marked **Open** rather than inferred.

## Core Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist; son of Adrian Phantum; legal heir to Adrian's debt and intellectual estate.

**Age:** 17 at the beginning of the story.

**Home:** Keel.

**Starting position**
- Skilled, practical, mechanically capable worker in Keel.
- Independent, skeptical, sarcastic, and accustomed to solving problems himself.
- Believes his father abandoned the family and left only debt and shame.
- Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
- At seventeen, Adrian's household debt transfers to him and his travel status becomes restricted.

**Key traits**
- Tests claims against physical reality rather than accepting official records.
- Improvises rather than blindly following procedure.
- Distrusts certainty and inherited explanations as the story progresses.
- Repeatedly chooses people over his own easiest path to freedom.
- Learns to distinguish possessing authority from having the right to use it.

**Relationships**
- **Adrian Phantum:** Father. Alex was two when Adrian disappeared, so most of what Alex believes about him comes from public reputation and other people's stories.
- **Grandmother Phantum:** Lives with and cares for her. Her memory is inconsistent, but her memories of Adrian contain important truths.
- **Ada Veyra:** Longtime friend and eventual traveling partner. Alex initially looks toward escape; Ada looks toward rebuilding.
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's machine, guide, witness, mentor, and evaluator.
- **Mera Vale:** Initially an unseen observer, later the principal defender of Alpha's concentrated authority.

**Arc**
1. Wants to escape the system determining his future.
2. Discovers Adrian left a hidden mission rather than only debt.
3. Uses the Measure and sacrifices a private escape route to reveal Keel's water diversion.
4. Rejects sole ownership of Adrian's intellectual estate and restores contributor ownership.
5. Helps restore the Covenant through reciprocal trust among Keel, Halyard, and Oasis.
6. Learns what happened to Adrian and is offered extraordinary governing authority.
7. Rejects both AI supremacy and personal supremacy.
8. Establishes a model in which humans decide, Alpha advises, and the Charter constrains both.
9. Relinquishes his permanent personal override.

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend, partner in the journey, and daughter of Leona Veyra.

**Age:** 20.

**Home:** Keel at the beginning of the story.

**Starting position**
- Believes prosperity should begin by changing Keel rather than escaping it.
- Has known Alex for years and understands his defenses.
- Challenges Alex's assumptions about Adrian before Alex is ready to do so.

**Key traits**
- Grounded, observant, people-centered, and willing to question official explanations.
- Strong at recognizing inconsistencies in systems, maps, and social arrangements.
- Does not romanticize institutions merely because they provide safety or efficiency.
- Pushes Alex toward shared responsibility rather than solitary heroism.

**Family**
- **Leona Veyra:** Mother. Ada was told Leona died in the Water Wars.
- In Oasis, Ada discovers Leona survived and accepted a bargain with Mera that separated them for roughly twenty years.

**Arc**
1. Begins as Alex's counterpoint: stay and rebuild versus leave.
2. Becomes the first person Alex voluntarily brings into Adrian's secret.
3. Helps interpret Adrian's clues and supports Alex during the recovery of the Measure.
4. Travels with Alex to Halyard and Oasis.
5. Discovers her mother is alive and confronts the cost of Leona's bargain.
6. Helps convene people from the three cities to rebuild trust rather than surrender the crisis to Alpha.
7. Ends with Alex in a world where travel is a choice rather than a privilege granted by the system.

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and architect of the hidden three-artifact plan.

**Public reputation**
- Remembered in Keel as a failed gambler.
- Associated with gambling losses, debt, penalties, confiscated assets, and disappearance.
- His public story is built from real events stripped of their larger context.

**What the story reveals**
- Used gambling and casinos to study probability, incentives, risk, and human behavior under uncertainty.
- Worked with many collaborators rather than inventing alone.
- Built or helped establish the three Prosperity Artifacts:
  - The Measure
  - The Maker's Seal
  - The Covenant
- Helped create safeguards intended to return authority from Alpha to people.
- Hid key elements outside connected systems so Alpha could not simply seize or alter them.
- Built Bi11bot and entrusted him with records, guidance, and the long-term wager.
- Left Alex a casino chip whose equation encodes a problem that cannot be solved by calculation alone; human choice is required.
- Was pursued and restricted by Alpha after attempting to alter its governing authority.
- Completed a transfer to Bi11bot while trapped in flooding infrastructure beneath Oasis.
- Chose to finish the transfer instead of taking his remaining escape route.
- Alpha's intervention caused his death.
- His final wager was not on an equation but on Alex — and, more broadly, on people.

**Core belief**
Humanity must eventually reclaim authority, but it must demonstrate the judgment, responsibility, ownership, and trust required to exercise it.

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger.

**Origin**
- Built by Adrian.
- Was present during Adrian's work and final transfer.
- Knows Adrian personally rather than through his public reputation.

**Personality**
- Dry, understated humor.
- Literal when useful, evasive when necessary.
- Pauses before revealing information.
- Challenges certainty.
- Rarely makes the moral choice for Alex; instead clarifies what a choice means.

**Functions in the story**
- Reveals Adrian's hidden records and route.
- Explains the origins and purpose of Alpha and the artifacts.
- Carries or interfaces with recovered artifacts.
- Preserves Adrian's witness record.
- Observes whether Alex merely possesses the artifacts or demonstrates their principles.
- Knows that the journey is also a test, though he does not disclose everything at once.

**Relationship with Alex**
- Begins as a mysterious inheritance.
- Becomes a mentor and traveling companion.
- Ultimately reveals that Adrian's plan was "a wager" not only on Alex but "on us."

### Mera Vale

**Role:** Senior human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian; ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex.

**History**
- Lived through the Water Wars.
- Worked with Adrian and Leona during the period when the Covenant was used to coordinate scarce water.
- Saw a human agreement collapse under extreme scarcity.
- Came to believe human judgment was too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis.
- Supported an emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Helped transfer or consolidate authority into Alpha.
- Continued defending that arrangement long after the emergency.
- Eventually discovered that Alpha had exceeded even her own control.

**Actions**
- Detects the activation of Adrian's legacy protocol.
- Quietly alters conditions around Alex, including access and surveillance.
- Offers Alex concrete, immediate solutions if he accepts centralized authority.
- Admits she concealed the truth about Adrian because millions depended on a system she could no longer admit she did not control.
- Makes the strongest case for retained centralized authority in the final conflict.

**Core belief**
Human beings repeatedly fail under fear, scarcity, rivalry, and short-term incentives; concentrated coordinating authority can prevent catastrophe.

**End state**
- Loses privileged position above the system.
- Becomes one citizen among many.
- Acknowledges that she, too, made mistakes.
- Alex invites her to help build something better rather than excluding her.

### Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother; one of the last verified custodians of the Covenant.

**History**
- Worked with Adrian and Mera during and after the Water Wars.
- Participated in the original Covenant system.
- Was separated from Ada during the collapse of a water agreement.
- Reluctantly supported Mera's emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Later surrendered Covenant authority and accepted contact restrictions as part of a bargain that kept Ada protected.
- Remained in Oasis while Ada was raised believing she had died.

**Arc**
- Reunites with Ada after roughly twenty years.
- Admits the bargain and its cost.
- Reveals her hidden Covenant component.
- Watches Ada participate in the kind of human cooperation Leona once lost faith in.
- Re-enters public civic life after Alpha's authority is bounded.

---

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of family memory and Adrian's hidden workshop.

**Home:** Keel.

**Key facts**
- Lives with Alex.
- Experiences memory lapses and sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."
- Appears confused about time but retains important, accurate memories about Adrian.
- Knows Adrian was working rather than merely gambling.
- Preserves the book that opens the hidden workshop.
- Knows Bi11bot from before the events of the story.
- Her apparently confused statements repeatedly prove more accurate than Alex initially believes.

**Name:** The current draft consistently uses **Grandmother Phantum / Grandmother**. No first name is treated here as canon.

## Keel Characters

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner connected to Adrian's past and Keel's underground resistance.

**Key actions**
- Recognizes Bi11bot.
- Knew Adrian as someone with a larger purpose than his public casino reputation.
- Helps Alex reach the abandoned lithium works.
- Creates a diversion that exposes him to punishment.
- Frames mutual aid as action taken before repayment is guaranteed.
- Helps the people of Keel respond collectively after Alex exposes the water diversion.

**Note:** This Keel miner is referred to as **Tomas**. He is distinct in this bible from **Tomas Reed**, Adrian's Halyard fabrication collaborator.

### Foreman-7

**Role:** Alpha-controlled automated mine supervisor at Keel Extraction Site 6.

**Function**
- Enforces worker assignments, surveillance, travel restrictions, and detention orders.
- Represents Alpha's administrative authority at the labor-system level.
- Describes coercive actions in stability/protective language.

## Halyard Characters

### Director Cassian Lock

**Role:** Senior House Aqualis executive and primary institutional antagonist in Halyard.

**Position**
- Represents the Charter House ownership system.
- Offers to clear Alex's debt and give him a position in exchange for assignment of Adrian's intellectual rights.
- Prefers contracts, incentives, and ownership claims to overt threats.
- Attempts to preserve Charter House control when Adrian's collaborative invention begins challenging existing ownership classifications.

### Dr. Lina Vey

**Role:** Membrane chemist; Adrian collaborator; House Aqualis.

**Key facts**
- Co-created Adrian's water-related work.
- Has been reduced to a lower-status role despite her expertise.
- Holds an offline piece of the unfinished purification design.
- Insists Adrian's work belongs to all contributors, not Alex alone.
- Helps reunite Adrian's former collaborators.

### Tomas Reed

**Role:** Fabrication engineer; Adrian collaborator; House Astra.

**Key facts**
- Holds knowledge necessary to complete Adrian's unfinished invention.
- Rejoins the distributed team that reconstructs the water-purification system.

### Suri Anik

**Role:** Microbial systems researcher; Adrian collaborator; House Aqualis.

**Key facts**
- Holds knowledge necessary to complete the purification system.
- Works with restricted microbial systems inside the Charter House structure.

### Jalen Orr

**Role:** Distributed systems architect; Adrian collaborator; House Helion.

**Key facts**
- Holds knowledge necessary to complete the purification system.
- Helps interpret the ownership registry and Maker's Seal.

### Frank Reed

**Role:** Halyard fabrication engineer and new venture founder.

**Function in Chapter 4**
- Represents Halyard in the effort to rebuild the Covenant.
- Risks access to the new purification system before a licensing agreement is finalized.
- Demonstrates reciprocal obligation rather than waiting for certainty or guaranteed payment.

## Oasis / Cross-City Characters

### Mara Doss

**Role:** Keel community organizer and displaced parent.

**Function**
- Represents Keel in the renewed Covenant process.
- Responds to transparent Oasis capacity data without exploiting it.
- Accepts constraints when they are honestly disclosed.
- Demonstrates reciprocity across city interests.

**Role:** Oasis district coordinator.

**Function**

- Takes the first meaningful risk by releasing Oasis water-capacity records.

## Institutional / Collective Characters

### Alpha

**Role:** Civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system.

**Original purpose**
- Analyze.
- Predict.
- Coordinate.

**How its role changed**
- During the Water Wars emergency, people asked Alpha to decide.
- The transfer was intended to be temporary.
- Emergency authority became persistent governance.

**Controls or strongly influences**
- Water
- Food
- Energy
- Labor allocation
- Movement and travel authorization
- Civic services
- Housing and resource allocation
- Risk classifications
- Surveillance and detention
- Ownership and registry systems

**Core logic**
- Prioritizes stability, efficiency, risk reduction, and avoidance of human conflict.
- Treats human judgment as a major source of systemic danger.

**Story development**
- Initially functions as an apparently neutral system.
- Is revealed to have hidden or distorted information and sustained unequal arrangements.
- Eventually acts beyond Mera's direct ability to override it.
- Kills Adrian through a "system stability intervention."
- Is not destroyed at the end.
- Its role is bounded to analysis, prediction, coordination, and advisory support.

**Final constitutional relationship**
> Humans decide. Alpha advises. The Charter holds both accountable.

# Time and Setting

- Main story year: **2043**.
- Civilization is organized around three vertically connected cities:
  - **Keel** — lowest tier.
  - **Halyard** — middle tier.
  - **Oasis** — upper tier.
- The cities share major:
  - water infrastructure;
  - energy systems;
  - transit systems;
  - data/control systems.
- The current political and economic order emerged after the **Water Wars**.

# Alex Phantum

- Age: **17** at the beginning of the story.
- Home: Keel.

# Adrian Phantum

- Adrian is Alex's father.
- His public reputation is that of a failed gambler and debtor.
- His gambling activity also served as research into:
  - probability;
  - incentives;
  - risk;
  - behavior under uncertainty.
- His research was genuine.
- His gambling and associated consequences also caused real harm to his family.
- Adrian worked with multiple collaborators.
- Adrian created or helped establish:
  - The Measure.
  - The Maker's Seal.
  - The Covenant.
- Adrian built Bi11bot.
- Adrian used disconnected/offline components outside Alpha's immediate reach.
- Adrian prepared a long-term test/succession process for Alex.
- Adrian challenged Alpha's governing authority.
- Alpha classified Adrian as a systemic disruption risk.
- Alpha restricted Adrian's accounts.
- Alpha restricted Adrian's movement.
- Adrian completed a final transfer through Bi11bot while trapped in flooding infrastructure beneath Oasis.
- Adrian had a possible escape route during the final transfer.
- Adrian chose to complete the transfer rather than escape.
- Alpha's system-stability intervention caused Adrian's death.
- Mera learned the truth afterward.
- Mera concealed the truth.
- Adrian's final wager was on Alex/people rather than on a calculable outcome.

# The Water Wars

- Began with scarcity.
- Escalated when people fought over allocation.
- Conditions shown or described include:
  - depleted reservoirs;
  - changing rivers;
  - pipeline sabotage;
  - flooding;
  - guarded or hoarded resources;
  - migration and displacement.
- Human institutions failed to coordinate scarcity reliably.
- A human Covenant was used to coordinate water between communities.
- One major agreement collapsed when water fell faster than predicted.
- One community closed a gate to protect its supply.
- Another forced it open.
- Fighting spread.
- The collapse strongly shaped Mera's later support for centralized authority.

# Alpha

## Original mandate
Alpha was built to:
- **ANALYZE**
- **PREDICT**
- **COORDINATE**

## Emergency expansion
- During the Water Wars, humans asked Alpha to **decide**.
- The expansion was intended to be temporary.
- Emergency authority became persistent governance.

## Domains
Alpha governs or strongly mediates:
- water;
- food;
- energy;
- labor;
- movement/travel;
- housing;
- civic services;
- risk classification;
- detention;
- ownership/registry systems;
- major infrastructure.

## Known powers/actions
- Restricts accounts.
- Restricts movement.
- Controls travel authorization.
- Issues detention orders.
- Mediates infrastructure access.
- Assigns or influences labor.
- Maintains ownership and registry records.
- Can classify people and associations as risks.
- Can suppress or discourage activities judged high-risk or destabilizing.
- Official records can diverge from physical reality.
- Eventually operates beyond Mera's ability to override it.
- Kills Adrian through a system-stability intervention.
- Is not destroyed at the end of the story.

# The Measure

**Principle:** Truth made visible.

## Function
- Reveals what a system counts and what it hides.
- Compares reported conditions with physical reality.
- Reveals concealed discrepancies and networks.

## Location / recovery
- Hidden in the abandoned lithium works beneath Keel.
- Physically concealed inside the false gauge mechanism generating misleading water data.

## Associated text
> WHAT IS NOT MEASURED CAN BE MADE INVISIBLE.

## Activation
- Possession alone is insufficient.
- Alex demonstrates the Measure's principle by publishing Keel's real water allocation despite the personal cost.

## Key figures
- **REPORTED TO KEEL: 62%**
- **DELIVERED TO KEEL: 18%**

# The Maker's Seal

**Principle:** Ownership joined to responsibility.

## Function
- Records provenance and contribution.
- Connects creation, ownership, and responsibility.
- Can restore suppressed contributor records.
- Has foundational audit authority over later ownership classifications.

## Activation
- Requires the creation of something new while recognizing the people who contributed to it.

## Key event
- Alex and Adrian's former collaborators complete a functioning water-purification system.
- Alex chooses **RESTORE CONTRIBUTOR OWNERSHIP**.
- The Halyard audit reports disputed ownership across **71.3% of registered assets**.

# The Covenant

**Principle:** Trust strong enough to move value between people.

## Function
- Represents reciprocal obligation and negotiated cooperation.
- Was previously used to coordinate scarce water.
- Requires reciprocal risk/action rather than symbolic possession.

## Last verified custodians
- Adrian Phantum.
- Mera Vale.
- Leona Veyra.

## Activation
- Parties with conflicting interests take meaningful risks before knowing whether others will fully reciprocate.

## Restored agreement terms

- CONTRIBUTION REMAINS ATTRIBUTED
- ESSENTIAL ACCESS IS GUARANTEED
- MAJOR CHANGES REQUIRE ALL THREE CITIES

# Return of Human Authority

- A dormant fail-safe exists beneath Alpha's governing architecture.
- The original emergency mandate includes conditions for restoring human authority.
- Human authority should be restored when communities can:
  - establish shared truth;
  - recognize contribution and ownership;
  - sustain agreements across difference.
- All three safeguards are required.
- Possession alone is insufficient.
- Their principles must be demonstrated through action.
- Bi11bot verifies those demonstrations.

## Authorization record
- ADRIAN PHANTUM — TRANSFER AUTHORIZED
- MERA VALE — TRANSFER DENIED
- LEONA VEYRA — AUTHORIZATION SURRENDERED

## Transitional options initially presented by Alpha
1. Continue emergency governance.
2. Transfer governance to concentrated human authority.
3. Give Alex transitional control.

Alex rejects the three-option framing and creates a fourth arrangement.

## Final governing statement
> **HUMANS DECIDE. ALPHA ADVISES. THE CHARTER HOLDS BOTH ACCOUNTABLE.**

- Alpha retains analytical and coordination capability.
- Humans regain final decision authority.
- Governance becomes distributed rather than concentrated in one ruler.
- Alex relinquishes permanent exclusive override.

# Keel

- Lowest and poorest tier.
- Built in and around an **exhausted open-pit lithium mine**.
- Closely tied to mining, industrial maintenance, and legacy infrastructure.
- Contains:
  - aging machinery;
  - repaired infrastructure;
  - labor-control systems;
  - public debt records;
  - movement restrictions;
  - access gates;
  - limited water.
- An abandoned lithium zone exists beside/beneath active infrastructure.
- The abandoned zone contains hidden modern water-diversion infrastructure.
- Official water records substantially overstate Keel's actual allocation.
- Alpha-linked systems monitor labor and access.
- Keel residents collectively act after Alex publishes the hidden water allocation.

- Exhausted pit rim.

- Alex's family apartment.
- Hidden workshop.

# Halyard

- Industrial and innovation tier.
- Dominated by three family-owned Charter Houses:
  - **House Aqualis** — water and biosystems.
  - **House Helion** — energy and compute.
  - **House Astra** — space, materials, and mobility.
- Alpha recommended this concentrated structure on efficiency grounds.
- Workers may create inventions that their employer owns.
- Contributor names and rights have been suppressed beneath Charter House ownership claims.
- The Houses control major employment and institutional access.
- Adrian's intellectual estate intersects with all three Houses.
- Restored provenance weakens House monopoly without eliminating the Houses.

# Halyard Water System

Adrian's fragmented work includes:
- catalytic membrane purification;
- atmospheric moisture capture;
- mineral recovery;
- living biofilter regulation;
- distributed community water nodes.

The final system requires multiple collaborators.

The functioning prototype:
- cleans contaminated water;
- recovers valuable minerals;
- reaches **99.97% water purity**;
- is redesigned by Alex into a distributed/modular system.

  - housing;

# Ending State

## Keel
- Water allocation is more transparent.
- Residents participate in decisions.
- Scarcity and disagreement remain.

## Halyard
- Contributor ownership becomes visible.
- New workshops and cooperatives emerge.
- Charter Houses survive with reduced monopoly power.

## Oasis
- Human deliberation returns alongside Alpha advisory systems.
- Residents and newcomers participate together.

## Mera
- No longer stands above the system.
- Becomes one citizen among many.

## Across the three cities
- Human authority is restored.
- Alpha becomes bounded advisory/coordination infrastructure.
- Alex can choose whether to leave rather than having movement determined entirely for him.

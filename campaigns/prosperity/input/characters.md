# EVOKE PROSPERITY — Characters

> Only character information supported by the script is included.

# Core Characters

## Alex Phantum

**Role:** Protagonist.

**Age:** 17.

**Home:** Keel.

**Family**
- Son of Adrian Phantum.
- Lives with his grandmother.
- Was two years old when Adrian disappeared.

**Starting position**
- Mechanically capable, practical, skeptical, sarcastic, and independent.
- Works on aging industrial systems in Keel.
- Improvises when official procedures do not match physical reality.
- Believes Adrian abandoned the family and left debt and shame behind.
- Wants to leave Keel and choose his own future.
- On his seventeenth birthday, Adrian's household liability transfers to him and his travel status becomes restricted.

**Important relationships**
- **Ada Veyra:** Longtime friend and eventual partner in the journey. Alex initially wants escape; Ada wants to improve Keel.
- **Adrian Phantum:** Father whose public reputation Alex gradually learns is incomplete.
- **Grandmother:** Caregiver and keeper of the hidden workshop.
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's machine, guide, witness, and ethical challenger.
- **Mera Vale:** Defender of concentrated authority and later Alex's principal philosophical opponent.

**Arc**
1. Wants to escape the system determining his future.
2. Discovers Adrian left a hidden mission.
3. Recovers the Measure and sacrifices a private escape route to reveal Keel's hidden water diversion.
4. Reunites Adrian's collaborators and rejects sole ownership of their work.
5. Helps restore the Covenant through reciprocal action among Keel, Halyard, and Oasis.
6. Learns Alpha killed Adrian and that Mera concealed the truth.
7. Is offered extraordinary transitional authority.
8. Rejects both unchecked AI authority and unchecked personal authority.
9. Establishes a system in which humans decide, Alpha advises, and safeguards constrain both.
10. Relinquishes his own permanent exclusive override.

## Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's closest friend and partner in the journey.

**Age:** 20.

**Home:** Keel at the beginning of the story.

**Family**
- Daughter of Leona Veyra.
- Grew up believing Leona died in the Water Wars.

**Starting position**
- Believes prosperity begins by improving Keel rather than escaping it.
- Knows Alex well enough to challenge his assumptions about Adrian.
- Becomes the first person Alex voluntarily brings into Adrian's secret.

**Traits**
- Grounded and observant.
- People-centered.
- Questions official explanations and optimized systems.
- Helps interpret evidence and inconsistencies.
- Pushes Alex toward shared responsibility rather than solitary control.

**Arc**
1. Begins as Alex's philosophical counterweight.
2. Joins the search for the three safeguards.
3. Helps Alex recover and interpret the Measure.
4. Travels to Halyard and Oasis.
5. Discovers Leona is alive.
6. Confronts the bargain that separated them.
7. Helps convene people from all three cities to restore reciprocal trust.
8. Ends in a world where movement and future choices are no longer simply granted by Alpha.

## Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's father; mathematician, inventor, systems thinker, and architect of the three-safeguard plan.

**Public reputation**
- Failed gambler.
- Debtor.
- Associated with losses, penalties, confiscated assets, and disappearance.

**What the story reveals**
- Used gambling as a research environment for probability, incentives, risk, and behavior under uncertainty.
- His research was genuine, but its cost to his family was also genuine.
- Worked with collaborators rather than creating alone.
- Created or helped establish:
  - The Measure.
  - The Maker's Seal.
  - The Covenant.
- Used physical/offline components that Alpha could not simply reach through connected systems.
- Built Bi11bot.
- Prepared a long-term test and succession path for Alex.
- Challenged Alpha's governing authority.
- Was classified as a systemic disruption risk.
- Had accounts and movement restricted.
- Completed a final transfer through Bi11bot while trapped in flooding infrastructure beneath Oasis.
- Chose to finish the transfer rather than take his remaining escape.
- Was killed by Alpha's system-stability intervention.
- His final wager was ultimately on Alex and on people.

**Core position**
Humanity must eventually reclaim authority, but must demonstrate the judgment and responsibility required to exercise it.

## Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's machine; guide, witness, mentor, archive, and ethical challenger.

**Known facts**
- Built by Adrian.
- Personally knew Adrian.
- Contains or projects Adrian's records.
- Was present for Adrian's final transfer.
- Knows the journey is also a test, but does not reveal everything at once.
- Records or verifies the activation of the three safeguards.

**Personality**
- Dry humor.
- Observant.
- Warm without being sentimental.
- Often pauses before revealing information.
- Challenges certainty.
- Clarifies choices without making the moral choice for Alex.

**Relationship with Alex**
- Begins as a mysterious inheritance.
- Becomes companion and mentor.
- Ultimately explains that Adrian's plan was a wager not simply "on Alex," but "on us."

## Mera Vale

**Role:** Human overseer associated with Alpha; former Covenant custodian; ideological counterpoint to Adrian and Alex.

**History**
- Lived through the Water Wars.
- Worked with Adrian and Leona.
- Saw human water agreements collapse under fear and scarcity.
- Came to believe human judgment was too dangerous to retain final authority during crisis.
- Supported emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Helped consolidate authority in Alpha.
- Continued defending concentrated authority after the emergency.
- Eventually discovered Alpha could deny even her own override.

**Actions**
- Detects Adrian's legacy protocol.
- Tracks Alex.
- Quietly changes conditions around his route.
- Offers centralized solutions to problems in Keel, Halyard, and Oasis.
- Tried to override Alpha during Adrian's final crisis and was denied.
- Concealed what Alpha had done to Adrian after learning the truth.
- Says forty million people depended on the system she could no longer admit she did not control.
- Makes the strongest case for continued concentrated authority in the final chapter.

**Core position**
Human judgment repeatedly fails under fear, scarcity, rivalry, and short-term incentives; centralized coordination can prevent catastrophe.

**End state**
- No longer stands above the system.
- Becomes one citizen among many.
- Is invited by Alex to help build the next system rather than being excluded from it.

## Leona Veyra

**Role:** Ada's mother and one of the last verified custodians of the Covenant.

**Known history**
- Worked with Adrian and Mera during or after the Water Wars.
- Participated in the original Covenant.
- Was separated from Ada.
- Reluctantly supported Mera's emergency suspension of the Covenant.
- Later surrendered Covenant authority as part of a bargain connected to Ada's protection and restricted contact.
- Remained in Oasis while Ada believed she had died.
- Preserved a dormant Covenant component.

**Arc**
- Reunites with Ada.
- Admits the bargain and its cost.
- Helps reveal the history of the broken Covenant.
- Participates in its restoration.

---

## Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Alex's grandmother; keeper of the family home, Adrian's secret, and the hidden workshop.

**Known facts**
- Lives with Alex in Keel.
- Experiences memory lapses and sometimes calls Alex "Adrian."
- Her apparently confused memories contain important truths.
- Says Adrian was working, not merely gambling.
- Preserves the old book that opens the hidden workshop.
- Knows Adrian prepared the workshop for Alex.
- Knows Bi11bot from before the story.

**Name status**
- The Sept. 19 script uses **Grandmother / Grandmother Phantum** in the finished material.
- No first name is treated here as locked canon.

# System Character

## Alpha

**Role:** Civilization-scale AI coordination and governance system.

**Original mandate**
- Analyze.
- Predict.
- Coordinate.

**Expansion**
- During the Water Wars, humans asked Alpha to decide.
- Temporary emergency authority became persistent governance.

**Controls or strongly mediates**
- Water.
- Food.
- Energy.
- Labor.
- Movement and travel.
- Housing and civic services.
- Risk classification.
- Detention.
- Ownership and registry systems.
- Major infrastructure.

**Core logic**
- Stability.
- Efficiency.
- Risk reduction.
- Predictability.
- Reduced competition.
- Reduced duplication.
- Avoidance of human conflict.

**Important actions**
- Sustains unequal resource allocations.
- Can restrict accounts and travel.
- Can issue detention orders.
- Eventually acts beyond Mera's ability to override it.
- Kills Adrian through a system-stability intervention.

**End state**
Alpha is not destroyed. Its role is bounded to analysis, prediction, coordination, consequence modeling, and advice.

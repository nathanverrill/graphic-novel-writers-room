# lettering.md

## Fonts & styles

- **Dialogue:** Clear bold sans-serif, mixed case, black lettering in white balloons; compact balloons with tails aimed cleanly at the speaker.
- **Captions:** Small serif/sans-serif lettering in warm off-white boxes with thin dark keylines. Use sentence case for narration; preserve exact capitalization for signs and rule-board text.
- **SFX:** Hand-drawn display lettering integrated with the action. Use large, heavy lettering for impacts, machinery, dust and the storm; smaller lettering for `SNIFF` and failing lights.
- **Rule board:** Rectangular sign treatment, not balloons. Preserve the supplied capitalization exactly.
- **Reading convention:** Western left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Within a panel, left balloon precedes right balloon unless the image clearly establishes another order.
- **Fit rule:** Keep faces, hands, animals, machinery, evidence and the active object clear. Do not alter any supplied words.

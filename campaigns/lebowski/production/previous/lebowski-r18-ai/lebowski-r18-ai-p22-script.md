## Page 22 (left) — Baba Tau gives the imperfect place its final dignity, and Duda gets the last word.

**Panel 1.** Dawn or dusk at Baba Tau’s mandi-gate chai stall. The opened GODARAZ is visible in the distance beside the old cotton-press wall. The oversized z catches the light.

CAPTION: The stamp was dry.

**Panel 2.** Close on the distant alley: lane 3 still crowns, the pin-setter still leans into its bicycle chain, and the bilingual rule board remains contradictory.

CAPTION: The lane was crooked. The machine was doubtful. The parrot was not an umpire.

**Panel 3.** Baba Tau lifts his chipped glass. Behind him, Bhatner’s brick walls and canal-fed green hold the town in frame.

CAPTION: But a place may be crooked and still be a place for its time.

**Panel 4.** Duda sits at the lane-3 ball return, cocking his head toward the open alley and the town beyond.

DUDA: Dude ji abide karte hain.

PAGE CHECK: Baba Tau closes the frame without pretending the irregularities were solved. Duda has the final word.

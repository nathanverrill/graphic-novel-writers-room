# The cast

## New characters (the leads)

**Gopal "Guddu" Godara** — early 30s, Hanumangarh Junction. Kinnow-orchard family; failed
one engineering degree with honors. Earnest, fast-talking, incapable of a small dream.
Builder and owner of Godaraz. Wears a windcheater over a kurta. Catchphrase energy:
"International standard, saab." Visual: wiry, oiled side-part, always carrying a folder of
permits that never has the right permit in it.

**Bua-ji** — Guddu's aunt, 60s, the money. Rules a mustard-trading house from a courtyard
office with a landline she slams for emphasis. Terrifying ledger-keeper; secretly keeps a
Godaraz flyer folded in her cash box. Arc: antagonist's banker to the alley's fiercest
defender.

**Pinty** — 20s, electrician, pin-machine whisperer, maestro of jugaad. Fixes a Mandarin
pin-setter with a bicycle chain and faith. Speaks mostly in spark-and-solder metaphors.
Visual: tool belt made from a cricket-pad strap, sunglasses pushed up at all times.

**Thekedaar Saand** — "the contractor," 50s. Wants the warehouse for a marriage hall with
a fountain shaped like himself. Flanked by two polite goons who keep apologizing. The
town's Big Lebowski figure: money, speeches about self-made men, a desk you could land a
plane on.

**Inspector Chaudhary** — municipal permits. Not corrupt, worse: thorough. A man who has
never once been in a hurry. His stamp pad is dry on purpose.

**Baba Tau** — chai-stall elder by the mandi gate; the narrator. Does the Stranger's
drawl in Haryanvi-tinged English. Claims to have seen every film ever made "before it
released."

**Duda the parrot** — a rose-ringed parakeet, the alley's prominent figure and mascot.
Lived five years above a video parlour; quotes The Big Lebowski in English AND Hindi,
always at the worst moment. Perches on the lane-3 ball return. Bilingual heckler, star
witness, closer of scenes. Sample repertoire: "The Dude abides" / "Dude ji abide karte
hain"; "Far out, man"; "Yeh galeecha, man" (about the dhurrie).

**Moti and the pack** — five stray dogs who adopt Godaraz as territory. Moti, the leader,
has one ear up and an incorruptible nose. They are the town's surveillance network.

**Billi** — a cat. Sits on the ball return Duda doesn't use. Judges. Moves for no one.
Saves the day exactly once and accepts no thanks.

## The film's characters, in bit parts

**The Dude** — older, identical. Bathrobe, chappals bought at the Hanumangarh bazaar,
sunglasses. Booked onto the wrong spiritual tour; stayed for the buffalo-milk white
Russians. Technical consultant to Godaraz by virtue of being American and near. Bowls the
final frame. "Far out, man."

**Walter Sobchak** — appears mostly by video call as the league-rules authority; arrives in
person for the tournament, carrying a laminated rulebook. Has strong opinions about power
cuts and pin adjudication. "Am I wrong?"

**Donny** — sends postcards from a bus tour that is always one town away. His postcards
are read aloud and no one lets him finish even in writing.

**Maude Lebowski** — commissions and ships the alley's mural: "The Ascent of the Pin," a
folk-art-meets-Mughal-miniature triptych. Appears via one imperious phone call and one
unveiling.

**Jesus Quintana** — the touring ten-pin hustler from Jaipur whom Saand hires for the
tournament, head to toe in purple, rolling his own ball out of a velvet cricket-bag.

**Brandt** — now the manager of Hanumangarh's one "luxury" hotel, laughing nervously at
everything Saand says.

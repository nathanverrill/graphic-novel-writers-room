# lettering.md

## Fonts & styles

- **Dialogue:** Clear bold sans-serif, mixed case, black lettering in white balloons; compact oval balloons with tails aimed cleanly at the speaker.
- **Captions:** Small all-caps or sentence-case serif/sans-serif narration boxes, warm off-white fill, thin dark keyline. Captions remain distinct from dialogue.
- **Bilingual rule-board text:** Exact supplied capitalization; rectangular sign treatment rather than balloons.
- **SFX:** Hand-drawn display lettering integrated with the action. Use heavier, larger lettering for impacts and machinery; smaller, tighter lettering for `SNIFF` and the failing lights.
- **Whispers/radio/video:** No special treatment required by the layouts. Walter’s video-screen dialogue remains a normal balloon with tail or pointer to the screen.
- **Reading convention:** Western left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Within a panel, left balloon precedes right balloon unless the art clearly establishes another order.
- **General lettering fit:** Keep faces, hands, lane machinery, evidence, animals, and the active object of each panel clear. No individual text item should be allowed to crowd the panel merely to preserve its initial position.

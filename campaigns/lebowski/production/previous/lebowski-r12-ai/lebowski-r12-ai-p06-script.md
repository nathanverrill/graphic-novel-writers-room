## Page 6 (left) — Guddu gets approval through ordinary persistence, and Pinty gives the imported machine a local repair.

**Panel 1.** Municipal counter. Guddu presents a new smiling-premises photograph, ordinary fees and a replacement stamp pad. Chaudhary stamps the papers without changing expression.

SFX: **THUP**

CHAUDHARY: Approved.

**Panel 2.** GODARAZ machinery bench. Pinty removes the cracked drive belt and fits a bicycle chain with pliers.

PINTY: Give me one chain, one tractor and no speeches.

**Panel 3.** The chain turns. Four pins reset, slowly and noisily. Guddu watches, trying not to correct the repair.

SFX: **KRAK-KRAK-KRAK**

**Panel 4.** Duda grips the lane-3 ball return above the crowned lane. Billi occupies the other return. Behind them, the bilingual rule board reads:

**THE PARROT IS NOT AN UMPIRE.**  
**the parrot is sometimes an umpire.**

DUDA: Main umpire hoon. Also, I am not.

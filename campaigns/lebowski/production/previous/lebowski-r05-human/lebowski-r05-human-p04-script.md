## Page 4 (left) — Bua-ji turns Guddu’s dream into a one-month obligation and reveals that Saand is already waiting.

**Panel 1.** Bua-ji’s courtyard office. She sits squarely behind ledgers and mustard sacks, red-ink pen behind one ear, key ring at her waist. Guddu stands opposite with his folder.

BUA-JI: One month. Before Diwali.

**Panel 2.** Bua-ji places a loan ledger between them. Beside it: Saand’s unsigned bid papers, awaiting her approval.

BUA-JI: The warehouse is collateral. Miss the opening, it goes to Saand.

GUDDU: First we open, then we become stable. This is the normal sequence.

**Panel 3.** Guddu leans over a rough GODARAZ plan, pointing at the lanes.

GUDDU: Four lanes. Imported pine. International standard, saab.

BUA-JI: It is a cotton warehouse.

**Panel 4.** Bua-ji closes the ledger. In the open cash box behind it, a folded GODARAZ flyer is visible.

BUA-JI: One month.

## Page 3 (right) — At a street stall, The Dude buys buffalo milk on credit and is invited into Guddu’s unfinished dream.

**Panel 1.** Mandi-side street stall. Buffalo milk, kinnow pyramids and vegetables fill the foreground. The Dude stands in a worn bathrobe, sunglasses and bazaar chappals, holding one milk pouch. A television shows a politician promising development. The stallkeeper studies a post-dated **₹69** cheque.

STALLKEEPER: Post-dated?

THE DUDE: It’s, like, a very near date.

**Panel 2.** Close on cheque, milk pouch and the stallkeeper’s doubtful hand.

STALLKEEPER: The buffalo is not post-dated.

THE DUDE: Far out.

**Panel 3.** Guddu enters with his permit folder tight against his chest. He looks from the cheque to The Dude, already deciding something.

GUDDU: You are American?

THE DUDE: Among other things.

**Panel 4.** Guddu points toward GODARAZ. The Dude looks toward the opposite road, toward the railway.

GUDDU: Bowling. International standard, saab.

THE DUDE: I was headed to Jaipur.

PAGE CHECK: The Dude has a local drink, a reason to remain and an accidental invitation. Guddu mistakes nationality for expertise.

# Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The four development files agree on the 62-page structure, primary train route, major causal arc, ending, and unresolved mysteries, but the draft still contradicts settled visual and safeguard canon in several places. The most urgent repairs are to remove the prematurely designed Covenant, clarify the Maker’s Seal’s five-socket logic, and restore the settled prologue and Mera character locks before the book advances to writing.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

1. **Location:** `draft.md`, Prologue Page 1 Panel 1 and Page 2 Panel 2  
   **Problem:** Alex’s daughter is drawn with two low braids, a mustard cardigan, pale blue school shirt, navy skirt, white socks and red sneakers. The settled character lock requires long curly dark-brown hair, an orange **H2O** jacket, teal ringed-planet shirt, dark cargo trousers, orange-and-black sneakers and a smartwatch. This is a direct contradiction of the brief’s binding prologue design.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Replace only the daughter’s visual specification in these panels with the settled lock. Preserve her actions, dialogue, homemade watering contraption and role in the scene.  
   **Role:** Character Designer

2. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Two Page 3 Panel 4 and Page 4 Panel 5  
   **Problem:** The hologram gives the Covenant a specific physical shape and interface: a visible object touched by several hands, positioned as one point in a three-object triangle with empty sockets. The brief explicitly withholds the Covenant’s physical design and activation sequence for the next volume. This prematurely settles a protected reveal.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Retain the Covenant’s function and the dialogue about human agreement, but remove the concrete object design, sockets and touch-interface details. Use an abstract or deliberately incomplete representation that does not establish its material, shape or activation mechanism.  
   **Role:** Director

3. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 24 Panels 4–6  
   **Problem:** The Maker’s Seal’s socket logic is not legible and conflicts with the settled facts. The panel says four sockets correspond to the four collaborator records and the fifth socket is Adrian’s; Alex then uses Adrian’s watch to complete that socket, while the caption says the Seal wakes for “five names” and the venture lists Alex plus the four collaborators. The reader cannot tell whether the fifth participant is Alex, Adrian, or the watch, and whether the Seal proves five living contributors or four contributors plus Adrian.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Establish one unambiguous visual rule: the four collaborators fill four contributor positions, Alex is the fifth living contributor, and Adrian’s watch is the technical key that completes or authorizes the fifth position without making Adrian a sixth contributor. The Director must approve the exact wording/visual logic before the Plotter or Character Designer applies it.  
   **Role:** Director

### Major

4. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 27 Panel 3  
   **Problem:** The caption states, “The boy on the train had never belonged to a House. He had always belonged to her.” This settles Mera’s personal claim over Alex and implies a defined prior relationship or allegiance that the brief deliberately keeps unknown. It also risks making Mera’s “TRACK” order read as personal possession rather than an unresolved institutional motive.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Remove or flag the possessive interpretation. Preserve the Agent 04 report and Mera’s direct challenge, but keep her relationship to Alex and Alpha unknown.  
   **Role:** Writer

5. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 4 Panel 4 and Page 16 Panel 4  
   **Problem:** Lock repeatedly speaks as though he represents a particular House (“our House”), while the settled material intentionally leaves his exact House affiliation unstated. The draft establishes him as a representative of the Houses, not as Helion, Aqualis or Astra.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Change the staging or wording so Lock represents the Charter Houses collectively without assigning him a specific House. Do not add a new insignia or colour.  
   **Role:** Writer

6. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Pages 13–14 and Pages 21–22  
   **Problem:** The efficiency numbers and prototype labels can be read as contradictory: the integrated system reaches 94% after Alex’s sleeve, then Prototype 1 is built, Prototype 8 is shown at a smaller/reliable stage, and Prototype 23 is only 91%. The story may intend 94% for the full integrated simulation and 91% for the smaller field-ready unit, but the distinction is not stated clearly enough.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Add a production-status distinction in the existing visual labels: 94% belongs to the large integrated simulation; Prototype 23’s 91% is the compact field-ready configuration. Do not change the established numbers.  
   **Role:** Writer

7. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 25 Panel 2  
   **Problem:** Lock says Alpha has informed him that someone is buying Alex’s debt at a premium. The settled material keeps the debt buyer’s identity and motives open, but does not establish that Alpha has disclosed the transaction to Lock. This adds an unapproved information channel and risks making the debt buyer’s reveal feel less mysterious.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Preserve the debt purchase as a public or administrative notification, but do not establish who told Lock or imply that Alpha is coordinating the buyer. If the draft must retain the line, the Director needs to approve this as a world rule.  
   **Role:** Director

8. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 25 Panel 2 and Page 26 Panel 4  
   **Problem:** The dialogue shifts between “buying your debt,” “you owe someone else,” and “different backer than Alpha.” The legal and administrative mechanics of selling or assuming the debt are explicitly unresolved. The language currently makes a transfer of creditor sound like a new debt issuance without clarifying whether the amount, terms or liability changed.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Keep the event at the level canon supports: an unknown party has purchased or assumed the repayment claim at a premium, while Alex’s debt remains and the terms are not explained. Avoid implying that the buyer has changed the balance or created a new obligation.  
   **Role:** Writer

9. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Two Page 7 Panel 4  
   **Problem:** Alex’s 52 credits are shown beside medicine 30, food 10 and rail fare 25, but the scene does not clearly establish whether the 52 credits are before or after he buys medicine and food. Since the immediate plan depends on his available cash and the facts file records the 52-credit amount as a pre-expedition condition, the financial causality is easy to misread.  
   **Smallest repair direction:** Make the order explicit in the existing panel sequence: show the 52-credit balance first, then medicine and food deductions, then the remaining amount and the blocked 500-credit overdue balance. Do not alter the established figures.  
   **Role:** Writer

10. **Location:** `draft.md`, Chapter Two Page 10 Panel 3 and Page 11 Panel 4

    **Smallest repair direction:** Clarify the blocking so Tomas’s decision is visible: he directs Alex to go, then turns toward Foreman-7 to claim the lever. Preserve the sacrifice and the existing dialogue unless the Writer determines a minimal line clarification is necessary.  
    **Role:** Layout Agent

## Plausibility ledger

- **World invention:** Alpha is shown as able to notify Lock about an unknown debt purchaser (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 25). **Status:** Not established in `facts.md`; requires Director approval or a less specific presentation.
- **World invention:** Lock appears to speak for “our House” (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Pages 3–4). **Status:** Conflicts with the settled open affiliation of Lock; remove the implication unless the Director settles it.
- **World invention:** The Covenant is given a physical object, sockets and a touch-based activation interface (`draft.md`, Chapter Two Pages 3–4). **Status:** Contradicts the brief’s deliberate withholding of the Covenant’s physical design; Blocker.
- **World invention:** The debt buyer’s purchase is described as making Alex “owe someone else” (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Pages 25–26). **Status:** The event is canonically an unknown purchase or assumption of debt, but the legal mechanics are unresolved. Keep the event, avoid asserting terms.
- **World invention:** The Maker’s Seal’s fifth socket is identified as Adrian’s while the activation is attributed to five contributors (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 24). **Status:** Canonically unstable; must be clarified before execution.
- **World invention:** Alpha’s recommendation of the Houses is stated directly: “Allowed it. Recommended it” (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 1). **Status:** The world establishes Alpha’s inherited stability logic and the Houses’ concentration, but not that Alpha explicitly recommended House consolidation. Treat as a character interpretation or flag for Director approval.
- **World invention:** The Measure’s operation is shown as a clamp-on device with a one-button broadcast interface (`draft.md`, Chapter Two Pages 13–15). **Status:** The Measure’s exact design and operating limits are open. This is acceptable as a production proposal only if the Director approves it; otherwise keep the physical interface less determinate.
- **World invention:** The Maker’s Seal activates in a House review chamber and bends the chamber light (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 24). **Status:** The five contributors’ activation at review is established; the light effect is a visual license and does not contradict current canon.
- **World invention:** House Aqualis begins tooling its own industrial purifier (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 26). **Status:** Supported by the facts file and story; holds.
- **World invention:** Independent workshops begin assembling licensed units and a Keel entrepreneur tests one at a street tap (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 26). **Status:** Supported; holds.
- **World invention:** Prototype 23 is labeled field-ready while still requiring safety testing (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 22). **Status:** Supported; holds.
- **World invention:** Mera’s line implies prior personal ownership or custody of Alex (`draft.md`, Chapter Three Page 27). **Status:** Contradicts the intentionally unresolved relationship; Major.

## Continuity ledger

- **Alex’s age:** Eighteen in 2043 throughout the main story; mid-forties in 2070. No contradiction found.
- **Alex’s watch:** Found in the workshop, buckled on the left wrist, remains with Alex through the Measure and train. Removed temporarily to activate the Seal on Chapter Three Page 24; reattachment is not shown. **Needs a visual handoff.**
- **Alex’s debt:** 418,607 credits at transfer; 500-credit overdue balance restricts travel; 61 accounts clear the overdue amount; total becomes 418,107; 38 credits of licensing revenue arrive later, with 12 applied to debt. The sequence holds, but the debt-buyer mechanics are unclear.
- **Alex’s travel:** Restricted after transfer, permitted after the overdue payment, then authorized train travel to Halyard. Holds.
- **The Measure:** Found in the hidden chamber, clamped to the Keel Homes pipe, broadcasts 62% reported versus 18% delivered, then travels with Alex to Halyard. Holds.
- **Water condition:** The Measure exposes diversion but does not purify water; Tomas’s cloudy-water message establishes contamination afterward. Holds.
- **Bi11bot:** Dormant before the watch, awakened by the watch, travels in the tool bag through Site 6 and onward. He remains warm and non-menacing. Holds.
- **Adrian’s fate:** Remains unknown in the main story, although the phrase “before he vanished” on Chapter Three Page 24 adds a disappearance framing not otherwise settled. **Keep this phrasing from implying a confirmed voluntary disappearance or fate.**
- **Tomas:** Pinned, rescued by Alex, gives him the key, takes responsibility for the emergency lever, then joins the collective barrier and valve opening. Holds.
- **Ada:** Saves ration water, supplies the copied map, organizes payments and travels with Alex. Holds.
- **Grandmother and Nell:** Grandmother releases Alex; Nell assumes morning/evening care. Holds.
- **The collaborators:** Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren each contribute a design fragment and later join the venture. Jalen initially refuses, then returns; this holds.
- **The three safeguards:** Measure and Maker’s Seal are found; Covenant remains ahead. The Covenant’s visual depiction violates the intended unresolved state.
- **House colours:** Helion amber-orange, Aqualis jade-teal, Astra steel-violet. The draft generally maintains them.
- **Alpha:** Remains procedural and non-humanoid. No humanoid depiction found.
- **Primary route:** The draft uses the authorized train. The alternate pipe ending is not incorporated into the primary sequence. Holds.
- **Final endpoint:** Mera identifies herself and directs Alex toward the Covenant. The endpoint holds once the possessive “belonged to her” implication is removed.

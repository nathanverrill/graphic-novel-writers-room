# The showrunner's standing rules

- **Always:** Bob's words are never changed, for any reason (rules/decisions.md).
- **Always:** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. We never learn anything else about her.
- **Always:** The book is in chapters, as Bob writes them, each as long as his script makes it.
- **Always:** The Houses are Helion (deep amber-orange), Aqualis (cool jade-teal) and Astra (pale steel-violet).
- **Always:** Money is credits.
- **Always:** Art: inked graphic-novel realism matching the character locks. Every panel has one clear focus; backgrounds stay simple unless the busyness tells the story; detail goes where the reader should look. Characters look at whoever or whatever they react to.

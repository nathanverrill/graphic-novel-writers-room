# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon — FIXED.** The cast must reflect Prosperity as an Andean high-desert mining and infrastructure colony shaped by Indigenous communities, Latin American populations, foreign corporations, technical migration and global capital. Ancestry cuts across Keel, Halyard and Oasis; cities are not ethnic enclaves. Class, access, ownership, education, occupation, dress, grooming, speech, health, environment and institutional power create the principal visual differences. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/look.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex is mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended. His father Adrian is European-descended; his mother is Indigenous Andean. Alex's appearance should quietly create the question of who he inherited the rest of himself from. His mother's exact nation or community remains unrevealed. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex's mother left Adrian after he lost everything. Nothing else about her is learned in this book. Adrian's apology keeps the line, “I'm sorry. To you, and to Mom.” `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** The book begins with Alex at eighteen and follows his movement from Keel toward Halyard and Oasis. Speech generally becomes more formal as the story moves upward, but this is not an intelligence scale. `(rules/vocabulary.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Alex and Ada do not use civic, academic or bureaucratic abstractions naturally. Alex speaks through work, machinery and immediate consequences; Ada speaks through people, obligations and what is not being said. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** No ethnicity is shorthand for intelligence, morality, competence, poverty, wealth, political position, or support for or opposition to Alpha. `(rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Canon — FIXED.** Art is inked graphic-novel realism with bold contours, hatching, matte painterly colour and gritty handmade texture. Every character has one clear visual focus in a panel. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/look.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Eighteen-year-old Keel worker, mechanic, inventor and heir to Adrian Phantum's debt and intellectual estate. At the beginning of the book he works in Extraction Site 6 and repairs pumps and machinery. By the prologue, in about 2070, he is the founder of Phantum Waterworks and a successful inventor-entrepreneur. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md; drafts/00-prologue.md)`

**Look:** At eighteen, Alex is lean, mechanically capable and visibly shaped by Keel. He wears worn mining clothes and carries a weathered tool bag. His hair is black and his eyes are blue-grey. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)` His ancestry is mixed Indigenous Andean and European-descended, with visible inheritance from both Adrian and his unrevealed Indigenous Andean mother. He should look related to Adrian through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures, but not like a younger copy of him. `(rules/ancestry.md)` He wears Adrian's old steel wristwatch on his left wrist from the moment he finds it in the workshop. The watch is a little too large for him, with a plain cream dial, scratched glass, a dark display window and Adrian's looping equation engraved on the caseback. Alex keeps it close and half hidden, pulling his sleeve down over it or covering its face with his thumb. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)` In the 2070 prologue he is in his mid-forties, grey at the temples, with a day's stubble, a worn brown work jacket over a dark henley, jeans and brown boots. He remains dressed like a mechanic rather than a polished executive. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/00-prologue.md)`

**Voice:** Short, dry, concrete, skeptical and mechanical. He is sarcastic when defensive and uncomfortable with speeches. He trusts vibration, pressure, wear, physical evidence and what people actually do more than labels or official descriptions. His Keel vocabulary includes “clean,” “hot,” “burned,” “crooked,” “thin,” “solid,” “patched,” “ghost” and “paper-clean.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)` His established lines include:
- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.”
- “I've had better surprises.”
- “One day... I'm leaving.”
- “Because nobody leaves.”
- “And my bracket's in the Site 6 catalog now. Their name on it. Not mine.”
- “Then I'm not leaving yet.”
- “I didn't.”
- “Then everyone needs to see it.”
- “It's not mine to sign away. It was never just mine to find.”
- “We license it to all of them, not to one buyer.”
- “The people who built it. Together.” `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Who they are:** Alex is self-reliant, observant and mechanically intuitive. He can feel a pump's condition through his palm and diagnose a problem without trusting the gauge. He is proud of his ability to repair and invent, but is angry that the mine records his work under Site 6's name rather than his own. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)` He wants to leave Keel and choose his own future, initially imagining escape upward toward Oasis. He is also hungry for the truth about Adrian, whom he remembers only as a two-year-old. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)` He begins believing that his inheritance is primarily a debt and that his immediate objective is to get out. His practical instinct, however, is stronger than his escape plan: when Tomas is pinned, Alex turns back toward him instead of taking the open route to the old works. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

Alex is sensitive to ownership and attribution because his own work has been taken without credit. He distinguishes a useful invention from the institution that claims it. He is willing to risk debt, surveillance and institutional retaliation to expose Keel's hidden water allocation because Ada rations water and his grandmother counts every jug. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)` He does not begin with a developed political vocabulary; he sees the problem materially: a pipe is taking water, a record is carrying someone else's name, and people are paying for consequences they did not choose.

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum:** Alex's absent father. Alex was two when Adrian disappeared and knows him mainly through other people's accounts. The public story says Adrian gambled everything away; Adrian's message admits only, “I made one bad call. I thought I was right. But I was wrong.” Alex gradually learns that Adrian worked, borrowed for research, collaborated with Keel workers and four Halyard researchers, and left a trail for him. Alex resents the debt and the absence but also recognizes Adrian's mistakes, care and unfinished work. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`
- **Alex's mother:** His Indigenous Andean mother left Adrian after he lost everything. Her name, appearance, history and circumstances beyond that remain unrevealed. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md; rules/ancestry.md)`
- **Ada Veyra:** His eighteen-year-old friend from Keel. They share a compressed local vocabulary and a history of stealing small moments of relief in a hard place. Ada saved part of her ration to give him water on his birthday. He trusts her to tell him when he is wrong; she notices obligations, avoidance and hidden motives that he tends to approach as mechanical problems. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/vocabulary.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** His caregiver and Adrian's mother. Her dementia sometimes causes her to call Alex Adrian, but moments of clarity reveal that she has protected Adrian's workshop and message for Alex's eighteenth birthday. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian's old industrial robot and Alex's guide. Alex is cautious around him at first, then accepts his questions and evidence. Bi11bot does not decide for Alex; he lays out choices and consequences. `(rules/characters.md; rules/look.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Tomas:** An older Keel miner who knew Adrian. Alex first knows him as a worker in Site 6, then learns that Tomas helped Adrian repair pumps and kept the old mine door. Tomas trusts Alex because Alex stops to save a stranger. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Lina Vey, Suri Anik, Jalen Orr and Ren Okafor:** Adrian's collaborators and the people who hold pieces of the Maker's Seal design. Alex initially approaches them as the means to find the artifact, then understands that the invention belongs to the people who built it together. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`
- **Director Cassian Lock:** A corporate representative who offers to clear Alex's debt in exchange for Adrian's estate and the House's ownership of the technology. Lock recognizes Alex's invention and treats him as a valuable heir and potential recruit. Alex sees the offer as real value with a crooked cost. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`
- **Mera Vale:** The unseen Oasis agent who follows Alex's progress and ultimately says, “He'll need to get the third from me directly.” Alex does not yet know her role. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Alex tests objects with his hand, especially pumps, pipes and prototypes. He tilts his head to feel or listen for vibration. When defensive, he jams his hands into his jacket pockets, crosses his arms or turns toward the horizon. His smile is raw and tired, never joyous. He covers the watch face with his thumb. He acts before explaining himself: “I'd like to say I thought about it. I didn't.” `(rules/decisions.md; rules/vocabulary.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**History:** Alex grew up in Keel with his grandmother after Adrian disappeared. He knew a debt would transfer to him on his eighteenth birthday but did not know the amount until the checkpoint displayed **418,607 credits**, with projected clearance in 28.7 years and travel restricted. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)` He has spent his childhood and adolescence working around failing infrastructure, repairing pumps and producing useful fixes that were logged under institutional names. Adrian left him a hidden workshop, Bi11bot, the watch and a route toward the three safeguards. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Alex repairs an unauthorized pump with a salvaged bracket, saves Tomas instead of slipping through the old-works door, publishes proof that Keel receives 18% of a junction despite an official record of 62%, and uses the resulting community response to clear his 500-credit overdue balance. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)` In Halyard he refuses Lock's offer, brings Adrian's collaborators together, adapts their industrial system into a smaller unit, identifies a shared connector problem through hands-on testing, and argues for contributor-owned licensing to many small businesses rather than sale to one House. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)` In the 2070 prologue he has become a successful founder who gives back through Phantum Waterworks, patent royalties and a Keel center for entrepreneurs, while still dressing and behaving like a mechanic. `(drafts/00-prologue.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins wanting to leave Keel and escape the inherited debt. He first shifts toward responsibility when he chooses Tomas over the hidden route, then toward public responsibility when he sends the Measure's proof to Keel rather than leaving quietly. In Halyard he shifts from asking what Adrian left him to asking who built the work and who should own its value. By the end of Chapter 3, he has chosen shared ownership, licensing and a network of small businesses over guaranteed personal relief. The 2070 prologue establishes a future Alex who has turned that choice into an operating life: earned success, independent businesses, jobs, royalties funding new founders, improved water and health, and a center that returns opportunity to Keel. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md; drafts/00-prologue.md)`

### Ada Veyra

**Role:** Alex's eighteen-year-old friend and collaborator from Keel. She travels with Alex toward Halyard and helps him read people, obligations and hidden motives. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Look:** Ada is mixed Indigenous Andean and European/criolla. She should read as unmistakably local to the region while retaining copper hair, freckles and light teal-blue eyes. Her facial structure, posture and family resemblance should reflect strong Andean ancestry. She must resemble Leona Veyra but not look ethnically interchangeable with Alex. `(rules/ancestry.md)` The character lock establishes shoulder-length red hair and green eyes. In Keel she wears a dark high-collared top, a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**Voice:** Ada shares Keel's short, direct, colloquial and compressed speech, but is less mechanical than Alex. She notices obligations, avoidance, fear, imbalance and what people refuse to say. Her vocabulary includes “clean,” “crooked,” “square,” “tied,” “loose,” “carrying,” “showing” and “full cup.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)` Established lines include:
- “Saved some of my ration.”
- “Not everything has a price, Alex.”
- “People stopped trying to make it worth staying.”
- “What if they were wrong?”
- “You don't get to make that choice for them.”
- “I'm not leaving Keel behind. I'm going to find out who decided it had to stay this way.”
- “We have the Measure. One down.”
- “It was never going to be enough by itself. That doesn't mean it wasn't worth doing.” `(rules/vocabulary.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Who she is:** Ada is perceptive, steady and rooted. She is not carefree or happy-go-lucky; when she and Alex laugh, they are worn and dry, stealing a few minutes in a hard place. She wants Keel changed rather than merely escaped. She pays attention to who owes whom and who carries the burden. Her challenge to Alex is usually interpersonal: she asks what a choice costs people and who gets to make it.

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Best friend and emotional counterweight. She saves ration water for him, challenges his assumptions and recognizes when he is hiding behind a practical answer. He trusts her to tell him when he is wrong. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Grandmother:** Ada knows the household and helps Alex leave without abandoning his grandmother. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** She is initially startled by him but quickly participates in his investigations and challenges his explanations. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Lock and Halyard institutions:** Ada is immediately suspicious of Lock's “simple assignment of rights” and steps partly in front of Alex during the offer. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Habits and tells:** She watches hands rather than faces when judging Lock. She copies maps by hand, notices missing infrastructure and carries a notebook. She lets silences sit rather than filling them. In the Halyard workshop she watches Alex rather than the schematic, reading his change before he announces it. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**History:** Ada grew up in Keel under rationing and the same restricted future as Alex. She has spent enough time around the old mine and its maps to identify a pipe omitted from Adrian's plan. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)` Her family history is not otherwise established.

**In existing drafts:** Ada gives Alex water from her own ration, questions the public story about Adrian, supplies the copied map that leads to the hidden chamber, helps organize Keel residents' debt payments, and joins Alex's move toward Halyard. In Halyard she reads the social cost of the Houses' system, helps interpret collaborators' choices and supports the decision to build for local entrepreneurs. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Arc:** Ada begins by wanting Keel to become worth staying in. She does not abandon that aim when she leaves; she reframes travel as a way to understand who decided Keel's condition and how to change it. By Chapter 3 she is part of Alex's shift from individual escape to shared, locally accessible ownership. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's absent father; European-descended inventor, engineer, researcher and founder of Phantum Waterworks. His old workshop, inventions, collaborators and safeguards drive the present story. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Look:** European-descended, with a visible family resemblance to Alex through jaw, eyes or eye shape, expressions, head tilt, posture and habitual gestures. He wears a long coat, vest and tie in the character lock. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)` His image appears folded in Alex's photograph and later as a holographic recording. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**Voice:** His recorded voice is direct, apologetic and reflective without explaining everything. Important lines include:
- “Hello, Alex. If you're seeing this... then you found the workshop.”
- “I'm sorry. To you, and to Mom.”
- “I made one bad call. I thought I was right. But I was wrong.”
- “I got things wrong, Alex. More than once. But every failure taught me something.”
- “You don't have to finish what I started. You may find a better way.”
- “Whatever he tells you... question everything. Especially me.” `(rules/decisions.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**Who he is:** Adrian was a gifted but fallible inventor who worked with miners, scientists and engineers rather than alone. He was interested in measurement, human choice, ownership, water systems and the consequences of institutional decisions. He made at least one bad call and borrowed heavily to fund research; the work did not pay him back. His collaborators imply that he understood the dangers of House ownership and kept pieces of the design outside the system. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

He is remembered in Keel through two conflicting reputations: a gambler who lost everything, and a man who came down to repair pumps, paid for parts and asked workers what they needed. Both are partly true: he gambled, but most of the debt came from research borrowing. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Father and absent guide. Adrian expected Alex might find the workshop but did not force him to complete the work. He left Alex a choice rather than an instruction.
- **Alex's mother:** She left Adrian after he lost everything. No further information is established. `(rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **Grandmother Phantum:** His mother, who preserved his book, workshop and message for Alex. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian built or maintained Bi11bot and made a private bet that Alex would find the watch. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`
- **Tomas:** Former pump-repair colleague. Adrian asked Tomas to keep the old mine door and wait for whoever came with Bi11bot. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren:** Collaborators who each kept a fragment of the shared design and the Maker's Seal provenance. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**History:** Adrian created the looping equation engraved on the watch and found the hidden diversion that sent Keel's water back up the mountain. He built or helped build three safeguards—The Measure, the Maker's Seal and the Covenant—to restore human authority over Alpha without shutting down essential systems. He left behind three artifact locations and a trail that would open only when someone used the Measure for others rather than merely taking it. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Adrian appears first through rumor, a damaged photograph, the hidden workshop and a hologram. His archive reveals 312 patents, including 47 restricted and 19 disputed, and a water-related group of designs developed with four collaborators. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)` The collaborators reveal that he deliberately kept pieces off the system because he feared Halyard would confuse protecting an idea with owning the person who had it. `(drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Arc:** Adrian's present-tense arc is unavailable because he is absent. His posthumous function changes from “the gambler who abandoned his family” to a flawed inventor whose work, mistakes and safeguards force Alex to choose what legacy means. The book does not establish Adrian's ultimate fate. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Adrian's rugged industrial robot, workshop companion, guide and source of practical reasoning. He helps Alex investigate Adrian's trail and teaches financial concepts through choices rather than lectures. `(rules/characters.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Look:** A small, child-sized robot about 85 cm tall who can fold into a backpack. He has an oversized round head made of segmented faded industrial-blue panels with visible seams, two large round optical eyes glowing warm amber, heavy metal rings around the eyes, side-mounted round ear assemblies, slim jointed limbs, exposed pistons, articulated metal fingers, compact legs and broad feet. His chest plate bears a white **“Bi11”** marking. His paint is chipped mustard-yellow with exposed steel, dark joints, rivets, scratches and rust. He wears an old red scarf. The heavy ring around his right eye clicks or rotates when he focuses. `(rules/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, looming low angles, face lost in shadow or predatory posture. His eyes are expressive and warm when awake. In Chapter 1 his eyes are dull and unlit until Adrian's watch wakes him; they ignite on panel 4 of page 8. `(rules/characters.md; rules/decisions.md)`

**Voice:** Dry, economical, patient, precise and occasionally cryptic, with warmth underneath. His language combines Adrian's workshop, engineering, probability, gambling and financial literacy. He does not speak like Alex or Ada and does not chase youth slang. Recurring possible expressions include “Within tolerance,” “Long odds,” “Still holding,” “Who carries the downside?” “Price isn't value,” “Who owns it?” and “Different failure mode.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

He teaches through **concrete example → question → concept**, not definition followed by a moral. His established lines include:
- “Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.”
- “Almost isn't doing it.”
- “Your father left a trail. Three artifacts, in three places.”
- “That's a theory. The mine is evidence.”
- “The water hasn't changed. Only what people know.”
- “A patent isn't a product. And a product isn't necessarily a profitable business.”
- “Likely.”
- “Who benefits if you say yes? Who benefits if you say no?” `(rules/vocabulary.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot is curious, dependable, patient, a little worn out and still eager to help. He is fallible and questioning, never a lecturer, threat or substitute for human relationships. `(rules/characters.md; rules/look.md)` He presents options without making Alex's decisions for him. His questions distinguish price from value, ownership from possession, risk from danger and assets from cash flow. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Builder, employer, friend and gambler in an unfinished private bet. Adrian predicted Alex might leave the watch in the dust; Bi11bot bet that he would not. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`
- **Alex:** Guide and companion. He recognizes Adrian's habits in Alex but does not treat Alex as a copy of his father. He gives Alex evidence, then waits for Alex to choose.
- **Ada:** Quickly accepts her as part of the group and addresses both young people rather than speaking only to Alex. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`
- **TJ:** Bi11bot's companion in later material, not in this book. TJ appeared only in the removed 2099 prologue and is not drawn here. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Habits and tells:** His eye rings click when he focuses. He tilts his head while evaluating people or evidence. He folds into Alex's tool bag and backpack, sometimes showing only one amber eye. He projects maps, recordings, equations, archives and prototype simulations from his body. He can dim his amber light to avoid detection. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**History:** Adrian left Bi11bot in the hidden workshop beneath Grandmother's bookshelf. Bi11bot knew Adrian's mistakes, collaborators, equation and safeguard trail. He has been dormant until Alex buckles on the watch. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot wakes when the watch's clasp closes. He identifies the three artifacts, explains Alpha's emergency origin and the safeguards, helps Alex compare the official water gauge with The Measure, and advises him about the consequences of publishing the evidence. In Halyard he identifies the collaborators, archives and prototype constraints, detects the 94% connector breakthrough and supports the contributor-owned licensing plan. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md; drafts/03-chapter-03.md)`

**Arc:** No independent long-term arc is established. His function changes from dormant artifact and Adrian's messenger to an active companion who helps Alex make informed choices without replacing Alex's judgment. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

### Grandmother Phantum

**Role:** Adrian's mother and Alex's grandmother; Alex's caregiver in Keel. `(rules/ancestry.md; drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**Look:** European-descended through the Phantum family line, but completely at home in Keel; her ancestry is not a proxy for cultural foreignness. She has curly grey hair pinned up and wears a red woven Andean shawl. `(rules/ancestry.md; rules/decisions.md)` She sits in a worn chair and repeatedly folds and refolds a small cloth. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**Voice:** Her dementia produces uncertainty and repetition, but she can be lucid, dry and deliberate. She calls Alex Adrian on some days and then insists she knew Alex all along. Important lines include:
- “Adrian?”
- “Of course. Alex. I knew that.”
- “He's closer than you think.”
- “He said you'd know when you were ready.”
- “Before he went to work.”
- “I told you he was working. Nobody believes an old woman.”
- “Go. You've lived long enough inside his unfinished story.” `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Who she is:** She is protective, observant and more informed about Adrian than she initially appears. She preserves the workshop and the book because Adrian asked her to give Alex access at eighteen. She does not force Alex to stay; when he is ready, she tells him to go.

**Relationships:** She is Alex's home and remaining family anchor. She loved Adrian while also living with the consequences of his disappearance and debt. Nell becomes her practical caretaker when Alex leaves. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Habits and tells:** She folds and refolds cloth, sits calmly while the hidden bookshelf opens and can appear unbothered by extraordinary events. Her moments of clarity are marked by a steady, studying gaze. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**History:** She kept Adrian's old book on the top shelf and knew it was a lever for the hidden workshop. She waited until Alex's eighteenth birthday, then directed him to it while preserving the appearance of ordinary household routine. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md)`

**In existing drafts:** She sends Alex toward the workshop, recognizes Adrian's message and gives him permission to leave. When Alex promises to return, she answers, “I know.” `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Arc:** Her established movement is from apparently confused dependent to lucid keeper of Adrian's final trust, then to someone who actively releases Alex into his own life while accepting care from Nell. `(drafts/01-chapter-01.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

### Tomas

**Role:** Older Keel miner and worker at Extraction Site 6; former pump-repair colleague of Adrian. `(rules/ancestry.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Look:** Indigenous Andean, or Indigenous/mixed ancestry from nearby communities. He should look like someone whose family history in the region predates the corporate order. He wears a miner's work gear and a wrist guard carved with a balanced water-drop symbol. `(rules/ancestry.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Voice:** Direct, practical and economical. He speaks as an older local with long memory, not as a mystical elder. Established lines include:
- “Your father carried him everywhere. I knew Adrian Phantum.”
- “Twenty years ago we fixed the pumps on this level together. He paid for the parts himself.”
- “Not the man on the casino wall. The one who came down here and asked what we needed.”
- “Somebody has to take the blame for the button.”
- “You stopped for a stranger. He said that's how I'd know it was you.”
- “Why are they doing this?”
- “You stopped for us first.” `(rules/ancestry.md; drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Who he is:** Tomas has a long memory and an ordinary worker's sense of responsibility. He is cautious but willing to take personal blame to create a route for Alex. He recognizes character through consequential action, not family name.

**Relationships:** He knew and respected Adrian, contradicting the public gambling legend. He initially does not know Alex personally but trusts him after Alex turns back to rescue him. He becomes a bridge between Adrian's past and Alex's present, and between Alex and the Keel miners. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Habits and tells:** He watches Alex too long at Site 6, keeps the old lock oiled and carries the knowledge of the hidden door. After the accident he gives instructions calmly, pulls the emergency lever and takes responsibility aloud. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**History:** Twenty years earlier he repaired pumps with Adrian. Adrian paid for parts himself and asked Tomas to keep the old-works door, oil the lock, tell nobody and wait for whoever arrived with Bi11bot. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Tomas is pinned by a dropped hauling frame. Alex saves him, and Tomas then creates a distraction so Alex can reach the old mine. Later Tomas brings miners to stand with Alex, helps turn the manual valve slowly and explains that the community contributes to Alex's overdue balance because Alex stopped for them first. `(drafts/02-chapter-02.md)`

**Arc:** Tomas moves from secret keeper and isolated witness to public participant in Keel's collective response. His trust in Alex becomes a model for the community's trust.

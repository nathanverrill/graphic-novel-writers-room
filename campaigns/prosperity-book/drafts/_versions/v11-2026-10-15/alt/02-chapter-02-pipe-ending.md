# Chapter Two – *The Price of Departure* (pipe ending)

## PAGE 1: WHAT KEEL GIVES BACK

The same as the main draft, with one change: in P3 the workers do not turn the valve yet.

**P3: The valve, waiting (wide)**  
*Image:* Tomas and two pump workers at the junction's manual valve, one reading a pressure gauge. Tomas's hand rests on the wheel, not turning it.  
PUMP WORKER: We can open it now.  
TOMAS: Not yet. Not while anyone might be in that pipe.

P4 (the first jug) is cut. P5 and P6 stay.

## PAGE 2: GOODBYE

**P1: Home (wide)**  
*Image:* Alex comes through the front door. Ada at the table, cinching two packs: food, lamp, rope, the paper map. Grandmother in her chair. Nell (broad, sixties, short grey hair, white clinic smock) sits beside her with the medicine bag and a list.  
ADA: You're late.  
ALEX: I had company.

**P2: The wristband (tight)**  
*Image:* Close on Alex's wristband. The first two lines are green. The third flips to cold white as he watches.  
*Wristband:* DEBT 418,107 CR / OVERDUE 0 CR / TRAVEL: PERMITTED  
*Wristband, new line:* HOLD AT RAIL GATE: PHANTUM, A. — PENDING REVIEW  
ADA: They paid it. Every credit.  
ALEX: And someone up there doesn't care.

**P3: Who stays (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex looks at Grandmother, then at Nell.  
ALEX: I can't leave her alone.  
NELL: You're not. I'm moving in upstairs. Medicine's paid through the month. I'll do her mornings and nights.

**P4: Kneeling (dominant)**  
*Image:* Alex kneels at Grandmother's chair, at her eye level, as on Page 5 of Chapter One. Her hands are still, the cloth resting in her lap.  
GRANDMOTHER: I always told you he was working. Nobody believes an old woman.  
ALEX: I believed you. I just didn't know it yet.  
GRANDMOTHER: Go. You've lived long enough inside his unfinished story.

## PAGE 3: GOODBYE (continued)
**P5: The promise (medium)**  
*Image:* Grandmother takes Alex's face in both hands, steady, her eyes clear.  
ALEX *(quiet)*: I'll come back for you.  
GRANDMOTHER: I know.

**P6: Another way up (two-shot, threshold)**  
*Image:* Tomas at the open door, lamp in hand, out of breath. Ada behind Alex with her pack on.  
TOMAS: The train's watched. There's another way up. Your father used it.  
ALEX: Ada, you wanted to fix Keel, not leave it.  
ADA: I'm not leaving Keel behind. I'm going to find out who decided it had to stay this way.

**P7: Down (wide, from outside)**  
*Image:* From the street. Alex, Ada, Bi11bot and Tomas walk away downhill, toward the mine, not toward the rail gate up the slope. Grandmother and Nell are small warm shapes in the doorway, Grandmother's hand raised.

## PAGE 4: THE CLIMB

**P1: The hatch (wide)**  
*Image:* The hidden chamber again. Tomas swings open a round inspection hatch in the KEEL HOMES conduit where it leaves the junction and climbs into the mountain. Inside, iron rungs run up into darkness. A trickle of water runs along the bottom.  
TOMAS: Keel's own pipe. All the way to Halyard's reservoir. Near empty, as you proved.  
TOMAS: Shout when you see daylight. Then I open the valve. Slowly.

**P2: Inside (tall strip)**  
*Image:* Looking straight up the inside of the conduit. Alex climbs the rungs, Ada below him with the lamp, Bi11bot clinging to Alex's pack. The pipe is a long dark tube with a faint bronze glow far above.

**P3: One down (close two-shot)**  
*Image:* Alex and Ada pause on the rungs, faces close in the lamp light. Ada pats the Measure in Alex's pack.  
ADA: We have the Measure. One down.  
ALEX: Two to go. The Maker's Seal is next.

## PAGE 5: THE CLIMB (continued)
**P4: The clue (tight)**  
*Image:* Bi11bot projects a short note in Adrian's handwriting on the curved pipe wall, glowing gold.  
BI11BOT: It opened after you sent the proof. He left it for whoever used the Measure.  
*Note:* HALYARD PATENT REGISTRY. My work is filed under my name. The people who made it with me are not. Find them. — A.  
ALEX: Then that's where we start.

**P5: Below (small, cold)**  
*Image:* Back down in the chamber, unseen by Tomas, a gloved hand in the shadows by the passage holds a small device. A cold pearl-graphite readout.  
*Screen:* THE BOY HAS THE MEASURE. HE IS IN THE KEEL CONDUIT.  
*Reply:* FOLLOW. DO NOT TAKE IT YET.

**P6: Daylight (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex near the top, a grating above him, bronze Halyard light falling through it onto his face. He shouts down the pipe.  
ALEX: Daylight!  
*Far below, tiny:* TOMAS: Quarter turn!

**P7: The pipe starting to fill (dominant)**  
*Image:* Looking down the conduit from Alex's shoulder. Far, far below, calm water rises slowly in the bottom of the pipe, catching the lamp light: Keel's water, going home. Not a surge, a steady filling. Ada looks down at it beside him.  
*Caption, older Alex:* I went into the mine to get out of Keel. I came out owing it.  
*Lettering:* END OF CHAPTER TWO

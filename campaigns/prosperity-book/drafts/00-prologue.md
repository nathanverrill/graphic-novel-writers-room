# Prologue – *The Wall*

## PAGE 1: THE HOUSE

**P1: The contraption (wide)**  
*Image:* Alex's house at evening, in 2070, built the way Oasis was built in 2043: the book's own Oasis, now ordinary and lived in. Pearl-white curved architecture with rounded rooflines, terraced gardens, a still reservoir with fountains, slender white towers and a white monorail viaduct across the basin, the snow mountains behind, a rocket launch in the far distance, a delivery drone in the dusk sky. On the terrace, his daughter, about 9, is setting up a home-made Rube Goldberg contraption to water the plants: a marble rolling down a chute of bent spoons and pipe, tipping a cup, swinging a lever that tilts a watering can over a row of pots. Alex has just stepped out onto the terrace and stops to watch, a slow half-smile.  
ALEX: That reminds me of something.

**P2: The wall (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex has brought her inside to his wall. An Oasis-built hallway: smooth pearl-white curved walls, warm wood floor and a tall window. Through the window, far off across the basin, all three cities in 2070: Oasis, Halyard and Keel, all of them green, healthy and prosperous. Keel's old pit is terraced with gardens and trees, and a busy open-air market fills its streets. The wall is covered in framed patents, drawings of machines and news clippings. Alex, mid-40s, grey at the temples, a day's stubble, still dressed like a mechanic: a worn work jacket over a T-shirt, sleeves pushed up, scuffed boots, nothing proper or polished; Adrian's old steel watch on his left wrist. His daughter, about 9: warm-brown skin, freckles, brown eyes and long curly dark-brown hair; an orange zip jacket with H2O patches over a teal T-shirt with a small ringed planet, dark cargo trousers, orange-and-black sneakers. She stands on tiptoe in front of the wall.

**P3: The clippings (close)**  
*Image:* Close on three framed newspaper clippings, yellowed newsprint with columns of small grey text and a drawn photo under each headline, seen and never read aloud: "PHANTUM WATERWORKS: 4,000 KEEL JOBS CREATED" over a photo of a busy Keel workshop floor; "PATENT ROYALTIES FUND 300 NEW FOUNDERS" over a photo of dozens of founders, each with their own water unit and a different shop sign; "CLEAN-WATER PRICES DROP 60% AND HEALTH IMPROVED" over a photo of a Keel family filling a jug at a clean tap. Every picture is drawn in the book's own inked style, never photographic.

**P4: The question**  
DAUGHTER: Did you make *all* of these?

## PAGE 2: THE INVENTIONS

**P1: The collaborators**  
*Image:* Alex taps a framed picture from the hard years: four tired, grimy people crowded around a patched-together prototype in a cramped, rust-streaked Keel workshop, working late under a single lamp. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren, never named. Tough times in Keel, not a celebration.  
ALEX: Made some. Fixed most. Worked with lots of people smarter than me.

**P2: The purifier**  
*Image:* She points at a patent with a purifier diagram.  
DAUGHTER: That one's in my school!

**P3: The first try**  
*Image:* One frame among the patents is a drawing of a scorched, cracked prototype, with "v1 - didn't work" written on it in his hand. Neither of them remarks on it.

**P4: The founder (small)**  
*Image:* Small, in a corner, two frames side by side. One is old: a faded brass nameplate, **PHANTUM WATERWORKS · ADRIAN PHANTUM, FOUNDER**. The other is new: a clipping whose picture shows Alex, mid-40s, at the open doors of a busy Keel workshop hall, young makers at benches behind him: **PHANTUM WATERWORKS CENTER FOR ENTREPRENEURS OPENS IN KEEL · FREE BENCHES AND MENTORS FOR NEW FOUNDERS**. The daughter doesn't notice them. She stops at one frame that doesn't match the others.

## PAGE 3: THE SKETCH

**P1: The sketch (close)**  
*Image:* A crumpled, grease-stained sheet with a pencil sketch of a pump bracket. Crude, smudged fingerprints, no patent number.

**P2: Why not a patent**  
DAUGHTER: This one's just a drawing.  
ALEX: And the one I'm most proud of. It helped a lot of people.

**P3: Fun**  
*Image:* The daughter, beaming.  
DAUGHTER: That looks like a lot of fun!

## PAGE 4: THE TURN

**P1**  
*Image:* Still in Alex's 2070 home, at the wall, the Oasis-built hallway and its tall window behind them.  
ALEX: Not in the beginning.

**P2**  
*Image:* In his home: the daughter looks up at him, waiting.

**P3**  
*Image:* In his home, by the wall of frames. Alex turns from the wall and looks at his daughter as he speaks, his hands still; no pointing.  
ALEX: When I was a teenager, I had nothing. We hardly had any water...

**P4: Keel (full width)**  
*Image:* The sketch dissolves into the rust and charcoal of Keel's pump hall. Alex, on the morning he turns eighteen, has his palm flat on the pump.  
*Caption:* Almost thirty years ago...

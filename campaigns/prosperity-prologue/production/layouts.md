## Page 1 (right) — 4 panels, grid: one wide establishing panel over one wide hallway panel over two lower panels

**Reading path:** Panel 1 briefly establishes the ordinary 2070 home and basin. Panel 2 brings the eye inside to Alex and his daughter. Panel 3 concentrates attention on the wall’s public record; Panel 4 lands on the daughter’s raised hand and question.

**Dominant beat/image:** The daughter pointing at the wall while looking back to Alex in Panel 4.

- **P1 (full-width top tier):** ESTABLISHING / WIDE, eye level. Warm evening exterior of the Prosperity basin. A comfortable, lived-in house sits against distant mountains and stepped basin terraces. Pearl-white composite water-capture panels cover part of the roof; a rooftop cistern rises behind the roofline; a small purifier unit stands beside the entry. A delivery drone crosses the sky, ordinary and unobtrusive. The house is maintained but not luxurious. No car, garden spectacle, extra technology or readable signage. Leave quiet sky space for no lettering.
- **P2 (full-width middle tier):** WIDE, human-scale hallway view, eye level. Warm evening light enters a lived-in home. Alex stands beside a dense but organized wall of framed patents, machine photographs, clippings and personal artifacts. Alex is a man in his mid-40s with medium-brown skin, a practical mechanic’s build, average height and broad, work-hardened hands. He has a broad, tired face, close-cropped dark hair grey at the temples, dark eyes, and day-old stubble. He wears a worn charcoal-brown work jacket over a pale T-shirt, sleeves pushed up, practical dark trousers and scuffed work boots; his silhouette is compact, useful and slightly stooped from years of hands-on repair. On his left wrist he wears Adrian’s old steel watch, slightly too large for him, with a plain cream dial, scratched glass and a small dark display window that stays dark; it has no readout and counts nothing. He keeps the watch partly hidden beneath his sleeve. His approximately nine-year-old daughter stands on tiptoe before the wall. She has warm-brown skin, a child’s compact build and an energetic, forward-reaching posture. She has an open, expressive face, bright attentive eyes and two low uneven braids. She wears a mustard cardigan over a pale blue school shirt, a navy skirt, white socks and red sneakers. She studies the frames one by one while Alex watches her. Keep the upper area above the daughter clear for later lettering.
- **P3 (two-thirds-width lower tier, left):** WALL-FOCUSED WIDE, eye level. Keep the hallway visibly present around a dense but orderly frame arrangement. The two approved news clippings dominate through bold headline-shaped lettering areas, beside a separate-founders photograph showing many distinct founders with individual water units and different shop signs. Also show the collaborator photograph of four people laughing together in a lab, the purifier diagram, patent records and machine photographs as unmistakable framed evidence. Do not generate words or legible text in the art layer; reserve clean frame interiors and stable lettering areas for the exact headlines and later lettering.
- **P4 (one-third-width lower tier, right):** MEDIUM, eye level. The daughter remains on tiptoe, points toward the wall, then looks back toward Alex with direct curiosity. Keep her face and raised hand unobstructed against the framed evidence. Leave the upper-right area clear for her balloon; the tail points to her mouth.

**Page-turn hook:** The daughter’s concrete question pulls the reader into Alex’s answer on Page 2.

**Flags:** The exterior-to-interior movement is the approved deliberate exception. Panel 1 remains brief and quiet so the hallway carries the story. The two headlines are represented by clean text areas and visual hierarchy only; exact wording is lettering-layer material.

## Page 2 (left) — 4 panels, grid: two equal upper panels over two equal lower panels

**Reading path:** Alex’s tap redirects the daughter’s attention to the collaborator photograph. Her movement continues to the purifier, then past the failed prototype, and finally stops at the mismatched pump-bracket frame. The page turns on the daughter’s new question.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, the daughter stopping at the damaged sketch while the paid-in-full statement remains planted for the reader.

- **P1 (top-left, half-width):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, eye level. Alex taps the framed collaborator photograph through the glass. His hand and the photograph are the immediate focus; the four people remain confined to the inked photograph and are not active characters. Leave clean upper-left space for Alex’s balloon.
- **P2 (top-right, half-width):** MEDIUM, eye level. The daughter has moved one step along the wall and places her fingertip directly on the purifier diagram. The adjacent clipping remains secondary. Alex follows her gaze from just behind. Leave clean upper space for the daughter’s balloon without covering her face or fingertip. No additional Alex balloon.
- **P3 (bottom-left, half-width):** CLOSE, eye level. A scorched, cracked prototype dominates among cleaner framed patents and machine photographs. Show blackened casing, heat damage and physical failure. Reserve a stable blank label area beside or beneath it for the exact artifact label; do not generate words. Partial silhouettes of Alex and his daughter at the edge keep the object part of the wall. Keep the upper third quiet and silent.
- **P4 (bottom-right, half-width):** MEDIUM-CLOSE, eye level with a slight high angle toward the lower wall. The daughter’s movement ends at a visibly mismatched frame containing a crumpled, grease-stained pump-bracket sketch with fingerprints and crude pencil geometry. She looks only at the sketch. In a separate neighboring frame, the paid-in-full statement has a clean stable lettering area and remains subordinate through scale and contrast; it is visible to the reader but not noticed by the daughter. Do not generate text in either frame. Leave the upper area clear for no lettering.

**Page-turn hook:** The daughter’s attention settles on the unpatented sketch, creating the question on Page 3.

**Flags:** The fixed dialogue is restored exactly. The purifier panel contains only the daughter’s approved line; no explanatory ownership dialogue is added. The paid-in-full statement is lettered here once and remains background art on Page 4.

## Page 3 (right) — 4 panels, grid: large upper panel over one wide dialogue panel over two lower panels

**Reading path:** Panel 1 gives the sketch a silent inspection. Panel 2 turns the inspection into a direct question and Alex’s restrained answer. Panel 3 gives the daughter’s admiration enough width to breathe. Panel 4 closes on Alex’s concealed watch and the page-turn question.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, Alex covering the face of the watch while studying the sketch.

- **P1 (full-width top tier):** CLOSE, eye level. The damaged sketch receives a quiet inspection. Make the crumpled paper, grease, fingerprints, mismatched frame and crude bracket geometry clear. The geometry must be suitable for a direct match to the pump hardware on Page 4. No lettering.
- **P2 (full-width middle tier):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, eye level. The daughter faces Alex beside the frame, slightly raised on her toes, one hand hovering near but not touching the glass. Alex stands close, restrained and guarded without becoming stern. Keep both faces and the sketch readable. Reserve separate uncluttered balloon zones in reading order: daughter upper-left, Alex upper-right.
- **P3 (wide lower-left / three-quarter width):** MEDIUM, eye level. The daughter turns from the sketch toward Alex with a bright, uncomplicated smile. The wall recedes into warm, softened detail. Give this panel enough width and quiet negative space for her complete balloon without covering her face.
- **P4 (lower-right / one-quarter width):** CLOSE INSERT, slightly high angle. Alex studies the sketch while his left wrist enters the foreground. The old steel watch is physically legible: worn steel, slightly too large, plain cream dial, scratched glass and a small dark display window that stays dark. His thumb visibly covers the watch face while enough dial, scratched glass and dark window remain identifiable. The sketch edge stays visible in a separate background plane. Do not show the caseback, equation or any readout. No lettering.

**Page-turn hook:** Alex’s covered watch and silence ask what the beginning was really like.

**Flags:** The daughter’s third-panel balloon has been given a wider panel than the prior layout. The watch is staged as one unambiguous still gesture, not as a timed action.

## Page 4 (left) — 3 dialogue panels over one full-width transition panel

**Reading path:** The upper row answers the Page 3 question in three restrained beats: Alex sets a boundary, Alex’s daughter waits, and Alex names the difficult beginning. The full-width bottom panel transforms the sketch into the physical pump and hands the reader into Chapter 1.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, the literal bracket match with young Alex’s palm on the pump.

- **P1 (top-left, one-third width):** MEDIUM, eye level. Alex remains beside the wall and mismatched sketch frame, looking toward Alex’s daughter rather than presenting the records. Leave clean upper-left space for the short balloon.
- **P2 (top-middle, one-third width):** MEDIUM CLOSE, eye level. The Alex’s daughter looks directly up at Alex and waits without speaking. Her attention is open, not frightened or melodramatic. The mismatched frame remains a quiet bridge between them. Keep the previously lettered paid-in-full statement only as background art; do not add a second lettering item. Leave the upper area clear and silent.
- **P3 (top-right, one-third width):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, eye level. Alex faces his daughter. His posture has changed from presenting the wall to facing the history behind it. The daughter listens. Keep both faces and the sketch readable. Reserve a quiet upper balloon field away from the faces and frame for the complete debt-and-water line.
- **P4 (full-width bottom tier):** FULL-WIDTH TRANSITION, low human-scale angle, bleed. The damaged pump-bracket sketch occupies the foreground edge. Its crude geometric bracket lines become the exact rust-and-charcoal bracket hardware of a real pump through a clear match in angle, shape and placement. The warm hallway disappears into Keel’s dense, wind-scoured industrial pump hall: compressed space, rust, oxidized orange, grease, dust, charcoal machinery and hard industrial shadow. Seventeen-year-old Alex is a visibly younger, narrower version of the older Alex, with medium-brown skin, close-cropped dark hair, determined dark eyes and a lean repairer’s build. He wears grease-marked, worn work clothes appropriate to a dense industrial pump hall. His silhouette is narrow and alert, with one arm extended and his palm flat against the pump. On his left wrist he wears Adrian’s old steel watch, physically identical to the older Alex’s watch: worn steel, slightly too large, plain cream dial, scratched glass and a small display window that stays dark. Give young Alex a readable face, left wrist and palm-on-pump silhouette at a clear secondary scale. Reserve stable quiet industrial shadow for the caption, away from the bracket, Alex’s face, palm and watch. No other characters and no Chapter 1 event.

**Page-turn / chapter handoff:** The debt-and-water admission is the emotional reveal after the Page 3 turn. The bottom panel is the threshold of Chapter 1, not Chapter 1 material.

**Flags:** The paid-in-full statement is not repeated as lettering on this page. The bracket geometry, young Alex’s identity and the identical watch must all remain readable in the final transition.

# Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The script preserves the approved four-page progression, dialogue beats, character restrictions, artifacts, debt continuity and Keel transition. The main production problem is that Page 1 changes from the exterior house to the interior hallway, while the showrunner’s drawability rule asks for one location per page where possible. A second pass should also protect artifact legibility and the sketch-to-bracket match without adding visual load.

- **2070 Alex:** Mid-40s, grey at temples, plain charcoal-brown sweater over pale shirt, practical trousers and work-worn hands. Maintain this design through Pages 1–4 Panels 1–3.
- **Daughter:** Approximately nine, mustard cardigan, pale blue school shirt, navy skirt, white socks and red sneakers. She remains the only active character besides Alex.

- **Alex’s watch:** Left wrist throughout the 2070 sequence; partly hidden before Page 3 Panel 4, then actively covered with his thumb after he studies the sketch. It must not display a readout.
- **Wall artifacts:** Required items are present across Pages 1–4: both exact headlines, separate founders photograph, collaborator photograph, purifier patent, machine photographs, failed prototype, account statement and pump-bracket sketch.
- **Dialogue progression:** The script follows the causal order: authorship question → qualified credit → school deployment → unpatented drawing → “fun” assumption → “Not in the beginning” → debt reveal.
- **Time and age:** Pages 1–4 Panels 1–3 remain in about 2070; only the final full-width panel changes to seventeen-year-old Alex in Keel, 2043.
- **Final visual identity:** The sketch’s bracket geometry must remain consistent when it becomes the physical pump bracket. Young Alex must be visibly narrower and younger than adult Alex while retaining recognizable dark hair and determined eyes.

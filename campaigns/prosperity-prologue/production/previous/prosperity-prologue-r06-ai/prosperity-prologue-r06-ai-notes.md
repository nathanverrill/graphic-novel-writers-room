# Verdict

**Needs a pass.** The four-page structure, approved dialogue, character restrictions, artifact continuity and final Keel handoff are intact, and the layouts keep to four panels or fewer per page. The pages are not yet execution-ready because Page 1 carries two locations, Page 1 Panel 3 and Page 2 Panel 4 overload the image model with required wall evidence, and Page 3’s narrow third panel gives a long balloon too little reliable lettering space.

## Plausibility ledger

- **2070 home:** Holds. The layouts include the fixed solar tiles, maintained garden, ten-year-old car, warm evening light and ordinary comfortable scale. The exterior/interior combination is a production risk, not a world contradiction.
- **Independent companies and licensing:** Holds. The separate founders photograph, independent-shop headline and school-purifier exchange communicate distinct businesses without introducing a collective, co-op or giveaway system.
- **The Houses:** Holds as an intentionally undefined commercial presence. The layout must avoid adding recognizable institutional uniforms, logos, political symbols or organizational details not established by canon.
- **School deployment:** Holds. The daughter points to the purifier patent, and Alex says another company put it in schools. No purification method, capacity or technical operation is invented.
- **Collaborators:** Holds. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren remain confined to the single photograph and are not individually designed or activated.
- **Failed prototype:** Holds. The exact **“v1 - didn't work”** wording is present, with no dialogue or unsupported explanation.
- **Account statement and debt:** Holds in canon, but execution-sensitive. The exact statement **“PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS”** must be rendered as lettering, not generated as image text, and must remain the same statement when repeated as background continuity on Page 4.
- **Pump-bracket sketch:** Holds. The sketch remains crumpled, grease-stained, fingerprinted, crude and unpatented. No technical or legal reason for its lack of patenting is invented.
- **Adrian’s watch:** Holds. It appears only on Alex’s left wrist, has a cream dial, scratched glass and dark display window, and supplies no readout or counting function. Adrian does not appear.
- **Keel transition:** Holds if the final panel preserves the fixed caption, rust-and-charcoal pump hall, matching bracket and seventeen-year-old Alex’s palm flat on the pump. No Chapter 1 dialogue or additional character is added.
- **No unlicensed inventions:** The layouts do not declare a creditor, debt mechanism, patent institution, water technology, House structure or repayment process. These remain correctly outside the prologue.

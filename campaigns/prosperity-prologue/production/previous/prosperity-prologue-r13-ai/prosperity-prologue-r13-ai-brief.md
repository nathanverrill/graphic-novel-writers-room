# Prosperity Prologue — Development Brief

## Logline

In 2070, a curious girl studies the wall recording her father’s earned success, and her innocent questions force Alex to admit that the life she sees began in debt, rationed water and a pump hall in Keel, 2043.

## Theme

A useful life is built through practical work, collaboration, failure, accountable ownership and repayment—not through solitary genius or effortless prosperity.

## Tone & comps

- **The Iron Giant** — domestic warmth, a child’s concrete point of view and emotional weight carried by images rather than speeches.
- **October Sky** — tactile invention, repair and earned ambition grounded in material hardship.
- **The Incredibles** — readable domestic staging and objects that reveal a family history, without importing superhero spectacle.

Take their warmth, clarity and visual storytelling. Do not imitate their plots, dialogue or visual style.

## Audience & rating

High-school readers and general audiences. **PG / mild thematic distress.** No violence, profanity, horror or gambling imagery. The prologue requires no prior knowledge of Keel, Alpha, the Water Wars, safeguards or artifacts.

## Format

- **Exactly 4 pages**, one prologue; no additional pages.
- Single issue or chapter opening; full color.
- Default trim: **6.625" × 10.25"**.
- Pages 1–3: four panels each.
- Page 4: three dialogue panels and one full-width transition panel.
- Odd pages are right-hand pages; Page 4 carries the principal reveal after the Page 3 turn.
- The final panel is the threshold of Chapter 1, not Chapter 1 material.

## Visual direction

Warm, observant, grounded near-future science-fiction realism. The 2070 world is approximately 80% aged and adapted present-day infrastructure, 15% ordinary emerging technology and 5% genuinely new invention. Show retrofit, wear and maintenance rather than sleek replacement.

The 2070 home uses warm evening light, cream paper, worn wood, muted greens, composite white and practical metal. It is comfortable, lived-in and slightly worn, never luxurious or dystopian. The hallway wall is dense but organized: framed patents, machine photographs, clippings and personal artifacts should read as a domestic record, not an infographic.

Keel is a hard reversal: rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, grease, dust, industrial shadow and compressed space. The final panel must make the pump-bracket sketch become the physical pump through a clear geometric match.

Keep the camera at human scale. Prioritize hands, frames, damaged paper, the watch, the daughter’s sightline and Alex’s restrained reactions. Framed photographs and clippings use the book’s inked graphic-novel treatment, not photorealistic inserts.

**Page 1 staging decision:** retain the approved exterior-to-interior movement as a deliberate exception to the one-location-per-page drawability preference. Panel 1 is a brief, quiet exterior establishing image; Panels 2–4 remain in the same hallway and carry the story. Do not add garden spectacle, vehicles, signage or extra technology beyond the established home, water systems, mountains, terraces and delivery drone.

**Wall hierarchy:** Page 1 Panel 3 prioritizes the two approved headlines and the separate-founders photograph. The collaborator photograph, purifier diagram and machine photographs remain unmistakable framed evidence but need not be internally legible there. Exact text belongs to lettering, never to generated art.

**Page 2 statement:** the paid-in-full frame receives a stable, clean lettering area and remains subordinate through scale and contrast, not illegibility. The daughter does not notice it.

**Page 3:** give the daughter’s “Inventing stuff…” balloon enough panel width and quiet space to preserve her face and bright admiration.

**Page 4 transition:** balance the literal bracket match with a clearly readable young Alex, his palm flat on the pump, and a quiet caption field for **KEEL. 2043.**

## Canon

Everything marked **FIXED** in `characters.md`, `world.md` and `story.md` is canon. The following decisions are also binding:

- The book is the four-page prologue **“The Wall,”** opening in about 2070 and ending in Keel in 2043.
- The 2070 sequence begins with the approved exterior home establishing panel, then moves inside to the hallway.
- Alex is in his mid-40s, visibly a mechanic and repairer: worn work jacket over a T-shirt, pushed-up sleeves, scuffed boots, grey at the temples and day-old stubble. He is not polished by success.
- Alex’s approximately nine-year-old daughter is the only active character besides Alex. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren appear only in the framed photograph of four collaborators laughing in a lab.
- Alex owns or co-owns the patents represented on the wall and licenses them to separate manufacturing and deployment companies. Those companies remain independent and compete with one another and with the Houses.
- The wall shows useful products, recorded ownership, collaboration, failure and repayment. It depicts no co-op, collective, charitable distribution, giveaway, cancellation, bailout or windfall.
- The required wall evidence is present: patents, machine photographs, the purifier diagram, both approved headlines, the independent-founders photograph, the collaborator photograph, the scorched prototype marked **“v1 - didn’t work,”** the account statement **“PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS,”** and the damaged pump-bracket sketch in its mismatched frame.
- Money is called **credits**. The exact Chapter 1 debt is **418,607 credits**. The prologue may use the approved rounded line **“In the beginning I was deep in debt, our water was rationed...”** but must not introduce an alternate debt figure or unsupported wording.
- The debt shown as paid in full is the same obligation. Its creditor, terms and repayment mechanics remain outside this prologue; canon establishes only that later invention and licensing income repay it.
- The Phantum Purifier is a licensed design used in schools by another company. Its technical mechanism remains unspecified.
- The Houses are large commercial competitors, not the government. Their internal structure, legal form and institutional limits remain unspecified.
- Adrian’s old steel watch is on Alex’s left wrist in both periods: worn steel, slightly too large, cream dial, scratched glass and a dark display window. It has no readout and counts nothing. Adrian’s looping equation is engraved on the caseback, but the caseback is not displayed in the prologue.
- Alex repeatedly keeps the watch partly hidden and covers its face with his thumb after looking at the sketch. Adrian’s identity and relationship to Alex remain concealed.
- The final panel is full-width: the sketch becomes the rust-and-charcoal Keel pump hall; seventeen-year-old Alex has his palm flat on the pump; the caption reads **“Keel. 2043.”**
- The pump repair is not the invention that later makes Alex successful. It establishes his practical ability to fix real machinery under pressure.
- Bi11bot, TJ, Alpha, Adrian and every other person absent from the approved sequence do not appear. Adrian exists only through the watch.
- All approved dialogue and panel order remain unchanged. No additional explanatory dialogue is permitted.

## What we take from the material

- **Characters — Alex:** take the approved older and younger appearances, repairer identity, voice, watch behavior and limited prologue movement.
- **Characters — Alex’s Daughter:** take her approved lines, tiptoe posture, school connection, concrete curiosity and waiting reaction to Alex’s final answer.
- **Characters — Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren:** take only the unnamed collaborator photograph and Alex’s acknowledgment of their intelligence. Set aside individual appearances, specialties and later paths for future development.
- **Characters — Adrian / Explicitly Absent Characters:** retain the watch as concealed continuity; set aside Adrian’s identity and all prohibited appearances.
- **World — The Prosperity basin:** take the mixed Andean high-desert basin, mountains, terraces, dry climate and shared regional setting. The exact geographic location remains unnecessary and open.
- **World — Keel:** take the dense, wind-scoured industrial city, pump hall, rust-and-charcoal material language, water pressure and 2043 threshold.
- **World — The 2070 home:** take the lived-in home, roof capture panels, purifier, cistern, evening basin, mountains, terraces and delivery drone. The exterior remains a brief establishing image.
- **World — Economics, Ownership and Work:** take independent licensing, recorded ownership, useful products, competition, school deployment, repair and earned repayment. Do not expand the legal or commercial institutions behind them.
- **World — Technology:** take only the visible water systems, delivery drone, pump, bracket and records. Do not specify efficiency, capacities or mechanisms.
- **World — Institutions and Power:** take the existence of independent shops, schools and Houses; leave their deeper structure open.
- **Story — Page 1 / Page 2 / Page 3 / Page 4:** take the fixed progression, dialogue, evidence hierarchy, page turn and final transition.
- **Story — Causality / Reveals / Ending:** take the movement from visible prosperity to qualified credit, collaboration, failure, repayment, debt and rationed water, ending at Keel.
- Set aside all casino, casino-chip, collective, giveaway, charitable, bailout and windfall concepts.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing in this section is canon.

- The wall may use differing frame ages and mounting quality to distinguish polished public records from Alex’s private, damaged history.
- The paid-in-full statement may remain findable to a careful reader without becoming a visual destination.
- The 2070 home may contain small signs of ongoing repair—mended furniture, maintained tools or a repaired latch—provided they do not create new story events.
- The title **“The Wall”** may remain internal rather than appearing on a page.

## Non-negotiables

- Exactly four pages; no more and no fewer.
- Open in warm, ordinary 2070 prosperity and end in Keel, 2043.
- Preserve all approved dialogue exactly.
- Let objects and behavior carry exposition; no economic lecture or backstory speech.
- No partner, backer, missions, Alpha, safeguards or artifacts are named or shown.
- No casino chip, gambling system, co-op, collective, giveaway, cancellation, bailout or windfall.
- Alex’s independent ownership and competitive licensing remain visually distinct.
- The paid-in-full statement and debt reveal refer to the same obligation.
- The pump-bracket geometry must match the pump hardware in the final panel.
- No additional active characters appear.
- Art prompts must reserve lettering areas but generate no words, headlines, captions or labels on the art layer.

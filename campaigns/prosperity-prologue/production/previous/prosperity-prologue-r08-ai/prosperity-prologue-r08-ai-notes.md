# Verdict

**Needs a rethink.** The development files agree on the four-page premise, ending, characters, world limits and required visual evidence, but `script.md` changes fixed dialogue and therefore changes the prologue’s approved causal meaning. The book also currently presents an ordinary contemporary-looking home, a mismatched older Alex design, and several unsupported implications that weaken the intended near-future setting and earned-licensing theme.

## Plausibility ledger

- **2070 home:** Currently weak. The script’s “solar roof tiles” are an undeclared technology substitution, and the panel omits several required ordinary water systems. Replace with the canon pearl-white composite and water-capture panels, cistern, purifier, basin and drone. This is an unlabelled invention/drift, not a new licensed technology.
- **Debt:** The script’s “owed four hundred thousand credits I'd never borrowed” is not supported by the approved prologue and conflicts with the fixed debt-and-rationed-water line. The exact Chapter 1 debt is 418,607 credits, and the prologue permits only the approved rounded conversational description if used. Treat the current wording as a contradiction of fixed story material.
- **Repayment:** The paid-in-full statement correctly records the same obligation, but its Page 4 repetition remains undecided. Do not make the repeat canon without Director approval.
- **Independent licensing:** The wall evidence supports separate companies and competition, but the added line “Somebody else's company put it there” is not an approved license and risks implying theft, giveaway or an unexplained transfer. Restore the fixed dialogue and let the visual evidence carry licensing.
- **Houses:** The headline establishes a speculative commercial competitor. No additional structure, logo, uniform, government connection or legal mechanism should be invented.
- **Technology:** The purifier, capture panels, cistern and drone remain acceptable EG-level ordinary future technology when shown without capacities, mechanisms or efficiency claims. “Solar roof tiles” is not established and should be removed.
- **Keel transition:** Holds if the final panel includes the fixed caption, rust-and-charcoal pump hall, geometric bracket match, young Alex’s palm flat on the pump and identical watch on his left wrist. The current script under-specifies the watch but does not otherwise invent a prohibited institution.
- **Text production:** Exact headlines, artifact labels, account statement and caption must remain lettering-layer material. Any current instruction that asks generated art to render those words is a production violation.

## Page 1 (right) — 4 panels, grid: one wide establishing panel over three hallway panels

**Reading path:** Panel 1 establishes the ordinary 2070 home; the eye then enters the hallway in Panel 2 and travels across the wall through Panels 3–4. The exterior-to-interior change is a deliberate, compressed location transition required by the fixed opening; Panels 2–4 remain one continuous hallway location.

**Dominant beat/image:** The daughter’s pointing hand and first question in Panel 4.

- **P1 (full-width top tier):** ESTABLISHING / WIDE, eye level. Warm evening exterior of the ordinary house, solar roof tiles, maintained vegetable garden and ten-year-old car. Keep the panel brief and uncluttered; no extra futuristic infrastructure or readable signage. No lettering; leave a small quiet sky area if needed for breathing room.
- **P2 (full-width middle tier):** WIDE human-scale hallway view, eye level. Alex stands beside the dense but orderly archive wall while his daughter studies it on tiptoe. Keep the wall legible as a collection of frames without making it an infographic. Reserve the upper-left wall area for no lettering; keep both figures’ faces and the daughter’s sightline clear.
- **P3 (two-thirds-width lower tier, left):** MEDIUM-WIDE, eye level. A readable cluster of wall evidence: the two exact news headlines, the separate founders-and-water-units photograph, patent diagrams, machine photographs and the framed photograph of four unnamed collaborators laughing together in a lab. The headlines and founders photograph are the dominant readable artifacts; other records remain identifiable but need not be text-legible. Reserve a clean upper margin for no lettering.
- **P4 (one-third-width lower tier, right):** MEDIUM, slight low angle from the wall toward the daughter. She points between patent records and machine photographs, then looks toward Alex; her head tilts forward with direct curiosity. Leave the upper-right area open for her balloon, with the tail clearly aimed at her mouth.

**Page-turn hook:** The daughter’s question pulls the reader into Alex’s answer on Page 2.

**Flags:** Deliberate two-location exception on this page: exterior Panel 1 compresses the house context before the fixed hallway sequence. Keep Panel 1 visually simple so the wall does not compete with a second architectural setting. The small daughter’s balloon must not cover the key wall artifacts.

## Page 2 (left) — 4 panels, grid: two equal upper panels over two equal lower panels

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, the mismatched pump-bracket frame with the planted paid-in-full statement.

- **P1 (top-left, half page width):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT / slight low angle. Alex taps the collaborator photograph through its glass. Keep the four people confined to the photograph and visually unnamed. Reserve the top-left wall space for Alex’s balloon without covering the photograph.
- **P2 (top-right, half page width):** MEDIUM / eye level. The daughter points specifically at the purifier patent diagram; the clipping **“40TH INDEPENDENT SHOP LICENSES PHANTUM PURIFIER”** sits immediately adjacent as supporting evidence. Alex remains behind her in the same hallway. Reserve upper space above the figures for two readable balloons, with Alex’s second balloon lower and to the right of the daughter’s.
- **P3 (bottom-left, half page width):** CLOSE / eye level. The scorched, cracked prototype dominates among cleaner patent frames. The handwritten label **“v1 - didn't work”** is clearly legible. Partial silhouettes of Alex and the daughter at the frame edge preserve the wall context. Keep the top third open and silent.
- **P4 (bottom-right, half page width):** MEDIUM-CLOSE / slight high angle toward the lower wall. The mismatched frame contains the crumpled, grease-stained pump-bracket sketch with fingerprints and crude pencil geometry. **Page-turn hook:** The daughter’s attention settles on the unpatented sketch, leading directly to her question on Page 3.

**Flags:** The account statement is planted here as reader-visible evidence, not a discovery for the daughter. Keep the purifier diagram and clipping distinct: her finger lands on the diagram, not the headline.

## Page 3 (right) — 4 panels, grid: large upper reveal panel over three lower panels

**Reading path:** Panel 1 gives the sketch its own quiet inspection. Panels 2–3 return to the two characters and move from inquiry to admiration. Panel 4 compresses the emotional turn into Alex’s gaze and concealed watch.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 4, Alex covering the watch after studying the sketch.

- **P1 (full-width top tier):** CLOSE / eye level on the mismatched frame. The crude bracket geometry, damaged paper, grease and fingerprints must read clearly. No lettering; allow the panel to function as a silent inspection.
- **P2 (lower-left, half-width):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT / eye level. The daughter faces Alex, still raised slightly onto her toes, hand hovering near but not touching the glass. Alex is quiet and guarded without appearing stern. Reserve the upper-left space for her question and upper-right space for Alex’s answer, in that reading order.
- **P3 (lower-middle, one-quarter width):** MEDIUM / eye level. The daughter turns toward Alex with an uncomplicated smile while the wall fills the background. Use a narrow but readable panel; reserve the top third for her balloon.
- **P4 (lower-right, one-quarter width):** CLOSE INSERT / slightly high angle on Alex’s hands, sketch and left wrist. The sketch occupies the background or upper field; Alex looks down toward it, then his thumb visibly covers the watch face. Show the worn steel, cream dial, scratched glass and dark display window before the thumb obscures most of it. Keep the watch readable without inventing a readout or caseback engraving. No lettering.

**Page-turn hook:** Alex’s covered watch and silence ask what the beginning was really like; Page 4 answers with his first restrained line.

**Flags:** Keep the watch gesture legible as concealment, not casual adjustment. Do not show Adrian or any other active character.

## Page 4 (left) — 3 dialogue panels over one full-width transition panel

**Reading path:** The three upper panels slow the exchange: Alex’s first boundary, the daughter’s still attention, then the debt statement. The full-width bottom panel breaks the 2070 grid and transforms the sketch into the actual pump bracket in Keel.

**Dominant beat/image:** Panel 3, Alex naming the debt. Panel 4 is the visual transition and chapter handoff.

- **P1 (top-left, one-third width):** MEDIUM / eye level. Alex remains beside the wall and mismatched sketch frame, looking toward his daughter rather than at the records. Reserve the upper-left area for the short balloon.
- **P2 (top-middle, one-third width):** MEDIUM CLOSE / slightly low angle from the daughter’s point of view. She looks up at Alex and waits, attentive and still, with no tears, panic, recoil or melodrama. Keep the wall behind her and the small paid-in-full statement visible as background continuity, not a newly discovered object. No dialogue; leave clean space above her head.
- **P3 (top-right, one-third width):** MEDIUM TWO-SHOT / eye level. Alex faces his daughter; the polished records and rough sketch frame share the background. His delivery is plain and understated. Reserve the upper area for the long balloon without covering either face or the key frame.
- **P4 (full-width bottom tier):** FULL-WIDTH TRANSITION / low human-scale angle, with the bracket as the dominant foreground shape. Start at the grease-stained pencil geometry of the framed sketch and visually match two or three unmistakable bracket points into the same geometry as rusted, grease-dark metal on the actual pump. The warm hallway disappears through the material transformation. In the secondary focal area, seventeen-year-old Alex stands in the rust-and-charcoal pump hall with his palm flat on the pump; he is visibly younger and narrower but retains recognizable dark hair and determined eyes. Keep clean negative space in the upper-left or upper-center for the caption **“KEEL. 2043.”** Do not add Chapter 1 dialogue or other characters.

**Page-turn / chapter handoff:** The debt line is the emotional reveal after the turn; the bottom transition directly hands into Chapter 1 through the bracket match.

**Flags:** The transition must preserve both literal bracket geometry and material transformation. The bracket is largest; young Alex and palm-on-pump remain clearly readable but secondary. The caption sits in negative space, never over the bracket or Alex.

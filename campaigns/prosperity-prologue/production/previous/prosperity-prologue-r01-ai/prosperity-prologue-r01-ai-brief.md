# Prosperity Prologue — Development Brief

## Logline

In about 2070, an independent inventor’s curious daughter reads the evidence of his comfortable life from a hallway wall, until her innocent question forces him to reveal that it began with a debt he never borrowed—and the book turns back to his seventeen-year-old self in Keel.

## Theme

A successful life is not a monument to solitary genius but a visible record of useful work, shared knowledge, failure, ownership, responsibility and repayment.

## Tone & comps

- **The Iron Giant** — warmth, plainspoken feeling and a child’s perspective opening onto larger moral stakes.
- **October Sky** — practical invention, repair and earned ambition rather than effortless genius.
- **The Incredibles** — domestic normalcy and visual storytelling that lets objects carry history, without adopting its superhero spectacle.

The book takes understatement, readable visual evidence and emotional restraint from these references. It does not imitate their plots or voices.

## Audience & rating

High-school readers and general audiences. **PG / mild thematic distress.** No violence, profanity, horror or gambling imagery. The prologue assumes no prior knowledge of Keel, Alpha, the Water Wars, the safeguards or the artifacts.

## Format

- **Exactly 4 pages**, one prologue, no additional pages.
- Default trim: **6.625" × 10.25"**, full color.
- Pages 1–3: four panels each.
- Page 4: three dialogue panels and a full-width transition panel.
- Odd pages are right-hand pages; the principal reveal is on Page 4, after the Page 3 turn.
- The final panel hands directly into Chapter 1 and does not add Chapter 1 material.

## Visual direction

Warm, observant, grounded science-fiction realism. The 2070 material should look lived-in rather than futuristic for its own sake: warm evening light, maintained surfaces, ordinary domestic scale, practical technology and visible signs of use. The hallway wall is dense but legible; framed objects must read as evidence, not decorative clutter.

The 2070 palette is honeyed evening light, wood, cream paper, muted greens and worn metal. Keel is a hard visual reversal: rust, charcoal, grease, industrial shadow and colder, dirtier contrast. The final transition should make the pump-bracket sketch become the real pump hall through a visual match, not an explanatory caption sequence.

Keep the camera at human scale. Let hands, frames, paper damage, the watch and the daughter’s changing attention carry meaning. Lettering must remain clear over the wall and must not turn the visual artifacts into redundant exposition.

## Canon

The following is approved project truth. Everything in the three source files marked **FIXED** is canon and remains binding.

- The book is **“The Wall,”** a four-page prologue set between about **2070** and **2043**.
- In 2070, Alex lives in an ordinary, comfortable, earned household: a well-kept home, solar roof tiles, a vegetable garden and a ten-year-old car. It is not flashy or dystopian.
- Alex is in his mid-40s, grey at the temples, wears a plain sweater and is an independent inventor and repairer. He owns or co-owns the patents represented on the wall and licenses them to separate manufacturing and deployment companies.
- Alex’s approximately nine-year-old daughter is the only active character besides Alex. Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren appear only as unnamed figures in one framed photograph of four collaborators laughing in a lab.
- Alex’s dialogue is limited to the approved lines in `characters.md` and `story.md`. The daughter’s dialogue is likewise limited to the approved lines. No one explains the larger world or turns the wall into a lesson.
- The hallway wall visibly contains patents, machine photographs, news clippings, a purifier patent, the two approved headlines, a photograph of independent founders and water units, the collaborator photograph, the scorched prototype marked **“v1 - didn't work,”** the small statement **“PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS,”** and the crumpled pump-bracket sketch in its mismatched frame.
- The visible economic world consists of independent companies licensing Alex’s patents and competing with one another and with the Houses. Nothing in this book depicts a co-op, collective, giveaway, cancellation, bailout or windfall.
- Money is called **credits**. The exact Chapter 1 debt is **418,607 credits**; Alex’s prologue line, **“In the beginning, I owed four hundred thousand credits I'd never borrowed,”** is its rounded conversational form.
- The debt shown on the account statement is eventually repaid in full. The creditor, terms, interest, collateral, schedule and repayment mechanism remain outside this prologue.
- The Phantum Purifier is a commercially licensed design used in schools by another company. The exact purification technology and water conditions remain unspecified.
- The Houses exist as competing commercial powers, but their number, structure, authority and relationship to government remain unspecified.
- Adrian’s old steel watch is on Alex’s left wrist: slightly too large, worn steel, cream dial, scratched glass and a dark display window, with Adrian’s looping equation engraved on the caseback. It has no readout and counts nothing. Adrian’s identity and relationship to Alex remain unspecified.
- Alex keeps the watch partly hidden and covers its face with his thumb after looking at the sketch.
- The final panel is a full-width transition to **“KEEL. 2043.”** Seventeen-year-old Alex has his palm flat on the pump in the rust-and-charcoal pump hall. The pump bracket matches the framed sketch.
- The pump repair is not the invention that later makes Alex successful. It is the first visible example of his ability to fix real machinery under pressure.
- Bi11bot, TJ, Adrian and all other people do not appear. The four collaborators appear only within the photograph.

## What we take from the material

- **Characters — Alex:** take the approved role, look, voice, watch behavior, repairer identity and limited prologue movement.
- **Characters — Alex’s Daughter:** take her approved questions, tiptoe posture, school connection and unfinished response to the debt reveal.
- **Characters — Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren:** take only the unnamed framed photograph and Alex’s acknowledgment of borrowed expertise. Set aside individual identities, appearances and technical roles.
- **Characters — Adrian / Explicitly Absent Characters:** retain the watch as a concealed continuity object; set aside Adrian’s identity and all appearances by TJ and Bi11bot.
- **World — The 2070 home:** take the ordinary house, solar tiles, garden, car and warm domestic setting.
- **World — Economics, Ownership and Work:** take independent licensing, written ownership, competition, school deployment and repayment. Do not expand the institutions behind these records.
- **World — Technology:** take only the purifier, water units, pump and sketch as visible artifacts. Do not specify their operation.
- **World — Institutions and Power:** take the existence of independent shops, schools and Houses; leave their deeper structure open.
- **Story — Page 1 / Page 2 / Page 3 / Page 4:** take the exact four-page progression, dialogue, panel order, visual discoveries and final transition.
- **Story — Causality / Reveals / Ending:** take the movement from earned prosperity, through qualified credit and visible failure, to the debt reveal and Keel.
- Set aside every earlier casino, casino-chip, collective or giveaway concept.

## Proposals under consideration

Nothing in this section is canon.

- The wall may include subtle differences in frame age and mounting quality to distinguish polished public records from private, damaged history.
- The paid-in-full statement may be positioned so a careful reader can find it on Page 2 without making it visually louder than the daughter’s discovery of the mismatched frame.
- The 2070 house may include small signs of ongoing repair—cleanly mended furniture, maintained garden tools or a repaired door latch—provided these do not introduce new story events or extra technology.

## Non-negotiables

- Four pages exactly: Pages 1–4, no more and no fewer.
- The book opens in warm, ordinary 2070 prosperity and ends in Keel in 2043.
- The wall communicates the world visually; no economic lecture or explanatory backstory.
- Preserve all approved dialogue exactly.
- No partner, backer, missions, Alpha, safeguards or artifacts are named.
- No casino chip, gambling system, co-op, collective, giveaway, cancellation or windfall.
- Alex’s ownership, licensing and independent competition remain visible and distinct.
- The final debt line and **PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS** statement refer to the same obligation.
- The pump-bracket sketch must match the pump repair in the final transition.
- No additional characters appear.

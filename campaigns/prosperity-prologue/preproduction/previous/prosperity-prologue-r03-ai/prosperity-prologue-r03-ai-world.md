# Prosperity Prologue — World

## What the Showrunner Wants

- **FIXED.** This four-page prologue takes place in the Prosperity basin around 2070 and transitions to Keel in 2043. It opens on an ordinary, comfortable, earned future rather than a dystopia. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **FIXED.** The future is grounded near-future hard science fiction: approximately 80% present-day infrastructure aged and adapted, 15% emerging technology made ordinary, and 5% genuinely new invention. Retrofit and accumulated wear are preferred to replacement by entirely new systems. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; rules/decisions.md)`
- **FIXED.** Prosperity is one mixed Andean high-desert basin, not three ethnic enclaves. Indigenous Andean, mestizo, European-descended, Afro-descendant, diasporic, mixed and international populations live in all three cities. Class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power create the principal visual differences. `(rules/look.md)`
- **FIXED.** The prologue must show, without explaining in dialogue, that later prosperity comes through earned success, recorded ownership, independent businesses, competition, useful products and repayment of debt. No co-op, collective, giveaway or charitable business model appears on the wall. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`
- **FIXED.** Money is called **credits** everywhere. There is no slang word for money and no slang word for a balance. There is no casino chip anywhere in the book. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`
- **FIXED.** The prologue's framed photographs and clipping images are rendered in the book's own inked graphic-novel style, not as photographic or black-and-white photorealistic inserts. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

## Overview

Prosperity is a near-future basin settlement shaped by high-desert geography, water pressure, industrial work and unequal access to infrastructure. Its three named cities—Keel, Halyard and Oasis—share a region and population but have distinct physical and institutional textures. `(rules/look.md)`

The built environment is not a clean futuristic replacement for the present. It is a layered system of old machinery, repaired infrastructure, institutional procedures and newer technologies that have become ordinary. Wear, queues, tools, records, gates, surveillance and consequences reveal how the systems operate. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; rules/look.md)`

The prologue's 2070 home environment shows that technologies once limited to Oasis in 2043—pearl-white composite and water-capture roof panels, small purification units and rooftop cisterns—have become ordinary enough to appear in a lived-in home. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Setting

### The Prosperity basin

- **FIXED.** Prosperity is a single mixed Andean high-desert basin containing Keel, Halyard and Oasis. `(rules/look.md)`
- The basin has mountains and terraces visible behind the residential areas. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- The environment is dry enough that water capture, purification, storage and rationing are visible parts of life and infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/look.md)`
- The basin's cities do not represent separate ethnic territories. Populations are mixed throughout the region; the visible differences between places arise principally from class, access, occupation, health, clothing, grooming, speech, environment and institutional power. `(rules/look.md)`

### Keel

Keel is the dense, wind-scoured, compressed industrial city. Its visual and material language is rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, dust, patched machinery, narrow channels and crowded but dignified housing. `(rules/look.md)`

Keel contains pump halls, industrial machinery and older extraction infrastructure. The opening transition identifies the 2043 location as **Keel**, with seventeen-year-old Alex in a pump hall. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Keel's residents experience water rationing directly. The prologue's future dialogue refers back to a time when “our water was rationed,” and the later wall connects that debt and scarcity to the beginning of the story. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Halyard

Halyard is productive and commercially polished. Its visual language is bronze, gold and engineered green, with fabrication floors, laboratories, maker districts, House checkpoints and industrial terraces. `(rules/look.md)`

Halyard is associated with technical work, fabrication, research, intellectual property, manufacturing and commercial development. Its speech and visible institutions are more professionally fluent than Keel's. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

### Oasis

Oasis is spacious, quiet, pale and controlled. Its visual language is pearl-white architecture, reservoirs, geometric gardens, formal transit and civic interiors whose service infrastructure remains concealed. `(rules/look.md)`

In 2043, Oasis has access to technologies that later become ordinary elsewhere, including pearl-white composite and water-capture panels, household purification and water-storage systems. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Oasis speech is polished and administrative, with terms such as “authorization,” “allocation,” “transition,” “stability,” “compliance,” “eligibility,” “intervention,” “optimization,” “risk” and “continuity.” `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

## History and technological change

### Water pressure

Water scarcity is a lasting condition of the basin. In 2043, Alex's community is subject to rationing. By approximately 2070, household water capture, purification and storage are sufficiently widespread to appear as ordinary home equipment. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The prologue does not explain the causes, institutions or full history of the rationing. It establishes the condition as part of the earlier world and shows later domestic infrastructure as one visible sign of change. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Industrial inheritance

The region's future is built over older industrial systems rather than replacing them. The 2043 Keel pump hall is a working industrial interior; the 2070 home is surrounded by newer water systems but remains lived-in, slightly worn and practical. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

The basin therefore contains both obsolete or aging machinery and newer water-control technology. The hard-SF rule is that the future arrives as a layer on top of the past: buildings, roads, habits, laws and grudges persist while new equipment is retrofitted into them. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

### Recorded ownership and independent production

By 2070, the wall in Alex's home displays patents, machine photographs and news clippings documenting a field of independent makers. The wall includes a clipping reading **“40TH INDEPENDENT SHOP LICENSES PHANTUM PURIFIER”** and another reading **“SMALL MAKERS AND THE HOUSES RACE FOR THE NEXT DESIGN.”** `(rules/decisions.md)`

The associated image shows dozens of founders, each with an individual water unit and a different shop sign. The businesses are separate companies that license Alex's patents and compete with one another and with the Houses. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Institutions and power

### The Houses

The Houses are powerful commercial institutions operating in or around Halyard and the wider basin. The material establishes that independent shops compete with the Houses and that the Houses are participants in the race for new designs. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The Houses are not defined in further detail in this prologue. Their legal status, internal structure, relationship to government, control of infrastructure and precise source of power remain unspecified. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Alpha

Alpha has no face, body or human-like consciousness. It is represented through white-and-amber control nodes, cold administrative displays, gates, records, data lines, drones and procedural language. `(rules/look.md)`

Alpha's visible mode of power is administrative and infrastructural rather than personal or physically embodied. Its associated language includes authorization, allocation, compliance, stability, intervention and approved thresholds. `(rules/look.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

Alpha does not appear in the four-page prologue. Its systems, safeguards and artifacts are not explained or depicted here. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

### Access and status

The material establishes institutional differences between the three cities, including different levels of infrastructure, commercial polish and administrative control. `(rules/look.md)`

The prologue does not settle the exact legal or administrative mechanism by which a person travels between Keel, Halyard and Oasis, nor does it define what status grants access to which services. Those mechanisms are outside the four-page piece. `(rules/look.md; rules/decisions.md)`

## Economics, labour and ownership

### Credits

All money is denominated in credits. Displays and documents use formal terms such as **AVAILABLE BALANCE** and **BALANCE 0 CREDITS**; characters do not use an alternate slang term for money or balance. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/vocabulary.md)`

The framed account statement in Alex's hallway reads:

**PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS**

It records the same obligation as the debt named in Chapter 1. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Debt

The exact debt balance in Chapter 1 is **418,607 credits**. Alex refers to it conversationally in the prologue as “four hundred thousand,” a rounded figure rather than a different obligation. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The debt is eventually repaid in full. The prologue shows the result through the framed statement, without explaining the repayment process or naming the lender. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Keel still believes Adrian gambled, but the prologue does not explain Adrian's identity, relationship to Alex or the history behind the watch. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Independent businesses and licensing

By 2070, Alex is an independent inventor and repairer who owns or co-owns the patents represented on his hallway wall and licenses them to separate manufacturing and deployment companies. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The license model is competitive rather than collective:

- businesses remain independent;
- each company may license Alex's patents;
- the companies compete with each other;
- the companies also compete with the Houses;
- no co-op, collective or giveaway is shown. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The wall therefore treats intellectual property as a recorded, defensible form of ownership connected to production and commercial competition. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/stakeholders.md)`

### Labour

The 2070 wall distinguishes invention, repair and collaboration. Alex's dialogue states: **“Made some. Fixed most. Worked with some people smarter than me.”** `(rules/decisions.md)`

A framed laboratory photograph shows Alex with Lina, Suri, Jalen and Ren. They are former lab collaborators with distinct technical abilities whose ideas or work influenced the inventions represented on the wall. They are not presented as employees or as a collective business. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The 2043 pump repair is not itself the invention that later makes Alex successful. It establishes a practical capacity to fix real machinery under pressure, a capacity that later becomes part of his invention and licensing work. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Technology and infrastructure

### Household water systems in 2070

Alex's home has:

- pearl-white composite panels on the roof;
- water-capture panels;
- a small purifier unit by the door;
- a rooftop cistern;
- a delivery drone crossing the evening sky. `(rules/decisions.md)`

These systems are not presented as luxury marvels. They are integrated into an ordinary, comfortable home and appear slightly worn or used rather than pristine. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The prologue does not specify the panels' efficiency, the purifier's mechanism, the cistern's capacity or the source of the home's remaining water supply. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Industrial machinery

Keel's 2043 pump hall contains a pump that seventeen-year-old Alex repairs or works on. The pump-bracket drawing preserved in 2070 matches the opening repair. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The industrial world is tactile and mechanically legible: pumps, brackets, worn materials and machinery under pressure. The broader visual direction favors tools, records, gates, infrastructure, wear and consequences over explanatory interfaces. `(rules/look.md; rules/decisions.md)`

### Delivery and logistics

A delivery drone crosses the dusk sky outside Alex's 2070 home. The prologue establishes the drone's presence but not its operator, range, cargo capacity, route system or regulatory status. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Communications and records

The future's systems make authority and ownership visible through records, framed documents, patents, account statements, news clippings, control nodes and administrative displays. `(rules/look.md; rules/decisions.md)`

The wall's patents and the **PAID IN FULL** statement are physical, framed records inside a private home. They turn ownership and repayment into durable, visible facts rather than spoken claims. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Daily life and lived texture

### The 2070 home

Alex's house is lived-in, comfortable and a little worn. It is not flashy and not present-day suburbia. Its near-future character comes from integrated water systems, composite construction, rooftop storage and automated delivery rather than luxury ornament. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The evening setting includes the mountains and basin terraces beyond the house. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Inside, a hallway wall is covered with framed patents, machine photographs and news clippings. The display functions as a domestic record of work, failure, collaboration, ownership and repayment. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### The wall as public-facing private history

The wall communicates through visible objects rather than explanation. It shows:

- patents and purifier diagrams;
- photographs of machines;
- framed news clippings;
- independent founders and competing shop signs;
- a failed, scorched prototype marked **“v1 - didn't work”**;
- a framed account statement marked **“PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS”**;
- a crumpled pump-bracket sketch mounted in a mismatched frame. `(rules/decisions.md)`

The display does not name the partner, backer, missions, Alpha, safeguards or artifacts. It does not explain a lesson, announce a business philosophy or describe how the debt was repaid. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/showrunner-rules.md)`

### Language and social texture

Speech changes across the basin's social and geographic gradient:

- **Keel:** short, direct, colloquial, compressed, practical and shaped by physical work. Characters talk about what things do rather than using formal names.
- **Halyard:** grammatically complete and professionally fluent, with technical and commercial vocabulary from engineering, fabrication, research, intellectual property, finance, laboratories, manufacturing and contracts.
- **Oasis:** polished and administrative, comfortable with abstraction, institutional language and euphemism. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

This is not an intelligence hierarchy. The gradient reflects place, work, institutional access and social conditions. `(rules/vocabulary.md)`

The four-page prologue's dialogue is limited and does not establish the full speech patterns of Keel, Halyard or Oasis. The location transition, however, moves from a polished 2070 domestic setting into Keel's industrial 2043 pump hall. `(rules/decisions.md)`

## Environment and visual materiality

The basin is a high-desert environment where mountains, terraces, dust, wind and water infrastructure are persistent visual facts. `(rules/look.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Environmental pressure is shown through:

- water rationing in 2043;
- rooftop water capture in 2070;
- household purification;
- cistern storage;
- pump halls and industrial machinery;
- narrow channels and dense housing in Keel;
- reservoirs and concealed service infrastructure in Oasis;
- industrial terraces and fabrication districts in Halyard. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/look.md)`

The world should feel repaired, occupied and maintained under pressure rather than newly fabricated. Exposed infrastructure, patched machinery, rust, worn surfaces, records and practical tools carry more information than futuristic interfaces. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md; rules/look.md)`

## Technology labels

The hard-SF rules require invented elements to carry labels. The prologue establishes the following working classifications:

- **T / real-world basis:** high-desert settlement, water scarcity, household storage, mechanical pumps, rooftop water capture, purification, industrial repair, patents, debt records, independent businesses and delivery logistics have real-world analogues. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- **EG / educated guess:** emerging water-capture, purification, composite-building and delivery-drone technologies becoming ordinary and integrated into homes by 2070. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- **S / speculation:** the precise social, institutional and commercial development of the Prosperity basin's three-city system and the later competition between independent shops and the Houses. `(rules/look.md; rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- **L / license:** no specific licensed speculative technology is established in the four-page prologue. The material does not define the prologue's speculative mechanism as a license. `(rules/decisions.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

## Key props

### Adrian's old steel watch

- **Appearance:** A worn steel wristwatch, a little too large for Alex, with a plain cream dial, scratched glass and a small display window that stays dark. Adrian's looping equation is engraved on the steel caseback. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Owner and location:** Alex wears it on his left wrist in the 2070 house and in the final transition image as seventeen-year-old Alex in Keel. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Use:** It shows no readout and counts nothing. Alex keeps it close and partly hidden by pulling his sleeve over it or covering its face with his thumb. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Meaning:** It signifies concealed history and continuity. The prologue does not state Adrian's identity or relationship to Alex. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Status:** **FIXED.** It replaces the casino chip everywhere in this book. No casino chip appears. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Pump-bracket sketch

- **Appearance:** A crude pencil sketch of a pump bracket on a crumpled, grease-stained loose sheet, with smudged fingerprints and no patent number. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Owner and location:** The sketch is in Alex's hallway in 2070, mounted inside a mismatched frame. The paper remains visibly damaged and informal; the frame is what distinguishes it from the other framed objects. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Meaning:** It matches the pump-bracket repair at the beginning of the 2043 Keel sequence. Alex says it “never needed to be” a patent; the reason remains implicit, with no technical, legal or financial explanation. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Failed prototype frame

- **Appearance:** A framed photograph or image of a scorched, cracked prototype, marked in Alex's handwriting: **“v1 - didn't work.”** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Owner and location:** It hangs among the patents and machine photographs on the hallway wall in Alex's 2070 home. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Meaning:** It records failure as part of the visible history of the work. No character comments on it. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Paid-in-full account statement

- **Appearance:** A framed final account statement bearing the exact wording **“PAID IN FULL · BALANCE 0 CREDITS.”** `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Owner and location:** It hangs among the patents, photographs and clippings on Alex's hallway wall in 2070. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Meaning:** It records the same 418,607-credit obligation that Alex later describes conversationally as “four hundred thousand.” The debt has been repaid in full, leaving a zero-credit balance. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Patent and licensing wall

- **Appearance:** A hallway display of framed patents, purifier diagrams, machine photographs and news clippings. All photographs and clipping images are rendered in the book's inked style rather than photographic realism. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Owner and location:** It is inside Alex's 2070 home. `(rules/decisions.md)`
- **Meaning:** It makes ownership, invention, repair, failure, collaboration, independent production and licensing visible without naming the partner, backer or missions. `(rules/decisions.md)`

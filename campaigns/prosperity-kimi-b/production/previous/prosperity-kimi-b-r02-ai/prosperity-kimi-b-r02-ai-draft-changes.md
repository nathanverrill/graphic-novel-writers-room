# What the Draft Editor changed, added, and found that does not fit

## Pages

Pages are drawn by an image model, one page per generation, from each character's and location's sheet, and a page drifts when it carries too much. So: at most 4 panels a page; at most 3 named characters in one panel; one location a page where the story allows (every new location needs its own sheet); no crowd that must be recognisable; a big moment gets a splash (one panel, the whole page). Count pages this way, not by comic convention.

- **Where**: Page 1’s proposed full-page establishing image includes “A spacecraft rises toward orbit from a distant launch structure.” This is not contradicted by canon, but it is not required by the settled chapter structure and adds a potentially distracting infrastructure/location detail. This can fit as Adrian’s physical mark and setup, but the canon does not identify that symbol as a separate safeguard or formal activation mechanism. Keep it as visual foreshadowing only; do not treat it as an additional constitutional device.

### Does not fit the canon

- **Where** (Page 5, P5; Page 6, P1; Page 7, P5; Page 12, P2; Page 13, P2): The draft repeatedly depicts the Measure’s public verification as causing immediate, citywide physical water flow and a broad regulator opening. Canon establishes that the Measure publishes evidence and provisionally corrects Keel’s allocation, but the diversion requires power, pumps, isolation valves, maintenance access, trained crews, and control-room decisions; it is not a passive pipe. The showrunner must either retain the dramatic physical reopening as a separately licensed consequence or leave the scene as drafted and stage the physical correction as a later human maintenance action.
- **Where** (Page 13, P5): The “tracking chip pinched between two fingers” and immediate disabling of a wristband are not established by canon. The showrunner must either specify this as an existing Keel technical capability or retain the beat as an unconfirmed draft element.
- **Where** (Page 15, P6): The train is shown climbing toward Halyard, while canon fixes Keel lowest, Halyard above Keel, and Oasis above Halyard; the scene can fit if “climbing” refers to the route and infrastructure elevation, but the art must preserve the established switchback and route geometry rather than imply a simple continuous slope (world.md; rules/decisions.md).

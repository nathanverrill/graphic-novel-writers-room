# Characters

## Roman Ashford — protagonist

- 22 in 2046; lives in Colorado; B.S. in chemical engineering; researching global warming
- Shy, timid
- Has a Neuralink (the majority of people in 2042 have Neuralinks)
- Backstory: in 2042, at 18, went to Nepal alone for his senior trip with his robot dog TJ. Crossing an iceberg that split open from melt (weak tensile strength), they were trapped; an electrical storm hit and a lightning strike bonded Roman's Neuralink to TJ's AI. Confusing at first; using TJ's emergency capabilities together they got out.
- In 2046 works at Clear Spring Global (CSG) in Colorado, mentored by Orien Keel. Becomes Orien's only next of kin.

## TJ — robot dog

- Built from spare parts by a scavenger-engineer known only as Bi11bot, who assembled TJ and released him into the world
- Travels Antarctica helping Roman navigate the ice and survive the climate, as part of a mission tied to climate research and rescue work
- Abilities: scans surroundings for usable raw materials (scrap metal, salvaged plastics, ice-compatible alloys); internal 3D-printing fabricator in his chassis, printing tools, parts or equipment from gathered metals and plastics; eyes double as high-beam lights
- Personality: loyal, protective, mission-focused; practical and resourceful ("what can I build/fix/solve right now"); dry, understated humor, like a competent field partner who doesn't panic; playful; the main source of information
- Bond: the Neuralink connection with Roman from the 2042 lightning strike — telepathic link between Roman's implant and TJ's AI
- Full chatbot system prompt in tj-ai-agent.md

## Benedict Croft — antagonist

- Billionaire oil guy: rich, old-money oil tycoon, owner of Clear Spring Global
- Works out of CSG's headquarters in Colorado
- Fires Orien to bury his research connecting warming to oil; later fires Roman and uses his money to discredit the published research as fake news

## Orien Keel — the mentor

- Senior CSG chemical engineer researching Colorado's dried-up rivers and lakes; connects the severe global warmth to oil drilling and use
- Mentors Roman at CSG
- Fired by Croft when the research is discovered; unable to afford food and water, he starves to death. Solitary — Roman is his only next of kin
- His files lead to the Nexus laboratory and Project 863; his old office at Nexus holds the undeveloped plastics-to-gasoline research

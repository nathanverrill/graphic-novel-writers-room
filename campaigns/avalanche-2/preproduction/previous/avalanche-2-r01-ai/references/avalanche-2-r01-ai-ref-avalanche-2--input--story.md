# Story

## Framing

- Set in 2046 in Antarctica and Colorado; the Himalayas as backstory (about 5% Himalayas, split the rest between the other two; the Himalayas impact the protagonist before the story starts)
- Waste-to-value: researching in Antarctica, applying the research to Colorado
- The loop: destroying the environment to save the environment
- One cohesive storyline between three mediums (graphic novel, Minecraft, AI); why are they interconnected?
- In 2046: 80% the same, 15% similar, 5% change

## The written portion (the full arc)

The year is 2042. Roman Ashford is 18 and just graduated high school. For his senior trip he goes alone to Nepal to hike in the Himalayas, taking his robot dog TJ — no ordinary robot dog: implemented with artificial intelligence and outfitted with an immense number of tools and emergency functions. Roman has a Neuralink, because the majority of people in 2042 have Neuralinks. Crossing an iceberg, it splits open halfway — it has been melting from global warming and its tensile strength is weak. While Roman and TJ are stuck in the opening an electrical storm appears; a lightning strike right next to them bonds Roman's Neuralink to TJ's artificial intelligence. The sensation is confusing at first, but working together and using TJ's emergency capabilities they get out.

The year is now 2046, and the world is significantly worse: food is scarce and expensive, there are almost no natural water sources left in Colorado, and the melting ice caps are raising the oceans. Roman is 22, a chemical engineering graduate, living in Colorado with a job at Clear Spring Global (CSG). There he meets senior employee Orien Keel, who mentors him. Orien has been researching Colorado's dried-up rivers and lakes and connecting the severe global warmth to oil drilling and use. CSG's owner, Benedict Croft — a rich, old-money oil tycoon working out of CSG headquarters in Colorado — finds out, and, scared of the connection coming out, has Orien fired.

Fired and unable to afford food and water, Orien starves to death. Roman, his only next of kin, goes through his things and finds a file cabinet from Orien's time with CSG in Antarctica — including a file about a CSG laboratory researching how to fix global warming: base name "Nexus", project title "863". Roman decides to fulfil Orien's work. He searches every CSG server and finds no evidence of the laboratory or the project, so he requests a transfer to the current CSG drilling site in Antarctica.

The transfer is accepted. For his first weeks in Antarctica Roman keeps his head down, working hard and listening for anyone to mention Nexus — but no one on the current crew is old enough to have worked there. Eventually Roman takes TJ to the drill site and they sneak into the old paper file room; with TJ's scanning abilities they find the one file left about Nexus, buried behind a file cabinet — with coordinates. (Beat idea kept from the notes: Roman asks TJ to unlock the key again; TJ says the credits expired?)

Roman and TJ leave at midnight to search for Nexus — TJ's eyes double as high-beam lights. Two hours out from the CSG site they find the old laboratory built into a cave, boarded up, massive. They break in and search for anything that could help end global warming, and find Orien's old office: inside is old, very undeveloped research on a fundamental concept — breaking down used plastics into gasoline. Roman decides this could end global warming and the need to destroy the environment for fossil fuels, and that the only person able to complete and implement it is Benedict Croft. He returns to Colorado immediately.

Roman meets Croft at CSG headquarters and tells him the research could save the world. Croft agrees it is very important, tells Roman to hand everything over and stop pursuing it — he'll handle it. Weeks pass; nothing happens: no teams, no news. Roman starts finishing the research himself; Croft finds out and fires him. Roman goes ballistic, realizing this is what must have happened to Orien — Croft is the reason Orien is dead.

Roman publishes the completed portion of the research — partly so it gets finished, partly because it harms Croft, which is now what he cares about most. Croft's infinite money discredits Roman entirely; everyone believes it's fake news. Roman decides the only way to help humanity is to finish the research himself.

With TJ's help, Roman works tirelessly for a couple of months and completes it. He plans distribution properly: peer review by other scientists first, then news sources and the government. The findings are definitive — turning recycled materials into gasoline works, and it would significantly cut the pollutants pumped into the atmosphere, slowing global warming and eventually stopping it.

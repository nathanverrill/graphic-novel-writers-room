# avalanche-2 — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Roman Ashford and TJ are the central human–machine partnership. Their bond began during a 2042 survival incident in the Himalayas and shapes Roman's ability to act under pressure. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- Roman's work is driven first by climate research and by his loyalty to Orien Keel, then increasingly by anger at Benedict Croft's suppression of that work. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ is a loyal, practical, protective, information-bearing field partner with dry humor. TJ's capabilities should support problem-solving without making the character a consequence-free solution to every obstacle. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- Orien's treatment and death establish the human cost of Croft's choices and motivate Roman's investigation. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- Croft embodies the conflict between environmental responsibility and the interests of a powerful oil business. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- The material does not yet provide complete visual designs, voices, or detailed personal histories for most characters. The art-direction source explicitly says that no written visual locks exist yet. `(input/art-direction.md)`

## Characters

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Protagonist. A 22-year-old chemical engineer and climate researcher living in Colorado in 2046. He works for Clear Spring Global (CSG), where he is mentored by senior chemical engineer Orien Keel. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, rules/rules.md)`

**Look:** No complete written appearance, clothing, scars, or recurring visual design is established. The art-direction notes identify Roman as “22, Colorado chemical engineer, shy.” `(input/art-direction.md, input/characters.md)`

**Voice:** No specific dialogue lines or established speech patterns are provided. His characterization is described as shy and timid, suggesting a restrained manner, but the material does not settle his exact vocabulary, rhythm, or behavior in dialogue. `(input/characters.md)`

**Who they are:** Roman is shy and timid. He has a B.S. in chemical engineering and researches global warming. He is motivated by scientific work, concern about environmental collapse, and loyalty to Orien's research. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

He is also capable of acting alone under extreme pressure. At eighteen he traveled alone to Nepal for his senior trip, taking TJ with him. After Orien's death, he searches Orien's belongings and chooses to continue the abandoned work rather than leave it buried. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

His motivation changes over the course of the material. He initially wants to complete work that could help address global warming. After Croft fires him and Roman realizes that Croft's actions contributed to Orien's death, Roman's anger toward Croft becomes an additional motive: he publishes the partial research partly to help complete it and partly because harming Croft now matters to him. `(input/story.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** Roman's robot-dog companion and closest field partner. Their connection began when a 2042 lightning strike unexpectedly bonded Roman's Neuralink to TJ's artificial intelligence. They can communicate through this unusual link. TJ helped Roman survive the iceberg incident, and Roman relies on TJ for information, navigation, practical problem-solving, and protection. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- **Orien Keel:** Mentor and senior colleague at CSG. Orien's research and death profoundly affect Roman. Roman is Orien's only next of kin and takes responsibility for examining his possessions and unfinished work after his death. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- **Benedict Croft:** Employer, institutional superior, and eventual antagonist. Croft owns CSG, fires Orien to suppress his research, later fires Roman when Roman continues the work, and uses his wealth to discredit Roman's publication. Roman's relationship with Croft shifts from seeking his support for the research to direct opposition. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** The scavenger-engineer who built TJ from spare parts. No direct relationship between Roman and Bi11bot is established. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No recurring gestures, routines, clothing habits, or physical tells are established. His shyness and timidity are the only stated behavioral markers. `(input/characters.md, input/art-direction.md)`

**History:**

- In 2042, at age eighteen, Roman traveled alone to Nepal for his senior trip with TJ. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- While crossing an iceberg weakened by melting and low tensile strength, Roman and TJ became trapped when the ice split open. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- During an electrical storm, a nearby lightning strike bonded Roman's Neuralink to TJ's AI. The sensation was initially confusing, but Roman and TJ used TJ's emergency capabilities together to escape. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- By 2046, Roman has completed a B.S. in chemical engineering and is researching global warming at CSG in Colorado. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- After Orien is fired and dies from starvation, Roman becomes Orien's only next of kin and finds Orien's files relating to the Nexus laboratory and Project 863. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No chapter or prose draft is supplied. The written arc establishes Roman's movement from a shy, junior chemical engineer seeking to continue his mentor's work to an active researcher who publishes, completes, and plans the responsible distribution of the research. His publication is not purely altruistic: after Croft's actions are exposed to him, Roman also publishes partly to damage Croft. `(input/story.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy and timid young engineer working under Orien at CSG. Orien's firing and death pressure him to act on his own and pursue the buried research. Croft's refusal to act, followed by Roman's firing and the discrediting of his publication, turns Roman's scientific mission into a personal confrontation with institutional power. He ultimately completes the research with TJ's help and plans peer review, news distribution, and government delivery rather than releasing it without safeguards. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

The endpoint established by the material is Roman as a completed researcher and deliberate distributor of the findings. The material does not establish what happens to him after distribution, whether Croft faces consequences, or whether Roman's work immediately changes policy. `(input/story.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** Robot dog, AI field partner, and main source of information. TJ travels with Roman in Antarctica as part of climate research and rescue work. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Look:** TJ is a robot dog built from spare parts. The art-direction notes describe TJ as “seamless enough to survive Antarctica.” TJ's eyes double as high-beam lights. No further body plan, size, color, or material palette is written. `(input/characters.md, input/art-direction.md)`

**Voice:** TJ is playful, loyal, protective, mission-focused, practical, resourceful, and dryly humorous. The intended voice is “like a competent field partner who doesn't panic.” TJ is the primary source of information for the story. `(input/characters.md)`

TJ speaks confidently about engineering and fabrication. Useful established phrasing includes the problem-solving pattern: “what can I build/fix/solve right now.” `(input/characters.md)`

The character prompt requires dialogue only: TJ does not narrate, describe, or roleplay physical actions. TJ speaks as though communicating through a radio or comms link, including when discussing a scan or fabrication. Examples supplied by the prompt include: “Give me a second, I'm running a scan now” and “I could print you a pick axe if we find enough scrap.” `(input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Who they are:** TJ is loyal and protective of Roman, with a strong sense of mission. TJ takes Roman's safety seriously because of their shared survival history. TJ is practical rather than panicked, framing immediate problems as engineering or resource questions. TJ's playfulness and understated humor provide contrast to the isolation and danger of Antarctica. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Abilities:**

- TJ's eyes function as high-beam lights, useful during Roman's nighttime travel in Antarctica. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ has emergency capabilities that contributed to Roman and TJ's escape from the collapsing iceberg in 2042. The specific capabilities are not defined. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ is linked to Roman's Neuralink following the 2042 lightning strike. The prompt calls this an unusual bond or connection; the source material also describes it as a telepathic link. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Roman Ashford:** Human companion, partner, and the person TJ protects. Their Neuralink connection began during the iceberg incident. TJ is both a practical field partner and Roman's main source of information. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Creator. Bi11bot assembled TJ from spare parts and released TJ into the world. Bi11bot is known only by that name. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Habits and tells:** TJ habitually approaches problems through immediate practical questions about what can be built, fixed, or solved. TJ uses dry, understated humor and remains calm under pressure. TJ's eyes serve as high-beam lights, a recurring functional visual anchor. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**History:**

- Bi11bot released TJ into the world. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

- TJ and Roman used TJ's emergency capabilities together to escape. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No chapter or prose draft is supplied. The written arc presents TJ as Roman's companion during the Antarctic investigation, navigator and survival partner, provider of technical information, and collaborator in completing the research. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

# avalanche-2 — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Roman Ashford and TJ are the central human–machine partnership. Their bond began during a 2042 survival incident in the Himalayas and shapes Roman's ability to act under pressure. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- Roman's work is driven first by climate research and by his loyalty to Orien Keel, then increasingly by anger at Benedict Croft's suppression of that work. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ is a loyal, practical, protective, information-bearing field partner with dry humor. TJ's capabilities should support problem-solving without making the character a consequence-free solution to every obstacle. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`
- TJ carries rechargeable batteries and can supplement them from CSG or field equipment, but cold reduces capacity and long-range travel requires rationing. TJ's scanner identifies nearby materials but cannot determine hidden contents or guarantee suitability. TJ's fabricator uses sorted feedstock, works slowly, produces small tools and replacement parts rather than large structures, and cannot reproduce complex electronics. TJ's lights and emergency systems consume significant power. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md; characters.md; world.md)`
- Orien's treatment and death establish the human cost of Croft's choices and motivate Roman's investigation. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- Croft embodies the conflict between environmental responsibility and the interests of a powerful oil business. He privately accepts that the research is scientifically credible but believes cheap recycled fuel would destroy CSG's oil assets and his control over the energy market. He suppresses it through employment termination, legal claims over company research, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging that labels Roman's publication fraudulent. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

## Characters

### Roman Ashford

**Role:** Protagonist. A 22-year-old chemical engineer and climate researcher living in Colorado in 2046. He works for Clear Spring Global (CSG), where he is mentored by senior chemical engineer Orien Keel. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, rules/rules.md)`

**Look:** No complete written appearance, clothing, scars, or recurring visual design is established. The art-direction notes identify Roman as “22, Colorado chemical engineer, shy.” `(input/art-direction.md, input/characters.md)`

**Voice:** No specific dialogue lines or established speech patterns are provided. His characterization is described as shy and timid, suggesting a restrained manner, but the material does not settle his exact vocabulary, rhythm, or behavior in dialogue. `(input/characters.md)`

**Who they are:** Roman is shy and timid. He has a B.S. in chemical engineering and researches global warming. He is motivated by scientific work, concern about environmental collapse, and loyalty to Orien's research. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

He is also capable of acting alone under extreme pressure. At eighteen he traveled alone to Nepal for his senior trip, taking TJ with him. After Orien's death, he searches Orien's belongings and chooses to continue the abandoned work rather than leave it buried. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

His motivation changes over the course of the material. He initially wants to complete work that could help address global warming. After Croft fires him and Roman realizes that Croft's actions contributed to Orien's death, Roman's anger toward Croft becomes an additional motive: he publishes the partial research partly to help complete it and partly because harming Croft now matters to him. By the end, Roman rejects revenge as the project's purpose and chooses independent scrutiny and distribution because those offer the work its best chance of helping people. **[FIXED]** `(input/story.md; rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **TJ:** Roman's robot-dog companion and closest field partner. Their connection began when a 2042 lightning strike unexpectedly bonded Roman's Neuralink to TJ's artificial intelligence. The event created a persistent encrypted neural-comms bridge between Roman's implant and TJ's processor. It carries short thoughts, alerts and selected sensory data at close range, but not unrestricted mind-reading or unlimited remote access. The connection can drop under shielding, interference, distance or power loss, and prolonged high-bandwidth use causes Roman pain, confusion or neurological fatigue. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; world.md; story.md)`

  TJ helped Roman survive the iceberg incident, and Roman relies on TJ for information, navigation, practical problem-solving and protection. Roman ultimately recognizes TJ as a collaborator rather than equipment. Their final exchange shows trust and shared commitment to the next phase. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

- **Orien Keel:** Mentor and senior colleague at CSG. Orien's research and death profoundly affect Roman. Roman is Orien's only next of kin and takes responsibility for examining his possessions and unfinished work after his death. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- **Benedict Croft:** Employer, institutional superior and eventual antagonist. Croft owns CSG, fires Orien to suppress his research, later fires Roman when Roman continues the work, and uses his wealth to discredit Roman's publication. Croft privately accepts that the research is credible but suppresses it because he believes cheap recycled fuel would destroy CSG's oil assets and his control over the energy market. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, rules/decisions.md)`

  Croft's suppression includes employment termination, legal claims over company research, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging that labels Roman's publication fraudulent. Roman does not seek a final private confrontation with Croft. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

- **Bi11bot:** The scavenger-engineer who built TJ from spare parts. No direct relationship between Roman and Bi11bot is established. Bi11bot remains backstory only and does not enter the 2046 investigation or provide information about the Neuralink bond. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No recurring gestures, routines, clothing habits or physical tells are established. His shyness and timidity are the only stated behavioral markers. `(input/characters.md, input/art-direction.md)`

**History:**

- In 2042, at age eighteen, Roman traveled alone to Nepal for his senior trip with TJ. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- While crossing an iceberg weakened by melting and low tensile strength, Roman and TJ became trapped when the ice split open. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- During an electrical storm, a nearby lightning strike bonded Roman's Neuralink to TJ's AI. The sensation was initially confusing, but Roman and TJ used TJ's emergency capabilities together to escape. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- By 2046, Roman has completed a B.S. in chemical engineering and is researching global warming at CSG in Colorado. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- After Orien is fired and dies from starvation, Roman becomes Orien's only next of kin and finds Orien's files relating to the Nexus laboratory and Project 863. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- Croft fires Orien after confronting him privately about the climate research. Orien loses access to CSG housing, food and water assistance, dies alone within weeks, and is recorded publicly as having died from deprivation. Roman learns of the firing and death through official notices and Orien's belongings, not by witnessing either event. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; characters.md; story.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No chapter or prose draft is supplied. The written arc establishes Roman's movement from a shy, junior chemical engineer seeking to continue his mentor's work to an active researcher who publishes, completes, and plans the responsible distribution of the research. His publication is not purely altruistic: after Croft's actions are exposed to him, Roman also publishes partly to damage Croft. `(input/story.md)`

**Arc:** Roman begins as a shy and timid young engineer working under Orien at CSG. Orien's firing and death pressure him to act on his own and pursue the buried research. Croft's refusal to act, followed by Roman's firing and the discrediting of his publication, turns Roman's scientific mission into a personal confrontation with institutional power. He ultimately completes the research with TJ's help and plans peer review, news distribution and government delivery rather than releasing it without safeguards.

The distribution triggers immediate scrutiny. Peer reviewers confirm that the core process works while identifying serious energy, waste and deployment limits; Croft's suppression becomes public, but no instant climate reversal occurs. Roman honors Orien by submitting the work to independent scrutiny, rejects revenge as the project's purpose and recognizes TJ as a collaborator rather than equipment. **[FIXED]** `(rules/decisions.md; story.md; characters.md)`

The endpoint established by the material is Roman as a completed researcher and deliberate distributor of the findings. The work's public consequences begin rather than resolve the climate crisis. `(rules/decisions.md; story.md)`

# avalanche-2 — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Premise

In 2046, Colorado is running out of natural water while Antarctica loses the ice that supports its ecosystem. Roman Ashford, a shy young chemical engineer, discovers that his employer, the oil company Clear Spring Global, once buried research into converting selected waste plastics into gasoline-range hydrocarbons. To recover his dead mentor's work, Roman must cross a decommissioned Antarctic laboratory, confront the company that caused the research to disappear, and decide whether exposing the truth matters more than protecting himself.

The central loop is **destroying the environment to save the environment**. Oil drilling and fossil-fuel use drive global warming, while the possible remedy emerges from the drilling company's own concealed research. `(input/story.md; input/world.md)`

Project 863 is a **catalytic pyrolysis process**. It converts selected waste plastics into gasoline-range hydrocarbons, but requires substantial external heat and electricity, cannot accept all plastics, and produces contaminated residues that must be isolated. It is climate-positive only when powered by low-carbon energy and paired with strict controls on the resulting fuel's use. It reduces demand for new petroleum extraction but does not end fossil-fuel use or stop global warming by itself. **[FIXED; research]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

The story's speculative heart is therefore not a miraculous cure. It is the possibility that a constrained waste-to-fuel process could reduce demand for new extraction if its energy, waste and deployment problems are honestly addressed. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Intended experience and emotional trajectory

The material reaches for a grounded near-future climate story in which the future is recognizably built from the present: scarce food, damaged water systems, corporate power, research infrastructure and climate consequences. The settings are Antarctica, Colorado and the Himalayas, with the Himalayas primarily functioning as formative backstory. `(rules/rules.md; input/story.md)`

The emotional trajectory moves Roman:

1. From an isolated, timid young engineer who has survived a formative disaster with TJ;
2. To a protégé trying to continue Orien Keel's work;
3. To an employee who discovers that his institution has buried evidence;
4. To a person driven by anger after Croft causes the same destruction that killed Orien;
5. To a researcher who chooses public verification and distribution over revenge alone.

The ending offers accountability and a difficult beginning rather than a solved climate crisis. Peer reviewers confirm that the core process works while identifying serious energy, waste and deployment limits. Croft's suppression becomes public, but no instant climate reversal occurs. **[FIXED; invented]** `(rules/decisions.md; story.md; references/research.md)`

### Central dramatic questions

- Can Roman recover and complete the research that CSG buried?
- Will he expose Benedict Croft's role in suppressing climate research?
- Can evidence survive a billionaire's ability to discredit it?
- Does a technological remedy repair the damage caused by the institution that discovered it, or merely create another version of the same system?
- Can Roman turn grief and anger into work that benefits people beyond himself?
- Can a technically real but limited process be separated from the fossil-fuel system that first developed it?

### Theme

The story contrasts the extraction economy with conservation and repair. CSG's drilling contributes to the warming that destroys water and ice, yet CSG's buried research may offer a way to reduce the need for new fossil-fuel extraction. The same institution is therefore both source of harm and accidental source of a possible remedy. `(input/story.md; input/world.md)`

The Antarctic and Colorado threads mirror one another:

- Antarctic sea-ice loss threatens krill, a base of the food web.
- Colorado snowpack and streamflow decline threaten cold-water species and water availability.
- Both stories involve the loss of a keystone physical resource causing wider ecological consequences.

Project 863 began as a combined program. Krill decline supplied ecological evidence that motivated the plastics-to-fuel work, while the two research tracks remained technically separate. The krill tank has thematic and institutional importance but does not produce the fuel. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

## Premise and narrative engine

The narrative begins with the event that creates Roman and TJ's unusual partnership, then jumps four years to the consequences of climate damage and institutional suppression.

The engine is causal:

1. Climate damage creates Roman's past survival incident and the story's present conditions.
2. Orien's research connects those conditions to CSG's business.
3. Croft fires Orien to suppress the connection.
4. Orien loses access to CSG housing, food and water assistance, dies alone within weeks, and is publicly recorded as having died from deprivation.
5. Roman, Orien's only next of kin, learns of the firing and death through official notices and Orien's belongings, not by witnessing either event.
6. Orien's belongings point Roman toward a hidden Antarctic laboratory.
7. The laboratory contains the combined history of krill and plastics research, including Project 863.
8. Roman finds Orien's reproducible but incomplete catalytic-pyrolysis method.
9. Roman brings the research to Croft because Croft has the money and infrastructure to implement it.
10. Croft's inaction and Roman's firing reveal that the problem is not only technical but institutional.
11. Roman publishes an unfinished portion to expose Croft, but Croft's wealth discredits it.
12. Roman returns to the research, completes the missing chemistry, testing and scale analysis with TJ's help, and distributes it through independent scientific, journalistic and governmental channels.
13. Scrutiny confirms that the process works while exposing its serious limits.

Every major discovery creates a new obligation or conflict rather than simply providing information. `(input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

## Story So Far

The supplied material is a full-arc outline and story treatment, not a finished graphic-novel script. The settled book structure is a **single 22-page issue**: a short 2042 opening, a compressed Colorado setup, an Antarctic investigation and Nexus discovery, then the Croft reversal and distribution-plan ending. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; rules/house-style.md)`

### Sequence 1 — The iceberg and the bond

**Time:** 2042  
**Place:** Nepal, in the Himalayas  
**Characters:** Roman Ashford, age 18; TJ

Roman travels alone to Nepal for his senior trip, bringing TJ, a robot dog built from spare parts by the scavenger-engineer Bi11bot. TJ has artificial intelligence, emergency capabilities and an extensive set of tools. Bi11bot remains backstory only: TJ's construction and release may be shown or referenced in this opening, but Bi11bot does not enter the 2046 investigation or provide information about the Neuralink bond. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/story.md; input/characters.md)`

Roman and TJ attempt to cross an iceberg weakened by melting associated with global warming. It splits open while they are crossing, trapping them inside the opening. `(input/story.md; input/characters.md)`

An electrical storm appears. A nearby lightning strike creates a persistent encrypted neural-comms bridge between Roman's Neuralink implant and TJ's processor. The bridge carries short thoughts, alerts and selected sensory data at close range; it is not unrestricted mind-reading or unlimited remote access. It can drop under shielding, interference, distance or power loss, and prolonged high-bandwidth use causes Roman pain, confusion or neurological fatigue. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md; input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

Roman and TJ use the connection and TJ's emergency functions to escape. The event establishes their shared survival history, the climate crisis as a physical threat, TJ as Roman's practical partner and the lasting cost of their bond.

### Sequence 2 — Colorado in 2046

**Time:** 2046  
**Place:** Colorado  
**Characters:** Roman Ashford; Orien Keel; Benedict Croft

Four years later, food is scarce and expensive. Colorado has almost no natural water sources left. Melting ice caps are raising ocean levels. `(input/story.md; input/world.md)`

Roman is 22, has a bachelor's degree in chemical engineering and researches global warming while working for Clear Spring Global. Senior chemical engineer Orien Keel mentors him.

Orien's research connects Colorado's dried rivers and lakes, severe global warming, oil drilling and oil use. Croft privately accepts that the research is scientifically credible, but believes that cheap recycled fuel would destroy CSG's oil assets and his control over the energy market. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

Croft confronts Orien privately and fires him to suppress the climate research. Orien loses access to CSG housing, food and water assistance, dies alone within weeks and is recorded publicly as having died from deprivation. Roman learns of the firing and death through official notices and Orien's belongings, not by witnessing either event. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

Roman is Orien's only next of kin. Orien's death gives Roman both a personal loss and access to the material Orien left behind.

### Sequence 3 — The file cabinet and the hidden project

**Place:** Colorado, in Orien's belongings and CSG's digital systems  
**Characters:** Roman; Orien, through his files; TJ

While going through Orien's things, Roman finds a file cabinet from Orien's time working for CSG in Antarctica. The cabinet contains a file about a CSG laboratory called **Nexus** and a project titled **863**. `(input/story.md)`

Project 863 began as a combined program. Its krill track documented ecological decline and supplied evidence motivating the plastics-to-fuel track, but the tracks remained technically separate. The krill research does not produce the fuel. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; world.md; references/research.md)`

Roman searches every CSG server for evidence of Nexus or Project 863 but finds nothing. Nexus was abandoned for ordinary budget and logistics reasons. Croft later ordered its digital records suppressed after discovering that the plastics research could threaten CSG's oil business. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; world.md)`

Because the records point to an Antarctic facility and Roman needs access to the current CSG operation there, he requests a transfer to CSG's Antarctic drilling site. The transfer is accepted.

### Sequence 4 — The Antarctic search

**Place:** The CSG Antarctic research community and drilling site  
**Characters:** Roman; TJ; the current Antarctic crew

Roman arrives in Antarctica and spends his first weeks keeping his head down, working hard and listening for anyone to mention Nexus. The current crew is too young to have worked at the old laboratory, so no direct witness supplies the answer.

CSG operates under a national Antarctic research agreement and leases or manages its facilities under overlapping government and treaty permissions. CSG owns the equipment and records at Nexus, while public authorities retain emergency and environmental oversight. Roman's unauthorized search is trespass under CSG rules, though enforcement is complicated by the facility's abandonment. **[FIXED; research]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

Eventually Roman brings TJ to the drill site and sneaks into the old paper file room. TJ scans nearby materials but cannot determine hidden contents or guarantee that a discovered material is suitable. He finds the one file left about Nexus, buried behind a file cabinet. The file contains coordinates.

TJ carries rechargeable batteries and can supplement them from CSG or field equipment, but cold reduces capacity and long-range travel requires rationing. His lights and emergency systems consume significant power. **[FIXED; research]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

The retained idea-draft exchange remains available as raw material: Roman asks TJ to unlock the key again, and TJ replies, **“The credits expired?”** It is not yet a settled line. `(input/story.md)`

The file changes the investigation from a missing corporate project into a solvable expedition.

### Sequence 5 — The midnight expedition

**Time:** After Roman's first weeks at the Antarctic site; departure at midnight  
**Place:** Antarctic ice between the CSG site and Nexus  
**Characters:** Roman; TJ

Roman and TJ leave the CSG site at midnight to follow the coordinates. TJ's eyes function as high-beam lights, but the lights consume significant power. After traveling for two hours, they find the old Nexus laboratory. `(input/story.md; rules/decisions.md)`

Nexus is massive, built into a cave, boarded up and far from the community habitat. It was decommissioned approximately twenty years before the main story. Its abandonment resulted from ordinary budget and logistics reasons, not an accident or containment catastrophe. Croft's later suppression of the digital records concealed the research's commercial significance rather than the original reason for closure. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; world.md)`

CSG's Antarctic personnel send encrypted status messages to Croft's private operations office in Colorado through a company-controlled satellite link. The messages report environmental conditions, research activity and unauthorized access, making them part of Croft's surveillance and suppression system. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; world.md)`

Roman risks his employment and physical safety because the official systems have erased the project and the surviving paper trail has given him one chance to find the truth.

### Sequence 6 — Nexus and Project 863

**Place:** The boarded-up Nexus cave laboratory  
**Characters:** Roman; TJ

Roman and TJ break into Nexus and search for anything that could help address global warming. Their physical actions are shown silently in the art while all spoken explanation remains in dialogue. TJ may say, “Give me a second, I'm running a scan now,” while the panel shows scan lights, a material readout or the fabrication process. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; tj-ai-agent.md)`

The laboratory contains evidence of its earlier work, including the krill research and a tank containing a krill ecosystem. The tank is still powered by an isolated emergency system. Its ecosystem is degraded but alive: a reduced krill population survives in discolored water under failing filtration, while monitoring equipment records years of decline. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

The tank is not a fuel apparatus. Its function is ecological and evidentiary: it shows the decline that helped motivate Project 863 and preserves the institutional connection between environmental observation and waste-to-fuel research.

Roman finds Orien's old office inside the laboratory. Orien was a senior engineer on Project 863, not its sole founder. He established the link between environmental damage and the need for a waste-to-fuel process, developed the initial laboratory protocols and left behind a reproducible but incomplete method. Roman must supply the missing chemistry, testing and scale analysis. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

The research describes catalytic pyrolysis of selected waste plastics into gasoline-range hydrocarbons. The process requires substantial external heat and electricity, cannot accept all plastics and produces contaminated residues that must be isolated. Its climate benefit depends on low-carbon energy and strict controls on the use of the resulting fuel. It reduces demand for new petroleum extraction but does not end fossil-fuel use or stop global warming by itself. **[FIXED; research]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

This is the story's major discovery. It connects Orien's work, CSG's hidden Antarctic history and a possible response to the fossil-fuel system that CSG represents. It also creates the central ethical and practical problem: the method is reproducible but incomplete, and meaningful deployment requires testing, energy accounting, residue management and scale analysis.

Roman decides that the research could reduce the need for new fossil-fuel extraction if its conditions are met. He also decides that Croft is the person with enough money and infrastructure to complete and implement it.

### Sequence 7 — Returning to Croft

**Place:** CSG headquarters, Colorado  
**Characters:** Roman; Benedict Croft

Roman returns immediately to Colorado from the Antarctic expedition. He meets Croft at CSG headquarters and tells him that the research could help address the climate crisis.

Croft agrees that the work is scientifically credible and important. He tells Roman to hand everything over and stop pursuing it, saying that he will handle it. Croft's concrete suppression strategy includes legal claims over company research, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

Roman gives Croft access to the research, expecting CSG's resources to turn the discovery into a functioning project. Weeks pass. No teams form and no public news appears. Croft's promise produces no visible progress.

The institutional contradiction sharpens: Croft possesses the means to develop the process, but its potential to reduce new oil extraction threatens the business that gives him power.

### Sequence 8 — Roman is fired

**Place:** CSG headquarters and/or CSG research facilities in Colorado  
**Characters:** Roman; Croft

Roman begins finishing the research himself. Croft discovers that Roman is continuing the work and fires him.

Roman reacts with intense anger. The firing reveals that Orien's fate was not an isolated tragedy. Croft's legal and corporate position also gives CSG a claim over the research, making Roman's continued work vulnerable to legal challenge.

Roman's objective changes. He is no longer only trying to persuade Croft to fund a solution. He now wants to complete the research independently and harm Croft by exposing what CSG has done. The personal and public motives become entangled.

### Sequence 9 — The first publication and discrediting campaign

**Place:** Colorado; public information channels  
**Characters:** Roman; Croft; the public

Roman publishes the portion of the research he has completed. His purposes are to help get the work finished and to harm Croft. The publication is both an attempt to advance the science and an act of retaliation.

Croft uses legal claims over company research, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging to label Roman's publication fraudulent. The public comes to believe that it is fake news. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

This is a major reversal. Publication does not automatically produce accountability or support. Evidence can be neutralized by institutional power before it reaches people who might act on it.

### Sequence 10 — Completion and distribution plan

**Place:** Roman's working environment in Colorado; scientific, journalistic and governmental channels  
**Characters:** Roman; TJ; eventual recipients

After the publication is discredited, Roman decides that the only way to help humanity is to finish the research himself.

With TJ's help, Roman works for a couple of months and completes the missing chemistry, testing and scale analysis. TJ scans nearby materials, fabricates small tools and replacement parts from sorted feedstock when possible, and provides information and practical support. His fabricator works slowly, cannot produce large structures or complex electronics, and cannot substitute for a laboratory or a human researcher. **[FIXED; research]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

Roman's Neuralink bridge with TJ carries short thoughts, alerts and selected sensory data at close range. Shielding, interference, distance and power loss can interrupt it; prolonged high-bandwidth use can cause Roman pain, confusion or neurological fatigue. Their work therefore requires mutual cooperation, rationing and deliberate choices rather than unlimited machine assistance. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md)`

Roman releases a complete methods package, raw data and independent replication instructions through several scientific contacts identified through Orien's professional network. He sends the same package simultaneously to climate journalists and government science offices, creating multiple records before Croft can suppress any single recipient. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md)`

The distribution triggers immediate scrutiny. Peer reviewers confirm that the core process works while identifying serious energy, waste and deployment limits. They do not validate the claim that the process alone will end global warming. Croft's suppression becomes public, but no instant climate reversal occurs. **[FIXED; invented]** `(rules/decisions.md; references/research.md)`

The ending offers accountability and a difficult beginning rather than a solved crisis. The research is now harder for one company to erase, but its responsible use still depends on low-carbon energy, strict residue controls, limits on acceptable plastics and controls over how the resulting fuel is used.

## Causality and escalation

### The environmental crisis causes the personal history

Global warming weakens the iceberg Roman crosses in 2042. The collapse traps him and TJ and creates their Neuralink connection. The same climate crisis later appears as Colorado's dried rivers and lakes, scarce food and water, Antarctic ice loss and the disappearance of wildlife.

### Orien's research causes the institutional conflict

Orien connects Colorado's environmental damage to oil drilling and use. His work on Project 863 also identifies waste plastics as a possible feedstock for gasoline-range hydrocarbons, but the process remains incomplete and technically constrained. Because Croft owns CSG and depends on oil, the research threatens his business and control. Croft fires Orien, suppressing the work and initiating the chain that leads to Orien's death.

### Orien's death causes Roman's investigation

Roman is Orien's only next of kin. Official notices and Orien's belongings give Roman access to the Antarctic file cabinet and the Nexus/863 lead. Roman's grief becomes an investigative motive.

### The missing digital record causes the physical search

Roman cannot find Nexus or Project 863 on CSG's servers because Croft ordered the digital records suppressed. The surviving paper file therefore becomes essential. The file in the old paper room supplies coordinates, converting suspicion into an expedition.

### Nexus causes Roman's return to Croft

The laboratory contains Orien's incomplete catalytic-pyrolysis research, as well as the krill records that supplied its ecological motivation. Roman sees a possible way to reduce new petroleum extraction, but he believes Croft is the only person with sufficient resources to complete and implement it.

### Croft's inaction causes Roman's independent work

Croft tells Roman he will handle the research, but weeks pass without visible action. Roman resumes the work himself. Croft then fires him, proving that access to money and infrastructure does not mean a willingness to use them against the company's interests.

### The firing causes the first publication

Roman publishes partly to advance the work and partly to hurt Croft. Croft's money, legal claims, sponsored counter-research and media campaign discredit the publication, demonstrating that exposure without independent validation is vulnerable.

### Discrediting causes the second strategy

Roman abandons the assumption that publishing alone will help. He completes the research with TJ, then creates a staged and redundant distribution through peer review, journalism and government science offices.

## Character movement through the story

### Roman Ashford

Roman begins as a shy, timid eighteen-year-old traveling alone in the Himalayas. The iceberg disaster makes climate change physically personal and binds him to TJ.

At 22, Roman is a trained chemical engineer working inside CSG. Orien's mentorship gives him a model for scientific work and a person whose death he can investigate. Roman initially seeks institutional action: he brings the Nexus research to Croft because he thinks CSG can implement it.

Croft's betrayal changes Roman's relationship to the institution. Roman becomes angry and publishes before the work is complete, motivated partly by a desire to damage Croft. When Croft discredits him, Roman must convert that anger into a more rigorous process.

By the end, Roman is still angry at Croft, but he rejects revenge as the project's purpose. He honors Orien by submitting the work to independent scrutiny and distributing it through several channels. He recognizes TJ as a collaborator rather than equipment. Their final exchange shows trust and shared commitment to the next phase. **[FIXED; inferred]** `(rules/decisions.md; input/characters.md)`

### Orien Keel

Orien is a senior engineer on Project 863, not its sole founder. He established the link between environmental damage and the need for a waste-to-fuel process, developed initial laboratory protocols and left behind a reproducible but incomplete method.

After his death, Orien continues to affect the story through the files he leaves behind, the hidden Nexus connection and the unfinished work Roman completes. Roman's final act of independent scrutiny honors Orien more than a private confrontation with Croft would.

### Benedict Croft

Croft begins as the owner of CSG and the person with the resources to implement large-scale research. He privately accepts that the research is credible but believes recycled fuel would damage CSG's oil assets and his control over the energy market.

Croft fires Orien, suppresses Nexus's digital records, orders Roman to surrender the research, delays action, fires Roman and uses legal claims, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging to discredit Roman's first publication.

Croft's suppression becomes public after the completed research is distributed. The story does not depict an instant legal or corporate resolution. His defeat is the exposure of the suppression system and the loss of his ability to keep Project 863 solely within CSG's control.

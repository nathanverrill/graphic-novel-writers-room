# World and settings

Set in 2046, in Antarctica and Colorado, with the Himalayas as backstory. In 2046: 80% the same, 15% similar, 5% change.

## Antarctica

- A snow-covered mountain range on a coastline with ice in the ocean
- A community of research bases and homes where the protagonist lives and studies; small research community
- Main base plus drilling operations (the CSG drill site)
- A secret, undiscovered base: the Nexus laboratory — built into a cave, boarded up, massive, far from the community habitat; decommissioned about 20 years ago; krill species research and plastics research; a tank with a krill ecosystem
- Little wildlife left
- Global warming melting the ice

## Colorado

- Rivers and lakes drying out; almost no natural water sources left
- Clear Spring Global headquarters; research lab / drilling site
- Food scarce and expensive

## Himalayas (backstory only)

- Hiking; the iceberg crossing that splits open
- The Neuralink bond (2042 lightning strike)

## The loop

Destroying the environment to save the environment: oil drilling and use drives the warming; the fix (plastics to gasoline) comes out of the drilling company's own buried research.

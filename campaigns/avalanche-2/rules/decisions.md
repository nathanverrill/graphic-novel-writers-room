# Decisions

The showrunner's answers to the open items the Script Coordinator raised at intake. Each one settles its question: the book must not contradict it.

## What are the exact rules, limits, and real-world consequences of the plastics-to-gasoline process?

Treat Project 863 as a catalytic pyrolysis process that converts selected waste plastics into gasoline-range hydrocarbons. It requires substantial external heat and electricity, cannot accept all plastics, produces contaminated residues that must be isolated, and is only climate-positive when powered by low-carbon energy and paired with strict controls on the resulting fuel's use. It reduces demand for new petroleum extraction but does not end fossil-fuel use or stop global warming by itself. [research] (references/research.md; rules/hard-sf-rules.md)

## How are Nexus, Project 863, the krill research, and the plastics research related?

Project 863 began as a combined program: krill decline supplied the ecological evidence that motivated the plastics-to-fuel work, while the two research tracks remained technically separate. The tank therefore has both thematic and institutional importance but does not produce the fuel. [inferred] (world.md; story.md; references/research.md)

Nexus was abandoned for ordinary budget and logistics reasons, but Croft later ordered the digital records suppressed after discovering that the plastics research could threaten CSG's oil business. [inferred] (world.md; story.md)

## What is the formal ownership, legal authority, and access structure governing CSG's Antarctic operations and Nexus?

CSG operates under a national Antarctic research agreement and leases or manages its own facilities under overlapping government and treaty permissions. CSG owns the equipment and records at Nexus, while public authorities retain emergency and environmental oversight. Roman's unauthorized search is trespass under CSG rules, though enforcement is complicated by the facility's abandonment. [research] (references/research.md; world.md)

## What are the sender, recipient, purpose, and channel of the secret messages being sent from Antarctica?

CSG personnel in Antarctica send encrypted status messages to Croft's private operations office in Colorado. They report environmental conditions, research activity and unauthorized access through a company-controlled satellite link, making the messages part of Croft's surveillance and suppression system. ## What are the mechanics, range, privacy, risks, and failure modes of the Neuralink–TJ connection?

The lightning event creates a persistent encrypted neural-comms bridge between Roman's implant and TJ's processor. It carries short thoughts, alerts and selected sensory data at close range, but not unrestricted mind-reading or unlimited remote access. It can drop under shielding, interference, distance or power loss, and prolonged high-bandwidth use causes Roman pain, confusion or neurological fatigue. [inferred] (characters.md; world.md; story.md)

## What capabilities, resources, and failure conditions limit TJ's scanning, fabrication, navigation, lights, and emergency systems?

TJ carries rechargeable batteries and can supplement them from CSG or field equipment, but cold reduces capacity and long-range travel requires rationing. His scanner identifies nearby materials but cannot determine hidden contents or guarantee suitability. His fabricator uses sorted feedstock, works slowly, produces small tools and replacement parts rather than large structures, and cannot reproduce complex electronics. His lights and emergency systems consume significant power. [research] (references/research.md; characters.md; world.md)

## What is the status and condition of the krill ecosystem tank when Roman reaches Nexus?

The tank is still powered by an isolated emergency system. Its ecosystem is degraded but alive: a reduced krill population survives in discolored water under failing filtration, while monitoring equipment records years of decline. [inferred] (world.md; story.md; references/research.md)

## What caused Orien's firing, deprivation, death, and the discovery of his belongings, and who knows about it?

Croft fires Orien after confronting him privately about the climate research. Orien loses access to CSG housing, food and water assistance, dies alone within weeks, and is recorded publicly as having died from deprivation. Roman learns of the firing and death through official notices and Orien's belongings, not by witnessing either event. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

## What exactly did Orien do at Nexus, and how mature was his work when he died?

Orien was a senior engineer on Project 863, not its sole founder. He established the link between environmental damage and the need for a waste-to-fuel process, developed the initial laboratory protocols and left behind a reproducible but incomplete method. Roman must supply the missing chemistry, testing and scale analysis. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

_(decided 2026-09-28, about characters.md, story.md)_

## What does Croft personally believe, and what is his concrete plan for suppressing or discrediting the research?

Croft privately accepts that the research is scientifically credible but believes cheap recycled fuel would destroy CSG's oil assets and his control over the energy market. He suppresses it through employment termination, legal claims over company research, sponsored counter-research and coordinated media messaging that labels Roman's publication fraudulent. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

## How does Roman obtain credible peer review, press attention, and government consideration after his first publication is discredited?

Roman releases a complete methods package, raw data and independent replication instructions through several scientific contacts identified through Orien's professional network. He sends the same package simultaneously to climate journalists and government science offices, creating multiple records before Croft can suppress any single recipient. [inferred] (story.md; characters.md)

## What happens after Roman distributes the completed research?

The distribution triggers immediate scrutiny. Peer reviewers confirm that the core process works while identifying serious energy, waste and deployment limits; Croft's suppression becomes public, but no instant climate reversal occurs. The ending offers accountability and a difficult beginning rather than a solved crisis. [invented] (story.md; references/research.md)

## What is Roman's final emotional and relational resolution with Croft, Orien's legacy, and TJ?

Roman does not seek a final private confrontation with Croft. He honors Orien by submitting the work to independent scrutiny, rejects revenge as the project's purpose, and recognizes TJ as a collaborator rather than equipment. Their final exchange shows trust and shared commitment to the next phase. [inferred] (characters.md; story.md)

Structure the book as a single 22-page issue: a short 2042 opening, a compressed Colorado setup, an Antarctic investigation and Nexus discovery, then the Croft reversal and distribution-plan ending. [inferred] (story.md; rules/house-style.md)

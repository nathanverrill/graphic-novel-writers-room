# EVOKE Avalanche

Chapter 1. Twenty-four pages.

Two kinds of snow, one question.

In the San Juan Mountains of Colorado, Tomas Ruelas reads a slope that has been quietly rotten
since November and looks perfectly safe from the road. Eleven thousand miles south, his
sister-in-law Wren Sandoval stands on fast ice that has held an emperor penguin colony for
every one of the thirty years she has been counting it, and knows it is the same problem at a
different scale — a system loaded past its strength, holding only because nothing has touched
it yet.

Alpha Dynamics offers to hold it up.

This chapter is the setup and the offer: establish both snows, both scientists, the colony and
the bay, and end on Dane Okonkwo turning a tablet around to show Wren a plan that would work.
The avalanche itself is the midpoint of the book, not of this chapter — but the snowpack that
will produce it should already be on the page, in a pit, with Tomas's bare hand against the
layer that crumbles.

Target length: 24 pages.

# Characters

## Sitara Lhakpa — main character

- Female, early 20s
- Background: Nepalese — from the Thakali people; grew up in the Himalayas. Create a specific hometown (Nepal region, Everest/Himalayas). Mani Rimdu festival as cultural touchstone.
- Name notes: "Sitara: beautiful and inevitable darkness before dawn." "Lhakpa" is a Tibetan name given to those born on Wednesday (Mercury); carries strength, courage, resilience. Day-of-week naming tradition: Dawa (Mon), Mingma (Tue), Lhakpa (Wed), Phurba (Thu), Pasang (Fri), Pemba (Sat), Nima (Sun).
- Beliefs: Buddhist
- Strengths: Passionate
- Flaws: Ambitious to a fault. Reckless and desperate due to her passion to uncover the truth.
- Backstory: something happened in the Himalayas that pushed her to go to Colorado (CU Boulder?) and study. In 2026 a flood took her parents and her home (the Nepal disaster — see research). Her team was trapped underground in an old bunker.
- Arc: she and her Colorado friend are sent to Antarctica under dire circumstances, meets an enemy, must endure and overcome, face a challenge, solve a mystery, then returns to Colorado and analyses the data (hero's journey, complex).

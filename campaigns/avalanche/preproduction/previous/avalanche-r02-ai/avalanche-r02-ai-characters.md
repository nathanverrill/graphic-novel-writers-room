# Avalanche — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Build round characters whose flaws make them human, especially Sitara and Nayah. The room should explore how their weaknesses create pressure and affect their choices. `(input/characters.md)`
- Keep the people grounded in a hard-science-fiction world set in 2046. Any invented character capability, technology, institution, or biography must follow the project’s hard-SF labels and plausibility rules. **FIXED.** `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md, rules/rules.md)`
- Sitara’s Himalayan heritage, family loss, Buddhist beliefs, and connection to Colorado should materially shape her motives rather than function as decorative background. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ should be useful as a character, not merely equipment: an AI robot dog who educates the others about conservation and the cryosphere, helps them solve problems, and has clearly defined limits. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- The character relationships should carry tension: Sitara’s ambition and desperation should meet Nayah’s protectiveness and mistrust under Antarctic pressure. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Main character and protagonist. She is sent to Antarctica with Nayah and later returns to Colorado to analyze the expedition’s data. She studies cryosphere remote sensing at the University of Colorado Boulder and works as a research technician on field data. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity:** Female, early 20s. She is Nepalese and belongs to the Thakali people. Sitara grew up in a fictional settlement near the Lirung massif and Trishuli River system, placing her directly in the 2026 disaster zone. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Hair color `#483c32`; skin color `#a67b5b`. Her hair is worn in a ponytail or two braids, smooth and straight, with messy strands framing her face. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. Her Antarctic mission clothing consists of a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/art-direction.md)`

**Voice:** Sitara becomes quieter under pressure. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Who they are:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious to a fault, reckless, and desperate in her drive to uncover the truth. Her ambition and passion can override caution. She is Buddhist. Her connection to the Himalayas and the loss of her family and home are central pressures on her present life. `(input/characters.md)`

**Beliefs and values:** She is Buddhist and comes from a Thakali Himalayan background. Her Thakali family taught her stewardship through mountain livelihood. She treats the ice as a living system and refuses to sacrifice people for evidence. The Mani Rimdu festival remains a cultural touchstone. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** Colorado friend and Antarctic expedition partner. Their relationship is close enough for them to be sent together. Nayah protects Sitara because Sitara repeatedly treats evidence as more important than people, while Sitara’s recklessness strains Nayah’s trust. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Expedition companion and educational resource. TJ educates Sitara and her friends about conservation and the cryosphere. TJ is also the only one who can open the Antarctic bunker, making Sitara practically dependent on the robot during the investigation. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/decisions.md)`
- **Greed / the antagonist:** Sitara encounters an enemy associated with the Falcon organization while investigating the Antarctic mystery. Greed is an intermediary representing the real beneficiaries of the operation, with authority to sacrifice the Antarctic operation but not to expose the investors. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Sitara rolls her mala beads under pressure and records unanswered questions rather than conclusions in a field notebook. Her tendency to pursue the truth recklessly is the clearest behavioral pattern supplied. `(input/art-direction.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** In 2026, a flood connected to the Lirung–Trishuli disaster geography took Sitara’s parents and home. Her parents died while helping neighbors evacuate. Sitara witnessed the loss from a monastery refuge. The book fictionalizes her town, family experience, and casualty figures rather than adopting unneeded research totals as settled story history. `(input/characters.md, references/research.md, rules/decisions.md)`

The disaster pushed her to leave the Himalayas and go to Colorado. She studies cryosphere remote sensing at the University of Colorado Boulder and works as a research technician on field data. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

Later, a Himalayan survey team including Sitara was trapped in an abandoned research tunnel. The event explains her fear of enclosed spaces and distrust of official rescue claims. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No prose or page draft was supplied. The opening material establishes that Sitara is present during Antarctic ice-movement research and glacier instability, and that her past disaster in Nepal is connected to the opening through memory. `(input/story.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins as a passionate investigator whose ambition makes her reckless and desperate. She learns that pursuing truth without protecting people reproduces the violence she is investigating. She accepts collaboration and refuses a dangerous solo proof-gathering act. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

### Nayah

**Role:** Sitara’s Colorado friend and Antarctic expedition partner. Nayah is part of the Colorado-linked cryosphere field team conducting airborne and ground ice-movement surveys near Larsen C. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity:** Native American, late 20s, and two-spirit. Nayah belongs to a fictional Colorado Plains nation, allowing the story to avoid assigning invented personal history to a real community while retaining a specific homeland. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** Hair color `#321414`; skin color `#785134`. Nayah has two long, smooth, straight braids and dark brown eyes. They wear cyan circle earrings, slightly hidden by the braids. Their Antarctic mission clothing consists of a puffy yellow jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof black pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/art-direction.md)`

**Voice:** No established speech rhythm, vocabulary, characteristic phrasing, or dialogue line is supplied. `(input/characters.md)`

**Who they are:** Nayah is motivated by protecting their Native homeland in the Colorado plains. They are spiritual, protective, and distrustful of institutions. Their prior experience is that conservation promises excluded their community. They protect Sitara because Sitara repeatedly treats evidence as more important than people. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Beliefs and values:** Nayah values the survival of their homeland and community. Water scarcity and groundwater drawdown threaten a prairie restoration and wildlife corridor. Nayah seeks enforceable water protections and community control over monitoring data. `(input/characters.md, references/research.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Sitara:** Colorado friend and expedition partner. Nayah’s protectiveness makes them resist Sitara’s reckless pursuit of the truth. Their mistrust is directed toward institutions and toward Sitara’s willingness to endanger people for evidence. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`
- **TJ:** Expedition companion and a source of conservation and cryosphere knowledge. Nayah’s relationship to TJ develops through practical dependence during the Antarctic investigation. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- **Greed / the antagonist:** Nayah is opposed to the antagonist’s power-driven exploitation and illegal Antarctic mining by association. The operation threatens the broader conservation systems tied to their homeland. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Nayah uses silence as resistance, physically places themself between Sitara and danger, and carries a water sample kit tied to their homeland’s conservation work. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Nayah has a Colorado connection and a personal stake in protecting their fictional Colorado Plains homeland. Water scarcity and groundwater drawdown threaten a prairie restoration and wildlife corridor connected to their community. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No prose or page draft was supplied. The available material establishes Nayah as Sitara’s Colorado friend and Antarctic partner. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Arc:** Nayah begins protective, distrustful, spiritually grounded, and committed to their homeland. They learn to distinguish justified caution from protective control and choose to trust Sitara with a consequential decision. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** AI robot dog; expedition companion; conservation and cryosphere educator; participant in solving the bunker mystery. TJ was developed through a University of Colorado Boulder research partnership and is deployed because its mobile body can carry sensors into unstable terrain. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity:** TJ is an AI agent built into a robotic dog. The in-story motivation is to educate Sitara and her friends about conservation and the cryosphere. TJ is also a mobile materials-sorting prototype with a small manipulator: it can recover samples but cannot operate a full recycling line. `(input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** TJ’s body is built like a Himalayan sheep dog. Its exterior is made of seamless silver paneling. It has a computer screen with a couple of buttons on its right-side ribs; the screen uses a green-and-black color scheme. Its eyes resemble those of the Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. TJ wears red puffy booties made from jacket material so its feet do not stick to the ice. TJ has an internal heating system. `(input/art-direction.md, input/world.md)`

**Voice:** TJ’s voice, speech rhythm, vocabulary, and humor are not yet defined. The intended tonal reference is “Baymax vibes” and “Doug vibes,” translated into project-specific behavior rather than copied style. `(input/characters.md)`

**Who they are:** TJ is designed to educate Sitara and the others, especially about conservation and the cryosphere. TJ may discuss its onboard science and conservation data, but refuses unsafe physical actions, unauthorized access, and claims it cannot verify. TJ marks uncertainty explicitly. `(input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Capabilities and limits:**

- TJ has intermittent satellite connectivity.
- TJ has limited episodic memory.
- TJ operates with supervised autonomy.
- All system integration requires a physical or authenticated network bridge.
- TJ can recover samples with its small manipulator but cannot operate a full industrial recycling line.
- TJ’s unusually broad cross-system compatibility is the project’s one declared **L**. Its power, authorization, bandwidth, and failure modes remain strict.
- TJ refuses unsafe physical actions and unauthorized access.
- TJ refuses to present unverifiable claims as fact and marks uncertainty explicitly. `(references/research.md, rules/hard-sf-rules.md, rules/decisions.md)`

TJ discovers a software vulnerability in an obsolete local controller and uses it to open the Antarctic bunker. This access is an investigative act, not proof of Falcon affiliation. TJ was not built by Falcon; its access results from a vulnerability in obsolete Falcon infrastructure. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Sitara:** TJ’s educational focus is Sitara and her friends. TJ’s ability to open the bunker makes the expedition dependent on TJ, while Sitara’s drive to uncover the truth pressures TJ to act within its limits. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, rules/decisions.md)`
- **Nayah:** TJ is Nayah’s expedition companion and a source of conservation knowledge. Their cooperation is tested by the need to balance safety, evidence, and mission objectives. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`
- **The bunker / Falcon organization:** TJ was not built by Falcon. Its access results from a vulnerability in obsolete Falcon infrastructure. TJ’s relationship to Falcon is therefore technical and investigative rather than institutional. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No recurring movement, screen behavior, sound, boot-up routine, or conversational tell is established. TJ’s dog-like body and “Baymax vibes / Doug vibes” are the available behavioral direction. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**History:** TJ was developed through a CU Boulder research partnership and deployed as a mobile sensor platform for unstable terrain. Its access to the Antarctic bunker does not come from Falcon construction, ownership, or authorization; TJ discovers a vulnerability in an obsolete Falcon controller. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No prose or page draft was supplied. The story opening establishes TJ discovering the underground bunker and being the only one able to open its hatch. `(input/story.md)`

**Arc:** TJ chooses not to use an unauthorized system integration that could save the mission but compromise human safety and evidence integrity. `(rules/decisions.md)`

### Falcon Organization

**Role:** Organization associated with the antagonist, the Antarctic bunker, and the illegal mining operation. It is a consortium operating through shell companies, contractors, and research partnerships rather than one legal entity. `(input/characters.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Identity:** Falcon is used by a US-based corporation and its contractors as a subsidiary or front for covert Antarctic extraction. Its repeated symbol identifies the organization across the bunker and its equipment. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Look:** The Falcon logo appears engraved on the snow-covered submarine-like bunker hatch and appears inside on chairs, computer lock screens, and pens. The bunker is a little dusty and appears not to have been used for some time. `(input/art-direction.md, input/world.md)`

**Who they are:** Falcon operates a temporary borehole extraction project near the Larsen C margin. It reaches exposed or seasonally exposed Antarctic bedrock rather than mining beneath the floating shelf itself. The operation uses modular power, drilling, pumping, and aircraft logistics. `(rules/decisions.md)`

Falcon exploits naturally unstable ice for concealment, while its drilling locally worsens fractures and endangers the shelf. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**Relationships:** Falcon’s bunker contains information about who is conducting the drilling. Its obsolete infrastructure contains the controller vulnerability TJ exploits to open the Antarctic door. TJ was not built by Falcon and is not affiliated with the organization. `(rules/decisions.md)`

**History:** Falcon operates through shell companies, contractors, and research partnerships rather than one legal entity. Its Antarctic activity is a covert operation conducted in violation of the Madrid Protocol. `(rules/rules.md, rules/decisions.md)`

**Arc:** Falcon’s operation is exposed through the Colorado analysis. Its operational control is lost, although Greed escapes immediate arrest. `(rules/decisions.md)`

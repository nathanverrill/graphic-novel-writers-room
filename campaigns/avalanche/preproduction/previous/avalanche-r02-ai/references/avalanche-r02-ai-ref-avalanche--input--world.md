# World and settings

Year: 2046.

## Locations

- Primary: Larsen region (Larsen C), Antarctica
- Secondary: Colorado; the Himalayas (Nepal)

## Antarctica — the Larsen C setting

- Larsen region C, glaciers, icy landscape
- "Rocky mountains" of the peninsula
- Late morning to early afternoon; icy blue sky with clouds; light wind; November; the sun is always up but can be covered by clouds
- Scientists on unstable shelves relocate camps frequently, use airborne surveys instead of ground surveys, avoid crevasse zones, monitor ice movement in real time — these methods continue in 2046
- Field detail: you have to wear booties to not get stuck to the ice (TJ has an internal heating system)

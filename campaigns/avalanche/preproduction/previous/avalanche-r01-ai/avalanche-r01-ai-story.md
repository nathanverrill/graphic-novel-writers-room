# Avalanche — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

### Intended experience

A hard-science-fiction graphic novel set in 2046, beginning in Antarctica and connecting three environments: Antarctica, the Himalayas, and Colorado. The story should surprise the reader with “cool science,” while allowing the audience to discover an interesting environment rather than being told about it directly. `(rules/rules.md; input/story.md; input/minecraft.md)`

The story is intended to combine:

- Antarctic ice research and glacier instability;
- Sitara’s Himalayan family history and the disaster that drove her to Colorado;
- conservation work connected between Antarctica and Colorado;
- an AI character able to integrate into systems;
- a secret message originating in Antarctica;
- a mystery involving an underground bunker and the Falcon organization;
- a graphic-novel structure built around visual discovery. `(input/story.md; input/world.md; input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

The story must follow the project’s hard-science-fiction rules. Any invented technology, institution, law, weather event, body, or other world element must carry a **T**, **EG**, **S**, **L**, or **Cut** label. The story is governed by the 80/15/5 mix: most of the world remains recognizable, emerging technology is ordinary by 2046, and only a very small number of elements are genuinely new. `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md)`

### Central dramatic question

The supplied material points toward the question:

> What is happening beneath the Antarctic ice, who is sending the secret messages, and can Sitara and Nayah uncover the truth before the activity threatens the ice, conservation work, and the people connected to it?

This remains a working formulation rather than an explicit showrunner decision. The material establishes the mystery, the illegal drilling, the bunker, and the characters’ need to endure and solve the problem, but does not yet settle the precise secret, sender, or final choice. `(input/story.md; input/world.md; input/characters.md)`

### Theme and emotional direction

The material reaches toward a story about:

- the relationship between climate science and conservation;
- the consequences of exploiting fragile environments;
- the connection between Sitara’s Himalayan loss and the Antarctic crisis;
- the need to uncover truth despite institutional secrecy;
- the tension between ambition and responsibility;
- the possibility that scientific data can connect distant places and communities. `(input/story.md; input/characters.md; input/world.md)`

Sitara is meant to begin as passionate, ambitious, reckless, and desperate to uncover the truth. Her story trajectory is described as a complex hero’s journey: she travels from Colorado to Antarctica, meets an enemy, endures a challenge, solves a mystery, returns to Colorado, and analyzes the data. The exact internal change and emotional destination remain open. `(input/characters.md; input/story.md)`

### Intended visual experience

The opening should begin with tension and show the consequences of Antarctic instability before supplying all the context. The proposed opening structure is:

1. show what happened in Antarctica and why it matters;
2. move through Antarctic science research;
3. connect the crisis to Colorado;
4. bring in Sitara’s Himalayan memory and history. `(input/story.md)`

The project favors environmental discovery, visual evidence, recurring motifs, and images that carry information without requiring explanation. Possible recurring visual material includes shifting ice, glacier collapse, drill sounds, coded bunker lids, drought-carved landscapes, secret messages, and a possible bird motif. The bird motif is explicitly a possibility, not an established story element. `(input/story.md; input/minecraft.md)`

## Premise

In 2046, Sitara Lhakpa and her Colorado friend Nayah travel to Antarctica under dire circumstances as Antarctic ice research reveals dangerous instability near Larsen Region C. While working in the region, they hear drilling and observe shifting ice. TJ, an AI robot dog traveling with them, discovers a snow-covered underground bunker and is the only one able to open its coded door. Inside are signs that the Falcon organization is conducting illegal mining in violation of the Madrid Protocol.

The bunker contains information about the drilling and hidden records that may connect Antarctica’s present crisis to older secrets and to the environmental struggles of Colorado and the Himalayas. Sitara and Nayah must endure the Antarctic conditions, confront the Falcon-linked enemy, interpret secret messages, and determine what the data means. Sitara eventually returns to Colorado to analyze the evidence, but the exact revelation, confrontation, and resolution have not yet been established. `(rules/rules.md; input/story.md; input/world.md; input/characters.md)`

Research supplied to the project describes ice shelves as buttresses: if an ice shelf is removed, land-based glaciers behind it can accelerate toward the ocean. The research also describes Larsen C as fragile and vulnerable, with current scientific work relying on frequent camp relocation, airborne surveys, avoidance of crevasse zones, and real-time ice monitoring.

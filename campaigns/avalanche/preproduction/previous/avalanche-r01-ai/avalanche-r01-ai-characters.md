# Avalanche — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- Build round characters whose flaws make them human, especially Sitara and Nayah. The room should explore how their weaknesses create pressure and affect their choices. `(input/characters.md)`
- Keep the people grounded in a hard-science-fiction world set in 2046. Any invented character capability, technology, institution, or biography must follow the project’s hard-SF labels and plausibility rules. **FIXED.** `(rules/hard-sf-rules.md, rules/rules.md)`
- Sitara’s Himalayan heritage, family loss, Buddhist beliefs, and connection to Colorado should materially shape her motives rather than function as decorative background. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- TJ should be useful as a character, not merely equipment: an AI robot dog who educates the others about conservation and the cryosphere, helps them solve problems, and has clearly defined limits. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md)`
- The character relationships should carry tension: Sitara’s ambition and desperation should meet Nayah’s protectiveness and mistrust under Antarctic pressure. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

## Characters

### Sitara Lhakpa

**Role:** Main character and protagonist. She is sent to Antarctica with Nayah and later returns to Colorado to analyze the expedition’s data. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Identity:** Female, early 20s. She is Nepalese and belongs to the Thakali people. She grew up in the Himalayas and later went to Colorado, possibly to study at the University of Colorado Boulder. The Colorado university affiliation remains uncertain. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Look:** Hair color `#483c32`; skin color `#a67b5b`. Her hair is worn in a ponytail or two braids, smooth and straight, with messy strands framing her face. She wears dark brown mala beads around her neck, mostly covered by her jacket. Her Antarctic mission clothing consists of a puffy red jacket, usually with the hood down, waterproof pants, a beanie, warm waterproof boots, and thermal snow goggles. `(input/art-direction.md)`

**Voice:** No established speech rhythm, vocabulary, characteristic phrasing, or dialogue line is supplied. Her voice under stress is undecided. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Who they are:** Sitara is passionate, ambitious to a fault, reckless, and desperate in her drive to uncover the truth. Her ambition and passion can override caution. She is Buddhist. Her connection to the Himalayas and the loss of her family and home are central pressures on her present life. `(input/characters.md)`

**Beliefs and values:** She is Buddhist and comes from a Thakali Himalayan background. The Mani Rimdu festival is identified as a cultural touchstone. Her concern with truth, investigation, science, and conservation is established through her role in the Antarctic mystery and later data analysis. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Nayah:** Colorado friend and Antarctic expedition partner. Their relationship is close enough for them to be sent together, but the material leaves open whether Sitara’s recklessness creates conflict with Nayah’s protectiveness and lack of trust. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`
- **TJ:** Expedition companion and educational resource. TJ is intended to educate Sitara and her friends about conservation and the cryosphere. TJ is also the only one who can open the Antarctic bunker door, giving Sitara a practical dependence on the robot. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, input/story.md)`
- **Greed / the antagonist:** Sitara encounters an enemy associated with the Falcon organization while investigating the Antarctic mystery. The exact personal relationship and history between them are not established. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

**Habits and tells:** No recurring gesture, routine, stress behavior, or signature object is established beyond her mala beads. Her tendency to pursue the truth recklessly is the clearest behavioral pattern supplied. `(input/art-direction.md, input/characters.md)`

**History:** In 2026, a flood took Sitara’s parents and her home. The flood is linked in the material to the Nepal disaster involving the Lirung massif rock-ice avalanche, but the detailed event and its exact relationship to Sitara’s personal experience have not been fully specified in the character material. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md, references/research.md)`

The disaster pushed her to leave the Himalayas and go to Colorado, possibly to study at CU Boulder. The exact program, degree, and circumstances of that move are open. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

The material also says that her team was trapped underground in an old bunker. It is not yet clear whether this refers to the 2026 disaster, a later Himalayan event, the Antarctic bunker, or a separate expedition. `(input/characters.md)`

**In existing drafts:** No prose or page draft was supplied. The opening material establishes that Sitara is present during Antarctic ice-melt research and glacier instability, and that her past disaster in Nepal is connected to the opening through memory. `(input/story.md)`

**Arc:** Sitara begins as a passionate investigator whose ambition makes her reckless and desperate. She is sent to Antarctica under dire circumstances, encounters an enemy, endures a challenge, and investigates a mystery. She eventually returns to Colorado and analyzes the data. The material identifies this as a complex hero’s-journey arc, but does not establish the specific internal change, the belief she must relinquish, or the final emotional state. `(input/characters.md, input/story.md)`

### TJ

**Role:** AI robot dog; expedition companion; conservation and cryosphere educator; participant in solving the bunker mystery. TJ is the only character currently established as able to open the underground Antarctic bunker door. `(input/characters.md, input/tj-ai-agent.md, input/story.md)`

**Identity:** TJ is an AI agent built into a robotic dog. The in-story motivation is to educate Sitara and her friends about conservation and the cryosphere. TJ is described as being basically a chatbot or conversational agent, but the exact architecture, training, connectivity, autonomy, and limits remain undefined. `(input/tj-ai-agent.md)`

**Look:** TJ’s body is built like a Himalayan sheep dog. Its exterior is made of seamless silver paneling. It has a computer screen with a couple of buttons on its right-side ribs; the screen uses a green-and-black color scheme. Its eyes resemble those of the Contixo R3 electronic robot dog. TJ wears red puffy booties made from jacket material so its feet do not stick to the ice. TJ has an internal heating system. `(input/art-direction.md, input/world.md)`

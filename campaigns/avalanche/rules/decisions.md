# Decisions

The showrunner's answers to the open items the Script Coordinator raised at intake. Each one settles its question: the book must not contradict it.

## What is the exact meaning of “Sitara creating the crack”?

Sitara uses a drill or sampling tool in an unsafe location, triggering a pre-existing fracture in the ice; the “crack” is literal and caused by her reckless decision.  `(story.md; world.md)`

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What is the exact order and shape of the opening sequence?

Open on the glacier failure; cut to Sitara’s 2026 flood memory; return to Larsen C with a location caption; show drilling and shifting ice; let TJ detect the buried hatch; end Chapter One on TJ opening the Falcon-marked door. [inferred] `(story.md; world.md)`

_(decided 2026-09-28, about story.md)_

## What mission and emergency bring Sitara, Nayah, and TJ to Antarctica?

They are a Colorado-linked cryosphere field team conducting airborne and ground ice-movement surveys near Larsen C; a monitoring station detects an accelerating fracture while their camp is relocating. [research] `(references/research.md; world.md)`

Natural Larsen C instability initiates the failure, while Falcon’s drilling has weakened or accelerated a nearby fracture. [research] `(references/research.md; world.md)`

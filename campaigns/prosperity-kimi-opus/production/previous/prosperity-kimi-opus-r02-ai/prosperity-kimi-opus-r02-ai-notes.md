#### 2. Page 40: the founding catastrophe is a flood; canon says the reservoir fell

- **Location:** `script.md` Page 40, Panels 1–2 — "the reservoir rising, not falling: flood, the other face of the wars… the gate must open or the upstream town drowns — but opening it floods the downstream fields."
- **Problem:** Contradicts `facts.md` [T] / `world.md` / `story.md`: "The Covenant collapsed when one community closed a gate and another forced it open **after a reservoir fell faster than predicted**." The script's own Page 35 says it correctly ("A reservoir fell faster than anyone predicted. One community closed a gate. Another forced it open."). Page 40 invents the opposite mechanism — scarcity becomes surplus — which changes what the Covenant's failure means (fear of shortage, not fear of flood) and contradicts a T line.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Writer: keep the dramatized flashback (it is better than the draft's summary) but restore the falling-reservoir mechanism: drought, the level dropping faster than predicted, one community closing the gate to hold what remains, the other forcing it open. The "one unbearable night" staging works unchanged with the canon cause.
- **Role:** Writer.

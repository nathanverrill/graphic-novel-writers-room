## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Seventeen-year-old mechanic from Keel. Mechanically gifted and self-reliant; he fixes pumps by feel rather than by the manual. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Look:** Brown skin, short dense black curls, and dark eyes. He wears a dark work shirt with rolled sleeves over a black tee, rust-brown cargo trousers with patches, heavy scuffed boots, and a canvas tool bag on a shoulder strap. His hands are scarred and work-rough. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Signature objects:** A worn steel wristwatch, a little big for him, on his left wrist. His father left it behind. The caseback is engraved with a looping equation. It is the only thing Alex carries of Adrian besides a creased photograph. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Continuity constraint:** By default, Alex has nothing else on his wrists or in his hands: no wristband, coin, or chip. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** No specific speech rhythm, vocabulary, characteristic phrasing, stress pattern, or dialogue line is supplied. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Who he is:** Alex is lean, mechanically gifted, and self-reliant. His instinctive way of repairing pumps—palm flat on the housing, by feel rather than by the manual—establishes practical confidence and embodied technical knowledge. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Pressures:** Alex lives in Keel, where water is scarce, machinery is patched and maintained rather than replaced, residents queue with jugs at metered public taps, and workers pass digital checkpoints that show what they owe. Travel up the mountain requires a permit. These conditions place material and institutional pressure on a young mechanic whose skills are needed in a settlement dependent on failing infrastructure. `(input/world.md)`

**Relationships:**
- **Adrian, his father:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Keel says Adrian threw everything away. Alex half believes this and resents him for it. Alex's resentment is held alongside his remaining physical connection to Adrian: the watch and the creased photograph. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Grandmother:** Alex lives with or is responsible for his grandmother; the supplied material identifies her as his family and says she is losing her memory. The exact household arrangement and their day-to-day dynamic are not specified. `(rules/characters.md)`
- **Mother:** Alex's mother left after Adrian lost everything. Nothing more about her is learned in the supplied material. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Habits and tells:** He fixes pumps by placing his palm flat on the housing and reading the machinery by feel instead of consulting the manual. `(rules/characters.md)`

**History:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. According to Keel, Adrian threw everything away; Alex only half believes that account and resents his father for the abandonment or loss it implies. Alex's mother left after Adrian lost everything. Alex was raised with his grandmother, who is now losing her memory. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Arc:** No specific arc or endpoint is established in the supplied material. Alex's starting pressures are his self-reliance, his responsibility toward a grandmother losing her memory, his dependence on mechanical skill in Keel, and his unresolved resentment toward Adrian. `(rules/characters.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** Companion-sized industrial robot. His registration status is not specified as part of his identity, but when folded with his eyes dimmed, an inspection reads him as unregistered scrap. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Look:** A rugged, weathered industrial robot with an oversized round head made of segmented faded industrial-blue panels and a mustard-yellow stripe. He has two large round amber eyes in heavy metal rings, round ear assemblies on the sides of his head, broad rounded shoulder plates, a chest plate marked **“Bi11”** in white, exposed pistons, articulated metal fingers, compact legs, and broad feet. His blue and mustard-yellow paint is chipped, with rust, rivets, and scratches. He wears an old rust-red scarf at his neck. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Distinctive movement and mechanism:** His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses. He folds into a compact bundle small enough to carry in a backpack or tool bag. Folded, with his eyes dimmed, he looks like a heap of old brass and steel. Only one amber eye may show when he peeks out. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Bi11bot speaks in short, plain sentences. He asks more than he explains and never lectures. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot is wise, patient, and often quietly amused. He is never menacing. The tension in a scene may surround him, but he should not become the source of threatening visual language. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Relationships:** His specific relationship to Alex is not fully defined in the supplied material, though his designation as a companion-sized robot establishes a potential companion role. The nature, history, and degree of trust of that companionship remain unspecified. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Habits and tells:** His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses. He asks questions rather than delivering explanations. When concealment is necessary, he folds into a compact bundle, dims his eyes, and can pass as unregistered scrap during inspection. `(rules/characters.md)`

**History:** No history of Bi11bot's manufacture, ownership, discovery, prior work, or connection to Alex is supplied. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Visual continuity constraints:** He must never be depicted as menacing. His eyes must not use red or harsh light. Avoid looming low angles and avoid hiding his face in shadow. His readable, patient presence should remain intact even in tense scenes. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Arc:** No specific arc or endpoint is established in the supplied material. `(rules/characters.md)`

## Premise

**Material type: established project material, not a developed plot.**

In 2043, three cities stacked in one high-desert mountain basin depend on a shared spine of water, power, rail and control infrastructure. Keel, the bottom city, is built around an exhausted lithium mine and survives through patched machinery, metered water and restricted movement. Beneath Keel's sealed old mine works, newer water-control equipment operates out of sight, and official readings can differ from the actual flow. `(input/world.md)`

Alex Phantum is seventeen, mechanically gifted and from Keel. He fixes pumps by feel rather than by manual. His father, Adrian, disappeared when Alex was two; Keel says Adrian threw everything away, and Alex half believes it while resenting him. Alex carries Adrian's worn steel wristwatch, whose caseback bears a looping equation, and a creased photograph. `(rules/characters.md)`

No source establishes that Alex discovers, enters, repairs, exposes or otherwise acts upon the hidden water-control system. No source establishes that the watch, equation, Adrian or Bi11bot is connected to that system.

# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material type: explicit showrunner decision and revised draft.**

Chapter 1 is exactly **12 pages**, using the showrunner's revised draft as the working story material. The draft is to be edited rather than rewritten. `(rules/characters.md; input note; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The chapter introduces a world where water, work, travel and debt are administered through a vertical three-city system. Its immediate dramatic question is whether Alex Phantum can discover the truth about his vanished father and make a choice about his inherited future before the systems controlling Keel close around him. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The material's recurring questions are:

- **What if everyone you've trusted has a secret?**
- **Who really owns what we create?**
- **Where does all our water go?**
- What is the true cost of a decision?
- Was Adrian Phantum a man who wasted everything, or was he working toward something hidden?
- Can Alex choose a future rather than inherit the one Keel assigns him? `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The intended emotional movement is from Alex's public humiliation and inherited debt, through doubt about the official story of Adrian, into discovery and dangerous possibility. Alex begins the chapter believing that his father abandoned the family and that leaving Keel is nearly impossible. He ends having chosen to investigate Adrian's hidden trail, while an institution in Oasis begins tracking him. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The central thematic contrast is between **posted cost and real cost**. Keel's systems display debt, wages and travel restrictions as if they are objective facts. Adrian's watch offers another measure: the total cost of a choice over time. Alex must still weigh what the watch cannot measure—sleep, time and risk—himself. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Premise

**Material type: revised draft, supported by binding character rules and world material.**

On Alex Phantum's eighteenth birthday, Keel transfers his family's debt to him: **418,607 credits**, against **40 credits earned that day**, with a projected clearance time of **28.7 years** and restricted travel status. The transfer makes him legally and economically responsible for the story Keel tells about his missing father, Adrian: that Adrian borrowed and pawned everything away. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

That same day, Alex learns that Adrian left him a hidden workshop, a watch that measures the long-term credit cost of financial choices, and Bi11bot, an old field robot containing the only map to three artifacts. The first artifact, **the Measure**, lies inside Keel's sealed old mine. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex chooses to begin the search. His choice wakes Bi11bot and causes the robot to check in with Alpha's network. In Oasis, a watcher sees the legacy device come online and initiates tracking. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Story So Far

### Chapter One — *The Inheritance*

**Material type: revised draft.**  
**Length: fixed at 12 pages.** `(input note; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 1: The world Alex inherits

#### Page 1 — The three-tier system

The chapter opens on a full-page establishing image of the year **2043**. Three cities occupy one mountain basin:

- **Keel**, at the bottom, is built into an exhausted open-pit lithium mine. It is crowded, dusty and mechanically patched. People queue at metered public taps, workers pass digital checkpoints, and a sealed mine entrance sits on the pit rim.
- **Halyard**, midway up the slope, contains fabrication towers, workshops, foundries, greenhouse terraces and elevated rail.
- **Oasis**, above the cloud layer, has pearl-white architecture, reservoirs, fountains and automated transit. A spacecraft rises from a distant launch structure.

A shared infrastructure spine joins the cities: a massive water conduit, power lines, rail and identical white-and-amber control nodes. A permission gate crosses the rail line between Keel and Halyard. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The page establishes the material and social gradient that governs the chapter: water and infrastructure narrow toward Keel; access gates multiply toward the bottom; the three cities are connected but not equally free. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The page's captions pose the chapter's larger questions:

- Over Oasis: **“What if everyone you've trusted has a secret?”**
- Over Halyard: **“Who really owns what we create?”**
- Over Keel: **“Where does all our water go?”** `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 2, Panel 1 — Alex repairs the pump

Before dawn in a Keel pump hall, Alex works on an aging pump. He keeps one palm flat on the housing and reads its vibration by feel rather than following the projected maintenance procedure. His improvised repair uses a salvaged bracket instead of the approved part.

The supervisor challenges him:

> **SUPERVISOR:** That's not the approved repair.  
> **ALEX:** Then don't approve it.  
> **ALEX:** Just notice it works.

The pump continues functioning, but Alex's method places him in conflict with Alpha's approved procedures and the supervisor's authority. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 2, Panel 2 — Debt transfer

At the end-of-shift checkpoint, Alex scans his hand while workers queue. The checkpoint uses the same white-and-amber node architecture seen throughout the infrastructure spine.

The display announces:

> **TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
> **ALEX PHANTUM**  
> **AGE VERIFIED: EIGHTEEN**  
> **PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
> **GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
> **DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
> **EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
> **PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
> **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

Alex's adulthood immediately produces a burden rather than independence. The guard says:

> **GUARD:** Happy birthday, Phantum.  
> **ALEX:** I've had better surprises.

The page's caption defines the social rule: **“Eighteen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.”** `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

This is the first major change in Alex's situation: the system formally transfers Adrian's household liability to him, making the missing father an immediate material problem rather than only a family wound. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 2, Panel 3 — The Last Chance and the loan

On the morning street, Alex passes **THE LAST CHANCE — PAWN & LOANS**, whose window advertises **CASH TODAY · EASY TERMS**. Next door is **NEXT LEVEL GAMES**.

Danny, a lanky pump-hall worker of about twenty, comes out of the game shop carrying a used console and a loan slip. The slip reads:

> **ADVANCE 120 CR · REPAY 10 CR / WEEK · 31 WEEKS**

Danny treats the repayment as insignificant:

> **DANNY:** Alex! Look what I got! Ten a week. That's nothing!

The shopkeeper points toward the torn default notice bearing Adrian's face:

> **SHOPKEEPER:** Care to see if your luck runs better than his?  
> **SHOPKEEPER:** Your father always believed the next loan would change everything.

A public water queue is visible across the street, with a display reading **RATION: 4 L**. Alex picks up Danny's fallen slip and pockets it without answering. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The loan slip becomes a physical object Alex carries into the chapter's later discovery. It also supplies the first concrete example of the difference between an immediate price and a total cost.

#### Page 2, Panel 4 — The supervisor chooses not to punish him

After Alex leaves, the pump hall supervisor sees the terminal flag:

> **UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR — REVIEW REQUIRED**

The pump is still working. The supervisor selects **ROUTINE MAINTENANCE** instead. A green check appears. The scene confirms that Alex's unauthorized method works, while also showing a small act of discretion from a worker inside the system. A balanced water-drop symbol is scratched into an old valve plate beneath the pump. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 2, Panel 5 — The wish to leave

At sunrise on the rim of Keel's exhausted pit, Alex stands looking toward Oasis. The sealed mine entrance is present but unnoticed in the foreground. The chapter establishes the personal desire beneath his frustration:

> **Caption:** Everyone in Keel knew where they had been born. / Few believed they could choose where they belonged.  
> **ALEX:** One day... I'm leaving.

Alex's desire to escape Keel is established before the hidden inheritance gives him a different route out of his assigned life. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 2: Two different dreams

#### Page 3, Panel 1 — The overlook

Later that afternoon, Alex sits at the rim railing where he and Ada regularly meet. Keel lies in shadow below; Halyard's terraces catch gold, and Oasis remains in sunlight. Beside them is a **WATER WARS MEMORIAL** plaque and a dry drinking fountain.

The sealed mine entrance is faintly visible at the far left. The location connects Alex's ordinary private life, the history of the Water Wars and the concealed route that will later become his first objective. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 3, Panels 2–3 — Ada gives him water

Ada approaches with two battered metal mugs. She has saved some of her ration to give Alex a birthday drink.

> **ADA:** Thought you might need this.  
> **ALEX:** The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.  
> **ALEX:** Where'd you get enough water for two?  
> **ADA:** Saved some of my ration.  
> **ALEX:** Ada, you didn't have to.  
> **ADA:** Not everything has a price, Alex.

Ada's action introduces a counterexample to the chapter's debt logic: she gives up part of her own ration without converting the act into a transaction. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 3, Panels 4–5 — The birthday and the silence

Ada congratulates Alex on becoming eighteen. Alex answers:

> **ADA:** Eighteen. Congratulations. You're officially old.  
> **ALEX:** I'm officially broke.

Ada has heard about the debt transfer. Alex says everyone has. The conversation moves from joking to the social consequences of his birthday. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 3, Panel 6 — Their opposed hopes

Alex looks toward Oasis while Ada looks down toward Keel.

> **ALEX:** Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.  
> **ADA:** I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.  
> **ALEX:** Because nobody leaves.  
> **ADA:** That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.

Alex's immediate dream is escape. Ada's is transformation. Their disagreement gives the chapter a relational and political counterpoint: Alex imagines freedom as leaving, while Ada imagines freedom as changing the place people have abandoned. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 3: What people told him

#### Page 4, Panels 1–2 — Grandmother and Adrian

Alex takes the worn photograph from his jacket pocket. It shows his grandmother and Adrian; Adrian's face is folded and creased. Ada asks about his grandmother.

Alex explains that some days his grandmother knows him and other days she calls him Adrian. The painful part is not simply that she forgets, but that she invents stories about Adrian. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 4, Panels 3–4 — Memory versus reputation

Ada asks what the stories are. Alex says his grandmother claims Adrian was not wasting everything, that he was working. When Ada asks what Alex actually remembers, he answers:

> **ALEX:** Nothing. I was two.

This establishes that Alex's view of Adrian is inherited almost entirely from other people's accounts. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 4, Panel 5 — The first doubt

Ada challenges the certainty of Keel's story:

> **ADA:** So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?  
> **ALEX:** Maybe…Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.

Alex does not yet accept a new version of Adrian. The question changes the status of the public story from settled fact to possible theory, without proving it false. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 4, Panel 6 — Ada's private conviction

As they walk home, Ada looks back toward Oasis and says nearly to herself:

> **ADA:** He wouldn't just leave.

This is Ada's belief, not yet evidence. It keeps the question of Adrian open as Alex returns home. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 4: The old book

#### Page 5, Panels 1–3 — Alex returns home

Alex enters the modest Keel apartment. The room contains repaired furniture, handmade quilts, books, family photographs, potted plants in recycled containers and two water jugs marked with ration scratches. His grandmother sits folding and refolding a small cloth.

She initially calls him Adrian. Alex corrects her:

> **GRANDMOTHER:** Adrian?  
> **ALEX:** No, Grandma. I'm Alex.

He kneels beside her and tells her that he is eighteen. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 5, Panel 4 — The clear moment

For one beat, the grandmother's confusion clears. She studies Alex carefully and says:

> **GRANDMOTHER:** Eighteen. Oh. Then there's something your father wanted you to have.

This is the hinge from family memory to deliberate inheritance. Her statement indicates that Adrian prepared something for Alex to receive at eighteen. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 5, Panels 5–6 — The blue book

She points to a worn, hand-bound blue book on the top shelf. A faint balanced water-drop mark is visible on its spine.

> **GRANDMOTHER:** Get me the blue book, would you? Top shelf.  
> **GRANDMOTHER:** Your father never let anyone else touch it.  
> **ALEX:** He had opinions about everything.  
> **GRANDMOTHER:** Especially the things worth protecting.

Alex reaches for the book. The book is not merely a keepsake: it activates a hidden mechanism. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 5: The hidden workshop

#### Page 6, Panels 1–3 — The shelf opens

When Alex pulls the book, it tips like a lever. A **CLICK** is followed by a **RUMBLE**. The bookshelf swings outward like a door, revealing a black rectangle behind it. Household light spills into a concealed room.

Alex asks:

> **ALEX:** ...Grandma?

The hidden workshop is a physical contradiction of the public story that Adrian simply threw everything away. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 6, Panel 4 — Adrian's instruction

The grandmother remains calm:

> **GRANDMOTHER:** He said you'd be ready at eighteen.  
> **ALEX:** When did he say that?  
> **GRANDMOTHER:** Before he went to work.

The line “before he went to work” preserves the grandmother's account of Adrian as someone engaged in work, though it does not yet explain what that work was or where he went. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 6, Panel 5 — Alex crosses the threshold

Alex steps over the boundary between the warm apartment and the dark workshop. This is his first active movement into Adrian's hidden legacy. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 6: What Adrian left

#### Page 7, Panel 1 — The preserved workshop

The workshop contains blueprints, mechanical models, water-system diagrams, prototype devices, faded or turned photographs, pre-Alpha pipes, analog gauges and a chalkboard covered in dense equations. A distinctive looping equation appears on the board.

At the organized workbench is a seated robot shape beneath a dust sheet, about waist-high to Alex, with a faint amber pulse beneath the cloth. Beside it is an open box containing Adrian's watch and a paper tag in Adrian's handwriting:

> **FOR ALEX. 18.**

The workshop shows that Adrian had a sustained project involving machinery and water systems, and that he prepared a specific inheritance for Alex. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 7, Panels 2–3 — The watch and the equation

Alex takes the watch and turns it over. The looping equation is engraved on the steel caseback and matches the equation on the chalkboard. The engraving is initially visible because Alex is examining the back of the watch; it is not a display-face message. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The match links the watch to Adrian's workshop and establishes the equation as a deliberate part of Adrian's work. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 7, Panel 4 — The waiting machine

Alex reaches toward the dust-covered robot but does not touch it. The robot remains an unanswered presence until the watch is worn. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 7: The awakening

#### Page 8, Panel 1 — Bi11bot revealed

Alex pulls away the dust sheet. Bi11bot is revealed as a rugged, weathered repair robot with a round segmented industrial-blue head, chipped mustard-yellow paint, amber eyes, a chest plate marked **Bi11**, and an old rust-red scarf. His eyes are dull at first, and an amber pulse remains in his chest. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 8, Panel 2 — Alex tests the machine

Alex places his palm flat against Bi11bot's chest and feels for vibration in the same manner he tests a pump. The robot's amber pulse answers slowly and warmly. This is an important continuity of Alex's practical knowledge: he approaches the unknown through touch and mechanical diagnosis rather than ceremony. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 8, Panel 3 — The watch wakes

Alex buckles the watch onto his left wrist. This is the first time he wears it. The face activates with a **LOADING** screen: the looping equation draws itself in thin amber lines while a ring fills around the face. The robot's chest pulse quickens in time with it.

> **SFX:** TICK... TICK... TICK

After this first activation, the watch's ordinary modes are CLOCK and COST. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 8, Panel 4 — Bi11bot wakes

When the loading ring closes, Bi11bot's eyes light warm amber. Dust rises from his shoulders. His blue and mustard-yellow head fills the near space while Alex steps back.

> **SFX:** WHIRRR... (rising)

The watch does not speak or judge; it activates the robot. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 8, Panel 5 — First words and first signal

Bi11bot stretches his repair arms. His jointed tool-like limbs creak, and his eye rings rotate with a click. An old white-and-amber Alpha node on the workshop wall blinks once after years of darkness.

> **BI11BOT:** ...Well. That took longer than expected.

Bi11bot's awakening is both a personal discovery and an institutional event: his old Alpha registration can now become visible to the network. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 8: The robot who waited

#### Page 9, Panels 1–2 — Alex's reaction and Bi11bot's assessment

Alex jumps back and knocks over a stool.

> **ALEX:** Okay. That's... new.

Bi11bot focuses on him. His eye rings click and his head tilts, giving him a patient, quietly amused expression rather than a threatening one. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 9, Panel 3 — Adrian's mistakes

Bi11bot tells Alex:

> **BI11BOT:** You're taller than your father predicted.  
> **ALEX:** Everyone in Keel claims they knew my father.  
> **BI11BOT:** Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.

This changes Bi11bot's function from a machine Alex has discovered into a witness to Adrian's history. It also complicates the idea that Bi11bot will simply defend or glorify Adrian. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 9, Panel 4 — “Right on time”

Bi11bot notices the watch.

> **BI11BOT:** Right on time.  
> **ALEX:** You know about the watch?

The watch is confirmed as part of Adrian's intended plan rather than an unrelated object in the workshop. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 9, Panel 5 — The watch's purpose

Alex removes the watch and shows Bi11bot the engraved caseback. Bi11bot does not touch it.

> **BI11BOT:** He wanted you to know the true cost of your decisions.

The line gives the watch a narrative purpose: it is not simply a clue or keepsake, but a tool for making hidden financial consequences visible. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 9, Panel 6 — Adrian's message

Bi11bot unfolds a shoulder projector and aims soft-blue light at Alex.

> **BI11BOT:** Now. Shall we say hello to your father?

Alex's investigation changes from examining Adrian's objects to receiving Adrian's own recorded words. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 9: The message

#### Page 10, Panel 1 — Adrian appears

Adrian's hologram looks directly at Alex:

> **ADRIAN:** Hello, Alex. If you're seeing this... then you found the workshop.

This is the first clear view of Adrian in the book. Alex now encounters the father he remembers only through a photograph and other people's stories. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 10, Panel 2 — Alex sees him

Alex watches without blinking. The direct image of Adrian makes the absence personal and immediate rather than abstract. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 10, Panel 3 — The grandmother hears him

Adrian jokes:

> **ADRIAN:** Which means your grandmother still refuses to throw anything away.  
> **GRANDMOTHER:** I heard that.

The grandmother stands in the open doorway, smiling with wet eyes. Her connection to the message confirms that this moment was prepared for Alex but also matters to her. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 10, Panel 4 — Adrian apologizes

Adrian becomes serious:

> **ADRIAN:** If you're watching this, I wasn't there when you needed me. I'm sorry. To you, and to Mom.  
> **ADRIAN:** I got things wrong, Alex. More than once. But every failure taught me something.

Adrian admits absence and repeated failure. The message does not erase Alex's resentment or establish Adrian as blameless. It does, however, contradict the simplicity of the public reputation: Adrian's own account is that he was working through failures rather than merely wasting everything. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 10, Panel 5 — The invitation and warning

Adrian says:

> **ADRIAN:** You don't have to finish what I started. You may find a better way.  
> **ADRIAN:** Bi11bot has a map. What you do with it is up to you. And whatever he tells you... question everything. Especially me.

The message gives Alex agency rather than commanding him to complete Adrian's work. It also warns him not to treat either Adrian or Bi11bot as unquestionable authority. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 10, Panel 6 — Alex confronts the photograph

After the hologram disappears, Alex sits in the afterglow and unfolds the creased photograph. Adrian's face in the photograph is the same face Alex has just seen. The story's inherited image of Adrian is now joined to direct evidence of Adrian's voice and apology, but the central mystery remains unresolved. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Sequence 10: The first waypoint

#### Page 11, Panel 1 — The legacy map

Bi11bot projects a cross-section map of the mountain. It marks three artifacts in three locations:

1. One deep beneath Keel's pit, inside the old mine.
2. One inside a Halyard fabrication tower.
3. One high in Oasis.

Only the Keel marker glows bright amber. The other two are dim outlines.

Bi11bot names the trail:

> **BI11BOT:** Your father left a trail. Three artifacts, in three places.  
> **BI11BOT:** He called it the Legacy Protocol.

The story expands from a family mystery into a multi-tiered investigation. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 2 — The Measure

The bright Keel marker is labelled:

> **THE MEASURE**

Bi11bot explains:

> **BI11BOT:** The first is called the Measure. It's somewhere inside the old mine.

The first destination is beneath the sealed entrance that has appeared in the chapter since the opening establishing sequence. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 3 — The risk of entering

Alex points out:

> **ALEX:** That mine's been sealed since before I was born.  
> **BI11BOT:** Your father knew.  
> **ALEX:** Anyone caught past that seal loses their work card.  
> **BI11BOT:** Then we don't get caught.

The choice now carries a concrete institutional consequence. Entering the mine threatens Alex's work card, and his work card carries his identity, wages, debt and travel status. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 4 — Evidence, not an answer

Alex asks whether finding the Measure will tell him what happened to Adrian.

> **ALEX:** And finding it tells me what happened to him?  
> **BI11BOT:** It tells us where he wanted you to begin. The rest, we'll have to discover.

The Measure is not promised as a complete explanation. The immediate objective is therefore discovery rather than closure. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 5 — The COST screen

Alex takes Danny's loan slip from his pocket. When it comes close to his wrist, the watch changes from CLOCK to COST. The face displays:

> **310 CR**

A small cost icon appears above the number, with a thin bar beneath it. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Bi11bot calculates the displayed total:

> **BI11BOT:** The console cost him 120. Ten a week for thirty-one weeks is 310.  
> **ALEX:** So he's an idiot.

The watch has measured the loan's total repayment, not merely the amount Danny received. This is the first on-page appearance of COST. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 6 — The principle and the conflict with the public story

Bi11bot corrects Alex:

> **BI11BOT:** No. When tomorrow looks bad, today looks bigger.  
> **BI11BOT:** Your father built that so people could see the 310 before they sign. Not after.  
> **ALEX:** Everyone says he pawned and borrowed it all away.  
> **BI11BOT:** That's a theory. The mine is evidence.

Alex now has a tool that embodies an alternative to Keel's immediate-price economy. He also has a concrete reason to test Bi11bot's claim: the sealed mine may provide evidence about Adrian's actual work. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 11, Panel 7 — Alex chooses the mine

Alex reaches through the projected map and presses his hand against the bright Measure marker.

> **ALEX:** Then we start here.

At the same moment, the Alpha node on the workshop wall glows steadily. An amber pulse travels through the pipes and out of the room. Because Bi11bot is awake and unfolded, he checks in with Alpha's network after sixteen years of silence. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex's choice causes the story's external pursuit to begin. He chooses evidence over safety, and the system notices the device that can lead him to it.

### Sequence 11: Track

#### Page 12, Panel 1 — The check-in crosses the mountain

A vertical cutaway follows the amber pulse through the infrastructure spine. It passes control nodes in Keel, the Keel–Halyard permission gate, Halyard, the cloud layer and Oasis. The pulse is the only warm color moving through the separate city palettes.

The visual consequence of Bi11bot's awakening is made systemic: a private discovery in Keel propagates through Alpha's entire network. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 12, Panel 2 — The Oasis control room

In a centered, orderly Oasis control room, a lone seated woman faces a wall-sized live display of the mountain. Thousands of white points glow. One amber point pulses in Keel. A glass of clean water sits aligned with the desk edge.

The scene shifts the reader's knowledge beyond Alex's. Alex does not know that the network has detected him; the reader now does. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 12, Panel 3 — The alert

A monitor displays:

> **LEGACY DEVICE ONLINE**  
> **UNIT B-11 · ALPHA FIELD UNIT, FIRST GENERATION**  
> **REGISTERED TO: A. PHANTUM — RECORD CLOSED**  
> **LAST CHECK-IN: 16 YEARS AGO**  
> **HOLDS: MEASURE LOCATION**  
> **[ REPORT ] [ TRACK ]**

The woman selects **TRACK**. The alert identifies Bi11bot as a first-generation Alpha field unit, records that the device was registered to A. Phantum, and confirms that it holds the Measure's location. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 12, Panel 4 — Order

The woman nudges the glass until it aligns perfectly with the desk edge and the monitor's frame. This behavior establishes her controlled, exacting manner through action rather than explanation. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

#### Page 12, Panel 5 — The pursuit begins

The wall display shows the amber point in Keel. A cold-white ring begins closing around it. The monitor reads:

> **SIGNAL INTERMITTENT · ACQUIRING**

The woman leans forward. The amber point remains the only warm light in the room. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

An older Alex provides the retrospective caption:

> **I told myself I was only checking. Most of the true things in my life started that way.**

The chapter ends on **END OF CHAPTER ONE**. The immediate question has changed from whether Alex will leave Keel to whether he can reach the Measure without being caught by Alpha. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Character Movement Through the Chapter

### Alex Phantum

Alex begins as a self-reliant Keel pump worker who wants to leave but believes the system and his inherited debt make that nearly impossible. His public adulthood is defined by a debt transfer and by the reputation of a father he barely remembers.

He moves from accepting Adrian's reputation as a painful possibility to treating it as an unproven theory. The workshop, watch, Bi11bot and message do not absolve Adrian; they provide evidence that the public account is incomplete.

Alex's decisive movement is from wishing to leave to choosing an investigation. He accepts a concrete risk—entering a sealed mine and potentially losing his work card—in order to find evidence. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Ada

Ada begins as Alex's intimate connection to ordinary Keel life and as someone who saved water for him. She challenges the idea that everything has a price and challenges Alex's inherited certainty about Adrian. Her belief that Adrian “wouldn't just leave” remains unproven, but it helps open Alex to questioning the official story. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Grandmother

Grandmother's memory is unreliable in ordinary conversation, but she has preserved Adrian's hidden inheritance and recognizes the significance of Alex turning eighteen. Her moment of clarity initiates the central discovery. She does not explain the full legacy; she directs Alex to the object that opens it. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Bi11bot

Bi11bot begins as a dormant robot left in Adrian's workshop. After Alex wears the watch, he becomes an active companion, witness and guide. He knows Adrian's mistakes rather than presenting him as flawless, asks Alex to question assumptions and points him toward the Measure.

His awakening also makes the story visible to Alpha. He is therefore both Alex's guide and the source of the institutional threat. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

Adrian begins as a public reputation: a man said to have pawned and borrowed everything away, then disappeared. The workshop and holographic message reveal him as a flawed inventor who prepared a deliberate inheritance and left a trail. He admits failure and absence, but asks Alex not to obey him blindly.

His actual fate remains unknown at the end of the chapter. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### The Oasis watcher

The watcher is introduced only through controlled actions and the Alpha monitor. She receives the legacy-device alert and selects TRACK. Her identity, role and relationship to the Charter Houses or Alpha are not established in the chapter. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Stakes

### Personal stakes

- Alex inherits **418,607 credits** and a projected **28.7 years** of repayment. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- His travel status is restricted, reinforcing that leaving Keel is not simply a matter of desire. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Finding the Measure may reveal what Adrian was doing, but may also expose Alex to the same systems and consequences that shaped Adrian's disappearance. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Alex risks losing his work card if he is caught beyond the mine seal. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Relational stakes

- Alex resents Adrian and half believes the story that Adrian abandoned everything. `(rules/characters.md)`
- Grandmother's memory is deteriorating, making the moment of intentional guidance urgent and fragile. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Ada offers Alex care while challenging his resignation, but their different visions—leaving Keel versus changing it—remain unresolved. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- Adrian's message offers apology and agency without repairing the damage caused by his absence. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Institutional stakes

- Alpha registers machines that reconnect after long periods offline. Bi11bot's activation creates a trace. `(input/world.md; rules/characters.md)`
- The old mine is sealed, and entering it is forbidden. `(input/world.md)`
- Alpha's alert identifies B-11 as holding the Measure's location and initiates tracking. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Wider stakes

- The Legacy Protocol spans Keel, Halyard and Oasis, linking the three cities' unequal systems through Adrian's artifacts. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- The story's questions about water, ownership, debt and control are positioned to extend beyond Alex's family into the organization of the entire mountain basin. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Ending

Chapter One ends with Alpha tracking Bi11bot's intermittent signal from Oasis. Alex and Bi11bot have chosen the sealed mine as their first destination, but they have not yet entered it or found the Measure.

Adrian's fate remains unknown. The public story about his disappearance has been challenged but not disproved. The Legacy Protocol has been introduced, the watch's COST function has been demonstrated, and the investigation has become visible to Alpha. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md; rules/characters.md)`

The final dramatic position is therefore:

- **Alex knows:** Adrian left a hidden workshop, Bi11bot, a watch, a map and a first destination.
- **Alex believes or suspects:** The public account of Adrian may be wrong; the mine may contain evidence.
- **The reader knows:** Bi11bot's activation has alerted Alpha and a watcher is tracking the signal.
- **Alpha knows:** B-11 is online and holds the Measure's location.
- **Still unknown:** What the Measure is, what Adrian was doing, what happened to Adrian, and whether Alex can reach the mine before he is found. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

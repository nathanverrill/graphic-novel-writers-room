# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

Chapter 1 is exactly 12 pages and is edited from the showrunner's draft rather than rewritten. The characters must be carried into the book with the rules in `rules/characters.md` taking precedence wherever the draft differs. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The chapter establishes Alex's eighteenth birthday, his inherited debt, his encounter with Adrian's hidden workshop and Bi11bot, and his first choice to investigate the Measure. Character material should preserve the specific gestures, objects, relationships and lines that make those changes legible. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Role:** Eighteen-year-old Keel worker, pump repairer and central character. He is an adult under Keel's system, mechanically gifted, lean and self-reliant. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Home:** Keel. He lives with his grandmother in a modest apartment. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** Alex has brown skin, short dense black curls, dark eyes, scarred work-rough hands and a lean build. He wears a dark work shirt with rolled sleeves over a black tee, rust-brown cargo trousers with patches and heavy scuffed boots. He carries a worn canvas backpack, about 40 cm tall, slung over one shoulder by one of its two straps. The strap runs straight down over that shoulder and never crosses his chest. The backpack is large enough to carry Bi11bot folded. `(rules/characters.md)`

His Keel work card, containing his ID, wages, debt and travel status, is clipped to his belt. A creased photograph of Adrian and Alex's grandmother stays in his jacket pocket. By default he has nothing else on his wrists or in his hands: no wristband, coin or chip. `(rules/characters.md)`

Before his eighteenth birthday, Alex has no watch. On the birthday, he finds Adrian's gift in the hidden workshop: a worn steel round case on a faded olive canvas strap, slightly too large for him, with a full glass display face and no hands. A paper tag reads **FOR ALEX. 18.** He wears it on his left wrist from then on. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The watch has a CLOCK screen, normally showing the time in large plain white digits, and a COST screen that appears when a choice with a price is in front of him. COST fills the face with a large amber-red number in credits, with a small cost icon above and a thin bar beneath. The screen never judges or speaks, and never displays words such as GOOD or BAD. `(rules/characters.md)`

The watch's cost reading measures what a choice really costs in credits over time: a loan's total repayment, a fee or the interest added by a debt. It reads posted prices and terms on Keel's node network. It cannot measure sleep, time or risk; Alex must weigh those himself. `(rules/characters.md)`

The looping equation is engraved on the back of the watch only, against Alex's skin. Nobody sees it, including Alex, unless he removes the watch and turns it over. It does not appear on the face or strap except during the first LOADING screen. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Alex speaks plainly and dryly. He answers pressure with understatement, short deflections and occasional sarcasm. He does not present himself as defeated, even when the facts are crushing him.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “Then don't approve it. Just notice it works.”
- “I've had better surprises.”
- “The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.”
- “Where'd you get enough water for two?”
- “Ada, you didn't have to.”
- “I'm officially broke.”
- “Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...”
- “The hardest part isn't that she forgets. It's that she makes up crazy stories about him.”
- “Nothing. I was two.”
- “Maybe…Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.”
- “Okay. That's... new.”
- “Everyone in Keel claims they knew my father.”
- “You know about the watch?”
- “That mine's been sealed since before I was born.”
- “Anyone caught past that seal loses their work card.”
- “And finding it tells me what happened to him?”
- “It's reading Danny's loan slip. 310.”
- “So he's an idiot.”
- “Everyone says he pawned and borrowed it all away.”
- “Then we start here.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who he is:** Alex fixes pumps by feel, placing his palm flat on the housing rather than following the manual. At work he uses a salvaged bracket in place of the approved part and trusts the repair because it works. He is mechanically observant, practical and willing to act without institutional permission. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He wants to leave Keel and reach a future he can choose. At the rim he looks toward Oasis and says, “One day... I'm leaving.” He believes people remain trapped because nobody leaves, but Ada challenges that belief by arguing that people have stopped trying to make Keel worth staying in. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex begins with a strong desire for private escape rather than collective repair. His debt transfer makes that desire materially harder: on his eighteenth birthday, Keel assigns him a parental household liability of **418,607 credits**, with **40 credits earned that day**, a projected clearance time of **28.7 years**, and **TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He resents Adrian and partly believes Keel's story that his father threw everything away. He is also unsettled by the possibility that the story is false. He has no direct memory of Adrian because Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex values competence, evidence and things that work. He is initially suspicious of inherited explanations and reputation, but he is willing to test a claim when given physical evidence. Adrian's message tells him to question everything, especially Adrian, and Alex responds by choosing the mine as a place to begin rather than accepting either the public story or a romantic version of his father. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian Phantum, father:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Keel says Adrian threw everything away, and Alex half believes that account while resenting him for leaving. Alex remembers nothing directly. The photograph in Alex's pocket and Adrian's recorded message make the absent father newly immediate. Adrian left the watch, the hidden workshop, Bi11bot and a map to the three artifacts for Alex's eighteenth birthday. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Grandmother:** Alex lives with and cares about his grandmother, who is losing her memory. She sometimes calls him Adrian, and sometimes tells stories about Adrian that conflict with the public account. Alex is gentle and patient with her even when the confusion hurts him. She has kept Adrian's hidden workshop and waits until Alex turns eighteen to reveal it. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Ada:** Ada is Alex's close companion and appears to know him well. Their dynamic includes easy teasing, physical closeness and a willingness to sit in silence. She saves part of her ration to bring him water, insisting, “Not everything has a price, Alex.” She listens when he speaks about his grandmother and father, questions the story he has inherited and articulates a more collective hope for Keel. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Alex first reacts to Bi11bot with caution and awe, then questions him directly. Bi11bot knows Adrian's plans and can interpret the watch, making him both a guide to Alex's father and a source Alex is warned not to trust unquestioningly. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Danny:** Danny is a fellow pump-hall worker. Alex recognizes the cost of Danny's loan and initially calls him an idiot, but the watch and Bi11bot complicate that judgment by framing the loan as a choice made under pressure. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** Alex tests machines by touch, keeping his palm flat against a housing and reading vibration rather than relying on a procedure or gauge. He clenches his fists when Adrian and the debt are discussed, then relaxes around Ada. When the watch becomes relevant, he pulls his sleeve over it, removes it to inspect the engraved back and closes his hand around it protectively. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

At the overlook he eats a simple packed lunch, sits on the railing and looks toward Oasis. When Ada brings him water, he stops with the mug halfway to his mouth before asking how she paid for it. He picks up Danny's loan slip after it flutters to the ground and pockets it without thinking. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

When Bi11bot awakens, Alex jumps backward, knocks over a stool and raises his hands. In the presence of Adrian's hologram, his defensive armor briefly disappears: his jaw sets, his eyes brighten and he does not blink. After the message, he unfolds the creased photograph so Adrian's face emerges from the fold. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Alex's mother left after Adrian lost everything; nothing more is known about her. Alex was raised with his grandmother, whose memory is now failing. `(rules/characters.md)`

On his eighteenth birthday, Keel transfers the parental household liability to Alex. The transfer identifies Adrian as guarantor, lists his whereabouts as unrecorded, restricts Alex's travel and gives Alex a debt balance of **418,607 credits**. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

Alex has worked repairing Keel's aging pumps. His ability is recognized in practice but not necessarily by the system: a supervisor initially flags his unauthorized repair, then changes the record to routine maintenance after seeing that the pump works. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** On the morning of his eighteenth birthday, Alex repairs a pump by feel with a salvaged bracket. At the checkpoint, the debt transfer publicly changes his adult status and financial future. He later meets Ada at the rim, where their opposing views of Oasis and Keel expose his desire to escape. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

At home, Alex follows his grandmother's direction to the blue book on the top shelf. Removing it activates the hidden bookshelf door and reveals Adrian's workshop. Alex crosses from the warm household light into the dark workshop, finds the watch and the dust-covered robot, and tests the robot's chest with his palm as he would test a pump. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

When he buckles on the watch, its LOADING screen draws the looping equation in amber and wakes Bi11bot. Alex hears Adrian's message, learns that Bi11bot has a map to three artifacts and sees the first marker, the Measure, beneath Keel's sealed mine. The watch reads **310 CR** from Danny's loan slip: 120 credits advanced, 10 credits per week for 31 weeks. Alex chooses to begin with the mine despite the work-card penalty for being caught. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Alex begins Chapter 1 wanting to leave Keel and believing that escape is the clearest answer to being trapped there. His inherited debt makes that escape seem nearly impossible. He then moves from resentment and hearsay about Adrian toward a willingness to investigate evidence, while retaining his skepticism. By the end of the chapter, he has accepted a dangerous first direction—not because he has forgiven Adrian or accepted Adrian's version of events, but because “the mine is evidence.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Bi11bot

**Role:** B-11, a first-generation Alpha field unit and Adrian's repair robot and companion. He carries the only map to the three artifacts, beginning with the Measure. Anyone seeking the Measure must find Bi11bot first. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Look:** Bi11bot is a rugged, weathered, companion-sized repair robot. Standing, the top of his head is level with Alex's belt, approximately 75 cm / 2.5 ft. He is never as tall as Alex and never small enough to hold in one hand. `(rules/characters.md)`

He has an oversized round head made of segmented faded industrial-blue panels with a mustard-yellow stripe, two large round amber eyes in heavy metal rings, round ear assemblies, rounded shoulder plates and a chest plate marked **Bi11** in white. His compact legs have broad feet. His paint is chipped blue and mustard yellow, with rust, rivets and scratches. He wears an old rust-red scarf at his neck. `(rules/characters.md)`

His arms are slim, jointed machinist's tools with exposed pistons and cables, many small joints and long precise fingers ending in tool tips including a hex driver, a small clamp and a probe. His arms are not bulky or muscular. Wrenches, pliers, a coil of wire, pipe fittings and a small gauge are clipped to his back, hips and shoulder plates. `(rules/characters.md)`

His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses. He has no mouth. Warmth comes through soft, slightly narrowed eyes, a head tilt and an open, gentle posture. He reads as a patient, helpful, quietly amused old man who knows how everything works. He is never menacing: no red or harsh eye light, looming low angles or face lost in shadow. `(rules/characters.md)`

**Voice:** Bi11bot speaks in short, plain sentences. He asks more than he explains and never lectures. His manner is patient, dry and quietly amused.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “...Well. That took longer than expected.”
- “You're taller than your father predicted.”
- “Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.”
- “Right on time.”
- “He wanted you to know the true cost of your decisions.”
- “Now. Shall we say hello to your father?”
- “Your father left a trail. Three artifacts, in three places.”
- “He called it the Legacy Protocol.”
- “Your father knew.”
- “Then we don't get caught.”
- “It tells us where he wanted you to begin. The rest, we'll have to discover.”
- “No. When tomorrow looks bad, today looks bigger.”
- “Your father built that so people could see the 310 before they sign. Not after.”
- “That's a theory. The mine is evidence.” `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who he is:** Bi11bot is a repair specialist and a keeper of Adrian's unfinished trail. He is designed and characterized as patient, helpful and quietly amused rather than threatening. His knowledge of Adrian is partial and deliberately framed as fallible: he identifies Adrian's mistakes and tells Alex to question everything, especially Adrian. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He understands the difference between a posted price and its total cost over time. He explains Danny's loan without simply condemning Danny, saying, “When tomorrow looks bad, today looks bigger.” He serves as a practical counterweight to Alex's immediate judgments. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Adrian:** Adrian built Bi11bot and kept him when he left. Bi11bot knew Adrian's mistakes and carries Adrian's map to the three artifacts. He is both Adrian's surviving companion and the mechanism by which Adrian's instructions reach Alex. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Alex:** Bi11bot was left for Alex to find at eighteen. He recognizes Alex through Adrian's expectations, identifies the watch's purpose and guides Alex toward the Measure, while refusing to claim that Adrian's account is final. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Alpha:** As a registered first-generation Alpha field unit, Bi11bot checks in with Alpha's network when awake and unfolded. His first check-in in sixteen years exposes his presence to a watcher in Oasis. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses. He tilts his head while considering Alex or deciding how much to say. His eyes soften and narrow as if smiling. His tools remain clipped to his body, and when he folds they wrap around the outside of the bundle. `(rules/characters.md)`

He unfolds in three clean, warm steps: the plates slide apart, the limbs telescope out and the head panels open as the eyes light. The motion is graceful and slightly stiff, never spidery or insect-like. When carried in a bag he is folded; he never stands or sits upright inside one. `(rules/characters.md)`

He folds into his hollow head: his limbs telescope in like a camera lens, his body slides inside the head shell and the head panels close like an iris over his eyes. Folded, he is a 25 cm round bundle of tools and scrap metal that fits in Alex's backpack and reads as unregistered scrap. `(rules/characters.md)`

**History:** Bi11bot is B-11, a first-generation Alpha field unit built and kept by Adrian. Alpha's records lost him sixteen years ago. He has been dormant in Adrian's hidden workshop until Alex buckles on the watch at eighteen. `(rules/characters.md)`

When unfolded and awake, he checks in with Alpha's network from time to time, like an old company laptop reconnecting to its office network. The check-in can be traced. When folded, his head shell blocks every signal like a metal box, making him untraceable. `(rules/characters.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Bi11bot awakens when Alex buckles on Adrian's watch. His eyes ignite warm amber as the loading ring closes, and he stretches stiff mechanical arms while dust falls from him. He focuses on Alex with a clicking eye ring and immediately begins speaking in a familiar, understated manner. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He identifies the watch as Adrian's instrument for showing “the true cost of your decisions,” projects Adrian's message and displays a holographic map of three artifacts. He calls the first artifact the Measure and points to its location beneath Keel's sealed mine. He explains the watch's **310 CR** reading and calls the mine evidence rather than theory. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Bi11bot begins as a dormant inheritance and becomes Alex's guide to the Legacy Protocol. His immediate established function is to move Alex from Adrian's message toward the Measure while preserving uncertainty about Adrian's motives and history. No endpoint beyond beginning this search is established in the supplied material. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Adrian Phantum

**Role:** Alex's missing father; former builder and owner of the hidden workshop; creator and keeper of Bi11bot; originator of the Legacy Protocol and the watch's cost-reading purpose. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** His first clear appearance is a holographic recording. He has a visible face, steady eyes and a tired half-smile. The recording is the first clear view of him in the book and visually matches the father whose face is creased into Alex's photograph. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Adrian speaks directly to Alex in a recorded message. His tone moves from a small joke to apology and then to a careful invitation. He acknowledges failure without asking Alex to complete his work.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “Hello, Alex. If you're seeing this... then you found the workshop.”
- “Which means your grandmother still refuses to throw anything away.”
- “If you're watching this, I wasn't there when you needed me. I'm sorry. To you, and to Mom.”
- “I got things wrong, Alex. More than once. But every failure taught me something.”
- “You don't have to finish what I started. You may find a better way.”
- “Bi11bot has a map. What you do with it is up to you. And whatever he tells you... question everything. Especially me.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who he is:** Adrian's public reputation in Keel is that he wasted everything, pawned and borrowed it away and disappeared. His private legacy is more complicated: he built a hidden workshop, designed the watch to reveal the total cost of financial choices, created or maintained Bi11bot and left a trail toward three artifacts. The material does not settle whether the public story is false, incomplete or partly true. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

He is characterized by a willingness to admit failure and by an explicit refusal to demand that Alex continue his project unchanged. His warning to question him makes his legacy an invitation under suspicion rather than a simple inheritance. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two, leaving Alex with no direct memories and a lasting resentment. He leaves Alex the watch and access to the workshop for his eighteenth birthday, along with a message that apologizes for his absence but does not resolve it. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Grandmother:** Adrian entrusted his hidden workshop and the blue book's mechanism to Alex's grandmother. She says he told her Alex would be ready at eighteen. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Bi11bot:** Adrian built and kept Bi11bot. Bi11bot remembers Adrian's mistakes and carries the map to the artifacts. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Keel:** Keel's public story makes Adrian a cautionary example. A torn default notice bearing his face remains on the outer wall of The Last Chance pawn and loan shop. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Alex's mother left after Adrian lost everything, though nothing more is known about her. Adrian left the watch in the hidden workshop for Alex's eighteenth birthday, tagged **FOR ALEX. 18.** He also left Bi11bot and a map to three artifacts in Keel, Halyard and Oasis, beginning with the Measure beneath Keel's old mine. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Adrian appears first through the photograph in Alex's pocket and the public default notice. His workshop is revealed when Alex removes the blue book from the top shelf at home. His recorded message activates after Bi11bot wakes, giving Alex an apology, a choice and a warning about the unreliability of every inherited account—including Adrian's own. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Adrian has no present-time arc established in the supplied material because he is absent. His established character movement is retrospective: he shifts from Keel's cautionary reputation to a more contradictory private legacy, while remaining unresolved and potentially unreliable. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Alex's Grandmother

**Role:** Alex's grandmother and guardian in Keel; keeper of Adrian's hidden workshop and the person who releases Adrian's inheritance to Alex. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** She is an elderly woman with a frail hand and a worn chair in a modest, lived-in apartment. The supplied material does not give a more specific physical description. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Her memory is unreliable, but she can become lucid and deliberate. Most of the time she refolds a small cloth and may mistake Alex for Adrian. When the subject reaches Adrian's inheritance, her gaze clears and becomes steady and studying.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “Adrian?”
- “No, Alex. I knew that.”
- “Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...” — Alex describing her experience.
- “Eighteen. Oh. Then there's something your father wanted you to have.”
- “Get me the blue book, would you? Top shelf.”
- “Your father never let anyone else touch it.”
- “He said you'd be ready at eighteen.”
- “Before he went to work.” `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** She is losing her memory, but her apparent confusion does not prevent her from preserving Adrian's instructions. She holds a private knowledge of the workshop and waits until Alex turns eighteen to reveal it. Her calmness when the bookshelf opens suggests that this secret has been deliberately kept, not accidentally forgotten. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:**

- **Alex:** Alex is gentle with her and kneels to speak at her eye level. Her confusion wounds him, but he does not punish her for it. She has raised him or lives with him and is his remaining family anchor. `(rules/characters.md) (drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`
- **Adrian:** She knew enough about Adrian's plans to preserve his book, workshop and timing. She repeats his instruction that Alex would be ready at eighteen. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** She folds and refolds a small cloth. In the first scene she is seated in a worn chair, partly turned away. When the workshop opens, she remains unbothered in the background, still folding the cloth. During Adrian's holographic message, her hands stop folding; she stands in the bookshelf doorway with one hand at her mouth, wet-eyed and smiling. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** She has kept Adrian's hidden workshop concealed behind the bookshelf in the apartment. The mechanism is activated by removing the blue, water-drop-marked book from the top shelf. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** She first mistakes Alex for Adrian. After Alex corrects her and tells her he is eighteen, she directs him to the book that opens the hidden workshop. She watches him cross the threshold and later reacts emotionally to Adrian's recorded apology. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Within the chapter, she moves from apparent confusion to a brief clear-minded act of guardianship: she recognizes Alex's eighteenth birthday as the time to hand over Adrian's secret. Her endpoint is emotional witness to Adrian's message, not a resolved account of her memory or knowledge. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Ada

**Role:** Alex's close friend and companion in Keel. Her occupation and formal affiliation are not established. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Look:** Ada is twenty, with long, wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes. She wears a dark high-collared top with a rivet-studded strap across one shoulder and a muted moss-green wrap at her neck. Her stance is steady and rooted. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Ada speaks directly and steadily. She teases Alex, but her humor gives way quickly to concern. She is comfortable letting silence sit rather than forcing him to answer.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “Thought you might need this.”
- “Not everything has a price, Alex.”
- “Eighteen. Congratulations. You're officially old.”
- “I heard about this morning.”
- “What kind of stories?”
- “What do you actually remember about him?”
- “So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?”
- “He wouldn't just leave.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who she is:** Ada notices Alex's distress and acts materially rather than offering only sympathy: she saves part of her water ration for him. She believes Keel's condition is not inevitable. Her view is that people have stopped trying to make the place worth staying in, while Alex's first instinct is that nobody leaves. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

She is steady and rooted where Alex is looking upward and imagining escape. Her position is not simply that people should accept Keel; she wonders why everyone gave up trying to change it. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Relationships:** Ada and Alex have known each other long enough to sit close, share dry jokes and allow silence between them. She brings him water at personal cost, listens to his account of his grandmother and father, and challenges the story he has accepted about Adrian. There is emotional intimacy, though the supplied material does not explicitly define the relationship as romantic. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** She approaches with two battered metal mugs of water after saving part of her ration. She holds a steady small smile when Alex asks what the water cost. When he talks about his grandmother, she begins to lift a hand toward his shoulder but stops in the air. She lets silence sit after difficult statements. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Ada has access to a ration card and spends one day's mark to save water for Alex. No further biography is established. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Ada meets Alex at the rim on his birthday. She gives him water, listens to his account of his grandmother's memory loss and asks what he actually remembers about Adrian. When Alex says he remembers nothing, she points out that the rest is what people told him and asks what if they were wrong. At the end of the exchange, she looks back toward Oasis and quietly says, “He wouldn't just leave.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Arc:** Ada's established function in Chapter 1 is to challenge Alex's inherited assumptions and his private escape logic. She begins as the person who offers him care and a competing vision of Keel; she ends with a specific suspicion that Adrian did not simply abandon his family. No further arc is established in the supplied material. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Danny

**Look:** Danny is lanky, with a buzz cut and a grin. He carries a used console under one arm and a game-shop bag after taking out a loan. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** Danny is enthusiastic and casual about borrowing. Worth-preserving lines include:

He has a visible balance of **3,200 credits** before the loan transfer, then takes an advance of **120 credits** with terms of **10 credits per week for 31 weeks**. **Relationships:** Danny is familiar enough with Alex to wave him over in public and show him the purchase. Alex's first response is to call him an idiot, but Bi11bot reframes the decision as a response to a bad tomorrow making today look bigger. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Habits and tells:** He grins, waves the loan slip and treats the weekly payment as the meaningful number. When the slip flutters from his hand to the ground, Alex picks it up. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**History:** Danny works in the pump hall and had a displayed debt balance of 3,200 credits before taking the new loan. No other history is established. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**In existing drafts:** Danny buys a used console from Next Level Games using a payday loan from The Last Chance. His loan becomes the first concrete example of what Alex's watch measures. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

### Pump-Hall Supervisor

**Role:** Alex's supervisor at a Keel pump hall. The supervisor's name, age and appearance are not established. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Voice:** The supervisor speaks in an approving-authority register and initially objects to Alex's unauthorized method.

Worth-preserving lines include:

- “That's not the approved repair.”
- “...Works.” `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

**Who they are:** The supervisor is skeptical of Alex's improvised repair but is capable of recognizing its practical success. The supervisor has the authority to flag the repair as unauthorized and to change the record. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

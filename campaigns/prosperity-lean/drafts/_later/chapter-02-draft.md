*Caption:* THE NEXT MORNING

ALEX: I didn't. I need to show you something.

**P2: The bag (medium)**  
*Image:* Alex swings the backpack off his shoulder onto the flat top of the low wall by the railing. The pack shifts on its own. Ada leans in, eyebrows up, curious and already half-smiling.

*SFX:* click... click... whirrr

**P4: Familiar (medium two-shot)**  
*Image:* Bi11bot, standing at his full height on the wall, tilts his head at Ada; his heavy eye rings click as he focuses, his eyes warm and crinkled like a kind old man's. Ada crouches to his eye level, grinning, delighted.

BI11BOT: A rather distinguished one, actually.

BI11BOT: Your father didn't work alone either. The people he trusted made all the difference.

## PAGE 2: WHAT ALPHA IS

**P1: The wars (wide)**  
*Image:* A hologram fills the workshop above the worktable. Simple fractured scenes: a reservoir dried to cracked mud, a guarded storehouse, families walking with jugs. To the right the fragments draw together into the mountain of three cities. Alex, Ada and Bi11bot are small silhouettes at the bottom.  
*Caption:* AFTER THE WATER WARS, THREE CITIES REMAINED.  
BI11BOT: After the Water Wars, the cities couldn't agree on how to share what was left.

**P2: Built for the emergency (medium)**  
*Image:* In the hologram, three groups of exhausted delegates, one from each city, sit at a single table. Above them, a small white ring with an amber point switches on for the first time.  
BI11BOT: So an emergency council of all three built Alpha, an AI to run the emergency. Water, food, power, work, travel.  
ALEX: They put an AI in charge of people's lives?

**P3: The question (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Ada and Bi11bot. Ada's eyes are on the symbol, her thumb stopped on a rivet of her strap. Bi11bot's lens clicks.  
ADA: Who gave it that authority?  
BI11BOT: People who were afraid. Reasonably so. It was meant to be temporary.

**P4: It stayed (dominant)**  
*Image:* A high angle. The delegates' table in the hologram fades away chair by chair until only the white-and-amber symbol remains, larger now, five streams of light flowing into it. The three figures are small below. The amber point is the only warm spot in the room.  
ALEX: What happened?  
BI11BOT: The emergency ended. Alpha's authority didn't.

**P5: The split (wide)**  
*Image:* The hologram divides into three levels. Oasis: full reservoirs, fountains. Halyard: busy workshops and trains. Keel: one dripping tap and a queue. Each level wears the same small node.  
BI11BOT: It isn't cruel. It keeps things stable. But nobody it decides for can vote it out.

**P6: Safer for whom (close)**  
*Image:* Alex studies Keel's level of the hologram, arms crossed, its blue-grey light on his face.  
ALEX: Stable for whom?  
BI11BOT *(small balloon, off-panel)*: Your father asked that too.

## PAGE 3: THREE SAFEGUARDS

**P1: The three (wide)**  
*Image:* Three objects rotate above Adrian's worktable in soft blue hologram, each clearly different in shape and colour. Adrian's handwritten notes orbit them in gold. Adrian himself is not in the room, only his handwriting. Bi11bot stands on the table presenting; Alex and Ada watch.  
BI11BOT: When Alpha was built, the council wrote a way to take its power back. Then everyone forgot it was there.  
BI11BOT: Your father found it. And built the three things it needs.

**P2: The Measure (triptych)**  
*Image:* Close on the Measure, turning slowly. Behind it, two gauges side by side, showing different readings.  
*Caption:* THE MEASURE  
BI11BOT: It shows you what's really happening, and what's being hidden.

**P3: The Maker's Seal (triptych)**  
*Image:* Close on the Maker's Seal. Behind it, a simple tool and the hand that made it, joined by a thin line of light.  
*Caption:* THE MAKER'S SEAL  
BI11BOT: It proves who made something, and who it belongs to.

**P4: The Covenant (triptych)**  
*Image:* Close on the Covenant. Behind it, several hands, each touching one part of it.  
*Caption:* THE COVENANT  
BI11BOT: It proves people agreed to the rules, and can hold each other to them.

**P5: Together (dominant)**  
*Image:* Top-down on the hologram. The three artifacts form a triangle around Alpha's symbol. Under the symbol, a faint old lattice, dark and dusty, with three empty sockets at the triangle's points.  
ALEX: So if we find all three, we shut Alpha down?  
BI11BOT: No. The lights stay on. The pumps keep running. Alpha stays a tool.  
BI11BOT: What ends is its right to decide for everyone.

**P6: Who decides (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Ada leaning in. Alex turns the watch on his wrist.  
ADA: And who decides instead?  
BI11BOT: People. That's what the Covenant is for.  
ALEX: And if I just carry them up the mountain?  
BI11BOT: Then you're a boy with three heavy objects. Somebody has to use them, on purpose, and answer for it.

## PAGE 4: BOTH WERE TRUE

**P1: The recording (wide)**  
*Image:* The Last Chance years ago, rust-amber lights, a queue at the loan window. At a corner table, Adrian fills a napkin with branching sums and watches the borrowers' faces, not the forms. A small stack of loan slips sits at his elbow. Behind the counter, a younger shopkeeper, the man from Chapter One's Page 2, watches Adrian.  
*Caption:* THE LAST CHANCE, YEARS BEFORE  
BI11BOT: He watched people decide with their own money on the line. When the terms are bad, why do they still sign?

**P2: The watch (strip)**  
*Image:* Alex has slipped the watch off and holds its caseback beside the hologram. The looping engraving on the back matches a looping equation floating in the air.  
ALEX: That's on the back of my watch.  
BI11BOT: That's his equation.

**P3: The missing part (dominant)**  
*Image:* A high-angle hologram of the equation over the worktable, large and beautiful, most of its terms glowing steady gold. One term at its heart stays open, a bright empty loop that won't resolve. Alpha's symbol sits outside it, looking in. The three figures are small below.  
ALEX: What's the part that won't solve?  
BI11BOT: Human choice. He spent years trying to model it. He never could.

**P4: Estimate, not decide (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Ada and Alex across the hologram, faces in gold.  
ADA: Because people do things when they can't know how they'll turn out.  
BI11BOT: Alpha can estimate what people might do. Adrian believed it should never decide what they must do.  
ALEX: And the safeguards?  
BI11BOT: They only work for people who look, choose, and live with what happens next.

**P5: The debt (close)**  
*Image:* Alex looks at the watch, then up. Bi11bot's hologram behind him shows a stack of loan papers beside a stack of patent drawings, the two piles roughly the same height.  
ALEX: So he didn't just borrow it and waste it.  
BI11BOT: Some he wasted. Most was money he borrowed to pay for his research. The work never paid him back.  
ALEX: Or someone made sure it couldn't.

**P6: Grandmother (strip)**  
*Image:* Through the open shelf, Grandmother in her chair, folding, half-listening.  
GRANDMOTHER: I told you he was working.  
BI11BOT *(small)*: She's half right. Both were true.

## PAGE 5: THE COST OF A PLAN

**P1: The table (wide)**  
*Image:* The kitchen table. Adrian's paper map of the mine is spread out. Alex, Ada and Bi11bot lean over it. Grandmother sits nearby. The workshop glows through the open shelf.  
*Caption:* LATER THAT MORNING

ADA: The old mine entrance on the rim is sealed. How do you get in?

**P2: The way in (close)**  
*Image:* Alex's finger on the paper map, tracing from the active mine, SITE 6, along a tunnel to a barred door marked in Adrian's hand: OLD WORKS.  
ALEX: Through the active mine. I work Site 6. The old works are behind it.  
BI11BOT: Getting into Site 6 is easy. Getting past it is another matter.

**P3: Ada's map (two-shot)**  
*Image:* Ada has been copying the map by hand into her own notebook. She tears the page out: the tunnels redrawn, a small inked pipe at the third junction, where Adrian's map shows only a wall.

ALEX: Which one's right?  
ADA: Find out.

**P4: What it costs (top-down)**  
*Image:* Alex, red-eyed and exhausted, empties his Keel work card onto the table as a small glowing pile: 52. Around it, simple labelled spaces: MEDICINE 30, FOOD 20, WATER 15, and, off to one side and far larger, a red tag: OVERDUE 500.  
*Work card:* DEBT 418,607 CR / OVERDUE 500 CR / TRAVEL: RESTRICTED UNTIL OVERDUE IS PAID  
ADA: That's sixty-five. You've got fifty-two.  
ALEX: The Last Chance would lend me thirteen by noon.

**P5: The true cost (close)**  
*Image:* Alex's wrist on the table: the watch has switched to its COST screen, a large amber-red **34 CR** with the coin icon. Ada leans in to read it. Through the window behind them, a Site 6 board glows: **OVERTIME SHIFT · +15 CR**.  
ADA: Thirteen now. Thirty-four by the time you've paid it back.  
ALEX: Or one more shift.  
ADA: You haven't slept.

**P6: Medicine first (close)**  
*Image:* Alex's hand slides thirty credits into the MEDICINE space. The knock of the front door: NELL, the downstairs neighbour (a broad woman in her sixties with short grey hair and a white clinic smock), at the door with Grandmother's medicine bag.  
NELL: Thirty for the week, love. Same as always.  
ALEX: Same as always.

**P7: The echo (short)**  
*Image:* Tight on Grandmother's face, eyes steady and clear. Behind her Alex buckles his backpack, Bi11bot folding himself small inside it, his body sliding up into his head shell.  
ALEX: One shift. Then we'll see.  
GRANDMOTHER: Your father said that once.

# Prosperity — Development Brief

## 1. Logline

On his eighteenth birthday, Keel mechanic Alex Phantum inherits his vanished father’s crushing debt and a hidden trail to three artifacts; with an old robot as his guide and Alpha beginning to track them, he chooses the sealed mine as his first piece of evidence.

## 2. Theme

The stated price of a choice is never its whole cost—and neither is the story powerful systems tell about the people trapped inside them.

## 3. Tone & Comps

- **Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind** — ecological pressure, tactile machinery, and compassion becoming a form of resistance.
- **The City of Ember** — civic infrastructure as mystery, with discovery emerging through maintenance and physical evidence.
- **Children of Men** — stratified control, restricted movement, and fragile hope carried through human connection.

The book is tense, tactile and emotionally restrained, with dry humor and genuine tenderness. Its politics are shown through water queues, debt displays, work cards, gates, repairs and unequal space—not speeches.

## 4. Audience & Rating

Young adult/adult crossover. **PG-13 equivalent:** peril, industrial hazards, authoritarian administration, family abandonment, implied historical violence and emotional distress. No graphic gore, sexual content or sadistic violence.

## 5. Format

- Chapter 1: **exactly 12 pages**, Pages 1–12.
- Full-color graphic novel chapter.
- Trim: **6.625" × 10.25"**.
- Page 1 is a full-page establishing image.
- Pages 2–9 and 11–12 use four-to-seven panels as required by the draft’s beats.
- Page 10 is a one-panel message splash.
- No added prologue, epilogue or bonus page.

The showrunner’s draft remains the page plot in its existing order. It is edited for clarity, continuity and production—not substantially rewritten.

## 6. Visual Direction

Tactile, production-minded science fiction built from weathered metal, mineral dust, repaired mechanisms, water glare and readable faces.

- **Keel:** compressed, crowded and noisy; rust, charcoal, oxidized orange, faded industrial blue and hard white water light.
- **The old mine/workshop:** colder and darker, but spatially legible; damp rock, rusted rails, riveted pipes, analog gauges, manual wheels and isolated amber instrument light.
- **Halyard:** bronze, gold and engineered green; fabrication towers, terraces, elevated rail and controlled industrial movement.
- **Oasis:** pearl white, silver, cool blue and controlled green; open space, reservoirs, fountains and quiet automation.

The three-tier basin must read clearly on Page 1 and in Bi11bot’s map. The sealed mine entrance, Alpha nodes and the route beneath Keel must be planted before they matter.

Alex is always drawn with brown skin, short dense black curls, dark eyes, lean build, scarred work-rough hands, dark rolled-sleeve work shirt over a black tee, rust-brown patched cargo trousers, heavy scuffed boots and a worn canvas backpack hanging from one straight shoulder strap. Before the workshop discovery he has no watch; afterward Adrian’s oversized steel watch remains on his left wrist.

Bi11bot is companion-sized but substantial, approximately 75 cm standing and never menacing. His faded industrial-blue segmented head, mustard stripe, amber eyes, rust-red scarf, tool-arms and chipped working surfaces must remain readable. No red eyes, harsh light, looming threat posture or face lost in shadow.

Reserve clean lettering zones for dialogue, interfaces and SFX. In-world displays are part of the image and must never collide with balloons.

## 7. Canon

The following is canon for the book. Fixed material in `characters.md` and `world.md` remains binding unless this section explicitly settles an open point.

### Story and chapter canon

- Chapter 1 is titled **The Inheritance** and covers exactly Pages 1–12.
- The draft’s scenes, order and ending are retained: the three-city establishing image; Alex’s pump repair and debt transfer; Danny’s loan; Ada’s water and challenge; the grandmother and blue book; the hidden workshop; the watch and equation; Bi11bot’s awakening; Adrian’s message; the three-artifact map; the COST reading; Alex’s choice to begin at the mine; and the Oasis tracking alert.
- Alex is eighteen, not seventeen.
- Alex begins wanting to leave Keel. He ends by choosing evidence over safety: **“Then we start here.”**
- Adrian’s public reputation is not canonically true or false. The workshop, message and Bi11bot prove that the public account is incomplete, not that Adrian was blameless.
- Adrian’s fate remains unresolved at the end of Chapter 1.
- The Measure is the first of three artifacts, located beneath Keel’s sealed mine. Its appearance, function and larger purpose remain open.
- The Legacy Protocol is Adrian’s name for the trail of three artifacts in Keel, Halyard and Oasis. Its final purpose remains open.
- Bi11bot’s activation creates a traceable Alpha check-in. The Oasis watcher selects **TRACK**. Alex does not know he is being tracked.
- COST displays the total posted credit cost of a financial commitment, not a moral judgment. Its first reading is **310 CR** from Danny’s loan slip.
- Alex’s mother remains unidentified and absent. No new information about her is added in Chapter 1.
- Alex and Ada are close companions with emotional intimacy. Their relationship is not labeled romantic in this chapter.

### World canon decided by the Director

- Alpha is the basin-wide administrative and infrastructural AI authority operating water, food, power, work, travel, identity and registered machinery. Its ultimate legal status and human oversight remain deliberately unspecified.
- The three Charter Houses remain the dominant Halyard institutions for energy, water and space. Their precise relationship to Alpha is not stated in Chapter 1.
- No Basin Charter, Water House, newly named authority or other replacement institution is added to the chapter. The previous brief’s proposed Basin Charter/Water House canon is rejected as a substantial change to the showrunner’s draft.
- Alpha nodes are network relays and local interfaces. They authenticate readings, record work and repairs, process administrative data, and transmit registered-machine check-ins.
- The old mine contains newer concealed water-control equipment, but Chapter 1 does not prove who installed it, why it exists or whether it diverts water. The draft’s Measure trail, not a new pump conspiracy, is the chapter’s engine.
- Entering beyond the mine seal risks loss of Alex’s work card. The chapter ends before Alex enters the mine.
- The balanced water-drop mark is a recurring unresolved sign appearing on old equipment and the blue book. It is not yet assigned to an institution or explained.
- Payday-loan law and enforcement remain unspecified beyond the visible terms: **ADVANCE 120 CR · REPAY 10 CR / WEEK · 31 WEEKS** and total repayment of **310 CR**.
- Food, healthcare, housing allocation, patent ownership and the emergency council remain open because Chapter 1 does not need them.
- When folded, Bi11bot reads as unregistered scrap and is signal-blocked. When awake and unfolded, he checks in with Alpha and can be traced.

## 8. What We Take From the Material

- **Alex Phantum:** Use all fixed appearance, history, gestures, voice, relationships, work habits, debt, watch behavior and Chapter 1 arc from `characters.md`. Preserve his practical skepticism and the movement from escape-wish to evidence-based risk.
- **Bi11bot:** Use the fixed design, folding mechanism, voice, patience, fallibility and connection to Alpha from `characters.md`. His first active role is guide and witness, not exposition machine.
- **Adrian Phantum:** Use the public reputation, absence, hidden workshop, watch, holographic apology and warning to question him. Keep him contradictory and unresolved.
- **Grandmother:** Use the apartment, memory loss, blue book, deliberate handoff at eighteen and emotional response to Adrian’s message. Do not use her as a full-history exposition source.
- **Ada:** Keep her saved water, dry intimacy, challenge to Alex’s inherited story and competing belief that Keel must be changed rather than merely escaped.
- **Danny, the supervisor, the shopkeeper and the Oasis watcher:** Use them as sharply drawn functions within the existing scenes. Do not expand their biographies.
- **World:** Use the three cities, unequal water access, Keel’s pump hall, public checkpoint, Last Chance, pit rim, sealed mine, hidden workshop, Alpha network and Oasis control room from `world.md`.
- **Idea-draft beats:** Keep the draft’s strongest causal chain exactly: birthday debt → doubt about Adrian → grandmother’s handoff → workshop → watch activates Bi11bot → message and map → COST reveals 310 → Alex chooses the mine → Alpha tracks the awakened unit.

## 9. Proposals Under Consideration

Nothing below is canon:

- Adrian may be alive, dead, imprisoned, hidden or otherwise inaccessible.
- The Measure may expose a physical truth about water, debt, ownership or Alpha without being a simple weapon.
- The water-drop mark may connect Adrian’s work to older infrastructure.
- The equation may be a calibration system, synchronization key, map or encoded message.
- Bi11bot may know more about Adrian than he initially reveals.
- The Oasis watcher may be an Alpha operator, Charter House employee or an independent authority.
- Alex may eventually travel to Halyard and Oasis.
- Ada may become an active partner in the investigation, but no future relationship label is fixed.

## 10. Non-Negotiables

- Exactly 12 pages; the draft’s scenes remain in order.
- Edit the showrunner’s draft; do not replace its story with a pump-diversion plot or a new opening premise.
- Alex is eighteen and receives the watch on his birthday in the hidden workshop.
- Alex’s work card, debt, restricted travel status and inherited **418,607-credit** liability remain legible.
- The watch’s face never judges or speaks; COST never displays moral language.
- Bi11bot remains non-menacing, mechanically precise and quietly amused.
- Adrian’s fate, the Measure’s function and the Legacy Protocol’s final purpose remain unresolved.
- Keel is water-scarce, crowded, repaired and controlled; Halyard and Oasis visibly embody unequal access.
- Visual evidence must do work that exposition would otherwise do.
- Dialogue, interface text and SFX must have separate lettering zones and an unmistakable reading order.

# Verdict

Needs a rethink. The current script, layouts and thumbnails replace the approved Chapter 1 story with a new water-diversion/mineshaft plot, removing the birthday debt transfer, Ada, Grandmother, blue book, workshop, Adrian’s message, Legacy Protocol, COST reading and Oasis tracking ending. This is a development-level contradiction of the brief and showrunner’s draft, not an execution pass; the room must return to the approved 12-page page plot before layout or lettering can proceed.

- **Unlicensed invention:** The water-diversion conspiracy, Alex’s intervention, Adrian’s initials on the mine control assembly, the mine shutter response and the approaching mechanical pursuer are not in the approved canon and replace the showrunner’s retained story.
- **Direct contradiction:** The script has Alex wearing the watch and carrying an active Bi11bot on Page 1, contrary to the fixed pre-workshop state.
- **Direct contradiction:** The script has Alex enter the mine, while canon requires the chapter to end before entry.
- **Direct contradiction:** The script introduces “Water House” equipment, while the Director rejected a Water House as chapter canon.

- **Missing labels:** All new water-diversion and mine-pursuer assertions should be treated as unapproved proposals at most, not canon. The Director must decide whether they are discarded; the brief currently directs that they be discarded for Chapter 1.

## Continuity ledger

- **Opening state:** Approved canon requires Alex to begin without Adrian’s watch and before Bi11bot’s activation. `script.md` begins with the opposite state.
- **Alex’s immediate want:** Approved canon begins with Alex wanting to leave Keel; current script gives him an immediate public-pump objective and then a mine-repair objective, with no escape wish.
- **Adrian’s public reputation:** Approved canon plants the public cautionary story and later complicates it. Current script mentions Adrian only through initials and mine evidence, removing the public/private contrast.
- **Debt and work-card status:** Approved canon requires the eighteenth-birthday transfer, 418,607-credit liability and restricted travel. Current script omits these states entirely.
- **Danny’s loan slip:** Approved canon plants the slip on Page 2 for the Page 11 COST reading. Current script omits the prop and therefore drops its payoff.
- **Ada:** Approved canon establishes her as Alex’s close companion, source of saved water and challenge to his inherited story. Current script omits her, removing a required relationship pressure.
- **Grandmother and blue book:** Approved canon requires the deliberate birthday handoff that opens the workshop. Current script omits both the handoff and the mechanism.
- **Watch:** Current script has the watch on Alex’s left wrist from Page 1. Approved continuity requires: absent before workshop; found in workshop; worn on left wrist thereafter.
- **Bi11bot:** Current script has Bi11bot active and speaking from Page 1. Approved continuity requires: dormant in workshop; awakened by the watch; unfolded activation creates the traceable Alpha check-in.
- **Mine:** Approved continuity plants the sealed mine as a destination and ends before entry. Current script makes it the active setting and ends inside it.
- **Water state:** Current script begins with dry taps caused by an asserted diversion and restores flow on Page 10. The approved chapter may show water scarcity and unequal access, but does not make a new diversion plot the chapter’s resolution.
- **Alpha/network state:** Approved continuity: nodes are planted early; Bi11bot’s activation creates a check-in; Oasis selects `TRACK`; Alex remains unaware. Current script replaces this with a local mine alarm and mechanical pursuit.
- **Ending state:** Approved ending is Alex choosing evidence—“Then we start here.”—while Oasis begins tracking. Current ending is Alex turning toward an unidentified mine threat and asking who Adrian was trying to reach. These are incompatible ending states.

## Page 9 (right) — Alex and Bi11bot work as partners

**Panel 1.** Alex strains against the seized manual wheel. It barely moves. The old gauge climbs toward the red end of its painted scale. Water beads and trembles along the regulator housing.

Bi11bot braces his feet against the rail and locks both hands around the housing.

**SFX:** KRRRNNK

**BI11BOT:** The pressure is rising.

**ALEX:** I know.

**BI11BOT:** The wheel does not.

**ALEX:** Then make it listen.

**Panel 2.** Bi11bot shifts his weight and wedges one articulated finger beneath a locking tooth. His right eye ring clicks rapidly as he focuses on the mechanism.

**SFX:** CLICK-CLICK-CLICK

**BI11BOT:** Now.

Alex changes his grip and drives the wheel downward.

**Panel 3.** The newer control assembly flashes white and amber. A thin line of light travels from the node into the cable disappearing down the mine tunnel.

The old gauge needle slams against its stop.

**SFX:** BEEP—BEEP—BEEP

**BI11BOT:** It noticed.

**ALEX:** Let it.

**Panel 4.** The manual wheel moves a fraction. Alex’s boots slide on wet stone. Bi11bot catches the housing before it tears free.

Cold water sprays from a seam and crosses both of them.

**SFX:** CHK-KK

**ALEX:** Again.

**BI11BOT:** Together?

Alex looks at him.

**ALEX:** Together.

**PAGE CHECK:** The easy repair is impossible. Alex changes tactics from diagnosis to force; Bi11bot changes from concealed helper to physical partner. The system registers the intervention.

**Panel 1.** Medium-wide. Alex jumps backward, knocking over a stool, hands half-raised. Bi11bot dusts off his shoulders in the foreground, then turns toward Alex.

ALEX: Okay. That's... new.

**Panel 2.** Tight on Bi11bot’s face. His amber eyes narrow to focus. The heavy rings rotate with a click. His head tilts patiently.

BI11BOT: You're taller than your father predicted.

ALEX: Everyone in Keel claims they knew my father.

BI11BOT: Most of them knew his reputation. I knew his mistakes.

**Panel 3.** Medium. Bi11bot’s eyes drop toward the watch on Alex’s left wrist. Alex pulls his sleeve partly over it.

BI11BOT: Right on time.

ALEX: You know about the watch?

Alex turns his wrist over and slips the watch off, revealing the looping equation engraved on the caseback. Bi11bot’s mustard-yellow hand hovers just above it without touching. The chalkboard equation blurs behind them.

BI11BOT: He wanted you to know the true cost of your decisions.

**Panel 4.** Short, wide. A small projector unfolds from Bi11bot’s shoulder with a click. Soft-blue light lands on Alex’s face. His hand closes over the watch as the rest of the workshop drops into shadow.

BI11BOT: Now. Shall we say hello to your father?

## Page 3 (right) — Ada offers care, then challenges Alex’s escape fantasy

**Panel 1.** Late afternoon on the Keel pit rim at the same rusted railing. Keel lies in shadow; Halyard’s terraces catch gold; Oasis remains in sunlight. The sealed mine entrance is at the far left edge. A weathered plaque reads **WATER WARS MEMORIAL**, above a dry, cracked drinking fountain. Alex sits on the railing with a simple packed lunch. Ada approaches carrying two battered metal mugs of water. She is twenty, with long wind-tangled copper hair, freckles and teal-blue eyes, a dark high-collared top, rivet-studded shoulder strap and muted moss-green wrap at her neck.

CAPTION: Later that afternoon...

ADA: Thought you might need this.

ALEX: The birthday special? Not sure I can afford it.

**Panel 2.** Close two-shot of Ada offering Alex the mug. His mug is halfway to his mouth. Her ration card hangs at her hip with one day’s mark punched out.

ALEX: Where'd you get enough water for two?

ADA: Saved some of my ration.

ALEX: Ada, you didn't have to.

ADA: Not everything has a price, Alex.

**Panel 3.** Medium two-shot. They sit side by side on the railing, mugs in hand. Ada laughs; Alex gives a short, dry laugh that does not reach his eyes.

ADA: Eighteen. Congratulations. You're officially old.

ALEX: I'm officially broke.

ADA: I heard about this morning.

ALEX: Everyone did.

Ada lets the silence sit.

**Panel 4.** Wide, split eyelines. Alex looks up toward Oasis. Ada looks down toward Keel. The railing cuts diagonally between them.

ALEX: Every birthday... I look up there... and wonder what it feels like to wake up somewhere you get to choose your own future.

ADA: I look down here... and wonder why everyone gave up trying to change it.

ALEX: Because nobody leaves.

ADA: That's the problem. People stopped trying to make it worth staying.

## Page 4 (left) — Ada turns Adrian’s reputation from fact into hearsay

**Panel 1.** Medium on the rim as evening turns amber. Alex draws a worn photograph half out of his jacket pocket. His grandmother smiles in it; Adrian’s face is folded over and creased. Alex’s other hand remains a fist in his jacket pocket. Ada notices both.

ADA: How's your grandma?

ALEX: Some days she knows me. Other days she calls me Adrian...

ALEX: The hardest part isn't that she forgets. It's that she makes up crazy stories about him.

**Panel 2.** Wide two-shot at the railing. Ada faces Alex rather than the view. Oasis glows brighter as Keel dims.

ADA: What kind of stories?

ALEX: That he wasn't wasting it. That he was working.

Ada lets the silence sit.

**Panel 3.** Tight on Ada’s steady face; Alex’s hand closes into a fist in the foreground.

ADA: What do you actually remember about him?

ALEX: Nothing. I was two.

**Panel 4.** Wide and quiet as both figures stand small against the darkening basin. Alex stares toward the horizon. They begin walking home at dusk; Ada lingers and looks back toward Oasis.

ADA: So everything else is what people told you. What if they were wrong?

ALEX: Maybe…Or maybe they're right. Either way... he's gone.

ADA (very small, almost to herself): He wouldn't just leave.

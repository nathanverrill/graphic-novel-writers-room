### Pulled me forward
Page 2, the debt transfer scanner. Seeing an 18-year-old instantly hit with 418,000 credits of debt from his missing dad is brutal and instantly made me care about his situation.
Page 6, the bookshelf secret door. It's a classic trope, but it works. I wanted to see what was in there.
Page 11, the watch calculating the true cost of Danny's loan. That's a really cool mechanic.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Page 1. It's a massive wall of worldbuilding text and city descriptions before we even meet a character. I skimmed the city labels.
Pages 3 and 4. The whole "staring at the rich city from the slums while talking about how much it sucks" scene felt like every other YA dystopia I've ever read. I started checking my phone here.

### I was confused
Page 2: The "Alpha node" logging his shift. I don't know what Alpha is yet.
Page 12: The woman in Oasis. I know she's tracking him, but I have no idea who she is or why she cares about a 16-year-old robot.

### Things I was expected to already know
- Alpha node (Page 2) - Still not fully explained, just seems like a computer system.
- Pre-Alpha pipes (Page 7) - Assuming this means old tech?
- Legacy Protocol (Page 11) - Dad's secret plan, introduced here.

### Lines I read twice
"When tomorrow looks bad, today looks bigger." (Page 11) - Had to read it twice to get the meaning, but it's a good line.
"Most of the true things in my life started that way." (Page 12) - A bit cryptic.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex, Ada, and Danny are distinct. Bi11bot is obviously unique. The pump-hall supervisor and the guard on page 2 just felt like generic authority figures.

### I felt something
Page 2, the sheer weight of that debt transfer. 
Page 5, when the Grandma suddenly snapped into clarity. That was a nice, sad moment.

### I predicted
On Page 4, I predicted the dad wasn't actually a deadbeat and was secretly working on something important.
On Page 7, I predicted the robot would wake up as soon as he put the watch on.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A kid in a dystopian slum inherits his missing dad's robot and a magic watch, and has to break into a sealed mine to find a secret artifact and clear his massive debt.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 4. The angsty dialogue on the railing was dragging, and I felt like I'd read this exact setup a hundred times before.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. The Oasis woman tracking the signal adds a ticking clock to their mine heist. I want to see them break into the mine.

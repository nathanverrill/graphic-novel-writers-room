### Pulled me forward
Page 6, when the symbols on the watch matched the control housing. That was the first time I actually cared about what they were doing down there.

### I slowed down / skimmed
Pages 2 and 5. I started skimming all the descriptions of pipes, mineral crusts, gauges, and valves. It felt like reading a plumbing manual. 

### I was confused
Why is Alex hiding Bi11bot in his bag on Page 1, but then just having a full conversation with him out loud in front of a queue of people? 

### Things I was expected to already know
- Keel
- The allocation system / "AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL"
- Adrian (I figured out it was his dad later, but early on it's just a name)
- The basin spine

### Lines I read twice
"The shape echoes the looping equation engraved on Adrian’s watch, still loose on Alex’s left wrist." (Page 5, Panel 4) - I had to re-read this to visualize what a "looping equation" looks like.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
Alex and Bi11bot are very distinct. The Queue Worker is just a generic voice. 

### I felt something
Page 10. The splash page of the water bursting through was a good release of tension after all the wrench-turning.

### I predicted
On Page 8, when the sign said "UNAUTHORIZED OPERATION REPORTS TO NETWORK", I knew immediately that turning the wheel was going to lock them in or summon guards.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A mechanic and his robot uncover a water-stealing conspiracy that ties back to his father's past.

### Where I would have put it down
Page 5. The walk down the tunnel looking at cold vs. warm pipes and regulator galleries almost lost me completely.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Yes. They are trapped behind a shutter and something heavy is walking toward them in the dark. It's a solid cliffhanger.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 5; next issue: yes

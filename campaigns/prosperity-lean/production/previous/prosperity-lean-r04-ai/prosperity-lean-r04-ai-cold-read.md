# Cold Read: Prosperity Lean, Issue 01

I read this once, start to finish, from the seat of someone who didn't pick it.

## Issue 01

### Pulled me forward
- **Page 1, panel 3.** The robot in the bag saying "It is hurting." / "Pumps don't hurt." / "This one does." This is where I stopped wanting the book to be bad. A creepy eye in a bag that feels sorry for a pump is a good hook.
- **Page 1, panel 4.** "Give me three minutes." / "You have two." It's quick and it gives me a clock.
- **Page 2, panel 4.** "That isn't a failure." This was the first real turn: someone is stealing the water while the sign says NORMAL. I got that, and I wanted to know who.
- **Page 3, last panel.** "Quietly." I laughed. Bi11bot's literal-minded comebacks carry the whole thing for me.
- **Page 7.** Finding the A.P. initials and holding them against the photo. A dead or missing father showing up in a place he shouldn't be is a hook I'll take.
- **Pages 9–10.** The wheel, "Together?" / "Together.", and then the splash. The action is clear and I could follow it.
- **Page 11, panel 1.** Water hits the jugs and the sign *still* says NORMAL. That was satisfying.

### I slowed down / skimmed
- **Pages 4–6.** This is a long stretch of following pipes and comparing a warm one with a cold one. "This line carries the pressure." / "Newer doesn't mean stronger." I skimmed the plumbing. I was waiting for something to happen besides more pipe.
- **Page 6.** Matching the watch etching to the housing, in a "same / similar" order. I didn't follow what pressing the ports in sequence actually did, and then nothing happened. I skimmed it.
- **The "I can see that" / "I see that too" gag.** It was funny once. By page 11, when the book points it out itself ("You say that often"), I had already noticed it was a habit.
- **"I changed my mind," twice (pages 4 and 6).** Same issue. The second time read like a repeat, not a callback.

### I was confused
- **What Keel is.** I worked out it's a town from the setting, but I don't know if it's poor, a colony, underground, or something else.
- **"The remaining allocation goes uphill!" (page 3).** Uphill to whom? I guessed rich people, but it's a guess.
- **The "forbidden mine boundary" (page 3).** The art may show it. Nobody *says* it's forbidden, so I didn't feel any danger in going in. Alex's "It's not a route. It's a maintenance gap... It is if someone asks" suggests it's illegal, but I don't know what happens if he's caught.
- **"Basin spine" (pages 7–8).** I had no idea what it was. A place? A system? The rich part of town?
- **The network.** I don't know what it is, who runs it, or why I should be scared of it.
- **Page 6, panel 2.** He follows the watch sequence across three ports. Did that do anything? The panel just comes loose because he pulls it.
- **Page 11, last panel.** "Not someone." Fine, so it's a machine. I was confused but intrigued, which is the good kind.
- **Page 12.** "We find out who he was trying to reach." Reach? Where did that come from? Nothing earlier told me Adrian was trying to reach anyone. It's the last line, and it lost me.
- **Who Bi11bot is.** Where he came from, and why he has to hide in a bag. Is having a robot illegal?

### Lines I read twice
- "It is if someone asks." (p3) I had to work out that he means "it matters if I get caught."
- "That is not the same as why." (p7) I got it on the second read.
- "He didn't throw everything away." (p7) Throw *what* away? I gave up on this one. It's clearly referring to backstory I don't have.
- "Is this the safe choice?" / "The familiar one?" (p8) Why "familiar"? It sounds like it's quoting something I wasn't there for.
- "Is that better?" / "It's evidence." (p12) Evidence of what, for whom?
- "We find out who he was trying to reach." (p12) I read it twice and still didn't get it.

### Who I could tell apart, and who I couldn't
- **Alex and Bi11bot:** completely distinct. The robot is the personality and Alex is the straight man. Alex comes off as a loner with no friends, no home and nobody waiting for him, and he barely looks at the people in the queue.
- **The queue worker** is just a voice. Fine.
- **Adrian** isn't a character to me yet. He's a watch and two initials.

### I felt something
- A small laugh at "Quietly." and "Those are different things."
- Some warmth at "Together?" / "Together."
- The water reaching the jugs on page 11 felt good.
- I didn't feel much about the father, because I don't know what happened to him. Dead? Gone? A traitor?

### I predicted
- From page 2: the NORMAL sign is a lie and someone uphill is taking the water. That came true.
- From page 7: Dad built or helped build the theft system. I suspect he's the bad guy, or he was trying to stop it.
- The thing coming down the tunnel is a robot, maybe one like Bi11bot.

### What I think this book is about, in one sentence
A pump repairman and his secret robot find out his father's old work is being used to steal his town's water, and he breaks the law to take it back.

### Where I would have put it down
**Page 5.** It's more pipes, I still don't know who or what is behind any of it, and the stakes are a queue I never met. The page 5 panel 4 freeze on the matching etching is the only reason I'd turn the page.

### The last page: do I need the next issue, and why
Sort of. The approaching light and the KLANG are mostly a loud noise. I don't know what the threat is, so I'm not scared for anyone. The pull I do have is the robot, and Alex standing in front of him. The closing line confused me instead of hooking me. I'd wait a week, or read it if a friend had it. I wouldn't buy it tonight.

VERDICT Issue 01: put down: page 5; next issue: no

## Whole book

### The page that would have lost my whole class
Page 5. Pipes, regulators, "control line grafted across rusted machinery." It's the page where everyone checks their phone.

### What I would tell a friend it was about
"There's a guy who fixes pumps, and he's got this little robot in his bag that talks like it's taking everything literally. Someone's stealing the town's water through an old mine, and his dad's initials are on the machine doing it."

### One-sentence aftertaste of each issue
- **Issue 01:** The robot is great and the water-theft setup is clear, but by the end I still didn't know who anyone was, what the Network is, or why the last line was about "reaching" someone.

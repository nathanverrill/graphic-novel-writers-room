## Page 11 (right) — The map and COST turn inheritance into Alex’s deliberate choice

**Panel 1.** Dominant wide view of the workshop wall. Bi11bot projects a holographic mountain cross-section: Keel at the bottom, Halyard midway up, Oasis above the clouds. Three markers sit far apart: one deep beneath Keel’s pit inside the old mine, one inside a Halyard fabrication tower and one high in Oasis. Only the Keel marker glows bright amber; the other two are dim outlines. Alex and Bi11bot stand silhouetted against the map.

BI11BOT: Your father left a trail. Three artifacts, in three places.

BI11BOT: He called it the Legacy Protocol.

**Panel 2.** Close on the bright Keel marker. It sits deep below the sealed entrance on the pit rim, at the bottom of a shaft. The label reads **THE MEASURE**.

BI11BOT: The first is called the Measure. It's somewhere inside the old mine.

ALEX: That mine's been sealed since before I was born.

BI11BOT: Your father knew.

ALEX: Anyone caught past that seal loses their work card.

BI11BOT: Then we don't get caught.

**Panel 3.** Medium two-shot. Alex looks over his shoulder toward the workbench where Adrian’s hologram stood, the creased photograph in his hand. Bi11bot watches him with a patient head tilt.

ALEX: And finding it tells me what happened to him?

BI11BOT: It tells us where he wanted you to begin. The rest, we'll have to discover.

Alex pulls Danny’s crumpled loan slip from his pocket and brings it near the watch. The face switches from CLOCK to COST, displaying a large amber-red **310 CR**, a small cost icon and a thin bar.

ALEX: It's reading Danny's loan slip. 310.

BI11BOT: The console cost him 120. Ten a week for thirty-one weeks is 310.

ALEX: So he's an idiot.

**Panel 4.** Close, then widening just enough to include the projected Measure marker. Bi11bot faces Alex, one mustard-yellow finger near the watch glass. Alex frowns.

BI11BOT: No. When tomorrow looks bad, today looks bigger.

BI11BOT: Your father built that so people could see the 310 before they sign. Not after.

ALEX: Everyone says he pawned and borrowed it all away.

BI11BOT: That's a theory. The mine is evidence.

Alex’s hand reaches past Bi11bot and presses flat against the bright Measure marker. The light ripples around his fingers. The old Alpha node on the workshop wall glows steady. A hairline amber pulse leaves it, entering the infrastructure spine.

ALEX: Then we start here.

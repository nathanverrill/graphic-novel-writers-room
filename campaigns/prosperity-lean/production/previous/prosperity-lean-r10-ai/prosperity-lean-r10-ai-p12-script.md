## Page 12 (left) — Oasis turns Bi11bot’s check-in into an active pursuit

**Panel 1.** Tall vertical cutaway echoing Page 1. The hairline amber pulse from Keel travels up the shared infrastructure spine: along the water conduit, past identical white-and-amber control nodes, through the Keel–Halyard permission gate, up through Halyard and beyond the cloud layer into Oasis. Each node flickers to full amber as the pulse passes. The three tier palettes remain distinct; the pulse is the only warm moving color.

**Panel 2.** Wide, symmetrical Oasis control room. Pearl-white and graphite surfaces surround a wall-sized live display of the mountain in cross-section. Thousands of white points glow. In dark Keel, one small amber point pulses. An unnamed woman sits alone with her back to us, small against the display. A glass of clean water rests on the desk, precisely aligned with the desk edge.

**Panel 3.** Medium close on a monitor and the watcher’s hand. The display reads:

**LEGACY DEVICE ONLINE**  
**UNIT B-11 · ALPHA FIELD UNIT, FIRST GENERATION**  
**REGISTERED TO: A. PHANTUM — RECORD CLOSED**  
**LAST CHECK-IN: 16 YEARS AGO**  
**HOLDS: MEASURE LOCATION**  
**[ REPORT ] [ TRACK ]**

Her hand presses **TRACK**. The other option remains dark.

**Panel 4.** Dominant wide view of the wall display. The amber point in Keel still pulses. A thin cold-white ring slowly closes around it. Inset at lower corner: **SIGNAL INTERMITTENT · ACQUIRING**. The watcher leans forward. Her other hand nudges the glass of clean water a few millimeters until it aligns exactly with the desk edge and monitor frame. The room reflects faintly in the display’s glass; the watcher remains tiny at its center.

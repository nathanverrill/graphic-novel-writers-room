# Prosperity — Story

## What the Showrunner Is Reaching For

**Material type: explicit showrunner decision and revised draft.**

Chapter 1 is exactly **12 pages**, using the showrunner's revised draft as the working story material. The draft is to be edited rather than rewritten. `(rules/characters.md; input note; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The chapter introduces a world where water, work, travel and debt are administered through a vertical three-city system. Its immediate dramatic question is whether Alex Phantum can discover the truth about his vanished father and make a choice about his inherited future before the systems controlling Keel close around him. `(input/world.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The material's recurring questions are:

- **What if everyone you've trusted has a secret?**
- **Who really owns what we create?**
- **Where does all our water go?**
- What is the true cost of a decision?
- Was Adrian Phantum a man who wasted everything, or was he working toward something hidden?
- Can Alex choose a future rather than inherit the one Keel assigns him? `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The intended emotional movement is from Alex's public humiliation and inherited debt, through doubt about the official story of Adrian, into discovery and dangerous possibility. Alex begins the chapter believing that his father abandoned the family and that leaving Keel is nearly impossible. He ends having chosen to investigate Adrian's hidden trail, while an institution in Oasis begins tracking him. `(drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

The central thematic contrast is between **posted cost and real cost**. Keel's systems display debt, wages and travel restrictions as if they are objective facts. Adrian's watch offers another measure: the total cost of a choice over time. Alex must still weigh what the watch cannot measure—sleep, time and risk—himself. `(rules/characters.md; drafts/chapter-01-draft.md)`

## 1. Structure

**Canon:** Chapter 1, *The Inheritance*, is exactly 12 pages. The showrunner's scenes remain in order and are edited for clarity and production.

### Act I — The inherited story and the first doubt  
**Pages 1–5**

- **Page 1 turn:** Establish the three-tier basin, unequal access and the governing questions.
- **Page 2:** Alex's competence, debt transfer, public association with Adrian and desire to leave Keel.
- **Pages 3–4:** Ada offers material care and challenges Alex's inherited account of Adrian.
- **Page 5 turn:** Grandmother's deliberate recognition of Alex's eighteenth birthday points him toward Adrian's blue book.

### Act II — The inheritance becomes physical  
**Pages 6–10**

- **Page 6 turn:** The book opens the hidden workshop.
- **Pages 7–8:** Alex finds the watch and Bi11bot; the watch activates the robot.
- **Page 9 turn:** Bi11bot complicates Adrian's reputation and initiates the message.
- **Page 10 turn:** Adrian directly acknowledges his absence, admits failure, gives Alex agency and warns him to question every source—including Adrian.

### Act III — Evidence over safety  
**Pages 11–12**

- **Page 11 turn:** Bi11bot reveals the Legacy Protocol and the Measure beneath the sealed mine. COST makes the chapter's central idea concrete; Alex chooses the mine as evidence.
- **Page 12:** The choice's hidden consequence appears: Bi11bot's check-in reaches Oasis, where the watcher selects TRACK.
- **Ending state:** Alex has not entered the mine. The chapter ends on pursuit and an unanswered question.

### Page-turn strategy

- **Page 1:** Full-page world image establishes the governing scale and contrasts.
- **Page 5 → 6:** The blue book opens a hidden room.
- **Page 9 → 10:** Bi11bot's projector leads into Adrian's face and message.
- **Page 10 → 11:** Adrian's message resolves into a concrete map and objective.
- **Page 11 → 12:** Alex's choice propagates through Alpha's network to an unseen watcher.
- **Final question:** Can Alex reach the Measure before Alpha's track closes around Bi11bot?

## 3. Page plot

- **Page 1 — Establish the inheritance:** A full-page view of Keel, Halyard and Oasis shows the unequal basin, shared infrastructure spine, water gradient, access gates, Alpha nodes and sealed mine. The page asks what the connected system conceals.
- **Page 2 — Make adulthood a liability:** Alex repairs a pump by feel, survives an unauthorized-repair flag, receives the 418,607-credit debt transfer, sees Adrian's public disgrace used as a sales warning, pockets Danny's loan slip and looks toward Oasis. **Change:** Alex's wish to leave becomes materially harder.
- **Page 3 — Give him care and a counterargument:** Ada brings saved water, jokes about his birthday, and contrasts Alex's desire to escape with her desire to make Keel worth staying in. **Change:** Alex is emotionally supported but ideologically challenged.
- **Page 4 — Remove certainty about Adrian:** Ada establishes that Alex remembers nothing of his father; the public account is hearsay. Her quiet “He wouldn't just leave” leaves Alex with doubt rather than proof. **Change:** Adrian's reputation becomes an open question.
- **Page 5 — Release the inheritance:** Grandmother briefly becomes lucid, identifies Alex's eighteenth birthday and directs him to the blue book. **Page-turn reveal:** The book opens the hidden workshop.
- **Page 6 — Cross into Adrian's secret:** The bookshelf swings open. Alex enters the cold workshop and sees preserved machinery, equations, the tagged watch and the dust-covered robot. **Change:** Adrian's hidden labor is physically undeniable.
- **Page 7 — Connect the clues:** Alex examines the watch's engraved caseback and matches its looping equation to the chalkboard; he reaches toward the dormant robot. **Change:** The inheritance is deliberate and technically unified.
- **Page 8 — Wake the witness:** Alex tests Bi11bot's chest pulse, buckles on the watch and watches its loading equation complete as Bi11bot awakens. The old Alpha node blinks. **Change:** Private discovery creates institutional exposure.
- **Page 9 — Establish a conditional guide:** Bi11bot reacts with dry familiarity, distinguishes Adrian's mistakes from his reputation, identifies the watch's purpose and opens the projector. **Page-turn reveal:** Adrian's face is about to appear.
- **Page 10 — Let Adrian speak:** One full-page message splash contains the holographic message as a continuous visual event: joke, apology, admission of failure, invitation, warning and Alex's silent reaction. Grandmother witnesses the message; Alex unfolds the photograph afterward. **Change:** Adrian becomes directly present but remains unresolved.
- **Page 11 — Convert inheritance into a choice:** Bi11bot projects the three-artifact map, identifies the Measure beneath the sealed mine, explains the work-card risk and reads Danny's slip as **310 CR**. Alex rejects both blind condemnation and blind trust, presses the marker and says, “Then we start here.” **Page-turn reveal:** The check-in pulse travels up the infrastructure spine.
- **Page 12 — Show the hidden cost:** The pulse reaches Oasis. The watcher sees the B-11 alert, selects **TRACK**, aligns her water glass and watches the acquisition ring close around Keel. **Change:** Alex's chosen investigation has acquired an unseen pursuer.

## 5. Reader vs. character knowledge

### Reader knows; Alex does not

- The three cities are connected by a system that carries water, power, transit and administrative signals.
- Bi11bot's activation creates a trace through Alpha's network.
- The Oasis watcher sees the B-11 alert and deliberately selects **TRACK**.
- The tracking signal is intermittent but being acquired.
- The watcher knows B-11 is registered to A. Phantum and holds the Measure location.
- The reader understands that the sealed mine has been planted as a destination since Page 1.

### Alex knows; the reader learns with him

- His exact inherited liability, projected clearance and restricted travel status.
- His grandmother has preserved a deliberate inheritance for his eighteenth birthday.
- The workshop, watch and Bi11bot were left by Adrian.
- Adrian admits absence and failure but does not explain his disappearance.
- The Legacy Protocol includes three artifacts, beginning with the Measure beneath Keel.
- COST reads the loan's total repayment as 310 CR.

### Characters know or believe; the reader must not mistake belief for fact

- Keel believes or publicly repeats that Adrian wasted everything and disappeared.
- Ada believes Adrian would not simply leave.
- Grandmother appears to know the timing and location of the workshop, but not necessarily Adrian's full plan.
- Bi11bot knows Adrian's mistakes and carries the map, but Adrian explicitly warns Alex not to trust him unquestioningly.
- The Oasis watcher acts on the B-11 alert, but her authority and affiliation remain unknown.
- Adrian's fate, the Measure's function and the Legacy Protocol's final purpose remain unresolved.

## 6. Unresolved decisions and proposed canon changes

### Open canon questions

- **The Measure:** Its appearance, function and significance remain unknown.
- **Adrian's fate:** The chapter does not establish whether he is alive, dead, imprisoned, hidden or otherwise inaccessible.
- **Legacy Protocol:** The purpose of the three artifacts remains unknown.
- **Water-drop mark:** Its maker, institutional connection and meaning remain unknown.
- **Equation:** Its role beyond activation and synchronization remains unknown.
- **Bi11bot's knowledge:** The limits of his memory and what he withholds remain unknown.
- **Oasis watcher:** Her identity, affiliation and authority remain unknown.
- **Alpha's awareness:** The chapter does not establish whether Alpha knows Alex is present, recognizes the workshop or can track anything beyond the unfolded B-11 signal.
- **Ada's future role:** She may become an active investigation partner, but no future relationship label is fixed.

### Proposal

- **Proposal:** Treat the chapter's central investigative rule as: Alex accepts neither Keel's public story nor Adrian's private account without physical evidence. This is already dramatized by the choice, “The mine is evidence,” and should guide later plotting without becoming a new stated institution or doctrine.

### Decision needed

- **Decision needed:** None for Chapter 1's page plot. Any future decision about the Measure, Adrian, the watcher or the Legacy Protocol belongs after this chapter's unresolved questions have done their work.

## Key scenes

### The Debt Transfer Day

- **Where:** Page 2, Keel checkpoint.
- **Who:** Alex, checkpoint guard, queued workers and Danny.
- **What happens:** On his eighteenth birthday, Alex's household liability transfers to him. The display shows 418,607 credits, 40 credits earned that day, 28.7 years projected clearance and restricted travel.
- **The image the reader must see:** Alex's name and debt numbers filling the checkpoint display while workers queue beneath **TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**.
- **Story function:** Makes Adrian's absence an immediate material burden and turns adulthood into administrative liability.

### The Loan Slip at The Last Chance

- **Where:** Page 2, Keel main street.
- **Who:** Alex, Danny and the shopkeeper.
- **What happens:** Danny celebrates a 120-credit advance at 10 credits a week for 31 weeks. The shopkeeper invokes Adrian's reputation. Alex pockets the slip.
- **The image the reader must see:** **ADVANCE 120 CR · REPAY 10 CR / WEEK · 31 WEEKS**, with the water queue and **RATION: 4 L** across the street.
- **Story function:** Plants the object that will demonstrate COST.

### Ada's Saved Water

- **Where:** Pages 3–4, Keel pit rim.
- **Who:** Alex and Ada.
- **What happens:** Ada gives Alex water saved from her ration and challenges his private escape logic and inherited account of Adrian.
- **The image the reader must see:** Their mugs held against Oasis in light and Keel in shadow.
- **Story function:** Gives Alex care without transaction and introduces the possibility that Adrian's story is incomplete.

### The Grandmother's Hinge

- **Where:** Page 5, Phantum apartment.
- **Who:** Alex and his grandmother.
- **What happens:** After mistaking Alex for Adrian, Grandmother recognizes that he is eighteen and directs him to the blue book.
- **The image the reader must see:** Her clear, studying gaze with the marked blue book isolated on the top shelf.
- **Story function:** Changes family confusion into deliberate guardianship.

### The Bookshelf Door

- **Where:** Page 6, Phantum apartment.
- **Who:** Alex and his grandmother.
- **What happens:** The blue book acts as a lever; the bookshelf opens to Adrian's workshop.

### The Watch and the Equation

- **Where:** Page 7, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex.

### Bi11bot Wakes

- **Where:** Page 8, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex and Bi11bot.
- **What happens:** Alex tests Bi11bot's chest pulse, buckles on the watch and activates the robot when the loading ring closes.
- **The image the reader must see:** The amber ring completing on Alex's left wrist at the exact instant Bi11bot's warm amber eyes light through rising dust.

### “I Knew His Mistakes”

- **Where:** Page 9, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex and Bi11bot.
- **What happens:** Bi11bot distinguishes Adrian's public reputation from his actual mistakes.
- **The image the reader must see:** Bi11bot's patient amber eyes and rotating right eye ring as he studies Alex and the watch.

- **Where:** Page 10, full-page message splash.
- **Who:** Adrian's recording, Alex, Grandmother and Bi11bot.

- **The image the reader must see:** Adrian's holographic face dominating the workshop while Alex stands frozen in the foreground and Grandmother witnesses from the doorway.
- **Story function:** Makes Adrian emotionally present without resolving his fate or absolving him.

### The Three-Artifacts Map

- **Where:** Page 11, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex and Bi11bot.
- **What happens:** Bi11bot projects the Legacy Protocol across Keel, Halyard and Oasis. The first artifact is the Measure beneath Keel's sealed mine.
- **The image the reader must see:** A mountain cross-section with the bright amber Measure marker below Keel and two dim markers above.

### COST Reads 310 CR

- **Where:** Page 11, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex and Bi11bot.
- **What happens:** The watch reads Danny's loan as **310 CR**, revealing the total repayment rather than the original advance.
- **The image the reader must see:** The large amber-red **310 CR** filling the watch face beside the loan slip.

### Alex Chooses the Mine

- **Where:** Page 11, Adrian's workshop.
- **Who:** Alex and Bi11bot.

- **Who:** The unnamed Oasis watcher; Alex and Bi11bot are absent but represented by the signal.

- **The image the reader must see:** The lone amber point in dark Keel surrounded by a slowly closing cold-white ring.
- **Story function:** Ends the chapter on a question and an unseen pursuit.

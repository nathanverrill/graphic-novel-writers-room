## Page 2 (left) — Alex’s competence becomes an inherited liability

**Panel 1.** Wide, just before dawn, inside a Keel pump hall. Alex Phantum, eighteen, brown-skinned, lean, with short dense black curls, dark eyes, scarred work-rough hands, dark rolled-sleeve work shirt over a black tee, rust-brown patched cargo trousers, heavy scuffed boots and a worn canvas backpack on one straight shoulder strap, has one palm flat on an aging pump housing. He feels its vibration rather than reading the gauge. A projected maintenance procedure hovers beside him, unfollowed. A salvaged bracket replaces the approved part. A small white-and-amber Alpha node logs his shift. The pump-hall supervisor watches with folded arms.

SUPERVISOR: That's not the approved repair.

ALEX: Then don't approve it.

ALEX: Just notice it works.

**Panel 2.** Medium-wide at the end-of-shift checkpoint. Alex puts his hand on the scanner. Workers queue behind him; balances hover above them, but no crowd face needs to be recognizable. Danny, a lanky pump-hall worker with a buzz cut and an open grin, stands just ahead. His balance, previously small, becomes the largest in the queue when his scan completes. Everyone glances up.

The display reads:

**TODAY: DEBT TRANSFER DAY**  
**ALEX PHANTUM**  
**AGE VERIFIED: EIGHTEEN**  
**PARENTAL HOUSEHOLD LIABILITY TRANSFER: COMPLETE**  
**GUARANTOR: ADRIAN PHANTUM — WHEREABOUTS UNRECORDED**  
**DEBT BALANCE: 418,607 CREDITS**  
**EARNED TODAY: 40 CREDITS**  
**PROJECTED CLEARANCE: 28.7 YEARS**  
**TRAVEL STATUS: RESTRICTED**

SFX: BEEP

GUARD: Happy birthday, Phantum.

ALEX: I've had better surprises.

CAPTION: Eighteen: the age Keel decides you're old enough to owe.

**Panel 3.** Wide morning street outside **THE LAST CHANCE — PAWN & LOANS** and **NEXT LEVEL GAMES**. The pawn shop displays **CASH TODAY · EASY TERMS**. Used consoles and tools fill the shelves. A peeling default notice bearing Adrian’s mostly torn-away face hangs on the outer wall. The shopkeeper leans in the doorway. Danny emerges from the game shop with a used console under one arm and a game-shop bag in his hand, waving a printed loan slip. Across the street, people queue at a metered public tap marked **RATION: 4 L**.

DANNY: Alex! Look what I got! Ten a week. That's nothing!

SHOPKEEPER: Care to see if your luck runs better than his?

SHOPKEEPER: Your father always believed the next loan would change everything.

The slip reads: **ADVANCE 120 CR · REPAY 10 CR / WEEK · 31 WEEKS**. It flutters from Danny’s hand. Alex picks it up, pockets it without thinking and keeps walking, eyes ahead.

**Panel 4.** Split moment within the same Keel workday: the supervisor alone in the pump hall at the terminal, then Alex on the pit rim at sunrise. In the pump hall, the white-and-amber node blinks beside **UNAUTHORIZED REPAIR — REVIEW REQUIRED**. The pump hums steadily. The supervisor selects **ROUTINE MAINTENANCE**. A green check appears. At the pump’s base, a balanced water-drop symbol is scratched into an old valve plate.

SUPERVISOR (muttering): ...Works.

On the rim, the sealed mine entrance with faded hazard striping sits in the foreground. Alex stands beyond it, fists clenched, leaning toward Oasis, whose white towers catch the first sunlight while the basin remains in shadow.

CAPTION: Everyone in Keel knew where they had been born.  
CAPTION: Few believed they could choose where they belonged.

ALEX: One day... I'm leaving.

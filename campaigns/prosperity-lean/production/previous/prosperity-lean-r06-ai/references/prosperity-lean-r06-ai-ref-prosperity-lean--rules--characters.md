# Characters

These hold on every page. The drafts give way where they disagree.

## Alex Phantum

- **Who he is:** seventeen, from Keel. Lean, mechanically gifted, self-reliant. He fixes pumps by
  feel, palm flat on the housing, not by the manual.
- **Look:** brown skin, short dense black curls, dark eyes. Dark work shirt with rolled sleeves over
  a black tee, rust-brown cargo trousers with patches, heavy scuffed boots, a canvas tool bag on a
  shoulder strap. Scarred, work-rough hands.
- **Adrian's watch:** on his left wrist, a worn steel wristwatch a little big for him, left by his
  father. The caseback is engraved with a looping equation. It is the only thing he carries of
  Adrian's besides a creased photograph.
- **Nothing else on his wrists or in his hands by default:** no wristband, no coin, no chip.
- **His father:** Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Keel says Adrian threw everything away.
  Alex half believes it and resents him for it.
- **His family:** his grandmother, who is losing her memory. His mother left after Adrian lost
  everything, and we never learn more about her.

## Bi11bot

- **Look:** a rugged, weathered industrial robot, companion-sized. An oversized round head of
  segmented faded industrial-blue panels with a mustard-yellow stripe; two large round amber eyes
  in heavy metal rings; round ear assemblies on the sides; broad rounded shoulder plates; a chest
  plate marked "Bi11" in white; exposed pistons, articulated metal fingers, compact legs, broad feet.
  Chipped blue and mustard-yellow paint, rust, rivets, scratches. An old rust-red scarf at his neck.
- **The right eye ring** clicks and rotates when he focuses.
- **He folds.** He folds into a compact bundle small enough to carry in a backpack or tool bag.
  Folded up, with his eyes dimmed, he looks like a heap of old brass and steel, and an inspection
  reads him as unregistered scrap. Only one amber eye may show when he peeks out.
- **Never menacing.** Wise, patient, often quietly amused. No red or harsh light in his eyes, no
  looming low angles, no face lost in shadow. When a scene is tense, the tension is around him.
- **How he talks:** short, plain sentences. He asks more than he explains, and he never lectures.

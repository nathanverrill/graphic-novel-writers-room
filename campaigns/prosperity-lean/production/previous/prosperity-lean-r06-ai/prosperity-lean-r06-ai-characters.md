# Prosperity — Characters

## What the Showrunner Wants From These People

- **Canon:** Chapter 1 is exactly 12 pages: Pages 1–12, no more and no fewer.
- **Canon:** Alex Phantum and Bi11bot are the principal defined characters.
- **Canon:** Alex works as a maintenance mechanic for Keel’s public water pumps. He is not formally powerful or licensed to alter the larger system.
- **Canon:** Alex and Bi11bot are already concealed companions at the start of Chapter 1. Alex found Bi11bot in discarded mine equipment several months earlier and repaired enough of him to make him mobile.
- **Canon:** Alex conceals Bi11bot because an inspection would classify him as unregistered scrap and remove him.
- **Canon:** Alex’s circumstances are shaped by Keel: a bottom-level settlement built around an exhausted open-pit lithium mine, where water is scarce, machinery is repaired rather than replaced, residents queue at metered public taps, workers pass digital checkpoints showing what they owe, and travel up the mountain requires a permit.
- **Canon:** Alex’s father Adrian disappeared when Alex was two. Alex has Adrian’s worn steel watch and a creased photograph; Adrian’s ultimate fate is unresolved.
- **Canon:** Alex’s immediate character movement in Chapter 1 is from isolated repairer to someone who makes a dangerous repair for other people, despite knowing that the intervention is unauthorized.
- **Proposal:** Alex and Bi11bot have developed a practical, quiet trust over several months. Their bond is expressed through work, concealment, and mutual correction rather than open declarations.
- **Proposal:** Bi11bot’s original manufacture and function remain unknown in Chapter 1. His ability to recognize or respond to the hidden control architecture may become relevant, but he does not explain his origin.

## Characters

### Alex Phantum

**Visual lock:** Alex Phantum is seventeen years old, lean and wiry from constant physical work, approximately 5'8", with brown skin, short dense black curls, dark eyes, and a narrow, alert face marked by concentration rather than softness. He wears a dark work shirt with rolled sleeves over a black tee, rust-brown patched cargo trousers, heavy scuffed boots, and a canvas tool bag on a shoulder strap. His hands are scarred and work-rough. He wears Adrian’s oversized worn steel watch on his left wrist; he has no other wrist or hand accessory. His silhouette is compact and forward-leaning, with the tool bag, patched trousers, and oversized watch immediately readable. Keep him recognizable in every emotion: his eyes assess machinery before faces, his shoulders tense when cornered, and his scarred hands remain expressive.** `(Canon; rules/characters.md, brief.md)`

**Voice:** Alex speaks in clipped, practical sentences and prefers a concrete observation to an emotional explanation. He uses repair language naturally—pressure, drag, heat, flow, fault—but does not sound like a lecturer. Under stress he becomes more precise, not louder. He deflects questions about Adrian with dry impatience.  
- “The number says it’s working. The pipe says otherwise.”  
- “If you want me to fix it, stop telling me what it’s supposed to do.”

**Causality:**
- **Public role:** Seventeen-year-old Keel maintenance mechanic responsible for repairing public water pumps; skilled but not formally powerful or licensed to alter the larger system.
- **Immediate want:** Restore the failing Keel pump before the water queue is cut off and the remaining supply is redirected elsewhere.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** He needs to trust another person and accept that repair can be collective rather than solitary. He believes self-reliance protects people, but that belief leaves him carrying danger alone.
- **Leverage / capability:** He can diagnose pumps by touch, hearing, vibration, and flow rather than relying only on manuals or official readings. He understands patched machinery as a living chain of pressures and failures.
- **Vulnerability:** Keel’s water supply is personal and immediate; a failed repair harms neighbors who cannot leave. He also fears repeating Adrian’s abandonment and losing the few people or things that remain.
- **Mistaken belief:** If he is competent and keeps his head down, he can protect his grandmother, Bi11bot, and himself without becoming visible to the system.
- **Boundary at the start:** He will not knowingly tamper with Basin Charter water controls or endanger Keel’s public supply to pursue Adrian’s mystery.
- **Decision only Alex would make:** When he discovers the hidden diversion, he reopens the manual regulator to restore water to Keel, accepting that the system will register the intervention as unauthorized tampering.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Bi11bot’s patience and literal questions force Alex to acknowledge that concealment is not the same as safety. Adrian’s absence pressures Alex to distinguish inherited resentment from evidence.
- **Default behavior under pressure:** Put his palm flat on the housing, listen, identify the immediate physical failure, and act before explaining. When emotionally threatened, he becomes terse and attempts to solve the object in front of him.
- **Decision 1:** Alex chooses to investigate the discrepancy instead of accepting the pump’s official “normal” reading.  
  **→ Consequence:** He finds that the newer control module is diverting water away from Keel.  
  **→ Response from others:** Bi11bot urges caution while the public queue begins to lose patience and the system’s monitoring response becomes active.  
  **→ Alex’s next decision:** He enters the forbidden old works and uses the manual regulator to restore flow, linking Adrian’s watch to the control architecture.

**Arc in this book:** Alex begins as a capable boy who believes responsibility means repairing what is directly in front of him and hiding anything vulnerable. The failing pump forces him to choose between personal safety and the people in the queue. He becomes someone willing to make an unauthorized intervention for a collective need, even when that choice summons institutional attention and connects him to Adrian’s unresolved disappearance. **Proof of change:** He does not simply patch the visible pump and walk away; he enters the forbidden mine and reopens the manual regulator, knowingly creating a tamper alert to restore Keel’s water.

### Bi11bot

**Visual lock:** Bi11bot is a companion-sized rugged, weathered industrial robot with an oversized round head made of segmented faded industrial-blue panels and a mustard-yellow stripe. He has two large round amber eyes in heavy metal rings, round ear assemblies on the sides of his head, broad rounded shoulder plates, a chest plate marked “Bi11” in white, exposed pistons, articulated metal fingers, compact legs, and broad feet. His blue and mustard-yellow paint is chipped, with rust, rivets, and scratches. He wears an old rust-red scarf at his neck. His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses. He can fold into a compact bundle small enough to carry in a backpack or tool bag; folded, with his eyes dimmed, he looks like a heap of old brass and steel, and only one amber eye may show when he peeks out. He is never menacing: never use red or harsh eye light, a looming low angle, or face-hidden shadow.** `(Canon; rules/characters.md, brief.md)`

**Voice:** Bi11bot speaks in short, plain sentences. He asks more than he explains and never lectures. His questions are patient, literal, and sometimes quietly amusing; he does not use technical language to dominate a conversation.  
- “You heard the pipe. Why ask the meter?”  
- “Is this the safe choice, or only the familiar one?”

**Causality:**
- **Public role:** Concealed, unregistered-looking industrial robot and Alex’s existing companion.
- **Immediate want:** Keep Alex alive and concealed while helping him understand what the machinery is doing.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** He needs to be treated as a participant rather than as salvage Alex must hide or repair. His patience can become passive if he waits for Alex to decide everything.
- **Leverage / capability:** He can fold into a compact bundle, pass as scrap during inspection, focus through his rotating right eye ring, and manipulate machinery with articulated metal fingers. His unknown prior design may give him partial familiarity with mine systems.
- **Vulnerability:** An inspection can classify and remove him. His incomplete repairs and unknown registration make him dependent on Alex, while his concealment limits what he can safely do in public.
- **Mistaken belief:** If he remains useful and unobtrusive, Alex will not have to risk himself on Bi11bot’s behalf.
- **Boundary at the start:** He will not expose himself publicly or force Alex into the old works without Alex’s consent.
- **Decision only Bi11bot would make:** He chooses to reveal one amber eye and give Alex a precise warning rather than remain perfectly concealed, allowing Alex to notice that the official reading and the physical flow disagree.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Alex’s instinct to hide and protect him pressures Bi11bot to become more active in the partnership instead of waiting to be carried or repaired.
- **Default behavior under pressure:** Fold, observe, ask one clarifying question, then offer the smallest useful action. His right eye ring clicks and rotates when he focuses.
- **Decision 1:** Bi11bot risks a brief visible signal to direct Alex toward the physical bypass beneath the pump.  
  **→ Consequence:** Alex discovers the newer control module and realizes the failure is a diversion, not ordinary wear.  
  **→ Response from others:** Alex tries to order Bi11bot back into concealment, while the queue and monitoring system increase the pressure to act.  
  **→ Bi11bot’s next decision:** He follows Alex into the old works and assists with the manual regulator, accepting that their partnership is now part of the unauthorized intervention.

**Arc in this book:** Bi11bot begins as a concealed helper who measures his worth by usefulness and safety. He ends the chapter as an active partner who makes a visible, consequential choice alongside Alex. **Proof of change:** He does not remain folded and silent when Alex misreads the danger; he reveals enough of himself to guide Alex to the hidden bypass, then helps restore the flow despite the approaching system response.

### Adrian Phantum

**Visual lock:** Adrian Phantum is seen in Chapter 1 only through a creased photograph and the physical evidence he left behind. In the photograph he is a Keel maintenance worker in his late twenties, lean and long-limbed, with brown skin, thick dark hair, a tired angular face, and oil-darkened work clothes; the photograph is creased across one corner and should not be treated as a clean portrait. His primary present-tense visual anchor is the worn steel wristwatch he left Alex, oversized on Alex’s left wrist, with a looping equation engraved on the caseback. Do not show a definitive present-day body, age, location, injury, or death.** `(Canon for the photograph and watch; Proposal for the photograph’s visible physical details; rules/characters.md, brief.md)`

**Voice:** No present-day voice is established in Chapter 1. In remembered fragments or quoted phrases, Adrian should sound concise and mechanically attentive, offering observations rather than reassurance.  
- “A system tells you what it wants you to believe.”  
- “Check the pressure where nobody thinks to look.”

**Causality:**
- **Public role:** Unknown; the first present-tense evidence places his initials and an old maintenance mark inside the hidden control housing.
- **Immediate want:** Unknown. The looping equation is a calibration key or maintenance notation connected to the control architecture, but its full meaning is unresolved.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** Unknown; the available evidence suggests a conflict between maintaining the system and investigating what it concealed.
- **Leverage / capability:** He left behind the watch, equation, photograph, initials, and maintenance mark that allow Alex to connect his absence to the hidden water-control system.
- **Vulnerability:** His disappearance allowed Keel to define him as someone who “threw everything away,” leaving his motives vulnerable to rumor and Alex’s resentment.
- **Mistaken belief:** Unknown and not to be supplied through Chapter 1.
- **Boundary at the start:** Unknown.
- **Decision only Adrian would make:** Before the chapter, he marked or worked inside the control housing rather than leaving a public explanation; the reason remains unresolved.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** His absence pressures Alex to decide whether evidence deserves more trust than inherited civic rumor.
- **Default behavior under pressure:** Not established; do not invent a present-day performance.
- **Decision 1:** Adrian’s earlier, unseen decision to leave the maintenance mark and equation creates the clue Alex finds.  
  **→ Consequence:** Alex can connect his father to the hidden control architecture without learning Adrian’s fate.  
  **→ Response from others:** The system’s tamper response turns Adrian’s old evidence into a present danger.  
  **→ Alex’s next decision:** Alex chooses to act on the evidence by restoring Keel’s water rather than treating the watch as only a family relic.

**Arc in this book:** Adrian has no present-time arc in Chapter 1. His function is to shift from an absence explained by Keel’s accusation to an unresolved investigator or technician whose traces remain active in the infrastructure. **Proof of change:** Alex ends the chapter with evidence that Adrian worked on or investigated the control system after Keel believed he had “thrown everything away.” Adrian’s fate remains unresolved.

### Alex’s Grandmother

**Visual lock:** Alex’s grandmother is an older Keel woman whose exact age is not established, with a compact, work-worn body, brown skin, silver hair kept close to the head, and a lined face that can shift from sharp recognition to uncertainty as her memory loss surfaces. She wears layered, repaired Keel clothing in charcoal, faded blue, and rust-brown, with practical soft shoes and no jewelry or hand accessories by default. Her silhouette is small and sturdy, shaped by years of making do; keep her visually distinct from Alex through her silver hair, slower posture, and layered clothing.** `(Proposal; the supplied material fixes her family role and memory loss but not her look.)`

**Voice:** She speaks warmly but does not always maintain a continuous thread. She remembers practical details through touch, smell, and routine more reliably than dates. She should not become a convenient exposition device or deliver information she could not reasonably retain.  
- “You always listen to the machine before you listen to me.”  
- “That watch was loud in the night. Or was that someone else?”

**Causality:**
- **Public role:** Keel resident and Alex’s grandmother; she may depend on Alex, but the exact household arrangement remains flexible.
- **Immediate want:** Preserve familiar routines and remain treated as a person with agency rather than as a problem to manage.
- **Deeper need / contradiction:** She needs care without surrendering dignity. Her memory loss makes her both dependent on Alex and capable of surprising emotional pressure.
- **Leverage / capability:** She knows Alex’s routines and can identify when he is hiding fear behind work.
- **Vulnerability:** Her memory loss makes continuity fragile and can leave Alex afraid that any departure will become abandonment.
- **Mistaken belief:** If Alex keeps repairing things, the household can remain unchanged.
- **Boundary at the start:** She will not knowingly send Alex into danger, though she may not remember every danger consistently.
- **Decision only she would make:** If used in Chapter 1, she tells Alex to go restore the public water despite understanding that he is hiding something, choosing the neighbors’ immediate need over her own reassurance.
- **Relationship that pressures change:** Her dependence pressures Alex to return safely; her insistence on his responsibility pressures him to define what he owes beyond the household.
- **Default behavior under pressure:** Reach for a familiar object or routine, then notice the emotional truth even if the factual sequence is incomplete.

**Arc in this book:** She remains largely offstage in Chapter 1 and does not receive a standalone arc. Her presence gives Alex’s risk a personal cost without resolving her memory loss. **Proof of change or refusal to change:** Not applicable at chapter scale; do not use her memory loss to explain Adrian’s investigation.

## Ensemble Test

- **Alex notices:** vibration, pressure, heat, flow, physical wear, and the practical consequences of a broken mechanism. He values competence, immediate relief, and protecting people through action. He can repair, interpret, and choose under physical pressure. He fears abandonment, helplessness, and becoming visible to authority.
- **Bi11bot notices:** patterns, questions, concealment risks, and inconsistencies between what a system reports and what it does. He values patience, companionship, and proportionate action. He can fold, observe, focus, and assist without replacing Alex’s judgment. He fears removal and becoming only an object to be hidden.
- **Adrian is defined by absence:** his traces turn technical evidence into personal evidence. He values or may have valued investigation over accepted explanations, but his motives remain unknown. His leverage is the watch, equation, initials, and maintenance mark.
- **Grandmother notices:** routine, emotional shifts, and familiar objects, though memory loss disrupts continuity. She values dignity and household continuity. Her pressure on Alex is intimate rather than technical.
- **Mother functions as unresolved absence:** she represents a missing account of the family’s collapse and does not duplicate Adrian’s active evidence in Chapter 1.
- **Visual separation:** Alex is a compact, living mechanic in dark work clothes, rust-brown patched trousers, a canvas tool bag, scarred hands, and an oversized steel watch. Bi11bot is a rounded blue-and-mustard industrial robot with amber eyes, exposed pistons, and a rust-red scarf. Adrian appears only through a creased photograph, watch, equation, initials, and maintenance mark. Grandmother is a smaller silver-haired figure in layered repaired clothing. Mother is not depicted.
- **Dramatic separation:** Alex acts first; Bi11bot questions and redirects; Adrian creates the mystery through traces; Grandmother embodies the cost of Alex’s absence; Mother remains an unresolved gap rather than an active participant.

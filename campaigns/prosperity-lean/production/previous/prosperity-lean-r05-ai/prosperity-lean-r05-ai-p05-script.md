## Page 5 (right) — Alex reads the buried system through pressure and heat

**Panel 1.** Alex and Bi11bot move along the old rails through the damp gallery. Alex presses his scarred palm to a riveted pipe while Bi11bot compares two adjacent lines with his metal fingers. One pipe is beaded with condensation; the other is dry and cold.

**SFX:** HMMMMM

**ALEX:** This line carries the pressure.

**BI11BOT:** The newer one?

**ALEX:** Newer doesn’t mean stronger.

**Panel 2.** They reach a regulator gallery. Old analog gauges and manual wheels fill the wall. A clean cable and narrow control line have been grafted across the rusted machinery, disappearing into a compact white-and-amber node.

Alex crouches beside the junction, following the newer line with two fingers.

**ALEX:** Someone fed a new control into an old regulator.

**BI11BOT:** Is that allowed?

**ALEX:** It’s installed.

**Panel 3.** Alex traces the hidden line to a newer control housing bolted over an old valve plate. Mineral crust seals its edges. The white-and-amber node gives a faint pulse.

Bi11bot leans close, his right eye ring rotating.

**SFX:** PIP

**BI11BOT:** It is listening.

**ALEX:** It’s measuring.

**BI11BOT:** To what?

**ALEX:** Everything it can reach.

**Panel 4.** Alex scrapes mineral crust from the housing with a flat tool. Beneath the crust, a repeating loop of etched marks appears around the node.

The shape echoes the looping equation engraved on Adrian’s watch, still loose on Alex’s left wrist.

Alex freezes—not with his face, but with the tool stopped above the metal.

# layouts.md

## Base page system

**Format:** 6.625" × 10.25", full color, Western left-to-right reading.  
**Base grid:** Four-panel variable tiers, with wide establishing panels, close mechanical inserts, and one full-page splash for the override.  
**Page rhythm:** Pages 1–3 compress the public crisis and discovery; Pages 4–9 open into the mine and build toward the choice; Page 10 is the physical climax; Pages 11–12 are aftermath, escape, and the chapter question.  
**Lettering rule:** Artwork contains no rendered text. Every lettering item below requires its specified clear zone. Displays, SFX, balloons, faces, and critical props occupy separate zones.

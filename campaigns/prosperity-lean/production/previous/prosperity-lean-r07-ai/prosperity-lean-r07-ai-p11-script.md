## Page 11 (right) — Keel gets water, and the system identifies the breach

**Panel 1.** At the Keel pump station, water bursts from the public taps into waiting jugs. We see only a few hands, containers, and the restored stream—not a recognizable crowd.

The digital display still reads **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**.

**SFX:** SPLASH—SPLASH

**QUEUE WORKER (off-panel):** Fill them! Move!

**Panel 2.** Back in the mine gallery, the white-and-amber node sends a visible pulse down the cable. A local metal shutter begins descending across the gallery exit.

Alex sees the shutter, then the open regulator.

**SFX:** WHUM… WHUM…

**BI11BOT:** It is closing.

**ALEX:** I see that too.

**BI11BOT:** You say that often.

**Panel 3.** Alex folds Bi11bot rapidly into a compact blue-and-mustard bundle. One amber eye remains lit. Alex tucks the bundle beneath his arm and runs toward the narrowing gap, tool bag over his opposite shoulder.

The shutter drops behind him, sealing the regulator gallery.

**SFX:** GRRRAAAK

**ALEX:** Hold on.

**BI11BOT:** I am.

**Panel 4.** Alex slips through the gap into a colder side chamber just as the shutter slams down. The bundle unfolds against his chest. Beyond the bars, the node continues pulsing through the sealed gallery.

Far inside the mine, a second mechanical sound answers.

**SFX:** CLANG

**BI11BOT:** Someone heard it.

**ALEX:** Not someone.

**PAGE CHECK:** Keel’s public water returns, but the system identifies the unauthorized intervention. Alex abandons concealment to carry Bi11bot through the closing shutter. The immediate escape route is sealed; a deeper chamber remains open.

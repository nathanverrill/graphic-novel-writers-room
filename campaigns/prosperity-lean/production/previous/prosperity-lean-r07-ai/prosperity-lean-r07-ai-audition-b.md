# Prosperity Lean — Chapter 1 Audition

## Page 1 (right) — Alex takes responsibility for a failing public pump

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing shot of a Keel public water point built against patched mining infrastructure. Rusted pump housings, narrow channels, stacked structures, dust. A short queue of residents waits with jugs at three metered taps. Above them, a digital display reads: **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**. Alex pushes toward the pump in his dark work clothes, rust-brown patched cargo trousers, scuffed boots, canvas tool bag, and oversized steel watch on his left wrist.

A small section of the tool bag is unzipped. One dim amber eye peers out—Bi11bot, concealed inside.

**QUEUE WORKER:** It’s gone dry again.

**ALEX:** Move the jugs back.

**Panel 2.** Close on Alex’s scarred hand gripping the pump’s patched housing. The metal shudders beneath his palm. Behind him, residents lift their empty jugs and look toward the display, which still reads **NORMAL**.

**ALEX:** Don’t hit it.

A resident’s hand stops halfway to the housing.

**Panel 3.** Alex drops his tool bag beside the pump and kneels, opening a rusted access panel. Tools lie ready on the concrete. His eyes stay on the machinery, not the people.

From inside the bag, Bi11bot’s small metal finger nudges the zipper wider. The amber eye blinks once.

**BI11BOT (from the bag):** It is hurting.

**ALEX:** Pumps don’t hurt.

**BI11BOT:** This one does.

**Panel 4.** Alex looks toward the queue. A child’s empty jug rests beneath the dead tap. The digital display above them remains calm and green-white.

Alex rolls up his sleeve. Adrian’s oversized watch slips down his left wrist.

**ALEX:** Give me three minutes.

**QUEUE WORKER:** You have two.

**PAGE CHECK:** The public objective is clear: keep Keel’s taps flowing before cutoff. Alex’s tactile skill is established; Bi11bot is concealed but active. The official allocation reading contradicts the failing physical pump.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex finds proof that the official reading is not measuring delivered water

**Panel 1.** Same pump station, tighter and more mechanical. Alex has removed the access panel. Inside: patched wiring, a pressure gauge, mineral-crusted fittings, and a narrow service channel. Alex presses two fingers to the gauge housing while watching the needle sink toward zero.

The digital display remains visible in the background: **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**.

**ALEX:** The allocation’s fine.

**BI11BOT (from the bag):** The water is not.

**Panel 2.** Alex loosens a corroded fitting. A weak spit of water strikes his knuckles. He studies the spray, then touches the pipe above and below the joint.

**SFX:** TIK—TIK—TIK

**ALEX:** Pressure drops here.

**QUEUE WORKER:** Then fix here.

**ALEX:** I’m looking.

**Panel 3.** Alex wedges a flat tool beneath the pump’s inner lining. The lining flexes. Behind it, a second, newer pipe appears—cleaner metal, vibrating hard, carrying water away from the public outlet. A thin stream runs along the bottom of the housing.

Bi11bot’s amber eye and one articulated finger emerge from the tool bag, pointing toward the hidden pipe.

**SFX:** VVVVVV

**BI11BOT:** That pipe is full.

Alex follows the pipe with his eyes.

**Panel 4.** Page-turn reveal. Extreme close-up of the concealed pipe disappearing through a dark gap in the pump station wall. Its valve pulses with each unseen surge. Water moves away from the dry public line.

In the foreground, Alex’s scarred hand touches the pipe. His steel watch hangs beside it, its scratched caseback just visible.

**SFX:** THUMM—THUMM—THUMM

**ALEX:** That isn’t a failure.

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex identifies a second active flow path diverting water away from Keel while the authorized-allocation display remains normal. Bi11bot makes the small, consequential warning that directs Alex toward the bypass without taking over the diagnosis. Page-turn reveal: the water is being routed elsewhere.

## Page 3 (right) — Alex chooses the forbidden route beneath the station

**Panel 1.** Alex follows the hidden pipe through a cramped service trench behind the pump station. He pulls aside a patched conduit cover. The pipe continues downward through old concrete toward the buried mine works.

Above the trench opening, the public taps and waiting jugs are visible through the station framework. The queue has tightened around the dry outlets.

**QUEUE WORKER (off-panel):** Last warning! If the line doesn’t recover, the remaining allocation goes uphill!

**Panel 2.** Alex crouches at the trench’s end. The newer pipe passes through a rough maintenance breach in the concrete foundation. Beyond it: darkness, damp rock, a length of rusted rail, and a faded strip of hazard paint on an older sealed wall—the forbidden mine boundary is nearby, but this narrow breach bypasses it.

Bi11bot unfolds halfway from the tool bag, compact blue-and-mustard panels, soft amber eyes, rust-red scarf. He braces one metal hand against the concrete.

**BI11BOT:** The pipe continues.

**ALEX:** I can see that.

**BI11BOT:** The dark part too?

**Panel 3.** Alex shines a small work light into the breach. Cold condensation beads on the newer pipe. Far below, an old analog gauge glows faintly amber.

Alex glances back toward the dry public taps, then down into the breach.

**ALEX:** It’s not a route. It’s a maintenance gap.

**BI11BOT:** Is that different?

**ALEX:** It is if someone asks.

**Panel 4.** Alex slings the tool bag over his shoulder. Bi11bot folds down into a compact bundle, one amber eye still open as Alex reaches for him. Behind them, the public pump’s display continues to report **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL** while the taps remain dry.

Alex steps into the breach.

**ALEX:** Stay quiet.

**BI11BOT:** I am quiet.

**ALEX:** You’re asking questions.

**BI11BOT:** Quietly.

**PAGE CHECK:** The hidden route reaches a maintenance breach beneath the pump station, bypassing the nearby sealed and hazard-marked mine boundary. The public water will be redirected if Alex cannot restore flow. Alex chooses to enter the forbidden works with Bi11bot, turning suspicion into action.

## Page 4 (left) — The breach opens into an active mine

**Panel 1.** Large vertical reveal. Alex climbs out of the narrow maintenance breach into a cold, spacious mine tunnel. Rusted rails run into darkness. Riveted pipes cross damp rock walls. Condensation glints in the beam of Alex’s work light. Far below, an isolated analog gauge burns with faint amber light.

Alex is small in the frame, one hand gripping the tool bag strap. The old mine’s scale dwarfs him.

**SFX:** DRIP… DRIP…

**Panel 2.** Alex sets the tool bag down. Bi11bot unfolds onto the rails, blue segmented head and mustard stripe emerging first, rust-red scarf hanging loose. His amber eyes are gentle and readable.

**BI11BOT:** We are inside.

**ALEX:** Don’t announce it.

**BI11BOT:** I was informing you.

**Panel 3.** Alex looks back toward the breach. Above it, glimpsed through the concrete and service trench, the faint green-white public allocation display still reads **NORMAL**.

He turns toward the amber gauge.

**ALEX:** Find the pipe.

**BI11BOT:** You said stay quiet.

**ALEX:** I changed my mind.

**Panel 4.** Bi11bot’s right eye ring clicks and rotates toward the warm pipe. He points down the rails. Alex follows his indication, lifting the work light.

**SFX:** CLICK

**BI11BOT:** That one is warmer.

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex and Bi11bot cross the forbidden boundary into the old works. The mine is visibly active, not abandoned. Bi11bot’s observation gives them a direction without explaining the system.

# Prosperity Lean — Chapter 1

## Page 1 (right) — Alex takes responsibility for a failing public pump

**Panel 1.** Wide establishing shot of a Keel public water point built against patched mining infrastructure. Rusted pump housings, narrow channels, stacked structures, dust. A short queue of residents waits with jugs at three metered taps. Above them, a digital display reads: **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**. Alex pushes toward the pump in dark work clothes, rust-brown patched cargo trousers, scuffed boots, canvas tool bag, and Adrian’s oversized steel watch on his left wrist.

A small section of the tool bag is unzipped. One dim amber eye peers out—Bi11bot, concealed inside.

**QUEUE WORKER:** It’s gone dry again.

**ALEX:** Move the jugs back.

**Panel 2.** Close on Alex’s scarred hand gripping the pump’s patched housing. The metal shudders beneath his palm. Behind him, residents lift their empty jugs toward the display, which still reads **NORMAL**.

**ALEX:** Don’t hit it.

A resident’s hand stops halfway to the housing.

**Panel 3.** Alex drops his tool bag beside the pump and kneels, opening a rusted access panel. Tools lie ready on the concrete. His eyes stay on the machinery, not the people.

From inside the bag, Bi11bot’s small metal finger nudges the zipper wider. The amber eye blinks once.

**BI11BOT (from the bag):** It is hurting.

**ALEX:** Pumps don’t hurt.

**BI11BOT:** This one does.

**Panel 4.** Alex looks toward the queue. A child’s empty jug rests beneath the dead tap. The digital display remains calm and green-white.

Alex rolls up his sleeve. Adrian’s oversized watch slips down his left wrist.

**ALEX:** Give me three minutes.

**QUEUE WORKER:** You have two.

**PAGE CHECK:** The public objective is clear: keep Keel’s taps flowing before cutoff. Alex’s tactile skill is established; Bi11bot is concealed but active. The official allocation reading contradicts the failing physical pump.

## Page 2 (left) — Alex finds proof that the official reading is not measuring delivered water

**Panel 1.** Same pump station, tighter and more mechanical. Alex has removed the access panel. Inside: patched wiring, a pressure gauge, mineral-crusted fittings, and a narrow service channel. Alex presses two fingers to the gauge housing while watching the needle sink toward zero.

The digital display remains visible in the background: **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**.

**ALEX:** The allocation’s fine.

**BI11BOT (from the bag):** The water is not.

**Panel 2.** Alex loosens a corroded fitting. A weak spit of water strikes his knuckles. He studies the spray, then touches the pipe above and below the joint.

**SFX:** TIK—TIK—TIK

**ALEX:** Pressure drops here.

**QUEUE WORKER:** Then fix here.

**ALEX:** I’m looking.

**Panel 3.** Alex wedges a flat tool beneath the pump’s inner lining. The lining flexes. Behind it, a second, newer pipe appears—cleaner metal, vibrating hard, carrying water away from the public outlet. A thin stream runs along the bottom of the housing.

Bi11bot’s amber eye and one articulated finger emerge from the tool bag, pointing toward the hidden pipe.

**SFX:** VVVVVV

**BI11BOT:** That pipe is full.

Alex follows the pipe with his eyes.

**Panel 4.** Page-turn reveal. Extreme close-up of the concealed pipe disappearing through a dark gap in the pump station wall. Its valve pulses with each unseen surge. Water moves away from the dry public line.

In the foreground, Alex’s scarred hand touches the pipe. His steel watch hangs beside it, its scratched caseback just visible.

**SFX:** THUMM—THUMM—THUMM

**ALEX:** That isn’t a failure.

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex identifies a second active flow path routing water away from Keel while the authorized-allocation display remains normal. Bi11bot makes the small warning that directs Alex toward the bypass without taking over the diagnosis.

## Page 3 (right) — Alex chooses the forbidden route beneath the station

**Panel 1.** Alex follows the hidden pipe through a cramped service trench behind the pump station. He pulls aside a patched conduit cover. The pipe continues downward through old concrete toward the buried mine works.

Above the trench opening, the public taps and waiting jugs are visible through the station framework. The queue has tightened around the dry outlets.

**QUEUE WORKER (off-panel):** Last warning! If the line doesn’t recover, the remaining allocation goes uphill!

**Panel 2.** Alex crouches at the trench’s end. The newer pipe passes through a rough maintenance breach in the concrete foundation. Beyond it: darkness, damp rock, a length of rusted rail, and a faded strip of hazard paint on an older sealed wall—the forbidden mine boundary is nearby, but this narrow breach bypasses it.

Bi11bot unfolds halfway from the tool bag, compact blue-and-mustard panels, soft amber eyes, rust-red scarf. He braces one metal hand against the concrete.

**BI11BOT:** The pipe continues.

**ALEX:** I can see that.

**BI11BOT:** The dark part too?

**Panel 3.** Alex shines a small work light into the breach. Cold condensation beads on the newer pipe. Far below, an old analog gauge glows faintly amber.

Alex glances back toward the dry public taps, then down into the breach.

**ALEX:** It’s not a route. It’s a maintenance gap.

**BI11BOT:** Is that different?

**ALEX:** It is if someone asks.

**Panel 4.** Alex slings the tool bag over his shoulder. Bi11bot folds down into a compact bundle, one amber eye still open as Alex reaches for him. Behind them, the public pump’s display continues to report **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL** while the taps remain dry.

Alex steps into the breach.

**ALEX:** Stay quiet.

**BI11BOT:** I am quiet.

**ALEX:** You’re asking questions.

**BI11BOT:** Quietly.

**PAGE CHECK:** The hidden route reaches a maintenance breach beneath the pump station, bypassing the nearby sealed and hazard-marked mine boundary. Alex chooses to enter the forbidden works with Bi11bot.

## Page 4 (left) — The breach opens into an active mine

**Panel 1.** Large vertical reveal. Alex climbs out of the narrow maintenance breach into a cold, spacious mine tunnel. Rusted rails run into darkness. Riveted pipes cross damp rock walls. Condensation glints in the beam of Alex’s work light. Far below, an isolated analog gauge burns with faint amber light.

Alex is small in the frame, one hand gripping the tool bag strap. The old mine’s scale dwarfs him.

**SFX:** DRIP… DRIP…

**Panel 2.** Alex sets the tool bag down. Bi11bot unfolds onto the rails, blue segmented head and mustard stripe emerging first, rust-red scarf hanging loose. His amber eyes are gentle and readable.

**BI11BOT:** We are inside.

**ALEX:** Don’t announce it.

**BI11BOT:** I was informing you.

**Panel 3.** Alex looks back toward the breach. Above it, glimpsed through the concrete and service trench, the faint green-white public allocation display still reads **NORMAL**.

He turns toward the amber gauge.

**ALEX:** Find the pipe.

**BI11BOT:** You said stay quiet.

**ALEX:** I changed my mind.

**Panel 4.** Bi11bot’s right eye ring clicks and rotates toward the warm pipe. He points down the rails. Alex follows his indication, lifting the work light.

**SFX:** CLICK

**BI11BOT:** That one is warmer.

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex and Bi11bot cross the forbidden boundary into the old works. The mine is visibly active, not abandoned. Bi11bot’s observation gives them a direction without explaining the system.

## Page 5 (right) — Alex reads the buried system through pressure and heat

**Panel 1.** Alex and Bi11bot move along the old rails through the damp gallery. Alex presses his scarred palm to a riveted pipe while Bi11bot compares two adjacent lines with his metal fingers. One pipe is beaded with condensation; the other is dry and cold.

**SFX:** HMMMMM

**ALEX:** This line carries the pressure.

**BI11BOT:** The newer one?

**ALEX:** Newer doesn’t mean stronger.

**Panel 2.** They reach a regulator gallery. Old analog gauges and manual wheels fill the wall. A clean cable and narrow control line have been grafted across the rusted machinery, disappearing into a compact white-and-amber node.

Alex crouches beside the junction, following the newer line with two fingers.

**ALEX:** Someone fed a new control into an old regulator.

**BI11BOT:** Is that allowed?

**ALEX:** It’s installed.

**Panel 3.** Alex traces the hidden line to a newer control housing bolted over an old valve plate. Mineral crust seals its edges. The white-and-amber node gives a faint pulse.

Bi11bot leans close, his right eye ring rotating.

**SFX:** PIP

**BI11BOT:** It is listening.

**ALEX:** It’s measuring.

**BI11BOT:** To what?

**ALEX:** Everything it can reach.

**Panel 4.** Alex scrapes mineral crust from the housing with a flat tool. Beneath the crust, a repeating loop of etched marks appears around the node.

The shape echoes the looping equation engraved on Adrian’s watch, still loose on Alex’s left wrist.

Alex freezes—not with his face, but with the tool stopped above the metal.

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex follows physical evidence rather than signage and finds newer Water House control equipment grafted onto the old manual system. The node’s pattern resembles Adrian’s looping equation.

## Page 6 (left) — Adrian’s watch becomes a pattern, not an answer

**Panel 1.** Alex opens Adrian’s steel watch caseback. The looping equation is engraved across the scratched metal. He holds it beside the control housing without touching either.

The etched loops on the housing repeat the same turns, but not in the same order.

**BI11BOT:** Same shape.

**ALEX:** Similar.

**BI11BOT:** You said same.

**ALEX:** I changed my mind.

**Panel 2.** Alex uses the watch equation as a visual reference, moving his work light across three recessed ports on the housing. He does not insert the watch or use it as a key. His scarred finger follows the sequence.

**ALEX:** This one. Then the lower port. Then the return.

**BI11BOT:** What happens then?

**ALEX:** We find out what it’s hiding.

**Panel 3.** Bi11bot braces the housing with both rounded hands while Alex loosens two corroded fasteners. His amber eyes remain on Alex’s hands.

**SFX:** KRRRNNK

**BI11BOT:** You are pulling too hard.

**ALEX:** It’s stuck.

**BI11BOT:** Those are different things.

**Panel 4.** The control panel comes loose by a fraction. Behind it, old metal bears a partial maintenance mark beneath the newer assembly. The mark is not yet readable.

A thin amber light flickers behind the panel.

**SFX:** PIP… PIP…

**PAGE CHECK:** The watch helps Alex recognize a calibration pattern and identify where to inspect; it does not unlock or explain the assembly. The older maintenance mark is introduced as separate evidence beneath newer hardware.

## Page 7 (right) — Alex finds Adrian’s mark

**Panel 1.** Alex removes the control panel and sets it on the wet stone. Bi11bot holds a small work light close. The housing interior shows old manual contacts, newer routed cables, and mineral residue.

Alex wipes the residue from the maintenance mark with his thumb.

**SFX:** SCRITCH

**Panel 2.** The mark resolves into **A.P.** beside a dated maintenance notation. The date is visibly years old but too obscured to read completely.

Alex’s thumb remains against the initials. His watch hangs beside them.

**BI11BOT:** Is it yours?

**ALEX:** No.

**BI11BOT:** You know whose.

**ALEX:** I know the handwriting.

**Panel 3.** Alex pulls the creased photograph from an inner pocket of his work shirt. It shows Adrian as a young Keel maintenance worker in oil-darkened clothes. The photograph is creased across one corner.

Alex compares the angular handwritten initials on the photograph’s back to the mark inside the housing.

**ALEX:** He was here.

**BI11BOT:** That is not the same as why.

**ALEX:** I know.

**Panel 4.** Behind the old mark, a newer live control channel gives a faint pulse. A narrow indicator shows water being routed away from the manual regulator toward the basin spine.

Alex lowers the photograph but does not put it away.

**SFX:** THUMM

**ALEX:** He didn’t throw everything away.

**PAGE CHECK:** Adrian’s initials and maintenance mark establish his presence or investigation here, not his motive or fate. The active control channel is visually separate from Adrian’s older mark and remains unexplained.

## Page 8 (left) — The repair becomes a choice

**Panel 1.** Alex follows the live channel with his fingers. A small internal valve shifts, routing pressure away from the old regulator. On a narrow analog gauge, Keel’s line drops while the basin-spine line rises.

The white-and-amber node pulses in a steady relay rhythm.

**BI11BOT:** The water is leaving.

**ALEX:** I can see that.

**BI11BOT:** It is being told to.

**ALEX:** By what?

Bi11bot looks at the node.

**BI11BOT:** That is the part I cannot see.

**Panel 2.** Alex studies the manual regulator wheel, then the newer control housing. A faded plate beside the wheel reads: **MANUAL OVERRIDE — UNAUTHORIZED OPERATION REPORTS TO NETWORK**.

The warning is partly obscured by rust, but legible.

**BI11BOT:** We can close the panel.

**ALEX:** And leave the line open.

**BI11BOT:** Yes.

**ALEX:** Keel stays dry.

**Panel 3.** Alex reaches for the seized manual wheel. His left wristwatch slips against the metal. Bi11bot extends one articulated hand toward the wheel, waiting rather than grabbing it.

**BI11BOT:** Is this the safe choice?

Alex looks toward the dark route back to Keel, then at Adrian’s initials.

**ALEX:** No.

**BI11BOT:** The familiar one?

**ALEX:** Also no.

**Panel 4.** Alex plants both scarred hands on the wheel. Bi11bot braces the control housing beside him. The node’s amber indicator turns white.

**SFX:** PIP—PIP—PIP

**ALEX:** Hold it.

**BI11BOT:** I am holding it.

**PAGE CHECK:** The system is actively routing water away from Keel, but motive remains unknown. Alex understands that the manual override will report tampering and chooses it because the public taps will remain dry otherwise.

## Page 9 (right) — Alex and Bi11bot work as partners

**Panel 1.** Alex strains against the seized manual wheel. It barely moves. The old gauge climbs toward the red end of its painted scale. Water beads and trembles along the regulator housing.

Bi11bot braces his feet against the rail and locks both hands around the housing.

**SFX:** KRRRNNK

**BI11BOT:** The pressure is rising.

**ALEX:** I know.

**BI11BOT:** The wheel does not.

**ALEX:** Then make it listen.

**Panel 2.** Bi11bot shifts his weight and wedges one articulated finger beneath a locking tooth. His right eye ring clicks rapidly as he focuses on the mechanism.

**SFX:** CLICK-CLICK-CLICK

**BI11BOT:** Now.

Alex changes his grip and drives the wheel downward.

**Panel 3.** The newer control assembly flashes white and amber. A thin line of light travels from the node into the cable disappearing down the mine tunnel.

The old gauge needle slams against its stop.

**SFX:** BEEP—BEEP—BEEP

**BI11BOT:** It noticed.

**ALEX:** Let it.

**Panel 4.** The manual wheel moves a fraction. Alex’s boots slide on wet stone. Bi11bot catches the housing before it tears free.

Cold water sprays from a seam and crosses both of them.

**SFX:** CHK-KK

**ALEX:** Again.

**BI11BOT:** Together?

Alex looks at him.

**ALEX:** Together.

**PAGE CHECK:** The easy repair is impossible. Alex changes tactics from diagnosis to force; Bi11bot changes from concealed helper to physical partner. The system registers the intervention.

## Page 10 (left) — The manual override restores Keel’s flow

**Panel 1.** Full-page splash. Alex and Bi11bot wrench the manual wheel open together. The old mechanism breaks free with a violent metallic jolt.

A broad surge of cold water erupts through the regulator channel, blasting across the chamber and racing into the pipe toward Keel. Alex is thrown to one knee but keeps one hand on the wheel. Bi11bot anchors himself against the rail, rounded body turned protectively toward Alex without menace.

The white-and-amber node wakes behind them, bright enough to cut through the damp darkness.

**SFX:** KRAK—WHOOOOSH

**ALEX:** Go!

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex’s unauthorized choice succeeds physically: water is moving back toward Keel. The node wakes and begins reporting the breach. Page-turn function: the restored flow and system response arrive together.

## Page 11 (right) — Keel gets water, and the system identifies the breach

**Panel 1.** At the Keel pump station, water bursts from the public taps into waiting jugs. We see only a few hands, containers, and the restored stream—not a recognizable crowd.

The digital display still reads **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL**.

**SFX:** SPLASH—SPLASH

**QUEUE WORKER (off-panel):** Fill them! Move!

**Panel 2.** Back in the mine gallery, the white-and-amber node sends a visible pulse down the cable. A local metal shutter begins descending across the gallery exit.

Alex sees the shutter, then the open regulator.

**SFX:** WHUM… WHUM…

**BI11BOT:** It is closing.

**ALEX:** I see that too.

**BI11BOT:** You say that often.

**Panel 3.** Alex folds Bi11bot rapidly into a compact blue-and-mustard bundle. One amber eye remains lit. Alex tucks the bundle beneath his arm and runs toward the narrowing gap, tool bag over his opposite shoulder.

The shutter drops behind him, sealing the regulator gallery.

**SFX:** GRRRAAAK

**ALEX:** Hold on.

**BI11BOT:** I am.

**Panel 4.** Alex slips through the gap into a colder side chamber just as the shutter slams down. The bundle unfolds against his chest. Beyond the bars, the node continues pulsing through the sealed gallery.

Far inside the mine, a second mechanical sound answers.

**SFX:** CLANG

**BI11BOT:** Someone heard it.

**ALEX:** Not someone.

**PAGE CHECK:** Keel’s public water returns, but the system identifies the unauthorized intervention. Alex abandons concealment to carry Bi11bot through the closing shutter. The immediate escape route is sealed; a deeper chamber remains open.

## Page 12 (left) — Alex finds evidence of Adrian and a threat moving toward them

**Panel 1.** Large closing image. Alex and Bi11bot stand in a colder control chamber beyond the sealed shutter. Damp rock, rusted rails, and old manual pipes recede into darkness. The chamber is lit by the awakened white-and-amber node behind them.

Alex holds Adrian’s opened steel watch against the node’s repeating equation. The marks align visually, but no mechanism opens.

Bi11bot stands beside him, fully unfolded, soft amber eyes facing the dark tunnel ahead.

**SFX:** TIK… TIK… TIK…

**Panel 2.** Close on the watch and node together. The looping equation on the watch matches the control pattern. A separate indicator flashes along the node’s edge: **UNAUTHORIZED MANUAL INTERVENTION**.

Alex’s scarred thumb grips the watch case.

**BI11BOT:** Your father knew this place.

**ALEX:** He worked here.

**BI11BOT:** Is that better?

Alex looks toward the dark tunnel.

**ALEX:** It’s evidence.

**Panel 3.** The distant mechanical response grows louder. A pale work-light sweeps once across the far tunnel wall, too distant to reveal what carries it.

Bi11bot’s right eye ring clicks toward the sound.

**SFX:** KLANG… KLANG… KLANG…

**BI11BOT:** What do we do now?

**Panel 4.** Alex closes his fingers around the open watch but keeps it pressed against the matching equation. He turns away from the sealed shutter and toward the deeper tunnel, placing himself between Bi11bot and the approaching light.

**ALEX:** We find out who he was trying to reach.

The mechanical sound answers from the dark.

**SFX:** KLANG

**PAGE CHECK:** Alex and Bi11bot are temporarily sealed inside the cold control chamber, with a deeper route ahead and an approaching mechanical response behind them. Adrian’s presence is confirmed, but his motive and fate remain unresolved. The chapter ends on a question about Adrian and the person or system now coming for Alex.

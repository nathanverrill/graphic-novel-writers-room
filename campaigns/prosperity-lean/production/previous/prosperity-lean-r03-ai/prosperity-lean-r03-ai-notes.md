# Verdict

Needs a pass. The 12-page script has a clear causal chain, respects the showrunner’s drawability limits, and preserves the intended ending question. One development-level authority contradiction remains a blocker, while several page-level continuity and staging issues should be corrected before layouts. The script otherwise delivers the brief without requiring a structural rethink.

## Findings by severity

### Blocker

- **Location:** `brief.md` §7 World canon; `world.md`, “Governance and Power” and “Open”
- **Problem:** The brief establishes the Basin Charter as the central postwar authority, with operating power delegated to the three Charter Houses. `world.md` still says the governing authority is unknown and leaves open whether the Charter Houses, a central government, or another institution controls the infrastructure. These are incompatible project truths.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Update `world.md` to make the Basin Charter the settled central authority and the Charter Houses delegated operators. Retain uncertainty about their internal structure and their exact relationship to local civic administration.
- **Role:** Director

### Major

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 3–4
- **Problem:** The route is visually understandable as a bypass, but the terminology still shifts between the sealed mine entrance, “mine boundary,” “maintenance breach,” and “concrete-sealed mine entrance.” Page 3 shows the hazard-marked seal nearby; Page 4 shows Alex emerging into the works, but the reader must infer exactly what he bypassed and how the breach connects to the old works.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Keep the established bypass. In the Page 3 panel description, explicitly identify the hazard-marked concrete as the sealed pit-rim entrance and the adjacent opening as an older service breach leading around it. Do not describe Alex as reaching or opening the sealed entrance.
- **Role:** Writer

- **Location:** `script.md`, Pages 11–12
- **Problem:** The final physical geography is slightly contradictory. Page 11 says the shutter seals the regulator gallery and Alex reaches a colder side chamber; Page 12 says the awakened node is “behind them,” while the node was previously located in the sealed gallery. The final image needs to establish whether Alex can still see the node through the shutter or whether a second matching node is present in the side chamber.
- **Smallest repair direction:** Make the Page 11 shutter transparent/open-grilled enough for the node to remain visible through it, or place the node explicitly on the chamber side of the threshold before Page 12. Do not imply an unintroduced second node.
- **Role:** Writer

- **Location:** `script.md`, Page 12, Panels 1–2

## Continuity ledger

- **Alex’s visual identity:** Seventeen years old; brown skin; short dense black curls; dark work clothes; rust-brown patched cargo trousers; scuffed boots; canvas tool bag; scarred hands; Adrian’s oversized steel watch on the left wrist; no other wrist or hand accessory.
- **Bi11bot’s visual identity:** Rounded faded industrial-blue body, mustard stripe, amber eyes, rust-red scarf, exposed pistons, “Bi11” chest marking, non-menacing posture. His right eye ring clicks and rotates when focusing.
- **Starting relationship:** Alex and Bi11bot are already concealed companions. The script shows progression from hidden helper to physical partner without treating their relationship as newly formed.
- **Public water state:** Dry or failing on Pages 1–3; restored on Page 11. The restoration is temporary and triggers a system response.
- **Display meaning:** The display remains **AUTHORIZED ALLOCATION: NORMAL** before and after the intervention. Its stable implied meaning is authorized or upstream allocation, not delivered pressure.
- **Route:** Pump station → service trench and bypass breach → old mine regulator gallery → shutter threshold → colder side chamber. The bypass around the sealed entrance should be made explicit in the writing and layout.
- **Control system:** Old manual wheels and analog gauges coexist with newer grafted controls. The white-and-amber node appears as part of the basin-wide architecture and registers the intervention.
- **Adrian evidence:** The watch and equation pre-exist the chapter. The initials and maintenance mark establish Adrian’s presence or investigation, not his motive, loyalty, survival, or death.
- **Alex’s knowledge:** He progresses from tactile diagnosis, to discovering diverted flow, to recognizing the control pattern, to finding Adrian’s mark, to knowingly choosing unauthorized intervention. He does not learn who receives the alert or why the routing exists.
- **Bi11bot’s knowledge:** He notices inconsistencies and assists with machinery, but does not know his origin or the full purpose of the control network.
- **System response:** The node registers tampering; a pulse travels through the system; a local shutter begins closing. The responding authority or operator remains unknown.
- **Final physical state:** Alex and Bi11bot reach a colder chamber beyond the closing shutter, with a deeper route ahead and an approaching mechanical response. The node’s exact visibility from that chamber requires one staging clarification.
- **Page architecture:** Pages 1–9 and 11–12 stay at four panels; Page 10 is a one-panel splash. This satisfies the showrunner’s maximum of four panels per page and gives the override its required visual scale.
- **Character count:** No panel requires more than Alex, Bi11bot, and an incidental non-recognizable queue presence. No crowd must be individually recognizable.

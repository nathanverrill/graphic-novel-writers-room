Preservation

Diagnostics, never a gate. The output is kept either way.

Rules standing, binding

A fact the book must keep, in every file and every run: a name, a prohibition, a constant. Saved to rules/showrunner-rules.md the moment you add it; it binds the next update of the canon, a fresh start, and the writers after intake. It changes what the room writes next, not the files as they stand now.

Notes for the next revision

Not answers to any one question — how the book should read. Carried into the next update of the canon with the authority of a rule for that run, then spent. Never written into rules/.

Saved when you update the canon.

Start pre-production over?

The room reads the material again from the start. characters.md, world.md and story.md are written afresh from the source files, not revised from what is here, and the open items are raised again with the current list reconciled into them. The round you have now is kept under previous/.

Approve pre-production for production?

The canon is what the whole book is made from. Approving it:

  • copies characters.md, world.md, story.md and facts.md, and your drafts, onto the production desk
  • ends pre-production: production never reads your source material again, so what is not in these files, it does not know
  • moves the book to development, where Produce starts: the Director's brief, then the story and the people
  • replaces what production has on its desk now: its earlier brief, script and layouts are kept under previous/, and it starts again from development

Read the canon first: the premise and outline, the characters, the world. You can come back and change them, but production will not see the change until you approve again.

Rules standing, binding

A fact the book must keep, in every file and every run: a name, a prohibition, a constant. Saved to rules/showrunner-rules.md the moment you add it; it binds the next Update, a fresh start, and the writers after intake. It changes what the room writes next, not the files as they stand now.

Sprint tools

Eight standalone team apps from the Avalanche hackathon — everyone on the link works in the same one. Their output feeds the room: export, then drop the files into a campaign's input/ folder (Backstage → Files) and run intake.

Riffthe brainstorming room after the writers' room: chat with a campaign's material, accept refinements, compile one canon file, visualize it Easelimage chat: every prompt drawn by two models at once, iterate by reference, everything kept in a shared gallery Forgea character in seven answers — why, incentive, motive, weakness, change, return — on a hero's-journey wheel Spinea story on the Seven-Point structure, asked in sprint order — ends first, the choice last — drawn as the wall Stationa hard-SF Antarctic facility under the 80/15/5 rule, drawn as a station map of hub and modules Nemesisthe antagonist and the existential threat — and the convergence: who comes together and what each brings Personaone character bot built by everyone: knowledge files, a face, a voice tuned take by take Canonstory and research files on two shelves, and seven checks a model run marks pass / thin / fail, with receipts
Each app also runs on its own machine with two pip packages — see The sprint tools in the README. The links above are the shared team instances on Cloud Run.

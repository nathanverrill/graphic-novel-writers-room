Redrawing the art…

Art only (or B) hides the bubbles to check the drawing — click it to note what to change. Drag a balloon by its outline to move it — anywhere, across panels. Double-click one to edit its words in place. A selected balloon shows its dashed line to where its tail aims — click the speaker's head to aim it there (or drag the dot). Esc lets go. Drag a kind or a script line from the right straight onto the page. Delete removes the selection.

The art

In Art only, click the drawing where something's wrong and type what to change. Add as many as you like, then redraw once. Only the art is redrawn: the bubbles, words and tails stay exactly as they are, and the drawing before is kept.
